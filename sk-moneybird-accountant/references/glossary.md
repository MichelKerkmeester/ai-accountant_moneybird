# Glossary of Dutch terms

**Rule:** Every reference leads with the English term and adds the Dutch term in brackets at its first use in a section, for example "additional assessment (naheffingsaanslag)". This glossary fixes the one English name per Dutch term that the references use more than once. Labels it leaves out are in [the additional glossary](glossary-additional-terms.md), and `scripts/check-english-first.py` holds the references to both.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Dutch stays where it is the data: routing signals, Dutch test prompts, quoted official text, form and box names, legal citations, source columns and the bilingual yearly overview template. Everywhere else the English name below leads.

A note of `plain` marks an everyday word or phrase whose Dutch adds nothing, so it needs no label. Every other term takes the label at its first use in each H2 section. A note of `official name` marks the name of an authority, a scheme or a product. Keep the Dutch name in brackets after the English at its first use in the file, because the Dutch name is what the official pages use.
