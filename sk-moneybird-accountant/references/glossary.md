# Glossary of Dutch terms

**Rule:** Every reference leads with the English term and adds the Dutch term in brackets at its first use in a section, for example "additional assessment (naheffingsaanslag)". This glossary fixes the one English name per Dutch term that the references use more than once.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Dutch stays where it is the data: routing signals, Dutch test prompts, quoted official text, form and box names, legal citations, source columns and the bilingual yearly overview template. Everywhere else the English name below leads.

An empty note means a plain term. A note of `official name` marks the name of an authority, a scheme or a product. Keep the Dutch name in brackets after the English at first use, because the Dutch name is what the official pages use.
