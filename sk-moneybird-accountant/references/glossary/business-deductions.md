# Glossary of business deductions terms

**Rule:** Every reference leads with the English term and adds the Dutch term in brackets at its first use in a section, for example "supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag)". [The glossary router](README.md) says which file holds which terms.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Entrepreneur deduction terms: the hours criterion, the self-employed and starter deductions and the SME profit exemption. A note of `plain` marks an everyday word or phrase whose Dutch adds nothing, so it needs no label. A note of `official name` marks the name of an authority, a law, a scheme, a form or a product, labelled once per file. A note of `abbreviation` marks a Dutch short form that English text keeps as it is after the full English name. An empty note marks a term that takes its label at its first use in each H2 section.
