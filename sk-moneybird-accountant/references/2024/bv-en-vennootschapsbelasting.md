## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the vennootschapsbelasting (Vpb, corporate income tax) for tax year 2024 and the rules that come with a BV, including the rate table, verliesverrekening (loss relief), the generieke renteaftrekbeperking (generic interest deduction limitation), the innovatiebox (innovation box), the deelnemingsvrijstelling (participation exemption), the fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity), the gebruikelijk loonregeling (customary wage rule) for a directeur-grootaandeelhouder (DGA, director and major shareholder), Zvw, box 2, dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax), the conditionele bronbelasting (conditional withholding tax), the jaarrekening (annual accounts) size classes and the administratieplicht (record-keeping duty). The law for 2024 is enacted and every value below carries its Check tag. What matters most in 2024: the Vpb rates stay at 19% up to € 200.000 and 25,8% above it, the art. 15b percentage stays at 20%, the gebruikelijk loon normbedrag rises to € 56.000, box 2 becomes a two bracket rate, the conditionele bronbelasting now also covers dividends, and the jaarrekening size thresholds are raised for book years from 1 January 2024. The open points for this year sit in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Vennootschapsbelasting, eerste schijf (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

The Vpb over a 2024 book year is 19% over the belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) up to and including € 200.000. Art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 is built as columns: the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag above the column I amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The Belastingdienst tarieven page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting). The art. 22 series prints the same 19% and 25,8% for 2023 to 2026, so no change of art. 22 falls inside a 2024 book year. Where a book year does start before and end on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 apportions the tax as (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).

### Vennootschapsbelasting, boven de eerste schijf (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

For the part of the belastbaar bedrag above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8% of that part (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The Belastingdienst tarieven page states 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting). The top rate also sets the rate of the conditionele bronbelasting, because art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 takes the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01).

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)

The saldo aan renten (interest balance, interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000. For 2024 the percentage is 20% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021, 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024 and 24,5% in 2025 and 2026 (each year from https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst change pages for 2025 and 2026 state 20% for 2024, 24,5% for 2025 and 24,5% for 2026, and say that non-deductible interest carries forward (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026).

### Verliesverrekening (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)

From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and carried forward without a time limit, but in each year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). For 2020 and 2021 the older rule applies: a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixes the loss by beschikking (decision) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01).

### Innovatiebox, breuk (art. 12b Wet Vpb 1969)

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H of the amount, H being the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2024. The effective rate on those profits is 9%. Belastingdienst: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox). Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb gives a K/T part, K being the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel (threshold) of art. 12bc. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01).

### Innovatiebox, kleinere belastingplichtige (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring (R&D declaration) for the innovatiebox. Smaller means that the advantages plus the acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000, and that the net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, same in the 2020 and 2026 texts, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). A taxpayer who is not smaller also needs an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b.

### Innovatiebox, forfait (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

For a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years and who chooses the forfait, the amount is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000. The forfait applies only when that profit is positive (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox).

### Deelneming, drempel (art. 13 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

Art. 13 lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969 leaves the advantages from a deelneming (participation) out of the profit. A deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, with a 1/3 interest as the connected test (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). A geruisloze inbreng (tax-free contribution) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html).

### Fiscale eenheid, bezitseis (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)

For a fiscale eenheid the mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). Belastingdienst adds that the mother is a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or a stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie (or a comparable foreign form), that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both must have the same book years and profit rules and that both must be effectively established in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/). The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends the fiscale eenheid for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10).

### Verzamelbesluit fiscale eenheid

The verzamelbesluit fiscale eenheid is the Besluit van 2 april 2024, nr. 2024-186206, published in Staatscourant 2024, 11814 on 25-04-2024 and in force the day after publication. It replaces the decision of 2010 and the 2022 winstsplitsing decision. The besluit works through examples on loss attribution, among them a kleindochter and a hived-off Y BV (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html). Option rights: the fiscale eenheid ends when the option holder exercises and the mother drops below 95%, even with an immediate offer duty (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html).

### Gebruikelijk loon, normbedrag (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies and (c) the normbedrag, which is € 56.000 for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01). The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor (indexation factor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling (private use addition for a car), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). Belastingdienst page: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Gebruikelijk loon, vergelijkbaar loon

Amount (a) is 100% of the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). In 2020 to 2022 it was 75% of that wage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). For 2024 the 100% test holds (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).

### Gebruikelijk loon, ondergrens

The gebruikelijk loonregeling does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and in connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This floor sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).

### Gebruikelijk loon, escape (art. 12a lid 2 Wet LB 1964)

From 2023, if the highest of the three amounts exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). In 2020 to 2022 the escape worked on the 75% basis: if the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) made plausible that the highest amount exceeded 75% of the comparable wage, the wage was 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the normbedrag (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 notes that a vaststellingsovereenkomst (settlement agreement) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).

### Start-up relief gebruikelijk loon

In the statute of 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4 Wet LB 1964) a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies could use a wage of at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount (WML, statutory minimum wage), or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). Belastingdienst: the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. So in 2024 only starters who first used the relief for 2022 continue, and 2024 is the last year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the same Handboek: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).

### Bijdrage Zvw

A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw (Zorgverzekeringswet, Health Insurance Act), so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie (payroll records) and withholds loonheffing (payroll tax). A DGA who, alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (employee insurance schemes). For him the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw, and the BV withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw (income-related Zvw contribution) from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen (Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). For 2024 the bijdrage is 5,32% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Maximum bijdrage-inkomen Zvw

The maximum bijdrage-inkomen (contribution income) for 2024 is € 71.628. A wage above that amount does not raise the bijdrage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Werkgeversheffing Zvw (insured DGA)

A DGA who is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. For 2024 that is 6,57% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/). The FISIN page for 2024 says that DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Box 2, eerste schijf (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

Box 2 is the tax on the income from an aanmerkelijk belang. For 2024 the first bracket runs to and including € 67.000 at 24,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). In 2023 the box 2 rate was a flat 26,9%, and in 2021 to 2023 that flat rate applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Box 2, boven de eerste schijf (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

Above € 67.000 the box 2 tax is € 16.415 plus 33% of the part above € 67.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor. Individuals credit the dividendbelasting withheld on a dividend in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies credit it in the aangifte Vpb (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/).

### Dividendbelasting (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 puts the tax at 15% of the opbrengst (proceeds) in every version read, from the 2020 version to the 2025 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01). Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. Art. 6 lid 1: if the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85. Art. 9 lid 1: once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota (dividend statement) is due. Belastingdienst: a deelnemingsdividend (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the company is invited to file. A beleggingsdividend (investment dividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification decision) is applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen). On an inkoop van eigen aandelen (buyback of own shares) the dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html).

### Dividendbelasting, aangifte en dividendnota

The return and the payment of dividendbelasting are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. Once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota is due (art. 9 lid 1 Wet DB 1965) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01). A deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder needs no return when it is fully exempt, unless the company is invited to file. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/).

### Conditionele bronbelasting (art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021)

The tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969, so 25,8% for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01). Interest and royalties are in scope from the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01). Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividendbelasting, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01). The levy hits payments to a related body (a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (a profit tax below 9% or a place on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. Bronbelasting on dividends is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividendbelasting withheld on the same payment is credited (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/).

### Uiteindelijk gerechtigde, dividend stripping (art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965)

The dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text already holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01).

### Jaarrekening, grootteklassen (Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397)

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. From Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 the criteria for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, are: micro at most € 450.000 assets, at most € 900.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 miljoen, € 15 miljoen and fewer than 50 employees, medium € 25 miljoen, € 50 miljoen and fewer than 250 employees. The employee counts are unchanged. The old statute values of 2020 were € 350.000 and € 700.000 for micro, € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 for small and € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 for medium (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/). Section 7 records an open conflict on the micro amounts in art. 395a.

### Jaarrekening, grote klasse

The large class is above € 50 miljoen net turnover (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html). The KVK table prints more than € 51 mln, while Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 miljoen. The settled value is € 50 miljoen (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/).

### Jaarrekening, deponering (art. 394 Boek 2 BW)

The board deposits the annual accounts within eight days after the vaststelling (adoption) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01). Ondernemersplein: there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).

### Btw-eenheid, beleidsbesluit

The btw fiscale eenheid of art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking (decision), on request or given by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, was published in Staatscourant 2024, 38545 on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html). For a change of legal form to a BV: the new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which must go to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. For btw, a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer (entrepreneur) and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm).

### Koepelvrijstelling en BUA

Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. The beleidsbesluit does not cover the koepelvrijstelling (umbrella exemption) and the BUA (Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968), and the Wet OB article and the BUA were not opened in the sources for this file (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html). This item is carried in section 7.

### Geruisloze omzetting (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not to have ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets must be placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for the profit determination. The contributor may be credited for the IB, the premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. A contribution into an existing company is possible only when it already runs a business in the same line, and into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Selling the shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst (preliminary agreement) is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. Belastingdienst: request at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring (declaration of intent) with the Geleideformulier, and the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm). A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties (onderdeel 14.2 of the old Besluit). In the published case the request could be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment was irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst (transfer gain) was reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment decided. Standpunt adopted 27-06-2022, published 12-01-2023 (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/). The version checked here is the Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 24 October 2025, published in Staatscourant 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication, which replaces the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M. Its substantive changes against the old text include 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right that amounts to medegerechtigdheid), 3.4 (the holding approval widened to an indirect holding and to a new or activity-free daughter), 4.2.2 (letteraandelen besides cumulatief preferente aandelen, plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw bijdrage credit), 12 (a new eighth standard condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (the incidental tax advantage clarified), 13.2 (S&O approval in the voor-voorperiode) and 15.3 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html). A consolidated version is BWBR0051693, which the sources for this file did not re-read.

### Stakingsaftrek (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001)

Without the art. 3.65 facility a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays IB on the stakingswinst (termination profit), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra lijfrente (annuity) premium deduction applies. Stille reserves (hidden reserves) realised by a direct onttrekking (withdrawal) before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente (art. 3.129) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Aangifte Vpb en uitstel

A book year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. A broken book year: file within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending 31 December: file before 1 June, and one ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar book year and runs to 1 November, requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months, must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends and must give reasons. The pages for these steps are current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting).

### Bewaartermijn administratie

Art. 52 AWR (Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen) makes lichamen administratieplichtig (subject to the record-keeping duty) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the paper profit and loss statement. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968: records on onroerende zaken (immovable property) are kept for nine years following the year in which the use began, in effect ten years. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968: OSS records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction. The AWR wording is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).

### Vpb on a belastbaar bedrag of € 60.000, € 200.000 and € 300.000 (Calculated example)

- € 60.000: below € 200.000, so only the first bracket applies. 19% of € 60.000 = € 11.400.
- € 200.000: exactly the bracket, so 19% of € 200.000 = € 38.000.
- € 300.000: € 38.000 plus 25,8% of (€ 300.000 - € 200.000 = € 100.000). 25,8% of € 100.000 = € 25.800. Total € 38.000 + € 25.800 = € 63.800.

Figures from section 2. Source for the bracket values: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Art. 15b: a saldo aan renten of € 1.500.000 (Calculated example)

- Case 1, gecorrigeerde winst € 4.000.000. 20% of € 4.000.000 = € 800.000. The higher of € 800.000 and € 1.000.000 is € 1.000.000. The saldo of € 1.500.000 exceeds € 1.000.000 by € 500.000, so € 500.000 is not deductible and € 1.000.000 is deductible.
- Case 2, gecorrigeerde winst € 8.000.000. 20% of € 8.000.000 = € 1.600.000. The higher of € 1.600.000 and € 1.000.000 is € 1.600.000. The saldo of € 1.500.000 stays below € 1.600.000, so the whole € 1.500.000 is deductible.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Verliesverrekening with a 2024 profit of € 1.400.000 (Calculated example)

A loss of € 1.200.000 is carried forward from an earlier year. The 2024 cap is € 1.000.000 plus 50% of (€ 1.400.000 - € 1.000.000 = € 400.000) = € 1.000.000 + € 200.000 = € 1.200.000. The full loss of € 1.200.000 fits inside the cap, so the belastbaar bedrag is € 1.400.000 - € 1.200.000 = € 200.000. Vpb = 19% of € 200.000 = € 38.000. If the carried-forward loss had been € 1.500.000, only € 1.200.000 could have been set off in 2024 and € 300.000 would carry forward.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Innovatiebox with a voordeel of € 100.000 (Calculated example)

A BV has € 400.000 of other profit and a € 100.000 advantage from a self-made qualifying intangible asset.

- Without the innovatiebox: belastbaar bedrag € 500.000. Vpb = € 38.000 + 25,8% of (€ 500.000 - € 200.000 = € 300.000) = € 38.000 + € 77.400 = € 115.400.
- With the innovatiebox: the voordeel counts for 9/25,8 of € 100.000 = € 34.883,72. Belastbaar bedrag € 400.000 + € 34.883,72 = € 434.883,72. Vpb = € 38.000 + 25,8% of € 234.883,72 = € 38.000 + € 60.600,00 = € 98.600,00.
- The Vpb on the voordeel itself is 25,8% of € 34.883,72 = € 9.000,00, which is 9% of € 100.000. The box saves € 115.400 - € 98.600 = € 16.800.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovatiebox forfait (Calculated example)

- Profit before art. 12b of € 80.000: 25% of € 80.000 = € 20.000. That is below the € 25.000 maximum, so the forfait is € 20.000.
- Profit before art. 12b of € 200.000: 25% of € 200.000 = € 50.000, capped at € 25.000.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Fiscale eenheid of a moeder and a dochter (Calculated example)

A moeder BV holds 100% of a dochter BV, which is more than the 95% requirement, and both ask for a fiscale eenheid. The dochter makes a loss of € 100.000 and the moeder a profit of € 250.000. Tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, so the belastbaar bedrag is € 250.000 - € 100.000 = € 150.000. Vpb = 19% of € 150.000 = € 28.500.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Gebruikelijk loon and the escape (Calculated example)

- Case 1: comparable wage € 50.000, highest wage in the body or connected bodies € 70.000, normbedrag € 56.000. The highest of the three amounts is € 70.000, which exceeds the comparable wage, so the wage is the comparable wage of € 50.000.
- Case 2: comparable wage € 60.000, highest other wage € 40.000, normbedrag € 56.000. The highest amount is € 60.000, so the wage is at least € 60.000.
- Case 3: the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is € 5.000 in total. That is not more than the € 5.000 floor, so the gebruikelijk loonregeling does not apply.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### Zvw for an uninsured and an insured DGA (Calculated example)

- Insured DGA, wage € 71.628. Werkgeversheffing = 6,57% of € 71.628 = € 4.705,96, paid by the BV.

### Box 2 and dividendbelasting on a dividend of € 100.000 (Calculated example)

A BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to an individual DGA.

- Dividendbelasting: 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000 withheld.
- Box 2: the first € 67.000 at 24,5% = € 16.415, and the remaining € 100.000 - € 67.000 = € 33.000 at 33% = € 10.890. Box 2 tax = € 16.415 + € 10.890 = € 27.305.
- Credit: € 27.305 - € 15.000 = € 12.305 remains to be paid in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01.

### Dividendbelasting gross-up and inkoop van eigen aandelen (Calculated example)

- Gross-up: the company bears the tax on an agreed net amount of € 85.000. Art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the opbrengst by 100/85: € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000. Dividendbelasting is 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000, leaving € 85.000 net.
- Inkoop van eigen aandelen: the average paid-up capital on the shares is € 20.000 and the buyback price is € 120.000. The difference is € 120.000 - € 20.000 = € 100.000. Dividendbelasting = 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Conditionele bronbelasting on interest and royalties (Official example)

Belastingdienst page example: interest of € 100.000 gross, 25,8% withheld = € 25.800, net € 74.200. Royalties borne by the payer: a net of € 100.000 needs about € 134.771 gross. Check of the second amount: 25,8% of € 134.771 = € 34.771, and € 134.771 - € 34.771 = € 100.000.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Deelnemingsvrijstelling (Calculated example)

A holding BV holds 100% of a subsidiary, which is above the 5% deelneming threshold, and receives a dividend of € 200.000. The advantage is left out of the profit under art. 13 lid 1. The dividend is a deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder, so it is usually exempt from dividendbelasting withholding and no return is needed for the fully exempt payment unless the holding is invited to file.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Jaarrekening size class (Calculated example)

A BV has a balance sheet total of € 400.000, a net turnover of € 850.000 and 7 employees on two consecutive balance dates. € 400.000 is at most € 450.000, € 850.000 is at most € 900.000 and 7 is fewer than 10, so it meets all three micro criteria and is a micro legal person.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.

### Ruisende inbreng and the stakingsaftrek (Calculated example)

A change of legal form without the art. 3.65 facility is a cessation. With a stakingswinst of € 60.000 the stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime: € 60.000 - € 3.630 = € 56.370 remains as stakingswinst. The income tax rate is not in this file, so no IB is computed here.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Gebruikelijk loon normbedrag: € 51.000 in 2023 to € 56.000 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).
- Start-up relief gebruikelijk loon: in 2023 lapsed for new cases with 2021 starters continuing, in 2024 only 2022 starters continue and it is the last year for them, and the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- Bijdrage Zvw: 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen Zvw: € 66.956 in 2023 to € 71.628 in 2024 (same two FISIN pages).
- Werkgeversheffing Zvw for an insured DGA: 6,68% in 2023 to 6,57% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Box 2: a flat 26,9% in 2023 to t/m € 67.000 at 24,5% and above that € 16.415 plus 33% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Conditionele bronbelasting: 25,8% on interest and royalties in 2023, and from the 2024 version dividends enter the levy (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividendbelasting) at the same 25,8% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01).
- Uiteindelijk gerechtigde: the 2023 text to the 2024 text, which now reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01).
- Jaarrekening size thresholds: the old values applied by default in boekjaar 2023 with the raised values optional, and from boekjaar 2024 the raised values apply (micro € 350.000 and € 700.000 to € 450.000 and € 900.000, small € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 to € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, medium € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 to € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01).
- Fiscale eenheid: a new verzamelbesluit, Besluit van 2 april 2024, nr. 2024-186206, Stcrt. 2024, 11814, published 25-04-2024 and in force the day after publication, replaces the decision of 2010 and the 2022 winstsplitsing decision (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html).
- Btw-eenheid: the Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, was published on 10 December 2024 in Stcrt. 2024, 38545 and is in force from 1 July 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Unchanged against 2023: the Vpb rates of 19% and € 38.000 plus 25,8%, the art. 15b percentage of 20% and its € 1.000.000 threshold, the verliesverrekening cap of € 1.000.000 plus 50%, the innovatiebox fraction of 9/H and its forfait of 25% with a € 25.000 maximum, the deelneming threshold of 5%, the fiscale eenheid bezitseis of 95%, the dividendbelasting of 15% and the stakingsaftrek of at most € 3.630 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The BV runs the DGA wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. For a DGA who is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen the BV withholds the Zvw bijdrage on the net wage at 5,32% up to a maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 71.628 and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For an insured DGA the BV pays the werkgeversheffing Zvw of 6,57% (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Record the gebruikelijk loon test in the payroll file: the normbedrag of € 56.000, the comparable wage and the highest wage in connected bodies, tested on the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- On a dividend, issue the dividendnota when the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, file the dividendbelasting return and pay within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available, and credit the withheld tax in the aangifte Vpb (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen).
- Conditionele bronbelasting on a dividend is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividendbelasting withheld on the same payment is credited (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/).
- In a fiscale eenheid the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/).
- Keep the records for seven years (art. 52 AWR). Records on onroerende zaken for nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records for ten years. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the paper profit and loss statement (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).
- Deposit the annual accounts within eight days after the vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts and mark them as unadopted. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- File the Vpb return for a book year equal to the calendar year before 1 June of the next year, or request the online extension to 1 November from 1 March to 1 June (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting).
- On a switch to a BV: register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, request the conversion at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, expect a new btw-identificatienummer to pass on to EU suppliers and a new loonheffingennummer when there are employees, and note that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Micro size thresholds. Stb. 2024, 52 art. 1 raises art. 2:395a Boek 2 BW to € 450.000 and € 900.000, and KVK applies those amounts. The consolidated Boek 2 BW at 2025-01-01 and 2026-07-01 still prints € 350.000 and € 700.000 in art. 395a, while it prints the raised amounts in art. 396 and 397. The Nota van toelichting says the AMvB basis for 395a was missing and was to be added to art. 2:398 lid 4, which the 2026-07-01 text now does. The settled value used in this file is € 450.000 and € 900.000. A KVK or Justitie statement on the legal effect for boekjaar 2024 would close the point (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html).
- Large class turnover bound. KVK prints more than € 51 mln, Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 miljoen. Settled at € 50 miljoen, recorded here because the two sources differ (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/).
- Koepelvrijstelling and BUA. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. The beleidsbesluit does not cover them and the Wet OB article and the BUA were not opened in the sources for this file (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Zvw route for an uninsured DGA. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 paragraph 8.2.2 says the BV withholds the bijdrage on the net wage (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), while the Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/). This file follows the Handboek on the withholding route. A dated Belastingdienst statement on the collection route would settle it.
- Filing and deposit deadlines. The aangifte, uitstel and deponering steps come from current Belastingdienst and Ondernemersplein pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. The sources for this file have no 2024 dated text for those deadlines (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- Geruisloze omzetting version. The art. 3.65 conditions in this file are those of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 24 October 2025, published in Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication. The sources for this file do not contain the text of the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, that it replaces, so the 2024 conversion conditions in that older text are not verified here (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html).
