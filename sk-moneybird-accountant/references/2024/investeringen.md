# Investeringsaftrek en afschrijving 2024

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2024 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the investeringsaftrek (investment allowance) in its four forms, the KIA (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek, small-scale investment allowance), the EIA (energie-investeringsaftrek, energy investment allowance), the MIA (milieu-investeringsaftrek, environment investment allowance) and the Vamil (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, random depreciation for environmental investments), together with the ordinary and random depreciation of bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) in tax year 2024. It is written for a sole trader and notes where the checked sources describe the same instruments for a BV. The 2024 law is enacted. The headline change for 2024 is the bodemwaarde (floor value) for buildings: the 50% rule for a building in own use is gone from 1 January 2024 and one bodemwaarde applies, the full WOZ-waarde of the building.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The KIA is a deduction from the profit, granted per onderneming (business). A taxpayer with winst uit onderneming (profit from business) claims it by choice in the aangifte (tax return), and the Belastingdienst carries the same table on its vennootschapsbelasting pages, so a BV meets the same instrument. The deduction is calculated on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year, not per asset. The 2024 table has five bands. Nil up to € 2.800 of investment. 28% of the investment from € 2.800 up to € 69.765. A fixed € 19.535 from € 69.765 up to € 129.194. From € 129.194 up to € 387.580 the fixed amount of € 19.535 minus 7,56% of the part above € 129.194. Nil above € 387.580. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership, for example a vof or maatschap) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum. The Belastingdienst adds that each firmant or vennoot (partner) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst (profit share), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

Several assets never earn the KIA. Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (a treaty for the avoidance of double taxation), gronden (land) including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (land improvement), woonhuizen and woonschepen (dwellings and houseboats), personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992, vaartuigen of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, effecten (securities), vorderingen, goodwill, vergunningen (permits), ontheffingen and concessies, and dieren (animals). For the KIA only, art. 3.45 lid 2 adds assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties, and assets with an investment amount below € 450. Art. 3.45 lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. Source: [art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

No investeringsaftrek is granted on obligations between the taxpayer and members of the same household, blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members, heirs to an estate the asset belongs to, or a person holding at least one third of a lichaam (entity) and that lichaam. The Minister can disapply this. FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" (the BV of which you are a shareholder for at least one third) gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (in principle no right to investeringsaftrek). Sources: [art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years. At staking (cessation) of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking. This cap applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. Sources: [art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### KIA per-asset minimum (€ 450)

Art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes from the KIA bedrijfsmiddelen whose investment amount stays below € 450. The test is per asset. FISIN 2024 prints the same € 450. Such an asset can still be depreciated under the ordinary rules of art. 3.30, and a voorwerp van geringe waarde (low-value item) that is normally treated as a current cost is written off at once in the year of acquisition. Sources: [art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.30 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### EIA percentage (40%)

The EIA is available to a taxpayer who invests in a new bedrijfsmiddel that appears on the Energielijst. For 2024 the percentage is 40% of the investment amount. Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as voortbrengingskosten (production costs). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km. The EIA cannot be combined with the MIA for one asset, and it can be combined with the KIA. Where an asset already received EIA, no MIA follows on those costs, although Vamil remains possible. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The per-asset minimum of € 2.500 and the maximum of € 149.000.000 apply, and the investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

One research rule could not be confirmed this year: that a medegerechtigde (a person entitled to a share of the profit) or a geldverstrekker (a provider of money) cannot use the EIA or MIA. The fetched pages do not state it. It is listed in section 7 as UNVERIFIED. Source of the failed check: [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (€ 149.000.000)

Art. 3.42 lid 4 sets the maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA at € 149.000.000 for 2024, and FISIN 2024 prints the same amount. The checked sources do not state whether this ceiling runs per asset, per year, or over the combined EIA and MIA base, so no calculation here goes near it. A separate ceiling of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year is stated by RVO for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### MIA percentages (45% / 36% / 27%)

The MIA applies to a new bedrijfsmiddel that appears on the Milieulijst, with an investment amount of at least € 2.500 and within the maximum amounts. From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I (45%), codes starting with A or D to categorie II (36%) and codes starting with B or E to categorie III (27%). For 2024 there is no MIA change and the three percentages are again 45%, 36% and 27%. The MIA can be combined with the KIA and with the Vamil. The MIA cannot be combined with the EIA for one asset. Art. 3.45 lid 3 provides that for the MIA the exclusions of lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn MIA. Art. 3.45 lid 4 excludes, for the EIA and MIA, assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises, and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500. Sources: [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Milieulijst 2022, in force 1 January 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (€ 2.500)

Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b shuts the EIA and the MIA for bedrijfsmiddelen whose investment amount stays below € 2.500. FISIN 2024 gives the same € 2.500. RVO phrases the floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (at least € 2.500 per application), so the statute ties the amount to the asset and RVO to the application. Sources: [art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### Vamil percentage (75% willekeurig)

Art. 3.31 lid 1 provides that the costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven" (may be depreciated at will for 75 percent by the taxpayer). The rest of the cost follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that have not been used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil. RVO states that the total tax benefit of MIA and Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (can rise net to over 14% of the investment amount), and that page does not state a year. Sources: [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).

### Ordinary depreciation caps (10% and 20%)

Art. 3.30 lid 1 makes the yearly depreciation the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten (acquisition or production costs) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2 caps the yearly depreciation at 10% of the cost for goodwill and at 20% of the cost for other bedrijfsmiddelen, which means at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for the other assets. Lid 3 allows voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) to be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging. Lid 4 writes voorwerpen van geringe waarde that are normally treated as current costs off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging. The same text applies in every year 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Bodemwaarde for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1 allows depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde, and never for more than the difference. Lid 2 treats the building, its parts, the ground under it and the aanhorigheden (appurtenances) as one bedrijfsmiddel, with detachable werktuigen (machines) as separate assets. Lid 3 gives the 2024 rule: one bodemwaarde, the WOZ-waarde of the building. From 2020 up to and including 2023 the rule was the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (investment building) and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another building, so the 50% rule for own use is gone from 1 January 2024. Lid 4 takes the WOZ-waarde as the value set by beschikking (assessment) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text of the article also has lid 5 to 10 on co-owned buildings, ground owned by an unrelated party and related persons and verbonden lichaam, which the 2024 version as read does not show. Sources: [art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2027 text of the article](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold (€ 2.800)

Art. 3.47 lid 1 adds to the profit, when in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of those transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage that was taken. For 2024 the threshold is € 2.800. FISIN 2024 gives the same amount and the Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page confirms it with "in 2024 was dit € 2.800". Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling term (five years)

Art. 3.47 lid 2 limits the bijtelling to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken. Lid 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the onderneming (a), a change of destination that brings the asset under art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, for the KIA) or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, for the EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings the asset under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). Lid 4 counts the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair market value) as the transfer price in those cases. The Belastingdienst phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term. Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 2 to 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling, investment undone

Art. 3.47 lid 5 treats the undoing of an investment, and the receipt of a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding (a reduction, refund or compensation), as a disposal for that amount. Lid 6 brings under undoing the case that at least 25% of the investment amount has not been paid within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and the case that the asset has not been taken into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6. Source: [art. 3.47 lid 5 to 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap (€ 387.580)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001, which allows willekeurige afschrijving (random depreciation) on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship. URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the startersaftrek, starter's allowance) applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst als ondernemer. URWA art. 8 allows the willekeurige afschrijving "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground. The top of the 2024 KIA table is € 387.580. The checked material marks this cap as Corrected because it is read from the cross-reference and no fetched page prints the cap as a number, see section 7. The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is open in an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Sources: [URWA art. 7 and 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.34 and art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving, herwaardering period (five or ten years)

If the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf (forestry business) within the term, the asset is revalued to its normal book value and the difference is winst. URWA art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs, and art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen (sea vessels) of art. 10. Sources: [URWA art. 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil (3 months)

The investment is notified to RVO within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order for aanschafkosten, and within 3 months after the quarter in which voortbrengingskosten were made. Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst that is valid on the signing or order date applies. FISIN 2020 to 2026 all state "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)." Sources: [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### MIA and Vamil maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year (€ 25 miljoen)

RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. The fetched RVO page does not state a year, so the amount carries Status unknown in the figures table and is listed in section 7. Source: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example: starters willekeurige afschrijving on a truck

**Official example.** The Belastingdienst gives this example on its page for the starting entrepreneur. A truck is bought on 1 July 2024 for € 150.000,00, with a 7-year life and a residual value of € 10.000,00. The entrepreneur depreciates € 60.000,00 at will in 2024 and € 80.000,00 at will in 2025, which leaves a book value of € 10.000,00 at the end of 2025 against a normal book value of € 120.000,00 on 1 January 2026. Rental from 1 January 2026 falls within 5 years after 1 January 2024, so herwaardering to € 120.000,00 gives € 110.000,00 boekwinst in 2026, and a normal depreciation of € 20.000,00 in 2026 remains. The arithmetic: € 150.000,00 - € 60.000,00 - € 80.000,00 = € 10.000,00, and € 120.000,00 - € 10.000,00 = € 110.000,00. Source: [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

### Calculated example: KIA in the 28% band

**Calculated example.** A sole trader invests € 40.000,00 in machines in 2024. Every machine costs more than € 450,00 and no exclusion of art. 3.45 or art. 3.46 applies. Step 1: the total investment is above € 2.800,00, so the first band does not apply. Step 2: € 40.000,00 is above € 2.800,00 and below € 69.765,00, so the 28% band applies. Step 3: KIA = 28% × € 40.000,00 = € 11.200,00. The taxpayer can claim € 11.200,00 in the 2024 aangifte, next to the ordinary depreciation of the machines.

### Calculated example: KIA in the third band with the 7,56% reduction

**Calculated example.** The total investment in 2024 is € 150.000,00. Step 1: € 150.000,00 is above € 129.194,00, so the table gives € 19.535,00 minus 7,56% of the excess. Step 2: excess = € 150.000,00 - € 129.194,00 = € 20.806,00. Step 3: 7,56% × € 20.806,00 = € 1.572,93, rounded down. Step 4: KIA = € 19.535,00 - € 1.572,93 = € 17.962,07. Check: € 17.962,07 + € 1.572,93 = € 19.535,00.

### Calculated example: KIA nil at the edges of the table

**Calculated example.** A total investment of € 400.000,00 in 2024 is above € 387.580,00, so the KIA is nil. A total investment of € 2.800,00 is not above € 2.800,00, so the KIA is also nil. At € 2.801,00 the 28% band starts, and the KIA would be 28% × € 2.801,00 = € 784,28.

### Calculated example: KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

**Calculated example.** A vof invests € 70.000,00 in 2024. One vennoot has a € 10.000,00 share in that investment, in proportion to the (over)winst. He also invests € 10.000,00 privately in the same year. Step 1: add the vof investment and the private investment, € 70.000,00 + € 10.000,00 = € 80.000,00. Step 2: the table applied to € 80.000,00 gives the fixed € 19.535,00, because € 80.000,00 is above € 69.765,00 and at most € 129.194,00. Step 3: his own investment amount is € 10.000,00 + € 10.000,00 = € 20.000,00. Step 4: the fraction is € 20.000,00 / € 80.000,00 = 1/4. Step 5: KIA = € 19.535,00 × 1/4 = € 4.883,75.

### Calculated example: EIA plus KIA

**Calculated example.** A new machine on the Energielijst is bought for € 100.000,00 in 2024 and taken into use in the same year. The RVO notification is sent within 3 months. Step 1: EIA = 40% × € 100.000,00 = € 40.000,00. Step 2: KIA on the same € 100.000,00 is the fixed € 19.535,00, because € 100.000,00 is above € 69.765,00 and at most € 129.194,00. Step 3: total investeringsaftrek = € 40.000,00 + € 19.535,00 = € 59.535,00. The MIA is not available on this asset, because the EIA and MIA cannot be combined.

### Calculated example: MIA plus KIA in the three categories

**Calculated example.** A new machine on the Milieulijst is bought for € 100.000,00 in 2024. The KIA is the fixed € 19.535,00 in every variant. Categorie I, code starting with F or G: MIA = 45% × € 100.000,00 = € 45.000,00, total with KIA = € 45.000,00 + € 19.535,00 = € 64.535,00. Categorie II, code starting with A or D: MIA = 36% × € 100.000,00 = € 36.000,00, total with KIA = € 36.000,00 + € 19.535,00 = € 55.535,00. Categorie III, code starting with B or E: MIA = 27% × € 100.000,00 = € 27.000,00, total with KIA = € 27.000,00 + € 19.535,00 = € 46.535,00. A machine of € 2.400,00 stays below the € 2.500,00 floor, so it earns neither EIA nor MIA.

### Calculated example: Vamil 75%

**Calculated example.** A machine of € 100.000,00 is designated as a milieu-bedrijfsmiddel with a code starting with A, B, C or F. Step 1: 75% × € 100.000,00 = € 75.000,00 may be depreciated at will. Step 2: the remaining € 25.000,00 follows the normal depreciation schedule. If the same machine also earns the MIA, RVO states that the combined benefit can rise net to over 14% of the investment amount, and the MIA and Vamil combination is allowed, unlike an EIA and MIA combination.

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

**Calculated example.** A machine costs € 40.000,00. The 20% cap gives at most 20% × € 40.000,00 = € 8.000,00 of depreciation per year, so the machine is depreciated over at least 5 years. Goodwill costs € 50.000,00. The 10% cap gives at most 10% × € 50.000,00 = € 5.000,00 per year, so the goodwill is depreciated over at least 10 years.

### Calculated example: bodemwaarde on an own-use building

**Calculated example.** An own-use building has a book value of € 450.000,00 on 1 January 2024 and a WOZ-waarde of € 400.000,00 for 2024. Depreciation is allowed only while the book value stays above the bodemwaarde, and at most for the difference. Step 1: the difference is € 450.000,00 - € 400.000,00 = € 50.000,00. Step 2: over the remaining life the total depreciation on this building cannot exceed € 50.000,00. The 50% rule for own-use buildings is gone from 1 January 2024, so the 2024 bodemwaarde is the full WOZ-waarde.

### Calculated example: desinvesteringsbijtelling

**Calculated example.** A machine is bought in 2024 for € 40.000,00 and the KIA of 28% is taken, € 11.200,00. In the same year the machine is sold for € 5.000,00. Step 1: is the transfer price above the € 2.800,00 threshold? € 5.000,00 is above € 2.800,00, so yes. Step 2: bijtelling = 28% × € 5.000,00 = € 1.400,00, using the investeringsaftrek percentage that was taken. Step 3: net effect = € 11.200,00 - € 1.400,00 = € 9.800,00. If the transfer price had been € 2.800,00, no amount would be added. The disposal falls within five years after the start of 2024, and € 5.000,00 stays below the € 40.000,00 over which the KIA was taken.

### Calculated example: starters willekeurige afschrijving and the cap

**Calculated example.** A starter whose startersaftrek applies buys a machine on 1 April 2024 for € 300.000,00 and takes it into use in 2024. Step 1: the costs stay below the yearly cap of € 387.580,00. Step 2: the starter writes € 200.000,00 off at will in 2024. Step 3: book value on 31 December 2024 = € 300.000,00 - € 200.000,00 = € 100.000,00. If the machine is rented out, brought under a double-tax treaty or used in the bosbedrijf within five years after 1 January 2024, the asset is revalued to its normal book value and the difference is winst, along the lines of the official example above. Cap illustration: an investment of € 400.000,00 lies € 400.000,00 - € 387.580,00 = € 12.420,00 above the cap, so only € 387.580,00 of those costs can carry willekeurige afschrijving.

### Calculated example: payment cap for an asset not yet in use

**Calculated example.** A machine costs € 40.000,00, is ordered in 2024 and is not in use on 31 December 2024. The trader has paid € 15.000,00. Step 1: the investeringsaftrek for 2024 is calculated on the payments made, € 15.000,00. Step 2: KIA = 28% × € 15.000,00 = € 4.200,00. Step 3: the remaining € 40.000,00 - € 15.000,00 = € 25.000,00 of the investment follows in later years, when more is paid or the asset is in use.

### Calculated example: RVO notification timing

**Calculated example.** A koopovereenkomst for a machine on the Milieulijst is signed on 15 March 2024. The notification to RVO must follow within 3 months of that date. For voortbrengingskosten made in the second quarter of 2024, the notification is due within 3 months after that quarter. The Milieulijst valid on 15 March 2024 applies.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2023

- KIA table, old to new: 2023 was nil up to € 2.600, 28% up to € 63.716, € 17.841 up to € 117.991, € 17.841 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 117.991 up to € 353.973 and nil above € 353.973. 2024 is nil up to € 2.800, 28% up to € 69.765, € 19.535 up to € 129.194, € 19.535 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 129.194 up to € 387.580 and nil above € 387.580. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). The Belastingdienst phrased the 2023 window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" on the [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- EIA percentage, € 45,5% in 2023 to 40% in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.42 lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount, € 136.000.000 in 2023 to € 149.000.000 in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 4 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [art. 3.42 lid 4 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- MIA percentages, no change in 2024: 2023 was already 45% (categorie I), 36% (categorie II) and 27% (categorie III), and 2024 repeats those three percentages. The step from 36% / 27% / 13,5% to 45% / 36% / 27% took effect on 1 January 2022, not in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42a lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.42a lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html).
- Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold, € 2.600 in 2023 to € 2.800 in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 1 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.47 lid 1 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap, € 353.973 in 2023 to € 387.580 in 2024, following the top of the KIA table. Sources: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Bodemwaarde, old to new: 2023 was the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another building. From 1 January 2024 there is one bodemwaarde, the WOZ-waarde of the building, and the 50% rule for own use is gone. Sources: [art. 3.30a lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.30a lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Unchanged from 2023: the KIA per-asset minimum of € 450, the EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500, the Vamil percentage of 75% willekeurig, and the ordinary depreciation caps of 10% for goodwill and 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen. Sources: [art. 3.45, art. 3.31 and art. 3.30 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.45, art. 3.31 and art. 3.30 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Still running in 2024 from the 2023 temporary scheme: costs for which the obligation was entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in 2023 can be depreciated at will once, at most 50%, in the year of the obligation or the costs, and the asset must be taken into use before 1 January 2026, with that date moving by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. The scheme covers income tax and vennootschapsbelasting. Sources: [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the investment per bedrijfsmiddel with the date of the obligation, the aanschafkosten or voortbrengingskosten and the payments made in the year, because an asset that is not in use at the year end carries the investeringsaftrek only over the payments made and the excess follows in later years, and because at staking the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking. Sources: [art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Claim the KIA in the aangifte itself, as a per-onderneming choice on the total investment in the calendar year. Source: [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).
- Keep the RVO notification with the signed koopovereenkomst or the order confirmation, because the EIA, MIA and Vamil must be reported to RVO within 3 months and the Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies. Sources: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).
- Keep proof that an asset is new ("niet eerder gebruikt"), because a second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset earns no EIA and no MIA, while adaptation costs and self-installed parts can qualify as voortbrengingskosten. Source: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).
- Keep the WOZ beschikking for a building, because depreciation stops at the bodemwaarde and the WOZ-waarde of the calendar year of the depreciation counts. Sources: [art. 3.30a lid 3 and 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Track disposals, rentals and staking for five years after the investment year. Transfer prices above € 2.800 in one year add an amount to the profit at the investeringsaftrek percentage that was taken, rental counts for the KIA and not for the EIA or MIA, and staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term also counts. Sources: [art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Do not claim investeringsaftrek on an asset bought from a BV in which at least one third of the shares is held, because that obligation is excluded in principle. Sources: [art. 3.46 lid 1 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- For a BV, the same investeringsaftrek instruments appear on the Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting pages, including the yearly KIA, EIA and MIA changes. Source: [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- Keep the depreciation schedule per asset for the normal book value, because the starters willekeurige afschrijving must be corrected through a herwaardering to the normal book value if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf within five years, or ten years for zeeschepen. Sources: [URWA art. 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).
