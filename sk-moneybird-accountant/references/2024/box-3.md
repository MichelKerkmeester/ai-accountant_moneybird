# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2024

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2024 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Tax year 2024 uses the enacted category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3. The Belastingdienst calculates a fictitious return on banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden, and taxes the part above the heffingsvrij vermogen at 36%. For 2024 the percentages are 1,44% for banktegoeden, 6,04% for overige bezittingen and 2,61% for schulden. Green investments get a separate exemption and a korting, and a taxpayer may report the actual return when it is lower than the fictitious voordeel, also after the Hoge Raad ruling of 6 June 2024.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

### System 2024

Tax year 2024 uses the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3, which applies to everyone from 1 January 2023. The law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, brought the category method in place of the old band method of 2020 to 2022. The 2024 calculation follows six steps. Step 1 sets the belastbaar rendement at banktegoeden times the bank percentage, plus overige bezittingen times the other-assets percentage, minus the deductible part of the schulden times the debt percentage. Step 2 sets the rendementsgrondslag at bezittingen minus the deductible schulden. Step 3 sets the grondslag sparen en beleggen at the rendementsgrondslag minus the heffingsvrij vermogen. Partners may divide that grondslag in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 sets the aandeel at the grondslag divided by the rendementsgrondslag times 100. Step 5 sets the voordeel at the belastbaar rendement times the aandeel. Step 6 sets the tax at the box 3 rate times the voordeel. A negative rendement is set at nil. The forfaitaire voordeel is at least nil under art. 4.14 lid 1 and art. 5.2 lid 2. Art. 5.24 addresses peildatumarbitrage. Transactions around 1 January within a three-month period that starts before and ends after the peildatum are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons lay behind them.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Peildatum

The peildatum for tax year 2024 is 1 January 2024. The values of banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden on that date enter the six-step calculation. The same date is the reference point for the green-investment exemption and the groene beleggingen korting. The peildatumarbitrage rule of art. 5.24 uses a three-month window around 1 January. Transactions in that window are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons lay behind them.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Heffingsvrij vermogen

The heffingsvrij vermogen is € 57.000 per person for 2024. It doubles to € 114.000 with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17. The amount reduces the rendementsgrondslag to the grondslag sparen en beleggen in step 3. It does not apply to the actual-return route. The Hoge Raad has determined that the actual return does not take the heffingsvrij vermogen into account.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Box 3 tarief

The box 3 rate for 2024 is 36%. It applies to the voordeel from step 5. The tax is 36% of the voordeel. The rate is in the statute and is confirmed on the 2024 calculation page. The tarief interacts with the heffingsvrij vermogen and the category percentages. It does not apply to the rendementsgrondslag directly, only to the voordeel.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Schuldendrempel

The schuldendrempel for 2024 is € 3.700 per person. It is € 7.400 with a whole-year partner. Only the part of total debts above the drempel is deductible. The Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return. Statute art. 5.3 lid 3 writes the rule as "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...". The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns. The drempel enters step 1 and step 2. It reduces the deductible debt before the debt percentage is applied and before the rendementsgrondslag is set.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01

### Bank percentage

The bank percentage for 2024 is 1,44%. It applies to banktegoeden. The percentage is fixed after the year under art. 10.6ter lid 2 of the statute. The method is the sum of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice, divided by twelve. So it is an average, not a twelve-month sum. The regulation of 7 March 2025, published on 14 March 2025, replaced the 0,92% figure and applied back to 1 January 2024. The 1 January 2024 text of the statute therefore carried 0,92% until the regulation replaced it. The bank percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Other-assets percentage

The other-assets percentage for 2024 is 6,04%. It applies to overige bezittingen. The figure is in the 1 January 2024 text of the statute. Under art. 10.6ter lid 3 the percentage is replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation. The other-assets percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage for 2024 is 2,61%. It applies to the deductible debt, so only the part of the schulden above the drempel. The percentage is fixed after the year under art. 10.6ter lid 4. The method is the same as for the bank percentage, applied to the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The eleven monthly figures from 1 January to 30 November count, November is counted twice, and the sum is divided by twelve. The regulation of 7 March 2025 replaced 2,46% and applied back to 1 January 2024. The debt percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Aandeel rounding

The aandeel in step 4 is rounded to 3 decimals on the 2024 calculation page. The page's examples also use 3 decimals. The rounding matters because the voordeel in step 5 is the belastbaar rendement times the aandeel. For 2024 the taxpayer uses 3 decimals.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

The vrijstelling groene beleggingen for 2024 is € 71.251 per person. It is € 142.502 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 excludes green investments from the bezittingen up to that amount. The exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The exemption interacts with step 1 and step 2. Exempt green investments do not enter the banktegoeden or overige bezittingen. The groene beleggingen korting is then calculated on the exempt amount.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Korting groene beleggingen

The korting groene beleggingen for 2024 is 0,7% of the exempt amount on the peildatum. Art. 8.19 gives the rate. The Belastingdienst computes the korting automatically. For a whole-year partner the korting is split between the partners in the same ratio as the green balance. The 2024 rate of 0,7% applies in the period 2020 to 2024. The korting is a heffingskorting, so it reduces the tax rather than the voordeel. It interacts with the vrijstelling groene beleggingen, because the exempt amount is the base.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen

### Actual-return reporting 2024

The tegenbewijsregeling of afdeling 5.6 applies for 2024 under art. 5.25 and 5.26. If the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. The actual return is at least nil. Whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Art. 5.26 defines the actual return as all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus vermogensaanwas. Income plus value changes over the calendar year count, including changes during the year. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen is used. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. The form is the form provided by the inspecteur under art. 5.25 lid 6, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Further documents can be sent up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8 says lids 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. For 2024 the route is the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, "Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven". The 2021 to 2024 years may always be filed without a letter. Paper forms are available via 0800 - 023 01 07 for one form per call, and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. If a recalculation for 2024 leads to persoonsgebonden aftrek, it follows the partners' chosen split. Another split requires an ambtshalve-vermindering request.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte

### Hoge Raad 6 June 2024

On 6 June 2024 the Hoge Raad ruled that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. The cases are ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. The ruling interacts with the tegenbewijsregeling. A taxpayer who can make the lower actual return plausible can use that lower amount. The Hoge Raad ruling does not change the 2024 rates or thresholds in this file.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Single, savings only (Official example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 150.000, no other assets, no debts, no partner.

Step 1: banktegoeden € 150.000 x 1,44% = € 2.160. Belastbaar rendement is € 2.160.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag is € 150.000.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen is € 150.000 - € 57.000 = € 93.000.
Step 4: aandeel is € 93.000 / € 150.000 x 100 = 62,000%.
Step 5: voordeel is € 2.160 x 62,000% = € 1.339.
Step 6: tax is 36% x € 1.339 = € 482.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Example 2: Same with a fiscal partner (Official example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 150.000, no other assets, no debts, fiscal partner for the whole year.

Step 1: banktegoeden € 150.000 x 1,44% = € 2.160.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag is € 150.000.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen is € 150.000 - € 114.000 = € 36.000. The partners split it as € 18.000 each.
Step 4: aandeel is € 18.000 / € 150.000 x 100 = 12,000%.
Step 5: voordeel is € 2.160 x 12,000% = € 259 for each partner.
Step 6: tax is 36% x € 259 = € 93 for each partner.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Example 3: Single, savings, investments, second home and debt (Official example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 150.000, investments € 75.000, second home € 200.000, debt € 100.000, no partner.

Step 1:
- banktegoeden € 150.000 x 1,44% = € 2.160.
- overige bezittingen € 75.000 + € 200.000 = € 275.000. Return € 275.000 x 6,04% = € 16.610.
- aftrekbare schuld € 100.000 - € 3.700 = € 96.300. Return € 96.300 x 2,61% = € 2.513.
- belastbaar rendement € 2.160 + € 16.610 - € 2.513 = € 16.257.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag € 150.000 + € 275.000 - € 96.300 = € 328.700.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen € 328.700 - € 57.000 = € 271.700.
Step 4: aandeel € 271.700 / € 328.700 x 100 = 82,658%.
Step 5: voordeel € 16.257 x 82,658% = € 13.437.
Step 6: tax 36% x € 13.437 = € 4.837.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Example 4: Same with a fiscal partner (Official example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 150.000, investments € 75.000, second home € 200.000, debt € 100.000, fiscal partner for the whole year.

Step 1:
- banktegoeden € 150.000 x 1,44% = € 2.160.
- overige bezittingen € 75.000 + € 200.000 = € 275.000. Return € 275.000 x 6,04% = € 16.610.
- schuldendrempel € 7.400. Aftrekbare schuld € 100.000 - € 7.400 = € 92.600. Return € 92.600 x 2,61% = € 2.417.
- belastbaar rendement € 2.160 + € 16.610 - € 2.417 = € 16.353.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag € 150.000 + € 275.000 - € 92.600 = € 332.400.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen € 332.400 - € 114.000 = € 218.400. The partners split it as € 109.200 each.
Step 4: aandeel € 109.200 / € 332.400 x 100 = 32,851%.
Step 5: voordeel € 16.353 x 32,851% = € 5.372 for each partner.
Step 6: tax 36% x € 5.372 = € 1.933 for each partner.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Example 5: Partners with green investments (Official example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 5.000, green investments € 150.000, second home abroad € 250.000, no debt, fiscal partners for the whole year.

Step 1:
- banktegoeden € 5.000 x 1,44% = € 72.
- green exemption € 142.502 with a whole-year partner. Taxable green investments € 150.000 - € 142.502 = € 7.498.
- overige bezittingen € 7.498 + € 250.000 = € 257.498. Return € 257.498 x 6,04% = € 15.552.
- belastbaar rendement € 72 + € 15.552 = € 15.624.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag € 5.000 + € 257.498 = € 262.498.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen € 262.498 - € 114.000 = € 148.498, allocated to one partner.
Step 4: aandeel € 148.498 / € 262.498 x 100 = 56,571%.
Step 5: voordeel € 15.624 x 56,571% = € 8.838.
Step 6: tax 36% x € 8.838 = € 3.181.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Example 6: Korting groene beleggingen (Calculated example)

This example continues the inputs of example 5. The exempt green amount is € 142.502 because the partners hold € 150.000 of green investments and the whole-year partner exemption is € 142.502. The korting is 0,7% x € 142.502 = € 997,51. The Belastingdienst computes the korting automatically. For whole-year partners the korting is split in the same ratio as the green balance. This example uses only the 2024 figures from section 2.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen

### Example 7: Actual return lower than the fictitious voordeel (Calculated example)

This example builds on example 1. The fictitious voordeel is € 1.339 and the fictitious tax is € 482. Suppose the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is € 300. The box 3 income is then € 300 minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. With no persoonsgebonden aftrek, the tax is 36% x € 300 = € 108. The Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount, so the taxpayer pays € 108 instead of € 482. The actual return does not use the heffingsvrij vermogen.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Example 8: Negative actual return set at nil (Calculated example)

Suppose the actual return has € 200 of interest and a value loss of € 500. The net actual return is € 200 - € 500 = minus € 300. A negative total is set at € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. The box 3 income on the actual-return route is € 0.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the values of banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden on 1 January 2024. The six-step calculation uses those values. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Record the green investments on 1 January 2024 and separate green overige bezittingen from green banktegoeden. The exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- The Belastingdienst computes the schuldendrempel in the return. Only the part of total debts above € 3.700 per person or € 7.400 with a whole-year partner is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- The box 3 income and the 36% tax land on the income tax return. The heffingsvrij vermogen is € 57.000 per person or € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- For the actual-return route for 2024, use the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst. The route may be used without a letter. Paper forms are available via 0800 - 023 01 07 for one form per call, and several forms via 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement
- Keep documents that support the actual return. If the inspecteur sends the form, further documents can be sent up to six weeks after the form is sent. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If a 2024 recalculation leads to persoonsgebonden aftrek, the partners' chosen split applies. A different split requires an ambtshalve-vermindering request. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If the Hoge Raad ruling of 6 June 2024 affects the box 3 income, a letter follows. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
