# Box 2 en aanmerkelijk belang 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers box 2 and the aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) rules for tax year 2021. The year has one flat rate of 26,9%, with no brackets. The 5% test of art. 4.6 still has four grounds only, sub a to d, and the excessief-lenen (excessive borrowing) fiction for loans from the own BV is not in force, it starts on 1 January 2023. The rules for 2021 are enacted law.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### Box 2 tarief (art. 2.12)

Belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (taxable income from a substantial interest) is taxed at one flat rate of 26,9% in 2021. There is no bracket split in 2021. Art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each fiscal partner pays the rate on his own share of the income from the aanmerkelijk belang. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. FISIN 2021 belastingberekening also states 26,90%: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening. The two-bracket table with a higher rate above the boundary exists only from 2024 and does not apply here: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01.

### Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

The income from an aanmerkelijk belang is the reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs, plus the vervreemdingsvoordelen (benefits on disposal), minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang. Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds further groups. Sub a is the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling (exempt investment institution) or a foreign investment company. Sub b to d are certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction. Sub e is bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling. Sub f, the excessief-lenen fiction, exists from 2023 and not in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html. Four further points. Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available. Salary from the company is box 1 loon. Deductible is interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs. Dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing (withholding tax). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Vervreemdingsvoordeel, verkrijgingsprijs en onzakelijke prijs

The vervreemdingsvoordeel is the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price) minus the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price). The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Special cases are inheritance, a gift with or without doorschuiving (rollover), reaching the 5% threshold during the year and an onzakelijke prijs (non-arm's-length price). At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists the cases that count as a disposal even when no disposal takes place. The list is inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen (profit-sharing certificates), liquidatie-uitkeringen (liquidation payments), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid (activity), no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie, shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of shares that carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang under art. 4.40. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang (loss from a substantial interest) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years. Losses are offset in the order in which they arose. The loss is set by beschikking (decision) together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment and is open to objection. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.

### Losses from 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states the longer carry-forward period: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar". Such a loss is offset over nine following years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.

### Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and his partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a belastingkorting (tax credit). A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death. Lid 2 sets the percentage, 26,9% in the 2021 text. Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking, open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces the box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without an aanmerkelijk belang: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting. Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block the conversion, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/.

### Aanmerkelijk belang threshold (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly meets one of the grounds. Sub a: at least 5% of the issued capital. Sub b: rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital. Sub c: winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds. Sub d: at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a. The 2021 text has sub a to d only. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, covering a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01. Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, covering a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html. In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01. The Belastingdienst adds that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang. The meesleepregeling (art. 4.9) and meetrekregeling (art. 4.10) widen the belang. Once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that company also belong to it. Shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or the partner does also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang. The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners. Art. 4.7 to 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang. Art. 4.11 adds a fictief aanmerkelijk belang for shares or winstbewijzen that fall outside the other articles when art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. The text of art. 4.11 is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a are a fictieve vervreemding under art. 4.16 and not an art. 4.11 case. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. For a non-resident art. 7.5 limits the Dutch base to an aanmerkelijk belang in a company established in the Netherlands that is not part of an enterprise. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Minor children

The taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of his minor children, until the child turns 18. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Division of box 2 income between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen (common income components) of the taxpayer and his partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return. FISIN 2026 states that with a fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang may be divided and that "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner applies his own rate to his share. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang. The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount, see section 7.

### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount. The same amount is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares. This is what art. 4.12a carries. The facility does not carry the excessief-lenen fiction. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from this facility from 1 January 2027, see section 7: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf.

### Forfaitair voordeel vbi or foreign investment company (art. 4.14 lid 1)

The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year. It is reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, it is never below nil, and it is time-apportioned. For 2021 the percentage is 5,69%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

### Excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap

Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 was issued on 27 December 2022 and enters into force on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html. In 2021 the fiction is therefore not in force, so a loan from the own BV does not create box 2 income under these rules in 2021. From 2023 the fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the verbonden-personen rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01. In the 2023 text the maximum is € 700.000, from the 2024 text it is € 500.000. Debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. The debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Belastingdienst adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the drempel never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt.

### Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a lid 1 sub c)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of three amounts. Sub a is the loon from the most comparable employment. Sub b is the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies. Sub c is a fixed amount, € 47.000 for 2021. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. If the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the 75% of the comparable loon, the comparable loon applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel. The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Gebruikelijk loon comparable-loon test (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and which is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur. The test is 75% of the loon from that employment in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text. For 2021 the test is 75%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### Gebruikelijk loon de-minimis (art. 12a lid 6)

The gebruikelijk-loon rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year. This is lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts (lid 4 from 2023). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Start-up gebruikelijk loon (art. 12a lid 3 and 4)

For a body with an S&O-verklaring (R&D declaration) to which the verhoogd starterspercentage (increased starter percentage) applies, the loon was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable loon if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. The Check tag Corrected rests on the old description "the minimum wage". The statute says 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage.

### Start-up rule maximum duration

The start-up rule could be used at most 3 years. A first use in 2021 could run to 2023. The Belastingdienst states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen", and the rule is absent from the 2023 text. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### PEB afkoop and ODV conversion

Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen, Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017), governs the end of pensioen in eigen beheer (PEB, pension in the own company). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules as at 31 December 2016 for a remaining PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed prijsgeven (waiving) of the part of the claim above the fiscale balanswaarde, on the condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly afgekocht (commuted) or geruisloos converted into an oudedagsverplichting (ODV, old-age obligation). Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also a former partner who kept part of the claim. Art. 38o set the taxable loon on afkoop at the afkoopwaarde minus 34,5% of the fiscale balanswaarde, then 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual. Artikel VI ends all of this. From 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]". The Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen states afkoop or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019". So in 2021 there is no afkoop and no ODV conversion. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/. A remaining PEB keeps the old rules (art. 38n). If the scheme provided for indexation the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/. Art. 10a.18 lid 3 of the Wet IB 2001 keeps the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares unchanged for the 2017, 2018 and 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking (box 1). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html.

### ODV marktrente for the 2021 increase

The oudedagsverplichting is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation. Art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011 uses the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year. For the 2021 increase the marktrente is -0,382%. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/. A negative market rate must also be applied. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. In the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. The ODV can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente (annuity), a lijfrenterekening or a lijfrentebeleggingsrecht. It can also be paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5). This was added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01.

### APV loss deferral term

The APV rules defer the loss until a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang. The doorschuiving list for gift, inheritance and emigration was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

**Official example. Loss from 2021 converted into a belastingkorting.** FISIN 2022 and FISIN 2023 describe shares sold in 2021 with an unoffset loss of € 20.000. The taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang afterwards and requests conversion in 2023. Step 1: the loss is € 20.000. Step 2: the korting is 20.000 x 26,9% = € 5.380. Step 3: the remainder is usable until 2030. This fits art. 4.53 lid 4, the loss year 2021 plus nine years is 2030. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Dividend of € 60.000 from the own BV.** The BV pays a gross dividend of € 60.000 in 2021 and there are no deductible costs. Step 1: the dividend is a reguliere voordeel of € 60.000. Step 2: the belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang is € 60.000. Step 3: box 2 tax is 60.000 x 26,9% = € 16.140. Any dividendbelasting withheld is not deducted from the dividend but credited as voorheffing. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Sale of shares.** The shares are sold for € 200.000. The verkrijgingsprijs is € 120.000, notary costs included. Step 1: the vervreemdingsvoordeel is 200.000 minus 120.000 = € 80.000. Step 2: box 2 tax is 80.000 x 26,9% = € 21.520. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Onzakelijke prijs.** Shares are sold to a family member for € 20.000, while a third party would pay € 35.000. At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides. Step 1: the overdrachtsprijs is € 35.000. Step 2: if the verkrijgingsprijs is € 20.000, the voordeel is 35.000 minus 20.000 = € 15.000. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Forfaitair voordeel.** At the start of 2021 the shares in a vbi are worth € 100.000 and there are no other reguliere voordelen from those shares. Step 1: the forfaitair voordeel is 100.000 x 5,69% = € 5.690. Step 2: box 2 tax on this part is 5.690 x 26,9% = € 1.530,61. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example. Loss offset against the previous year.** A loss of € 20.000 arises in 2021. Box 2 income in 2020 was € 12.000. Step 1: offset against 2020: 20.000 minus 12.000 = € 8.000 remaining. Step 2: the € 8.000 is carried to 2022 to 2027, the six following years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example. Gebruikelijk loon.** The most comparable loon is € 80.000. The highest loon of the employees is € 50.000. The fixed amount is € 47.000. Step 1: 75% of € 80.000 = € 60.000. Step 2: the highest of € 60.000, € 50.000 and € 47.000 is € 60.000. The loon is therefore at least € 60.000 and it is declared in box 1, not in box 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. ODV with a negative market rate.** The ODV balance at the start of 2021 is € 100.000. The marktrente for 2021 is -0,382%. Step 1: the yearly increase is 100.000 x -0,382% = -€ 382. Step 2: the balance at the end of 2021 is 100.000 minus 382 = € 99.618. A negative market rate must also be applied. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.

**Calculated example. Excessief lenen does not apply in 2021.** A loan from the own BV of € 600.000 is outstanding in 2021. The excessief-lenen rules are not in force in 2021, so these rules add no box 2 income for 2021. The measure enters into force on 1 January 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.

**Calculated example. Inherited aanmerkelijk belang.** Shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer. Within 24 months after the death the heir receives a reguliere voordeel of € 10.000. On request the € 10.000 is not counted as box 2 income and is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs, up to the amount that was taxed with the deceased. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

- Box 2 rate: 26,25% in 2020 to 26,9% in 2021, a rise of 0,65 point. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss: 26,25% in the 2020 text of art. 4.53 lid 2 to 26,9% in the 2021 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Forfaitair voordeel vbi or foreign investment company: 5,28% in 2020 to 5,69% in 2021. The 1 January 2020 text printed 5,33%, replaced retroactively to 1 January 2020 by Stcrt. 2020, 20572, so the standing 2020 figure is 5,28%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount: € 46.000 in 2020 to € 47.000 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01.
- ODV marktrente: -0,107% for 2020 to -0,382% for 2021. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.
- Art. 4.6: no change in 2021, the text still has sub a to d. Sub e appears in the 1 January 2022 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- PEB: afkoop and ODV conversion remain closed in 2021, as in 2020, because Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115 ended them from 1 January 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html.
- Excessief lenen: still not in force in 2021, it enters into force on 1 January 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.
- Loss offset: unchanged, the previous year and six following years, with nine following years for losses from 2018 or earlier. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Start-up gebruikelijk loon: unchanged in 2021. A first use in 2021 could run to 2023 and the rule is absent from the 2023 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record a received dividend as a reguliere voordeel for box 2. Deductible costs reduce that income. Dividendbelasting withheld is not a cost, it is credited as voorheffing. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Record the overdrachtsprijs and the verkrijgingsprijs of sold shares. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs, selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs, and interest on a loan taken to buy the shares is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Keep the beschikking that sets the verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang. It is issued together with the assessment, shown separately on it and open to objection. The loss is offset in the order the losses arose, against the previous year and the six following years. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.
- Request conversion of an unoffset loss only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without an aanmerkelijk belang. In 2021 the korting is 26,9% and it reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- The gebruikelijk loon is loon for the loonadministratie and goes to box 1, not to box 2. The inhoudingsplichtige must be able to make plausible the amounts used in the test. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Divide the box 2 income of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner in the return, any split, as long as the total is 100%. Declare the box 2 income of minor children in the parent's return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- In 2021 the excessief-lenen rules put nothing in the box 2 return, because they are not in force. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.
- For a remaining PEB the company must index the accrued rights each year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision each year actuarially. The ODV is increased each year with the market rate, -0,382% for 2021. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/ and https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.

- The FISIN 2021 aanmerkelijk belang chapter URL returned 404, so the 2021 rate rests on the statute and on FISIN 2021 belastingberekening. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
