# BV en vennootschapsbelasting 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the vennootschapsbelasting (corporate income tax, Vpb) for a BV in 2021, the DGA (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, director and major shareholder) wage rules, dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax), the conditionele bronbelasting (conditional withholding tax), the fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity) and the switch from an eenmanszaak (sole proprietorship) to a BV. The 2021 Vpb rate is 15% for the first € 245.000 of belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) and € 36.750 plus 25% above that bracket, and the innovatiebox effective rate is 9%, up from 7% in 2020. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 All stated 2021 values are enacted. The only Corrected items are the dividend stripping article in the Wet DB 1965 and the Zvw route for an uninsured DGA.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### 3.1 Vennootschapsbelasting tarief

A BV that is subject to Vpb pays tax on its belastbaar bedrag. Art. 22 computes the tax as the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag above the column I amount. For 2021 the series is t/m € 245.000 at 15%, and above that € 36.750 plus 25%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The tax on a belastbaar bedrag of exactly € 245.000 is therefore € 36.750. The tax is reported in the aangifte vennootschapsbelasting. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen

### 3.2 Rates for a book year that straddles 1 January 2022

Art. 31 applies when a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect. The tax is then (X/B × To) + (Y/B × Tn). X is the number of days before the change, Y the number of days from the change, B all days of the book year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and the 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 A 2021 book year that straddles 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changes again on that date. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The 2022 series is t/m € 395.000 at 15%, and above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01

### 3.3 Generieke renteaftrekbeperking

The generieke renteaftrekbeperking (generic interest deduction limitation) of art. 15b lid 1 applies to a Vpb taxpayer with a saldo aan renten (net interest expense). The saldo is interest costs on loans minus interest income. It is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 30% of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The Belastingdienst change pages state that non-deductible interest carries forward. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026 The percentage drops to 20% in 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01

### 3.4 Verliesverrekening

For 2020 and 2021 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixes it by beschikking (decision). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 From 2022 a loss is set off one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 A 2021 loss that carries into 2022 therefore meets the new per-year cap.

### 3.5 Innovatiebox fraction

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H. H is the highest art. 22 rate, so the effective rate is 9% from 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Belastingdienst states: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%." https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox

### 3.6 Innovatiebox forfait

Art. 12bd gives a forfait to a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses it. The forfait is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000, and it applies only when that profit is positive. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.7 Innovatiebox smaller taxpayer

A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring (R&D notification). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years are below € 37.500.000 and that net turnover over those five years is below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.8 Nexus multiplier

Art. 12bb applies a K/T part. K is the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3. The outcome is capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 No amount for that drempel is stated in the sources used here.

### 3.9 Deelnemingsvrijstelling

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming (participation) out of the profit. A deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (art. 13 lid 2). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies (1/3 interest). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html For btw a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html

### 3.10 Fiscale eenheid

The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Belastingdienst adds that the mother is a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or a stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie (or a comparable foreign form), that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both have the same book years and profit rules and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.11 Gebruikelijk loon normbedrag

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies and (c) the normbedrag (standard amount). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 For 2021 the normbedrag is € 47.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling (company car addition), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.12 Gebruikelijk loon comparable wage

In 2021 the comparable wage counts at 75%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The escape of art. 12a lid 2 for 2020 to 2022 reads: if the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the normbedrag. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 From 2023 the rule reads: if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 notes that a vaststellingsovereenkomst (settlement agreement) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.13 Gebruikelijk loon floor

The gebruikelijk loon rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (lid 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01

### 3.14 Start-up relief for starters

Statute 2020 to 2022, art. 12a lid 3 and 4: for a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 Belastingdienst states that the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and that an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf Separate policy in the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.15 Zvw for a DGA

A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A DGA who (alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity) can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. For him the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw, and instead withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A DGA who is insured falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet For 2021 the bijdrage is 5,75%, the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 58.311 and the werkgeversheffing is 7,00%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet The FISIN page says DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if he is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

### 3.16 Zvw route for an uninsured DGA

The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025, paragraph 8.2.2, states that for the uninsured DGA the BV withholds the bijdrage from the net wage and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf This corrects the statement that the uninsured DGA pays the bijdrage himself by aanslag. The Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/

### 3.17 Box 2 rate

The box 2 rate for 2021 is 26,9% flat. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 An individual credits the dividendbelasting withheld on a dividend in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert

### 3.18 Dividendbelasting

Art. 5 sets the tax at 15% of the opbrengst, in every version read. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividendnota once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Belastingdienst states that a deelnemingsdividend (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. A beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification decision) is applied. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

### 3.19 Brutering

Art. 6 lid 1 provides that if the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares on an inkoop van eigen aandelen (buyback of own shares), also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

### 3.20 Conditionele bronbelasting

Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividendbelasting), so dividends are not in scope for 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 The levy hits payments to a related body (qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (profit tax below 9% or on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/

### 3.21 Dividend stripping rule

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01

### 3.22 Jaarrekening size classes

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 The 2021 thresholds are micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6 miljoen and € 12 miljoen and fewer than 50 employees, and medium € 20 miljoen and € 40 miljoen and fewer than 250 employees. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html These are the values stated as the values before boekjaar 2024. Stb. 2024, 52 raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, and € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts are unchanged. For 2021 the old values apply. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/

### 3.23 Jaarrekening deposit

Art. 394 requires deposit within eight days after vaststelling (adoption) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 Ondernemersplein states that there is no extension and that late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/

### 3.24 Stakingsaftrek

Without the geruisloze omzetting facility, a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays IB on the stakingswinst (cessation profit), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen The stakingsaftrek of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 An extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente (art. 3.129). https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html

### 3.25 Bewaartermijnen

Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. The wording is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 requires records on onroerende zaken to be kept for nine years following the year in which use began. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 require OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

### 3.26 Aangifte Vpb deadlines

Book year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. Broken book year: file within 5 months after its end. Short book year ending 31 December: before 1 June. Short book year ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen Online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting These are current Belastingdienst pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.27 Geruisloze omzetting

Art. 3.65 provides that on request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html Into an existing company is possible only when it already runs a business in the same line, and into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst (pre-agreement) is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html Standard conditions in the Besluit of 24 October 2025 (Stcrt. 2025, 37092, in force the day after publication on 4 November 2025, replacing the Besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M) include a rebuttable presumption that selling shares within three years after the contribution is part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html The contributor may be credited for IB, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html Belastingdienst states that the request goes to the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, that the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring is registered with the Geleideformulier, and that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm The Kennisgroep standpunt KG:212:2022:1 holds that the inspector decides by beschikking open to bezwaar that contains the conditions, and that a request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties. In that case the request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. The standpunt was adopted 27-06-2022 and published 12-01-2023. https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/

### 3.28 Btw fiscale eenheid

The new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm Btw fiscale eenheid (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968) arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking (on request or by the inspector) states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025". https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Calculated example: Vpb in the two brackets

Input: a BV has a belastbaar bedrag of € 500.000 in 2021.

Step 1: the first bracket t/m € 245.000 at 15% gives € 245.000 × 0,15 = € 36.750.

Step 2: the part above the bracket is € 500.000 - € 245.000 = € 255.000.

Step 3: 25% of € 255.000 is € 255.000 × 0,25 = € 63.750.

Step 4: total Vpb is € 36.750 + € 63.750 = € 100.500.

Check: the art. 22 column III amount is € 36.750, so the outcome matches the statutory series in section 2.

### Calculated example: book year straddling 1 January 2022

Input: a book year runs from 1 July 2021 to 30 June 2022 with a belastbaar bedrag of € 300.000. Art. 22 changes on 1 January 2022, so art. 31 applies.

Step 1: B is 365 days, X is 184 days (July to December 2021) and Y is 181 days (January to June 2022). 184 + 181 = 365.

Step 2: To under the 2021 art. 22 is € 245.000 × 0,15 = € 36.750, plus 25% × (€ 300.000 - € 245.000) = 25% × € 55.000 = € 13.750, so To = € 36.750 + € 13.750 = € 50.500.

Step 3: Tn under the 2022 art. 22 is € 300.000 × 0,15 = € 45.000, because € 300.000 is below € 395.000.

Step 4: tax = (184/365 × € 50.500) + (181/365 × € 45.000) = € 25.457,53 + € 22.315,07 = € 47.772,60.

### Calculated example: generieke renteaftrekbeperking

Input: a BV has a saldo aan renten of € 2.000.000 and a gecorrigeerde winst of € 5.000.000 in 2021.

Step 1: 30% of € 5.000.000 = € 1.500.000.

Step 2: the higher of € 1.500.000 and € 1.000.000 is € 1.500.000.

Step 3: the saldo exceeds € 1.500.000 by € 2.000.000 - € 1.500.000 = € 500.000.

So € 1.500.000 is deductible in 2021 and € 500.000 is not.

### Calculated example: verliesverrekening

Input: a BV has a loss of € 80.000 in 2021 and a profit of € 60.000 in 2020.

Step 1: one year back, € 60.000 of the 2021 loss is set off against the 2020 profit.

Step 2: the remaining loss is € 80.000 - € 60.000 = € 20.000. It carries forward to the six following years, 2022 to 2027.

Step 3: the inspector fixes the loss by beschikking, and set-off follows the order in which losses arose.

Check: the € 20.000 is below the 2022 per-year cap of € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000, so the cap does not limit this loss.

### Calculated example: innovatiebox fraction, forfait and nexus

Input: a qualifying advantage of € 100.000 in 2021. H is the highest art. 22 rate, 25%.

Step 1: the fraction is 9/25 = 0,36.

Step 2: the amount that enters the taxable profit is 0,36 × € 100.000 = € 36.000.

Step 3: at the top rate of 25%, the tax is € 36.000 × 0,25 = € 9.000, which is 9% of the € 100.000 advantage.

Forfait check: a profit before art. 12b of € 80.000 gives 25% × € 80.000 = € 20.000, below the € 25.000 maximum. A profit before art. 12b of € 160.000 gives 25% × € 160.000 = € 40.000, capped at € 25.000.

Nexus check: a qualifying expenditure of € 100.000 gives K = € 100.000 × 1,3 = € 130.000. The K/T part is capped at the whole advantage after the art. 12bc drempel. No amount for that drempel is stated in the sources used here.

### Calculated example: deelnemingsvrijstelling

Input: a BV holds 5% of the nominal paid-up capital of another BV and receives a dividend of € 40.000.

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves the advantage out of the profit, so no Vpb is due on the € 40.000. A holding below 5% is not a deelneming for art. 13 lid 2.

### Calculated example: fiscale eenheid

Input: a mother BV holds 95% of a daughter BV. The daughter has a profit of € 200.000 and the mother has a loss of € 50.000.

Step 1: the combined belastbaar bedrag is € 200.000 - € 50.000 = € 150.000.

Step 2: Vpb is € 150.000 × 0,15 = € 22.500, because the amount is below € 245.000.

Check: without the fiscale eenheid the daughter would pay € 200.000 × 0,15 = € 30.000 and the mother's loss would stand on its own.

### Calculated example: gebruikelijk loon

Input: a DGA works for his BV. The loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking is € 80.000. The highest wage of the employees of the body and connected bodies is € 55.000. The normbedrag is € 47.000.

Step 1: 75% of € 80.000 = € 60.000.

Step 2: the highest of € 60.000, € 55.000 and € 47.000 is € 60.000. The wage must be at least € 60.000.

Escape check: if the highest amount were € 70.000 and the BV makes plausible that this exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, € 60.000, but at least the normbedrag of € 47.000. The outcome stays € 60.000.

Floor check: if the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is € 4.500, the rule does not apply.

### Calculated example: start-up relief

Input: a starter in 2021 with an S&O-verklaring to which the starterspercentage applies.

The statutory minimum is 108% × 12 × the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount. No 2021 WML amount is stated in the sources used here, so no euro outcome is computed. The check is against the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. A first use in 2021 can run at most 3 years.

### Calculated example: Zvw for a DGA

Input: an uninsured DGA with a wage of € 58.311 in 2021.

Step 1: the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 58.311.

Step 2: the bijdrage is 5,75% × € 58.311 = € 3.352,88.

Step 3: a wage of € 80.000 gives the same outcome, because only € 58.311 counts.

Input: an insured DGA with a wage of € 58.311 in 2021. The werkgeversheffing is 7,00% × € 58.311 = € 4.081,77.

### Calculated example: box 2 and dividendbelasting

Input: an individual receives a dividend of € 60.000 from a BV in 2021.

Step 1: box 2 tax at 26,9% is € 60.000 × 0,269 = € 16.140.

Step 2: dividendbelasting withheld at 15% is € 60.000 × 0,15 = € 9.000.

Step 3: the credit in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting leaves € 16.140 - € 9.000 = € 7.140.

### Calculated example: dividendbelasting and the dividendnota

Input: a BV pays an opbrengst of € 60.000 to a Dutch shareholder who is not exempt.

Step 1: dividendbelasting of 15% × € 60.000 = € 9.000 is withheld.

Step 2: the shareholder receives € 60.000 - € 9.000 = € 51.000.

Step 3: a dividendnota is due and the return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available.

### Calculated example: brutering

Input: an opbrengst of € 85.000 where the company bears the dividendbelasting.

Step 1: art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the opbrengst by 100/85: € 85.000 × 100/85 = € 100.000.

Step 2: dividendbelasting is 15% × € 100.000 = € 15.000.

Step 3: the shareholder receives € 85.000.

### Calculated example: conditionele bronbelasting

Input: a BV pays € 200.000 of royalties to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction in 2021.

Step 1: interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, dividends are not.

Step 2: the rate is the highest art. 22 rate, 25% in 2021.

Step 3: the tax is € 200.000 × 0,25 = € 50.000.

### Calculated example: jaarrekening size classes

Input: a BV meets these criteria on two consecutive balance dates: assets € 400.000, net turnover € 800.000, 8 employees.

Micro: assets € 400.000 is above € 350.000 (fail), turnover € 800.000 is above € 700.000 (fail), 8 employees is fewer than 10 (meet). Only one of three, so not micro.

Small: € 400.000 is at most € 6 miljoen (meet), € 800.000 is at most € 12 miljoen (meet), 8 is fewer than 50 (meet). Three of three, so small.

Second input: assets € 6.500.000, net turnover € 13.000.000, 40 employees.

Small: assets fail, turnover fails and 40 employees meets the fewer than 50 test. Only one of three, so not small.

Medium: assets are at most € 20 miljoen (meet), turnover is at most € 40 miljoen (meet) and 40 employees is fewer than 250 (meet). Three of three, so medium.

### Calculated example: stakingsaftrek

Input: a ruisende inbreng with a stakingswinst of € 50.000.

Step 1: the stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630.

Step 2: the taxable stakingswinst is € 50.000 - € 3.630 = € 46.370.

Check: the stakingsaftrek is once in a lifetime, and an extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente.

### Official example: retroactive geruisloze omzetting to 1 January 2021

KG:212:2022:1 works a geruisloze omzetting with retroactive effect to 1 January 2021. The request was received on 15 December 2021 and the incorporation followed on 1 February 2022. The Kennisgroep held the request timely. https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/

- Dividendbelasting: 15% and the 100/85 gross-up are unchanged. The 2020 version of the Wet DB 1965 geldt 2020 to 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- Dividend stripping: art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 in both years, corrected from art. 4 lid 3. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

A BV records the DGA wage in its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. For a DGA who is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen, the BV withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw on the net wage up to the maximum bijdrage-inkomen and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For 2021 that is 5,75% up to € 58.311. An insured DGA falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw of 7,00% paid by the BV. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

File the aangifte vennootschapsbelasting. A calendar book year is filed before 1 June of the next year, a broken book year within 5 months after its end, a short book year ending 31 December before 1 June and a short book year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension to 1 November is requested from 1 March to 1 June and is only for a calendar book year. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. These rules come from the current Belastingdienst pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting

Record a dividendnota once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The dividendbelasting return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A fully exempt deelnemingsdividend needs no return unless invited. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking is applied. The shareholder credits the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting or, for a body, in the aangifte Vpb. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

Deposit the jaarrekening within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as such. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/

Keep the administratie for seven years. Records on onroerende zaken are kept nine years following the year in which use began. OSS records are kept ten years after the year of the transaction. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

For a geruisloze omzetting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting. The BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. The new legal form may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once, and a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm

In a fiscale eenheid the daughter files no separate return and the results of the companies are combined in one Vpb return. The daughter keeps existing and losses of one company offset profits of another. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- The Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 30 June 2010, in force through 4 November 2025, was not read. The 2021 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions in this file therefore rests on the successor Besluit of 24 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 37092. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle the 2021 text. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html
- Corrected: the dividend stripping rule is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not art. 4 lid 3. The rule can be read in the dated texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- Corrected route for the Zvw bijdrage of an uninsured DGA: the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 paragraph 8.2.2 states that the BV withholds the bijdrage on net wage and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. The Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/
- Proposed law with no effect on 2021: Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022) changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j in Article XIX, and deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 in Article XX. It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- The 2026-01-01 text of the Wet Vpb 1969 notes that changes to art. 22 without an entry date exist. No amount or date for those changes is given, and they do not affect the 2021 rate. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01
- No further conflicts for 2021.
