# Investeringsaftrek en afschrijving 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2021 investment allowances, the KIA (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek, small-scale investment allowance), the EIA (energie-investeringsaftrek, energy investment allowance), the MIA (milieu-investeringsaftrek, environmental investment allowance) and the Vamil, plus the per-asset exclusions and minimums, the payment cap, the desinvesteringsbijtelling (disposal addition to profit) and the depreciation rules for bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) of a sole trader who may also own a BV. What matters most in 2021 is the KIA table with a top of € 328.721, the EIA of 45,5%, the MIA percentages still at 36%, 27% and 13,5%, the 3-month RVO notification for EIA, MIA and Vamil, and the starters willekeurige afschrijving (free depreciation for starting entrepreneurs) whose yearly cap equals the top of the KIA table. The year is fully enacted law. The values come from the Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001 as it read for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) and from the Belastingdienst FISIN 2021 chapter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), and the cross-year rules of the checked sources apply in full (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The KIA (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek, small-scale investment allowance) is a deduction per onderneming (business), claimed by choice in the aangifte (tax return), on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2021 the table has five bands (art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). An investment up to € 2.400 gives no KIA. An investment from € 2.400 up to € 59.170 gives 28% of the investment. An investment from € 59.170 up to € 109.574 gives the fixed amount of € 16.568. An investment from € 109.574 up to € 328.721 gives € 16.568 minus 7,56% of the part above € 109.574. An investment above € 328.721 gives no KIA.

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by that sum (art. 3.41 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3). The Belastingdienst adds that each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst (profit), and that another reasonable key is allowed if applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

Two limits work on the base before the table is applied. An asset with an investment amount below € 450 counts for nothing (art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), and an asset that is not in use at the end of the calendar year counts only for the payments made (art. 3.44 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44, see the payment cap subsection below).

### KIA per-asset minimum (€ 450)

A bedrijfsmiddel with an investment amount below € 450 does not count for the KIA (art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same lid 2 also excludes, for the KIA only, assets that are intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties.

For every investeringsaftrek, art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for certain uses. The list is: use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (double taxation relief) in onderdeel a, gronden (land) including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (soil improvement) in b, woonhuizen (dwellings) and woonschepen (houseboats) in c, personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 in d, vaartuigen (vessels) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b in e, effecten (securities), vorderingen (receivables), goodwill, vergunningen, ontheffingen and concessies in f, and dieren (animals) in g (art. 3.45, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The Minister may disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a (lid 5). The same amounts hold in every version from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### EIA percentage (45,5%)

The EIA (energie-investeringsaftrek, energy investment allowance) is 45,5% of the investment for 2021 (art. 3.42 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The scheme is administered by RVO, and the asset must be on the Energielijst (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that have not been used before. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, as can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer, as voortbrengingskosten (production costs). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset, and there is no MIA on costs that already received EIA, while Vamil remains possible on such an asset. EIA can be combined with the KIA on the same asset. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). The € 2.500 per-asset minimum applies, the maximum investment amount of € 126.000.000 applies, and the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months (the details are in the subsections below). The rule that a medegerechtigde or a geldverstrekker cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages and is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (€ 126.000.000)

For 2021 the EIA and the MIA have a maximum investment amount of € 126.000.000 (art. 3.42 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The allowance percentage is applied within that amount. RVO states a further maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). That RVO page does not state the year of the € 25 miljoen maximum, so for 2021 the amount is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### MIA percentages (36%, 27% and 13,5%)

For 2021 the MIA (milieu-investeringsaftrek, environmental investment allowance) percentages are 36% for categorie I, 27% for categorie II and 13,5% for categorie III (art. 3.42a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must be new and must be on the Milieulijst. MIA and Vamil can be combined on the same asset, MIA and EIA cannot (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Under art. 3.45 lid 3 the MIA can still be earned on woonhuizen and personenauto's, which art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d exclude from the other investeringsaftrekken (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Under art. 3.45 lid 4 the € 2.500 minimum applies and assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises are excluded, unless the Minister disapplies that onderdeel a (see below).

The increase of the MIA percentages to 45% for categorie I, 36% for categorie II and 27% for categorie III takes effect on 1 January 2022, so it does not apply to a 2021 investment (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). The Milieulijst 2022 regulation that maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I, A or D to categorie II and B or E to categorie III belongs to those 2022 rules too and is in force from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). Which code in the 2021 Milieulijst maps to which categorie is not in the checked sources (section 7).

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (€ 2.500)

An asset with an investment amount below € 2.500 earns no EIA and no MIA (art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same lid 4 onderdeel a excludes assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises, unless the Minister disapplies that onderdeel (lid 5). RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The checked FISIN 2020 chapter phrased the EIA and MIA minimum as an amount per bedrijfsmiddel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute ties the amount to the investment in the asset, while RVO ties the floor to the application.

### Excluded obligations, including assets bought from an own BV (art. 3.46)

No investeringsaftrek is given for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam (body) and that lichaam (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 (lid 2), which is the ontheffing (exemption) route (art. 3.46, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46). The FISIN chapter phrases buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" as giving "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). For a sole trader who also holds at least one third of a BV, an asset bought from that BV therefore in principle gives no investeringsaftrek, with the ministerial ontheffing as the exception route. The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Vamil percentage (75% willekeurig)

The costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", and the rest follows the normal depreciation schedule (art. 3.31 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, have not been used before and materially reduce environmental harm can be designated, and the asset must be on the Milieulijst (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). RVO states: "Uw totale belastingvoordeel (MIA en Vamil) kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Ordinary depreciation caps (goodwill 10%, other 20%)

Yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffingskosten (acquisition costs) or voortbrengingskosten attributable to the year (art. 3.30 lid 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost may be depreciated for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for other bedrijfsmiddelen (lid 2). Voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging (lid 3). Voorwerpen van geringe waarde (low value items) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (lid 4). The same text holds in 2020 to 2027 (art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The caps run next to the investeringsaftrekken. The KIA, EIA and MIA lower the profit, and Vamil changes the depreciation schedule by allowing 75% of the cost to be depreciated at will while the remaining part follows the normal schedule.

### Bodemwaarde (WOZ-waarde, 50% for own use)

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde (floor value), and at most up to the difference (art. 3.30a lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden (appurtenances) count as one bedrijfsmiddel, while detachable werktuigen count separately (lid 2). For 2021 the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (building held as an investment) and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another, own use, building (lid 3). The WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking (assessment) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (lid 4). The 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 texts have these two bodemwaarden, and from 2024 there is one bodemwaarde, the WOZ-waarde of the building. The 2021 and 2022 texts were read directly and both print the 50% rule (art. 3.30a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Vamil assets are not subject to the bodemwaarde limit (see the Vamil subsection above).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling (threshold € 2.400)

If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices of more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to the profit (art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The threshold for 2021 is € 2.400, and FISIN 2021 prints the threshold from the other side as "€ 2.400 of minder". The bijtelling applies only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken (lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).

Lid 3 treats as a disposal: withdrawing the good from the onderneming (a), changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, for the KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, for the EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). In those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair value) counts as the transfer price (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding (reduction, refund or compensation), counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7). The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling). The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use and staking (art. 3.44)

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (lid 1). At staking (termination) of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking (lid 2). The rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA (art. 3.44, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44). The FISIN 2020 chapter phrases the same rule as "tot het bedrag dat in het kalenderjaar is betaald" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving (cap € 328.721)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 of the Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001 for willekeurige afschrijving (free depreciation) on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.34). URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3, the startersaftrek (starter's allowance), applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst als ondernemer (profit as an entrepreneur). Art. 8 allows willekeurige afschrijving for the joint amount of those costs in the calendar year up to the maximum amount in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table over which KIA can be obtained, and not for assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. For 2021 that maximum is the top of the KIA table, € 328.721 (URWA art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, read with https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2). Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs, and art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen (sea-going vessels) of art. 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is for an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek can be used, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). Herwaardering (revaluation) to the normal book value, with the difference counted as winst, applies within 5 years (10 years for zeeschepen) if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf (forestry) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). The cap of € 328.721 rests on reading the URWA art. 8 cross-reference to the top of the KIA table, and no primary page prints the cap as a number (section 7).

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil (3 months)

An investment is reported within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order for aanschafkosten (acquisition costs), and within 3 months after the quarter in which voortbrengingskosten were made (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines, and the Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). FISIN 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The notification goes to RVO, and the deduction itself is claimed in the aangifte.

### MIA and Vamil maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The page does not state the year of that maximum, so for 2021 the amount is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving (none found for 2021)

No temporary willekeurige afschrijving scheme was found for 2021 (status unknown, check UNVERIFIED). The Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html). The URWA 2025 text lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. The 2023 scheme itself sits in URWA art. 13 to 15, covers assets with obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in calendar year 2023 that are taken into use before 1 January 2026, and allows at most 50% of the costs to be depreciated at will, so it is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All inputs below are invented and round. Calculated examples are built from the figures in section 2, with every step written out.

### Official example: starters willekeurige afschrijving on a truck

`Official example` (the second example on the Belastingdienst page, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). A truck is bought on 1 July 2021 for € 150.000. In 2021 € 60.000 is randomly depreciated and in 2022 € 80.000. Together that is € 60.000 + € 80.000 = € 140.000 of the € 150.000 cost, below the € 328.721 yearly cap. At the end of 2025 the book value is € 10.000 against a normal book value of € 60.000. The truck is rented out from 10 January 2026. That date falls outside 5 years after 1 January 2021, so no herwaardering applies.

### Calculated example: KIA in the 28% band

Input: total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2021 is € 50.000, each asset at least € 450, no excluded assets.
Step 1: € 50.000 is more than € 2.400 and less than € 59.170.
Step 2: the table gives 28% of the investment.
Step 3: KIA = 28% × € 50.000 = € 14.000.

At the edges: a total investment of € 2.000 gives KIA nil, because it stays below € 2.400. A total investment of € 400.000 gives KIA nil, because it is above € 328.721.

### Calculated example: KIA in the phase-out band

Input: total investment in 2021 is € 150.000.
Step 1: € 150.000 is more than € 109.574 and less than € 328.721, so the fourth band applies.
Step 2: the part above € 109.574 is € 150.000 minus € 109.574 = € 40.426.
Step 3: 7,56% of € 40.426 is 0,0756 × € 40.426 = € 3.056,2056, rounded to € 3.056,21.
Step 4: KIA = € 16.568 minus € 3.056,21 = € 13.511,79.

### Calculated example: KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

Input: the samenwerkingsverband invests € 60.000 in 2021 and the taxpayer's own investments are € 20.000.
Step 1: the sum is € 60.000 + € 20.000 = € 80.000.
Step 2: € 80.000 is more than € 59.170 and less than € 109.574, so the table amount for the sum is € 16.568.
Step 3: the taxpayer's own share is € 16.568 × (€ 20.000 / € 80.000) = € 16.568 × 0,25 = € 4.142.

### Calculated example: EIA at 45,5%

Input: one new machine on the Energielijst, bought in 2021, cost € 100.000, above the € 2.500 minimum and far below the € 126.000.000 maximum.
Step 1: EIA = 45,5% × € 100.000.
Step 2: 0,455 × € 100.000 = € 45.500.

### Calculated example: MIA in each categorie

Input: three assets on the Milieulijst of € 100.000 each, one per categorie.
Step 1: categorie I gives 36% × € 100.000 = € 36.000.
Step 2: categorie II gives 27% × € 100.000 = € 27.000.
Step 3: categorie III gives 13,5% × € 100.000 = € 13.500.

### Calculated example: KIA and MIA on the same asset

Input: total 2021 investments are € 40.000. One Milieulijst asset in categorie II costs € 30.000, and a machine without EIA, MIA or Vamil costs € 10.000.
Step 1: the KIA runs on the total of € 40.000. That is more than € 2.400 and less than € 59.170, so 28% × € 40.000 = € 11.200.
Step 2: the MIA runs on the € 30.000 asset in categorie II, so 27% × € 30.000 = € 8.100.
Step 3: the two deductions together are € 11.200 + € 8.100 = € 19.300.

### Calculated example: Vamil at 75%

Input: a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel bought in 2021 for € 60.000. The taxpayer chooses Vamil and no MIA.
Step 1: 75% of € 60.000 = € 45.000 may be depreciated at will in 2021.
Step 2: the remaining cost is € 60.000 minus € 45.000 = € 15.000, which follows the normal schedule.
Step 3: the ordinary cap for other bedrijfsmiddelen is 20% of the cost per year, so 20% × € 60.000 = € 12.000 per year.
Step 4: writing off € 15.000 at € 12.000 per year takes at least 2 years, for example € 12.000 in the next year and € 3.000 in the year after.

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

Input A: a machine costing € 24.000. The cap is 20% × € 24.000 = € 4.800 per year, so at least 5 years (5 × € 4.800 = € 24.000).
Input B: goodwill costing € 50.000. The cap is 10% × € 50.000 = € 5.000 per year, so at least 10 years (10 × € 5.000 = € 50.000).
Input C: voorwerpen van geringe waarde that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition.

### Calculated example: bodemwaarde

Input A: an own use building with a WOZ-waarde for 2021 of € 500.000. The bodemwaarde is 50% × € 500.000 = € 250.000. The book value is € 300.000, so the maximum depreciation for the year is € 300.000 minus € 250.000 = € 50.000.
Input B: a gebouw ter belegging with a WOZ-waarde of € 500.000. The bodemwaarde is the full WOZ-waarde of € 500.000. The book value is € 520.000, so the maximum depreciation for the year is € 520.000 minus € 500.000 = € 20.000.

### Calculated example: desinvesteringsbijtelling

Input: a machine bought in 2021 for € 20.000, on which a KIA of 28% was taken, so € 5.600. The machine is sold in a later year within the five-year term, say 2023, for € 12.000.
Step 1: the disposal is within five years after 1 January 2021, so the bijtelling applies (art. 3.47 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).
Step 2: the transfer price of € 12.000 is more than the € 2.400 threshold.
Step 3: the bijtelling is 28% × € 12.000 = € 3.360, added to the profit of the year of disposal.
Step 4: the cap of the investment amount over which aftrek was taken is € 20.000, and € 3.360 stays below it.

### Calculated example: payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Input: a machine is ordered in 2021 for € 100.000. In 2021 € 40.000 is paid and the machine is not in use on 31 December 2021.
Step 1: art. 3.44 lid 1 gives a deduction over the payments made, so the base for 2021 is € 40.000, not € 100.000.
Step 2: the excess is € 100.000 minus € 40.000 = € 60.000 and follows in later years.
Step 3: if the € 40.000 base falls in the 28% KIA band, the KIA for 2021 is 28% × € 40.000 = € 11.200.

### Calculated example: starters willekeurige afschrijving cap

Input: assets with obligations entered in 2021, a year in which the startersaftrek applies, total costs € 350.000.
Step 1: the yearly cap is the top of the 2021 KIA table, € 328.721.
Step 2: willekeurige afschrijving is possible up to € 328.721.
Step 3: the remainder is € 350.000 minus € 328.721 = € 21.279, which follows the normal schedule.

### Calculated example: RVO notification deadline

Input: the order for an Energielijst asset is placed on 15 March 2021.
Step 1: the notification must be made within 3 months after placing the order.
Step 2: 15 March 2021 plus 3 months is 15 June 2021.

For 2021 the 28% band ends at € 59.170, the fixed amount is € 16.568, the phase-out starts above € 109.574 and the table ends at € 328.721 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap: € 323.544 in 2020 to € 328.721 in 2021, both being the top of the KIA table of the year (URWA art. 8 read with the KIA table, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).

- Ordinary depreciation caps: goodwill 10% per year and other bedrijfsmiddelen 20% per year in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2).

FISIN 2021 prints "€ 2.400 of minder", which matches the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record per bedrijfsmiddel the date of the koopovereenkomst or the order, the cost, the amount paid in the year and the date the asset is taken into use. The 3-month RVO notification runs from the signing or order date, the Milieulijst valid on that date applies, and the 25% within twelve months and three-year use tests in art. 3.47 lid 6 use the obligation date and the investment year (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).
- Keep the RVO notification and the RVO decision with the investment records. The deduction itself is claimed in the aangifte, and the KIA is a per-onderneming deduction claimed by choice on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). FISIN 2021 repeats that the investment is reported to RVO within 3 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Mark per asset which regeling is used: KIA alone, KIA with EIA, KIA with MIA, or MIA with Vamil. EIA and MIA cannot be combined on one asset, and MIA is not available on costs that already received EIA (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).
- When an asset is not yet in use at year end, record the payments made, because the 2021 deduction is limited to those payments (art. 3.44, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44).
- Watch disposals, rental and staking within five years after the start of the investment year. Transfer prices above € 2.400 in a calendar year lead to a desinvesteringsbijtelling at the percentage of the aftrek taken, and the Belastingdienst page counts rental for the KIA only (art. 3.47 lid 1 and lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Sole trader: the investeringsaftrek is claimed in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, as a deduction per onderneming on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Sole trader with a BV: when the taxpayer holds at least one third of a BV, an obligation between the taxpayer and that BV gives in principle no investeringsaftrek, and the ontheffing route of art. 3.46 lid 2 is the exception (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Temporary willekeurige afschrijving for 2021: unknown. No temporary scheme was found for 2021. The Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), and the URWA 2025 text lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. A scheme in a 2021 ministerial regulation or an announcement of the Minister would settle it. The 2023 scheme itself sits in URWA art. 13 to 15, applies to assets with obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in calendar year 2023 that are taken into use before 1 January 2026, and allows at most 50% of the costs to be depreciated at will, so it is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving cap: the € 328.721 rests on reading the URWA art. 8 cross-reference to the maximum amount in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table over which KIA can be obtained, which is the top of the KIA table. The pages do not print the cap as a number (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). The cap has also been read as the end of the 28% band of the KIA table, which gives € 58.238 for 2020 and € 59.170 for 2021, and that reading is not what the URWA wording says. A primary text that states the maximum amount for willekeurige afschrijving as a number would settle it.
- The rule that a medegerechtigde or a geldverstrekker cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages and is UNVERIFIED (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). A primary text on the EIA or MIA entitlement of a medegerechtigde or a financier would settle it.
- RVO maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil: the RVO page states the amount but not the year, so for 2021 it is UNVERIFIED (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). A 2021 version of the RVO page or the Milieulijst would settle it.
- 2021 Milieulijst codes: the code-letter mapping of the Milieulijst (F or G to categorie I, A or D to categorie II, B or E to categorie III) is stated for the Milieulijst 2022, in force from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). Which 2021 Milieulijst code maps to categorie I, II or III is not in the checked sources, while the 2021 percentages are 36%, 27% and 13,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3). The Milieulijst 2021 would settle it.
- Desinvesteringsbijtelling 2020 conflict, resolved: the law gave € 2.400 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1) against € 2.300 on the FISIN 2020 page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), settled to € 2.400. FISIN 2021 prints "€ 2.400 of minder", so 2021 has no conflict on this threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1).
- MIA percentages 2022 and 2023 conflict, resolved and outside 2021: the statute and the Milieulijst 2022 give 45%, 36% and 27% from 1 January 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel), while the FISIN 2022 and 2023 chapters kept 36%, 27% and 13,5%. The 2021 text still reads 36%, 27% and 13,5%, which is the enacted 2021 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3).
- Bodemwaarde: the 2026 text has further lids 5 to 10 on co-owned buildings, ground owned by an unrelated party and related persons and verbonden lichaam (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a). Whether the 2021 text contains those lids is not stated in the checked sources. Reading the 2021 text would settle it. For 2021 the two bodemwaarden of lid 3 were read directly (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3).
