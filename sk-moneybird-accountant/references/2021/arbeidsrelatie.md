# Arbeidsrelatie en zelfstandigheid 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

In 2021 the Wet DBA (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) was in force and the handhavingsmoratorium still applied, so a case that was not kwaadwillend first got an aanwijzing instead of a direct correction. The webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie ran as a pilot from 11 January 2021 to 10 July 2021 and had no rechtskracht, and the moratorium was extended at least to 1 October 2021 by the zevende voortgangsbrief of 20 September 2021, which left the decisions on the webmodule and the moratorium to the next cabinet. The 2021 rules are enacted law and enforcement policy, while the VBAR hourly rate presumption and the BAZ bill in section 2 are later items that did not apply to tax year 2021.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### Wet DBA

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties is the Wet van 3 februari 2016 and entered into force on 1 May 2016 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). It replaced the VAR, the earlier work-relationship declaration (same source). The name refers to the assessment of work relationships. In 2021 the substantive question is still the ordinary test for loondienst (employment), set out in the next subsection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). A source that is often mixed up: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking (deemed employment relationship) for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement (same source).

### Loondienst test

The test applies to every work relationship where loondienst is at issue for the 2021 rules (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Loondienst has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (the employer's authority over the work), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid (the work must be done personally), and pay for the work (same source). The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur (same source). There is no calculation. The test is a weighing of the facts of the case (same source). Exception and interaction: not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT, so the work-relationship qualification and the qualification as ondernemer for income tax or VAT are separate questions (same source). The outcome of this test is what the enforcement route of the moratorium acts on, and an approved modelovereenkomst supports the no-loondienst position only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Fictieve dienstbetrekkingen and the written opt-out

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen, among them aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden, stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). For gelijkgestelden the page gives the condition of usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (same source). Exception: thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay (same source). The consequence of the exclusion is that the opdrachtnemer has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW (same source). ZW is the Ziektewet (sickness benefit), WIA is the Wet werk en inkomen naar arbeidsvermogen (disability benefit) and WW is the Werkloosheidswet (unemployment benefit). The exclusion is the subject of the Besluit van 8 april 2016, Staatsblad 2016, 165, which is not the Wet DBA itself (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). The timing rule is strict: the exclusion must be written in the contract before the first payment of pay (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### Handhavingsmoratorium

In 2021 the handhavingsmoratorium applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). The moratorium is enforcement policy, not statute (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). In a case that was not kwaadwillend the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing (instruction) instead of correcting directly (same handhaving source). The aanwijzing says to reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige (self-employed person), or to process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll return), usually within 3 months (same source). If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting (correction obligation) applied from the date of the aanwijzing (same source). Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan (intentionally creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment), knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking (same source). Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes (same source). The second restriction of the moratorium exception applied from 1 January 2020: enforcement also when aanwijzingen were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020, announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 was also announced in that brief (same source). Later context, not for 2021: the moratorium applied until 1 January 2025, and since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply with no aanwijzingen, so schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, in which case up to 5 years back, and only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years (same source and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Deadline step: the follow-up on the aanwijzing must usually be done within 3 months, and the correctieverplichting starts on the date of the aanwijzing if it is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Moratorium extension 2021

The zevende voortgangsbrief of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The same brief left the decisions on the webmodule and the moratorium to the next cabinet (same source). In 2020 the zesde voortgangsbrief of 16 November 2020 had put the restart of handhaving at the earliest 1 October 2021 (same source). Effect: through 2021 the aanwijzing route of the previous subsection stayed in force (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). For the file this means no direct correction for a case that was not kwaadwillend during 2021 before an aanwijzing (same source).

### Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/). It gives three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible (same source). It is not a legal decision and gives no rights (same source). Limits: it is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon, or for a foreign opdrachtnemer (same source). The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021 (same source and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). There was no rechtskracht (legal effect) during or after the pilot (same sources). The online pilot environment stayed active after the pilot (same sources). The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring as one outcome (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Interaction: an outcome can inform the parties, but because it is not a legal decision and gives no rights it does not by itself establish the qualification (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).

### Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst (model agreement) means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). For 2021 this route was in use and the checked material states no 2021-specific change (same source and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Later changes, not for 2021: the approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging (free substitution) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed, and approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done (same source). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Interaction with 2021: the condition that the parties work as described applies in 2021, so the administration should record the actual way of working (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Case law on the arbeidsovereenkomst

For 2021 the relevant ruling in the checked material is HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of partijbedoeling (the intention of the parties) (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The checked material gives the subject of the ruling, not its full holding. Later rulings, not for 2021: HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), with an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding (embedding) and ondernemerschap (entrepreneurship), and vrije vervanging not as an absolute element (same source). HR Uber (prejudiciële vragen): no rangorde between the gezichtspunten, so ondernemerschap is a full criterion (same source). The government states this has no effect on handhaving (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 for the Uber ruling are UNVERIFIED, see section 7 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### VBAR bill status

Not applicable to tax year 2021. The internet consultation of the original VBAR (rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van een uurtarief) ran from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering). The bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024 (same source). Bill 36 783 was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and on 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer (same source and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783). The nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking (clarification) part (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering). The Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026, all parties except FVD, and the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (same source). It became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). It enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). For tax year 2021 none of this applied, so the 2021 qualification uses the ordinary loondienst test and the moratorium described above (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### VBAR low hourly rate presumption

Not applicable to tax year 2021. New art. 7:610aa BW: lid 1 someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). Lid 2 the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount (same source). Lid 3 the presumption is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business (same source). Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years (same source). Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling (ministerial regulation) (same source). Effect: the werkende can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule (same source). The amount: the memorie van toelichting explains € 36 as 120% of the WML (wettelijk minimumloon, minimum wage) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag (surcharge) for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, which gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025). Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026 is stated on the Eerste Kamer page, on rijksoverheid.nl and on Ondernemersplein (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). The build-up of the € 36 and the indexation to € 38 are in section 4.

Not applicable to tax year 2021. Bill 36 912 was ingediend on 24 March 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

The checked sources give no official examples for this topic for 2021. Every example below is a Calculated example built here from the section 2 figures, with clearly invented facts. No real person's data is used.

### Example 1: Loondienst test on invented facts (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): from 1 February 2021 a graphic designer works 4 days a week for one client for 8 months. The client sets the working hours and the place of work. The designer uses the client's equipment and email address and works in the client's team. The contract says the work must be done personally. The fee is a fixed monthly amount. No commercial risk sits with the designer.

Step 1: check the three elements from section 2. Werkgeversgezag: the client sets work and working hours, so the possibility of authority is present. Persoonlijke arbeid: the contract requires the designer to do the work personally. Loon: there is a fixed monthly fee. All three elements are present.

Step 2: weigh the section 2 factors. Embedding in the organisation is high. The duty to work personally is present. The fee is a fixed monthly amount and is not linked to a result. Commercial risk is low. The designer has one client and no own equipment, so the designer does not act as an entrepreneur.

Step 3: factors that could point the other way: the duration is 8 months and the parties chose the label overeenkomst van opdracht. The label is part of how the agreements were made, which is one of the weighing factors in section 2. On these facts the embedding and the set hours and place of work point to loondienst.

Step 4: a conclusion of no loondienst would still not make the designer an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. That is a separate test.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### Example 2: Written opt-out for a gelijkgestelde (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): a thuiswerker works for one client 3 days a week for 6 months. The pay is above 2/5 of the minimum wage.

Step 1: the section 2 condition for gelijkgestelden is usually 2 or more days a week for the same client. With 3 days a week the first part of the condition is met. The 2/5 minimum wage part cannot be checked in euros here, because the checked sources give no 2021 minimum wage.

Step 2: if the gelijkgestelden rule applies, the parties can exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay.

Step 3: the effect is that the opdrachtnemer has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### Example 3: Aanwijzing term of usually 3 months (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): an opdrachtgever receives an aanwijzing dated 1 March 2021.

Step 1: the section 2 term is usually 3 months.

Step 2: 1 March 2021 plus 3 months is 1 June 2021.

Step 3: by that date the opdrachtgever must reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte.

Step 4: if the aanwijzing is not followed, the correctieverplichting applies from the date of the aanwijzing, so from 1 March 2021.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

### Example 4: Modelovereenkomst not followed in practice (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): the parties use an approved modelovereenkomst. In practice the opdrachtnemer works at the opdrachtgever's office during hours set by the opdrachtgever and takes instructions from the opdrachtgever's team lead, so the work is not done as the modelovereenkomst describes.

Step 1: the section 2 rule is no loondienst only if the parties work as described.

Step 2: here they do not, so the modelovereenkomst does not give the no-loondienst position.

Step 3: the qualification then follows how the work is actually done, and on these facts the set hours, the set place of work and the instructions point to loondienst.

Step 4: the administration should show the actual way of working, because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### Example 5: Webmodule not useful for a contract with a bv (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): an opdrachtgever wants to check a contract with an opdrachtnemer that is a bv, a rechtspersoon.

Step 1: the webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands.

Step 2: the section 2 limit says it is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv.

Step 3: even in a useful case the outcome is not a legal decision and gives no rights, so it cannot be used as the qualification.

Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

### Example 6: Partijbedoeling weighed with the other factors (Calculated example)

Facts (invented): the parties call the contract an overeenkomst van opdracht and state that no dienstbetrekking is intended. In practice the client sets the hours and the place of work and the work is embedded in the client's team.

Step 1: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, concerns the role of partijbedoeling.

Step 2: the label and the stated intention are part of how the agreements were made, which is one of the weighing factors in section 2.

Step 3: they are weighed with the other factors. The checked material gives the subject of the ruling, not its full holding, so this example uses the section 2 weighing factors only.

Step 4: on these facts the embedding, the set hours and the set place of work point to loondienst.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### Example 7: VBAR build-up of € 36 and indexation to € 38 (Calculated example, not applicable to tax year 2021)

This example shows the later VBAR method only. It does not apply to tax year 2021.

Step 1: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 = € 16,872.

Step 2: 25% of € 16,872 = € 4,218.

Step 3: 15% of € 16,872 = € 2,5308.

Step 4: € 16,872 + € 4,218 + € 2,5308 = € 23,6208.

Step 5: € 23,6208 times 1,5 = € 35,4312, the unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025.

Step 6: rounded up to whole euros = € 36.

Step 7: every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026. € 14,71 / € 14,06 = about 1,04623.

Step 8: € 35,43 times 1,04623 = about € 37,07 unrounded.

Step 9: rounded up to whole euros = € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. The € 38 itself is stated on the primary pages, the step from the unrounded amount is calculated here.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.

### Example 8: BAZ docket summary on invented facts (Calculated example, not applicable to tax year 2021)

This example shows the later BAZ bill only. It does not apply to tax year 2021.

Facts (invented): a self-employed person with an invented profit of € 30.000 becomes unable to work.

Step 1: the section 2 docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage. 70% of € 30.000 = € 21.000, but the basis is capped at the minimum wage and the checked sources give no minimum wage amount for the BAZ, so no benefit amount is computed here.

Step 2: the waiting period is about one year.

Step 3: an opt-out is possible with a qualifying private policy.

Step 4: the bill was not law in 2021 and was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag on 2026-10-11.

Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

- **Moratorium end date.** 2020: the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 was announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019, and the zesde voortgangsbrief of 16 November 2020 put the restart of handhaving at the earliest 1 October 2021 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: the zevende voortgangsbrief of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 and left the decisions on the webmodule and the moratorium to the next cabinet (same source).
- **Webmodule status.** 2020: not yet live, with the pilot expected to start on 11 January 2021 (zesde voortgangsbrief of 16 November 2020, same source). 2021: the pilot ran from 11 January 2021 to 10 July 2021, the online environment stayed active and there was no rechtskracht during or after the pilot (same source and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).
- **Enforcement exception.** From 1 January 2020: enforcement also when aanwijzingen were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020 (derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: this stayed in force through the extension (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- **Legislative pause.** 2020: the vijfde voortgangsbrief of 18 June 2020 stated that the cabinet stopped working out new legislation (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: the checked sources state that the decisions on the webmodule and the moratorium were left to the next cabinet (zevende voortgangsbrief of 20 September 2021, same source).
- **Case law.** 2020: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on partijbedoeling was decided in that year (same source). 2021: that ruling applies in the 2021 test, and the later Deliveroo and Uber rulings do not apply to 2021 (same source).
- **Modelovereenkomsten.** 2020: the 2020 file records approved modelovereenkomsten in use with no 2020-specific change found, marked UNVERIFIED (same source). 2021: the checked material again states no 2021-specific change, and the next changes are the withdrawal of the vrije vervanging approval on 14 August 2023 and the stop on new assessments from 6 September 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- **VBAR and BAZ.** Neither existed in 2020 or 2021. The VBAR internet consultation started on 6 October 2023 and the BAZ bill was ingediend on 24 March 2026 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep the signed overeenkomst, the approved modelovereenkomst if one is used, and a record of how the work is actually done. Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- If a thuiswerker, gelijkgestelde or artiest is used and the parties want to exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking, the written exclusion must be in the contract before the first payment of pay. The consequence is no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW for the opdrachtnemer (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- If the Belastingdienst gives an aanwijzing, keep the aanwijzing and the follow-up in the file. The relationship must be reshaped or processed in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months, and a correctieverplichting runs from the date of the aanwijzing if it is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- The winst uit onderneming of the sole trader is part of the income tax return for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). The return had to arrive before 1 May 2022 and the filing period opened on 1 March (Status enacted, Check Corrected, https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). With uitstel the date was before 1 September 2022 (Status enacted, Check Corrected, https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Without an aangiftebrief the 2021 return could still be filed until 31 December 2026 (Status enacted, Check Verified, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).
- Keep the administration for seven years (AWR art. 52 lid 4, same text in the 2020 and 2026 versions, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). AWR art. 52 lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (same sources).
- A webmodule outcome is not a legal decision and gives no rights, so keep the contract and the facts as the basis of the file (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).
- Not being in loondienst does not make the opdrachtnemer an ondernemer for income tax or VAT, so registration and returns follow their own tests (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- **Modelovereenkomsten 2021.** The checked material contains no 2021-specific event for modelovereenkomsten. The 2020 file recorded the same gap as UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). A 2021 Belastingdienst page or a 2021 voortgangsbrief that lists a modelovereenkomsten change would settle whether anything changed in 2021.
- **Uber ruling date.** The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling, so they are UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The ruling text on rechtspraak.nl or the Hoge Raad site would settle the date and the ECLI.
- **2/5 of the minimum wage for gelijkgestelden in 2021.** The condition is at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), but the checked sources give no 2021 minimum wage, so no 2021 euro amount is computed here. A 2021 WML source would settle it.
- **VBAR first-application amount.** The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). Publication of that regeling would settle it.
- **BAZ status.** The BAZ bill 36 912 is proposed. On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). A nota and a vote would settle its status. It did not exist in tax year 2021.
- **VBAR status for 2021.** The VBAR was adopted on 18 June 2026 as Stb. 2026, 158 and enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). It does not apply to tax year 2021.
- **Resolved, not open: € 36 against € 38.** Both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, built from the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 at € 14,71 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- **Resolved, not open: BAZ verslag dates 27 May against 29 May 2026.** Both are correct. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May 2026 and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- **Resolved, not open: Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA.** It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).
- The 2021 material states no further open points beyond the cross-year items listed here.
