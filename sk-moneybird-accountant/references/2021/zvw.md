# Zvw-bijdrage 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers only the income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet) that an entrepreneur pays over the profit, its rate of 5,75% for 2021 and the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 58.311. It also covers who pays through the aanslag, how the base follows the belastbare winst uit onderneming and how the rate differs from the 7,00% werkgeversheffing that an employer pays. The 2021 rates and the maximum are enacted and verified in the checked sources. One element stays UNVERIFIED for 2021: the base rule rests on Zvw art. 43 read in the 2026 version, because the 2021 version of that article was not read.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### Zvw bijdrage by (voorlopige) aanslag, 5,75%

The entrepreneur who is not in loondienst (employment) pays the income-dependent Zvw contribution by (voorlopige) aanslag. This channel covers the winst uit Nederland, which does not include the conversion of an oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) into a lijfrente (annuity), plus inkomsten uit overig werk, loon from a foreign employer per the 2020 to 2024 pages, pension from a foreign uitkeringsinstantie and periodieke uitkeringen without loonheffing such as partneralimentatie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The ingehouden bijdrage (withheld contribution) is also 5,75% in 2021 and is withheld from pension, lijfrente and other uitkeringen with loonheffing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, in the article text read in the 2026 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, see section 7).

At AOW age (state pension age) https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 footnote 2 states that zelfstandigen (self-employed) and gepensioneerden pay the verlaagde IAB. No AOW-age exemption or reduction appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), and the FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. This is noted in section 7.

### Maximum bijdrage-inkomen, € 58.311

The maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 58.311 applies once per person across all channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom, so only the amount up to the maximum is charged. Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the bijdrage-inkomen at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation, in the article text read in the 2026 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, see section 7). The checked material lists these exceptions: militairen in active service or on buitengewoon verlof with retention of military income, deelvissers (share fishermen) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw werkgeversheffing, 7,00%

The werkgeversheffing (employer levy) is paid by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie over loon uit loondienst, DGA loon (director-major shareholder salary) when insured for the werknemersverzekeringen, bijstand, early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for ziekenfonds-insured people of 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). It is levied on the employer, while the ingehouden bijdrage is withheld from the income of the insured person and the entrepreneur pays by aanslag. For 2021 the employer rate is 7,00% and the rate for the ingehouden bijdrage and the aanslag is 5,75% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The same maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 58.311 applies across the channels, so loon that already bore the werkgeversheffing uses up the headroom for the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw base for winst uit onderneming

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit from business) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 in turn defines belastbare winst as winst minus ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur deductions) minus MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). So the Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer is the profit after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Both texts were read in the 2026 version only, so this base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2021 and is listed in section 7.

Interaction with the oudedagsreserve: the 2021 addition is 9,44% of the profit, with a maximum of € 9.395 reduced by the beroepspensioenpremie (occupational pension premium) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). An addition to the reserve lowers the winst and thereby the Zvw base, while a decrease that is added under art. 3.70 lid 2 is excluded from the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek, € 6.670

Interaction only. The zelfstandigenaftrek for 2021 is € 6.670, or € 3.335 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the winst before the MKB-winstvrijstelling and so lowers the belastbare winst that forms the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The conditions, among them the urencriterium (hours criterion), belong to the ondernemersaftrek topic, see section 8.

### MKB-winstvrijstelling, 14%

Interaction only. The MKB-winstvrijstelling for 2021 is 14% of the winst after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is applied after the ondernemersaftrek in the step from winst to belastbare winst, so it lowers the Zvw base as well (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The detailed conditions belong to the ondernemersaftrek topic, see section 8.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

**Example 1. Official example (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), the multiplication written out here.**
Loon € 40.000 plus freelance income € 20.000.
Step 1: maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 58.311.
Step 2: the loon of € 40.000 already bore the werkgeversheffing, so it uses up € 40.000 of the headroom.
Step 3: remaining headroom is € 58.311 minus € 40.000 = € 18.311.
Step 4: the freelance income of € 20.000 is above the remaining headroom, so the 5,75% applies to € 18.311 only.
Step 5: bijdrage is € 18.311 times 5,75% = € 1.052,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 2. Official example (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).**
Loon € 60.000 plus freelance income € 15.000.
Step 1: the loon of € 60.000 is above the maximum of € 58.311, so the headroom is € 0.
Step 2: no Zvw bijdrage by aanslag is charged over the freelance income. The page states this gives no bijdrage.

**Example 3. Calculated example.**
Profit (winst) € 60.000, entrepreneur below AOW age.
Step 1: winst is € 60.000.
Step 2: zelfstandigenaftrek € 6.670, so winst after ondernemersaftrek is € 60.000 minus € 6.670 = € 53.330.
Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of € 53.330 = € 7.466,20.
Step 4: belastbare winst is € 53.330 minus € 7.466,20 = € 45.863,80. This is below the maximum of € 58.311, so the full amount is the bijdrage-inkomen.
Step 5: bijdrage is € 45.863,80 times 5,75% = € 2.637,17 rounded to the cent.

**Example 4. Calculated example.**
Profit (winst) € 100.000, entrepreneur below AOW age.
Step 1: € 100.000 minus € 6.670 = € 93.330.
Step 2: 14% of € 93.330 = € 13.066,20.
Step 3: belastbare winst is € 93.330 minus € 13.066,20 = € 80.263,80.
Step 4: the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 58.311, so the base is capped at € 58.311.
Step 5: bijdrage is € 58.311 times 5,75% = € 3.352,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 5. Calculated example.**
Belastbare winst of € 20.000 plus loon of € 50.000 that already bore the werkgeversheffing.
Step 1: headroom is € 58.311 minus € 50.000 = € 8.311.
Step 2: the belastbare winst of € 20.000 is above the headroom, so the contribution is charged over € 8.311.
Step 3: bijdrage is € 8.311 times 5,75% = € 477,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 6. Calculated example, employer rate against the other channels.**
Loon € 40.000.
Step 1: werkgeversheffing is 7,00% of € 40.000 = € 2.800,00.
Step 2: the 5,75% rate over the same € 40.000 = € 2.300,00.
Step 3: difference is € 2.800,00 minus € 2.300,00 = € 500,00.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

- Zvw bijdrage by aanslag and ingehouden bijdrage: 5,45% in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to 5,75% in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen: € 57.232 in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to € 58.311 in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Zvw werkgeversheffing: 6,70% in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, also the werkgeversheffing series 2020 to 2024) to 7,00% in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Base rule: the checked material states the same rule for both years, belastbare winst uit onderneming after ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling, and marks it UNVERIFIED in both years because only the 2026 texts of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 were read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). No change can be confirmed from the checked material.
- Channels, the once-per-person maximum and its exceptions: the checked material states the same for 2020 and 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- The entrepreneur's own Zvw contribution is billed by aanslag, and the aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Record the billed amount as a separate liability from the income tax entry when the aanslag is received.
- The base is the belastbare winst uit onderneming after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 versions, UNVERIFIED for 2021). Keep the winst calculation so that the zelfstandigenaftrek of € 6.670 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 14% for 2021 are visible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 58.311 applies once per person, so when there is also loon uit loondienst or an uitkering that already bore a contribution, only the headroom is charged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The 2021 percentages are set by ministerial regulation under Zvw art. 45 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).
- Filing route and deadline: the checked Zvw sources do not state the 2021 declaration route or dates. The aanslag follows the inkomstenbelasting route per https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, and the deadline belongs to the procedure topic. This is listed as UNVERIFIED in section 7.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Zvw base for the winst-ondernemer, UNVERIFIED for 2021. The rule, belastbare winst uit onderneming after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, with the art. 3.70 lid 2 addition for an oudedagsreserve decrease excluded, rests on Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 as read in the 2026 version only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reading the text valid on 1 January 2021 of Zvw art. 43 and of Wet IB 2001 afdeling 3.2 would settle it.
- Declaration and deadlines, UNVERIFIED. The checked 2021 material does not state whether the same aangifte is used or which deadlines apply. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms that the aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting, and the filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic. The 2021 procedure pages would settle it.
- AOW age, no stated rule. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 footnote 2 states that zelfstandigen and gepensioneerden pay the verlaagde IAB. No AOW-age exemption or reduction appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. The 2021 text of Zvw art. 41 to 45 would settle it.
- Rates and maximum for 2021: no conflict between the checked sources. All 2021 values come from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and are marked Verified.
