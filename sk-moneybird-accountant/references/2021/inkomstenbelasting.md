# Inkomstenbelasting box 1 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2021 box 1 rates, the variants for people at AOW age, the heffingskortingen (tax credits, being the algemene heffingskorting, arbeidskorting and inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting), the tariefsaanpassing (rate adjustment for deductions) and the route from winst uit onderneming (business profit) to taxable income. The ordinary below-AOW table applies 37,10% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that amount. AOW age changes the first-bracket rate and the heffingskorting tables, and the tariefsaanpassing of 6,5% limits the value of a deduction in the top bracket to 43%. The 2021 rules in this file are enacted. A separate Zvw contribution also exists on profit, and its calculation belongs in the Zvw file for 2021.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

For a taxpayer below AOW age the published 2021 table applies 37,10% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening). The statute separates the first band into income tax only at 9,45% up to € 35.129, then a fixed € 3.319 plus 37,10% up to € 68.507, then € 15.702 plus 49,50% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The premie volksverzekeringen of 27,65% is charged over the premie-inkomen up to a ceiling that equals the end of that first band, € 35.129 in 2021, and above the premium ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the combined rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen).

### Box 1 for a whole year at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer who is at AOW age for the whole year and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first-bracket rate is 19,20% up to € 35.129, then 37,10% to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening). Once the AOW age is reached no AOW premium is due, which is why the AOW-age first-bracket rate is lower (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). That page states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age, and the same file notes that the continuation of the Anw and Wlz premiums at AOW age is inference from the AOW-age rates and stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule.

### Box 1 for a whole year at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 the first-bracket boundary is € 35.941 instead of € 35.129. The rates above it are 37,10% to € 68.507 and 49,50% above. Article 2.10a of the Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001 holds this table, and the statute works with the fixed amounts € 3.396 and € 15.477 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10a).

### First-bracket rate in the month AOW age is reached

In the year in which the AOW age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it and rises by about 1,49 points per month from the AOW-age rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). The 2021 monthly rates run from 19,20% in January to 35,60% in December, in the order January 19,20%, February 20,69%, March 22,18%, April 23,67%, May 25,16%, June 26,65%, July 28,15%, August 29,84%, September 31,13%, October 32,62%, November 34,11%, December 35,60%. The first bracket ends at € 35.129 and schijven 2 and 3 keep the normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling and components

The premium ceiling is € 35.129, or € 35.941 for a person born before 1 January 1946 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen). The standard components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%, together 27,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen). The AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### Tariefsaanpassing

When grondslagverminderende posten have been deducted, the tax under article 2.10 lid 1 is raised by a percentage of the amount by which the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning plus those posten exceeds the top bracket boundary, capped at that percentage of the deducted posten. For 2021 the percentage is 6,5%, so a deduction that falls in the top bracket is worth at most 43%, being 49,50% minus 6,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

The posten are the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions, being onderhoudsverplichtingen including partneralimentatie, weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten, aftrekbare giften, specifieke zorgkosten and the restant persoonsgebonden aftrek voorgaande jaren), the aftrek kosten eigen woning (hypotheekrente, periodieke erfpacht, opstal or beklemming, kosten afsluiten hypotheek and restschuldkosten), the ondernemersfaciliteiten (zelfstandigenaftrek, aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek and mkb-winstvrijstelling) and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The 2021 Belastingdienst calculation page names the tariefsaanpassing but prints no percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

The algemene heffingskorting is granted to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer below AOW age the maximum is € 2.837. The phase-out base in 2021 is the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning. The credit is reduced by 5,977% of that income above € 21.043 and reaches nil at € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The AHK and AK afbouwpunten are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

### Algemene heffingskorting for a whole year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the maximum algemene heffingskorting is € 1.469. It is reduced by 3,093% of the phase-out base above € 21.043 and reaches nil at € 68.507. The phase-out base remains the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Arbeidskorting below AOW age

The arbeidskorting is granted over the arbeidsinkomen. For an IB-ondernemer the arbeidsinkomen includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and excludes the part earned as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen). The 2021 below-AOW table builds the credit in three bands at 4,581%, then € 463 plus 28,771%, then € 3.837 plus 2,663%, and phases it out in the final band at 6% until it is nil above € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Arbeidskorting for a whole year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the arbeidskorting uses the separate rates and fixed amounts, being 2,371% up to € 10.108, then € 240 plus 14,890%, then € 1.987 plus 1,378%, and finally € 2.178 minus 3,105% until the credit is nil above € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

The IACK is 11,45% of the arbeidsinkomen above € 5.153 up to a maximum of € 2.815, reached at an arbeidsinkomen of € 29.739. For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the rate is 5,926% and the maximum is € 1.458 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag and not in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting). The taxpayer qualifies with no fiscal partner, with a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or with a lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### IACK child and co-ouder test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2008 and be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months. For a co-ouder (co-parent) the child must spend at least 156 days in 2021 with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Payout of unused heffingskortingen to a lower-earning partner

In 2021, 86⅔% of the unused credit was withheld and 13⅓% could be paid. The payout is limited to the part of the credit that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and premiums, the partners must have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months and the partner must owe enough tax (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The afbouw has run since 2009 and ends in 2023. The AK and IACK payouts lapsed from 2023 and the AHK payout lapsed from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later, while it continues for people born before 1 January 1963, who were never in the afbouw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Payout income limits

The payout is subject to the own-income limits of € 8.170 for a person born before 1963 and € 1.024 for a person born later, and to the partner income limit of € 24.650, or € 29.350 at AOW age. No payout applies to someone born after 31 December 1999 who is maintained by parents with at least € 439 per quarter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### IACK payout percentage

The checked 2021 source states no separate payout percentage for the IACK. The general 86⅔% afbouw figure is not confirmed as the IACK-specific percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen

Each heffingskorting splits into a belastingdeel (the income-tax part, 9,45% in 2021) and premiedelen for AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%, together 37,10%. Someone not insured for one scheme loses that part, and a part-year premieplicht gives a time-proportional premiedeel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Heffingskortingen in the year AOW age is reached

The checked 2021 page states no rule or amount for the heffingskortingen in the year in which the AOW age is reached. This stays UNVERIFIED for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

The zelfstandigenaftrek reduces business profit for a taxpayer who meets the conditions, including the urencriterium (hours criterion). The 2021 amount is € 6.670, or € 3.335 at AOW age. Eligibility detail belongs to the ondernemersaftrek topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Startersaftrek

The startersaftrek is an additional ondernemersaftrek of € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

This deduction for an entrepreneur with a disability decreases over the years of use from € 12.000 to € 8.000, then € 4.000 and then € 0, and it is capped at the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Meewerkaftrek

The meewerkaftrek is a deduction for a partner who works in the business, at 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% of the profit over the applicable hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked 2021 material does not state the hour-band thresholds.

### Stakingsaftrek

The stakingsaftrek has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

The MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of the profit after the ondernemersaftrek and is part of the route from profit to belastbare winst uit onderneming (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Oudedagsreserve addition

An ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and is below AOW age may add 9,44% of the profit to the oudedagsreserve, with a yearly maximum of € 9.395 reduced by the beroepspensioenpremie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The addition reduces the profit that flows into box 1.

### Winst uit onderneming and belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning

A business is assessed for income tax by weighing whether it is run for the taxpayer's account, whether the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and whether the taxpayer is liable for its debts, and Handelsregister registration alone does not decide the question. The Belastingdienst also weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

Belastbare winst uit onderneming is the winst as ondernemer reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning is the inkomen uit werk en woning reduced by te verrekenen verliezen, and that income includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). The MKB-winstvrijstelling is computed on the winst after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The complete sequence was read in the 2026 version of the statute and the 2021 ondernemersaftrek page, so the 2021 statutory wording was not read for this sequence.

A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen

Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen are taken out of the winst first. The listed categories are the bosbouwvrijstelling, the landbouwvrijstelling, the vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst for the part above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies and benefits under the eindheffing, mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject vrijstellingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### Box 1 saldo, tariefsaanpassing and heffingskortingen in sequence

The box 1 saldo is taxed by article 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the tariefsaanpassing, and the heffingskortingen reduce the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Calculated example: box 1 below AOW age

Assume a taxable amount in box 1 of € 40.000 for a taxpayer below AOW age.

1. The first statutory amount is € 3.319.
2. The income above € 35.129 is € 40.000 minus € 35.129 = € 4.871.
3. The amount on that part is € 4.871 times 37,10% = € 1.807,141.
4. The total is € 3.319 plus € 1.807,141 = € 5.126,141.

This uses the 2021 statutory formula and does not subtract heffingskortingen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### Calculated example: first bracket in the month AOW age is reached

Assume the taxpayer reaches AOW age in July and has € 10.000 of income in the first bracket.

1. The July first-bracket rate is 28,15%.
2. € 10.000 times 28,15% = € 2.815.

The first bracket ends at € 35.129 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### Calculated example: tariefsaanpassing

Assume a belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning of € 70.000 that was reached after € 10.000 of qualifying deductions.

1. Add the deductions back: € 70.000 plus € 10.000 = € 80.000.
2. The amount above the top bracket boundary is € 80.000 minus € 68.507 = € 11.493.
3. Apply 6,5% to that amount: € 11.493 times 6,5% = € 747,045.
4. The cap is 6,5% of the deducted posten: € 10.000 times 6,5% = € 650.
5. The adjustment is the lower amount, so € 650.
6. The maximum value of the € 10.000 of deductions in the top bracket is € 10.000 times 43% = € 4.300.

This illustrates the statutory cap and the maximum deduction value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

### Calculated example: algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

Assume a belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning of € 30.000.

1. The income above the phase-out point is € 30.000 minus € 21.043 = € 8.957.
2. The reduction is € 8.957 times 5,977% = € 535,35989.
3. The remaining credit is € 2.837 minus € 535,35989 = € 2.301,64011 before rounding.

The checked material does not state a rounding method for this calculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Calculated example: arbeidskorting below AOW age

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 40.000.

1. The amount above € 35.652 is € 40.000 minus € 35.652 = € 4.348.
2. The reduction in the final band is € 4.348 times 6% = € 260,88.
3. The credit is € 4.205 minus € 260,88 = € 3.944,12.

This uses the final-band formula (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Calculated example: IACK below its maximum

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 15.000 and that all eligibility conditions are met.

1. The income above € 5.153 is € 15.000 minus € 5.153 = € 9.847.
2. The calculated credit is € 9.847 times 11,45% = € 1.127,4815.
3. This is below the listed maximum of € 2.815.

The result is the calculated amount before rounding (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Calculated example: business profit into belastbare winst uit onderneming

Assume a profit of € 60.000 and a qualifying zelfstandigenaftrek of € 6.670, with other deductions and losses ignored.

1. The profit after the zelfstandigenaftrek is € 60.000 minus € 6.670 = € 53.330.
2. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is € 53.330 times 14% = € 7.466,20.
3. The belastbare winst uit onderneming is € 53.330 minus € 7.466,20 = € 45.863,80.

This follows the deduction order in the checked material (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### Official example: AK payout to a partner

An unused arbeidskorting of € 459 gives a payout of € 62, being € 459 times 13⅓% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Official example: AHK payout to a partner

Someone born on 3 February 1963 with an algemene heffingskorting of € 2.837 receives € 379, being € 2.837 times 13⅓% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The same page also carries an arithmetic slip of € 1.484 against € 1.489.

### Calculated example: payout of an unused credit

Assume an eligible unused arbeidskorting of € 750 and that the general 2021 payout conditions are met.

1. The payable share is 13⅓%.
2. € 750 times 13⅓% = € 100.

This does not establish a separate IACK payout percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The below-AOW combined first rate changed from 37,35% to 37,10%. The first statutory boundary changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129. The top rate stayed 49,50% and the € 68.507 boundary stayed the same. The income-tax part changed from 9,70% to 9,45%, while the premium components stayed AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- For a whole year at AOW age the first rate changed from 19,45% to 19,20%. The first boundary changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129 for people born on or after 1 January 1946, and from € 35.375 to € 35.941 for people born before 1 January 1946. The middle rate changed from 37,35% to 37,10% and the top rate stayed 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- The monthly first-bracket transition rates changed from January through December as follows: 19,45% to 19,20%, 20,94% to 20,69%, 22,43% to 22,18%, 23,92% to 23,67%, 25,41% to 25,16%, 26,90% to 26,65%, 28,40% to 28,15%, 30,09% to 29,84%, 31,38% to 31,13%, 32,87% to 32,62%, 34,36% to 34,11% and 35,85% to 35,60% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- The premie volksverzekeringen ceiling changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129, and for people born before 1 January 1946 from € 35.375 to € 35.941 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/premie_volksverzekeringen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen).
- The tariefsaanpassing changed from 3,5% to 6,5%. The maximum value of a deduction in the top bracket fell from 46% to 43% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).
- The below-AOW algemene heffingskorting changed from € 2.711 to € 2.837. Its phase-out percentage changed from 5,672% to 5,977% and its phase-out point changed from € 20.711 to € 21.043. Its nil point stayed € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The whole-year AOW algemene heffingskorting changed from € 1.413 to € 1.469. Its phase-out percentage changed from 2,954% to 3,093% and its phase-out point changed from € 20.711 to € 21.043. Its nil point stayed € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The below-AOW arbeidskorting schedule changed from up to € 9.921 at 2,812%, then € 279 plus 28,812% to € 21.430, then € 3.595 plus 1,656% to € 34.954 and then € 3.819 minus 6% to € 98.604, to up to € 10.108 at 4,581%, then € 463 plus 28,771% to € 21.835, then € 3.837 plus 2,663% to € 35.652 and then € 4.205 minus 6% to € 105.736. The nil point moved from € 98.604 to € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The whole-year AOW arbeidskorting schedule changed from up to € 9.921 at 1,464%, then € 146 plus 15,004% to € 21.430, then € 1.873 plus 0,862% to € 34.954 and then € 1.989 minus 3,124% to € 98.604, to up to € 10.108 at 2,371%, then € 240 plus 14,890% to € 21.835, then € 1.987 plus 1,378% to € 35.652 and then € 2.178 minus 3,105% to € 105.736. The nil point moved from € 98.604 to € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The IACK threshold changed from € 5.072 to € 5.153 and the maximum from € 2.881 at € 30.233 to € 2.815 at € 29.739. The whole-year AOW rate changed from 5,963% to 5,926% and the whole-year AOW maximum from € 1.502 to € 1.458 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The IACK child birth cutoff changed from after 31 December 2007 to after 31 December 2008. The 2021 co-ouder test states at least 156 days with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

- The payout own-income limits changed from € 7.855 to € 8.170 for people born before 1963, and from € 1.440 to € 1.024 for people born in 1963 or later. The partner limit changed from € 23.790 to € 24.650, and from € 28.390 to € 29.350 at AOW age. The under-21 birth cutoff changed from after 31 December 1998 to after 31 December 1999 and the quarterly parental amount changed from € 433 to € 439 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The zelfstandigenaftrek changed from € 7.030 to € 6.670, and its AOW-age amount from € 3.515 to € 3.335. The startersaftrek stayed € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age. The MKB-winstvrijstelling stayed 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The oudedagsreserve maximum changed from € 9.214 in 2020 to € 9.395 in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Reconcile the business profit to the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and to any losses used in the box 1 calculation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and art. 3.2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Keep the arbeidsinkomen base for the arbeidskorting in view, because it includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and excludes income as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen).

The algemene heffingskorting and arbeidskorting can be applied in the return and in the payroll, the IACK is claimed in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, and the tariefsaanpassing is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

The checked sources do not state a bookkeeping retention period or a filing deadline for this topic. An inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw is also charged, and its calculation belongs in the Zvw file for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The IACK-specific payout percentage for 2021 is unknown. The checked 2021 page states no separate percentage, and the general 86⅔% afbouw figure is not confirmed for the IACK (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The amount and the calculation rule for the heffingskortingen in the year in which the AOW age is reached are UNVERIFIED for 2021. The checked 2021 page states no rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The statement that the Anw and Wlz premiums continue at AOW age is inference from the AOW-age first-bracket rates and stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule. The premium page states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- The route from winst uit onderneming to belastbare winst uit onderneming and belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning was read in the 2026 version of the Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001 and on the 2021 ondernemersaftrek page. The 2021 statute versions of articles 3.1 and 3.2 were not read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The order in which the box 1 tax, the tariefsaanpassing and the heffingskortingen are applied is an inference from the structure of article 2.10, because no checked page prints the full sequence as one statement (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The 2021 meewerkaftrek source gives the percentage schedule but the checked material does not state the hour-band thresholds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The official AHK payout example page carries an arithmetic slip of € 1.484 against € 1.489. The checked material does not provide the inputs that would settle it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer is documented as the belastbare winst uit onderneming after ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling, but only the 2026 version of Zvw art. 43 was read, so the 2021 wording is UNVERIFIED. That calculation belongs to the Zvw file (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- In the previous-year comparison, the 2020 oudedagsreserve maximum is given as € 9.214 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans) and as € 9.218 in the baseline citing the 2020 statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.68). Reading article 3.68 of the 2020 statute would settle it.
