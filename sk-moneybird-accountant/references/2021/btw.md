# BTW 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Dutch VAT (btw) in 2021 generally used the standard rate, with a reduced rate for supplies in Tabel I and a zero rate for qualifying supplies in Tabel II. The optional small businesses scheme (KOR) exempted qualifying businesses from charging VAT, while denying deduction of input VAT. The main change during the year was the e-commerce package from 1 July 2021, including the EU distance-sales threshold and replacement of MOSS by the one-stop shop. The laws described here were enacted, but some 2021 filing details and historical guidance remain unverified.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### Standard VAT rate

The standard rate is 21%. Apply it where no reduced rate, zero rate or exemption applies. Calculate output VAT by multiplying the taxable amount by the applicable rate. The rate is in Article 9, paragraph 1 of the Wet op de omzetbelasting. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)

### Reduced VAT rate

The rate is 9% for goods and services listed in Tabel I. Whether a supply belongs in Tabel I depends on the relevant entry and its conditions. The 2021 statutory text confirms the reduced rate, but the checked current Belastingdienst lists do not establish the complete historical 2021 list. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to supplies in Tabel II if the conditions set by general administrative order are met. A zero-rated supply is still taxable. Input VAT connected to it can remain deductible, unlike input VAT connected to an exempt supply. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)

For an intra-EU supply of goods, the records must prove that the goods went to another EU country. The buyer must have a valid VAT ID from another member state. The opgaaf ICP must be filed on time and be correct and complete. A late or incorrect ICP may put the zero rate at risk and can lead to a fine. [Belastingdienst, toelichting opgaaf ICP](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Exemptions

Article 11 exemptions cover the listed categories, including supplies and letting of immovable property subject to exceptions, care in institutions, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money and securities, fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, the universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, services by composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list also includes investment gold and canteens, and says the exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies do not carry a right to deduct input VAT. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Logies

Logies was in Tabel I at 9% in 2021. The later law provides that the Tabel I logies entry lapses from 1 January 2026, supporting the 9% treatment before that change. [Wet behoud verlaagd btw-tarief op cultuur, media en sport](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Electronic publications

The 9% rate applied from 1 January 2020 to electronic supplies or lending of publications and access to news websites under post b.21 of Tabel I. Access to news websites is included unless the website or app mainly provides advertising, video or audible music. [Besluit Toelichting Tabel I](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html)

### KOR turnover ceiling

The old KOR in force in 2021 was an optional exemption for an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a fixed establishment there. The Dutch turnover ceiling was no more than € 20.000 in a calendar year. Legal persons, including a BV, could also participate under the 2020 handbook guidance. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

The KOR test for 2021 was the turnover in the current calendar year only. The 2021 version of Article 25 does not impose a separate preceding-year test. Count Dutch taxable turnover at any rate, including reverse-charged supplies, and certain exempt supplies. The 2020 handbook describes the taxable turnover and exempt categories used for this test. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

While using the KOR, the business does not charge VAT on its invoices and cannot deduct input VAT. The KOR does not provide an option to tax letting of immovable property. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR notice, minimum term and re-entry

The entrepreneur had to notify the tax inspector at least 4 weeks before the intended tax period. The exemption applied for at least 3 years after it began. After voluntary revocation, re-entry was possible 3 years later. Voluntary deregistration took effect only on the first day of a tax period. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

If turnover exceeded € 20.000, the supply that crossed the ceiling was taxable and the exemption ended from that supply onward. Re-entry was possible 3 years after the overrun. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)

### KOR registration threshold without notice

An approval allowed an entrepreneur who was not yet registered to apply for the KOR without the usual notice if annual turnover was no more than € 1.800. The approval was issued in 2021 and applied retroactively to 1 January 2020. It does not establish the broader conditions enacted for the later KOR regime. [Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html)

### KOR investment-goods adjustment

The 2020 handbook says no herziening, or adjustment of input VAT previously deducted, is needed when the total annual adjustment for investment goods used before that year is less than € 500. Treat this as the guidance checked for the 2021 administration. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Regular VAT return periods

Quarterly returns are usual. An entrepreneur may request monthly or yearly returns by letter to the tax office. The office replies within 6 weeks, and an approved change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can result in a monthly period being imposed. [Belastingdienst, changing the VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

The 2020 handbook says that entrepreneurs who usually pay more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter have monthly returns. The checked material does not establish that trigger for 2021, so do not use it as a confirmed 2021 rule. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Yearly-return eligibility

The checked handbooks describe a yearly return for a natural person or a partnership of natural persons only, with no Article 23 licence and separate limits for intra-EU supplies, services, acquisitions and services received. They give a VAT limit of less than € 1.883 and a limit of less than € 10.000 for each of the four EU activity categories. The sources establish these amounts for 2020 and 2026, but not for 2021. The exact 2021 eligibility must therefore be checked against a 2021 publication. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### VAT filing and payment term

The statutory term for filing a period return is within a deadline set by the inspector of at least 1 month after the end of the period. Payment is due within 1 month after the period ends. The Belastingdienst describes monthly and quarterly filing and payment as due by the last day of the following month. An annual-return date and the exact 2021 calendar dates are not established by the checked 2021 materials. [AWR, Article 10 and Article 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, filing and payment](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/)

### Published 2021 due-date table

The current Belastingdienst due-date table does not provide the 2021 table, and no archived table was found in the checked material. Use the statutory period rules above, but verify any specific 2021 calendar deadline against an archived official publication. [Belastingdienst, VAT due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Suppletie timing and reach

If a VAT return was incorrect, the entrepreneur had to correct returns for the previous 5 calendar years as soon as possible and before knowing or suspecting that the inspector already knew about the error. The 8-week correction deadline was added later and does not describe the rule for 2021. [UBOB, Article 15, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html)

### Suppletie small-correction boundary

The checked 2020 handbook says a correction of less than € 1.000 may be included in the next return. The 2026 handbook says € 1.000 or less, and the current Belastingdienst page says a maximum of € 1.000. The wording that applied in 2021 is unverified. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, correcting a return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

### E-commerce package

The e-commerce package took effect on 1 July 2021. From that date the EU distance-sales threshold applied to qualifying B2C distance sales of goods and digital services together. [Wet implementatie richtlijnen elektronische handel](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html)

### MOSS and one-stop shop

MOSS ended on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop took its place. The one-stop shop is an optional route for reporting relevant EU distance sales in one return. [Belastingdienst, changes to digital services and VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)

### EU distance-sales threshold

The € 10.000 threshold applies only to a supplier established in one member state. The total of qualifying sales must not exceed the threshold in either the current or previous calendar year. Goods count when sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country and the supplier arranges or pays for transport. Margin goods, nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies are excluded from that goods rule. Digital services to consumers also count toward the threshold, measured excluding VAT. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 6k](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, goods to private customers in the EU](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst, EU VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)

Below the threshold, the supplier may choose to charge VAT in the customer’s country. That choice lasts at least 2 years. If the threshold is exceeded, destination-country VAT applies from the supply that crosses it. Dutch VAT can apply again from 1 January of a later year after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The threshold does not apply where the supplier has a fixed establishment in another EU country. [Belastingdienst, EU VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/), [Belastingdienst, goods to private customers in the EU](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

### One-stop shop (OSS) mechanics

The Union scheme return is quarterly, due by the last day of the following month, including a nil return. It can be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For a first supply, registration must arrive by the 10th day of the month following the month of supply. Three consecutive quarters without a return can lead to exclusion for 2 years. A return cannot be resubmitted. Corrections go in a later return within 3 years of the original due date. Keep the OSS records for 10 years. Report in euro, use the European Central Bank rate from the last day of the quarter where conversion is needed, and make one payment for all member states. [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

The Invoerregeling is monthly and applies to shipments with a value no higher than € 150. Three consecutive missed monthly returns can lead to exclusion for 2 years. [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### Quarterly ICP for goods

An entrepreneur may file the ICP for goods quarterly if goods supplies do not exceed € 50.000 in the current quarter and in each of the previous 4 quarters. If the limit is exceeded, the reporting period changes according to when the overrun occurs. Services may be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with permission. The € 50.000 test does not apply to services. If goods and services are combined in one ICP, the goods reporting rules apply. [Belastingdienst, ICP reporting period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)

### ICP deadline for 2021

The exact 2021 ICP deadline was not found. Do not treat a later ICP guide as proof of the 2021 deadline. The 2021 official explanation is needed to settle it. [Belastingdienst, digital ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Intra-EU B2B services and ICP

For a general B2B service supplied to a customer in another EU country, VAT is generally due in the customer’s country. Check the customer’s VAT ID, issue an invoice without Dutch VAT marked “btw verlegd”, and report the service in the VAT return and ICP. Exceptions include certain services connected with immovable property, passenger transport, admission to events and services exempt or zero-rated in the customer’s country. [Belastingdienst, services to customers in other EU countries](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen), [Belastingdienst, digital ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Reverse charge for specified electronics

The reverse-charge rule for specified electronics applies to mobile phones, chips before they become part of a final product, game consoles, tablets and laptops when the total is € 10 000 or more per delivery per kind of good. The invoice must say “btw verlegd”, include the customer’s VAT ID and show the consideration per rate. The supplier remains responsible for correct invoicing. [UBOB, Article 24ba](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, when to reverse charge VAT](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [Belastingdienst, how reverse charging works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

Other reverse-charge cases include certain foreign suppliers, subcontracting and staff lending, waste, immovable property, execution sales, gas and electricity certificates, gold, emission rights and telecom services between providers in the Netherlands. The customer reports domestic reverse-charge VAT in rubriek 2 or VAT from abroad in rubriek 4. The customer may deduct that VAT if the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [UBOB, Articles 24b, 24ba and 24bb](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, when to reverse charge VAT](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/)

### Invoice issue deadline

Issue an invoice to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. There is generally no invoice requirement for private customers, subject to exceptions. Issue the invoice by the 15th day of the month after the month of supply. The invoice must include the parties’ names and addresses, VAT ID where required, KVK number if registered, invoice date, a unique consecutive number, description and quantity or scope, supply or prepayment date, amounts excluding VAT per rate, rate and VAT amount. [Belastingdienst, making invoices](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

A simplified invoice may be used at most € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice. It must include the issue date, name and address, the goods or services, the VAT or information needed to calculate it, and a reference to the original invoice for a correction. Simplified invoices are not allowed for specified cross-border supplies, distance sales or reverse-charged supplies in a country where the supplier is not established. [Belastingdienst, simplified invoices](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur)

A digital invoice requires customer agreement and safeguards for the authenticity of origin, integrity of content and readability. There is no general digital-invoice requirement established for 2021 by the checked material. [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

### Kasstelsel threshold

The cash accounting method, or kasstelsel, is compulsory for specified trades. It may also be requested where more than 80% of supplies go to consumers. Other businesses generally use the invoice method. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, making invoices](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/)

### Business car private-use adjustment

Private use includes commuting. If records do not establish the private-use amount, the decision provides a 2,7% calculation based on catalogue price including VAT and bpm. The amount is capped at VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of the VAT on the purchase. The lower 1,5% calculation applies if no purchase VAT could be deducted or after the fourth year following the year the entrepreneur started using the car. For a part year, calculate a proportional amount. The adjustment goes in the last VAT return of the year. [Decision on VAT for private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst, private use of a business car](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### BUA provisions and gifts threshold

The Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting (BUA) can deny input VAT deduction for certain staff benefits, gifts and private benefits. For provisions to the same person, total cost of no more than € 227 per accounting year stays outside the exclusion. A contribution from the recipient could reduce the tested amount under the policy applied before the policy was ended. VAT charged to the recipient can also affect the exclusion under the decree. [BUA, Article 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Decision of 6 October 2023, nr. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### BUA staff bicycle conditions

The BUA carve-out covers a staff bicycle costing at most € 749 including VAT if the conditions are met. No bicycle may have been given to that employee in the relevant year or the 2 preceding years. On 50% or more of travel days, the employee must not otherwise have had travel covered by the employer. [BUA, Article 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### BUA staff-meal calculation

For staff meals charged below cost plus 25%, the decree excludes deduction up to 9% of the difference. Food and drink for staff is listed among the BUA carve-outs, but the meal calculation applies in the specified circumstances. [BUA, Articles 1 and 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Corona special deferral

The special deferral arrangement for tax debts ran through 2021. The checked source confirms the scheme but does not give an exact 2021 end date in the material supplied for this file. [Belastingdienst, repayment arrangement for tax debt built up under special deferral](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel)

### General B2B e-invoicing duty

The checked material does not establish whether a general B2B e-invoicing duty applied in 2021. The current business.gov.nl page describes invoicing to government organisations but is not a 2021 historical source. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Standard rate: calculated example

A business sells a taxable service for an invented amount of € 100 excluding VAT.

1. Taxable amount: € 100.
2. VAT: € 100 × 21% = € 21.
3. Total invoice amount: € 100 + € 21 = € 121.

### Reduced rate: calculated example

A business sells an invented item that qualifies for Tabel I for € 100 excluding VAT.

1. Taxable amount: € 100.
2. VAT: € 100 × 9% = € 9.
3. Total invoice amount: € 100 + € 9 = € 109.

### Zero rate and exemption: calculated examples

A business sends goods to a VAT-registered buyer in another EU country. It keeps evidence that the goods were transported there, checks the buyer’s valid VAT ID and files a timely, correct and complete ICP. If all conditions are met, it applies the zero rate and retains the right to deduct related input VAT.

A business provides an exempt service. It does not charge VAT on that service and cannot deduct input VAT connected with the exempt supply. These examples show why a zero-rated supply and an exempt supply have different deduction treatment.

### Logies: calculated example

A business provides qualifying logies for an invented price of € 100 excluding VAT.

1. Taxable amount: € 100.
2. VAT at 9%: € 100 × 9% = € 9.
3. Total: € 100 + € 9 = € 109.

### Electronic publication: calculated example

A business supplies a qualifying electronic publication for an invented price of € 100 excluding VAT.

1. Taxable amount: € 100.
2. VAT at 9%: € 100 × 9% = € 9.
3. Total: € 100 + € 9 = € 109.

### KOR ceiling: calculated example

An entrepreneur using the KOR has invented Dutch turnover of € 19.000 before another supply.

1. Turnover before the supply: € 19.000.
2. Add a supply of € 1.001: € 19.000 + € 1.001 = € 20.001.
3. The supply that crosses the € 20.000 ceiling is taxable from that supply onward under the 2021 rule.

The preceding calendar year’s turnover does not enter this 2021 test.

### KOR registration without notice: calculated example

An entrepreneur who is not yet registered has invented annual turnover of € 1.800.

1. Maximum turnover under the approval: € 1.800.
2. The turnover is at the maximum, not above it.
3. The entrepreneur may seek to use the notice-free route if the other conditions of the approval are met.

### KOR investment-goods adjustment: calculated example

For invented investment goods used before the year, the annual herziening calculation is € 400.

1. Annual adjustment: € 400.
2. The stated de minimis amount is less than € 500.
3. Because € 400 is less than € 500, the 2020 handbook says no adjustment is needed.

### OSS deadline: official example

The official guidance gives Q3 as July through September. The return is made in October and is due by 31 October. [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### EU distance-sales threshold: calculated example

A supplier established in one member state has invented qualifying distance sales and digital services of € 8.000 in the previous calendar year and € 9.000 so far in the current calendar year. It then makes a further qualifying sale of € 2.000.

1. Previous-year total: € 8.000. This stays within € 10.000.
2. Current-year total before the sale: € 9.000. This stays within € 10.000.
3. Current-year total after the sale: € 9.000 + € 2.000 = € 11.000.
4. € 11.000 is above € 10.000, so the current year no longer stays within the threshold.
5. The two years are tested separately. The previous year's € 8.000 is never added to the current year's sales.

### Invoerregeling cap: calculated example

An invented shipment has a value of € 150.

1. Shipment value: € 150.
2. The cap is € 150 per shipment.
3. The shipment is at the cap. A shipment valued at € 151 would be above it.

### Quarterly ICP for goods: calculated example

An entrepreneur has invented intra-EU goods supplies of € 40.000 in the current quarter and € 40.000 in each of the previous 4 quarters.

1. Current quarter: € 40.000, which is no more than € 50.000.
2. Each of the previous 4 quarters is also € 40.000, each no more than € 50.000.
3. The goods reporting condition for quarterly ICP is met.

### Reverse charge for electronics: calculated example

An invented delivery contains one kind of specified electronic good with a total price of € 10 000.

1. Total for that kind of good in that delivery: € 10 000.
2. The reverse-charge trigger is € 10 000 or more per delivery per kind.
3. The trigger is met, so the invoice states “btw verlegd” and identifies the customer’s VAT ID.

### Invoice timing: official example

The handbook example is a supply delivered on 10 April. The invoice must be issued by 15 May. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Simplified invoice: calculated example

A correction invoice has an invented total of € 100 including VAT and refers to the original invoice.

1. Total including VAT: € 100.
2. The simplified-invoice ceiling is at most € 100 including VAT.
3. The amount meets the ceiling, subject to the other required contents and exceptions.

### Kasstelsel: calculated example

An entrepreneur has invented consumer sales equal to 81% of all supplies.

1. Consumer share: 81%.
2. The stated threshold for requesting the cash method is more than 80%.
3. The consumer share is above that threshold, so the entrepreneur may request the method.

### Business car private-use adjustment: calculated example

An entrepreneur has an invented business car catalogue price of € 20.000 and has no records of private use.

1. At 2,7%: € 20.000 × 2,7% = € 540.
2. If the lower 1,5% rate applies: € 20.000 × 1,5% = € 300.
3. Apply the relevant cap to the calculated amount before recording the annual adjustment.

### BUA provisions and gifts: calculated example

An employee receives invented provisions costing € 240 in the accounting year.

1. Total cost: € 240.
2. Threshold: € 227.
3. Excess over the threshold: € 240 − € 227 = € 13.
4. The threshold test is exceeded by € 13. The amount of any VAT adjustment depends on the VAT and other facts for the provisions.

### BUA staff bicycle: calculated example

An employer provides an invented staff bicycle costing € 749 including VAT.

1. Bicycle cost including VAT: € 749.
2. The maximum under the carve-out is € 749.
3. The cost is at the maximum, but the employee must also meet the other bicycle conditions.

### BUA staff meal: calculated example

A staff meal has an invented cost of € 100 and is charged below cost plus the stated 25%.

1. Cost: € 100.
2. Cost plus 25%: € 100 + (€ 100 × 25%) = € 125.
3. The difference between the threshold and the amount charged is the base for the stated 9% calculation.
4. The deduction exclusion is limited to 9% of that difference.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

- The 21%, 9% and zero rates continued from 2020 into 2021. The 2020 and 2021 statutory versions show the same rate structure. [2020 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)
- The KOR ceiling remained € 20.000, and the 2021 rule still tested the current calendar year only. The preceding-year test described in later guidance does not apply to the old 2021 Article 25 rule. [2020 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)
- From 1 July 2021, the e-commerce package introduced the € 10.000 EU distance-sales threshold for qualifying goods and digital services together. [Wet implementatie richtlijnen elektronische handel](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, goods to private customers in the EU](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)
- MOSS ended on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop took its place. Before the change, MOSS applied to B2C digital services. [Belastingdienst, changes to digital services and VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)
- A 2021 approval allowed certain entrepreneurs not yet registered to apply for the KOR without the usual notice if annual turnover was no more than € 1.800. It applied retroactively to 1 January 2020, so it did not introduce a new effective date for 2021. [Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html)
- The checked material says the KOR, rate and suppletie rules of 2020 continued unchanged in 2021, except for the e-commerce changes described above. [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [UBOB, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01)

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT return, including invoices, the rate applied, and any reverse-charge treatment. An invoice record should show the required parties, dates, consecutive invoice number, description, taxable amounts per rate, rate and VAT amount. [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)
- Issue invoices by the 15th day of the month after supply when an invoice is required. A customer must agree to digital invoices, and the supplier must preserve authenticity of origin, content integrity and readability. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)
- Reconcile output VAT, deductible input VAT and reverse-charged amounts to the VAT return. For domestic reverse charge, the customer reports in rubriek 2. For reverse charge from abroad, the customer reports in rubriek 4 and deducts the VAT only where the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Belastingdienst, how reverse charging works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)
- For intra-EU goods, retain transport evidence and the customer’s VAT ID, and file the ICP correctly and on time to support the zero rate. Report intra-EU goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country, and call-off stock transfers in the ICP. The ICP total equals rubriek 3b for the same period. [Belastingdienst, ICP reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, ICP reporting period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)
- For OSS supplies, keep the records required by the scheme and file a return even when there is nothing to report. Make corrections in a later return and retain records for the stated retention period. [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- Put the annual private-use adjustments in the last VAT return of the year. Review private use of goods and services, the business car, the taxable and exempt turnover ratio, and staff provisions, gifts and business gifts above the BUA threshold. Also review gas, water and electricity, deemed services and herziening where relevant. If the last return has already been filed, correct it with a suppletie. [Belastingdienst, private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Belastingdienst, last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)
- If using the KOR, do not charge VAT on invoices and do not deduct input VAT. Keep turnover records that allow the business to monitor the calendar-year ceiling. Legal persons, including a BV, could use the 2021 KOR, but confirm which entity made each supply and incurred each cost. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)
- File and pay within the applicable period. The exact official 2021 date table and exact 2021 ICP deadline were not found in the checked sources. [AWR, filing and payment terms](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, VAT due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
