# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2021

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2021 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 3 (sparen en beleggen, savings and investments) for 2021 taxes a fictitious return on the assets and debts held on the peildatum (reference date) of 1 January 2021. The year runs on the old band method with three bands over the grondslag sparen en beleggen and a rate of 31%. The Kerstarrest of the Hoge Raad of 24 December 2021 (ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) falls in this year, and the later Wet rechtsherstel box 3 brings every 2021 IB/PVV assessment into scope of a category recalculation, with the Belastingdienst applying the most favourable of the two methods. All 2021 values in the checked material are enacted, the rechtsherstel law itself was enacted on 21 December 2022 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

### 3.1 Heffingsvrij vermogen

The heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free wealth allowance) for 2021 is € 50.000 per person and € 100.000 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [art. 5.5, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The allowance is deducted from the rendementsgrondslag to give the grondslag sparen en beleggen, so a single person pays box 3 tax only on wealth above € 50.000 on the peildatum of 1 January 2021. The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership, with the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17 ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)). In the actual-return route the allowance is not applied, because the Hoge Raad has determined that no account may be taken of the heffingsvrij vermogen in that calculation ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### 3.2 Schuldendrempel

Only the part of the total box 3 debts above € 3.200 is deductible. With a whole-year partner the schuldendrempel is € 6.400 ([FISIN 2021, bezittingen en schulden box 3](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_), [art. 5.3 lid 3, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The statute phrases this as debts of which the total "daarvan meer bedraagt dan" the drempel, meaning the threshold is not deductible itself ([art. 5.3 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return, so the taxpayer does not apply it manually ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). A joint drempel requires both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 5.3 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). In both the band method and the category recalculation the drempel is subtracted from the debts before the return is computed.

### 3.3 Box 3 tarief

The box 3 rate for 2021 is 31% ([art. 2.13, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The rate is applied to the voordeel uit sparen en beleggen, whichever method produces that voordeel. In 2020 the rate was 30%, so 2021 is the first year of the 31% rate ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### 3.4 Rendementsklasse percentages

Art. 5.2 lid 1 splits the fictitious return into two rendementsklassen (return classes), klasse I for savings at 0,03% and klasse II for investments at 5,69% ([art. 5.2 lid 1 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The two percentages are not applied per asset in this method. They are mixed per band, as sections 3.5 to 3.7 show.

### 3.5 Band 1

The first band covers the part of the grondslag sparen en beleggen up to € 50.000 and is treated as 67% rendementsklasse I and 33% rendementsklasse II, an average return of 1,898% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page writes the same limit from below as "tot € 50.001". The statute writes the limits as "meer dan / niet meer dan" and gives € 50.000 for 2021. Both descriptions cover the same band ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.6 Band 2

The second band covers the grondslag sparen en beleggen from € 50.000 to € 950.000 and is treated as 21% rendementsklasse I and 79% rendementsklasse II, an average return of 4,501% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The Belastingdienst page writes the same band as € 50.001 to € 950.001. The statute limit for 2021 is € 950.000 ([art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.7 Band 3

The third band covers the grondslag sparen en beleggen above € 950.000 and is treated as 0% rendementsklasse I and 100% rendementsklasse II, so the full 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The bands are applied to the grondslag sparen en beleggen, that is the rendementsgrondslag after the heffingsvrij vermogen. The voordeel from the three bands is added and taxed at 31% ([art. 2.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.8 Rechtsherstel category percentages

The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 recalculates the 2021 voordeel with three category percentages, banktegoeden 0,01%, overige bezittingen 5,69% and schulden 2,46% ([Belastingdienst, met welke percentages is het fictief rendement berekend](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend), [Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 3 of that law multiplies the banktegoeden by the column I percentage, the overige bezittingen by column II and the deductible schulden by column III, all at 1 January of the year ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). These percentages differ from the rendementsklasse percentages of 2021 and are used only in the recalculation.

### 3.9 Rechtsherstel scope 2021

Art. 1 lid 2 of the Wet rechtsherstel brings all IB/PVV assessments for 2021 into scope ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 1 lid 3 applies the law only where it leads to a lower voordeel, and art. 2 sets the voordeel at the outcome of art. 3 with a floor of nil ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 3 lid 4 provides that whole-year partners use the joint figures ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). The recalculation is therefore built on the assets and debts on 1 January 2021, split into the three categories.

### 3.10 Most favourable method

For 2021 and 2022 the Belastingdienst computes box 3 income under the old method and the current method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment. This also applies if the taxpayer had rechtsherstel over the period 2017 to 2022 ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). There is no election to make, the comparison is made by the Belastingdienst. Section 4 shows both computations for the same taxpayer, once with the category method winning and once with the band method winning.

### 3.11 Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

Green investments (groene beleggingen) are not counted as bezittingen up to € 60.429 in 2021. The amount is doubled to € 120.858 for whole-year partners ([art. 5.13, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)). The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January ([Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The text of art. 5.13 lid 2 from 2023 orders the exemption first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden, the ordering in the 2021 text is not in the checked material ([art. 5.13, text from 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).

### 3.12 Korting groene beleggingen

Art. 8.19 gives the korting voor groene beleggingen (green investment credit) of 0,7% of the exempt amount on the peildatum ([art. 8.19 lid 2, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The 0,7% applies for 2020 to 2024 ([Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)). The credit is computed automatically and is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

### 3.13 Actual-return reporting 2021

For 2021 the taxpayer may always file the online form Opgaaf werkelijk rendement in Mijn Belastingdienst, without a letter. The page states "Voor 2021 tot en met 2024 mag u altijd het formulier Opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen" ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)). Without a letter the form asks "Nee" to the letter question and the amount needed is the "Voordeel sparen en beleggen", three amounts with a whole-year partner ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07, one form per call, and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543 ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)). The checked pages state no deadline for the 2021 form ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)). The route for 2021 runs through art. 6a of the Wet rechtsherstel, added by art. IV of the Wet tegenbewijsregeling, which works back to 1 January 2017, with a form provided by the inspecteur and up to six weeks for further documents after the form is sent ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). If the taxpayer makes the lower actual return plausible, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek, and the actual return is at least nil ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The actual return is income plus value changes over the calendar year, gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year, costs are not deductible except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality and interest paid on a box 3 debt, and the report is optional with the Belastingdienst using the most favourable amount ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst, werkelijk rendement belastingaangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)). The taxpayer never pays more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### 3.14 Kerstarrest 24 December 2021

The Kerstarrest is the ruling of the Hoge Raad of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963 ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html), [Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). The memorie van toelichting to the Wet rechtsherstel describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM", because the forfaitaire system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Forfaits are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). The memorie van toelichting stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on the actual return for the rechtsherstel, but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). The claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.15 Wet rechtsherstel box 3, entry into force

The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, issued on 27 December 2022, Kamerstuk 36 203 ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 7 puts the law in force on the day after the issue date of the Staatsblad, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support, see section 7 ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). Art. 5 lets an extra persoonsgebonden aftrek follow the partners' chosen division of specific care costs or gifts, with an ambtshalve-vermindering request for another split, and art. 6 recomputes the double-taxation relief ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Mass objectors get rechtsherstel for 2017 to 2020 ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). For 2021 the scope is every assessment, see section 3.9.

### 3.16 Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3

The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. It entered into force by the besluit of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, so on 19 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). Art. IV works back to 1 January 2017 and puts art. 6a in the Wet rechtsherstel with the same form and six-week rule, which is the route for a 2021 assessment ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The nota van toelichting of Stb. 2025, 196 names HR 6 June 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705, HR 14 June 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857, HR 2 August 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084, and HR 20 December 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788 ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). In the 6 June 2024 ruling the Hoge Raad held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). On 25 June 2026 the Hoge Raad held that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". Whether late objectors are covered as well is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

The checked material contains no official example for 2021, so every example below is a `Calculated example` built from the section 2 figures. The inputs are invented round amounts.

### Calculated example 1. Single taxpayer, both methods, most favourable

Inputs on 1 January 2021: banktegoeden € 200.000, overige bezittingen € 150.000, schulden € 20.000, no partner.

Band method, step by step:

1. Bezittingen: € 200.000 + € 150.000 = € 350.000.
2. Deductible debts: € 20.000 - € 3.200 schuldendrempel = € 16.800.
3. Rendementsgrondslag: € 350.000 - € 16.800 = € 333.200.
4. Heffingsvrij vermogen € 50.000, so the grondslag sparen en beleggen is € 333.200 - € 50.000 = € 283.200.
5. Band 1: € 50.000 x 1,898% = € 949,00.
6. Band 2: € 283.200 - € 50.000 = € 233.200, and € 233.200 x 4,501% = € 10.496,33.
7. Band 3: nothing, the grondslag stays below € 950.000.
8. Voordeel: € 949,00 + € 10.496,33 = € 11.445,33.
9. Tax: € 11.445,33 x 31% = € 3.548,05.

Category method under the Wet rechtsherstel, step by step:

1. Step 1 belastbaar rendement: € 200.000 x 0,01% = € 20,00, plus € 150.000 x 5,69% = € 8.535,00, together € 8.555,00, minus € 16.800 x 2,46% = € 413,28, so € 8.141,72.
2. Step 2 rendementsgrondslag: € 350.000 - € 16.800 = € 333.200.
3. Step 3 grondslag sparen en beleggen: € 333.200 - € 50.000 = € 283.200.
4. Step 4 aandeel: € 283.200 / € 333.200 x 100 = 84,9939976%. The checked sources do not state the 2021 rounding for this step, so the unrounded percentage is used here.
5. Step 5 voordeel: € 8.141,72 x 84,9939976% = € 6.919,97.
6. Step 6 tax: € 6.919,97 x 31% = € 2.145,19.

Most favourable method: € 2.145,19 from the category method is lower than € 3.548,05 from the band method, so the definitive assessment uses € 2.145,19.

### Calculated example 2. Whole-year partners, band method wins

Inputs on 1 January 2021: joint banktegoeden € 80.000, joint overige bezittingen € 90.000, joint schulden € 10.400, whole-year partners.

Band method, step by step:

1. Bezittingen: € 80.000 + € 90.000 = € 170.000.
2. Deductible debts: € 10.400 - € 6.400 drempel for whole-year partners = € 4.000.
3. Rendementsgrondslag: € 170.000 - € 4.000 = € 166.000.
4. Heffingsvrij vermogen € 100.000 for partners, so the grondslag sparen en beleggen is € 166.000 - € 100.000 = € 66.000.
5. Band 1: € 50.000 x 1,898% = € 949,00.
6. Band 2: € 66.000 - € 50.000 = € 16.000, and € 16.000 x 4,501% = € 720,16.
7. Voordeel: € 949,00 + € 720,16 = € 1.669,16.
8. Tax: € 1.669,16 x 31% = € 517,44.
9. The partners may divide the joint grondslag in any ratio that totals 100%. With a 50/50 division each partner takes € 33.000 of the grondslag, half of the voordeel (€ 834,58) and half of the tax (€ 258,72).

Category method under the Wet rechtsherstel, step by step:

1. Step 1 belastbaar rendement: € 80.000 x 0,01% = € 8,00, plus € 90.000 x 5,69% = € 5.121,00, together € 5.129,00, minus € 4.000 x 2,46% = € 98,40, so € 5.030,60.
2. Step 2 rendementsgrondslag: € 166.000.
3. Step 3 grondslag sparen en beleggen: € 66.000.
4. Step 4 aandeel: € 66.000 / € 166.000 x 100 = 39,7590361%.
5. Step 5 voordeel: € 5.030,60 x 39,7590361% = € 2.000,12.
6. Step 6 tax: € 2.000,12 x 31% = € 620,04.

Most favourable method: € 517,44 from the band method is lower than € 620,04 from the category method, so the definitive assessment uses € 517,44.

### Calculated example 3. Green investments with the exemption and the korting

Inputs on 1 January 2021: single person, green beleggingen € 70.000, banktegoeden € 150.000, no debts.

1. The green exemption is € 60.429 for a single person, so € 60.429 of the € 70.000 green investments is not counted.
2. Counted bezittingen: € 150.000 + (€ 70.000 - € 60.429) = € 150.000 + € 9.571 = € 159.571.
3. No debts, so the rendementsgrondslag is € 159.571 and the grondslag sparen en beleggen is € 159.571 - € 50.000 = € 109.571.
4. Band 1: € 50.000 x 1,898% = € 949,00.
5. Band 2: € 109.571 - € 50.000 = € 59.571, and € 59.571 x 4,501% = € 2.681,29.
6. Voordeel: € 949,00 + € 2.681,29 = € 3.630,29.
7. Tax: € 3.630,29 x 31% = € 1.125,39.
8. Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% x € 60.429 = € 423,00.
9. Tax after the credit: € 1.125,39 - € 423,00 = € 702,39.

The exemption is capped at the value of the green investments on 1 January, so a green balance below € 60.429 would exempt only that lower balance.

### Calculated example 4. Actual return with a loss

Inputs on 1 January 2021: single person, banktegoeden € 100.000, overige bezittingen € 50.000, no debts.

Fictitious calculation, band method:

1. Grondslag sparen en beleggen: € 150.000 - € 50.000 = € 100.000.
2. Band 1: € 50.000 x 1,898% = € 949,00.
3. Band 2: € 50.000 x 4,501% = € 2.250,50.
4. Voordeel: € 949,00 + € 2.250,50 = € 3.199,50.
5. Tax: € 3.199,50 x 31% = € 991,85.

Fictitious calculation, category method:

1. Step 1 belastbaar rendement: € 100.000 x 0,01% = € 10,00, plus € 50.000 x 5,69% = € 2.845,00, so € 2.855,00.
2. Step 2 rendementsgrondslag: € 150.000.
3. Step 3 grondslag sparen en beleggen: € 100.000.
4. Step 4 aandeel: € 100.000 / € 150.000 x 100 = 66,6666667%.
5. Step 5 voordeel: € 2.855,00 x 66,6666667% = € 1.903,33.
6. Step 6 tax: € 1.903,33 x 31% = € 590,03.

Actual return reported for the year: regular benefits € 500 plus value changes of minus € 2.400, total minus € 1.900. The actual return is at least nil, so the box 3 income becomes € 0, no heffingsvrij vermogen is applied in this calculation, and the negative € 1.900 cannot be carried to another year.

Outcome: the lowest fictitious amount is € 590,03 from the category method, the actual return gives € 0, and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. The box 3 income is € 0 and the tax is 31% x € 0 = € 0.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 30.846 per person becomes € 50.000, and € 61.692 becomes € 100.000 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [art. 5.5 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 5.5 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.100 becomes € 3.200 per person, and € 6.200 becomes € 6.400 with a whole-year partner ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)).
- Box 3 tarief: 30% becomes 31% ([art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Rendementsklasse percentages: klasse I 0,07% becomes 0,03%, klasse II 5,28% becomes 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Band 1: the statute limit of € 72.797 becomes € 50.000, the page limit "tot € 72.798" becomes "tot € 50.001", and the average return 1,789% becomes 1,898% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [2020 statute](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [2021 statute](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Band 2: € 72.797 to € 1.005.572 becomes € 50.000 to € 950.000, the page band € 72.798 to € 1.005.573 becomes € 50.001 to € 950.001, and the average return 4,185% becomes 4,501% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Band 3: the threshold above € 1.005.572 becomes above € 950.000, and the return 5,28% becomes 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Rechtsherstel category percentages, applied per year by the later law: bank 0,04% becomes 0,01%, other 5,28% becomes 5,69%, and schulden 2,74% becomes 2,46% ([Belastingdienst, met welke percentages is het fictief rendement berekend](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend), [Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 59.477 becomes € 60.429, and € 118.954 becomes € 120.858 with a whole-year partner ([art. 5.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 5.13 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)).
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% in 2020 and 0,7% in 2021, so no change in this year ([art. 8.19 lid 2 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- Actual-return reporting: for 2020 the form could only be filed with a letter ("Voor 2017 tot en met 2020 zijn alle brieven verstuurd"), while for 2021 it may always be filed without a letter ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)).
- Scope of the later Wet rechtsherstel: for 2020 only assessments not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 are in scope, while for 2021 all assessments are in scope ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- New in this year: the Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963, falls in 2021 and set the rechtsherstel process in motion ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html), [Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the box 3 assets and debts at 1 January 2021. The category recalculation of art. 3 of the Wet rechtsherstel uses the banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden at 1 January ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- Debts count only above the schuldendrempel of € 3.200, or € 6.400 for whole-year partners. The Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return, so the records only need to show the total debts on the peildatum ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Keep the 1 January valuation of green investments. The exemption of € 60.429 (€ 120.858 for whole-year partners) never exceeds that value, and the 0,7% korting is computed on the exempt amount and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 8.19 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- The most favourable of the old method and the category method is applied automatically on the definitive assessment, so the return itself needs no method choice ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- For 2021 the actual return can always be reported through the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst without a letter, and further documents can follow up to six weeks after the form is sent ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen), [Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).
- The box 3 voordeel lands on the IB/PVV return for 2021. The checked pages state no filing deadline for the actual-return form ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Whether the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return is UNVERIFIED. The memorie van toelichting describes a breach of art. 1 EP with art. 14 EVRM and a recalculation in three categories, and hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3. The ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl would settle it.
- Entry date of the Wet rechtsherstel box 3. The statute art. 7 says the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking" (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The text of the statute settles it.
- Whether the Hoge Raad ruling of 25 June 2026 also covers late objectors is UNVERIFIED. The part about non-objectors for 2017 to 2020 is confirmed by the Rijksoverheid timeline (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The "or objected late" part is not on that page. The ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl would settle it, and hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11.
- The rounding instruction for step 4 of the category calculation in 2021 is not in the checked sources. The six-step method is Verified, but the checked Belastingdienst pages give the rounding only for 2023 to 2026, namely 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages and 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026). The 2021 rounding would have to be confirmed on a 2021 page.
- No deadline for the 2021 actual-return form is stated on the checked pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement).
