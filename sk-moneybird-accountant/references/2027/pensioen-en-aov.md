## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the jaarruimte (annual deduction room) and reserveringsruimte (carry-forward room) for lijfrentepremies, the lijfrente products and their uitkering conditions, the stakingswinst conversion, the AOV premium deduction and the state of the arbeidsongeschiktheid rules for a ZZP sole trader in 2027. The jaarruimte percentage of 30% is enacted in the 2027-01-01 text of the law, but almost every indexed 2027 amount is still missing and waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation. The year is a mix of three layers: the enacted 2027-01-01 text of the law with 2026 amounts still in place, the Belastingplan 2027 bill that proposes a freeze of the aftoppingsgrens and a limited indexation of the lijfrente maxima, and the BAZ bill that is not in force. There is no mandatory AOV for self-employed persons in 2027.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Jaarruimte percentage

Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year deduct lijfrentepremies up to a percentage of the premiegrondslag, reduced by the art. 3.127 lid 4 reduction for pension accrual ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 that percentage is 30% ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027-01-01 text exists at the base URL of the law. The percentage is not fixed in the law itself. Art. 10.2b lid 3 sets the jaarruimte percentage each year equal to the art. 18a lid 1 Wet LB 1964 percentage, so the December regulation can reset it ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 30% runs from the Wtp and the Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" ([Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The old percentage was 13,3% in the 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01 texts, and up to 2022 the outcome was also reduced by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

The retroactivity of the surrounding changes is narrower than the 30% change itself. Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6, back to 1 January 2023, and Stb. 2025, 431, in force 1 January 2026, only rewrites the partnerpensioen wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023 ("partnerpensioen bij overlijden op of na pensioendatum ... voor zover deze ingelegde premies het gevolg zijn van de toeneming van de diensttijd in dat kalenderjaar") ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2026-01-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

Art. 3.127 lid 3 builds the premiegrondslag from the previous calendar year. The components are (a) winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek in the 2023-07-01 and later texts, and in the 2020 to 2023-01-01 texts winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek, (b) belastbaar loon, (c) belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden and (d) belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ([art. 3.127, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The sum is counted up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (aftoppingsgrens, cap) and then reduced by the laatstvermelde bedrag (franchise, threshold) ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

For 2027 the aftoppingsgrens in the text is still € 137.800 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes this amount with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor in the December regulation, so the number in the text changes with that regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). In the 2020 text art. 10.2b lid 1 derived the FOR maximum from the art. 3.127 lid 3 amounts times the FOR percentage and lid 2 indexed the aftoppingsgrens ([art. 10.2b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingplan 2027 memorie says the aftoppingsgrens was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (amendement Grinwis c.s., Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90) ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)). Art. 3.127 lid 5 gives a staking option. On (partial) staking the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

### Franchise premiegrondslag

The franchise is the laatstvermelde bedrag subtracted from the capped income ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the AOW benefit for married persons without toeslag plus vakantieuitkering times 100/75, fixed in the December regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 franchise is not published. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 19.172, which is the 2026 amount ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Any 2027 calculation made with € 19.172 is therefore provisional and marked UNVERIFIED.

### Maximum jaarruimte

For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: percentage x (min(income of the previous year, aftoppingsgrens) minus franchise) ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). No maximum jaarruimte for 2027 is published and no Ministry table for 2027 exists ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

When the taxpayer accrues pension, the outcome is reduced by the art. 3.127 lid 4 amount. The 2020 to 2023-07-01 texts use 6,27 keer the aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction is the premiums ingelegd in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen and partnerpensioen, excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor ([art. 3.127, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964, and that also covers accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2 gives, for a fixed premium per dienstjaar under art. 38r, the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 July 2023: A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor. The older formula was A = (1 - (X - Y) / X) x B ([art. 10a.25, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

Deduction timing follows the payment. Premiums count in the year they are paid or verrekend (art. 3.130 lid 1) ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Premiums for the stakingswinst conversion (art. 3.129), the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2) ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14 ([art. 10a.29, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum

Art. 3.127 lid 2 caps the reserveringsruimte ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 42.753, which is the 2026 amount ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). From the 2023-07-01 text there is one flat euro maximum, with no percentage cap and no age split ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). In 2020 to 2022 the cap was at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount, raised to a higher euro amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). Belastingplan 2027 art. LVII proposes to index the reserveringsruimte maximum with 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, and that proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Reserveringsruimte look-back window

Art. 3.127 lid 2 lets unused jaarruimte of the preceding period be used on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text says "tien jaar", so for 2027 the window is the jaarruimte of 2017 to 2026 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page states unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027 and advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)). The period was "zeven jaar" in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

### Age limit jaarruimte

From the Wtp to 2027 the taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age at the start of the calendar year ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe the limit as "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). In 2020 to 2022 and in the 2023-01-01 text the limit was the AOW age itself ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Belastingdienst page for 2026 adds that a taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the jaarruimte 2026 but may still use the reserveringsruimte 2026 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)). No 2027 birth date limit is published.

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

There are three lijfrente forms: the lijfrenteverzekering, the lijfrenterekening and the lijfrentebeleggingsrecht ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). For an oudedagslijfrente under art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel a the first termijn starts at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, and the payments run until death. The FISIN example gives AOW age in 2030 and a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. For a rekening or beleggingsrecht the termijn runs at least 20 years plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year, so a start at 63 with AOW age 67 gives 24 years ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). A nabestaandenlijfrente under art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel b for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and for a rekening or beleggingsrecht it runs at least 20 years, or at least 5 years for children under 30 ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind is deductible without a pensioentekort (art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel b) ([art. 3.124, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

The tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel c needs at least five years of termijnen, starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The caps per year were € 22.089 (2020), € 22.443 (2021), € 22.735 (2022), € 24.168 (2023), € 26.463 (2024), € 26.781 (2025) and € 27.192 (2026) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The 2027 cap is not published. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 27.192 ([art. 3.125, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN adds that premiums above the cap are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Belastingplan 2027 proposes to index this maximum with 1,01248 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Lijfrente einddatum 2026 deadline

A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum, called the einddatum in FISIN 2023 and later, must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2026 gives einddatum 2025 meaning by 31 December 2026, and einddatum 2026 meaning by 31 December 2027. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop or deblokkering, the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente is due ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Nabestaandenlijfrente deadline for a death in 2026

For a nabestaandenlijfrente the deadline is 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2023 and later phrase it as "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", with the same outcome. FISIN 2026 gives a death in 2026 meaning by 31 December 2028. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Stakingswinst to lijfrente maxima

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at a tier maximum minus the provisions already built. The amounts to subtract are the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts and, from 2023, the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The tiers are: tier a for a taxpayer whose age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or who is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt with the lijfrente starting within six months, or who stakes by death, tier b for an age at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or a lijfrente that starts immediately, and tier c for the other cases ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 574.867, € 287.445 and € 143.732. The 2027 amounts are not published ([art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023, when the AOW age was 66 years and 10 months ([FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd" and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). No 2027 age figure is published. Belastingplan 2027 proposes to index the stakingswinst maximum with 1,01248 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows afkoop of a small lijfrente in one payment below a yearly amount ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 5.513, which is the 2026 amount. The December 2026 regulation is pending ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). A refund of a deducted premium is a negatieve uitgave without revisierente, and for a lijfrente only if the refund is undone within 30 days ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Afkoop maximum bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

Under art. 3.133 lid 9 afkoop is not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer or gerechtigde is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if that is higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 51.440, which is the 2026 amount of art. 3.133 lid 9 onderdeel c. The 2027 amount is not published ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The overbruggingslijfrente is only available for lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching AOW age or in the year of starting a pensioen. The yearly maximum is € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2027 returns 404, so the 2027 value is carried at € 63.288 and marked UNVERIFIED.

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen, with no ruimte cap in the article ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN states the premiums can be "volledig aftrekken" ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The article is unchanged in the 2027 text ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Four exclusions apply. Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for loonheffing, mandatory Ziektewet and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen, and Zvw premiums. These four exclusions and section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

For an ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" and loonbelasting is withheld as voorheffing ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)). The page gives the rule of thumb that a premium that is not deductible means the payout is not taxed, and the reverse.

### AOV premium with a lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)). This follows from art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c, which requires rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen, so a policy that pays a lump sum such as a kapitaalverzekering falls outside the deduction ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Oudedagsreserve toevoeging

Access to a FOR toevoeging (addition to the old-age reserve) requires the urencriterium and not yet having reached AOW age at the start of the year (art. 3.67). Art. 3.68 set the toevoeging at 9,44% of the winst, capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst, and not above the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen. Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows an afname by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at staking, at AOW age at the start of the year, or on failing the urencriterium this year and last year ([art. 3.67, 3.68, 3.70, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).

From 2023 no toevoeging is possible and an existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 add the transition for the jaarruimte. For a taxpayer with a FOR the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, vóór toevoeging en vóór ondernemersaftrek, and for 2023 and 2024 the jaarruimte is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the year-end afneming ([art. 10a.29, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023 ([art. 3.128, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the onderneming and takes the FOR over on request ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The 2027-01-01 text keeps the FOR closed to new toevoegingen ([art. 10a.29, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Aftoppingsgrens freeze 2027 to 2032

Article LVII of the Belastingplan 2027 bill proposes no indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensioengevend loon at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the amount would stay € 137.800. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)). Without the bill, art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor in the December 2026 regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Limited indexation of the lijfrente maxima

The bill applies a limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, to the lijfrente maxima listed in the memorie van toelichting. Those are the maximum for lijfrentevoorzieningen (art. 3.125), the maximum for the rekening and the beleggingsrecht, the maximum in aanmerking te nemen premies (art. 3.127), the stakingswinst maximum (art. 3.129) and the maximum for negatieve uitgaven. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### BAZ in force in 2027

No. The Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, bill 36 912, entered the Tweede Kamer on 24 March 2026 and the docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag". The inbreng verslag was delivered on 27 May 2026 and the Verslag document 36 912 nr. 5 was published on 29 May 2026. There is no plenary date. Entry into force is by koninklijk besluit and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315)). There is no mandatory AOV for self-employed persons in 2027.

### BAZ premium estimate

The bill provides a wachttijd of 104 weken in art. 16 lid 1 ([bill text](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313)). The premium would be set each year by UWV and is estimated at about 5,4% of the winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag. The MvT examples use a maximum grondslag of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month ([MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315)). Self-employed persons who are also employees have their SV-loon subtracted from the maximum premiegrondslag. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, and UWV benefits would carry loonheffing. Private policies keep the current tax treatment ([MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315)). All of this is proposed and not in force in 2027.

### BAZ benefit maximum

The benefit is 70% of the grondslag. The grondslag is at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so the benefit is at most 100% of the minimumloon. The MvT examples give about € 171 per month for a winst of € 38.000 or € 45.000, about € 113 per month for a winst of € 25.000, € 0 for a negative winst, and a grondslag of € 13.000 with about € 59 per month for a winst of € 25.000 plus € 25.000 SV-loon ([MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315)). All of this is proposed and not in force in 2027.

### BAZ wachttijd

Bill art. 16 lid 1 sets a wachttijd of 104 weken ([bill text](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313)). The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with the bill text. The bill text and MvT 5.3.1 settle this in favour of 104 weken ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [bill text](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313)).

### Zelfstandigenwet start

The internetconsultatie for the Zelfstandigenwet runs until 29 October 2026. The bill introduces a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets that lead to a "veilige haven", with a beoogde inwerkingtreding of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension ([Rijksoverheid, Zelfstandigenwet](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers)). It is not an AOV.

### Sectoraal pensioenfonds proef

A self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible and the proef runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimenteerbepaling for zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions ([art. 10a.26, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Separately from the proef, a mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples for 2027 were found in the checked sources, so every example below is marked `Calculated example`. Every figure used comes from section 2. All inputs are invented round amounts.

### Calculated example: jaarruimte 2027 with a profit of € 60.000

A self-employed person without pension accrual and without a FOR had a profit of € 60.000 in 2026. The 2027 jaarruimte uses the 2026 income. The 2027 franchise is UNVERIFIED, so the calculation uses the € 19.172 still in the 2027 text.

1. Cap the income at the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 60.000, € 137.800) = € 60.000.
2. Subtract the franchise: € 60.000 - € 19.172 = € 40.828.
3. Apply the percentage: € 40.828 x 0,30 = € 12.248,40.

Outcome: at most € 12.248,40 of lijfrentepremies is deductible in 2027. If the 2027 franchise is set higher than € 19.172, the deductible amount falls.

### Calculated example: maximum jaarruimte 2027 with the full aftoppingsgrens

A taxpayer had an income of € 200.000 in 2026.

1. Cap the income at the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 200.000, € 137.800) = € 137.800.
2. Subtract the franchise: € 137.800 - € 19.172 = € 118.628.
3. Apply the percentage: € 118.628 x 0,30 = € 35.588,40.

Outcome: € 35.588,40 on the amounts still in the 2027 text. This matches the open point that with the frozen aftoppingsgrens and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589.

### Calculated example: reserveringsruimte in 2027

A taxpayer has unused jaarruimte of € 8.000 from 2017 and € 5.000 from 2018.

1. Window for 2027: the jaarruimte of 2017 to 2026. Both years fall inside it. Unused 2016 ruimte cannot be used because it lapses in 2027.
2. Unused ruimte inside the window: € 8.000 + € 5.000 = € 13.000.
3. Cap: min(€ 13.000, € 42.753) = € 13.000. The € 42.753 is the amount still in the 2027 text and is UNVERIFIED.

Outcome: up to € 13.000 of extra lijfrentepremies is deductible on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first. Using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte prevents the older 2017 ruimte from lapsing.

### Calculated example: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente above the cap

A taxpayer wants a yearly uitkering of € 30.000 with 5 yearly termijnen and pays premiums in 2027. The 2027 cap is not published, so the calculation uses the € 27.192 still in the 2027 text.

1. Amount above the cap: € 30.000 - € 27.192 = € 2.808.
2. Termijnen: 5 years, which meets the minimum of at least five years of termijnen.
3. Deductibility: the premiums above the cap are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. With 5 termijnen the € 2.808 above the cap is not deductible.

Outcome: with 5 termijnen the premiums up to the cap are deductible and the above-cap part is not. If the same uitkering runs at least 20 years, the above-cap premiums stay deductible.

### Calculated example: stakingswinst to lijfrente in tier b

A taxpayer stakes the onderneming in 2027 at an age 10 years below the AOW age and does not start the lijfrente immediately. That puts the case in tier b. The 2027 amounts are not published, so the calculation uses the € 287.445 still in the 2027 text. The stakingswinst is € 200.000 and € 25.000 of lijfrentepremies was deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years.

1. Cap after subtracting the earlier deductions: € 287.445 - € 25.000 = € 262.445.
2. Deduction limit: min(€ 200.000, € 262.445) = € 200.000.

Outcome: up to € 200.000 of the stakingswinst can be converted into a lijfrente. Because the case is tier b, not tier a, the lower cap applies. The cap amounts are UNVERIFIED for 2027.

### Calculated example: the proposed 1,01248 indexation

Belastingplan 2027 art. LVII proposes to index the lijfrente maxima with 1,01248. The bill was not adopted on 2026-10-11, so these outcomes are arithmetic on the amounts still in the 2027 text, not published figures.

- Reserveringsruimte maximum: € 42.753 x 1,01248 = € 42.753 + (€ 42.753 x 0,01248) = € 42.753 + € 533,56 = € 43.286,56.
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap: € 27.192 x 1,01248 = € 27.192 + (€ 27.192 x 0,01248) = € 27.192 + € 339,36 = € 27.531,36.
- Stakingswinst tier a: € 574.867 x 1,01248 = € 574.867 + (€ 574.867 x 0,01248) = € 574.867 + € 7.174,34 = € 582.041,34.
- Stakingswinst tier b: € 287.445 x 1,01248 = € 287.445 + (€ 287.445 x 0,01248) = € 287.445 + € 3.587,31 = € 291.032,31.
- Stakingswinst tier c: € 143.732 x 1,01248 = € 143.732 + (€ 143.732 x 0,01248) = € 143.732 + € 1.793,78 = € 145.525,78.
- Afkoop kleine lijfrente: € 5.513 x 1,01248 = € 5.513 + (€ 5.513 x 0,01248) = € 5.513 + € 68,80 = € 5.581,80.
- Afkoop bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid: € 51.440 x 1,01248 = € 51.440 + (€ 51.440 x 0,01248) = € 51.440 + € 641,97 = € 52.081,97.

### Calculated example: AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen against a lump-sum policy

A self-employed person with a profit of € 60.000 pays an invented AOV premium of € 2.400 in 2027.

1. Policy with periodic uitkeringen: the whole € 2.400 is an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and is deductible in full. It is not a business cost, so the winst uit onderneming stays € 60.000.
2. Policy that pays a lump sum: the € 2.400 is not deductible, so the deductible amount is € 0. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

Outcome: the form of the uitkering decides the treatment, not the size of the premium.

### Calculated example: proposed BAZ premium and benefit

The BAZ is not in force in 2027, so this example only shows the proposed percentages from the MvT.

1. Winst € 38.000: € 38.000 x 5,4% = € 2.052 per year. € 2.052 / 12 = € 171 per month.
2. Winst € 25.000: € 25.000 x 5,4% = € 1.350 per year. € 1.350 / 12 = € 112,50, about € 113 per month.
3. Winst € 45.000: above the maximum grondslag of € 38.000 used in the MvT examples, so the premium stays about € 171 per month.
4. Benefit check: 0,70 x 1,4286 = 1,00002, so at most about 100% of the minimumloon.
5. Winst € 25.000 plus € 25.000 SV-loon: the grondslag becomes € 13.000 and the premium about € 59 per month.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record lijfrentepremies in the year of payment or verrekening, because art. 3.130 lid 1 counts them in that year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Keep the premiums above the ruimte visible in the records. They are never deductible but they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book the AOV premium as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and keep it out of the winst uit onderneming, because it is not a business cost ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Book a mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium as a business cost of the onderneming, not as a lijfrentepremie ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep the FOR history, including the FOR at the start of the year and earlier conversions, because those amounts reduce the art. 3.129 cap ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- For the stakingswinst conversion and the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5, pay within six months after the year end and record the choice for the aangifte, because art. 3.130 lid 2 then counts the premium in the earlier year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Diary the einddatum of every lijfrente. An einddatum in 2026 must be converted or started by 31 December 2027, and a death in 2026 opens a nabestaandenlijfrente window that ends on 31 December 2028 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Do not finalise the 2027 deduction figures before the December 2026 indexation regulation and the vote on Belastingplan 2027, because the franchise, the maximum jaarruimte, the reserveringsruimte maximum, the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap, the stakingswinst maxima and the afkoop amounts are not published yet ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- If the sectoraal pensioenfonds of the branch allows joining, the premiums are deductible during the proef that runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Franchise premiegrondslag 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 19.172. Settling source: the Staatscourant indexation regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

With the frozen aftoppingsgrens of € 137.800 and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589, but the exact figure cannot be given until the franchise is published ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Reserveringsruimte maximum 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 42.753. Belastingplan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, and the bill was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 27.192 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Stakingswinst to lijfrente maxima 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 574.867, € 287.445 and € 143.732 ([art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Afkoop amounts 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 5.513 and € 51.440, and the December 2026 regulation is pending ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- All indexed 2027 amounts wait for the December 2026 regulation and the vote on Belastingplan 2027. Settling sources: the Staatscourant regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation. The Belastingplan 2027 items, the aftoppingsgrens freeze for 2027 to 2032 and the 1,01248 indexation, are proposed and were not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED at € 63.288. No 2027 page exists, because FISIN 2027 returns 404, so the amount is carried from FISIN 2026. Settling source: the FISIN 2027 chapter ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
