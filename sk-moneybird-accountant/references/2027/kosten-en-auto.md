## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

### 3.1 Gemengde kosten drempel, base in the 2027 text

Art. 3.15 lid 1 Wet IB says costs connected with voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen (food, drink and stimulants), representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible up to a yearly drempel (threshold). The related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No onttrekking (withdrawal) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). The 2027 text prints € 5.700 before indexation, and the drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Source: [art. 3.15 and art. 10.1, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

The content of the posts is worked out on the Belastingdienst overview page and in the 2020 beleidsbesluit. Voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien. Drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank. Genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken generally fall under representatie. Relatiegeschenken are a subcategory of representatiekosten per the wetsgeschiedenis of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Sources: [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten) and [Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html).

Two mechanical points. The fixed amount is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a boekjaar longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either ([section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food costs that qualify as loonkosten are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals to staff the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura ([section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### 3.2 Gemengde kosten drempel, effective 2027

The effective 2027 amount is not published. It waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation, and Belastingplan 2027 does not amend art. 3.15 lid 1 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The bill uses a tabelcorrectiefactor of 1,01248 for other amounts in art. L, and that factor is not confirmed for art. 3.15. Until the regulation is published, work from the base amount of € 5.700. See section 7.

### 3.3 Keuze in plaats van de drempel

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters, and the 2027 text keeps this percentage. Source: [art. 3.15 lid 5, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The drempel and the 80% election are alternatives, so the election is a choice made in the return and not an addition to the drempel.

### 3.4 Vpb variant of the drempel (BV)

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscale eenheid does it apply once, at the parent. Verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute. Sources: [Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01) (art. 8 lid 5), [beleidsbesluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked. See section 7.

### 3.5 Reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen

Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8 Wet IB limit travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep, and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke. They are not deductible above € 1500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The same congres and studiereis posts also stand in the art. 3.15 lid 1 list, where their travel and stay count as part of those posts (art. 3.15 lid 2).

### 3.6 Kilometervergoeding, enacted 2027 text

Art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b Wet IB limit the costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (auto, motor, fiets) to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The amount is all-in, so brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The 2027 text prints € 0,23 per km. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1, because art. 10.1 lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only. Sources: [art. 3.15 and art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). For 2026 an approving beleidsbesluit allowed € 0,25 retroactively to 1 January 2026 and that decree lapses on 1 January 2027, so € 0,25 in 2027 depends on the bill ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html)).

### 3.7 Kilometervergoeding, Belastingplan 2027

The bill proposes € 0,25 per km in art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b (art. I onderdelen C and D). Bill status on 2026-10-11: introduced 15 September 2026, verslag 1 October 2026, nota van wijziging 2 October 2026, awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, no plenary debate or vote and no Eerste Kamer activity. Sources: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html) and [wetsvoorstel 37022 in the Tweede Kamer](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022). The bill also codifies the 2026 € 0,25 approval retroactively to 1 January 2026 per art. LXIII lid 1 a. Until adoption, use € 0,23.

### 3.8 Verhuiskosten

Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a Wet IB gives moving costs as the cost of moving the inboedel plus € 7750. The amount is unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.9 Dubbele huisvesting

The same article allows the costs of housing outside the place of residence for at most two years. Unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.10 Bijtelling auto, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto (value of the car) is an onttrekking per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer is not private (lid 3). The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation. Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods, for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled (lid 5 a). A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible (lid 6 to 10). Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [privegebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

The waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Belastingdienst describes as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer (WEV) replaces it. A laadpaal for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Source: [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). Private use of an asset that sits in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking. Source: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

### 3.11 Bijtelling auto, pre-2017 car

Art. 10a.4 Wet IB sets the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a at 25% instead of 22% for a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also kept the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Sources: [art. 10a.4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) and [art. 10a.4, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.12 500 km rule and part-year availability

With a rittenregistratie or another means of showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, though trips can still be checked. Source: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Part-year availability is pro-rated, so 5 months gives 5/12 times 22% times the catalogue value, and private km are converted to a yearly basis, so 100 km in 5 months is 240 km. Source: [privegebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

### 3.13 Youngtimer age, enacted 2027 text

Art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b makes 35% of the waarde van de auto the onttrekking for a car first taken into use more than the age threshold ago. The 2027 text sets that threshold at 25 years. The step to 25 years was set by Belastingplan 2026 art. II onderdeel aA, per the Kennisgroep publication. The 2026 youngtimer decree states that per 1 January 2027 the threshold rises to 25 years and the transition lapses, and Rijksoverheid still writes that from 2027 the threshold goes from 16 to 25 years. Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [KG:213:2026:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html) and [Rijksoverheid on the car of the zaak](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen).

The WEV switch has its own age. Art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 years (2026) and 25 years (2027 text). The Belastingdienst applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026, and Belastingplan 2027 would repair lid 5 onderdeel b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. Sources: [art. 3.20 lid 5, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto) and [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### 3.14 Youngtimer age, Belastingplan 2027

The bill proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028, and art. XLVI onderdeel B removes the 25-year step (art. I onderdeel E and art. II onderdeel B). Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The bill is not adopted on 2026-10-11, so use 25 years until adoption. Only the vote and Staatsblad publication settle the conflict. See section 7.

### 3.15 Youngtimer transition 2027, Belastingplan 2027

The bill keeps the 2026 rule in 2027 for a car that was available on or before 31 December 2025 and that became more than 16 but not more than 17 years old during 2026 (art. XLVII lid 3). Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). For 2026 the transition was an optional approval: the 2025 rule of 35% of the WEV stayed for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025, under the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, nr. 2026-2493, retroactive to 1 January 2026 and lapsing on 1 January 2027 or on codification. Source: [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html).

### 3.16 Zero-emission car, first admission 2027

Art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11 reduce the 22% for a car with 0 g CO2 per km by a percentage of the value, capped in euros. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission, and after that the rules then in force apply (lid 11). For a car first admitted in 2027 the reduction is 2% capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000 and 22% above. Cars on waterstof are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead, and from the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in wattpiek divided by consumption in wattuur per km is at least 7. Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.20, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [art. 3.20, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

Reduction by year of first admission, per the statute: 2020 14% off capped € 6300 (so 8% up to € 45.000), 2021 10% off capped € 4.000 (so 12% up to € 40.000), 2022 6% off capped € 2.100 (so 16% up to € 35.000), 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800 (so 16% up to € 30.000), 2025 5% off capped € 1.500 (so 17% up to € 30.000), 2026 4% off capped € 1.200 (so 18% up to € 30.000), 2027 text 2% off capped € 600 (so 20% up to € 30.000). Source: [art. 3.20 lid 2, versions 2020 to 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Only waterstof was uncapped in 2020. Belastingplan 2027 does not amend art. 3.20 lid 2. Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### 3.17 Bijtelling fiets

When a fiets (bicycle, including an e-bike or speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (consumer advice price) is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address. Source: [art. 3.20a, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.18 Werkruimte thuis

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, lid 13 and from 2025 lid 14). With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig. The overview page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning).

From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The MvT calls this a codification of case law. For a non-zelfstandige workspace in such a home the huurderslasten (inrichting such as meubilair, stoffering, behang- en schilderwerk, and gas, water en licht) are not deductible. Corrected: the Belastingdienst page still presents the second route (owner and home in the ondernemingsvermogen) without this limit, and the statute decides. Sources: [MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html) and [art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01). The 2027 text of art. 3.16 matches 2026.

### 3.19 Telefoonabonnement thuis

Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a Wet IB makes telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home not deductible. In practice a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible while call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100%. The statutory basis is unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.16 lid 2 a, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten) and [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.20 Private computers en apparatuur

Art. 3.16 lid 3 Wet IB excludes the costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). The income-tax treatment of a laptop or computer that is business property follows vermogensetikettering, so apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible. Source: [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Source: [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.21 Overige uitsluitingen: literatuur, kleding, persoonlijke verzorging

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: literatuur except vakliteratuur (art. 3.16 lid 2 b), kleding except werkkleding (lid 2 c, werkkleding defined in lid 5) and persoonlijke verzorging (lid 2 d). Also not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business such as a rijbewijs, health costs such as pedicure and zorgverzekering, and personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding is deductible. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.22 Betaling aan de partner

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). Unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.23 Vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel en auto

A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen (compulsory private assets), 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen (compulsory business assets), and in between is keuzevermogen (optional assets) within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once. A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year, and verplicht privévermogen when used 90% or more privately. All other cases are keuzevermogen. Sources: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel) and [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid (special circumstance). A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The Kennisgroep applies this to the youngtimer change, so revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Sources: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel) and [KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/).

### 3.24 Kleine aanschaffingen

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur and onderhoud van de auto. A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work, and receipts and invoices must be kept. Source: [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen stay at 0% under art. 3.16 lid 3. Source: [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.25 Geldboetes en dwangsommen

Not deductible under art. 3.14 lid 1: costs connected with geldboeten from a strafrechter and settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten and EU fines (onderdeel c), costs connected with a misdrijf for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and dwangsommen under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (onderdeel i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints Geldboetes 0%. Sources: [art. 3.14, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [beleidsbesluit section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Two scope rules. Costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible, while costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring, and costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught. Source: [KG:213:2024:5, published 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking, are excluded. The example in the besluit is a taxpayer convicted only for possession of hennepplanten and not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Source: [beleidsbesluit section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html).

### 3.26 Studiekosten als bedrijfskosten

Studiekosten are deductible as a business cost when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw. The 2022 abolition removed only the persoonsgebonden route, not this business-cost route. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f. Sources: [studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). Studiekosten are also named under the costs with a mixed character that are deductible only for the business part. Source: [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.27 Studiekostenaftrek (persoonsgebonden route)

Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven (lesgeld, cursusgeld, collegegeld, examengeld, promotiekosten and required leermiddelen excluding computers) were deductible above a drempel of € 250 and, outside the standaardstudieperiode, up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30). No deduction applied when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study. The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349, and in the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022. Sources: [art. 6.27 to 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 6.27, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) and [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).

Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the prestatiebeurs case. When the diplomatermijn (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the prestatiebeurs. The caps are € 1.693 per study year (mbo) and € 2.443 per study year (hbo or wo), part-year € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo), and when the actual prestatiebeurs was lower the lower amount applies. The € 250 drempel applies per person. FISIN 2026 narrows the case to an extended diplomatermijn of at most 15 years, so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. Sources: [art. 10a.16, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [FISIN 2026, aftrek studiekosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten). No FISIN 2027 is published, so the 2027 position is unknown, although art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text. See section 7.

The non-fiscal replacement was the STAP-budget, which took effect at the same moment as the abolition and had a maximum subsidy of € 1.000 including btw per scholing with six application windows per calendar year (art. 7 of the regeling). The regeling is marked Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024. Sources: [Regeling STAP-budget, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01) and [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).

### 3.28 Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview. Source: [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### 3.29 BTW on a private car used for business

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet. Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, though it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA is not the btw-ondernemer, he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Source: [zakelijk gebruik van de priveauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### 3.30 KOR and the income-tax cost base

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, the KOR case, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need herziening, the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers. Sources: [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten) and [wat betekent meedoen met de kleineondernemersregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The KOR threshold history per year belongs to the BTW topic.

### 3.31 Pseudo-eindheffing new petrol or diesel lease cars (BV)

Separate from the income-tax car rules, and on the same Rijksoverheid page, from 1 January 2027 employers pay a pseudo-eindheffing of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars that are also used privately, with a transition until 16 September 2030 for cars provided before 2027. This is relevant for a DGA with a BV car and it was not checked further in the material. Source: [Rijksoverheid on the car of the zaak](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen). See section 7.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

All inputs below are invented round numbers. Calculated examples use only the section 2 figures and the checked material. Every product is shown with two decimals. No step in these examples requires rounding, so no rounding step is marked.

### 4.1 Calculated example: gemengde kosten drempel against the 80% keuze

A sole trader has € 6.000 of gemengde kosten in 2027, made up of zakenlunches and recepties.

Step 1, drempel route with the base amount of € 5.700 from the 2027 text: deductible is € 6.000 minus € 5.700 = € 300,00. The effective 2027 drempel is not published yet, so this step uses the base amount only.

Step 2, keuze route per art. 3.15 lid 5: 80% of € 6.000 = € 4.800,00 deductible.

Step 3, the election in the return gives the higher deduction here, € 4.800,00 against € 300,00. Check: 0,80 times € 6.000 = € 4.800,00.

### 4.2 Calculated example: congress travel cap

A sole trader spends € 2.000 on travel and stay for a congres in 2027.

Step 1, art. 3.16 lid 8 caps the deduction at € 1.500. Deductible € 1.500,00, not deductible € 2.000 minus € 1.500 = € 500,00.

Step 2, if the nature of the work makes attendance necessary, the cap does not apply and the full € 2.000,00 is deductible.

### 4.3 Calculated example: kilometre allowance with a private car

A sole trader drives 12.000 business kilometres in 2027 in a car that belongs to the private assets. Brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren are not deductible separately.

Step 1, enacted 2027 text: 12.000 km times € 0,23 = € 2.760,00.

Step 2, if Belastingplan 2027 is adopted: 12.000 km times € 0,25 = € 3.000,00.

Step 3, the difference is € 3.000,00 minus € 2.760,00 = € 240,00. Until adoption, use € 2.760,00. Check: 0,23 times 12.000 = 2.760,00 and 0,25 times 12.000 = 3.000,00.

### 4.4 Calculated example: bijtelling at 22% with the autokosten cap

A car in the ondernemingsvermogen has a catalogue price of € 30.000 and is used privately for more than 500 km in 2027.

Step 1, 22% of € 30.000 = € 6.600,00 onttrekking.

Step 2, if the total autokosten including depreciation are € 10.000, the full € 6.600,00 is taken into account.

Step 3, if the total autokosten including depreciation are € 5.000, art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation, so € 5.000,00 is taken into account.

Check: 0,22 times 30.000 = 6.600,00.

### 4.5 Calculated example: pre-2017 car

A car first admitted on 1 June 2016 has a catalogue price of € 20.000 and is used privately for more than 500 km in 2027.

Step 1, art. 10a.4 gives at least 25% instead of 22%, so 25% of € 20.000 = € 5.000,00.

Step 2, without art. 10a.4 the 22% would have given € 4.400,00. The difference is € 600,00. Check: 0,25 times 20.000 = 5.000,00 and 0,22 times 20.000 = 4.400,00.

### 4.6 Calculated example: part-year availability and the 500 km rule

A car with a catalogue price of € 24.000 is available to the sole trader for 5 months in 2027 and is used privately for more than 500 km.

Step 1, pro-rate the yearly amount: 5/12 times 22% times € 24.000. First 22% of € 24.000 = € 5.280,00.

Step 2, 5/12 of € 5.280,00 = € 2.200,00.

Step 3, the 500 km rule applies to the whole year. Private km in a part year are converted to a yearly basis. If a rittenregistratie shows 200 private km in the 5 months, the yearly basis is 200 times 12 / 5 = 480,00 km, which is at most 500, so the onttrekking is nil. If it shows 480 private km, the yearly basis is 480 times 12 / 5 = 1.152,00 km, which is more than 500, so the onttrekking stays € 2.200,00.

Check: 5.280,00 times 5 = 26.400,00 and 26.400,00 / 12 = 2.200,00. Also 200 times 12 = 2.400,00 and 2.400,00 / 5 = 480,00. And 480 times 12 = 5.760,00 and 5.760,00 / 5 = 1.152,00.

### 4.7 Calculated example: youngtimer, enacted text against the bill

A car was first used between 17 and 25 years ago, so it is older than the bill threshold but not older than the enacted 2027 threshold of 25 years. Its WEV is € 6.000.

Step 1, under the enacted 2027 text art. 3.20 lid 5 b still uses more than 15 years for the WEV switch, so the WEV of € 6.000 replaces the catalogue price. The Belastingdienst applies 16 years from 2026, and the car is older than 17 years, so the WEV applies under both readings.

Step 2, enacted 2027 text: the car is not older than 25 years, so the 35% youngtimer rate does not apply. The standard 22% applies to the WEV: 0,22 times € 6.000 = € 1.320,00.

Step 3, Belastingplan 2027: the threshold would be 17 years in 2027, so 35% of € 6.000 = € 2.100,00.

Step 4, the difference is € 2.100,00 minus € 1.320,00 = € 780,00. Check: 0,22 times 6.000 = 1.320,00 and 0,35 times 6.000 = 2.100,00.

### 4.8 Official example: heretikettering after the youngtimer change

Kennisgroep casus KG:213:2026:1 describes a youngtimer bought in 2024 as keuzevermogen, where the move to 25 years from 2027 can justify a new etiketkeuze after 1 January 2027. Official example, source: [KG:213:2026:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/).

### 4.9 Calculated example: zero-emission car first admitted in 2027

A zero-emission car with a catalogue price of € 40.000 is first admitted in 2027 and used privately for more than 500 km.

Step 1, 20% on the part up to € 30.000: 0,20 times € 30.000 = € 6.000,00.

Step 2, 22% on the part above € 30.000: 0,22 times € 10.000 = € 2.200,00.

Step 3, total onttrekking € 6.000,00 plus € 2.200,00 = € 8.200,00.

Check with the euro cap: 22% of € 40.000 = € 8.800,00 and the reduction cap is € 600,00, so € 8.800,00 minus € 600,00 = € 8.200,00.

### 4.10 Calculated example: fiets of the business

A business e-bike has a consumentenadviesprijs of € 2.500 and is also used privately.

Step 1, 7% of € 2.500 = € 175,00 onttrekking, capped at the costs borne.

Step 2, if the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil. Check: 0,07 times 2.500 = 175,00.

### 4.11 Calculated example: laptop and the € 450 rule

A laptop of € 1.800 is business property, used longer than a year, with a restwaarde of € 300 and a five-year life.

Step 1, depreciate: € 1.800 minus € 300 = € 1.500,00.

Step 2, per year: € 1.500,00 / 5 = € 300,00.

Step 3, a laptop stand of € 40 is deducted in one go because it is under € 450. If the laptop were used 10% or less for business, it would be verplicht privévermogen and 0% deductible.

### 4.12 Calculated example: verhuiskosten

Moving the inboedel costs € 1.200.

Step 1, deductible is the cost of moving the inboedel plus € 7.750: € 1.200 plus € 7.750 = € 8.950,00. Check: 1.200 plus 7.750 = 8.950,00.

### 4.13 Calculated example: btw correction on a private car used for business

A private car with a catalogue price including btw and bpm of € 40.000 is used for business without a sluitende kilometeradministratie, and 4 months of the year are in the business.

Step 1, the year-end correction is 1,5% of € 40.000 = € 600,00.

Step 2, pro rata for 4 months: 4/12 times € 600,00 = € 200,00.

Step 3, the correction is never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance, so if that btw is € 150, the correction is € 150,00. Check: 0,015 times 40.000 = 600,00 and 600,00 times 4 / 12 = 200,00.

### 4.14 Calculated example: openbaar vervoer

A business train trip costs € 96. The full € 96,00 is deductible, and the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview shows the cost.

### 4.15 Calculated example: posts rated at 0% and at 100% for telefoon and boetes

Step 1, a private telephone subscription of € 30 per month is not deductible. Business calls made through it are deductible, for example € 18 of business call costs in a month.

Step 2, a Wahv verkeersboete of € 180 is not deductible under art. 3.14 lid 1 and the overview page, which prints Geldboetes 0%.

### 4.16 Calculated example: KOR and the cost base

A sole trader in the KOR buys office supplies for € 1.210 including 21% btw.

Step 1, the btw part is € 210,00 and the amount excluding btw is € 1.000,00. Check: 1.000 times 21% = 210,00 and 1.000 plus 210 = 1.210,00.

Step 2, under the KOR no btw is deducted, so the IB deduction is the amount including btw, € 1.210,00. If the btw were deductible in a btw-aangifte, the IB deduction would be € 1.000,00.

### 4.17 Calculated example: werkruimte test

A sole trader rents a home for an invented € 1.200 per month and uses one room as an office. The room has no own entrance, no own sanitary facilities and could not be rented to a third party. The room is therefore not a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and the workspace costs are 0% deductible. A room with its own entrance, its own sanitary facilities and a realistic rental option, in which the taxpayer earns most of his income, can qualify under art. 3.16 lid 1.

### 4.18 Official example: pseudo-eindheffing

Official example from Rijksoverheid: a car of € 30.000 gives 12% = € 3.600,00 per year, which is € 300,00 per month. Official example, source: [Rijksoverheid on the car of the zaak](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2026

- Gemengde kosten drempel: 2026 was € 5.700, final and enacted ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026)). For 2027 the 2027 text gives € 5.700 before indexation, and the effective amount is not published yet, so the 2027 value is unknown until the December 2026 indexation regulation ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Keuze in plaats van de drempel: 80% in both years, also for resultaatgenieters ([2026 change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Vpb variant: 73,5% and the fixed amount or 0,4% of loon if higher, verified for 2026 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked, see section 7.
- Reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen: maximum € 1500 in both years, not indexed ([2026 overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Kilometervergoeding: 2026 applied € 0,25 per km retroactively to 1 January 2026 under the Beleidsbesluit of 17 May 2026, nr. 2026-8260, and that decree lapses on 1 January 2027 ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026)). The enacted 2027 text goes to € 0,23, and Belastingplan 2027 proposes € 0,25 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Same decree, other reiskosten in 2026: the loonbelasting reiskostenvergoeding of art. 31a lid 2 a 3° Wet LB went to € 0,25 per km instead of € 0,23, and the other forfaitaire reiskosten in the Wet IB (art. 6.17 lid 6 a, 6.26 with art. 40 lid 1 b URIB and 6.36 lid 2) also went to € 0,25 ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html)).
- Verhuiskosten and dubbele huisvesting: the cost of moving the inboedel plus € 7750 and a maximum of two years for housing outside the place of residence, unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Bijtelling auto standard 22% and the pre-2017 minimum of 25%: unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Youngtimer: 2026 used 16 years, with an optional transition for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html)). The enacted 2027 text goes to 25 years and the 2026 transition lapses per 1 January 2027, while the bill proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028 with its own transition for cars covered by the 2026 rule ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Zero-emission reduction: 2026 was 4% off capped € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000, and the 2027 text goes to 2% off capped € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Bijtelling fiets 7%, werkruimte thuis 0% with exceptions, telefoonabonnement thuis 0%, private computers en apparatuur 0%, geldboetes 0%, openbaar vervoer 100% of actual costs, KOR turnover limit € 20.000 and the btw car forfait of 1,5%: unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/), [btw en de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Studiekostenaftrek: 2026 was limited to the prestatiebeurs case with an extended diplomatermijn of at most 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten)). No FISIN 2027 is published, so the 2027 position is unknown, although art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- New from 1 January 2027: the pseudo-eindheffing of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately, with a transition until 16 September 2030 for cars provided before 2027 ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record gemengde kosten separately from other costs, so the drempel or the 80% keuze can be computed. The keuze is made in the return per art. 3.15 lid 5, and the drempel is not time-proportioned when the enterprise starts during the year ([art. 3.15, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [besluit section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).
- Keep receipts and invoices for deductible costs. Deduct costs excluding btw when the btw is deductible in the btw-aangifte, and including btw when no btw-aangifte is filed or the btw may not be deducted ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Under the KOR, no btw is charged or paid and no btw on business costs and investments is deducted, the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers, and exit is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year ([KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- For a private vehicle used for business, record the business kilometres, because the deduction is the fixed amount per kilometre and fuel, insurance, toll and parking are not deductible separately ([art. 3.15 lid 6, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- For the car, keep a rittenregistratie or another record to show private kilometres. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, and a verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible ([art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is recorded as a privéonttrekking ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).
- For a private car used for business on the btw side, without a sluitende kilometeradministratie deduct all btw on use and maintenance and correct at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance ([btw en de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Keep the proof for travel: the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview for openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig, where 100% of the actual costs is deductible ([overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).
- For a BV, the Vpb drempel variant applies per body when there is at least one employee in the Wet LB sense, and in a fiscale eenheid it applies once at the parent ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), art. 8 lid 5, and [besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Effective gemengde kosten drempel for 2027 is unknown. It waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation. Belastingplan 2027 does not amend art. 3.15 lid 1. The bill uses a tabelcorrectiefactor of 1,01248 for other amounts in art. L, and that factor is not confirmed for art. 3.15. Sources: [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The published regulation settles it.
- Kilometre allowance 2027, € 0,23 against € 0,25. The enacted 2027 text prints € 0,23 in art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 b ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Belastingplan 2027 proposes € 0,25 (art. I onderdelen C and D, [kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The bill is not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([wetsvoorstel 37022](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022)), so use € 0,23 until adoption.
- Youngtimer age 2027, 25 years against 17 years. The enacted 2027 text sets 25 years ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Belastingplan 2027 proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028, with transitional rules in art. XLVII ([kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). Only the vote and Staatsblad publication settle it, and the instruction here is to use 25 until adoption. The claim that the CPB note describes a further gradual raise to 25 years is not supported, because the bill moves away from 25.
- WEV switch age. Art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 years (2026) and 25 years (2027 text) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026 ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). Belastingplan 2027 would replace vijftien by 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a, [kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)), proposed. In practice the Belastingdienst position settles it, in law only adoption does.
- Vpb variant of the drempel for 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The mechanism of 0,4% of loon if higher than the fixed amount and the 73,5% election is verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked. Checking the 2027 Wet Vpb text settles it.
- Studiekostenaftrek 2027 is unknown. No FISIN 2027 is published, and the FISIN 2026 page is the last checked one ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten)). Art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)), but whether any extended diplomatermijn case remains in 2027 is not on a fetched page. A published FISIN 2027 page settles it.
- The content of the nota van wijziging of 2 October 2026 on Belastingplan 2027 was not fetched, because kst-37022 numbers above 2 did not resolve on zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl. It could touch these items. The published stuk settles it.
- CPB certification date. The certification note is dated 1 March 2026 on Rijksoverheid and is an annex to a Kamerbrief of 24 March 2026 ([certificeringsnotitie](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/2026/03/01/certificeringsnotitie-cpb-verhogen-leeftijdsgrens-youngtimerregeling)). A CPB certification date of 27 March 2026 from cpb.nl is not confirmed by a listed primary source, and the note page itself does not state the age path.
- Pseudo-eindheffing for new petrol or diesel lease cars from 1 January 2027 is UNVERIFIED beyond the Rijksoverheid page, which was not checked further ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen)). It concerns a BV with a lease car and not the income-tax car rules. The underlying wet text settles it.
