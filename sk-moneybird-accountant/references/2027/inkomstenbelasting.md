# Inkomstenbelasting box 1 2027

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2027 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the box 1 brackets and rates for 2027, the algemene heffingskorting, arbeidskorting and inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, the tariefsaanpassing and the route from winst uit onderneming (business profit) to taxable box 1 income. Three layers apply for 2027. The Wet IB 2001 text valid from 1 January 2027 is adopted but not yet in force, Belastingplan 2027 is still a bill with a Tweede nota van wijziging, and several tables and amounts are not published at all. The proposed box 1 tables, the heffingskortingen and the startersaftrek can therefore still change before the year starts.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

### Box 1 table, enacted text (art. 2.10)

The statute version valid from 1 January 2027 prints the income-tax part of the box 1 table. The first band is 8,01% up to € 38.883. The second band prints a fixed amount of € 3.114 plus 37,59% up to € 79.569. Above € 79.569 the rate is 49,50% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 2.10). This text is adopted for 2027 but not yet in force, and it is the version before the December indexation. The premie volksverzekeringen is charged on top of the income-tax part up to a ceiling equal to the end of the first band. The Belastingplan 2027 bill replaces this table, so the enacted table is the fallback if the bill does not pass in this form.

### Box 1 table for people born before 1 January 1946, enacted text (art. 2.10a)

Art. 2.10a gives a separate table for people born before 1 January 1946. It has a higher first boundary, because the AOW premium position of this group differs. The income-tax part is 8,01% up to € 41.123, then € 3.293 plus 37,59% up to € 79.569, then € 17.744 plus 49,50% above (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 2.10a). This is the enacted text for 2027, not yet in force. The bill changes the first boundary to € 41.637 and, through the Tweede nota van wijziging, the top boundary and the fixed amount of the top band.

### Premie volksverzekeringen, cross-year structure behind the box 1 first bracket

The first band of the published box 1 table is a combined rate. The income-tax part is the rate in art. 2.10 lid 1, which holds only the income-tax part. The premie volksverzekeringen of 27,65% is charged over the premie-inkomen up to a ceiling that equals the end of that first band (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). The 27,65% consists of AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. The published combined first-bracket rate is the income-tax part plus 27,65%. Above the premium ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the combined rate, which is why the tables checked for 2020 to 2024 show one combined rate up to the top boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). Once the AOW age is reached, no AOW premium is due, which is why the AOW-age first-bracket rate is lower (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). That the Anw and Wlz parts continue at AOW age is inference from the AOW-age first-bracket rates and stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule.

### Tariefsaanpassing, enacted text (art. 2.10 lid 2)

When grondslagverminderende posten have been deducted, the tax under lid 1 is raised by a percentage of the amount by which the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning plus those posten exceeds the top bracket boundary, capped at that percentage of the deducted posten. In the enacted 2027 text the percentage is 11,91% and the top bracket boundary is € 79.569 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). The effective value of a deduction is 49,50% minus the percentage. The posten are the persoonsgebonden aftrek (onderhoudsverplichtingen including partneralimentatie, weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten, aftrekbare giften, specifieke zorgkosten and a restant persoonsgebonden aftrek from earlier years), the aftrek kosten eigen woning (hypotheekrente, periodieke erfpacht, opstal of beklemming, kosten afsluiten hypotheek and restschuldkosten), the ondernemersfaciliteiten (zelfstandigenaftrek, aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek and mkb-winstvrijstelling) and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag.

### Box 1 below AOW age, bill before the Tweede nota van wijziging

The bill version of 15 September 2026 proposes a combined first rate of 36,23%, of which 8,58% is the income-tax part. The first boundary is € 39.247. The second band prints € 3.367 plus 38,16% up to € 78.426, and above € 78.426 the rate is 49,50% with a fixed amount of € 18.317 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html art. I onderdeel A and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). This is a proposed table. The Tweede nota van wijziging later replaces the top bracket boundary and the fixed amount of the top band, so only the first two rates and the first boundary survived that amendment unchanged.

### Box 1 below AOW age, bill as amended

The amended bill keeps the rates of 36,23%, 38,16% and 49,50% and moves the top bracket boundary from € 78.426 to € 80.578. The fixed amount of the top band becomes € 19.138 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329 onderdeel 1 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). The first boundary stays at € 39.247 and the second band then runs from € 39.247 to € 80.578. This table is still proposed. The nota naar aanleiding van het verslag confirms that the rates themselves are unchanged by the amendment (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).

### Box 1 for people born before 1 January 1946, bill as amended

For people born before 1 January 1946 the bill gives an income-tax part of 8,58% up to € 41.637. The earlier bill version then had € 3.572 plus 38,16% up to € 78.426 and € 17.610 plus 49,50% above, and the amended version moves the boundary to € 80.578 and the top-band fixed amount to € 18.431 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html art. I onderdeel B and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329). This table is still proposed. The higher first boundary sits alongside the higher first boundary in the enacted art. 2.10a.

### Box 1 for a full year at AOW age, bill

A person who is at AOW age for the whole of 2027 falls in a separate proposed table. The first bracket rate is 18,33%, which is much lower than the 36,23% for people below the AOW age, because no AOW premium is due from the AOW age. The first boundary is € 39.247 for people born in 1946 or later and € 41.637 for people born before 1 January 1946. The remaining rates are 38,16% and 49,50% (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). This table is proposed. The AOW-age first-bracket rate can also be read as the income-tax part of 8,58% plus the remaining Anw and Wlz premiedelen.

### First bracket rate in the year the AOW age is reached

The published tables checked for earlier years use a separate first-bracket rate for each month of the year in which the AOW age is reached, rising by about 1,49 points per month from the AOW-age rate, while schijven 2 and 3 keep the normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). The monthly 2027 rates are not published in the checked material (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). Do not use the full-year AOW rate of 18,33% for a transition-year calculation.

### No indexation of the box 1 tables at the start of 2027

The bill states that art. 10.1 does not apply to art. 2.10 and 2.10a at the start of 2027, so the box 1 tables are not indexed in the usual way (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Other amounts that are indexed in the bill use a factor of 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor of 1,026 (art. LI) (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). The Tweede nota van wijziging undoes the freeze of the top bracket boundary for 2027, which is why the amended bill shows € 80.578 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329). The same amendment freezes the top boundary for 2028 at € 80.578 and drops the planned 2028 raise of that boundary, while keeping the planned 2028 rate cuts of 0,09 and 0,04 point (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329 onderdeel 2 and toelichting).

### Aftrektarief tariefmaatregel in the bill

The fiscal key table for 2027 states an aftrektarief of 38,16% for the tariefmaatregel (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf). That is the value of a deduction in the proposed structure and it matches the proposed second bracket rate. It is a proposed figure. The aftrektarief matters for a taxpayer whose income before deductions is above the top bracket boundary.

### Tariefsaanpassing percentage under the bill

The bill excerpt in the checked material shows no change to the text of art. 2.10 lid 2, so no proposed 2027 tariefsaanpassing percentage is stated (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf). Treat the enacted 11,91% of art. 2.10 lid 2 as the enacted-text value and not as a confirmed final value for the bill. This point stays open in section 7.

### Algemene heffingskorting, enacted chapter 8 amounts in the 2027 text

Chapter 8 of the 2027 statute text was not reachable in the checked material, so the enacted 2027 amounts and phase-out percentages of the algemene heffingskorting are not read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The values shown below for 2027 come from the bill. Do not present them as enacted.

### Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age, bill before the Tweede nota van wijziging

### Algemene heffingskorting at AOW age, bill before the Tweede nota van wijziging

For a full year at AOW age the bill gives € 1.596, an afbouwpunt of € 30.910 and a phase-out of 3,358% up to the then top bracket boundary of € 78.426 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301). ### Algemene heffingskorting, general grant and phase-out base

The algemene heffingskorting is granted to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie in the payroll. The phase-out base is the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning in 2020 to 2024 and the verzamelinkomen from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The phase-out runs from the afbouwpunt to the top bracket boundary. At and above the top bracket boundary the korting is nil, and the tables and the yearly Belastingdienst pages that differ by one euro on the nil point describe this one rule. The AHK and AK afbouwpunten are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

### Verzamelinkomen

Het verzamelinkomen is the sum of the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning, the belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang and the belastbaar inkomen uit sparen en beleggen, minus the te verrekenen persoonsgebonden aftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18). It is the phase-out base for the algemene heffingskorting from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026). For a sole trader with a BV next to the onderneming, the box 2 income therefore also pushes the algemene heffingskorting down from 2025 onward, even though box 2 has its own rate.

### AHK payout to a minstverdienende partner

An unused algemene heffingskorting can still be paid out to a minstverdienende partner, but only for a person born before 1 January 1963. The conditions are that the partners were fiscal partners for more than 6 months, that the partner owes enough tax, and that only the part that cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and premie is paid (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The payout was afgebouwd since 2009 and the arbeidskorting and IACK payouts lapsed from 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). An earlier Belastingdienst page mentioned 2024 as the end date, and the checked pages settle the end at 2023. The 2027 income limits for the payout are not in the checked material.

### Arbeidskorting, enacted chapter 8 amounts in the 2027 text

Chapter 8 of the 2027 statute text was not reachable, so the enacted arbeidskorting table for 2027 is not read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The 2027 values in this file come from the bill and are proposed.

### Arbeidskorting, bill before the Tweede nota van wijziging

The bill gives step amounts of € 1.182 and € 5.540, a maximum of € 5.929, an afbouwpunt of € 47.834 and a phase-out of 6,51% (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf). Art. I onderdeel J raises the first amount in art. 8.11 lid 2 onderdelen a, b and c by € 173 and art. L indexes art. 8.11 by 1,01248 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). These are proposed figures and the afbouwpunt waits for the minimum wage decision in November 2026.

### Arbeidskorting, bill as amended

The Tweede nota van wijziging lowers the increase to each of the three step amounts from € 173 to € 133. The step amounts become € 1.142 and € 5.500 and the maximum becomes € 5.889. The maximum changed from € 5.929 in the earlier bill version to € 5.889 in the amended version. This is the proposed arbeidskorting for 2027.

### Arbeidskorting, table structure and arbeidsinkomen base

The arbeidskorting table has four bands. A build-up percentage applies first, then two further build-up steps, then a phase-out to nil (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For an IB-ondernemer the arbeidsinkomen includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and excludes the part earned as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The korting is applied automatically in the return. From 2024 the year tables state that if the employer applied a higher arbeidskorting and the arbeidsinkomen is not above the third-band boundary, the Belastingdienst takes over the employer's amount up to the maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

### Arbeidskorting band income thresholds, build-up percentages and AOW-age table

The 2027 band income thresholds, the build-up percentages and the AOW-age arbeidskorting table are not published in the checked material. Only the step amounts, the afbouwpunt and the 6,51% phase-out are printed (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). Do not calculate the full arbeidskorting from the step amounts alone, because the thresholds and build-up percentages are missing and the afbouwpunt is still linked to the minimum wage of November 2026.

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen of the heffingskortingen

Each heffingskorting splits into a belastingdeel, the income-tax part of the first-bracket rate, and premiedelen for AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). Someone who is not insured for one of these schemes loses that part of the korting. A part-year premieplicht gives a time-proportional premiedeel. This is why the heffingskorting of a person with a partial insurance year is lower than the full-year table amount, and why the AOW-age variants of the tables are lower. The exact belastingdeel for 2027 follows the income-tax part of the 2027 first bracket, which is 8,01% in the enacted text and 8,58% in the bill.

### IACK phase-down

The inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting is phased down in nine steps from 2027 and is abolished per 2035. The first step is € 149, then steps of € 355 follow at 2025 prices (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2025D26369). Belastingplan 2024, art. IV onderdeel B and art. V onderdeel B, enacted this afbouw for 2027 and 2028. An earlier parliamentary answer expects an IACK of € 2.663 in 2028 and € 2.333 in 2029, subject to indexation (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2025D26369). The 2027 amount itself depends on the indexation in Belastingplan 2027.

### IACK, bill as amended

The proposed IACK for 2027 has a threshold of € 6.316, a rate of 11,45% above that threshold and a maximum of € 2.918 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). The 2027 phase-down step is enacted, while the amount depends on the proposed indexation of 1,01248. The Tweede nota van wijziging leaves the IACK unchanged. The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### IACK eligibility, child and co-ouder conditions

The taxpayer needs a child under 12 on 1 January that belongs to the household for at least 6 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting). A death of the taxpayer or the child before the 6 months are completed still gives the korting. There must be no fiscal partner, or a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or a lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner qualifies. Qualifying children are own, step, adopted, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child, and a pleegkind only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a pleegvergoeding. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES can qualify. For co-ouders the page covering 2024 to 2026 requires roughly equal care with at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated if co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year, which gives 117 days when the co-parenting starts on 1 April. No 2027-specific page for these conditions was checked, so the 2027 continuation rests on the cross-year rule.

The proposed threshold, rate and maximum of € 6.316, 11,45% and € 2.918 do not supply the missing cap income or the AOW-age variant.

The MKB-winstvrijstelling for 2027 is 12,70% (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf). A medegerechtigde or a geldverstrekker receives neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). The MKB-winstvrijstelling is one of the ondernemersfaciliteiten in the tariefsaanpassing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). Art. 3.79a of the 2027 text was not reachable, so the statutory article reference for 2027 rests on the baseline citation.

The proposed meewerkaftrek rates from 1 January 2027 are 0,313%, 0,5%, 0,75% and 1% and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/). ### Stakingsaftrek

The proposed stakingsaftrek is € 908 from 1 January 2027 and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/). This is a proposed figure and the detailed conditions belong in the ondernemersaftrek reference. The stakingsaftrek is part of the ondernemersfaciliteiten in the tariefsaanpassing.

### Oudedagsreserve

Since 2023 no new oudedagsreserve can be formed, and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). The reserve is reduced or released when a lijfrente is bought with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the ondernemingsvermogen, on cessation of the onderneming, on reaching the AOW age on 1 January, on failing the urencriterium in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request. A release adds to the winst of the year and therefore to the box 1 income. The addition percentage of 9,44% of the profit applied only to the years in which additions were still possible.

### Winst uit onderneming into box 1

Winst uit onderneming means business profit. The business must be run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer must be able to make binding agreements for it and the taxpayer must be liable for its debts. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide the matter. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Start by taking the vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen out of the profit. The checked examples are the bosbouwvrijstelling, the landbouwvrijstelling, the vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst for the part above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies, and benefits under the eindheffing, mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject vrijstellingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Then reduce the remaining profit by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The result is the belastbare winst uit onderneming (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2, read in the version valid on 1 January 2026). A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling.

The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning, and that income is reduced by te verrekenen verliezen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). The box 1 saldo is taxed by art. 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the tariefsaanpassing, and the heffingskortingen reduce the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference. An income-related Zvw contribution also exists for zelfstandigen, and its base and rates belong in the Zvw reference (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf).

In 2026 the first rate was 17,85% up to € 38.883, then 37,56% to € 78.426, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2027 the bill gives 18,33%, with the first boundary at € 39.247 for people born in 1946 or later (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- People born before 1 January 1946. In 2026 the first rate was 17,85% up to € 41.123, then 37,56% to € 78.426, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2027 the enacted text keeps the € 41.123 boundary at 8,01%, and the amended bill moves the first boundary to € 41.637 and the top-band fixed amount to € 18.431 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329).
- Premie volksverzekeringen. In 2026 the ceiling was € 38.883 at 27,65%, giving a maximum premie of € 10.751 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). The checked 2027 material states the end of the first band as € 38.883 in the enacted text and € 39.247 in the bill but gives no separate 2027 premium ceiling or maximum premie (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- Tariefsaanpassing. In 2026 the percentage was 11,94% above € 78.426 and a deduction was worth at most 37,56% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The enacted 2027 text gives 11,91% above € 79.569 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). The bill states an aftrektarief of 38,16% and no new lid 2 percentage (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf).
- Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age. In 2026 the amount was € 3.115 up to € 29.736 with a phase-out of 6,398% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026). The amended bill for 2027 gives € 3.154 up to € 30.910 with a phase-out of 6,350% (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). The phase-out base is the verzamelinkomen from 2025 onward.
- Algemene heffingskorting at AOW age. In 2026 the amount was € 1.556 with a phase-out of 3,195% above € 29.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026). The 2027 bill gives € 1.596, an afbouwpunt of € 30.910 and 3,358% up to € 78.426, and the amended AOW-age table is not published (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- Arbeidskorting. In 2026 the maximum was € 5.685, the afbouwpunt € 45.593 and the phase-out 6,510% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026). The 2027 bill first gave a maximum of € 5.929 and the amended bill gives € 5.889, with step amounts of € 1.142 and € 5.500, an afbouwpunt of € 47.834 and the same 6,51% phase-out (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329). The 2027 band thresholds and build-up percentages are not published, so a full band by band comparison with 2026 is not possible.
- IACK. In 2026 the threshold was € 6.239, the rate 11,450% and the maximum € 3.032 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026). The 2027 bill gives a threshold of € 6.316, the same 11,45% rate and a maximum of € 2.918 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). The IACK phase-down starts in 2027 under the enacted Belastingplan 2024 measure (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2025D26369).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek. In 2026 the amount was € 1.200, or € 600 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). For 2027 it is € 900, or € 450 at AOW age under the 50% rule (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/zelfstandigenaftrek-verder-omlaag-0/ and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- Startersaftrek. In 2026 the amount was € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The 2027 enacted text is listed at the same € 2.123 and € 1.062 but is UNVERIFIED because art. 3.76 of the 2027 text was not reachable, and the bill reduces the amount by € 2.113 to € 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- MKB-winstvrijstelling. The rate stays the same, printed as 12,7% for 2026 and as 12,70% for 2027 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- Meewerkaftrek. In 2026 the rates were 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). From 1 January 2027 the proposed rates are 0,313%, 0,5%, 0,75% and 1%, and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/).
- Stakingsaftrek. In 2026 the maximum was € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). From 1 January 2027 the proposed amount is € 908, and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/).
- Oudedagsreserve. No new additions have been possible since 2023 and this does not change for 2027 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Indexation. The bill stops the indexation of art. 2.10 and 2.10a at the start of 2027 and indexes other amounts by 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor of 1,026, while the Tweede nota van wijziging undoes the freeze of the top bracket boundary for 2027 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329).
- The AHK and AK afbouwpunten for 2027 are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon and are final only after that wage is set in November 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the profit calculation in the steps the law uses: winst, then removal of the vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen, then the ondernemersaftrek (including the zelfstandigenaftrek of € 900 for 2027 and the startersaftrek when it applies), then the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 12,70% over the profit after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- The result is the belastbare winst uit onderneming that enters box 1. That income is combined with the other income from work and home and reduced by te verrekenen verliezen at that level (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Keep the deduction items visible in the records, because the tariefsaanpassing is calculated from them when the income before deductions exceeds the top bracket boundary of € 79.569 in the enacted text. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).
- The algemene heffingskorting and the arbeidskorting are applied automatically in the return and can be applied in the payroll by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie. The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).
- The AHK payout to a minstverdienende partner is limited to a person born before 1 January 1963 and needs fiscal partnership for more than 6 months and enough tax at the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The 2027 AHK and AK afbouwpunten are still linked to the wettelijk minimumloon of November 2026, and the Belastingdienst may process the 2027 voorlopige aanslagen on older parameters if the amendments come too late (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329).
- The checked sources state no 2027 income-tax filing deadline and no record-retention period. The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic.
- An income-related Zvw contribution exists alongside the income tax, and its base and rates belong in the Zvw reference (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Belastingplan 2027 is still a bill. The Tweede Kamer overview shows no vote at the time checked and a wetgevingsoverleg was scheduled around 11 or 12 October 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022). The adopted law decides box 1, the algemene heffingskorting, the arbeidskorting and the IACK for 2027.
- Every proposed value in this file is open until the bill is adopted. That includes the bill box 1 tables (36,23%, 38,16% and 49,50% with boundaries € 39.247, € 41.637 and € 80.578 and fixed amounts € 19.138 and € 18.431), the algemene heffingskorting (€ 3.154, € 30.910, 6,350% and the AOW-age € 1.596 and 3,358%), the arbeidskorting (€ 1.142, € 5.500, € 5.889, € 47.834 and 6,51%), the IACK (€ 6.316, 11,45% and € 2.918), the aftrektarief of 38,16%, the startersaftrek reduction by € 2.113 to € 10, the meewerkaftrek rates and the stakingsaftrek of € 908 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329).
- The enacted 2027 amounts of the algemene heffingskorting, the arbeidskorting and the IACK in chapter 8 of the 2027 text were not reachable, so the enacted chapter 8 values are unknown (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
- The AOW-age algemene heffingskorting after the Tweede nota van wijziging is not published. The 3,358% belongs to the bill version ending at € 78.426, while the amendment moves the top bracket boundary to € 80.578 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- The arbeidskorting band income thresholds, the build-up percentages and the AOW-age arbeidskorting table for 2027 are not published. Only the step amounts, the afbouwpunt and 6,51% are printed (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- The first bracket rate for the year in which the AOW age is reached in 2027 is not published (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- The IACK cap income and the AOW-age IACK for 2027 are not published (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- The tariefsaanpassing percentage under the bill is not stated. The bill excerpt shows no change to the art. 2.10 lid 2 text, and the fiscal key table for 2027 gives only the aftrektarief of 38,16% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf).
- The 2027 startersaftrek in the enacted text is UNVERIFIED because art. 3.76 of the 2027 text was not reachable. The listed € 2.123 and € 1.062 conflict with the proposed reduction by € 2.113 to € 10, and the adopted law settles that (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- Conflict on the top bracket boundary. The earlier bill text has € 78.426 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and the Tweede nota van wijziging has € 80.578 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329). The € 80.578 is now the bill text, and it is still proposed. The nota naar aanleiding van het verslag confirms the rates are unchanged (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335).
- Conflict on the arbeidskorting maximum. The earlier bill text has € 5.929 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf) and the amended bill has € 5.889 after an increase of € 133 instead of € 173 on each of the three step amounts (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). The € 5.889 is now the bill text, and it is still proposed.
- Conflict on the below-AOW algemene heffingskorting phase-out. The earlier bill text has 6,638% (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf) and the amended bill has 6,350%, because the phase-out now ends at € 80.578 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335). The 6,350% is now the bill text, and it is still proposed.
- Inference, not a stated rule. The continuation of the Anw and Wlz premiedelen at AOW age follows from the AOW-age first-bracket rates. The checked cross-year material shows the split for 2026, where 17,85% equals 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65%. The page checked states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age, so this continuation stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).
- Inference, not a printed sequence. The order of the box 1 saldo under art. 2.10 lid 1, then the tariefsaanpassing under lid 2, then the heffingskortingen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen is not printed as one statement on any page read. It rests on the article structure (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The AHK and AK afbouwpunten of € 30.910 and € 47.834 are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon and are final only after the minimum wage is set in November 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).
- The Belastingdienst may process the 2027 voorlopige aanslagen on older parameters if the amendments come too late (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329).
- The 2027 eligibility continuation of the IACK child and co-ouder conditions is not confirmed by a 2027-specific page. The rules stated here come from the page covering 2024 to 2026 and from the annual pages for those years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The 2027 income limits for the AHK payout to a minstverdienende partner are not in the checked material. Only the age condition, the 6-month partnership condition and the set-off rule are checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
