## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Vpb rate table (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

The 2027 table reads t/m € 200.000 19%, and above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([Wet Vpb 1969, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) above the column I amount. This value is carried because https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2027-01-01 returns HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11, so the Check tag is UNVERIFIED. Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022) does not touch art. 22 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The Belastingdienst tarieven page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([tarieven page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting)). If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 gives (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22 ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The earlier series read: 2020 t/m € 200.000 at 16,5% and above that € 33.000 plus 25%, 2021 t/m € 245.000 at 15% and above that € 36.750 plus 25%, 2022 t/m € 395.000 at 15% and above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%, and 2023 to 2026 t/m € 200.000 at 19% and above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.2 Verliesverrekening (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)

A loss is set off against the profit of another year. From 2022 the rule is one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. In 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by beschikking (decision) ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01)). For 2027 the current rule is carried and UNVERIFIED ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.3 Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)

The saldo aan renten (net interest balance, interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021, 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024, and 24,5% in 2025 and 2026 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) to [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), each year). The Belastingdienst change pages state 20% for 2024, 24,5% for 2025 and 24,5% for 2026, and say non-deductible interest carries forward ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025), [change page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026)). For 2027 the values are 24,5% and € 1.000.000, carried and UNVERIFIED ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.4 Innovatiebox fraction (art. 12b tot 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H, H being the highest art. 22 rate, so an effective 9% ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The Belastingdienst says "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" ([innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The 2020 text reads 7/H, an effective 7% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01)). Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate ([innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The value is carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.5 Innovatiebox forfait (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

For a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses the forfait, the amount is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000, and only when that profit is positive ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.6 Innovatiebox forfait ceiling in the bill

Belastingplan 2027 Article XIX A raises the forfait ceiling to € 100.000 instead of € 25.000, and Article LXIII lid 1 sub d makes it first apply to book years starting on or after 1 January 2027 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The item is proposed and was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.7 Innovatiebox smaller taxpayer (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring (R&D statement). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and that the net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Other taxpayers also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets (trademarks, logos and similar assets) are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb gives the K/T part, K being the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel (threshold) of art. 12bc ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.8 Deelnemingsvrijstelling (art. 13 lid 1 en 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming (participation) out of the profit. A deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (art. 13 lid 2) ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test (customary wage test) compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, where connected means a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.9 Art. 13 lid 7 Wet Vpb 1969 in the bill

The bill replaces "vooraf" with "op verzoek van de belastingplichtige" (on request of the taxpayer), uses "niet-ingeprijsd voordeel" (non-priced advantage) and adds two new paragraphs ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The item is proposed and was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.10 Transitie art. 34j in the bill

Art. 34j keeps the old art. 13 lid 7 for advantages attributable before the first 2027 book year when the beschikking or the full request came before it, and through 31 December 2027 for transactions entered into and requested before 15 September 2026 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The article is proposed ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.11 Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022)

Belastingplan 2027 enters into force on 1 January 2027 if adopted. Article XIX A and B first apply to book years starting on or after 1 January 2027. In Article XIX the bill changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j. In Article XX it deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.13 Fiscale eenheid bezitseis (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)

A fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity) needs the mother to hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the mother must be a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or a stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie, or a comparable foreign form, that the daughter must be a bv or nv, that both need the same book years and profit rules and that both must be effectively established in the Netherlands ([Belastingdienst fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)). The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10) ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.16 Gebruikelijk loon test and floor (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

The wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment), at 100% from 2023 and 75% in 2020 to 2022, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the normbedrag ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) to [1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), each year, [future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (lid 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year) ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling (addition for private use of a company car), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). From 2023, if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage ([1 January 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A vaststellingsovereenkomst (settlement agreement) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). For starters, the statute of 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4) gave a lower wage for a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applied, at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. The de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, and the Handboek 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). Separate policy (Handboek 2025): a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The test and the floor are carried for 2027 ([future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01)).

### 3.18 DGA Zvw route

A DGA is in dienst (employed) of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie (payroll records) and withholds loonheffing ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)). A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (employee insurances) ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A DGA who is insured falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### 3.22 Dividendbelasting rate (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 makes the tax 15% of the opbrengst (proceeds), in every version read ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. If the company bears the tax, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the opbrengst by 100/85. Once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota is due (art. 9 lid 1) ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). A deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder with a holding of 5% or more is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Belastingdienst invites one. A beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)). The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification decision) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting and bodies in the aangifte Vpb ([dividendbelasting aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen), [dividendbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/)). The 15% is carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).

From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). ### 3.29 Geruisloze omzetting credit (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000 ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst (pre-agreement) is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The request is filed at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring is registered with the Geleideformulier, and the BV is a bv in oprichting (BV in formation) until the notarial deed ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)). The inspector decides by beschikking open to bezwaar (objection) that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties. In the published case the request could be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment was irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst was reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment decides. The standpunt was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023 ([KG:212:2022:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/)).

### 3.32 Btw fiscale eenheid (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968)

A btw fiscale eenheid arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Staatscourant 2024, 38545, published on 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). A pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer for btw and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html), [Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### 3.33 Aangifte Vpb deadline

For a book year equal to the calendar year, file before 1 June of the next year. For a broken book year, file within 5 months after its end. For a short book year ending 31 December, file before 1 June, and for a short book year ending in another month, file before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)). These are the current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.34 Uitstel aangifte Vpb

The online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons ([uitstel aangifte vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)). These are the current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.35 Jaarrekening size classes (Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397)

A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html)). Micro is at most € 450.000 assets, € 900.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees. Small is € 7,5 miljoen, € 15 miljoen and fewer than 50. Medium is € 25 miljoen, € 50 miljoen and fewer than 250. The 2020 statute read micro at most € 350.000 and € 700.000 and fewer than 10, small € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250 ([Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01)). Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raises the amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, with an option from 1 January 2023, and leaves the employee counts unchanged ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). The turnover bound is settled at € 50 miljoen against a KVK page printing more than € 51 mln ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)).

### 3.36 Jaarrekening deposit (art. 394 Boek 2 BW)

The annual accounts are deposited within eight days after vaststelling (adoption) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends ([jaarrekening indienen](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)). If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as such. There is no extension, and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR ([jaarrekening indienen](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### 3.37 Administratieplicht and bewaartermijn (art. 52 AWR)

Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig (subject to the bookkeeping obligation) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement ([AWR, 1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). No basis was found for a longer general term ([AWR, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### 3.38 Btw record terms (art. 34a, 28rl, 28sj Wet OB 1968)

Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on onroerende zaken (immovable property) for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten. Art. 28rl and 28sj keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction ([Wet OB 1968, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples were found in the checked material for 2027. Every example below is a calculated example built from the section 2 figures, with each step written out.

### Example 1. Vpb on a profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

- Input: belastbaar bedrag € 60.000.
- Step 1: € 60.000 is below € 200.000, so all of it is in the first bracket at 19%.
- Step 2: 19% x € 60.000 = € 11.400.
- Result: € 11.400.

### Example 2. Vpb on a belastbaar bedrag of € 260.000 (Calculated example)

- Input: belastbaar bedrag € 260.000.
- Step 1: first bracket 19% x € 200.000 = € 38.000.
- Step 2: part above the bracket € 260.000 - € 200.000 = € 60.000.
- Step 3: 25,8% x € 60.000 = € 15.480.
- Step 4: € 38.000 + € 15.480 = € 53.480.
- Result: € 53.480. The law prints the same structure, the amount € 38.000 plus 25,8% of the part above € 200.000.

### Example 3. Verliesverrekening with a profit of € 3.000.000 (Calculated example)

- Input: profit before set-off € 3.000.000, a loss of € 2.500.000 from an earlier year.
- Step 1: yearly cap € 1.000.000 + 50% x (€ 3.000.000 - € 1.000.000) = € 1.000.000 + € 1.000.000 = € 2.000.000.
- Step 2: set off € 2.000.000 of the loss. The remaining loss € 2.500.000 - € 2.000.000 = € 500.000 goes forward without time limit.
- Step 3: belastbaar bedrag € 3.000.000 - € 2.000.000 = € 1.000.000.
- Step 4: Vpb € 38.000 + 25,8% x (€ 1.000.000 - € 200.000) = € 38.000 + € 206.400 = € 244.400.
- Result: € 244.400, with € 500.000 of loss carried forward.

### Example 4. Art. 15b interest limit (Calculated example)

- Input: saldo aan renten € 2.000.000, gecorrigeerde winst € 4.000.000.
- Step 1: 24,5% x € 4.000.000 = € 980.000.
- Step 2: higher of € 980.000 and € 1.000.000 = € 1.000.000.
- Step 3: deductible € 1.000.000. Non-deductible € 2.000.000 - € 1.000.000 = € 1.000.000, carried forward.
- Result: € 1.000.000 deductible, € 1.000.000 carried forward. If the gecorrigeerde winst were € 5.000.000, 24,5% gives € 1.225.000, and that percentage arm would become the cap.

### Example 5. Innovatiebox fraction on a € 100.000 advantage (Calculated example)

- Input: qualifying advantage € 100.000, H is 25,8.
- Step 1: fraction 9 / 25,8 = about 0,3488.
- Step 2: part included in the profit € 100.000 x 9 / 25,8 = € 34.883,72.
- Step 3: tax 25,8% x € 34.883,72 = € 9.000,00.
- Result: € 9.000,00, which is 9% of € 100.000.

### Example 6. Innovatiebox forfait (Calculated example)

- Case A: profit before art. 12b € 60.000, positive. Forfait 25% x € 60.000 = € 15.000, below the € 25.000 maximum.
- Case B: profit before art. 12b € 200.000. 25% x € 200.000 = € 50.000, capped at € 25.000.
- Result: € 15.000 and € 25.000. If the bill is adopted, the cap becomes € 100.000 and the case B forfait would be € 50.000 for book years from 1 January 2027.

### Example 7. Gebruikelijk loon test (Calculated example)

- Case A: comparable wage € 40.000, highest employee wage € 35.000, normbedrag € 58.000. The highest is € 58.000, so the wage is at least € 58.000. The December 2026 indexation of the normbedrag is still open.
- Case B: total wage for all work in the body and connected bodies € 4.800, not more than € 5.000, so the rule does not apply.
- Case C: comparable wage € 50.000, highest employee wage € 70.000, normbedrag € 58.000. The highest amount € 70.000 exceeds the comparable wage € 50.000, so the wage may be the comparable wage of € 50.000.

### Example 8. Zvw for an insured and an uninsured DGA (Calculated example)

- Both figures are the proposed 2027 figures.
- Insured DGA, wage € 60.000: werkgeversheffing 6,27% x € 60.000 = € 3.762, paid by the BV.
- Uninsured DGA, wage € 100.000: the bijdrage is withheld on net wage up to the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 82.547, so 5,02% x € 82.547 = € 4.143,86.
- Result: € 3.762 and € 4.143,86.

### Example 9. Box 2 on a € 100.000 dividend and the indexation factor (Calculated example)

- Input: dividend € 100.000 to a DGA in box 2, future text table before indexation.
- Step 1: first bracket € 68.843 at 24,5%, the table prints € 16.866.
- Step 2: part above € 100.000 - € 68.843 = € 31.157.
- Step 3: 31% x € 31.157 = € 9.658,67.
- Step 4: € 16.866 + € 9.658,67 = € 26.524,67.
- Indexation step: € 68.843 x 1,01248 = € 68.843 + € 859,16 = € 69.702,16, about € 69.702.
- Result: € 26.524,67 on the future text table. The secondary figure is € 69.607 for the bracket end, so that end is still open.

### Example 10. Dividendbelasting on a € 100.000 dividend and on a buyback (Calculated example)

- Step 1: opbrengst € 100.000, tax 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000, net paid € 85.000.
- Step 2: if the company bears the tax and the shareholder receives € 85.000 net, the opbrengst is € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000 and the tax is € 15.000.
- Buyback: buyback price € 50.000 and average paid-up capital € 20.000 give a base of € 50.000 - € 20.000 = € 30.000. Tax 15% x € 30.000 = € 4.500.
- Result: € 15.000 and € 4.500.

### Example 11. Conditionele bronbelasting on € 100.000 interest (Calculated example)

- Input: interest € 100.000 to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction.
- Step 1: rate 25,8%.
- Step 2: 25,8% x € 100.000 = € 25.800.
- Result: € 25.800.

### Example 12. Stakingsaftrek on a stakingswinst of € 50.000 (Calculated example)

- Step 1 with the future text: € 50.000 - € 3.630 = € 46.370.
- Step 2 with the proposal: € 50.000 - € 908 = € 49.092.
- Result: € 46.370 under the future text and € 49.092 under the proposal.

### Example 13. Art. 3.65 rounding credit (Calculated example)

- Case A: paid-up amount € 200.000. 5% x € 200.000 = € 10.000, below € 30.000, so the credit is € 10.000.
- Case B: paid-up amount € 1.000.000. 5% x € 1.000.000 = € 50.000, capped at € 30.000.
- Result: € 10.000 and € 30.000.

### Example 14. Filing deadlines (Calculated example)

- Case A: calendar book year 2027. File before 1 June 2028. Online extension to 1 November 2028, requested from 1 March 2028 to 1 June 2028.
- Case B: broken book year ending 30 June 2027. File within 5 months, by 30 November 2027. More time needs a paper request.
- Case C: short book year ending 30 September 2027. File before 1 April 2028.
- Result: 1 June 2028, 30 November 2027 and 1 April 2028.

### Example 15. Jaarrekening class and deposit date (Calculated example)

- Input: assets € 500.000, net turnover € 1.000.000, 8 employees on two consecutive balance dates.
- Micro test: assets above € 450.000 fails, turnover above € 900.000 fails, employees below 10 passes. One of three, so not micro.
- Small test: assets below € 7,5 miljoen, turnover below € 15 miljoen, employees below 50. All three pass, so small.
- Deposit: book year ends 31 December 2027 and the accounts are adopted on 15 May 2028. Deposit within 8 days, by 23 May 2028, and at the latest on 31 December 2028.
- Result: small class, deposit by 23 May 2028 and no later than 31 December 2028.

- Draw up and adopt the jaarrekening and deposit it within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends, through SBR from boekjaar 2025 ([jaarrekening indienen](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).
- Keep a loss schedule in the order the losses arose and apply the € 1.000.000 plus 50% cap each year ([Wet Vpb 1969](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).
- Track the saldo aan renten and the gecorrigeerde winst and book non-deductible interest as carried forward ([Wet Vpb 1969](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025), [change page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026)).
- For a geruisloze omzetting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier. The BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).
- On the change of legal form, note the new btw-identificatienummer, send it to EU suppliers at once, and apply for a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- No dated 2027 Wet Vpb 1969 text. The 2027-01-01 URL returns HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11, so every Vpb value for 2027 is carried and UNVERIFIED. This touches the rate table, art. 15b (24,5% and € 1.000.000), verliesverrekening (€ 1.000.000 plus 50%), the innovatiebox fraction 9/H, the € 25.000 forfait, the smaller taxpayer thresholds € 37.500.000 and € 250.000.000, the 5% deelneming threshold and the 95% fiscale eenheid bezitseis ([Wet Vpb 1969](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). A 2027-01-01 version on wetten.overheid.nl would settle them.
- Dividendbelasting has no dated 2027 text either. The 2025-01-01 version reads "geldend t/m heden", so 15% and art. 4 lid 7 are carried and UNVERIFIED ([Wet DB 1965](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). A 2027 text would settle it.
- Conditionele bronbelasting 2027: the 25,8% holds only if art. 22 stays at 25,8% ([Wet bronbelasting 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)). A 2027 Vpb text or a change of art. 22 would settle it.
- Art. 22 in the 2026 text carries a note that changes without an entry date exist ([Wet Vpb 1969](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). A dated 2027 or later text would show them.
- Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022) was not adopted on 2026-10-11. Proposed and open: the € 100.000 forfait ceiling (Article XIX A, first for book years from 1 January 2027 per Article LXIII lid 1 sub d), the art. 13 lid 7 wording changes, the new art. 34j, the 1,01248 factor for the art. 10.1 lid 1 amounts, and the deletion of ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). Adoption and publication would settle them.
- Box 2 first bracket end 2027: the future text keeps € 68.843 ([Wet IB 2001, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)), the secondary summary gives € 69.607 ([wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)), and the bill factor 1,01248 on € 68.843 gives about € 69.702 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The December 2026 indexation regulation will settle it.
- Gebruikelijk loon 2027: the future text prints € 58.000, but the indexed amount is not published. Art. 12a lid 7 uses the tabelcorrectiefactor, and the bill's 1,01248 clause does not name art. 12a ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The December 2026 regulation under art. 12a lid 7 will settle it.
- Zvw 2027: 5,02%, 6,27% and € 82.547 come from the baseline budget rows as a secondary source. The Belastingdienst percentages page names only 2025 and 2026 ([percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)). The Regeling zorgverzekering 2027 will settle them.
- Stakingsaftrek 2027: the future text gives € 3.630 ([Wet IB 2001, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)) and the proposal gives € 908 from 1 January 2027 and gone in 2030 ([ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The page names no bill. The legislative process will settle it.
- The filing deadlines and the DGA Zvw route rest on current Belastingdienst pages and the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025. The Handboek 2026 and 2027 were not read ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen), [uitstel aangifte vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)). The 2027 filing-season pages and the 2027 Handboek would settle them.
- Sources agree that the uninsured DGA bijdrage is collected by inhouding op nettoloon. The Handboek 2025 says the BV withholds it on net wage, the Belastingdienst table adds "of by aanslag" ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).
