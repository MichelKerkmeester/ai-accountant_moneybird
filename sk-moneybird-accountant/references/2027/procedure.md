# Aangifte en procedure 2027

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2027 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the procedure for tax year 2027 for a ZZP sole trader and for a BV owner. It describes the income tax return deadline and uitstel (extension), the VAT filing and payment dates, the voorlopige aanslag (provisional assessment), belastingrente (tax interest), the navorderingstermijn (recovery period), bezwaar (objection), the bewaarplicht (record retention duty) and the KVK and Belastingdienst registration duties. 2027 is a mixed year: the AWR terms, the Invorderingswet terms and the VBAR hold in their current texts, the Belastingplan 2027 bill as introduced changes no AWR, Invorderingswet or belastingrente rule, and several 2027 amounts were still unpublished on 2026-10-11, including the belastingrente rate, the aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens, the boete amounts and the VBAR hourly amount after the 1 January 2027 indexation. From 1 January 2027 the remaining zachte landing in the enforcement of arbeidsrelaties lapses, so verzuimboetes are possible again and the default bedrijfsbezoek start ends.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

One subsection per item in the key figures table, in the same order. Every statement follows the linked checked source.

### 3.1 IB return over 2026: filing period and deadline

Applies to anyone who receives an aangiftebrief for the income tax return over 2026. The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. The aangiftebrief itself sets the date, so a different date in the brief replaces the usual date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.2 IB filing period opens

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March. This gives the taxpayer the window in which the return can be filed before the deadline in the aangiftebrief. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.3 IB uitstel

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. Uitstel usually means belastingrente is charged. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.4 IB uitstel channel

Online in Mijn Belastingdienst is possible until 1 May and gives uitstel to 1 September, but only for someone who received an aangiftebrief. The uitstel page says online is not possible. Read in full, that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both texts are current Belastingdienst text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date and the paper form otherwise. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.5 Fiscal partner and the uitstel form

The fiscal partner must request uitstel separately. The form can be used only for the first uitstel request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.6 AWR art. 9 lid 1 and 2

For aanslagbelastingen the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the invitation. Art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions and no 2027 change was found. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.7 Definitive assessment term, AWR art. 11 lid 3

The power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. The same text holds on 1 January 2020 and on 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.8 Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2027

The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before the end of the year count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not. For 2027 the two values were not published on 2026-10-11. Older captures used gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, which for whole-euro amounts describes the same threshold. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.9 Navorderingstermijn, AWR art. 16

Lid 1 requires a new fact. The fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw. Lid 2 adds the cases where a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar, always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3 gives 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and for a fout-based navordering only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Belastingdienst page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering was possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

### 3.10 Payment terms of assessments, Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9

An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, with the first termijn one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. The text is the same on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, and no 2027 change was found. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01

### 3.11 Bezwaartermijn

Bezwaar is filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. For income tax it can be filed online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if the bezwaar asks for that. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. Against a voorlopige aanslag IB no bezwaar is possible, only a change. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar

### 3.12 Verzoek om vermindering

After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible. It can be filed online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The page gives aanslag 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the verzoek goes by letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.13 Voorlopige aanslag change deadline

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year. With an aangiftebrief the deadline is the filing date or the uitstel date, and changing is no longer possible after filing. The page example for 2025 gives usually until 1 May 2026, with uitstel until 1 September 2026, and a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen

### 3.14 Belastingrente 2027 rate

The 2027 rate was not published on 2026-10-11. It is set before 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB herfinancieringsrente published before 1 November, plus 3 points for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen, rounded to half percentages, with a minimum of 4,5%. For Vpb, bronbelasting, minimumbelasting and solidariteitsbijdrage the addition is 5,5 points with a minimum of 5,5%. ### 3.16 Belastingrente on income tax in 2027 and the count basis

Belastingrente is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. For the return over 2026 the rente starts 1 July 2027 at the 2027 rate. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### 3.17 Belastingrente on btw

For a naheffingsaanslag it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

All rules, rates, thresholds and official examples below come from the figures table or the checked material. Round example inputs are used only to show the method. Every step is written out. Calculated examples are built here. Official examples are copied from a primary source with the URL.

**Example 1. Uitstel for the income tax return over 2026 (Calculated example).**
Input: the figures table marks the deadline for the return over 2026 as not published and usually 1 May 2027. The taxpayer requests uitstel before that date. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra.
Step 1: start from 1 May 2027.
Step 2: add 4 months. The four months are May, June, July and August.
Step 3: the new filing date is 1 September 2027.
Note: uitstel usually means belastingrente is charged.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

**Example 2. Bezwaar and the verzoek om vermindering (Calculated example).**
Input: a definitive aanslagbiljet dated 15 June 2027. The taxpayer disagrees.
Step 1: the bezwaartermijn is 6 weeks, which is 6 x 7 = 42 days.
Step 2: 15 June plus 15 days is 30 June, since June has 30 days.
Step 3: 42 minus 15 leaves 27 days, so the last day for bezwaar is 27 July 2027.
Step 4: the checked material states acknowledgement within 3 weeks. The example does not give the date the bezwaar is received, and the material does not give a clear start point for this term, so no acknowledgement date is calculated here.
Step 5: after the bezwaar term a verzoek om vermindering is possible. The online route needs an online filed return and fewer than 5 years after the tax year. The examples in the material are aanslag 2020 through 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 through 31 December 2028. For an aanslag over 2027 the same pattern gives 31 December 2032, since 2027 plus 5 is 2032.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

**Example 3. Payment terms on assessments (Calculated example).**
Inputs: three assessments, each with the date 1 June 2027.
Step 1: aanslag. Invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date, which is 6 x 7 = 42 days. 1 June plus 29 days is 30 June. 42 minus 29 leaves 13 days, so 13 July 2027.
Step 2: navorderingsaanslag. Invorderbaar 1 month after the date, so 1 July 2027.
Step 3: naheffingsaanslag. Invorderbaar 14 days after the date, so 15 June 2027.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01

Step 2: the aanslag is payable in 9 equal monthly termijnen.
Step 3: the first termijn is one month after the date, 15 April 2027. The termijnen then fall on 15 May, 15 June, 15 July, 15 August, 15 September, 15 October, 15 November and the last on 15 December 2027.

**Example 5. Navordering (Official example, then Calculated example).**

**Example 6. Belastingrente on the 2026 return (Calculated example, method only).**
Inputs: the return over 2026 is filed on 20 April 2027, before 1 May. The aanslag deviates from the return, so rente is charged. Aanslag date 1 October 2027. Tax due € 10.000,00.
Step 1: the period starts 1 July 2027.
Step 2: it ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, 6 x 7 = 42 days. 1 October plus 30 days is 31 October. 42 minus 30 leaves 12 days, so 12 November 2027.
Step 3: count with 30 days per month and 360 days per year. 1 July to 1 November is 4 months, 4 x 30 = 120 days. 1 November to 12 November is 11 days. 120 plus 11 is 131 days.
Step 4: the 2027 rate was not published on 2026-10-11, so the 2027 amount cannot be given. With the last set rate of 5% from 1 January 2026 as a method illustration only:
€ 10.000,00 x 5% = € 500,00 per year.
€ 500,00 x 131 = € 65.500,00.
€ 65.500,00 / 360 = € 181,9444, rounded down to whole euros is € 181,00.
Note: the 5% is the 2026 rate, not the 2027 rate, so € 181,00 is not the 2027 amount.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

**Example 7. Definitive assessment term for the return over 2027 (Calculated example).**
Input: assume the usual 1 May 2028 filing date and a granted uitstel to 1 September 2028.
Step 1: the Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2028.
Step 2: 3 years from 1 January 2028 is 1 January 2031.
Step 3: the term is extended by the duration of the uitstel. The uitstel runs from 1 May 2028 to 1 September 2028, which is 4 months.
Step 4: on a month count 1 January 2031 plus 4 months is 1 May 2031.
Note: the sources state the 3 years, the counting from 1 January and the extension by the duration of the uitstel. They do not print the exact end day.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

**Example 8. Bewaarplicht for a 4-year lease starting in 2027 (Calculated example).**
Input: a lease with a term of 4 years, starting 1 January 2027.
Step 1: the retention term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. For the lease that is after the 4 years of the lease, so after 31 December 2030.
Step 2: the lease records are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.
Step 3: 4 plus 7 is 11 years in total from the start of the lease. The 7-year retention term runs from 1 January 2031 and ends 31 December 2037.
Note: basisgegevens are kept 7 years. Onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data are kept 10 years. This example uses the 7-year term for the lease records.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

**Example 9. KVK registration for a business that starts on 1 June 2027 (Calculated example).**
Step 1: the registration window starts one week before the start. 1 June minus 7 days is 25 May 2027.
Step 2: the window ends one week after the start. 1 June plus 7 days is 8 June 2027.
Step 3: registering within the window gives the KVK-nummer immediately.
Step 4: registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen, and ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/

**Example 10. Betaalverzuimboete (Calculated example, method only, 2026 terms).**
Input: an open amount of € 4.000,00 on a (voorlopige) aanslag.
Step 1: the 2027 boete amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. The 2026 terms are 5% of the open amount, minimum € 50,00, maximum € 6.709,00 per default.
Step 2: 5% of € 4.000,00 is € 200,00.
Step 3: € 200,00 is above the minimum of € 50,00 and below the maximum of € 6.709,00, so under those terms the boete is € 200,00. No rounding is needed.
Note: this shows the method with 2026 terms, not the 2027 amount.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2026

- DBA verzuimboetes: in 2026 no verzuimboetes and vergrijpboetes possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse, so verzuimboetes are possible again and the default bedrijfsbezoek start ends. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf
- DBA handhaving organisation: 2026 had a separate handhavingsplan, versie 2.0 of 31 December 2025, with 80 fte, NLA meldingen usable from 2026 and extra attention for government bodies as opdrachtgevers, the detectiemodule TKA and horizontal supervision levels. For 2027 the ambition is full integration into regular handhaving with no separate plan, and no separate 2027 plan was published on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

- VBAR hourly amount: € 36 statutory base with unrounded € 35,43 at peildatum 1 January 2025, and € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. For 2027 about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, may change with the WML of 1 January 2027, UNVERIFIED, and the regulation was not published. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- Btw aangifteverzuimboete: 2025 and 2026 € 82 after 7 kalenderdagen coulance, against € 68 for 2020 to 2024. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- IB filing deadline: the 2025 return had a usual date of 1 May 2026, with uitstel until 1 September 2026 in the page example. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

- For a BV, the Vpb belastingrente follows the general rate from 2022 after HR 16 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

The ingroeimodel still limits corrections to periods from 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an earlier aanwijzing was not followed, and only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years.
