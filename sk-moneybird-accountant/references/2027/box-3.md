# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2027

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2027 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 3 (sparen en beleggen, savings and investments) taxes a fictitious return on banktegoeden (bank balances), overige bezittingen (other assets) and schulden (debts) with the category method, because the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is still a bill and the Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028" ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). For 2027 the published text of the act, adopted but not yet in force on 2026-10-11, carries a rate of 36%, a heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free allowance) of € 59.357 before the December 2026 indexation, a schuldendrempel (debt threshold) of € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, and green investment facilities of € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner plus a 0,1% korting (tax credit) ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The year is a mix of law, bill and announcement: bill 37 022 proposes an indexation factor of 1,01248 for the art. 10.1 amounts, and the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes an heffingsvrij vermogen of € 30.846 and a rise of 1,5 percentage point in the forfait for overige bezittingen ([bill](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The December 2026 indexation and the final 2027 percentages for banktegoeden, schulden and overige bezittingen are not published on 2026-10-11, so the figures printed in the 2027 text are the working values for this year.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

### Rekenmethode (categorieënmethode, art. 5.2)

From 1 January 2023 the Overbruggingswet box 3 (law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534) applies the category method to everyone, and the 2027 text of the act still carries it in art. 5.2 ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Under art. 5.2 the voordeel (box 3 income) is the effective rendementspercentage times the grondslag sparen en beleggen (taxable base), a negative rendement is set at nil, and art. 4.14 lid 1 keeps the forfaitaire voordeel at least nil ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)). The Belastingdienst describes the calculation in six steps ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)):

1. belastbaar rendement (taxable return) = banktegoeden x bank percentage + overige bezittingen x other-assets percentage - (schulden - drempel) x debt percentage.
2. rendementsgrondslag (return base) = bezittingen - (schulden minus drempel).
3. grondslag sparen en beleggen = rendementsgrondslag - heffingsvrij vermogen. Partners may divide it in any ratio that totals 100%.
4. aandeel (share) = grondslag / rendementsgrondslag x 100.
5. voordeel = belastbaar rendement x aandeel.
6. tax = rate x voordeel.

The six-step description comes from the calculation pages for 2023 to 2026 ([2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023), [2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024), [2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). The checked sources do not include a 2027 calculation page, and they list no official 2027 example. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year in the checked material, with 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages and 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages, so the 2027 instruction is not stated. It applies to everyone with assets and debts in box 3 on the peildatum.

### Peildatum

The box 3 values are taken at the peildatum (reference date) 1 January. Art. 5.24 speaks of "die peildatum" and the green exemption is capped at the value of the green investments on 1 January, art. 5.13 ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html), [art. 5.13, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). Art. 5.24 adds a rule against peildatumarbitrage: transactions around 1 January within one continuous three-month period that starts before and ends after the peildatum are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that zakelijke overwegingen (business reasons) lay behind them ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)). That rule does not apply to the actual return route, where income and value changes over the calendar year count and changes during the year count ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### Heffingsvrij vermogen, 2027 text

The heffingsvrij vermogen applies to every taxpayer with box 3 assets. The 2027 text prints € 59.357 per person, before the December 2026 indexation. Art. 5.5 is in the art. 10.1 indexation list, so the published amount is indexed and the indexed figure is not published on 2026-10-11 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)). Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17, and the 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint rendementsgrondslag and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount enters step 3 of the six-step calculation. If the rendementsgrondslag is lower than the heffingsvrij vermogen, there is no grondslag to tax.

### Heffingsvrij vermogen, Cabinet letter

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes to bring the heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027 ([Cabinet letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The proposal is not in bill 37 022 as published, which touches no chapter 5 article ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). It therefore has no effect unless it is adopted in a bill text. The proposal conflicts with the € 59.357 in the 2027 text, see section 7.

### Indexatiefactor art. 10.1, bill 37 022

Bill 37 022 art. LI proposes indexation factor 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor for the amounts in the art. 10.1 list ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). That list includes the heffingsvrij vermogen (art. 5.5) and the schuldendrempel (art. 5.3), so this bill is the second reason why the definitive 2027 amounts for those two items are not yet known ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amounts are not published. Status proposed.

### Tarief box 3

The rate is 36% for 2027 (art. 2.13 of the 2027 text). It applies to the voordeel that comes out of step 6 of the six-step calculation, whatever the composition of the assets is ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). For the actual return route, art. 5.25 lid 1 replaces the voordeel by the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions), so the rate is then applied to that amount ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

### Forfait overige bezittingen

The forfait (fixed return rate) for overige bezittingen applies to everything in box 3 that is not a banktegoed, including beleggingen (investments), a second home and the non-exempt part of green investments ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The 2027 text prints 6%. Unlike the heffingsvrij vermogen and the drempel, this percentage is not indexed. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 has it replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds ([2027 text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 figure is therefore unknown until that regulation is published, and the printed 6% is what the text says until the replacement. The percentage enters step 1 as the multiplier on overige bezittingen. Its effect also works through the aandeel, because the same base is in step 2 as well.

### Forfait overige bezittingen, Cabinet letter

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a rise of 1,5 percentage point in the forfait for overige bezittingen in 2027, including rent and own-use benefits ([Cabinet letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The resulting percentage is not stated in the checked material, so it cannot be used in a calculation, and no bill text with the change was published on 2026-10-11. See section 7.

### Schuldendrempel

Only the part of the total schulden (debts) above the drempel is deductible. Art. 5.3 lid 3 says "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...", and the Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text prints € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, before the December 2026 indexation. Art. 5.3 is in the art. 10.1 indexation list, so the definitive 2027 figure is not published on 2026-10-11. The drempel doubles for whole-year partners, and a joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s) ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). It enters steps 1 and 2 as the amount (schulden minus drempel). Debts below the drempel are left out completely.

### Bank- en schuldenpercentage

Art. 10.6ter lid 2 fixes the bank percentage after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January, at the sum of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, divided by twelve, with November counted twice. So it is an average, not a twelve-month sum. Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages ([2027 text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The final 2027 percentages are therefore set after 2027 and are unknown on 2026-10-11. The 2027 text prints banktegoeden 1,37% and schulden 2,70%. These equal the 2025 figures in the published series, so they are carry-over ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, percentages](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)). The checked material includes two regulation pages ([2025 regulation](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html), [2026 regulation](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)). Both percentages enter step 1. The other-assets percentage follows the different route of art. 10.6ter lid 3 described above.

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

Art. 5.13 keeps green investments out of the bezittingen up to € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner. The amount is not indexed for 2027 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2027 text art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). Conditions and exceptions: under art. 5.13 lid 2, from the 2023 text, the exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([2027 text art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The exempt part is not a bezitting, so it leaves both step 1 and step 2 of the calculation.

### Korting groene beleggingen

Art. 8.19 gives a korting (tax credit) of 0,1% of the exempt amount on the peildatum. The rate is 0,1% for 2025 to 2027 ([2027 text art. 8.19 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)). It is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). In 2027 the korting follows the exemption amounts of € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner.

### Einde groene regelingen

Afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 lapse on 1 January 2028. Stb. 2025, 444 art. XXXVIII onderdeel B moved the date from 1 January 2027 to 1 January 2028, and the 2028 text has no art. 5.13 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2028 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2028-01-01)). Both facilities therefore still apply in 2027. The Rijksoverheid page says something different, see section 7.

### Heffingsvrij resultaat

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a heffingsvrij resultaat (tax-free result) of € 1.000 from 2028 ([Cabinet letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). It is not a 2027 rule and it is not law on 2026-10-11. It belongs to the package around the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3.

### Wet werkelijk rendement box 3

Bill 36 748 was submitted on 16 May 2025 and adopted by the Tweede Kamer on 12 February 2026. The Eerste Kamer held a mondeling overleg (oral consultation) on 17 March 2026, a verslag (written report) on 7 April 2026, a nota naar aanleiding van het verslag (memorandum in reply) on 24 April 2026 and expert meetings on 19 May 2026, received a second verslag on 29 May 2026 and a reply on 12 June 2026, received a letter on 19 June 2026 about improvements and the vermogenswinst route, held a plenary session on 30 June 2026 with motions Van Rooijen (L), Van den Oetelaar c.s. (K), Kroon c.s. (J) and Schalk c.s. (M), where member Crone made an ordevoorstel (procedural proposal) to postpone the vote until the plenary treatment of the announced novelle(s), and voted on motion Schalk on 7 July 2026. Letters followed on 14 September 2026 (EK Q) and 30 September 2026 (EK 37.020 / 36.748, B), and a committee mondeling overleg on 5 October 2026. There is no Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 2026-10-11. Entry into force is by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028 ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). So 2027 runs on the category method, and the Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 announces a novelle (amending bill to a bill) that extends the vermogenswinstbelasting (capital gains tax on assets) to all financial instruments from 2028, about 90% of the assets with value development, with a full switch for the other assets in 2030. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The Eerste Kamer dossier lists the letter under 37.020 ([Cabinet letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255), [Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). The Kamerstuk number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 are not on a fetched page, see section 7.

### Opgaaf werkelijk rendement

The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 (law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195) entered into force on 19 July 2025 by the besluit of 14 July 2025 (Stb. 2025, 196), issued 18 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). Art. V works arts. I and III back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV back to 1 January 2017. Art. II (own use of real estate, art. 5.27a) applies from 1 January 2026. The core rule is art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lids 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the inspecteur, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lids 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus vermogensaanwas (value changes). Art. III gives a rule for the persoonsgebonden aftrek after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024, and art. IV adds art. 6a to the Wet rechtsherstel with the same form and six-week rule ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Content rules for the actual return: income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. There is no heffingsvrij vermogen in this calculation, "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen". Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement), [Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [Belastingdienst, werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

Background: the route follows the Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021 (Hoge Raad, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) and the later rulings. On 6 June 2024 the Hoge Raad held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3), [2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)). The nota van toelichting to Stb. 2025, 196 also names 14 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857), 2 August 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084) and 20 December 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788) ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). On 25 June 2026 the Rijksoverheid timeline confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode" ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

Own use of real estate: art. 5.27a sets the benefit at the economic rent value per year and excludes days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value, with peildatum 1 January of the previous year, times the usable days is a Belastingdienst calculation method and it is not in the text of Stb. 2025, 195 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). The checked sources state that percentage for 2026, not for 2027.

Route: 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD, outside Mijn Belastingdienst. 2020 to 2024 used an online form in Mijn Belastingdienst ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"), or a paper form via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call) and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. 2017 to 2020 needed a letter, and all those letters had been sent. From 2025 the reporting is in the income tax return. No deadline is stated on these pages ([formulier page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [zonder brief page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen), [werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)). So in 2027 the opgaaf goes in the 2027 income tax return.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

The checked material lists no official example for 2027 ("None found"), so every example below is a **Calculated example**. Each example uses the figures printed in the 2027 text from section 2. The bank percentage (1,37%), the debt percentage (2,70%) and the other-assets percentage (6%) are not final for 2027, and the heffingsvrij vermogen (€ 59.357) and the schuldendrempel (€ 3.800 and € 7.600) are still before the December 2026 indexation. The outcomes show the method, not a final 2027 assessment. No example applies the proposed indexation factor 1,01248 or the proposed plus 1,5 percentage point, because the checked sources do not state the resulting figures. Example 7 shows the effect of the proposed heffingsvrij vermogen of € 30.846, which is a stated amount.

**Example 1. Calculated example. Single taxpayer, banktegoeden € 150.000 on 1 January 2027, no other assets, no debts.**

- Step 1: belastbaar rendement = € 150.000 x 1,37% = € 2.055,00.
- Step 2: schulden minus drempel = € 0, so rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000 - € 0 = € 150.000.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 150.000 - € 59.357 = € 90.643.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 90.643 / € 150.000 x 100 = 60,428666%, rounded to three decimals 60,429%. The 2027 rounding instruction is not in the checked sources, so this example uses the three-decimal instruction printed for 2024 and 2026.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 2.055,00 x 60,429% = € 1.241,82.
- Step 6: tax = 36% x € 1.241,82 = € 447,06.

**Example 2. Calculated example. Single taxpayer with banktegoeden € 150.000, beleggingen € 75.000, a second home of € 200.000 and a debt of € 100.000 on 1 January 2027.**

- Overige bezittingen = € 75.000 + € 200.000 = € 275.000.
- Step 1: bank return = € 150.000 x 1,37% = € 2.055,00. Other-assets return = € 275.000 x 6,00% = € 16.500,00. Schulden minus drempel = € 100.000 - € 3.800 = € 96.200. Debt return = € 96.200 x 2,70% = € 2.597,40. Belastbaar rendement = € 2.055,00 + € 16.500,00 - € 2.597,40 = € 15.957,60.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000 + € 275.000 - € 96.200 = € 328.800.
- Step 3: grondslag = € 328.800 - € 59.357 = € 269.443.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 269.443 / € 328.800 x 100 = 81,947384%, rounded 81,947%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 15.957,60 x 81,947% = € 13.076,77.
- Step 6: tax = 36% x € 13.076,77 = € 4.707,64.

**Example 3. Calculated example. The same assets as example 2, with a whole-year fiscal partner, grondslag split 50/50.**

- The drempel doubles to € 7.600. Schulden minus drempel = € 100.000 - € 7.600 = € 92.400.
- Step 1: bank return € 2.055,00 + other-assets return € 16.500,00 - debt return (€ 92.400 x 2,70% = € 2.494,80) = € 16.060,20.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000 + € 275.000 - € 92.400 = € 332.600.
- Step 3: the heffingsvrij vermogen doubles, € 59.357 x 2 = € 118.714. Joint grondslag = € 332.600 - € 118.714 = € 213.886. Half each = € 213.886 / 2 = € 106.943.
- Step 4: personal aandeel = € 106.943 / € 332.600 x 100 = 32,153638%, rounded 32,154%. The personal share is taken over the joint rendementsgrondslag, which is how the 2026 official partner example works.
- Step 5: voordeel per partner = € 16.060,20 x 32,154% = € 5.164,00.
- Step 6: tax per partner = 36% x € 5.164,00 = € 1.859,04.
- The partners may divide the joint grondslag in any ratio that totals 100%, so the 50/50 split here is one choice.

**Example 4. Calculated example. Whole-year partners with banktegoeden € 5.000, green investments € 150.000 and a second home abroad of € 250.000 on 1 January 2027, no debts. The whole grondslag goes to one partner.**

- Green exemption: € 400 for the couple. Under art. 5.13 lid 2 it applies first to green overige bezittingen, so the taxable green investments are € 150.000 - € 400 = € 149.600.
- Step 1: bank return = € 5.000 x 1,37% = € 68,50. Overige bezittingen = € 149.600 + € 250.000 = € 399.600. Other-assets return = € 399.600 x 6,00% = € 23.976,00. Belastbaar rendement = € 68,50 + € 23.976,00 = € 24.044,50.
- Step 2: bezittingen = € 5.000 + € 150.000 + € 250.000 = € 405.000. The exempt € 400 is not a bezitting. Rendementsgrondslag = € 405.000 - € 400 = € 404.600.
- Step 3: heffingsvrij vermogen = € 59.357 x 2 = € 118.714. Grondslag = € 404.600 - € 118.714 = € 285.886, allocated to one partner.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 285.886 / € 404.600 x 100 = 70,658922%, rounded 70,659%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 24.044,50 x 70,659% = € 16.989,60.
- Step 6: tax = 36% x € 16.989,60 = € 6.116,26.
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,1% x € 400 = € 0,40 for the couple, computed automatically and split in the same ratio as the green balance. If the green investments are held 50/50, each partner gets € 0,20.

**Example 5. Calculated example. Single taxpayer, banktegoeden € 50.000 on 1 January 2027, no other assets, no debts.**

- Step 1: belastbaar rendement = € 50.000 x 1,37% = € 685,00.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 50.000 - € 0 = € 50.000.
- Step 3: grondslag = € 50.000 - € 59.357 = negative. There is no grondslag to tax, and the forfaitaire voordeel cannot be negative (art. 4.14 lid 1, Stb. 2022, 534).
- Step 5: voordeel = € 0. Step 6: tax = € 0.

**Example 6. Calculated example. The actual return against the fictitious voordeel, single taxpayer with the assets of example 1.**

- The fictitious voordeel from example 1 is € 1.241,82 and the fictitious tax is € 447,06.
- Case A: the actual return over the calendar year 2027 is € 600 and the taxpayer makes this plausible. € 600 is lower than € 1.241,82, so the income becomes € 600 minus any persoonsgebonden aftrek. With no persoonsgebonden aftrek, the voordeel is € 600 and the tax is 36% x € 600 = € 216,00.
- Case B: the actual return is a loss of € 1.000. The actual return is set at least at nil (art. 5.25 lid 2), so the voordeel is € 0 and the tax is € 0.
- The actual return route uses no heffingsvrij vermogen. Reporting is optional, the Belastingdienst applies the most favourable amount, and you never pay more than the fictitious calculation of € 447,06 for this asset position.

**Example 7. Calculated example, based on a proposed figure. The Cabinet letter proposal of € 30.846 against example 1.**

- The rendementsgrondslag stays € 150.000 and the belastbaar rendement stays € 2.055,00.
- Grondslag under the proposal = € 150.000 - € 30.846 = € 119.154.
- Aandeel = € 119.154 / € 150.000 x 100 = 79,436%.
- Voordeel = € 2.055,00 x 79,436% = € 1.632,41.
- Tax = 36% x € 1.632,41 = € 587,67.
- Against € 447,06 in example 1 that is € 140,61 more tax. The € 30.846 is proposed in the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 and is not in a published bill text, so this outcome has no legal effect on 2026-10-11.
