## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur's allowance) and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small-business profit exemption) for tax year 2027, the last year in this knowledge base, and every part of it requires status as an IB-ondernemer (ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting), which the Belastingdienst tests per activity and separately from the btw ([Belastingdienst, wanneer bent u ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [art. 3.4 and 3.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The ondernemersaftrek has five parts under art. 3.74, and the startersaftrek is not a sixth part but a verhoging of the zelfstandigenaftrek under art. 3.76 lid 3 ([art. 3.74](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 statute text, the version of the law valid on 1 January 2027, is adopted but not yet in force, so the rows taken from it carry the status adopted-not-yet-in-force, and it largely repeats 2026: the zelfstandigenaftrek is € 900, the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 12,7% and the enacted tariefsaanpassing is 11,91% above € 79.569 ([art. 2.10, 3.6, 3.74, 3.76, 3.78, 3.78a, 3.79, 3.79a and 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Against that adopted text stand two pending proposals, Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022), which would cut the startersaftrek to € 10 from 1 January 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028, and the Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups, which would cut the meewerkaftrek and the stakingsaftrek and abolish both in 2030 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)).

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Urencriterium

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer, and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is grotendeels spent on the businesses, or (b) the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The hours criterion is a condition for the zelfstandigenaftrek (art. 3.76 lid 1), for the startersaftrek through its link to the zelfstandigenaftrek, for the meewerkaftrek (art. 3.78) and for the S&O aftrek. Who is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged per activity and separately from the btw, weighing whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts, and activities in the hobby or family sphere do not count ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)). Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk, calculated the same way, without the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/)). All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). Standby time does not count, so the Belastingdienst example of a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage, and hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)), and a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium)).

### Second test waived, lookback

Art. 3.6 lid 1 onderdeel b waives the second test (the grotendeels test) for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, so for 2027 the lookback window runs over 2022 to 2026 ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The second test comes back only after five straight years as an ondernemer. The FISIN handbooks print the window per year and older editions contain misprints: FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 prints "20167 tot en met 2021", against the statute, which gives 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021 ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For 2027 the window is 2022 to 2026.

### Samenwerkingsverband, mainly supporting

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (a working partnership) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household, and a samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and an ondermaatschap as the example for (b) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Pregnancy interruption

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof for employees ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Verlaagd urencriterium

Art. 3.78a lid 3 replaces the 1.225 hours of the art. 3.6 test with 800 hours ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, so it does not open the zelfstandigenaftrek, the meewerkaftrek or the S&O aftrek. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the zelfstandigenaftrek to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium, and lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year at € 900 for 2027 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount is a fixed amount in the statute and is not indexed, and the law with this amount is adopted ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). Lid 5 caps the aftrek at the profit unless the ondernemer qualifies for the startersaftrek, and the amount cut off by that cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek. If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Medegerechtigden (persons entitled to a share of the profit other than as an entrepreneur) get no zelfstandigenaftrek on their medegerechtigde profit ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 that is € 450, being 50% of € 900. The Belastingdienst page prints the 50% rule ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, carry-forward

The amount cut off by the cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, and the inspecteur sets it in a beschikking (a decision) open to bezwaar (objection) under art. 3.76 lid 6 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Lid 7 offsets it in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8 states that the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst explains the same mechanism on its carry-forward page ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).

### Startersaftrek, enacted text

The startersaftrek is a verhoging of the zelfstandigenaftrek under art. 3.76 lid 3, not a separate part of the art. 3.74 list ([art. 3.74 and 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The enacted 2027 text prints € 2.123. The arrangement requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, in which case the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)). Bill 37 022 would replace the € 2.123 from 1 January 2027, see the bill row below.

### Startersaftrek at AOW age, enacted text

At the AOW age at the start of the year the startersaftrek is halved by art. 3.76 lid 4, so the enacted 2027 text gives € 1.062, being 50% of € 2.123 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 50% rule is printed by the Belastingdienst ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). No page states what this amount becomes under bill 37 022.

### Startersaftrek prior use limit

Art. 3.76 lid 3 gives the verhoging if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (a geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, a silent conversion of a BV into a sole trader business) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Belastingdienst adds that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and it words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)).

### Startersaftrek, bill 37 022

Bill 37 022 (Belastingplan 2027) is a proposal dated 29 September 2026 in the Tweede Kamer, and Artikel I onderdeel G would set the startersaftrek at € 10 from 1 January 2027, with Artikel II onderdeel C abolishing it from 1 January 2028 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The MvT says abolition of the startersaftrek on 1 January 2027 is not workable, so it first drops to € 10 on 1 January 2027 and is abolished on 1 January 2028, without transitional rules, also for ondernemers who started in 2025 ([MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). The same bill would end the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid on 1 January 2029 and the willekeurige afschrijving for starters on 1 January 2028 through the Eindejaarsregeling 2026 ([MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). Ondernemersplein states that the Tweede Kamer and Eerste Kamer still have to decide on this proposal ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The retroactivity conflict on the € 10 sits in section 7.

### Startersaftrek at AOW age, bill 37 022

No page states what the startersaftrek at AOW age becomes under bill 37 022, so the value is unknown and UNVERIFIED ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The bill leaves art. 3.76 lid 4 unchanged, which would give 50% of € 10 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It is not for an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then, and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 and unchanged by the bill for 2027 ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst 2026 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026)).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid abolition

Bill 37 022, Artikel III onderdelen F and G, would delete the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid from art. 3.74 onderdeel d from 1 January 2029 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The proposal is pending and Ondernemersplein states that the Tweede Kamer and Eerste Kamer still have to decide ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)).

### Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, enacted text

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D deduction) is part of the ondernemersaftrek under art. 3.74 and is worked out in art. 3.77 ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). It is a base amount plus a starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period, and FISIN adds that it requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The amounts are indexed per year, and the enacted 2027 text prints € 15.979, which is the 2026 amount before the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amount is not published.

### S&O starter increase, enacted text

The starter increase on the S&O aftrek follows the same conditions as the base amount, being a taxpayer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and who had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The enacted 2027 text prints € 7.996, the 2026 amount before the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amount is not published.

### S&O hours condition

The S&O aftrek requires at least 500 hours of S&O work in the year, next to the S&O-verklaring from RVO and the urencriterium ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

Art. 3.79a makes the MKB-winstvrijstelling a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek, and for 2027 that percentage is 12,7% ([art. 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 3.2 defines the belastbare winst uit onderneming as the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling ([art. 3.2 and 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No urencriterium is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). There is no vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde, and in a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing.

### Meewerkaftrek, enacted text

Art. 3.78 gives the meewerkaftrek (allowance for a working partner) to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay ([art. 3.78](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Exactly 875 hours therefore falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band ([art. 3.78](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening, profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. The hours must be made plausible, and medegerechtigden get no meewerkaftrek on their medegerechtigde profit ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).

### Pay to the partner, art. 3.16 lid 4

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit ([art. 3.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). In that case the meewerkaftrek can still apply if the hours band and the other conditions are met. The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### Meewerkaftrek, start-ups bill (MvT and Ondernemersplein)

The Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups, a Kamerstuk of 15 September 2026, has Artikel I cutting the meewerkaftrek percentages and the stakingsaftrek maximum, Artikel II deleting art. 3.78 and 3.79 and art. 10a.29 lid 7, and Artikel XI letting the entry into force run by koninklijk besluit, with Artikel II at the start of the third calendar year after Artikel I onderdelen B and C took effect ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)). The MvT says the cuts follow the start of the option scheme on 1 January of a year set by koninklijk besluit, and Ondernemersplein gives 1 January 2027 and 2030 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The MvT table and Ondernemersplein print the cut percentages as 0,313% / 0,5% / 0,75% / 1%, and Ondernemersplein states that the Tweede Kamer and Eerste Kamer still have to decide.

### Meewerkaftrek, start-ups bill article text, first band

The article text of the start-ups bill, Artikel I onderdeel B, prints 0,32% for the first band, where the bill MvT table and Ondernemersplein print 0,313% ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)). The article text is the operative proposal, and the conflict is in section 7.

### Meewerkaftrek abolition

Artikel II onderdeel C and Artikel XI lid 2 of the start-ups bill would delete art. 3.78, and Ondernemersplein says 2030, being the start of the third calendar year after the cuts ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The proposal is pending.

### Stakingsaftrek, enacted text

Art. 3.79 gives the stakingsaftrek (cessation allowance) on profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses ([art. 3.79](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under doorschuiving (a tax-free transfer) on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. The maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek)). Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65), that onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([art. 3.79 and 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2027 the relevant rule is art. 10a.29 lid 7.

### Stakingsaftrek, start-ups bill

Artikel I onderdeel C of the start-ups bill would cut the stakingsaftrek maximum to € 908, and Ondernemersplein agrees ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The proposal is pending.

### Stakingsaftrek abolition

Under the start-ups bill the stakingsaftrek would be abolished in 2030, being the start of the third calendar year after the cut, and Artikel II also deletes art. 10a.29 lid 7 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The bill text gives the timing through Artikel XI lid 2, and Ondernemersplein says 2030 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)).

### Oudedagsreserve additions

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition to the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) is possible from 2023, and 2027 follows that position ([art. 3.67 to 3.72](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 ([art. 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business, lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the stakingsaftrek for an afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027.

### Tariefsaanpassing, enacted text

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the tax base), expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions ([art. 2.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 the enacted text sets that percentage at 11,91% above € 79.569. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage, see the next row.

### Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling, enacted text

The maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing percentage, so the enacted 2027 text gives 49,50% minus 11,91% and a maximum value of 37,59% ([art. 2.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Belastingplan 2027 proposes a different figure, see the next row.

### Maximum deduction value, Belastingplan 2027

Belastingplan 2027 proposes a maximum deduction value of 38,16%, printed in the sleuteltabel row "Aftrektarief tariefmaatregel 38,16%" and in the MvT second bracket ([sleuteltabel 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf)). The figure is proposed and the enacted text keeps 37,59% until the bill is adopted.

### Zvw maximum contribution income

The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, and the maximum for 2027 is not in the checked material ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [rijksbegroting XVI](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)). This interaction is verified for 2020 and 2021 only, and the detail belongs to the Zvw topic. FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into een lijfrente ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for the tax year 2027 was found in the checked sources, so every example below is marked `Calculated example` and is built from the section 2 figures and the checked material. The inputs are invented round numbers and no real person's data is used. The enacted 2027 figures used here are adopted-not-yet-in-force. Every product is written with two decimals, and every step that rounds says "rounded" or "rounded down". Where an example touches a proposal, the proposal is named as proposed.

### Calculated example A. Zelfstandigenaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling on a profit of € 60.000

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 60.000, the urencriterium is met, no right to the startersaftrek.

1. Profit as ondernemer: € 60.000.
2. Zelfstandigenaftrek under art. 3.76 lid 2: € 900. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not bite because € 900 is less than the profit of € 60.000.
3. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 minus € 900 = € 59.100.
4. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 59.100 = € 7.505,70.
5. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 59.100 minus € 7.505,70 = € 51.594,30.
6. The maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling together is 37,59% under the enacted text (49,50% minus 11,91%). Belastingplan 2027 proposes 38,16% instead.

### Calculated example B. Starter with the enacted startersaftrek against the proposed bill amount

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 40.000, the urencriterium is met, the starter conditions of art. 3.76 lid 3 are met, below the AOW age.

Enacted 2027 text:

1. Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900.
2. Startersaftrek: € 2.123.
3. Total ondernemersaftrek: € 900 plus € 2.123 = € 3.023.
4. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 40.000 minus € 3.023 = € 36.977.
5. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 36.977 = € 4.696,08 (rounded).
6. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 36.977 minus € 4.696,08 = € 32.280,92.

Under the € 10 that bill 37 022 proposes:

7. Total ondernemersaftrek: € 900 plus € 10 = € 910.
8. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 40.000 minus € 910 = € 39.090.
9. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 39.090 = € 4.964,43.
10. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 39.090 minus € 4.964,43 = € 34.125,57.

The difference is € 34.125,57 minus € 32.280,92 = € 1.844,65 more belastbare winst under the proposed € 10. Bill 37 022 is still a proposal. It would abolish the startersaftrek from 1 January 2028. The Tweede Kamer and the Eerste Kamer still have to decide on it.

### Calculated example C. Starter at the AOW age

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 30.000, the urencriterium is met, the starter conditions are met, the AOW age has been reached at the start of 2027.

1. Zelfstandigenaftrek under art. 3.76 lid 4: 50% of € 900 = € 450,00.
2. Startersaftrek under art. 3.76 lid 4: 50% of € 2.123 = € 1.061,50, rounded to € 1.062.
3. Total ondernemersaftrek: € 450 plus € 1.062 = € 1.512.
4. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 30.000 minus € 1.512 = € 28.488.
5. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 28.488 = € 3.617,98 (rounded).
6. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 28.488 minus € 3.617,98 = € 24.870,02.

This example uses the enacted text only, because the AOW-age startersaftrek under bill 37 022 is not stated in the checked material.

### Calculated example D. The profit cap and niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 800, the urencriterium is met, no right to the startersaftrek.

1. Nominal zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900.
2. Cap of art. 3.76 lid 5: the aftrek is not more than the profit, so € 900 is reduced to € 800.
3. Niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900 minus € 800 = € 100. The inspecteur sets this amount by beschikking open to bezwaar.
4. The € 100 is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. The offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar.
5. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 800 minus € 800 = € 0.
6. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 0 = € 0,00.
7. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 0.
8. If the cap sets the aftrek to nil in a year, that year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count.

### Calculated example E. Starter whose combined aftrek exceeds the profit

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 2.000, the urencriterium is met, the starter conditions of art. 3.76 lid 3 are met.

1. Zelfstandigenaftrek € 900 plus startersaftrek € 2.123 = € 3.023.
2. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not apply, because of the exception for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek.
3. The combined aftrek exceeds the profit by € 3.023 minus € 2.000 = € 1.023. The Belastingdienst states that this excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years.
4. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is a percentage of the amount after the ondernemersaftrek. The Belastingdienst notes that in a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which it calls disadvantageous.

### Calculated example F. Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk and the S&O starter increase

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 60.000, the urencriterium is met, at least 500 hours of S&O work, an S&O-verklaring from RVO, and the starter conditions for the increase are met. The amounts are the enacted text before the December 2026 indexation, because the indexed 2027 amounts are not published.

1. Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 15.979.
2. S&O starter increase: € 7.996.
3. S&O total: € 15.979 plus € 7.996 = € 23.975.
4. Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900, because the urencriterium is met.
5. Total ondernemersaftrek: € 23.975 plus € 900 = € 24.875.
6. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 minus € 24.875 = € 35.125.
7. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 35.125 = € 4.460,88 (rounded).
8. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 35.125 minus € 4.460,88 = € 30.664,12.

### Calculated example G. Meewerkaftrek for a partner with 1.000 hours

Inputs: profit € 50.000 with no excluded components in the art. 3.78 base, the urencriterium is met, the partner works in the business without pay for 1.000 hours.

1. Band: 1.000 hours lies between 875 and under 1.225 hours, so the percentage is 2%.
2. Meewerkaftrek: 2% of € 50.000 = € 1.000,00.
3. Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900.
4. Total ondernemersaftrek: € 900 plus € 1.000 = € 1.900.
5. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 50.000 minus € 1.900 = € 48.100.
6. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 48.100 = € 6.108,70.
7. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 48.100 minus € 6.108,70 = € 41.991,30.

Boundary check: at exactly 1.225 hours the 3% band applies, giving 3% of € 50.000 = € 1.500,00.

Pay check: if the partner were paid € 4.000, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible from profit. The partner then counts as working without pay, and the pay is no income for the partner.

The percentages are the enacted 2027 text. The Start-ups bill proposes lower percentages and abolition from 2030, both still proposed.

### Calculated example H. Stakingsaftrek on a staking profit of € 5.000

Inputs: profit made with the staking of one whole business € 5.000, no stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years.

1. Stakingsaftrek: it equals that profit, at most € 3.630, so the aftrek is € 3.630.
2. Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 5.000 minus € 3.630 = € 1.370.
3. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 1.370 = € 173,99.
4. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 1.370 minus € 173,99 = € 1.196,01.

Variant: if € 1.000 of stakingsaftrek had been enjoyed in earlier years, the maximum would be reduced, not below nil, to € 3.630 minus € 1.000 = € 2.630, and the aftrek would be € 2.630. The profit after the ondernemersaftrek would then be € 5.000 minus € 2.630 = € 2.370, the MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 2.370 = € 300,99, and the belastbare winst uit onderneming € 2.370 minus € 300,99 = € 2.069,01.

The maximum of € 3.630 is the enacted 2027 text. The Start-ups bill proposes a maximum of € 908 and abolition from 2030, both still proposed.

No zelfstandigenaftrek applies because the urencriterium is not met. The MKB-winstvrijstelling applies with no hours condition.

   - MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 8.000 = € 1.016,00.

2. Aftrek applied for one year: € 8.000.
   - Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000 minus € 8.000 = € 12.000.
   - MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 12.000 = € 1.524,00.
   - Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 12.000 minus € 1.524,00 = € 10.476,00.
3. Aftrek applied for two years: € 4.000.
   - Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000 minus € 4.000 = € 16.000.
   - MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,7% of € 16.000 = € 2.032,00.
   - Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 16.000 minus € 2.032,00 = € 13.968,00.
4. Aftrek applied for more than two years: the statute has no tier, so the aftrek is € 0.
   - Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000.

### Calculated example J. Tariefsaanpassing on € 11.000 of deductions

Inputs: belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning after the deductions € 90.000, ondernemersaftrek plus MKB-winstvrijstelling together € 11.000.

1. Taxable box 1 income plus the deductions: € 90.000 plus € 11.000 = € 101.000.
2. Excess over the top bracket start of € 79.569: € 101.000 minus € 79.569 = € 21.431.
3. 11,91% of € 21.431 = € 2.552,43 (rounded down).
4. Cap: at most 11,91% of the deductions, being 11,91% of € 11.000 = € 1.310,10.
5. The tariefsaanpassing therefore adds € 1.310,10, the lower of the two outcomes.
6. Cross-check on the maximum value: 49,50% minus 11,91% = 37,59% under the enacted text. Belastingplan 2027 proposes 38,16% instead.

- Verlaagd urencriterium: unchanged at 800 hours, and the Belastingdienst wording in 2026 was at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([art. 3.78a, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

The indexed 2027 amount is not published.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business in a way that can be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. All hours on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count, so the Belastingdienst example counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage for an owner who is reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).
- Record the hours of a partner who works in the business without pay, because the meewerkaftrek band depends on them and those hours must be made plausible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).
- The ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are applied on the winst uit onderneming in the inkomstenbelasting return, in the order profit, then ondernemersaftrek, then MKB-winstvrijstelling ([art. 3.2 and 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- A capped zelfstandigenaftrek creates a niet-gerealiseerde amount that the inspecteur sets by beschikking open to bezwaar, and the offset in a later year is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar, so keep the beschikking with the year's working papers ([art. 3.76 lid 6 and lid 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- For an existing oudedagsreserve, lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve that are paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return ([art. 10a.29 lid 14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- On death the oudedagsreserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports the profit as inkomsten uit overig werk, calculated the same way, without the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)).
- Because the startersaftrek, meewerkaftrek and stakingsaftrek proposals are still with the Tweede Kamer and Eerste Kamer, keep the enacted 2027 text and the proposed amounts apart in the working papers ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Conflict on the € 10 startersaftrek date. Artikel LXIII lid 1 onderdeel a of bill 37 022 names Artikel I onderdeel G, the startersaftrek of € 10, among the parts that work back to 1 January 2026, while the MvT says the € 10 applies from 1 January 2027 and that 2026 is unaffected ([bill 37 022 and MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). The nota van wijziging of 2 October 2026 (2026D48289) does not change this. Settled only by a later nota van wijziging or the adopted text.
- Conflict on the first meewerkaftrek band under the start-ups bill: 0,32% in the bill article text against 0,313% in the bill MvT table and on Ondernemersplein ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The article text is the operative proposal. Settled by the adopted text.
- Open timing of the start-ups bill: its text leaves the date to a koninklijk besluit, and only the MvT and Ondernemersplein name 1 January 2027 and 2030 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). Settled by the koninklijk besluit or the adopted text.
- Conflict on the 2027 startersaftrek: KVK states that the startersaftrek "vervalt" in 2027 and that 2026 is the last year, which matches neither the enacted 2027 text of € 2.123 nor bill 37 022 with € 10 in 2027 and abolition in 2028 ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). Settled by the adopted text.
- Unknown: the startersaftrek at AOW age under bill 37 022 is not stated on any checked page, status unknown and UNVERIFIED ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The bill leaves art. 3.76 lid 4 unchanged, which would give 50% of € 10. Settled by a later nota van wijziging, the adopted text or an explanatory page.
- Unknown: the indexed 2027 amounts of the S&O aftrek and the S&O starter increase are not published, because they wait for the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Settled by the published indexation.
- Unknown: the Zvw maximum contribution income for 2027 is not published, status unknown and UNVERIFIED ([rijksbegroting XVI](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)). Settled in the Zvw topic.
- Proposed and still pending, with the Tweede Kamer and Eerste Kamer yet to decide: startersaftrek € 10 from 1 January 2027 and abolition from 1 January 2028, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid abolition from 1 January 2029 and the end of the willekeurige afschrijving for starters on 1 January 2028 through the Eindejaarsregeling 2026 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). Also proposed: meewerkaftrek 0,313% / 0,5% / 0,75% / 1% with 0,32% in the article text, stakingsaftrek € 908 and abolition of both in 2030, the deletion of art. 10a.29 lid 7, and a maximum deduction value of 38,16% under Belastingplan 2027 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/), [sleuteltabel 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf)). Settled by the votes in both chambers and the published texts.
