# Box 2 en aanmerkelijk belang 2027

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2027 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers box 2 income from an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial shareholding) in tax year 2027, so the 5% test, the two-bracket rate, dividends and vervreemdingsvoordelen, the belastingkorting on an unoffset loss, the excessief lenen (excessive borrowing) fiction for loans from the own BV and the link with the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) of a director-major shareholder. The law for 2027 is a mix of enacted law, bills and an announcement. The future Wet IB 2001 text per 1 January 2027 keeps the 2026 box 2 table, the € 500.000 excessief lenen maximum and the art. 4.6 to 4.16 texts, and the future Wet LB 1964 text keeps € 58.000 and € 5.000. The December 2026 indexation regulation, the Belastingplan 2027 bill (Kamerstuk 37 022 nr. 2), the Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill (Kamerstuk 37 023) and the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 still have to settle the final numbers. The most important open items are the indexed 2027 bracket boundary, the proposed 29,2% second-bracket rate from 2027 and the proposed phase-down of the excessief lenen maximum. All sources in this file were checked on 2026-10-11.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

### Box 2 bracket 1 (art. 2.12)

Box 2 income is the reguliere voordelen minus the deductible costs plus the vervreemdingsvoordelen, minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek (art. 4.12) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief lenen fiction (sub f) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The text per 1 January 2027 taxes the part of the box 2 income up to € 68.843 at 24,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). That version is the pre-indexation text and it "kan voor inwerkingtreding nog wijzigen" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor, so the boundary normally changes each January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The table applies per taxpayer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). With fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang are a gemeenschappelijk inkomensbestanddeel that the partners may divide as they choose in the return, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is", and because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer each partner applies his own first bracket to his share (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The taxpayer with the aanmerkelijk belang declares the income, and the Belastingdienst states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked material gives no separate filing deadline for box 2 income beyond the return itself.

### Tax at the top of bracket 1 (art. 2.12, column III)

The art. 2.12 table states the tax at the top of bracket 1 in column III, € 16.866 in the text per 1 January 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). This is the amount that the calculation uses for the part of the income in bracket 1 when part of the income falls in bracket 2 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The figure belongs to the pre-indexation text, so the December 2026 ministerial regulation can replace it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Box 2 bracket 2 (art. 2.12)

Income above the bracket 1 boundary is taxed at 31% in the text per 1 January 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The two brackets exist from 2024. One flat rate applied in 2020 to 2023, 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The second bracket was announced in Belastingplan 2023 and took effect on 1 January 2024, with 24,5% over the first € 67.000 per person and 33% above it (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024). The 33% itself came from Belastingplan 2024, where the amendement Van der Lee c.s. raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). Belastingplan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). The rate is per taxpayer, like bracket 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Second-bracket rate 2027 to 2030 (cabinet letter of 29 September 2026)

The cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 ("Kamerbrief over voorstellen op box 3, koopkracht werkenden en sociale zekerheid", ministerie van Financiën) proposes "voor de duur van 4 jaar het tarief van de tweede schijf in box 2 met 1,8%-punt verlaagd naar 29,2% per 2027" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). So the proposal is a second-bracket rate of 29,2% for 4 years from 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text with the measure was found on 2026-10-11, and the letter does not name the legislative vehicle (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The measure is proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). This corrects the earlier note that no box 2 change was proposed for 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). A change of the second-bracket rate would not change the belastingkorting percentage, which follows the first-row percentage of the art. 2.12 table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Indexation multiplier for art. 10.1 amounts in 2027 (Belastingplan 2027)

Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37 022 nr. 2, 15 September 2026) sets in Artikel LI lid 1 the 2027 multiplier for the art. 10.1 amounts at 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Art. 2.12 is an art. 10.1 amount, so the box 2 boundary follows this multiplier if the bill is adopted (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The bill does not change art. 2.12, chapter 4 Wet IB 2001 or art. 12a Wet LB 1964 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The factsheet states the tabelcorrectiefactor is applied for 48% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). On 2026-10-11 the bill is not adopted, the Tweede Kamer dossier shows a nota van wijziging of 2 October 2026 and no vote yet (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The multiplier does not name art. 12a Wet LB 1964, so it does not state the indexed gebruikelijk loon amount (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### Indexed 2027 bracket 1 boundary

The indexed boundary is not published on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The December 2026 ministerial regulation will settle it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Two secondary sources disagree. ABN AMRO gives € 69.607 (https://financialfocus.abnamro.nl/fiscaliteiten/prinsjesdag-2026-negen-fiscale-aandachtspunten-voor-de-dga/) and holdwise.nl gives € 69.703 (https://holdwise.nl/kennisbank/box-2-tarief-2027). Both figures are secondary and the material marks the value UNVERIFIED (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### Aanmerkelijk belang, the 5% test (art. 4.6)

A taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of the profit, the liquidation proceeds or the voting rights (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). In sub d "tot" became "om" in the same period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The future text per 1 January 2027 keeps the art. 4.6 to 4.16 texts unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner", so the holdings of the fiscal partner count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children, until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The meesleepregeling (art. 4.9) brings the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in the same company into the aanmerkelijk belang once such a belang exists (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The meetrekregeling (art. 4.10) brings shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner does into an aanmerkelijk belang (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.7 to 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.11) exists for shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%, on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang, and art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The vbi status is a fictieve vervreemding case listed by the Belastingdienst, not the art. 4.11 list (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.16 also lists inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen, becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death, granting a koopoptie, shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a, and a liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Forfaitair voordeel for a vbi or a foreign investment company (art. 4.14)

The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a applies to shares in a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). It is a yearly percentage of the value at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The percentage is a yearly value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In the text per 1 January 2027 the percentage is 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The shares starting to yield this forfaitair voordeel are also a fictieve vervreemding under art. 4.16 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, the reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and they are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessief lenen fiction, that fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill (Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from this facility from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). That proposal is not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1). The art. 4.49 text is identical in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states the loss is "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar", so the offset period is 9 years instead of 6 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53)

Lid 1 applies when the taxpayer and his partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before. The unoffset loss is then converted on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 2 gives the percentage, 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", which is 24,5% in 2024 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 3 lets the inspecteur set the korting by beschikking open to objection (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 4 lets the korting reduce the box 1 tax of that year and of the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 5 allows part of the korting to pass to the surviving partner on the death of a partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting). Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block the conversion, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/). The FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 statement "De korting is 26,9%" is overruled by art. 4.53 lid 2, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Excessief lenen maximum (art. 4.14a lid 2)

The excessief lenen fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ("het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)"), where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations, and the amount rule sits in art. 4.14a, with the verbonden-person rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The statute is Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022), in force 1 January 2023 under Artikel III, and it is cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). Lid 1 makes a positive amount equal to the total debts above the maximum, and a negative amount equal to the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier or under a comparable foreign rule and not yet reversed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 2 sets the maximum at € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The bill proposed € 500.000, the derde nota van wijziging (Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced it by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1, and the amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html). The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Lid 3 counts debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both once for the two together and applies the maximum to them jointly (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 4 measures the debts at the end of the calendar year at nominal value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 5 applies lid 1 as if the taxpayer still had an aanmerkelijk belang and the debts were nil when he no longer has an aanmerkelijk belang at year end other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, which releases a negative amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 6 excludes an eigenwoningschuld to the extent a recht van hypotheek on the home was given to the company, and art. 10a.23 excludes an eigenwoningschuld existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 7, added by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023), removes the double counting of a samenwerkingsverband debt among several participants (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html). Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a verbonden person for a debt of a samenwerkingsverband (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Those lids were inserted by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023 as lid 6 and 7, numbered lid 4 and 5 for 2023, and Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" retroactive to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). The Belastingdienst adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added and that the drempel "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). The loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividendbelasting on the part taxed as box 2 income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). A negative fictief regulier voordeel is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The text per 1 January 2027 keeps € 500.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Indexation of the excessief lenen maximum

Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.14a is not on that list, so the € 500.000 maximum of art. 4.14a is not indexed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2027 text keeps the same € 500.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessief lenen, verbonden personen and immigration (art. 4.14b, art. 4.14c)

Debts of a verbonden person, a blood or aanverwant in the rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner, to a company where the taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000 from 2024, applied to that person and his partner together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 500.000 from 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2027 text keeps € 500.000 in art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessief lenen phase-down (cabinet letter of 29 September 2026)

The cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a phase-down of the excessief lenen maximum to € 100.000 in five steps of € 80.000 from 2027, and it states "Leningen ten behoeve van de eigen woning worden hierin niet meegenomen" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text with the measure was found on 2026-10-11 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The measure is proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).

### Excessief lenen maximum at 31 December 2027 under the letter (€ 420.000)

One step of € 80.000 below € 500.000 gives € 420.000 for 31 December 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). The letter does not print € 420.000. The figure is arithmetic from the letter and is stated by secondary sources, so the material marks it UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).

### Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a lid 7 Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies and (c) a fixed amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). A lower loon applies if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable loon, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor, in lid 9 in 2020 and lid 7 from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The text per 1 January 2027 prints € 58.000, which is the pre-indexation text, and art. 12a lid 7 replaces it at the start of 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01). The Belastingplan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 does not name art. 12a, and the indexed amount is not published (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The material corrects the reading that € 58.000 is itself the 2027 norm (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01). The start-up rule of art. 12a lid 3 and 4 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January or the comparable loon if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling, is absent from the 2023 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The Belastingdienst states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen", and the rule could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 to 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). The statement that the loon reduces the residual profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Gebruikelijk loon, comparable loon (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

The loon from the most comparable employment counts at 100% from the 2023 text, against 75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). A lower loon applies if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable loon, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01).

### Gebruikelijk loon minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The gebruikelijk loon rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year, in lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts and in lid 4 from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The text per 1 January 2027 keeps € 5.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01).

### ODV marktrente for 2027

The oudedagsverplichting (ODV) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation, art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The 2026 rate is 2,593%, set by Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025, which replaced "2,602%" by "2,593%" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). The 2027 rate is not published on 2026-10-11. The 2026 table runs to October 2026 with 2,871%, and the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation will set the 2027 rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). A negative market rate must also be applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The ODV can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente, lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it, and an earlier start lengthens the twenty years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5, added by Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). From 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapsed, and the 2020 Wet LB 1964 text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). Afkoop or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked vervallen (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/). A remaining pensioen in eigen beheer keeps the old rules of art. 38n, the company must index the accrued rights every year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision every year actuarially, and "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2, in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p, and PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking in box 1 (art. 10a.18 lid 2 and 3) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).

### Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 (Kamerstuk 37 023) box 2 measures

The memorie van toelichting of Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 (Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) carries two box 2 measures from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). The first excludes the fictief regulier voordeel from the art. 4.12a inherited-belang facility (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). The second sets the verkrijgingsprijs at the value in het economisch verkeer when a company moves its seat to the Netherlands (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). Both measures are proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Calculated example: dividend inside bracket 1

Input: a dividend from the BV of € 60.000, no dividendbelasting withheld, no deductible costs.

Step 1: the boundary in the 2027 future text is € 68.843, so the whole € 60.000 falls in bracket 1.

Step 2: tax is € 60.000 × 24,5% = € 14.700,00.

The outcome follows the pre-indexation text. A higher boundary from the December 2026 regulation does not change this outcome for an amount of € 60.000.

### Calculated example: dividend above bracket 1

Input: a dividend of € 80.000.

Step 1: the tax at the top of bracket 1 is € 16.866 (art. 2.12, column III, text per 1 January 2027).

Step 2: the part above the boundary is € 80.000 − € 68.843 = € 11.157.

Step 3: bracket 2 tax is € 11.157 × 31% = € 3.458,67.

Step 4: total is € 16.866 + € 3.458,67 = € 20.324,67, rounded to € 20.325.

The € 16.866 in the table is a rounded figure, so the sum is approximate. If the proposed 29,2% rate is enacted, step 3 becomes € 11.157 × 29,2% = € 3.257,84, rounded down, and the total becomes € 20.123,84.

### Calculated example: indexation of the bracket boundary under the proposed multiplier

Input: the proposed multiplier of 1,01248 from Belastingplan 2027 on the pre-indexation boundary of € 68.843.

Step 1: € 68.843 × 0,01248 = € 859,16.

Step 2: € 68.843 + € 859,16 = € 69.702,16, rounded down to € 69.702.

Secondary sources give € 69.607 and € 69.703. The outcome € 69.702 fits the second figure, not the first. The December 2026 regulation sets the final figure.

### Official example: FISIN 2026 excessief lenen step for 2027

FISIN 2026 gives the 2027 step as follows: 2027 drempel € 650.000, repayment € 125.000, negative fictief regulier voordeel of € 125.000. This follows the € 500.000 rule and would change if the phase-down is enacted. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Calculated example: excessief lenen with the € 500.000 maximum

Input: debts to the BV at 31 December 2027 of € 600.000 at nominal value, no amounts taxed earlier, no excluded eigenwoningschuld, no debts of verbonden personen.

Step 1: the maximum is € 500.000 plus nil taxed earlier = € 500.000.

Step 2: the positive fictief regulier voordeel is € 600.000 − € 500.000 = € 100.000, taxed in box 2.

Step 3: in 2028 the maximum becomes € 500.000 + € 100.000 = € 600.000. If the debts are € 450.000 at 31 December 2028, the shortfall is € 600.000 − € 450.000 = € 150.000, capped at the € 100.000 taxed earlier and not yet reversed.

Step 4: the negative fictief regulier voordeel is € 100.000 and is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years.

The 2027 future text is not yet in force. The proposed phase-down would change the 2027 maximum.

### Calculated example: belastingkorting on an unoffset loss

Input: an unoffset verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang of € 20.000, definitively established, and no aanmerkelijk belang in the year and the year before.

Step 1: the percentage of art. 4.53 lid 2 is the first-row percentage of the art. 2.12 table, 24,5% in the 2027 future text.

Step 2: the korting is € 20.000 × 24,5% = € 4.900,00.

The 26,9% printed in FISIN 2026 is a stale handbook figure, see section 7. The proposed 29,2% second-bracket rate would not change this outcome.

### Calculated example: gebruikelijk loon

Input: the comparable loon is € 50.000, the highest loon of the employees is € 45.000 and the fixed amount in the 2027 future text is € 58.000.

Step 1: the norm is the highest of the three amounts, € 58.000.

Step 2: if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that € 58.000 exceeds the comparable loon of € 50.000, the comparable loon of € 50.000 applies.

The amount is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel. The € 58.000 is the pre-indexation figure and is replaced at the start of 2027 by ministerial regulation.

### Calculated example: forfaitair voordeel for a vbi

Input: shares in a vbi with a value of € 100.000 on 1 January 2027, held for the whole year, and other reguliere voordelen from those shares of € 2.000.

Step 1: the 6% amount is € 100.000 × 6% = € 6.000,00.

Step 2: the actual reguliere voordelen are € 2.000,00. The total amount taxed from the vbi is the higher of the 6% amount and the actual reguliere voordelen, so € 6.000,00 is taxed.

The outcome is not below nil and needs no time apportionment because the shares were held for the whole year.

### Calculated example: the 5% test

Input: a company with 100 issued shares.

The taxpayer holds 5 shares alone, which is 5%, so there is an aanmerkelijk belang under art. 4.6 sub a.

If the taxpayer holds 3 shares and the fiscal partner 3 shares, the two together hold 6%, so the test is also met.

### Calculated example: partner split of box 2 income

Input: box 2 income of € 100.000 in 2027, fiscal partnership for the whole year, division 50/50.

Step 1: each partner applies his own first bracket. Partner 1: € 50.000 × 24,5% = € 12.250,00. Partner 2: € 50.000 × 24,5% = € 12.250,00.

Step 2: total € 12.250,00 + € 12.250,00 = € 24.500,00.

If the whole € 100.000 were allocated to one partner: the part above the boundary is € 100.000 − € 68.843 = € 31.157. Tax on that part is € 31.157 × 31% = € 9.658,67. The total is € 16.866 + € 9.658,67 = € 26.524,67. The € 16.866 is the rounded table figure, so this total is approximate.

No primary page states a summed two-partner first-bracket amount. The totals above are the arithmetic of two or one applications of the € 68.843 boundary.

- Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14). 2026: 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). No change.
- ODV marktrente. 2026: 2,593%, set by Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025, which replaced "2,602%" by "2,593%" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). 2027: not published on 2026-10-11, to be set by the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/).
- New 2027 proposal. Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 (Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from the art. 4.12a inherited-belang facility and to set the verkrijgingsprijs at the value in het economisch verkeer when a company moves its seat to the Netherlands, both from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record every dividend and other profit distribution from the BV with the date, the amount and the dividendbelasting withheld. Dividendbelasting is not deducted from the box 2 income but credited as voorheffing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Keep the verkrijgingsprijs per share. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs, selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs, and at an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Keep the balance of all debts to the company at 31 December, at nominal value. Debts at several companies are added, receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, and the drempel "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)". Keep the amounts taxed earlier in a separate record (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- The loan stays a loan in full. The company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividendbelasting on the part taxed as box 2 income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 loon (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang is set by beschikking together with the assessment and shown separately on it, open to objection. The offset runs against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose, and losses that arose in 2018 or earlier get the longer 9-year term (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).
- The belastingkorting request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang. The inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking open to objection. The korting reduces the box 1 tax of that year and of the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting).
- A remaining pensioen in eigen beheer keeps the old rules. The company must index the accrued rights every year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision every year actuarially, and not indexing can be seen as a form of prijsgeven with possible fiscal consequences. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking in box 1 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The ODV is increased each year by the market rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- The taxpayer declares the box 2 income in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting and must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked material gives no other filing deadline for box 2 income.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Indexed 2027 bracket 1 boundary: unknown and UNVERIFIED. Two secondary sources conflict, € 69.607 (ABN AMRO, https://financialfocus.abnamro.nl/fiscaliteiten/prinsjesdag-2026-negen-fiscale-aandachtspunten-voor-de-dga/) against € 69.703 (holdwise.nl, https://holdwise.nl/kennisbank/box-2-tarief-2027). The Belastingplan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 for the art. 10.1 amounts is proposed and not adopted (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Section 4 works the multiplication out and the outcome about € 69.702 fits the higher figure. The December 2026 ministerial regulation settles it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).
- The 29,2% second-bracket rate for 4 years from 2027 is proposed in the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text was found on 2026-10-11, the letter does not name the legislative vehicle, and it is open whether the rate enters a nota van wijziging or a separate bill and whether it is adopted before 1 January 2027 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The Tweede Kamer dossier and the Staatsblad settle it.
- The excessief lenen phase-down to € 100.000 in five steps of € 80.000 from 2027 is proposed in the same letter (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). The per-peildatum amounts and the legislative vehicle are open, and the letter does not print them (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). A bill text and the Staatsblad settle it.
- Excessief lenen maximum at 31 December 2027 under the letter, € 420.000: proposed and UNVERIFIED. The figure is arithmetic from the letter (€ 500.000 minus one step of € 80.000) and is stated by secondary sources, the letter itself does not print € 420.000 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). A bill text and the Staatsblad settle it.
- Indexed 2027 gebruikelijk loon amount: not published. It depends on the tabelcorrectiefactor regulation, and the Belastingplan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 does not name art. 12a Wet LB 1964 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).
- ODV marktrente for 2027: not published. The 2026 table runs to October 2026 with 2,871%, and the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation set the 2027 rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/).
- Summed two-partner first bracket: UNVERIFIED. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount. Each partner applies his own first bracket of € 68.843, and section 4 shows the arithmetic of a 50/50 division (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Doorschuiving list for gift, inheritance and emigration: UNVERIFIED. The list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The APV loss deferral term of 10 years is Verified, with offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Non-resident statement about a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht: UNVERIFIED. It was not found on the fetched Belastingdienst pages. Art. 7.5 is Verified for the base of a non-resident, an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands-established company that is not part of an enterprise (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Resolved conflict, belastingkorting percentage: FISIN 2026 states "De korting is 26,9%" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) against art. 4.53 lid 2, which refers to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", 24,5%, with the live page giving € 4.900 for a loss of € 20.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Settled at 24,5%, the handbook figure is stale.
- Resolved conflict, placement of the excessief lenen fiction: art. 4.12a against art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f. Settled at art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c, while art. 4.12a carries the inherited-belang facility (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html).
- Resolved conflict, art. 4.6 "unchanged 2020 to 2027" against the year texts. Settled at sub e from the 2022 text and sub f from 2025 with retroactive effect (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html).
- Resolved conflict, "no box 2 change proposed for 2027" against the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 and the Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill. Settled, box 2 changes are proposed (29,2% second bracket, excessief lenen phase-down, two technical measures), and none is adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).
- Resolved conflict, Belastingplan 2023 against Belastingplan 2024 as the source of the 2024 brackets. Both are right for different parts. Belastingplan 2023 introduced the second bracket, Belastingplan 2024 (amendement Van der Lee c.s.) set its rate at 33% (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).
- Resolved conflict, PEB start date of 1 April 2017 against 1 July 2017. The measures apply from 1 April 2017 (consolidated BWBR0039380 "Geldend van 01-04-2017", Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen), the coulance period ran to 30 June 2017 and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which is the date the Belastingdienst page uses for the end of accrual (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html). The published Stb. 2017, 115 Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under the Wet raadgevend referendum (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).
