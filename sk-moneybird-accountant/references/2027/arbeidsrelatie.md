## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Tax year 2027 is the second calendar year in which the normal enforcement rules for arbeidsrelaties apply, after the handhavingsmoratorium (enforcement moratorium) ended on 1 January 2025. The Wet DBA (the law on assessing work relationships) and the case law on loondienst (employment) still decide whether work is an arbeidsovereenkomst (employment contract). The VBAR (the act on a legal presumption of an employment contract based on an hourly rate) is in force from 31 December 2026, so its low hourly rate presumption applies throughout 2027 at about € 38. On 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the DBA zachte landing (soft landing) lapse, and the Belastingdienst ambition is to move to regular handhaving, but no separate 2027 handhavingsplan was published on 2026-10-11. The BAZ bill and the Zelfstandigenwet (Self-Employed Act) are proposed and are not in force in 2027.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

Applies to every opdrachtgever (client) and opdrachtnemer (contractor) in the Netherlands. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (the previous declaration). The law did not create a separate test. The qualification follows the elements of loondienst and the facts of the case. Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

One correction on a detail. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking (deemed employment) for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer (entrepreneur) for income tax or VAT. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). No filing step follows from the law itself.

### 3.2 Loondienst test

Loondienst has three elements. The possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid, and pay for the work. The courts weigh, among others, the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

Case law fills this in. HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (the parties' intention). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), gives an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding and ondernemerschap, with vrije vervanging (free replacement) not as an absolute element. HR Uber (prejudiciële vragen, preliminary questions) holds that there is no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten (criteria), so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving (enforcement). Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Treat that date as UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

Filing step: none. The test decides the qualification, and the qualification decides whether the pay belongs in the loonaangifte (payroll return) of the opdrachtgever or in the opdrachtnemer's own profit. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### 3.3 Opt-out fictieve dienstbetrekking

Applies to thuiswerkers (home workers), gelijkgestelden (persons treated as employees) and artiesten (artists). The loondienst page also lists aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden, stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair (intermediary) as fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. Gelijkgestelden usually work 2 or more days a week for the same client and for at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. Only thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can opt out. Condition: the exclusion is in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. Effect: the opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefits), WIA (disability benefits) and WW (unemployment benefits). Sources: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst) and [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). Interaction: the opt-out removes only the fictieve dienstbetrekking. The ordinary loondienst test in 3.2 still applies. Deadline: before the first payment, not after.

### 3.4 Enforcement from 1 January 2025

Applies to opdrachtgevers and opdrachtnemers where schijnzelfstandigheid (false self-employment) is possible. Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing (instruction): reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige (self-employed person), or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting (correction obligation) applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend (acting in bad faith) means opzettelijk (intentionally) creating or continuing a situation of evident schijnzelfstandigheid while knowing or being able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (additional payroll tax assessments) and boetes. The second restriction of the moratorium exception and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

The webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie is a tool for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It gives three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon, or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active, with no rechtskracht (legal effect) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring (client declaration) as one outcome. Sources: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Vooroverleg (advance consultation) is available. The Belastingdienst takes a standpoint on a complete verzoek vooroverleg. The standpoint gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn (target term) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

Filing step: the parties themselves have no filing step under this item. The opdrachtgever may have to correct the loonaangifte.

### 3.5 Ingroeimodel 2027

Applies to the lookback period on corrections. In 2027 the ingroeimodel (phase-in model) continues. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date that was not followed. The full 5-year lookback returns only in 2030. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Interaction: this limit sits in front of the normal naheffingstermijnen for loonheffingen. It does not limit a correction for a pre-2025 aanwijzing that was not followed.

The richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB (guidelines on the knock-on effect to income tax and VAT) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie (source qualification). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Filing step: none. The opdrachtnemer's own returns stay in the regular IH or OB supervision.

### 3.6 DBA boetebeleid 2027

Applies to the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. This is enforcement policy, not statute. 2025: no verzuimboetes (late-filing penalties) and no vergrijpboetes (penalties for intent or gross negligence) for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties, per Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266. For correcties over 2025 this stays so. 2026: no verzuimboetes, while vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. 2027: the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse on 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2027 verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek (business visit) start. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Interaction: the change is in the boete policy and the start of a control, not in the correction lookback in 3.5. Deadline: none.

### 3.7 Regular handhaving

Applies to the organisation of the Belastingdienst enforcement of arbeidsrelaties. The ambition is that from 1 January 2027 the enforcement is fully part of regular handhaving, with no separate plan. This is stated as an ambition, so the status is proposed. The tranche 2025 plan had stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Interaction: if the ambition is met, the separate DBA reporting line ends. No confirmed date beyond the ambition is in the checked sources.

### 3.8 Separate 2027 handhavingsplan

Applies to anyone who follows the enforcement policy for 2027. A separate handhavingsplan for 2027 was not published on 2026-10-11. The status is unknown and the check is UNVERIFIED. The handhaving page says nothing about 2027. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Interaction: the 2027 rules in this file come from the Handhavingsplan 2026, which is the last plan in the checked material. What would settle it: a new plan or an update of the handhaving page. See section 7.

### 3.9 Modelovereenkomsten, end of assessment

Applies to opdrachtgevers and opdrachtnemers who use a modelovereenkomst (model contract). Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Reason given: modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The tranche 2025 plan adds that the agreements are honoured to end 2029 after Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275, on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Interaction with 3.10: the agreement's own end date is not decisive. Deadline: 31 December 2029.

### 3.10 Modelovereenkomsten, condition

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described in it. If the actual work differs, the modelovereenkomst does not protect. Source: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging (free replacement) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Interaction: the modelovereenkomst is evidence for the qualification, not a guarantee. The ordinary loondienst test in 3.2 stays in place. Filing step: keep the approved agreement and evidence of the actual way of working in the administration, see section 6.

### 3.11 VBAR entry into force

Applies to work relationships where someone works for another for pay. The VBAR is bill 36 783. The original bill had an internet consultation from 6 October 2023 with an intended start on 1 July 2025. It went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024, and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend (submitted) on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. The nota van wijziging (memorandum of amendment) of 10 March 2026, 36 783, nr. 6, removed the verduidelijking part. The Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026, with all parties except FVD in favour. The Eerste Kamer adopted it on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (standing vote). The Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, was published on 29 June 2026. The act is in force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026. That date was chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan (Recovery and Resilience Plan) milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. So the VBAR applies throughout 2027. The status at the check date is adopted-not-yet-in-force, because the status changes on 31 December 2026. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

### 3.12 VBAR statutory rule

Applies to someone who works for another for pay. New art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling (ministerial regulation). Effect: the werkende (worker) can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. The rule is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). Interaction: the ordinary loondienst test in 3.2 still decides a case where the presumption is rebutted or does not apply. The amount is € 36 in the statutory text and is indexed later, see 3.13 and 3.14.

### 3.13 VBAR hourly amount, base and indexation

The € 36 in the statutory text is built as follows. 120% of the WML (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, the statutory minimum wage) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag (surcharge) for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2025. That rounds up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/), [WML 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025) and [WML 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026). The calculation steps are written out in section 4.

### 3.14 VBAR hourly amount 2027

### 3.16 BAZ status

Applies to self-employed persons without employee insurance. Bill 36 912 is the BAZ. It was ingediend (submitted) on 24 March 2026. A technische briefing (technical briefing) was held on 13 May 2026. ### 3.18 Zelfstandigenwet

Applies to future qualification rules for self-employed persons. The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026. The beoogde inwerkingtreding (intended entry into force) is January 2028. The proposal has a zelfstandigentoets (self-employed test) with self-invoicing, KVK registration, number of opdrachtgevers, and provisions against ondernemersrisico's. ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for this topic was found in the checked 2027 material, so every example below is marked `Calculated example`.

**Example 1, `Calculated example`: the VBAR amounts and the unsettled 2027 rate test.**
Rule: art. 7:610aa BW, in force from 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, so it applies throughout 2027. The werkende is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst when the pay is at most the applicable hourly amount. Lid 2 indexes the amount with the WML, rounded up from the unrounded amount. The first applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2. When the presumption applies, the werkende can invoke it and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst.
Amounts in the checked material: € 36 (the statutory base text, peildatum 1 January 2025) and € 38 (the indexed amount, peildatum 1 January 2026).
Input: invented rates. A ZZP works for a bv.
- € 34 per hour: € 34 is at most € 36 and at most € 38.
- € 37 per hour: € 37 is more than € 36 and at most € 38.
- € 40 per hour: € 40 is more than € 36 and more than € 38.
The lines are arithmetic against the two stated amounts. They are not a settled 2027 outcome, because the applicable 2027 amount is not settled. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 was not found, and the 2027 amount is UNVERIFIED, about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. In particular, the result at € 40 cannot be concluded, since the applicable 2027 amount is not settled. The same reservation applies at € 34 and € 37.
Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html), [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

**Example 2, `Calculated example`: the VBAR hourly amount by the MvT method.**
Inputs from the checked material: WML € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025 and € 14,71 per hour at 1 January 2026.
Step 1: 120% of the WML: € 14,06 × 1,20 = € 16,87 (rounded down).
Step 2: the 25% opslag for AOV and pension and the 15% opslag for general costs together are 40%, so × 1,40: € 16,87 × 1,40 = € 23,62 (rounded).
Step 3: × 1,50 for non-billable hours: € 23,62 × 1,50 = € 35,43. This is the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2025, excl. btw.
Step 4: € 35,43 becomes € 36,00 (rounded up to whole euros).
Step 5: for peildatum 1 January 2026: € 14,71 × 1,20 = € 17,65 (rounded down).
Step 6: € 17,65 × 1,40 = € 24,71.
Step 7: € 24,71 × 1,50 = € 37,07 (rounded). The checked material gives about € 37,07 for this peildatum.
Step 8: € 37,07 becomes € 38,00 (rounded up to whole euros).
Check: every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The € 36 and € 38 match the amounts stated in the checked material. The exact 2027 amount is UNVERIFIED, about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027.
Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [minimumloon 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025), [minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

**Example 3, `Calculated example`: the ingroeimodel lookback in a 2027 correction.**
Inputs: the relationship is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Neither exception applies: no kwaadwillendheid, and no aanwijzing given before 1 January 2025 that was not followed. The Belastingdienst corrects in 2027.
Step 1: the correction period starts on 1 January 2025.
Step 2: the correction can cover the calendar years 2025, 2026 and 2027 up to the correction date. That is 3 calendar years.
Step 3: a full 5-year lookback from 2027 would reach 2022. Corrections are blocked before 1 January 2025, so 2022, 2023 and 2024 are outside the correction period.
Step 4: only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst go back the full 5 years. Check: 2030 minus 5 = 2025, so from 2030 the full lookback reaches 2025.
Sources: [Handhavingsplan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

**Example 4, `Calculated example`: the boetebeleid by year for the same relationship.**
Input: the same relationship, no kwaadwillendheid.
- Correcties over 2025: no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. This stays so.
- Correcties over 2026: no verzuimboetes. A vergrijpboete is possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026.
- Correcties over 2027: the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse on 1 January 2027. Verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start.
The period from 1 January 2025 to 1 January 2027 is 24 months, or 2 years. This is enforcement policy, not statute.
Sources: [Handhavingsplan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

**Example 5, `Calculated example`: the modelovereenkomst end date.**
Input: an approved modelovereenkomst that was valid on 6 September 2024 and states that it is valid until 31 December 2027.
Step 1: the stated end date is 31 December 2027.
Step 2: the checked rule allows use up to and including 31 December 2029, even though the agreement states another validity date.
Step 3: the extra period is 2028 and 2029, 2 years.
Condition: the parties must work as described. If not, the modelovereenkomst gives no protection.
Sources: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer), [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

**Example 6, `Calculated example`: the Zelfstandigenwet timing.**

Step 1: in 2027 the zelfstandigentoets and werkrelatietoets are not in force.
Step 2: the period from 1 January 2027 to 1 January 2028 is 12 months.
Step 3: a relationship in 2027 cannot rely on the veilige haven.
Source: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the facts that decide the qualification for each engagement. The contract, any modelovereenkomst, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, whether the work must be done personally, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. These are the weighing factors of the loondienst test. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Not being in loondienst does not by itself make the income winst uit onderneming (business profit) for income tax or a business for VAT. Check the qualification separately for each box. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The checked sources for this topic do not state the exact return line. They state only that not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT.
- Record the hourly rate for each engagement. Under art. 7:610aa BW a rate of at most € 36 per hour, or the indexed amount, gives the werkende a rechtsvermoeden (legal presumption). The opdrachtgever then must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html) and [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The first-application regulation is not published, see section 7.
- If a modelovereenkomst is used, keep the approved agreement and evidence that the parties work exactly as described. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used to and including 31 December 2029. Sources: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- If the qualification is a dienstbetrekking, the pay goes into the loonaangifte of the opdrachtgever. Since 1 January 2025 there is no aanwijzing step and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. From 1 January 2027 verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Where a bv is the opdrachtgever, the same Wet DBA test and the VBAR presumption apply. The VBAR does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).
- Keep the follow-up of any standpunt or vooroverleg in the file. Vooroverleg gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie. Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Bewaarplicht (record-keeping duty): AWR art. 52 lid 2 covers natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (income from other activities). Keep the gegevensdragers for seven years under AWR art. 52 lid 4. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table gives 7 years for basisgegevens, 10 years for onroerende zaken (immovable property) and One Stop Shop data, and 7 years or shorter for other data by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- The checked 2027 sources state no separate arbeidsrelatie filing deadline. For completeness, the income tax return for tax year 2026, filed in 2027, was not published on 2026-10-11 and is usually due 1 May 2027. Source: [Belastingdienst aangifte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The 2026 plan text settles it.
- Settled conflict, VBAR € 36 against € 38: both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, from unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 of € 14,71. Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). No open point remains for 2027 beyond the indexation in section 3.14.
- Settled conflict, BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026: both are true. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May, the verslag was published on 29 May. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Correction, Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA: it is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for some fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force on 1 May 2016. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
