## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the investment deductions for 2027, the kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction), the energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction), the milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction), the Vamil (random depreciation for designated environmental assets), the desinvesteringsbijtelling (disposal clawback), ordinary depreciation including the bodemwaarde (floor value) for buildings, and the willekeurige afschrijving (random depreciation) for starters. The year is a mix: the text of the Wet IB 2001 that is valid on 1 January 2027 has been published and still prints the 2026 amounts before indexation, bill 37 022 was submitted on Prinsjesdag, its kamerstuk was published on 29 September 2026 and it was not adopted on 2026-10-11, and the December 2026 indexation regulation with the final 2027 amounts is not yet published. The bill proposes a higher EIA percentage, a multiplier of 1,01248 on the art. 10.1 amounts and the abolition of the starters scheme per 2028. Everything proposed or not yet published is marked in section 2 and collected in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 KIA base table in the 2027 text, before indexation

The kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction) is a per-onderneming deduction, claimed by choice in the aangifte (tax return), on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) in the calendar year. The 2027 text of art. 3.41 lid 2 has five bands. Nil up to € 2.900. 28% of the investment up to € 71.683. A fixed € 20.072 up to € 132.746. Then € 20.072 minus 7,56% of the part above € 132.746, up to € 398.236. Nil above € 398.236. Those amounts are the 2026 amounts before the indexation of art. 10.1, so the final 2027 amounts are not yet known (section 7). The Belastingdienst year pages phrase the same window as a range the taxpayer must invest in, for 2023 as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" and for 2026 as "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236". The deduction is taken once on the total investment in the calendar year and not per asset, while the per-asset minimum of 3.3 still applies to every asset.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026

### 3.2 KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership, for example a vof or a maatschap) the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by that sum. This is art. 3.41 lid 3. The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot (partner) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst (profit share), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently. The same article is in the 2027 text, so the method holds for 2027.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia

### 3.3 KIA per-asset minimum and the exclusions of art. 3.45

Every asset must have an investment amount of at least € 450 to count for the KIA. That is art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b in the 2027 text. Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for certain uses from every investeringsaftrek, so from the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. The list is use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (a tax treaty rule) (a), gronden (land) including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (soil improvement) (b), woonhuizen (dwellings) and woonschepen (houseboats) (c), personenauto's (passenger cars) as defined in art. 3 of the Wet BPM 1992 (d), vaartuigen (vessels) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), effecten (securities), vorderingen (receivables), goodwill, vergunningen (permits), ontheffingen (exemptions) and concessies (concessions) (f), and dieren (animals) (g). Lid 2 adds two KIA-only exclusions. Assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (b). Lid 3 makes the MIA more generous, art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply to the MIA, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn the MIA. Lid 4 adds two EIA and MIA exclusions, assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (b). Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag".

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.4 Excluded obligations, including assets bought from an own BV

Art. 3.46 gives no investeringsaftrek for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam (body, for example a BV) and that lichaam (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 under lid 2, which is the ontheffing (exemption) route. FISIN states it plainly. Buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek". This matters for a sole trader who also owns a BV.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.5 KIA 2027 indexation factor

Bill 37 022, Article LI lid 1, multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts at the start of 2027 by 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor (table correction factor). The bill was submitted on Prinsjesdag, its kamerstuk was published on 29 September 2026 and it was not adopted on 2026-10-11, so the factor is proposed. The bill text itself contains no change to art. 3.41, so the bands and the percentages of the KIA stay as they are and only the amounts move.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2

### 3.6 Share of the inflation correction applied

Only secondary sources state that 48% of an inflation correction of 2,6% is applied. Those documents were not readable to the fetch tool. The bill text itself states the factor 1,01248 and not the 48% share. So this item is UNVERIFIED and it is listed again in section 7.

Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1

### 3.7 Indexed 2027 KIA amounts

The indexed 2027 KIA amounts are not published. They wait for the December 2026 indexation regulation and for the vote on bill 37 022. For 2027 the unindexed 2027 text table of 3.1 is the only published version, and it carries status adopted-not-yet-in-force.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html

### 3.8 EIA percentage, enacted 2027 text

The energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction) is 40% in art. 3.42 lid 3 of the 2027 text. The EIA applies only to new assets, so to assets that are "niet eerder gebruikt" (not used before). A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. The EIA asset must be on the Energielijst. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Energielijst requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as voortbrengingskosten (costs of own production). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km, see 3.14. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset. The EIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15. The maximum investment amount for the EIA is € 153.000.000, see 3.10. Whether the rate stays at 40% or becomes 45,5% depends on the adoption of bill 37 022, see 3.9 and section 7.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.9 EIA percentage, bill 37 022

Article I part F of bill 37 022 replaces "«40 percent»" with "«45,5 percent»" from 1 January 2027. The status is proposed, not adopted on 2026-10-11. The Rijksoverheid news item of 15 September 2026 says "van 40% naar 45%". The bill text is the legal proposal, and the difference is a conflict that only the adopted law settles. It is listed in section 7.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien

### 3.10 EIA and MIA maximum investment amount

The maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 153.000.000 in art. 3.42 lid 4 of the 2027 text, before indexation. The bill multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts, so the final maximum waits for the December 2026 regulation. Separately, RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum, see 3.16. From 2026 the EIA page adds the investments in an eenmanszaak and in a samenwerkingsverband together for the EIA maximum, and the checked 2027 material states no change to that rule.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia

### 3.11 MIA percentages

The milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction) has three categorieën. From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I (45%), codes starting with A or D to categorie II (36%) and codes starting with B or E to categorie III (27%). The 2027 text of art. 3.42a lid 3 has 45%, 36% and 27%, and bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.42a. The MIA asset must be on the Milieulijst. Only new assets qualify, with the same newness conditions as the EIA, see 3.8. A MIA investment has a minimum of € 2.500 per asset, see 3.12. No MIA is given on costs that already received the EIA, and the Vamil remains possible for those costs. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset, while the MIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The MIA must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15. The checked sources give no 2027 budget for the MIA.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.12 EIA and MIA per-asset minimum

An asset with an investment amount below € 2.500 does not qualify for the EIA or the MIA. That is art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b in the 2027 text. RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag". The per-asset amount is the investment amount per bedrijfsmiddel and not the total of the year.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.13 Vamil percentage

Art. 3.31 lid 1 says that the costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven" (may be depreciated at will for 75 percent by the taxpayer), and that the rest follows the normal schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit for buildings, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that were not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil. RVO states that the total tax benefit of the MIA and the Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (can add up to more than 14% of the investment amount net). The asset must be on the Milieulijst and must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.14 RVO newness test for a vehicle

Only new assets qualify for the EIA and the MIA, so an asset that was never used before. RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as voortbrengingskosten.

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.15 RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

The investment must be reported to the Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO) within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order for aanschafkosten (acquisition costs). For voortbrengingskosten the report is due within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst that is valid on the signing or order date applies. FISIN 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)."

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.16 MIA and Vamil maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for the MIA and the Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. The RVO page states no year, so it is unknown which years the cap covers for 2027. It is a condition of the aanwijzing (designation) and stands next to the statutory maximum of € 153.000.000 of 3.10. The Milieulijst regulation of each year would settle the years and any lower maximum. This item is UNVERIFIED and is listed again in section 7.

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4

### 3.17 Payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Art. 3.44 lid 1 applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments that were made, and the excess follows in later years. Lid 2 states that at staking (cessation) of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.44, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.18 Ordinary depreciation caps

Art. 3.30 lid 1 says that the yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten (acquisition or production costs) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2 caps the yearly depreciation at 10% of the cost for goodwill and at 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for other bedrijfsmiddelen. Lid 3 allows voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) to be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging. Lid 4 allows voorwerpen van geringe waarde (low-value items) that are normally treated as current costs to be written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30

### 3.19 Bodemwaarde for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1 allows depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde (floor value), and the depreciation is at most the difference. Lid 2 treats a building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden (appurtenances) as one bedrijfsmiddel, with detachable werktuigen (machines) as separate assets. Lid 3 sets one bodemwaarde, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" (the WOZ value of the building). That has been the rule from 2024, while 2020 to 2023 used the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (investment building) and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another building. Lid 4 says the WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking (decision) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text also has lid 5 to 10 on a co-owned building pro rata, ground owned by an unrelated party that reduces the WOZ-waarde, and related persons and verbonden lichaam (affiliated bodies). Bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.30a.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a

### 3.20 Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold

Art. 3.47 lid 1 says that if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage that was taken is added to profit. The 2027 text has € 2.900 as the base amount. The indexed amount is unknown until the December 2026 regulation, and the bill multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts, so the final threshold is not published yet. For 2026 the threshold was € 2.900.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

### 3.21 Desinvesteringsbijtelling window and what counts as a disposal

Lid 2 limits the bijtelling to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken. Lid 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the onderneming (a), a change of its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). Lid 4 counts the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair value) as the transfer price in those cases. Lid 5 counts the undoing of an investment, or the receipt of a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding (reduction, refund or compensation), as a disposal for that amount. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6. The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA and not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of part or all of the onderneming within the term. Bill 37 022 would drop ", onder 2°" from art. 3.47 lid 3 onderdeel c from 1 January 2029, if it is adopted.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article III part E

### 3.22 Desinvestering undo test

Lid 6 defines the undoing of an investment to include not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply this lid.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 6 and lid 7, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

### 3.23 Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap

The willekeurige afschrijving (random depreciation) for starters rests on art. 3.34 of the Wet IB 2001, which allows willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship. URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek (increased self-employed deduction) of art. 3.76 lid 3, the startersaftrek, applies. Lid 2 also covers the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst als ondernemer (profit as an entrepreneur). Art. 8 allows the willekeurige afschrijving "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground. For 2027 that maximum is the top of the 2027 KIA table, € 398.236 before indexation, and the final amount follows the indexed KIA table. The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is open to an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for the investeringsaftrek count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Herwaardering (revaluation) to the normal book value, with the difference as winst, is required within 5 years, or 10 years for zeeschepen (sea-going vessels), if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf (forestry business). The cap rests on reading the art. 8 cross-reference. No primary page prints the cap as a number.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 and art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer

### 3.24 Starters willekeurige afschrijving period

URWA art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10. The period is the window in which the revaluation rule of 3.23 can be triggered.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 9 and art. 12

### 3.25 Starters willekeurige afschrijving abolition

The Rijksoverheid news item of 15 September 2026 states that the starters scheme is abolished per 2028. The status is proposed and the scheme is still available in 2027. The URWA is a ministerial regulation, and the fetched bill text does not name the scheme. Bill 37 022 sets the art. 3.76 lid 3 startersaftrek amount to € 10 under Article I part G, and the fetched summary gave its entry date inconsistently, so the ondernemersaftrek check owns that date. URWA art. 7 only needs the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek to apply, so a € 10 startersaftrek would keep the starters scheme available in 2027.

Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part G, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7

### 3.26 Temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023

URWA art. 13 lid 1 covers assets not used before for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in calendar year 2023 and which the taxpayer took into use before 1 January 2026. Lid 2 excludes gebouwen, schepen, vliegtuigen, bromfietsen, motorrijwielen and personenauto's, except for beroepsvervoer over de weg (professional road transport) or 0 gram CO2 per km, and also immateriële activa, dieren, assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, and assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Lid 3 moves the 1 January 2026 date by the days that special circumstances justify. Art. 14 allows at most 50% of the costs, once, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15 ends the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 on 31 December 2025. The Belastingdienst adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year. IB and Vpb both. In 2027 no new claim can arise, because the use deadline has passed.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 13 to 15, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples were found for 2027, so every example below is a `Calculated example`. All inputs are invented round amounts. Every calculation uses the 2027 base figures before indexation, because the indexed 2027 KIA table, the indexed desinvestering threshold and the indexed EIA and MIA maximum were not yet published.

### Calculated example, KIA on a total investment of € 60.000

Input: the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2027 is € 60.000,00, and every asset is at least € 450.

1. Compare with the 2027 text table of art. 3.41 lid 2. € 60.000,00 is above € 2.900 and below € 71.683, so the 28% band applies.
2. 28% of € 60.000,00 is 60.000,00 × 28 / 100 = € 16.800,00.
3. The KIA is € 16.800,00.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2

### Calculated example, KIA on a total investment of € 200.000

Input: the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2027 is € 200.000,00.

1. € 200.000,00 is above € 132.746 and below € 398.236, so the fourth band applies.
2. The part above € 132.746 is 200.000,00 - 132.746,00 = € 67.254,00.
3. 7,56% of € 67.254,00 is 67.254,00 × 7,56 / 100 = 508.440,24 / 100 = € 5.084,4024, rounded to € 5.084,40.
4. The KIA is 20.072,00 - 5.084,40 = € 14.987,60.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2

### Calculated example, KIA below the first threshold and above the top

1. Total investment € 2.000,00. That is in the first band, nil up to € 2.900, so the KIA is € 0,00. Each asset must also be at least € 450.
2. Total investment € 400.000,00. That is above the top of € 398.236, so the KIA is € 0,00.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2 and art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b

### Calculated example, KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

Input: the partnership investments are € 80.000,00 and the own investments of the taxpayer are € 20.000,00.

1. The sum is 80.000,00 + 20.000,00 = € 100.000,00.
2. The table on € 100.000,00 gives the fixed € 20.072,00, because € 100.000,00 is above € 71.683 and below € 132.746.
3. The own share is 20.000,00 / 100.000,00 = 0,20.
4. The KIA for this taxpayer is 20.072,00 × 0,20 = € 4.014,40.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia

### Calculated example, EIA on a new Energielijst asset of € 100.000

Input: a new asset on the Energielijst, investment € 100.000,00.

1. € 100.000,00 is above the € 2.500 EIA per-asset minimum and below the € 153.000.000 maximum, so neither limit binds.
2. Under the enacted 2027 text the EIA is 40%, so 100.000,00 × 40 / 100 = € 40.000,00.
3. Under bill 37 022 the EIA would be 45,5%, so 100.000,00 × 45,5 / 100 = € 45.500,00.
4. The KIA on the same € 100.000,00 is the fixed € 20.072,00, because € 100.000,00 is above € 71.683 and below € 132.746.
5. The EIA and the KIA may be combined, so the total deduction is 40.000,00 + 20.072,00 = € 60.072,00 under the enacted text, or 45.500,00 + 20.072,00 = € 65.572,00 under the bill.
6. The MIA may not be combined with the EIA for this asset.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3 and art. 3.41 lid 2, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia

### Calculated example, MIA and Vamil on a Milieulijst asset of € 50.000

Input: a new asset with a Milieulijst code starting with F, so categorie I and 75% Vamil.

1. The investment of € 50.000,00 is above the € 2.500 per-asset minimum.
2. The MIA is 45%, so 50.000,00 × 45 / 100 = € 22.500,00.
3. The Vamil allows 75% willekeurige afschrijving, so 50.000,00 × 75 / 100 = € 37.500,00 may be depreciated at will.
4. The remaining 50.000,00 - 37.500,00 = € 12.500,00 follows the ordinary schedule. Under art. 3.30 lid 2 the ordinary yearly depreciation of another bedrijfsmiddel is at most 20% of the cost, so 50.000,00 × 20 / 100 = € 10.000,00 per year, and the remaining € 12.500,00 takes at least two years.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, art. 3.31 lid 1 and art. 3.30 lid 2, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html

### Calculated example, ordinary depreciation of a machine and of goodwill

Input: a machine and goodwill, each with a cost of € 30.000,00.

1. The machine is another bedrijfsmiddel, so the yearly cap is 20%: 30.000,00 × 20 / 100 = € 6.000,00 per year. The asset needs at least 30.000,00 / 6.000,00 = 5 years.
2. Goodwill, so the yearly cap is 10%: 30.000,00 × 10 / 100 = € 3.000,00 per year. The asset needs at least 30.000,00 / 3.000,00 = 10 years.
3. Voortbrengingskosten of an immaterieel activum may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging under art. 3.30 lid 3.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2 and lid 3

### Calculated example, bodemwaarde of a building

Input: an own-use building, WOZ-waarde for the calendar year € 400.000,00, book value on 1 January 2027 € 500.000,00.

1. The bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde of the building, so € 400.000,00 (art. 3.30a lid 3).
2. The book value is above the bodemwaarde, so depreciation is possible while that is the case (art. 3.30a lid 1).
3. The depreciation for the year is at most the difference, 500.000,00 - 400.000,00 = € 100.000,00 (art. 3.30a lid 1). The yearly cap of art. 3.30 lid 2 applies in addition.
4. The building, its parts, the ground under it and the aanhorigheden are one bedrijfsmiddel, detachable werktuigen are separate (art. 3.30a lid 2).

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 1, lid 2 and lid 3

### Calculated example, desinvesteringsbijtelling on a machine

Input: a machine bought in 2027 for € 50.000,00 and disposed of in the same calendar year 2027 for € 40.000,00. The machine is the only investment of 2027, and the KIA is taken at 28%.

1. The investment of € 50.000,00 falls in the 28% band of the KIA table, so the KIA taken is 50.000,00 × 28 / 100 = € 14.000,00. The investeringsaftrek percentage taken is 28%.
2. The transfer price of € 40.000,00 is above the € 2.900 base threshold of art. 3.47 lid 1.
3. The disposal is within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, so 2027 is inside the window of art. 3.47 lid 2.
4. The addition to profit is 40.000,00 × 28 / 100 = € 11.200,00.
5. Art. 3.47 lid 2 caps the addition at the investeringsaftrek actually taken on the machine, € 14.000,00. The addition of € 11.200,00 stays below that cap, so the addition is € 11.200,00.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1 and lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

### Calculated example, starters willekeurige afschrijving

Input: a starter with the startersaftrek in 2027 buys qualifying assets for € 60.000,00 in total in 2027.

1. The yearly maximum of URWA art. 8 is the top of the 2027 KIA table, € 398.236 before indexation.
2. € 60.000,00 does not go above € 398.236, so the willekeurige afschrijving is allowed on those costs.
3. The minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply to this scheme.
4. If the total had been € 450.000,00, the amount above the maximum would be 450.000,00 - 398.236,00 = € 51.764,00, and art. 8 would not cover that part.
5. If the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf, the asset must be revalued to the normal book value within 5 years (10 years for the zeeschepen of art. 10) and the difference is winst.

The scheme still applies in 2027. Abolition per 2028 is proposed. The final cap follows the indexed KIA table.

Basis: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 8 and art. 9, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record each investment with the date of the obligation or the voortbrengingskosten, the supplier invoice and the amount paid, because the investeringsaftrek is taken on the total investment in the calendar year and the KIA is claimed by choice in the aangifte. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 1 and lid 2
- If an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, take the investeringsaftrek only over the payments made and carry the excess to later years. At staking of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.44
- Keep the newness evidence for assets intended for the EIA or the MIA, because a second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify, and a vehicle counts as new only if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden
- Register the Energielijst or Milieulijst code of each asset. The code decides the deduction, with F or G as MIA categorie I (45%), A or D as categorie II (36%) and B or E as categorie III (27%), and codes starting with A, B, C or F for 75% Vamil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html
- File the RVO notification within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst or placing the order, and within 3 months after the quarter in which voortbrengingskosten were made. The Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers
- Watch the € 450 per-asset minimum for the KIA and the € 2.500 per-asset minimum for the EIA and MIA. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b and lid 4 onderdeel b
- An asset bought from a BV in which the taxpayer holds at least one third of the shares in principle gives no investeringsaftrek, with the ministerial ontheffing route as the exception. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek
- Keep each asset in the records for five years after the investment year, because a disposal within that window triggers the desinvesteringsbijtelling, rental counts for the KIA, a withdrawal or a change of use counts as a disposal, and the 25% payment test and the three-year use test apply. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling
- Keep the yearly WOZ-beschikking for each building, because the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde for the calendar year and depreciation is possible only while the book value is above it and at most up to the difference. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 1 and lid 4
- Keep a 10-year schedule for goodwill and a 5-year schedule for other bedrijfsmiddelen at the maximum yearly rates of 10% and 20%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2
- For a starter, keep the startersaftrek evidence and record the free choice of assets. The minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply to the willekeurige afschrijving, and a rented-out asset, an asset under a double-tax treaty or an asset used in the bosbedrijf must be revalued to the normal book value within 5 years, or 10 years for zeeschepen. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9 and art. 12, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer
- The temporary willekeurige afschrijving of 2023 applied to IB and Vpb both, but the use deadline of 1 January 2026 has passed, so no new claim can be booked in 2027. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- EIA 2027 conflict. The bill text says 45,5%, the Rijksoverheid news item says "van 40% naar 45%". Bill text: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F. News item: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien. The bill text is the legal proposal. The conflict is settled only by the adopted law.
- Indexed 2027 KIA amounts, desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold and EIA and MIA maximum. Not published. They wait for the December 2026 indexation regulation and the vote on bill 37 022. Until then the 2027 text amounts apply, with status adopted-not-yet-in-force. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1
- Share of the inflation correction applied. The 48% of 2,6% rests on secondary sources only, and those documents were not readable to the fetch tool. The bill text states the factor 1,01248. A readable kader document or the December 2026 indexation regulation would settle it. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1
- Two KIA claims could not be confirmed. First, that an earlier plan to slim the KIA was replaced by another measure. Second, a search snippet of the factsheet that suggested a lower KIA maximum. The fetched bill text contains no change to art. 3.41. The factsheet PDF exceeded the fetch limit and the CW 3.1 PDF was unreadable. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- MIA and Vamil maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year. The RVO page states no year, so it is unknown which years the cap covers for 2027. It is a condition of the aanwijzing, separate from the statutory € 153.000.000 of art. 3.42 lid 4. The Milieulijst regulation of each year would settle it. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden
- Startersaftrek in bill 37 022. The bill sets the art. 3.76 lid 3 amount to € 10 under Article I part G, and the fetched summary gave its entry date inconsistently, so the ondernemersaftrek check owns that date. URWA art. 7 only needs the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek to apply, so a € 10 startersaftrek keeps the starters scheme available in 2027. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part G, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7
- The rule that a medegerechtigde (co-entitled person) or a geldverstrekker (financier) cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages. UNVERIFIED. The pages checked are https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia
