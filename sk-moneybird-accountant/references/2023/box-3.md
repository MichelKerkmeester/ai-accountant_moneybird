# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers box 3 (sparen en beleggen, savings and investments) for the 2023 income tax year of a sole trader and for anyone who also owns a BV. For 2023 the Overbruggingswet box 3 replaced the old band method with a category method for everyone, with three category percentages, a rate of 32% and a heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free allowance) of € 57.000 per person. 2023 is a fully enacted year. The tegenbewijsregeling (counter-evidence scheme), which lets a taxpayer prove a lower actual return, takes statutory effect from 1 January 2023, but the law that introduced it entered into force on 19 July 2025.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

### System

2023 is the first year of the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3. 2020 to 2022 used the old band method with rendementsklassen I and II in art. 5.2 lid 1, from 1 January 2023 the category method applies to everyone. Art. II of the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, states "Deze wet treedt in werking met ingang van 1 januari 2023". https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

The Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box

For 2021 and 2022 the Belastingdienst computes box 3 income under the old method and the current method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment. "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen That most-favourable comparison is a 2021 and 2022 rule. The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 keeps covering 2017 to 2022, not 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html

Background. Hoge Raad 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The memorie van toelichting to the Wet rechtsherstel describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM": the forfaitaire system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Forfaits are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The MvT stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on actual return for the rechtsherstel but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html

### Box 3 tarief

The box 3 rate for 2023 is 32%, enacted. It applies to the voordeel from sparen en beleggen after the six-step calculation below. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

### Heffingsvrij vermogen

The heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free allowance) is € 57.000 per person for 2023, and € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. The allowance doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17. The 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint rendementsgrondslag and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

The heffingsvrij vermogen is subtracted in step 3 of the six-step calculation, after the rendementsgrondslag is reduced by the deductible part of the debts. It is not used in the tegenbewijsregeling, because "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Schuldendrempel

Only the part of total debts above the schuldendrempel (debt threshold) is deductible, and the Belastingdienst computes it in the return. Statute: art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

For 2023 the drempel is € 3.400 per person and € 6.800 with a whole-year partner. The schuldendrempel doubles for whole-year partners and a joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel

### Category percentages

Bank 0,92%, other assets 6,17%, debts 2,46%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

The fixed category percentages are set under art. 10.6ter. The bank percentage (lid 2) is set after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January, at "de door twaalf te delen som" of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. So it is an average (sum divided by twelve), not a twelve-month sum. The debt percentage (lid 4) uses the same method on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The other-assets percentage (lid 3) is replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

The law as published printed 0,01% / 5,69% / 2,46% in art. 5.2 lid 2. These were replaced for 2023 by the figures in the 2023 row above. So the 1 January 2023 text of the statute printed 0,01% bank, replaced after the year under art. 10.6ter. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

Category percentage series 2017 to 2025, as spaargeld / beleggingen en andere bezittingen / schulden: 2017 0,25 / 5,39 / 3,43, 2018 0,12 / 5,38 / 3,20, 2019 0,08 / 5,59 / 3,00, 2020 0,04 / 5,28 / 2,74, 2021 0,01 / 5,69 / 2,46, 2022 0,00 / 5,53 / 2,28, 2023 0,92 / 6,17 / 2,46, 2024 1,44 / 6,04 / 2,61, 2025 1,37 / 5,88 / 2,70 (all %). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Aandeel rounding

For 2023 the Belastingdienst rounding instruction for step 4 is "Rond af op 2 decimalen achter de komma". The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year: 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages, 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

### Six-step category calculation

The Belastingdienst method runs in six steps. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

1. Belastbaar rendement = banktegoeden x bank percentage plus overige bezittingen x other percentage minus (schulden minus drempel) x debt percentage.
2. Rendementsgrondslag = bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel).
3. Grondslag sparen en beleggen = rendementsgrondslag minus heffingsvrij vermogen. Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%.
4. Aandeel = grondslag / rendementsgrondslag x 100, rounded to 2 decimals for 2023.
5. Voordeel = belastbaar rendement x aandeel.
6. Tax = rate x voordeel.

Interactions. This six-step method replaced the band calculation of 2020 to 2022. The full list of category percentages shows the sequence from 2017 to 2025 and confirms the 2023 row. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

Art. 5.13: green investments are not counted as bezittingen up to the stated amount, doubled for whole-year partners. For 2023 that amount is € 65.072, or € 130.144 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 lid 2 (from the 2023 text): the exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Korting groene beleggingen

Art. 8.19 gives a korting voor groene beleggingen of 0,7% (2020 to 2024) of the exempt amount on the peildatum. It is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 For 2023 the rate is 0,7%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Tegenbewijsregeling start

The tegenbewijsregeling applies from 1 January 2023. Art. V: arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023, art. IV to 1 January 2017. Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. The law itself, Stb. 2025, 195, entered into force on 19 July 2025 by the besluit of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Form and six-week rule

Art. 5.25 lid 6: a form provided by the inspecteur, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Actual-return reporting 2023

For 2020 to 2024 the actual return is reported on the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call), and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. For 2021 to 2024 it may always be filed without a letter. From 2025 it is reported in the income tax return. No deadline is stated on these pages. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte

Content rules. Income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. No heffingsvrij vermogen applies. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Peildatumarbitrage rule (art. 5.24)

New art. 5.24 (peildatumarbitrage): transactions around 1 January within "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum" are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

### Floor on a negative return

Art. 4.14 lid 1 is amended so the forfaitaire voordeel is at least nil. In art. 5.2 a negative rendement is set at nil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Persoonsgebonden aftrek after a 2023 recalculation

Art. III: persoonsgebonden aftrek after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024 follows the partners' chosen split. A different split goes by ambtshalve-vermindering request. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Wet rechtsherstel scope

The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 keeps covering 2017 to 2022. Art. 1 lid 2: applies to IB/PVV assessments for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022. So it does not cover 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

Every example below uses only the 2023 figures from section 2. Mark each example either `Official example` (copied from a primary source, linked) or `Calculated example` (built here from the section 2 figures).

### Example 1: single person with € 150.000 savings (Official example)

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

Step 1, belastbaar rendement: € 150.000 x 0,92% = € 1.380.
Step 2, rendementsgrondslag: € 150.000, no debts.
Step 3, grondslag: € 150.000 - € 57.000 = € 93.000.
Step 4, aandeel: € 93.000 / € 150.000 x 100 = 62,00%.
Step 5, voordeel: € 1.380 x 62,00% = € 855.
Step 6, tax: 32% x € 855 = € 273.

### Example 2: partners with € 350.000 savings (Official example)

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

Step 1: € 350.000 x 0,92% = € 3.220.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag € 350.000.
Step 3: € 350.000 - € 114.000 = € 236.000, split € 118.000 each.
Step 4: € 236.000 / € 350.000 x 100 = 67,4286%, printed rounded as 33,71% per partner (one half of 67,43%).
Step 5: € 3.220 x 33,71% = € 1.085 each.
Step 6: 32% x € 1.085 = € 347 each.

### Example 3: partners with savings, investments and a debt (Official example)

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

Inputs: € 100.000 savings, € 150.000 investments, € 50.000 debt.
Step 1a, asset return: € 100.000 x 0,92% = € 920, plus € 150.000 x 6,17% = € 9.255. Total € 10.175.
Step 1b, deductible debt: € 50.000 - € 6.800 = € 43.200. Debt return: € 43.200 x 2,46% = € 1.063 (rounded).
Step 1c, belastbaar rendement: € 10.175 - € 1.063 = € 9.112.
Step 2, rendementsgrondslag: € 100.000 + € 150.000 - € 43.200 = € 206.800.
Step 3, grondslag: € 206.800 - € 114.000 = € 92.800, all to one partner.
Step 4, aandeel: € 92.800 / € 206.800 x 100 = 44,87%.
Step 5, voordeel: € 9.112 x 44,87% = € 4.088 (rounded).
Step 6, tax: 32% x € 4.088 = € 1.308 (rounded).

### Example 4: negative actual return set at nil (Official example)

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

Werkelijk rendement 2023: € 800 interest and crypto down € 1.200 give minus € 400, set at € 0. The loss cannot be carried to another year.

### Example 5: invented single taxpayer (Calculated example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 80.000, overige bezittingen € 20.000, schulden € 10.000.
Step 1a, asset return: € 80.000 x 0,92% = € 736. € 20.000 x 6,17% = € 1.234. Sum € 1.970.
Step 1b, deductible debt: € 10.000 - € 3.400 = € 6.600. Debt return: € 6.600 x 2,46% = € 162,36.
Step 1c, belastbaar rendement: € 1.970 - € 162,36 = € 1.807,64.
Step 2, rendementsgrondslag: € 100.000 - € 6.600 = € 93.400.
Step 3, grondslag: € 93.400 - € 57.000 = € 36.400.
Step 4, aandeel: € 36.400 / € 93.400 x 100 = 38,9722%, rounded to 2 decimals = 38,97%.
Step 5, voordeel: € 1.807,64 x 38,97% = € 704,44 (rounded).
Step 6, tax: 32% x € 704,44 = € 225,42.
Check: the voordeel is positive, so art. 4.14 lid 1 does not set it at nil.

### Example 6: invented figures that give a nil voordeel (Calculated example)

Inputs: banktegoeden € 60.000, overige bezittingen € 10.000, schulden € 25.000.
Step 1a, asset return: € 60.000 x 0,92% = € 552. € 10.000 x 6,17% = € 617. Sum € 1.169.
Step 1b, deductible debt: € 25.000 - € 3.400 = € 21.600. Debt return: € 21.600 x 2,46% = € 531,36.
Step 1c, belastbaar rendement: € 1.169 - € 531,36 = € 637,64.
Step 2, rendementsgrondslag: € 70.000 - € 21.600 = € 48.400.
Step 3, grondslag: € 48.400 - € 57.000 = negative, so nil.
Because the grondslag is nil, the aandeel is nil and the voordeel is nil, and the box 3 tax is nil for this person. This is the effect of the heffingsvrij vermogen of € 57.000. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Example 7: green investments and the exemption order rule (Calculated example)

Inputs: a single taxpayer holds € 40.000 green banktegoeden and € 40.000 green overige bezittingen on 1 January 2023. The 2023 exemption for a single person is € 65.072. Art. 5.13 lid 2 applies the exemption first to the green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to the green banktegoeden. So € 40.000 of the exemption goes to the green overige bezittingen, leaving € 25.072 of room for the green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January, and here € 65.072 is less than the total of € 80.000, so the full exemption room is used. The korting groene beleggingen is 0,7% of the exempt amount on the peildatum, so 0,7% x € 65.072 = € 455,50, computed automatically. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 61.215 and € 122.430 with whole-year partner in 2022 to € 65.072 and € 130.144 with whole-year partner in 2023. The art. 5.13 lid 2 order rule (first green overige bezittingen, then green banktegoeden) is now in the statute from the 2023 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% in 2022 and 0,7% in 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- New in 2023: peildatumarbitrage, art. 5.24, with a three-month window around 1 January and a business-reasons exception. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- New in 2023: no negative voordeel. Art. 4.14 lid 1 floors the forfaitaire voordeel at nil and art. 5.2 sets a negative rendement at nil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- Tegenbewijsregeling: statutory effect from 1 January 2023 for 2023, and 1 January 2017 inside the Wet rechtsherstel. The law itself is in force from 19 July 2025. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- Most favourable method: for 2021 and 2022 the Belastingdienst compares the old method and the current method, also after rechtsherstel for 2017 to 2022. That comparison is a 2021 and 2022 rule, not a 2023 rule. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen
- Reporting route unchanged for 2023: the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, and the return may always be filed without a letter for 2021 to 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- The box 3 calculation uses the values on 1 January 2023. Step 1 takes banktegoeden x 0,92%, overige bezittingen x 6,17% and (schulden minus drempel) x 2,46%. Step 2 takes bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel). Keep the 1 January 2023 balance statements, the bank and investment overviews, the debt statements and the WOZ or valuation records with the records of the year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- The Belastingdienst computes the deductible part of the debts in the return, so the drempel of € 3.400 per person (€ 6.800 with a whole-year partner) does not need to be entered manually. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Box 3 assets and debts land on the income tax return (IB/PVV) for 2023, not on the business profit. The box 3 result is separate from the winst uit onderneming of the sole trader. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- When partners divide the grondslag sparen en beleggen, the two parts must total 100%. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- A partner rule for the joint calculation stands in art. 5.2 lid 5. For 2023 the allowance doubles with a fiscal partner. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- For green investments, record the value on 1 January 2023 and keep the statements that show the green status. The exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden, and the exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. The korting of 0,7% of the exempt amount is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- If the actual return is lower than the forfaitaire voordeel, the taxpayer can make that plausible. For 2023 the route is the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, and it may be filed without a letter. The form comes from the inspecteur and further documents can follow up to six weeks after the form is sent. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- For the actual return, keep the records of income and value changes over the calendar year. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- For a BV, the BV's own balance sheet does not carry box 3. Box 3 applies to the person who holds the assets or debts. Shares in a BV are overige bezittingen at 6,17% for a holder in box 3, unless the substantial-interest rules of box 2 apply. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- The three-month window around 1 January 2023 can catch transactions around the peildatum. Transactions within "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum" are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- No Moneybird menu paths are given here. The tax return and the actual-return form are filed with the Belastingdienst. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- The 2026 calculation page uses 3 decimals for the aandeel, which differs from the 2023 and 2025 pages that use 2 decimals. This does not affect 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- The 2027 and 2028 texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028". For 2023 this changes nothing. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- For the tegenbewijsregeling inside the Wet rechtsherstel, art. IV works back to 1 January 2017, and the law itself entered into force on 19 July 2025. For 2023 the statutory effect runs from 1 January 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- The Hoge Raad of 6 June 2024 held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. For 2023, the effect on the return is via the tegenbewijsregeling route. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- No 2023 open point is in the checked material.
