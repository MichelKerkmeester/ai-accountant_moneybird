# Aangifte en procedure 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2023 for a sole trader and for a BV where a rule applies to a BV: the income tax filing deadline and uitstel (deferral), the btw (VAT) filing and payment calendar, the voorlopige aanslag (preliminary assessment), belastingrente (tax interest), the navorderingstermijn (recovery period), bezwaar (objection), the bewaarplicht (record-keeping duty) and registration at KVK and the Belastingdienst. The law for 2023 is enacted, so every Status tag below is enacted. The two 2023 events with the widest effect are the belastingrente rate moving from 4% to 6% on 1 July 2023 and the new rentestop for requested naheffingsaanslagen (additional assessments) from 1 January 2023, which is not yet in force for btw. The return for tax year 2023 is filed in 2024, so the filing deadline, the definitieve aanslag (final assessment) window and the belastingrente on tax year 2023 all run in 2024 and later.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

### Aangifteperiode inkomstenbelasting

The Belastingdienst opens the income tax filing period on 1 March each year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). The return for tax year 2023 is the return filed in 2024, and its deadline is the date printed in the aangiftebrief (the invitation to file) and set out in the next subsection. The filing period runs from 1 March in the filing year to that deadline.

### IB return 2023 filing deadline

The return for tax year 2023 must arrive before 1 May 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). The general rule is that the return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, which is often 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). With a granted uitstel the 2023 return is due before 1 September 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### Uitstel rules for the IB return

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, which moves a 1 May deadline to 1 September ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The form can be used only for the first uitstel request ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The fiscal partner must request uitstel separately ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). Uitstel usually means belastingrente is charged, even when the return is later taken over unchanged ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). Art. 9 lid 2 AWR gives the inspecteur the power to extend the filing term under conditions, which is the statutory basis for this uitstel ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Uitstel channel

Uitstel can also be requested online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May, and that route gives uitstel to 1 September and is open only to someone who received an aangiftebrief ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The uitstel page says online is not possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Read in full, that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Both texts are current Belastingdienst text, so the practical reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date and the paper form otherwise.

### AWR art. 9 filing term

For aanslagbelastingen the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the invitation ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The text of art. 9 is the same in the version valid on 1 January 2020 and the version valid on 1 January 2026 ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Definitive aanslag 2023

The definitive aanslag for tax year 2023 can be set until 31 December 2026 at the latest when the return is filed before 1 May 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). With uitstel to 1 September 2024 the latest date is 30 April 2027 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). These are the official examples on the page. Filing the return does not end the period, the period only limits the moment the aanslag can be imposed.

### Definitive assessment term

Art. 11 lid 3 AWR says the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld (the tax debt) arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For tax year 2023 that counting produces the two dates in the previous subsection.

### Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2023

The current Belastingdienst page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For 2023 the current page prints € 51 and € 16 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Pages up to the 2023 captures used gelijk aan of meer dan (equal to or more than) and printed each grens one euro higher, so € 52 and € 17 for 2023 ([April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading in the checked material. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### Filing without an invitation, tax year 2023

Without an aangiftebrief a return can still be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). For tax year 2023 that date is 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). This route matters when no aangiftebrief arrived but a refund or a correction is still wanted.

### Bezwaar

Bezwaar is possible within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The motivation can follow later if the bezwaar asks for that ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). There is no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag IB, only a change ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

### Verzoek om vermindering aanslag 2023

A verzoek om vermindering can be filed online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)). For the aanslag 2023 the online route is open until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)). Otherwise the verzoek goes by letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

### Voorlopige aanslag

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return, and changing it is not mandatory ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Reasons to change include buying a home, a change in mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). An entrepreneur changes the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming screen ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Paid or received amounts are settled in the return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with an aangiftebrief until the filing date or the uitstel date, and no longer after the return is filed ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)).

### Belastingrente rates

For all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen the rate is 4% for the period to 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). Amounts over tax year 2023 that are still open in later years follow the later rates, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). So a 2023 debt can carry two or more rates across its life, and the period is split at each rate change.

### Belastingrente on tax year 2023 IB

Belastingrente on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also when uitstel was granted ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged it ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). For tax year 2023 the rate is 7,5% from 1 July 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). There is no rente when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).

### Belastingrente on btw

Btw belastingrente is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). On late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). On a naheffingsaanslag the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or for a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### Rentestop requested naheffingsaanslagen

The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen applies from 1 January 2023, so rente ends at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). The extension is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### Navordering AWR art. 16

Art. 16 lid 1 AWR allows a navorderingsaanslag on a new fact, which cannot be used if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw (in bad faith) ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text as on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). Lid 2 adds the case where a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and the case of a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar, which is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Lid 3 sets the term at 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and for a fout-based navordering at 2 years after the assessment or decision ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Lid 4 sets 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page gives the official example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering was still possible until 31 May 2022 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).

### Voluntary correction

A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). For a 2023 return that means a correction made in time reduces the risk of both a navorderingsaanslag and a boete.

### Payment terms

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 sets the payment terms, with the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01)). An aanslag is invorderbaar (payable) 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A navorderingsaanslag is payable 1 month after its date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A naheffingsaanslag is payable 14 days after its date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, with the first termijn one month after the date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).

### Btw filing and payment rule

Art. 10 lid 2 AWR sets the btw filing term at a term chosen by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak (period) ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Art. 19 lid 1 AWR sets the payment at one month after the end of the tijdvak ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst tables give, for every year, the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together for monthly and quarterly filers, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). Return and payment must both be received by that date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).

### Btw software submission

Software can submit a btw return from the 24th of the month before the period ends ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). That opening date is earlier than the deadline and does not change the deadline itself.

### Btw monthly dates 2023

For monthly filers the 2023 table gives the following last days for return and payment together: December 2022 on 31 January 2023, January on 28 February 2023, February on 31 March 2023, March on 30 April 2023, April on 31 May 2023, May on 30 June 2023, June on 31 July 2023, July on 31 August 2023, August on 30 September 2023, September on 31 October 2023, October on 30 November 2023, November on 31 December 2023 and December 2023 on 31 January 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Btw quarterly dates 2023

For quarterly filers the 2023 table gives Q4 2022 on 31 January 2023, Q1 2023 on 30 April 2023, Q2 2023 on 31 July 2023, Q3 2023 on 31 October 2023 and Q4 2023 on 31 January 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Btw yearly return 2023

A yearly btw return for 2023 must be filed and paid by 31 March 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Btw uitstel

Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties, meaning an ernstige calamiteit (a serious calamity) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)). The request goes in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)). Even when uitstel is granted, a naheffingsaanslag and a boete remain possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)).

### Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2023

For 2023 there are 7 kalenderdagen coulance and then a boete of € 68 for a missing or late btw return ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). The cross-year rule keeps € 68 for 2020 to 2024 and raises it to € 82 for 2025 and 2026 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).

### IB boetes 2023

The 2023 amounts are a verzuimboete of € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, and a betaalverzuimboete of up to € 5.514 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). The source lists them in this form and does not split them further for this file.

### IB missed deadline route

After the filing deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering (reminder), then an aanmaning (formal demand) with 10 werkdagen (working days) to comply ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). The Belastingdienst can also stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Filing no return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag (an assessment made by the Belastingdienst) plus a verzuimboete ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete, also with a navorderingsaanslag ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)).

### Vergrijpboete and no-boete rules

The vergrijpboete is 25% for grove schuld (gross negligence), 50% for opzet (intent), in box 3 75% and 150%, and in aggravated cases 100% and 300% ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). No boete is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). A return can be requested within 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks coulance ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### Bewaarplicht

Art. 52 lid 4 AWR requires administratieplichtigen to keep the gegevensdragers (data carriers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text as on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (business profit) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager, except for the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). A sole trader with a 2023 administratie therefore falls under the seven-year rule in all these forms.

### Bewaarplicht terms

The Belastingdienst table gives basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken (immovable property) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). Basisgegevens are the grootboek, the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, the voorraadadministratie, the in- and verkoopadministratie, the loonadministratie, and data relevant for the taxation of third parties ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).

### KVK registration opgaven

Under art. 20 lid 1 Handelsregisterwet 2007 the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). For a business that starts in 2023 this window is the trigger for both the KVK registration and, through the data transfer, the tax registration.

### KVK practical

KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when registration happens within the two-week window ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). This lets a starter arrange the identity check and appointment before the start date and still register within the window.

### Belastingdienst registration

KVK passes the registration data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration with the Belastingdienst is needed ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example, belastingrente when the 2023 return arrives after 1 May

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 3 May 2024 with € 1.000 to pay. The aanslag is dated 4 December 2024 and takes the return over unchanged.

Steps:
1. The rente period starts on 1 July 2024.
2. Because the aanslag follows the return unchanged, the period ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. That is 13 September 2024.
3. The 19-week point is earlier than 6 weeks after the aanslag date, so the period ends on 13 September 2024.
4. Under the 30/360 count, July has 30 days, August has 30 days and September has 13 days. Total 73 days.
5. Rente is 73/360 x 7,5% x € 1.000 = € 15,21, rounded down to € 15.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, same facts with an earlier aanslag

Facts: same return and same amount, but the aanslag is dated 27 June 2024.

Steps:
1. The rente period starts on 1 July 2024.
2. The aanslag follows the return unchanged, so the period ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt. That cap is 13 September 2024.
3. Six weeks after the aanslag date is 9 August 2024, which is earlier than the cap. So the period ends on 9 August 2024.
4. Under the 30/360 count, July has 30 days and August has 9 days. Total 39 days.
5. Rente is 39/360 x 7,5% x € 1.000 = € 8,13, rounded down to € 8.
6. An earlier aanslag lowers the rente because it shortens the period.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, the aanslag deviates from the return

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 28 April 2024 with too low an income. The aanslag of € 2.500 extra is dated 11 December 2024.

Steps:
1. Belastingrente is charged because the aanslag deviates from the return, even though the return was filed before 1 May 2024.
2. The period starts on 1 July 2024.
3. The period ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, on 22 January 2025.
4. Under the 30/360 count, the period has 180 days in 2024 and 22 days in 2025, so 202 days.
5. The rate is 7,5% for 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025.
6. 180 days at 7,5%: 180/360 x 7,5% x € 2.500 = € 93,75, rounded down to € 93.
7. 22 days at 6,5%: 22/360 x 6,5% x € 2.500 = € 9,93, rounded down to € 9.
8. Total rente is € 93 + € 9 = € 102.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, no belastingrente

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 30 April 2024 and is taken over unchanged.

Steps:
1. No belastingrente is charged because the return arrived before 1 May 2024 and the aanslag follows it unchanged.
2. Filing early removes the rente risk even though the period would otherwise have started on 1 July 2024.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, definitive aanslag 2023

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed before 1 May 2024.

Steps:
1. The definitive aanslag can be set until 31 December 2026 at the latest.
2. The uitstel from 1 May 2024 to 1 September 2024 is 4 months.
3. The term is extended by the duration of the uitstel, so the latest date becomes 30 April 2027.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### Official example, bewaarplicht term start

Facts: the administratie contains a 4-year lease.

Steps:
1. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual.
2. The lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### Calculated example, navorderingstermijn for a 2023 return with uitstel

Facts: the return for tax year 2023 is filed with uitstel, so the deadline is 1 September 2024 instead of 1 May 2024.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. The 5-year navorderingstermijn runs from the moment the belastingschuld arose, and the Belastingdienst counts from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2024 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
2. 5 years from 1 January 2024 runs to 31 December 2028.
3. The uitstel from 1 May 2024 to 1 September 2024 is 4 months, and the term is extended by the duration of the uitstel.
4. 31 December 2028 plus 4 months is 30 April 2029.

This matches the method of the official example, where tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel gave 31 May 2022 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). A fout-based navordering has a shorter term of 2 years after the assessment or decision ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Calculated example, payment schedule of a voorlopige aanslag dated in the tax year

Facts: a voorlopige aanslag for 2023 is dated 15 March 2023.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. Months remaining after the month of the date (March) are April to December, which is 9 months ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. The number of equal monthly termijnen is therefore 9.
3. The first termijn is one month after the date, so 15 April 2023.

### Calculated example, payment schedule of a voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year

Facts: a voorlopige aanslag for 2023 is dated 20 December 2022.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. The first termijn falls at the end of January 2023.

### Calculated example, payment term of an aanslag

Facts: an aanslag IB is dated 1 September 2024.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. An aanslag is payable 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. 6 weeks is 42 days.
3. 1 September 2024 plus 29 days is 30 September 2024, and 13 more days brings the end at 13 October 2024.

A quarterly filer: the Q2 2023 return and payment must both be received by 31 July 2023 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Calculated example, KVK registration window

Facts: a business starts on 1 June 2023.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. The window is two weeks, starting one week before and ending one week after the start of the business ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)).
2. One week before 1 June 2023 is 25 May 2023.
3. One week after 1 June 2023 is 8 June 2023.
4. So the opgave must be made between 25 May 2023 and 8 June 2023. KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately within that window ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)).

### Calculated example, btw belastingrente on a 2023 correction

Facts: an error is found in a 2023 btw period.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. Rente on a btw naheffingsaanslag runs from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2024 for a 2023 period ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
2. It ends 14 days after the date of the naheffingsaanslag ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
3. A voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year gives no rente, so a suppletie filed before 1 April 2024 avoids rente for a 2023 period ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
4. A naheffingsaanslag dated 15 November 2024 therefore carries rente over 1 January 2024 to 29 November 2024, at the rate for 2024 of 7,5% ([rate](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The checked btw source does not state the 30/360 day count for btw, so no euro amount is worked out here.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

- Belastingrente rate: 2022 had 4% for the whole year. In 2023 the rate is 4% to 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).
- Belastingrente per tax year: rente on the tax year 2022 starts 1 July 2023 at 6%, rente on the tax year 2023 starts 1 July 2024 at 7,5% and moves to 6,5% from 1 January 2025 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).
- New in 2023: the rentestop for requested naheffingsaanslagen applies from 1 January 2023, rente ends at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, and the extension is not in force for btw ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
- Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens: 2022 was € 48 and € 15 in the higher than wording and € 49 and € 16 under the older wording. For 2023 it is € 51 and € 16 in the higher than wording and € 52 and € 17 under the older wording ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- IB filing deadline per year: the 2022 return was due before 1 May 2023 with uitstel to 1 September 2023, the 2023 return is due before 1 May 2024 with uitstel to 1 September 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Definitive aanslag window: for 2022 the latest dates were 31 December 2025, or 30 April 2026 with uitstel. For 2023 they are 31 December 2026, or 30 April 2027 with uitstel ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2022 could be filed until 31 December 2027, tax year 2023 until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).
- Verzoek om vermindering: for the aanslag 2023 the online route is open until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).
- Btw monthly dates: 2022 ran from December 2021 with 31 January 2022 through December 2022 with 31 January 2023. 2023 runs from December 2022 with 31 January 2023 through December 2023 with 31 January 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

### Wording of the aanslaggrens and the teruggaafgrens

The current Belastingdienst page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens, and prints € 51 and € 16 for 2023 ([current page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). The April 2023 capture uses gelijk aan of meer dan and prints € 52 and € 17 for 2023 ([April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading in the checked material, so this is a wording difference rather than a value conflict. A Belastingdienst page or law text that states the 2023 grens in one fixed wording would settle it fully.

### KVK term for the btw-number

The KVK page carries both wordings for the term in which the btw-number arrives, two weeks and ten werkdagen ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). Ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks, and the Belastingdienst states that registration in its administration takes at most 10 werkdagen ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). The two do not differ in substance. A single rewording of the KVK page would settle the presentation.

### Rentestop not in force for btw

The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). A technical change that makes the rule executable for btw would settle it. Until then a requested btw naheffingsaanslag does not get the 10-week end point.

No other 2023 value in this file is marked UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown in the checked material.
