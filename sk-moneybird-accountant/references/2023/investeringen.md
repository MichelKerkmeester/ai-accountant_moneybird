## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the investment deductions and depreciation rules for tax year 2023: the kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction), the energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction), the milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction), the Vamil (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, random depreciation for environmental investments), ordinary depreciation, the bodemwaarde (floor value) for buildings, the willekeurige afschrijving for starters and the temporary 2023 scheme, and the desinvesteringsbijtelling (disposal addition). The most important 2023 points are the wider KIA table (nil up to € 2.600 and a top of € 353.973), the EIA at 45,5%, the MIA at 45%, 36% and 27%, the Vamil at 75% random depreciation, and a temporary willekeurige afschrijving of at most 50% for assets with 2023 costs that are in use before 1 January 2026. The 2023 rules are enacted law, checked against the 2023 statute text and the Belastingdienst pages. 2023 is the last year in which the bodemwaarde of an own-use building is 50% of the WOZ-waarde, from 2024 one bodemwaarde applies.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The KIA is a deduction per onderneming, claimed by choice in the aangifte, on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year 2023 ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). The table has five bands. A total up to € 2.600 gives no KIA. A total above € 2.600 and up to € 63.716 gives 28% of the investment. A total above € 63.716 and up to € 117.991 gives the fixed amount of € 17.841. A total above € 117.991 and up to € 353.973 gives € 17.841 minus 7,56% of the part above € 117.991. A total above € 353.973 gives no KIA ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The same 2023 figures are printed on the Belastingdienst page about the 2023 vennootschapsbelasting changes, which states that investments can get MIA and KIA together, that MIA and EIA cannot be combined and that the EIA must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The KIA cannot be used for the assets excluded by art. 3.45, and each separate asset must clear the € 450 floor ([art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### KIA window as printed by the Belastingdienst

The Belastingdienst phrases the 2023 KIA window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). An investment total below € 2.601 or above € 353.973 therefore gives no KIA. This window matches the table, which opens above € 2.600 and closes at € 353.973.

### KIA per-asset minimum

Art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes assets with an investment amount below € 450 from the KIA ([art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The same amount appears in the FISIN chapter for 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). An asset below the floor therefore does not enter the KIA base, while it does stay part of the ordinary business assets and keeps its own depreciation schedule.

### KIA in a samenwerkingsverband (art. 3.41 lid 3)

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership) the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum ([art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). The pro rata outcome is the amount each participant claims in the aangifte.

### Assets excluded from every investeringsaftrek (art. 3.45 lid 1)

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes, for every investeringsaftrek, assets intended mainly for use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (onderdeel a), gronden including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (onderdeel b), woonhuizen and woonschepen (onderdeel c), personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (onderdeel d), vaartuigen of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (onderdeel e), effecten, vorderingen, goodwill, vergunningen, ontheffingen and concessies (onderdeel f), and dieren (onderdeel g) ([art. 3.45 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). These exclusions hold for the KIA, the EIA and the MIA.

### Further exclusions (art. 3.45 lid 2, lid 3, lid 4, lid 5)

Lid 2 adds two KIA-only exclusions, being assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (onderdeel a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (onderdeel b). Lid 3 disapplies lid 1 onderdelen c and d for the MIA, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn MIA. Lid 4 adds two exclusions for the EIA and the MIA, being assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (onderdeel a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (onderdeel b). Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### EIA percentage

The EIA is 45,5% of the investment amount for 2023 ([art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The asset must be on the Energielijst, and the EIA must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). The EIA can be combined with the KIA for the same asset, but not with the MIA ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount

The 2023 maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 136.000.000 ([art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst prints "€ 136 miljoen" on the 2023 Vpb page ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The FISIN chapter for 2023 prints € 128.000.000 in its EIA sentence and € 136.000.000 in its MIA sentence ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The 2023 value is settled at € 136.000.000 on the statute text and the Vpb page, see section 7.

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum

Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b sets a floor of € 2.500 per asset for the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.45 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). RVO applies the same floor to the Vamil with the phrase "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### EIA and MIA conditions

Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify for the EIA and the MIA. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, as can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer, as voortbrengingskosten. RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). MIA is not available for costs that already received EIA, and Vamil remains possible in that case. EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset. The EIA and the MIA can each be combined with the KIA. The EIA asset must be on the Energielijst and the MIA and Vamil asset on the Milieulijst ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). A rule that a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the pages checked, see section 7.

### MIA percentages and Milieulijst codes

From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I with 45%, codes starting with A or D to categorie II with 36%, and codes starting with B or E to categorie III with 27% ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). These percentages also hold for 2023, so 2023 brought no MIA change. The FISIN chapter for 2023 prints 36%, 27% and 13,5%, which is the losing source, see section 7.

### Vamil

The costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", and the rest follows the normal schedule ([art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets not yet common in the Netherlands, not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated ([art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). RVO states that the total tax benefit of MIA and Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

Apply within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst or placing the order for aanschafkosten, within 3 months after the quarter in which voortbrengingskosten were made, and for milieu-advieskosten together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The FISIN chapters for 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)", and the 2023 Vpb page states that the EIA must be reported to RVO in advance ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### Excluded obligations (art. 3.46)

There is no investeringsaftrek for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (onderdeel b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (onderdeel c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam and that lichaam (onderdeel d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 under lid 2, which is the ontheffing route ([art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (art. 3.44)

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (lid 1). At staking of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking (lid 2). This applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold

The 2023 threshold for the desinvesteringsbijtelling is € 2.600 ([art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to profit (lid 1). This applies only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken (lid 2). Lid 3 treats as disposal the withdrawal of the good from the onderneming (onderdeel a), a change of destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a for the KIA or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a for the EIA and MIA (onderdeel b), and a change bringing it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (onderdeel c). In those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer counts as transfer price (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding, counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7) ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of part or all of the onderneming within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Ordinary depreciation caps

Yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffingskosten or voortbrengingskosten attributable to the year (lid 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost is allowed for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 and 5 years (lid 2). Voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging (lid 3). Voorwerpen van geringe waarde that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (lid 4) ([art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Bodemwaarde for buildings

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde, and at most for the difference (lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden are one bedrijfsmiddel, and detachable werktuigen are separate (lid 2). For 2023 the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another, own-use building (lid 3). 2023 is the last year of the 50% rule, from 2024 one bodemwaarde applies, the WOZ-waarde of the gebouw. The WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (lid 4) ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving (URWA art. 7 to 9)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001, willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship ([art. 3.34](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3, the startersaftrek, applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst as ondernemer. Art. 8 allows willekeurige afschrijving "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10 ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is for an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply ([Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). A herwaardering to the normal book value, with the difference as winst, must take place within 5 years, or 10 years for zeeschepen, if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf ([Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). The cap amount for 2023 is € 353.973, the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table, read from the URWA art. 8 cross-reference. This is a reading of a cross-reference, no primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7.

### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023 (URWA art. 13 to 15)

Art. 13 lid 1 designates assets not used before, for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in calendar year 2023 and which are taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026. Lid 2 excludes gebouwen, schepen, vliegtuigen, bromfietsen, motorrijwielen, personenauto's except for beroepsvervoer over de weg or 0 gram CO2 per km, immateriële activa, dieren, assets mainly made available to third parties with short rental to successive hirers excepted, public roads and paths, and assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Lid 3 moves the 1 January 2026 date by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14 allows the random depreciation once, at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15 sets the end of the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 at 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The Belastingdienst adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year, and that the scheme covers both the inkomstenbelasting and the vennootschapsbelasting ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for 2023 was found in the checked sources. Every example below is a `Calculated example` built from the figures in section 2.

### Calculated example: KIA in the 28% band

A sole trader invests € 60.000,00 in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2023. Each asset clears the € 450,00 floor and the whole amount qualifies for the KIA.

Step 1: € 60.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the second band applies.

Step 2: 28% of € 60.000,00 = 0,28 x € 60.000,00 = € 16.800,00.

Step 3: € 16.800,00 is lower than the fixed amount of € 17.841,00, so the KIA is € 16.800,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in the fixed band

The same sole trader invests € 100.000,00 instead.

Step 1: € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the fixed band applies.

Step 2: the KIA is € 17.841,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in the fourth band

A sole trader invests € 120.000,00.

Step 1: € 120.000,00 is above € 117.991,00 and at or below € 353.973,00, so the fourth band applies.

Step 2: the part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00.

Step 3: 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88.

Step 4: € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12. The KIA is € 17.689,12.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: no KIA above the top of the table

A sole trader invests € 360.000,00.

Step 1: € 360.000,00 is above € 353.973,00, so the table gives nil. The KIA is nil.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

A vof invests € 80.000,00 and one vennoot invests € 40.000,00 privately. Both amounts qualify for the KIA.

Step 1: the sum is € 80.000,00 plus € 40.000,00 = € 120.000,00.

Step 2: the table on € 120.000,00 gives € 17.841,00 minus 7,56% of € 2.009,00. The part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00. 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88. The table result is € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12.

Step 3: the own share is € 40.000,00 divided by € 120.000,00 = 1/3.

Step 4: the vennoot's KIA is € 17.689,12 x 1/3 = € 5.896,373333..., rounded down € 5.896,37.

Rule source: [art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: the € 450 floor in the KIA base

A sole trader invests in several bedrijfsmiddelen in 2023. The assets that clear the € 450,00 floor together come to € 50.000,00.

Step 1: art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b leaves every asset with an investment amount below € 450,00 out of the KIA base.

Step 2: the KIA base here is € 50.000,00. € 50.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the 28% band applies.

Step 3: 28% of € 50.000,00 = 0,28 x € 50.000,00 = € 14.000,00. The KIA is € 14.000,00.

Step 4: an asset below the floor does not enter the KIA base.

Source: [art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: EIA together with KIA

A sole trader buys a new machine of € 100.000,00 that is on the Energielijst and above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the EIA is 45,5% of € 100.000,00 = 0,455 x € 100.000,00 = € 45.500,00.

Step 2: the same € 100.000,00 also enters the KIA. € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the KIA is € 17.841,00.

Step 3: the total investment deductions are € 45.500,00 plus € 17.841,00 = € 63.341,00.

Step 4: the MIA cannot be added on top of the EIA, because EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset.

Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### Calculated example: MIA in the three categories

A trader buys three new assets of € 50.000,00 each, all on the Milieulijst and each above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the asset with code F or G falls in categorie I, MIA = 45% of € 50.000,00 = 0,45 x € 50.000,00 = € 22.500,00.

Step 2: the asset with code A or D falls in categorie II, MIA = 36% of € 50.000,00 = 0,36 x € 50.000,00 = € 18.000,00.

Step 3: the asset with code B or E falls in categorie III, MIA = 27% of € 50.000,00 = 0,27 x € 50.000,00 = € 13.500,00.

Step 4: the total MIA is € 22.500,00 plus € 18.000,00 plus € 13.500,00 = € 54.000,00.

Source: [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: Vamil on a designated asset

A trader buys a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel of € 100.000,00 that has not been used before and is above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: 75% of € 100.000,00 = 0,75 x € 100.000,00 = € 75.000,00 may be depreciated at will.

Step 2: the remaining € 25.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023

A trader buys a new machine in 2023 for € 40.000,00. The machine has not been used before. The obligation is entered in 2023 and the trader takes the machine into use on 1 November 2023, so it is in use at 31 December 2023.

Step 1: the asset is not used before, the obligation is in 2023, and the machine is in use before 1 January 2026. These conditions are met.

Step 2: the random depreciation is allowed once, for at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation. 50% of € 40.000,00 = 0,50 x € 40.000,00 = € 20.000,00 in 2023.

Step 3: because the machine is in use at 31 December 2023, the payment cap for an asset not yet in use does not reduce the deduction. If the machine were not in use at 31 December 2023, the deduction would be capped at the amount paid in 2023.

Step 4: the remaining € 20.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

### Calculated example: desinvesteringsbijtelling

A trader buys an asset in 2023 for € 20.000,00 and takes a KIA of 28% = 0,28 x € 20.000,00 = € 5.600,00. This is the only investment that year. The trader sells the asset later in 2023 for a transfer price of € 15.000,00. The transfer price is above the yearly threshold of € 2.600,00, and the sale is within five years after the start of 2023.

Step 1: the bijtelling percentage equals the investeringsaftrek percentage taken, 28%.

Step 2: 28% of € 15.000,00 = 0,28 x € 15.000,00 = € 4.200,00.

Step 3: the transfer price of € 15.000,00 is below the investment amount of € 20.000,00 over which aftrek was taken, so the whole € 4.200,00 is added to profit.

Source: [art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: bodemwaarde on a building

A trader owns a building with a WOZ-waarde of € 300.000,00 and a book value of € 350.000,00.

Step 1: for a gebouw ter belegging the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde of € 300.000,00. Depreciation may not bring the book value below that bodemwaarde, so the total remaining amount above the bodemwaarde is € 350.000,00 minus € 300.000,00 = € 50.000,00.

Step 2: for another building, such as an own-use building, the bodemwaarde is 50% of € 300.000,00 = 0,50 x € 300.000,00 = € 150.000,00. The total remaining amount above that bodemwaarde is € 350.000,00 minus € 150.000,00 = € 200.000,00.

Source: [art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

A trader buys a machine for € 30.000,00 and goodwill for € 30.000,00.

Step 1: the machine may be depreciated at 20% per year at most, so 0,20 x € 30.000,00 = € 6.000,00 per year, which means at least 5 years.

Step 2: the goodwill may be depreciated at 10% per year at most, so 0,10 x € 30.000,00 = € 3.000,00 per year, which means at least 10 years.

Source: [art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: starters willekeurige afschrijving within the cap

A starter with the startersaftrek invests € 80.000,00 in assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek.

Step 1: the yearly cap is € 353.973,00 of total costs, read from the top of the KIA table.

Step 2: € 80.000,00 is below € 353.973,00, so the whole € 80.000,00 of costs stays within the cap.

Step 3: the starter may choose freely which assets to depreciate at will, and the € 2.600,00 minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply.

Sources: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

- Temporary willekeurige afschrijving: new for assets with obligations or voortbrengingskosten in 2023, at most 50% once in the investment year and use before 1 January 2026 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). For 2022 no temporary scheme was found, marked unknown and UNVERIFIED, and the URWA text checked lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme either, an absence that one page cannot confirm ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The KIA is claimed by choice in the aangifte over the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen of the calendar year, per onderneming, so the annual investment list has to be complete and split per asset ([art. 3.41](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Keep the koopovereenkomst or the order date. The EIA, MIA and Vamil must be reported to RVO within 3 months after signing or ordering, and the Milieulijst valid on that date governs the application ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).
- The EIA must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).
- When an asset is not in use at the end of the year, only the amount paid enters the deduction, and the excess follows in later years. Note the full deduction at staking in the year of staking ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Track every disposal of an asset within five years after the start of the investment year, because the desinvesteringsbijtelling adds the same percentage as the investeringsaftrek taken over the transfer price. Withdrawal from the onderneming, a change of destination, a received vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding and staking within the term can count as a disposal ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).
- Monitor the two art. 3.47 lid 6 tests, being at least 25% of the investment amount paid within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and use within three years after the start of the investment year ([art. 3.47 lid 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Buying an asset from your own BV in which you hold at least one third of the shares gives in principle no investeringsaftrek ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- For assets under the temporary 2023 scheme, record the date the asset was taken into use, since use before 1 January 2026 is required, and only 2023 costs count ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).
- Keep the WOZ-beschikking of the year in the building file, because building depreciation is capped by the bodemwaarde, which is the WOZ-waarde of the calendar year of the depreciation ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- These items land in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting for the sole trader, and the same 2023 figures are printed on the Belastingdienst page about the 2023 vennootschapsbelasting changes for the BV ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- EIA and MIA for a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker: UNVERIFIED. The rule that a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched RVO and Belastingdienst pages ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). What would settle it: the statutory text of [art. 3.42 and art. 3.42a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) or an RVO page that names which persons can use the EIA and the MIA.
- Settled conflict, EIA and MIA maximum investment amount 2023: € 136.000.000 against € 128.000.000. [art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and the [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023) page ("€ 136 miljoen") give € 136.000.000. The [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) chapter prints € 128.000.000 in its EIA sentence and € 136.000.000 in its MIA sentence. The 2023 value is settled at € 136.000.000 on the statute text and the Vpb page, and the FISIN EIA sentence is the losing source.
- Settled conflict, MIA percentages 2023: [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), the [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html) in force on 1 January 2022 and the [Rijksoverheid Belastingplan 2022](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel) news give 45%, 36% and 27%, with the increase "vanaf 1 januari 2022 verhoogd van 13,5%, 27% en 36% naar respectievelijk 27%, 36% en 45%". The [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) chapter prints 36%, 27% and 13,5%. The 2023 value is settled at 45%, 36% and 27%, and the FISIN chapter is the losing source. 2023 itself brought no MIA change.
- Reading of a cross-reference, not a printed number, UNVERIFIED: the starters willekeurige afschrijving cap of € 353.973 for 2023 rests on the [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0) wording "het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", read with the top of the KIA table ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). No primary page prints the cap as a number. What would settle it: a page that states the cap amount for the year directly.
- Year not stated on the page: the € 25 miljoen maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil comes from the current RVO page, which does not state a year in its text ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). What would settle it: the Milieulijst or an RVO page for 2023 that prints the maximum for the year.
- Temporary willekeurige afschrijving before 2023: UNVERIFIED (absence). No temporary scheme was found for 2020, 2021 or 2022 ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The URWA text checked lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme, and the Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year. An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. What would settle it: a year page for 2020, 2021 or 2022 that names such a scheme, or a statement that none existed.
