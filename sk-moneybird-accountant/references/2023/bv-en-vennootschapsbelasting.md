# BV en vennootschapsbelasting 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the Dutch vennootschapsbelasting (Vpb, corporate income tax) for a BV in 2023, plus the main related rules for a directeur-grootaandeelhouder (DGA, director and major shareholder), dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax), the conditionele bronbelasting (conditional withholding tax), the jaarrekening (annual accounts) and the switch from a sole trader to a BV. The 2023 rate is 19% up to a belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) of € 200.000 and € 38.000 plus 25,8% above that amount. The gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) comparable wage moves to 100% in 2023. The law for 2023 is enacted.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

### Vpb rate
The rate applies to a BV and other lichamen subject to vennootschapsbelasting. Article 22 sets the table. For 2023 the first bracket is t/m € 200.000 at 19%. For the part of the belastbaar bedrag above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8%. The Belastingdienst tarieven page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting The rate interacts with verliesverrekening, the art. 15b renteaftrekbeperking, the innovatiebox and the deelnemingsvrijstelling. The result goes on the aangifte vennootschapsbelasting.

### Art. 31 apportionment for a book year straddling 1 January 2023
This applies when a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect. For this file the relevant date is 1 January 2023. The tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 The 2022 material states the art. 31 apportionment applies to a book year straddling 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 Report the apportioned tax in the Vpb return.

### Verliesverrekening
This applies to a BV with a loss. From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and unlimited following years. Per year the set-off is only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. In 2020 and 2021 the rule was different, one preceding year and six following years, after the inspector fixes the loss by beschikking. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 The limit interacts with the Vpb rate because only the remaining profit is taxed.

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking percentage
Article 15b lid 1 applies to a BV with a saldo aan renten. The saldo is interest costs on loans minus interest income. The saldo is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the gecorrigeerde winst and € 1.000.000. For 2023 the percentage is 20%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 Non-deductible interest carries forward. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking threshold
The threshold is € 1.000.000. The taxpayer compares 20% of the gecorrigeerde winst with € 1.000.000 and uses the higher amount. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 If 20% is lower, € 1.000.000 applies. If 20% is higher, that higher amount applies.

### Innovatiebox
This applies to a taxpayer with advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset. The advantages count for 9/H, H being the highest art. 22 rate, so the effective rate is 9% in 2023. Belastingdienst states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring. Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover over those five years below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2). Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb gives a K/T part, K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. The art. 12bd forfait is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive and the taxpayer made a qualifying asset in the year or two preceding years and chooses it. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01

### Deelneming threshold
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming out of the profit. A deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (art. 13 lid 2). This is identical in every text 2020 to 2026. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies with a 1/3 interest. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Fiscale eenheid bezitseis
A fiscale eenheid requires the mother to hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The mother can be a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie or a comparable foreign form. The daughter must be a bv or nv. Both must have the same book years and profit rules and be effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html

### Gebruikelijk loon normbedrag
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (100% from 2023), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the normbedrag of € 51.000. The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 7 from 2023). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling, that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Gebruikelijk loon comparable wage
The comparable wage is 100% from 2023. In 2020 to 2022 it was 75%. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 changed. From 2023, if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. In 2020 to 2022, if the inhoudingsplichtige made plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage was 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when that was lower than the normbedrag. A vaststellingsovereenkomst made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Gebruikelijk loon minimum floor
The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This is lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01

### Start-up relief
Statute 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4): for a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. Belastingdienst states the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 states the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the Handboek 2025: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A DGA who is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen falls under the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw. ### Maximum bijdrage-inkomen
The maximum bijdrage-inkomen for 2023 is € 66.956. The bijdrage Zvw and the werkgeversheffing Zvw are calculated up to this amount. ### Werkgeversheffing Zvw
For 2023 the werkgeversheffing Zvw is 6,68%. A DGA who is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. A DGA who is not insured pays no werkgeversheffing and instead the BV withholds the bijdrage Zvw. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

### Box 2 rate
The box 2 rate for 2023 is 26,9% flat. Box 2 applies to income from an aanmerkelijk belang. Individuals credit the dividendbelasting withheld in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Dividendbelasting
Dividendbelasting is 15% of the opbrengst (art. 5 Wet DB 1965). If the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85 (art. 6 lid 1). The shareholder is the taxpayer and the company withholds (art. 1). Once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota is due (art. 9 lid 1). The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

### Deelnemingsdividend and beleggingsdividend
A deelnemingsdividend, a holding of 5% or more, to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling. No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. A beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert

### Inkoop van eigen aandelen
Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

### Conditionele bronbelasting
The conditionele bronbelasting of the Wet bronbelasting 2021 applies in 2023 to interest and royalties. Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. With the 2023 top rate this is 25,8%. It hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction where the profit tax is below 9% or which is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, and abuse cases. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividendbelasting). Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/

### Dividend stripping rule
The dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01

### Jaarrekening size thresholds
A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. For boekjaar 2023 the default values are micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000, fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000, fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts are unchanged. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html

### Jaarrekening deposit
The board deposits the annual accounts within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein states there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/ and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/

### Stakingsaftrek
Without the geruisloze omzetting facility, a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays inkomstenbelasting on the stakingswinst, the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The stakingsaftrek of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630, once in a lifetime, and an extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente (art. 3.129). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html

### Willekeurige afschrijving 2023
For 2023 the willekeurige afschrijving allows up to 50% of qualifying new assets, with costs made in 2023 and the asset in use before 1 January 2026. The facility excludes buildings, intangibles, assets mainly for rent and non-zero-emission passenger cars. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

### Vpb return and extension
For a book year equal to the calendar year, file the Vpb return before 1 June of the next year. For a broken book year, file within 5 months after its end. For a short book year ending 31 December, file before 1 June. For a short book year ending in another month, file before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting

### Administratieplicht and bewaartermijn
Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig and requires them to keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. ### Geruisloze omzetting
Art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001 lets a sole trader convert to a BV on request without a cessation. The whole business must be contributed, the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. Belastingdienst states the request goes to the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring is registered with the Geleideformulier, and the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm

### Calculated example: geruisloze omzetting compared with ruisende inbreng
Step 1: fiscal book values total € 150.000,00, so the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is € 150.000,00.
Step 2: in a geruisloze omzetting the business is deemed not ceased, so no stakingswinst arises from the contribution.
Step 3: in a ruisende inbreng the real value is € 400.000,00 and the book value is € 150.000,00, so the stakingswinst is € 400.000,00 minus € 150.000,00 = € 250.000,00.
Step 4: stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630,00.
Step 5: taxable stakingswinst = € 250.000,00 minus € 3.630,00 = € 246.370,00.
Step 6: the rounding credit in the geruisloze route is at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000,00. The paid-up amount on the fully paid-up shares for the contributed assets is € 150.000,00, so the credit is at most 5% x € 150.000,00 = € 7.500,00.
Answer: no stakingswinst in the geruisloze route, € 246.370,00 taxable stakingswinst in the ruisende route, and a rounding credit of at most € 7.500,00.

### Calculated example: willekeurige afschrijving 2023
Step 1: asset cost € 100.000,00.
Step 2: maximum random depreciation = 50% x € 100.000,00 = € 50.000,00.
Step 3: the costs must be made in 2023 and the asset must be in use before 1 January 2026.
Answer: € 50.000,00.

### Calculated example: fiscale eenheid with mother profit € 300.000 and daughter loss € 100.000
Step 1: combined taxable amount = € 300.000,00 minus € 100.000,00 = € 200.000,00.
Step 2: Vpb in the fiscale eenheid = 19% x € 200.000,00 = € 38.000,00.
Step 3: mother alone would pay € 200.000,00 x 19% = € 38.000,00 plus € 100.000,00 x 25,8% = € 25.800,00, so € 63.800,00.
Step 4: difference = € 63.800,00 minus € 38.000,00 = € 25.800,00.
Answer: € 38.000,00 in the fiscale eenheid, a difference of € 25.800,00.

### Calculated example: deelnemingsvrijstelling on a dividend of € 50.000
Step 1: the 5% deelneming means art. 13 lid 1 leaves the advantage out of the profit, so the Vpb base effect is € 0,00.
Step 2: without the exemption the Vpb at 19% would be € 50.000,00 x 19% = € 9.500,00.
Answer: € 0,00 under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, € 9.500,00 without it.

### Calculated example: inkoop van eigen aandelen
Step 1: buyback price € 100.000,00 minus average paid-up capital € 20.000,00 = € 80.000,00.
Step 2: dividendbelasting = 15% x € 80.000,00 = € 12.000,00.
Step 3: if the company bears the tax, gross-up = € 80.000,00 x 100/85 = € 94.117,65 rounded.
Step 4: dividendbelasting = 15% x € 94.117,65 = € 14.117,65 rounded.
Answer: € 12.000,00, or € 14.117,65 if the company bears the tax.

### Calculated example: Vpb filing dates for a calendar book year 2023
Step 1: file before 1 June 2024.
Step 2: online extension runs to 1 November 2024 and is requested from 1 March to 1 June 2024.
Step 3: a paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons.
Answer: 1 June 2024, or 1 November 2024 with the online extension.

### Calculated example: bewaartermijnen
Step 1: general records seven years.
Step 2: records on onroerende zaken nine years following the year in which use began, which is effectively ten years.
Step 3: OSS records ten years after the year of the transaction.
Answer: 7 years for the general records, nine years following the year in which use began (effectively 10 years) for onroerende zaken, and 10 years for OSS records.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

- Vpb first bracket: 2022 t/m € 395.000 at 15% to 2023 t/m € 200.000 at 19%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Vpb above the bracket: 2022 € 59.250 plus 25,8% to 2023 € 38.000 plus 25,8%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Art. 31 apportionment applies to a book year straddling 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01
- Verliesverrekening is unchanged from 2022. One year back, unlimited forward, per year € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Art. 15b percentage is 20% in 2022 and 2023. The threshold is € 1.000.000 in both years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Innovatiebox fraction is 9/H in 2022 and 2023. The forfait is 25% with a maximum of € 25.000 in both years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Deelneming threshold is 5% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Fiscale eenheid bezitseis is at least 95% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Gebruikelijk loon normbedrag: 2022 € 48.000 to 2023 € 51.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Gebruikelijk loon comparable wage: 2022 75% to 2023 100%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Gebruikelijk loon minimum floor is € 5.000 in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Start-up relief: in 2022 it was the last year of first use and could run to 2024. In 2023 it lapsed for new cases. A 2021 starter continues in 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Bijdrage Zvw: 2022 5,50% to 2023 5,43%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen: 2022 € 59.706 to 2023 € 66.956. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Werkgeversheffing Zvw: 2022 6,75% to 2023 6,68%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Box 2 rate is 26,9% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Dividendbelasting is 15% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01
- Conditionele bronbelasting is 25,8% on interest and royalties in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Dividend stripping rule is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 in 2022 and 2023. The research had art. 4 lid 3 and unknown. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01
- Jaarrekening size thresholds: in 2022 the old values applied. For boekjaar 2023 the old values apply by default, and the raised values are optional from 1 January 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html
- Stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630 in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Willekeurige afschrijving 2023 allows up to 50% of qualifying new assets, with costs made in 2023 and use before 1 January 2026. The checked 2022 material does not state a 2022 figure, so no old-to-new comparison can be made from the checked sources. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023
- No more betalingskorting for paying a voorlopige aanslag Vpb in one sum from 1 January 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023
- Gebruikelijk loon escape: in 2022 still on the 75% basis with the € 48.000 floor, in 2023 the comparable wage itself. A vaststellingsovereenkomst made on the old basis keeps its 100% wage for its term. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Zvw route for an uninsured DGA: corrected to the BV withholding the bijdrage on net wage, not an aanslag. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Dividend stripping article: corrected from art. 4 lid 3 to art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- The Besluit winstsplitsing, spoedreparatie en artikel 10a, Stcrt. 2022, 11028, is named as withdrawn by the 2024 Verzamelbesluit. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

Online extension to 1 November 2024 can be requested from 1 March to 1 June 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting
- Record a dividend decision and a dividendnota. - Keep records for seven years. Keep onroerende zaken records for nine years following the year use began. Keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01
