# Pensioen, lijfrente en AOV 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the lijfrente (annuity) rules for a sole trader in 2023, the jaarruimte (annual deduction room) and reserveringsruimte (catch-up room), the lijfrente product conditions and their deadlines, the end of the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve, FOR) toevoeging, the stakingswinst (profit on cessation) conversion and the deductibility of AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) premiums. 2023 is the year the Wet toekomst pensioenen (Future Pensions Act, Wtp) widened the lijfrente rules. The text of art. 3.127 as valid from 1 July 2023 gives a 30% jaarruimte percentage, a ten-year look-back, an age window of the AOW age plus five years and a flat reserveringsruimte maximum of € 38.000, and the Belastingdienst states that this widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so these values hold for the whole year even though the text valid on 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3%. Every value in section 2 is enacted law, so no bill or announcement affects the 2023 return.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

### Jaarruimte percentage

The percentage applies to a taxpayer who has a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets that taxpayer deduct lijfrentepremies up to a percentage of the premiegrondslag (premium base), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). For 2023 the percentage is 30% in the text of 1 July 2023. The text of 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3% ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Belastingdienst page on the Wtp states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" and confirms that the lijfrente widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so the whole of 2023 uses 30% ([Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The calculation for a self-employed person without pension accrual is 30% x (min(income of the previous year, € 128.810) minus € 13.646). The income of the previous year matters because the ruimte of a year rests on the income of the year before. Up to the text of 1 January 2023 the outcome was also reduced by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve (additions to the old-age reserve) under lid 1 and lid 4 sub b. From the text of 1 July 2023 lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 adds its own premiegrondslag and reduction rules for 2023 and 2024 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Maximum jaarruimte

The maximum for 2023 is € 34.550. It applies when the income of the previous year is at or above the aftoppingsgrens of € 128.810, so that the whole capped base minus the franchise is used. The Ministry table for 2024 prints € 34.550 and carries the footnote that the 2023 amounts changed retroactively for the Wtp ([Ministry, belangrijkste wijzigingen belastingen 2024](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 30% x (€ 128.810 minus € 13.646) = 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This is the one Corrected value in this file. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Maximum jaarruimte under the pre-Wtp rules

The amount of € 15.317 comes from the Ministry table of 9 January 2023 and rests on the 13,3% percentage that the text of art. 3.127 valid on 1 January 2023 still had ([Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-a585ce5bd38ddc5d9e2adb1fd6f796a7cbaaa775/pdf)). The retroactive Wtp changes superseded it, so a 2023 return uses the ceiling of € 34.550 and not € 15.317. The € 15.317 row is kept here only so a reader who finds the older table can place it.

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

The aftoppingsgrens for 2023 is € 128.810. Art. 3.127 lid 3 builds the premiegrondslag as the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) winst uit onderneming (profit from the business), (b) belastbaar loon (taxable wages), (c) belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (taxable result from other activities) and (d) belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen (taxable periodic payments), counted up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (the first-mentioned amount, the aftoppingsgrens) and then reduced by the laatstvermelde bedrag (the last-mentioned amount, the franchise) ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The amount is the same in the text of 1 January 2023 and in both Ministry tables. The wording of part a differs between the texts. In the 2020 to 1 January 2023 texts it is winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek. From the 1 July 2023 text it is winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek. For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 keeps the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Art. 3.127 lid 5 gives one relief: on (partial) staking the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. The amounts counted are the amounts of the previous calendar year, so the 2023 ruimte rests on 2022 income.

### Franchise premiegrondslag

The franchise for 2023 is € 13.646 ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). It is subtracted after the cap of € 128.810 is applied to the four income components of art. 3.127 lid 3. The subtraction happens once, on the capped total, and not per income component. The same income year applies as for the aftoppingsgrens, so the 2023 franchise is subtracted from 2022 income.

### Factor A multiplier

The multiplier for 2023 is 6,27. In the 2023 texts, art. 3.127 lid 4 reduces the uitkomst of lid 1 by 6,27 times the aangroei (increase) of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd (service time), plus nettopensioen (net pension) premiums divided by the nettofactor (net factor) ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The 2023 jaarruimte rests on pension accrual in 2022. The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25 and not in art. 3.127. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964 (the old norms), and that old text also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023, so accrual in the first half of 2023 stays under the old reduction rule for later jaarruimte. From the text of 1 January 2024 the reduction covers the premiums ingelegd (paid in) in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen (old-age pension) and partnerpensioen (partner's pension), excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor. For a fixed premium per dienstjaar under art. 38r the formula is A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor, and that formula applies with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023 ([art. 10a.25, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Look-back window

The window for 2023 is ten years, covering the jaarruimte of 2013 to 2022. Art. 3.127 lid 2 says that unused jaarruimte of the preceding period may be used on request in the aangifte (income tax return), oldest unused year first ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The text of 1 January 2023 said "zeven jaar", the text of 1 July 2023 says "tien jaar". FISIN 2023 prints the window as 2013 to 2022 ([FISIN 2023, uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The Belastingdienst advises using the reserveringsruimte before the jaarruimte, so that older unused ruimte does not lapse. The same page notes that unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027, which shows the ten-year window in practice ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum

The maximum for 2023 is € 38.000 flat. The text of art. 3.127 lid 2 valid from 1 July 2023 names one flat euro maximum, with no percentage and no age split ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). FISIN 2023 and the Ministry table for 2024 agree ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). The maximum applies to the part of the deduction that comes from unused jaarruimte of 2013 to 2022. That part must be requested in the aangifte and is used oldest year first, before the jaarruimte of 2023.

### Reserveringsruimte under the pre-Wtp rules

The amounts of € 8.065 and € 15.922 come from the text of art. 3.127 lid 2 valid on 1 January 2023 and from the Ministry table of 9 January 2023 ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). That text capped the reserveringsruimte at 17% of the premiegrondslag and at a euro amount, raised to the higher amount of € 15.922 for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year was at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. The Wtp replaced both amounts with the flat € 38.000, retroactively for 2023, so a 2023 return uses € 38.000.

### Age limit jaarruimte

The taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age (state pension age) at the start of the year. The text of art. 3.127 valid from 1 July 2023, applied to all of 2023, states this, and FISIN 2023 uses the same wording ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The text of 1 January 2023 still said that the taxpayer must not yet have reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Belastingdienst adds that lijfrente premiums may be paid up to 5 years after the AOW age ([Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The age test is done at the start of the year, so a taxpayer who passes the limit during the year still fails the test for that year.

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

The cap for 2023 is € 24.168 per year. It applies to the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 c. The conditions are at least five years of termijnen (instalments), a start not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, and a yearly amount up to the statutory cap. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment ([art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). FISIN 2023 adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The cap also appears in the Ministry tables ([Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). The regular oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 a has its first termijn at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five and pays until death. FISIN 2023 gives the example that with AOW age in 2030 the start must be at the latest on 31 December 2035. For a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht the term is at least 20 years plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year.

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The cap for 2023 is € 63.288 per year ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The overbruggingslijfrente (bridge annuity) is only available for lijfrentetegoeden (annuity balances) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, on reaching the AOW age or on starting a pensioen (pension). The same amount of € 63.288 appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text, so the FISIN chapters are the working source. The cap is a yearly maximum per year of payment, so a planned yearly payment above it is not covered by this product.

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

The tier a maximum for 2023 is € 510.970 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the stakingswinst (profit on cessation), capped at a tier maximum minus the already built provisions. Tier a applies when the taxpayer is at the most five years below the AOW age at the moment of staking, or is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt (occupationally disabled) with the lijfrente starting within six months, or stakes by death. FISIN 2023 describes tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches 2023 because the AOW age was 66 years and 10 months ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The law keeps the wording "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". The provisions that reduce the tier maximum are the value of pension rights charged to profit, the amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts and, from 2023, the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

The tier b maximum for 2023 is € 255.495 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Tier b applies when the taxpayer is at the most fifteen years below the AOW age, or when the lijfrente starts immediately. The same provision deduction applies as in tier a, so the effective room is the tier amount minus the already built provisions.

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

The tier c maximum for 2023 is € 127.755 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Tier c covers all other cases. The deduction is never more than the stakingswinst itself, and never more than the tier amount minus the already built provisions. The payment can on choice be counted in the year of staking when it is made within six months after the year end, which for 2023 means before 1 July 2024 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

The amount for 2023 is € 4.898. Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows afkoop (commutation, a lump-sum surrender) of a small lijfrente in one payment when the yearly amount stays below this figure ([art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). The Ministry table for 2024 agrees. The background rule is that a lijfrente which stops meeting the conditions, for example through gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop or deblokkering, is taxed on its value and in most cases carries revisierente (a penalty interest). The small-lijfrente exception is the route that lets a small policy end in one payment without a prohibited act ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

The cap for 2023 is € 45.722 ([art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes afkoop not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer (policyholder) or gerechtigde (beneficiary) is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt (long-term occupationally disabled) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached the AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap of € 45.722 or, if that is higher, within the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn (instalment) for the tax rules. All three conditions must hold, so the ministerial disability conditions and the AOW age limit are tested as well as the amount.

### FOR toevoeging

No toevoeging (addition) to the oudedagsreserve is possible from 1 January 2023 ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The Ministry 2023 report section "Uitfasering oudedagsreserve" agrees with the phase-out. Up to 2022 a toevoeging required the urencriterium (hours test) and that the taxpayer had not yet reached the AOW age at the start of the year, which is art. 3.67. Art. 3.68 set the toevoeging as a percentage of the winst, capped at a euro amount, reduced by the pension premiums charged to the winst and not above the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) ([art. 3.67 and 3.68, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). An existing FOR stays under the old rules. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70 to 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows an afname (reduction) of the FOR by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at staking, at the AOW age at the start of the year, or when the urencriterium fails this year and last year. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the onderneming and takes the FOR over on request ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).

### FOR transition in the jaarruimte

The transition sits in art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11, not in art. 3.127, and it applies for 2023 and 2024 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For a taxpayer with a FOR the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek. For 2023 and 2024 the jaarruimte is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the afneming at the end of that previous year. The deduction for a FOR-lijfrente conversion is art. 10a.29 lid 12, up to the afname under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a. Art. 10a.29 lid 13 counts the FOR in the stakingswinst provisions that reduce the art. 3.129 tier maximum. A reader who finds the transition described under art. 3.127 lid 10 and 11 should use the art. 10a.29 location instead.

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Such premiums are fully deductible. Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval (periodic payments in case of disability, illness or accident) for the taxpayer uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenditure for income provisions). The article contains no ruimte cap and FISIN 2023 says "volledig aftrekken" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Four exclusions apply. Not deductible are premiums that the employer takes into account for loonheffing (payroll tax), mandatory Ziektewet and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen (capital insurances), and Zvw (Health Insurance Act) premiums. The four exclusions and section 15.4 of FISIN are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. For an ondernemer (self-employed person) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" (income from a former employment) with loonheffing withheld as voorheffing (prepayment). The Belastingdienst gives the rule of thumb that a premium which is not deductible means a payout which is not taxed, and the reverse ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

Such a premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). This covers policies that pay a lump sum, such as kapitaalverzekeringen. The rule of thumb applies here in the other direction. Because the premium is not deductible, the payout stays outside box 1 and is only a box 3 asset. A taxpayer who switches treatment between the two policy types breaks the link between deduction and taxation, so the policy type decides the whole treatment ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

### Payment deadline FOR or stakingswinst conversion

Premiums count in the year in which they are paid or verrekend (settled) under art. 3.130 lid 1 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Premiums for the stakingswinst conversion of art. 3.129, for the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, for the FOR conversion of art. 3.128 may on choice be counted in the year itself when they are paid within six months after the year end, under art. 3.130 lid 2. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. For 2023 the deadline for these choices is a payment before 1 July 2024. Two further limits apply. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible, but they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Lijfrente einddatum termijn

A lijfrente that reaches its einddatum (end date) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). FISIN 2023 uses the word einddatum where FISIN 2022 used ingangsdatum (start date). The official examples are that an einddatum in 2022 means convert or start by 31 December 2023 and an einddatum in 2023 by 31 December 2024. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden (special circumstances). The three product forms are the lijfrenteverzekering (annuity insurance), the lijfrenterekening (annuity account) and the lijfrentebeleggingsrecht (annuity investment right). For the oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 a the first termijn must be at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, and the payments run until death ([art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions, for example through gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop or deblokkering, the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente is due. A refund of a deducted premium is a negatieve uitgave (negative expenditure) without revisierente, and for a lijfrente only if the undoing happens within 30 days ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Nabestaandenlijfrente termijn

A nabestaandenlijfrente (survivor's annuity) must start within the year of death plus 2 calendar years. FISIN 2023 words the deadline that way, and the outcome is the same as the 2022 wording of 2 full calendar years ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The official example is that a death in 2022 means a start by 31 December 2024. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden. The product conditions of art. 3.125 lid 1 b are that for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner the nabestaandenlijfrente ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and that for a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht the term is at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. A lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind (adult disabled child or grandchild) is deductible without a pensioentekort under art. 3.124 lid 1 b ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Jaarruimte with a profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: winst uit onderneming over 2022 of € 60.000, no pension accrual in 2022, no FOR, no income from the other three components of art. 3.127 lid 3.

- Step 1, cap the income at the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 60.000, € 128.810) = € 60.000.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000 minus € 13.646 = € 46.354.
- Step 3, apply the 30% percentage: € 46.354 x 30% = € 13.906,20.

The jaarruimte 2023 is € 13.906,20. A lijfrentepremie paid in 2023 up to that amount is deductible when the other conditions are met. A premium above that amount is not deductible, and the excess is taken into account when the uitkering is taxed. Source of the method and the figures: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Example 2: Jaarruimte at the maximum, profit of € 200.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: winst uit onderneming over 2022 of € 200.000, no pension accrual.

- Step 1: min(€ 200.000, € 128.810) = € 128.810.
- Step 2: € 128.810 minus € 13.646 = € 115.164.
- Step 3: € 115.164 x 30% = 34.549,2.

The Ministry table prints the maximum jaarruimte 2023 as € 34.550. Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This is the rounding open point in section 7. Use the printed € 34.550 as the ceiling. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf).

### Example 3: Reserveringsruimte with unused years (Calculated example)

Inputs: unused jaarruimte of € 5.000 from 2019 and € 3.000 from 2021, jaarruimte 2023 of € 13.906,20 from example 1, and lijfrentepremies paid in 2023 of € 20.000.

- Step 1, list the unused years oldest first: 2019 with € 5.000, then 2021 with € 3.000.
- Step 2, total the reserveringsruimte: € 5.000 plus € 3.000 = € 8.000.
- Step 3, test the flat maximum: € 8.000 is below € 38.000, so the whole € 8.000 fits.
- Step 4, total the deduction room: € 13.906,20 plus € 8.000 = € 21.906,20.
- Step 5, apply the premiums: € 20.000 is below € 21.906,20, so the full € 20.000 is deductible.
- Step 6, the remainder: € 21.906,20 minus € 20.000 = € 1.906,20 of the 2023 jaarruimte stays unused and remains available for a later year within the ten-year window.

The Belastingdienst advises using the reserveringsruimte before the jaarruimte, so the older 2019 and 2021 ruimte is used first and does not lapse. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 4: Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente within and above the cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: two policies, each with termijnen starting in the AOW year and running six years.

- Case A, yearly uitkering of € 20.000. This is below the cap of € 24.168, so the cap is respected and the premiums are deductible within the jaarruimte and reserveringsruimte.
- Case B, yearly uitkering of € 30.000. This is above the cap of € 24.168, so the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. Six years is not 20 years, so the premiums of case B are not deductible.

The term of six years meets the minimum of five years in both cases, and a start in the AOW year is allowed because the start may not be before the year of AOW age. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 5: Overbruggingslijfrente cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: a pre-2006 lijfrentetegoed and a planned yearly payment of € 70.000.

- Step 1, take the yearly maximum: € 63.288.
- Step 2, compare the planned payment: € 70.000 minus € 63.288 = € 6.712 above the cap.
- Step 3, a planned payment of € 60.000 stays within the cap, because € 60.000 is below € 63.288.

The product is only for balances built up before 2006, and it ends at choice in the year of turning 65, on reaching the AOW age or on starting a pensioen. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 6: Stakingswinst conversion in tier c (Calculated example)

Inputs: stakingswinst of € 200.000, taxpayer aged 50 at staking, no lijfrente starting immediately, so the case falls in tier c with a maximum of € 127.755. Already built provisions are earlier deductions under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 of € 40.000, a FOR at the start of 2023 of € 30.000 and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 of € 10.000.

- Step 1, total the provisions: € 40.000 plus € 30.000 plus € 10.000 = € 80.000.
- Step 2, subtract the provisions from the tier maximum: € 127.755 minus € 80.000 = € 47.755.
- Step 3, test the stakingswinst: the deduction is also never more than the stakingswinst of € 200.000, so the binding cap is € 47.755.

A tier a or tier b amount would require the age, disability or immediate-start conditions of those tiers, which this taxpayer does not meet. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 7: Afkoop of a small lijfrente and the disability cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: two situations.

- Case A, a yearly uitkering of € 4.000. This is below € 4.898, so the small-lijfrente exception of art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d applies and the afkoop in one payment is allowed.
- Case B, a yearly uitkering of € 6.000 with langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid and a taxpayer below the AOW age. The small-lijfrente exception does not apply here. The disability cap of € 45.722 applies, so an afkoop received in the year of € 45.000 stays within the cap, since € 45.722 minus € 45.000 = € 722 of room remains. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn.

The alternative cap of the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year applies only when it is higher than € 45.722. Sources: [art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 8: FOR transition in the jaarruimte (Calculated example)

Inputs: winst uit onderneming over 2022 of € 60.000, a FOR at the start of 2023 of € 30.000, no afneming of the FOR in 2023, a toevoeging over 2022 of € 9.000 and an afneming at the end of 2022 of € 3.000.

- Step 1, fix part a of the premiegrondslag under art. 10a.29 lid 10: the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and vóór ondernemersaftrek is € 60.000.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000 minus € 13.646 = € 46.354.
- Step 3, apply 30%: € 46.354 x 30% = € 13.906,20.
- Step 4, compute the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the afneming at the end of that year: € 9.000 minus € 3.000 = € 6.000.
- Step 5, reduce the jaarruimte: € 13.906,20 minus € 6.000 = € 7.906,20.

The jaarruimte 2023 is € 7.906,20 for this FOR holder. The reduction applies for 2023 and 2024. Source: [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Example 9: AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen against a lump-sum policy (Calculated example)

Inputs: two policies, each with a premium of € 3.600 in 2023, both covering invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer.

- Policy A pays periodic uitkeringen. The premium is fully deductible with no ruimte cap, so the deduction is € 3.600 and the later benefits are taxed as inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking with loonheffing withheld as voorheffing.
- Policy B pays a lump sum. The premium is not deductible, so the deduction is € 0. The later payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

The rule of thumb is that a premium which is not deductible means a payout which is not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen) and [Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Example 10: Deadlines from FISIN 2023 (Official example)

- An einddatum in 2022 means convert or start the lijfrente by 31 December 2023. An einddatum in 2023 means convert or start by 31 December 2024.
- A death in 2022 means start the nabestaandenlijfrente by 31 December 2024.
- With AOW age in 2030 the oudedagslijfrente must start at the latest on 31 December 2035.
- For a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht the term is at least 20 years plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year, so a start at 63 with AOW age 67 means 20 plus 4 = 24 years.

Source: [FISIN 2023, uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record AOV premiums for a policy with periodic uitkeringen as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen in box 1, and not as a business cost of the onderneming. They are fully deductible with no ruimte cap ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Do not deduct premiums for AOV policies that pay a lump sum. Keep those policies in the box 3 records, because the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Book a mandatory beroepspensioen premium for an ondernemer as a business cost with a restriction, and not as a lijfrentepremie ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book lijfrentepremies in the year in which they are paid or verrekend. For the stakingswinst conversion and the FOR conversion a payment before 1 July 2024 may on choice be counted in 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Keep the premium records and the ruimte calculation together. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible, they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep a per-year schedule of unused jaarruimte for 2013 to 2022, because the reserveringsruimte must be requested in the aangifte and the oldest unused year is used first ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Record an existing FOR under the old rules. No toevoeging can be made in 2023. Record the FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction under art. 10a.29 lid 12, up to the afname under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Keep the policy documents with the einddatum and the death date records for a nabestaandenlijfrente. The conversion or start must happen within 1 full calendar year, and the nabestaanden start deadline is the year of death plus 2 calendar years. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Periodic AOV benefits arrive as inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking with loonheffing withheld as voorheffing, so record them together with the withheld loonheffing ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Maximum jaarruimte 2023, rounding. The Ministry table prints € 34.550. Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 30% x (€ 128.810 minus € 13.646) = 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This file follows the Ministry figure of € 34.550. A Belastingdienst calculation (hulpmiddel) would show the rounding the Belastingdienst uses. URLs: [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf) and [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Two law texts for 2023. The text of art. 3.127 valid on 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3%, with a maximum jaarruimte of € 15.317, a reserveringsruimte of € 8.065 or € 15.922, a seven-year look-back and an age limit of not yet AOW age. The text valid from 1 July 2023 reads 30%, with € 34.550, € 38.000, ten years and AOW age plus five. The Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" and confirms the lijfrente widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so this file follows the Wtp values for the whole of 2023. URLs: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Proposed items that do not apply to 2023. The BAZ bill (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, 36 912) is proposed and had docket status "In afwachting nota nav verslag" on 2026-10-11. Bill art. 16 proposes a wachttijd of 104 weken, the MvT proposes a benefit of 70% of the grondslag with the grondslag at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so at most the minimumloon, and a premium set yearly by UWV estimated at about 5,4% of winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag. The MvT examples use a maximum grondslag of € 38.000 and about € 171 gross per month, and assume 1 January 2030 for entry into force by koninklijk besluit. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, and UWV benefits would carry loonheffing. Private policies keep the current tax treatment. The docket summary says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16. URLs: [dossier 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313), [bill text](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).
- Proposed Belastingplan 2027 items. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3 at the start of 2027 to 2032, so it would stay € 137.800, and a limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, for the lijfrente maxima. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11. URL: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Adjacent proposed track. The Zelfstandigenwet had an internetconsultatie until 29 October 2026, with a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets leading to a "veilige haven", and a beoogde inwerkingtreding of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension. URL: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
