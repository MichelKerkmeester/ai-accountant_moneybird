# Kosten en auto 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the deduction of business costs (zakelijke kosten) and the car rules for a sole trader in tax year 2023. It runs through the drempel (threshold) for gemengde kosten (mixed costs such as food, drink and representation) against the 80% election, the kilometre allowance for a private vehicle, bijtelling (the profit addition for private use of a business asset), the workspace, telephone, computer and fine exclusions, and the studiekosten position. The most important 2023 figures are the drempel of € 5.100, the kilometre allowance of € 0,21 per kilometre, the 22% standard bijtelling with the 35% youngtimer rule and the zero-emission reduction of 6% capped at € 1.800 for cars first admitted in 2023. All 2023 amounts are enacted law, read in the statute text valid on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

### 3.1 Gemengde kosten drempel

The drempel applies to a sole trader and also to a resultaatgenieter. Costs connected with voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen (food, drink and stimulants), representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible up to a yearly amount of € 5.100 in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods and services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No onttrekking is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). Voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien, drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank, and genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken generally fall under representatie. Food costs that qualify as loonkosten are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals to staff the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, sections 3.2 and 3.3). The drempel is not time-proportioned: it is not reduced when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a boekjaar longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either (same besluit, section 3.1). For a BV, art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 replaces the fixed amount with 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher and sets the election at 73,5% instead of 80%, applied per body, and only once at the parent inside a fiscale eenheid (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).

### 3.2 Keuze in plaats van de drempel

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters. The choice covers the same posts as the drempel in 3.1, so it is one yearly choice over the total of those posts. Which route gives more depends on the total cost level, and the comparison is worked out in section 4. For a BV the election percentage is 73,5% (art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01).

### 3.3 Kosten met een gemengd karakter

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive). Examples with only a business part or with a restriction are huur and onderhoud of a pand split into a business and a private part, telefoonkosten, a car of the business, congressen, studiereizen, symposia, seminars en excursies, zakenlunches en diners, recepties and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business such as a rijbewijs, health costs such as pedicure and zorgverzekering, and personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding (work clothing) is deductible.

### 3.4 Reis- en verblijfkosten congressen en cursussen

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. The same travel and stay also count as one of the gemengde kosten posts of art. 3.15 lid 1 through art. 3.15 lid 2, so both rules have to be tested. Studiekosten themselves stay deductible as a business cost only to maintain existing vakkennis (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers).

### 3.5 Kilometervergoeding

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, so an auto, motor or fiets, are limited to a fixed € 0,21 per business kilometre in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The amount is all-in: brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed. A vehicle of the ondernemingsvermogen instead follows the balance sheet and, for private use, the onttrekking rules of 3.9 to 3.14.

### 3.6 Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview. This route sits next to the € 0,21 kilometre allowance in 3.5, which applies to a private or privately rented vehicle.

### 3.7 Verhuiskosten

Moving costs for the enterprise are the cost of moving the inboedel (household contents) plus € 7750 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The rule is unchanged in every year 2020 to 2027.

### 3.8 Dubbele huisvesting

Under the same provision, the cost of housing outside the place of residence is deductible for at most two years (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The rule is unchanged from 2020 to 2027.

### 3.9 Bijtelling auto, standaard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year, and woon-werkverkeer is not private (lid 3). Auto means a personenauto or a bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is almost exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private kilometres times the actual cost per kilometre are settled instead. For such a bestelauto a verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible (lid 6 to 10). The waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, described by the Belastingdienst as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). The RDW site carries this value for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer replaces it. The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation. A laadpaal for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price.

### 3.10 Auto met eerste toelating tot en met 31 december 2016

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars in 3.11, because the two minimums apply to different cars.

### 3.11 Youngtimer

At 35% of the waarde van de auto sits the regime for a car that was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The threshold is 15 years in 2023. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer (WEV) replaces the catalogue price. When the threshold is crossed during the year the year is split by month. The 35% works on the WEV, while the 25% minimum of art. 10a.4 works on cars first admitted up to 31 December 2016 that are not old cars.

### 3.12 Zero-emissieauto, eerste toelating 2023

For a car with 0 g CO2 per kilometre the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). For a car first admitted in 2023 the reduction is 6% with a maximum of € 1.800, which amounts to 16% up to € 30.000. Cars on waterstof are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead, and the 1 kilowattpiek test is the statute text for 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### 3.13 Vast percentage vanaf eerste toelating

The reduction that applied at the first admission of the car stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after the first admission (art. 3.20 lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). After those 60 months the rules then in force apply. A zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 therefore keeps the 2022 reduction of 6% capped at € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000, during 2023 and the rest of its 60 month period.

### 3.14 500 km regel

A car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year (art. 3.20 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). With a rittenregistratie (trip registration) or another way of showing at most 500 private kilometres the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, although trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto). Woon-werkverkeer is not private use for this test (lid 3).

### 3.15 Bijtelling fiets

When a fiets of the business, including an e-bike or a speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power, is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, so that outcome is also open for 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.16 Studiekostenaftrek

In 2023 the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deduction) for studiekosten is nil, except for the prestatiebeurs case in 3.17 to 3.19 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven were deductible above a drempel of € 250 and, outside the standaardstudieperiode, up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349, and in the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). Studiekosten stay deductible as a business cost when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only serve to maintain existing vakkennis, and costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, although the boundary is hard to draw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.17 Prestatiebeurs, mbo

When the diplomatermijn, usually 10 years, has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the prestatiebeurs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). For mbo the cap is € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for a part year. When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower, the lower amount applies. FISIN 2023 gives an official example, worked out in section 4. The case is verified for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 in FISIN (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).

### 3.18 Prestatiebeurs, hbo of wo

The same case applies with the hbo or wo caps of € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for a part year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower than the cap, the lower amount applies.

### 3.19 Drempel in de prestatiebeurszaak

The € 250 drempel applies per person in the prestatiebeurs case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).

### 3.20 STAP-budget

The STAP-budget was the non-fiscal replacement for the abolished studiekostenaftrek and took effect at the same moment as the abolition, 1 January 2022. Art. 29 of the regeling links its start to the Wet afschaffing, and Stb. 2021, 349 states that it applies from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The maximum subsidy is € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). Corrected: the research said at most € 1.000 per year, the regeling text says per scholing. The regeling is marked Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024, so 2023 is the last year of the scheme.

### 3.21 STAP-aanvraagvensters

There are six application windows per calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).

### 3.22 Werkruimte thuis

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party, so a loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen. The lid 14 limit for an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf applies from 1 January 2025 and does not change 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).

### 3.23 Telefoonabonnement thuis

Telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The practical rule is that a private telephone subscription is not deductible but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible but call costs for private calls on it are not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100%. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.16 lid 11).

### 3.24 Kleding, literatuur en persoonlijke verzorging

Not deductible: literatuur except vakliteratuur (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b), kleding except werkkleding (onderdeel c) with werkkleding defined in lid 5, and persoonlijke verzorging (onderdeel d) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The overview page keeps the same line and names a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business as not deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.25 Privécomputers en apparatuur

Costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are therefore 0% deductible. Whether a laptop or computer belongs to the business follows vermogensetikettering, see 3.29.

### 3.26 Betaling aan de partner

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11).

### 3.27 Geldboetes en dwangsommen

Not deductible are costs connected with geldboeten from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten and EU fines (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c), costs connected with a misdrijf for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and dwangsommen under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (onderdeel i) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines, and the overview page prints Geldboetes 0% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1). Timing: costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible, costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible, and for a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught. Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking, are excluded (same besluit, section 2).

### 3.28 Bedrijfsmiddel onder € 450

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur or onderhoud van de auto (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they were made for the work, and receipts and invoices must be kept.

### 3.29 Vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddelen

A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen (obligatory private assets), 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, and in between it is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once. A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid. Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking. A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule, which the Kennisgroep applies to the youngtimer change that takes effect on 1 January 2027 (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/).

### 3.30 Vermogensetikettering auto

A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. A car used 90% or more privately is verplicht privévermogen. All other cases are keuzevermogen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). Once the car is in the ondernemingsvermogen, private use is settled with the onttrekking of 3.9 to 3.14.

### 3.31 BTW op de privéauto

VAT on the use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto). Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, although it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA is not the btw-ondernemer but counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance.

### 3.32 Kostengrondslag en btw

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, which is the KOR case, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted, also not VAT paid in another EU country, earlier deductions may need herziening, the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The KOR threshold history by year belongs to the btw topic.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

The calculated examples use invented round amounts. Every step uses only the section 2 figures. Every product is written with two decimals before rounding. No step in these examples requires rounding.

### Example 1: gemengde kosten, drempel against 80% (Calculated example)

Costs for voedsel, drank en representatie of € 8.000,00 in 2023.

- Drempel route: € 8.000,00 minus € 5.100,00 = € 2.900,00 deductible.
- 80% route: 80% x € 8.000,00 = € 6.400,00 deductible.
- Difference: € 6.400,00 minus € 2.900,00 = € 3.500,00, so the 80% election is better at this cost level (art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 2: crossover point of the two routes (Calculated example)

Same posts, € 25.500,00 in 2023.

- Drempel route: € 25.500,00 minus € 5.100,00 = € 20.400,00.
- 80% route: 80% x € 25.500,00 = € 20.400,00.
- The two routes are equal here, so € 25.500,00 is the crossover point. Below € 25.500,00 the 80% election gives more, above it the drempel route gives more.

### Example 3: kilometre allowance (Calculated example)

A privately owned car drives 6.000 business kilometres in 2023.

- Deduction: 6.000 x € 0,21 = € 1.260,00.
- Fuel, insurance, tol and parkeren of € 2.100,00 cannot be deducted on top, because the amount is all-in. The deduction stays € 1.260,00 (art. 3.15 lid 6, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 4: openbaar vervoer and taxi (Calculated example)

Train tickets for business trips of € 940,00 and a business taxi ride of € 60,00.

- Deduction: 100% x € 940,00 = € 940,00, plus 100% x € 60,00 = € 60,00, together € 1.000,00.
- The taxpayer keeps the tickets and the OV-chipkaart trip overview as proof (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### Example 5: congress cap (Calculated example)

A taxpayer visits a congres abroad that is not necessary because of the nature of the work. Travel and stay cost € 1.850,00.

- Cap: € 1.850,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 350,00 is not deductible.
- The remaining € 1.500,00 is part of the gemengde kosten posts of art. 3.15 and is subject to the drempel or the 80% election (art. 3.16 lid 2 f and lid 8, and art. 3.15 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 6: verhuiskosten (Calculated example)

Moving the inboedel to a new business location costs € 2.500,00.

- Deduction: € 2.500,00 plus € 7.750,00 = € 10.250,00 (art. 3.17 lid 1 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 7: bijtelling capped at the autokosten (Calculated example)

A car in the ondernemingsvermogen has a waarde of € 32.000,00, was first taken into use in 2019 and is used privately above 500 km. Total autokosten including depreciation are € 5.500,00.

- Standard onttrekking: 22% x € 32.000,00 = € 7.040,00.
- The onttrekking is capped at the total autokosten of € 5.500,00. The amount taken into account is € 5.500,00 (art. 3.20 lid 1 a and lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 8: the 500 km rule (Calculated example)

A rittenregistratie shows 460 private kilometres and 31.000 business kilometres in 2023.

- Private use is at most 500 km a year, so the onttrekking is nil and no bijtelling is added.
- Woon-werkverkeer does not count as private use for this test. With a keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem the registration is assumed sluitend (art. 3.20 lid 1 and lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 9: zero-emissieauto first admitted in 2023 (Calculated example)

A car with 0 g CO2 per kilometre, first admitted in 2023, catalogue price € 40.000,00.

- Standard: 22% x € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00.
- Reduction for first admission in 2023: 6% x € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00, capped at € 1.800,00, so € 1.800,00.
- Onttrekking: € 8.800,00 minus € 1.800,00 = € 7.000,00.
- Control through the 16% up to € 30.000,00 reading: 16% x € 30.000,00 = € 4.800,00 plus 22% x € 10.000,00 = € 2.200,00, together € 7.000,00 (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 10: zero-emissieauto first admitted in 2022, used in 2023 (Calculated example)

The same car, catalogue price € 40.000,00, but first admitted in 2022, so the 2022 reduction stays fixed for 60 months.

- Standard: 22% x € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00.
- Reduction: 6% x € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00, capped at € 2.100,00, which is 16% up to € 35.000,00, so € 2.100,00.
- Onttrekking: € 8.800,00 minus € 2.100,00 = € 6.700,00.
- Control: 16% x € 35.000,00 = € 5.600,00 plus 22% x € 5.000,00 = € 1.100,00, together € 6.700,00 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 11: fiets of the business (Calculated example)

An e-bike of the business has a consumentenadviesprijs of € 2.400,00 and is also used privately. The business bore the full € 2.400,00.

- Onttrekking: 7% x € 2.400,00 = € 168,00, capped at the costs borne of € 2.400,00, so € 168,00 (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 12: prestatiebeurs, mbo (Official example)

FISIN 2023 example. An mbo study ran from August 2013 to July 2017 and the diplomatermijn ends in August 2023. The fixed amounts are € 706,00, € 1.693,00 and € 988,00 against actual prestatiebeurs amounts of € 333,00, € 800,00 and € 467,00. For each period the actual prestatiebeurs was lower than the cap, so the lower amounts are used.

- Base: € 333,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 467,00 = € 1.600,00.
- Deduction after the drempel: € 1.600,00 minus € 250,00 = € 1.350,00 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).

### Example 13: STAP-budget (Calculated example)

A scholing costs € 1.200,00 including btw and is claimed in one of the six application windows.

- Maximum subsidy: € 1.000,00 including btw per scholing.
- If the maximum is granted, € 1.000,00 of the € 1.200,00 is covered and € 1.200,00 minus € 1.000,00 = € 200,00 remains for the taxpayer (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).

### Example 14: werkruimte thuis (Calculated example)

A taxpayer works from a loose desk in the living room and spends € 1.800,00 on a desk, a chair and a kast.

- The workspace is not zelfstandig, so the costs are not deductible and € 0,00 of the € 1.800,00 is deducted.
- With an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities and a space that could be rented to a third party, and with the income test met, the space would be zelfstandig and the costs would fall under the workspace rules (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 15: telefoonabonnement thuis (Calculated example)

A private telephone subscription costs € 360,00 for the year and business calls through it cost € 75,00.

- The subscription of € 360,00 is not deductible.
- The € 75,00 of business calls is deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### Example 16: the € 450 rule (Calculated example)

Assume the laptop and printer belong to the ondernemingsvermogen. A taxpayer buys a laptop of € 1.200,00 for the business and a printer of € 350,00. Both are used longer than a year.

- The laptop of € 1.200,00 is depreciated, taking the restwaarde into account.
- The printer of € 350,00 is deducted in one go in the year of purchase.
- If the btw on both is deductible in the btw-aangifte, the amounts are taken excluding btw. Under the KOR, they are taken including btw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### Example 17: btw correction on the private car (Calculated example)

A private car used for business has a catalogue price including btw and bpm of € 30.000,00. There is no sluitende kilometeradministratie, so all VAT is deducted during the year.

- Jaarcorrectie: 1,5% x € 30.000,00 = € 450,00 in rubriek 1d.
- If only € 400,00 of VAT was deducted on use and maintenance during the year, the correction cannot exceed € 400,00 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### Example 18: geldboete (Calculated example)

The car-rental company passes on a Wahv verkeersboete of € 340,00 for a business trip.

- The € 340,00 is not deductible and cannot be booked as a business cost (art. 3.14 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

- Gemengde kosten drempel: € 4.800 in 2022 to € 5.100 in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Kilometervergoeding: € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Zero-emissie bijtelling for cars first admitted in the year: 2022 gave 6% off capped at € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000, and 2023 gives 6% off capped at € 1.800, which is 16% up to € 30.000 (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 keeps the 2022 reduction for 60 months from the first day of the month after its first admission, so in 2023 the 2022 amounts still apply to that car (art. 3.20 lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Studiekostenaftrek: 2022 was the first year of the abolition of the persoonsgebonden aftrek for scholingsuitgaven, and art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). 2023 continues that position, nil except the prestatiebeurs case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).
- STAP-budget: started on 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In 2023 the maximum stays € 1.000 including btw per scholing and 2023 is the last year of the scheme, because the regeling is marked Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).
- Unchanged from 2022 to 2023: the € 1.500 cap on reis en verblijf for congressen en cursussen, verhuiskosten of the inboedel plus € 7750 and dubbele huisvesting for at most two years, the 22% standard bijtelling, the 25% minimum for cars first admitted up to 31 December 2016, the 35% youngtimer rule with the 15 year threshold, the 7% bijtelling for the fiets, the workspace 0% rule with the art. 3.16 lid 1 exceptions, the 0% for telefoonabonnement thuis and for private computers en apparatuur, and the 0% for geldboetes and dwangsommen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The 2023 statute keeps the same rule set as 2022, so apart from the indexed and adjusted amounts above there is no substantive change in this topic (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep receipts and invoices for every cost. A purchase under € 450 or a cost that covers one year is deducted at once, and a purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde into account (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Book the gemengde kosten posts together. The € 5.100 drempel applies to the yearly total of those posts, and the choice for the 80% alternative is made in the return (art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Book costs excluding btw when the btw is deductible in the btw-aangifte, and including btw when no btw-aangifte is filed or when the KOR applies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- A car in the ondernemingsvermogen lands in the winst as an onttrekking of at least 22% of the waarde, or 35% for an old car, capped at the total autokosten including depreciation, and nil when at most 500 private km are shown with a rittenregistratie (art. 3.20, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- A fiets of the business that is available privately lands in the winst as 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs, capped at the costs borne, and nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Business travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig is booked at the actual costs, with the ticket or the OV-chipkaart trip overview as the record (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).
- For a private car used for business, without a sluitende kilometeradministratie deduct all VAT during the year and correct 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d at year end, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).
- Record verhuiskosten as the inboedel cost plus € 7750 in the year of the move, and dubbele huisvesting for at most two years (art. 3.17 lid 1 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Geldboetes, dwangsommen and related costs never belong in the deductible cost lines (art. 3.14, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The persoonsgebonden studiekostenaftrek goes into the IB return only in the prestatiebeurs case, with the € 250 drempel per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).
- For a bestelauto that is almost exclusively suited for goods, a verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible (art. 3.20 lid 6 to 10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they were made for the work (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

None for 2023.

One scope note outside the 2023 figures. The Wet Vpb 1969 variant of the gemengde kosten drempel in the cross-year checked material (0,4% of the employees' wage total when higher than the fixed amount, election at 73,5%, per body, once at the parent in a fiscale eenheid) is marked Verified for 2020 and 2026 only, and the checked sources do not state the 2023 text. So those two BV percentages are not confirmed for 2023 in this file. Reading art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 in the version valid on 1 January 2023 would settle it, in the same statute series that the checked sources used for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).
