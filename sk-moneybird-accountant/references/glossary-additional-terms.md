# Glossary of additional Dutch terms

**Rule:** The same as [the main glossary](glossary.md): the English term leads and the Dutch term follows in brackets at its first use in an H2 section. This file holds the labels the main glossary leaves out, so every label in the references has one English name.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

A note of `plain` marks an everyday word or phrase whose Dutch adds nothing. A note of `official name` marks the name of an authority, a law, a scheme, a form or a product. A note of `abbreviation` marks a Dutch short form that English text keeps as it is, such as BV or Vpb, after the full English name. An empty note marks a term that takes its label at first use.
