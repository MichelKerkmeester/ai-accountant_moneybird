# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2025

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 3 taxes savings and investments on a fictitious return. In 2025 the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3 applies to everyone, with three categories: banktegoeden (bank and savings balances), overige bezittingen (other assets such as investments and a second home) and schulden (debts). The percentages for 2025 are 1,37% for banktegoeden, 5,88% for overige bezittingen and 2,70% for schulden, the heffingsvrij vermogen is € 57.684 and the rate is 36%. The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 entered into force on 19 July 2025, so a taxpayer who makes it plausible that the actual return is lower pays over the actual return, and from 2025 the actual return is reported inside the income tax return. The fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028", and the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### Heffingsvrij vermogen

The heffingsvrij vermogen (untaxed threshold) is subtracted from the rendementsgrondslag in step 3 of the calculation. For 2025 it is € 57.684 per person and € 115.368 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year. It also doubles for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)).

Partners allocate the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share allocated under art. 2.17, and the partners may divide the joint grondslag in any ratio as long as the shares total 100% ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The statute text valid on 1 January 2027 adds that the effective percentage then uses the joint rendementsgrondslag and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values ([statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Box 3 tarief

The voordeel from step 5 of the calculation is taxed at 36% in step 6 ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the rate is 36%.

The voordeel itself cannot be negative. A negative rendement is set at nil under art. 5.2 of the Overbruggingswet box 3, and art. 4.14 lid 1 makes the forfaitaire voordeel at least nil ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

### Schuldendrempel

Only the part of the total debts above the drempel (threshold) is deductible. The Belastingdienst computes this in the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

The statute writes the rule in art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1 ([statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the drempel is € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

The drempel doubles for whole-year partners. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

### Bank percentage

Banktegoeden (bank and savings balances) are multiplied by the bank percentage. The percentage is fixed after the year by ministerial regulation under art. 10.6ter lid 2, with retroactive effect to 1 January. It is the average of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice, so the sum divided by twelve ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

For 2025 the bank percentage is 1,37%. The figure previously printed for this calculation, 1,44%, was replaced by the regeling of 12 February 2026 with effect back to 1 January 2025 ([regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)). The Belastingdienst percentages page lists 1,37% for 2025 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).

### Other-assets percentage

Overige bezittingen (other assets, for example investments and a second home) are multiplied by the other-assets percentage ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)).

The percentage is not an indexed base value. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 replaces it at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the value is 5,88%, printed in the statute text valid on 1 January 2025 and on the Belastingdienst percentages page ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage applies only to the deductible part of the debts, which is the total debts minus the drempel ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)).

Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method as the bank percentage, on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the debt percentage is 2,70%, replacing 2,61% back to 1 January 2025 by the regeling of 12 February 2026. The regeling cites no monthly DNB figures ([regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)).

### Berekening box 3 inkomen

The Belastingdienst computes the box 3 income for 2025 in six steps ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)):

1. Belastbaar rendement = banktegoeden x bank percentage plus overige bezittingen x other-assets percentage minus (schulden minus drempel) x debt percentage.
2. Rendementsgrondslag = bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel).
3. Grondslag sparen en beleggen = rendementsgrondslag minus heffingsvrij vermogen. Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%.
4. Aandeel = grondslag / rendementsgrondslag x 100. The 2025 page instructs 2 decimals.
5. Voordeel = belastbaar rendement x aandeel.
6. Tax = 36% x voordeel.

The values of the assets and debts are taken at the peildatum, 1 January (art. 5.24 speaks of "die peildatum" and of "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum") ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

Transactions around the peildatum inside that three-month period are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes it plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them (art. 5.24, peildatumarbitrage) ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year. The 2023 and 2025 pages use 2 decimals, the 2024 and 2026 pages use 3 decimals ([2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)).

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

Art. 5.13 keeps groene beleggingen (green investments) out of the bezittingen up to a fixed amount. The amount doubles for whole-year partners ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the amount is € 26.312 per person and € 52.624 with a whole-year partner, after the Belastingplan 2025 cut of the green facilities ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Art. 5.13 lid 2, in the text from 2023, applies the exemption first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January ([statute text, art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)).

A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The Belastingdienst example for 2025 is a minor child with a green account of € 80.000 on 1 January 2025. The child claims € 26.312 in its own return and the parents report € 53.688 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).

### Korting groene beleggingen

Art. 8.19 gives a korting voor groene beleggingen (green investment tax credit) of 0,1% for 2025 of the exempt amount on the peildatum. The amount is computed automatically and is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

The rate was 0,7% for 2020 to 2024 and is 0,1% for 2025 to 2027 ([statute text, art. 8.19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

### Wet tegenbewijsregeling dates

The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. Entry into force followed from the besluit of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, in force the day after issue, so 19 July 2025. The Eerste Kamer adopted the law on 8 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html), [Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

Art. V gives retroactive effect. Arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV to 1 January 2017. Art. II, on own use of real estate in art. 5.27a, applies from 1 January 2026 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes it plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income from sparen en beleggen is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the inspecteur applies, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents may follow up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus vermogensaanwas (increase in value) ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. III: the persoonsgebonden aftrek after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024 follows the partners' chosen split, and another split follows from an ambtshalve-vermindering request. Art. IV: art. 6a in the Wet rechtsherstel with the same form and six-week rule ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

The nota van toelichting of Stb. 2025, 196 names Hoge Raad 6 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705), 14 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857), 2 August 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084) and 20 December 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788) ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)).

Background: the Hoge Raad ruled on 6 June 2024 that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3), [2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)).

Content rules for the actual return: income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen is used ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

### Actual-return reporting 2025

From 2025 the actual return is reported in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting (income tax return). The return asks whether you want to report the actual return when your assets exceed the heffingsvrij vermogen. Reporting is not mandatory ("Het doorgeven is niet verplicht") and the Belastingdienst uses the amount that is most favourable for you ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

No deadline is stated on these pages ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

Earlier routes: 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD, outside Mijn Belastingdienst. 2020 to 2024 used an online form in Mijn Belastingdienst ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"), a paper form via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call) or several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. For 2017 to 2020 all letters have been sent, and without a letter you may not report ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example: single person, savings only

Source: [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025). One person, € 150.000,00 of banktegoeden on the peildatum, no other assets and no debts.

- Step 1: € 150.000,00 × 1,37% = € 2.055,00 belastbaar rendement.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000,00.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 150.000,00 - € 57.684,00 = € 92.316,00.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 92.316,00 / € 150.000,00 × 100 = 61,544%, rounded down = 61,54%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 2.055,00 × 61,54% = € 1.264,65, rounded down = € 1.264,00.
- Step 6: belasting = 36% × € 1.264,00 = € 455,04, rounded down = € 455,00.

### Official example: same case with a fiscal partner

Source: [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025). Same € 150.000,00 of savings, now with a fiscal partner and a 50/50 split.

- Step 1: € 150.000,00 × 1,37% = € 2.055,00 belastbaar rendement.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000,00.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 150.000,00 - € 115.368,00 = € 34.632,00, split into € 17.316,00 per person.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 17.316,00 / € 150.000,00 × 100 = 11,544%, rounded down = 11,54%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 2.055,00 × 11,54% = € 237,15, rounded down = € 237,00 per person.
- Step 6: belasting = 36% × € 237,00 = € 85,32, rounded down = € 85,00 per person.

### Official example: single person with investments, a second home and a debt

Source: [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025). One person with € 150.000,00 of savings, € 75.000,00 of investments, a € 200.000,00 second home and a € 100.000,00 debt.

- Bank return: € 150.000,00 × 1,37% = € 2.055,00.
- Overige bezittingen: € 75.000,00 + € 200.000,00 = € 275.000,00. Return: € 275.000,00 × 5,88% = € 16.170,00.
- Deductible debt: € 100.000,00 - € 3.800,00 = € 96.200,00. Debt return: € 96.200,00 × 2,70% = € 2.597,40, rounded = € 2.597,00.
- Step 1: belastbaar rendement = € 2.055,00 + € 16.170,00 - € 2.597,00 = € 15.628,00.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000,00 + € 275.000,00 - € 96.200,00 = € 328.800,00.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 328.800,00 - € 57.684,00 = € 271.116,00.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 271.116,00 / € 328.800,00 × 100 = 82,4562%, rounded down = 82,45%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 15.628,00 × 82,45% = € 12.885,29, rounded down = € 12.885,00.
- Step 6: belasting = 36% × € 12.885,00 = € 4.638,60, rounded down = € 4.638,00.

### Official example: same case with a fiscal partner

Source: [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025). Same assets and debt, now with a whole-year partner, so the schuldendrempel is € 7.600,00.

- Deductible debt: € 100.000,00 - € 7.600,00 = € 92.400,00. Debt return: € 92.400,00 × 2,70% = € 2.494,80, rounded = € 2.495,00.
- Step 1: belastbaar rendement = € 2.055,00 + € 16.170,00 - € 2.495,00 = € 15.730,00.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 425.000,00 - € 92.400,00 = € 332.600,00.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 332.600,00 - € 115.368,00 = € 217.232,00, split into € 108.616,00 per person.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 108.616,00 / € 332.600,00 × 100 = 32,6566%, rounded down = 32,65%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 15.730,00 × 32,65% = € 5.135,85, rounded down = € 5.135,00 per person.
- Step 6: belasting = 36% × € 5.135,00 = € 1.848,60, rounded down = € 1.848,00 per person.

### Official example: partners with green investments and a second home abroad

Source: [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025). Whole-year partners with € 5.000,00 of savings, € 150.000,00 of green investments and a € 250.000,00 second home abroad, no debts.

- Bank return: € 5.000,00 × 1,37% = € 68,50, rounded down = € 68,00.
- Green exemption: € 52.624,00. It applies first to the green overige bezittingen, so the taxable green part is € 150.000,00 - € 52.624,00 = € 97.376,00.
- Overige bezittingen: € 97.376,00 + € 250.000,00 = € 347.376,00. Return: € 347.376,00 × 5,88% = € 20.425,71, rounded down = € 20.425,00.
- Step 1: belastbaar rendement = € 68,00 + € 20.425,00 = € 20.493,00.
- Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 5.000,00 + € 347.376,00 = € 352.376,00.
- Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 352.376,00 - € 115.368,00 = € 237.008,00.
- Step 4: aandeel = € 237.008,00 / € 352.376,00 × 100 = 67,2599%, rounded down = 67,25%.
- Step 5: voordeel = € 20.493,00 × 67,25% = € 13.781,54, rounded down = € 13.781,00.
- Step 6: belasting = 36% × € 13.781,00 = € 4.961,16, rounded down = € 4.961,00.

### Official example: actual return against the fictitious voordeel

- Savings of € 150.000,00 with € 1.875,00 of interest. The fictitious voordeel is € 1.264,00, so the actual return is higher and reporting the actual return gives no benefit.
- The same savings with € 1.050,00 of interest. The actual return is lower than the fictitious voordeel of € 1.264,00, so reporting the actual return does give a benefit.
- Savings of € 100.000,00 with € 1.200,00 of interest plus investments moving from € 150.000,00 to € 160.000,00, with € 1.200,00 bought, € 3.000,00 sold and € 500,00 of dividend. The investments give € 12.300,00, so the actual return is € 1.200,00 + € 12.300,00 = € 13.500,00. The fictitious voordeel is € 7.838,00: bank return € 100.000,00 × 1,37% = € 1.370,00, other-assets return € 150.000,00 × 5,88% = € 8.820,00, belastbaar rendement € 1.370,00 + € 8.820,00 = € 10.190,00, rendementsgrondslag € 100.000,00 + € 150.000,00 = € 250.000,00, grondslag sparen en beleggen € 250.000,00 - € 57.684,00 = € 192.316,00, aandeel € 192.316,00 / € 250.000,00 × 100 = 76,9264%, rounded down = 76,92%, voordeel € 10.190,00 × 76,92% = € 7.838,15, rounded down = € 7.838,00. The actual return is higher, so reporting the actual return gives no benefit.
- Partners with € 350.000,00 of savings. The fictitious voordeel is € 1.606,00 each. Joint bank return € 350.000,00 × 1,37% = € 4.795,00. Joint grondslag sparen en beleggen € 350.000,00 - € 115.368,00 = € 234.632,00, split into € 117.316,00 per person. Aandeel per person € 117.316,00 / € 350.000,00 × 100 = 33,5188%, rounded down = 33,51%. Voordeel per person € 4.795,00 × 33,51% = € 1.606,80, rounded down = € 1.606,00. With € 3.500,00 of joint interest, which is € 1.750,00 per person, the interest is higher than the fictitious voordeel, so reporting the actual return gives no benefit. With € 2.500,00 of joint interest, which is € 1.250,00 per person, the interest is lower, so reporting the actual return does give a benefit.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Value the box 3 assets and debts at the peildatum 1 January 2025 and keep them split over the three categories, because each category uses its own percentage: 1,37% for banktegoeden, 5,88% for overige bezittingen and 2,70% for the deductible part of the schulden ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).
- Only the part of the total debts above € 3.800, or above € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, enters the calculation. The Belastingdienst computes this in the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Partners choose the split of the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen, and the shares must total 100%. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Keep the records that support the optional actual return: income and value changes over the calendar year, because gains and losses are netted and a negative total becomes € 0 with no carry-forward. Keep the documents for interest paid on a box 3 debt and for a second-home investment that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, because those are the only deductible costs ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement)).
- The actual return is reported in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2025 and the report is optional. The Belastingdienst uses the most favourable of the fictitious and the actual amount, and you never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).
- For the counterproof route the inspecteur provides a form, and further documents can be requested up to six weeks after the form is sent. This does not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).
- A minor child with green investments files its own return for the exemption of € 26.312 and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).
- No deadline for reporting the actual return is stated on the Belastingdienst pages ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Open point 2025: the rekenvoorbeelden page prints € 11.500 in step 4 of the debt example while its own sum is € 13.000. Only a correction of the page by the Belastingdienst settles it. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/rekenvoorbeelden-berekening-werkelijk-rendement
- Conflict in the bank and debt percentages: the yearly regulation replaces the figures in the statute text and works back to 1 January, so the two can differ. Operative for 2025 are 1,37% for banktegoeden and 2,70% for schulden under the regeling of 12 February 2026, which replaced 1,44% and 2,61% back to 1 January 2025. The statute text at https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 carries the earlier figures. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Conflict on the entry date of the Wet rechtsherstel box 3: statute art. 7 gives the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". The statute text settles it. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- UNVERIFIED: the claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return. hogeraad.nl still returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. The memorie van toelichting describes the Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021 as a breach of art. 1 EP in combination with art. 14 EVRM, and stresses that the legislator chose a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories, not a levy on the actual return. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- UNVERIFIED: the "or objected late" part of the finding of 25 June 2026 on non-objectors. The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms only that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- Proposed: the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, bill 36 748, submitted 16 May 2025. Adopted by the Tweede Kamer on 12 February 2026. No Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 2026-10-11, so it is not law. Entry into force by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028. https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- Proposed: the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 with a novelle to bill 36 748. The Kamerstuk number is UNVERIFIED. The letter mentions a vermogenswinstbelasting for all financial instruments from 2028 (about 90% of assets with value development), a full switch for the other assets in 2030, the box 3 heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027, a heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028 and a rise of the forfait for overige bezittingen by 1,5 percentage point in 2027. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page. https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255 and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
