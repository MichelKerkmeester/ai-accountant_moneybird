# Inkomstenbelasting box 1 2025

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the 2025 box 1 rates, the general tax credit, employment tax credit, income-related combination tax credit, and the rate adjustment for certain deductions. The 2025 rates and credit tables in the checked material are enacted. Below AOW age, box 1 has three published brackets, and the first-bracket rate differs for people who have reached AOW age.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### Box 1 below AOW age

The published rate includes the income-tax part and, up to the premium ceiling, the premie volksverzekeringen (national insurance contributions). The income-tax part of the first bracket is 8,17%, and the premium rate is 27,65%, giving the published combined rate of 35,82%. The next brackets are 37,48% and 49,50%. The statutory fixed amounts are listed in the figures table. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

The rate applies to taxable income from work and home. The premium component is charged only on premie-inkomen up to the stated ceiling. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Box 1 for whole year at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For this group, the first bracket ends at € 38.441 and has a published rate of 17,92%. The other published rates and the bracket boundary are the same as for the below-AOW table. The lower first-bracket rate reflects that the AOW premium stops when AOW age is reached. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Box 1 for whole year at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For this group, the first bracket ends at € 40.502. The remaining bracket boundary and rates follow the figures table. Wet IB 2001 article 2.10a provides the higher first boundary for this birth group. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10a.)

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

The first-bracket rate depends on the month in which AOW age is reached. Use the month-by-month rate in the figures table for that year. The rates in brackets 2 and 3 remain the normal rates. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening.)

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling and rate

The premium is charged on premie-inkomen up to the ceiling in the figures table. Its components are AOW, Anw, and Wlz. The source states that the AOW premium stops at AOW age. The precise treatment of the Anw and Wlz components after AOW age is noted in section 7. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen.)

### Tariefsaanpassing

Tariefsaanpassing (rate adjustment) applies where grondslagverminderende posten have been deducted and taxable income from work and home plus those deductions exceeds the top bracket boundary. The tax calculated under article 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 12,02% of the amount above that boundary, but the increase cannot exceed 12,02% of the deductions themselves. The resulting maximum value of the deductions is 37,48%. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025.)

The relevant deductions include persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions), such as maintenance obligations including partner maintenance, weekend expenses for disabled people, deductible gifts, specific healthcare costs, and carried-forward personal deductions. They also include deductible owner-occupied home costs, ondernemersfaciliteiten (entrepreneur facilities), and the exemption for making assets available. Entrepreneur facilities in this rule include zelfstandigenaftrek, research and development deduction, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek, and MKB-winstvrijstelling. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.)

### Algemene heffingskorting

The AHK is granted to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the income tax return and can also be applied by an employer or benefit provider in payroll. In 2025 its phase-out base is verzamelinkomen (aggregate income), rather than only taxable income from work and home. Verzamelinkomen consists of taxable income from work and home, taxable income from a substantial interest, and taxable income from savings and investments, less personal deductions that can be set off. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18.)

Below AOW age, the maximum AHK is reduced as verzamelinkomen rises above the stated phase-out point. For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year, the separate amount and phase-out rate in the figures table apply. The ordinary table does not apply in the year AOW age is reached. For that transition year, calculate the amount using the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in Mijn Belastingdienst. A rounding difference of a few euros from the table can occur, and the aanslag amount is correct. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Arbeidskorting

The AK is based on arbeidsinkomen (employment income for credit purposes). For an IB entrepreneur, arbeidsinkomen includes winst uit onderneming before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. It excludes the portion earned as a medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The AK builds up in the first three bands and phases out in the fourth band. The amounts for a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year use the separate schedule in the figures table. In the year AOW age is reached, the ordinary AK table does not apply. Use the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in Mijn Belastingdienst. A rounding difference of a few euros from the table can occur, and the aanslag amount is correct. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

From 2024, where an employer applied a higher AK and the arbeidsinkomen is not above the third-band boundary, the Belastingdienst takes over the employer's amount up to the applicable maximum. The 2025 limit and maximum are in the figures table. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

The IACK is calculated over arbeidsinkomen above the threshold in the figures table, up to its maximum. The whole-year AOW-age rate and maximum are lower. The IACK is claimed in the income tax return or through a voorlopige aanslag. It is not applied in payroll. In the year AOW age is reached, use the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in Mijn Belastingdienst. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The child must be under age 12 on 1 January and born after 31 December 2012. The child must belong to the taxpayer's household for at least 6 months. If the taxpayer or child dies before that period is complete, the credit can still apply. Qualifying children include a taxpayer's own, step, adopted, or foster child, a child from a spouse's earlier relationship, or a fiscal partner's child. A foster child must be raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a foster care allowance. Residents of the EU, EEA, Switzerland, or BES can qualify. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

A taxpayer qualifies if there is no fiscal partner, the fiscal partnership lasts 6 months or less, or the taxpayer has lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner. If both incomes are equal, only the older partner qualifies. Co-parenting has two different day-count descriptions in the checked sources. Follow the open point in section 7 until the source wording is settled. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Payout of unused heffingskorting

The payout is limited to the AHK for people born before 1 January 1963. It applies only to the part of the credit that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and premiums. The fiscal partner must owe enough tax, and the couple must have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The own-income and partner-income limits are in the figures table. The partner limit is higher where the partner is at AOW age. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Heffingskorting belastingdeel

Each heffingskorting has a belastingdeel (income-tax part) and premium parts. The 2025 belastingdeel is 8,17%. For taxpayers below AOW age, the total first-bracket rate is 35,82%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

### Heffingskorting premiedelen

The premium parts are for AOW, Anw, and Wlz. A person who is not insured for one of these schemes does not receive that part of the credit. Where someone is liable for premiums for only part of the year, the relevant premium part is time-proportional. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

The 2025 amount and AOW-age amount are in the figures table. The checked material provides these amounts but does not set out the qualifying tests in detail. The calculation of winst uit onderneming uses the applicable ondernemersaftrek before calculating the MKB-winstvrijstelling. ### Winst uit onderneming and taxable income

Winst uit onderneming (business profit) requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it, and that the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide whether someone is an entrepreneur for income tax. The Belastingdienst weighs factors including profit, independence, capital, time spent, customers, marketing, and business risk. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.)

Start with business profit and remove exempt profit components first. These include the forestry exemption, agricultural exemption, the qualifying part of debt forgiveness profit, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, compensation for business termination, Waz benefits, certain forestry and nature subsidies, and the stated final levy, mobility project, and peak avoidance exemptions. A medegerechtigde or money lender receives neither ondernemersaftrek nor MKB-winstvrijstelling. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.)

Belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable business profit) is profit after ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated after ondernemersaftrek. Taxable income from work and home includes taxable business profit and is reduced by losses available to be set off. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.)

The resulting box 1 income is subject to the statutory box 1 calculation, the tariefsaanpassing where it applies, and the heffingskortingen. The complete ordering of those final steps as one calculation is an inference from the statutory article structure and is noted in section 7. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

An income-related Zvw contribution also exists. Its amounts and calculation belong in the Zvw file. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.)

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Box 1 below AOW age

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income from work and home is € 60.000, before tariefsaanpassing and heffingskortingen.

1. First bracket: € 38.441 × 35,82% = € 13.769,57.
2. Income in bracket 2: € 60.000 − € 38.441 = € 21.559.
3. Tax on bracket 2: € 21.559 × 37,48% = € 8.080,31.
4. Total at the published bracket rates: € 13.769,57 + € 8.080,31 = € 21.849,88.

This illustrates the published combined rates. It does not calculate tariefsaanpassing or heffingskortingen. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

### Box 1 for whole year at AOW age

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income is € 20.000 and the taxpayer is at AOW age for the whole year, born on or after 1 January 1946.

1. € 20.000 is within the first bracket, which ends at € 38.441.
2. € 20.000 × 17,92% = € 3.584.

For a person born before 1 January 1946, the first bracket instead ends at € 40.502. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10a.)

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

**Calculated example.** Assume AOW age is reached in July and taxable income is € 20.000.

1. The July first-bracket rate is 26,87%.
2. € 20.000 × 26,87% = € 5.374.

This is a bracket-rate illustration before credits and any applicable rate adjustment. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening.)

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling and rate

**Calculated example.** Assume premie-inkomen is € 20.000 and is below the ceiling.

1. € 20.000 × 27,65% = € 5.530.

The published maximum premium at the 2025 ceiling is € 10.628. This example does not determine personal insurance status. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Tariefsaanpassing

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income after relevant deductions is € 80.000 and those deductions total € 10.000.

1. Income before those deductions: € 80.000 + € 10.000 = € 90.000.
2. Amount above the top bracket boundary: € 90.000 − € 76.817 = € 13.183.
3. Rate adjustment on that excess: € 13.183 × 12,02% = € 1.584,60.
4. Maximum adjustment based on the deductions: € 10.000 × 12,02% = € 1.202.
5. The adjustment is capped at the lower amount, so it is € 1.202.

(Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2.)

### Algemene heffingskorting

**Calculated example.** Assume the taxpayer is below AOW age and has verzamelinkomen of € 50.000.

1. Income above the phase-out point: € 50.000 − € 28.406 = € 21.594.
2. Phase-out: € 21.594 × 6,337% = € 1.368,41.
3. AHK after phase-out: € 3.068 − € 1.368,41 = € 1.699,59.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)

**Calculated example, whole year at AOW age.** Assume verzamelinkomen is € 40.000.

1. Income above the phase-out point: € 40.000 − € 28.406 = € 11.594.
2. Phase-out: € 11.594 × 3,170% = € 367,53.
3. AHK after phase-out: € 1.536 − € 367,53 = € 1.168,47.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

**Calculated example.** Assume arbeidsinkomen is € 20.000 and the taxpayer meets the IACK conditions.

1. Income above the threshold: € 20.000 − € 6.145 = € 13.855.
2. IACK at 11,45%: € 13.855 × 11,45% = € 1.586,40.
3. This is below the maximum of € 2.986, so the calculated credit is € 1.586,40.

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year, the same income above the threshold gives € 13.855 × 5,73% = € 793,89, below the maximum of € 1.495. ### Payout of unused heffingskorting

**Official example.** The source gives an example of a taxpayer born on 20 December 1962 with no income, for whom the full AHK of € 3.068 is paid out. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Heffingskorting belastingdeel and premiedelen

**Official example.** For an AK of € 184 with one third of the year subject to premium liability, the source gives a belastingdeel of € 42 and a premiedeel of € 143. One third of € 143 is € 48. The total is € 42 + € 48 = € 90. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Winst uit onderneming and taxable income

**Calculated example.** Assume business profit is € 60.000, the taxpayer qualifies for the stated zelfstandigenaftrek, and no other entrepreneur deductions apply.

1. Business profit: € 60.000.
2. Subtract zelfstandigenaftrek: € 60.000 − € 2.470 = € 57.530.
3. MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 57.530 × 12,7% = € 7.306,31.
4. Taxable business profit: € 57.530 − € 7.306,31 = € 50.223,69.
5. If there are no other items of income from work and home or losses to set off, taxable income from work and home is € 50.223,69 before any further box 1 calculation.

(Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.)

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

Keep the business records needed to establish profit and identify exempt profit components and applicable ondernemersaftrek. Use the resulting taxable business profit in the box 1 calculation, where it forms part of income from work and home. The checked material describes the calculation but does not state a record-retention period or an income tax filing deadline. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.)

The AHK is applied automatically in the return and may also be applied in payroll. The AK can be affected by an amount already applied by an employer. The IACK is claimed in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in payroll. In the year AOW age is reached, use the 2025 provisional assessment form for the AHK, AK, and IACK. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2025.)

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- **IACK co-parent day test, unresolved for 2025.** The conditions page at https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting says at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated where co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year. The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen says at least 78 days in a period of 6 months in a repeating pattern with each parent. Both are marked enacted and Verified in the checked material. The checked material does not settle how the tests apply to shorter or irregular periods. The current statutory text or a clarifying primary-source statement from the Belastingdienst would settle the wording.
- **AOW transition-year bracket 2 conflict, resolved in favor of 37,48%.** The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening returned 36,97% for bracket 2 in the transition-year table. The pages https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 give 37,48%, which governs.
- **AHK nil point wording, resolved as one rule.** The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025 says nil from € 76.818, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen says nil from € 76.817. The checked material resolves this as the same rule: the phase-out ends at the top bracket boundary and the credit is nil for higher income.
- **Prior-year AHK AOW phase-out rate used for comparison.** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 gives 3,421% for 2024, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives 3,420%. The checked material does not resolve this conflict. The figures in section 5 use the year table value.
- **Prior-year IACK maximum point used for comparison.** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024 shows the maximum of € 2.950 from € 31.839. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives € 31.837. The checked material does not settle the exact point.
- **AOW-age insurance components.** The checked material states that the AOW premium stops at AOW age. Whether the Anw and Wlz premiums continue is an inference from the published AOW-age rates, not a stated rule. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1.)
- **Order of the final box 1 steps.** The checked material verifies the box 1 balance, tariefsaanpassing, and heffingskortingen, but does not print the full sequence as one statement. The sequence described in section 3 is an inference from the article structure. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)
