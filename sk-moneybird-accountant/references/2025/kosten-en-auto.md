# Kosten en auto 2025

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

For tax year 2025 this file covers the business costs that a sole trader can deduct and the treatment of the car. The year's key figures are the gemengde kosten drempel (mixed-costs threshold) of € 5.700, the kilometre allowance of € 0,23 per business kilometre, and the car percentages of 22%, at least 25% for a car first admitted by 31 December 2016, and 35% of the value for an old car. Two 2025 points stand out: art. 3.16 lid 14 is new from 1 January 2025 and limits the costs of a workspace in an owned home, and zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during the year. All 2025 values in this file are enacted and Verified, except the art. 3.16 lid 14 item, which is Corrected and returns in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### 3.1 Gemengde kosten drempel

Costs connected with voedsel (food), drank (drinks) and genotmiddelen (stimulants such as tobacco), with representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and with congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke (congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like) are not deductible up to a yearly drempel of € 5.700 ([art. 3.15 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Travel and stay that belong to these posts count as part of them ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover ([lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). No onttrekking (withdrawal for private use) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Per category, voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners (business lunches and dinners) including fooien (tips). Drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank. Genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken (business gifts) generally fall under representatie, as a subcategory per the wetsgeschiedenis (legislative history) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964 ([Belastingdienst overview](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The drempel is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a boekjaar (financial year) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either ([section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food costs that qualify as loonkosten (wage costs) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (wages in kind) ([section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 2026 Belastingdienst page on the drempel confirms the 2025 amount: "In 2025 was de drempel ook € 5.700" ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026)).

### 3.2 Keuze in plaats van drempel

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 5, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters (persons with income from other activities). The election replaces the drempel, so a taxpayer does not apply both at once. The election is made in the return itself, so no separate request is needed. Which route gives the higher deduction depends on the level of the costs and on the rest of the return.

### 3.3 Vpb variant van de drempel voor een BV

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount, which is the art. 3.15 lid 1 amount of € 5.700 in 2025. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The rule applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscale eenheid does it apply once, at the parent ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). See section 7 for the coverage note on the 2025 text of the Wet Vpb.

### 3.4 Reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep (courses and training for study and profession), and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst overview](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). So € 1.500 stays deductible and everything above it does not. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary ([same sources](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Travel and stay for congressen and studiereizen also count as part of the gemengde-kosten posts of art. 3.15 lid 2 ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.5 Studiekosten als bedrijfskosten

Studiekosten (study costs) are deductible when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs. The boundary between the two is hard to draw in practice ([studiekosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers), [overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The persoonsgebonden aftrek for scholingsuitgaven was abolished, so this business route is the route that remains. See section 3.24 for the prestatiebeurs exception.

### 3.6 Kilometervergoeding

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, such as an auto, motor of fiets (car, motorcycle or bicycle), are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. For 2025 the amount is € 0,23 per kilometre ([art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is all-in, so brandstof (fuel), verzekering (insurance), tol (toll) and parkeren (parking) cannot be deducted separately (same source). Household members are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.15 lid 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 2026 Belastingdienst page confirms: "In 2025 was dit € 0,23" ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026)).

### 3.7 Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer (public transport), taxi or vliegtuig (aircraft) is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

### 3.8 Verhuiskosten en dubbele huisvesting

Under art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a the moving costs are the cost of moving the inboedel (household effects) plus € 7.750. Housing outside the place of residence is deductible for at most two years. The rule is unchanged from 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.17, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.9 Vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddelen

The income-tax treatment of a laptop and computer follows vermogensetikettering (asset labelling). Apparatuur en instrumenten (equipment and instruments) that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) are 0% deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A bedrijfsmiddel (business asset) used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen (mandatory private assets). Used 90% or more for business it is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen (mandatory business assets). In between it is keuzevermogen (elective assets), within reasonable bounds ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

For a car the same page gives the boundaries. A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately it is verplicht privévermogen. All other cases are keuzevermogen ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde (purchase value) under € 450 is expensed at once ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag (assessment) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid (special circumstance). Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking (private withdrawal). A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes it plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [Kennisgroep publication of 6 March 2026](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/)).

### 3.10 Werkruimte thuis

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting (furnishing) in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting (according to common opinion) zelfstandig (independent) part of the home and an income test is met ([art. 3.16 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [werkruimte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)).

The income test has two forms. With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, that income must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it (same sources). Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht (lease) is ondernemingsvermogen (same sources).

The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig. The overview page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen (with exceptions) ([werkruimte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning), [overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

### 3.11 Werkruimte in an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen

From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf (main residence) are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private ([art. 3.16 lid 14, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The lid is absent in the 1 January 2024 text ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

The memorie van toelichting calls this a codification of case law. For a non-zelfstandige workspace in such a home the huurderslasten are not deductible. The MvT names inrichting such as meubilair, stoffering, behang- en schilderwerk, and gas, water en licht ([MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). The Belastingdienst page still presents the owner route without this limit. See section 7.

### 3.12 Telefoonabonnement thuis

A telefoonabonnement for a connection in the taxpayer's own home is not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In practice a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but the call costs for private calls made on it are not. The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100% ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).

### 3.13 Private computers en apparatuur

Costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For a laptop or computer that does belong to the business, the treatment follows the vermogensetikettering of section 3.9. In short, an asset used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen and gives 0% deduction, an asset used 90% or more for business is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, and in between it is keuzevermogen ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.14 Kleine aankopen onder € 450

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are kleine kantoorartikelen (small office supplies), vakliteratuur (professional literature) and onderhoud van de auto (car maintenance). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).

### 3.15 Overige uitsluitingen art. 3.16

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027 are literatuur except vakliteratuur ([lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)), kleding except werkkleding ([lid 2 onderdeel c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), werkkleding defined in [lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) and persoonlijke verzorging ([lid 2 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction are huur and onderhoud of a pand split into business and private parts, telefoonkosten, a car of the business, congressen, studiereizen, symposia, seminars en excursies, zakenlunches en diners, recepties and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (for example a rijbewijs), health costs (pedicure, zorgverzekering), and personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding is deductible ([zakelijk of prive page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive)).

### 3.16 Partnervergoeding

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5.000 ([art. 3.16 lid 4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.17 Geldboetes

Not deductible are costs connected with geldboeten (fines) from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten and EU fines ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Also not deductible are costs connected with a misdrijf (crime) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted ([onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable ([onderdeel e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)), and dwangsommen (penalty payments) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb ([onderdeel i](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Wahv verkeersboeten (traffic fines) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The overview page prints Geldboetes 0% ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Timing matters. Costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met (relate to) the imposed fine, and they stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring (forfeiture). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught ([Kennisgroep publication of 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).

The misdrijf rule is scoped to the offence. Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking, are excluded. The besluit gives the example of a taxpayer convicted only for the possession of hennepplanten and not for trade, where only the costs of the possession are excluded ([section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### 3.18 Bijtelling auto, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto (value of the car) is an onttrekking per year ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). This income-tax addition for private use of a business car is often called bijtelling.

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that the private use is at most 500 km a year. With a rittenregistratie (trip log) or another way of showing at most 500 private kilometres, the onttrekking is nil ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [privegebruik auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend (conclusive), though trips can still be checked (same sources). Woon-werkverkeer (commuting) is not private for this rule ([art. 3.20 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In practice it is capped at the total autokosten (car costs) including depreciation (same source).

The waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Belastingdienst describes it as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm, and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued. The RDW site carries the catalogue price for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer (WEV, market value) replaces it ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto), [art. 3.20 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A laadpaal (charging point) for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price (same sources).

The term auto covers a personenauto or a bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is almost exclusively suited for the transport of goods ([lid 5 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For that excepted bestelauto the private kilometres are settled at the actual cost per kilometre. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto (statement of exclusive business use) is possible ([lid 6 to 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.19 Bijtelling auto, pre-2017 car

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% ([art. 10a.4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10a.4 does not affect the 35% rule for old cars ([art. 10a.4, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months (same source).

### 3.20 Youngtimer

When a car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, the onttrekking is 35% of the waarde van de auto ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the age threshold is 15 years, so the rule covers cars first taken into use more than 15 years before. Such a car is often called a youngtimer. The 35% is calculated on the WEV of the car, because for an old car the WEV replaces the catalogusprijs ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). As with the 22%, the onttrekking is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 25% minimum of art. 10a.4 does not affect this rule ([art. 10a.4, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). When the threshold is crossed mid-year, the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)).

### 3.21 Zero-emission

The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). After those 60 months the rules then in force apply (same source).

By year of first admission, up to 2025:
- 2020: 14% off, capped at € 6.300 (8% up to € 45.000). Only waterstof (hydrogen) was uncapped in 2020.
- 2021: 10% off, capped at € 4.000 (12% up to € 40.000).
- 2022: 6% off, capped at € 2.100 (16% up to € 35.000).
- 2023 and 2024: 6% off, capped at € 1.800 (16% up to € 30.000).
- 2025: 5% off, capped at € 1.500 (17% up to € 30.000).

Cars on waterstof are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead. From the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in wattpiek divided by the consumption in wattuur per km is at least 7 ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). All this is the statute ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

During 2025, cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation, because the 14% off regime ends 60 months after the month after first admission. From then the rules in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.22 Bijtelling fiets

When a fiets (bicycle, including an e-bike or a speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (consumer advice price) is an onttrekking per year ([art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.20a, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The onttrekking is capped at the costs borne (same source). Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability (same source). A lid 5, added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (same source, the note on terugwerkende kracht is also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### 3.23 Btw-correctie private car

Btw (VAT) on the use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet (taxed turnover). Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, although it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA is not the btw-ondernemer for this purpose, he counts as an employee ([btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie (conclusive kilometre records) all the btw may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogusprijs including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d. For a part year the correction is pro rata, and it is never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance (same source).

### 3.24 Prestatiebeurs cap, mbo

Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven (training expenses) were deductible above a drempel of € 250 and, outside the standaardstudieperiode, up to € 15.000 ([art. 6.27 to 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No deduction applied when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study (same source). The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven that relate to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the prestatiebeurs case ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

For 2025 the exception works as follows. When the diplomatermijn (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the prestatiebeurs ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). For an mbo study the cap is € 1.693 per study year, or € 141,09 per month for a part-year (same source).

### 3.25 Prestatiebeurs cap, hbo or wo

For an hbo or wo study the cap is € 2.443 per study year, or € 203,59 per month for a part-year ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower than the cap, the lower amount applies (same source).

### 3.26 Drempel in the prestatiebeurs case

The € 250 drempel applies per person ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). When a return covers more than one study year, the amounts are added and the drempel is applied once. FISIN 2025 gives the example € 333 plus € 467 is € 800 minus € 250 gives € 550 (same source). The non-fiscal STAP-budget that replaced the deduction took effect at the same moment as the abolition, 1 January 2022, with a maximum subsidy of € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7) and six application windows per calendar year ([regeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). The regeling is marked "Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024", so there is no STAP-budget in 2025 (same source).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1. Gemengde kosten, drempel against the 80% keuze

**Calculated example.** A sole trader has € 8.000 of gemengde kosten in 2025.

- Route 1, the drempel: € 8.000 minus € 5.700 = € 2.300 deductible.
- Route 2, the 80% keuze: 80% x € 8.000 = € 6.400,00, rounded to € 6.400.

In this example the 80% keuze gives the higher deduction. The figures used are the 2025 drempel of € 5.700 and the 80% of art. 3.15 lid 5.

### Example 2. Cursus travel above the cap

**Calculated example.** Travel and stay for a cursus cost € 2.000 in 2025. Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f limits the deduction of travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep to € 1.500 per year. Art. 3.16 lid 8 makes that cap apply only when attendance is not necessary because of the nature of the work. Assume attendance is not necessary. The amount above the cap is € 2.000 minus € 1.500 = € 500. That € 500 is not deductible. If the nature of the work makes attendance necessary, the cap does not apply and the € 2.000 is not capped by this rule. In both cases the amount within the cap, or the full amount when the cap does not apply, must still meet the other conditions for the cost concerned.

### Example 3. Kilometre allowance with a car in the privévermogen

**Calculated example.** The sole trader drives 12.000 business kilometres in 2025 in a car that he keeps in his privévermogen. The deduction is 12.000 km x € 0,23 = € 2.760,00, rounded to € 2.760. Fuel, insurance, toll and parking sit inside this amount and cannot be claimed separately. Under the rule for openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig the actual costs of business travel are deductible at 100%, so a business train trip with a ticket of € 120 is deductible in full.

### Example 4. Verhuiskosten

**Calculated example.** The move of the inboedel costs € 850 in 2025. Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a allows the cost of moving the inboedel plus the fixed amount of € 7.750. The deduction is € 850 plus € 7.750 = € 8.600. Housing outside the place of residence is a separate part of the same onderdeel and is allowed for at most two years. No such double housing is claimed in this example.

### Example 5. Small purchase against asset

**Calculated example.** A laptop that belongs to the ondernemingsvermogen and has an aanschafwaarde of € 400 is below the € 450 line, so € 400 is deducted in one go in 2025. A laptop that belongs to the ondernemingsvermogen and has an aanschafwaarde of € 900 is € 450 or more and is used longer than a year, so it is depreciated taking the restwaarde into account instead of being expensed at once. This example does not compute a depreciation amount because no restwaarde is given.

### Example 6. Zero-emission car first admitted in 2025

**Calculated example.** The car has 0 g CO2 per km, was first admitted in 2025 and has a waarde van de auto of € 40.000.

- Route 1: 22% x € 40.000 = € 8.800,00, rounded to € 8.800. The reduction is 5% x € 40.000 = € 2.000,00, rounded to € 2.000, but the cap is € 1.500, so the reduction is € 1.500. The onttrekking is € 8.800 minus € 1.500 = € 7.300.
- Route 2, the same rule printed as 17% up to € 30.000: 17% x € 30.000 = € 5.100,00, rounded to € 5.100, plus 22% x € 10.000 = € 2.200,00, rounded to € 2.200, together € 7.300.

For a waarde of exactly € 30.000 the outcome is 17% x € 30.000 = € 5.100,00, rounded to € 5.100.

### Example 7. Youngtimer

**Calculated example.** A car first taken into use more than 15 years before 2025 has a WEV of € 10.000. The onttrekking is 35% x € 10.000 = € 3.500,00, rounded to € 3.500. If the total autokosten including depreciation that the taxpayer bore are € 2.800, the onttrekking is capped at € 2.800.

### Example 8. Fiets

**Calculated example.** A business e-bike with a consumentenadviesprijs of € 2.000 is also used privately. The onttrekking is 7% x € 2.000 = € 140,00, rounded to € 140 per year, capped at the costs borne. If the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil under lid 5.

### Example 9. Btw correction on a private car

**Calculated example.** The catalogusprijs of a private car used for business, including btw and bpm, is € 40.000 and there is no sluitende kilometeradministratie. The year-end correction is 1,5% x € 40.000 = € 600,00, rounded to € 600 in rubriek 1d. It is never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance of the car.

### Example 10. 500 km rule

**Calculated example.** The private use of a business car is 400 km in 2025 and the taxpayer shows this with a rittenregistratie. Because 400 km is at most 500 km, the onttrekking is nil and the 22% is not applied for the year.

### Example 11. Prestatiebeurs, official example

**Official example.** In the FISIN 2025 example the prestatiebeurs amounts are € 333 and € 467. € 333 plus € 467 = € 800. The € 250 drempel is subtracted: € 800 minus € 250 = € 550 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)).

### Example 12. Prestatiebeurs, calculated example

**Calculated example.** After the diplomatermijn has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, one mbo study year has an actual prestatiebeurs of € 1.000. The cap of € 1.693 per study year is not binding, so the lower amount of € 1.000 applies. The € 250 drempel applies per person. € 1.000 minus € 250 = € 750 deductible.

### Example 13. Vpb variant for a BV

**Calculated example.** A BV has employees, a loonsom of € 1.500.000 and € 10.000 of gemengde kosten in 2025. 0,4% x € 1.500.000 = € 6.000,00, rounded to € 6.000, which is higher than the fixed amount of € 5.700, so the drempel is € 6.000.

- Drempel route: € 10.000 minus € 6.000 = € 4.000 deductible.
- Keuze route: 73,5% x € 10.000 = € 7.350,00, rounded to € 7.350 deductible.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Business costs go into the winstaangifte, the profit part of the IB return. Book them net of btw when the btw is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the btw may not be deducted, which is the KOR case, deduct the costs including btw in the IB return ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [KOR page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- The gemengde kosten drempel or the 80% election is applied in the winstaangifte. The election is made in the return ([art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Keep the receipts and invoices for every cost. Keep a rittenregistratie or other proof for the 500 km rule on the car, and a sluitende kilometeradministratie for the btw position on the car ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [privegebruik auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto), [btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Private use of a car or fiets in the ondernemingsvermogen is an onttrekking. It is added in the winstberekening and not booked as a cost, and it is capped at the autokosten including depreciation ([art. 3.20 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- For travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig, keep the ticket or the OV-chipkaart trip overview to show the actual costs ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).
- The btw correction of 1,5% of the catalogusprijs including btw and bpm goes in rubriek 1d of the btw-aangifte at year end, pro rata for a part year and never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance ([btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Costs after a geldboete that relate to the fine cannot be booked as deductible costs. The date of the fine decides ([Kennisgroep publication of 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).
- Under the KOR no btw is charged or paid and no btw on business costs and investments is deducted, also not btw paid in another EU country. Earlier deductions may need herziening. The low bestelauto mrb rate stays available. Exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter, and immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year. The choice is per ondernemer and covers all subnummers ([KOR page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- For a BV: the drempel applies per body when there are one or more employees in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher, and in a fiscale eenheid it applies once, at the parent ([Wet Vpb 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [Wet Vpb 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Werkruimte in an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen. The Belastingdienst page on the werkruimte in de woning has no date, does not mention art. 3.16 lid 14 and still presents the route for an owner whose home is in the ondernemingsvermogen ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). The 2025 statute and the MvT bar costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, and the MvT names the huurderslasten ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [MvT section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). A dated Belastingdienst publication or case law on art. 3.16 lid 14 would settle the practical line for 2025.
- Which owner costs of a non-zelfstandige workspace stay deductible, for example afschrijving or onderhoud, is not stated on a fetched primary page beyond the MvT's huurderslasten list. A Belastingdienst page on the werkruimte or an official publication would settle it.
- The BV variant of the drempel rests here on the checked 2020 and 2026 texts of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 and on section 3.4 of the besluit ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [besluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). A 2025 text of the Wet Vpb was not among the checked sources. The 2025 statute text would settle the exact 2025 wording.
