# Changes tax year 2025

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2025 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. Unique to 2025

- The KOR changed from the current-calendar-year test with a minimum participation and re-entry period to the 2025 rules for a business established in the Netherlands, with turnover information for the preceding and current calendar years. [btw](btw.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply to work relationships, no aanwijzingen are given and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. [arbeidsrelatie](arbeidsrelatie.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 3.750 in 2024 to € 2.470 in 2025. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek.md#5-changes)
- The MKB-winstvrijstelling fell from 13,31% in 2024 to 12,7% in 2025. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek.md#5-changes)
- The below-AOW box 1 table became three brackets, 35,82% up to € 38.441, 37,48% to € 76.817 and 49,50% above. [inkomstenbelasting](inkomstenbelasting.md#5-changes)
- The heffingsvrij vermogen rose from € 57.000 per person in 2024 to € 57.684 in 2025. [box-3](box-3.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2025 gets 5% off capped at € 1.500, so 17% up to € 30.000. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025. [zvw](zvw.md#5-changes)
