## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the Dutch VAT rules relevant to a sole trader, including rates, exemptions, the small-business scheme, returns, invoicing and cross-border transactions. The main changes for 2025 are the revised KOR rules and a new eight-week deadline for correcting VAT returns. The 2025 rules described here are enacted unless a row is marked otherwise or the open-points section says the value is unverified.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### Standard rate

The standard VAT rate is 21%. Apply it to taxable goods and services unless a reduced rate, zero rate, exemption or reverse-charge rule applies. [Article 9(1), Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services listed in Tabel I. The checked lists include food, water, ornamental plants, medicines and aids, art and antiques, books and periodicals, bicycle and clothing repairs, hairdressers, certain work on homes, camping, cultural and recreational access, performing artists, sport, bathing, passenger transport, qualifying electronic publications, and certain medical-aid and book services. Check whether the specific supply falls within a listed entry because the reduced rate does not apply merely because a product or service seems similar. [Article 9(2) and Tabel I, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst list of goods at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Belastingdienst list of services at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/).

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to goods and services listed in Tabel II when the statutory conditions are met. A zero-rated supply is still a taxable supply, so it generally retains the right to deduct input VAT. For an intra-EU supply of goods, keep evidence that the goods went to another EU country, check that the buyer has a valid VAT ID in another Member State, and file the opgaaf ICP on time and correctly. A late or incorrect ICP can lead to the loss of the zero rate and a fine. [Article 9(2), Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Exemptions

Article 11 exemptions include specified supplies and letting of immovable property, care, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money and securities, fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, services of composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list also refers to investment gold and canteens, and says the exemptions also apply to imports. An exempt supply does not carry a right to deduct input VAT attributable to that supply. Where the business makes both taxable and exempt supplies, apportion input VAT and settle the relevant private or exempt-use amount in the final return of the year. [Article 11(1), Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst list of exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).

### Solar panels

The 0% rate applies from the stated effective date to the supply and installation of qualifying solar panels on or near homes. It covers listed panel types and related installation items such as cables, a PV distributor, mounting materials, optimizers and inverters, and removal and reinstallation. It does not cover listed items such as heat pumps, batteries, roof reinforcement, a replacement inverter bought separately, or a full replacement of the group cupboard. Some multifunctional panels and panels on non-residential business or public buildings remain taxable at the standard rate. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home follow the rate for the home, while a separate supply and installation of the panels can qualify for the zero rate. [Decision of 16 June 2023, no. 2023-6393](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html), [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen).

### Logies

Logies remains at 9% for 2025. The enacted change that removes the reduced rate for logies takes effect from the following year, so it does not change the 2025 rate. Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain distinct supplies and are treated at the applicable rate for those services. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html), [Belastingdienst logies guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies).

### KOR Dutch turnover ceiling and test

The Dutch KOR is an exemption for a business established in the Netherlands whose annual turnover in the Netherlands does not exceed € 20.000. For 2025, the preceding calendar year's turnover also matters. The notification states turnover for the preceding year and the current year up to the notification. If the ceiling is exceeded, the supply that crosses it is taxable, and the exemption is unavailable for the rest of that calendar year and the following calendar year. [Articles 25, 25a and 25b, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [transition provisions, Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html).

Count Dutch taxable supplies at any rate, including zero-rated supplies and domestic supplies for which VAT is reverse-charged to the customer. Certain exempt supplies involving immovable property, means of payment, securities, and financial or insurance services in the Netherlands also count. Do not count VAT on private use, sales of business-used investment goods, domestic supplies reverse-charged to the business as buyer, supplies taxed in another country or intra-community acquisitions. Before joining, measure turnover excluding VAT. While participating, count the total amount charged. Add the turnover of all subnummers. [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR Union ceiling for a business established outside the Netherlands

A business established in another EU Member State can use the Dutch exemption if its Union turnover stays within the statutory limit and the Dutch-specific conditions are met. The law distinguishes this route from the domestic KOR for a business established in the Netherlands. The Dutch KOR condition is establishment in the Netherlands, not merely a Dutch fixed establishment. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### Dutch KOR establishment condition

The Dutch exemption is available to a business established in the Netherlands. For the previous KOR rules, a fixed establishment in the Netherlands could qualify, but the 2025 wording of the domestic route uses establishment in the Netherlands. A Dutch sole trader considering the KOR must also account for all Dutch turnover across their activities and subnummers. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Article 25, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, 2024 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01).

### KOR start

The exemption starts from the first tax period that is at least four weeks after the Belastingdienst receives the notification. In practice, apply only after receiving the omzetbelastingnummer and VAT ID. Quarterly filers should use the published cut-offs. Returns continue until the definitive KOR start date. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR application guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor).

### KOR minimum term

The 2025 statute does not set a minimum participation term. A participant can give notice to leave, subject to the statutory effective date and the later re-entry restriction. This differs from the former scheme. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Article 25, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, 2024 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01).

### KOR voluntary exit and re-entry

A voluntary withdrawal takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter, at least four weeks after the withdrawal notice is received. After voluntary exit, re-entry is barred for the calendar year in which the exemption ended and the following calendar year. The Belastingdienst page describes exit by the first day of the aangiftetijdvak. That wording conflicts with the statute for a monthly filer. The statute is the controlling source for the statutory rule. See section 7. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR withdrawal guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor).

### KOR overrun

When turnover exceeds the ceiling, the supply that crosses the ceiling is taxable. The exemption no longer applies for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year. This makes the timing and value of each supply relevant. Keep a running turnover total and identify the supply that crosses the ceiling. [Article 25a(9), Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### KOR registration threshold without notification

A business that has not registered with the inspector or the KVK, and is not required to register, can apply the KOR without the ordinary notification when its annual turnover is within the stated threshold. The business also must not use the exemption in another Member State. The threshold was increased for the 2025 regime. [Article 25b, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Decision of 21 November 2024, no. 2024-29602](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html).

### KOR quarterly notification cut-offs and confirmation

The practical application page sets quarterly cut-offs and says a late application starts in the next quarter. The application must arrive before the intended start and the Belastingdienst sends a confirmation. Use the statutory start condition as well as the practical cut-off, since the exemption does not begin merely because an application has been sent. Paper forms are no longer handled. [Belastingdienst KOR application guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor).

### EU KOR availability and Union turnover limit

From 2025, a business can apply for VAT exemption in other EU Member States under the EU KOR. The business needs to meet the Union turnover condition in the current and preceding calendar years. Some Member States can apply an extended reference period. The EU KOR can be combined with the Union scheme for countries where that scheme applies, but not with the Import Scheme. New means of transport remain outside the exemption. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html), [Belastingdienst EU KOR guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen).

### EU KOR notifications and quarterly reporting

Before using the EU KOR, notify the inspector and obtain an individual number ending in EX. The exemption starts when the inspector issues or confirms that number. The application can be refused if the Union limit has been exceeded, a national threshold has been exceeded, conditions are not met, or more time is needed to address fraud risk. The notification includes business details, Member States and turnover information. Submit turnover per Member State each quarter, including a zero amount where there was no turnover. Notify the inspector if the Union turnover threshold is exceeded. Intra-EU acquisitions generally remain taxable in the arrival state, subject to the stated exception where the supplier also uses the KOR. [Articles 25c to 25e, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst quarterly-turnover statement guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet).

### Usual VAT return period and yearly return eligibility

Quarterly filing is usual. A business may ask to file monthly or yearly by letter to the tax office. The Belastingdienst replies within the stated response period, and a change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a monthly period being imposed. Yearly filing is limited to eligible natural persons or partnerships of natural persons and is subject to a VAT limit, a restriction on the use of vergunning artikel 23 and separate limits for each listed intra-EU activity. The 2025 VAT limit is unverified. [Belastingdienst guidance on changing the VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Period return filing, payment and software submission

A period return must be filed within the term set by the inspector, which must be at least one month after the period ends. Payment is due within one month after the period ends. For monthly and quarterly returns, the Belastingdienst gives the last day of the following month as the deadline. For yearly returns, it gives a deadline before 1 April of the following year. The filing date is the day the return is received, and filing and payment share the deadline. An extension is available only for a calamity. Software returns may be submitted from the stated day in the month before the period ends. [Articles 10(2) and 19(1), Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### 2025 return dates

The domestic due date for the final quarterly or December return for 2025 is stated in the key figures table. The yearly 2025 return also has a stated date there. These dates are distinct from the general rule that period returns are due by the last day of the following month. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Foreign-established filing dates

The listed filing dates for foreign-established businesses differ from the domestic return dates. They apply to foreign-established businesses as described on the Belastingdienst page and should not be used as the due dates for a domestic Dutch sole trader. [Belastingdienst due dates for foreign-established businesses](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte).

### Suppletie

Correct an incorrect or incomplete VAT return for the relevant past calendar years using the prescribed method. From 2025, the correction must be made no later than eight weeks after the taxpayer discovers the error or omission. The earlier deadline applies if the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector already knows. Failure to report can be an offence, and the statutory power to impose a vergrijpboete expires under the stated limitation rule. The amount boundary for putting a correction directly in the next return is unverified for 2025. [Article 15, Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren).

### Opgaaf ICP

The opgaaf ICP reports intra-EU supplies of goods and services, movements of own goods and call-off stock transfers. The total must match rubriek 3b for the same period. Report goods in the period of the invoice date and services in the period when supplied. Do not file an ICP for a period with nothing to report. Correct an error in the next ICP. Goods are usually reported monthly, with quarterly reporting allowed while the goods threshold is met. Services can be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with a permit, and the goods threshold does not apply to services. A combined ICP follows the goods rules. The checked material does not establish the domestic 2025 filing deadline. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp), [2024 digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

### Intra-EU services and EU distance sales

For many business-to-business services supplied to a customer in another EU Member State, VAT is due in the customer's country. Invoice without Dutch VAT, state that VAT is reverse-charged, verify the customer's VAT ID, and report the service in the return and the opgaaf ICP. Some services are excepted from this rule or are not listed in the ICP. [Belastingdienst guidance for services to businesses in other EU countries](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen), [2024 digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

The distance-sales threshold concerns certain goods sent from the Netherlands directly to consumers in another EU country where the supplier arranges or pays for transport. It excludes listed margin goods, nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital services to consumers also count toward the threshold. If the threshold is exceeded, destination-country VAT applies from the overrun. Below the threshold, the supplier may opt for destination-country VAT, and that choice is binding for the stated minimum period. A fixed establishment in another EU country removes the threshold route, although a warehouse alone is not a fixed establishment. [Article 6k, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst EU consumer-goods guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst EU digital-services guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

### OSS and Import Scheme

The Union scheme allows a business to report covered cross-border supplies through one quarterly return for the participating EU countries. File a nil return when there was no relevant turnover. The first use generally starts from the first day of the quarter after registration, subject to the stated first-supply registration rule. A return cannot be resubmitted. Make corrections in a later return within the stated correction period. Keep the supporting records for the stated period. The Import Scheme is a separate monthly route for eligible low-value consignments. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

### Private use and the final return

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur's family. It can arise from mixed-use assets, such as a car or business computer, and from goods withdrawn for exclusively private use. The final return of the year settles private use of goods and services, the business car, the apportionment between taxable and exempt turnover, and staff provisions and gifts subject to the BUA rules. It also covers the other stated year-end adjustments, including utilities, internal services and revisions where applicable. If the final return has already been filed, correct it by suppletie. [Belastingdienst private-use guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Belastingdienst final return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Business car private use

Commuting counts as private use for VAT. Without a detailed record of private use, the forfait is based on the catalogue price including VAT and bpm. Use the lower forfait rate when no purchase VAT was deductible or after the stated period of use. A part-year use is apportioned, and exempt use reduces the amount proportionally. The result is entered in the final return of the year. The forfait is capped at the VAT deduction described in the key figures table. [Decision on VAT and private use of business cars](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst private use of a business car](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### BUA

The Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting, or BUA, can exclude input VAT deduction on certain staff benefits, business gifts and other gifts. The listed categories include staff housing, wages in kind, sport and leisure, and private transport. The statute has carve-outs for specified food and drink, licensed private bus transport for commuting, commuting bicycles that meet the conditions, and outplacement. The annual recipient threshold and related staff meal calculation are in the key figures table. From the stated effective date, do not subtract an employee contribution when testing the threshold. [BUA, Article 1 to Article 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Decision of 6 October 2023, no. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html), [Belastingdienst staff provisions threshold guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken).

### Verleggingsregeling

Under a reverse-charge rule, the customer accounts for VAT instead of the supplier. The rules cover specified cases such as foreign suppliers, subcontracting and staff lending in construction and shipbuilding, waste and scrap, some electronics, certain immovable-property supplies, enforcement sales, gas and electricity certificates, gold, emission rights and telecom services between providers in the Netherlands. For subcontracting, exceptions include work mainly carried out in the supplier's own workshop, staff lent for that work, and subordinate work on goods sold. The invoice states “btw verlegd,” the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate. The supplier remains responsible for applying the rule correctly. The customer reports domestic reverse-charge VAT in rubriek 2 or VAT from abroad in rubriek 4, and may deduct it when used for taxable activity. [Articles 12, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Articles 24b, 24ba and 24bb, Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst cases for reverse charge](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [Belastingdienst reverse-charge invoicing and return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).

### Invoicing

Issue an invoice for supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. An invoice is usually not required for a private customer, subject to exceptions. The invoice must identify the parties and supply, show the applicable VAT rate and amount, and have a unique consecutive number and invoice date. It must also contain the other required business and transaction details. A digital invoice requires customer agreement and assurance of authenticity, content integrity and readability. A simplified invoice is allowed only for eligible transactions and is not allowed for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or relevant reverse-charge supplies. The kasstelsel is compulsory for listed kinds of trade, and may be imposed when the consumer-sales condition in the key figures table applies. [Belastingdienst invoice-making guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [simplified invoice rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### ViDA and e-invoicing

The checked 2025 parliamentary letter sets out a future timetable for ViDA e-invoicing and digital reporting, with separate provisions for platforms. The timetable is adopted but not yet in force. The checked material does not establish a general Dutch domestic business-to-business e-invoice duty for 2025. Suppliers to central government must send e-invoices, while municipalities, provinces and water boards may require them. [Kamerbrief 26 June 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-22112-4090.html), [Business.gov.nl government e-invoicing guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).

### Late filing and payment penalties

The penalty amounts in the key figures table were checked on a Belastingdienst page for businesses established outside the Netherlands. Do not apply them as a general penalty schedule for a Dutch-established sole trader without a source that covers that taxpayer. [Belastingdienst penalties for foreign-established businesses](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte).

### Corona special deferral

The special repayment arrangement concerns tax debts built up during the stated pandemic period. The repayment window, instalments, pause and extension conditions are listed in the key figures table. For recipients of the specified UHT letter, the repayment schedule runs from the separately stated start date. Keep the tax debt and repayments identifiable in the books so payments can be matched to the arrangement. [Belastingdienst special deferral repayment arrangement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Standard and reduced rates

**Calculated example.** A business sells a taxable service for € 1.000 before VAT at the standard rate.

1. VAT is € 1.000 × 21% = € 210.
2. The customer pays € 1.000 + € 210 = € 1.210.

For an eligible Tabel I supply at the reduced rate:

1. VAT is € 1.000 × 9% = € 90.
2. The customer pays € 1.000 + € 90 = € 1.090.

The actual rate depends on the statutory category for the supply. [Article 9, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### Zero rate and exemption

**Calculated example.** A qualifying zero-rated intra-EU goods supply has a price of € 1.000. Output VAT is € 1.000 × 0% = € 0. The seller must still meet the evidence, buyer VAT ID and ICP conditions to use the zero rate. If the same transaction were exempt instead, the exemption would not give the seller the same input VAT deduction right. [Article 9, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [2024 digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

### KOR overrun

**Calculated example.** A business is eligible to participate at the start of the year and has Dutch turnover of € 19.500 before a new supply. It then makes a supply for € 1.000.

1. Turnover becomes € 19.500 + € 1.000 = € 20.500.
2. The new supply crosses the € 20.000 ceiling.
3. The entire crossing supply is taxable from that point under the applicable VAT rate.
4. If the supply is taxable at 21%, VAT is € 1.000 × 21% = € 210.
5. The KOR does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year.

The business must also check the preceding calendar year's turnover before entering the scheme. [Article 25a(5) and (9), Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### Distance sales

**Calculated example.** A qualifying seller has € 9.000 of relevant EU distance sales and then makes a further qualifying sale for € 2.000.

1. Total relevant sales become € 9.000 + € 2.000 = € 11.000.
2. This is above the € 10.000 threshold.
3. Destination-country VAT applies from the supply that crosses the threshold, subject to the scheme and conditions that apply to the seller.

This example uses only the threshold and does not calculate foreign VAT. [Belastingdienst EU consumer-goods guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren).

### OSS quarterly statement

**Official example.** The official example says the EU KOR quarterly turnover statement for the first quarter of 2025 is due by 30 april 2025, including when there was no turnover. [Belastingdienst quarterly-turnover statement guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet).

### Business car private use

**Calculated example.** A car has a catalogue price of € 30.000 including VAT and bpm, and the business cannot establish the private-use amount from records.

1. The 2,7% forfait is € 30.000 × 2,7% = € 810.
2. Suppose VAT deducted in the year is € 500 and VAT on purchase was € 10.000.
3. One fifth of purchase VAT is € 10.000 ÷ 5 = € 2.000.
4. The cap is € 500 + € 2.000 = € 2.500.
5. The € 810 forfait is below the € 2.500 cap, so the cap does not reduce it.

For a car to which the 1,5% forfait applies, the calculation on the same catalogue price is € 30.000 × 1,5% = € 450. [Decision on VAT and private use of business cars](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).

### BUA threshold

**Calculated example.** A business provides one recipient with provisions costing € 150 and a gift costing € 100 in the same financial year.

1. Total cost is € 150 + € 100 = € 250.
2. € 250 is above the € 227 threshold.
3. The business must apply the BUA rules to the relevant input VAT. The threshold test is not reduced by the recipient's own contribution.

The example does not calculate the deductible VAT because the applicable VAT rate and amounts charged are not specified. [BUA, Article 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Decision of 6 October 2023, no. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).

### Reverse charge

**Calculated example.** A supplier performs a domestic service worth € 1.000 that qualifies for reverse charge.

1. The supplier invoices € 1.000 without charging VAT and states “btw verlegd.”
2. The customer calculates the applicable VAT. At 21%, this is € 1.000 × 21% = € 210.
3. The customer reports the VAT in the applicable return section.
4. The customer may deduct the € 210 only to the extent the purchase is used for taxable activity.

[Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).

### Invoice deadline

**Official example.** The Handboek example says that goods delivered on 10 april must be invoiced by 15 mei. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### ICP matching

**Calculated example.** For a period, suppose the intra-EU supplies reported in rubriek 3b are € 1.000 of goods and € 2.000 of services.

1. Rubriek 3b total is € 1.000 + € 2.000 = € 3.000.
2. The ICP total for the same period must also be € 3.000.
3. The goods and services must be reported in the periods specified for each supply type.

[Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2024

- The KOR changed from the previous scheme, which tested turnover in the current calendar year, allowed a Dutch fixed establishment, and had a minimum participation and re-entry period, to the 2025 rules for a business established in the Netherlands. The 2025 notification requires turnover information for the preceding and current calendar years. The new scheme has no minimum term and uses the re-entry restriction described above. [2024 version of Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [2025 version of Articles 25 to 25e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- The KOR registration threshold without notification rose from € 1.800 to € 2.200 from 1 januari 2025. [Decision of 21 November 2024, no. 2024-29602](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html).
- The EU KOR became available from 1 januari 2025. It was not in force in 2024. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html).
- Suppletie timing changed from “as soon as possible” to a deadline of no later than eight weeks after discovering an error or omission. The eight-week rule applies from 1 januari 2025. [Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html).
- The standard, reduced and zero rates remain 21%, 9% and nihil respectively. [2024 version of Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [2025 version of Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- The € 227 BUA threshold and the rule not to subtract an employee contribution were already in place for 2024. No change for 2025 is shown in the checked material. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Decision of 6 October 2023, no. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).
- The yearly-return VAT limit is not a confirmed old-to-new change. Its 2025 value is unverified, and the checked 2024 handbook URL is unavailable. See section 7. [2025 Belastingdienst return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep sales records that identify the supply, customer, date, consideration, VAT rate and VAT amount. Issue invoices to business customers and relevant legal persons by the stated deadline. Retain the required invoice details and make sure simplified invoices are used only where allowed. [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).
- Separate taxable, zero-rated and exempt turnover in the records. Track input VAT by the business use that supports deduction. Zero-rated turnover can retain input VAT deduction, while exempt turnover does not. Reconcile mixed taxable and exempt use in the final return. [Article 11, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).
- Track KOR turnover across every activity and subnummer. Keep the turnover calculation and the notification evidence, and flag the supply that would take the total above the ceiling. While participating, do not charge VAT or state VAT on invoices, and do not deduct input VAT. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden).
- Reconcile each return to sales invoices, purchase invoices, bank records and any reverse-charged transactions. Report domestic reverse charge in rubriek 2 and foreign reverse charge in rubriek 4. Deduct reverse-charged VAT only where the purchase supports taxable activity. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).
- For intra-EU supplies and services, retain transport and VAT-ID evidence where required. Reconcile the ICP total to rubriek 3b. File an ICP only for a period with reportable activity and correct an error in the next ICP. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).
- File and pay by the applicable return deadline. The filing date is when the Belastingdienst receives the return. For a software return, observe the stated submission window. [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- In the final return of the year, settle private use, the business-car adjustment, taxable and exempt turnover apportionment, and BUA items. If the final return has already been filed, use suppletie for a correction. [Belastingdienst final return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren).
- Keep OSS records for the required retention period and retain evidence for the country and rate of each reported supply. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
