# Arbeidsrelatie en zelfstandigheid 2025

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the work-relationship side of the rules for a ZZP (self-employed person without employees): the Wet DBA (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, the act on the assessment of work relationships) and its enforcement, the loondienst test (test for employment), modelovereenkomsten (model agreements), the VBAR bill with the low hourly rate presumption, and the status of the BAZ bill. The most important fact for 2025 is that the handhavingsmoratorium (enforcement moratorium) ended on 1 January 2025: the normal rules apply, no aanwijzingen (formal instructions) are given, and schijnzelfstandigheid (apparent self-employment) can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen (correction liabilities) and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (additional payroll tax assessments). The ingroeimodel (phase-in model) keeps corrections to 1 January 2025 and later unless there is kwaadwillendheid (bad faith) or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, and for 2025 no verzuimboetes (default penalties) and no vergrijpboetes (intent penalties) are charged for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. The Wet DBA itself is enacted law since 2016, the 2025 enforcement stance is policy rather than statute, modelovereenkomsten valid on 6 September 2024 stay usable to and including 31 December 2029, and the VBAR and BAZ bills were not in force in tax year 2025.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### Wet DBA

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties is the Wet van 3 februari 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, the old advance declaration about the status of a work relationship). Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The act governs how a work relationship is assessed, and for 2025 the enforcement of that assessment changed because the moratorium ended, see the later subsections. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Loondienst test

Loondienst (employment) has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid, and pay for the work. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Same source. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer (entrepreneur) for income tax or VAT. Same source.

### Fictieve dienstbetrekkingen and the opt-out

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen (deemed employments), among them aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden (usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage), stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefit), WIA (disability benefit for work and income) and WW (unemployment benefit). Same source.

### Stb. 2016, 165

Correction to a common mistake: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. Source: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).

### Case law: HR 6 November 2020 and HR 24 March 2023

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (the intention of the parties). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), works with an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding (embedding in the organisation) and ondernemerschap (entrepreneurship), and vrije vervanging (free replacement of the worker) is not an absolute element. Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### HR Uber ruling

In the Uber case (prejudiciële vragen, preliminary questions) the Hoge Raad held that there is no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten (points of view), so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, 27 March 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### End of the handhavingsmoratorium

Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing: reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll tax return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Same source.

### How far back corrections go

Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). This is the ingroeimodel that runs to 2030.

### DBA boetebeleid and zachte landing 2025

For 2025 there are no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties, and for correcties over 2025 this stays so. The basis is motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The zachte landing in 2025 consisted of the ingroeimodel, the supporting measures of the Kamerbrief of 6 September 2024 and the adopted moties of 1 October 2024. Under that letter there were no vergrijpboetes for 2025 for parties demonstrably working on correct qualification, then extended by motie Flach/Aartsen to no verzuim- and vergrijpboetes at all. Same source. After motie Aartsen (31 311, nr. 264) risk-based handhaving continues so problem cases can get naheffingen. Same source. Forward path: for 2026 there are no verzuimboetes and vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026, and on 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). This is enforcement policy, not statute.

### Enforcement practice 2025

In 2025 a control in principle starts with a bedrijfsbezoek (business visit), possibly with a waarschuwing (warning) that is not an official instrument like the aanwijzing. A boekenonderzoek (full audit) remains possible with large risks. Capacity in 2025 was 80 fte (full-time equivalents), with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. NLA signals are expected to be picked up in 2025 through the GKT-meldingenproces. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Vooroverleg

The Belastingdienst takes a standpoint on a complete verzoek vooroverleg (advance consultation request). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn (target term) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Regular handhaving per the 2025 plan

The tranche 2025 plan stated regular handhaving from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Doorwerking naar IH en OB

Richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB (guidelines on the knock-on effect to income tax and VAT) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie (qualification at the source). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging

The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon, or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months, ended on 10 July 2021, and the online pilot environment stayed active, with no rechtskracht (legal force) during or after the pilot. Sources: [Ondernemersplein, webmodule beoordeling arbeidsrelatie](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring (client statement) as one outcome. Same sources.

### VBAR bill ingediend

The internet consultation of the original VBAR ran from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025. The bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and on 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer, with the bill documents dated 9 July 2025. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The bill was not in force in tax year 2025. The later path: the nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part, the Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Eerste Kamer adopted it on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (the sitting-and-standing vote), and it became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR presumption in art. 7:610aa BW

New art. 7:610aa BW reads as follows. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the article is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). The presumption does not apply in tax year 2025 because the law was not in force yet, see the subsection on entry into force.

### VBAR effect and evaluation

The effect is that the werkende (the working person) can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 provides that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR amount at peildatum 1 January 2025

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

The checked material contains no official example for this topic, so every example below is a Calculated example built from the section 2 figures.

### Calculated example: applying the loondienst elements

Invented facts: a ZZP works 4 days a week for one client, the client sets the planning and the working hours, the work is done as part of the client's team, the ZZP must do the work personally, and the pay is € 45 per hour.

1. The possibility of werkgeversgezag: the client sets planning and working hours, so this element is present.
2. The duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid: the work must be done personally, so this element is present.
3. Pay for the work: € 45 per hour, so this element is present.

The weighed factors point the same way: a long duration, embedding in the organisation and little commercial risk. Conclusion for this invented case: the relationship has the features of loondienst. Each case is decided on its own facts, and not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. Sources: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### Calculated example: the 2/5 minimum wage threshold for gelijkgestelden

The gelijkgestelden rule uses at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. With the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025 (section 2):

1. One fifth: € 14,06 / 5 = € 2,812.
2. Two fifths: € 2,812 × 2 = € 5,624, so about € 5,62 per hour.

Invented case one: someone works 3 days a week for the same client at € 6,00 per hour. The element of usually 2 or more days a week and the pay element of at least € 5,62 are both met, so the gelijkgestelden rule can apply unless the parties opted out in writing before the first payment of pay. Invented case two: the same person earns € 5,00 per hour, which is below € 5,62, so the pay condition is not met on the amount alone. The rule says "usually", so it stays a factual test. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### Calculated example: the look-back window

1. The normal rule: a correction goes no further back than 1 January 2025 (section 2).
2. Suppose a correction is issued in 2026. The reach ends at 1 January 2025, so the reachable period is 2025 and 2026, which is 2 calendar years. Tax years 2020 to 2024 are outside the reach in this normal case.
3. The full 5 years is possible again in 2030: 2030 minus 5 years = 2025. So from 2030 the reach can again cover the full 5 years.
4. Exception: with kwaadwillendheid, or when an aanwijzing given before 1 January 2025 was not followed, the Belastingdienst can go up to 5 years back.

Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Calculated example: no DBA boete over 2025

Invented case: the Belastingdienst corrects a client over 2025 and the correction is € 10.000 in loonheffingen for the qualification of an arbeidsrelatie.

1. Verzuimboete for the qualification: € 0.
2. Vergrijpboete for the qualification over 2025: € 0.
3. The correction itself stands: € 10.000 in the naheffingsaanslag loonheffingen.

So the amount payable for this invented correction is € 10.000, without a qualification boete. From 1 January 2026 vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules, also for correcties over 2026. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Calculated example: the vooroverleg streeftermijn

A complete verzoek vooroverleg is filed on 3 February 2025. The streeftermijn is 8 weeks.

1. 8 weeks = 8 × 7 = 56 days.
2. February 2025 has 28 days. From 3 February 2025, 28 days later is 3 March 2025.
3. 56 minus 28 = 28 days remain.
4. 3 March 2025 plus 28 days is 31 March 2025, because March has 31 days.

So the streeftermijn ends about 31 March 2025. It may be extended under a large request stream, and the standpoint gives only conditional certainty. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Calculated example: the use period of a modelovereenkomst

Invented case: an approved modelovereenkomst was valid on 6 September 2024 and the agreement text states validity until 31 December 2025.

1. The regime says agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date.
2. Part of that use period falls after the stated date: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2029 is 4 years.
3. Condition: the parties must work exactly as described in the agreement.

So the invented agreement can be used for 4 years longer than the date in its own text, provided the practice matches the description. Sources: [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Calculated example: the VBAR amount at peildatum 1 January 2025

1. Start from the WML per hour at 1 January 2025: € 14,06 (section 2).
2. 120% of the WML: € 14,06 × 1,20 = € 16,872.
3. The 25% opslag for AOV and pension and the 15% opslag for general costs together add 40%, so multiply by 1,40: € 16,872 × 1,40 = € 23,6208.
4. Times 1,5 for non-billable hours: € 23,6208 × 1,5 = € 35,4312.
5. The unrounded amount is € 35,4312, which is the € 35,43 excl. btw stated in the memorie van toelichting.
6. Rounded up to whole euros, starting from the unrounded amount: € 36,00, the amount in the statutory text at peildatum 1 January 2025.

Source: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

### Calculated example: the VBAR amount at peildatum 1 January 2026

1. At peildatum 1 January 2026 the WML is € 14,71 per hour (section 2).
2. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage (section 2).
3. WML factor: € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,04623, rounded to 5 decimals.
4. Unrounded amount: € 35,43 × 1,04623 = € 37,0679, so about € 37,07.
5. Rounded up to whole euros: € 38,00, the amount that the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state for peildatum 1 January 2026.

So € 36 is the base text at peildatum 1 January 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026) and [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).

### Calculated example: the hourly threshold applied to three invented rates

The presumption in art. 7:610aa BW lid 1 uses a rate of at most € 36 per hour (section 2). Lid 3 excludes the case where the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business.

1. ZZP A works for a bv for € 30 per hour. € 30 is at most € 36, so the presumption applies when the law is in force, and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst.
2. ZZP B works for the same bv for € 45 per hour. € 45 is more than € 36, so the amount-based presumption does not apply. The ordinary test still decides whether there is an arbeidsovereenkomst.
3. ZZP C works for a private client, a natural person not acting in a profession or business, for € 25 per hour. The rate is at most € 36, but lid 3 excludes the presumption because of who the other party is.

For tax year 2025 the law was not in force, so these outcomes do not yet apply. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### Calculated example: the BAZ docket percentage

The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage. Illustration of the percentage only: 70% of an invented covered amount of € 1.000 is € 1.000 × 0,70 = € 700. The material gives no annual minimum wage in euros and the bill is proposed, so no benefit can be computed on these facts. The waiting period is about one year and there is an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Source: [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2024

- Handhavingsmoratorium: 2024 was the last year of the moratorium. From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply, no aanwijzingen are given, and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Aanwijzing route: under the moratorium a non-kwaadwillend case first got an aanwijzing to reshape the relationship or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months, and a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. From 1 January 2025 no aanwijzingen are given. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Look-back: the old route gave a correctieverplichting from the date of the aanwijzing. The new rule keeps corrections to 1 January 2025 and later, unless kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, and then up to 5 years. The full 5 years is possible again only in 2030. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- DBA boetes: 2025 has no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and this stays so for correcties over 2025. The checked 2024 material states no DBA boetebeleid for 2024. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Enforcement approach: from 2025 a control in principle starts with a bedrijfsbezoek, possibly with a waarschuwing, while a boekenonderzoek remains possible with large risks. Capacity was 80 fte, with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Modelovereenkomsten: the stop started on 6 September 2024, with no new assessments and no renewals, and existing agreements stayed usable to and including 31 December 2029. In 2025 the tranche 2025 plan confirms that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), if they comply with law and regulations. Sources: [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2024 the bill was with the Raad van State (21 June 2024) and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. The original plan had an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2025 bill 36 783 was ingediend, on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer, and it was not in force in 2025. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- VBAR amount: new in the bill as it stands for 2025 is the amount of € 36 at peildatum 1 January 2025, built from the WML of € 14,06 and the opslagen in the memorie van toelichting, while the € 38 that appears on the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein pages is the indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [rijksoverheid.nl, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers).
- Preparations in 2024 for 2025: the Kamerbrief of 6 September 2024 announced lifting the moratorium per 1 January 2025, the commissiedebat of 12 September 2024 and the moties of 1 October 2024 shaped the zachte landing, and the handhavingsplan tranche 2025 (versie 1.0 of 18 December 2024, sent to the Tweede Kamer that day, 28 pages) set out the approach. The 2024 enforcement plan was published on 28 February 2024 with a perspectiefnota and the memo richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB, and the Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking was published on 1 November 2024. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep the basis of the qualification together: the written contract, any approved modelovereenkomst and any vooroverleg standpoint. Be able to show that the work is carried out exactly as described, because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done and a modelovereenkomst can create schijnzekerheid. Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten), [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- For thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten the exclusion must be agreed in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay, otherwise the fictieve dienstbetrekking stands and the entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW is not excluded. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- A reclassification correction lands at the opdrachtgever as a correctieverplichting and a naheffingsaanslag loonheffingen. For correcties over 2025 this carries no verzuimboete and no vergrijpboete for the qualification, per the 2025 policy. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Doorwerking at the opdrachtnemer: after LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Keep the administratie for seven years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 requires administratieplichtigen to keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise, and art. 52 lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions of the AWR. Sources: [AWR 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst, hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Timing: a doorwerking to the 2025 winst uit onderneming lands in the 2025 income tax return. That return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May, here 1 May 2026, and uitstel requested by then gives 1 September 2026. Sources: [Belastingdienst, kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte) and [Belastingdienst, wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).
