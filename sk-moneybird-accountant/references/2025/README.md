# Tax year 2025

**Rule:** Every file in this folder holds values for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Tax year 2025 is a past year. Its law is enacted, and later changes do not alter it. Each topic file below gives the figures, how the rules work in 2025, worked examples, the changes from 2024, notes for the books and the open points. Across the twelve files the figures tables hold 318 values: 295 Verified, 10 Corrected and 13 UNVERIFIED, with 18 not marked enacted. The general rules, settled conflicts and record duties are in [Dutch tax rules](../dutch-tax-rules.md). Corrections to these values are recorded in the [correction log](changelog.md).
