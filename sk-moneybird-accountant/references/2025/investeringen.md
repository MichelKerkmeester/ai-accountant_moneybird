## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the 2025 rules for investment deductions and depreciation: the kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction), the energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction), the milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction), the Vamil (extra random depreciation for designated environmental assets), the desinvesteringsbijtelling (addition to profit on disposal) and ordinary depreciation. The 2025 KIA table runs from € 2.900 to € 392.230 with a fixed € 19.769 in the middle band, the EIA is 40%, the MIA is 45%, 36% or 27%, the Vamil lets 75% of the cost be depreciated at will, the EIA and MIA maximum investment amount is € 151.000.000 and the desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold is € 2.900. All 2025 law values here are enacted and verified against the 2025 text of the Wet IB 2001, the URWA and the Belastingdienst pages for 2025. A few figures on the RVO applicant pages come without a stated year and are marked unknown and UNVERIFIED in section 2 and section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### Kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA), table

The KIA is a per-onderneming (per business) deduction that is claimed by choice in the aangifte (tax return). It is taken on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) in the calendar year. The 2025 table in art. 3.41 lid 2 of the Wet IB 2001 (the Dutch Income Tax Act 2001) has five bands, read from the 2025 text ([art. 3.41 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

- Nil up to € 2.900.
- 28% of the investment for a total above € 2.900 up to € 70.602.
- A fixed € 19.769 for a total above € 70.602 up to € 130.744.
- € 19.769 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 130.744 for a total up to € 392.230.
- Nil above € 392.230.

An investment total under € 2.900 therefore gives no KIA, and a total above € 392.230 gives no KIA either. The deduction is optional, so the taxpayer may take less or skip it. The KIA can be combined with the EIA or with the MIA on the same asset. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined with each other for one asset ([Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). The Belastingdienst also covers the KIA on its vennootschapsbelasting pages, so the same figures come back in the Vpb setting ([Vpb 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

For a samenwerkingsverband (partnership) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added. The table is applied to that sum. The outcome is then multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum ([art. 3.41 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot (partner) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst (profit share), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). Pick one key and keep it for the year. The KIA itself remains a per-onderneming deduction for each partner.

### KIA per-asset minimum of € 450

Art. 3.45 lid 2 adds two exclusions for the KIA on top of lid 1. Assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties are excluded (onderdeel a), and assets with an investment amount below € 450 are excluded (onderdeel b) ([art. 3.45, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The € 450 is a per-asset floor, not a yearly total. An asset below it drops out of the KIA base while the rest of the year keeps counting. A taxpayer with only assets below € 450 reaches no KIA at all.

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Art. 3.44 lid 1: if the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments that were made. The excess follows in later years. Lid 2: at staking (termination) of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking. The rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.44, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). This matters for orders placed late in the year that are delivered or installed after the year end. Keep the payment record per asset so the split between this year and later years can be shown.

### EIA percentage of 40%

The EIA is 40% of the investment amount for a new asset on the Energielijst ([art. 3.42 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The Belastingdienst EIA page states that in 2025 and 2026 the EIA is 40% ([Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that were not used before ("niet eerder gebruikt"). A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as voortbrengingskosten (production costs) ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The EIA cannot be combined with the MIA for one asset. The EIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The asset must be reported to RVO within the notification deadline.

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount

The maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 151.000.000 ([art. 3.42 lid 4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). This caps the investment amount on which the EIA or the MIA can be taken. It sits far above the investments of a normal sole trader or small BV. The RVO pages add a separate maximum for the MIA and the Vamil per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year, see that item below.

### MIA percentages and Milieulijst categories

The MIA is 45% for categorie I, 36% for categorie II and 27% for categorie III ([art. 3.42a lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes that start with F or G to categorie I, codes that start with A or D to categorie II and codes that start with B or E to categorie III ([Milieulijst 2022, in force 1 January 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). Only new assets qualify, the same test as for the EIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). No MIA is given on costs that already received the EIA, while the Vamil remains possible for those costs. The MIA can be combined with the KIA. The asset must be on the Milieulijst and be reported to RVO within the deadline.

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500

For the EIA and the MIA, art. 3.45 lid 4 excludes assets that are made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (onderdeel a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (onderdeel b) ([art. 3.45, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). RVO phrases the floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (per application) ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). Lid 3 carves out an exception for the MIA: art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply to the MIA, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn the MIA. Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a.

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

Report the investment to the Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO) within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order for aanschaffingskosten (acquisition costs). For voortbrengingskosten (production costs) the deadline is within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The Milieulijst that is valid on the signing or order date applies, so keep that list version with the file. FISIN repeats the deadline for every year from 2020 to 2026 with the sentence "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### RVO maximum for MIA and Vamil

RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for the MIA and the Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The page does not state a year for this figure, so it cannot be tied to 2025 on the checked material, see section 7. The amount does not affect normal small investments.

### RVO test for a vehicle counting as new

RVO treats a vehicle as new if it has been used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The page does not state a year for this test, see section 7. For the rest, the general new-asset test applies. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify for the EIA or the MIA, but costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### Vamil

The costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", so 75% of the cost may be depreciated at will in a chosen year ([art. 3.31 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The rest follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit for buildings, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that were not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes that start with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). RVO speaks of the combined MIA and Vamil benefit, so the Vamil can sit on the same asset as the MIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts. The asset must be reported to RVO within the deadline.

### RVO statement on the combined MIA plus Vamil benefit

RVO states: "Uw totale belastingvoordeel (MIA en Vamil) kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). That is a statement about the combined net benefit of the MIA deduction and the Vamil acceleration, not a statutory rate. The page does not state a year, see section 7.

### Assets excluded from every investeringsaftrek

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes, for the KIA, the EIA and the MIA alike, assets that are intended mainly for certain uses ([art. 3.45 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The list is:

- use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (a rule to prevent double taxation), onderdeel a
- gronden (land) including the ground under buildings, except grondverbetering (land improvement), onderdeel b
- woonhuizen (dwellings) and woonschepen (houseboats), onderdeel c
- personenauto's (passenger cars) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992, onderdeel d
- vaartuigen (vessels) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, onderdeel e
- effecten (securities), vorderingen (receivables), goodwill, vergunningen (permits), ontheffingen (exemptions) and concessies (concessions), onderdeel f
- dieren (animals), onderdeel g

For the EIA and the MIA, lid 4 adds the assets made available mainly to non-residents or foreign enterprises and the € 2.500 floor. Lid 3 lifts the woonhuizen and personenauto's exclusion for the MIA only. Lid 5 gives the Minister a route to disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a.

### Own-BV and related-party exclusion

No investeringsaftrek is given on obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (onderdeel b), heirs to an estate to which the asset belongs (onderdeel c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam (entity) and that lichaam (onderdeel d) ([art. 3.46, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Minister can disapply lid 1 under lid 2, which is the ontheffing (exemption) route. FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). A sole trader who holds one third or more of a BV and buys an asset from that BV therefore loses the investeringsaftrek on that asset in principle. The cross-year rules apply in 2025 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold of € 2.900

Art. 3.47 lid 1: if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage that was taken is added to profit ([art. 3.47 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the threshold is € 2.900. FISIN 2025 prints "€ 2.800 of minder", and the statute prevails, see section 7 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The threshold is stated per calendar year.

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling period and cap

The addition only applies to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment. The addition is never taken over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken ([art. 3.47 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Both limits apply together. The Belastingdienst describes the term on its desinvesteringsbijtelling page ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling deemed disposals and undone investments

Lid 3 treats as a disposal: withdrawing the good from the onderneming (onderdeel a), changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, the KIA case) or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents or foreign enterprises, the EIA and MIA case) (onderdeel b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (onderdeel c). Lid 4: in those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair market value) counts as the transfer price. Lid 5: undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering (reduction), teruggaaf (refund) or vergoeding (compensation), counts as a disposal for that amount. Lid 6: undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7: the Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 ([art. 3.47 lid 3 to 6, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA and not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Ordinary depreciation caps

Art. 3.30 lid 1: the yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten (acquisition or production costs) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2: per year at most 10% of the cost for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 and 5 years. Lid 3: voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging. Lid 4: voorwerpen van geringe waarde (items of small value) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging ([art. 3.30, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The text is the same in 2020 to 2027. A Vamil asset takes 75% at will and puts the rest through this normal schedule.

### Bodemwaarde for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1: depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde (floor value), and at most for the difference. Lid 2: a building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden (appurtenances) are one bedrijfsmiddel, while detachable werktuigen (tools) are separate. Lid 3: for 2020 to 2023 the bodemwaarde was the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (investment building) and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another (own use) building. From 2024 there is one bodemwaarde, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" ([art. 3.30a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Lid 4: the WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking (decision) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text adds lid 5 (co-owned building pro rata), lid 6 (ground owned by an unrelated party reduces the WOZ-waarde) and lid 7 to lid 10 (related persons and verbonden lichaam). For 2025 the one bodemwaarde of the WOZ-waarde applies.

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap

Statutory basis: art. 3.34 of the Wet IB 2001 allows willekeurige afschrijving (random depreciation) on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship ([art. 3.34, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In the URWA, the regulation that designates the assets, art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the startersaftrek, starter's deduction) applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits this to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst as ondernemer. Art. 8 allows the willekeurige afschrijving for the part of the total costs in the calendar year that does not go above the maximum amount in the KIA table over which KIA can be obtained, and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground ([URWA art. 7 to 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). For 2025 that cap reads as € 392.230, the top of the KIA table ([art. 3.41 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). No primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7. The Belastingdienst adds: eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, the startersaftrek conditions met, only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek, free choice of assets, and the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). Herwaardering (revaluation) to the normal book value, with the difference as winst, applies within 5 years, or 10 for zeeschepen, if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf (forestry business).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving period

Art. 9 of the URWA sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs ([URWA art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10 ([URWA art. 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The same 5-year and 10-year terms come back in the herwaardering rule on the Belastingdienst page ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023

Art. 13 lid 1 of the URWA: assets not used before, with obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in calendar year 2023, taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026 ([URWA art. 13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Lid 2 excludes gebouwen, schepen, vliegtuigen, bromfietsen, motorrijwielen, personenauto's except for beroepsvervoer over de weg or 0 gram CO2 per km, immateriële activa, dieren, assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, and assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Lid 3: the 1 January 2026 date moves by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14: once, at most 50% of the costs, taken in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15: the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 ends 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). The Belastingdienst adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset that is not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year ([Vpb 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The scheme applies to IB and Vpb both. In 2025 the scheme is in its final window: assets from 2023 costs must be in use before 1 January 2026.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1. KIA in the 28% band (Calculated example)

Inputs: one machine bought and taken into use in 2025 for € 60.000, no other investments, winst before the investeringsaftrek € 80.000.

1. Total investment 2025: € 60.000.
2. € 60.000 is above € 2.900 and at or below € 70.602, so the second band applies: 28% of the investment.
3. KIA = 28% × € 60.000 = € 16.800.
4. Winst after the KIA = € 80.000 − € 16.800 = € 63.200.

### Example 2. KIA in the fixed band (Calculated example)

Inputs: total investment € 100.000.

1. € 100.000 is above € 70.602 and at or below € 130.744, so the third band applies.
2. KIA = € 19.769, the fixed amount for that band.

### Example 3. KIA in the tapering band (Calculated example)

Inputs: total investment € 200.000.

1. € 200.000 is above € 130.744 and at or below € 392.230, so the fourth band applies.
2. Part above € 130.744: € 200.000 − € 130.744 = € 69.256.
3. 7,56% of € 69.256 = € 5.235,75.
4. KIA = € 19.769 − € 5.235,75 = € 14.533,25.

### Example 4. Investment above the top of the table (Calculated example)

Inputs: total investment € 400.000.

1. € 400.000 is above € 392.230.
2. KIA = € 0.

### Example 5. KIA in a samenwerkingsverband (Calculated example)

Inputs: the vof invests € 60.000 in 2025, the taxpayer invests € 20.000 in the own onderneming.

1. Sum: € 60.000 + € 20.000 = € 80.000.
2. The table on € 80.000: above € 70.602 and at or below € 130.744, so € 19.769.
3. Own share: € 20.000 / € 80.000 = 25%.
4. KIA = € 19.769 × 25% = € 4.942,25.

### Example 6. The per-asset minimum of € 450 (Calculated example)

Inputs: three assets bought in 2025 for € 5.000, € 4.700 and € 300.

1. The asset of € 300 is below € 450, so it is excluded from the KIA base.
2. Base: € 5.000 + € 4.700 = € 9.700.
3. € 9.700 is above € 2.900 and at or below € 70.602, so 28%.
4. KIA = 28% × € 9.700 = € 2.716.

### Example 7. Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (Calculated example)

Inputs: a new Energielijst asset ordered in 2025 for € 50.000, not in use at the end of 2025, € 20.000 paid in 2025.

1. Deduction base for 2025: the payments made, € 20.000.
2. EIA = 40% × € 20.000 = € 8.000 in 2025.
3. Still to follow: € 50.000 − € 20.000 = € 30.000 of cost, over which the EIA follows in later years.

### Example 8. EIA plus KIA on one asset (Calculated example)

Inputs: a new Energielijst asset of € 100.000, in use in 2025, reported to RVO within 3 months.

1. EIA = 40% × € 100.000 = € 40.000.
2. KIA: total investment € 100.000, third band, € 19.769.
3. Total deductions = € 40.000 + € 19.769 = € 59.769.
4. The MIA cannot be added to this asset, because the EIA and the MIA cannot be combined.

### Example 9. EIA above the maximum investment amount (Calculated example)

Inputs: an Energielijst asset of € 160.000.000.

1. Maximum investment amount: € 151.000.000.
2. EIA base: € 151.000.000.
3. EIA = 40% × € 151.000.000 = € 60.400.000.
4. Above the maximum: € 160.000.000 − € 151.000.000 = € 9.000.000, which earns no EIA.

### Example 10. MIA categories and the KIA combination (Calculated example)

Inputs: a Milieulijst asset of € 50.000, in use in 2025.

1. Categorie I (code F or G): 45% × € 50.000 = € 22.500.
2. Categorie II (code A or D): 36% × € 50.000 = € 18.000.
3. Categorie III (code B or E): 27% × € 50.000 = € 13.500.
4. KIA on the same € 50.000: 28% × € 50.000 = € 14.000.
5. Categorie I with the KIA gives € 22.500 + € 14.000 = € 36.500 in total deductions.

### Example 11. The EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (Calculated example)

Inputs: an Energielijst asset of € 2.400.

1. € 2.400 is below € 2.500.
2. No EIA on this asset.

### Example 12. RVO notification deadline (Calculated example)

Inputs: the koopovereenkomst for an asset is signed on 15 October 2025.

1. The deadline is 3 months after signing.
2. 15 October 2025 plus 3 months gives 15 January 2026 as the date to report to RVO by.

### Example 13. Vamil (Calculated example)

Inputs: a Vamil asset of € 40.000, in use in 2025.

1. 75% at will: € 40.000 × 75% = € 30.000.
2. The rest: € 40.000 − € 30.000 = € 10.000, which follows the normal depreciation schedule.

### Example 14. Desinvesteringsbijtelling (Calculated example)

Inputs: a machine bought in 2025 for € 30.000 with the KIA at 28% (€ 8.400). The machine is sold within five years for € 20.000, and this is the only disposal in that year.

1. Disposal proceeds: € 20.000, which is more than the threshold of € 2.900.
2. Addition = the investeringsaftrek percentage taken × the transfer price = 28% × € 20.000 = € 5.600.
3. Cap check: the addition may not go above the investment amount over which aftrek was taken, € 30.000. € 5.600 is below the cap.
4. The € 5.600 is added to the profit of the year of the disposal.

### Example 15. Disposal below the threshold (Calculated example)

Inputs: total transfer prices of disposals in one year € 2.500.

1. € 2.500 is not more than € 2.900.
2. No desinvesteringsbijtelling.

### Example 16. Personenauto and own-BV interaction (Calculated example)

Inputs: the taxpayer holds 50% of a BV and buys a machine from that BV for € 10.000.

1. The taxpayer holds at least one third of the BV.
2. Under art. 3.46 lid 1 onderdeel d the obligation is between the taxpayer and that BV.
3. In principle no KIA, EIA or MIA on this machine, unless the Minister grants an ontheffing.

A personenauto (passenger car) of € 30.000 is excluded from the KIA and the EIA by art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel d. For the MIA, lid 3 lifts onderdelen c and d, so a personenauto on the Milieulijst can still earn the MIA.

### Example 17. Ordinary depreciation caps (Calculated example)

Inputs: a machine of € 50.000 and goodwill of € 30.000, both acquired in 2025.

1. Machine: at most 20% × € 50.000 = € 10.000 per year, so € 50.000 / € 10.000 = 5 years at least.
2. Goodwill: at most 10% × € 30.000 = € 3.000 per year, so € 30.000 / € 3.000 = 10 years at least.

### Example 18. Bodemwaarde (Calculated example)

Inputs: a building with a WOZ-waarde of € 300.000 and a book value of € 320.000 at the start of 2025.

1. The book value is above the bodemwaarde by € 320.000 − € 300.000 = € 20.000.
2. Maximum depreciation for 2025 = € 20.000.
3. If the book value were € 290.000, it would be below the bodemwaarde of € 300.000, so no depreciation would be allowed.

### Example 19. Starters willekeurige afschrijving cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: designated assets with obligations or costs in 2025 of € 400.000.

1. The 2025 cap is the top of the KIA table: € 392.230.
2. Random depreciation is possible on up to € 392.230 of the costs.
3. Above the cap: € 400.000 − € 392.230 = € 7.770, which follows the normal schedule.

### Example 20. Temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023 (Calculated example)

Inputs: a machine with an order in 2023 for € 40.000, taken into use in 2025 before 1 January 2026.

1. At most 50% of the costs: 50% × € 40.000 = € 20.000, taken once in the year of the obligation or the costs (2023).
2. The use in 2025 meets the deadline of before 1 January 2026.
3. The period in which the asset must keep meeting the art. 13 conditions ends 31 December 2025.

### Example 21. Starters willekeurige afschrijving truck (Official example)

The Belastingdienst starters willekeurige afschrijving example. A truck is bought on 1 July 2024 for € 150.000, with a 7-year life and a residual value of € 10.000. The taxpayer randomly depreciates € 60.000 in 2024 and € 80.000 in 2025. At the end of 2025 the book value is € 10.000, while the normal book value on 1 January 2026 is € 120.000. If the truck is rented out from 1 January 2026, that moment is within 5 years after 1 January 2024, so the asset must be revalued (herwaardering) to € 120.000. That gives € 120.000 − € 10.000 = € 110.000 boekwinst in 2026. A normal depreciation of € 20.000 in 2026 remains. The 2025 part of this example is the € 80.000 random depreciation ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). The example was published with the 2024 page and runs on into 2025.

- RVO statement that the combined MIA and Vamil benefit can run up to ruim 14% of the investment amount: the RVO page does not state a year, so it is marked unknown and UNVERIFIED for 2025 ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).
- The research rule that a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages, so it stays UNVERIFIED ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)).
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving cap: the URWA names the maximum amount in the KIA table over which KIA can be obtained, and no primary page prints the cap as a number ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [2025 KIA table](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the reading gives € 392.230. A Belastingdienst or RVO page that prints the cap as a number would settle it.
