# Ondernemersaftrek en MKB-winstvrijstelling 2025

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2025 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur's deductions) and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption) for the IB-ondernemer (entrepreneur for income tax purposes) in tax year 2025. The main figures are the zelfstandigenaftrek (self-employed persons' deduction) of € 2.470, the startersaftrek (starter's deduction) of € 2.123 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 12,7%, all built on the urencriterium (hour criterion) of 1.225 hours. Tax year 2025 is enacted law and the rules are the same as 2024 apart from the indexed and reduced amounts. Additions to the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) are not possible in 2025, an existing reserve stays in place under the transitional rules of art. 10a.29.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

### 3.1 IB-ondernemer status

The Belastingdienst judges per activity whether someone is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting. This test stands apart from the btw. A btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst looks at more conditions for the inkomstenbelasting. The Belastingdienst weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer. Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek (investment deduction). The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the zelfstandig beroep (independent profession) counts as an onderneming (art. 3.5). The fetched pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an IB-ondernemer, they say that the Belastingdienst makes its own assessment after registration. Sources: [Belastingdienst, wanneer bent u ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein, wanneer bent u ondernemer](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/), [art. 3.4 and 3.5, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.2 What the ondernemersaftrek consists of

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D deduction), the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (starter's deduction for disability) and the stakingsaftrek (cessation deduction). The startersaftrek is not a separate list item. It is a verhoging (increase) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3. The article text of art. 3.74 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Medegerechtigden (persons entitled to a share of the profit, for example a limited partner) get no zelfstandigenaftrek, no meewerkaftrek, no stakingsaftrek and no MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit. For 2025 the list applies as printed and the 2025 amounts are in the subsections below. Sources: [art. 3.74, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.74, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2020, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 Urencriterium, 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer. On top of that hours test, one of two further tests must be met. Test (a): the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is grotendeels (largely) spent on the businesses. Test (b): the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. The Belastingdienst gives a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in a weekend who counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst gives a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. The test is applied in the inkomstenbelasting return. Sources: [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.4 Second test waived and the 2020 to 2024 lookback

The second test of art. 3.6 lid 1 falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback. It comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. FISIN 2025 prints the lookback window for 2025 as 2020 to 2024. For comparison, FISIN 2020 printed 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 printed "20167 tot en met 2021", both misprints against the statute, which gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.5 Urencriterium and a samenwerkingsverband with connected persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (cooperation) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more. The Belastingdienst gives a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and an ondermaatschap (junior partnership) for (b). Sources: [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.6 Urencriterium and pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity and birth leave) for employees. The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. Sources: [art. 3.6 lid 5, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2023, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.7 Verlaagd urencriterium

The verlaagd urencriterium (reduced hour criterion) in art. 3.78a lid 3 is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid. Sources: [art. 3.78a lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.78a lid 3, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### 3.8 Zelfstandigenaftrek, € 2.470

Art. 3.76 applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium (lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age (state pension age) at the start of the year. For 2025 that amount is € 2.470. Only an IB-ondernemer qualifies, so not a medegerechtigde and not someone who reports inkomsten uit overig werk. The amount of € 2.470 is printed in art. 3.76 valid on 1 January 2025 and the FISIN 2025 table agrees. Watch out: the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 carry-forward examples print € 2.740, which is an example typo. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age, € 1.235

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year, the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too. For 2025 the amount is € 1.235. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.10 Profit cap and the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek

Art. 3.76 lid 5: the aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (non-realised self-employed persons' deduction). If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count. Lid 6: the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking open to bezwaar (decision open to objection). Lid 7: the amount is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar. The text is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts. The beschikkingen and the carry-forward balance belong in the records. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, verrekenen niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### 3.11 Startersaftrek, € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the zelfstandigenaftrek by the startersaftrek when the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period. For 2025 the amount is € 2.123. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, tax-free return of a business from a BV) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Belastingdienst adds that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The Belastingdienst words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute. Sources: [art. 3.76 lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.12 Startersaftrek at AOW age, € 1.062

At the AOW age at the start of the year the startersaftrek is halved under art. 3.76 lid 4. For 2025 the amount is € 1.062. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.13 Startersaftrek prior-years condition

The condition is that the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied at most twice in that period. FISIN 2025 prints this condition for 2025. The Belastingdienst wording "niet elk jaar ondernemer" means the same as the statute, so one single non-ondernemer year in the five-year window is enough. The Belastingdienst also caps the use at 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer and lets a year in which the profit cap set the zelfstandigenaftrek to nil still count as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek. A geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 that started in the year or one of the five years before blocks the startersaftrek. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76 lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.14 Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance (disability or accident insurance). The amount is € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.78a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.78a, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026).

### 3.15 Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, € 15.738

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D deduction) is part of the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77). For 2025 the base amount is € 15.738. FISIN adds that the aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium. The amounts are indexed per year. Sources: [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.16 S&O starter increase, € 7.875

On top of the base amount a starter increase of € 7.875 applies for 2025 to an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period. The same S&O-verklaring from RVO, the 500-hour S&O test and the urencriterium apply. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.17 MKB-winstvrijstelling, 12,7%

Art. 3.79a makes the MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek. Art. 3.2 says the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit from business) is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The order is therefore profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. No urencriterium is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none. There is no vrijstelling (exemption) on profit as medegerechtigde. In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing. Sources: [art. 3.2 and 3.79a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### 3.18 Meewerkaftrek bands

Art. 3.78 gives the meewerkaftrek to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number. Exactly 875 hours therefore falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. The hours of the partner must be made plausible. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.78, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.78 lid 2, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.78, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, meewerkaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.19 Pay to the partner below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. This links the pay rule to the meewerkaftrek bands above. Sources: [art. 3.16, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.16, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, meewerkaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.20 Stakingsaftrek, € 3.630

Art. 3.79 gives the stakingsaftrek on profit made with or on the staking (cessation) of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde. Special cases: for a business continued under doorschuiving (roll-over) on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (marital community of property, art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), the aftrek applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming (decrease) of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022. Sources: [art. 3.79, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.79, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.79 and 10a.29, version 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, stakingsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.21 Oudedagsreserve in 2025

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition to the oudedagsreserve is possible from 2023, also not in 2025. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023. The afneming rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente (annuity) premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenditure for income provisions) in the year. It also decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (huwelijksgemeenschap, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve and lid 7 keeps the stakingsaftrek for an afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return. The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027. For an old reserve the additions were ruled by art. 3.67 to 3.72 as they read on 31 December 2022, with an addition of 9,44% of the profit as ondernemer up to a yearly maximum and reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and the statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all printed 9,44%. Sources: [art. 3.67 to 3.72 and 10a.29, version 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 10a.29, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.67 to 3.72, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.67 to 3.72, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### 3.22 Tariefsaanpassing, 12,02% above € 76.817

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (base-reducing items), expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. For 2025 the percentage is 12,02% and the start of the top bracket is € 76.817. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Sources: [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### 3.23 Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling, 37,48%

For 2025 the maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 37,48%. The Belastingdienst page prints this as 49,50% minus 12,02%. The tariefsaanpassing of art. 2.10 is what delivers the cap. Sources: [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: urencriterium in a part year (Official example)

**Official example.** KVK gives a start on 1 May, which leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours. Arithmetic as printed: 117 working days x 8 hours = 936 hours. The Belastingdienst gives a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year.

Source: [KVK, de magische 1.225 uur](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium) and [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Example 2: zelfstandigenaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** A sole trader below the AOW age meets the urencriterium, has no startersaftrek and no other deductions. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: profit € 60.000.
- Step 2: zelfstandigenaftrek € 2.470.
- Step 3: profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 2.470 = € 57.530.
- Step 4: MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 57.530: € 57.530 x 0,127 = € 7.306,31.
- Step 5: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 57.530 - € 7.306,31 = € 50.223,69.
- Step 6: tariefsaanpassing test: the deductions are € 2.470 + € 7.306,31 = € 9.776,31 and the test amount is € 50.223,69 + € 9.776,31 = € 60.000. That is below € 76.817, so no tariefsaanpassing with this income alone.

### Example 3: same profit at AOW age (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Same facts as Example 2, but the ondernemer has reached the AOW age at the start of 2025 and meets the urencriterium.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 1.235.
- Step 2: profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 1.235 = € 58.765.
- Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 58.765: € 58.765 x 0,127 = € 7.463,155, rounded to € 7.463,16.
- Step 4: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 58.765 - € 7.463,16 = € 51.301,84.

### Example 4: starter with profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years, meets the urencriterium and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied at most twice in that period. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 2.470 plus startersaftrek € 2.123 = € 4.593.
- Step 2: profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 4.593 = € 55.407.
- Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 55.407: € 55.407 x 0,127 = € 7.036,689, rounded to € 7.036,69.
- Step 4: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 55.407 - € 7.036,69 = € 48.370,31.

### Example 5: low profit with startersaftrek (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Same starter as Example 4, but the profit is € 4.000.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 2.470 plus startersaftrek € 2.123 = € 4.593.
- Step 2: the profit cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not apply to an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek, so the full € 4.593 is deducted: € 4.000 - € 4.593 = -€ 593.
- Step 3: the excess of € 593 is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years.

### Example 6: niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (Official example)

**Official example.** The Belastingdienst carry-forward page prints: 2025 profit € 1.500 against a zelfstandigenaftrek of € 2.470. The aftrek is limited to the profit, so € 1.500 is used and € 970 is unused (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek): € 2.470 - € 1.500 = € 970. The page then prints for 2026: profit € 50.000 minus € 1.200 gives € 48.800, minus € 970 gives € 47.830 and the remainder of the unused amount is € 0.

Source: [Belastingdienst, verrekenen niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### Example 7: carry-forward across three years (Official example, with a printed typo)

**Official example.** FISIN 2025 section 8.4.3 prints: 2023 profit € 5.000 against € 5.030, so € 30 is unused (€ 5.030 - € 5.000 = € 30). 2024 shows a loss of € 25.000 and € 3.750 is unused, so the saldo is € 30 + € 3.750 = € 3.780. 2025 profit € 100.000 with a printed right of € 2.740 (which should be € 2.470) gives € 97.260 (€ 100.000 - € 2.740 = € 97.260), minus € 3.780 gives € 93.480 (€ 97.260 - € 3.780 = € 93.480).

Corrected calculation with the section 2 figure of € 2.470, not printed on the page: € 100.000 - € 2.470 = € 97.530, then € 97.530 - € 3.780 = € 93.750.

Source: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 8: meewerkaftrek (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The ondernemer meets the urencriterium and the partner works in the business without pay for exactly 1.225 hours. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: exactly 1.225 hours falls in the 3% band, because the band runs from 1.225 hours to under 1.750 hours.
- Step 2: meewerkaftrek 3% of € 60.000: € 60.000 x 0,03 = € 1.800,00.
- Step 3: ondernemersaftrek total: € 2.470 + € 1.800,00 = € 4.270,00.
- Step 4: profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 4.270,00 = € 55.730,00.
- Step 5: MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 55.730: € 55.730 x 0,127 = € 7.077,71.
- Step 6: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 55.730,00 - € 7.077,71 = € 48.652,29.

### Example 9: stakingsaftrek (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Case A: the profit on the staking of the whole business is € 10.000 and € 1.000 of stakingsaftrek was used in an earlier year. The maximum is then € 3.630 - € 1.000 = € 2.630, which is below the € 10.000 profit, so the aftrek is € 2.630. Case B: the profit on the staking is € 2.000 and no stakingsaftrek was used before. The aftrek equals the profit, so € 2.000, which is below the maximum of € 3.630.

### Example 10: startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years, has a right to a qualifying disability benefit, meets the verlaagd urencriterium but not the urencriterium, and is below the AOW age.

- First use, profit € 3.000: the amount is € 12.000, capped at the profit, so the aftrek is € 3.000.
- Second use, profit € 50.000: the amount is € 8.000, below the profit, so the aftrek is € 8.000.
- Third use, profit € 50.000: the amount is € 4.000, below the profit, so the aftrek is € 4.000.
- Fourth use, profit € 50.000: the statute has no tier for more than two earlier uses, so the aftrek is € 0.

### Example 11: aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the S&O aftrek applied at most twice in that period, so the S&O starter increase applies. The zelfstandigenaftrek was applied more than twice in that period, so no startersaftrek applies. The ondernemer has an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work in 2025 and meets the urencriterium. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 2.470.
- Step 2: aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: base amount € 15.738 plus starter increase € 7.875 = € 23.613.
- Step 3: total ondernemersaftrek: € 2.470 + € 23.613 = € 26.083.
- Step 4: profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 26.083 = € 33.917.
- Step 5: MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,7% of € 33.917: € 33.917 x 0,127 = € 4.307,459, rounded to € 4.307,46.
- Step 6: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 33.917 - € 4.307,46 = € 29.609,54.

### Example 12: tariefsaanpassing (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Use the taxpayer of Example 2, with deductions of € 9.776,31 (€ 2.470 ondernemersaftrek plus € 7.306,31 MKB-winstvrijstelling) and a belastbare winst uit onderneming of € 50.223,69.

- Case A: if this is the only income, the test amount is € 50.223,69 + € 9.776,31 = € 60.000, below € 76.817, so the tax is not raised.
- Case B: if other box 1 income brings the test amount to € 81.817, the excess is € 81.817 - € 76.817 = € 5.000. The rise is 12,02% of € 5.000: € 5.000 x 0,1202 = € 601,00. The cap is 12,02% of € 9.776,31: € 9.776,31 x 0,1202 = € 1.175,112462, rounded down to € 1.175,11, so the rise of € 601,00 stands.
- Case C: if the test amount is € 90.000, the excess is € 90.000 - € 76.817 = € 13.183. The rise would be 12,02% of € 13.183: € 13.183 x 0,1202 = € 1.584,5966, rounded to € 1.584,60, which is above the cap of € 1.175,11, so the rise is limited to € 1.175,11.
- The maximum value of the deductions is 37,48%, which is 49,50% minus 12,02%.

### Example 13: oudedagsreserve with a broken urencriterium (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** An existing oudedagsreserve is € 50.000 at the start of 2025 and the ondernemingsvermogen at the end of 2025 is € 30.000. The urencriterium was not met in 2025 and not met in 2024. There is no staking and the AOW age was not reached.

- Step 1: no addition to the reserve is possible in 2025, so on that count the reserve stays at € 50.000.
- Step 2: because the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year, the reserve decreases by the excess of the reserve over the ondernemingsvermogen: € 50.000 - € 30.000 = € 20.000.
- Step 3: the decrease of € 20.000 is added to profit.

- The € 2.740 that the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 carry-forward examples print as the 2025 zelfstandigenaftrek is an example typo. The statute valid on 1 January 2025 and the FISIN 2025 table print € 2.470. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The Zvw interaction of the belastbare winst uit onderneming is verified in the checked material for 2020 and 2021 only, so this file makes no Zvw statement for 2025. Sources: [FISIN 2020, inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [FISIN 2021, inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The Zvw detail belongs to the Zvw topic, and a 2025 Zvw source would settle it.
