# Ondernemersaftrek en MKB-winstvrijstelling 2026

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2026 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur's allowance) and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption) for a sole trader in tax year 2026. The rules are the same as in 2025, with new amounts: the zelfstandigenaftrek is € 1.200, the startersaftrek is € 2.123, the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 12,7% and the tariefsaanpassing (rate adjustment) on these deductions is 11,94% above € 78.426. Every 2026 value in this file is enacted and verified against the statute valid on 1 January 2026, and the current Belastingdienst pages on the urencriterium, startersaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek, MKB-winstvrijstelling and oudedagsreserve match the statute. The MvT of Belastingplan 2027 states that ondernemers can still use the startersaftrek in 2026, and later-year proposals are kept apart in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

The ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 has five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek. The startersaftrek is not a separate list item, it is a verhoging (increase) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Only an IB-ondernemer can use these schemes. Whether someone is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged per activity by the Belastingdienst, separately from the btw, on profit, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk, calculates the profit the same way but has no right to the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). Medegerechtigden get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit. The order of application is the profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Urencriterium, 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example starts a webhosting business on 1 July, which leaves six months as an ondernemer, and still requires at least 1.225 hours in that period. KVK gives the same reading for a start on 1 May: eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, including non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Second test and the lookback window 2021 to 2025

Art. 3.6 lid 1 has a second element. Besides the 1.225 hours, either the total time spent on the businesses and on employment and other work must be grotendeels (largely) spent on the businesses, or the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. FISIN 2026 prints that lookback window as 2021 to 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The effect is that the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the window, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. The current Belastingdienst urencriterium page does not carry the dated starter examples for this test, see section 7.

### Verlaagd urencriterium, 800 hours

Art. 3.78a lid 3 applies the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 hours and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, so someone between 800 and 1.225 hours does not get the zelfstandigenaftrek but can get that variant if the other conditions are met (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Samenwerkingsverband with connected persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (cooperation) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads mainly supporting as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and an ondermaatschap for (b) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Pregnancy and the 16 weeks

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity and birth leave) for employees. The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek, € 1.200

Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the zelfstandigenaftrek to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium. For 2026 the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year is € 1.200 (lid 2), and FISIN 2026 and Ondernemersplein agree (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Art. 3.76 lid 5: the aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is called niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (unrealised self-employed deduction), and if the cap sets the aftrek to nil the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age, € 600

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year, the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2, so € 600, and the startersaftrek is halved too. FISIN 2026 prints € 600 for the zelfstandigenaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, offset over nine calendar years

Art. 3.76 lid 6: the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection). Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek). The Belastingdienst prints the mechanism on its carry-forward page, with the 2025 to 2026 example used in section 4.

### Startersaftrek, € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the zelfstandigenaftrek by the startersaftrek if the conditions in the next subsection are met. The Belastingdienst adds that the starter needs a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The startersaftrek does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (a geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, a silent return from a private limited company) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). For 2026 the amount is € 2.123, and the MvT of Belastingplan 2027 states that ondernemers can still use the startersaftrek in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

### Startersaftrek at AOW age, € 1.062

Because art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the amount under lid 3 as well, the startersaftrek is € 1.062 at the AOW age at the start of the year, which is 50% of € 2.123. FISIN 2026 prints € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Startersaftrek prior-years condition

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the startersaftrek applies if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period. For 2026 the five preceding years are 2021 to 2025. The Belastingdienst words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test, and adds that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer. FISIN 2026 prints the condition in the same way (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium of at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance (disability or accident insurance). The amounts are € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then, and FISIN prints € 0 for that case (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026).

### Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, € 15.979

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk is part of the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77). For 2026 the amount is € 15.979, and FISIN 2026 agrees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). FISIN adds that the aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring (research and development certificate) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### S&O starter increase, € 7.996

Art. 3.77 gives a base amount plus a starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period. For 2026 the starter increase is € 7.996, per FISIN 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Meewerkaftrek bands

Art. 3.78 applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. The statute bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). The checked sources give the meewerkaftrek as a percentage of the profit and do not state the base order against the other parts of the ondernemersaftrek, see section 7.

### Pay to the partner below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. This matters for the meewerkaftrek, because the band can still apply while the wage cannot be deducted (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.16, text identical 2020 to 2027, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### Stakingsaftrek, € 3.630

Art. 3.79 applies to profit made with or on the staking (discontinuation) of one or more whole businesses. The aftrek equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under doorschuiving on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022. The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and that there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### Oudedagsreserve in 2026

Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition to the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) is possible in 2026. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023. The afneming (decrease) rules are that the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year, and that it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business, lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the stakingsaftrek for an afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 10a.29, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling, 12,7%

Art. 3.79a makes the MKB-winstvrijstelling a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek. For 2026 the percentage is 12,7%. Art. 3.2 defines the belastbare winst uit onderneming as the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. No urencriterium is needed, because the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none. There is no vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde. In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Tariefsaanpassing, 11,94% above € 78.426

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the tax base), expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. For 2026 the percentage is 11,94% and the start of the top bracket is € 78.426 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Maximum value of the deductions, 37,56%

The maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling in 2026 is 37,56%, which is the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing of 11,94% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1, urencriterium in a part year (Official example)

A webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer in the year, and the taxpayer still has to meet the full 1.225 hours, because the hours are not prorated to the period in which the taxpayer was an ondernemer. The KVK example adds a start on 1 May: eight months remain, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 117 × 8 = 936,00 hours, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium.

### Example 2, zelfstandigenaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling at a profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit as ondernemer € 60.000,00, the taxpayer has not reached the AOW age at the start of 2026, the urencriterium is met, no startersaftrek, no other ondernemersaftrek.

- Step 1, zelfstandigenaftrek: € 1.200,00.
- Step 2, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 1.200,00 = € 58.800,00.
- Step 3, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 58.800,00 × 12,7% = € 7.467,60.
- Step 4, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 58.800,00 minus € 7.467,60 = € 51.332,40.

### Example 3, starter at a profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit € 60.000,00, the taxpayer has not reached the AOW age at the start of 2026, the urencriterium is met, and the prior-years conditions for the startersaftrek are met: the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of 2021 to 2025, and the zelfstandigenaftrek was used at most twice in that period.

- Step 1, zelfstandigenaftrek: € 1.200,00.
- Step 2, startersaftrek: € 2.123,00.
- Step 3, total ondernemersaftrek: € 1.200,00 plus € 2.123,00 = € 3.323,00.
- Step 4, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 3.323,00 = € 56.677,00.
- Step 5, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 56.677,00 × 12,7% = € 7.197,979, rounded to € 7.197,98.
- Step 6, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 56.677,00 minus € 7.197,98 = € 49.479,02.

### Example 4, AOW age at the start of 2026 (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit € 60.000,00, the taxpayer had reached the AOW age before 1 January 2026, the urencriterium is met, and the starter conditions for the startersaftrek are met.

- Step 1, zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 600,00.
- Step 2, startersaftrek at AOW age: € 1.062,00.
- Step 3, total ondernemersaftrek: € 600,00 plus € 1.062,00 = € 1.662,00.
- Step 4, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 1.662,00 = € 58.338,00.
- Step 5, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 58.338,00 × 12,7% = € 7.408,926, rounded to € 7.408,93.
- Step 6, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 58.338,00 minus € 7.408,93 = € 50.929,07.

### Example 5, meewerkaftrek at 1.750 hours and at 875 hours (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit € 60.000,00, the ondernemer meets the urencriterium, the partner works in the business without pay. This example isolates the meewerkaftrek, so no other part of the ondernemersaftrek is included.

- At 1.750 hours or more the band is 4%: € 60.000,00 × 4% = € 2.400,00 meewerkaftrek.
- Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 2.400,00 = € 57.600,00.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 57.600,00 × 12,7% = € 7.315,20.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 57.600,00 minus € 7.315,20 = € 50.284,80.
- Boundary check at exactly 875 hours: the statute band runs from 875 hours to under 1.225 hours, so the rate is 2%, not 1,25%. At a profit of € 60.000,00 that is € 60.000,00 × 2% = € 1.200,00.

### Example 6, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit € 20.000,00, the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of 2021 to 2025, has a right to a WIA benefit in 2026, does not meet the urencriterium but meets the verlaagd urencriterium with at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours, has not reached the AOW age at the start of 2026, and the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years.

- Step 1, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid: € 12.000,00, not more than the profit of € 20.000,00, so € 12.000,00.
- Step 2, no zelfstandigenaftrek, because the urencriterium is not met. The verlaagd urencriterium is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, not of the zelfstandigenaftrek.
- Step 3, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000,00 minus € 12.000,00 = € 8.000,00.
- Step 4, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 8.000,00 × 12,7% = € 1.016,00. The MKB-winstvrijstelling still applies, because the statute sets no urencriterium for it.
- Step 5, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 8.000,00 minus € 1.016,00 = € 6.984,00.
- Variant with profit € 5.000,00 and no earlier use: the aftrek is capped at the profit, so € 5.000,00.

### Example 7, aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit € 60.000,00, the taxpayer has not reached the AOW age at the start of 2026, the urencriterium is met, and the taxpayer holds an S&O-verklaring from RVO and made at least 500 hours of S&O work.

- Step 1, zelfstandigenaftrek: € 1.200,00.
- Step 2, aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 15.979,00.
- Step 3, total ondernemersaftrek: € 1.200,00 plus € 15.979,00 = € 17.179,00.
- Step 4, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 17.179,00 = € 42.821,00.
- Step 5, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 42.821,00 × 12,7% = € 5.438,267, rounded to € 5.438,27.
- Step 6, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 42.821,00 minus € 5.438,27 = € 37.382,73.
- If the starter conditions for the S&O increase are also met, the total ondernemersaftrek would be € 1.200,00 plus € 15.979,00 plus € 7.996,00 = € 25.175,00.

### Example 8, tariefsaanpassing (Calculated example)

Case 1. Inputs: taxable box 1 income after the deductions is € 80.000,00 and the ondernemersaftrek plus MKB-winstvrijstelling together are € 10.000,00.

- Step 1, income plus the deductions: € 80.000,00 plus € 10.000,00 = € 90.000,00.
- Step 2, excess over € 78.426,00: € 90.000,00 minus € 78.426,00 = € 11.574,00.
- Step 3, 11,94% of the excess: € 11.574,00 × 11,94% = € 1.381,9356, rounded to € 1.381,94.
- Step 4, cap: 11,94% of the deductions, € 10.000,00 × 11,94% = € 1.194,00.
- Step 5, the tax rise is the lower of the two, so € 1.194,00.

Case 2. Inputs: taxable box 1 income after the deductions is € 70.000,00 and the deductions are € 5.000,00. Income plus deductions is € 75.000,00, which does not exceed € 78.426,00, so there is no tariefsaanpassing.

### Example 9, stakingsaftrek (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit from the staking of one whole business € 5.000,00, no stakingsaftrek was used in earlier years, and no other ondernemersaftrek applies in this example.

- Step 1, stakingsaftrek: at most € 3.630,00 and at most the profit of € 5.000,00, so € 3.630,00.
- Step 2, profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 5.000,00 minus € 3.630,00 = € 1.370,00.
- Step 3, MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 1.370,00 × 12,7% = € 173,99.
- Step 4, belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 1.370,00 minus € 173,99 = € 1.196,01.
- Earlier use: the maximum of € 3.630,00 is available once in a lifetime and is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. Earlier use does not remove the stakingsaftrek for a later staking, it reduces the remaining lifetime maximum by the amount used. If € 1.000,00 was used in an earlier year, the maximum for a later staking is € 3.630,00 minus € 1.000,00 = € 2.630,00. An unused remainder can be used at a later staking.

### Example 10, carry-forward from 2025 to 2026 (Official example)

The Belastingdienst carry-forward example: a profit of € 1.500,00 in 2025 against a zelfstandigenaftrek of € 2.470,00, so the aftrek in 2025 is limited to € 1.500,00 and € 970,00 is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (€ 2.470,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 970,00). In 2026 the profit is € 50.000,00 and the zelfstandigenaftrek is € 1.200,00. The offset is at most the profit minus that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, so at most € 50.000,00 minus € 1.200,00 = € 48.800,00. The full € 970,00 fits within that cap: € 48.800,00 minus € 970,00 = € 47.830,00. The unused amount remaining after 2026 is € 0,00.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek

### Example 11, FISIN 2026 carry-forward example as printed (Official example)

FISIN 2026 section 8.4.3 prints a carry-forward example with typos in the year labels and amounts. The printed figures are:

- Year 1: profit € 3.000,00 against a zelfstandigenaftrek of € 3.750,00, so € 750,00 is unused (€ 3.750,00 minus € 3.000,00 = € 750,00).
- Year 2: a loss of € 25.000,00 and € 2.740,00 unused. The € 2.740,00 is an example typo, the correct amount for that year is € 2.470,00. The printed saldo is € 3.490,00, which is € 750,00 plus the printed € 2.740,00 (€ 750,00 plus € 2.740,00 = € 3.490,00). With the correct € 2.470,00 the saldo would be € 3.220,00 (€ 750,00 plus € 2.470,00 = € 3.220,00).
- Year 3: profit € 100.000,00 minus the zelfstandigenaftrek of € 1.200,00 gives € 98.800,00. The printed example subtracts the printed saldo of € 3.490,00 and gives € 95.310,00 (€ 98.800,00 minus € 3.490,00 = € 95.310,00). With the corrected saldo of € 3.220,00 it would give € 95.580,00 (€ 98.800,00 minus € 3.220,00 = € 95.580,00).

This example is reproduced as printed. The corrected amounts are not part of the printed FISIN example.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the hours spent on the business, because the 1.225 hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. All hours on the business count, including non-billable work, but standby time does not count (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Record the partner's hours in the business, because the meewerkaftrek bands of 1,25% to 4% depend on those hours and the hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Pay to a partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit and is no income for the partner. The Belastingdienst treats such a partner as working without pay, so the meewerkaftrek can apply (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Keep the S&O-verklaring from RVO for the S&O aftrek, together with the proof of at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Keep the beschikkingen for the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek and for the offset of that amount, because both are given by beschikking open to bezwaar and the offset runs over the next nine calendar years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- No addition to the oudedagsreserve can be booked in 2026. An existing reserve stays and follows the art. 10a.29 afneming rules. Decreases are added to profit, and lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve are deductible up to the decrease of that year. Premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 10a.29, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- The belastbare winst uit onderneming is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, and it is taxed in box 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.2 and 3.79a).
- Keep the documentation of a staking of a whole business, because the stakingsaftrek of at most € 3.630 is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Not in the checked sources for 2026: the dated starter examples for the second test of the urencriterium (starter in 2026, since 2024, since 2022, a 2018 starter who stopped in 2023 and restarted in 2026, since 2018). Fetched on 2026-10-11 the Belastingdienst urencriterium page has only the two webhosting examples. They are consistent with the statute and with the FISIN 2026 window of 2021 to 2025, but keep them out unless a Belastingdienst page with them is found (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Conflict in the FISIN 2026 carry-forward example. FISIN 2026 section 8.4.3 prints € 2.740 unused in the loss year, while the material marks € 2.470 as correct. The 2025 statute prints € 2.470. The example also has typos in the year labels and amounts. Both values and both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. The statute and the FISIN 2025 table settle it at € 2.470.
- Not in the checked sources: the base of the meewerkaftrek percentage. Art. 3.78 gives the aftrek as a percentage of the profit, and the checked sources do not state whether that base is first reduced by the other parts of the ondernemersaftrek. The statute text of art. 3.78 as valid on 1 January 2026 and a Belastingdienst calculation example would settle it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Not verified for 2026: the interaction with the Zvw contribution. The checked material covers the belastbare winst uit onderneming as part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The detail belongs to the Zvw topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. Bill 37 022 would cut the startersaftrek to € 10 in 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028, and would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) from 1 January 2029. The adopted 2027 statute text still holds € 2.123 for 2027, adopted but not yet in force. Both layers and both URLs: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. The Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups would cut the meewerkaftrek and the stakingsaftrek, with Artikel I onderdelen B and C carrying the cuts and Artikel II the abolition, and it would delete the meewerkaftrek and the stakingsaftrek (onderdelen c and d after renumbering) three calendar years after its cuts take effect. The enacted 2027 text still holds the 1,25% to 4% bands and the € 3.630 maximum, adopted but not yet in force. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Conflict for 2027, not 2026: the enacted statute text holds a tariefsaanpassing of 11,91% with a maximum value of 37,59%, while the Belastingplan 2027 figure is 38,16% (proposed). Both URLs: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf.
