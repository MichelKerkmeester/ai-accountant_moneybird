# Pensioen, lijfrente en AOV 2026

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2026 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the rules for deducting pension premiums, lijfrentepremies (annuity premiums), and arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering (AOV, disability insurance) premiums for a Dutch sole trader (ZZP) in tax year 2026. The most important figures are the jaarruimte (annual deduction room) of up to € 35.589 and the reserveringsruimte (catch-up room) of up to € 42.753. The law for 2026 is enacted and in force. The Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen (BAZ) is a proposed law that is not yet in force.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

### 3.1 Jaarruimte percentage

This applies to any taxpayer who had a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year (2025). Art. 3.127 lid 1 allows a deduction for lijfrentepremies (annuity premiums) up to 30% of the premiegrondslag (premium base), reduced by the reduction for pension accrual under lid 4. The 30% rate applies to 2026 because it was enacted in the 2023-07-01 law text and has been confirmed in the 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01, and 2027-01-01 texts. The Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" (retroactively from 1 January 2023). For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: 30% x (min(income of previous year, € 137.800) minus € 19.172). Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).

### 3.2 Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

The aftoppingsgrens (cap) for the premiegrondslag is € 137.800 for 2026. This amount is the maximum income base taken into account when calculating the jaarruimte. The amount was not indexed at the start of 2026 (amendement Grinwis c.s., Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90). Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingplan 2027 memorie](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).

### 3.3 Franchise premiegrondslag

The franchise (threshold) for the premiegrondslag is € 19.172 for 2026. This amount is subtracted from the capped income to arrive at the premiegrondslag. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### 3.4 Maximum jaarruimte

The maximum jaarruimte for 2026 is € 35.589. This is calculated as 30% x (€ 137.800 minus € 19.172), a base of € 118.628, which the Ministry 2026 table and the ODB 2026 sheet give as € 35.589. Source: [Ministry of Finance 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file), [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### 3.5 Reserveringsruimte maximum

The reserveringsruimte (catch-up room) maximum for 2026 is € 42.753. This is the maximum amount of unused jaarruimte from previous years that can be used in 2026. Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows a taxpayer to use unused jaarruimte of the preceding period on request in the aangifte (tax return), oldest unused year first. The look-back period is 10 years, covering jaarruimte from 2016 to 2025. The Belastingdienst advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse. Unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.6 Look-back window

The look-back window for 2026 is 10 years, covering jaarruimte from 2016 to 2025. This means unused jaarruimte from those years can be used in 2026 as reserveringsruimte. The period was 7 years in the 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023-01-01 texts, and was extended to 10 years from the 2023-07-01 text. FISIN 2026 agrees. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.7 Jaarruimte birth date limit

A taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the jaarruimte 2026 but may still use the reserveringsruimte 2026. This is stated on the Belastingdienst page for 2026. Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.8 Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

The tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente (temporary old-age annuity) has a yearly cap of € 27.192 for 2026. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c requires at least five years of termijnen (installments), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to the statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN 2026 shows € 27.192 for the verzekering row, but its rekening row still prints € 26.781 (stale). The Ministry and ODB give € 27.192. Source: [Art. 3.125 lid 1 c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### 3.9 Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The overbruggingslijfrente (bridge annuity) cap is € 63.288 per year for 2026. This product is only for lijfrentetegoeden (annuity balances) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age, or starting a pensioen. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text but appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. Source: [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.10 Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

The stakingswinst (cessation profit) maximum for tier a is € 574.867 for 2026. Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions. Tier a applies if the age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or if the taxpayer is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt (disabled) with the lijfrente starting within six months, or on staking by death. The ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for this tier. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.11 Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

The stakingswinst maximum for tier b is € 287.445 for 2026. Tier b applies if the age at staking is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or if the lijfrente starts immediately. The ODB 2026 sheet gives "52 to under 62" for this tier. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.12 Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

The stakingswinst maximum for tier c is € 143.732 for 2026. Tier c applies to all other cases. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.13 Afkoop kleine lijfrente

Afkoop (commutation) of a small lijfrente in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount of € 5.513 for 2026. This is in art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d. Source: [Art. 3.133 lid 2 d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.14 Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

The afkoop cap for langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid (long-term disability) is € 51.440 for 2026. Art. 3.133 lid 9 states that afkoop is not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer (policyholder) or gerechtigde (beneficiary) is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn. Source: [Art. 3.133 lid 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.15 AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) premiums are fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c if the policy pays periodic uitkeringen (benefits) for invalidity, illness, or accident. FISIN 2026 states "volledig aftrekken" (fully deductible). There is no ruimte cap in the article. As an ondernemer (self-employed person) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing. Source: [Art. 3.124 lid 1 c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### 3.16 AOV premium with lump-sum payout

An AOV premium for a policy that pays a lump sum (kapitaalverzekering) is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. The rule of thumb is: premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. The four exclusions and section 15.4 are the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. Source: [Belastingdienst: AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### 3.17 BAZ bill status

The BAZ (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen) bill 36 912 was ingediend (submitted) on 24 March 2026. The docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag" (awaiting note after report). There is no plenary date. The inbreng (input) took place on 27 May 2026, and the Verslag document 36 912 nr. 5 was published on 29 May 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).

### 3.18 BAZ wachttijd in the bill

The BAZ bill art. 16 lid 1 sets a wachttijd (waiting period) of 104 weken. The docket summary still says "ongeveer één jaar" (about one year), which conflicts with the bill text. The bill text is authoritative. Source: [Bill 36912, art. 16](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313).

### 3.19 BAZ premium estimate

The BAZ premium is set yearly by UWV and estimated at about 5,4% of winst uit onderneming (profit from business) up to the maximum grondslag (base). At a maximum grondslag of € 38.000 the MvT examples give about € 171 gross per month. Self-employed persons who are also employees have their SV-loon (social insurance wage) subtracted from the maximum premiegrondslag. Source: [MvT 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).

### 3.20 BAZ benefit maximum

The BAZ benefit is 70% of the grondslag. The grondslag is at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so the benefit is at most 100% of the minimumloon. Source: [MvT 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).

### 3.21 BAZ premium tax treatment

The public BAZ premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenditure for income provisions) and not from the winst. UWV benefits would carry loonheffing (payroll tax). Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Source: [MvT 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).

### 3.22 BAZ entry into force

The BAZ entry into force is by koninklijk besluit (royal decree). There is no statutory date. The MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. Source: [MvT 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Jaarruimte calculation (Calculated example)

A sole trader has a winst uit onderneming (profit) of € 60.000 in 2025 and no pension accrual. The premiegrondslag for 2026 is: min(€ 60.000, € 137.800) = € 60.000 minus franchise € 19.172 = € 40.828. The jaarruimte is 30% x € 40.828 = € 12.248,40. This is the maximum deductible lijfrentepremie for 2026, assuming no reduction for pension accrual. Source for figures: [Art. 3.127](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Example 2: Jaarruimte with maximum income (Calculated example)

A sole trader has a winst uit onderneming of € 150.000 in 2025 and no pension accrual. The premiegrondslag is: min(€ 150.000, € 137.800) = € 137.800 minus franchise € 19.172 = € 118.628. The jaarruimte is 30% x € 118.628, which the Ministry 2026 table gives as € 35.589. Source: [Art. 3.127](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry of Finance 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file).

### Example 3: Official example of jaarruimte and premiums paid (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives an example where the jaarruimte is € 1.555 and € 1.500 in premiums are paid. The € 1.500 is deductible, and the € 55 carries into the reserveringsruimte of a later year. Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 4: Official example of exceeding the jaarruimte (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives an example where the jaarruimte 2026 is € 1.600, there is no reserveringsruimte, and € 1.500 premium plus € 500 storting (deposit) is paid. At most € 1.600 is deductible. The € 400 is never deductible but is counted at the uitkering (payout). Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 5: BAZ premium (Calculated example based on proposed law)

The MvT gives examples for the proposed BAZ. At a winst of € 38.000 or € 45.000 the premium is about € 171 per month. At a winst of € 25.000 the premium is about € 113 per month. Negative winst gives € 0. A winst of € 25.000 plus € 25.000 SV-loon gives a grondslag of € 13.000 and about € 59 per month. These are proposed figures and not enacted law. Source: [MvT 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

For a sole trader, record lijfrentepremies and AOV premiums as private expenditures that are deductible in the inkomstenbelasting (income tax) return, not as business costs on the winst. The AOV premium is an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen" under art. 3.124 lid 1 c. Keep the policy documents and payment records. For a FOR or stakingswinst conversion, pay before 1 July of the following year (before 1 July 2027 for 2026). The deduction is claimed in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting. Source: [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Art. 3.124](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst: AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- The 2026 Belastingdienst birth date limit (1 September 1953) was not recomputed against the AOW age table of art. 7a AOW. Settling source: art. 7a AOW at wetten.overheid.nl. Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- The ODB 2026 sheet lists factor A 6,27 under art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a, but the 2026 law text no longer has a factor A in lid 4. It survives only through art. 10a.25 lid 1. Treat the ODB factor A line as outdated. Source: [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf), [Art. 3.127](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- The BAZ bill is proposed and not enacted. All BAZ figures are proposed. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- The FISIN 2026 rekening row for the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente prints € 26.781, while the law, Ministry and ODB give € 27.192. Use € 27.192. Source: [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Art. 3.125](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The docket summary for the BAZ says "ongeveer één jaar" for the wachttijd, while bill art. 16 says 104 weken. Use 104 weken. Source: [Docket 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [Bill 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313).
- No other open points for 2026.
