## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the Dutch investment deductions for 2026, the kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction), the energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction), the milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction) and the Vamil (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, random depreciation for environmental investments). It also covers ordinary depreciation of bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets), the bodemwaarde of buildings, willekeurige afschrijving for starters and the desinvesteringsbijtelling. The 2026 figures are higher than the 2025 figures on several points, the EIA and MIA maximum investment amount is € 153.000.000, and from 2026 the EIA investments of an eenmanszaak and of a samenwerkingsverband are added together for that maximum. All 2026 values in this file are enacted law or published conditions, except the RVO cap of € 25 miljoen for MIA and Vamil, which carries no year on the checked page and is listed in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA), table

The KIA is a per-onderneming deduction. It is claimed by choice in the aangifte, on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41). For 2026 the table has five bands. Up to € 2.900 the deduction is nil. From € 2.901 up to € 71.683 the deduction is 28% of the investment amount. From € 71.683 up to € 132.746 the deduction is a fixed € 20.072. From € 132.746 up to € 398.236 the deduction is € 20.072 minus 7,56% of the part above € 132.746. Above € 398.236 the deduction is nil (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2). The Belastingdienst phrases the 2026 window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236 investeren" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026). The table is a step function, so the percentage in the second band applies to the whole investment and the amounts in the third and fourth bands are absolute amounts.

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3). The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

If an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 1). At staking of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 2). This payment rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA.

The KIA can be combined with the EIA or with the MIA on the same asset, but the EIA and the MIA cannot be combined with each other for one asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

### 3.2 KIA per-asset minimum of € 450

An asset with an investment amount below € 450 does not count for the KIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b). The same lid excludes for the KIA assets that are intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (onderdeel a).

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes for every investeringsaftrek assets intended mainly for use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (onderdeel a), gronden including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (b), woonhuizen and woonschepen (c), personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (d), vaartuigen of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), effecten, vorderingen, goodwill, vergunningen, ontheffingen and concessies (f), and dieren (g). Lid 3 makes an exception for the MIA, where lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn MIA. Lid 4 adds for the EIA and MIA the exclusions in section 3.5. Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. These amounts are the same in every version of the article from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45).

Art. 3.46 excludes obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam and that lichaam (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1, which is the ontheffing route (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46). The FISIN 2026 chapter states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 EIA percentage of 40%

The EIA is 40% of the investment amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must appear on the Energielijst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that have not been used before. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and so can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the entrepreneur himself, as voortbrengingskosten (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km (same URL).

Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. Costs that already received EIA cannot also receive MIA, while Vamil remains possible (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). From 2026 the investments in an eenmanszaak and in a samenwerkingsverband such as a vof are added together for the EIA maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). The investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months, see section 3.11.

### 3.4 EIA and MIA maximum investment amount of € 153.000.000

For 2026 the investment amount on which the EIA and the MIA can be granted is capped at € 153.000.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4). The FISIN 2026 chapter prints the amount as "€ 153 miljoen" and the Belastingdienst EIA page states the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). From 2026 the EIA investments in an eenmanszaak and in a samenwerkingsverband are added together for this maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

For MIA and Vamil RVO publishes a separate and much lower yearly maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). That page does not state a year, so the value is UNVERIFIED here and is repeated in section 7.

### 3.5 EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500

Art. 3.45 lid 4 adds for the EIA and the MIA that assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises do not qualify (onderdeel a) and that assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 do not qualify (onderdeel b). The € 2.500 floor also appears in the FISIN 2026 chapter and on the Belastingdienst EIA page (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). RVO phrases the same floor for MIA and Vamil as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### 3.6 MIA percentages

The MIA is 45% for categorie I, 36% for categorie II and 27% for categorie III (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must appear on the Milieulijst. The percentages changed from 36% / 27% / 13,5% in the 2021 text of art. 3.42a lid 3 to 45% / 36% / 27% with effect from 1 January 2022, and that has been the position since. The losing figures of 36% / 27% / 13,5% in the FISIN 2022 and 2023 chapters are settled to the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### 3.7 MIA and Vamil code mapping from the Milieulijst

From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps the code of a milieu-bedrijfsmiddel to a MIA categorie. Codes starting with F or G belong to categorie I at 45%. Codes starting with A or D belong to categorie II at 36%. Codes starting with B or E belong to categorie III at 27%. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023). The Milieulijst version that applies is the one valid on the date the koopovereenkomst is signed or the order is placed (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### 3.8 MIA and Vamil budget 2026

For 2026 the RVO budget is € 135.000.000 for the MIA and € 20.000.000 for the Vamil (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Both are enacted and published. The checked 2025 material states no budget figure, so no old value is given for these amounts in section 5.

### 3.9 MIA and Vamil maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The checked page states no year for this cap. It is a condition of the aanwijzing and is separate from the statutory € 153.000.000 maximum of art. 3.42 lid 4 for the EIA and MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4). Because the year is not stated, the value is unknown and UNVERIFIED in this file and is listed again in section 7.

### 3.10 Vamil percentage of 75%

Art. 3.31 lid 1 states that the costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven". The remaining part of the cost follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.31). Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that have not been used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts of the article (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.31).

Vamil can be used together with the MIA on the same asset, and it remains possible on costs that already received EIA. RVO states that the total tax benefit of MIA and Vamil combined can be "netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months, see section 3.11.

### 3.11 RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

For aanschafkosten the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst or placing the order. For voortbrengingskosten the report must be made within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Milieu-advieskosten are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst that is valid on the signing or order date applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The FISIN chapters for 2020 to 2026 all state "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same report covers the EIA, the MIA and the Vamil (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).

### 3.12 Ordinary depreciation caps

The yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten that is attributable to the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost may be written off for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so the minimum periods are 10 years for goodwill and 5 years for other assets (lid 2). Voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging (lid 3). Voorwerpen van geringe waarde that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (lid 4). The same text applies in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30).

### 3.13 Bodemwaarde of a building

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde, and at most up to the difference (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden form one bedrijfsmiddel, while detachable werktuigen are separate assets (lid 2). From 2024 the bodemwaarde is "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" (lid 3), except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ-waarde until 3 years after first use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand). In 2020 to 2023 the bodemwaarde was the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another, own use, building (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3). The WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 4). The 2026 text also has lid 5 for a co-owned building pro rata, lid 6 where the ground is owned by an unrelated party which reduces the WOZ-waarde, and lid 7 to 10 on related persons and verbonden lichaam (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a).

### 3.14 Starters willekeurige afschrijving, yearly cap of € 398.236

The statutory basis is art. 3.34, which allows willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.34). Under art. 7 lid 1 of the URWA the designated assets are assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3, the startersaftrek, applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9). Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 restricts the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst als ondernemer. Art. 8 allows willekeurige afschrijving "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground. For 2026 that maximum is the top of the KIA table, € 398.236 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026). This cap is a reading of the cross-reference in art. 8 of the URWA, no primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7.

Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 allows ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9 and 12). The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is open to an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek can be used, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Herwaardering to the normal book value takes place within 5 years, or 10 years for zeeschepen, if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf, and the difference is winst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

The temporary willekeurige afschrijving of URWA art. 13 to 15 does not apply to 2026 investments. It covered assets not used before for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten were made in calendar year 2023 and that were taken into use before 1 January 2026, at most 50% of the costs once, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Its exclusions include gebouwen, schepen, vliegtuigen, bromfietsen, motorrijwielen, personenauto's except for beroepsvervoer over de weg or 0 gram CO2 per km, immateriële activa, dieren, assets mainly made available to third parties, public roads and paths, and assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. The period in which the asset had to keep meeting the art. 13 conditions ended on 31 December 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 13 to 15, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).

### 3.15 Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold of € 2.900

If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than € 2.900 in total, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1). For 2026 the threshold is € 2.900. The FISIN 2026 chapter and the Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page state the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### 3.16 Desinvesteringsbijtelling term and undoing tests

The bijtelling applies only to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 2). Lid 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the onderneming (a), a change of destination so that the good meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a, mainly made available to third parties for the KIA, or lid 4 onderdeel a, made available to non-residents for the EIA and MIA (b), and a change that brings the good under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). In those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer counts as the transfer price (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding, counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7).

The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of part or all of the onderneming within the term as a case that triggers the bijtelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. KIA in the 28% band (Calculated example)

A sole trader invests € 60.000 in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2026. No asset is excluded and every asset costs more than € 450.

Step 1. The investment of € 60.000 is above € 2.900 and at or below € 71.683, so the second band applies at 28%.
Step 2. KIA = 28% × € 60.000 = € 16.800.
Step 3. Check against the next band. 28% × € 71.683 = € 20.071,24, which is just below the fixed € 20.072 of the third band, as the table states.

The KIA is € 16.800. Figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 2. KIA in the fixed band (Calculated example)

The same sole trader invests € 100.000 instead.

Step 1. € 100.000 is above € 71.683 and at or below € 132.746.
Step 2. The third band gives a fixed amount, so KIA = € 20.072.
Step 3. The 28% of the second band is not used here, because the table is a step function.

The KIA is € 20.072 and not € 28.000. Figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 3. KIA in the fourth band (Calculated example)

The same sole trader invests € 150.000.

Step 1. € 150.000 is above € 132.746 and at or below € 398.236.
Step 2. Part above € 132.746 = € 150.000 minus € 132.746 = € 17.254.
Step 3. 7,56% × € 17.254 = € 1.304,4024, rounded to € 1.304,40.
Step 4. KIA = € 20.072 minus € 1.304,40 = € 18.767,60.

The KIA is € 18.767,60. Figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 4. KIA above the top amount (Calculated example)

The same sole trader invests € 400.000.

Step 1. € 400.000 is above € 398.236.
Step 2. In the top band the deduction is nil, so KIA = € 0.

The KIA is nil, even though individual assets still cost more than € 450. Figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 5. KIA in a samenwerkingsverband (Calculated example)

A vof has two vennoten. The vof invests € 40.000 in 2026. One vennoot also invests € 10.000 in his own onderneming.

Step 1. Add the partnership investments and the own investments, € 40.000 + € 10.000 = € 50.000.
Step 2. Apply the table to the sum, € 50.000 is in the 28% band, so 28% × € 50.000 = € 14.000.
Step 3. Multiply by the own investment divided by the sum, € 10.000 / € 50.000 = 0,2.
Step 4. KIA for this vennoot = € 14.000 × 0,2 = € 2.800.

The KIA is € 2.800. Rule from https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3, figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 6. EIA combined with the KIA (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys one new machine for € 100.000 in 2026. The machine is on the Energielijst, it was not used before, and it is reported to RVO within 3 months.

Step 1. The machine is above the € 2.500 EIA floor and far below the € 153.000.000 maximum.
Step 2. EIA = 40% × € 100.000 = € 40.000.
Step 3. The same machine may also enter the KIA base. The total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen for 2026 is € 100.000, so the KIA is the fixed € 20.072 of the third band.
Step 4. The MIA cannot be claimed on this same machine, because the EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset.

The deductions are € 40.000 EIA plus € 20.072 KIA. Figures from section 2, EIA percentage, EIA and MIA maximum, EIA and MIA minimum and KIA table rows.

### Example 7. MIA and Vamil on one asset (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a new asset of € 40.000 in 2026. Its Milieulijst code starts with B, it is not used before, and the investment is reported to RVO within 3 months.

Step 1. Code B maps to categorie III at 27%.
Step 2. MIA = 27% × € 40.000 = € 10.800.
Step 3. The € 40.000 is above the € 2.500 floor and below the € 153.000.000 maximum.
Step 4. Code B also qualifies for 75% Vamil, so 75% × € 40.000 = € 30.000 may be depreciated at will, and the remaining € 10.000 follows the normal depreciation schedule.

The MIA is € 10.800 and € 30.000 of the cost can be depreciated at will under the Vamil. Figures from section 2, MIA percentages, MIA and Vamil code mapping, EIA and MIA minimum and Vamil percentage rows.

### Example 8. Ordinary depreciation caps (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a machine for € 40.000 and acquires goodwill for € 30.000 in 2026.

Step 1. Machine, at most 20% per year, so 20% × € 40.000 = € 8.000 per year, which means at least 5 years.
Step 2. Goodwill, at most 10% per year, so 10% × € 30.000 = € 3.000 per year, which means at least 10 years.

Both assets need a depreciation schedule that stays within these yearly caps. Figures from section 2, ordinary depreciation caps row.

### Example 9. Building and bodemwaarde (Calculated example)

A sole trader owns a building that is a bedrijfsmiddel. The book value at the start of 2026 is € 400.000 and the WOZ-waarde 2026 is € 300.000.

The building was taken into use before 2021 and has been depreciated for more than 3 years, so the transition exception does not apply.

Step 1. The bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde, € 300.000.
Step 2. The book value of € 400.000 is above the bodemwaarde, so depreciation is allowed.
Step 3. The maximum for 2026 is the difference, € 400.000 minus € 300.000 = € 100.000.
Step 4. After a depreciation of € 100.000 the book value equals the bodemwaarde and no further depreciation on the building is possible until the WOZ-waarde changes.

Figures from section 2, bodemwaarde row.

### Example 10. Desinvesteringsbijtelling (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a machine for € 50.000 in 2026 and claims the KIA. The investment falls in the 28% band, so the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is 28%. He sells the machine in 2028 for € 30.000.

Step 1. The sale is within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, 2026.
Step 2. The transfer price of € 30.000 is above the € 2.900 threshold.
Step 3. Bijtelling = 28% × € 30.000 = € 8.400.
Step 4. Check the cap. The investment amount over which aftrek was taken is € 50.000, and € 8.400 is below that, so the full € 8.400 stands.

The 2028 profit includes a desinvesteringsbijtelling of € 8.400. Figures from section 2, KIA table and desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold rows.

### Example 11. Starters willekeurige afschrijving (Calculated example)

A starter meets the startersaftrek conditions in 2026 and buys a machine for € 30.000 in 2026. The asset qualifies for investeringsaftrek.

Step 1. Check the yearly cap. The total costs stay at € 30.000, which is below € 398.236, so art. 8 of the URWA does not limit the willekeurige afschrijving on this machine (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9).
Step 2. The willekeurige afschrijving must fit within the period of art. 3.38, five years from the start of 2026.
Step 3. The minimum total investment of the KIA table, € 2.900, does not apply to the willekeurige afschrijving, so a small total does not block the scheme (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

Figures from section 2, starters willekeurige afschrijving cap and KIA table rows.

### Example 12. Asset not yet in use, payment cap (Calculated example)

A sole trader orders a machine for € 20.000 in 2026 and pays € 8.000 in 2026. The machine is not in use at the end of 2026.

Step 1. The investment is not yet in use at the end of the calendar year, so the KIA base is limited to the payments made, € 8.000.
Step 2. € 8.000 is above € 2.900 and at or below € 71.683, so the 28% band applies.
Step 3. KIA for 2026 = 28% × € 8.000 = € 2.240.
Step 4. The remaining € 12.000 of the cost follows in later years, when further payments are made or the asset is in use.

The rule is in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 1 and applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. At staking of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 2). Figures from section 2, KIA table row.

### Example 13. Truck examples ending in 2026 (Official example)

The Belastingdienst truck examples on the page about willekeurige afschrijving for starters end in 2026. The 2024 truck example ends in 2026 with a herwaardering from € 10.000 to € 120.000, a boekwinst of € 110.000 and € 20.000 normal depreciation in 2026. Check, € 120.000 minus € 10.000 = € 110.000. The 2021 truck example ends in 2026 with no herwaardering.

Source, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep a list of every bedrijfsmiddel with its aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten and the date of the obligation or the costs. The KIA is taken per onderneming on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41).
- Assets below € 450 do not count for the KIA, and assets below € 2.500 do not count for the EIA or the MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b).
- Record the koopovereenkomst or order date and the RVO notification, because the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months and the Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date is the one that applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).
- Where an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, take the deduction only over the payments made in that year and carry the excess to later years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 1).
- Set up the depreciation schedule within the caps, at most 10% per year for goodwill and at most 20% per year for other bedrijfsmiddelen. Voorwerpen van geringe waarde are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30).
- For a building, depreciate only while the book value is above the bodemwaarde, which is the WOZ-waarde of the building for the calendar year of the depreciation, except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ-waarde until 3 years after first use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 4).
- Record disposals, rentals and changes of use of any asset that received an investeringsaftrek. A disposal within five years above € 2.900 of transfer prices leads to a desinvesteringsbijtelling, and making the asset available to third parties counts as a disposal. Rental counts for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Buying an asset from a BV in which the taxpayer holds at least one third of the shares gives in principle no investeringsaftrek, which is a point to check before recording the purchase (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The KIA is claimed by choice in the aangifte (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41). For a BV, the same MIA percentages and the same EIA maximum are stated on the Belastingdienst Vpb information page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- In a samenwerkingsverband each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and a different reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).
