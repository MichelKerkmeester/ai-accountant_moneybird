# BV en vennootschapsbelasting 2026

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2026 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the vennootschapsbelasting (Vpb, corporate income tax) position of a BV in 2026 and the rules that touch the BV and its directeur-grootaandeelhouder (DGA, director and majority shareholder). It runs through the two Vpb brackets, loss set-off, the generic interest deduction limit, the innovatiebox, the fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity), the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) test, the Zvw contribution, box 2, dividendbelasting, the conditionele bronbelasting, the jaarrekening (annual accounts) rules and the switch from an eenmanszaak to a BV. The headline 2026 figures stay familiar, with 19% up to € 200.000 and 25,8% above it, a 24,5% interest limit with a € 1.000.000 threshold and a gebruikelijk loon normbedrag of € 58.000. The 2026 law is enacted, and the values below come from the Wet Vpb 1969 text valid on 1 January 2026 and from current Belastingdienst pages.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

### Vpb-tarief, eerste schijf

A BV is subject to Vpb on its belastbaar bedrag (taxable profit). Under art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag above the column I amount. For 2026 the first bracket runs t/m (up to and including) € 200.000 at 19%. The Belastingdienst tarieven page prints 19,0% and 25,8% and states the same rates for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 22), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Vpb-tarief, boven de eerste schijf

For the part of the belastbaar bedrag above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8% of that part. The € 38.000 is the fixed column III amount and equals 19% of the first € 200.000. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 22). If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969 spreads the tax as (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn), with X the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. No change of art. 22 is enacted for 2026, so this spread does not arise for a 2026 book year. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 31), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 31).

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b)

Under art. 15b lid 1 the saldo aan renten (balance of interest, meaning interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the gecorrigeerde winst and € 1.000.000. The percentage in the 2026 text is 24,5%. The Belastingdienst page for the 2026 changes states 24,5% and notes that non-deductible interest carries forward. The taxpayer tests both legs and uses the higher one, so a saldo at or below € 1.000.000 is not limited at all, and a saldo above 24,5% of the gecorrigeerde winst is limited only where that percentage leg is itself above € 1.000.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Verliesverrekening (art. 20)

From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and after that carried forward without a time limit. Per year the set-off is limited to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. The 2020 and 2021 texts were different, a loss was then set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by beschikking. For a 2026 book year only the one-year-back and unlimited-forward system with the € 1.000.000 plus 50% cap applies. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 20), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 (art. 20), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 (art. 20).

### Innovatiebox, breuk (art. 12b)

Under art. 12b to 12bd Wet Vpb 1969 qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H, where H is the highest art. 22 rate. With H at 25,8% this gives an effective rate of 9%. The Belastingdienst page states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring for the R&D. A taxpayer who is not smaller also needs an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12b), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovatiebox, forfait (art. 12bd)

A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years and chooses the forfait applies 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000. The forfait is only available when that profit is positive. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12bd).

### Innovatiebox, kleinere belastingplichtige (art. 12ba lid 2)

A smaller taxpayer is one whose advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and whose net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000. A smaller taxpayer needs only the S&O-verklaring and no access ticket. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12ba lid 2). Where the taxpayer does not use the forfait, art. 12bb limits the box to K/T of the advantage, with K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12bb, art. 12bc).

### Deelnemingsvrijstelling (art. 13)

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming out of the profit. A deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (art. 13 lid 2). The threshold is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure this matters twice, the profit on the shares stays untaxed at the level of the holder, and a deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from dividendbelasting withholding, see the dividendbelasting subsections below. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 13), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 13).

### Fiscale eenheid (art. 15)

The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Belastingdienst adds that the mother is a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie, or a comparable foreign form, that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both have the same book years and profit rules and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### Gebruikelijk loon, normbedrag (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, (c) the normbedrag. The dated 2026-01-01 text of the Wet LB 1964 prints a normbedrag of € 58.000, matching the Belastingdienst page. The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022, lid 7 from 2023). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Gebruikelijk loon, vergelijkbaar loon en escape

Leg (a) is 100% of the comparable wage from 2023, and was 75% in 2020 to 2022. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 reads, from 2023, that if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The inhoudingsplichtige must make that plausible. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling, that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. A vaststellingsovereenkomst made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. A starting company that falls outside the start-up relief may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a lid 1 en lid 2), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2), https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Gebruikelijk loon, ondergrens

The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. That floor sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. A DGA who takes only a small wage from all the bodies together therefore stays outside the minimum. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a lid 6).

### Bijdrage Zvw, niet-verzekerde DGA

A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs his wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. A DGA who alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. For him the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw. Instead the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw is levied up to the maximum bijdrage-inkomen and paid through the aangifte loonheffingen. The 2026 bijdrage is 4,85%. The Belastingdienst table says the uninsured DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 describes the withholding on net wage by the BV (paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1). The Handboek for 2026 was not read, see section 7. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Maximum bijdrage-inkomen Zvw

The maximum bijdrage-inkomen for 2026 is € 79.409. The bijdrage and the werkgeversheffing are levied up to this maximum, so a wage above it does not increase the contribution. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. Each FISIN page says DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if he is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen.

### Werkgeversheffing Zvw, verzekerde DGA

A DGA who is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. For 2026 the percentage is 6,10%, up to the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 79.409. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

### Box 2, tarief

For the DGA as a shareholder, dividends and gains on the shares are taxed in box 2 of the inkomstenbelasting, the income from an aanmerkelijk belang. For 2026 the first bracket runs to € 68.843 at 24,5%, and above it the tax is € 16.866 plus 31% of the part above € 68.843 (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 2.12 en art. 10.1 lid 1).

### Dividendbelasting

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 sets the tax at 15% of the opbrengst in every version read, including the version geldend from 1 January 2025 that is still current, because the 2026-01-01 URL returns 404. The shareholder is the taxpayer and the company withholds (art. 1). If the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85 (art. 6 lid 1). Once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota is due (art. 9 lid 1). The Belastingdienst states that a deelnemingsdividend, a holding of 5% or more, to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the company is invited to file. A beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 1, art. 5, art. 6 lid 1, art. 9 lid 1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Dividendbelasting bij inkoop van eigen aandelen

Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Dividend stripping (uiteindelijk gerechtigde)

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7).

### Conditionele bronbelasting

Under art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 the tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969, which is 25,8% in 2026. The Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividendbelasting). The levy hits payments to a related body, a qualifying interest that is always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction where the profit tax is below 9% or that is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, and abuse cases. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 (art. 4.1), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 (art. 3.4a en art. 5.2), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Aangifte Vpb, termijnen

A book year equal to the calendar year must be filed before 1 June of the next year, so the return for book year 2025 is due before 1 June 2026. A broken book year must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending 31 December is filed before 1 June, and one ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Uitstel aangifte Vpb

An online extension is only available for a calendar book year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for a request of more than 5 months, and it must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting. A voorlopige aanslag Vpb 2026 for a foreign taxpayer can only be requested digitally. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Jaarrekening, grootteklassen

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria, balance sheet total, net turnover and number of employees. The 2026 thresholds are micro at most € 450.000 assets and € 900.000 net turnover with fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen with fewer than 50 employees, and medium € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen with fewer than 250 employees. Stb. 2024, 52 raised the amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, and the employee counts are unchanged from the older statute. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01 (art. 395a, 396 en 397 Boek 2 BW), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.

### Jaarrekening, deponering en SBR

The annual accounts are deposited within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends (art. 394 Boek 2 BW). If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein states there is no extension, that late filing is a criminal offence, and that from boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 394 Boek 2 BW).

### Administratie- en bewaartermijnen

Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig and requires them to keep their records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the profit and loss statement. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on onroerende zaken for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 (art. 52 AWR), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01 (art. 34a, 28rl en 28sj Wet OB 1968).

### Stakingsaftrek bij ruisende inbreng

Without the geruisloze omzetting facility a change of legal form is a cessation (ruisende inbreng, a taxed contribution). The owner draws up a final balance and pays inkomstenbelasting on the stakingswinst, the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The stakingsaftrek (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001) is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente (art. 3.129 Wet IB 2001). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.79 en art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/.

### Geruisloze omzetting (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not ceased (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001). The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for inkomstenbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. The Besluit geruisloze omzetting 2025 of 24 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 37092, published on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication, replaces the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M. Its standard conditions include that selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. Into an existing company the contribution is allowed only when it already runs a business in the same line, and into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. The besluit also changes or clarifies onderdeel 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right qualifies when it amounts to medegerechtigdheid), 3.4 (approval for a holding, widened to an indirect holding and to a new or activity-free daughter), 4.2.2 (letteraandelen besides cumulatief preferente aandelen, plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw bijdrage credit), 12 (a new eighth standard condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (an incidental tax advantage defined more sharply) and 13.2 (S&O approval in the voor-voorperiode). Retroactivity, if the voorovereenkomst is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Belastingdienst says to request at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, to register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, and that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties, and the inspector decides by beschikking open to bezwaar that contains the conditions (onderdeel 14.2 of the old Besluit, standpunt of 27-06-2022, published 12-01-2023). Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

### Btw bij een BV en btw-fiscale eenheid

The new legal form is a new business for the btw and may receive a new btw-identificatienummer, which must go to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change of legal form. A btw fiscale eenheid (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968) arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published on 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025", so it is in force in 2026. A pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 13 lid 1).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

No official examples were found in the checked sources for 2026, so every example below is a `Calculated example` built from the section 2 figures.

### Example 1, Vpb at two profit levels

`Calculated example`. A BV has a belastbaar bedrag of € 300.000,00 for 2026.

Step 1, first bracket: € 200.000,00 x 19% = € 38.000,00.
Step 2, the part above the first bracket: € 300.000,00 - € 200.000,00 = € 100.000,00.
Step 3, tax on that part: € 100.000,00 x 25,8% = € 25.800,00.
Step 4, total: € 38.000,00 + € 25.800,00 = € 63.800,00.
Step 5, check against the art. 22 formula: € 38.000,00 + 25,8% x € 100.000,00 = € 38.000,00 + € 25.800,00 = € 63.800,00. The effective rate is € 63.800,00 / € 300.000,00 = 21,27% (rounded).

A second BV at exactly € 200.000,00 pays € 200.000,00 x 19% = € 38.000,00. A third BV at € 1.000.000,00 pays € 38.000,00 + 25,8% x € 800.000,00 = € 38.000,00 + € 206.400,00 = € 244.400,00.

### Example 2, generieke renteaftrekbeperking

`Calculated example`. A BV has a gecorrigeerde winst of € 4.000.000,00 and a saldo aan renten of € 2.000.000,00. The saldo aan renten is the interest costs on loans minus the interest income.

Step 1, percentage leg: € 4.000.000,00 x 24,5% = € 980.000,00.
Step 2, amount leg: € 1.000.000,00.
Step 3, the threshold is the higher of the two: € 1.000.000,00.
Step 4, the saldo aan renten is deductible up to € 1.000.000,00, so € 2.000.000,00 - € 1.000.000,00 = € 1.000.000,00 is not deductible and carries forward.

Second case, gecorrigeerde winst € 10.000.000,00 and saldo aan renten € 3.000.000,00.

Step 1, percentage leg: € 10.000.000,00 x 24,5% = € 2.450.000,00.
Step 2, amount leg: € 1.000.000,00.
Step 3, the threshold is the higher of the two: € 2.450.000,00.
Step 4, not deductible: € 3.000.000,00 - € 2.450.000,00 = € 550.000,00, which carries forward.

### Example 3, verliesverrekening

`Calculated example`. A BV has a profit of € 3.000.000,00 in 2026 and a carry-forward loss from 2023.

Step 1, set-off ceiling: € 1.000.000,00 + 50% x (€ 3.000.000,00 - € 1.000.000,00) = € 1.000.000,00 + 50% x € 2.000.000,00 = € 1.000.000,00 + € 1.000.000,00 = € 2.000.000,00.
Step 2, the loss is set off up to € 2.000.000,00. If the loss is € 2.500.000,00, € 2.000.000,00 is used in 2026 and € 2.500.000,00 - € 2.000.000,00 = € 500.000,00 carries forward.
Step 3, belastbaar bedrag for 2026: € 3.000.000,00 - € 2.000.000,00 = € 1.000.000,00.
Step 4, Vpb on € 1.000.000,00: first bracket € 200.000,00 x 19% = € 38.000,00, then € 800.000,00 x 25,8% = € 206.400,00, total € 38.000,00 + € 206.400,00 = € 244.400,00.

If the loss were € 500.000,00, all of it would be set off because € 500.000,00 is below the € 2.000.000,00 ceiling. A loss of 2026 itself may also be carried back one year, to 2025.

### Example 4, innovatiebox effective rate

`Calculated example`. A smaller taxpayer made a qualifying asset itself and holds an S&O-verklaring. The qualifying advantage is € 500.000,00 and falls in the top bracket, so without the innovatiebox it would be taxed at 25,8%.

Step 1, without the box: € 500.000,00 x 25,8% = € 129.000,00.
Step 2, with the 9/H fraction the effective rate is 9%: € 500.000,00 x 9% = € 45.000,00.
Step 3, the saving against the rate without the box is € 129.000,00 - € 45.000,00 = € 84.000,00.

### Example 5, innovatiebox forfait

`Calculated example`. A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years chooses the art. 12bd forfait.

Case A, profit before art. 12b of € 80.000,00: € 80.000,00 x 25% = € 20.000,00, which is below the € 25.000,00 maximum, so the forfait is € 20.000,00.
Case B, profit before art. 12b of € 200.000,00: € 200.000,00 x 25% = € 50.000,00, capped at € 25.000,00.
In both cases the profit before art. 12b must be positive for the forfait to apply.

### Example 6, gebruikelijk loon and the escape

`Calculated example`. A DGA works for his BV. The comparable wage is € 55.000,00, the highest wage of the other employees in the body and connected bodies is € 50.000,00, and the 2026 normbedrag is € 58.000,00.

Step 1, the three amounts are € 55.000,00, € 50.000,00 and € 58.000,00.
Step 2, the highest of them is € 58.000,00, so the wage must be at least € 58.000,00.
Step 3, the escape of art. 12a lid 2: the highest amount of € 58.000,00 exceeds the comparable wage of € 55.000,00, so the wage is the comparable wage, € 55.000,00.

Floor case, if the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is € 4.500,00, the rule does not apply because € 4.500,00 is not more than € 5.000,00.

### Example 7, Zvw for the two DGA routes

`Calculated example`. A DGA has a wage of € 100.000,00, above the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 79.409,00.

Case A, DGA not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen.
Step 1, the contribution is € 79.409,00 x 4,85% = € 3.851,3365, rounded to € 3.851,34.
Step 2, the wage above € 79.409,00 does not increase the contribution.
Step 3, the checked 2026 material states that this DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag, and the BV pays no werkgeversheffing and no premies werknemersverzekeringen for him.

Case B, DGA insured for the werknemersverzekeringen.
Step 1, the BV pays the werkgeversheffing of € 79.409,00 x 6,10% = € 4.843,949, rounded to € 4.843,95.

The 2026 Handboek Loonheffingen was not read, so the 2026 collection route rests on the 2025 Handboek and the 2026 table.

### Example 8, box 2 on a dividend

`Calculated example`. A DGA receives a dividend of € 100.000,00, above the first bracket of € 68.843,00.

Step 1, the part above the bracket: € 100.000,00 - € 68.843,00 = € 31.157,00.
Step 2, tax on that part: € 31.157,00 x 31% = € 9.658,67.
Step 3, per the art. 2.12 table, the tax is the column III amount of € 16.866,00 plus € 9.658,67 = € 26.524,67.
Step 4, note on the table. € 68.843,00 x 24,5% = € 16.866,535, and € 16.866,535 rounded down to whole euros is € 16.866,00, the amount printed in column III. A separate calculation with the exact 24,5% amount throughout would give € 16.866,535 + € 9.658,67 = € 26.525,205, rounded to € 26.525,21. The example follows the printed amount, so the tax is € 26.524,67.

### Example 9, dividendbelasting

`Calculated example`. A BV pays a dividend of € 100.000,00 to its shareholder, a beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder.

Step 1, withholding: € 100.000,00 x 15% = € 15.000,00.
Step 2, the return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available.
Step 3, the shareholder credits the € 15.000,00, a body in the aangifte Vpb and an individual in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting.

If instead the BV bears the tax itself, art. 6 lid 1 grosses up the opbrengst: € 100.000,00 x 100/85 = € 117.647,0588, rounded to € 117.647,06. The 15% tax on the grossed-up amount is € 117.647,0588 x 15% = € 17.647,0588, rounded to € 17.647,06.

If the shareholder is a Dutch body with a holding of 5% or more, the deelnemingsdividend is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the company is invited to file.

### Example 10, inkoop van eigen aandelen

`Calculated example`. A BV buys back its own shares. The buyback price is € 50.000,00 and the average paid-up capital on those shares is € 20.000,00.

Step 1, taxable base: € 50.000,00 - € 20.000,00 = € 30.000,00.
Step 2, dividendbelasting: € 30.000,00 x 15% = € 4.500,00.
Step 3, if the company bears the tax itself and the art. 4c facility for listed companies does not apply, the 100/85 gross-up gives € 30.000,00 x 100/85 = € 35.294,1176, rounded to € 35.294,12. The 15% tax on the grossed-up base is € 35.294,1176 x 15% = € 5.294,1176, rounded to € 5.294,12.

### Example 11, conditionele bronbelasting

`Calculated example`. A BV pays € 200.000,00 of royalties to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction.

Step 1, the rate is the highest art. 22 rate, so 25,8% for 2026.
Step 2, the tax is € 200.000,00 x 25,8% = € 51.600,00.

The same rate applies to in-scope interest, and to dividends since the 2024 version of the Wet bronbelasting 2021.

### Example 12, fiscale eenheid

`Calculated example`. A mother holds at least 95% of the shares in her daughter, and the two form a fiscale eenheid for the whole of 2026. The mother has a profit of € 400.000,00 and the daughter has a loss of € 150.000,00.

Step 1, combined belastbaar bedrag: € 400.000,00 - € 150.000,00 = € 250.000,00.
Step 2, first bracket: € 200.000,00 x 19% = € 38.000,00.
Step 3, the part above the bracket: € 250.000,00 - € 200.000,00 = € 50.000,00, tax € 50.000,00 x 25,8% = € 12.900,00.
Step 4, total Vpb for the eenheid: € 38.000,00 + € 12.900,00 = € 50.900,00.
Step 5, without the fiscale eenheid the mother alone would pay € 38.000,00 + 25,8% x € 200.000,00 = € 38.000,00 + € 51.600,00 = € 89.600,00. The difference is € 89.600,00 - € 50.900,00 = € 38.700,00.

### Example 13, jaarrekening size class

`Calculated example`. A BV has a balance sheet total of € 500.000,00, a net turnover of € 800.000,00 and 8 employees at the balance date, with the same picture one year earlier.

Step 1, assets of € 500.000,00 against the micro bound of € 450.000,00, not met.
Step 2, turnover of € 800.000,00 against the micro bound of € 900.000,00, met.
Step 3, 8 employees against the micro bound of fewer than 10 employees, met.
Step 4, two of the three criteria are met on two consecutive balance dates, so the BV is a micro company.

The accounts must be deposited within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends, and from boekjaar 2025 all legal persons file through SBR.

### Example 14, stakingsaftrek on a ruisende inbreng

`Calculated example`. A sole trader brings the business into a BV without the art. 3.65 facility, so the change is a cessation. The stakingswinst is € 40.000,00.

Step 1, the stakingsaftrek of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630,00 and is used once in a lifetime.
Step 2, remaining stakingswinst: € 40.000,00 - € 3.630,00 = € 36.370,00.
Step 3, the inkomstenbelasting is computed on the remaining € 36.370,00, and an extra lijfrente premium deduction is available (art. 3.129).

Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not qualify for the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente.

- Dividend stripping article: corrected to art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, readable in the dated texts, with the 2024 text adding "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7).
- Conditionele bronbelasting: 25,8% in both years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.
- Besluit geruisloze omzetting: the citation is Stcrt. 2025, 37092, published 4 November 2025, not Stcrt. 2025, 3709. The besluit of 24 October 2025 replaced the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, and is in force the day after publication, so 2026 requests follow it. It adds a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000, and the Zvw bijdrage credit. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
- Btw-eenheid beleidsbesluit: the Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, has been in force since 1 July 2025 and applies in 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- Jaarrekening thresholds: unchanged from 2024 and 2025 at € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen. The KVK figure of more than € 51 mln for the large class conflicts with Stb. 2024, 52 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 and is settled at € 50 miljoen. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 396 en 397), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01 (art. 395a, 396 en 397), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.
- SBR deposit: from boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR, so the 2026 deposit season is the first full SBR season. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.
- Stakingsaftrek: € 3.630 in both years. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.79), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.79).
- Aangifteseizoen: the Vpb return for book year 2025 is due before 1 June 2026, with an online extension to 1 November, requested from 1 March to 1 June. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Aangifte Vpb: record the return for book year 2025 before 1 June 2026, or request the online extension between 1 March and 1 June, which runs to 1 November. A broken book year is filed within 5 months after its end, with a paper extension request if needed. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Loonadministratie: run the DGA wage through the loonadministratie and withhold loonheffing. Record the gebruikelijk loon test against € 58.000 and the comparable wage, and use the € 5.000 floor only when all work in the body and connected bodies stays at or below it. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a), https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Zvw: for a non-insured DGA record the 4,85% bijdrage on net wage up to € 79.409 and pay it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For an insured DGA record the 6,10% werkgeversheffing paid by the BV up to € 79.409. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- Dividend: record the dividendnota when the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, withhold 15% dividendbelasting, file and pay within 1 month after the dividend is made available, and credit the withheld tax in the aangifte Vpb. A fully exempt deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder usually needs no return unless the company is invited to file. A kwalificatiebeschikking triggers a return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.
- Jaarrekening: draw up the accounts within the drawing-up term, deposit them within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends, and file through SBR from boekjaar 2025 onward. Late filing is a criminal offence and there is no extension. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 394 Boek 2 BW).
- Bewaarplicht: keep the records for seven years (art. 52 AWR), for nine years for onroerende zaken (art. 34a Wet OB 1968) and for ten years for OSS records (art. 28rl en 28sj Wet OB 1968). Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 (art. 52 AWR), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01 (art. 34a, 28rl en 28sj Wet OB 1968).
- Fiscale eenheid: the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below the 95% bezitseis ends the eenheid at that moment. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.
- Conditionele bronbelasting: record 25,8% on in-scope interest and royalties to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction, and on in-scope dividends since the 2024 version of the Wet bronbelasting 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 (art. 4.1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- The Handboek Loonheffingen 2026 was not read, so the 2026 Zvw withholding route for a non-insured DGA rests on the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 (paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1) and on the 2026 Belastingdienst table. A Handboek Loonheffingen 2026 would settle it. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- The 2026-01-01 URL of the Wet DB 1965 returns 404. The 15% rate is read from the version geldend 01-01-2025 t/m heden. A consolidated 2026-01-01 text would settle it. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01.
- The Wet Vpb 1969 text valid on 2026-01-01 reads "Geldend van 01-01-2026 t/m heden" and announces "Toekomstige wijziging(en) voorzien met ingang van: 01-01-2029". Art. 22 carries a note that changes without an entry date exist. The 2027-01-01 URL of the Wet Vpb 1969 returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11. Belastingplan 2027 (bill 37 022) is proposed and not enacted, it changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j in Article XIX, and it deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 in Article XX. It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b. Publication of the entry dates in a later consolidated text would settle the picture. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html.
- The koepelvrijstelling and the BUA are unknown in the checked sources, which mark them UNVERIFIED and not opened, with the beleidsbesluit as the only URL. Reading the besluit would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- The checked sources found no basis for bewaartermijnen longer than seven years besides the btw terms, nine years for onroerende zaken under art. 34a Wet OB 1968 and ten years for OSS under art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.
