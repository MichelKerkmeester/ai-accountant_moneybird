# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2026

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2026 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 3 for 2026 taxes a fictitious return on savings and investments under the Overbruggingswet box 3. The year uses the category method with a provisional bank percentage of 1,28%, an other-assets percentage of 6,00% and a provisional debt percentage of 2,70%. The heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free allowance) is € 59.357 per person and the schuldendrempel (debt threshold) is € 3.800 per person. New from 1 January 2026 is art. 5.27a on own use of real estate, while the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 is still a bill and not law on 11 October 2026.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

From 1 January 2023 the Overbruggingswet box 3 applies the category method to everyone. The 2026 and later texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 is not law on 11 October 2026. Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, probably 1 January 2028. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box

The 2026 calculation runs in six steps. Step 1 belastbaar rendement = banktegoeden x bank percentage plus overige bezittingen x other-assets percentage minus (schulden minus drempel) x debt percentage. A negative rendement is set at nil. Art. 4.14 lid 1 keeps the forfaitaire voordeel at least nil. Step 2 rendementsgrondslag = bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel). Step 3 grondslag sparen en beleggen = rendementsgrondslag minus heffingsvrij vermogen. Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 aandeel = grondslag / rendementsgrondslag x 100. Step 5 voordeel = belastbaar rendement x aandeel. Step 6 tax = rate x voordeel. The 2026 rate is 36%. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year. The 2023 and 2025 pages say 2 decimals. The 2024 and 2026 pages say 3 decimals. The 2026 examples show 2 decimals. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025

Background for 2026 is the Hoge Raad ruling of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The memorie van toelichting to the Wet rechtsherstel describes the ruling as a breach of art. 1 EP in connection with art. 14 EVRM. The forfaitaire system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Forfaits are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533. The Overbruggingswet box 3 is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, in force 1 January 2023. Hoge Raad 6 June 2024 held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. Hoge Raad 25 June 2026 held that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 have no right to recovery. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Heffingsvrij vermogen

The heffingsvrij vermogen for 2026 is € 59.357 per person. It doubles to € 118.714 with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17. Partners may divide the grondslag in any way that totals 100%. The amount reduces the rendementsgrondslag in step 3 of the calculation. In the actual return option the Hoge Raad has determined that no account may be taken of the heffingsvrij vermogen. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Schuldendrempel

Only the part of total debts above the drempel is deductible. The amount for 2026 is € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner. Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return. Statute art. 5.3 lid 3 says the deductible part is the amount by which total debts exceed the drempel. The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns. In steps 1 and 2 the calculation uses schulden minus drempel. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Box 3 tarief

The box 3 rate for 2026 is 36%. It is applied in step 6 to the voordeel. The same rate applies to the fictitious voordeel and to the actual return when the actual return is lower and is reported. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

### Bank percentage

The bank percentage for 2026 is 1,28% provisional. It applies to banktegoeden on the peildatum 1 January 2026. The 2026 calculation page says the percentages for banktegoeden and schulden are still provisional and will be fixed at the start of 2027. The statute text of 1 January 2026 still printed 1,44% as a carry-over. Art. 10.6ter lid 2 fixes the final bank percentage after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January. It is the average of the DNB monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. The sum is divided by twelve. The final figure can therefore differ from 1,28%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Other-assets percentage

The other-assets percentage for 2026 is 6,00% fixed. It applies to overige bezittingen on the peildatum. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 replaces the figure at the start of the year by ministerial regulation. The figure is set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. The 2026 statute prints 6%. Taxable green investments above the exemption count as overige bezittingen at 6,00%. A second home is an overige bezitting. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage for 2026 is 2,70% provisional. It applies only to the part of debts above the schuldendrempel. Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method as the bank percentage on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The final figure is due by ministerial regulation early 2027. The 2026 statute text printed 2,61% as a carry-over. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

The exemption for groene beleggingen (green investments) is € 26.715 per person and € 53.430 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 says green investments are not counted as bezittingen up to that amount. Art. 5.13 lid 2, from the 2023 text, applies the exemption first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The excess counts as other assets at 6,00%. Belastingplan 2026, Stb. 2025, 444, art. XLI lid 3 kept art. 5.5 out of the special 2026 indexation factor 1,027782. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Korting groene beleggingen

The korting voor groene beleggingen (green investment discount) is 0,1% for 2026. Art. 8.19 sets it at 0,1% of the exempt amount on the peildatum. Belastingdienst computes it automatically. With whole-year partners it is split in the same ratio as the green balance. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen

### Own use real estate, fixed method

Art. 5.27a applies from 1 January 2026. It sets the benefit from own use of real estate at the economic rent value per year. Days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable are excluded. Belastingdienst offers a fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value times usable days divided by 365. This 5,06% method is a Belastingdienst calculation method on its werkelijk-rendement page. It is not in the text of Stb. 2025, 195. Days of own use must be tracked. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### WOZ peildatum for the fixed method

The WOZ value used in the 2026 fixed method has peildatum 1 January 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Own use real estate start date

Art. II of Stb. 2025, 195 makes art. 5.27a apply from 1 January 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Voorlopige aanslag 2026

Belastingdienst cannot take the actual return into account in the voorlopige aanslag 2026. The actual return is adjusted in the aangifte 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

### Aandeel rounding

Step 4 of the 2026 calculation page says "Rond af op 3 decimalen achter de komma". The official examples on the same page show 2 decimals and cut off rather than round. Examples are 90.643 / 150.000 = 60,4287% shown as 60,42%, 15.643 / 150.000 shown as 10,42%, and 269.443 / 328.800 = 81,947% shown as 81,94%. The conflict is on the same page. Rounding changes the voordeel and the tax. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

### Actual-return reporting 2026

Reporting the actual return is optional. Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount and the taxpayer never pays more than the fictitious calculation. From 2025 the report is made in the income tax return. For 2026 that is the aangifte 2026. No deadline is stated on the reporting pages. For earlier years the route differed. 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD outside Mijn Belastingdienst. 2020 to 2024 used an online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, with a paper form available. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. 2017 to 2020 all letters have been sent, and without a letter the taxpayer may not report. ### Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 dates

The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. It entered into force by the besluit of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, in force the day after issue, so 19 July 2025. The Eerste Kamer adopted it on 8 July 2025. Art. V says arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV works back to 1 January 2017. Art. II, on own use of real estate in art. 5.27a, applies from 1 January 2026. Art. 5.25 lid 1 says that if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. Lid 2 sets the actual return at least nil. Lid 4 and 5 say whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3

### Actual-return documents deadline

Art. 5.25 lid 6 uses a form provided by the inspecteur, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7 allows further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8 says lid 6 and lid 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Peildatumarbitrage window

Art. 5.24 disregards transactions around 1 January within a three-month continuous period that begins before and ends after the peildatum, unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons lay behind them. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example: single with € 150.000 savings

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

- Banktegoeden € 150.000 x 1,28% = € 1.920.
- Rendementsgrondslag = € 150.000.
- Grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 150.000 - € 59.357 = € 90.643.
- Aandeel = € 90.643 / € 150.000 x 100 = 60,42% on the page. The exact figure is 60,4287%.
- Voordeel = € 1.920 x 60,42% = € 1.160.
- Tax = € 1.160 x 36% = € 417.

This is an `Official example`. The page prints these amounts.

### Official example: same savings with a partner

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

- Grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 150.000 - € 118.714 = € 31.286.
- The partners split it as € 15.643 each.
- Aandeel = € 15.643 / € 150.000 x 100 = 10,42% on the page.
- Voordeel = € 1.920 x 10,42% = € 200 each.
- Tax = € 200 x 36% = € 72 each.

This is an `Official example`. The page prints these amounts.

### Official example: single with savings, investments, a second home and debt

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

- Bank return = € 150.000 x 1,28% = € 1.920.
- Other assets = € 75.000 investments plus € 200.000 second home = € 275.000 x 6,00% = € 16.500.
- Aftrekbare schuld = € 100.000 - € 3.800 = € 96.200.
- Debt return = € 96.200 x 2,70% = € 2.597,40, printed as € 2.598.

- Rendementsgrondslag = € 425.000 - € 96.200 = € 328.800.
- Grondslag = € 328.800 - € 59.357 = € 269.443.
- Aandeel = € 269.443 / € 328.800 x 100 = 81,94% on the page.
- Voordeel = € 15.822 x 81,94% = € 12.964.
- Tax = € 12.964 x 36% = € 4.667.

This is an `Official example`. The page prints these amounts.

### Official example: partners with savings, green investments and a second home abroad

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

- Bank return = € 5.000 x 1,28% = € 64.
- Green exemption = € 53.430. Taxable green = € 150.000 - € 53.430 = € 96.570.
- Other assets = € 96.570 plus € 250.000 second home = € 346.570 x 6,00% = € 20.794.
- Belastbaar rendement = € 64 + € 20.794 = € 20.858.
- Rendementsgrondslag = € 5.000 + € 150.000 + € 250.000 - € 53.430 = € 351.570.

### Calculated example: actual return lower than the fictitious return

Inputs are invented. Single person. Banktegoeden € 150.000 and overige bezittingen € 50.000 on 1 January 2026. No debts. During 2026 the bank pays € 500 interest and the investments fall by € 3.000.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- Aandeel rounding conflict. The 2026 calculation page instruction says round to 3 decimals. The official examples on the same page show 2 decimals and cut off rather than round. The page gives 90.643 / 150.000 = 60,4287% shown as 60,42%, 15.643 / 150.000 shown as 10,42%, and 269.443 / 328.800 = 81,947% shown as 81,94%. Both are on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026. The 2026 aangifte software or a corrected page would settle it.
- Final 2026 bank and debt percentages are unknown. The 2026 calculation page carries 1,28% and 2,70% provisionally. The 2026 statute text still printed 1,44% and 2,61% as carry-over under art. 10.6ter. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01. A ministerial regulation early 2027 under art. 10.6ter lid 2 and lid 4 would settle it.
- The 5,06% own-use method is a Belastingdienst calculation method, not a statutory figure. The statute text of Stb. 2025, 195 sets the economic rent value per year. The 5,06% method is on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement. A regulation or a corrected source attribution would settle it.
- Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, bill 36 748, is proposed and not law on 11 October 2026. There was no Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 11 October 2026. Entry into force is by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028. Source: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 contains a novelle to bill 36 748. It proposes a vermogenswinstbelasting for all financial instruments from 2028, about 90% of assets with value development, with a full switch for the other assets in 2030. It proposes a box 3 heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027, a heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028 and an increase of the forfait for overige bezittingen, including rent and own-use benefits, by 1,5 percentage point in 2027. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The Kamerstuk number is UNVERIFIED. The number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page. Sources: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255 https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- Green facilities 2027 conflict. The Rijksoverheid page says both facilities lapse completely on 1 January 2027. The enacted 2027 statute text and Stb. 2025, 444 say the 2027 amount is € 200 per person and € 400 for partners, with a 0,1% korting, and move the repeal of afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 to 1 January 2028. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html. The enacted text or a later law would settle it.
- Wet rechtsherstel box 3 entry date conflict. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". The statute art. 7 says it entered into force the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html. The statute text settles it.
- Kerstarrest 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963, the claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return stays UNVERIFIED because hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 11 October 2026. A reachable Hoge Raad text would settle it.
- Hoge Raad 25 June 2026. The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". The "or objected late" part is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
