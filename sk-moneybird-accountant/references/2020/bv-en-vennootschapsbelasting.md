# BV en vennootschapsbelasting 2020

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2020 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the BV and the vennootschapsbelasting (corporate income tax) for tax year 2020. It sets out the 2020 Vpb rates, loss relief, the interest deduction limit, the gebruikelijk loonregeling (customary salary rule) for a DGA (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, director and major shareholder), dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax), box 2, the fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity), the deelnemingsvrijstelling (participation exemption), the innovatiebox (innovation box), the switch from an eenmanszaak (sole proprietorship) to a BV, and the jaarrekening (annual accounts) rules. The year's law is enacted. The checked 2020 sources state the values. One point about the 2020 text of the geruisloze omzetting (tax-neutral conversion) conditions is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

### 3.1 Vpb-tarief en berekening 2020

The Vpb applies to a BV. The tax is calculated on the belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount). For 2020 the first € 200.000 is taxed at 16,5%. Above € 200.000 the tax is € 33.000 plus 25% of the part above € 200.000. This is the table in art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. The € 33.000 is the tax on the first € 200.000 at 16,5%. A BV files an aangifte vennootschapsbelasting. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### 3.2 Art. 31 pro-rata bij een boekjaar over de jaargrens

If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, the tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change. Y is the days from the change. B is all days of the year. To is the tax under the old art. 22. Tn is the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. The pro-rata formula applies to a 2020 book year that straddles 1 January 2021 because art. 22 changed. For such a book year, To is the 2020 table and Tn is the 2021 table: t/m € 245.000 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### 3.3 Verliesverrekening 2020

For 2020 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years. The inspector fixes the loss by beschikking (decision). Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. This is the art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969 rule for 2020 and 2021. From 2022 the rule changes to one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. A BV claims the set-off in the aangifte vennootschapsbelasting. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 20 lid 2) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### 3.4 Generieke renteaftrekbeperking art. 15b

The saldo aan renten (interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 30% of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000. For 2020 the percentage is 30%. The threshold is € 1.000.000. Non-deductible interest carries forward. The rule interacts with the Vpb calculation because the non-deductible part is added back to the taxable amount. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1). Later years use other percentages, but for 2020 the text gives 30%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025.

### 3.5 Aangifte Vpb en uitstel

A book year equal to the calendar year ends on 31 December. The return must be filed before 1 June of the next year. A broken book year must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending on 31 December must be filed before 1 June. A short book year ending in another month must be filed before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar book year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months. That request must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### 3.6 Gebruikelijk loonregeling

The rule applies to an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest). The wage is at least the highest of three amounts. First, the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment). In 2020, 2021 and 2022 the test uses 75% of that comparable wage. Second, the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies. Third, the normbedrag (standard amount). For 2020 the normbedrag is € 46.000. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This is lid 6 in the 2020 text. The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. That is lid 9 in 2020 to 2022. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling. A connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party. The rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### 3.7 Gebruikelijk loon escape

For 2020 to 2022, if the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage. That amount must be at least the normbedrag. If the comparable wage itself is lower than the normbedrag, the wage is the comparable wage. From 2023 the escape changes. If the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 notes that a vaststellingsovereenkomst (agreement with the tax office) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### 3.8 Start-up relief en lagere lonen

For 2020 to 2022, art. 12a lid 3 and 4 Wet LB 1964 gives a start-up relief. It applies to a body with an S&O-verklaring (R&D certificate) for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies. The wage is then at least 108% of twelve times the WML amount of art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML. A lower comparable wage is allowed if made plausible. The relief must stay within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. The Belastingdienst says the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA. An S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy says a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage. That lower wage starts from the WML for the hours worked. The 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. The 2020 WML amount itself is not in the checked sources, so the 108% minimum cannot be worked out here. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### 3.9 Zvw voor de DGA

A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw. The BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie (payroll records) and withholds loonheffing. A DGA who can decide on his own dismissal, alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. The same holds for a DGA who holds an equal share with all fellow directors. For an uninsured DGA the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw. Instead the BV withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. A DGA who is insured falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. For 2020 the bijdrage is 5,45%, the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 57.232, and the werkgeversheffing is 6,70%. The FISIN page for 2020 says DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if he is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. The checked Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 corrects the route: the BV withholds the bijdrage on net wage. The Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.

### 3.10 Box 2-tarief

For 2020 the box 2 rate is 26,25% flat. Box 2 applies to an individual shareholder with an aanmerkelijk belang, including a DGA. Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor. An individual credits dividendbelasting withheld by the BV in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.12) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01.

### 3.11 Dividendbelasting

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 sets the tax at 15% of the opbrengst (proceeds). Art. 6 lid 1 says that if the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85. Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividendnota once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The Belastingdienst says a deelnemingsdividend (participation dividend) to a Dutch shareholder with a holding of 5% or more is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling. No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the tax office invites one. A beleggingsdividend (portfolio dividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification decision) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting. Bodies credit it in the aangifte vennootschapsbelasting. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 1, 5, 6 lid 1 en 9 lid 1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/.

### 3.12 Dividend stripping

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. It is not art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds this rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01.

### 3.13 Inkoop van eigen aandelen

Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares. This also applies to an exchange buyback. If the company bears the tax, art. 6 Wet DB 1965 grosses it up by 100/85. The art. 4c facility for listed companies can avoid that. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### 3.14 Conditionele bronbelasting

There is no conditionele bronbelasting (conditional withholding tax) in 2020. The 2020 version of the Wet bronbelasting 2021 holds only headings and an amending chapter. The levy articles first appear in the 2021 version. Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version. Dividends enter in the 2024 version. The tax hits payments to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction and abuse cases. For 2020 the practical point is that no such levy applies. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01.

### 3.15 Fiscale eenheid Vpb

The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter. Those shares must carry at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Belastingdienst adds that the mother must be a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or a stichting or vereniging acting as a woningcorporatie, or a comparable foreign form. The daughter must be a bv or nv. Both must have the same book years and profit rules and be effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return. Losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment. That also happens when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only, under art. 15 lid 10. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### 3.16 Deelnemingsvrijstelling

Art. 13 lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969 leaves advantages from a deelneming out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2 says a deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital. That is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies with a 1/3 interest. A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met. For btw a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid. A holding that steers the group as a policy-making body can join on written request. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 13) and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

### 3.17 Innovatiebox

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 7/H in the 2020 text. H is the highest art. 22 rate. The effective rate in 2020 is 7%. The Belastingdienst page states 9% for the current regime, which matches the 9/H fraction from 2021. A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring. Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover over those five years below € 250.000.000. This is art. 12ba lid 2 and is the same in 2020 and 2026. Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb gives the K/T part. K is the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. Art. 12bd gives a forfait for a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses it. The forfait is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 12b, 12ba, 12bb, 12bc en 12bd) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### 3.18 Geruisloze omzetting art. 3.65

On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed. The shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The standard conditions in annex 1 of the Besluit 2025 include a three-year rule. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business, unless the contributor proves otherwise. This is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB and premie volksverzekeringen. From the 2025 Besluit the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition is also credited. The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company is only allowed when it already runs a business in the same line. Into a fiscale eenheid is only allowed through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year. This is strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Belastingdienst says the request goes to the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting. The voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring is registered with the Geleideformulier. The BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. Request timing: the inspector decides by beschikking open to bezwaar that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties. In the case KG:212:2022:1 the request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. That standpunt was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

### 3.19 Onzekerheid 2020-tekst geruisloze omzetting

The Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, was in force through 4 November 2025. It was not read. The 2020 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions therefore rests on the 2025 successor only. The checked 2025 Besluit also mentions a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. Whether that credit existed in 2020 is not in the checked sources. This point is UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.

### 3.20 Ruisende inbreng en stakingsaftrek

Without the facility, a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays inkomstenbelasting on the stakingswinst. That is the difference between the book value and the real value of the business. The fiscal reserves are released. The stakingsaftrek of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630, once in a lifetime. An extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek. They may go into a lijfrente under art. 3.129. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.79).

### 3.21 Jaarrekening grootteklassen en deponering

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. The 2020 statute in Boek 2 BW gives these classes. Micro: at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees. Small: € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50. Medium: € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 raises these amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. The employee counts stay the same. Deposit under art. 394: within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein says there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/ and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/.

### 3.22 Administratieplicht en bewaartermijn

Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig. They keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. That is art. 52 lid 4 AWR. Data may move to another carrier, except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. The 2020 and 2026 texts are identical on this point. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on onroerende zaken for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten years. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

### 3.23 BTW bij omzetting naar BV

The new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer. That number goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. A btw fiscale eenheid under art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined. It changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking, on request or by the inspector, states the start date. That beschikking is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, was published on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025". Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Vpb on a profit of € 60.000

**Calculated example.** A BV has a belastbaar bedrag of € 60.000. The amount is below € 200.000. Tax = 16,5% x € 60.000 = € 9.900,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### Example 2: Vpb on a profit of € 300.000

**Calculated example.** A BV has a belastbaar bedrag of € 300.000. First bracket: € 200.000 x 16,5% = € 33.000,00. Excess: € 300.000 - € 200.000 = € 100.000,00. Tax on excess: € 100.000 x 25% = € 25.000,00. Total tax: € 33.000,00 + € 25.000,00 = € 58.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### Example 3: Art. 31 pro-rata for a book year over 1 January 2021

**Calculated example.** A BV has a book year from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2021 and a profit of € 100.000. Days before 1 January 2021: July 31 + August 31 + September 30 + October 31 + November 30 + December 31 = 184 days. Days from 1 January 2021: January 31 + February 28 + March 31 + April 30 + May 31 + June 30 = 181 days. B = 184 + 181 = 365 days. To under the 2020 table: € 100.000 x 16,5% = € 16.500,00. Tn under the 2021 table: € 100.000 x 15% = € 15.000,00. First part: (184/365) x € 16.500 = € 8.317,81 (rounded). Second part: (181/365) x € 15.000 = € 7.438,36 (rounded). Total: € 8.317,81 + € 7.438,36 = € 15.756,17, rounded down € 15.756. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01.

### Example 4: Verliesverrekening

**Calculated example.** A BV has a 2019 loss of € 50.000 and a 2020 profit of € 60.000. The loss is set off against the 2020 profit. Taxable 2020 profit: € 60.000 - € 50.000 = € 10.000,00. Vpb: € 10.000 x 16,5% = € 1.650,00. The inspector must fix the loss by beschikking. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 20 lid 2).

### Example 5: Art. 15b renteaftrekbeperking

**Calculated example.** A BV has a saldo aan renten of € 2.000.000. Case A: gecorrigeerde winst € 3.000.000. 30% x € 3.000.000 = € 900.000,00. The higher of € 900.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00 is € 1.000.000,00. Non-deductible: € 2.000.000 - € 1.000.000 = € 1.000.000,00. Case B: gecorrigeerde winst € 5.000.000. 30% x € 5.000.000 = € 1.500.000,00. The higher of € 1.500.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00 is € 1.500.000,00. Non-deductible: € 2.000.000 - € 1.500.000 = € 500.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1).

### Example 6: Filing a 2020 Vpb return

**Calculated example.** A BV has a book year equal to the calendar year 2020. The return must be filed before 1 June 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen.

### Example 7: Gebruikelijk loon

**Calculated example.** A DGA works for his BV. The loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking is € 80.000. 75% of that is € 80.000 x 75% = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees of the BV is € 40.000. The normbedrag is € 46.000. The highest of € 60.000,00, € 40.000,00 and € 46.000,00 is € 60.000,00. The wage is therefore at least € 60.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.

### Example 8: Gebruikelijk loon escape

**Calculated example.** A DGA has a comparable wage of € 80.000. 75% of that is € 80.000 x 75% = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees is € 70.000. The normbedrag is € 46.000. The highest amount is € 70.000,00. That is higher than 75% of the comparable wage. The escape applies. The wage is 75% of the comparable wage, so € 60.000,00. That is above the normbedrag of € 46.000,00. The wage is therefore € 60.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2).

### Example 9: Zvw for a DGA

**Calculated example.** An uninsured DGA has a wage of € 60.000. The maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 57.232. The wage is above that. Bijdrage Zvw: 5,45% x € 57.232 = € 3.119,14 (rounded), rounded down € 3.119. If the DGA is insured, the werkgeversheffing Zvw is 6,70% x € 57.232 = € 3.834,54 (rounded), rounded € 3.835. If the uninsured DGA has a wage of € 50.000, the bijdrage is 5,45% x € 50.000 = € 2.725,00. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Example 10: Box 2 and dividendbelasting

**Calculated example.** A BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to an individual DGA. Dividendbelasting: 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000,00 withheld. Net received: € 100.000 - € 15.000 = € 85.000,00. Box 2 tax: 26,25% x € 100.000 = € 26.250,00. Credit for dividendbelasting: € 15.000,00. Remaining box 2 tax: € 26.250,00 - € 15.000,00 = € 11.250,00. ### Example 15: Innovatiebox

The 2020 WML amount is not in the checked sources, so the exact 108% amount cannot be worked out here and no product is calculated. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.

### Official example: KG:212:2022:1

**Official example.** The Kennisgroep case KG:212:2022:1 works a geruisloze omzetting with retroactive effect to 1 January 2021 where the 2020 IB assessment was already final. The request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Run the DGA wage through the loonadministratie and withhold loonheffing. For an uninsured DGA, withhold the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from net wage and pay it through the aangifte loonheffingen. - Record dividend decisions. A dividendnota is due once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The dividendbelasting return and payment are due within 1 month after the dividend is made available. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 9 lid 1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.
- File the Vpb return for a calendar book year before 1 June of the next year. For 2020 that is before 1 June 2021. - Keep the administration for seven years under art. 52 lid 4 AWR. Records on onroerende zaken are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- UNVERIFIED: the 2020 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions for the geruisloze omzetting. The Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 30 June 2010 was in force through 4 November 2025 but was not read. The 2020 wording therefore rests on the 2025 successor. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
- Unknown: the WML amount of art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML for 2020 is not in the checked sources. The start-up relief minimum of 108% of twelve times that amount therefore cannot be worked out here. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.
- Source vintage: the filing and extension rules come from current Belastingdienst pages read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. The checked material does not contain a 2020-dated page for these deadlines. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Source vintage: the btw for a BV rule comes from a 2024 beleidsbesluit in force from 1 July 2025. The 2020 text is not in the checked sources. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- Source vintage: the inkoop van eigen aandelen rule comes from a 2025 publication. The 2020 text is not in the checked sources. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.
- Source vintage: the route for the uninsured DGA, where the BV withholds the bijdrage on net wage, is checked in the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 and in the 2024 to 2026 Belastingdienst table. The 2020 FISIN page states only that DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing if the DGA is insured. The 2020 route itself is not in the checked sources. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- The 2025 Besluit geruisloze omzetting mentions a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. Whether that credit existed in 2020 is not in the checked sources. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
