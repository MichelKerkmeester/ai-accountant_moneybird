# Kosten en auto 2020

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2020 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the deduction of business costs and the car rules for a ZZP sole trader (self-employed without employees), plus the BV variant of the gemengde-kosten drempel, in tax year 2020. The most important 2020 items are the drempel (threshold) of € 4.700 for gemengde kosten (mixed costs) with the 80% election, the kilometre allowance of € 0,19 for a private vehicle, and the car bijtelling (private-use addition) of 22% with separate rules for cars first admitted on or before 31 December 2016, youngtimers and zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020. All values in this file come from the 2020 statute text or from Belastingdienst pages that carry no year label, and all are enacted law for 2020. Nothing in this file is proposed or not yet in force. Points that remain open are in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

### Gemengde kosten drempel (art. 3.15 lid 1)

Costs connected with voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen (food, drink and stimulants), with representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and with congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke (congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like) are not deductible up to a yearly drempel of € 4.700 in 2020 ([art. 3.15 lid 1, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The 2020 beleidsbesluit prints the same amount as "€ 4.700 (wettekst 2020)" ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The related travel and stay count as part of these posts ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The restriction does not apply when the enterprise itself makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of the turnover ([art. 3.15 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No onttrekking (withdrawal) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

What falls inside the posts: voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners (business lunches and dinners) including fooien (tips). Drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank. Genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank and genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken (business gifts) generally fall under representatie ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [Besluit section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The drempel is not time-proportioned when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a boekjaar longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally ([Besluit section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The drempel is indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed.

Food costs that qualify as loonkosten (labour costs) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (wages in kind) ([Besluit section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### Keuze in plaats van de drempel (art. 3.15 lid 5)

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 5, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters (taxpayers with income from other work) ([art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The election replaces the drempel for that year and is made in the IB return. With low total gemengde kosten the drempel can leave a smaller deduction than the 80% election, so the two methods can be compared per year. Section 4 gives a calculated comparison.

### Vpb variant van de drempel (art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969)

For a BV: under [art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a lichaam (body) when there are one or more employees in the sense of the Wet LB (Wage Tax Act) in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The 2020 text prints "hoger is dan € 4.700" ([art. 8 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01)). It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount. Only in a fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity) does it apply once, at the parent ([Besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### Reis en verblijf voor congressen en cursussen (art. 3.16 lid 2 f en lid 8)

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep (courses and training for study and profession), and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f en lid 8, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary ([art. 3.16 lid 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.16, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The overzicht page repeats the cap ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Studiekosten (study costs) are deductible as a business cost when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw ([studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers)). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap ([studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers)).

### Kilometervergoeding privévervoermiddel (art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 b)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (auto, motor, fiets) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The 2020 amount is € 0,19 ([art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is all-in. Brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately ([art. 3.15 lid 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Household members are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.15 lid 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is not indexed, because art. 10.1 lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). This rule is for a vehicle in the privévermogen (private assets) or rented privately. A car in the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) follows the art. 3.20 rules ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The page carries no year label.

### Verhuizing en dubbele huisvesting (art. 3.17 lid 1 a)

Moving costs consist of the cost of moving the inboedel (household contents) plus € 7.750 ([art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Housing outside the place of residence, dubbele huisvesting (double housing), is deductible for at most two years ([art. 3.17](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The rule is unchanged in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.17, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Bijtelling auto, standaard (art. 3.20 lid 1 a)

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer (commuting) is not private ([art. 3.20 lid 1 en lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself ([art. 3.20 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), for which the private kilometres times the actual cost per kilometre are settled. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible ([art. 3.20 lid 6 tot en met 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

The waarde is the catalogusprijs (list price) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Belastingdienst describes it as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair market value) replaces the catalogusprijs ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). When the age threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). A laadpaal (charging point) for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)).

Vermogensetikettering (classification of assets): a car used 90% or more for business, or a car with which the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year, is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen (mandatory business assets). Used 90% or more privately it is verplicht privévermogen (mandatory private assets). All other cases are keuzevermogen (assets that may be classified either way) ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag (assessment) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid (special circumstance). Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking (private withdrawal) ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Bijtelling auto, auto van voor 2017 (art. 10a.4)

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% ([art. 10a.4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). In the 2020 text art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months ([art. 10a.4 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. This rule is separate from the youngtimer rule below, which uses 35% of the waarde in het economische verkeer.

### Youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b)

A car that was first taken into use more than 15 years ago gives an onttrekking of 35% of the waarde van de auto ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The age threshold is 15 years in 2020 ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer replaces the catalogusprijs ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)), and in 2020 that switch is also at fifteen years. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). The onttrekking is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation, as with the standard rule. The 35% rule is not affected by art. 10a.4.

### Zero-emissie auto, first admission 2020 (art. 3.20 lid 2)

For a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For a car first admitted in 2020 the reduction is 14% capped at € 6.300, which gives 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above ([art. 3.20 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Cars on waterstof (hydrogen) were uncapped in the 2020 text. The 2020 text has no solar-cell exemption yet, that came later ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### 500 km private use (art. 3.20 lid 1)

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year ([art. 3.20 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). With a rittenregistratie (trip log) or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil ([art. 3.20 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem (certified trip registration system) is assumed to be sluitend (watertight), though trips can still be checked ([privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). Woon-werkverkeer is not private ([art. 3.20 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Bijtelling fiets (art. 3.20a)

When a fiets of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (consumer advisory price) is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne ([art. 3.20a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A fiets includes an e-bike or speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power. Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability ([art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address ([art. 3.20a, 2026 text, where the note on terugwerkende kracht also appears in the 2020 to 2025 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The nil rule therefore applies to tax year 2020.

### Werkruimte thuis (art. 3.16 lid 1 en lid 13)

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting (furnishing) in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible, unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home (a separate part by ordinary standards) and an income test is met ([art. 3.16 lid 1, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it ([art. 3.16 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig ([werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht (leasehold right) is ondernemingsvermogen ([art. 3.16 lid 13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The overzicht page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The Belastingdienst page also offers deduction for a non-zelfstandige workspace when the home is owned and part of the ondernemingsvermogen ([werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). The limit of art. 3.16 lid 14 for such an owned home starts on 1 January 2025 and does not apply to 2020 ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### Telefoonabonnement thuis (art. 3.16 lid 2 a)

Telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). The overzicht page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100% ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The statutory basis is unchanged in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Kleding en persoonlijke verzorging (art. 3.16 lid 2 c en d)

Kleding is not deductible, except werkkleding (work clothing) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), werkkleding being defined in lid 5. Persoonlijke verzorging is not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The zakelijk of prive page names a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (rijbewijs), health costs (pedicure, zorgverzekering) and personal care such as clothing and the kapper as not deductible at all. Werkkleding is deductible ([zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive)).

### Literatuur (art. 3.16 lid 2 b)

Literatuur is not deductible, except vakliteratuur (professional literature) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Private computers en apparatuur (art. 3.16 lid 3)

Costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible. A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen, 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, and in between it is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Loon partner (art. 3.16 lid 4)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5.000 ([art. 3.16 lid 4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Kleine aanschaffingen (€ 450 rule)

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur and onderhoud van de auto ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Receipts and invoices must be kept ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). The page carries no year label.

### Scholingsuitgaven drempel (art. 6.30)

Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven (study expenses) were a persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deduction). They covered lesgeld, cursusgeld, collegegeld, examengeld, promotiekosten and required leermiddelen and beschermingsmiddelen, excluding computer equipment ([art. 6.27 en art. 6.28, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). They were deductible above a drempel of € 250 ([art. 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No deduction applied when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study ([art. 6.27 en art. 6.28, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Scholingsuitgaven maximum buiten standaardstudieperiode (art. 6.30)

Outside the standaardstudieperiode (standard study period) the scholingsuitgaven were deductible up to € 15.000 ([art. 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The deduction ended later: the Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, art. 6.28 and art. 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 ([art. 6.27, 6.28 en 6.30, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the prestatiebeurs (performance grant) case ([art. 10a.16, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A non-fiscal subsidy replaced the tax deduction from 1 January 2022 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html), [regeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01)). For tax year 2020 the deduction still exists.

### Geldboetes en dwangsommen (art. 3.14 lid 1 c en i)

Not deductible: costs connected with geldboeten (fines) from a strafrechter (criminal court), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten, comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten and EU fines ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Also not deductible: costs connected with a misdrijf for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable ([onderdeel e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), and dwangsommen (penalty payments) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb ([onderdeel i](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.14, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines ([Besluit section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The overzicht page prints Geldboetes 0% ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Timing: costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring (forfeiture). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught ([Kennisgroep publicatie, published 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).

Scope: only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking, are excluded. The besluit gives the hennepplanten example, where a taxpayer convicted only for possession and not for trade loses only the costs of possession ([Besluit section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### Btw-correctie privégebruik auto

Btw on use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet (taxed turnover) ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, though it is business for the inkomstenbelasting (income tax) ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). In a BV the DGA (directeur-grootaandeelhouder) is not the btw-ondernemer, he counts as an employee for this purpose ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all btw may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). The page carries no year label.

### Official example: bijtelling of 22% and the cap at the costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). Step 1: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00 onttrekking.
Step 2: € 10.000,00 - € 6.600,00 = € 3.400,00 deductible.

### Official example: cap at the actual costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page has no year label.

Input: onttrekking before the cap € 11.000,00, total autokosten € 9.000,00.
Step 1: the onttrekking is € 11.000,00.
Step 2: the cap in art. 3.20 lid 4 limits the onttrekking to the total autokosten of € 9.000,00. The onttrekking is € 9.000,00.

### Calculated example: gemengde kosten, drempel against the 80% election

Input: gemengde kosten of € 6.000,00 in 2020.

Drempel route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 1.300,00 deductible.

80% route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 × 80% = € 4.800,00 deductible.
Difference: € 4.800,00 - € 1.300,00 = € 3.500,00 more deduction with the election.

Break-even.
Costs € 23.500,00. Drempel route: € 23.500,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 18.800,00. 80% route: € 23.500,00 × 80% = € 18.800,00. Equal.
At € 24.000,00 the drempel route gives € 24.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 19.300,00 and the 80% route gives € 24.000,00 × 80% = € 19.200,00. So above € 23.500,00 the drempel route gives more. Below € 23.500,00 the 80% election gives more.

### Calculated example: kilometre allowance

Input: 12.000 business kilometres with a private car in 2020.
Step 1: 12.000 × € 0,19 = € 2.280,00 deductible. Fuel, insurance, toll and parking cannot be added on top.

### Calculated example: zero-emission car first admitted in 2020

Input a: catalogue price € 40.000,00.
Step 1: € 40.000,00 × 22% = € 8.800,00.
Step 2: € 40.000,00 × 14% = € 5.600,00. This is below the € 6.300,00 cap.
Step 3: € 8.800,00 - € 5.600,00 = € 3.200,00 onttrekking. Control: € 40.000,00 × 8% = € 3.200,00.

Input b: catalogue price € 60.000,00.
Step 1: € 60.000,00 × 22% = € 13.200,00.
Step 2: € 60.000,00 × 14% = € 8.400,00, capped at € 6.300,00.
Step 3: € 13.200,00 - € 6.300,00 = € 6.900,00 onttrekking.

### Calculated example: youngtimer

Input: car first taken into use 20 years ago, waarde in het economische verkeer € 10.000,00, total autokosten € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 10.000,00 × 35% = € 3.500,00.
Step 2: the cap applies, so the onttrekking is € 2.000,00.
Step 3: € 2.000,00 - € 2.000,00 = € 0,00 net deduction.

### Calculated example: 500 km rule

Input: private kilometres in 2020 are 480. 480 is at most 500, so the onttrekking is nil. If the record shows 1.200 private kilometres, the car counts as available for private use and the normal onttrekking applies.

### Calculated example: fiets

Input: consumentenadviesprijs € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 × 7% = € 140,00 onttrekking, capped at the costs borne. If the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil.

### Calculated example: verhuizing

Input: cost of moving the inboedel € 1.500,00.
Step 1: € 1.500,00 + € 7.750,00 = € 9.250,00 deductible. Dubbele huisvesting is deductible for at most two years.

### Calculated example: congress travel cap

Input: travel and stay for a congres € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 - € 1.500,00 = € 500,00 not deductible, unless the nature of the work makes attendance necessary.

### Calculated example: scholingsuitgaven

Input: lesgeld and cursusgeld € 1.200,00, no right to studiefinanciering.
Step 1: € 1.200,00 - € 250,00 = € 950,00 as persoonsgebonden aftrek.

### Calculated example: Vpb variant for a BV

Input a: a BV with employees, total loon € 200.000,00.
Step 1: € 200.000,00 × 0,4% = € 800,00.
Step 2: € 800,00 is lower than € 4.700,00, so the fixed amount of € 4.700,00 applies.

Input b: total loon € 1.500.000,00.
Step 1: € 1.500.000,00 × 0,4% = € 6.000,00.
Step 2: € 6.000,00 is higher than € 4.700,00, so € 6.000,00 applies and the election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%.

### Calculated example: kleine aanschaffingen

Input: a printer of € 300,00 and a laptop of € 800,00, both for the business.
The printer of € 300,00 is below € 450,00 and is deducted in one go in 2020.
The laptop of € 800,00 is € 450,00 or more and is used longer than a year, so it is depreciated. There is no one-off deduction of € 800,00.

### Calculated example: 0% items

Input: a Wahv fine of € 250,00. Deduction € 0,00.
Input: a private telephone subscription of € 300,00, of which the business calls are € 90,00. Deduction € 90,00, the other € 210,00 is not deductible.

### Calculated example: btw correction on a private car

Input: catalogue price including btw and bpm € 30.000,00, no sluitende kilometeradministratie, full year.
Step 1: € 30.000,00 × 1,5% = € 450,00 correction in rubriek 1d. The correction is never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance.
Part year control: six months gives € 450,00 × 6/12 = € 225,00.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- FISIN 2020 studiekosten: the FISIN 2020 page under the slug aftrek_studiekosten did not return content when the sources were checked. Status unknown. The statute settles the 2020 values ([art. 6.27, 6.28 en 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A working FISIN 2020 page would settle the remaining uncertainty.
- Zero-emission 2020 description: one description gives 8% up to € 45.000, another gives 14% off capped at € 6.300. The material resolves these as the same regime. The 2020 statute prints 14% with a maximum of € 6.300 ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Only waterstof was uncapped in 2020. The statute decides.
- Old-car minimum, 35% against 25%: these are sometimes presented as competing values. They apply to different cars, 35% of the waarde in het economische verkeer for a youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b) and at least 25% instead of 22% for a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 (art. 10a.4) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No open conflict for 2020.
- Per-year status of telefoon, computers, werkruimte and geldboetes: this was unknown before the statute texts were read in every version 2020 to 2027. For 2020 the statute settles them ([art. 3.14 en art. 3.16, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The only later change is art. 3.16 lid 14 from 1 January 2025, which does not apply to 2020 ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- 2019 comparison: the checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic. Status unknown. Section 5 can only describe what began in 2020.
