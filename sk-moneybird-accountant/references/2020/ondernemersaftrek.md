# Ondernemersaftrek en MKB-winstvrijstelling 2020

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2020 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur's deductions) and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption) for tax year 2020, including the urencriterium (hours criterion), the startersaftrek (starter's allowance), the meewerkaftrek (allowance for unpaid work by the partner), the stakingsaftrek (cessation allowance), the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D deduction) and the interaction with the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) and the Zvw contribution. The law for 2020 is enacted and the amounts below come from the statute as valid on 1 January 2020, with the FISIN 2020 handbook of the Belastingdienst as a secondary source. Two FISIN 2020 misprints are flagged in section 7: the oudedagsreserve maximum and the lookback window of the second test of the urencriterium. No value for 2020 in the checked material is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

### 3.1 Urencriterium

Art. 3.6 lid 1 of the statute asks for at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year in which someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example is a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). KVK gives a second example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4 excludes hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (joint venture) with connected persons. Such hours do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and an ondermaatschap as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.2 Second test waived, lookback 2015 to 2019

Next to the 1.225 hours, art. 3.6 lid 1 gives two alternative second tests. Either the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is "grotendeels" (for the greater part) spent on the businesses, or the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The second test therefore falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. For tax year 2020 the five preceding calendar years are 2015 to 2019. FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018, which is a misprint against the statute, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 Verlaagd urencriterium

The verlaagd urencriterium (lowered hours criterion) of art. 3.78a lid 3 is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and replaces the 1.225 hours for that item only.

### 3.4 Pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5 states that if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity and birth leave) for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The effect is that the hours criterion is tested as if the interrupted weeks had been worked.

### 3.5 Zelfstandigenaftrek

The zelfstandigenaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium (art. 3.76 lid 1) and is € 7.030 for an ondernemer below the AOW age (state pension age) at the start of the year (art. 3.76 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Whether someone is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged by the Belastingdienst per activity, separately from the btw (VAT). Someone who is a btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst looks at more conditions for the inkomstenbelasting. It weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the zelfstandig beroep (independent profession) counts as an onderneming (art. 3.5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek (investment deduction) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The fetched pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an IB-ondernemer. They say only that the Belastingdienst makes its own assessment after registration (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/).

Art. 3.76 lid 5 caps the aftrek at the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (unrealised self-employed allowance). If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count. Lid 6 says the inspecteur (tax inspector) sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection). Lid 7 offsets it in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8 says the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

Medegerechtigden (persons entitled to a share of the profit without being an ondernemer) get no zelfstandigenaftrek, no meewerkaftrek, no stakingsaftrek and no MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.6 Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age

Art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the lid 2 and lid 3 amounts for an ondernemer who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year, so the startersaftrek is halved too (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 this gives € 3.515, being 50% of € 7.030 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The test is the AOW age at the start of the calendar year, not during the year.

### 3.7 Startersaftrek

The startersaftrek is not a separate item on the art. 3.74 list of the ondernemersaftrek, but a verhoging (raise) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3. Art. 3.74 names five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Art. 3.76 lid 3 gives € 2.123 for 2020 to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, a silent conversion back from a BV) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The Belastingdienst adds that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.8 Startersaftrek at AOW age

Under art. 3.76 lid 4 the startersaftrek is halved for an ondernemer who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year, so the 2020 amount is € 1.062, being 50% of € 2.123 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Startersaftrek prior-years condition

The prior-years test of art. 3.76 lid 3 is a single test: the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst words the same test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting" (you were not an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting in each of the past 5 years), which means the same as the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong, see section 7. A year in which the zelfstandigenaftrek was set to nil by the profit cap still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for this count (art. 3.76 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.10 Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, who has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (occupational disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, who does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and who has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance (disability or accident insurance). The amount is € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then, and FISIN prints € 0 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026). The text and the amounts are identical in every year version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.11 Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk is part of the ondernemersaftrek under art. 3.74 and is worked out in art. 3.77. The base amount for 2020 is € 12.980 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN adds that it requires an S&O-verklaring (R&D statement) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium. The amounts are indexed per year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.12 S&O starter increase

On top of the base amount, art. 3.77 gives a starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period. For 2020 the increase is € 6.494 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.13 MKB-winstvrijstelling

Art. 3.79a makes the MKB-winstvrijstelling a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek. Art. 3.2 states that the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit from business) is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The order is therefore profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. For 2020 the percentage is 14% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

No urencriterium is needed. The statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). There is no vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing of art. 2.10, see 3.19 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.14 Meewerkaftrek bands

Art. 3.78 gives the meewerkaftrek to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Boundary hours therefore go to the higher band: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The hours of the partner must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.15 Meewerkaftrek partner pay limit

Art. 3.16 lid 4 states that pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, so the meewerkaftrek can still apply, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.16 Stakingsaftrek

Art. 3.79 gives the stakingsaftrek on profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630 in 2020, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For a business continued under doorschuiving (silent carry-over) on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (marital community of property, art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), the aftrek applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

In 2020 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming (decrease) of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and that there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.17 Oudedagsreserve addition percentage

Art. 3.67 states that the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year "kan" (may) add to the oudedagsreserve, so the addition is optional. FISIN 2020 says the same: you may add each year, but it is not compulsory. The urencriterium is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

Art. 3.68 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit as ondernemer, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the ondernemingsvermogen (business capital) at the end of the year exceeds the oudedagsreserve at the start of the year. The profit here is the profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by AMvB, lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pensioenrichtleeftijd (pension commencement age) changes, and published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no AMvB change was made in those years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

FISIN 2020 computes the addition from the fiscal profit corrected for the maximumverliesbepaling, exempt profit parts, non-deductible costs, investment schemes, changes in the egalisatiereserve (equalisation reserve) and herinvesteringsreserve (reinvestment reserve) and pension premiums, then 9,44% capped, then minus pension premiums. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.18 Oudedagsreserve addition maximum

The yearly maximum of the addition for 2020 is € 9.218 under art. 3.68 lid 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in two places, which is a misprint, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

Art. 3.69 lets the addition be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year, where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from a lijfrente (annuity) formed from an oudedagsreserve, capped at the ondernemingsvermogen at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71 defines the ondernemingsvermogen as the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the oudedagsreserve, corrected for a terugkeerreserve. Art. 3.72 covers more than one business (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The decrease rules sat in art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73, which were in force in 2020. Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, and art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps the art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 text as it read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year. It also decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). FISIN 2020 defines the ondernemingsvermogen for the decrease as eigen vermogen (equity) plus oudedagsreserve, without the egalisatiereserve and herinvesteringsreserve (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.19 Tariefsaanpassing

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3 states that if grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that reduce the tax base, expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which the taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 the percentage is 3,5% and the start of the top bracket is € 68.507 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The tariefsaanpassing is calculated as a separate step on top of the income tax and it does not change the deductions themselves.

### 3.20 Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing percentage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 that is 49,50% minus 3,5%, so 46%. This figure is the cap on what a euro of ondernemersaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling can save in 2020.

### 3.21 Zvw self-assessed rate

The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet) is levied, up to a maximum contribution income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2020 the self-assessed rate is 5,45% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This interaction is verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The contribution is not levied on the omzetting (conversion) of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente. Wet Zvw art. 43 itself was not reopened. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.22 Zvw maximum contribution income

The maximum contribution income for the self-assessed Zvw contribution in 2020 is € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Income above that amount does not raise the contribution.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1 (Calculated example): zelfstandigenaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling at a profit of € 60.000

Facts: profit as ondernemer € 60.000, the ondernemer meets the urencriterium, is below the AOW age at the start of the year and does not qualify for the startersaftrek.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 7.030. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not bite because € 7.030 is less than the profit of € 60.000.
- Step 2: profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 7.030 = € 52.970.
- Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling 14% of € 52.970 = € 7.415,80.
- Step 4: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 52.970 - € 7.415,80 = € 45.554,20.
- Check: the total reduction is € 7.030 + € 7.415,80 = € 14.445,80, and € 60.000 - € 14.445,80 = € 45.554,20.

### Example 2 (Calculated example): starter with a profit of € 20.000

Facts: profit as ondernemer € 20.000, meets the urencriterium, was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period, below the AOW age at the start of the year.

- Step 1: zelfstandigenaftrek € 7.030.
- Step 2: startersaftrek € 2.123, so the raise brings the aftrek to € 7.030 + € 2.123 = € 9.153, which is less than the profit of € 20.000.
- Step 3: profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000 - € 9.153 = € 10.847.
- Step 4: MKB-winstvrijstelling 14% of € 10.847 = € 1.518,58.
- Step 5: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 10.847 - € 1.518,58 = € 9.328,42.
- Check: € 10.847 × 0,14 = € 1.518,58 and € 10.847 - € 1.518,58 = € 9.328,42.

### Example 3 (Calculated example): profit below the zelfstandigenaftrek

Facts: profit as ondernemer € 4.000, meets the urencriterium, no startersaftrek.

- Step 1: the zelfstandigenaftrek is not more than the profit, so the aftrek for 2020 is € 4.000, not € 7.030.
- Step 2: the amount cut off is € 7.030 - € 4.000 = € 3.030 of niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, set by beschikking open to bezwaar and to be offset in the next nine calendar years, oldest first, at most by the amount by which each later year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek.
- Step 3: profit after the ondernemersaftrek is € 0, so the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of € 0 = € 0,00.
- Step 4: the year counts as a year in which the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied for the startersaftrek count. Art. 3.76 lid 5 also counts a year in which the cap sets the aftrek to nil.

### Example 4 (Official example, FISIN 2020 section 8.4.3): carry-forward of niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek

The Belastingdienst example: in 2018 the profit was € 5.000 against a right of € 7.280, so € 2.280 was unused. In 2019 there was a loss of € 25.000, so the whole € 7.280 of that year was unused, giving a saldo of € 2.280 + € 7.280 = € 9.560. In 2020 the profit of € 100.000 minus € 7.030 gives € 92.970, minus the € 9.560 gives € 83.410 and a saldo of € 0. The € 7.280 is the 2018 and 2019 zelfstandigenaftrek as printed in the source example, not the 2020 amount of € 7.030 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 5 (Official example, FISIN 2020 section 7.7.1.3): forced decrease of the oudedagsreserve

Activa € 6.000, eigen vermogen minus € 1.000, oudedagsreserve € 4.000 and crediteuren € 3.000. The ondernemingsvermogen is € 6.000 - € 3.000 = € 3.000, which equals minus € 1.000 + € 4.000. The reserve of € 4.000 is higher than the ondernemingsvermogen of € 3.000, so the reserve decreases by € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000, and that € 1.000 is added to profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### Example 6 (Calculated example): meewerkaftrek at 1.300 hours

Facts: profit € 60.000, meets the urencriterium, the partner works 1.300 hours in the business without pay.

- Step 1: 1.300 hours is at least 1.225 and below 1.750, so the band is 3%.
- Step 2: 3% of € 60.000 = € 1.800,00 meewerkaftrek.
- Variation: exactly 875 hours would fall in the 2% band, because the band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" 875, and 2% of € 60.000 = € 1.200,00. Exactly 1.750 hours falls in the 4% band, and 4% of € 60.000 = € 2.400,00.
- Variation: if the partner were paid € 4.500, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible and the partner counts as unpaid, and the bands still apply.

### Example 7 (Calculated example): stakingsaftrek with earlier use

Facts: profit on the staking of one whole business € 10.000, stakingsaftrek enjoyed in an earlier year € 1.500.

- Step 1: the maximum of € 3.630 is reduced by the earlier € 1.500, giving € 3.630 - € 1.500 = € 2.130.
- Step 2: the aftrek equals the staking profit, at most € 2.130, so the stakingsaftrek is € 2.130.
- Variation: had the earlier use been the full € 3.630, the maximum would be reduced to nil and there would be no stakingsaftrek this year.
- Variation: had the staking profit been € 2.000 with no earlier use, the aftrek would be € 2.000.

### Example 8 (Calculated example): startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Facts: was not an ondernemer in one or more of 2015 to 2019, has a WIA benefit in 2020, works 900 hours in the business, profit € 30.000, below the AOW age at the start of the year, no geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv.

- Step 1: 900 hours is at least 800 and not more than 1.225, so the verlaagd urencriterium is met and the urencriterium of 1.225 hours is not met.
- Step 2: the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, so the amount is € 12.000.
- Step 3: the amount is not more than the profit of € 30.000, so the aftrek is € 12.000.
- Step 4: profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 30.000 - € 12.000 = € 18.000.
- Step 5: MKB-winstvrijstelling, no urencriterium needed: 14% of € 18.000 = € 2.520,00.
- Step 6: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 18.000 - € 2.520,00 = € 15.480,00.
- Follow-on years: one earlier year of use gives € 8.000, two years give € 4.000, and after more than two uses there is no aftrek and FISIN prints € 0. Each amount is capped at the profit.

### Example 9 (Calculated example): S&O aftrek with the starter increase

Facts: profit € 100.000, has an S&O-verklaring from RVO, 600 hours of S&O work, meets the urencriterium, was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period.

- Step 1: base amount € 12.980.
- Step 2: starter increase € 6.494, so the total S&O aftrek is € 12.980 + € 6.494 = € 19.474.
- Step 3: profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 100.000 - € 19.474 = € 80.526.
- Step 4: MKB-winstvrijstelling 14% of € 80.526 = € 11.273,64.
- Step 5: belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 80.526 - € 11.273,64 = € 69.252,36.
- Check: € 80.526 × 0,14 = € 11.273,64 and € 80.526 - € 11.273,64 = € 69.252,36.

### Example 10 (Calculated example): oudedagsreserve addition

Facts: meets the urencriterium, below the AOW age at the start of the year, profit for the addition € 60.000, no pension premiums charged to that profit, ondernemingsvermogen at year end € 80.000, oudedagsreserve at the start of the year € 20.000, and the ondernemer chooses to make the addition.

- Step 1: 9,44% of € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
- Step 2: the maximum of € 9.218 is not exceeded, so € 5.664,00 stands.
- Step 3: the cap is the ondernemingsvermogen at year end minus the oudedagsreserve at the start: € 80.000 - € 20.000 = € 60.000, so this cap does not bite either.
- Step 4: the addition is € 5.664,00 and the reserve at year end is € 20.000 + € 5.664,00 = € 25.664,00.
- Variation: at a profit of € 120.000, 9,44% is € 11.328,00, which is above the maximum, so the addition is capped at € 9.218.
- Variation: at a profit of € 60.000 but with an ondernemingsvermogen at year end of € 21.000 against a reserve at the start of € 20.000, the headroom is € 21.000 - € 20.000 = € 1.000, so the addition is capped at € 1.000.
- Variation: a negative outcome of the calculation gives a nil addition.

### Example 11 (Calculated example): tariefsaanpassing

Facts: taxable box 1 income after the deductions € 58.507 and grondslagverminderende posten of € 20.000, being the ondernemersaftrek plus the MKB-winstvrijstelling.

- Step 1: taxable box 1 income plus the deductions: € 58.507 + € 20.000 = € 78.507.
- Step 2: the amount by which that sum exceeds the start of the top bracket: € 78.507 - € 68.507 = € 10.000.
- Step 3: 3,5% of € 10.000 = € 350,00. This is the base amount under art. 2.10 lid 2.
- Step 4: the cap is 3,5% of the deductions: 3,5% of € 20.000 = € 700,00. The € 350,00 is below the cap, so the tariefsaanpassing is € 350,00.
- Step 5: the maximum value of the deductions is 46% in total, being 49,50% minus 3,5%.
- Variation: with taxable box 1 income of € 108.507 and the same € 20.000 of deductions, the sum is € 128.507, the excess is € 128.507 - € 68.507 = € 60.000, and 3,5% of € 60.000 = € 2.100,00. The cap of € 700,00 then applies, so the tariefsaanpassing is € 700,00.

### Example 12 (Calculated example): MKB-winstvrijstelling in a loss year

Facts: loss as ondernemer € 10.000, meets the urencriterium.

- Step 1: the zelfstandigenaftrek is capped at the profit. With a loss the aftrek is nil, and the € 7.030 is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek. Under art. 3.76 lid 5 this year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count, because the cap set the aftrek to nil.
- Step 2: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of the amount after the ondernemersaftrek, so 14% of minus € 10.000 = minus € 1.400,00.
- Step 3: the fiscal loss becomes € 10.000 + € 1.400,00 = € 11.400,00. The Belastingdienst calls this enlargement of the loss disadvantageous in a loss year.

### Example 13 (Calculated example): Zvw contribution

Facts: belastbare winst uit onderneming € 40.000, no other contribution income.

- Step 1: the self-assessed rate for 2020 is 5,45%.
- Step 2: 5,45% of € 40.000 = € 2.180,00.
- Variation: at contribution income above the maximum of € 57.232, the contribution is at most 5,45% of € 57.232 = € 3.119,14, rounded down.
- Note: no contribution is levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente.

### Example 14 (Official examples): part-year working and standby time

The Belastingdienst example: a business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. The same page: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The KVK example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so 1.225 - 936 = 289 extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2019

- The checked material contains no 2019 figures for this topic, so this file cannot give an old-to-new comparison for 2020. The points below state what the sources say about the 2020 position.
- 2020 is the first year in this knowledge base, so there is no previous-year file to compare with.
- The oudedagsreserve could still be added to in 2020. Art. 3.67 to 3.72 were in force (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The addition percentage was 9,44% and the maximum was € 9.218 (art. 3.68 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Those articles lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition is possible from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- In 2020 the stakingsaftrek still covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting under art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The tariefsaanpassing for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which gives the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling a maximum value of 46% (art. 2.10 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). No other year's percentage is stated in the 2020 material.
- For 2020 the second test of the urencriterium is waived for anyone who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, so for 2015 to 2019 (art. 3.6 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Two 2020 FISIN misprints differ from the statute and are listed in section 7: the oudedagsreserve maximum of € 9.214 against € 9.218 and the lookback window of 2014 to 2018 against 2015 to 2019.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices to make the hours of the ondernemer and of a working partner plausible. Non-billable hours count, standby time does not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- The ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are applied in the inkomstenbelasting return in the winst uit onderneming, in the order profit, then ondernemersaftrek, then MKB-winstvrijstelling (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- The niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek and its later offset are set by the inspecteur in a beschikking open to bezwaar, so record the amount and check the beschikking (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible and the partner counts as unpaid, so record such pay as a non-deductible item and check whether the meewerkaftrek bands apply (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The oudedagsreserve addition is optional, needs the urencriterium and an age below the AOW age at the start of the year, and is worked out in the winst en verliesrekening en balans. FISIN 2020 defines the ondernemingsvermogen for a decrease as eigen vermogen plus oudedagsreserve, without the egalisatiereserve and herinvesteringsreserve. A decrease is added to profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).
- The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the 2020 self-assessed Zvw contribution of 5,45% is levied, up to € 57.232. No contribution is levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The S&O aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium, so keep the verklaring and the hour records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- For a sole trader who also owns a BV: the startersaftrek and the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid do not apply if the business is continued under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) and that started in the calendar year or one of the five recent years (art. 3.76 lid 3 and 3.78a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- No value for 2020 in the checked material is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: oudedagsreserve addition maximum 2020. Art. 3.68 lid 1 as valid on 1 January 2020 gives € 9.218 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in two places (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The statute is the primary source and prevails. A corrected FISIN page or a Belastingdienst statement would settle it fully.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: the lookback window of the second test of the urencriterium for 2020. Art. 3.6 gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute prevails.
- Conflict, settled: the startersaftrek prior-years condition. The reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Art. 3.76 lid 3 requires that the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst words the same test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).
- Corrected item, not an open point: the startersaftrek is not a separate part of the art. 3.74 list of the ondernemersaftrek, but a raise of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Corrected item, not an open point: for 2020 the stakingsaftrek on an afneming of the oudedagsreserve sits in art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b, not in a transitional article, which only came in on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
