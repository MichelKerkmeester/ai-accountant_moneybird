**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2020 topic files records. ## 1. Unique to 2020

- The new KOR replaced the old VAT-based reduction from 1 January 2020, with a Dutch calendar-year turnover ceiling of € 20.000. [btw](btw.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which caps the value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling at 46%. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 heffingsvrij vermogen is € 30.846 per person and € 61.692 with a fiscal partner. [box-3](box-3.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 Zvw rate by aanslag is 5,45% and the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 57.232. [zvw](zvw.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 KIA table gives 28% of the investment up to € 58.238 and a fixed € 16.307 up to € 107.848, with a € 450 per-asset minimum. [investeringen](investeringen.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2020 gets a reduction of 14% off with a cap of € 6.300, so 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto.md#5-changes)
- The maximum jaarruimte for 2020 is € 12.986. [pensioen-en-aov](pensioen-en-aov.md#5-changes)
- The second restriction of the moratorium exception applies from 1 January 2020, when enforcement also follows if aanwijzingen are not followed within a reasonable term. [arbeidsrelatie](arbeidsrelatie.md#5-changes)
