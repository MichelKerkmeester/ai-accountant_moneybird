## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers Dutch VAT for a sole trader or a BV, including rates, exemptions, input VAT, the small-business scheme, returns, cross-border supplies, invoicing and private use. The main rates were 21%, 9% for supplies in Tabel I and nihil for supplies in Tabel II when the required conditions were met. The new KOR applied from 1 January 2020. The rules described for 2020 are enacted, with historical source gaps and one corrected KOR turnover test identified below.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard rate

The standard rate is 21%. It applies when a taxable supply is not covered by a reduced rate or the zero rate. Record the taxable amount and VAT separately on the invoice. [Wet OB, article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services listed in Tabel I. The checked year-specific source establishes the rate and the table reference. The detailed current Belastingdienst list is not a complete year-specific record of every Tabel I item for 2020. [Wet OB, article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01).

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to supplies in Tabel II when the conditions set by general administrative order are met. A zero-rated supply is not the same as an exempt supply. Input VAT can still be deducted for zero-rated supplies, while exempt supplies do not carry that deduction right. For an intra-EU supply of goods, the seller needs records showing transport to another EU country, the buyer's valid VAT ID from another member state and an ICP that is timely, correct and complete. A late or incorrect ICP can jeopardize the zero rate and lead to a fine. [Wet OB, article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst ICP instructions](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Logies

Logies was in the 9% category in 2020. The later law states that the relevant Tabel I post lapses only from 1 January 2026, and ties the transitional rule to logies. [Wet behoud verlaagd btw-tarief op cultuur, media en sport](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html).

### Electronic publications

From 1 January 2020, Tabel I post b.21 applied the 9% rate to electronic supplies or lending of publications and access to news websites. [Toelichting Tabel I besluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html).

### Mondkapjes

An optional approval allowed no VAT to be charged on mondkapjes from 25 May 2020 until 1 September 2020. The supplier retained the right to deduct input VAT. The approval was optional, so record whether it was applied to the relevant supplies. [Besluit van 19 mei 2020, nr. 2020-10789](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-28349.html).

### Exemptions

Wet OB article 11 lists exemptions covering, among other things, certain supplies and letting of immovable property, care, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money and securities, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal services, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods, organs and blood, member services, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list also names beleggingsgoud and kantines. Exempt supplies carry no right to deduct input VAT. An entrepreneur with both taxed and exempt activity must account for the effect on deductible input VAT. [Wet OB, article 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).

### New KOR in force

The modernised KOR applied from 1 January 2020. It is an exemption, not a VAT discount. An entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a Dutch vaste inrichting may choose it if the applicable turnover condition is met. Legal persons such as a BV, NV, foundation or association may also participate. [Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0041782), [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR Dutch turnover ceiling

The ceiling was € 20.000 of Dutch turnover in the calendar year. For 2020, the test concerned the current calendar year only. The checked text of article 25 does not set a preceding-year test. Count all Dutch taxed turnover whatever the rate, including supplies taxed under the reverse-charge rules, plus some exempt supplies. All business activities under the entrepreneur's sub-nummers are included. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR establishment

The 2020 scheme covered an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a vaste inrichting there. The 2020 Handbook says legal persons, including a BV, could participate. The materials do not establish a special turnover ceiling for a BV. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR notice

The entrepreneur had to notify the tax authority at least 4 weeks before the relevant tax period. The tax authority could refuse the choice by decision where it was plausible that the conditions would not be met. For the initial 2020 transition, notices for the new scheme could be made from 1 June 2019, with a deadline of 20 November 2019 for a 1 January 2020 start. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [transition notice](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-4774.html).

### KOR minimum participation

After starting the exemption, the entrepreneur had to remain in it for at least 3 years. The KOR had no right to deduct input VAT and the entrepreneur could not state VAT on the invoice. The KOR was not available for an option to tax letting of immovable property. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR return after voluntary withdrawal

Voluntary withdrawal took effect only from the first day of a tax period. Re-entry was barred until 3 years after revocation. The 2020 Handbook says deregistration was processed with a lead time of 4 weeks. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR overrun

If turnover exceeded the ceiling, the exemption stopped from the supply that crossed the ceiling onward. The entrepreneur had to report the overrun and could re-enter only 3 years after the overrun. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01).

### KOR transition notice for a 2020 start

The transition notice was due by 20 November 2019 for a start on 1 January 2020. The transition explanation also says that entrepreneurs with an exemption under the old KOR on 31 December 2019 moved automatically into the new KOR and could leave during the minimum term. [Transition notice and explanation](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-4774.html) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR registration threshold without notice

A later decision allowed an entrepreneur not yet registered with annual turnover of at most € 1.800 to use the KOR without the usual notice, retroactive to 1 January 2020. This approval was issued after 2020, but covers the 2020 period. [Decision of 10 June 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html).

### KOR investment-goods herziening de minimis

The 2020 Handbook says no herziening is required when the total adjustment for investment goods used before the relevant year is less than € 500 per year. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Usual VAT return period

The usual period was a quarter. A business could request a monthly or yearly period by letter to its tax office. The tax authority was to reply within 6 weeks, and the change began at the start of a new period. Late filing or payment could lead to a monthly period being imposed. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst period changes](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

### Monthly return trigger

The 2020 Handbook says the period was monthly when the entrepreneur usually paid more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter. The Handbook describes this as a practice rule, not a statutory threshold. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Yearly return VAT limit

A yearly return was available only when the entrepreneur owed less than € 1.883 VAT for the year. The entrepreneur also had to meet the legal-form and intra-EU conditions below and could not hold a vergunning under article 23. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Yearly return intra-EU limits

The entrepreneur also had to remain below € 10.000 in each of four categories separately: intra-EU supplies of goods, intra-EU services, intra-EU acquisitions and received intra-EU services. The yearly option was for a natural person or a partnership of natural persons, such as a maatschap or vof. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Period return filing term

A period return was filed within a term set by the inspector of at least 1 month after the end of the period. The Belastingdienst states that monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the following month. [AWR, article 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01) and [Belastingdienst VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/).

### Period return payment term

Payment was due within 1 month after the end of the period. The Belastingdienst uses the same deadline for filing and payment. The filing date is the date the return is received. An extension is stated only for a calamity. [AWR, article 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) and [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### General monthly and quarterly deadline

The Belastingdienst states that monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the month following the period. The exact published due-date table for 2020 was not found. [Belastingdienst VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) and [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

[Belastingdienst VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) and [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Suppletie correction that may be included in the next return

For a correction of less than € 1.000, the 2020 Handbook says the entrepreneur may include it in the next return. The 2020 wording is strict about being less than that amount. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Suppletie correction period

The 2020 rule covers corrections to returns for the past 5 calendar years. [UBOB, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).

### Suppletie timing

The entrepreneur had to correct as soon as possible, before knowing or reasonably suspecting that the inspector knew of the error or omission. Failure was an offence. The later eight-week rule was not part of the 2020 text. [UBOB, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).

### Suppletie penalty limitation

The power to impose a vergrijpboete lapsed 5 years after the end of the year. [UBOB, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).

### Reverse charge for specified electronics

For mobile phones, chips before they become part of an end product, game consoles, tablets and laptops, the reverse charge applied only for a total of € 10.000 or more per delivery per type of good. The invoice states “btw verlegd”, the buyer’s VAT ID and the consideration per rate. The supplier remained responsible for the correct treatment. The customer reported domestic reverse-charge VAT in return section 2, or foreign reverse-charge VAT in section 4, and deducted it when used for taxed activity. [UBOB, article 24ba](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [when to reverse charge](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/) and [how reverse charge works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).

### Call-off stock reporting

From 1 January 2020, the call-off stock simplification removed the need to register in the arrival state when its conditions were met. The supplier reported the transfer in the ICP. The listed conditions include an agreement with a known buyer, that buyer’s VAT ID, the supplier’s own transport, no establishment in the arrival state, a register and a supply within the stated period. The ICP identifies each buyer for the period in which transport began. The detailed conditions are described in the 2024 guide, so check section 7 for the historical evidence limit. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp) and [ICP digital return guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

### Quarterly ICP goods threshold for 2020

The current Belastingdienst page says goods can be reported quarterly when supplies stay at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and in each of the previous quarters. The checked sources do not verify that threshold for 2020, so do not rely on it as a confirmed 2020 rule. [Current ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).

### ICP deadline for 2020

The 2024 digital guide gives filing information, but the 2020 ICP deadline was not found. The 2020 filing deadline should be checked against a period-specific primary source before applying a date. [ICP digital return guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

### Goods distance-sales thresholds

For goods sold to consumers in other EU states, the 2020 scheme used destination-state thresholds. The checked source does not verify the individual 2020 amounts. Do not apply the later € 10.000 EU threshold to 2020. [EU VAT in the European Community annex](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2021-02/vat_in_ec_annexi.pdf).

### B2C digital services distance-sales scheme

MOSS applied to B2C digital services in 2020. It continued until the new one-stop-shop system took effect on 1 July 2021. Do not treat the later OSS mechanics as the 2020 process. [Belastingdienst digital services and EU VAT notification](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

### Last return of the year

The last return of the year settles private use of goods and services, the business car, the pro rata for taxed and exempt turnover and personnel provisions, gifts and business gifts above € 227. It also covers gas, water and electricity, internal services and herzieningen. If the last return has already been filed, correct it with a suppletie. [Belastingdienst private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Business-car private-use forfait

If private-use records were not kept, the forfait was 2,7% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm for a car in the business assets or obtained in another way. Commuting counted as private use. The amount was capped by the relevant VAT amounts. [Besluit heffing privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst business-car private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Reduced business-car forfait

The 1,5% forfait applied when no purchase VAT could be deducted or when the car had been in use for more than 5 years. The year of purchase counted as a full year. The private-use amount was prorated for a part year and reduced proportionally where the car was used for exempt activity. [Besluit heffing privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst business-car private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Business-car private-use cap from 4 July 2020

From 4 July 2020, the amount was capped at the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of the VAT on purchase for the stated period. After that period, the cap was the VAT deducted in the year. The decision replaced the earlier decision. The checked material does not establish the cap rule from the start of 2020 through 3 July. [Besluit heffing privégebruik auto, effective from 4 July 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).

### BUA personnel and gifts threshold

The BUA excludes input VAT deduction for certain staff and business benefits, including maintaining a certain social status, business gifts, staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport. The exclusion does not apply where the total cost for the same recipient is no more than € 227 per book year. The decree also lists exceptions, including specified food and drink for staff and certain commuting provisions. [Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### BUA own contribution

The 2020 website policy allowed an employee contribution to reduce the amount tested against the BUA threshold. The policy was ended from 1 January 2024, so this statement is limited to the 2020 treatment. [Decision of 6 October 2023, nr. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).

### BUA staff bicycle

The commuting bicycle exception was capped at € 749 including VAT and had conditions, including no bicycle in the year or two before and restrictions where another travel arrangement covered at least half of travel days. [Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23).

### BUA staff meals

For staff meals charged below cost plus 25%, the excluded deduction was 9% of the difference. The BUA also sets out the treatment of food and drink for staff. [Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23).

### Invoice deadline

An invoice to another entrepreneur or to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur had to be issued by the 15th day of the month after the supply. An invoice was generally not required for private customers, subject to exceptions. VAT was due at the latest in the period when the customer paid. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/).

### Simplified invoice limit

A simplified invoice was allowed up to € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice. It needed the issue date, names and addresses, description of the goods or services, VAT or the information needed to calculate it, and a reference to the original invoice for a correction. This format was not allowed for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or certain foreign reverse-charge supplies. [Belastingdienst simplified invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur).

### Kasstelsel request condition

The kasstelsel was compulsory for listed trades. The factuurstelsel applied to mainly business-to-business suppliers. The 2020 Handbook says the kasstelsel could also be requested when more than 80% of supplies went to consumers. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### B2B e-invoicing duty

The current government business page describes e-invoicing duties for suppliers to central government and says other public bodies may require them. The checked source does not establish the historical 2020 position for general B2B e-invoicing. See section 7. [Business.gov.nl e-invoicing government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).

### Corona bijzonder uitstel debt period

The repayment arrangement covers tax debts built up from 12 March 2020 through 31 March 2022. The repayment period ran from 1 October 2022 to 1 October 2027, with the first term due 31 October 2022. [Belastingdienst payment arrangement for debts built up under special deferral](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

### Corona repayment terms

The standard arrangement uses 60 monthly terms, with quarterly payment also possible. One pause of up to 6 months or 2 quarters is possible. Extension to 84 months is limited to a scheme of at least € 10.000. For recipients of the specified UHT letter, the 60 months run from 1 July 2025. [Belastingdienst payment arrangement for debts built up under special deferral](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example (domestic rates).** Assume a taxable supply with a price excluding VAT of € 1.000. At 21%, calculate € 1.000 × 21 ÷ 100 = € 210 VAT, then € 1.000 + € 210 = € 1.210 including VAT. At 9%, calculate € 1.000 × 9 ÷ 100 = € 90 VAT, then € 1.000 + € 90 = € 1.090 including VAT. At the zero rate, output VAT is nil, but the right to deduct input VAT can remain if the supply qualifies. The rates and deduction distinction come from [Wet OB, article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [the intra-EU supply guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example (KOR ceiling).** Assume an entrepreneur has € 19.000 of Dutch turnover in the current calendar year and makes another supply worth € 1.500. Calculate € 19.000 + € 1.500 = € 20.500. The ceiling is crossed by that supply, so the exemption stops from that supply onward. The preceding year is not part of the 2020 test. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01).

**Calculated example (yearly return eligibility).** Assume a natural-person sole trader with no article 23 vergunning owes € 1.500 VAT for the year. Calculate € 1.500 < € 1.883. Assume each of the four intra-EU categories is € 9.000. For each category, calculate € 9.000 < € 10.000. Subject to meeting all other conditions, the figures pass the VAT and intra-EU tests for a yearly return. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

**Official example (invoice timing).** The 2020 Handbook gives this example: a supply made on 10 April must be invoiced by 15 May. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

**Calculated example (reverse charge on electronics).** Assume one delivery of the same type of goods totals € 10.000 and meets the other reverse-charge conditions. The threshold is met because € 10.000 is at least € 10.000. If the applicable rate is 21%, the customer records € 10.000 × 21 ÷ 100 = € 2.100 as VAT due under the reverse charge, then deducts that amount only to the extent the goods are used for taxed activity. The supplier issues the invoice marked “btw verlegd”. [UBOB, article 24ba](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen) and [Wet OB, article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01).

**Calculated example (intra-EU zero rate and ICP).** Assume a Dutch entrepreneur sells goods to a buyer in another EU country. The supplier applies the zero rate only if records show the goods went to that country, the buyer has a valid VAT ID from another member state, and the ICP is timely, correct and complete. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example (business-car private use).** Assume a catalogue price including VAT and bpm of € 20.000 and no private-use records. Calculate € 20.000 × 2,7 ÷ 100 = € 540 under the standard forfait, before applying the cap. If the 1,5% forfait applies, calculate € 20.000 × 1,5 ÷ 100 = € 300, again before applying the cap. [Besluit heffing privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

**Calculated example (BUA threshold).** Assume the total cost of provisions to one person is € 200 in a book year. Calculate € 200 ≤ € 227, so the BUA threshold exclusion does not apply to that amount. If the total is € 250, calculate € 250 > € 227, so the threshold is exceeded. The separate rules for charged consideration and the type of benefit still matter. [Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23).

**Calculated example (suppletie).** Assume the filed return reports € 4.000 VAT due but the correct amount is € 4.900. Calculate € 4.900 minus € 4.000 = € 900 correction. Since € 900 is less than € 1.000, the 2020 Handbook says it may be included in the next return. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

**Official example (corona repayment).** The Belastingdienst gives these examples for the repayment arrangement: a € 15.000 debt over 60 months gives a monthly term of € 271 including invorderingsrente. A € 25.000 debt gives € 451 per month. Paying € 10.000 at once lowers the monthly term to € 271 and saves about € 828. [Belastingdienst payment arrangement for debts built up under special deferral](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

The checked material does not include a complete 2019 comparison. It identifies these changes or transitions that began in 2020:

- An optional approval allowed no VAT on mondkapjes from 25 May 2020 until 1 September 2020 while preserving input VAT deduction. [Besluit van 19 mei 2020, nr. 2020-10789](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-28349.html).

[Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp).

- The special deferral repayment scheme applies to qualifying tax debts built up from 12 March 2020. [Belastingdienst payment arrangement for debts built up under special deferral](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Record sales by VAT treatment and rate, and keep invoices showing the taxable amount and VAT per rate. Invoices to another entrepreneur or a legal person that is not an entrepreneur need the required party details, VAT ID, KVK number where registered, invoice date, unique consecutive number, description and quantity or scope, supply or prepayment date, and amounts excluding VAT by rate. [Belastingdienst invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

Keep evidence for zero-rated intra-EU goods supplies, including the buyer's valid VAT ID, transport records and ICP submission. For intra-EU B2B services, verify the customer’s VAT ID, invoice without VAT marked “btw verlegd”, and report the service in the VAT return and ICP where required. [Belastingdienst ICP instructions](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst intra-EU services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen).

A KOR participant does not deduct input VAT and does not state VAT on invoices. Track Dutch turnover across the entrepreneur’s activities to monitor the current-year ceiling. A BV may participate if the conditions are met. [Wet OB, article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

Reconcile the VAT return to the sales and purchase records for the period. File and pay by the applicable deadline. Keep records for private use, business-car use and personnel provisions so that the final return can settle the private-use amount, pro rata, BUA and herzieningen. If a return already filed needs correction, use a suppletie subject to the 2020 correction rules. [AWR, articles 10 and 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) and [UBOB, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Old KOR full-relief point: UNVERIFIED.** The figure € 1.345 is derived from the old statutory formula and is not printed in the statute. The statute states the € 1.883 limit and the reduction formula. The primary statute does not independently confirm the derived point. [Wet OB, 2019 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2019-01-01).

- **Exact 2020 VAT due-date table: unknown and UNVERIFIED.** The live page starts with later periods. The general deadline rule is stated in section 3, but the exact 2020 calendar dates were not found. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

- **2020 ICP deadline: unknown and UNVERIFIED.** The checked digital guide is for 2024. A period-specific 2020 primary source would settle the date. [ICP digital return guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).

- **Quarterly ICP goods threshold: UNVERIFIED for 2020.** The current page gives € 50.000, but the checked page does not establish the threshold for 2020. A dated 2020 Belastingdienst instruction or applicable legal text would settle it. [Current ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).

- **Goods distance-sales thresholds by destination state: UNVERIFIED.** The checked EU table was not re-keyed as a verified set of primary-source amounts. A dated primary source listing each destination state’s threshold for 2020 would settle the amounts. [EU VAT in the European Community annex](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2021-02/vat_in_ec_annexi.pdf).

- **Business-car cap from 1 January through 3 July 2020: unknown in the checked material.** The checked decision applies from 4 July 2020 and replaces BLKB 2012/639M. The earlier decision would need to be checked to establish the cap for the earlier part of the year. [Decision effective from 4 July 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).

- **B2B e-invoicing duty in 2020: UNVERIFIED.** The current government page describes duties for suppliers to central government and possible requirements from other public bodies, but does not establish the 2020 rule for general B2B invoices. A dated 2020 primary source would settle it. [Business.gov.nl e-invoicing government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).

- **Resolved KOR turnover-test conflict.** The 2020 rule tests turnover in the current calendar year only. The corrected earlier baseline that used both the year of application and the preceding year does not apply to 2020. The later KOR law requires prior-year turnover information and changes the test from 2025. [Wet OB, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Wet OB, 2024 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01) and [Wet OB, 2025 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

- **Suppletie threshold wording varies by year.** The 2020 Handbook says a correction of less than € 1.000 may go into the next return. The 2026 Handbook says € 1.000 or less. These are year-specific wordings. The exact treatment of a correction of exactly € 1.000 under the 2020 wording is not established by the checked 2020 Handbook. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) and [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

- **Full historic Tabel I item list for 2020: not established item by item in the checked material.** The current Belastingdienst pages list current reduced-rate goods and services but do not carry a year. The year-specific enacted source establishes the 9% rate for Tabel I. A complete dated Tabel I text for the relevant 2020 version would settle the historical list. [Wet OB, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [current 9% goods list](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw) and [current 9% services list](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/).
