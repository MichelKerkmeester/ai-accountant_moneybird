# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 3 (sparen en beleggen, savings and investments) taxes a forfaitair (fixed) return on assets and debts. For 2022 the old band method applies, with the grondslag sparen en beleggen (the base for savings and investments) split over three bands that each mix rendementsklasse I (return class I, savings) and rendementsklasse II (return class II, investments) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 (Box 3 Legal Restoration Act) is enacted and adds a second calculation for 2022 with the actual composition of the assets in three categories (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The Belastingdienst computes both methods and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). All 2022 values in this file are enacted and Verified.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Heffingsvrij vermogen

For 2022 the heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free capital) is € 50.650 for one person and € 101.300 with a fiscal partner. The statute valid on 1 January 2022 and the Belastingdienst page both give these amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen).

The rendementsgrondslag (return base) minus the heffingsvrij vermogen gives the grondslag sparen en beleggen, the amount that the bands or the category calculation use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The first band ends at € 50.650, the same amount as the single-person heffingsvrij vermogen for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the partners' share of the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen under art. 2.17 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Whole-year partners use the joint figures in the recalculation (art. 3 lid 4 of the Wet rechtsherstel, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).

### Schuldendrempel

For 2022 the schuldendrempel (debt threshold) is € 3.200 for one person and € 6.400 with a whole-year partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

Only the part of the total debts above the drempel counts in box 3, and the Belastingdienst computes this in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The statute places the drempel in art. 5.3 lid 3, written as "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...", and the amount is indexed under art. 10.1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

The debts above the drempel are subtracted together with the rest of the bezittingen in the rendementsgrondslag, and they enter the rendement with the debt percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). With a whole-year partner the drempel doubles, and a joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel).

### Box 3 tarief

The box 3 rate for 2022 is 31% (art. 2.13, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The rate applies to the voordeel uit sparen en beleggen (the box 3 return) under both methods (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rate is the same as in 2021, while 2020 used 30% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Peildatum

The values in the Wet rechtsherstel calculation are those at 1 January, the peildatum (reference date). Art. 3 defines the rendement with the banktegoeden (bank balances), the overige bezittingen (other assets) and the schulden at 1 January (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The exemption for green investments also never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Rendementsklasse percentages

For 2022 the return classes in art. 5.2 lid 1 are klasse I at -0,01% and klasse II at 5,53% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). Klasse I is the savings class and klasse II is the investments class. The old method mixes the two classes per band, and the Belastingdienst prints the band outcomes as averages of 1,818%, 4,366% and 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Band 1

The first band covers the part of the grondslag sparen en beleggen up to € 50.650. The statute writes the upper limit as "niet meer dan € 50.650" and the Belastingdienst page writes "tot € 50.651". Both describe the same band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). In this band the fictitious return is 67% klasse I and 33% klasse II, printed as an average of 1,818% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The parts of the grondslag in the three bands are each multiplied by their band percentage, and the parts are added to give the old-method voordeel.

### Band 2

The second band runs from € 50.650 to € 962.350 in the statute. The Belastingdienst page writes € 50.651 to € 962.351 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The mix is 21% klasse I and 79% klasse II, an average of 4,366% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Band 3

Above € 962.350 the whole amount is klasse II, so 0% klasse I and 100% klasse II, and the percentage is 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Wet rechtsherstel box 3, issue and entry

The Wet rechtsherstel box 3 is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, Kamerstuk 36 203, issued on 27 December 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 7 puts the law in force on the day after the issue date of the Staatsblad, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017, so it is in force on 28 December 2022. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). Section 7 records this conflict.

The law reacts to the Kerstarrest (Christmas ruling), Hoge Raad 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The memorie van toelichting (explanatory memorandum to the bill) describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM", because the forfaitaire system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Forfaits are allowed, but they must approach reality as closely as possible (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The MvT says the legislator did not choose a levy on the actual return for the rechtsherstel, but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html).

### Rechtsherstel category percentages 2022

For 2022 the operative category percentages for the recalculation are 0,00% for banktegoeden, 5,53% for overige bezittingen and 2,28% for schulden (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend).

The law as published printed 0,01% in column I and 2,46% in column III. Art. 4 orders the 2022 percentages of columns I and III to be replaced within two months after 2022 by ministerial regulation on DNB data for January to November 2022. The Belastingdienst percentages page gives the replaced, operative values (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend). Section 7 records this conflict.

The calculation under art. 3 runs through these amounts (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). First the rendement: column I x the banktegoeden plus column II x the overige bezittingen minus column III x the schulden at 1 January. Then the rendementsgrondslag: the bezittingen minus the schulden above the drempel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The effective rendementspercentage is the rendement divided by the rendementsgrondslag. The voordeel is that percentage times the grondslag sparen en beleggen, which is the rendementsgrondslag after the heffingsvrij vermogen (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen). Art. 2 sets the voordeel at the outcome of art. 3, not below nil. Art. 3 lid 4 makes whole-year partners use the joint figures (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 3 is given in the checked sources with column III x schulden, while only the debts above the drempel count in box 3. Section 7 records this wording difference.

### Rechtsherstel scope 2022

Art. 1 lid 2 of the Wet rechtsherstel makes the law apply to IB/PVV assessments (income tax and national insurance contributions assessments) for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 that were not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). For 2022 the law applies to all IB/PVV assessments for 2022, without the condition that the assessment was not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 1 lid 3 adds that the law applies only where it leads to a lower voordeel (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).

Art. 5 lets the extra persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) follow the partners' chosen division of specific care costs or gifts, with an ambtshalve vermindering (a reduction by the tax office on its own initiative) request for another split. Art. 6 recomputes the double-taxation relief (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Mass objectors get rechtsherstel for 2017 to 2020, as the memorie van toelichting states (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html).

### Most favourable method

The Belastingdienst computes the box 3 income under the old band method and under the category method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rule also holds when the taxpayer already had rechtsherstel for the period 2017 to 2022: "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." That means: even if you had rechtsherstel for the period 2017 to 2022, we apply the most favourable method (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rate on either outcome is 31% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen

Green investments (groene beleggingen) are not counted as bezittingen up to € 61.215 in 2022, or € 122.430 with a whole-year partner (art. 5.13, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_). The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The 2023 text of art. 5.13 lid 2 applies the exemption first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The checked sources do not state that ordering rule for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Korting groene beleggingen

Art. 8.19 lid 2 gives a korting voor groene beleggingen (tax credit for green investments) of 0,7% of the exempt amount on the peildatum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The rate is 0,7% for 2020 to 2024, so also for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The Belastingdienst computes the credit automatically, and it is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen).

### Actual-return reporting 2022

For 2022 the actual return (werkelijk rendement) can be reported on the online form "Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven" in Mijn Belastingdienst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement). For 2021 to 2024 the form may always be filed without a letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call), and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement). The pages state no deadline (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen). From 2025 the report goes in the income tax return itself (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).

The actual return is income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen is applied: "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen." That means the Hoge Raad has ruled that the heffingsvrij vermogen may not be taken into account there. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value (the official property valuation) and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional, and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).

The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 (counter-evidence scheme) is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195, in force on 19 July 2025 by the besluit of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html). Art. IV puts art. 6a in the Wet rechtsherstel with the same form and six-week rule for further documents, working back to 1 January 2017, so it also covers the 2022 rechtsherstel (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html). Hoge Raad 6 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705) held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet box 3 still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

All examples use invented amounts and only the 2022 figures from section 2. The asset and debt values are the values on 1 January 2022 that the Wet rechtsherstel calculation uses.

**Calculated example 1. Single person, mixed portfolio, old method against category method.**

Facts. Banktegoeden € 80.000. Overige bezittingen € 100.000. Schulden € 0. No fiscal partner.

Old band method.
1. Bezittingen = € 80.000 + € 100.000 = € 180.000. With no debts, the rendementsgrondslag is € 180.000.
2. Grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 180.000 - € 50.650 = € 129.350.
3. Band 1: € 50.650 × 1,818% = € 920,82.
4. Band 2: € 129.350 - € 50.650 = € 78.700. € 78.700 × 4,366% = € 3.436,04.
5. Voordeel = € 920,82 + € 3.436,04 = € 4.356,86.
6. Belasting = € 4.356,86 × 31% = € 4.356,86 × 0,31 = € 1.350,63.

Category method under the Wet rechtsherstel.
1. Rendement = € 80.000 × 0,00% + € 100.000 × 5,53% - € 0 × 2,28% = € 0 + € 5.530,00 - € 0 = € 5.530,00.
2. Rendementsgrondslag = € 180.000.
3. Effective rendementspercentage = € 5.530,00 / € 180.000 = 3,072222%.
4. Voordeel = 3,072222% × € 129.350 = € 3.973,92. Control sum: € 129.350 / € 180.000 = 71,861111%, and € 5.530,00 × 71,861111% = € 3.973,92.
5. Belasting = € 3.973,92 × 0,31 = € 1.231,92.

Most favourable method. € 3.973,92 is lower than € 4.356,86, so the category method applies and the box 3 tax is € 1.231,92 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

**Calculated example 2. Single person, savings only.**

Facts. Banktegoeden € 200.000. No overige bezittingen, no schulden.

Old band method. Grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 200.000 - € 50.650 = € 149.350. Band 1: € 50.650 × 1,818% = € 920,82. Band 2: € 149.350 - € 50.650 = € 98.700, and € 98.700 × 4,366% = € 4.309,24. Voordeel = € 920,82 + € 4.309,24 = € 5.230,06. Belasting = € 5.230,06 × 0,31 = € 1.621,32.

Category method. Rendement = € 200.000 × 0,00% = € 0. Voordeel = € 0. Belasting = € 0.

Most favourable method. The category method gives € 0 against € 5.230,06 under the old method, so the category method applies and the box 3 tax is € 0.

**Calculated example 3. Whole-year partners, green investments and debts.**

Facts. Banktegoeden € 200.000. Groene beleggingen € 70.000. Schulden € 12.000. Whole-year fiscal partners.

Green exemption. € 70.000 is below € 122.430, so the full € 70.000 of green investments is not counted as bezittingen.
Schuldendrempel. Only the debts above € 6.400 count, so € 12.000 - € 6.400 = € 5.600.
Bezittingen = € 200.000. Rendementsgrondslag = € 200.000 - € 5.600 = € 194.400.
Grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 194.400 - € 101.300 = € 93.100.

Old band method. Band 1: € 50.650 × 1,818% = € 920,82. Band 2: € 93.100 - € 50.650 = € 42.450, and € 42.450 × 4,366% = € 1.853,37. Voordeel = € 920,82 + € 1.853,37 = € 2.774,19. Belasting = € 2.774,19 × 0,31 = € 860,00.

Category method. Rendement = € 200.000 × 0,00% + € 0 × 5,53% - € 5.600 × 2,28% = € 0 + € 0 - € 127,68 = -€ 127,68. Art. 2 sets the voordeel not below nil, so the voordeel is € 0 and the box 3 tax is € 0.

Korting groene beleggingen. 0,7% × € 70.000 = € 490. The credit is split between the partners in the same ratio as the green balance. If the balance is held 50/50, each partner's korting is € 245.

Most favourable method. The category method gives € 0 against € 2.774,19 under the old method, so the voordeel is € 0.

**Official example. Actual return of € 1.000 interest and a € 300 loss on shares.**

The Belastingdienst example for 2022: € 1.000 of interest received and shares that are down € 300 give an actual return of € 700 (€ 1.000 - € 300 = € 700) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement). A calculated link: if the taxpayer of Calculated example 1 reported an actual return of € 700 for 2022, the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount, so € 700 instead of the fictitious € 3.973,92 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 50.000 per person and € 100.000 with a fiscal partner in 2021, to € 50.650 and € 101.300 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Rendementsklasse I: 0,03% in 2021 to -0,01% in 2022. Rendementsklasse II: 5,69% in 2021 to 5,53% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Band 1: the upper limit moves from € 50.000 in 2021 (page: tot € 50.001) to € 50.650 in 2022 (page: tot € 50.651), and the average drops from 1,898% to 1,818% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Band 2: the limits move from € 50.000 and € 950.000 in 2021 (page: € 50.001 to € 950.001) to € 50.650 and € 962.350 in 2022 (page: € 50.651 to € 962.351), and the average drops from 4,501% to 4,366% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Band 3: the threshold moves from € 950.000 to € 962.350, and the percentage drops from 5,69% to 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Rechtsherstel category percentages: 0,01% for banktegoeden, 5,69% for overige bezittingen and 2,46% for schulden in 2021, to 0,00%, 5,53% and 2,28% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 60.429 and € 120.858 in 2021, to € 61.215 and € 122.430 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_).
- Box 3 tarief: 31% in 2021 and 31% in 2022, so unchanged. The 2020 rate was 30% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.200 and € 6.400 in 2021 and in 2022, so unchanged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_).
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% in 2021 and 0,7% in 2022, so unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- New in 2022: the Wet rechtsherstel box 3 of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, issued on 27 December 2022 and in force on 28 December 2022, retroactive to 1 January 2017. It governs the recalculation for the assessments for 2021 and 2022 and for the 2017 to 2020 assessments that were not irrevocable on 24 December 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- The Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021 (ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) falls at the end of 2021 and is the reason for the 2022 law. The MvT describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3).
- Most favourable method: for 2021 and 2022 the Belastingdienst computes the old method and the category method and applies the more favourable one, so no change between 2021 and 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Rechtsherstel scope: all IB/PVV assessments for 2021 and all IB/PVV assessments for 2022, so no change (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Actual-return reporting: the separate online form may be filed without a letter in 2021 to 2024, so 2022 follows the same route as 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- The 2022 box 3 return and the recalculation need the values at 1 January of the banktegoeden, the overige bezittingen and the schulden, because art. 3 of the Wet rechtsherstel uses the amounts at 1 January (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Only the debts above the schuldendrempel of € 3.200 count, or € 6.400 with a whole-year partner, and the Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel).
- Whole-year partners: heffingsvrij vermogen € 101.300, drempel € 6.400, and the partners divide the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s), and whole-year partners use the joint figures in the recalculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Green investments: record the balance on 1 January and the exemption applied. The korting of 0,7% of the exempt amount is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen).
- If the actual return was lower than the fictitious voordeel, the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst can be used for 2022, and for 2021 to 2024 the form may always be filed without a letter. Paper forms run via 0800 - 023 01 07 and 0800 - 0543 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen).
- The form and the return use the income and value changes of the year, netted, with a negative total at € 0, and without the heffingsvrij vermogen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement).
- From 2025 the actual-return report is made in the income tax return itself (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).
- The box 3 voordeel is part of the IB/PVV return for 2022 and is taxed at 31%, and the Belastingdienst applies the most favourable method to the definitive assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- The checked 2022 sources do not cover BV-specific box 3 rules or the treatment of a sole trader's ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) in box 3.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

The checked 2022 figures are all enacted and Verified, and the year's list of values records no open points. The points below still matter for 2022 work.

- UNVERIFIED. The claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return. The memorie van toelichting describes the Kerstarrest as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM" and says the legislator chose a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories instead (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html). The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11, so the ruling text could not be checked. What would settle it: the text of ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963 on hogeraad.nl.
- Conflict, settled by the statute text. The entry date of the Wet rechtsherstel box 3. Art. 7 of Stb. 2022, 533 gives the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking" (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The statute governs the legal date. The Check tag for this item is Corrected.
- Conflict, settled by art. 4 and the Belastingdienst page. The 2022 category percentages. The table in Stb. 2022, 533 as published prints 0,01% for column I and 2,46% for column III (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 4 orders these 2022 percentages to be replaced within two months after 2022 by ministerial regulation on DNB data for January to November 2022. The Belastingdienst percentages page gives the operative 2022 values: 0,00% for banktegoeden, 5,53% for overige bezittingen and 2,28% for schulden (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend). The replacement regulation itself is not in the checked sources.
- Wording difference for the debt component. Art. 3 as given in the checked sources describes the rendement as column I x banktegoeden plus column II x overige bezittingen minus column III x schulden (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The drempel page says only the part of the total debts above the drempel is deductible in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The two descriptions can give different outcomes when the debts are below or just above the drempel. What would settle it: the full text of art. 3 together with the Belastingdienst 2022 calculation page for the category method, which are not both in the checked sources.
