# BV en vennootschapsbelasting 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

The vennootschapsbelasting (corporate income tax, Vpb) for 2022 has a first bracket of 15% up to € 395.000 and 25,8% above it, read in the text valid on 1 January 2022, and these 2022 figures are enacted law. Two large switches in the same year matter for a BV owner, the generieke renteaftrekbeperking (generic limitation on interest deduction) falls from 30% to 20%, and verliesverrekening (loss set-off) becomes one year back with unlimited forward set-off, capped at € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000 per year. For the directeur-grootaandeelhouder (DGA, director and major shareholder) the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) normbedrag (standard amount) is € 48.000, and 2022 is the last year in which the comparable wage counts at 75%. This file also covers the dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax), the fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity), the innovatiebox (innovation box) and the switch from an eenmanszaak (sole proprietorship) to a BV.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Vpb-tarief (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
A BV established in the Netherlands is a taxpayer for the vennootschapsbelasting. In the text valid on 1 January 2022 the first € 395.000 of the belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) carries 15%. Above € 395.000 the tax is € 59.250 plus 25,8% of the part above that amount. Art. 22 is built as a column I amount, a column III amount and a column IV percentage, so the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag above the column I amount. Every year from 2020 to 2026 has its own series in the dated text, so the 2022 figures must be read in the 2022 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Boekjaar dat 1 januari 2022 of 1 januari 2023 overschrijdt (art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969)
A book year that starts before and ends on or after the date on which a change of art. 22 takes effect is split. The tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the number of days before the change, Y the number of days from the change, B all days of the book year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31, checked in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Two straddles concern this year. A book year that straddles 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changed again (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01). A book year that straddles 1 January 2023 is apportioned as well (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01). For the part from 1 January 2023 the new series is t/m € 200.000 at 19%, above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01).

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)
The saldo aan renten (net interest balance) is the interest costs on loans minus the interest income. In 2022 that saldo is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 20% of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000. The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021 and 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024. The Belastingdienst change pages state 24,5% for 2025 and 2026 and say that non-deductible interest carries forward. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Verliesverrekening (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)
For 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by beschikking (decision). From 2022 a loss can be set off against one preceding year and then against unlimited following years, but in each year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### Innovatiebox, breuk (art. 12b Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 7/H in the 2020 text and 9/H in the texts from 2021 to 2026, where H is the highest art. 22 rate. For 2022 that rate is 25,8%, so the effective rate on the advantage is 9%. The Belastingdienst page states that the effective vennootschapsbelasting rate for these profits is 9%. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovatiebox, forfait (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years, and who chooses the forfait, sets the qualifying advantage at 25% of the profit before art. 12b. The maximum is € 25.000, and the forfait applies only when that profit is positive. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Innovatiebox, voorwaarden en nexus (art. 12ba en 12bb Wet Vpb 1969)
A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring (R&D declaration). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and the net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000, in art. 12ba lid 2, the same in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Other taxpayers also need an access ticket under art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded under art. 12ba lid 6. Under art. 12bb the qualifying part is K/T, where K is the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel (threshold) of art. 12bc. The checked material does not give the art. 12bc amount or the full definition of T. See section 7. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### Deelnemingsvrijstelling (art. 13 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming (participation) out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2 creates a deelneming from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, and a connected body is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party. A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met. For btw a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer (entrepreneur) and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

### Fiscale eenheid (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Belastingdienst adds that the mother is a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie, or a comparable foreign form, that the daughter is a bv or nv, that the book years and profit rules match, and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. A subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only, under art. 15 lid 10. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Fiscale eenheid, beleidsbesluit 2022
The Besluit winstsplitsing, spoedreparatie en artikel 10a was published as Stcrt. 2022, 11028. The 2024 Verzamelbesluit names it as withdrawn. The text of the 2022 besluit was not read in the checked material, so its conditions for 2022 are not stated here. See section 7. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### Gebruikelijk loon, normbedrag (art. 12a lid 1 Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest), the wage is at least the highest of three amounts. They are the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment), the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and the normbedrag. The normbedrag for 2022 is € 48.000. It is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001, in lid 9 in 2020 to 2022 and in lid 7 from 2023. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen, including the car bijtelling (company car addition), and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Gebruikelijk loon, vergelijkbaar loon op 75%
For 2020 to 2022 the comparable wage counts at 75%, and 2022 is the last year on that basis. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 reads for 2020 to 2022 as follows. If the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the normbedrag. From 2023 the rule reads that if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 notes that a vaststellingsovereenkomst (agreement) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Gebruikelijk loon, ondergrens van € 5.000
The gebruikelijk loon rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. The condition sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.

### Startersaftrek gebruikelijk loon (art. 12a lid 3 en 4 Wet LB 1964)
For a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the statute for 2020 to 2022 sets the wage at least at 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if that is made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. The Belastingdienst states that the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and that an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use in 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the Handboek 2025 allows a starting company to set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. The 2022 WML amount and the 2022 starterspercentage are not in the checked material, so the 108% floor has no euro value here. See section 7. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Bijdrage Zvw voor een niet-verzekerde DGA
A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie (payroll records) and withholds loonheffing. A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. For him the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw, and instead withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For 2022 the FISIN page gives a bijdrage of 5,50% and a maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 59.706. Each FISIN page says that DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if he is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.

### Werkgeversheffing Zvw voor een verzekerde DGA
A DGA who is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw, paid by the BV. For 2022 the FISIN page gives 6,75%. The FISIN pages give 6,70% for 2020, 7,00% for 2021, 6,75% for 2022 and 6,68% for 2023. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

### Zvw-route voor een niet-verzekerde DGA
The route in the books is a correction point. The Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon (withholding on net wage) or by aanslag (assessment). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, says the BV withholds the bijdrage on the net wage. This file follows the Handboek for the payroll treatment. The Handboek 2025 prints 5,26% up to € 75.864, which is its own 2025 figure and not a 2022 value. The 2022 values are on the FISIN 2022 page. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Box 2-tarief (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
For 2022 the box 2 rate is a single 26,9%. Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor. The rate is read in the text valid on 1 January 2022. A DGA who receives a dividend from his BV pays this rate and credits the dividendbelasting that the BV withheld. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Dividendbelasting, tarief en brutering (art. 5 en art. 6 lid 1 Wet DB 1965)
The tax is 15% of the opbrengst (proceeds), in every version read from 2020 to 2025. Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company withholds. If the company bears the tax itself, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the opbrengst by 100/85. Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividendnota (dividend note) once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The Belastingdienst states that a deelnemingsdividend (participation dividend, holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Belastingdienst invites one. A beleggingsdividend (portfolio dividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, and bodies credit it in the aangifte Vpb. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/.

### Aangifte en betaling dividendbelasting
The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification decision) is applied. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Dividend stripping (art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965)
The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text already holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01.

### Inkoop van eigen aandelen
Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares. That also holds for an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Conditionele bronbelasting, tarief (art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021)
The tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version, in art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividendbelasting. With art. 22 that gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. The Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. For 2022 the rate on interest and royalties is therefore 25,8%, following the 2022 art. 22 top rate. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Conditionele bronbelasting, toepassingsbereik
The tax hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction, meaning a profit tax below 9% or a place on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, and abuse cases. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Jaarrekening, grootteklassen en indeling
A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. The statute in Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396 and 397 sets micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small at € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50 employees, and medium at € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250 employees. Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raises these amounts to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, and € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen, for book years from 1 January 2024 and optionally from 1 January 2023. The employee counts are unchanged. This file uses the pre-2024 amounts for 2022. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/.

### Jaarrekening, deponering (art. 394 Boek 2 BW)
The board deposits the accounts within eight days after vaststelling (adoption) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted. Ondernemersplein states that there is no extension and that late filing is a criminal offence. Ondernemersplein also states that from boekjaar 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. That last point is not a 2022 rule. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.

### Bewaartermijnen (art. 52 AWR, art. 34a, 28rl en 28sj Wet OB 1968)
Lichamen (bodies) are administratieplichtig (required to keep records) and keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. The seven year term is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Records on onroerende zaken (immovable property) are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten, under art. 34a Wet OB 1968. OSS records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction, under art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

### Aangifte Vpb, termijnen
A book year equal to the calendar year must be filed before 1 June of the next year. A broken book year must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending 31 December must be filed before 1 June, and a short book year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. These pages were read for the current filing seasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen.

### Uitstel aangifte Vpb
An online extension is only for a calendar book year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. For a broken book year, or for an extension of more than 5 months, a paper request is needed, filed within 5 months after the book year ends and with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Stakingsaftrek (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001)
Without the geruisloze omzetting facility a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays inkomstenbelasting on the stakingswinst (cessation profit), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The stakingsaftrek (cessation relief) is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra lijfrente (annuity) premium deduction applies. Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking (withdrawal) before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente, under art. 3.129. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/.

### Geruisloze omzetting (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The standard conditions in annex 1 of the Besluit of 24 October 2025, published as Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025, include the point that selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed to be part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, so it is a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for inkomstenbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. A contribution into an existing company is allowed only when it already runs a business in the same line, and a contribution into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. If the voorovereenkomst (pre-agreement) is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Belastingdienst says to request at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, to register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, and that the BV is a bv in oprichting (BV in incorporation) until the notarial deed. The Besluit of 24 October 2025 replaced the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, which was the text around 2022, and it lists more changed points, being 2.2.2.a for a rented-out business or profit right, 3.4 for the holding approval, 4.2.2 for letteraandelen and art. 14a transfers, 6.1 for the reserves to settle, 8 for the Zvw credit, 12 for the eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad, 13.1.2 for the incidental tax advantage, 13.2 for the S&O approval and 15.3. The 2010 text was not read. See section 7. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.

### Verzoek art. 3.65, termijn (KG:212:2022:1)
The inspector decides by beschikking (decision) that is open to bezwaar (objection) and that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties, under onderdeel 14.2 of the old Besluit. In the case the request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst (transfer profit) is reported for 2021, and then the 2021 assessment decides. The standpunt was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

### Btw-fiscale eenheid, beleidsbesluit
The new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. A btw fiscale eenheid under art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands. The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025". This is not a 2022 rule and is noted for context. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Vpb on € 60.000 and on € 500.000 (Calculated example)
Invented inputs. Profit € 60.000 for 2022.
Step 1: € 60.000 is below € 395.000, so the whole amount falls in the first bracket.
Step 2: 15% x € 60.000 = € 9.000,00.
Now the profit is € 500.000.
Step 3: the part above € 395.000 is € 500.000 minus € 395.000 = € 105.000.
Step 4: 25,8% x € 105.000 = € 27.090,00.
Step 5: the column III amount is 15% x € 395.000 = € 59.250,00.
Step 6: total tax = € 59.250,00 + € 27.090,00 = € 86.340,00.
Source for the rates: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 2: Book year from 1 July 2021 to 30 June 2022 (Calculated example)
Invented book year and invented profit of € 300.000 for the whole book year. Art. 31 applies because the book year starts before and ends after the change of art. 22 on 1 January 2022.
Step 1: X = 184 days (1 July 2021 to 31 December 2021), Y = 181 days (1 January 2022 to 30 June 2022), B = 365 days.
Step 2: To is the 2021 tax on € 300.000. The 2021 first bracket runs to € 245.000 at 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. The part above the bracket is € 300.000 minus € 245.000 = € 55.000. 25% x € 55.000 = € 13.750,00. To = € 36.750,00 + € 13.750,00 = € 50.500,00.
Step 3: Tn is the 2022 tax on € 300.000. € 300.000 is below € 395.000, so Tn = 15% x € 300.000 = € 45.000,00.
Step 4: (184/365) x € 50.500 = € 25.457,53 (rounded down). (181/365) x € 45.000 = € 22.315,07 (rounded).
Step 5: total tax = € 25.457,53 + € 22.315,07 = € 47.772,60.
Sources: 2021 rate https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, 2022 rate https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01, formula https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 3: Art. 15b interest limitation (Calculated example)
Invented: a gecorrigeerde winst of € 600.000 and a saldo aan renten of € 1.400.000.
Step 1: 20% x € 600.000 = € 120.000,00.
Step 2: the drempel is the higher of € 120.000,00 and € 1.000.000, so € 1.000.000.
Step 3: € 1.400.000 minus € 1.000.000 = € 400.000.
Step 4: € 400.000 of the saldo aan renten is not deductible in 2022. The Belastingdienst change pages state that non-deductible interest carries forward.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025.

### Example 4: Verliesverrekening (Calculated example)
Invented: a profit before set-off of € 2.400.000 for 2022 and an older loss of € 2.000.000.
Step 1: the per year ceiling is € 1.000.000 plus 50% x (€ 2.400.000 minus € 1.000.000).
Step 2: 50% x € 1.400.000 = € 700.000,00. Ceiling = € 1.000.000,00 + € 700.000,00 = € 1.700.000,00.
Step 3: € 1.700.000 of the € 2.000.000 loss is set off.
Step 4: belastbare winst = € 2.400.000 minus € 1.700.000 = € 700.000.
Step 5: remaining loss = € 2.000.000 minus € 1.700.000 = € 300.000. That amount goes forward, with the oldest losses first.
Carry-back part: with an invented 2022 loss of € 50.000 and a 2021 profit of € 80.000, the loss is set off against 2021 and leaves € 30.000 of 2021 profit.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 5: Innovatiebox (Calculated example)
Invented: a taxpayer that meets the conditions for the innovatiebox.
Breuk part of art. 12b, invented: a profit of € 600.000 before the innovatiebox, of which € 200.000 is a qualifying voordeel.
Step 1: the breuk is 9/H, and H is the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2022.
Step 2: the voordeel enters the belastbaar bedrag at (9/25,8) x € 200.000 = € 69.767,44 (rounded down).
Step 3: belastbaar bedrag = € 600.000 minus € 200.000 + € 69.767,44 = € 469.767,44.
Step 4: the part above € 395.000 is € 469.767,44 minus € 395.000 = € 74.767,44. 25,8% x € 74.767,44 = € 19.290,00 (rounded).
Step 5: Vpb = € 59.250,00 + € 19.290,00 = € 78.540,00.
Step 6: the profit that stays in the normal regime is € 600.000 minus € 200.000 = € 400.000. That already exceeds € 395.000, so the breuk amount is taxed at the top rate. The effective rate on the voordeel is then 9%, the rate that the Belastingdienst states: 9% x € 200.000,00 = € 18.000,00. That 9% follows from the breuk at the top rate and is not a separate rate.
Forfait part of art. 12bd, invented: the taxpayer chooses the forfait, the profit before art. 12b is € 80.000 and that profit is positive.
Step 7: 25% x € 80.000 = € 20.000,00, which is below the maximum of € 25.000.
Step 8: the voordeel of € 20.000 enters the belastbaar bedrag at (9/25,8) x € 20.000 = € 6.976,74 (rounded down).
Step 9: belastbaar bedrag = € 80.000 minus € 20.000 + € 6.976,74 = € 66.976,74.
Step 10: € 66.976,74 is below € 395.000, so Vpb = 15% x € 66.976,74 = € 10.046,51 (rounded down), which is € 10.047 when rounded to whole euros. The 9% is not a separate rate, the voordeel is taxed through the breuk in the belastbaar bedrag.
Step 11: if the profit before art. 12b were € 120.000, 25% x € 120.000 = € 30.000,00 and the forfait would be capped at € 25.000.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Example 6: Deelnemingsvrijstelling (Calculated example)
Invented: BV A holds 100% of BV B and receives a dividend of € 200.000. The holding of 100% is above the 5% drempel of art. 13 lid 2, so the voordeel from the deelneming is left out of the profit under art. 13 lid 1. No Vpb is due on the € 200.000. The payment is a deelnemingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder, so it is usually exempt from dividendbelasting withholding, and no return is needed unless the Belastingdienst invites one.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Example 7: Fiscale eenheid (Calculated example)
Invented: BV A has a profit of € 300.000 and BV B a loss of € 100.000, the 95% bezitseis and the other conditions for a fiscale eenheid are met, and the two form a fiscale eenheid.
Step 1: tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, so the combined result is € 300.000 minus € 100.000 = € 200.000.
Step 2: € 200.000 is below € 395.000, so Vpb = 15% x € 200.000 = € 30.000,00.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Example 8: Gebruikelijk loon (Calculated example)
Invented: the comparable wage is € 80.000, so 75% x € 80.000 = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies is € 120.000. The normbedrag is € 48.000.
Step 1: the main rule gives the highest of € 60.000,00, € 120.000 and € 48.000, so € 120.000.
Step 2: the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that this highest amount of € 120.000 exceeds 75% of the comparable wage of € 60.000,00.
Step 3: the escape applies and the wage becomes 75% of the comparable wage, that is 75% x € 80.000 = € 60.000,00, which is above the normbedrag of € 48.000, so the wage is € 60.000.
Second part: if the comparable wage were € 40.000, 75% x € 40.000 = € 30.000,00. The highest amount is at least the normbedrag of € 48.000, which exceeds € 30.000,00, so the escape applies. Under art. 12a lid 2 the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when the comparable wage is lower than the normbedrag. € 40.000 is lower than € 48.000, so the wage is € 40.000.
Third part: if the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000, the rule does not apply at all.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.

### Example 9: Startersaftrek (Calculated example)

### Example 10: Zvw for the DGA (Calculated example)
Invented wage of € 50.000.
Case A, an insured DGA: the BV pays the werkgeversheffing Zvw of 6,75%. 6,75% x € 50.000 = € 3.375,00.
Case B, an uninsured DGA: the BV withholds the bijdrage Zvw of 5,50% from the net wage and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. 5,50% x € 50.000 = € 2.750,00.
At an invented wage of € 70.000 the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 59.706 applies.
Case A: 6,75% x € 59.706 = € 4.030,16 (rounded).
Case B: 5,50% x € 59.706 = € 3.283,83.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Example 11: Box 2 and dividendbelasting (Calculated example)
Invented: the BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to its DGA.
Step 1: dividendbelasting 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000,00 withheld. Net received = € 85.000,00.
Step 2: box 2, 26,9% x € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.
Step 3: credit for the dividendbelasting withheld = € 15.000,00.
Step 4: additional inkomstenbelasting = € 26.900,00 minus € 15.000,00 = € 11.900,00.
Step 5: if the dividend is made available on an invented day of 15 June 2022, the dividendbelasting return and payment are due within 1 month after that day, so by 15 July 2022.
Brutering variant: if the BV bears the dividendbelasting and pays € 85.000 net, the opbrengst is € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000,00.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Example 12: Inkoop van eigen aandelen (Calculated example)
Invented: the buyback price is € 120.000 and the average paid-up capital on those shares is € 50.000.
Step 1: the difference is € 120.000 minus € 50.000 = € 70.000.
Step 2: dividendbelasting 15% x € 70.000 = € 10.500,00.
Step 3: if the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is grossed up by 100/85. € 70.000 x 100/85 = € 82.352,94 (rounded down). 15% x € 82.352,94 = € 12.352,94 (rounded down). The art. 4c facility for listed companies would switch the grossing off.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Example 13: Conditionele bronbelasting (Calculated example)
Invented: a Dutch BV pays € 400.000 of interest to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction. The rate is the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2022. 25,8% x € 400.000 = € 103.200,00.

### Example 14: Jaarrekening size class and deposit (Calculated example)
Invented first case: two consecutive balance dates with assets of € 400.000, net turnover of € 500.000 and 8 employees. The thresholds for a boekjaar before 2024 are used.
Step 1: micro needs at most € 350.000 assets (not met, € 400.000 is more), at most € 700.000 net turnover (met) and fewer than 10 employees (met).
Step 2: two of the three micro criteria are met on both dates, so the company is micro.
Invented second case: assets of € 8.000.000, net turnover of € 14.000.000 and 40 employees on both dates.
Step 3: micro is not met on assets or net turnover. Small is not met on assets (above € 6.000.000) or net turnover (above € 12.000.000). Medium is met on all three criteria, so the company is medium.
Deposit part: a boekjaar ending 31 December 2022 must be deposited at the latest twelve months after the boekjaar ends, so at the latest 31 December 2023, and within eight days after vaststelling.
Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.

### Example 15: Filing and extension dates (Calculated example)
A BV with a calendar boekjaar 2022 files its Vpb return before 1 June 2023. With an online extension, which applies only to a calendar boekjaar, the deadline moves to 1 November 2023, and the request runs from 1 March 2023 to 1 June 2023. A short boekjaar ending 31 December 2022 files before 1 June 2023, and a short boekjaar ending 30 September 2022 files before 1 April 2023. For a broken boekjaar the term is 5 months after its end, and a paper request within 5 months with reasons is needed for an extension.
Term note: a 2022 record is kept for seven years under art. 52 AWR. Onroerende zaken records run nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records ten years after the year of the transaction.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01.

### Example 16: Stakingsaftrek (Calculated example)
Invented: stakingswinst of € 120.000.
Step 1: the stakingsaftrek is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime.
Step 2: € 120.000 minus € 3.630 = € 116.370,00 that remains as stakingswinst.
An extra lijfrente premium deduction applies. No box 1 rate is computed here, because the checked material for this topic does not give the 2022 box 1 rates.

### Example 17: Geruisloze omzetting (Calculated example)
Invented: the fiscal book values of the contributed business total € 150.000 and the paid-up amount is € 200.000.
Step 1: the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values, so € 150.000.
Step 2: the rounding credit is at most 5% of the paid-up amount. 5% x € 200.000 = € 10.000,00, which is below the ceiling of € 30.000.
Counts: a sale of the shares within 3 years after the contribution is presumed part of a transfer plan unless the contributor proves otherwise, and the voorovereenkomst must be received within 9 months after the last full boekjaar for effect from the first day of the next boekjaar.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.

### Example 18: Request timing for art. 3.65, KG:212:2022:1 (Official example)
The known case: retroactivity to 1 January 2021 with the request received on 15 December 2021 and incorporation on 1 February 2022. The request was timely. The standpunt adds that a request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless overdrachtswinst is reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment decides. ## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

- Vpb first bracket: € 245.000 at 15% in 2021 moved to € 395.000 at 15% in 2022. Above the bracket: € 36.750 plus 25% in 2021 moved to € 59.250 plus 25,8% in 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b): 30% in 2021 moved to 20% in 2022. The drempel of € 1.000.000 is unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Verliesverrekening (art. 20): 2021 was one year back and six years forward, with the loss fixed by beschikking. From 2022 it is one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Art. 31 toerekening: the 2021 text states that a book year straddling 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changed again. The 2022 text states the same for a book year straddling 1 January 2023. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Gebruikelijk loon: the normbedrag moved from € 47.000 in 2021 to € 48.000 in 2022. The comparable wage stays at 75% in 2022, and 2022 is the last year on that basis. The € 5.000 floor is unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.
- Startersaftrek: a first use in 2021 could run 3 years. 2022 is the last year of first use, and the conditions for a 2022 start must still be met in 2024. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Zvw: the bijdrage moved from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022, the maximum bijdrage-inkomen from € 58.311 to € 59.706, and the werkgeversheffing from 7,00% to 6,75%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Box 2: 26,9% in 2021 and 26,9% in 2022, unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Dividendbelasting: 15% and a brutering of 100/85 in both years, unchanged. The 2021 values come from the version geldend 01-01-2020 t/m 31-12-2021, and the 2022 values from the 2022-01-01 version. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01.
- Conditionele bronbelasting: the rate follows the highest art. 22 rate, so 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Innovatiebox: the fraction 9/H, the forfait of 25% with a maximum of € 25.000 and the nexusvermenigvuldiger of 1,3 are the same in 2021 and 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Deelnemingsvrijstelling and fiscale eenheid: the 5% deelneming threshold and the 95% bezitseis are unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Jaarrekening size thresholds: unchanged in 2022, and stated as the values before boekjaar 2024. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.
- Stakingsaftrek: € 3.630 in both 2021 and 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Correction carried in this file: the dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3, and it can be read in the dated texts. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01.
- Recorded for completeness: the werkgeversheffing Zvw values for 2020 to 2023 are 6,70%, 7,00%, 6,75% and 6,68%, from the FISIN pages of each year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and the 2020, 2021 and 2023 equivalents.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record the DGA wage in the loonadministratie. The DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV withholds loonheffing. For an uninsured DGA record no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw, and record the bijdrage Zvw of 5,50% withheld on the net wage up to € 59.706 and paid through the aangifte loonheffingen. For an insured DGA record the werkgeversheffing Zvw of 6,75% paid by the BV. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Keep the evidence for the gebruikelijk loon test, including the comparable wage used for the 75% escape and the highest wage in the body and in connected bodies. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Record a dividend with the opbrengst, the 15% dividendbelasting and the dividendnota, and note the day the dividend is made available, because the return and payment are due within 1 month after that day. A fully exempt deelnemingsdividend usually needs no return unless the Belastingdienst invites one. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.
- File the Vpb return for a 2022 calendar book year before 1 June 2023. Request an online extension from 1 March to 1 June 2023, which runs to 1 November 2023. For a broken book year use the 5 month term and a paper request with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Keep the administratie for seven years, onroerende zaken btw records for nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.
- Deposit the jaarrekening within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as unadopted. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.
- After the switch from an eenmanszaak to a BV, use the new btw-identificatienummer with EU suppliers at once, and request a new loonheffingennummer when there are employees. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.
- For a fiscale eenheid the daughter files no separate return, and losses of one company can offset profits of another. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.
