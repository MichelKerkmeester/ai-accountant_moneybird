# BTW 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers VAT (btw) for a Dutch sole trader and the general VAT rules that also apply to a business with a BV. The fixed points for 2022 are the 21 percent standard rate, the 9 percent reduced rate for the goods and services in Tabel I, the nil rate for Tabel II, and the old kleineondernemersregeling (KOR, small businesses scheme) with its € 20.000 ceiling tested on the current calendar year. The 2022 rules described here were enacted. The corona deferral of tax debt closed for new debts on 31 maart 2022 and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 oktober 2022, and a few 2022 filing details could not be confirmed from a 2022 publication and are marked UNVERIFIED.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Standard, reduced and zero rates

Article 9 lid 1 of the Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968 sets the tax at 21 percent. Article 9 lid 2 sets 9 percent for the goods and services listed in Tabel I and nihil for those listed in Tabel II, in both cases only if the conditions set by algemene maatregel van bestuur are met. The identical text was read in the versions of 1 January 2020, 1 July 2021, 1 January 2022, 1 January 2023, 1 January 2024, 1 January 2025 and 1 January 2026, so 2022 uses 21 percent, 9 percent and the nil rate. A nil-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, while an exempt supply does not. [Wet OB art. 9, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).

### Tabel I goods at 9%

The 9 percent list for goods covers voedingsmiddelen (food), water, sierteeltproducten (horticultural products), geneesmiddelen en hulpmiddelen (medicines and aids), kunst, verzamelvoorwerpen en antiek (art, collectibles and antiques), and boeken en periodieken (books and periodicals). The Belastingdienst links the Besluit Omzetbelasting Toelichting Tabel 1 (BWBR0052086, 2026-01-01) as the explanation of the list. The current pages carry no year, so the dated post history of the list rests on the instruments named in this file. [Belastingdienst, goods at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw).

### Tabel I services at 9%

The 9 percent list for services covers the repair of bicycles, of shoes and leather goods and of clothing and household linen, hairdressers, certain work on homes, camping, access to cultural and recreational events and facilities, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, e-books comparable to a 9 percent physical publication, and access to news websites and apps unless they are mainly advertising, video or audible music. The page also names related services, including het opleveren van goederen, selling food in horeca, renting and repairing medical aids, lending books and lending art by the artist himself. [Belastingdienst, services at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/).

### Electronic publications and news access

Post b.21 of Tabel I applies from 1 January 2020 to the electronic supply or lending of publications and to access to news websites. The besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334 in the Toelichting Tabel I besluit settles that start date, so the 9 percent rate on qualifying electronic publications is part of the 2022 rules. [Besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html).

### Logies

Logies (accommodation) was in the 9 percent list in 2022. The Wet van 29 oktober 2025 (Stb. 2025, 339) removes post b.11 from Tabel I with effect from 1 januari 2026, so the rate goes to 21 percent from that later date, including for gas, electricity, water, sanitary, laundry and parking facilities tied to the logies, while a separately offered breakfast or pool and attraction access stays at 9 percent. That change does not apply to 2022. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html), [Belastingdienst, btw en logies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies).

### Zero rate conditions for an intra-EU goods supply

The zero rate is not an exemption. A zero-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, an exempt supply does not. For an intra-EU supply of goods the nil rate needs three conditions: proof from the records that the goods went to another EU country, a buyer with a valid VAT ID of another member state, and an opgaaf ICP filed on time, correctly and completely. A late or wrong ICP can cost the zero rate and draw a fine. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Exemptions (Wet OB art. 11 lid 1)

Article 11 lid 1 onderdelen a to w were read in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions with the same headings: immovable property supply and letting (with exceptions), care in institutions, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups (koepelvrijstelling), fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list adds beleggingsgoud (investment gold) and kantines, and states that the exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies carry no right to deduct input VAT. [Wet OB art. 11, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, vrijstellingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).

### KOR turnover ceiling

The KOR as it applied in 2022 comes from the Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling (Wet van 19 december 2018), whose Articles I and II entered into force on 1 januari 2020. Article 25 lid 1 applies to an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a vaste inrichting (fixed establishment) there. If turnover in the Netherlands in a calendar year is no more than € 20.000, the entrepreneur may choose the exemption for supplies in that and the following calendar years. [Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0041782), [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR turnover test year

Old Article 25 lid 1 tests de omzet in een kalenderjaar and no lid names the preceding calendar year. The 1 January 2020, 1 July 2021, 1 January 2022, 1 January 2023 and 1 January 2024 versions were checked and none contains a preceding-year test. A prior-year overrun mattered before 2025 only through the three-year lock in lid 8. From 1 January 2025 the prior year counts, because art. 25a lid 5 requires the prior-year turnover in the melding and lid 9 bars the year after an overrun. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [art. 25a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### KOR notice, minimum participation and re-entry

Article 25 lid 6 requires the notice uiterlijk vier weken voorafgaand aan het belastingtijdvak. The inspector refuses the option by beschikking when the conditions will plausibly not be met. Lid 7 keeps participation in place for at least three years after the start, and re-entry after a revocation is possible only three years later. Lid 8 provides that on exceeding the ceiling the exemption ends from the crossing supply onward, and that re-entry is barred for three years after the overrun. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR invoice and deduction rule

Article 25 lid 4 states that a KOR participant does not deduct input VAT and does not charge VAT on the invoice. This is the mirror image of the exemption and it is the reason the KOR is only attractive when little input VAT is at stake. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR special conditions from the 2020 handbook

The Handboek Ondernemen 2020 adds practical KOR conditions. Legal persons (bv, nv, stichting, vereniging) may join the KOR. A participant has no option for taxed letting under the KOR. Herziening (adjustment) stays out when the total is less than € 500 a year for investment goods used before that year. Deregistration is possible only per the first day of an aangiftetijdvak. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR registration threshold without notice

Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740 allows an entrepreneur who is not yet registered and has a jaaromzet of at most € 1.800 to apply the KOR without the usual notice, retroactive to 1 januari 2020. From 1 januari 2025 art. 25b Wet OB and art. 25 Uitvoeringsbeschikking omzetbelasting 1968 set the amount and raise it from € 1.800 to € 2.200 (besluit van 21 november 2024, nr. 2024-29602). Art. 25b also requires that the entrepreneur has not registered with the inspector or the KVK and is not obliged to, and does not use the exemption in other member states. [Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html), [Besluit van 21 november 2024, nr. 2024-29602](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html), [art. 25b, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### VAT return periods

Quarterly filing is the usual period. Monthly or yearly filing can be requested by letter to the belastingkantoor. The Belastingdienst replies within 6 weeks and the change starts at the start of the new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

### Monthly return trigger

The Handboek Ondernemen 2020 states that a business that usually pays more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter has a one-month period. The Handboek Ondernemen 2026 and the current period-change page no longer state that trigger, and no 2022 text was found, so whether the trigger applied in 2022 is not established. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

### Filing and payment term

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states that a period return is filed binnen een door de inspecteur gestelde termijn van ten minste een maand na het einde van het tijdvak. AWR art. 19 lid 1 states that payment is due binnen één maand na het einde van dat tijdvak. The same text appears in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions, so the 2022 term is one month after the period. [AWR art. 10 and art. 19, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Return deadlines for period returns and the yearly return

The Belastingdienst states that monthly and quarterly returns must be filed uiterlijk op de laatste dag van de maand die volgt op het kwartaal of de maand. The yearly return is due vóór 1 april van het eerstvolgende jaar, which is the same last day as 31 maart. The aangiftedatum is the day the return is received, and filing and payment share one date. Extension is granted only als er sprake is van een calamiteit. [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Published 2022 return-date table

No 2022 version of the Belastingdienst return-date table was found. The rule that fixes the dates is the last day of the month following the period for period returns and 31 maart for the yearly return. A dated 2022 table would settle the exact scheduled dates. [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Yearly return VAT limit

The condition reads less than € 1.883 VAT a year. The amount appears in the Handboek Ondernemen 2020 and the Handboek Ondernemen 2026, and the current Belastingdienst page names no year and says nothing on indexation. No 2022 text was found, so the amount for 2022 is UNVERIFIED. [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Other yearly return conditions

The yearly return also requires that there is no vergunning artikel 23, that the business is a natural person or a partnership of natural persons only (maatschap or vof), and that each of four intra-EU categories stays below € 10.000 a year voor elk van deze activiteiten afzonderlijk: intracommunautaire leveringen, intracommunautaire diensten, intracommunautaire verwervingen and afgenomen intracommunautaire diensten. The conditions are identical in the Handboek Ondernemen 2020 and the Handboek Ondernemen 2026. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Suppletie correction window and timing

UBOB art. 15 in the version valid for 2022 requires correction of returns of de afgelopen vijf kalenderjaren, before the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector knows, zo spoedig mogelijk op de door de inspecteur aangegeven wijze. Failure to correct is an overtreding, and the power to impose a vergrijpboete lapses five years after the end of the year. The later change that adds an eight-week outer limit and drops zo spoedig mogelijk entered into force on 1 januari 2025, so it is not the 2022 rule. [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024, Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html), [UBOB art. 15a, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01).

### Suppletie amount boundary

The amount boundary for correcting a small error in the next return is not established for 2022. The Handboek Ondernemen 2020 says a correctie van minder dan € 1.000 can go in the next return, the Handboek Ondernemen 2026 says een correctie van € 1.000 of minder, and the year words for 2021 to 2025 stay open. The current page states that a correction of at most € 1000 can go in the next return, that more than € 1.000 requires the form within 8 weeks, and that corrections are possible up to and including 5 years after the year. [Belastingdienst, aangifte corrigeren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### EU distance-sales threshold and option

From 1 July 2021 the € 10.000 threshold applies to the combined qualifying distance sales of goods and digital services, only for a supplier established in one member state, and only when the total in het lopende kalenderjaar niet hoger is dan € 10 000 and the previous calendar year also stayed within it. Over the threshold, foreign VAT applies from the moment of overrun, and Dutch VAT can resume vanaf 1 januari van het jaar daarna after a full calendar year at or below € 10.000. The goods rule covers goods sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country where the supplier arranges or pays for the transport, excluding margin goods, new or nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital services to consumers count toward the threshold and are measured excluding VAT. Below the threshold the supplier may opt for foreign VAT and the choice holds minimaal 2 jaar. There is no threshold when the supplier has a vaste inrichting in another EU country, and a warehouse alone is not one. The Unieregeling is optional and gives one return per quarter for all EU distance sales. Foreign input VAT cannot be reclaimed through it. [Wet OB art. 6k, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Besluit of 28 May 2021, Stb. 2021, 261](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, btw bij goederen naar EU-particulieren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

### OSS returns, corrections and records

The Unieregeling melding is made once per quarter in the month after the quarter, by the last day of that month, so the Q3 melding is made in oktober by 31 oktober. A nil melding is also required. The scheme can be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For a first supply the registration must arrive op de 10e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand van de levering. Three consecutive quarters without a melding give exclusion for 2 years. A melding cannot be resubmitted, corrections go in a later melding uiterlijk binnen 3 jaar after the original due date, and records are kept 10 years. Amounts are in euro, converted at the ECB rate of the last day of the quarter, and paid in one payment for all states. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

### Invoerregeling cap

The Invoerregeling is filed monthly and applies only up to € 150 per zending. Three consecutive missed months give an exclusion for 2 years. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

### EU KOR

The EU KOR was not in force in 2022. The Wet van 10 juli 2023 (Stb. 2023, 278) brings it into force on 1 januari 2025, with a transition provision from 1 oktober 2024. It therefore plays no part in the 2022 rules. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html).

### Opgaaf ICP deadline and period rules

The opgaaf ICP reports intra-EU supplies of goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. Its total equals rubriek 3b of the return for the same period. Goods go in the period of the invoice date, services in the period the service was supplied. No ICP is filed for a period with nothing to report, corrections go in the next ICP, and the form allows at most 100 lines per rubriek. The deadline for 2022 was not found. A later guide states monthly ICP reporting for goods, quarterly only when goods supplies stay at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and in each of the previous 4 quarters, a domestic deadline of binnen 1 maand na afloop van het gekozen tijdvak, a tweemaandelijks option, and 31 januari of the next year for both the quarterly Q4 ICP and the yearly ICP. Those period details come from a later guide, so their 2022 applicability is not separately established. Call-off stock conditions in that guide are an agreement with a known buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, own transport, no establishment in the arrival state, supply within 12 months of arrival, a register, and listing per buyer in the ICP of the period the transport began. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, tijdvak opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).

### Reverse-charge electronics threshold

The verleggingsregeling (reverse-charge scheme) of art. 12 Wet OB shifts the levy from the supplier to the customer in the cases listed in art. 12 lid 2 and 3 and in the AMvB cases of lid 5, ten einde voor de inning daarvan meer waarborgen te scheppen. Afdeling E of the Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968 holds arts. 24b, 24ba and 24bb. Art. 24b covers onderaanneming and staff lending in construction and shipbuilding, with the invoice note btw verlegd. Art. 24ba covers further cases including certain immovable property, gold, emission rights, gas and electricity certificates and electronics. Art. 24bb covers old materials, waste and scrap. For mobile phones, chips before end product, game consoles, tablets and laptops the rule applies only voor een totaalbedrag van € 10 000 of meer per levering per soort goed. The supplier invoices with btw verlegd, the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate, and blijft wel verantwoordelijk for the invoice. The customer reports the VAT in rubriek 2 for domestic reverse charges or rubriek 4 for supplies from abroad and deducts it when the goods or services are used for a taxed activity. For onderaanneming the exceptions are work carried out for more than half in the own workshop, staff lent for such work, and subordinate work on sold goods, and disputes go to the main contractor's belastingkantoor. [Wet OB art. 12, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [UBOB Afdeling E, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, wanneer btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [Belastingdienst, hoe werkt btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen), [Belastingdienst, uitzonderingen onderaanneming en personeel uitlenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/bij_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen/uitzonderingen_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen).

### Invoice issue date

An invoice must be issued for every supply to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. A private customer normally needs no invoice, with exceptions on a separate page. The invoice is issued by de 15e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand waarin u hebt geleverd. The handbook example is a supply on 10 April with the invoice due by 15 May. VAT is due at the latest in the period in which the customer paid. [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Invoice content

A full invoice contains the full name and address of both parties (a trade name is allowed when it is registered with the address at the KVK, and a PO box alone is not enough), the supplier's VAT ID with NL, the KVK number when registered, the invoice date, a unique consecutive number, the kind and quantity of the goods or the kind and scope of the services, the date of supply or prepayment, and the amounts excluding VAT per rate with the unit price, the rate and the VAT amount. A fiscal unity uses the VAT ID of the supplying part. A digital invoice needs customer agreement and assurance of de echtheid van herkomst, de inhoud en de leesbaarheid, and there is no general obligation to send digital invoices. [Belastingdienst, factuureisen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/).

### Simplified invoice threshold

A simplified invoice may be used at most € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice. It contains the issue date, name and address, the goods or services, the VAT or the data to calculate it, and for a correction the reference to the original invoice. It cannot be used for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or reverse-charged supplies in a state where the supplier is not established. [Belastingdienst, vereenvoudigde factuur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur).

### Compulsory cash accounting

The kasstelsel (cash accounting system) is compulsory for listed trades such as shops, market traders, hairdressers and horeca and others, and can be requested when more than 80 percent of turnover goes to consumers. The 2026 handbook list adds webshops. Under the kasstelsel VAT is accounted for when the customer pays. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Last return of the year

Private use by the entrepreneur includes use by family. There are two situations, mixed use (a car, a business computer, painting a partly private building) and withdrawal of goods for exclusively private use. Staff private use often limits deduction under the BUA. The last return of the year (Q4 or December) settles private use of goods and services, the car, the pro rata of taxed and exempt turnover, and personnel provisions, gifts and business gifts above € 227. The 2020 and 2026 handbooks also name gas, water and electricity, fictieve diensten and herzieningen. A last return that was already filed is corrected with a Suppletie btw. [Belastingdienst, privégebruik](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Belastingdienst, laatste btw-aangifte van het jaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Business car private use

Besluit Omzetbelasting, heffing privégebruik auto en toepassing BUA (25 juni 2020, geldend van 04-07-2020 t/m heden) replaces the older car decision. Without records the private use is 2,7% van de catalogusprijs (inclusief btw en bpm), for cars in the business assets and cars obtained op een andere wijze, capped at the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 deel van de btw on purchase. The rate is 1,5 percent when no purchase VAT could be deducted, and 1,5 percent also applies na afloop van het vierde jaar volgende op het jaar waarin de ondernemer de auto is gaan gebruiken. The handbooks describe that moment as more than 5 years in use, with the year of purchase counting as a full year. Commuting is private use, exempt use reduces the amount proportionally, a part year is pro rata, and the amount goes in the last return of the year. [Besluit privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst, privégebruik auto van de zaak](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### BUA threshold and exceptions

The Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968 (BUA, one version geldend van 23-02-2019 t/m heden) excludes input VAT deduction for het voeren van een zekere staat, business gifts and other gifts, staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport, with making a car available excluded from that list. Art. 1 lid 3 carve-outs cover food and drink for staff, licensed besloten busvervoer for commuting, a commuting bicycle of at most € 749 inclusief omzetbelasting (no bicycle in the year or two before, and not 50 percent or more of travel days otherwise covered), and outplacement. Art. 2 limits the exclusion to the VAT owed on a charged consideration. Art. 3 handles staff meals charged below cost plus 25 percent, where deduction is excluded tot 9 percent van het verschil. Art. 4 lid 1 keeps provisions to the same person whose total cost niet meer bedraagt dan € 227 per boekjaar outside the exclusion. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Belastingdienst, drempelbedrag personeelsvoorzieningen, giften en relatiegeschenken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### BUA own contribution

An own contribution by the recipient could reduce the amount tested against the € 227 threshold under the explanation that applied in 2022. Besluit van 6 oktober 2023, nr. 2023-20692 (Stcrt. 2023, 28124) ends that website explanation met ingang van 1 januari 2024. The change therefore does not alter the 2022 position. The 2026 handbook adds that gifts are hit when the recipient would have less than 30 percent deduction. [Besluit van 6 oktober 2023, Stcrt. 2023, 28124](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Corona special deferral and repayment

The corona bijzonder uitstel (special deferral) covered tax debts built up van 12 maart 2020 tot en met 31 maart 2022. The built-up debt is repaid van 1 oktober 2022 tot 1 oktober 2027, with the first term due on 31 oktober 2022 and 60 monthly terms. Quarterly payment is possible, and one pause of up to 6 months or 2 quarters is allowed. Extension to 84 months is available only for a scheme of at least € 10.000. New tax debts had to be paid on time again from 1 april 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

### General B2B e-invoicing duty

No general B2B e-invoicing duty for 2022 could be verified. The current government guidance states that suppliers to central government must send e-invoices and that municipalities, provinces and water boards may require them, with Peppol named as the network. That guidance does not settle the 2022 general B2B position. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

**Calculated example, VAT rate.** A taxable sale of € 100 excluding VAT.

1. At 21%, € 100 × 0,21 = € 21 VAT, so the invoice total is € 100 + € 21 = € 121.
2. If the supply qualifies for 9%, € 100 × 0,09 = € 9 VAT, so the invoice total is € 100 + € 9 = € 109.
3. If a supply qualifies for the zero rate, the VAT charged is nihil. That does not by itself make the supply exempt. [Wet OB art. 9, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

**Calculated example, KOR ceiling.** Assume an eligible business has € 19.000 of Dutch turnover in the current calendar year.

1. The ceiling is € 20.000.
2. € 20.000 - € 19.000 = € 1.000 of room remains under the ceiling.
3. If a later supply takes turnover over the ceiling, the exemption ends from the crossing supply onward and re-entry is barred for three years after the overrun. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

**Calculated example, yearly return limit.** Assume a business owes € 1.500 VAT for the year and the less-than-€ 1.883 limit applies.

1. € 1.883 - € 1.500 = € 383.
2. € 1.500 is below the limit. This is only the arithmetic against the stated amount. The € 1.883 limit is not verified specifically for 2022, and the other yearly return conditions still apply. [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

**Calculated example, period return deadline.** Assume a quarterly period ends on 30 June.

1. The term is one month after the end of the period.
2. The 31st day of the following month is 31 July, which is the deadline. [AWR art. 10 and art. 19, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/).

**Calculated example, EU distance sales.** Assume qualifying sales of € 7.000 are followed by € 4.000 in the relevant combined total.

1. € 7.000 + € 4.000 = € 11.000.
2. € 11.000 - € 10.000 = € 1.000 above the € 10.000 threshold.
3. The VAT treatment changes from the supply that causes the overrun, subject to the stated rules. [Besluit of 28 May 2021, Stb. 2021, 261](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, btw bij goederen naar EU-particulieren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren).

**Calculated example, reverse charge.** Assume a domestic reverse-charged purchase of € 1.000 excluding VAT, taxed at 21%.

1. The VAT to report is € 1.000 × 0,21 = € 210.
2. If the purchase is fully used for taxable business activity, the same € 210 may be deducted.
3. If it is not fully used for taxable activity, the deduction is limited accordingly. [Wet OB art. 12, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [Belastingdienst, hoe werkt btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).

**Official example, invoice timing.** The handbook gives a supply made on 10 April with the invoice due by 15 May, the 15th day of the month following the month of supply. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

**Calculated example, business car.** Assume a business car with a catalogue price of € 20.000, no private-use records and a full year of use.

1. At 2,7%, € 20.000 × 0,027 = € 540.
2. The result is then compared with the applicable cap based on the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT.
3. If the 1,5% rate applies instead, € 20.000 × 0,015 = € 300. [Besluit privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).

**Calculated example, BUA staff meal.** Assume the difference between the staff meal cost and the amount charged is € 100 and the below-cost-plus-25-percent condition applies.

1. The exclusion is up to 9 percent of the difference.
2. € 100 × 0,09 = € 9. [BUA art. 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23).

**Official example, corona repayment.** The Belastingdienst gives published amounts for the repayment scheme: a debt of € 15.000 gives € 271 a month, a debt of € 25.000 gives € 451 a month, and after paying € 10.000 the amount is lowered to € 271, a saving of about € 828. These amounts are copied from the source and are not recalculated here. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

**Calculated example, corona repayment by instalment count.** Assume an eligible debt of € 12.000 repaid over the stated 60 monthly terms.

1. € 12.000 ÷ 60 = € 200 per month.
2. This is a plain division by the stated instalment count. It ignores interest and any published recalculation such as the official example above. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

- The corona bijzonder uitstel ran through 2021 as an open deferral. In 2022 the build-up window closed on 31 maart 2022, new tax debts had to be paid on time again from 1 april 2022, and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 oktober 2022 with the first term due on 31 oktober 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).
- The rates did not change. The standard rate stayed at 21%, the reduced rate stayed at 9% for Tabel I and the zero rate stayed nihil for Tabel II. Logies stayed at 9%. [Wet OB, version 1 July 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html).
- The KOR rules did not change. The ceiling stayed at € 20.000, the test stayed on the current calendar year only, and the notice, three-year participation and three-year re-entry rules stayed the same. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 July 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).
- The suppletie timing did not change. The rule remained zo spoedig mogelijk under the 2020 text of UBOB art. 15. The eight-week outer limit only arrived on 1 januari 2025. [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024, Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html).
- The EU distance-sales threshold of € 10.000 was already in force from 1 juli 2021 and stayed unchanged in 2022. MOSS had already lapsed on 1 juli 2021 and the eenloketsysteem had taken its place. [Besluit of 28 May 2021, Stb. 2021, 261](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT rate used, the amounts per rate, VAT charged and input VAT deducted. Issue invoices to entrepreneurs and to legal persons that are not entrepreneurs by the 15th day of the month after the month of supply, and retain the invoice details the Belastingdienst requires. [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Belastingdienst, factuureisen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).
- Record reverse-charged purchases and sales with the btw verlegd note, the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate. Report domestic reverse charges in rubriek 2 and reverse charges from abroad in rubriek 4, and deduct input VAT only where the taxable use permits it. [Belastingdienst, hoe werkt btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).
- Reconcile intra-EU goods and services in the return and the opgaaf ICP. The ICP total equals rubriek 3b for the same period. Retain evidence for the zero rate, including transport records and the buyer's VAT ID. The 2022 ICP deadline was not established in the checked material. [Belastingdienst, opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).
- Track whether KOR turnover stays within the current-year ceiling of € 20.000. A KOR participant does not charge VAT and does not deduct input VAT, so the choice needs to be made with the input VAT position in view. Keep records that support the Dutch turnover test and the date a supply crosses the ceiling, if that occurs. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).
- File and pay by the deadline for the assigned period, which is the last day of the month following the period for monthly and quarterly filers. The yearly return is due by 31 maart of the following year. If a filed return needs correction, use the suppletie process that applied in 2022. The exact 2022 small-correction boundary is unknown. [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).
- In the last return of the year, settle private use, the business car, the pro rata of taxed and exempt turnover, personnel provisions and the gifts above € 227. If the last return has already been filed, use a Suppletie btw. [Belastingdienst, laatste btw-aangifte van het jaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar).
- For OSS transactions, file the separate quarterly melding, including a nil melding, and keep the specified records for 10 years. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through the OSS melding. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
- For a debt built up under the corona bijzonder uitstel, record the repayment terms in the 60-month schedule from 1 oktober 2022 to 1 oktober 2027 and the first term due on 31 oktober 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- **Yearly return VAT limit for 2022:** the amount of less than € 1.883 a year is stated in the Handboek Ondernemen 2020 and the Handboek Ondernemen 2026, and the current page names no year. No 2022 text was found, so the 2022 wording is UNVERIFIED. A 2022 Handboek Ondernemen would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).
- **Other yearly return conditions for 2022:** the conditions (no vergunning artikel 23, natural person or partnership of natural persons only, less than € 10.000 a year per intra-EU category) are identical in the 2020 and 2026 handbooks. No 2022 handbook text was found, so the 2022 wording of the whole condition set is not confirmed from a 2022 publication. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).
- **Monthly return trigger:** the Handboek Ondernemen 2020 gives more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter as a monthly-return trigger. The Handboek Ondernemen 2026 and the current period-change page no longer state it. Whether it applied in 2022 is not established. A 2022 handbook or a dated administrative instruction would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).
- **Published 2022 return-date table:** no 2022 table was found, so the scheduled 2022 dates are UNVERIFIED. An archived Belastingdienst table for 2022 would settle them. [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Suppletie amount boundary for 2022:** unknown. The Handboek Ondernemen 2020 says minder dan € 1.000 may go in the next return, the Handboek Ondernemen 2026 says € 1.000 of minder, and the wording for 2021 to 2025 stays open. A 2022 handbook would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, aangifte corrigeren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren).
- **Opgaaf ICP deadline and detailed period rules for 2022:** the deadline was not found. The later guide describes monthly goods periods, quarterly reporting only at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and each of the previous 4 quarters, a 1-month domestic deadline and a tweemaandelijks option, but it does not establish the 2022 deadline or confirm the 2022 wording. A 2022 ICP guide would settle both. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, tijdvak opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).
- **General B2B e-invoicing duty for 2022:** not verified. The current government guidance covers e-invoicing to central government and the option for other public bodies, and does not settle the 2022 general B2B position. A dated 2022 primary source or archived official guidance would settle it. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).
- **OSS inactivity:** the claim that participation ends after two years without supplies while the meldingen continue does not appear on the checked Belastingdienst page and stays UNVERIFIED. A primary rule or Belastingdienst guidance stating that condition would settle it. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
- **Tabel I list pages carry no year:** the Belastingdienst goods and services pages for the 9 percent rate carry no year, so the exact 2022 version of those lists is not separately dated on the pages. The post history for electronic publications rests on the besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334 and the logies post rests on Stb. 2025, 339. [Belastingdienst, goods at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html).
- **ViDA EU act dates:** the checked material records 11 and 25 March 2025 as the EU adoption dates and 14 April 2025 as the entry into force of the ViDA acts, but these dates were not found on a Dutch primary page and stay UNVERIFIED. No 2022 rule depends on them. [Kamerbrief 26 juni 2025, Kamerstuk 22112, nr. 4090](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-22112-4090.html).
- **Future legislation, no effect on 2022:** the Rijksoverheid news item of 11 september 2026 describes e-facturatie per 1 juli 2030 and rapportage per 1 juli 2031 for national B2B, with a bill expected before summer 2027 and KOR businesses vrijgesteld van e-facturatie en -rapportage. Bill 36.920 on the ViDA single VAT registration is adopted-not-yet-in-force, with entry into force on 1 January 2027. Neither affects 2022. [Rijksoverheid, 11 September 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven), [Eerste Kamer, bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn).
- **Resolved KOR turnover test conflict:** a general summary described the € 20.000 ceiling as applying to both the application year and the preceding year. For 2020 to 2024 the law tests de omzet in een kalenderjaar and no lid names the preceding year, so the preceding-year test is wrong for 2022. That test belongs to the rules from 1 January 2025 under art. 25a lid 5 and lid 9. [Wet OB, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- **Resolved yearly return deadline wording:** vóór 1 april and 31 maart put the last day in the same place, so there is no conflict. Both identify 31 maart as the last day for the yearly return. [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Resolved MOSS replacement:** MOSS lapsed on 1 juli 2021 and the eenloketsysteem took its place, so 2022 uses the eenloketsysteem. [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).
