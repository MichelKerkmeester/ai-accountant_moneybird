# Inkomstenbelasting box 1 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the box 1 bands and rates for 2022, the AOW-age variants, the algemene heffingskorting (general tax credit), the arbeidskorting (labour tax credit), the inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting (income-dependent combination credit), the tariefsaanpassing (rate adjustment for deductions) and the route from winst uit onderneming (business profit) to taxable box 1 income. The published first-bracket rate combines the income tax with the premie volksverzekeringen (national insurance contributions) below AOW age, while the statutory table separates those components. The 2022 figures in this file are enacted. The inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw (income-related health insurance contribution) exists alongside the income tax and its calculation belongs to the Zvw reference.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Box 1 below AOW age

For a taxpayer below AOW age, the published box 1 table has 37,07% up to € 69.398 and 49,50% above that amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). The statutory table in art. 2.10 lid 1 holds only the income-tax part and reads 9,42% up to € 35.472, then € 3.341 plus 37,07% up to € 69.398, then € 15.917 plus 49,50% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The published 37,07% equals the 9,42% income-tax part plus the premie volksverzekeringen of 27,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The first statutory band is income tax only and ends at the premie ceiling. Above the ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the published combined rate, which is why the published table shows a single combined rate up to the top boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The tax on the box 1 saldo is computed under art. 2.10 lid 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Box 1 for a full year at AOW age

For a taxpayer who was at AOW age for the whole of 2022 and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first rate is 19,17% up to € 35.472, then 37,07% up to € 69.398, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946, art. 2.10a gives a first boundary of € 36.409 with fixed amounts of € 3.429 and € 15.658 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10a). The lower AOW-age first-bracket rate follows from the AOW premium not being due from AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). The checked pages state only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age. Whether the Anw and Wlz parts continue is not stated as a rule in the checked pages and stays an inference, see section 7.

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

In the year in which the AOW age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it and rises by about 1,49 points per month from the AOW-age rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2022 the monthly rates are January 19,17%, February 20,66%, March 22,15%, April 23,64%, May 25,13%, June 26,62%, July 28,12%, August 29,81%, September 31,10%, October 32,59%, November 34,08% and December 35,57% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). The first bracket runs up to € 35.472 and the second and third brackets keep their normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening).

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling

The premie volksverzekeringen of 27,65% is charged over the premie-inkomen up to a ceiling. For 2022 that ceiling is € 35.472, or € 36.409 for a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The ceiling equals the end of the first statutory band (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The premium components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The checked page states that the AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### Tariefsaanpassing

When grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the base) have been deducted, the tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is raised by 9,5% of the amount by which the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning plus those posten exceeds the top bracket boundary of € 69.398. The increase is capped at 9,5% of the deducted posten (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). Above the boundary, deductions are therefore worth at most 40% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The posten are the persoonsgebonden aftrek (onderhoudsverplichtingen including partneralimentatie, weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten, aftrekbare giften, specifieke zorgkosten and the restant persoonsgebonden aftrek from earlier years), the aftrek kosten eigen woning (hypotheekrente, periodieke erfpacht, opstal or beklemming, kosten afsluiten hypotheek and restschuldkosten), the ondernemersfaciliteiten (zelfstandigenaftrek, aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling) and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and is shown on the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The 2022 box 1 page names the tariefsaanpassing but prints no percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening).

### Algemene heffingskorting

The AHK is granted to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The phase-out base in 2022 is the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer below AOW age the AHK is € 2.888 minus 6,007% of the box 1 income above € 21.317 and reaches nil at € 69.398 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the AHK is € 1.494 minus 3,106% above € 21.317 and reaches nil at € 69.398 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The phase-out points for the AHK and AK are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon (statutory minimum wage) (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301, footnote to Tabel 1).

### Arbeidskorting below AOW age

The AK is granted over the arbeidsinkomen. For an IB-ondernemer the arbeidsinkomen includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and excludes the part earned as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The 2022 table below AOW age has four bands: 4,541% up to € 10.350, then € 470 plus 28,461% up to € 22.356, then € 3.887 plus 2,610% up to € 36.649, then € 4.260 minus 5,860% up to € 109.346, and nil above € 109.346 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The amount of the table at each band boundary is the starting amount for the next step (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Where the AK cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and premie, the unused part can be paid out to a minstverdienende partner (lowest-earning partner) under the payout rules below (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Arbeidskorting for a full year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year, the AK table has its own percentages: 2,348% up to € 10.350, then € 244 plus 14,718% up to € 22.356, then € 2.011 plus 1,349% up to € 36.649, then € 2.204 minus 3,030% up to € 109.346, and nil above € 109.346 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For 2020 to 2023 the checked pages give no rule or amount for the heffingskortingen in the year in which the AOW age is reached, so that point is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

For a taxpayer below AOW age the IACK is 11,45% of the arbeidsinkomen above € 5.219, up to a maximum of € 2.534, reached at € 27.350 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the rate is 5,911% and the maximum is € 1.452 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag and not through the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The partner test on the IACK page that covers 2024 to 2026 is that the taxpayer has no fiscal partner, or a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or a lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### IACK child and co-ouder conditions

For 2022 the child must have been born after 31 December 2009 and must have been registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). A co-ouder qualifies when the child spends at least 156 days in 2022 with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The IACK page covering 2024 to 2026 adds that qualifying children are own children, stepchildren, adopted children, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child, and that a pleegkind qualifies only when it is raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a pleegvergoeding. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or the BES islands can qualify, and a death of the taxpayer or the child before the 6 months still gives the korting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting). The same page describes roughly equal care with at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated when co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### Payout of unused heffingskortingen to a partner

The payout to a minstverdienende partner was afgebouwd (phased down) since 2009: 80% was withheld in 2020, 86⅔% in 2021 and 93⅓% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). In 2022, 93⅓% was withheld and 6,667% was paid out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Conditions are that the partners were fiscal partners for more than 6 months and that the partner owes enough tax. Only the part that cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and premie is paid out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The afbouw ends in 2023, and no AHK afbouw applies to people born before 1 January 1963 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Payout income limits

For 2022 the limits are an own box 1 income of € 8.885 for people born before 1963, although the page also prints € 8.845 once, or € 523 for people born later. The partner limits are income above € 23.700, or € 30.400 at AOW age, or € 25.605 where the partner receives more heffingskortingen. The under-21 rule applies to people born after 31 December 2000 and uses € 446 per quarter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The € 8.885 and € 8.845 values conflict, see section 7.

### IACK payout percentage

The 2022 page states no IACK-specific payout percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED. Do not apply the general payout percentage to the IACK without a source that states it.

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen

Each heffingskorting splits into a belastingdeel, the income-tax part of the first-bracket rate, and premiedelen AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). For 2022 the belastingdeel is 9,42% and the total with the premiedelen is 37,07% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). A taxpayer who is not insured for one of the schemes loses that part, and a part-year premieplicht gives a time-proportional premiedeel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Winst uit onderneming to taxable box 1 income

Winst uit onderneming requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and that the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Registration in the Handelsregister alone does not decide the tax classification. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Belastbare winst uit onderneming is the winst as ondernemer reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming and is reduced by te verrekenen verliezen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen are taken out of the winst first. These include the bosbouwvrijstelling, the landbouwvrijstelling, the vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst for the part above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies and benefits under the eindheffing, mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject vrijstellingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). A medegerechtigde or a geldverstrekker gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). The MKB-winstvrijstelling is computed on the winst after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The box 1 saldo is taxed by art. 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the tariefsaanpassing, and the heffingskortingen reduce the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference, see section 7. The checked material read the definitions in arts. 3.1 and 3.2 in the 2026 version of Wet IB 2001.

### Ondernemersaftrek values relevant to business profit

The 2022 ondernemersaftrek values are zelfstandigenaftrek € 6.310, or € 3.155 at AOW age, startersaftrek € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 and then € 0, capped at the profit, meewerkaftrek 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the applicable hour bands, and stakingsaftrek with a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked material does not give the hour-band boundaries for the meewerkaftrek or the full eligibility conditions for these faciliteiten, so those details are not in this file. The zelfstandigenaftrek, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, the stakingsaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are among the grondslagverminderende posten for the tariefsaanpassing (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

The MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of the profit after the ondernemersaftrek for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). It reduces the belastbare winst uit onderneming (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The MKB-winstvrijstelling is also one of the grondslagverminderende posten for the tariefsaanpassing (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

### Oudedagsreserve addition

In 2022 an ondernemer who met het urencriterium (hours criterion) and was below AOW age could add 9,44% of the profit to an oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve), up to a maximum of € 9.632, reduced by the beroepspensioenpremie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). 2022 was the last year of additions. Since 2023 no new oudedagsreserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). Reduction or release of the reserve follows on buying a lijfrente with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the ondernemingsvermogen, on cessation, on reaching the AOW age on 1 January, on failing the urencriterium in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

Every example below uses the section 2 figures. Official examples are copied from the linked source page. Calculated examples are built here from the section 2 figures.

### Box 1 below AOW age

**Calculated example.** Assume a box 1 income of € 80.000 for a taxpayer below AOW age.

1. The published first band runs to € 69.398 at 37,07%.
2. The amount above that boundary is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602.
3. That amount falls in the 49,50% band.
4. Tax plus premie before credits: 37,07% × € 69.398 = € 25.725,8386 and 49,50% × € 10.602 = € 5.247,99, together € 30.973,8286.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening

### Box 1 for a full year at AOW age

**Calculated example.** Assume a box 1 income of € 80.000 for a taxpayer born on or after 1 January 1946 who was at AOW age for the whole year.

1. The first band runs to € 35.472 at 19,17%.
2. The second band runs from € 35.472 to € 69.398. Its width is € 69.398 minus € 35.472 = € 33.926, at 37,07%.
3. The amount above € 69.398 is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602, at 49,50%.
4. Tax plus premie before credits: 19,17% × € 35.472 = € 6.799,9824, 37,07% × € 33.926 = € 12.576,3682 and 49,50% × € 10.602 = € 5.247,99, together € 24.624,3406.

For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 the first band ends at € 36.409 instead. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10a

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

**Calculated example.** The 2022 monthly rates run from 19,17% in January to 35,57% in December. The difference between the highest and the lowest monthly rate is 35,57% minus 19,17% = 16,40 percentage points, applied to the first bracket of € 35.472. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling

### Tariefsaanpassing

**Calculated example.** Assume a taxable box 1 income after deductions of € 60.000 and qualifying grondslagverminderende posten of € 20.000.

1. Add the posten back for the threshold test: € 60.000 plus € 20.000 = € 80.000.
2. The amount above € 69.398 is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602.
3. The increase is 9,5% × € 10.602 = € 1.007,19.
4. The cap is 9,5% × € 20.000 = € 1.900.
5. The lower amount applies, so the illustrative tariefsaanpassing is € 1.007,19.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

Record the profit and the steps to the belastbare winst uit onderneming, including the ondernemersaftrek, the MKB-winstvrijstelling, any vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen, te verrekenen verliezen and the grondslagverminderende posten. Keep the calculations that support the amounts entered in the income tax return. The checked material does not state a filing deadline or a record-retention period for this topic. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.

The AHK and AK are applied automatically in the return and can also be applied through the payroll. The IACK is claimed in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag and is not applied through the payroll. The tariefsaanpassing is applied automatically and appears on the aanslag. The payout of unused heffingskortingen to a partner depends on the partner's tax and on the payout income limits. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

- The 2022 payout income limit for people born before 1963 is printed as both € 8.885 and € 8.845 on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen. A corrected primary page or an official correction would settle it.

- No IACK-specific payout percentage is stated for 2022 on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. A primary source that states the 2022 IACK payout rule would settle it.

- The heffingskortingen in the year in which the AOW age is reached: the 2022 page gives no rule or amount, and the checked material marks 2020 to 2023 UNVERIFIED on this point.
