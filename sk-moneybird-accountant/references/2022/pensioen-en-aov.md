# Pensioen, lijfrente en AOV 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the income-provision rules for tax year 2022 for a ZZP sole trader, and for a reader who may also own a BV: the jaarruimte (annual room for deductible lijfrentepremies) and reserveringsruimte (catch-up room for unused jaarruimte), the conditions for lijfrenteverzekering, lijfrenterekening and lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, the stakingswinst conversion of art. 3.129, the oudedagsreserve (FOR, old-age reserve), and the deductibility of AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) premiums. The 2022 values come from the text of the Wet inkomstenbelasting valid on 1 January 2022 and from the Belastingdienst FISIN 2022 chapter, so the law for this year is enacted. Two features of 2022 matter most: it is the last year in which a FOR dotatie is possible, and it is the last year in which the jaarruimte is reduced by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve. Section 7 lists the open points and conflicts in the checked material, including the age boundary for the higher reserveringsruimte.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Jaarruimte percentage

This item applies to a taxpayer with a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year, so the 2022 deduction looks at 2021. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets that taxpayer deduct lijfrentepremies up to 13,3% of the premiegrondslag (premium base), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual and, in 2022, also by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve (additions to the old-age reserve, lid 1 and lid 4 sub b). The 13,3% holds in the texts of 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01. The 30% rate belongs to the texts from 2023-07-01 and later and does not apply to 2022. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Maximum jaarruimte

For 2022 the maximum jaarruimte is € 13.570. The outcome of the percentage calculation is capped at this amount, which the Ministry tables print for 2022. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering (benefit) is taxed. Sources: [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

For 2022 the aftoppingsgrens is € 114.866. This is the eerstvermelde bedrag (first-mentioned amount) of art. 3.127 lid 3. The income components of the previous calendar year are counted up to this amount. Art. 10.2b indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor (collective wage development factor). In the 2020 text that is lid 2, in the 2024 to 2027 texts lid 1. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10.2b, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 10.2b, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Franchise premiegrondslag

For 2022 the franchise is € 12.837. This is the laatstvermelde bedrag (last-mentioned amount) of art. 3.127 lid 3, subtracted after the cap. In the 2020 text the franchise follows art. 10.1. In the 2024 to 2027 texts art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the AOW benefit for married persons without toeslag (supplement) plus vakantieuitkering (holiday allowance) times 100/75. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10.2b, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 10.2b, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Factor A multiplier

The multiplier for 2022 is 6,27. The reduction for pension accrual of art. 3.127 lid 4 is 6,27 keer (times) the aangroei (growth) of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd (service years), plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. This applies when the taxpayer accrues pension, for example through employment. From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction becomes the premiums ingelegd (paid in) in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen (old-age pension) and partnerpensioen (partner pension), excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor, with transitional rules in art. 10a.25. For 2022 the 6,27 formula holds. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10a.25, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Premiegrondslag composition

Art. 3.127 lid 3 sums over the previous calendar year (2021 for the 2022 deduction): (a) winst uit onderneming (business profit) vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek (before the addition to and the withdrawal from the old-age reserve and before the entrepreneur's allowance), (b) belastbaar loon (taxable wage), (c) belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (taxable result from other activities) and (d) belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen (taxable periodic benefits). The sum is counted up to the aftoppingsgrens and then reduced by the franchise. Art. 3.127 lid 5 adds that on (partial) staking the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Age limit jaarruimte

At the start of the calendar year the taxpayer must not yet have reached the AOW age (state pension age). That is the rule in the 2022 text, and FISIN 2022 uses the same wording. From the Wtp text of 2023-07-01, applied to all of 2023, the limit becomes five years above the AOW age. For 2022 the older rule stands. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Look-back window reserveringsruimte

Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows unused jaarruimte of the preceding period to be used on request in the aangifte (tax return), oldest unused year first. The period is "zeven jaar" (seven years) in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts, so for 2022 the window covers the jaarruimte of 2015 tot en met 2021 (2015 to 2021). From the 2023-07-01 text the period is "tien jaar" (ten years). The Belastingdienst page advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so that older unused ruimte does not lapse, and states that unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2022 prints the window as "2014 tot en met 2021", which does not match the law, see section 7.

### Reserveringsruimte percentage cap

For 2020 to 2022 the reserveringsruimte is at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount. That euro amount is raised to a higher amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. From the 2023-07-01 text there is one flat euro maximum, with no percentage and no age split. In 2022 both the 17% and the euro limit apply, so the lower of the two binds. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Age boundary for the higher reserveringsruimte

The law describes the boundary as an age at the start of the year of at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. The Ministry 2022 table uses 56 jaar en 7 maanden or older on 1 January 2022 for the higher amount. FISIN 2022 states the higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965, which looks shifted one year from 2021 without the AOW age change. The checked material does not fetch the AOW age of 2022 from the AOW law, so this boundary is not fully settled, see section 7. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum, younger group

For 2022 the lower amount is € 7.587. It applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is more than ten years below the AOW age. The Ministry table describes this group as younger than 56 jaar en 7 maanden on 1 January 2022. The amount can be further limited by the 17% cap and by the unused jaarruimte. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum, older group

For 2022 the higher amount is € 14.978. It applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age. The Ministry table uses 56 jaar en 7 maanden or older on 1 January 2022. FISIN 2022 states this higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965, see section 7. The amount can be further limited by the 17% cap and by the unused jaarruimte. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Lijfrentevormen en oudedagslijfrente

Three forms exist: lijfrenteverzekering (annuity insurance), lijfrenterekening (annuity account) and lijfrentebeleggingsrecht (annuity investment right). For an oudedagslijfrente (old-age annuity, art. 3.125 lid 1 a) the first termijn (instalment) must be at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, and payments run until death. The FISIN example: AOW age in 2030 means a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. For a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht the uitkering must run at least 20 years, plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year, so a start at 63 with AOW age 67 means 24 years. Nabestaandenlijfrente (survivor annuity, art. 3.125 lid 1 b) for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30. For a rekening or beleggingsrecht it must run at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. A lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind (adult disabled child or grandchild) is deductible without a pensioentekort, per art. 3.124 lid 1 b. Sources: [art. 3.124 and 3.125, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

For 2022 the cap is € 22.735 per year. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c requires at least five years of termijnen, starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to the statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. FISIN 2022 prints € 22.443 in its verzekering row and € 22.735 in its rekening row. The law text and the Ministry table give € 22.735, see section 7. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The cap is € 63.288 per year. The overbruggingslijfrente (bridge annuity) is only for lijfrentetegoeden (annuity assets) built up before 2006. The uitkeringen end at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching AOW age, or when a pensioen starts. The yearly maximum of € 63.288 appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

For 2022 the tier a maximum is € 480.686. Art. 3.129 allows a deduction of lijfrentepremies up to the stakingswinst (profit on cessation), capped at the tier maximum minus already built provisions. Those provisions are the value of pension rights charged to profit, the amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years and earlier art. 3.129 amounts. The elements that count "from 2023" are the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13, so they do not apply to 2022. Tier a applies when the age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or the taxpayer is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt (disabled) with the lijfrente starting within six months, or the staking happens by death. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

For 2022 the tier b maximum is € 240.352. Tier b applies when the age at staking is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or the lijfrente starts immediately. The same cap mechanics apply: deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at € 240.352 minus the already built provisions listed in the tier a subsection. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

For 2022 the tier c maximum is € 120.183, for the remaining cases. The same cap mechanics apply: deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at € 120.183 minus the already built provisions listed in the tier a subsection. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

For 2022 the amount is € 4.607. Afkoop (commutation) of a small lijfrente in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount, set in art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d. Source: [art. 3.133, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

For 2022 the cap is € 43.012. Art. 3.133 lid 9 states that afkoop is not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer (policyholder) or gerechtigde (beneficiary) is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt (long-term disabled) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached the AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap. If the alternative is higher, the test is the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn. Source: [art. 3.133, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### FOR toevoeging

For 2022 the toevoeging (addition) is 9,44% of the winst, at most € 9.632, per art. 3.68. The toevoeging is reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst and cannot exceed the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets). Access requires the urencriterium (hours criterion) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year, per art. 3.67. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. 2022 is the last year of a toevoeging. From 2023 no toevoeging is possible and an existing FOR stays under the old rules, per art. 10a.29 lid 1. In 2022 the dotatie also reduces the jaarruimte. Sources: [art. 3.67, 3.68 and 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### FOR afname

Art. 3.70 lid 1 provides the afname (withdrawal) of the FOR. The taxpayer may choose an amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or the afname follows from the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at staking, at AOW age on 1 January, or when the urencriterium fails in this year and the last year. FISIN 2022 repeats the example with € 3.000 against € 4.000. Sources: [art. 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022, winst en verliesrekening en balans](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### FOR-lijfrente conversion article

The FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022, up to the afname under art. 3.70 lid 1 a. In the 2022 text art. 3.127 lid 8 is the AMvB rule on pension accrual data, not the conversion right. The converted premium may count in the year itself when it is paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2), so before 1 July 2023 for 2022. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.128, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval (periodic benefits for disability, illness or accident) for the taxpayer himself an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenditure for income provisions). The premium is fully deductible and the article holds no ruimte cap. FISIN states "volledig aftrekken" (deduct in full). Four categories are not deductible: premiums that the employer takes into account for loonheffing (payroll tax), mandatory Ziektewet (Sickness Benefits Act) and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen (endowment policies), and Zvw (Health Insurance Act) premiums. For an ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits later are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" (income from former employment) with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing (advance levy). Art. 3.124 lid 1 b allows a deduction without a pensioentekort for a lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind. Sources: [art. 3.124, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.124, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. The Belastingdienst page gives the rule of thumb that a premium that is not deductible means the payout is not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Mandatory beroepspensioen premium

A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen (occupational or branch pension) premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction. Source: [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Sectoraal pensioenfonds voor zelfstandigen

A self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible. The proef (experiment) runs until 1 July 2028. Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimenteerbepaling (experimental provision) for zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions. Sources: [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij), [art. 10a.26, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Payment deadline FOR or stakingswinst conversion

Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year they are paid or verrekend (settled). Art. 3.130 lid 2 allows, on choice, a premium for the stakingswinst conversion (art. 3.129), the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) to be counted in the year itself when it is paid within six months after the year end. For 2022 that means payment before 1 July 2023. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Lijfrente ingangsdatum termijn

If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, a change of terms, afkoop or deblokkering (unblocking), the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente (penalty interest) is due. A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum (start date) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2022: an ingangsdatum in 2021 means convert or start by 31 December 2022, an ingangsdatum in 2022 means convert or start by 31 December 2023. From FISIN 2023 the term is described as the einddatum (end date). Extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden (special circumstances). A refund of a deducted premium is a negatieve uitgave (negative expenditure) without revisierente, for a lijfrente only when it is undone within 30 days. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Nabestaandenlijfrente termijn

For a nabestaandenlijfrente the termijn is 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2022: death in 2021 means start by 31 December 2023. From FISIN 2023 the wording is "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren" (the year of death plus the 2 calendar years), with the same outcome. Extension on request is possible in bijzondere omstandigheden. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

All inputs in the calculated examples are invented and use round amounts. Every statutory amount used comes from section 2 or from the 2021 figures in section 5. The jaarruimte examples assume a pensioentekort, no nettopensioenpremies and a taxpayer who has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2022. Products are shown before rounding, and each rounding step says rounded or rounded down.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with a profit of € 60.000,00 (no pension accrual, no FOR dotatie).**

- Input: winst uit onderneming over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no other income components, no pension accrual and no FOR dotatie.
- Step 1, cap at the aftoppingsgrens (art. 3.127 lid 3): min(€ 60.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 47.163,00 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, reductions: pension accrual € 0,00 and FOR dotatie € 0,00.
- Step 5, maximum check: € 6.272,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 6.272,68.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with pension accrual (factor A).**

- Input: the same winst over 2021 of € 60.000,00, the same premiegrondslag of € 47.163,00 and no FOR dotatie as above. The aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement is € 1.000,00.
- Reduction for pension accrual (factor A): 6,27 x € 1.000,00 = € 6.270,00, exact.
- Jaarruimte before the reduction: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Jaarruimte after the reduction: € 6.272,6790 - € 6.270,00 = € 2,68, rounded (exact € 2,6790).
- The maximum of € 13.570,00 does not bind.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with a FOR dotatie over 2021.**

- Input: the same winst over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no pension accrual and a FOR dotatie over 2021.
- Step 1, FOR dotatie over 2021: 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Step 2, cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2021 cap of € 9.395,00. This is the 2021 cap, because the jaarruimte 2022 uses the FOR dotatie of the previous calendar year (2021), not the 2022 cap.
- Step 3, premiegrondslag: the winst is taken before the FOR dotatie and before the ondernemersaftrek, so the base stays € 47.163,00 and 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, subtract the dotatie: € 6.272,6790 - € 5.664,00 = € 608,68, rounded (exact € 608,6790).
- Step 5, maximum check: € 608,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 608,68.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte at a high income (aftoppingsgrens).**

- Input: income components over 2021 of € 150.000,00, no pension accrual and no FOR dotatie.
- Step 1: min(€ 150.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 114.866,00.
- Step 2: € 114.866,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 102.029,00 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3: 13,3% x € 102.029,00 = € 13.569,86, rounded (exact € 13.569,8570).
- Step 4, maximum check: € 13.569,86 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00, so the maximum does not bind.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 13.569,86.

**Calculated example: reserveringsruimte cap and oldest-first use.**

- Input: premiegrondslag of € 50.000,00, unused jaarruimte 2016 of € 5.000,00 and unused jaarruimte 2017 of € 3.000,00. The taxpayer's age at the start of 2022 is more than ten years below the AOW age, so the younger-group maximum of € 7.587,00 applies and not the older-group maximum of € 14.978,00. Both years lie in the seven-year look-back window of 2015 tot en met 2021.
- Percentage limit: 17% x € 50.000,00 = € 8.500,00, exact.
- Younger-group maximum: min(€ 8.500,00, € 7.587,00) = € 7.587,00.
- Oldest unused year first, on request in the aangifte (art. 3.127 lid 2): € 5.000,00 from 2016 plus € 2.587,00 of the € 3.000,00 from 2017 = € 7.587,00.
- Reserveringsruimte 2022: € 7.587,00.

**Calculated example: stakingswinst conversion.**

- Input: stakingswinst of € 200.000,00 and already built provisions of € 80.000,00. The taxpayer is more than five years but at most fifteen years below the AOW age at the staking date and does not meet a tier a ground, so the tier b maximum of art. 3.129 applies.
- Tier b maximum: € 240.352,00.
- Cap after the provisions: € 240.352,00 - € 80.000,00 = € 160.352,00.
- Deductible premiums for the conversion: min(€ 200.000,00, € 160.352,00) = € 160.352,00.
- If a tier a ground applied instead, the tier a maximum of € 480.686,00 minus € 80.000,00 gives a cap of € 400.686,00, and the full stakingswinst of € 200.000,00 would be deductible. The example therefore states the age range that places the taxpayer in tier b.
- A payment made before 1 July 2023 may on choice still count in 2022 (art. 3.130 lid 2).

**Calculated example: FOR toevoeging over 2022.**

- Input: winst over 2022 of € 60.000,00, no pension premiums charged to the winst, enough growth of the ondernemingsvermogen, the urencriterium is met and the taxpayer has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2022.
- 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2022 cap of € 9.632,00 (art. 3.68).
- Toevoeging: € 5.664,00. 2022 is the last year in which a FOR toevoeging is possible.
- Interaction: this toevoeging does not reduce the jaarruimte 2022. The jaarruimte 2022 is reduced by the FOR dotatie over 2021, see the example above.

**Calculated example: AOV premium.**

- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with periodic uitkeringen: fully deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, so the deduction is € 2.400,00.
- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with a lump-sum payout: not deductible, and the later payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

**Calculated example: afkoop bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid.**

- Input: the taxpayer meets the ministerial conditions for langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid and has not yet reached the AOW age, and receives an afkoop of € 40.000,00 in 2022.
- 2022 cap: € 43.012,00.
- € 40.000,00 is below € 43.012,00, so the afkoop is not a prohibited act under art. 3.133 lid 9 and counts as a lijfrente termijn.

**Calculated example: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente.**

- Assume the uitkeringen run for at least five years and start in the allowed window, not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment.
- Yearly uitkering of € 20.000,00: below the 2022 cap of € 22.735,00, so the uitkering stays within the cap of the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 c.
- Yearly uitkering of € 25.000,00: above the cap. FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years.

**Official example: FISIN 2022 deadlines for the ingangsdatum and for nabestaanden.**

- Ingangsdatum in 2021: convert or start by 31 December 2022.
- Ingangsdatum in 2022: convert or start by 31 December 2023.
- Nabestaandenlijfrente after a death in 2021: start by 31 December 2023.
- Source: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

**Official example: FISIN 2022 FOR afname.**

- The taxpayer chooses an afname of € 3.000,00, while € 4.000,00 is available.
- Source: [FISIN 2022, winst en verliesrekening en balans](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

**Official example: oudedagslijfrente start and duration.**

- AOW age in 2030: start at the latest on 31 December 2035 (art. 3.125 lid 1 a).
- Start at 63 with AOW age 67: the uitkering starts 4 years before the AOW year, so a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht must run 20 + 4 = 24 years.
- Source: [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Record every lijfrentepremie with its payment date. Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts a premium in the year of payment or verrekening, and art. 3.130 lid 2 lets a payment before 1 July 2023 count in 2022 for the FOR conversion and the stakingswinst conversion. [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Record per policy whether the uitkeringen are periodic or a lump sum. A periodic AOV premium is fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 c. A lump-sum policy is not deductible and its payout belongs in box 3. [art. 3.124, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- Record the FOR toevoeging and the FOR afname. For 2022 a toevoeging of 9,44% of the winst up to € 9.632 is still possible if the urencriterium is met and the taxpayer has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of the year. This is the last year of the toevoeging. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. [art. 3.67, 3.68 and 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Keep a schedule of unused jaarruimte per year from 2015 to 2021. The reserveringsruimte is requested in the aangifte, the oldest unused year is used first, and the 17% and euro caps apply. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Book the AOV premium as an uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen in the box 1 return, not as a business cost of the onderneming. Periodic benefits later are inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking. [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- A mandatory beroepspensioen premium goes in as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction. [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Watch the dates on existing lijfrentes. Convert or start within 1 full calendar year after the ingangsdatum, and start a nabestaandenlijfrente within 2 full calendar years after the year of death. Extension is possible on request in bijzondere omstandigheden. [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Keep the ingangsdatum on file for each policy. The FISIN 2022 deadlines (ingangsdatum 2021 means 31 December 2022, ingangsdatum 2022 means 31 December 2023) are tested on that date. [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

**Open points and conflicts for 2022.**

- Age boundary for the higher reserveringsruimte. FISIN 2022 states the higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965. The Ministry 2022 table uses 56 jaar en 7 maanden or older on 1 January 2022. The law uses "ten hoogste tien jaar lager dan de in dat kalenderjaar geldende pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". The point is not fully settled because the AOW age of 2022 was not fetched from the AOW law. Settling source: art. 7a AOW as valid on 1 January 2022 on wetten.overheid.nl. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window. FISIN 2022 prints "2014 tot en met 2021", which is eight years. Art. 3.127 lid 2 of the 2022-01-01 text reads "zeven jaar", so the window is 2015 tot en met 2021. The law text prevails. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap for 2022. FISIN 2022 prints € 22.443 in its verzekering row and € 22.735 in its rekening row. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c and the Ministry tables give € 22.735. Settled in favour of € 22.735. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.125, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).
- Note on verification. The maximum jaarruimte of € 13.570 was derived and UNVERIFIED in earlier research. The Ministry tables confirm it, so section 2 marks it Verified. Sources: [Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf).

**Proposed and later-year items in the checked material, outside tax year 2022.**

- Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen (BAZ), bill 36 912. This is proposed, not 2022 law. Docket status on 2026-10-11: "In afwachting nota nav verslag". Bill art. 16 gives a wachttijd (waiting period) of 104 weken. The MvT gives a benefit of 70% of the grondslag (base), with the grondslag at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so at most the minimumloon. The premium is set yearly by UWV and estimated at about 5,4% of the winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag. The MvT examples use a maximum grondslag of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, and UWV benefits would carry loonheffing. Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force is by koninklijk besluit, and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16 (104 weken), settled at 104 weken. Sources: [dossier 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13313), [document 2026D13315](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D13315).
- Belastingplan 2027. Proposed, not adopted on 2026-10-11. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensioengevend loon at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the aftoppingsgrens would stay € 137.800. A limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the lijfrente maxima in the MvT, being the maximum for lijfrentevoorzieningen (art. 3.125), for rekening and beleggingsrecht, the maximum in aanmerking te nemen premies (art. 3.127), the stakingswinst maximum (art. 3.129) and the maximum for negatieve uitgaven. The checked material also notes that the aftoppingsgrens was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (amendement Grinwis c.s., Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90). Source: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Zelfstandigenwet. Proposed and adjacent, with an internetconsultatie until 29 October 2026, a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets leading to a "veilige haven" (safe harbour), and a beoogde inwerkingtreding (intended entry into force) in January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension. Source: [rijksoverheid news item](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- Later-year correction on the 30% jaarruimte percentage. The 30% runs from the Wtp with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023, per the Belastingdienst Wtp page. The research tied Stcrt. 2023, 34571 and Stb. 2025, 431 to that change, but the 2023-07-01 text shows that Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6 (back to 1 January 2023) and Stb. 2025, 431 (in force 1 January 2026) only rewrites the partnerpensioen wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Later-year correction on the pension accrual reduction. From the 2024-01-01 text the factor A reduction changes to the premiums ingelegd in the previous year. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964, also for accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2 gives for a fixed premium per dienstjaar (art. 38r) the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434 (retroactive to 1 July 2023): A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor. The research gave the older formula A = (1 - (X - Y) / X) x B. Sources: [art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10a.25, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Later-year conflict on the tier a age wording of art. 3.129. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. The law text governs. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).
- Later years, for completeness of the cross-year material. The FOR maximum of 2020 has a conflict between the law (€ 9.218) and FISIN 2020 (€ 9.214), settled at € 9.218. The tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap of 2026 has a conflict between € 27.192 and € 26.781, settled at € 27.192. The location of the FOR transition from 2023 is art. 10a.29 (not art. 3.127). The 2027 law text exists at wetten.overheid.nl with the 2026 amounts still in place. Sources: [art. 3.68, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Ministry 2020 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf), [art. 3.125, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 3.127, text 2027-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
