# Kosten en auto 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This file covers the deduction of business costs and the car, bicycle and travel rules for a Dutch sole trader in tax year 2022, plus the gemengde kosten (mixed costs) rules that also apply to a BV. The most important change in 2022 is the end of the persoonsgebonden aftrek voor scholingsuitgaven (personal deduction for training costs) on 1 January 2022, with the non-fiscal STAP-budget as its replacement. Study costs stayed deductible as business costs only when they maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). The gemengde kosten drempel (threshold) rose to € 4.800, and a zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 gets a reduction of 6% capped at € 2.100. The 2022 rules in the checked material are enacted law, verified in the statute text valid on 1 January 2022.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### 3.1 Gemengde kosten drempel

Costs connected with voedsel (food), drank (drinks), genotmiddelen (stimulants such as tobacco), representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible up to a yearly drempel. For 2022 the drempel is € 4.800 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Travel and stay related to these posts count as part of them (lid 2). The restriction does not apply when the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No onttrekking (drawing) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). Voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien, drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank, and genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Food, drinks and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Relatiegeschenken (business gifts) generally fall under representatie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.3). The fixed amount is not time-proportioned, so it is not reduced when the enterprise starts during the calendar year and not increased when a boekjaar runs longer than 12 months (same besluit, section 3.1). Food costs that qualify as loonkosten (wage costs) are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals the restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (payment in kind) (same besluit, section 3.2). The drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only.

### 3.2 Keuze in plaats van de drempel

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters (people with resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). The choice is made in the return, so it is a per-year decision and not a bookkeeping entry. Compare the two routes before filing, because the better route depends on the total amount of these costs. Example 1 in section 4 shows both routes with the 2022 figures.

### 3.3 Reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. This travel and stay also counts as part of the gemengde kosten posts under art. 3.15 lid 2, so it interacts with the € 4.800 drempel or with the 80% election (same URL and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### 3.4 Kilometervergoeding

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, such as an auto, motor or fiets, are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For 2022 that amount is € 0,19 per km. The amount is all-in, so brandstof, verzekering, tol en parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer for this rule (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Keep a kilometre record, because the deduction follows the business kilometres that are actually driven.

### 3.5 Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer (public transport), taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview. This route sits next to the € 0,19 per km rule of 3.4, which applies to a private or privately rented vehicle and not to public transport.

### 3.6 Verhuizing en dubbele huisvesting

Moving costs are the cost of moving the inboedel (household contents) plus € 7750 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Costs of housing outside the place of residence are deductible for at most two years. The rule is unchanged in 2020 to 2027.

### 3.7 Auto vermogensetikettering

Vermogensetikettering (asset labelling) decides whether the car sits in the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets) or in privévermogen. A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately the car is verplicht privévermogen. All other cases are keuzevermogen (optional assets) within reasonable bounds. A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag (assessment) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid. Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

### 3.8 Bijtelling auto, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer (commuting) is not private use (lid 3). With a rittenregistratie (mileage log) or another way of showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend (complete), though trips can still be checked. The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation. Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 onderdeel a), for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible (lid 6 to 10). The waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, described by the Belastingdienst as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). The RDW carries it for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer replaces the catalogusprijs.

### 3.9 Bijtelling auto, eerste toelating tot en met 31 december 2016

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. The rule does not affect the 35% rule for old cars of 3.10.

### 3.10 Youngtimer

When the car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, 35% of the waarde in het economische verkeer (WEV, fair market value) is the onttrekking (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The age threshold is 15 years in 2022. The WEV replaces the catalogusprijs for an old car (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. The 25% minimum for pre-2017 cars does not touch this 35% rule (art. 10a.4).

### 3.11 Zero-emission auto, first admission 2022

For a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, and the reduction is capped in euros (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For a car first admitted in 2022 the reduction is 6% off capped at € 2.100, which the checked material renders as 16% up to € 35.000. Cars on waterstof (hydrogen) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (lid 11). After those 60 months the rules then in force apply.

### 3.12 Bijtelling fiets

When a fiets (bicycle, including an e-bike or a speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (recommended consumer price) is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.20a, the note on terugwerkende kracht is also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### 3.13 Btw-correctie privéauto zonder sluitende kilometeradministratie

Btw on the use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet (taxed turnover). Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, although it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA (director and major shareholder) is not the btw-ondernemer and counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### 3.14 Kleine aankopen en eenjarige kosten

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur or onderhoud van de auto (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. For computers and other apparatuur the labelling rules of art. 3.16 lid 3 decide the deductible part, see 3.22.

### 3.15 Studiekostenaftrek abolished

Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven were deductible as persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deduction) above a drempel of € 250 and, outside the standaardstudieperiode (standard study period), up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30 in the 2021 text, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Scholingsuitgaven are lesgeld, cursusgeld, collegegeld, examengeld, promotiekosten and required leermiddelen excluding computers. There was no deduction when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study. The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the prestatiebeurs case of 3.16 to 3.18. Studiekosten stay deductible as business costs when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only maintain existing vakkennis. Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f.

### 3.16 Prestatiebeurs cap, mbo

When the diplomatermijn (diploma period, usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs (performance grant) into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount that could not be deducted earlier because of the prestatiebeurs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). For mbo study years the cap is € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for a part year. When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower, the lower amount applies.

### 3.17 Prestatiebeurs cap, hbo or wo

For hbo or wo study years the cap in the same case is € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for a part year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower, the lower amount applies. The amounts are applied per study year, and the deduction is claimed in the return year in which the diplomatermijn has passed.

### 3.18 Drempel in the prestatiebeurs case

The € 250 drempel applies per person in the prestatiebeurs case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). The official example on the FISIN 2022 page subtracts the drempel once from the total of the study years, see section 4 example 12.

### 3.19 STAP-budget

The STAP-budget is the non-fiscal replacement of the abolished deduction. Art. 29 of the regeling links its start to the Wet afschaffing, and Stb. 2021, 349 states that it applies from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The maximum subsidy is € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). The checked material marks this value as Corrected: the maximum is per scholing and not per year. There are six application windows per calendar year. The regeling is marked Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024.

### 3.20 Werkruimte thuis

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). With another workspace outside the home the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The official factors for zelfstandig (separate) are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen. The 2022 statute has no lid 14. That limit on costs of an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen was added from 1 January 2025 and does not apply to 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).

### 3.21 Telefoonabonnement thuis

Telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0%. The practical rule is that a private telephone subscription is not deductible but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible but the call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100% for that business part (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.22 Private computers en apparatuur

Costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For a computer the income-tax treatment follows vermogensetikettering. Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible. A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen, 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, and in between it is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.23 Literatuur except vakliteratuur

Literatuur except vakliteratuur (professional literature) is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Vakliteratuur is deductible and, under the € 450 rule of 3.14, a purchase under € 450 is deducted in one go.

### 3.24 Kleding except werkkleding

Kleding except werkkleding (work clothing) is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel c, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Werkkleding is defined in lid 5 and is deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive). Personal care such as the kapper is not deductible, while werkkleding is.

### 3.25 Persoonlijke verzorging

Costs of persoonlijke verzorging are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel d, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Health costs such as a pedicure or a zorgverzekering are not deductible as business costs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.26 Vergoeding partner

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11).

### 3.27 Geldboetes en dwangsommen

Not deductible are costs connected with geldboeten (fines) from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten (administrative fines) and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten and EU fines (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), costs connected with a misdrijf (crime) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and dwangsommen under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (onderdeel i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints Geldboetes 0%. Costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible, while costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring (forfeiture). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted are excluded, and the besluit gives the example of a conviction for possession of hennepplanten but not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, sections 1.1 and 2).

### 3.28 Gemengde kosten in de Vpb, lichaam with employees

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The rule applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity) does it apply once, at the parent (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.4). The fixed amount that is compared with the 0,4% is the art. 3.15 amount of € 4.800 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

All input amounts below are invented round amounts. The rates and thresholds are from section 2. Every product is written with two decimals before rounding. No step below rounds.

### Example 1: Gemengde kosten, drempel against the 80% keuze

**Calculated example.**

Case A, € 9.000,00 of gemengde kosten in 2022.
- Drempel route: € 9.000,00 minus € 4.800,00 = € 4.200,00 deductible.
- Keuze route: 80% of € 9.000,00 = € 7.200,00 deductible.
- The keuze route deducts more.

Case B, € 30.000,00 of gemengde kosten in 2022.
- Drempel route: € 30.000,00 minus € 4.800,00 = € 25.200,00 deductible.
- Keuze route: 80% of € 30.000,00 = € 24.000,00 deductible.
- The drempel route deducts more.

Case C, € 3.000,00 of gemengde kosten in 2022.
- Drempel route: the drempel of € 4.800,00 is not reached, so nothing is deductible.
- Keuze route: 80% of € 3.000,00 = € 2.400,00 deductible.
- The keuze route deducts more.

### Example 2: Reis en verblijf voor congressen en cursussen

**Calculated example.**

A congress in 2022 costs € 1.200,00 in travel and stay, and a second congress costs € 800,00.
- Total travel and stay: € 1.200,00 plus € 800,00 = € 2.000,00.
- Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f makes travel and stay not deductible above € 1.500,00 per year.
- If attendance is not necessary, the excess is not deductible: € 2.000,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 500,00 not deductible. The remaining € 1.500,00 is treated as gemengde kosten under art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 2. With no other gemengde kosten, the drempel route gives € 0,00 because € 1.500,00 is below the drempel of € 4.800,00. The 80% keuze gives 80% of € 1.500,00 = € 1.200,00.
- Art. 3.16 lid 8 lifts the € 1.500,00 cap when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary.
- If attendance is necessary, the full € 2.000,00 is gemengde kosten. With no other gemengde kosten, the drempel route gives € 0,00 because € 2.000,00 is below the drempel of € 4.800,00. The 80% keuze gives 80% of € 2.000,00 = € 1.600,00.

### Example 3: Kilometervergoeding and public transport

**Calculated example.**

A private car is used for 4.500 business kilometres in 2022.
- 4.500 km times € 0,19 = € 855,00 deductible.
- Brandstof, verzekering, tol en parkeren cannot be added separately, because the € 0,19 is all-in.
- A household member is treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). If the partner drives another 1.000 business kilometres for the same enterprise in that car, the same rate applies: 5.500 km times € 0,19 = € 1.045,00.
- A business train trip that is not travel for a congres or cursus costs € 240,00. Openbaar vervoer is deductible at 100% of the actual costs, so € 240,00.

### Example 4: Verhuizing

**Calculated example.**

Moving the inboedel costs € 1.200,00.
- Deduction for the move: € 1.200,00 plus € 7.750,00 = € 8.950,00.
- Dubbele huisvesting outside the place of residence can be added for at most two years.

### Example 5: Bijtelling 22% with the autokosten cap

**Calculated example.**

A car in the ondernemingsvermogen has a catalogusprijs including btw and bpm of € 30.000,00 and is used privately for more than 500 km.
- Onttrekking before the autokosten cap: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation. If those costs are € 5.200,00, the onttrekking is € 5.200,00.
- With a rittenregistratie showing 480 private km, the onttrekking is nil.

### Example 6: Pre-2017 car, minimum 25%

**Calculated example.**

A car first admitted in 2015 has a waarde of € 20.000,00.
- Art. 10a.4 sets the minimum percentage of art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a at 25% instead of 22% for this car.
- Onttrekking before the autokosten cap: 25% of € 20.000,00 = € 5.000,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation.
- If those costs are € 4.000,00, the onttrekking is € 4.000,00.
- If those costs are € 5.500,00, the onttrekking is € 5.000,00.

### Example 7: Zero-emission car first admitted in 2022

**Calculated example.**

Car A, waarde € 40.000,00, first admitted in 2022, 0 g CO2 per km.
- Reduction: 6% of € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00. The cap is € 2.100,00, so the reduction is € 2.100,00.
- Onttrekking before the autokosten cap: 22% of € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00 minus € 2.100,00 = € 6.700,00.
- Check with the other rendering: 16% of € 35.000,00 = € 5.600,00 plus 22% of € 5.000,00 = € 1.100,00 gives € 6.700,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation.

Car B, waarde € 30.000,00, first admitted in 2022, 0 g CO2 per km.
- Reduction: 6% of € 30.000,00 = € 1.800,00, below the cap of € 2.100,00.
- Onttrekking before the autokosten cap: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00 minus € 1.800,00 = € 4.800,00.
- Check with the other rendering: 16% of € 30.000,00 = € 4.800,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation.

### Example 8: Youngtimer

**Calculated example.**

A car of the business was first taken into use more than 15 years ago. The WEV is € 8.000,00.
- Onttrekking before the autokosten cap: 35% of € 8.000,00 = € 2.800,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the onttrekking at the total autokosten including depreciation. If those costs are € 2.000,00, the onttrekking is € 2.000,00.

### Example 9: Bijtelling fiets

**Calculated example.**

A business e-bike with a consumentenadviesprijs of € 2.200,00 is also available privately.
- Onttrekking before the costs-borne cap: 7% of € 2.200,00 = € 154,00.
- If the costs borne are € 100,00, the onttrekking is capped at € 100,00.
- If the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil (art. 3.20a lid 5, retroactive to 1 January 2020).

### Example 10: Btw-correctie on a private car

**Calculated example.**

A private car with a catalogusprijs including btw and bpm of € 30.000,00 is used for business, and there is no sluitende kilometeradministratie.
- Year-end correction: 1,5% of € 30.000,00 = € 450,00 in rubriek 1d.
- Part year, six months: € 450,00 divided by 2 = € 225,00.
- The correction is never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance.

### Example 11: The € 450 rule and labelling of computers

**Calculated example.**

- A laptop of € 400,00 is deducted in one go in the year of purchase.
- A laptop of € 1.200,00 used for several years is depreciated taking the restwaarde into account.
- A laptop used 95% for business is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen.
- A laptop used 90% or more privately is verplicht privévermogen and 0% deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### Example 12: Prestatiebeurs, mbo

**Official example**, copied from FISIN 2022, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten.

Mbo from August 2012 to July 2016, the diplomatermijn ends in August 2022. Per study year the deduction is the lower of the fixed amount and the actual prestatiebeurs.

| Study year | Fixed amount | Prestatiebeurs | Lower amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | € 706,00 | € 333,00 | € 333,00 |
| 2 | € 1.693,00 | € 800,00 | € 800,00 |
| 3 | € 1.693,00 | € 800,00 | € 800,00 |
| 4 | € 988,00 | € 467,00 | € 467,00 |

- Total of the lower amounts: € 333,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 467,00 = € 2.400,00.
- Minus the drempel per person: € 2.400,00 minus € 250,00 = € 2.150,00 deductible.

### Example 13: Prestatiebeurs, part-year hbo or wo

**Calculated example.**

An hbo study year is only partly in the case, five months. Assume the actual prestatiebeurs is at least the cap.
- Cap: 5 months times € 203,59 = € 1.017,95.
- Minus the drempel per person: € 1.017,95 minus € 250,00 = € 767,95 deductible.
- If the actual prestatiebeurs is lower than € 1.017,95, that lower amount applies before the drempel.

### Example 14: STAP-budget maximum

**Calculated example.**

A scholing costs € 1.200,00 including btw.
- The STAP-budget maximum is € 1.000,00 including btw per scholing.
- The lower of € 1.200,00 and € 1.000,00 is € 1.000,00.
- There are six application windows per calendar year.

### Example 15: Gemengde kosten in a BV

**Calculated example.**

A BV has employees. The wage total is € 1.500.000,00.
- 0,4% of € 1.500.000,00 = € 6.000,00, which is higher than € 4.800,00, so the drempel is € 6.000,00.
- Gemengde kosten of € 10.000,00, drempel route: € 10.000,00 minus € 6.000,00 = € 4.000,00.
- Keuze route at 73,5%: 0,735 times € 10.000,00 = € 7.350,00.
- The keuze route deducts € 7.350,00.

A second BV has employees and a wage total of € 1.000.000,00.
- 0,4% of € 1.000.000,00 = € 4.000,00, which is lower than € 4.800,00, so the fixed drempel of € 4.800,00 applies.

### Example 16: Items at 0%

**Calculated example.**

- A Wahv verkeersboete of € 300,00: € 300,00 times 0% = € 0,00 deductible (art. 3.14 lid 1).
- A private telefoonabonnement of € 40,00 per month: € 40,00 times 0% = € 0,00 deductible, while the business calls made through it are deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a).
- For a workspace in a private home, a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig, so the workspace costs are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 1 unless the workspace is a zelfstandig part and the income test is met. The same test applies under art. 3.16 lid 13 to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The restriction for an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf was added only from 1 January 2025 by art. 3.16 lid 14.
- Vergoeding partner of € 4.000,00 is below € 5.000,00 and is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 4).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

- Gemengde kosten drempel: € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 1).
- Zero-emission reduction for a car first admitted in the year: a 2021 admission got 10% off capped € 4.000, rendered as 12% up to € 40.000. A 2022 admission gets 6% off capped € 2.100, rendered as 16% up to € 35.000. A car first admitted in 2021 keeps the reduction that applied at first admission for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (art. 3.20 lid 11). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Scholingsuitgaven: the persoonsgebonden aftrek ends. In 2021 art. 6.27 to 6.30 still applied with a drempel of € 250 and a maximum of € 15 000 outside the standaardstudieperiode (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). In 2022 art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), and the Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven took effect on 1 January 2022 by Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
- STAP-budget starts on 1 January 2022 as the non-fiscal replacement, with a maximum of € 1.000 including btw per scholing and six application windows per calendar year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).
- Prestatiebeurs case: the route continues under art. 10a.16 for study years up to and including 2014/2015. FISIN 2022 gives the caps € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for mbo, € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for hbo or wo, and the € 250 drempel per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- Unchanged from 2021 to 2022: kilometrevergoeding € 0,19 per km, bijtelling auto 22%, pre-2017 car at least 25%, youngtimer 35% of the WEV for cars more than 15 years, cap of € 1.500 on travel and stay for congressen en cursussen, verhuizing inboedel plus € 7750, bijtelling fiets 7%, werkruimte thuis 0%, telefoonabonnement thuis 0%, private computers en apparatuur 0%, geldboetes 0% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- The text of art. 3.14 and art. 3.16 is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. The only change is art. 3.16 lid 14 from 1 January 2025, which is after 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Deduct costs excluding btw when the btw is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the btw may not be deducted, deduct the costs including btw in the IB return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Under the KOR (kleineondernemersregeling, small business scheme) no btw is charged or paid, no btw on business costs and investments is deducted (also not btw paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need herziening (revision), the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Keep receipts and invoices for purchases and costs, and keep them for purchases made before the start of the business too (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Keep proof of travel costs, for example the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).
- Keep a rittenregistratie or another record for the at most 500 private km. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, though trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).
- The 80% election for gemengde kosten is made in the return, so note the choice for the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 5).
- Private use of a car or fiets in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking and belongs in the winstberekening (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.20 and art. 3.20a, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
- Record study costs as business costs only when they maintain existing vakkennis. The persoonsgebonden route no longer exists for 2022. A prestatiebeurs deduction is claimed in the return year in which the diplomatermijn has passed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- For a BV record the wage total and the gemengde kosten per body, because the Vpb variant applies per body and only once in a fiscale eenheid, at the parent (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.4).
