# Box 2 en aanmerkelijk belang 2022

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2022 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Box 2 covers the income of a taxpayer with an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial shareholding), mainly dividends, deemed benefits and gains on shares. For 2022 the box has one flat rate of 26,9% with no bracket, the same rate as 2021, and the year's law is fully enacted. The main points for a dga (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, director and major shareholder) or any shareholder in a BV are the 5% test of art. 4.6 with its new sub e, the 5,53% forfaitair voordeel for a vbi (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling, exempt investment institution) and comparable foreign investment companies, and the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) fixed amount of € 48.000. The Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap was enacted on 21 December 2022 but applies only from 1 January 2023, so it does not touch 2022 itself.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

### Box 2 rate (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

For 2022 there is one flat rate of 26,9% on the whole box 2 income and no bracket ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). FISIN 2022 states 26,90% ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The same one-rate structure held from 2020, with 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023. From 1 January 2024 a two-bracket table applies, with 24,5% up to the bracket boundary and 33% above it in 2024, lowered to 31% from 2025 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The second bracket was announced in Belastingplan 2023 ([rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024)). The rate applies per taxpayer ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

The belastingkorting (tax credit) on an unoffset loss is 26,9% in 2022, unchanged from 2021 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.53 lid 2 prints 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts. From the 2024 text the percentage is the one in the first line of the fourth column of the art. 2.12 table, which is 24,5% ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Lid 1 allows the conversion on request if the taxpayer and the partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death ([art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang, offset period (art. 4.49)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang (loss from a substantial shareholding) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose ([art. 4.49, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment and is open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)). The text of art. 4.49 is the same in the 2020 to 2027 versions ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states that the loss is "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Belastingkorting period and further conditions (art. 4.53 lid 4)

Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking that is open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8, published on 26 September 2023 and updated on 22 July 2026, tests the condition for the tax year and not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block the conversion, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([Kennisgroep](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)).

### Aanmerkelijk belang, the 5% test (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds winstbewijzen (profit-sharing certificates) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a, or (e) in the 2022 text holds a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit ([art. 4.6, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub e is new in the 2022 text and is not in the 2021 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). In sub d the word "tot" of the 2021 text reads "om" in the 2022 text. Sub f is not part of the 2022 text, it was added later by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445), retroactive to 1 January 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). The Belastingdienst adds that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Meesleep and meetrek (art. 4.7 to 4.10)

Once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that company also belong to it (meesleepregeling, art. 4.9). Shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner, or a blood or aanverwant in the rechte lijn (blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line) of the taxpayer or the partner, has one, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang (meetrekregeling, art. 4.10). The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The text of art. 4.7 to 4.10 is identical in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.11) and fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)

Shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases) ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. The art. 4.11 text is the same in the 2020 to 2027 versions. Art. 4.16 lists as a sale: inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen, becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie, shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The art. 4.16 text is identical in the 2020 to 2027 versions ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Box 2 income of a minor child

The taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (art. 4.6). Gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen of the taxpayer and the partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return (art. 2.17 lid 2). With fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang may be divided, and "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own rate applies to his share ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). No primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two partners, so no combined figure is given here.

### Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

Income from aanmerkelijk belang is reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen (disposal gains), minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) (art. 4.12). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions. Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vbi or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief-lenen fiction (sub f) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available. Salary from the company is box 1 loon ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The voordeel on a transfer is the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price) minus the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price). The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs. Special cases are inheritance, gift with or without doorschuiving (rollover), reaching 5% during the year and an onzakelijke prijs (non-arm's-length price). At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Interest on a loan taken to buy the shares is deductible. Dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing (withholding tax credit) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14 lid 1)

The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned. For 2022 the percentage is 5,53% ([art. 4.14 lid 1, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). The percentage is a yearly value, 5,69% in 2021 and 5,53% in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessief-lenen fiction, which sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). The art. 4.12a text is identical in the 2020 to 2027 versions.

### APV loss deferral

For an afgezonderd particulier vermogen the loss is deferred and offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The doorschuiving routes on request for gift, inheritance and emigration are reported but not verified for 2022, see section 7 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap

Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022) is cited as the Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap and enters into force on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). Nothing in it applies to 2022. For the years from 2023 the fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the verbonden-person rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Under the later texts the maximum is € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, the amount is measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value, and art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 indexation list, so that maximum is not indexed ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the drempel "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).

### Excessief lenen maximum in the bill after the derde nota van wijziging

The derde nota van wijziging of 25 March 2022 set the excessief-lenen maximum at € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1, following the coalition agreement ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)). The bill had proposed € 500.000. Amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles ([Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The checked 2023 text prints € 700.000 in art. 4.14a and the 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Transitional eigenwoningschuld rule (art. 10a.23 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 4.14a lid 6 excludes an eigenwoningschuld (owner-occupied home debt) to the extent a recht van hypotheek on the home was given to the company. Art. 10a.23 adds that an eigenwoningschuld existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The date 31 December 2022 is the link between the 2022 records and the rules that start on 1 January 2023.

### Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). In 2022 the fixed amount is € 48.000. The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and that is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur. Lower loon: if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable loon, the comparable loon applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower. The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023).

### Gebruikelijk loon comparable-loon test

In the 2020 to 2022 texts the loon from the most comparable employment counts for 75% ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). From the 2023 text it counts for 100% ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).

### Gebruikelijk loon minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The art. 12a rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year. This is lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts and lid 4 from 2023 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Start-up rule gebruikelijk loon (S&O)

For a body with an S&O-verklaring to which the verhoogd starterspercentage applies, the loon was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable loon if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling ([art. 12a lid 3, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). The rule is absent from the 2023 text and the Belastingdienst says "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rule could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2022 could run to 2024.

### Gebruikelijk loon and box 2

The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel. The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### ODV marktrente for 2022

The oudedagsverplichting (ODV, old-age obligation) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year). For 2022 the rate is -0,410%, the December 2021 value ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)). A negative market rate must also be applied ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)). In the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p ([2020 Wet IB text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### ODV instalments (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The ODV can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente, lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. From Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).

### PEB afkoop and conversion window (Stb. 2017, 115)

Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and art. 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38p lid 5 was later filled again by Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, which is the conversion after the instalments have started described above. The afkoop and conversion window ran from 1 April 2017 to 31 December 2019, and V&A 17-007 is marked vervallen ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). The published Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under art. 12 Wet raadgevend referendum, the consolidated text of Stb. 2017, 115 shows "Geldend van 01-04-2017", and the coulance decision of 22 March 2017 let dga's adjust until 30 June 2017 and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Belastingdienst phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). In 2022 the afkoop and conversion routes are closed. A remaining PEB keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially, and "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

### Verkrijgingsprijs after PEB prijsgeven (art. 10a.18 lid 3 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 10a.18 lid 2 values the ODV in the company with the market rate of art. 38p. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking in box 1.

### Non-residents (art. 7.5)

Art. 7.5 confirms the base for a non-resident. The base is an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands-established company that is not part of an enterprise ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A statement that a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht has the same effect could not be confirmed on the fetched Belastingdienst pages and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

Every product below is written with two decimals. No product in these examples needs rounding, so no step is marked rounded or rounded down.

### Official example: an unoffset loss converted into a belastingkorting (FISIN 2022)

Source: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang). Shares were sold in 2021 with an unoffset loss of € 20.000. On request the loss is converted in 2023 into a belastingkorting of € 5.380,00 at the 26,9% of art. 4.53 lid 2, and the remainder can be used until 2030. Arithmetic: 26,9% × € 20.000 = € 5.380,00.

### Calculated example: dividend of € 100.000

Inputs: an aanmerkelijkbelanghouder receives a dividend of € 100.000 in 2022 and has no deductible costs.

Step 1, the dividend is part of the reguliere voordelen, so box 2 income is € 100.000.

Step 2, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

Dividendbelasting withheld by the company is not deducted but credited as voorheffing, so the amount to pay is € 26.900,00 minus that voorheffing. No dividendbelasting rate is in the checked sources, so no voorheffing amount is computed here.

### Calculated example: sale of shares with notary costs and selling costs

Inputs: purchase price € 100.000, notary costs on the purchase € 1.500, sale price € 250.000, selling costs € 2.500.

Step 1, verkrijgingsprijs: € 100.000 + € 1.500 = € 101.500.

Step 2, overdrachtsprijs: € 250.000 − € 2.500 = € 247.500.

Step 3, vervreemdingsvoordeel: € 247.500 − € 101.500 = € 146.000.

Step 4, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 146.000 = € 39.274,00.

### Calculated example: offset of a 2022 loss

Inputs: a verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang of € 20.000 arises in 2022 and there is no box 2 income in 2021.

Step 1, the loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment.

Step 2, it offsets box 2 income of 2021 and, if not used, of 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2027 and 2028, in the order the losses arose.

Step 3, assume box 2 income of € 30.000 in 2023. Then € 20.000 of the loss offsets it and € 30.000 − € 20.000 = € 10.000 remains taxable.

Step 4, box 2 tax for 2023: 26,9% × € 10.000 = € 2.690,00, because the one flat rate of 26,9% still applies in 2023.

### Calculated example: forfaitair voordeel for a vbi

Inputs: value at the start of 2022 € 200.000, percentage 5,53%, no other reguliere voordelen from those shares, no deductible costs, shares held for the whole year.

Step 1, forfaitair voordeel: 5,53% × € 200.000 = € 11.060,00.

Step 2, box 2 income is € 11.060,00.

Step 3, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 11.060 = € 2.975,14.

If the shares were held for part of the year, the forfaitair voordeel is time-apportioned. It is also reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares and never falls below nil.

### Calculated example: excessief lenen as proposed on 25 March 2022

The excessief-lenen fiction is not in force in 2022, so no amount is due for 2022. This example illustrates the bill as it stood after the derde nota van wijziging of 25 March 2022.

Inputs: debts from the own BV of € 800.000 at the end of the year and a maximum of € 700.000.

Step 1, excess: € 800.000 − € 700.000 = € 100.000.

Step 2, the box 2 rate is 26,9% in 2022 and in 2023. At that rate the excess would give 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

The law entered into force on 1 January 2023, the first year of the fiction. This is a calculation of the bill, not of a 2022 rule.

### Calculated example: gebruikelijk loon

Inputs: the most comparable loon is € 60.000, the highest loon within the body is € 52.000, the fixed amount is € 48.000, and the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is more than € 5.000.

Step 1, 75% of the comparable loon: 75% × € 60.000 = € 45.000,00.

Step 2, the highest of the three amounts: € 45.000, € 52.000 and € 48.000, so € 52.000.

Step 3, the loon is at least € 52.000.

If the loon for the work for the body and all connected bodies together had been not more than € 5.000, art. 12a would not apply at all.

### Calculated example: ODV with a negative market rate

Inputs: ODV at the start of 2022 € 200.000, market rate -0,410%.

Step 1, increase: -0,410% × € 200.000 = -€ 820,00.

Step 2, balance at the end of 2022: € 200.000,00 − € 820,00 = € 199.180,00.

A negative market rate must also be applied.

### Calculated example: division between fiscal partners

Inputs: fiscal partners for the whole year, box 2 income € 100.000, division 50/50.

Step 1, partner 1: € 50.000, box 2 tax 26,9% × € 50.000 = € 13.450,00.

Step 2, partner 2: € 50.000, box 2 tax 26,9% × € 50.000 = € 13.450,00.

Step 3, total € 26.900,00, which equals 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

Any other division is allowed as long as the total is 100%, and with a single flat rate in 2022 the total tax in this example does not change.

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

- Aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.6): the 2021 text has sub a to d only, the 2022 text adds sub e, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.6 sub d): the word "tot" of the 2021 text became "om" in the 2022 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14 lid 1): 5,69% in 2021 to 5,53% in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964): € 47.000 in 2021 to € 48.000 in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).
- ODV marktrente: -0,382% for 2021 (December 2020 value) to -0,410% for 2022 (December 2021 value) ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- Start-up rule gebruikelijk loon: the text is unchanged at 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage, but a first use in 2021 could run to 2023 and a first use in 2022 could run to 2024, because the rule could be used at most 3 years and is absent from the 2023 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Excessief lenen: not in force in 2021 and still not in force in 2022, but on 21 December 2022 the law was enacted (Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued 27 December 2022) and it enters into force on 1 January 2023, and the derde nota van wijziging of 25 March 2022 set the maximum at € 700.000 in the bill ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)).
- Unchanged in 2022: the box 2 rate of 26,9% with no bracket, the belastingkorting of 26,9% (art. 4.53 unchanged from 2021), the loss offset period of the previous year and the six following years, the € 5.000 gebruikelijk loon threshold and the 75% comparable-loon test ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 12a 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

- Box 2 income lands in the inkomstenbelasting return for the year as reguliere voordelen minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen, minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (art. 4.12) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Record a dividend from the BV as a reguliere voordeel, and treat dividendbelasting on it as a voorheffing, not as a cost ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Keep the purchase documents for the shares, including notary costs, for the verkrijgingsprijs, and the sale documents for the overdrachtsprijs, because selling costs reduce it ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- A salary from the BV is box 1 loon and is tested against the gebruikelijk loon of art. 12a, not against box 2 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Interest on money lent by the shareholder to the BV is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- When a loss arises, keep the assessment and the separate loss beschikking, because the loss offsets box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years and the beschikking is open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).
- For a later conversion of an unoffset loss into a belastingkorting, the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang, and the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)).
- Declare the box 2 income of minor children with the parent until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For the BV: no excessief-lenen fiction in the 2022 accounts, because the law takes effect on 1 January 2023. Note whether an eigenwoningschuld on the home existed on 31 December 2022 and whether a recht van hypotheek was given to the company, because art. 10a.23 uses that date for the exclusion ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- For a remaining PEB: index the accrued rights every year and value the provision actuarially every year if the scheme provided for indexation, because not indexing yearly "kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).
- Increase the ODV each year with the market rate, -0,410% for 2022 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- The division of the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang between fiscal partners is chosen in the return ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
