## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

A taxpayer with a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year may deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to a percentage of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual and, up to 2022, also by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The percentage in the 2020 text is 13,3%, and the Ministry of Finance table for 2020 says the same (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: percentage x (minimum(income of the previous year, income cap (aftoppingsgrens)) minus franchise) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). Lid 6 and lid 7 of the 2020 text allow the 13,3% and the 6,27 factor to be changed by an order in council (AMvB) linked to the pension reference age (pensioenrichtleeftijd). The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together, and premiums above the allowance (ruimte) are never deductible, but they are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). The percentage stays 13,3% in the 2021 and 2022 texts and becomes 30% only in the 2023-07-01 text, so 13,3% is the 2020 figure, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) ties the 30% to the Future Pensions Act (Wet toekomst pensioenen, Wtp) with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).

### Maximum annual allowance

The Ministry of Finance table "Uitgaven inkomensvoorzieningen" lists € 12.986 as the maximum annual allowance for 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). The same amount follows from the section 2 figures: 13,3% x (€ 110.111 minus € 12.472) = € 12.985,99, rounded to € 12.986 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The maximum binds a taxpayer whose income reaches the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) and who has no pension accrual and no FOR addition (dotatie). During the research phase this amount was derived and UNVERIFIED, and the Ministry table confirms it, see section 7 (same URL).

### Income cap for the contribution base (premiegrondslag)

Art. 3.127 lid 3 sums four components over the previous calendar year: profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming), taxable wage (belastbaar loon), taxable income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and taxable periodic payments (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen) and benefits in kind (verstrekkingen), counted up to the first-mentioned (eerstvermelde) amount (bedrag), the income cap (aftoppingsgrens), and then reduced by the last-mentioned (laatstvermelde) amount (bedrag), the franchise (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In the 2020 text component (a) is the profit from business activities before addition to and reduction of the old-age reserve and before the entrepreneur's allowance (winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek), so the FOR movement and the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) do not shrink this component (same source). Art. 10.2b lid 2 of the 2020 text indexes the income cap with the contract wage development factor (contractloonontwikkelingsfactor) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 10.2b).

### Franchise contribution base

The franchise is the last-mentioned amount (bedrag) that art. 3.127 lid 3 subtracts after the income cap has been applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). It is subtracted once, from the capped sum, so income at or below € 12.472 produces a contribution base of nil and therefore no annual allowance. In the 2020 text the franchise follows art. 10.1 for its indexation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 10.2b).

### Factor A multiplier 6,27

Art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a reduces the outcome of the percentage by 6,27 times the growth (aangroei) of the yearly pension entitlement from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The multiplier converts a yearly pension accrual into a premium equivalent that is taken off the annual allowance. Art. 3.127 lid 5 adds that a taxpayer who (partly) ceases (staakt) may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year (same source). From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction is rewritten to the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor, which is not the 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25).

### Age window for the annual allowance

In the 2020 to 2022 texts, and so in 2020, the taxpayer must not yet have reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The window widens only from the Wtp text of 2023-07-01 to the age five years above the AOW age, which is not the 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In the later FISIN chapters the same wording "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd" appears (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Look-back window for the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte)

Art. 3.127 lid 2 lets unused annual allowance of the preceding period be used on request in the tax return, oldest unused year first (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The 2020 text reads "zeven jaar", which the FISIN 2020 chapter translates into the annual allowance of 2013 to 2019 (same source and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration advises using catch-up allowance before annual allowance so that older unused allowance does not lapse (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). The period becomes "tien jaar" from the 2023-07-01 text, which does not apply to 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance percentage cap

In 2020 the catch-up allowance is at most 17% of the contribution base and at most a euro amount, and both limits apply (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The percentage is applied to the same contribution base as the annual allowance. From the 2023-07-01 text the cap is one flat euro maximum with no percentage and no age split, so the 2020 double cap is specific to the years 2020 to 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, younger group

FISIN 2020 gives € 7.371 as the euro cap for a taxpayer born after 31 August 1963 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Ministry of Finance describes this group as younger than 56 years (jaar) and 4 months (maanden) on 1 January 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). The actual catch-up allowance for this group is the lower of 17% of the contribution base and € 7.371 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, older group

The higher euro amount of € 14.552 applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). FISIN 2020 places a taxpayer born before 1 September 1963 in this group (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The 17% percentage cap still applies alongside the euro amount, so the lower of the two binds (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente), latest start (uiterste start)

For an old-age annuity the first instalment (termijn) is paid at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, with payments running until death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 a). FISIN 2020 gives the example of an AOW age in 2030, so the old-age annuity starts at the latest on 31 December 2035 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). If the annuity (lijfrente) reaches its start date (ingangsdatum) without a start, the one-year conversion rule of the start date subsection applies (same source). On transfer of the business to a successor no annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) is allowed at the successor, while an annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering) at the successor is allowed (same source).

### Annuity savings account or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), minimum term (minimum looptijd)

For an annuity savings account or an annuity investment right the benefit must run at least 20 years, plus the number of years the benefit starts before the AOW year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 works this out with a start at 63 and an AOW age of 67, which gives at least 20 + 4 = 24 years (same source). The same chapter names the annuity insurance, the annuity savings account and the annuity investment right as the three product forms (same source).

### Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente), maximum term (uiterste looptijd)

A survivor annuity for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and for an account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) the term is at least 20 years or at least 5 years for children under 30 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 gives two official examples: a child aged 21 at death has a term (looptijd) of at least 5 years and stops at the latest after 9 years, and a child aged 27 at death stops after 3 years (same source). The start deadline after a death is handled in the survivor annuity term subsection below (same source).

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c covers the temporary old-age annuity: at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). For 2020 that cap is € 22.089 per year (same source). FISIN 2020 and the Ministry of Finance table give the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Temporary old-age annuity above the cap

FISIN 2020 adds that above the cap of € 22.089 per year the premiums are deductible only if the benefits (uitkeringen) run at least 20 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). So a temporary annuity that pays more than the cap with a shorter term does not simply qualify against the normal five-year term. The cap is tested at the moment the premium is paid (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c).

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

The bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) is available only for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The benefit ends at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching AOW age or in the year of starting a pension (pensioen). The yearly maximum is € 63.288 (same source). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text, so the FISIN 2020 chapter is the 2020 source for it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.125).

### Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

On cessation (staking), part of the profit from business discontinuation may be converted into an annuity, with a deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation and capped at a tier maximum minus the provisions already built: the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.129). For 2020 art. 3.129 lid 2 a sets the tier a maximum at € 467.044 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 a). Tier a applies when the age at cessation is at most five years below the AOW age, or the taxpayer is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity starting within six months, or the cessation happens by death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129). The FISIN 2020 chapter lists what is deducted from the profit from business discontinuation first: the pension rights charged to profit, business termination payments (bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen), the FOR at the start of the year, annuity premiums deducted since 2001 except the basic deduction (basisaftrek) to 2002, deducted contributions and earlier conversions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

Tier b has a maximum of € 233.530 in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 b). It applies when the age at cessation is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or when the annuity starts immediately (same source). The same reduction for provisions already built and the same cap at the profit from business discontinuation apply as in tier a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.129).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

Tier c has a maximum of € 116.771 in 2020 and covers the other cases (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 c). A taxpayer who does not meet the tier a or tier b age or disability conditions falls into tier c (same source).

### Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows commutation of a small annuity in one payment when the yearly amount stays below a statutory amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d). For 2020 that amount is € 4.475 (same source). The Ministry of Finance table labels this item "afkoop lijfrente vrij van revisierente" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Commutation cap at long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes commutation not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, within the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 9 c). The 2020 cap is € 41.791 (same source). The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment (termijn). The research phase had this cap as unknown, and the 2020 law text supplies it, see section 7 (same source).

### Old-age reserve addition (FOR toevoeging)

Access to a FOR addition requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.67). Art. 3.68 lid 1 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit (winst) with a euro cap, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit, and art. 3.68 lid 2 adds that the addition may not exceed the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) (same source, art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2). For 2020 the euro cap is € 9.218 (same source). The Ministry of Finance 2020 table confirms 9,44% and € 9.218 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in its profit and loss account (winst en verliesrekening) chapter, which is a page error, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). Decrease (afname) of the FOR follows art. 3.70 lid 1: by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the business capital at cessation, at AOW age at the start of the year, or on failing the hours criterion this year and last year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.70 lid 1). The FOR to annuity conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022, and the converted amount must be added to the profit (same source, art. 3.128, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the business and takes the reserve over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### Annuity start date (ingangsdatum) term

An annuity that reaches its start date must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 gives both cases: a start date in 2019 means convert or start by 31 December 2020, and a start date in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021 (same source). Extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). If an annuity stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation or unblocking (deblokkering), the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due (same source). FISIN 2020 states that the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid, a sentence dropped from FISIN 2021 (same source). A refund of a deducted premium is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest (revisierente), and for an annuity only if the refund is undone within 30 days (same source).

### Survivor annuity term

A survivor annuity must start within 2 full calendar years after the year of death (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 states that a death in 2019 means the annuity must start by 31 December 2021 (same source). Extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). The term rules for these annuities (lijfrenten), including the end at the latest at age 30, are in the survivor annuity maximum term subsection above (same source).

### Payment deadline FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion

Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year in which they are paid or offset (verrekend) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130 lid 1). Art. 3.130 lid 2 adds that premiums for the profit from business discontinuation conversion of art. 3.129, for the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, for the FOR conversion of art. 3.128 may on choice be counted in the year itself when they are paid within six months after the year end (same source, art. 3.130 lid 2). For the 2020 year this means a payment made before 1 July 2021 can still be counted in 2020 (same source). FISIN 2020 prints "in 2020 of vóór 1 juli 2020" for the FOR conversion, which conflicts with the law, while its profit from business discontinuation line gives before 1 July 2021, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### AOV premium with periodic benefits

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind (periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) in respect of disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen), and the article puts no allowance cap on them (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c). FISIN 2020 says the AOV premium can be "volledig aftrekken" (fully deducted) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). For an entrepreneur the AOV premium is not a business cost but an expenditure for other income provisions, and the periodic benefits are income from former employment with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Zvw premiums (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). A mandatory occupational (beroeps) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie), and it is deductible as a business cost with a restriction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### AOV premium with a lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and it counts in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). FISIN 2020 lists this as the exclusion "verzekeringen die een bedrag ineens uitkeren" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The rule of thumb on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page is: if the premium is not deductible, the payout is not taxed, and the other way round (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium

A mandatory occupational (beroeps) or industry-wide pension premium is not an annuity premium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). It is deductible as a business cost of the business, with a restriction (same source). A self-employed person may join the sectoral pension fund (sectoraal pensioenfonds) of the branch when the fund allows it, and the premiums are then deductible, with the test (proef) running until 1 July 2028 and art. 10a.26 keeping the experimental provision (experimenteerbepaling) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) pension schemes inside the pension definitions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 10a.26).

### Refund of a deducted premium

A refund of a premium that was deducted is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest, and for an annuity the refund only escapes revision interest when it is undone within 30 days (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). By contrast, when an annuity stops meeting the conditions and has not started paying, the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid, and revision interest is due in most cases (same source). Both rules sit in the same FISIN 2020 chapter on expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen).
