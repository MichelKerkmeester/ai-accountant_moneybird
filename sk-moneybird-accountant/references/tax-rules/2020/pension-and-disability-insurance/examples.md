## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Calculated example 1: annual allowance (jaarruimte) with income below the income cap (aftoppingsgrens)

Fact pattern: a self-employed person (zelfstandige) has profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) of € 60.000 in 2019. That amount is the profit before addition to and withdrawal from the old-age reserve (winst vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve) and before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek), and there are no other income components in 2019. There is no pension accrual and no addition (toevoeging) or reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) in 2019. The annual allowance for the 2020 tax return (aangifte) is based on the income of 2019.

Step 1: the income components are counted up to the income cap: min(€ 60.000, € 110.111) = € 60.000 (art. 3.127 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
Step 2: the franchise is subtracted: € 60.000 - € 12.472 = € 47.528. This is the contribution base (premiegrondslag).
Step 3: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 47.528 = € 6.321,22 (€ 6.321,224 rounded down).
Step 4: the factor A reduction is nil and the addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve) over 2019 is € 0, so no reduction under art. 3.127 lid 4 applies and the outcome stays € 6.321,22.
Step 5: the maximum annual allowance of € 12.986 is not binding (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf), so the annual allowance for the 2020 tax return is € 6.321,22.

### Calculated example 2: the income cap caps the contribution base

Fact pattern: the person of example 1 has profits from business activities of € 150.000 in 2019 instead of € 60.000, with the same further facts (no pension accrual, no FOR movement, no other income components).

Step 1: the income components are counted up to the income cap: min(€ 150.000, € 110.111) = € 110.111.
Step 2: the franchise is subtracted after that cap, so it is still deducted in full: € 110.111 - € 12.472 = € 97.639. This is the contribution base.
Step 3: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 97.639 = € 12.985,99 (€ 12.985,987 rounded).
Step 4: the maximum annual allowance of € 12.986 is higher than € 12.985,99, so the maximum is not binding and the annual allowance for the 2020 tax return is € 12.985,99 (art. 3.127 lid 1 and lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127, and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Calculated example 3: pension accrual reduces the annual allowance

Fact pattern: the same situation as in example 1, except that the person has a growth of the annual pension entitlement (aangroei van het jaarlijkse pensioenrecht) from additional service time (extra diensttijd) of € 1.000 in 2019, which counts for the art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a reduction.

Step 1: the contribution base is € 47.528 as in example 1, so 13,3% x € 47.528 = € 6.321,22 (€ 6.321,224 rounded down).
Step 2: the factor A reduction is 6,27 x € 1.000 = € 6.270,00.
Step 3: the annual allowance is € 6.321,22 - € 6.270,00 = € 51,22. This is below the maximum of € 12.986, so the maximum does not reduce it further (art. 3.127 lid 1 and lid 4 sub a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Calculated example 4: a FOR addition (dotatie) reduces the annual allowance to nil

Fact pattern: a self-employed person meets the conditions for a FOR addition (art. 3.67: the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet AOW age at the start of 2019). The profits from business activities are € 30.000 in 2019, with no pension accrual and no other income components. In 2019 the addition to the old-age reserve (toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve) is € 2.832 and the reduction is € 0.

Step 1: the contribution base uses the profit before addition to and withdrawal from the old-age reserve and before the entrepreneur's allowance and subtracts the franchise: min(€ 30.000, € 110.111) - € 12.472 = € 30.000 - € 12.472 = € 17.528.
Step 2: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 17.528 = € 2.331,22 (€ 2.331,224 rounded down).
Step 3: the outcome is also reduced by the addition to the old-age reserve over 2019, that is the addition minus the reduction: € 2.832 - € 0 = € 2.832 (art. 3.127 lid 4 sub b).
Step 4: € 2.331,22 - € 2.832,00 = -€ 500,78, so the reduction exceeds the outcome. A negative annual allowance is not possible, so the annual allowance is nil (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Calculated example 5: the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) cap

Fact pattern: the person of example 1 was born after 31 August 1963 and has € 20.000 of unused annual allowance from 2013 to 2019. The contribution base is € 47.528.

Step 1: the percentage cap is 17% of the contribution base: 17% x € 47.528 = € 8.079,76.
Step 2: the euro cap for a person born after 31 August 1963 is € 7.371 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
Step 3: both caps apply, and the unused annual allowance of € 20.000 is higher than the lower cap of € 7.371, so the catch-up allowance is € 7.371.
Step 4: together with the full annual allowance of example 1 the deduction allowance for the 2020 tax return is € 6.321,22 + € 7.371,00 = € 13.692,22. The catch-up allowance is used on request in the tax return, oldest unused year first (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Calculated example 6: FOR addition

Fact pattern: a self-employed person meets the hours criterion (urencriterium) and has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2020 (art. 3.67). The profits from business activities for 2020 are € 60.000. The pension premiums charged to the profit (winst) are € 2.000 and the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) is € 6.000.

Step 1: the percentage is applied: 9,44% x € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
Step 2: the cap of € 9.218 is not binding.
Step 3: the pension premiums charged to the profit reduce the addition (art. 3.68 lid 1): € 5.664,00 - € 2.000,00 = € 3.664,00.
Step 4: the addition is also limited to the growth of the business capital (art. 3.68 lid 2). The growth of € 6.000 is higher than € 3.664,00, so no further reduction applies and the addition is € 3.664,00 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.67 and art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### Official example 7: FOR decrease (afname)

FISIN 2020 works out the decrease limit for an old-age reserve that is larger than the business capital: the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen, eigen vermogen plus oudedagsreserve) is € 3.000 and the FOR is € 4.000, so the FOR must decrease by € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### Calculated example 8: profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion cap

Fact pattern: at the cessation (staking) the person's age is at most five years below the AOW age, so the tier a maximum of art. 3.129 lid 2 applies. The profit from business discontinuation is € 500.000. There is no old-age reserve, no pension rights charged to profit and no earlier annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) or earlier conversions to take into account.

Step 1: the tier a maximum is € 467.044 (art. 3.129 lid 2 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
Step 2: the deduction is also limited to the profit from business discontinuation. The profit from business discontinuation of € 500.000 is higher than the tier a maximum, so the tier a maximum is the binding cap.
Step 3: at most € 467.044 of annuity premiums can therefore be deducted against the profit from business discontinuation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Calculated example 9: commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente)

Fact pattern: an annuity pays a yearly benefit (uitkering) of € 4.000. That is below the 2020 amount of € 4.475, so a commutation in one payment is allowed under art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d). If the same annuity paid € 5.000 per year, the amount would be above € 4.475 and this small-annuity exception would not apply.

### Calculated example 10: AOV premium deductible or not

Fact pattern: an entrepreneur pays an AOV premium of € 3.000 for a policy that pays periodic benefits (uitkeringen). The full premium is deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, with no allowance (ruimte) cap (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). The premium is not a business cost, so it does not reduce the profits from business activities. If the same € 3.000 premium buys a policy that pays a lump sum, the premium is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), a later payout is not box 1 income and it counts in box 3.

### Calculated example 11: temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

Fact pattern: a temporary old-age annuity pays € 20.000 per year for 5 years, starting in the year of AOW age. The term (termijn) of 5 years meets the minimum, the start in the year of AOW age is allowed and the yearly amount of € 20.000 is below the 2020 cap of € 22.089 per year, so the benefits meet the conditions of art. 3.125 lid 1 c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). If the benefit were € 25.000 per year, the amount would be above the cap, and the premiums would be deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Official example 12: start date (ingangsdatum) and survivors (nabestaanden) deadlines

FISIN 2020 works out the deadlines for the 2020 year: a start date in 2019 means convert or start by 31 December 2020, and a start date in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021. For a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) after a death in 2019 the annuity must start by 31 December 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
