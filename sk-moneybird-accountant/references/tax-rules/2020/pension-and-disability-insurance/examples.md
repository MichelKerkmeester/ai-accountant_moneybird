## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Calculated example 1: jaarruimte with income below the aftoppingsgrens

Fact pattern: a self-employed person has a winst uit onderneming of € 60.000 in 2019. That amount is the winst vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek, and there are no other income components in 2019. There is no pension accrual and no toevoeging or afneming of the oudedagsreserve in 2019. The jaarruimte for the 2020 aangifte is based on the income of 2019.

Step 1: the income components are counted up to the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 60.000, € 110.111) = € 60.000 (art. 3.127 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
Step 2: the franchise is subtracted: € 60.000 - € 12.472 = € 47.528. This is the premiegrondslag.
Step 3: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 47.528 = € 6.321,22 (€ 6.321,224 rounded down).
Step 4: the factor A reduction is nil and the dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve over 2019 is € 0, so no reduction under art. 3.127 lid 4 applies and the outcome stays € 6.321,22.
Step 5: the maximum jaarruimte of € 12.986 is not binding (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf), so the jaarruimte for the 2020 aangifte is € 6.321,22.

### Calculated example 2: the aftoppingsgrens caps the premiegrondslag

Fact pattern: the person of example 1 has a winst uit onderneming of € 150.000 in 2019 instead of € 60.000, with the same further facts (no pension accrual, no FOR movement, no other income components).

Step 1: the income components are counted up to the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 150.000, € 110.111) = € 110.111.
Step 2: the franchise is subtracted after that cap, so it is still deducted in full: € 110.111 - € 12.472 = € 97.639. This is the premiegrondslag.
Step 3: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 97.639 = € 12.985,99 (€ 12.985,987 rounded).
Step 4: the maximum jaarruimte of € 12.986 is higher than € 12.985,99, so the maximum is not binding and the jaarruimte for the 2020 aangifte is € 12.985,99 (art. 3.127 lid 1 and lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127, and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Calculated example 3: pension accrual reduces the jaarruimte

Fact pattern: the same situation as in example 1, except that the person has an aangroei van het jaarlijkse pensioenrecht uit extra diensttijd of € 1.000 in 2019, which counts for the art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a reduction.

Step 1: the premiegrondslag is € 47.528 as in example 1, so 13,3% x € 47.528 = € 6.321,22 (€ 6.321,224 rounded down).
Step 2: the factor A reduction is 6,27 x € 1.000 = € 6.270,00.
Step 3: the jaarruimte is € 6.321,22 - € 6.270,00 = € 51,22. This is below the maximum of € 12.986, so the maximum does not reduce it further (art. 3.127 lid 1 and lid 4 sub a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Calculated example 4: a FOR dotatie reduces the jaarruimte to nil

Fact pattern: a self-employed person meets the conditions for a FOR toevoeging (art. 3.67: the urencriterium and not yet AOW age at the start of 2019). The winst uit onderneming is € 30.000 in 2019, with no pension accrual and no other income components. In 2019 the toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve is € 2.832 and the afneming is € 0.

Step 1: the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek and subtracts the franchise: min(€ 30.000, € 110.111) - € 12.472 = € 30.000 - € 12.472 = € 17.528.
Step 2: the percentage is applied: 13,3% x € 17.528 = € 2.331,22 (€ 2.331,224 rounded down).
Step 3: the outcome is also reduced by the dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve over 2019, that is the toevoeging minus the afneming: € 2.832 - € 0 = € 2.832 (art. 3.127 lid 4 sub b).
Step 4: € 2.331,22 - € 2.832,00 = -€ 500,78, so the reduction exceeds the outcome. A negative jaarruimte is not possible, so the jaarruimte is nil (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Calculated example 5: the reserveringsruimte cap

Fact pattern: the person of example 1 was born after 31 August 1963 and has € 20.000 of unused jaarruimte from 2013 to 2019. The premiegrondslag is € 47.528.

Step 1: the percentage cap is 17% of the premiegrondslag: 17% x € 47.528 = € 8.079,76.
Step 2: the euro cap for a person born after 31 August 1963 is € 7.371 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
Step 3: both caps apply, and the unused jaarruimte of € 20.000 is higher than the lower cap of € 7.371, so the reserveringsruimte is € 7.371.
Step 4: together with the full jaarruimte of example 1 the deduction room for the 2020 aangifte is € 6.321,22 + € 7.371,00 = € 13.692,22. The reserveringsruimte is used on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Calculated example 6: FOR toevoeging

Fact pattern: a self-employed person meets the urencriterium and has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2020 (art. 3.67). The winst uit onderneming for 2020 is € 60.000. The pension premiums charged to the winst are € 2.000 and the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen is € 6.000.

Step 1: the percentage is applied: 9,44% x € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
Step 2: the cap of € 9.218 is not binding.
Step 3: the pension premiums charged to the winst reduce the toevoeging (art. 3.68 lid 1): € 5.664,00 - € 2.000,00 = € 3.664,00.
Step 4: the toevoeging is also limited to the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (art. 3.68 lid 2). The growth of € 6.000 is higher than € 3.664,00, so no further reduction applies and the toevoeging is € 3.664,00 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.67 and art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### Official example 7: FOR afname

FISIN 2020 works out the afname limit for an oudedagsreserve that is larger than the ondernemingsvermogen: the ondernemingsvermogen (eigen vermogen plus oudedagsreserve) is € 3.000 and the FOR is € 4.000, so the FOR must decrease by € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### Calculated example 8: stakingswinst conversion cap

Fact pattern: at the staking the person's age is at most five years below the AOW age, so the tier a maximum of art. 3.129 lid 2 applies. The stakingswinst is € 500.000. There is no oudedagsreserve, no pension rights charged to profit and no earlier lijfrentepremies or earlier conversions to take into account.

Step 1: the tier a maximum is € 467.044 (art. 3.129 lid 2 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
Step 2: the deduction is also limited to the stakingswinst. The stakingswinst of € 500.000 is higher than the tier a maximum, so the tier a maximum is the binding cap.
Step 3: at most € 467.044 of lijfrentepremies can therefore be deducted against the stakingswinst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Calculated example 9: afkoop kleine lijfrente

Fact pattern: a lijfrente pays a yearly uitkering of € 4.000. That is below the 2020 amount of € 4.475, so an afkoop in one payment is allowed under art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d). If the same lijfrente paid € 5.000 per year, the amount would be above € 4.475 and this small-lijfrente exception would not apply.

### Calculated example 10: AOV premium deductible or not

Fact pattern: an ondernemer pays an AOV premium of € 3.000 for a policy that pays periodic uitkeringen. The full premium is deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, with no ruimte cap (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). The premium is not a business cost, so it does not reduce the winst uit onderneming. If the same € 3.000 premium buys a policy that pays a lump sum, the premium is not deductible, a later payout is not box 1 income and it counts in box 3.

### Calculated example 11: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

Fact pattern: a tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente pays € 20.000 per year for 5 years, starting in the year of AOW age. The termijn of 5 years meets the minimum, the start in the year of AOW age is allowed and the yearly amount of € 20.000 is below the 2020 cap of € 22.089 per year, so the uitkeringen meet the conditions of art. 3.125 lid 1 c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). If the uitkering were € 25.000 per year, the amount would be above the cap, and the premiums would be deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Official example 12: ingangsdatum and nabestaanden deadlines

FISIN 2020 works out the deadlines for the 2020 year: an ingangsdatum in 2019 means convert or start by 31 December 2020, and an ingangsdatum in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021. For a nabestaandenlijfrente after a death in 2019 the lijfrente must start by 31 December 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
