## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked sources for tax year 2019 are not available, so this file cannot set old 2019 values against new 2020 values. The bullets below state what the sources establish for 2020 itself, and they mark the items that change in later years so that a change from another year is not read into 2020.
- Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage: 13,3% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag) in the law text of 1 January 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The same percentage stays in the 2021 and 2022 texts and only becomes 30% in the 2023-07-01 text, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) ties the 30% to the Future Pensions Act (Wet toekomst pensioenen, Wtp) with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023, so 13,3% is the 2020 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Maximum annual allowance: € 12.986 for 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Contribution base: income cap (aftoppingsgrens) € 110.111 and franchise € 12.472 in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Pension accrual reduction: 6,27 times the growth, plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor), in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age window: not yet AOW age at the start of the year for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The extension to five years above the AOW age comes with the 2023-07-01 text and is not a 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window: seven years in the 2020 text, being the annual allowance 2013 to 2019 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The ten-year period belongs to the 2023-07-01 text and later (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte): 17% of the contribution base plus a euro cap in 2020, € 7.371 for the younger group and € 14.552 for the older group, with both limits applying (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap: € 22.089 per year for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente): € 63.288 per year for 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maxima: € 467.044, € 233.530 and € 116.771 for tiers a, b and c in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2).
- Commutation (afkoop) amounts: € 4.475 for a small annuity (lijfrente) and € 41.791 for the long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) cap in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d and lid 9 c).
- FOR: 9,44% of the profit (winst) with a cap of € 9.218 in 2020, in addition to the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) limit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2). The Ministry of Finance 2020 table gives the same two figures (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Payment timing: premiums count in the year of payment or set-off (verrekening), with a six-month option after the year end for the profit from business discontinuation conversion, the art. 3.127 lid 5 cessation (staking) option and the FOR conversion, so 2020 payments made before 1 July 2021 can be counted in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130).
- AOV: a premium for periodic benefits is fully deductible in 2020 under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, a premium for a lump-sum policy is not deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) and AOV premiums as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in box 1, not as business costs of the business. For the AOV this is expressly stated: as entrepreneur the premium is not a business cost but an expenditure for other income provisions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). Art. 3.124 lid 1 c places the AOV premium in that category and puts no allowance (ruimte) cap on it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c).
- Keep the premium statements per product and per payment date. Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend), and the six-month option of art. 3.130 lid 2 for the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion, the art. 3.127 lid 5 cessation (staking) option and the FOR conversion only works on choice, so the payment date decides which year is claimed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130).
- The annuity (lijfrente) maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together, and premiums above the allowance are never deductible while they are still taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) is claimed on request in the tax return, oldest unused year first, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) advises using it before the annual allowance (jaarruimte).
- For the FOR, record the addition (toevoeging) and the decrease (afname) separately, keep the hours criterion (urencriterium) evidence and the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) figures, because art. 3.68 caps the addition at 9,44% of the profit (winst), at € 9.218 for 2020 and at the growth of the business capital (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.68). The decrease grounds are in art. 3.70 lid 1 (same source, art. 3.70 lid 1). The FOR to annuity conversion is art. 3.128 in 2020, and the converted amount is added to the profit (same source, art. 3.128, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- For a profit from business discontinuation conversion, keep the schedule of the items that come off the profit from business discontinuation, being the pension rights charged to profit, business termination payments (bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen), the FOR at the start of the year, annuity premiums deducted since 2001 except the basic deduction (basisaftrek) to 2002, deducted contributions and earlier conversions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The tier maximum is then reduced by the provisions already built (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129).
- Periodic AOV benefits belong in box 1 as income from former employment relationship, with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing), so record the insurer's payroll taxes (loonheffing) statements with the income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). A lump-sum payout is not box 1 income and is recorded in box 3 (same source).
- When an annuity reaches its start date (ingangsdatum), act within 1 full calendar year, and after a death act within 2 full calendar years, for example a start date in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). Record any commutation (afkoop), unblocking (deblokkering) or change of terms, because the value is then taxed and revision interest (revisierente) is due in most cases (same source). A refund of a deducted premium is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest when it is undone within 30 days (same source).
- A mandatory occupational (beroeps) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is deductible as a business cost with a restriction and is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie), so keep it out of the annuity records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- FOR maximum 2020, conflict between the law and FISIN 2020. The law art. 3.68 and the Ministry of Finance 2020 table give € 9.218, while FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in its profit and loss account (winst en verliesrekening) chapter (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The law text and the Ministry table settle it at € 9.218, and the FISIN figure is a page error.
- FOR payment line 2020, conflict between FISIN 2020 and art. 3.130 lid 2. FISIN 2020 prints "in 2020 of vóór 1 juli 2020" for the FOR conversion, while the law gives six months after the year end, so before 1 July 2021, and the FISIN profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) line itself says before 1 July 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130 lid 2). The law text settles it for before 1 July 2021.
- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2020, provenance. The research phase had € 12.986 as a derived and UNVERIFIED figure. The Ministry of Finance table "Uitgaven inkomensvoorzieningen" lists the same amount, so the figures table marks it Verified with the Ministry table as source (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Commutation (afkoop) cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid), provenance. The research phase had this cap as unknown. The 2020 law text art. 3.133 lid 9 c gives € 41.791 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 9 c).
- The Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), bill 36 912: proposed, not law for 2020. The docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag", the bill was submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026, there was no advisory process (adviestraject) by the fundamental rights (Grondrechten) committee on 2 April, the contribution date (inbrengdatum) was set on 7 April for 20 May, with a technical briefing (technische briefing) on 13 May and a postponement request on 15 May, and the committee input (inbreng) took place on 27 May 2026 with the report document 36 912 nr. 5 published on 29 May 2026. Bill art. 16 gives a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks (weken), while the docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", and the bill text settles it at 104 weeks. The MvT gives a benefit of 70% of the tax base (grondslag), a tax base of at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon) so at most the minimum wage, and a premium set yearly by UWV estimated at about 5,4% of the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) up to the maximum tax base, with MvT examples using a maximum tax base of € 38.000 that give about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit, and UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes (loonheffing). Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force is by royal decree (koninklijk besluit), and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912, [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027): proposed, and not adopted on 2026-10-11. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of art. 3.127 lid 3, and of the pensionable salary (pensioengevend loon), at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the amount would stay € 137.800. The limited inflation correction of 48% of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the annuity (lijfrente) maxima listed in the MvT, being the maximum for annuity provisions (lijfrentevoorzieningen) of art. 3.125, the maximum for an account (rekening) and investment right (beleggingsrecht), the maximum premiums to be taken into account (in aanmerking te nemen premies) of art. 3.127, the profit from business discontinuation maximum of art. 3.129 and the maximum for negative expenditures (negatieve uitgaven) (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): proposed and adjacent to this topic. The internet consultation (internetconsultatie) ran until 29 October 2026, the proposal has a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) leading to a "veilige haven", and the intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) is January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension, so no effect on this topic can be read from it (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
