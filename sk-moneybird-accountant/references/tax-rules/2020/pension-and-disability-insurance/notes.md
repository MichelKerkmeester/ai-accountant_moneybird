## 5. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked sources for tax year 2019 are not available, so this file cannot set old 2019 values against new 2020 values. The bullets below state what the sources establish for 2020 itself, and they mark the items that change in later years so that a change from another year is not read into 2020.
- Jaarruimte percentage: 13,3% of the premiegrondslag in the law text of 1 January 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The same percentage stays in the 2021 and 2022 texts and only becomes 30% in the 2023-07-01 text, and the Belastingdienst ties the 30% to the Wtp with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023, so 13,3% is the 2020 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Maximum jaarruimte: € 12.986 for 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Premiegrondslag: aftoppingsgrens € 110.111 and franchise € 12.472 in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Pension accrual reduction: 6,27 times the aangroei, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor, in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age window: not yet AOW age at the start of the year for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The extension to five years above the AOW age comes with the 2023-07-01 text and is not a 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window: seven years in the 2020 text, being the jaarruimte 2013 to 2019 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The ten-year period belongs to the 2023-07-01 text and later (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Reserveringsruimte: 17% of the premiegrondslag plus a euro cap in 2020, € 7.371 for the younger group and € 14.552 for the older group, with both limits applying (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap: € 22.089 per year for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). Overbruggingslijfrente: € 63.288 per year for 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Stakingswinst maxima: € 467.044, € 233.530 and € 116.771 for tiers a, b and c in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2).
- Afkoop amounts: € 4.475 for a small lijfrente and € 41.791 for the langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid cap in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d and lid 9 c).
- FOR: 9,44% of the winst with a cap of € 9.218 in 2020, in addition to the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen limit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2). The Ministry of Finance 2020 table gives the same two figures (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Payment timing: premiums count in the year of payment or verrekening, with a six-month option after the year end for the stakingswinst conversion, the art. 3.127 lid 5 staking option and the FOR conversion, so 2020 payments made before 1 July 2021 can be counted in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130).
- AOV: a premium for periodic benefits is fully deductible in 2020 under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, a premium for a lump-sum policy is not deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record lijfrentepremies and AOV premiums as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in box 1, not as business costs of the onderneming. For the AOV this is expressly stated: as ondernemer the premium is not a business cost but an uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). Art. 3.124 lid 1 c places the AOV premium in that category and puts no ruimte cap on it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c).
- Keep the premium statements per product and per payment date. Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year they are paid or verrekend, and the six-month option of art. 3.130 lid 2 for the stakingswinst conversion, the art. 3.127 lid 5 staking option and the FOR conversion only works on choice, so the payment date decides which year is claimed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130).
- The lijfrente maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together, and premiums above the ruimte are never deductible while they are still taken into account when the uitkering is taxed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). Reserveringsruimte is claimed on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first, and the Belastingdienst advises using it before the jaarruimte (same source).
- For the FOR, record the toevoeging and the afname separately, keep the urencriterium evidence and the ondernemingsvermogen figures, because art. 3.68 caps the toevoeging at 9,44% of the winst, at € 9.218 for 2020 and at the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.68). The afname grounds are in art. 3.70 lid 1 (same source, art. 3.70 lid 1). The FOR to lijfrente conversion is art. 3.128 in 2020, and the converted amount is added to the winst (same source, art. 3.128, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- For a stakingswinst conversion, keep the schedule of the items that come off the stakingswinst, being the pension rights charged to profit, bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen, the FOR at the start of the year, lijfrentepremies deducted since 2001 except the basisaftrek to 2002, deducted stortingen and earlier conversions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The tier maximum is then reduced by the provisions already built (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129).
- Periodic AOV benefits belong in box 1 as inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing, so record the insurer's loonheffing statements with the income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). A lump-sum payout is not box 1 income and is recorded in box 3 (same source).
- When a lijfrente reaches its ingangsdatum, act within 1 full calendar year, and after a death act within 2 full calendar years, for example an ingangsdatum in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). Record any afkoop, deblokkering or change of terms, because the value is then taxed and revisierente is due in most cases (same source). A refund of a deducted premium is a negatieve uitgave without revisierente when it is undone within 30 days (same source).
- A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is deductible as a business cost with a restriction and is not a lijfrentepremie, so keep it out of the lijfrente records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- FOR maximum 2020, conflict between the law and FISIN 2020. The law art. 3.68 and the Ministry of Finance 2020 table give € 9.218, while FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in the winst en verliesrekening chapter (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The law text and the Ministry table settle it at € 9.218, and the FISIN figure is a page error.
- FOR payment line 2020, conflict between FISIN 2020 and art. 3.130 lid 2. FISIN 2020 prints "in 2020 of vóór 1 juli 2020" for the FOR conversion, while the law gives six months after the year end, so before 1 July 2021, and the FISIN stakingswinst line itself says before 1 July 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130 lid 2). The law text settles it for before 1 July 2021.
- Maximum jaarruimte 2020, provenance. The research phase had € 12.986 as a derived and UNVERIFIED figure. The Ministry of Finance table "Uitgaven inkomensvoorzieningen" lists the same amount, so the figures table marks it Verified with the Ministry table as source (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- Afkoop cap for langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid, provenance. The research phase had this cap as unknown. The 2020 law text art. 3.133 lid 9 c gives € 41.791 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 9 c).
- BAZ, Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, bill 36 912: proposed, not law for 2020. The docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag", the bill was ingediend on 24 March 2026, there was no adviestraject by the Grondrechten committee on 2 April, the inbrengdatum was set on 7 April for 20 May, with a technische briefing on 13 May and a postponement request on 15 May, and the committee inbreng took place on 27 May 2026 with the Verslag document 36 912 nr. 5 published on 29 May 2026. Bill art. 16 gives a wachttijd of 104 weken, while the docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", and the bill text settles it at 104 weken. The MvT gives a benefit of 70% of the grondslag, a grondslag of at most 142,86% of the minimumloon so at most the minimumloon, and a premium set yearly by UWV estimated at about 5,4% of the winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag, with MvT examples using a maximum grondslag of € 38.000 that give about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, and UWV benefits would carry loonheffing. Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force is by koninklijk besluit, and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912, [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Belastingplan 2027: proposed, and not adopted on 2026-10-11. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3, and of the pensioengevend loon, at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the amount would stay € 137.800. The limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the lijfrente maxima listed in the MvT, being the maximum for lijfrentevoorzieningen of art. 3.125, the maximum for a rekening and beleggingsrecht, the maximum in aanmerking te nemen premies of art. 3.127, the stakingswinst maximum of art. 3.129 and the maximum for negatieve uitgaven (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Zelfstandigenwet: proposed and adjacent to this topic. The internetconsultatie ran until 29 October 2026, the proposal has a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets leading to a "veilige haven", and the beoogde inwerkingtreding is January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension, so no effect on this topic can be read from it (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
