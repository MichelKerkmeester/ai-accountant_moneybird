## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

In 2020 box 3 tax is calculated with the old band method (oude berekening) over three bands of the tax base (grondslag) of savings and investments (sparen en beleggen). The box 3 rate is 30% and the values are measured on the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2020. The 2020 law is enacted. Later, the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) of 21 December 2022 can recalculate a 2020 assessment that was not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, but only when the recalculation gives a lower benefit (voordeel). The Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 25 June 2026 confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 have no right to restoration (herstel) of paid box 3 tax over that period.
