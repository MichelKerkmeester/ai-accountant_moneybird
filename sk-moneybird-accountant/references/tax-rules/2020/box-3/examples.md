## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples were found in the checked material for 2020. All examples below are calculated examples.

**Calculated example 1: old band method with a single person.**
Inputs: bank balances (banktegoeden) € 200.000,00, other assets (overige bezittingen) € 0,00, debts (schulden) € 10.000,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person, so tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) € 30.846,00 and debt threshold (schuldendrempel) € 3.100,00.
Step 1: deductible debt = € 10.000,00 - € 3.100,00 = € 6.900,00.
Step 2: return base (rendementsgrondslag) = € 200.000,00 - € 6.900,00 = € 193.100,00.
Step 3: tax base (grondslag) of savings and investments (sparen en beleggen) = € 193.100,00 - € 30.846,00 = € 162.254,00.
Step 4: band 1 = € 72.797,00. Band 2 = € 162.254,00 - € 72.797,00 = € 89.457,00.
Step 5: band 1 benefit (voordeel) = € 72.797,00 × 1,789% = € 1.302,34, rounded.
Step 6: band 2 benefit = € 89.457,00 × 4,185% = € 3.743,78, rounded.
Step 7: total benefit = € 1.302,34 + € 3.743,78 = € 5.046,12.
Step 8: box 3 tax = 30% × € 5.046,12 = € 1.513,84, rounded.

**Calculated example 2: whole year partners.**
Inputs: joint bank balances € 200.000,00, joint debts € 10.000,00. Whole year partners, so joint tax-free allowance € 61.692,00 and joint debt threshold € 6.200,00.
Step 1: deductible joint debt = € 10.000,00 - € 6.200,00 = € 3.800,00.
Step 2: joint return base = € 200.000,00 - € 3.800,00 = € 196.200,00.
Step 3: joint tax base of savings and investments = € 196.200,00 - € 61.692,00 = € 134.508,00.
Step 4: the partners may divide the joint tax base in any way that totals 100%.

**Calculated example 3: the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) recalculation.**
Inputs: bank balances € 100.000,00, other assets € 100.000,00, debts € 0,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person.
Step 1: bank return = € 100.000,00 × 0,04% = € 40,00.
Step 2: other asset return = € 100.000,00 × 5,28% = € 5.280,00.
Step 3: debt return = € 0,00 × 2,74% = € 0,00.
Step 4: return (rendement) = € 40,00 + € 5.280,00 - € 0,00 = € 5.320,00.
Step 5: return base = € 200.000,00 - € 0,00 = € 200.000,00.
Step 6: effective percentage = € 5.320,00 / € 200.000,00 = 0,0266 = 2,66%.
Step 7: tax base of savings and investments = € 200.000,00 - € 30.846,00 = € 169.154,00.
Step 8: restoration of rights benefit = € 169.154,00 × 2,66% = € 4.499,50, rounded.
Step 9: tax under restoration of rights = 30% × € 4.499,50 = € 1.349,85.
Step 10: old band method for the same assets: band 1 = € 72.797,00. Band 2 = € 169.154,00 - € 72.797,00 = € 96.357,00. Band 1 benefit = € 72.797,00 × 1,789% = € 1.302,34, rounded. Band 2 benefit = € 96.357,00 × 4,185% = € 4.032,54, rounded down. Total old benefit = € 1.302,34 + € 4.032,54 = € 5.334,88. Old tax = 30% × € 5.334,88 = € 1.600,46, rounded down.
Step 11: comparison. The restoration of rights benefit of € 4.499,50 is lower than the old benefit of € 5.334,88. Under art. 1 lid 3 the category recalculation applies only if it gives a lower benefit, so the restoration of rights amount would be used if the assessment is in scope and the taxpayer objected.

**Calculated example 4: green investments.**
Inputs: green investments € 50.000,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person.
Step 1: the 2020 exemption cap is € 59.477,00.
Step 2: the exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January, so the exempt amount is € 50.000,00.
Step 3: the credit (korting) is 0,7% of the exempt amount = 0,7% × € 50.000,00 = € 350,00.
Second part: with a whole year partner and green investments of € 130.000,00, the cap is € 118.954,00. The exempt amount is € 118.954,00. The credit is 0,7% × € 118.954,00 = € 832,68, rounded.

**Calculated example 5: actual return.**
Inputs: bank interest € 200,00, value increase of other assets € 1.000,00, interest paid on a box 3 debt € 50,00. Single person.
Step 1: actual return = € 200,00 + € 1.000,00 - € 50,00 = € 1.150,00.
Step 2: no tax-free allowance is subtracted.
Step 3: if the total were negative, it would be € 0,00 and could not be carried to another year.
Step 4: under the rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling), if this actual return is lower than the fictitious benefit, the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). The actual return is at least nil. If there are no personal deductions, the benefit is € 1.150,00. At the 2020 box 3 rate of 30%, the tax is 30% × € 1.150,00 = € 345,00.
For 2020 the online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) can only be used if you received a letter. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) uses the most favourable amount, so you never pay more than the fictitious calculation.
