## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Reference date (peildatum)
The box 3 values for 2020 are measured on the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2020. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) FISIN 2020 page states that the values are on the reference date 1 January 2020. In the old band method the actual composition of the assets does not matter for the band calculation. The value on the reference date determines the return base (rendementsgrondslag). For the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) recalculation, art. 3 uses the bank balances (banktegoeden), other assets (overige bezittingen) and debts (schulden) at 1 January. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)
The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) for 2020 is € 30.846 per person. With a fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) it is € 61.692. The 2020 statute art. 5.5 says "Het heffingvrije vermogen bedraagt € 30.846". FISIN 2020 agrees. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page on tax-free allowance states that the amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. For the actual-return calculation the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) determined that no account may be taken of the tax-free allowance. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.5), and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement.

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)
The debt threshold (schuldendrempel) for 2020 is € 3.100 per person and € 6.200 with a whole-year partner. Only the part of total debts above the threshold is deductible. The statute art. 5.3 lid 3 says "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...". The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the threshold in the return. The threshold doubles for whole-year partners and a joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.3 lid 3), and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.

### Box 3 rate (tarief)
The box 3 rate for 2020 is 30%. This is in art. 2.13. The rate applies to the benefit (voordeel) from savings and investments (sparen en beleggen). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.13) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening.

### Return class (rendementsklasse) percentages
The old band method uses two return classes (rendementsklassen). Return class I (klasse I) is savings and return class II (klasse II) is investments. For 2020 return class I is 0,07% and return class II is 5,28%. Art. 5.2 lid 1 was replaced by Stcrt. 2020, 20572 with effect from 1 January 2020. These percentages are not applied directly to the actual assets. They are mixed in the bands. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.2 lid 1).

### Band 1
Band 1 covers the part of the tax base (grondslag) of savings and investments up to € 72.797. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes the same limit as "tot € 72.798". The statute writes the limit as "niet meer dan" € 72.797 and the page writes "tot" € 72.798. Both describe the same band. In this band 67% is return class I and 33% is return class II. The average return is 1,789%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01.

### Band 2
Band 2 covers the part of the tax base of savings and investments from € 72.797 to € 1.005.572. The page writes € 72.798 to € 1.005.573. In this band 21% is return class I and 79% is return class II. The average return is 4,185%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.

### Band 3
Band 3 covers the part of the tax base of savings and investments above € 1.005.572. The page writes above € 1.005.573. In this band 0% is return class I and 100% is return class II. The return is 5,28%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.

### Restoration of rights category percentages
For the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) recalculation the category percentages for 2020 are bank 0,04%, other assets 5,28%, and debts 2,74%. These are the same figures in the law table. Art. 3 of the Restoration of Rights Act: the benefit (voordeel) is the effective rate of return (effectieve tarief, rendementspercentage) times the tax base of savings and investments. The effective percentage is the return (rendement) divided by the return base (rendementsgrondslag). The return is column I times bank balances (banktegoeden) plus column II times other assets (overige bezittingen) minus column III times debts at 1 January. Art. 3 lid 4: whole-year partners use the joint figures. For 2020 the percentages are as stated. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Restoration of rights scope 2020
The Restoration of Rights Act applies to IB/PVV (income tax and national insurance contributions, premie volksverzekeringen) assessments for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022. This is art. 1 lid 2. Art. 1 lid 3: it applies only where it leads to a lower benefit. Art. 2: the benefit is set at the outcome of art. 3, not below nil. Art. 5: extra personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) follow the partners' chosen division of specific care costs or gifts, with an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve-vermindering) request for another split. Art. 6: the double-taxation relief is recomputed. Art. 7: in force the day after the issue date of the Bulletin of Acts and Decrees (Staatsblad) of 27 December 2022, so in force (in werking) 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2017. The Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support. Mass objectors get restoration of rights for 2017 to 2020 (MvT, explanatory memorandum, memorie van toelichting). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html, and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.

### Most favourable method (meest gunstige methode)
For an assessment in scope of the Restoration of Rights Act the category recalculation applies only if it is lower, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies the most favourable method. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states: "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) 24 December 2021
Supreme Court of the Netherlands 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The explanatory memorandum to the Restoration of Rights Act describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP (First Protocol to the EVRM, European Convention on Human Rights), in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM": the flat-rate system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Flat rates (forfaits) are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The Government of the Netherlands timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban. The claim that the Supreme Court of the Netherlands itself ordered restoration of rights on the basis of the actual return (werkelijk rendement) stays UNVERIFIED, because the Supreme Court of the Netherlands website (hogeraad.nl) returned HTTP 403 again on 2026-10-11. The MvT stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on actual return for the restoration of rights but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.

### Supreme Court of the Netherlands 6 June 2024
The Restoration of Rights Act and the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) still conflict with the EVRM when the flat-rate return (forfaitair rendement) is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024.

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)
Art. 5.13: green investments (groene beleggingen) are not counted as assets up to the stated amount. For 2020 the amount is € 59.477. With a whole-year partner it is € 118.954. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The checked material records the ordering rule from the 2023 text: the exemption applies first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The 2020 text ordering is not stated in the checked material. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.13), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen.

### Credit for green investments (korting voor groene beleggingen)
Art. 8.19 lid 2: the credit for green investments (korting voor groene beleggingen) is 0,7% of the exempt amount on the reference date for 2020. This rate applies from 2020 to 2024. The credit is computed automatically. It is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 8.19 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen.

### Actual-return reporting 2020
For 2020 the actual return (werkelijke rendement) reporting route is an online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"). It is only available with a letter. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states: "Voor 2017 tot en met 2020 zijn alle brieven verstuurd." Without a letter you may not report. A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07, one form per call. Several forms are available via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. The content rules for the actual return are: income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No tax-free allowance is taken into account ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde, property valuation) and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte.

### Rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling) inside the Restoration of Rights Act
The Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195 added art. 6a to the Restoration of Rights Act. Art. IV works back to 1 January 2017 with its own form and six-week rule. Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit (voordeel), the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur), in deviation from the General Administrative Law Act (Algemene wet bestuursrecht, Awb) arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) plus increase in value (vermogensaanwas). The law entered into force (in werking trad) on 19 July 2025 by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025 (Stb. 2025, 196). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html.

### Supreme Court of the Netherlands 25 June 2026
Taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". The Government of the Netherlands timeline now confirms this. The "or objected late" part is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED. The Supreme Court of the Netherlands website (hogeraad.nl) still returns HTTP 403. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.
