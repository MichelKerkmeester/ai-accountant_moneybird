## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

The checked material contains no 2019 figures for box 3. This section therefore lists what began in 2020 where the checked material says so.

- The old band method applies from 2020 to 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.
- Art. 5.2 lid 1 was replaced by Stcrt. 2020, 20572 with effect from 1 January 2020. This set return class I (klasse I) at 0,07% and return class II (klasse II) at 5,28% for 2020. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01.
- The 2020 tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is € 30.846 per person and € 61.692 with a fiscal partner. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_.
- The 2020 debt threshold (schuldendrempel) is € 3.100 per person and € 6.200 with a whole-year partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.3 lid 3).
- The 2020 box 3 rate is 30%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.13) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening.
- The 2020 green investment exemption is € 59.477 and € 118.954 with a whole-year partner. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.13) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_.
- The 2020 credit for green investments (korting groene beleggingen) is 0,7% of the exempt amount. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 8.19 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen.
- The actual-return reporting route for 2020 is an online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) and only with a letter. "Voor 2017 tot en met 2020 zijn alle brieven verstuurd." Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen.
- Later, the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) made a category recalculation possible for 2020 assessments not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, only if lower. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the value of bank balances (banktegoeden), other assets (overige bezittingen) and debts (schulden) on the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2020. These values determine the return base (rendementsgrondslag) for the 2020 box 3 return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_.
- Box 3 is reported in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) for 2020. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes the debt threshold (schuldendrempel) in the return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.
- For a whole-year fiscal partner, the tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) doubles to € 61.692 and the debt threshold doubles to € 6.200. A joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). The partners may divide the joint tax base (grondslag) of savings and investments (sparen en beleggen) in any way that totals 100%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.
- For green investments, record the value on 1 January 2020. The exemption is up to € 59.477, or € 118.954 with a whole-year partner. The credit (korting) is 0,7% of the exempt amount and is computed automatically. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen.
- For actual-return reporting, if you have a letter, use the online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). Keep records of income, value changes, and the two deductible cost types. No deadline is stated on these pages. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement.
- If you did not object for 2020, the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 25 June 2026 means no restoration (herstel) of paid box 3 tax for 2017 to 2020. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.
- The Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) recalculation for 2020 applies only to assessments not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 and only if it gives a lower benefit (voordeel). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The claim that the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) itself ordered restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) on the basis of the actual return stays UNVERIFIED. The Supreme Court of the Netherlands website (hogeraad.nl) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. This would be settled by the ruling text on the Supreme Court of the Netherlands website (hogeraad.nl) or the judiciary website (rechtspraak.nl).
- The "or objected late" part of the Supreme Court of the Netherlands 25 June 2026 ruling is not on the Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) timeline and stays UNVERIFIED. This would be settled by the ruling text on the Supreme Court of the Netherlands website (hogeraad.nl) or the judiciary website (rechtspraak.nl), which returned HTTP 403 for hogeraad.nl.
- Band limit presentation conflict: the statute writes "meer dan / niet meer dan" with € 72.797 and € 1.005.572. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page writes "tot / vanaf" with € 72.798 and € 1.005.573. Both describe the same bands. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.
- Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) entry date conflict: statute art. 7 says the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. The Government of the Netherlands timeline says "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3. The statute is the legal date.
- The checked material records the art. 5.13 lid 2 ordering rule from the 2023 text. The 2020 text ordering of the green exemption over green other assets (overige bezittingen) and green bank balances (banktegoeden) is not stated in the checked material. This would be settled by the 2020 text of art. 5.13 on wetten.overheid.nl.
