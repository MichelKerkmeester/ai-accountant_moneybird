## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Record business income and expenses, keep the records that support the profit calculation, and determine whether the business profit qualifies as profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) for income-tax purposes. The return applies the relevant deductions and reports taxable box 1 income. The checked material does not provide a 2020 filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) for this topic. [FISIN page at https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming]

The Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) exists and is handled in the separate Zvw file. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, URL not supplied in checked material]

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Tax credits (heffingskortingen) in the year state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) is reached:** The 2020 page states no rule or amount. The 2020 transition-year treatment is unknown and UNVERIFIED. The 2020 FISIN page or another official 2020 source stating the rule would settle it. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material]

- **Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) maximum:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans gives € 9.214. The statutory baseline gives € 9.218, but art. 3.68 was not reachable in the fetched 2020 statute text. Reading the article in the 2020 version would settle the conflict. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans, URL not supplied in checked material], [statutory baseline](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

- **Hours criterion (urencriterium) lookback period:** The fetched FISIN page names 2014 to 2018. A topic-file note gives 2015 to 2019, but the checked material supplies no source URL for that note. A direct official 2020 source stating the applicable lookback period would settle it. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, URL not supplied in checked material]

- **End of partner payout:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen says the payout lapses in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen say it ended from 2023. The later pages settle the correction at 2023. The payout for AK and IACK ended from 2023. The AHK payout ended from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later and continued for people born before 1963. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, URLs not supplied in checked material]

- **AHK payout example:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen states that 20% of € 2.711 is € 543. The same checked material records the arithmetic result as € 542,20. The source does not explain the discrepancy. A source explanation of its rounding method would settle it. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material]

- **Zvw base for business profit:** The 2020 value is UNVERIFIED. The checked legal texts for this base are the 2026 versions of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB art. 3.2. Checking the relevant 2020 statutory provisions would settle whether the base in 2020 was taxable business profit after entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). [https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, URLs not supplied in checked material]

- **Business profit sequence:** The checked material verifies parts of the sequence using the 2026 statutory text. The order of the final box 1 tax, rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and tax credits steps is an inference from the article structure and is not printed as one sequence in the checked sources. A 2020 source stating the full sequence would settle it.

- **Source URLs:** The checked material provides identifiers for the 2020 FISIN pages but does not include their exact page URLs. Those identifiers are retained in this file rather than replaced with guessed links. The source register with the original page URLs would settle the missing links.
