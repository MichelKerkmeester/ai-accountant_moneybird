## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2020 box 1 rates, the AOW-age variants, the tax credits (heffingskortingen), rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and how taxable business profit enters box 1. The main points are the distinction between the combined first-bracket rate and the income-tax rate in the statute, the separate AOW-age tables, and the reductions for the general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting), employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) and IACK. The checked 2020 rates and amounts are marked enacted, with unresolved items identified below.
