## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 rates below AOW age

The published combined rate is 37,35% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that boundary. The statutory income-tax rate is 9,70% up to € 34.712, then 37,35% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above. The first combined rate includes income tax and premie volksverzekeringen up to the premium ceiling. Above that ceiling, the statutory income-tax rate equals the published combined rate. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

### Statutory income-tax bands

The statutory schedule provides the income-tax part of the calculation. It is 9,70% up to € 34.712, then € 3.367 plus 37,35% up to € 68.507, then € 15.989 plus 49,50% above € 68.507. The first band is income tax only in the statute. The premie volksverzekeringen is calculated separately on premie-inkomen up to its ceiling. [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 2.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

### Box 1 rates for a full year at AOW age

A taxpayer at AOW age for the full year, born on or after 1 January 1946, has a first rate of 19,45% up to € 34.712. The rate is 37,35% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that. Someone born before 1 January 1946 has the higher first boundary of € 35.375, with the same rates above that boundary. The higher boundary is set out in art. 2.10a. Once AOW age is reached, the AOW premium stops. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 2.10a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/premie_volksverzekeringen, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

The first-bracket rate depends on the month in which the taxpayer reaches AOW age. It ranges from 19,45% when AOW age is reached in January to 35,85% when it is reached in December. The first bracket ends at € 34.712. Brackets 2 and 3 retain the normal rates. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling and components

The premie volksverzekeringen is charged on premie-inkomen up to € 34.712, or up to € 35.375 for someone born before 1 January 1946. Its components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. The total is 27,65%. The AOW component stops when AOW age is reached, which explains the lower first-bracket rate for people at AOW age. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/premie_volksverzekeringen, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Tariefsaanpassing

The tariefsaanpassing applies when grondslagverminderende posten have been deducted. The tax calculated under art. 2.10 is increased by 3,5% of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds € 68.507. The increase cannot exceed 3,5% of the deductions. The effective value of deductions in the highest bracket is at most 46%. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag. The listed deductions include persoonsgebonden aftrek, costs for an owner-occupied home, ondernemersfaciliteiten and the exemption for making assets available to another party. Persoonsgebonden aftrek is set off first against box 1, then box 3, then box 2. [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 2.10 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening, URL not supplied in checked material](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [TA, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Algemene heffingskorting

Every taxpayer can qualify for the AHK. The below-AOW maximum is € 2.711. It is reduced by 5,672% of taxable box 1 income above € 20.711 and reaches nil at € 68.507. For a full year at AOW age, the maximum is € 1.413 and the reduction rate is 2,954%, using the same stated income points. The reduction base in 2020 is taxable income from work and home ownership in box 1. The AHK is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or benefits agency through payroll. There is also a special increase for some people who are not insured for the national insurance schemes and have low income in boxes 1, 2 and 3. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Arbeidskorting

The AK is based on arbeidsinkomen. For an IB entrepreneur, arbeidsinkomen includes business profit before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. It excludes the part earned as a medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser. Below AOW age, the credit builds through the first three bands and then falls by 6% to nil at € 98.604. For a full year at AOW age, the rates and band amounts are lower, and the final reduction rate is 3,124%. The amount is calculated using the applicable bands and the taxpayer's arbeidsinkomen. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

The IACK is 11,45% of arbeidsinkomen above € 5.072, up to € 2.881 at € 30.233. For a full year at AOW age the rate is 5,963%, with a maximum of € 1.502. The child must be under age 12 and born after 31 December 2007. The child must be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months. An eligible child can be the taxpayer's own, step, adopted or foster child, a child from a spouse's earlier relationship, or a fiscal partner's child. A foster child qualifies only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own and no foster-care payment is received. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6-month period ends does not remove the entitlement.

The taxpayer must have no fiscal partner, have a partner for 6 months or less, or have lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner. If incomes are equal, only the older partner qualifies. A co-parent can qualify if the child is cared for in each household for at least 156 days per calendar year. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES can qualify. The IACK is claimed in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not through payroll. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material], [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Payout of unused heffingskortingen to a lower-earning partner

A payout could be available when fiscal partners had been partners for more than 6 months, the partner owed enough tax, and part of the credit could not be set off against the recipient's own tax and premiums. In 2020, 80% of the unused amount was withheld from payout and 20% could be paid. The stated own-income limits were usually € 7.855 for a person born before 1963 and € 1.440 for a person born in 1963 or later. The partner generally had to owe enough tax at income above € 23.790, or € 28.390 at AOW age. A payout was unavailable for someone born after 31 December 1998 who was maintained by parents with at least € 433 per quarter.

The payout for AK and IACK ended from 2023. The AHK payout ended from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later, while it continued for people born before 1963. The 2020 page gives a different end year for the AHK payout, so see section 7. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, URLs not supplied in checked material]

### Tax and premium parts of heffingskortingen

Each heffingskorting has a tax part and premium parts. For 2020 the tax part is 9,70%, and the premium parts are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. Someone not insured for a scheme loses that scheme's part of the credit. A part-year premium obligation gives a time-proportional premium part. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Business profit flow into box 1

For income-tax purposes, the checked cross-year material defines taxable business profit as business profit reduced by ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated on profit after ondernemersaftrek. Taxable box 1 income includes taxable business profit and is reduced by losses that can be set off. The precise statutory wording was checked in the 2026 version, so its application as exact 2020 wording is unresolved.

A taxpayer must run the business for their own account, be able to make binding agreements for it and be liable for its debts to qualify as an ondernemer for income tax. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide the classification. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. [FISIN page at https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming], [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, arts. 3.1 and 3.2, URL not supplied in checked material]

### Ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling

The ondernemersaftrek figures include zelfstandigenaftrek of € 7.030, reduced to € 3.515 for someone at AOW age at the start of the year. The startersaftrek is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age. The startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid depends on prior use in the preceding 5 years and is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 or nil. The meewerkaftrek depends on hours worked by a partner, with the listed bands starting at 525 hours and reaching 4% from 1.750 hours. The stakingsaftrek is capped at € 3.630.

The MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of profit after ondernemersaftrek. The urencriterium is at least 1.225 hours and more time in the business than in other work, subject to the stated exception for someone who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the prior 5 years. The lookback years on the fetched page conflict with another topic-file note. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and MKB, URLs not supplied in checked material]

### Oudedagsreserve

An eligible entrepreneur could add 9,44% of profit to an oudedagsreserve, subject to a maximum and a reduction for beroepspensioenpremie. The FISIN page gives a maximum of € 9.214. A statutory baseline gives € 9.218, but the relevant 2020 article could not be verified in the fetched statute text. An addition requires the ondernemer to meet the urencriterium and to be below AOW age. [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans, URL not supplied in checked material], [statutory baseline, art. 3.68](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)
