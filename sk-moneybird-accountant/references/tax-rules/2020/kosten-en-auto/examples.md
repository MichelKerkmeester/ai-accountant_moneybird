### Official example: bijtelling of 22% and the cap at the costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page has no year label and uses 22%.

Input: catalogue price € 30.000,00, total autokosten € 10.000,00.
Step 1: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00 onttrekking.
Step 2: € 10.000,00 - € 6.600,00 = € 3.400,00 deductible.

### Official example: cap at the actual costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page has no year label.

Input: onttrekking before the cap € 11.000,00, total autokosten € 9.000,00.
Step 1: the onttrekking is € 11.000,00.
Step 2: the cap in art. 3.20 lid 4 limits the onttrekking to the total autokosten of € 9.000,00. The onttrekking is € 9.000,00.

### Calculated example: gemengde kosten, drempel against the 80% election

Input: gemengde kosten of € 6.000,00 in 2020.

Drempel route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 1.300,00 deductible.

80% route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 × 80% = € 4.800,00 deductible.
Difference: € 4.800,00 - € 1.300,00 = € 3.500,00 more deduction with the election.

Break-even.
Costs € 23.500,00. Drempel route: € 23.500,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 18.800,00. 80% route: € 23.500,00 × 80% = € 18.800,00. Equal.
At € 24.000,00 the drempel route gives € 24.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 19.300,00 and the 80% route gives € 24.000,00 × 80% = € 19.200,00. So above € 23.500,00 the drempel route gives more. Below € 23.500,00 the 80% election gives more.

### Calculated example: kilometre allowance

Input: 12.000 business kilometres with a private car in 2020.
Step 1: 12.000 × € 0,19 = € 2.280,00 deductible. Fuel, insurance, toll and parking cannot be added on top.

### Calculated example: zero-emission car first admitted in 2020

Input a: catalogue price € 40.000,00.
Step 1: € 40.000,00 × 22% = € 8.800,00.
Step 2: € 40.000,00 × 14% = € 5.600,00. This is below the € 6.300,00 cap.
Step 3: € 8.800,00 - € 5.600,00 = € 3.200,00 onttrekking. Control: € 40.000,00 × 8% = € 3.200,00.

Input b: catalogue price € 60.000,00.
Step 1: € 60.000,00 × 22% = € 13.200,00.
Step 2: € 60.000,00 × 14% = € 8.400,00, capped at € 6.300,00.
Step 3: € 13.200,00 - € 6.300,00 = € 6.900,00 onttrekking.

### Calculated example: youngtimer

Input: car first taken into use 20 years ago, waarde in het economische verkeer € 10.000,00, total autokosten € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 10.000,00 × 35% = € 3.500,00.
Step 2: the cap applies, so the onttrekking is € 2.000,00.
Step 3: € 2.000,00 - € 2.000,00 = € 0,00 net deduction.

### Calculated example: 500 km rule

Input: private kilometres in 2020 are 480. 480 is at most 500, so the onttrekking is nil. If the record shows 1.200 private kilometres, the car counts as available for private use and the normal onttrekking applies.

### Calculated example: fiets

Input: consumentenadviesprijs € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 × 7% = € 140,00 onttrekking, capped at the costs borne. If the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil.

### Calculated example: verhuizing

Input: cost of moving the inboedel € 1.500,00.
Step 1: € 1.500,00 + € 7.750,00 = € 9.250,00 deductible. Dubbele huisvesting is deductible for at most two years.

### Calculated example: congress travel cap

Input: travel and stay for a congres € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 - € 1.500,00 = € 500,00 not deductible, unless the nature of the work makes attendance necessary.

### Calculated example: scholingsuitgaven

Input: lesgeld and cursusgeld € 1.200,00, no right to studiefinanciering.
Step 1: € 1.200,00 - € 250,00 = € 950,00 as persoonsgebonden aftrek.

### Calculated example: Vpb variant for a BV

Input a: a BV with employees, total loon € 200.000,00.
Step 1: € 200.000,00 × 0,4% = € 800,00.
Step 2: € 800,00 is lower than € 4.700,00, so the fixed amount of € 4.700,00 applies.

Input b: total loon € 1.500.000,00.
Step 1: € 1.500.000,00 × 0,4% = € 6.000,00.
Step 2: € 6.000,00 is higher than € 4.700,00, so € 6.000,00 applies and the election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%.

### Calculated example: kleine aanschaffingen

Input: a printer of € 300,00 and a laptop of € 800,00, both for the business.
The printer of € 300,00 is below € 450,00 and is deducted in one go in 2020.
The laptop of € 800,00 is € 450,00 or more and is used longer than a year, so it is depreciated. There is no one-off deduction of € 800,00.

### Calculated example: 0% items

Input: a Wahv fine of € 250,00. Deduction € 0,00.
Input: a private telephone subscription of € 300,00, of which the business calls are € 90,00. Deduction € 90,00, the other € 210,00 is not deductible.

### Calculated example: btw correction on a private car

Input: catalogue price including btw and bpm € 30.000,00, no sluitende kilometeradministratie, full year.
Step 1: € 30.000,00 × 1,5% = € 450,00 correction in rubriek 1d. The correction is never above the btw deducted on use and maintenance.
Part year control: six months gives € 450,00 × 6/12 = € 225,00.
