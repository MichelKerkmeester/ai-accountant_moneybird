## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the deduction of business costs and the car rules for a ZZP sole trader (self-employed without employees), plus the corporate income tax variant of the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel), in tax year 2020. The most important 2020 items are the threshold (drempel) of € 4.700 for mixed costs (gemengde kosten) with the 80% election, the mileage allowance of € 0,19 for a private vehicle, and the private-use addition for a car (bijtelling) of 22% with separate rules for cars first admitted on or before 31 December 2016, youngtimers and zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020. All values in this file come from the 2020 statute text or from Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages that carry no year label, and all are enacted law for 2020. Nothing in this file is proposed or not yet in force. Points that remain open are in section 7.
