## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material for this file holds no 2019 values for this topic. No old-to-new amounts can be given. The values above are the values printed in the 2020 statute ([art. 3.15 and art. 3.16, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- What began in 2020 for zero-emission cars: for a car first admitted in 2020 the reduction is 14% off with a cap of € 6.300, giving 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. This reduction stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission ([art. 3.20 lid 2 en lid 11, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Rule that applies to 2020 by retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht): the nil withdrawal (onttrekking) for a business bicycle (fiets) stalled no more than incidentally at the home address was added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 ([art. 3.20a, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, as in the small businesses scheme (kleineondernemersregeling, KOR), deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax (IB) return ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [wat betekent meedoen met de KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs (bedrijfskosten) and investments is deducted, also not VAT paid in another EU country, and earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening) ([wat betekent meedoen met de KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)). The choice is per entrepreneur and covers all sub-numbers (subnummers) ([wat betekent meedoen met de KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- Keep receipts and invoices ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Keep mixed costs (gemengde kosten) visible in the records so the threshold or the 80% election can be applied. The election is made in the return ([art. 3.15 lid 5, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- For a car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) to show private use (privégebruik) of 500 km or less, or show it otherwise. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend) ([art. 3.20, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)).
- Record the private-use withdrawal (onttrekking) of a car or bicycle (fiets) as a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking), not as a cost ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).
- Keep fines and penalty payments (geldboetes en dwangsommen) out of the deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) ([art. 3.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Besluit section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).
- For VAT, without conclusive mileage records (zonder sluitende kilometeradministratie) correct at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- In a BV with employees, apply the corporate income tax variant (Vpb) at year end: 0,4% of the wage (loon) if that is higher than the fixed amount, per body ([art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [Besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- FISIN 2020 study costs (studiekosten): the FISIN 2020 page under the slug aftrek_studiekosten did not return content when the sources were checked. Status unknown. The statute settles the 2020 values ([art. 6.27, 6.28 en 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A working FISIN 2020 page would settle the remaining uncertainty.
- Zero-emission 2020 description: one description gives 8% up to € 45.000, another gives 14% off capped at € 6.300. The material resolves these as the same regime. The 2020 statute prints 14% with a maximum of € 6.300 ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Only hydrogen (waterstof) was uncapped in 2020. The statute decides.
- Old-car minimum, 35% against 25%: these are sometimes presented as competing values. They apply to different cars, 35% of the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) for a youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b) and at least 25% instead of 22% for a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 (art. 10a.4) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No open conflict for 2020.
- Per-year status of telephone (telefoon), computers, work space (werkruimte) and fines (geldboetes): this was unknown before the statute texts were read in every version 2020 to 2027. For 2020 the statute settles them ([art. 3.14 en art. 3.16, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The only later change is art. 3.16 lid 14 from 1 January 2025, which does not apply to 2020 ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- 2019 comparison: the checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic. Status unknown. Section 5 can only describe what began in 2020.
