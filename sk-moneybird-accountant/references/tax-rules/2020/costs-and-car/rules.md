## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) (art. 3.15 lid 1)

Costs connected with food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), with representation expenses (representatie, including receptions, festive gatherings and entertainment) and with congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and the like are not deductible up to a yearly threshold (drempel) of € 4.700 in 2020 ([art. 3.15 lid 1, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The 2020 policy decree (beleidsbesluit) prints the same amount as "€ 4.700 (wettekst 2020)" ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The related travel and stay count as part of these posts ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The restriction does not apply when the enterprise itself makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of the turnover ([art. 3.15 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

What falls inside the posts: food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including tips (fooien). Drink covers coffee, tea, milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank). Stimulants covers cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation expenses include receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [Besluit section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The threshold is not time-proportioned when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally ([Besluit section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The threshold is indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed.

Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wages in kind (loon in natura) ([Besluit section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel) (art. 3.15 lid 5)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 5, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters) ([art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The election replaces the threshold for that year and is made in the income tax (IB) return. With low total mixed costs (gemengde kosten) the threshold can leave a smaller deduction than the 80% election, so the two methods can be compared per year. Section 4 gives a calculated comparison.

### Corporate income tax variant of the threshold (Vpb variant van de drempel) (art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969)

For a BV: under [art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) the art. 3.15 restriction applies to an entity (lichaam) when there are one or more employees in the sense of the Wage Tax Act (Wet LB) in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The 2020 text prints "hoger is dan € 4.700" ([art. 8 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01)). It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount. Only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent ([Besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### Travel and accommodation for congresses and courses (reis en verblijf voor congressen en cursussen) (art. 3.16 lid 2 f en lid 8)

Travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession (beroep), and for congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f en lid 8, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary ([art. 3.16 lid 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.16, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The overview page repeats the cap ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Study costs (studiekosten) are deductible as a business cost when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw ([studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers)). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap ([studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers)).

### Mileage allowance for a private vehicle (kilometervergoeding privévervoermiddel) (art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 b)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, such as a car, motorcycle or bicycle (fiets), are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The 2020 amount is € 0,19 ([art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is all-in. Fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll and parking costs cannot be deducted separately ([art. 3.15 lid 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Household members are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.15 lid 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The amount is not indexed, because art. 10.1 lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). This rule is for a vehicle in the private assets (privévermogen) or rented privately. A car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) follows the art. 3.20 rules ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The page carries no year label.

### Relocation (verhuizing) and double accommodation (art. 3.17 lid 1 a)

Moving costs consist of the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7.750 ([art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Housing outside the place of residence, double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting), is deductible for at most two years ([art. 3.17](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The rule is unchanged in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.17, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Private-use addition for a car, standard (bijtelling auto, standaard) (art. 3.20 lid 1 a)

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private ([art. 3.20 lid 1 en lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself ([art. 3.20 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). Car means passenger car (personenauto) or van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), for which the private kilometres times the actual cost per kilometre are settled. A statement of exclusively business use (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik) is possible ([art. 3.20 lid 6 tot en met 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

The value (waarde) is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) describes it as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). For an old car the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) replaces the catalogue price ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). When the age threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)).

Asset classification (vermogensetikettering): a car used 90% or more for business, or a car with which the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year, is mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). Used 90% or more privately it is compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). All other cases are freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). A choice within the freely classifiable assets can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, afterwards only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use of an asset in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Private-use addition for an older car (auto van voor 2017) (art. 10a.4)

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% ([art. 10a.4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). In the 2020 text art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months ([art. 10a.4 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. This rule is separate from the youngtimer rule below, which uses 35% of the market value.

### Youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b)

A car that was first taken into use more than 15 years ago gives a withdrawal of 35% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The age threshold is 15 years in 2020 ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For an old car the market value replaces the catalogue price ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)), and in 2020 that switch is also at fifteen years. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). The withdrawal is capped at the total car costs including depreciation, as with the standard rule. The 35% rule is not affected by art. 10a.4.

### Zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto), first admission 2020 (art. 3.20 lid 2)

For a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For a car first admitted in 2020 the reduction is 14% capped at € 6.300, which gives 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above ([art. 3.20 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) were uncapped in the 2020 text. The 2020 text has no solar-cell exemption yet, that came later ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### 500 km private use (art. 3.20 lid 1)

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year ([art. 3.20 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil ([art. 3.20 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked ([privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). Commuting is not private ([art. 3.20 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets) (art. 3.20a)

When a bicycle of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne ([art. 3.20a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A bicycle includes an e-bike or speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power. Availability for commuting counts as private availability ([art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address ([art. 3.20a, 2026 text, where the note on terugwerkende kracht also appears in the 2020 to 2025 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The nil rule therefore applies to tax year 2020.

### Work space at home (werkruimte thuis) (art. 3.16 lid 1 en lid 13)

Costs of a workplace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) are not deductible, unless the workplace is self-contained (zelfstandig) according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting) and an income test is met ([art. 3.16 lid 1, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). With another workplace outside the home, the combined profit (winst), wage (loon) and income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workplace. Without another workplace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workplace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it ([art. 3.16 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The official factors for self-containment (zelfstandig) are its own entrance (opgang), own sanitary facilities, and whether the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained (zelfstandig) ([werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) ([art. 3.16 lid 13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The overview page prints 0% for a work space at home, with exceptions (uitzonderingen) ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page also offers a deduction for a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workplace when the home is owned and part of the business assets ([werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). The limit of art. 3.16 lid 14 for such an owned home starts on 1 January 2025 and does not apply to 2020 ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### Home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis) (art. 3.16 lid 2 a)

Telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). A private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). The overview page prints 0% for a home phone subscription and 100% for business telephone calls at home (zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis) ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The statutory basis is unchanged in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Clothing and personal care (kleding en persoonlijke verzorging) (art. 3.16 lid 2 c en d)

Clothing (kleding) is not deductible, except work clothing (werkkleding) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), work clothing being defined in lid 5. Personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) is not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The business or private page (zakelijk of privé) says that a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business, such as a driving licence (rijbewijs), health costs such as a pedicure or health insurance (zorgverzekering) and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser (kapper) are not deductible at all. Work clothing is deductible ([zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive)).

### Literature (literatuur) (art. 3.16 lid 2 b)

Literature is not deductible, except professional literature (vakliteratuur) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Private computers and equipment (apparatuur) (art. 3.16 lid 3)

Costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other such equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Equipment and instruments that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible. A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business is compulsory private assets, 90% or more is mandatory business assets, and in between it is freely classifiable assets within reasonable bounds ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### Partner pay (loon partner) (art. 3.16 lid 4)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5.000 ([art. 3.16 lid 4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Small acquisitions (kleine aanschaffingen) (€ 450 rule)

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are small office supplies (kantoorartikelen), professional literature and maintenance (onderhoud) of the car ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Receipts and invoices must be kept ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). The page carries no year label.

### Training expenses threshold (scholingsuitgaven drempel) (art. 6.30)

Up to and including 2021 training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) were personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). They covered tuition fees (lesgeld), course fees (cursusgeld), tuition fees (collegegeld), examination fees (examengeld), doctorate costs (promotiekosten) and required study materials (leermiddelen) and protective equipment (beschermingsmiddelen), excluding computer equipment ([art. 6.27 en art. 6.28, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). They were deductible above a threshold (drempel) of € 250 ([art. 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No deduction applied when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study ([art. 6.27 en art. 6.28, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Training expenses maximum outside the standard study period (buiten standaardstudieperiode) (art. 6.30)

Outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode) the training expenses were deductible up to € 15.000 ([art. 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The deduction ended later: the abolition (afschaffing) of the tax deduction (aftrek) for training expenses (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree of 12 July 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, art. 6.28 and art. 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 ([art. 6.27, 6.28 en 6.30, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case ([art. 10a.16, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A non-fiscal subsidy replaced the tax deduction from 1 January 2022 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html), [regeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01)). For tax year 2020 the deduction still exists.

### Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (art. 3.14 lid 1 c en i)

Not deductible: costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten), comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Also not deductible: costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)) or for which a penal order (strafbeschikking) became irrevocable ([onderdeel e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)), and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb ([onderdeel i](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.14, 2020 to 2027 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fines (verkeersboeten) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines ([Besluit section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The overview page prints 0% for fines ([overzicht aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Timing: costs incurred before the fine (geldboete) is imposed are not connected with the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment (dwangsom), the relevant point is when it is forfeited (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught ([Kennisgroep publicatie, published 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).

Scope: only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by settlement (transactie) or penal order, are excluded. The decree gives the cannabis plants (hennepplanten) example, where a taxpayer convicted only for possession and not for trade loses only the costs of possession ([Besluit section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### VAT correction for private use of a car (btw-correctie privégebruik auto)

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet) ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Commuting counts as private use for VAT, though it is business for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). In a BV the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer), he counts as an employee for this purpose ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance ([zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). The page carries no year label.
