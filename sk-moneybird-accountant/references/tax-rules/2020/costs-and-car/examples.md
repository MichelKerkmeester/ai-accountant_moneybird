### Official example: private-use addition (bijtelling) of 22% and the cap at the costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page has no year label and uses 22%.

Input: catalogue price € 30.000,00, total car costs (autokosten) € 10.000,00.
Step 1: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00 withdrawal (onttrekking).
Step 2: € 10.000,00 - € 6.600,00 = € 3.400,00 deductible.

### Official example: cap at the actual costs

Marked `Official example`. Source: [voorbeelden berekening privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page has no year label.

Input: withdrawal before the cap € 11.000,00, total car costs € 9.000,00.
Step 1: the withdrawal is € 11.000,00.
Step 2: the cap in art. 3.20 lid 4 limits the withdrawal to the total car costs of € 9.000,00. The withdrawal is € 9.000,00.

### Calculated example: mixed costs (gemengde kosten), threshold against the 80% election

Input: mixed costs (gemengde kosten) of € 6.000,00 in 2020.

Threshold route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 1.300,00 deductible.

80% route.
Step 1: € 6.000,00 × 80% = € 4.800,00 deductible.
Difference: € 4.800,00 - € 1.300,00 = € 3.500,00 more deduction with the election.

Break-even.
Costs € 23.500,00. Threshold route: € 23.500,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 18.800,00. 80% route: € 23.500,00 × 80% = € 18.800,00. Equal.
At € 24.000,00 the threshold route gives € 24.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 19.300,00 and the 80% route gives € 24.000,00 × 80% = € 19.200,00. So above € 23.500,00 the threshold route gives more. Below € 23.500,00 the 80% election gives more.

### Calculated example: mileage allowance

Input: 12.000 business kilometres with a private car in 2020.
Step 1: 12.000 × € 0,19 = € 2.280,00 deductible. Fuel, insurance, toll and parking cannot be added on top.

### Calculated example: zero-emission car first admitted in 2020

Input a: catalogue price € 40.000,00.
Step 1: € 40.000,00 × 22% = € 8.800,00.
Step 2: € 40.000,00 × 14% = € 5.600,00. This is below the € 6.300,00 cap.
Step 3: € 8.800,00 - € 5.600,00 = € 3.200,00 withdrawal. Control: € 40.000,00 × 8% = € 3.200,00.

Input b: catalogue price € 60.000,00.
Step 1: € 60.000,00 × 22% = € 13.200,00.
Step 2: € 60.000,00 × 14% = € 8.400,00, capped at € 6.300,00.
Step 3: € 13.200,00 - € 6.300,00 = € 6.900,00 withdrawal.

### Calculated example: youngtimer

Input: car first taken into use 20 years ago, market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) € 10.000,00, total car costs € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 10.000,00 × 35% = € 3.500,00.
Step 2: the cap applies, so the withdrawal is € 2.000,00.
Step 3: € 2.000,00 - € 2.000,00 = € 0,00 net deduction.

### Calculated example: 500 km rule

Input: private kilometres in 2020 are 480. 480 is at most 500, so the withdrawal is nil. If the record shows 1.200 private kilometres, the car counts as available for private use and the normal withdrawal applies.

### Calculated example: bicycle (fiets)

Input: recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 × 7% = € 140,00 withdrawal, capped at the costs borne. If the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the withdrawal is nil.

### Calculated example: relocation (verhuizing)

Input: cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) € 1.500,00.
Step 1: € 1.500,00 + € 7.750,00 = € 9.250,00 deductible. Double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting) is deductible for at most two years.

### Calculated example: congress travel cap

Input: travel and stay for a conference (congres) € 2.000,00.
Step 1: € 2.000,00 - € 1.500,00 = € 500,00 not deductible, unless the nature of the work makes attendance necessary.

### Calculated example: training expenses (scholingsuitgaven)

Input: tuition fees (lesgeld) and course fees (cursusgeld) € 1.200,00, no right to student finance (studiefinanciering).
Step 1: € 1.200,00 - € 250,00 = € 950,00 as personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek).

### Calculated example: corporate income tax variant for a BV (Vpb variant)

Input a: a BV with employees, total wage (loon) € 200.000,00.
Step 1: € 200.000,00 × 0,4% = € 800,00.
Step 2: € 800,00 is lower than € 4.700,00, so the fixed amount of € 4.700,00 applies.

Input b: total wage € 1.500.000,00.
Step 1: € 1.500.000,00 × 0,4% = € 6.000,00.
Step 2: € 6.000,00 is higher than € 4.700,00, so € 6.000,00 applies and the election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%.

### Calculated example: small acquisitions (kleine aanschaffingen)

Input: a printer of € 300,00 and a laptop of € 800,00, both for the business.
The printer of € 300,00 is below € 450,00 and is deducted in one go in 2020.
The laptop of € 800,00 is € 450,00 or more and is used longer than a year, so it is depreciated. There is no one-off deduction of € 800,00.

### Calculated example: 0% items

Input: a Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) fine of € 250,00. Deduction € 0,00.
Input: a private telephone subscription of € 300,00, of which the business calls are € 90,00. Deduction € 90,00, the other € 210,00 is not deductible.

### Calculated example: VAT correction on a private car (btw-correctie)

Input: catalogue price including VAT and bpm € 30.000,00, no conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie), full year.
Step 1: € 30.000,00 × 1,5% = € 450,00 correction in rubriek 1d. The correction is never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance.
Part year control: six months gives € 450,00 × 6/12 = € 225,00.
