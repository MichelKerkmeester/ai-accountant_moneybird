## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Corporate income tax rate (Vpb-tarief) and calculation (berekening) 2020

The Vpb applies to a BV. The tax is calculated on the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag). For 2020 the first € 200.000 is taxed at 16,5%. Above € 200.000 the tax is € 33.000 plus 25% of the part above € 200.000. This is the table in art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. The € 33.000 is the tax on the first € 200.000 at 16,5%. A BV files a corporate income tax return (vennootschapsbelasting). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### 3.2 Art. 31 pro-rata at a financial year spanning the year boundary (boekjaar over de jaargrens)

If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, the tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change. Y is the days from the change. B is all days of the year. To is the tax under the old art. 22. Tn is the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. The pro-rata formula applies to a 2020 book year that straddles 1 January 2021 because art. 22 changed. For such a book year, To is the 2020 table and Tn is the 2021 table: up to and including € 245.000, 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### 3.3 Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) 2020

For 2020 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years. The inspector fixes the loss by decision (beschikking). Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. This is the art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969 rule for 2020 and 2021. From 2022 the rule changes to one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. A BV claims the set-off in the corporate income tax return. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 20 lid 2) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### 3.4 Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) art. 15b

The balance of interest (saldo aan renten, interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 30% of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. For 2020 the percentage is 30%. The threshold is € 1.000.000. Non-deductible interest carries forward. The rule interacts with the Vpb calculation because the non-deductible part is added back to the taxable amount. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1). Later years use other percentages, but for 2020 the text gives 30%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025.

### 3.5 corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb) and deferral (uitstel)

A book year equal to the calendar year ends on 31 December. The return must be filed before 1 June of the next year. A broken book year must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending on 31 December must be filed before 1 June. A short book year ending in another month must be filed before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar book year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months. That request must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### 3.6 Customary wage scheme (gebruikelijk loonregeling)

The rule applies to an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang). The wage is at least the highest of three amounts. First, the wage (loon) from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking). In 2020, 2021 and 2022 the test uses 75% of that comparable wage. Second, the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies. Third, the standard amount (normbedrag). For 2020 the standard amount (normbedrag) is € 46.000. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This is lid 6 in the 2020 text. The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. That is lid 9 in 2020 to 2022. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage (loon) for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling). A connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party. The rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### 3.7 Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) escape

For 2020 to 2022, if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage. That amount must be at least the standard amount. If the comparable wage itself is lower than the standard amount, the wage is the comparable wage. From 2023 the escape changes. If the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The handbook payroll taxes 2025 notes that a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### 3.8 Start-up relief and lower wages

For 2020 to 2022, art. 12a lid 3 and 4 Wet LB 1964 gives a start-up relief. It applies to a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for R&D to which the start-up percentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies. The wage is then at least 108% of twelve times the WML amount of art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML. A lower comparable wage is allowed if made plausible. The relief must stay within the de minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) No 1407/2013 (verordening). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) says the de minimis condition did not apply to a DGA. An R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years. The handbook payroll taxes 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy says a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage. That lower wage starts from the WML for the hours worked. The 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. The 2020 WML amount itself is not in the checked sources, so the 108% minimum cannot be worked out here. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### 3.9 Zvw for the DGA

A DGA is employed by his own company for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw. The BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). A DGA who can decide on his own dismissal, alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). The same holds for a DGA who holds an equal share with all fellow directors. For an uninsured DGA the company pays no premiums (premies) for employee insurance schemes and no employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw. Instead the BV withholds the income-related contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage) Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). A DGA who is insured falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV. For 2020 the contribution is 5,45%, the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 57.232, and the employer levy is 6,70%. The FISIN page for 2020 says DGA wage (loon) falls under the employer levy only if he is insured for the employee insurance schemes. The checked handbook payroll taxes 2025 corrects the route: the BV withholds the contribution on net wage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding (inhouding) on net wage (nettoloon) or by assessment (aanslag). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.

### 3.10 Box 2 rate

For 2020 the box 2 rate is 26,25% flat. Box 2 applies to an individual shareholder with a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), including a DGA. Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the table correction factor. An individual credits dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld by the BV in the income tax return. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.12) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01.

### 3.11 Dividend tax

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 sets the tax at 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst). Art. 6 lid 1 says that if the company bears the tax, the proceeds (opbrengst) are multiplied by 100/85. Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividend statement (dividendnota) once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder with a holding of 5% or more is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling). No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the tax office invites one. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return. Bodies credit it in the corporate income tax return. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 1, 5, 6 lid 1 and 9 lid 1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/.

### 3.12 Dividend stripping

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. It is not art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds this rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01.

### 3.13 Purchase of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)

Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares. This also applies to an exchange buyback. If the company bears the tax, art. 6 Wet DB 1965 grosses it up by 100/85. The art. 4c facility for listed companies can avoid that. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### 3.14 Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting)

There is no conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) in 2020. The 2020 version of the Withholding Tax Act 2021 (Wet bronbelasting 2021) holds only headings and an amending chapter. The levy articles first appear in the 2021 version. Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version. Dividends enter in the 2024 version. The tax hits payments to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction and abuse cases. For 2020 the practical point is that no such levy applies. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01.

### 3.15 Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) Vpb

The parent company must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the subsidiary. Those shares must carry at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the parent company must be a BV, NV, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or a foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as a housing corporation (woningcorporatie), or a comparable foreign form. The subsidiary must be a BV or NV. Both must have the same book years and profit rules and be effectively established in the Netherlands. The subsidiary remains in existence but files no separate return. Losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment. That also happens when an option holder must offer the shares to the parent company at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only, under art. 15 lid 10. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### 3.16 Participation exemption

Art. 13 lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2 says a participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital. That is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the customary salary test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies with a 1/3 interest. A tax-neutral contribution (inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Tax-neutral Conversion Decree (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met. For VAT a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity. A holding that steers the group as a policy-making body can join on written request. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 13) and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

### 3.17 Innovation box (innovatiebox)

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 7/H in the 2020 text. H is the highest art. 22 rate. The effective rate in 2020 is 7%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states 9% for the current regime, which matches the 9/H fraction from 2021. A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring). Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover over those five years below € 250.000.000. This is art. 12ba lid 2 and is the same in 2020 and 2026. Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Trademarks (merken), logos (logo's) and comparable assets are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb gives the K/T part. K is the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold of art. 12bc. Art. 12bd gives a flat rate (forfait) for a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses it. The flat rate is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 12b, 12ba, 12bb, 12bc and 12bd) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### 3.18 Tax-neutral conversion art. 3.65

On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed. The shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The standard conditions in annex 1 of the 2025 decree include a three-year rule. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business, unless the contributor proves otherwise. This is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB and national insurance contributions. From the 2025 decree the Zvw contribution in substance due at the transition is also credited. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company is only allowed when it already runs a business in the same line. A contribution into a fiscal unity is only allowed through the parent company whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year. This is strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says the request goes to the office that handles the income tax. The preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) is registered with the accompanying form (Geleideformulier). The BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. Request timing: the inspector decides by decision open to objection (bezwaar) that contains the conditions. A request remains timely until failure to file it would have irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties. In the case KG:212:2022:1 the request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. That position (standpunt) was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

### 3.19 Uncertainty (onzekerheid) in the 2020 text for tax-neutral conversion

The Tax-neutral Conversion Decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, was in force through 4 November 2025. It was not read. The 2020 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions therefore rests on the 2025 successor only. The checked 2025 decree also mentions a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. Whether that credit existed in 2020 is not in the checked sources. This point is UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.

### 3.20 Taxable contribution (ruisende inbreng) and discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

Without the facility, a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays income tax on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst). That is the difference between the book value and the real value of the business. The fiscal reserves are released. The discontinuation deduction of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630, once in a lifetime. An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. Hidden reserves (stille reserves) realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not qualify for the discontinuation deduction. They may go into an annuity under art. 3.129. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.79).

### 3.21 Annual accounts (jaarrekening), size classes (grootteklassen) and filing (deponering)

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. The 2020 statute in Boek 2 BW gives these classes. Micro: at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees. Small: € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50. Medium: € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 raises these amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. The employee counts stay the same. Deposit under art. 394: within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein says there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From financial year (boekjaar) 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/ and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/.

### 3.22 Record-keeping obligation (administratieplicht) and retention period (bewaartermijn)

Art. 52 AWR makes entities (lichamen) subject to the record-keeping obligation (administratieplichtig). They keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. That is art. 52 lid 4 AWR. Data may move to another carrier, except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. The 2020 and 2026 texts are identical on this point. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten years. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

### 3.23 VAT at conversion to a BV

The new legal form is a new business for VAT (btw) and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer). That number goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. A VAT fiscal unity (btw-fiscale eenheid) under art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined. It changes by law on entry or exit. A decision, on request or by the inspector, states the start date. That decision is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The policy decree (Beleidsbesluit) on tax liability (belastingplicht) and the fiscal unity for turnover tax of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, was published on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025".
