## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material for 2019 is not available. This file cannot give old-to-new values for the step from 2019 to 2020. The 2020 sources state the 2020 position.
- Vpb 2020: t/m € 200.000 16,5%, above that € 33.000 plus 25%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).
- Verliesverrekening 2020: 1 year back and 6 years forward, after a beschikking. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 20 lid 2).
- Art. 15b 2020: 30% of the gecorrigeerde winst and a € 1.000.000 threshold. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1).
- Gebruikelijk loon 2020: normbedrag € 46.000 and 75% of the comparable wage. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.
- Box 2 2020: 26,25% flat. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.12).
- Dividendbelasting 2020: 15% and a 100/85 gross-up. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 5 en 6 lid 1).
- Stakingsaftrek 2020: at most € 3.630. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.79).
- What began in 2020: there is no conditionele bronbelasting in 2020. The levy articles first appear in the 2021 version. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01.
- The art. 31 pro-rata formula applies to a 2020 book year that straddles 1 January 2021 because art. 22 changed. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Run the DGA wage through the loonadministratie and withhold loonheffing. For an uninsured DGA, withhold the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from net wage and pay it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For 2020 the bijdrage is 5,45% up to a maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 57.232. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Record dividend decisions. A dividendnota is due once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The dividendbelasting return and payment are due within 1 month after the dividend is made available. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 9 lid 1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.
- File the Vpb return for a calendar book year before 1 June of the next year. For 2020 that is before 1 June 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen.
- Keep the administration for seven years under art. 52 lid 4 AWR. Records on onroerende zaken are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.
- Deposit the jaarrekening within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 (art. 394).
- In a fiscale eenheid the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return. Losses of one company offset profits of another. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.
- For a geruisloze omzetting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier. The BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.
- The new BV is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once. With employees, payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- UNVERIFIED: the 2020 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions for the geruisloze omzetting. The Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 30 June 2010 was in force through 4 November 2025 but was not read. The 2020 wording therefore rests on the 2025 successor. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
- Unknown: the WML amount of art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML for 2020 is not in the checked sources. The start-up relief minimum of 108% of twelve times that amount therefore cannot be worked out here. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.
- Source vintage: the filing and extension rules come from current Belastingdienst pages read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. The checked material does not contain a 2020-dated page for these deadlines. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Source vintage: the btw for a BV rule comes from a 2024 beleidsbesluit in force from 1 July 2025. The 2020 text is not in the checked sources. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- Source vintage: the inkoop van eigen aandelen rule comes from a 2025 publication. The 2020 text is not in the checked sources. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.
- Source vintage: the route for the uninsured DGA, where the BV withholds the bijdrage on net wage, is checked in the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 and in the 2024 to 2026 Belastingdienst table. The 2020 FISIN page states only that DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing if the DGA is insured. The 2020 route itself is not in the checked sources. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- The 2025 Besluit geruisloze omzetting mentions a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. Whether that credit existed in 2020 is not in the checked sources. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
