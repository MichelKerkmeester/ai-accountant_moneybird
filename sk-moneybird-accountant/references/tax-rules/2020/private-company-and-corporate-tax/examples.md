## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1: Vpb on a profit of € 60.000

**Calculated example.** A BV has a taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) of € 60.000. The amount is below € 200.000. Tax = 16,5% x € 60.000 = € 9.900,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### Example 2: Vpb on a profit of € 300.000

**Calculated example.** A BV has a taxable amount of € 300.000. First bracket (eerste schijf): € 200.000 x 16,5% = € 33.000,00. Excess: € 300.000 - € 200.000 = € 100.000,00. Tax on excess: € 100.000 x 25% = € 25.000,00. Total tax: € 33.000,00 + € 25.000,00 = € 58.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 22).

### Example 3: Art. 31 pro-rata for a book year over 1 January 2021

**Calculated example.** A BV has a book year from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2021 and a profit of € 100.000. Days before 1 January 2021: July 31 + August 31 + September 30 + October 31 + November 30 + December 31 = 184 days. Days from 1 January 2021: January 31 + February 28 + March 31 + April 30 + May 31 + June 30 = 181 days. B = 184 + 181 = 365 days. To (tax under the 2020 table): € 100.000 x 16,5% = € 16.500,00. Tn (tax under the 2021 table): € 100.000 x 15% = € 15.000,00. First part: (184/365) x € 16.500 = € 8.317,81 (rounded). Second part: (181/365) x € 15.000 = € 7.438,36 (rounded). Total: € 8.317,81 + € 7.438,36 = € 15.756,17, rounded down € 15.756. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01.

### Example 4: Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening)

**Calculated example.** A BV has a 2019 loss of € 50.000 and a 2020 profit of € 60.000. The loss is set off against the 2020 profit. Taxable 2020 profit: € 60.000 - € 50.000 = € 10.000,00. Vpb: € 10.000 x 16,5% = € 1.650,00. The inspector must fix the loss by decision. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 20 lid 2).

### Example 5: Art. 15b interest deduction limitation (renteaftrekbeperking)

**Calculated example.** A BV has a balance of interest (saldo aan renten) of € 2.000.000. Case A: adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) € 3.000.000. 30% x € 3.000.000 = € 900.000,00. The higher of € 900.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00 is € 1.000.000,00. Non-deductible: € 2.000.000 - € 1.000.000 = € 1.000.000,00. Case B: adjusted profit € 5.000.000. 30% x € 5.000.000 = € 1.500.000,00. The higher of € 1.500.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00 is € 1.500.000,00. Non-deductible: € 2.000.000 - € 1.500.000 = € 500.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1).

### Example 6: Filing a 2020 Vpb return

**Calculated example.** A BV has a book year equal to the calendar year 2020. The return must be filed before 1 June 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen.

### Example 7: Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon)

**Calculated example.** A DGA works for his BV. The wage (loon) from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking) is € 80.000. 75% of that is € 80.000 x 75% = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees of the BV is € 40.000. The standard amount (normbedrag) is € 46.000. The highest of € 60.000,00, € 40.000,00 and € 46.000,00 is € 60.000,00. The wage is therefore at least € 60.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.

### Example 8: Customary salary escape

**Calculated example.** A DGA has a comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) of € 80.000. 75% of that is € 80.000 x 75% = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees is € 70.000. The standard amount is € 46.000. The highest amount is € 70.000,00. That is higher than 75% of the comparable wage. The escape applies. The wage is 75% of the comparable wage, so € 60.000,00. That is above the standard amount of € 46.000,00. The wage is therefore € 60.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2).

### Example 9: Zvw for a DGA

**Calculated example.** An uninsured DGA has a wage of € 60.000. The maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 57.232. The wage is above that. Contribution Zvw: 5,45% x € 57.232 = € 3.119,14 (rounded), rounded down € 3.119. If the DGA is insured, the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw is 6,70% x € 57.232 = € 3.834,54 (rounded), rounded € 3.835. If the uninsured DGA has a wage of € 50.000, the contribution is 5,45% x € 50.000 = € 2.725,00. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Example 10: Box 2 and dividend tax (dividendbelasting)

**Calculated example.** A BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to an individual DGA. Dividend tax: 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000,00 withheld. Net received: € 100.000 - € 15.000 = € 85.000,00. Box 2 tax: 26,25% x € 100.000 = € 26.250,00. Credit for dividend tax: € 15.000,00. Remaining box 2 tax: € 26.250,00 - € 15.000,00 = € 11.250,00. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 5) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.12).

### Example 11: Dividend tax grossing up (brutering)

**Calculated example.** A BV pays a net dividend of € 85.000 and bears the dividend tax itself. The proceeds (opbrengst) are multiplied by 100/85: € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000,00. Dividend tax: 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 6 lid 1).

### Example 12: Purchase of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)

**Calculated example.** A BV buys back its own shares for € 100.000. The average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares is € 20.000. The difference is € 100.000 - € 20.000 = € 80.000,00. Dividend tax: 15% x € 80.000 = € 12.000,00. If the BV bears the tax, the proceeds are € 80.000 x 100/85 = € 94.117,65 (rounded). Dividend tax: 15% x € 94.117,65 = € 14.117,65 (rounded). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Example 13: Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid)

**Calculated example.** A parent BV has a profit of € 200.000. A subsidiary BV has a loss of € 50.000. With a fiscal unity the combined taxable amount is € 200.000 - € 50.000 = € 150.000,00. Vpb: € 150.000 x 16,5% = € 24.750,00. Without the fiscal unity the parent would pay 16,5% x € 200.000 = € 33.000,00. The difference is € 33.000,00 - € 24.750,00 = € 8.250,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Example 14: Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling)

**Calculated example.** A holding BV holds 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (nominaal gestort kapitaal) of another BV and receives a dividend of € 100.000. The advantage is left out of the profit under art. 13 lid 1. No Vpb is due on that € 100.000. If the holding were 4%, art. 13 lid 2 would not make it a participation (deelneming). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 13 lid 1 and lid 2).

### Example 15: Innovation box (innovatiebox)

**Calculated example.** A BV has a profit before art. 12b of € 300.000. A qualifying advantage from a self-made intangible asset (immaterieel activum) is € 100.000. For simplicity, the threshold of art. 12bc is disregarded. H is the highest art. 22 rate, so H = 25%. 7/H = 7/25 = 0,28. Included in the profit: 0,28 x € 100.000 = € 28.000,00. Taxable amount: € 300.000 - € 100.000 + € 28.000 = € 228.000,00. Vpb: first € 200.000 x 16,5% = € 33.000,00. Remaining € 28.000 x 25% = € 7.000,00. Total Vpb: € 33.000,00 + € 7.000,00 = € 40.000,00. Without the innovation box the tax would be € 33.000,00 + € 25.000,00 = € 58.000,00. The tax attributable to the € 100.000 advantage is € 7.000,00, which is 7% of € 100.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 12b and 12bb) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Example 16: Tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting)

**Calculated example.** An entrepreneur contributes his business to a BV. The fiscal book value is € 200.000. The real value is € 500.000. The profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) without the facility would be € 500.000 - € 200.000 = € 300.000,00. With the tax-neutral conversion the business is deemed not ceased. No profit from business discontinuation is taxed now. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is € 200.000. If the contribution (inbreng) is taxable, the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) is at most € 3.630. Taxable profit (belastbare winst) from business discontinuation: € 300.000 - € 3.630 = € 296.370,00. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.79) and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.

### Example 17: Taxable contribution (ruisende inbreng)

**Calculated example.** An entrepreneur ceases his business and pays tax on a profit from business discontinuation of € 300.000. The discontinuation deduction is € 3.630. Taxable profit from business discontinuation: € 300.000 - € 3.630 = € 296.370,00. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.79).

### Example 18: Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size class (grootteklasse)

**Calculated example.** A BV has a balance sheet total of € 400.000, a net turnover (netto-omzet) of € 800.000 and 12 employees. Micro test: assets € 400.000 is more than € 350.000, turnover € 800.000 is more than € 700.000, and 12 employees is more than 10. All three fail, so the BV is not micro. Small test: assets € 400.000 is less than € 6.000.000, turnover € 800.000 is less than € 12.000.000, and 12 employees is less than 50. All three pass. The BV falls in the small class when it meets at least two of the three criteria on two consecutive balance dates. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 (art. 395a, 396 and 397).

### Example 19: Keeping records (administratie bewaren)

**Calculated example.** A BV keeps its 2020 administration. Art. 52 lid 4 AWR requires a seven-year term. The records on any immovable property (onroerende zaak) must be kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

### Example 20: VAT at conversion to a BV

**Calculated example.** A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is converted into a BV. The BV is a new business for VAT (btw). It may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which must go to EU suppliers at once. If there are employees, payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.

### Example 21: Dividend stripping

**Calculated example.** A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. This rule is in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01.

### Example 22: Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting)

**Calculated example.** A BV pays interest in 2020 to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie). No conditional withholding tax is due in 2020. The levy articles first appear in the 2021 version. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01.

### Example 23: Start-up relief

**Calculated example.** A body has an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for R&D and meets the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA. Under art. 12a lid 3 and 4 Wet LB 1964 the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the WML amount of art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML, or a lower comparable wage if that is made plausible, within the de minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) No 1407/2013 (verordening). The 2020 WML amount is not in the checked sources, so the exact 108% amount cannot be worked out here and no product is calculated. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01.

### Official example: KG:212:2022:1

**Official example.** The knowledge group (Kennisgroep) case KG:212:2022:1 works a tax-neutral conversion with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2021 where the 2020 IB assessment was already final. The request can be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment is irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.
