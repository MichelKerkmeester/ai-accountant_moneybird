## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the BV and the corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) for tax year 2020. It sets out the 2020 Vpb rates, loss relief, the interest deduction limit, the customary wage scheme (gebruikelijk loonregeling) for a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA), dividend tax (dividendbelasting), box 2, the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling), the innovation box (innovatiebox), the switch from a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) to a BV, and the annual accounts (jaarrekening) rules. The year's law is enacted. The checked 2020 sources state the values. One point about the 2020 text of the tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) conditions is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.
