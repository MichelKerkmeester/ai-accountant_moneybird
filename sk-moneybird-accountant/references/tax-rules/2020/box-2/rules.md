## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 rate (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

In 2020 the article states one rate, 26,25%, so the whole taxable income from a substantial interest (belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) is taxed at that percentage and there is no bracket. The text reads "bedraagt 26,25%" ([art. 2.12, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The rate applies per taxpayer, which matters when fiscal partners divide the income under art. 2.17 lid 2 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor), but the 2020 text holds a single percentage without a bracket boundary, so the year's own text governs ([art. 10.1 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The same 26,25% returns in the tax credit (belastingkorting) rule of art. 4.53 lid 2, see below.

### Income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.12) and regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) (art. 4.13)

The income from a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) is the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten), plus the disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) of art. 4.12 ([text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits: sub a the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling, vbi) or a foreign investment company, sub b to d certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction, and sub e bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling). The 2020 text stops at sub e. Sub f, the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction, was inserted from 2023, so it plays no role in 2020 ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Not a box 2 benefit (voordeel): interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap), which is income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen), and salary from the company, which is box 1 wage ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Deductible in box 2 is interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted from the income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) vbi or foreign investment company (art. 4.14)

This rule applies to shares or profit rights in a vbi or a foreign investment company. The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of those rights at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned over the year ([art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The percentage is set per year. For 2020 it is 5,28%. The text of 1 January 2020 printed 5,33%, and that figure was replaced retroactively to 1 January 2020 by Stcrt. 2020, 20572, so 5,28% is the 2020 value ([art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel) and acquisition price

On a disposal the benefit is the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) minus the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) names the special cases: inheritance, gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching the 5% during the year, and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs). At a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs) the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Selling costs, such as notary and brokerage costs on the sale, reduce the transfer price ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Substantial interest threshold (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a ([art. 4.6, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The 2020 text stops at sub d. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts and covers a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969, 2022 numbering, with at least 5% of its profit ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub f was added by the Other Fiscal Measures Act (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2025 and covers a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights (stemrechten) ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts, so the 2020 text reads "tot" ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).

### Drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) and carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) (art. 4.9 and 4.10)

Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Drag-along rule (Meesleepregeling, the rule that other shares are pulled into the substantial interest, art. 4.9): once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that same company also belong to that substantial interest. Carry-along rule (the rule that shares of close relatives are carried into the substantial interest, art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer himself has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line (bloedverwant of aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner), plus their fiscal partners, and says that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11)

Shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). These are rollover cases. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%. On request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. The vbi status is a deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) case listed by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, not part of this art. 4.11 list ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Deemed disposal (art. 4.16)

The statute lists as a sale: buy-back (inkoop) of shares, commutation (afkoop) and buy-back of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a ([art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical in 2020 to 2027 ([art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Partner and minor children

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (alone or together with his partner), so shares of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner are added ([art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Common income components (gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen) of the taxpayer and partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return ([art. 2.17 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2026 states that with fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (any division is allowed, as long as the total is 100%) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own rate applies to his share, and for 2020 that rate is 26,25% for each of them ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). No primary page in the checked material states a summed two-partner amount, see section 7.

### Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the acquisition price ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. This article does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction, that fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### Loss offset (art. 4.49)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose, so the oldest loss goes first ([art. 4.49, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The loss is set by decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Losses from 2018 or earlier

For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that they can be offset "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (carried forward for the coming 9 years), which is longer than the standard period ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Tax credit on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a tax credit (belastingkorting). A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death ([art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Lid 2 gives the percentage: 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", which is 24,5% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Lid 3: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit (korting) by decision open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces the box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without substantial interest ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Knowledge group (kennisgroep) position (standpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block it, and ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)).

### Rollover (doorschuiving) on request

The checked research lists rollover (doorschuiving) on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. The list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check, so the value is UNVERIFIED with Status unknown in the table ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). A primary text of the rollover articles in the 2020 version would settle it. See section 7. FISIN 2020 does confirm the 10-year APV term below.

### APV loss deferral

The checked research lists an APV loss deferral, with offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The third-party sale and death triggers come from the research and are not confirmed on a primary page. See section 7.

### Excessive borrowing from the own company (excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap)

The Excess Borrowing from Own Companies Act (Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap) is the Act of 21 December 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued on 27 December 2022, in force (in werking) from 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the related-person rule (verbonden personen) in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). None of these rules exists in the 2020 text, so a loan from the own BV is not taxed as a deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) in 2020 ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (art. 12a lid 1 sub c Wet LB 1964)

Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) is the minimum salary a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder) must take from the body. For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment, 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). In 2020 the fixed amount is € 46.000 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor, in lid 9 of the 2020 text and in lid 7 from 2023 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Customary salary comparable-wage test (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

In 2020 the test uses 75% of the wage from the most comparable employment ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role, and it must be known to the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) and the tax inspector ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A lower wage is possible under lid 2. If the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount from the test exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), the comparable wage applies, and in the 2020 to 2022 texts the fixed amount acts as a floor in that route unless the comparable wage itself is lower ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page repeats the three-part test and the lower-wage route ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Customary salary minimum threshold (art. 12a lid 6)

The art. 12a rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). In the 2020 to 2022 texts this sits in lid 6, and from 2023 in lid 4 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (not per company) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Start-up customary salary (art. 12a lid 3 and 4)

For a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage (minimumloon) of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The rule is absent from the 2023 text ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" (from 2023 this exception has lapsed for new cases), and the rule could be used for at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The earlier research value "the minimum wage" is corrected: the statute says 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).

### PEB commutation and ODV conversion

The Act on the phasing out of pension in own management and other fiscal pension measures (Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen), Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017) contains the PEB rules ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules, as at 31 December 2016, for a PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed waiving (prijsgeven) of the part of the claim above the tax balance sheet value (fiscale balanswaarde), on condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly commuted (afgekocht) or tax-neutrally (geruisloos) converted into an old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting). Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also of a former partner who kept part of the claim ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38o: on commutation the taxable wage is the surrender value (afkoopwaarde) minus 34,5% of the tax balance sheet value, capped by reference to the 2015 balance, then 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Artikel VI: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (lapsed per 01-01-2020) ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen) states that commutation or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked lapsed (vervallen) ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). So in 2020 these routes are closed.

### Remaining PEB after the end of the facilities

A remaining PEB keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" (if the BV does not index yearly, we can see this as a form of waiving the rights), with possible fiscal consequences ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

### Old-age obligation (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The ODV is increased each year by a market rate (marktrente) set by ministerial regulation. Art. 12.3a of the Wage Tax Implementing Regulation (Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011) uses the average of the monthly U-returns (U-rendementen) of the preceding year ([Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The ODV can be used tax-neutrally for an annuity (lijfrente), annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking). Each instalment is the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). Added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020: under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity (lijfrente) after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5) ([Wet LB 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A negative market rate must also be applied, so the ODV can decrease ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

### ODV market rate (marktrente) for 2020

The market rate for 2020 is -0,107% ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)). This is the December 2019 market rate, applied to the 2020 accrual (oprenting) of the ODV, and a negative rate must be applied ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

### PEB and box 2 (art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 ([art. 10a.18, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 10a.18 lid 2: in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. PEB payouts are income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), so they land in box 1 and not in box 2 ([art. 10a.18, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)).

### Non-residents (art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 7.5 confirms that the base for a non-resident is a substantial interest in a Netherlands-established company, not part of an enterprise ([art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The statement that a 2025 decision (beschikking) on partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.
