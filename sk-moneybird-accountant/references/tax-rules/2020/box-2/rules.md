## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 rate (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

In 2020 the article states one rate, 26,25%, so the whole belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (taxable income from a substantial shareholding) is taxed at that percentage and there is no bracket. The text reads "bedraagt 26,25%" ([art. 2.12, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The rate applies per taxpayer, which matters when fiscal partners divide the income under art. 2.17 lid 2 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor, but the 2020 text holds a single percentage without a bracket boundary, so the year's own text governs ([art. 10.1 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The same 26,25% returns in the belastingkorting rule of art. 4.53 lid 2, see below.

### Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12) and reguliere voordelen (art. 4.13)

The income from an aanmerkelijk belang is the reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs, plus the vervreemdingsvoordelen (disposal gains), minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) of art. 4.12 ([text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen: sub a the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling (vbi, exempt investment institution) or a foreign investment company, sub b to d certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction, and sub e bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (investment institution). The 2020 text stops at sub e. Sub f, the excessief lenen fiction, was inserted from 2023, so it plays no role in 2020 ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Not a box 2 voordeel: interest on a receivable on the own company, which is income from making assets available, and salary from the company, which is box 1 loon ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Deductible in box 2 is interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Dividendbelasting (dividend tax) is not deducted from the income but credited as voorheffing (prepaid tax) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Forfaitair voordeel vbi or foreign investment company (art. 4.14)

This rule applies to shares or profit rights in a vbi or a foreign investment company. The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of those rights at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned over the year ([art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The percentage is set per year. For 2020 it is 5,28%. The text of 1 January 2020 printed 5,33%, and that figure was replaced retroactively to 1 January 2020 by Stcrt. 2020, 20572, so 5,28% is the 2020 value ([art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Vervreemdingsvoordeel and verkrijgingsprijs

On a disposal the voordeel is the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price) minus the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included. The Belastingdienst names the special cases: inheritance, gift with or without doorschuiving (rollover), reaching the 5% during the year, and an onzakelijke prijs (non-arm's length price). At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer (market value) applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Selling costs, such as notary and brokerage costs on the sale, reduce the overdrachtsprijs ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Aanmerkelijk belang threshold (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a ([art. 4.6, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The 2020 text stops at sub d. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts and covers a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969, 2022 numbering, with at least 5% of its profit ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025 and covers a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts, so the 2020 text reads "tot" ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).

### Meesleepregeling and meetrekregeling (art. 4.9 and 4.10)

Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Meesleepregeling (the rule that other shares are pulled into the belang, art. 4.9): once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that same company also belong to that aanmerkelijk belang. Meetrekregeling (the rule that shares of close relatives are carried into the belang, art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer himself has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in the rechte lijn (blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.11)

Shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). These are rollover cases. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%. On request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. The vbi status is a fictieve vervreemding case listed by the Belastingdienst, not part of this art. 4.11 list ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)

The statute lists as a sale: inkoop of shares (buy-back), afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen (liquidation payments), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie (call option), shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen (separated private estate) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a ([art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical in 2020 to 2027 ([art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Partner and minor children

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (alone or together with his partner), so shares of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner are added ([art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen (common income components) of the taxpayer and partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return ([art. 2.17 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2026 states that with fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (any division is allowed, as long as the total is 100%) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own rate applies to his share, and for 2020 that rate is 26,25% for each of them ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). No primary page in the checked material states a summed two-partner amount, see section 7.

### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. This article does not carry the excessief lenen fiction, that fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### Loss offset (art. 4.49)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang (loss from an aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose, so the oldest loss goes first ([art. 4.49, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The loss is set by beschikking (decision) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Losses from 2018 or earlier

For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states that they can be offset "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (carried forward for the coming 9 years), which is longer than the standard period ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a belastingkorting (tax credit). A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death ([art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Lid 2 gives the percentage: 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", which is 24,5% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces the box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block it, and ambtshalve vermindering (ex officio reduction) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)).

### Doorschuiving on request

The checked research lists doorschuiving (rollover) on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. The list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check, so the value is UNVERIFIED with Status unknown in the table ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). A primary text of the rollover articles in the 2020 version would settle it. See section 7. FISIN 2020 does confirm the 10-year APV term below.

### APV loss deferral

The checked research lists an APV loss deferral, with offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The third-party sale and death triggers come from the research and are not confirmed on a primary page. See section 7.

### Excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap

The Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap (Act on excessive borrowing from the own company) is the Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued on 27 December 2022, in force from 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the verbonden-person rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). None of these rules exists in the 2020 text, so a loan from the own BV is not taxed as a fictief regulier voordeel in 2020 ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a lid 1 sub c Wet LB 1964)

Gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) is the minimum salary a director and substantial shareholder must take from the body. For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). In 2020 the fixed amount is € 46.000 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor, in lid 9 of the 2020 text and in lid 7 from 2023 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Gebruikelijk loon comparable-loon test (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

In 2020 the test uses 75% of the loon from the most comparable employment ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role, and it must be known to the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) and the inspecteur ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A lower loon is possible under lid 2. If the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount from the test exceeds the (75% of the) comparable loon, the comparable loon applies, and in the 2020 to 2022 texts the fixed amount acts as a floor in that route unless the comparable loon itself is lower ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page repeats the three-part test and the lower-loon route ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Gebruikelijk loon minimum threshold (art. 12a lid 6)

The art. 12a rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). In the 2020 to 2022 texts this sits in lid 6, and from 2023 in lid 4 ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (not per company) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Start-up gebruikelijk loon (art. 12a lid 3 and 4)

For a body with an S&O-verklaring (an R&D declaration) to which the verhoogd starterspercentage (increased start-up percentage) applies, the loon was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable loon if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The rule is absent from the 2023 text ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" (from 2023 this exception has lapsed for new cases), and the rule could be used for at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The earlier research value "the minimum wage" is corrected: the statute says 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage ([art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).

### PEB afkoop and ODV conversion

The Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen, Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017) contains the PEB rules ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules, as at 31 December 2016, for a PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed prijsgeven (waiving) of the part of the claim above the fiscale balanswaarde, on condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly afgekocht or geruisloos (tax-neutrally) converted into an oudedagsverplichting. Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also of a former partner who kept part of the claim ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38o: on afkoop the taxable loon is the afkoopwaarde minus 34,5% of the fiscale balanswaarde, capped by reference to the 2015 balance, then 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Artikel VI: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (lapsed per 01-01-2020) ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen states that afkoop or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked vervallen (lapsed) ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). So in 2020 these routes are closed.

### Remaining PEB after the end of the facilities

A remaining PEB keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. The Belastingdienst: "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" (if the BV does not index yearly, we can see this as a form of waiving the rights), with possible fiscal consequences ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

### Oudedagsverplichting (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The ODV is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation. Art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011 uses the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year ([Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The ODV can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente (annuity), lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking (income from a former employment). Each instalment is the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). Added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020: under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5) ([Wet LB 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A negative market rate must also be applied, so the ODV can decrease ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

### ODV marktrente for 2020

The market rate for 2020 is -0,107% ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)). This is the December 2019 marktrente, applied to the 2020 oprenting (accrual) of the ODV, and a negative rate must be applied ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

### PEB and box 2 (art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 ([art. 10a.18, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 10a.18 lid 2: in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, so they land in box 1 and not in box 2 ([art. 10a.18, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)).

### Non-residents (art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 7.5 confirms that the base for a non-resident is an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands-established company, not part of an enterprise ([art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The statement that a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht has the same effect was not found on the fetched Belastingdienst pages and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.
