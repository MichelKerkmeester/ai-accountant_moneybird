## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Box 2 is the income tax box for a taxpayer with a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), normally at least 5% of the capital, the profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) or the votes in a company. For 2020 the law is enacted and box 2 has one flat rate of 26,25%, with no brackets. The main events of the year are the end of the commutation (afkoop) and conversion routes for a pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB) on 1 January 2020 and a negative market rate of -0,107% for the old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV). The excessive borrowing from the own company (excessief lenen) rules exist only from 1 January 2023 and do not apply to 2020.
