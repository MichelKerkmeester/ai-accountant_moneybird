## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Every example below is marked as an **Official example**, copied from the source named in it, or as a **Calculated example**, built here from the section 2 figures. The amounts in the calculated examples are invented round numbers.

**Calculated example, dividend and the 26,25% rate.** A taxpayer with a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) receives a gross dividend of € 10.000 in 2020. The bank charges € 400. The BV withholds € 1.500 dividend tax (dividendbelasting).
- Regular benefit (regulier voordeel): € 10.000.
- Deductible costs: € 400.
- Taxable income from a substantial interest (belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang): € 10.000 minus € 400 = € 9.600.
- Box 2 tax at the 2020 rate of 26,25%: € 9.600 x 26,25% = € 2.520,00.
- The dividend tax is not deducted from the income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing): € 2.520,00 minus € 1.500 = € 1.020,00 still to pay.
- Rules used: [art. 4.12 and 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

**Official example, FISIN 2020, dividend and credited dividend tax.** Gross dividend € 5.000, bank costs € 250, so the box 2 income is € 5.000 minus € 250 = € 4.750. The dividend tax of € 750 is credited as prepayment ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Calculated example, sale of shares.** Shares were acquired for € 60.000, with the notary costs included in that amount. They are sold in 2020 for € 100.000, and the selling costs are € 2.000.
- Transfer price (overdrachtsprijs): € 100.000 minus € 2.000 = € 98.000.
- Acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs): € 60.000, including the notary costs.
- Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel): € 98.000 minus € 60.000 = € 38.000.
- Box 2 tax at 26,25%: € 38.000 x 26,25% = € 9.975,00.
- Rules used: [Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

**Official example, FISIN 2020, non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs).** Shares are sold to a son-in-law for € 20.000, where a third party would pay € 35.000. The market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) of € 35.000 applies ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Calculated example, loss conversion into a tax credit (belastingkorting).** Shares were sold in 2020 with an unoffset loss of € 30.000. The sale ended the substantial interest of the taxpayer and the partner. They had no substantial interest in 2021 and 2022. The loss is definitively established and the request is made in 2022.
- Tax credit: € 30.000 x 26,25% = € 7.875,00.
- The credit (korting) reduces the box 1 tax of 2022 and the seven following years, so at the latest 2029, the ninth year after the loss year.
- Rules used: [art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst verlies omzetten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting).

**Official example, FISIN 2020, loss conversion.** Shares were sold in 2020. The unoffset loss is € 20.000 and it is converted on request in 2022 into a tax credit of € 20.000 x 26,25% = € 5.250,00. The credit remains usable until 2029 ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Calculated example, flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) vbi or foreign investment company.** The value of the shares at the start of 2020 is € 100.000. Other regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) from those shares in 2020 are € 1.000.
- Flat-rate benefit: € 100.000 x 5,28% = € 5.280,00.
- Reduced by the other regular benefits: € 5.280,00 minus € 1.000 = € 4.280,00.
- The outcome can never be below nil. If the other benefits were € 6.000, the calculation would give € 5.280,00 minus € 6.000 = minus € 720,00, and the benefit would be nil.
- Rule used: [art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

**Calculated example, customary salary (gebruikelijk loon).** A BV in which the taxpayer has a substantial interest pays a salary. The most comparable wage is € 60.000. The highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies is € 50.000. The fixed amount is € 46.000.
- 75% of the most comparable wage: € 60.000 x 75% = € 45.000,00.
- Highest of € 45.000,00, € 46.000 and € 50.000: € 50.000,00.
- The customary salary is at least € 50.000,00.
- A lower amount needs the lid 2 route, which the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) must make plausible. In 2020 the fixed amount of € 46.000 acts as a floor in that route unless the comparable wage itself is lower.
- The € 5.000 threshold does not help, because the wage is far above € 5.000.
- The customary salary is box 1 income, not a box 2 benefit (voordeel).
- Rules used: [art. 12a, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst loon](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).

**Calculated example, inherited substantial interest.** Shares were inherited, and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer. The death was in January 2020. In October 2020 the taxpayer receives a dividend of € 8.000, which is within 24 months after the death.
- On request the € 8.000 is not counted as box 2 income, up to the amount that was taken into account with the deceased.
- The € 8.000 is written off on the acquisition price.
- Rule used: [art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

**Calculated example, ODV market rate and instalments.** Part A: the old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) balance at the start of 2020 is € 200.000 and the 2020 market rate (marktrente) is -0,107%.
- Yearly increase: € 200.000 x -0,107% = minus € 214,00.
- New balance: € 200.000 minus € 214,00 = € 199.786,00.

Part B: an ODV in the instalment route has a balance of € 200.000 at the start of the year and 20 years remaining.
- Instalment: € 200.000 / 20 = € 10.000,00.
- Rules used: [art. 38p, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/).
