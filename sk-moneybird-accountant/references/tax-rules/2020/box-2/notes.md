## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

The checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic, so the bullets state what began in 2020 or the 2020 form of a rule where the sources say it.

- Pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer): the commutation (afkoop) and the tax-neutral (geruisloze) conversion into an old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting) ended on 1 January 2020. The routes had been open from 1 April 2017 to 31 December 2019. Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115 makes art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse from 1 January 2020, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)).
- ODV market rate: for 2020 the rate is -0,107%, the December 2019 market rate (marktrente) applied to the 2020 accrual (oprenting). A negative rate must be applied ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- ODV: art. 38p lid 5, conversion into an annuity (lijfrente) after the instalments have started, was added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, so it belongs to 2020 ([Wet LB 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).
- Box 2 rate: 26,25% is the 2020 rate, one flat rate. The checked material gives 26,25% for 2020 and 26,9% for 2021, and no 2019 rate, so no old-to-new line from 2019 can be given from these sources ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) test: the 2020 text of art. 4.6 has sub a to d only. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, and sub f applies from 1 January 2025 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)).
- Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen): not in force in 2020. The statute is in force from 1 January 2023, so for 2020 there is no deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from borrowing from the own BV ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon): the 2020 text prints € 46.000 for the fixed amount and 75% for the comparable-wage test. The checked material holds no 2019 figures, so the change from 2019 cannot be given ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).
- Start-up customary salary: the 2020 text gives 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage as at 1 January, or the lower comparable wage. The rule is absent from the 2023 text ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Wet LB 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- PEB and the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs): art. 10a.18 lid 3 keeps the acquisition price unchanged for the 2017, 2018 and 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2, which matters for shares held into 2020 ([art. 10a.18, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)).
- Losses: art. 4.49 gives the previous year and the six following years, and losses from 2018 or earlier can be offset in the nine following years ([art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst verlies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record dividends and other profit distributions as regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) in box 2 ([Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Do not deduct dividend tax (dividendbelasting) from the box 2 income. It is credited as prepayment (voorheffing), so keep the dividend notes that show the withheld amount ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Costs such as bank costs on the dividend and interest on a loan taken to buy the shares are deductible in box 2. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Keep the purchase and sale documents and the notarial deeds, because the acquisition price determines the disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel) ([Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- On a sale the transfer price minus the acquisition price is the benefit (voordeel), and at a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs) the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is set by decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and offset in the order in which the losses arose, so check the decision and record the loss separately ([art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst verlies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).
- With a fiscal partner, choose the division of the box 2 income in the return. Any division is allowed as long as the total is 100% ([art. 2.17 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Also declare the income from a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Check the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) of the BV against the 2020 amounts. It must be at least the highest of 75% of the comparable wage, the highest employee wage and € 46.000, with the € 5.000 threshold for all bodies together. It is box 1 wage and not a box 2 benefit ([Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst loon](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For a remaining PEB, the BV must index the accrued rights yearly if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision every year actuarially. Not indexing can be seen as waiving (prijsgeven) of the rights ([Belastingdienst uitfaseren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). PEB payouts are box 1 income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), not box 2 ([art. 10a.18](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- For an ODV, apply the 2020 market rate of -0,107% to the yearly increase ([CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/), [Wet LB 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).
- The excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) regime does not apply in 2020, so a loan from the BV is not taxed as box 2 income under that statute in this year ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- If the taxpayer ceases to be a resident other than by death, that emigration is a deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) for the shares ([art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

Open points for 2020:

- The summed box 2 amount of two fiscal partners is UNVERIFIED. No primary page in the checked material states a summed two-partner amount. The rule is that the partners may divide the income in any proportion as long as the total is 100%, and art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). A primary page that shows how the two individual amounts are presented together would settle it.
- The list of rollovers (doorschuiving) on request for gifts, inheritance and emigration is UNVERIFIED and its Status is unknown in the table. It comes from the research and was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). A primary text of the rollover articles in the 2020 version would settle it.
- The APV loss deferral triggers other than the 10-year term are not confirmed on a primary page. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term. A primary source on the APV offset after a third-party sale or death would settle it ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- The statement that a 2025 decision (beschikking) on partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect as a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) in a Netherlands company is UNVERIFIED. It was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages. Art. 7.5 itself is Verified ([art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- The statement that the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) reduces the profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

Resolved conflicts, listed for context and not open for 2020:

- Art. 4.6: settled that the 2020 text has sub a to d only, that sub e comes from the 2022 text and that sub f applies from 1 January 2025 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)).
- PEB start date: settled at 1 April 2017. The consolidated text BWBR0039380 shows "Geldend van 01-04-2017" and the Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen) gives 1 April 2017 ([BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [CAP](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). The leniency (coulance) decision of 22 March 2017 (Stcrt. 2017, 18118) let dga's (director-substantial shareholders) adjust "uiterlijk tot en met 30 juni 2017" and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Belastingdienst uitfaseren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). The published Stb. 2017, 115 Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under the Advisory Referendum Act (Wet raadgevend referendum) ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)).
- Flat-rate percentage (forfaitair percentage): the 1 January 2020 text printed 5,33%, replaced retroactively to 1 January 2020 by Stcrt. 2020, 20572 with 5,28% ([art. 4.14, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Loss-conversion percentage for 2024 to 2026: the statute gives 24,5% and the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 line "De korting is 26,9%" is a stale handbook figure ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)). For 2020 the 2020 text and FISIN 2020 agree on 26,25% ([art. 4.53, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
