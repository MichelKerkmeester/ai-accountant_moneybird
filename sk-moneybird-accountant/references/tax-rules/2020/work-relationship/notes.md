## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Second restriction of the moratorium exception: from 1 January 2020 enforcement also when instructions (aanwijzingen) are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020. Announced in the third progress letter (derde voortgangsbrief) of 24 June 2019. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Moratorium end date: the fourth postponement set it at 1 January 2021, decided on 24 June 2019, so this date framed 2020. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Legislative pause: the fifth progress letter (vijfde voortgangsbrief) of 18 June 2020 records that the cabinet stops working out new legislation. That is a 2020 event. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Web module (webmodule): still not live in 2020. The Sixth Progress Letter (zesde voortgangsbrief) of 16 November 2020 records the expected pilot start on 11 January 2021 and a restart of enforcement at the earliest on 1 October 2021. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Case law: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of intention of the parties (partijbedoeling), decided in 2020. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Tax interest (belastingrente) IB 2020: 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. The lower rate during the COVID period was new for 2020 and the reduction for the income tax started on 1 July 2020. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Tax interest on VAT (btw) 2020: 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- IB penalties (boetes) 2020: default fine (verzuimboete) € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514. These amounts stayed the same for 2020 to 2024. https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2020: € 68 after 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance). The same figure held for 2020 to 2024 and rose to € 82 for 2025 and 2026. https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht): AWR art. 52 lid 4, 7 years, same text in 2020 and 2026, so no change. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01
- Payment terms: Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, so no change. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01
- The 2019 comparison is not in the checked sources for this year. Where the sources state a 2020 start date, it is listed above. Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) show no 2020-specific change in the material.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record per client and per engagement what the parties agreed and how the work was actually done. The qualification depends on facts such as employer authority (werkgeversgezag), personal work (persoonlijke arbeid), pay, embedding, commercial risk and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst
- If an engagement falls under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst), keep that agreement in the file and check that the actual work matches the description. Working under an approved model agreement means no employment (loondienst) only if the parties work as described. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten
- Keep the records for 7 years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 applies to natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Basic records (basisgegevens) include the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren
- The 2020 income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) had to arrive before 8 May 2021, or before 1 September 2021 with 4 months deferral (uitstel). https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- No tax interest (belastingrente) is charged on a 2020 return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021, per the decree (Besluit) of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html
- Tax interest on the 2020 income tax runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%. It is not charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment (aanslag) date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting
- A payment of tax below the threshold (grens) is not collected. For 2020 the current wording gives assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) € 47 and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) € 15, the older wording gives € 48 and € 16. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before year end count toward the threshold, a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not. https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- VAT (btw) return and payment dates (betaaldatums) for 2020: monthly filers had December 2019 on 31 January 2020 through December 2020 on 31 January 2021, quarterly filers had Q4 2019 on 31 January 2020 through Q4 on 31 January 2021, and yearly filers had 2019 by 31 March 2020 and 2020 by 31 March 2021. Return and payment must both be received. https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte
- A late VAT return (btw-aangifte) after 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance) gave a default fine (verzuimboete) of € 68 in 2020. https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9: an assessment is collectible 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet), an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after, a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after, and a provisional assessment in monthly instalments (termijnen). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01
- A return for tax year 2020 could still be filed without a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) until 31 December 2025, and an online request (verzoek) for a reduction of the 2020 assessment was possible until and including 31 December 2025. https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting
- Additional assessment (navordering) for tax year 2020: 5 years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose plus deferral, and 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01
- A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag
- A VAT (btw) deferral request is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity, in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date (aangiftedatum), and a supplementary assessment and penalty remain possible. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) 2020 carries Status enacted and Check UNVERIFIED. The material lists no 2020-specific change for model agreements. What would settle it is a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page or policy decree (beleidsbesluit) dated in 2020 on the assessment or withdrawal of model agreements. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) for 2020 have two wordings. The current page says higher than and prints € 47 and € 15. Pages up to the 2023 captures used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed € 48 and € 16. Both are Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading used here. What would settle it is a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page version that states the 2020 threshold and its exact wording together. https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- The 2021 definitive-assessment (aanslag) page still uses before 1 May 2021 in its example while the deadline page and the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) decree (besluit) give 8 May 2021. Both are Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text. The 8 May date is the operative one for 2021 per the decree. What would settle it is a later version of the definitive-assessment page using 8 May in the example. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- Whether the deferral (uitstel) request deadline for the 2020 return also moved to 8 May 2021 is not stated on a primary page that was opened. What would settle it is an archived deferral page or a decree that names the request date for the 2020 return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte
- The ruling text of HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, was not opened. Only the case reference and its subject (the role of partijbedoeling) come from the opened material. What would settle it is the published ruling itself. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The low hourly rate presumption did not exist in 2020, so no 2020 amount for it exists. The first amount, € 36, rests on the WML of € 14,06 at 1 January 2025. The indexed amount printed on primary pages for reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2026 is € 38, while the statutory text keeps € 36. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 of the Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) that sets the amount for the first application (eerste toepassing) was not found on 2026-10-11. What would settle it is that ministerial regulation once published. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html
- The Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ) bill 36 912 was still awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag) on 2026-10-11, with no debate and no vote. Its figures (about 70% of income up to the minimum wage (minimumloon), a waiting period (wachttijd) of about one year, an opt-out with a qualifying private policy) are docket summary and proposed, not law, and they have no 2020 effect. https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912
- The Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) is proposed with an intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) in January 2028, with an internet consultation (internetconsultatie) open until 29 October 2026. It has no 2020 effect. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) itself. It is the decree of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. The Wet DBA itself is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force (in werking) 1 May 2016. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The dates in the case-law line for later years, the penalty policy (boetebeleid) by year, the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium) on 1 January 2025 and the enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) wording about integration from 2027 all sit outside 2020 and are recorded only as cross-year context. They are not 2020 values.
