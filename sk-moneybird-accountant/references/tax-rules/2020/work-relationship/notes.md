## 5. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Second restriction of the moratorium exception: from 1 January 2020 enforcement also when aanwijzingen are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020. Announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Moratorium end date: the fourth postponement set it at 1 January 2021, decided on 24 June 2019, so this date framed 2020. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Legislative pause: the vijfde voortgangsbrief of 18 June 2020 records that the cabinet stops working out new legislation. That is a 2020 event. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Webmodule: still not live in 2020. The zesde voortgangsbrief of 16 November 2020 records the expected pilot start on 11 January 2021 and a restart of handhaving at the earliest on 1 October 2021. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Case law: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of partijbedoeling, decided in 2020. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- Belastingrente IB 2020: 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. The lower rate during the COVID period was new for 2020 and the reduction for the inkomstenbelasting started on 1 July 2020. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Belastingrente btw 2020: 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- IB boetes 2020: verzuimboete € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, betaalverzuimboete up to € 5.514. These amounts stayed the same for 2020 to 2024. https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2020: € 68 after 7 kalenderdagen coulance. The same figure held for 2020 to 2024 and rose to € 82 for 2025 and 2026. https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- Bewaarplicht: AWR art. 52 lid 4, 7 years, same text in 2020 and 2026, so no change. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01
- Payment terms: Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, so no change. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01
- The 2019 comparison is not in the checked sources for this year. Where the sources state a 2020 start date, it is listed above. Modelovereenkomsten show no 2020-specific change in the material.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record per client and per engagement what the parties agreed and how the work was actually done. The qualification depends on facts such as werkgeversgezag, personal work, pay, embedding, commercial risk and ondernemerschap. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst
- If an engagement falls under an approved modelovereenkomst, keep that agreement in the file and check that the actual work matches the description. Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst only if the parties work as described. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten
- Keep the administratie for 7 years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 applies to natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. Basisgegevens include the grootboek, debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, voorraadadministratie, in- and verkoopadministratie, loonadministratie, and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren
- The 2020 income tax return had to arrive before 8 May 2021, or before 1 September 2021 with 4 months uitstel. https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- No belastingrente is charged on a 2020 return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021, per the Besluit of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html
- Belastingrente on the 2020 income tax runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%. It is not charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting
- A payment of tax below the grens is not collected. For 2020 the current wording gives aanslaggrens € 47 and teruggaafgrens € 15, the older wording gives € 48 and € 16. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not. https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- Btw return and payment dates for 2020: monthly filers had December 2019 on 31 January 2020 through December 2020 on 31 January 2021, quarterly filers had Q4 2019 on 31 January 2020 through Q4 on 31 January 2021, and yearly filers had 2019 by 31 March 2020 and 2020 by 31 March 2021. Return and payment must both be received. https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte
- A late btw return after 7 kalenderdagen coulance gave a verzuimboete of € 68 in 2020. https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9: an aanslag is collectible 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet, a navorderingsaanslag 1 month after, a naheffingsaanslag 14 days after, and a voorlopige aanslag in monthly termijnen. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01
- A return for tax year 2020 could still be filed without an aangiftebrief until 31 December 2025, and an online verzoek om vermindering for the aanslag 2020 was possible until and including 31 December 2025. https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting
- Navordering for tax year 2020: 5 years after the belastingschuld arose plus uitstel, and 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01
- A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag
- A btw uitstel request is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties (an ernstige calamiteit), in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date, and a naheffingsaanslag and boete remain possible. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Modelovereenkomsten 2020 carries Status enacted and Check UNVERIFIED. The material lists no 2020-specific change for modelovereenkomsten. What would settle it is a Belastingdienst page or beleidsbesluit dated in 2020 on the assessment or withdrawal of modelovereenkomsten. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens for 2020 have two wordings. The current page says higher than and prints € 47 and € 15. Pages up to the 2023 captures used gelijk aan of meer dan and printed € 48 and € 16. Both are Belastingdienst text. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading used here. What would settle it is a Belastingdienst page version that states the 2020 threshold and its exact wording together. https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- The 2021 definitive-aanslag page still uses before 1 May 2021 in its example while the deadline page and the Staatscourant besluit give 8 May 2021. Both are Belastingdienst text. The 8 May date is the operative one for 2021 per the besluit. What would settle it is a later version of the definitive-aanslag page using 8 May in the example. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- Whether the uitstel request deadline for the 2020 return also moved to 8 May 2021 is not stated on a primary page that was opened. What would settle it is an archived uitstel page or a besluit that names the request date for the 2020 return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte
- The ruling text of HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, was not opened. Only the case reference and its subject (the role of partijbedoeling) come from the opened material. What would settle it is the published ruling itself. https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The low hourly rate presumption did not exist in 2020, so no 2020 amount for it exists. The first amount, € 36, rests on the WML of € 14,06 at 1 January 2025. The indexed amount printed on primary pages for peildatum 1 January 2026 is € 38, while the statutory text keeps € 36. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 of the VBAR that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. What would settle it is that ministeriële regeling once published. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html
- The BAZ bill 36 912 was still awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag on 2026-10-11, with no debate and no vote. Its figures (about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, an opt-out with a qualifying private policy) are docket summary and proposed, not law, and they have no 2020 effect. https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912
- The Zelfstandigenwet is proposed with an intended inwerkingtreding in January 2028, with an internet consultation open until 29 October 2026. It has no 2020 effect. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force 1 May 2016. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file
- The dates in the case-law line for later years, the boetebeleid by year, the end of the moratorium on 1 January 2025 and the handhavingsplan wording about integration from 2027 all sit outside 2020 and are recorded only as cross-year context. They are not 2020 values.
