## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 DBA enforcement regime

The Assessment of Employment Relationships (Deregulation) Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) is the Act of 3 February (februari) 2016 and entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 May 2016. It replaced the VAR. The Wet DBA did not itself change the test for employment (loondienst), it changed who carries the risk of the qualification and how the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) acts. In 2020 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration worked under the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) that was already in force (in werking). Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied. In a case where the client is not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration first gave an instruction (aanwijzing): reshape the relationship so the contractor (opdrachtnemer) really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship (arbeidsrelatie, dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction (aanwijzing) was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally (opzettelijk) creating (ontstaan) or continuing (voortbestaan) a situation of evident false self-employment (evidente schijnzelfstandigheid), while knowing or being able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Cases of acting in bad faith could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) for payroll taxes (loonheffing, loonheffingen) and penalties (boetes).

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

The Wet DBA itself is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force from 1 May 2016. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the decree (Besluit) of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement.

Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html

### 3.2 Second restriction of the moratorium exception

From 1 January 2020 the enforcement exception grew. Enforcement also followed when instructions (aanwijzingen) were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020. This second restriction of the moratorium exception was announced in the third progress letter (derde voortgangsbrief) of 24 June 2019. Practical meaning for a 2020 relationship: if the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration inspected the relationship and found false self-employment, the first step was still an instruction, but a party that ignored it faced correction from the date of the instruction.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.3 Moratorium end date set at that time

The fourth postponement moved the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium) to 1 January 2021. That decision was taken on 24 June 2019, not in 2020. For the reader of a 2020 file this matters because it is the date that was operative during 2020 for the restart of enforcement. Later postponements moved the actual end of the moratorium to 1 January 2025, but that is not the 2020 position.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.4 Legislative pause

The fifth progress letter (vijfde voortgangsbrief) of 18 June 2020 records that the cabinet stops working out new legislation. So during the second half of 2020 there was no bill in progress on the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties). The replacement of the Wet DBA would only come later through the VBAR and the Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet), both outside this year.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.5 Case law

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of intention of the parties (partijbedoeling) in judging whether an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst) exists. The ruling text itself was not opened during the check, so only the case reference and its subject are recorded here. Case law from later years (Deliveroo in 2023, the Uber prejudiciële vragen) does not belong in a 2020 file.

The Work Relationship Assessment web module (webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie) was not yet live in 2020. The Sixth Progress Letter (zesde voortgangsbrief) of 16 November 2020 records that the pilot was expected to start on 11 January 2021. The same letter puts the restart of enforcement at the earliest on 1 October 2021. A client (opdrachtgever) who wanted a judgment in 2020 had no web module for it. This item carries the Check tag Corrected because the research had not found this letter.

### 3.7 model agreements (Modelovereenkomsten) 2020

A model agreement (modelovereenkomst) is an agreement text that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration assessed in advance. Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment, but only if the parties work as described in it. In 2020 approved model agreements were in use. The material lists no 2020-specific change for them, so the 2020 situation is UNVERIFIED for any change and the general rule stands. The end of the scheme came much later: since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed, and approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029. The approval of the model agreements free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. None of that changes what applies to 2020.

Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten

### 3.8 Tax interest (belastingrente) IB 2020

Tax interest (belastingrente) on income tax uses the general rate table for all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen). For 2020 the rate was 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. For the income tax the reduction started 1 July 2020. This table says what rate applied during a period of the year. It does not by itself say over which period interest runs.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.9 Tax interest on VAT (btw) 2020

For VAT (btw) the same table applies. In 2020 the rate was 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. Tax interest on VAT (btw) is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment. Late payment runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No tax interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. Days are counted as 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting

### 3.10 Tax interest on tax year 2020 IB

For the tax year 2020 the interest on income tax runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%, the rate from the rate table. Interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment (aanslag) deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral (uitstel). It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) it runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No tax interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. For 2020 the deadline was not 1 May but 8 May 2021, and a separate decree approved the week.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### 3.11 IB return 2020 filing deadline (aangiftetermijn)

The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. For the 2020 return the deadline was before 8 May 2021. The 1 May date was moved one week because of a technical failure (storing), per Stcrt. 2021, 24144. The same text appears in captures of 14 April and 3 May 2021. This item carries the Check tag Corrected because the research had not found this date.

### 3.12 Tax interest approval for the 8 May week

The decree of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, made under art. 63 AWR, states that no tax interest is charged if the assessment follows a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021. The decree expired on 1 January 2023. For the 2020 return this means the extra week did not create interest.

Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html

### 3.13 IB return 2020 with deferral (uitstel)

Deferral must be requested before the filing date (aangiftedatum), in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra. For the 2020 return that made the deadline 1 September 2021. The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest. Online deferral in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) was possible until 1 May for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). The paper form sent by post was the route after 1 May and for a filing date other than 1 May.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.14 Definitive assessment 2020 latest

AWR art. 11 lid 3 (same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026) says the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. For the 2020 return filed before 1 May 2021 the definitive assessment came at the latest on 31 December 2023. With 4 months deferral the return was due before 1 September 2021 and the assessment came at the latest on 30 April 2024. This is the official example on the 2021 page.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.15 Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2020

The assessment threshold and refund threshold are the thresholds below which no assessment is imposed and no refund is paid. The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold. For 2020 the current table prints € 47 and € 15. Pages up to the 2023 captures used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed € 48 and € 16. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before year end count toward the threshold (grens), a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not. The 2020 page applied the threshold only if no earlier provisional assessment with a payment or refund existed.

### 3.16 Filing without invitation, tax year 2020

Without a filing invitation letter a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2020 that made the last date 31 December 2025. A route to file without an invitation means a missing return does not close the file by itself.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen

### 3.17 Request for a reduction of the 2020 assessment online (verzoek om vermindering aanslag)

Objection (bezwaar) must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter. After the objection (bezwaar) term a request to (ambtshalve) reduction is possible. Online is allowed only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. For the assessment 2020 the online route ran until and including 31 December 2025. Otherwise the request goes by letter.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.18 VAT monthly dates 2020

AWR art. 10 lid 2 sets the return term for a period (tijdvak) at least one month after the end of the period (tijdvak), and AWR art. 19 lid 1 sets payment within one month after the end of the period. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together. For a monthly filer in 2020 the dates were: December 2019 on 31 January 2020, January on 29 February, February on 31 March, March on 30 April, April on 31 May, May on 30 June, June on 31 July, July on 31 August, August on 30 September, September on 31 October, October on 30 November, November on 31 December 2020, and December 2020 on 31 January 2021. Return and payment must both be received by these dates. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Deferral for a VAT return (btw-aangifte) is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity, in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date, and a supplementary assessment and a penalty (boete) remain possible.

For a quarterly filer in 2020 the dates were: Q4 2019 on 31 January 2020, Q1 on 30 April 2020, Q2 on 31 July 2020, Q3 on 31 October 2020, and Q4 on 31 January 2021. The same rules on return and payment apply as for the monthly filer.

### 3.21 VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2020

A late or missing VAT return has a leniency (coulance) of 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen). After that leniency a default fine (verzuimboete) of € 68 applies for 2020. The same figure applied for 2020 to 2024, and rose to € 82 for 2025 and 2026.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte

### 3.22 IB penalties 2020

After the deadline an IB filer gets a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. No return at all gives an ex officio (ambtshalve) assessment plus default fine. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty (vergrijpboete) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent, also with an additional assessment. No penalty applies when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. Amounts for 2020: default fine € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

### 3.23 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) 2020

AWR art. 52 lid 4 (same in 2020 and 2026) requires persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) to keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records are the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### 3.24 Payment terms 2020

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. A provisional assessment IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, with the first one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01

### 3.25 Links that carry over from the cross-year rules

The employment test (loondienst test, loondiensttoets) has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform personal work (persoonlijke arbeid), and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax or VAT. The employment page lists the deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen), among them contractors for work (aannemers van werk) and helpers, agents (agenten), artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden, minimumloon, usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage), interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors (opdrachtnemers) working through an intermediary (intermediair). Home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The contractor then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. These rules are the baseline that the Wet DBA leaves in place, so they hold in 2020. The Wet DBA itself is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force from 1 May 2016. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is the decree of 8 April 2016 on the opt-out for some deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen), not the Wet DBA.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html

### 3.26 The low hourly rate presumption and the BAZ link

The low hourly rate presumption (rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van een uurtarief) did not exist in 2020. It was built later, in the VBAR. The internet consultation (internetconsultatie) of the original VBAR ran from 6 October 2023 with intended start 1 July 2025. The bill went to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer). A memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the clarification part. The House of Representatives adopted it on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Senate on 16 June 2026 by a vote by sitting and standing (zitten en opstaan). It became the Act of 18 June (juni) 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, and entered into force on 31 December 2026 by decree of 13 July (juli) 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone (in force before 1 January 2027). The new art. 7:610aa BW lid 1 presumes that someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour works under an employment contract. Lid 2 lets the Minister of SZW change the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 of the Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3 makes it not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). The effect is that the worker (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783

The amount was first explained as € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (excl. btw) at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount printed on primary pages is € 38. So € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 2026. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. None of this was 2020 law.
