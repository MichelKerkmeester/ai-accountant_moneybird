## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 DBA enforcement regime

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties is the Wet van 3 februari 2016 and entered into force on 1 May 2016. It replaced the VAR. The Wet DBA did not itself change the test for loondienst, it changed who carries the risk of the qualification and how the Belastingdienst acts. In 2020 the Belastingdienst worked under the handhavingsmoratorium (enforcement moratorium) that was already in force. Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing (instruction): reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer (contractor) really works as a zelfstandige (self-employed person), or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

The exact Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force 1 May 2016. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement.

Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html

### 3.2 Second restriction of the moratorium exception

From 1 January 2020 the enforcement exception grew. Enforcement also followed when aanwijzingen were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020. This second restriction of the moratorium exception was announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019. Practical meaning for a 2020 relationship: if the Belastingdienst inspected the relationship and found schijnzelfstandigheid, the first step was still an aanwijzing, but a party that ignored it faced correction from the date of the aanwijzing.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.3 Moratorium end date set at that time

The fourth postponement moved the end of the moratorium to 1 January 2021. That decision was taken on 24 June 2019, not in 2020. For the reader of a 2020 file this matters because it is the date that was operative during 2020 for the restart of enforcement. Later postponements moved the actual end of the moratorium to 1 January 2025, but that is not the 2020 position.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.4 Legislative pause

The vijfde voortgangsbrief of 18 June 2020 records that the cabinet stops working out new legislation. So during the second half of 2020 there was no bill in progress on the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. The replacement of the Wet DBA would only come later through the VBAR and the Zelfstandigenwet, both outside this year.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.5 Case law

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (the intention of the parties) in judging whether an arbeidsovereenkomst exists. The ruling text itself was not opened during the check, so only the case reference and its subject are recorded here. Case law from later years (Deliveroo in 2023, the Uber prejudiciële vragen) does not belong in a 2020 file.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.6 Webmodule status 2020

The webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie (assessment tool for work relationships) was not yet live in 2020. The zesde voortgangsbrief of 16 November 2020 records that the pilot was expected to start on 11 January 2021. The same letter puts the restart of handhaving at the earliest on 1 October 2021. An opdrachtgever who wanted a judgment in 2020 had no webmodule for it. This item carries the Check tag Corrected because the research had not found this letter.

Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

### 3.7 Modelovereenkomsten 2020

A modelovereenkomst (model agreement) is an agreement text that the Belastingdienst assessed in advance. Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described in it. In 2020 approved modelovereenkomsten were in use. The material lists no 2020-specific change for them, so the 2020 situation is UNVERIFIED for any change and the general rule stands. The end of the scheme came much later: since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed, and approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029. The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. None of that changes what applies to 2020.

Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten

### 3.8 Belastingrente IB 2020

Belastingrente (tax interest) on income tax uses the general rate table for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen. For 2020 the rate was 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. For the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started 1 July 2020. This table says what rate applied during a period of the year. It does not by itself say over which period interest runs.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.9 Belastingrente btw 2020

For btw (VAT) the same table applies. In 2020 the rate was 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. Belastingrente on btw is charged on a naheffingsaanslag (additional assessment) and on late payment. Late payment runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. A naheffingsaanslag runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. Days are counted as 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting

### 3.10 Belastingrente on tax year 2020 IB

For the tax year 2020 the interest on income tax runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%, the rate from the rate table. Interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel (deferral). It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For a navorderingsaanslag it runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. For 2020 the deadline was not 1 May but 8 May 2021, and a separate besluit approved the week.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### 3.11 IB return 2020 filing deadline

The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief (invitation to file), often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. For the 2020 return the deadline was before 8 May 2021. The 1 May date was moved one week because of a technical storing (failure), per Stcrt. 2021, 24144. The same text appears in captures of 14 April and 3 May 2021. This item carries the Check tag Corrected because the research had not found this date.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.12 Belastingrente approval for the 8 May week

The Besluit of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, made under art. 63 AWR, states that no belastingrente is charged if the aanslag follows a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021. The besluit expired on 1 January 2023. For the 2020 return this means the extra week did not create interest.

Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html

### 3.13 IB return 2020 with uitstel

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra. For the 2020 return that made the deadline 1 September 2021. The form can be used only for the first uitstel request, and the fiscal partner must request separately. Uitstel usually means belastingrente. Online uitstel in Mijn Belastingdienst was possible until 1 May for someone who received an aangiftebrief. The paper form sent by post was the route after 1 May and for a filing date other than 1 May.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.14 Definitive aanslag 2020 latest

AWR art. 11 lid 3 (same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026) says the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. For the 2020 return filed before 1 May 2021 the definitive aanslag came at the latest on 31 December 2023. With 4 months uitstel the return was due before 1 September 2021 and the aanslag came at the latest on 30 April 2024. This is the official example on the 2021 page.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.15 Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2020

The aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens are the thresholds below which no assessment is imposed and no refund is paid. The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens. For 2020 the current table prints € 47 and € 15. Pages up to the 2023 captures used gelijk aan of meer dan (equal to or more than) and printed € 48 and € 16. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not. The 2020 page applied the grens only if no earlier voorlopige aanslag with a payment or refund existed.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag https://web.archive.org/web/20200421/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.16 Filing without invitation, tax year 2020

Without an aangiftebrief a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2020 that made the last date 31 December 2025. A route to file without an invitation means a missing return does not close the file by itself.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen

### 3.17 Verzoek om vermindering aanslag 2020 online

Bezwaar (objection) must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter. After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible. Online is allowed only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. For the aanslag 2020 the online route ran until and including 31 December 2025. Otherwise the request goes by letter.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.18 VAT monthly dates 2020

AWR art. 10 lid 2 sets the return term for a tijdvak (period) at least one month after the end of the tijdvak, and AWR art. 19 lid 1 sets payment within one month after the end of the tijdvak. The Belastingdienst tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together. For a monthly filer in 2020 the dates were: December 2019 on 31 January 2020, January on 29 February, February on 31 March, March on 30 April, April on 31 May, May on 30 June, June on 31 July, July on 31 August, August on 30 September, September on 31 October, October on 30 November, November on 31 December 2020, and December 2020 on 31 January 2021. Return and payment must both be received by these dates. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties (an ernstige calamiteit), in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date, and a naheffingsaanslag and boete remain possible.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.19 VAT quarterly dates 2020

For a quarterly filer in 2020 the dates were: Q4 2019 on 31 January 2020, Q1 on 30 April 2020, Q2 on 31 July 2020, Q3 on 31 October 2020, and Q4 on 31 January 2021. The same rules on return and payment apply as for the monthly filer.

For a yearly filer the return for 2019 was due by 31 March 2020 and the return for 2020 by 31 March 2021. Return and payment must both be received by that date.

### 3.21 Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2020

A late or missing btw return has a coulance of 7 kalenderdagen (calendar days). After that coulance a verzuimboete of € 68 applies for 2020. The same figure applied for 2020 to 2024, and rose to € 82 for 2025 and 2026.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte

### 3.22 IB boetes 2020

After the deadline an IB filer gets a herinnering (reminder), then an aanmaning with 10 werkdagen. Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete. The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag. No return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag plus verzuimboete. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete, also with a navorderingsaanslag. No boete applies when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief. Amounts for 2020: verzuimboete € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, betaalverzuimboete up to € 5.514. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete.

Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

### 3.23 Bewaarplicht 2020

AWR art. 52 lid 4 (same in 2020 and 2026) requires administratieplichtigen (persons with an administration duty) to keep the gegevensdragers (data carriers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (profit from business) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table says basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basisgegevens are the grootboek, debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, voorraadadministratie, in- and verkoopadministratie, loonadministratie, and data relevant for the taxation of third parties.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### 3.24 Payment terms 2020

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An aanslag is invorderbaar (collectible) 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, with the first one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01

### 3.25 Links that carry over from the cross-year rules

The loondienst test has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid, and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen (deemed employment relationships), among them aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden (usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage), stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair. Thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. These rules are the baseline that the Wet DBA leaves in place, so they hold in 2020. The Wet DBA itself is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force 1 May 2016. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for some fictieve dienstbetrekkingen, not the Wet DBA.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html

### 3.26 The low hourly rate presumption and the BAZ link

The low hourly rate presumption (rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van een uurtarief) did not exist in 2020. It was built later, in the VBAR. The internet consultation of the original VBAR ran from 6 October 2023 with intended start 1 July 2025. The bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. A nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part. The Tweede Kamer adopted it on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan. It became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, and entered into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone (in force before 1 January 2027). The new art. 7:610aa BW lid 1 presumes that someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour works under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2 lets the Minister of SZW change the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3 makes it not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. The effect is that the werkende can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783

The amount was first explained as € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag for AOV and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount printed on primary pages is € 38. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. None of this was 2020 law.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/

The BAZ (bill 36 912) is the other half of the later package. It was ingediend on 24 March 2026, a technische briefing followed on 13 May 2026, the inbreng verslag was delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026. On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote. The docket summary says about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. In 2020 this bill did not exist and had no effect on the qualification of arbeidsrelaties.

Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912
