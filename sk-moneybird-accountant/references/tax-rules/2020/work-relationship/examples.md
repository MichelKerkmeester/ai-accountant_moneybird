### Example 1: aanwijzing route for a non-kwaadwillende opdrachtgever (Calculated example)

This example uses only the rules in section 3.1 and 3.2. Inputs are invented.

- A client has one worker who invoices through a one-person business. The relationship in fact shows werkgeversgezag, personal work and pay, so it is a dienstbetrekking.
- The Belastingdienst finds this during a check. The case is not kwaadwillend.
- Step 1: the Belastingdienst gives an aanwijzing. The usual term is 3 months.
- Step 2: the client does nothing within the term.
- Step 3: a correctieverplichting applies from the date of the aanwijzing, not from the start of the relationship.
- Step 4: if the client had reshaped the relationship or processed it in the loonaangifte within the term, no correctieverplichting would have followed from the aanwijzing route.

Arithmetic: no amount is computed here because the material gives no rate or base for the correctie. The step that matters is the date, the date of the aanwijzing.

### Example 2: the two thresholds in the 2020 aanslag (Calculated example)

This example uses the section 3.15 figures. It reads the grens in the wording that was current until the 2023 captures, gelijk aan of meer dan, and prints the 2020 values € 48 and € 16 from that wording.

- Input: the final income tax calculation over 2020 comes out at a payable amount of exactly € 47.
- Under the older wording, € 47 is below gelijk aan of meer dan € 48, so no aanslag is imposed.
- Input: the calculation comes out at exactly € 48.
- Under the older wording, € 48 is gelijk aan of meer dan € 48, so an aanslag is imposed.
- Input: the calculation comes out as a refund of exactly € 15.
- Under the older wording, € 15 is below gelijk aan of meer dan € 16, so no refund is paid.
- Input: the calculation comes out as a refund of exactly € 16.
- Under the older wording, a refund of € 16 is gelijk aan of meer dan € 16, so the refund is paid.

Cross-check with the current wording: pay € 47 is not higher than € 47, so no aanslag, and € 48 is higher than € 47, so an aanslag. Refund € 15 is not higher than € 15, so no refund, and € 16 is higher than € 15, so a refund. For whole euros the two wordings give the same result.

### Example 4: belastingrente on a late 2020 return (Calculated example)

This example uses the section 3.8, 3.10 and 3.12 figures. Inputs are invented.

- The return for 2020 is filed on 20 May 2021, after the 8 May 2021 deadline. No uitstel was granted.
- Belastingrente runs from 1 July 2021 at 4% per year.
- Day count: 30 days per month, 360 days per year.
- Step 1: from 1 July 2021 to 31 December 2021 is 6 months, so 6 x 30 = 180 days.
- Step 2: from 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2022 is 6 months, so 6 x 30 = 180 days.
- Step 3: total period used in this example is 360 days, which is one 360-day year.
- Step 4: rente before rounding: 4% x 360 / 360 = 4% of the tax amount.
- Step 5: on an open amount of € 2.000 the rente is € 2.000 x 4% = € 80.
- Step 6: rounded down to whole euros: € 80.

The actual end date of the interest period is 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and the example fixes the period only to show the arithmetic.

### Example 5: a late btw return in 2020 (Calculated example)

This example uses the section 3.21 figure. Inputs are invented.

- A monthly filer sends the May 2020 return after the 30 June 2020 date.
- The coulance is 7 kalenderdagen.
- Step 1: the return is 20 days late, which is more than 7 days.
- Step 2: the coulance does not cover it.
- Step 3: the verzuimboete is € 68 for 2020.
- Interaction: belastingrente on btw runs from 1 January after the tax year, so for a May 2020 period the rente start is 1 January 2021. A voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year avoids rente.

### Example 6: the hourly amount method behind the later rechtsvermoeden (Calculated example)

This example uses the section 3.26 figures to show the method. It is not 2020 law and no 2020 rule uses it.

- Step 1: take 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025: 1,20 x € 14,06 = € 16,872.
- Step 2: add 25% opslag for AOV and pension and 15% opslag for general costs, so multiply by 1,40: € 16,872 x 1,40 = € 23,6208.
- Step 3: multiply by 1,5 for non-billable hours: € 23,6208 x 1,5 = € 35,4312.
- Step 4: round up to whole euros: € 36,00. This is the statutory base amount at peildatum 1 January 2025.
- Step 5: indexation follows the WML percentage. The WML rose from € 14,06 to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026. The ratio is € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,04623, or about 4,62%.
- Step 6: apply the ratio to the unrounded € 35,43: € 35,43 x 1,04623 = about € 37,07.
- Step 7: round up to whole euros: € 38. This is the amount printed on primary pages for peildatum 1 January 2026.
