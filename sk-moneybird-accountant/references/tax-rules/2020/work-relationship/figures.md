## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the work-relationship side of being a sole trader in 2020: whether the work is employment (loondienst) or self-employment (zelfstandigheid), the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) test, the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) and the instruction (aanwijzing) route, the use of model agreements (modelovereenkomsten), and the case law of that year. In 2020 the prohibition on false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid, disguised employment) was not actively enforced against parties who were not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend). A first restriction of the moratorium exception took effect on 1 January 2020 and the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium) was then postponed to 1 January 2021.

The year's law is enacted law on the DBA side plus enforcement policy. The legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) on the basis of an hourly rate (uurtarief) that later became the VBAR (Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden)) did not exist in 2020. The material for 2020 records no consultation, bill or statute on it. Work on new legislation was stopped by the fifth progress letter (vijfde voortgangsbrief) of 18 June 2020.
