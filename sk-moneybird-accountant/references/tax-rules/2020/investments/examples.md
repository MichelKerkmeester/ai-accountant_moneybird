## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

The checked material for 2020 contains no official examples. Every example below is a Calculated example built from the figures in section 2. The inputs are chosen to illustrate the figures. Where the checked material does not state a rule clearly, the example is left out.

### Example 1 (Calculated example): KIA in the 28% band

A sole trader invests € 40.000 in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2020. Every asset is above € 450, none is excluded and none is made available to third parties. The total falls in the second band of the KIA table, above € 2.400 and at or below € 58.238.

Step 1: the investment is € 40.000,00.
Step 2: the KIA is 28% of the investment, so € 40.000,00 × 0,28 = € 11.200,00.

Source of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 2 (Calculated example): KIA in the top band with the 7,56% reduction

The same trader invests € 150.000 in 2020. The total is above € 107.848 and at or below € 323.544.

Step 1: the part above € 107.848 is € 150.000,00 - € 107.848,00 = € 42.152,00.
Step 2: 7,56% of € 42.152,00 is € 42.152,00 × 0,0756 = € 3.186,69, rounded from € 3.186,6912.
Step 3: the KIA is € 16.307,00 - € 3.186,69 = € 13.120,31.

Source of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 3 (Calculated example): EIA on a new asset

A trader buys a new machine on the Energielijst for € 30.000. The asset is above the € 2.500 minimum and is new.

Step 1: the investment is € 30.000,00.
Step 2: the EIA is 45% of the investment, so € 30.000,00 × 0,45 = € 13.500,00.
Step 3: the maximum of € 124.000.000 is far from reached, so the whole investment counts.

The same asset can also enter the total investment for the KIA, because the EIA and the KIA can be combined. Sources of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 4 (Calculated example): MIA in the three categories

A trader buys a new asset on the Milieulijst for € 50.000. The asset is above the € 2.500 minimum. The 2020 Milieulijst decides the category.

Step 1: in categorie I the MIA is € 50.000,00 × 0,36 = € 18.000,00.
Step 2: in categorie II the MIA is € 50.000,00 × 0,27 = € 13.500,00.
Step 3: in categorie III the MIA is € 50.000,00 × 0,135 = € 6.750,00.

Sources of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 5 (Calculated example): Vamil on a designated asset

A trader buys a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel for € 100.000.

Step 1: 75% of the costs may be depreciated at will, so € 100.000,00 × 0,75 = € 75.000,00.
Step 2: the remaining part follows the normal schedule, so € 100.000,00 - € 75.000,00 = € 25.000,00.

Source of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 6 (Calculated example): ordinary depreciation caps

A trader buys a machine for € 20.000 and separately has € 20.000 of goodwill.

Step 1: the yearly depreciation on the machine is at most 20% of the cost, so € 20.000,00 × 0,20 = € 4.000,00 per year, which means at least 5 years.
Step 2: the yearly depreciation on the goodwill is at most 10% of the cost, so € 20.000,00 × 0,10 = € 2.000,00 per year, which means at least 10 years.

Source of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 7 (Calculated example): bodemwaarde for two buildings

A trader owns an own-use building with a WOZ-waarde of € 400.000 and an investment building with a WOZ-waarde of € 400.000.

Step 1: for the own-use building the bodemwaarde is 50% of the WOZ-waarde, so € 400.000,00 × 0,50 = € 200.000,00.
Step 2: with a book value of € 260.000,00 at the start of 2020, the maximum depreciation is € 260.000,00 - € 200.000,00 = € 60.000,00, before any other limit.
Step 3: for the investment building the bodemwaarde is the full WOZ-waarde of € 400.000,00, so no depreciation is possible while the book value is at or below € 400.000,00.

Source of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 8 (Calculated example): desinvesteringsbijtelling

A trader buys a bedrijfsmiddel in 2020 for € 10.000 and takes the KIA over that € 10.000 at 28%. In the same year the trader disposes of the asset for a transfer price of € 10.000. The disposal is within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment.

Step 1: the transfer price of € 10.000,00 is more than the threshold of € 2.400,00, so the bijtelling applies.
Step 2: the bijtelling is 28% of the transfer price, so € 10.000,00 × 0,28 = € 2.800,00 added to profit.
Step 3: the bijtelling is never more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken. That amount is € 10.000,00, so the € 2.800,00 stands.

Sources of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47 lid 1 and lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### Example 9 (Calculated example): starter's willekeurige afschrijving and the cap

A starter who meets the startersaftrek conditions enters into obligations for € 200.000 in 2020 for assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek.

Step 1: the cap for the year is € 323.544,00 of total costs, the top of the KIA table.
Step 2: the € 200.000,00 is below the cap, so willekeurige afschrijving is allowed on the whole € 200.000,00 and the choice of assets is free.
Step 3: if the total had been € 350.000,00, the part above the cap would be € 350.000,00 - € 323.544,00 = € 26.456,00, and that part could not be depreciated at will.

Sources of the figures: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Example 10 (Calculated example): payment cap for an asset not yet in use

A trader signs for an EIA asset of € 50.000 in 2020. At the end of 2020 the asset is not yet in use and € 30.000 has been paid.

Step 1: the deduction is taken over the payments made, so over € 30.000,00.
Step 2: the EIA is 45% of € 30.000,00, so € 30.000,00 × 0,45 = € 13.500,00.
Step 3: the excess of the investment over the payments is € 50.000,00 - € 30.000,00 = € 20.000,00, and that part can support aftrek in later years.

Sources of the figures: [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
