## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- 2020 is the first year in this knowledge base, so no previous-year file exists.
- The checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic, so no old-to-new comparison can be written. 2020 is the baseline year for the later files.
- What the 2020 year block fixes: the KIA bands nil up to € 2.400, 28% up to € 58.238, € 16.307 up to € 107.848, the 7,56% reduction up to € 323.544, then nil. The € 450 KIA per-asset minimum, the 45% EIA, the MIA percentages 36%, 27% and 13,5%, the € 2.500 EIA and MIA per-asset minimum, the € 124.000.000 EIA and MIA maximum, the € 2.400 disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold, the 75% Vamil, the 10% goodwill and 20% other depreciation caps, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) rules and the starters cap of € 323.544 ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2, art. 3.42 lid 3 and lid 4, art. 3.42a lid 3, art. 3.45, art. 3.47 lid 1, art. 3.31 lid 1, art. 3.30 lid 2 and art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).
- Values that later years change, for orientation only: the MIA percentages go from 36%, 27% and 13,5% in 2020 to 45%, 36% and 27% from 1 January 2022 ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Rijksoverheid Belastingplan 2022](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). From 2024 the minimum value becomes one amount, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw", in place of the 2020 minimum value of the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- No temporary arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) scheme is found for 2020 in the checked sources. The 2023 scheme in URWA art. 13 to 15 is not this year's law and the precedents named are 2009, 2010, 2011 and partly 2013 ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The KIA is a per-business (per-onderneming) deduction, claimed by choice in the tax return (aangifte), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year, so record every 2020 investment with its amount. ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01))
- Record the date of the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or the order for every EIA, MIA and Vamil asset. The notification to RVO must be made within 3 months of that date, and the Environmental List (Milieulijst) valid on that date applies. ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden))
- Record the RVO notification itself. FISIN 2020 states "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)." ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek))
- Record the payments on an asset that is not in use at the end of 2020. The deduction (aftrek) is then taken over the amount paid and the excess follows in later years. ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01))
- Record whether at least 25% of the investment amount is paid within twelve months after the obligation and whether the asset is taken into use within three years after the start of the investment year. A failure counts as undoing the investment. ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01))
- Record disposals of invested assets for five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment. The disinvestment addition then adds a percentage of the transfer price to profit. Renting out counts for the KIA but not for the EIA or the MIA. ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling))
- Buying an asset from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek", with the exemption (ontheffing) route of art. 3.46 lid 2. ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek))
- The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset, but each can be combined with the KIA, and Vamil remains possible on costs that received EIA. ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden))
- For a BV the checked material cites Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages for the corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) on the same KIA, EIA and MIA topics, for example the page for the changes in the corporate income tax in earlier years. ([Belastingdienst Vpb](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023))

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Temporary arbitrary depreciation for 2020: none found. UNVERIFIED absence. The explanatory note to the 2022 end-of-year scheme (Eindejaarsregeling) says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year, and the URWA text opened for this check lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme. An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0. What would settle it: a 2020 regulation or a 2020 end-of-year scheme that names a temporary scheme.
- MIA and Vamil maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year: the RVO page states no year, so the 2020 value is not confirmed in the checked sources. Source: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers. What would settle it: an RVO page or an Environmental List for 2020 that prints this maximum for 2020.
- The statement that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages. UNVERIFIED. Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers and https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden. What would settle it: a primary page on EIA or MIA entitlement for a co-entitled party or a lender.
- The Environmental List code mapping for 2020 is not in the checked sources. The checked material gives the mapping of codes starting with F or G, A or D and B or E to category I, II and III from 1 January 2022 only. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023. What would settle it: the Milieulijst for 2020.
- Starters arbitrary depreciation cap, UNVERIFIED: the € 323.544 rests on reading the cross-reference in URWA art. 8 to the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table. No primary page prints the cap as a number. An earlier reading used the end of the 28% band, € 58.238. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2. What would settle it: a primary page that prints the 2020 cap for the starter's arbitrary depreciation as a number.
- Settled conflict for 2020: the disinvestment addition threshold. Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47 lid 1 gives € 2.400 and FISIN 2020 prints "€ 2.300 of minder". Settled to € 2.400, because the statute prevails. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek. No further 2020 conflicts remain.
