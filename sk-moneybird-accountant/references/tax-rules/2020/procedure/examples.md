## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Definitive aanslag 2020 (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives this example itself. A return before 1 May 2021 gives the definitive aanslag at the latest on 31 December 2023. With 4 months uitstel the return is due before 1 September 2021 and the aanslag comes at the latest on 30 April 2024. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### Example 2. The week to 8 May 2021 (Calculated example)

A sole trader files the 2020 return on 6 May 2021. The 2020 deadline was moved from 1 May 2021 to before 8 May 2021. The besluit of 4 May 2021 covers a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021, so 6 May 2021 falls inside the window. If the aanslag follows that return, no belastingrente is charged for that reason. A return that arrives on 9 May 2021 falls outside the window. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.

### Example 3. Voorlopige aanslag in monthly termijnen (Calculated example)

A voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting for 2020 is dated 15 September 2020. Months remaining after the month of the date are October, November and December, so there are 3 termijnen. The first termijn is one month after the date, so 15 October 2020, then 15 November 2020 and 15 December 2020. The termijnen are equal, so an invented amount of € 3.000,00 gives 3 termijnen of € 1.000,00 each. Step by step: € 3.000,00 / 3 = € 1.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01.

### Example 4. Belastingrente on the 2020 income tax (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. The 2020 return is filed on 31 August 2021, before the uitstel date of 1 September 2021. The aanslag is dated 20 October 2021 and deviates from the return. Because the return arrived on or after 1 May after the tax year, and because the aanslag deviates from the return, belastingrente is charged. The period starts on 1 July 2021. The rente ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date.

Step 1: 6 weeks is 6 x 7 = 42 days.

Step 2: 20 October 2021 plus 42 days is 1 December 2021.

Step 3: at 30 days per month, 1 July 2021 to 1 December 2021 is 5 months, so 5 x 30 = 150 days.

Step 4: the rate is 4%, so on an invented tax due of € 10.000,00 the rente is € 10.000,00 x 0,04 = € 400,00 per 360 days.

Step 5: € 400,00 x 150 = € 60.000,00. € 60.000,00 / 360 = € 166,666..., rounded down to whole euros is € 166.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### Example 5. Navorderingstermijn for tax year 2020 (Calculated example)

The official example on the Belastingdienst page uses tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel and gives navordering until 31 May 2022. The Belastingdienst counts the 5 years from 1 January after the tax year to 31 December of the fifth year, so for 2016 the 5-year term ends on 31 December 2021, plus 5 months is 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). The same method for tax year 2020: the 5-year term ends on 31 December 2025. With 4 months uitstel the term runs to 30 April 2026. For income or assets held or arising abroad the term is 12 years, so the 12-year term ends on 31 December 2032. Source for the 5-year, 12-year and uitstel rules: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01.

### Example 6. Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2020 (Calculated example)

The definitive aanslag 2020 works out to a refund of € 16. Under the current wording a refund is paid only if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens of € 15, and € 16 is higher than € 15, so the € 16 is paid. Under the older wording the grens is € 16 with gelijk aan of meer dan, and € 16 meets that, so the outcome is the same. A refund of € 15 is not paid under either reading: it is not higher than € 15 and it is not gelijk aan of meer dan € 16. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### Example 7. Btw Q2 2020 plus the verzuimboete (Calculated example)

A quarterly filer closes Q2 2020 on 30 June 2020. The return and the payment must both be received by 31 July 2020. Step by step: the table date for Q2 is 31 July 2020. If the return is filed more than 7 kalenderdagen after that date, after the coulance, the aangifteverzuimboete for 2020 is € 68. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.

### Example 8. Belastingrente on late paid btw (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. An open btw amount over 2020 of € 3.000,00 is paid on 1 July 2022. This is not a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year, so belastingrente is charged. The rente runs from 1 January after the tax year, so 1 January 2021, to the payment date 1 July 2022.

Step 1: at 30 days per month, 1 January 2021 to 1 July 2022 is 18 months, so 18 x 30 = 540 days.

Step 2: the rate is 4%, so € 3.000,00 x 0,04 = € 120,00 per 360 days.

Step 3: € 120,00 x 540 = € 64.800,00. € 64.800,00 / 360 = € 180,00, rounded down to whole euros this stays € 180.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### Example 9. Bezwaartermijn on the same aanslag (Calculated example)

An aanslag inkomstenbelasting is dated 20 October 2021. Bezwaar is possible within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag.

Step 1: 6 weeks is 42 days.

Step 2: 20 October 2021 plus 42 days is 1 December 2021.

A bezwaar filed on 1 December 2021 is in time. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar.

### Example 10. Verzoek om vermindering aanslag 2020 (Official example)

For the aanslag 2020 an online verzoek om vermindering was possible up to and including 31 December 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Example 11. Bewaarplicht for a 4-year lease (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives the example of a 4-year lease. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so the lease data are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.

### Example 12. KVK registration window (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. A business starts on 15 June 2020. The opgave for the first inschrijving must be made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business.

Step 1: one week before 15 June 2020 is 8 June 2020.

Step 2: one week after 15 June 2020 is 22 June 2020.

Step 3: 8 June 2020 to 22 June 2020 is exactly two weeks, so the window runs from 8 June 2020 to 22 June 2020.

Registering inside that window gives the KVK-nummer immediately, and the Belastingdienst registration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.
