## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Definitive assessment (aanslag) 2020 (Official example)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) gives this example itself. A return before 1 May 2021 gives the definitive assessment at the latest on 31 December 2023. With 4 months deferral (uitstel) the return is due before 1 September 2021 and the assessment comes at the latest on 30 April 2024. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### Example 2. The week to 8 May 2021 (Calculated example)

A sole trader files the 2020 return on 6 May 2021. The 2020 deadline was moved from 1 May 2021 to before 8 May 2021. The decree (besluit) of 4 May 2021 covers a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021, so 6 May 2021 falls inside the window. If the assessment follows that return, no tax interest (belastingrente) is charged for that reason. A return that arrives on 9 May 2021 falls outside the window. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.

### Example 3. Provisional assessment in monthly instalments (termijnen) (Calculated example)

A provisional income tax assessment for 2020 is dated 15 September 2020. Months remaining after the month of the date are October, November and December, so there are 3 instalments (termijnen). The first instalment (termijn) is one month after the date, so 15 October 2020, then 15 November 2020 and 15 December 2020. The instalments are equal, so an invented amount of € 3.000,00 gives 3 instalments of € 1.000,00 each. Step by step: € 3.000,00 / 3 = € 1.000,00. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01.

### Example 4. Tax interest on the 2020 income tax (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. The 2020 return is filed on 31 August 2021, before the deferral date of 1 September 2021. The assessment is dated 20 October 2021 and deviates from the return. Because the return arrived on or after 1 May after the tax year, and because the assessment deviates from the return, tax interest is charged. The period starts on 1 July 2021. The tax interest ends 6 weeks after the assessment date.

Step 1: 6 weeks is 6 x 7 = 42 days.

Step 2: 20 October 2021 plus 42 days is 1 December 2021.

Step 3: at 30 days per month, 1 July 2021 to 1 December 2021 is 5 months, so 5 x 30 = 150 days.

Step 4: the rate is 4%, so on an invented tax due of € 10.000,00 the tax interest is € 10.000,00 x 0,04 = € 400,00 per 360 days.

Step 5: € 400,00 x 150 = € 60.000,00. € 60.000,00 / 360 = € 166,666..., rounded down to whole euros is € 166.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### Example 5. Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) for tax year 2020 (Calculated example)

The official example on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page uses tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral and gives additional assessment (navordering) until 31 May 2022. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 5 years from 1 January after the tax year to 31 December of the fifth year, so for 2016 the 5-year term ends on 31 December 2021, plus 5 months is 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). The same method for tax year 2020: the 5-year term ends on 31 December 2025. With 4 months deferral the term runs to 30 April 2026. For income or assets held or arising abroad the term is 12 years, so the 12-year term ends on 31 December 2032. Source for the 5-year, 12-year and deferral rules: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01.

### Example 6. Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2020 (Calculated example)

The definitive assessment 2020 works out to a refund of € 16. Under the current wording a refund is paid only if it is higher than the refund threshold of € 15, and € 16 is higher than € 15, so the € 16 is paid. Under the older wording the threshold (grens) is € 16 with equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan), and € 16 meets that, so the outcome is the same. A refund of € 15 is not paid under either reading: it is not higher than € 15 and it is not equal to or more than € 16. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### Example 7. VAT (btw) Q2 2020 plus the default fine (verzuimboete) (Calculated example)

A quarterly filer closes Q2 2020 on 30 June 2020. The return and the payment must both be received by 31 July 2020. Step by step: the table date for Q2 is 31 July 2020. If the return is filed more than 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) after that date, after the leniency (coulance), the filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) for 2020 is € 68. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.

### Example 8. Tax interest on late paid VAT (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. An open VAT amount over 2020 of € 3.000,00 is paid on 1 July 2022. This is not a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year, so tax interest is charged. The tax interest runs from 1 January after the tax year, so 1 January 2021, to the payment date 1 July 2022.

Step 1: at 30 days per month, 1 January 2021 to 1 July 2022 is 18 months, so 18 x 30 = 540 days.

Step 2: the rate is 4%, so € 3.000,00 x 0,04 = € 120,00 per 360 days.

Step 3: € 120,00 x 540 = € 64.800,00. € 64.800,00 / 360 = € 180,00, rounded down to whole euros this stays € 180.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### Example 9. Objection period (bezwaartermijn) on the same assessment (aanslag) (Calculated example)

An income tax assessment is dated 20 October 2021. Objection (bezwaar) is possible within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment.

Step 1: 6 weeks is 42 days.

Step 2: 20 October 2021 plus 42 days is 1 December 2021.

An objection filed on 1 December 2021 is in time. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar.

### Example 10. Request (verzoek) for a reduction of the 2020 assessment (Official example)

For the 2020 assessment an online request for a reduction was possible up to and including 31 December 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Example 11. Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) for a 4-year lease (Official example)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives the example of a 4-year lease. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so the lease data are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.

### Example 12. KVK registration window (Calculated example)

Invented inputs. A business starts on 15 June 2020. The statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) must be made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business.

Step 1: one week before 15 June 2020 is 8 June 2020.

Step 2: one week after 15 June 2020 is 22 June 2020.

Step 3: 8 June 2020 to 22 June 2020 is exactly two weeks, so the window runs from 8 June 2020 to 22 June 2020.

Registering inside that window gives the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.
