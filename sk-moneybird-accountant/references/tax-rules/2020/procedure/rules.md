## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2020, uiterste datum

The return for tax year 2020 had to arrive before 8 May 2021. The date was moved one week because of a technical storing at the Belastingdienst, and the Staatscourant publication Stcrt. 2021, 24144 records the week. The archived Belastingdienst deadline page gives the same text in captures of 18 March, 14 April and 3 May 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). The general rule is that the return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief (the filing invitation letter), which is often 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). Under AWR art. 9 lid 1 the inspecteur sets a term of at least one month after the invitation for aanslagbelastingen, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The text of art. 9 lid 1 and lid 2 is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Uitsteltermijn inkomstenbelasting

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, to 1 September (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The form can be used only for the first uitstel request, and a fiscal partner must request separately. Uitstel usually means belastingrente. For the channel: online in Mijn Belastingdienst is possible until 1 May for someone who received an aangiftebrief, and the paper form sent by post is the route for another filing date and after 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte).

### Aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2020 met uitstel

With uitstel the return for 2020 was due before 1 September 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The archived page prints this in the same official example that gives the definitive assessment dates. The uitstel adds 4 months to the regular filing date, as the uitstel pages state (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting).

### Belastingrente-uitsluiting voor de week tot 8 May 2021

A Besluit of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, under art. 63 AWR states that no belastingrente is charged when the aanslag follows a return that was filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html). The besluit expired on 1 January 2023. This rule existed only for the 2020 return in this set.

### Start van het aangiftetijdvak inkomstenbelasting

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). For tax year 2020 the filing window therefore ran from 1 March 2021 to 8 May 2021.

### Aangifte zonder uitnodiging, belastingjaar 2020

Without an aangiftebrief a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). For tax year 2020 that is until 31 December 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).

### Aangifte en definitieve aanslag 2019, gedaan in kalenderjaar 2020

Calendar year 2020 also held the filing of the 2019 return: before 1 May 2020, with 4 months uitstel before 1 September 2020, and the definitive aanslag 2019 at the latest 31 December 2022, or 30 April 2023 with uitstel (https://web.archive.org/web/20200421/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The deadline page as captured in 2020 said the return 2019 had to arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, without a fixed date (https://web.archive.org/web/20200514/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

### Huba-verlenging voor de aangifte 2019

People who needed in-person filing help (Huba) and could not get it because of the COVID measures had the term for the 2019 return extended from 1 January 2021 to 1 May 2021, per the Belastingdienst news item of 14 December 2020 (https://over-ons.belastingdienst.nl/verlenging-voor-mensen-die-hulp-bij-aangifte-nodig-hebben/).

### Definitieve aanslag 2020 uiterlijk

The Belastingdienst official example states that a return before 1 May 2021 gives the definitive aanslag at the latest 31 December 2023, and that with 4 months uitstel the return is due before 1 September 2021 and the aanslag comes at the latest 30 April 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). AWR art. 11 lid 3 gives the statutory term: the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The text is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Aanslaggrens en teruggaafgrens 2020

Tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens, and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For 2020 the current table prints € 47 and € 15, and pages up to the 2023 captures used the wording gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, so € 48 and € 16 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The 2020 page applied the grens only if no earlier voorlopige aanslag with a payment or refund existed (https://web.archive.org/web/20200421/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Navorderingstermijn inkomstenbelasting 2020

AWR art. 16 has the same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). Lid 1 requires a new fact that was not known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw. Lid 2 also allows navordering when a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and for a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar. Lid 3 gives 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and only 2 years after the assessment or decision for a fout-based navordering. Lid 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months when in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Belastingdienst page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering is possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

### Navordering bij een fout

A fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar counts for navordering, and always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01). For a fout-based navordering the term is 2 years after the assessment or decision (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01).

### Vrijwillige correctie inkomstenbelasting

A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

### Bezwaartermijn en ontvangstbevestiging

Bezwaar must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. For income tax it can be done online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if the bezwaar asks for that. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. Against a voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting no bezwaar is possible, only a change. After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).

### Verzoek om vermindering aanslag 2020

An online request is possible only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. For the aanslag 2020 an online verzoek om vermindering was possible up to and including 31 December 2025. Otherwise the request goes by letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting, aanvragen of wijzigen

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, a change in mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming screen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with an aangiftebrief until the filing date or the uitstel date, and no longer after the return is filed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen).

### Voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting, verrekening

Amounts paid or received under a voorlopige aanslag are settled in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). For 2020 this means the definitive aanslag 2020 settles what was paid or received during 2020 and 2021.

### Betalingstermijnen van aanslagen 2020

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. A voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, with the first one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date.

### Belastingrente inkomstenbelasting 2020

The rate table gives for all taxes except vennootschapsbelasting and toeslagen 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. For the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started on 1 July 2020, so the income tax rate was 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Belastingrente over belastingjaar 2020

Belastingrente on the tax year 2020 assessment runs from 1 July 2021 at 4% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). The 4% follows from the rate table, where 4% runs from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, a period that covers 1 July 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Belastingrente inkomstenbelasting, berekening

Belastingrente is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date. The page also covers the case of an aanslag that follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return, and states that no rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### Belastingrente inkomstenbelasting bij een navorderingsaanslag

For a navorderingsaanslag the belastingrente runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after the date of the navorderingsaanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### Belastingrente btw 2020

For btw the rate was 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Belastingrente btw, berekening

Belastingrente on btw is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment. For late payment the rente runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a naheffingsaanslag it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after the date of the naheffingsaanslag. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year, or for a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen (end at most 10 weeks after receipt) applies from 1 January 2023 and is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).

### Btw-aangifte en betaling, wettelijke termijn

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states that a return for a tijdvak is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak, and AWR art. 19 lid 1 states that the tax is paid within one month after the end of the tijdvak (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst tables give for every year the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together for a monthly or quarterly filer, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Btw uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums per maand 2020

For a monthly filer the dates in 2020 were: December 2019 by 31 January 2020, January by 29 February 2020, February by 31 March 2020, March by 30 April 2020, April by 31 May 2020, May by 30 June 2020, June by 31 July 2020, July by 31 August 2020, August by 30 September 2020, September by 31 October 2020, October by 30 November 2020, November by 31 December 2020, and December 2020 by 31 January 2021. Return and payment must both be received by these dates (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### Btw uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums per kwartaal 2020

For a quarterly filer the dates in 2020 were: Q4 2019 by 31 January 2020, Q1 by 30 April 2020, Q2 by 31 July 2020, Q3 by 31 October 2020, and Q4 by 31 January 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### Btw uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums per jaar

For a yearly return the 2019 return had to be in by 31 March 2020 and the 2020 return by 31 March 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### Btw-aangifte indienen via software

Software can submit a btw return from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Uitstel btw-aangifte

Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is given only in uitzonderlijke situaties, meaning an ernstige calamiteit, and must be requested in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date. A naheffingsaanslag and a boete remain possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte).

### Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2020

After 7 kalenderdagen coulance the btw aangifteverzuimboete for 2020 is € 68 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).

### IB boetes 2020

For the inkomstenbelasting in 2020 the verzuimboete was € 385, with a maximum of € 5.514. The boete for not requesting a return was € 2.757. The betaalverzuimboete (payment default penalty) could go up to € 5.514 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Vergrijpboete

Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete, also with a navorderingsaanslag. The percentages are 25% for grove schuld, 50% for opzet, 75% and 150% in box 3, and aggravated 100% and 300% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Route na de aangiftetermijn

After the deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering, then an aanmaning with 10 werkdagen. Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete. The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag. No return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag plus a verzuimboete. No boete is given when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig).

### Termijn voor het vragen van een aangifte

The Belastingdienst asks for a return within 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for inkomstenbelasting and vennootschapsbelasting, with 2 weeks coulance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Bewaarplicht 2020

AWR art. 52 lid 4 has the same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Administratieplichtigen keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Bewaarplicht, afwijkende termijnen en begin van de termijn

The Belastingdienst table gives basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so the data of a 4-year lease are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basisgegevens include the grootboek, the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, the voorraadadministratie, the in- and verkoopadministratie, the loonadministratie, and data that are relevant for the taxation of third parties (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### Bewaarplicht, omzetting gegevensdrager

AWR art. 52 lid 5 allows conversion of the data to another gegevensdrager, except for the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01).

### KVK eerste inschrijving en andere opgaven

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 states that the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16).

### KVK-nummer en identiteitscontrole

KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when the registration happens within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead, and appointments can be made up to eight weeks ahead (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/).

### Inschrijving Belastingdienst

KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Where registration at KVK is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
