## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Income tax return 2020, deadline (uiterste datum)

The return for tax year 2020 had to arrive before 8 May 2021. The date was moved one week because of a technical failure (storing) at the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), and the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) publication Stcrt. 2021, 24144 records the week. The archived Dutch Tax and Customs Administration deadline page gives the same text in captures of 18 March, 14 April and 3 May 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). The general rule is that the return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), which is often 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). Under AWR art. 9 lid 1 the inspector sets a term of at least one month after the invitation for taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen), and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions. The text of art. 9 lid 1 and lid 2 is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Income tax extension period (uitsteltermijn inkomstenbelasting)

Deferral (uitstel) must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and a fiscal partner must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). For the channel: online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) is possible until 1 May for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), and the paper form sent by post is the route for another filing date and after 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte).

### Income tax return 2020 with deferral

With deferral the return for 2020 was due before 1 September 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The archived page prints this in the same official example that gives the definitive assessment dates. The deferral adds 4 months to the regular filing date, as the deferral pages state (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting).

### Exclusion from tax interest (belastingrente-uitsluiting) for the week to 8 May 2021

A decree (Besluit) of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, under art. 63 AWR states that no tax interest is charged when the assessment (aanslag) follows a return that was filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html). The decree expired on 1 January 2023. This rule existed only for the 2020 return in this set.

### Start of the income tax return period (aangiftetijdvak)

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). For tax year 2020 the filing window therefore ran from 1 March 2021 to 8 May 2021.

### Return without an invitation (aangifte zonder uitnodiging), tax year 2020

Without a filing invitation letter a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). For tax year 2020 that is until 31 December 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250619/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).

### Tax return and final assessment (definitieve aanslag) 2019, filed (gedaan) in calendar year (kalenderjaar) 2020

Calendar year 2020 also held the filing of the 2019 return: before 1 May 2020, with 4 months deferral before 1 September 2020, and the definitive assessment 2019 at the latest 31 December 2022, or 30 April 2023 with deferral (https://web.archive.org/web/20200421/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The deadline page as captured in 2020 said the return 2019 had to arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter, without a fixed date (https://web.archive.org/web/20200514/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

### HUBA extension (Huba-verlenging) for the tax return 2019

People who needed in-person filing help (Huba) and could not get it because of the COVID measures had the term for the 2019 return extended from 1 January 2021 to 1 May 2021, per the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration news item of 14 December 2020 (https://over-ons.belastingdienst.nl/verlenging-voor-mensen-die-hulp-bij-aangifte-nodig-hebben/).

### Final assessment 2020 at the latest

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration official example states that a return before 1 May 2021 gives the definitive assessment at the latest 31 December 2023, and that with 4 months deferral the return is due before 1 September 2021 and the assessment comes at the latest 30 April 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). AWR art. 11 lid 3 gives the statutory term: the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The text is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2020

Tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold, and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For 2020 the current table prints € 47 and € 15, and pages up to the 2023 captures used the wording equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold (grens) one euro higher, so € 48 and € 16 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The 2020 page applied the threshold only if no earlier provisional assessment with a payment or refund existed (https://web.archive.org/web/20200421/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment before year end count toward the threshold, a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) income tax 2020

AWR art. 16 has the same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). Lid 1 requires a new fact that was not known or reasonably apparent to the inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2 also allows additional assessment (navordering) when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (voorlopige verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar). Lid 3 gives 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral, and only 2 years after the assessment or decision for an error-based additional assessment. Lid 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months when in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, where additional assessment is possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

### Additional assessment for an error

An error that was reasonably apparent counts for additional assessment, and always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01). For a error-based additional assessment the term is 2 years after the assessment or decision (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01).

### Voluntary income tax correction (vrijwillige correctie inkomstenbelasting)

A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

### Objection period (bezwaartermijn) and acknowledgement of receipt (ontvangstbevestiging)

Objection (bezwaar) must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment. For income tax it can be done online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if the objection asks for that. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. Against a provisional income tax assessment no objection is possible, only a change. After the objection term a request (verzoek) for an ex officio (ambtshalve) reduction is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).

### Request for a reduction of the 2020 assessment

An online request is possible only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. For the 2020 assessment an online request for a reduction was possible up to and including 31 December 2025. Otherwise the request goes by letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Provisional income tax assessment, request or amend (aanvragen of wijzigen)

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, a change in mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the profits from business activities (Winst uit onderneming) screen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after the return is filed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen).

### Provisional income tax assessment, set-off (verrekening)

Amounts paid or received under a provisional assessment are settled in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). For 2020 this means the definitive assessment 2020 settles what was paid or received during 2020 and 2021.

### Payment terms for assessments (betalingstermijnen van aanslagen) 2020

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. A provisional income tax assessment dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, with the first one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date.

### Tax interest on income tax 2020

The rate table gives for all taxes except corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) and allowances (toeslagen) 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020. For the income tax the reduction started on 1 July 2020, so the income tax rate was 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Tax interest for tax year 2020

Tax interest on the tax year 2020 assessment runs from 1 July 2021 at 4% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). The 4% follows from the rate table, where 4% runs from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, a period that covers 1 July 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Tax interest on income tax, calculation (berekening)

Tax interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date. The page also covers the case of an assessment that follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return, and states that no tax interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### Tax interest on income tax for an additional assessment

For an additional assessment the tax interest runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after the date of the additional assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### Tax interest on VAT (btw) 2020

For VAT (btw) the rate was 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, and 4% from 1 October 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### Tax interest on VAT (btw), calculation

Tax interest on VAT (btw) is charged on a supplementary assessment and on late payment. For late payment the tax interest runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after the date of the supplementary assessment. No tax interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year, or for a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) (end at most 10 weeks after receipt) applies from 1 January 2023 and is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).

### VAT return and payment (btw-aangifte en betaling), statutory period (wettelijke termijn)

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states that a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period, and AWR art. 19 lid 1 states that the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give for every year the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together for a monthly or quarterly filer, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### VAT latest return and payment dates (uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums) per month 2020

For a monthly filer the dates in 2020 were: December 2019 by 31 January 2020, January by 29 February 2020, February by 31 March 2020, March by 30 April 2020, April by 31 May 2020, May by 30 June 2020, June by 31 July 2020, July by 31 August 2020, August by 30 September 2020, September by 31 October 2020, October by 30 November 2020, November by 31 December 2020, and December 2020 by 31 January 2021. Return and payment must both be received by these dates (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### VAT latest return and payment dates (uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums) per quarter 2020

For a quarterly filer the dates in 2020 were: Q4 2019 by 31 January 2020, Q1 by 30 April 2020, Q2 by 31 July 2020, Q3 by 31 October 2020, and Q4 by 31 January 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### VAT latest return and payment dates (uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums) per year 2020

For a yearly return the 2019 return had to be in by 31 March 2020 and the 2020 return by 31 March 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### VAT return submission via software (btw-aangifte indienen)

Software can submit a VAT return from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Deferral for the VAT return (uitstel btw-aangifte)

Deferral for a VAT return is given only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), meaning a serious calamity (een ernstige calamiteit), and must be requested in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment and a penalty remain possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte).

### VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2020

After 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance) the VAT filing default penalty for 2020 is € 68 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).

### IB penalties (boetes) 2020

For the income tax in 2020 the default fine (verzuimboete) was € 385, with a maximum of € 5.514. The penalty for not requesting an invitation to file a return was € 2.757. The payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) could go up to € 5.514 (https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete)

Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment. The percentages are 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), 75% and 150% in box 3, and aggravated 100% and 300% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Route after the filing deadline (aangiftetermijn)

After the deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. No return at all gives an ex officio (ambtshalve) assessment plus a default fine. No penalty is given when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig).

### Term (termijn) for requesting an invitation to file a return (vragen van een aangifte)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration asks for a return within 6 months after the tax debt arose for income tax and corporate income tax, with 2 weeks leniency (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) 2020

AWR art. 52 lid 4 has the same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Record-retention obligation, different periods (afwijkende termijnen) and begin of the term

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so the data of a 4-year lease are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), the payroll records (loonadministratie), and data that are relevant for the taxation of third parties (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### Record-retention obligation, conversion of a data carrier (gegevensdrager)

AWR art. 52 lid 5 allows conversion of the data to another data carrier, except for the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01).

### KVK first registration (inschrijving) and other returns (opgaven)

Commercial Register Act (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1 states that the statement (opgave) for the first registration of a business is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required returns are made within one week after the fact (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16).

### Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) and identity check (identiteitscontrole)

KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number immediately when the registration happens within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead, and appointments can be made up to eight weeks ahead (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/).

### Registration with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration

KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the records of the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes at most 10 working days. Where registration at KVK is not possible, the start-up notification form (opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister)) is used (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
