## 5. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic, so this section states what began in 2020 and what the 2020 sources say about the 2020 tax year.
- Belastingrente rate for calendar year 2020: 4% until 31 May 2020 for btw and until 30 June 2020 for the inkomstenbelasting, then 0,01% until 30 September 2020, then 4% from 1 October 2020. The 0,01% period is the new element in 2020, and for the inkomstenbelasting it started one month later than for the other taxes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- Belastingrente on the tax year 2020 runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting.
- The 2020 income tax return deadline moved from 1 May 2021 to 8 May 2021 because of a technical storing, and a besluit of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, under art. 63 AWR states that no belastingrente is charged when the aanslag follows a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021. The besluit expired on 1 January 2023. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.
- COVID measure for the 2019 return: people who needed in-person filing help (Huba) and could not get it had the term extended from 1 January 2021 to 1 May 2021, per the news item of 14 December 2020. Source: https://over-ons.belastingdienst.nl/verlenging-voor-mensen-die-hulp-bij-aangifte-nodig-hebben/.
- Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens for the 2020 return: € 47 and € 15 in the wording of the current table, and € 48 and € 16 in the wording used on the pages up to the 2023 captures. The material gives no 2019 values, so no old-to-new comparison against 2019 is possible. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- For the 2020 return the archived page states that with 4 months uitstel the return is due before 1 September 2021. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- File the income tax return for 2020 before 8 May 2021, or with uitstel before 1 September 2021. Record the amounts of the voorlopige aanslag that were paid or received, because they are settled in the return. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen.
- Record the btw per tijdvak. For a quarterly filer in 2020 the dates are Q1 30 April 2020, Q2 31 July 2020, Q3 31 October 2020 and Q4 31 January 2021, and the return and the payment must both be received by that date. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
- A btw return that is more than 7 kalenderdagen late after the coulance triggers the aangifteverzuimboete of € 68 for 2020. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.
- A voluntary btw correction within 3 months after the tax year avoids belastingrente. A voluntary income tax correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag.
- Keep the administratie. Basisgegevens are kept 7 years, onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data 10 years, and the term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Basisgegevens include the grootboek, the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, the voorraadadministratie, the in- and verkoopadministratie and the loonadministratie. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- Register the onderneming with KVK within two weeks, starting one week before and ending one week after the start of the business. Other opgaven go to KVK within one week after the fact. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed, and the Belastingdienst registration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Conflict on the 2020 return deadline inside Belastingdienst text. The archived deadline page, capture of 18 March 2021, gives before 8 May 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), and the Staatscourant besluit Stcrt. 2021, 24144 records the move (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html). The archived definitive-aanslag page, capture of 13 April 2021, still uses before 1 May 2021 in its example for the 2020 return (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The 8 May 2021 date is the operative one per the besluit.
- Unknown: whether the uitstel request deadline for the 2020 return also moved to 8 May 2021. No primary page that was opened states this. A Belastingdienst page or a besluit that gives the uitstel request deadline for the 2020 return would settle it. Related source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.
- The aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2020 appear in two wordings. The table from the 2024 capture onwards prints € 47 and € 15 with the wording hoger dan (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Pages up to the 2023 captures print € 48 and € 16 with the wording gelijk aan of meer dan (https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). No opened source states that the two wordings describe the same threshold. For whole-euro amounts they describe the same boundary, but that reading is an interpretation, not a statement in a primary source. A Belastingdienst text that prints each grens with a defined operator would settle it.
- The btw filing and payment rule is given in the checked material with the consolidated AWR text of 1 January 2026 for art. 10 lid 2 and art. 19 lid 1, not with a 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). A 2020 consolidated text would settle the wording for 2020.
- The KVK registration window is cited from Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 in the version of 16 July 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). No 2020 version of that article was opened, so the 2020 text of the two-week window is not verified here.
- Resolved wording difference on the KVK regime: the KVK page carries both a two-week term and a ten werkdagen term for the period before the btw number is available (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), and the Belastingdienst states that registration takes at most 10 werkdagen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). Ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks, so the two wordings give the same period.
