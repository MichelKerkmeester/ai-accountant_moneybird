## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material holds no 2019 values for this topic, so this section states what began in 2020 and what the 2020 sources say about the 2020 tax year.
- Tax interest (belastingrente) rate for calendar year 2020: 4% until 31 May 2020 for VAT (btw) and until 30 June 2020 for the income tax, then 0,01% until 30 September 2020, then 4% from 1 October 2020. The 0,01% period is the new element in 2020, and for the income tax it started one month later than for the other taxes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- Tax interest on the tax year 2020 runs from 1 July 2021 at 4%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting.
- The 2020 income tax return deadline moved from 1 May 2021 to 8 May 2021 because of a technical failure (storing), and a decree (besluit) of 4 May 2021, nr. 2021-9389, under art. 63 AWR states that no tax interest is charged when the assessment (aanslag) follows a return filed after 1 May 2021 and before 8 May 2021. The decree expired on 1 January 2023. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.
- COVID measure for the 2019 return: people who needed in-person filing help (Huba) and could not get it had the term extended from 1 January 2021 to 1 May 2021, per the news item of 14 December 2020. Source: https://over-ons.belastingdienst.nl/verlenging-voor-mensen-die-hulp-bij-aangifte-nodig-hebben/.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) for the 2020 return: € 47 and € 15 in the wording of the current table, and € 48 and € 16 in the wording used on the pages up to the 2023 captures. The material gives no 2019 values, so no old-to-new comparison against 2019 is possible. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- For the 2020 return the archived page states that with 4 months deferral (uitstel) the return is due before 1 September 2021. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- File the income tax return for 2020 before 8 May 2021, or with deferral (uitstel) before 1 September 2021. Record the amounts of the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) that were paid or received, because they are settled in the return. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen.
- Record the VAT (btw) per period (tijdvak). For a quarterly filer in 2020 the dates are Q1 30 April 2020, Q2 31 July 2020, Q3 31 October 2020 and Q4 31 January 2021, and the return and the payment must both be received by that date. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
- A VAT return that is more than 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) late after the leniency (coulance) triggers the filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) of € 68 for 2020. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.
- A voluntary VAT correction within 3 months after the tax year avoids tax interest (belastingrente). A voluntary income tax correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag.
- Keep the records. Basic records (basisgegevens) are kept 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data 10 years, and the term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie) and the payroll records (loonadministratie). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- Register the business with KVK within two weeks, starting one week before and ending one week after the start of the business. Other returns (opgaven) go to KVK within one week after the fact. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), so no separate registration is needed, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Conflict on the 2020 return deadline inside Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text. The archived deadline page, capture of 18 March 2021, gives before 8 May 2021 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210318/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), and the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) decree (besluit) Stcrt. 2021, 24144 records the move (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html). The archived definitive-assessment (aanslag) page, capture of 13 April 2021, still uses before 1 May 2021 in its example for the 2020 return (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The 8 May 2021 date is the operative one per the decree.
- Unknown: whether the deferral (uitstel) request deadline for the 2020 return also moved to 8 May 2021. No primary page that was opened states this. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page or a decree that gives the deferral request deadline for the 2020 return would settle it. Related source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.
- The assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2020 appear in two wordings. The table from the 2024 capture onwards prints € 47 and € 15 with the wording hoger dan (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Pages up to the 2023 captures print € 48 and € 16 with the wording equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) (https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). No opened source states that the two wordings describe the same threshold. For whole-euro amounts they describe the same boundary, but that reading is an interpretation, not a statement in a primary source. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text that prints each threshold (grens) with a defined operator would settle it.
- The VAT (btw) filing and payment rule is given in the checked material with the consolidated AWR text of 1 January 2026 for art. 10 lid 2 and art. 19 lid 1, not with a 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). A 2020 consolidated text would settle the wording for 2020.
- The KVK registration window is cited from Commercial Register Act (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1 in the version of 16 July 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). No 2020 version of that article was opened, so the 2020 text of the two-week window is not verified here.
- Resolved wording difference on the KVK regime: the KVK page carries both a two-week term and a ten working days (werkdagen) term for the period before the VAT (btw) number is available (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that registration takes at most 10 working days (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). Ten working days equals two calendar weeks, so the two wordings give the same period.
