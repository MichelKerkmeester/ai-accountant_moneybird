## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Jaarruimte percentage

A taxpayer with a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year may deduct lijfrentepremies up to a percentage of the premiegrondslag (premium base), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual and, up to 2022, also by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The percentage in the 2020 text is 13,3%, and the Ministry of Finance table for 2020 says the same (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: percentage x (min(income of the previous year, aftoppingsgrens) minus franchise) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). Lid 6 and lid 7 of the 2020 text allow the 13,3% and the 6,27 factor to be changed by AMvB linked to the pensioenrichtleeftijd (same source). The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together, and premiums above the ruimte are never deductible, but they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). The percentage stays 13,3% in the 2021 and 2022 texts and becomes 30% only in the 2023-07-01 text, so 13,3% is the 2020 figure, and the Belastingdienst ties the 30% to the Wtp with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).

### Maximum jaarruimte

The Ministry of Finance table "Uitgaven inkomensvoorzieningen" lists € 12.986 as the maximum jaarruimte for 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). The same amount follows from the section 2 figures: 13,3% x (€ 110.111 minus € 12.472) = € 12.985,99, rounded to € 12.986 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The maximum binds a taxpayer whose income reaches the aftoppingsgrens and who has no pension accrual and no FOR dotatie. During the research phase this amount was derived and UNVERIFIED, and the Ministry table confirms it, see section 7 (same URL).

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

Art. 3.127 lid 3 sums four components over the previous calendar year: winst uit onderneming, belastbaar loon, belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden and belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen, counted up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (aftoppingsgrens) and then reduced by the laatstvermelde bedrag (franchise) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In the 2020 text component (a) is the winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek, so the FOR movement and the ondernemersaftrek do not shrink this component (same source). Art. 10.2b lid 2 of the 2020 text indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 10.2b).

### Franchise premiegrondslag

The franchise is the laatstvermelde bedrag that art. 3.127 lid 3 subtracts after the aftoppingsgrens cap has been applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). It is subtracted once, from the capped sum, so income at or below € 12.472 produces a premiegrondslag of nil and therefore no jaarruimte. In the 2020 text the franchise follows art. 10.1 for its indexation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 10.2b).

### Factor A multiplier 6,27

Art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a reduces the outcome of the percentage by 6,27 times the aangroei (growth) of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The multiplier converts a yearly pension accrual into a premium equivalent that is taken off the jaarruimte. Art. 3.127 lid 5 adds that a taxpayer who (partly) staakt may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year (same source). From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction is rewritten to the premiums ingelegd in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen and partnerpensioen, excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor, which is not the 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25).

### Age window for the jaarruimte

In the 2020 to 2022 texts, and so in 2020, the taxpayer must not yet have reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The window widens only from the Wtp text of 2023-07-01 to the age five years above the AOW age, which is not the 2020 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In the later FISIN chapters the same wording "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd" appears (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Look-back window for the reserveringsruimte

Art. 3.127 lid 2 lets unused jaarruimte of the preceding period be used on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The 2020 text reads "zeven jaar", which the FISIN 2020 chapter translates into the jaarruimte of 2013 to 2019 (same source and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Belastingdienst advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so that older unused ruimte does not lapse (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). The period becomes "tien jaar" from the 2023-07-01 text, which does not apply to 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Reserveringsruimte percentage cap

In 2020 the reserveringsruimte is at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount, and both limits apply (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The percentage is applied to the same premiegrondslag as the jaarruimte. From the 2023-07-01 text the cap is one flat euro maximum with no percentage and no age split, so the 2020 double cap is specific to the years 2020 to 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum, younger group

FISIN 2020 gives € 7.371 as the euro cap for a taxpayer born after 31 August 1963 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Ministry of Finance describes this group as younger than 56 jaar en 4 maanden on 1 January 2020 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). The actual reserveringsruimte for this group is the lower of 17% of the premiegrondslag and € 7.371 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Reserveringsruimte maximum, older group

The higher euro amount of € 14.552 applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). FISIN 2020 places a taxpayer born before 1 September 1963 in this group (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The 17% percentage cap still applies alongside the euro amount, so the lower of the two binds (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Oudedagslijfrente, uiterste start

For an oudedagslijfrente the first termijn is paid at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, with payments running until death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 a). FISIN 2020 gives the example of an AOW age in 2030, so the oudedagslijfrente starts at the latest on 31 December 2035 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). If the lijfrente reaches its ingangsdatum without a start, the one-year conversion rule of the ingangsdatum subsection applies (same source). On transfer of the onderneming to a successor no lijfrenterekening is allowed at the successor, while a lijfrenteverzekering at the successor is allowed (same source).

### Lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, minimum looptijd

For a lijfrenterekening or a lijfrentebeleggingsrecht the uitkering must run at least 20 years, plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 works this out with a start at 63 and an AOW age of 67, which gives at least 20 + 4 = 24 years (same source). The same chapter names the lijfrenteverzekering, the lijfrenterekening and the lijfrentebeleggingsrecht as the three product forms (same source).

### Nabestaandenlijfrente, uiterste looptijd

A nabestaandenlijfrente for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and for a rekening or beleggingsrecht the termijn is at least 20 years or at least 5 years for children under 30 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 gives two official examples: a child aged 21 at death has a looptijd of at least 5 years and stops at the latest after 9 years, and a child aged 27 at death stops after 3 years (same source). The start deadline after a death is handled in the nabestaandenlijfrente termijn subsection below (same source).

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c covers the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente: at least five years of termijnen, starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c). For 2020 that cap is € 22.089 per year (same source). FISIN 2020 and the Ministry of Finance table give the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente above the cap

FISIN 2020 adds that above the cap of € 22.089 per year the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). So a temporary annuity that pays more than the cap with a shorter termijn does not simply qualify against the normal five-year termijn. The cap is tested at the moment the premium is paid (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.125 lid 1 c).

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The overbruggingslijfrente (bridge annuity) is available only for lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The uitkering ends at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching AOW age or in the year of starting a pensioen (same source). The yearly maximum is € 63.288 (same source). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text, so the FISIN 2020 chapter is the 2020 source for it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.125).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

On staking, part of the stakingswinst may be converted into a lijfrente, with a deduction up to the stakingswinst and capped at a tier maximum minus the provisions already built: the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.129). For 2020 art. 3.129 lid 2 a sets the tier a maximum at € 467.044 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 a). Tier a applies when the age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or the taxpayer is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt with the lijfrente starting within six months, or the staking happens by death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129). The FISIN 2020 chapter lists what is deducted from the stakingswinst first: the pension rights charged to profit, bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen, the FOR at the start of the year, lijfrentepremies deducted since 2001 except the basisaftrek to 2002, deducted stortingen and earlier conversions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

Tier b has a maximum of € 233.530 in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 b). It applies when the age at staking is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or when the lijfrente starts immediately (same source). The same reduction for provisions already built and the same cap at the stakingswinst apply as in tier a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.129).

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

Tier c has a maximum of € 116.771 in 2020 and covers the other cases (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.129 lid 2 c). A taxpayer who does not meet the tier a or tier b age or disability conditions falls into tier c (same source).

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows afkoop of a small lijfrente in one payment when the yearly amount stays below a statutory amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 2 d). For 2020 that amount is € 4.475 (same source). The Ministry of Finance table labels this item "afkoop lijfrente vrij van revisierente" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).

### Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes afkoop not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer or gerechtigde is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, within the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.133 lid 9 c). The 2020 cap is € 41.791 (same source). The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn (same source). The research phase had this cap as unknown, and the 2020 law text supplies it, see section 7 (same source).

### FOR toevoeging

Access to a FOR toevoeging requires the urencriterium and not yet AOW age at the start of the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.67). Art. 3.68 lid 1 sets the toevoeging at 9,44% of the winst with a euro cap, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst, and art. 3.68 lid 2 adds that the toevoeging may not exceed the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (same source, art. 3.68 lid 1 and lid 2). For 2020 the euro cap is € 9.218 (same source). The Ministry of Finance 2020 table confirms 9,44% and € 9.218 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in its winst en verliesrekening chapter, which is a page error, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). Afname (decrease) of the FOR follows art. 3.70 lid 1: by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at staking, at AOW age at the start of the year, or on failing the urencriterium this year and last year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.70 lid 1). The FOR to lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022, and the converted amount must be added to the winst (same source, art. 3.128, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the onderneming and takes the reserve over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### Lijfrente ingangsdatum termijn

A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 gives both cases: an ingangsdatum in 2019 means convert or start by 31 December 2020, and an ingangsdatum in 2020 means convert or start by 31 December 2021 (same source). Extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden (same source). If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop or deblokkering, the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente (revision interest) is due (same source). FISIN 2020 states that the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid, a sentence dropped from FISIN 2021 (same source). A refund of a deducted premium is a negatieve uitgave without revisierente, and for a lijfrente only if the refund is undone within 30 days (same source).

### Nabestaandenlijfrente termijn

A nabestaandenlijfrente must start within 2 full calendar years after the year of death (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2020 states that a death in 2019 means the lijfrente must start by 31 December 2021 (same source). Extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden (same source). The looptijd rules for these lijfrenten, including the end at the latest at age 30, are in the nabestaandenlijfrente uiterste looptijd subsection above (same source).

### Payment deadline FOR or stakingswinst conversion

Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year in which they are paid or verrekend (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.130 lid 1). Art. 3.130 lid 2 adds that premiums for the stakingswinst conversion of art. 3.129, for the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, for the FOR conversion of art. 3.128 may on choice be counted in the year itself when they are paid within six months after the year end (same source, art. 3.130 lid 2). For the 2020 year this means a payment made before 1 July 2021 can still be counted in 2020 (same source). FISIN 2020 prints "in 2020 of vóór 1 juli 2020" for the FOR conversion, which conflicts with the law, while its stakingswinst line gives before 1 July 2021, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen, and the article puts no ruimte cap on them (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.124 lid 1 c). FISIN 2020 says the AOV premium can be "volledig aftrekken" (fully deducted) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). For an ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen, and the periodic benefits are inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for loonheffing, mandatory Ziektewet and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen, and Zvw premiums (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie, and it is deductible as a business cost with a restriction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### AOV premium with a lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and it counts in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). FISIN 2020 lists this as the exclusion "verzekeringen die een bedrag ineens uitkeren" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The rule of thumb on the Belastingdienst page is: if the premium is not deductible, the payout is not taxed, and the other way round (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Mandatory beroepspensioen premium

A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction (same source). A self-employed person may join the sectoraal pensioenfonds of the branch when the fund allows it, and the premiums are then deductible, with the proef running until 1 July 2028 and art. 10a.26 keeping the experimenteerbepaling zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 10a.26).

### Refund of a deducted premium

A refund of a premium that was deducted is a negatieve uitgave (negative expenditure) without revisierente, and for a lijfrente the refund only escapes revisierente when it is undone within 30 days (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). By contrast, when a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions and has not started paying, the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid, and revisierente is due in most cases (same source). Both rules sit in the same FISIN 2020 chapter on uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (same source).
