## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Hours criterion (urencriterium)

Art. 3.6 lid 1 of the statute asks for at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (ondernemer) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year in which someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) example is a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) gives a second example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4 excludes hours for a business that is part of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons. Such hours do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.2 Second test (tweede toets) waived, lookback 2015 to 2019

Next to the 1.225 hours, art. 3.6 lid 1 gives two alternative second tests. Either the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses, or the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The second test therefore falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur (non-ondernemer) year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. For tax year 2020 the five preceding calendar years are 2015 to 2019. FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018, which is a misprint against the statute, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium)

The reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) of art. 3.78a lid 3 is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and replaces the 1.225 hours for that item only.

### 3.4 Pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5 states that if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The effect is that the hours criterion is tested as if the interrupted weeks had been worked.

### 3.5 Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (art. 3.76 lid 1) and is € 7.030 for an entrepreneur below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) (state pension age) at the start of the year (art. 3.76 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Whether someone is an entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes is judged by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration per activity, separately from VAT (btw). Someone who is a VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration looks at more conditions for income tax. It weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The statute defines the entrepreneur as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (onderneming) (art. 3.5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The fetched pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. They say only that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration makes its own assessment after registration (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/).

Art. 3.76 lid 5 caps the deduction (aftrek) at the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek). The amount cut off by this cap is unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count. Lid 6 says the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Lid 7 offsets it in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Lid 8 says the offset is also set by decision open to objection (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

Persons entitled to a share of the profit without being an entrepreneur (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, no deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), no discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and no SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) on their profit as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.6 Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age

Art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the lid 2 and lid 3 amounts for an entrepreneur who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year, so the starter's deduction is halved too (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 this gives € 3.515, being 50% of € 7.030 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The test is the AOW age at the start of the calendar year, not during the year.

### 3.7 Starter's deduction

The starter's deduction is not a separate item on the art. 3.74 list of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), but an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3. Art. 3.74 names five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for research and development work (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs and the discontinuation deduction (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Art. 3.76 lid 3 gives € 2.123 for 2020 to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the self-employed person's deduction applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969, a tax-neutral return from a private limited company (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.8 Starter's deduction at AOW age

Under art. 3.76 lid 4 the starter's deduction is halved for an entrepreneur who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year, so the 2020 amount is € 1.062, being 50% of € 2.123 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Starter's deduction prior-years condition

The prior-years test of art. 3.76 lid 3 is a single test: the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the same test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting" (you were not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes in each of the past 5 years), which means the same as the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The reading "not an entrepreneur in each of the past 5 years" is wrong, see section 7. A year in which the self-employed person's deduction was set to nil by the profit cap still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for this count (art. 3.76 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.10 Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs

Art. 3.78a applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, who has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, who does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and who has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), and a periodic benefit from a disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then, and FISIN prints € 0 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026). The text and the amounts are identical in every year version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.11 Deduction for research and development work

The deduction for research and development work is part of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) under art. 3.74 and is worked out in art. 3.77. The base amount for 2020 is € 12.980 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN adds that it requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion. The amounts are indexed per year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.12 S&O starter increase

On top of the base amount, art. 3.77 gives a starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period. For 2020 the increase is € 6.494 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.13 SME profit exemption

Art. 3.79a makes the SME profit exemption a percentage of the total profit as an entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance. Art. 3.2 states that the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. The order is therefore profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. For 2020 the percentage is 14% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

No hours criterion is needed. The statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). There is no exemption (vrijstelling) on profit as co-entitled party (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) of art. 2.10, see 3.19 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.14 Deduction for assisting partner bands

Art. 3.78 gives the deduction for assisting partner to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Boundary hours therefore go to the higher band: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The hours of the partner must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.15 Deduction for assisting partner pay limit

Art. 3.16 lid 4 states that pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, so the deduction for assisting partner can still apply, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.16 Discontinuation deduction

Art. 3.79 gives the discontinuation deduction on profit made with or on the cessation of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630 in 2020, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap, marital community of property, art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) (art. 3.63), the deduction applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

In 2020 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and that there is no deduction on profit as co-entitled party (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.17 Old-age reserve addition percentage

Art. 3.67 states that the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year "kan" (may) add to the old-age reserve, so the addition is optional. FISIN 2020 says the same: you may add each year, but it is not compulsory. The hours criterion is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

Art. 3.68 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit as an entrepreneur, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of the year exceeds the old-age reserve at the start of the year. The profit here is the profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by AMvB, lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pension reference age (pensioenrichtleeftijd) changes, and published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no AMvB change was made in those years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

FISIN 2020 computes the addition from the fiscal profit corrected for the maximum loss provision (maximumverliesbepaling), exempt profit parts, non-deductible costs, investment schemes, changes in the equalisation reserve (egalisatiereserve) and reinvestment reserve (herinvesteringsreserve) and pension premiums, then 9,44% capped, then minus pension premiums. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.18 Old-age reserve addition maximum

The yearly maximum of the addition for 2020 is € 9.218 under art. 3.68 lid 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in two places, which is a misprint, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

Art. 3.69 lets the addition be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year, where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from an annuity (lijfrente) formed from an old-age reserve, capped at the business capital at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71 defines the business capital as the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the old-age reserve, corrected for a reversion reserve (terugkeerreserve). Art. 3.72 covers more than one business (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

The decrease rules sat in art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73, which were in force (in werking) in 2020. Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, and art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps the art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 text as it read on 31 December 2022 in force for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year. It also decreases by the excess over the business capital at year end if the business or part of it was discontinued in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). FISIN 2020 defines the business capital for the decrease as equity (eigen vermogen) plus old-age reserve, without the equalisation reserve and reinvestment reserve (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.19 Rate adjustment

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3 states that if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten, expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which the taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 the percentage is 3,5% and the start of the top bracket is € 68.507 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The rate adjustment is calculated as a separate step on top of the income tax and it does not change the deductions themselves.

### 3.20 Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2020 that is 49,50% minus 3,5%, so 46%. This figure is the cap on what a euro of entrepreneur's allowance or SME profit exemption can save in 2020.

### 3.21 Zvw rate by assessment

The taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is part of the income on which the Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag) is levied, up to a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2020 the rate by assessment is 5,45% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This interaction is verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The contribution is not levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve into an annuity. Wet Zvw art. 43 itself was not reopened. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.22 Zvw maximum contribution income

The maximum contribution income for the Zvw contribution by assessment in 2020 is € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Income above that amount does not raise the contribution.
