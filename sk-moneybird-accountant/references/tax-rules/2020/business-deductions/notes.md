## 5. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material contains no 2019 figures for this topic, so this file cannot give an old-to-new comparison for 2020. The points below state what the sources say about the 2020 position.
- 2020 is the first year in this knowledge base, so there is no previous-year file to compare with.
- The oudedagsreserve could still be added to in 2020. Art. 3.67 to 3.72 were in force (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The addition percentage was 9,44% and the maximum was € 9.218 (art. 3.68 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Those articles lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition is possible from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- In 2020 the stakingsaftrek still covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting under art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The tariefsaanpassing for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which gives the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling a maximum value of 46% (art. 2.10 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). No other year's percentage is stated in the 2020 material.
- For 2020 the second test of the urencriterium is waived for anyone who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, so for 2015 to 2019 (art. 3.6 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Two 2020 FISIN misprints differ from the statute and are listed in section 7: the oudedagsreserve maximum of € 9.214 against € 9.218 and the lookback window of 2014 to 2018 against 2015 to 2019.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices to make the hours of the ondernemer and of a working partner plausible. Non-billable hours count, standby time does not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- The ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are applied in the inkomstenbelasting return in the winst uit onderneming, in the order profit, then ondernemersaftrek, then MKB-winstvrijstelling (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- The niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek and its later offset are set by the inspecteur in a beschikking open to bezwaar, so record the amount and check the beschikking (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible and the partner counts as unpaid, so record such pay as a non-deductible item and check whether the meewerkaftrek bands apply (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The oudedagsreserve addition is optional, needs the urencriterium and an age below the AOW age at the start of the year, and is worked out in the winst en verliesrekening en balans. FISIN 2020 defines the ondernemingsvermogen for a decrease as eigen vermogen plus oudedagsreserve, without the egalisatiereserve and herinvesteringsreserve. A decrease is added to profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).
- The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the 2020 self-assessed Zvw contribution of 5,45% is levied, up to € 57.232. No contribution is levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The S&O aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium, so keep the verklaring and the hour records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- For a sole trader who also owns a BV: the startersaftrek and the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid do not apply if the business is continued under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) and that started in the calendar year or one of the five recent years (art. 3.76 lid 3 and 3.78a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- No value for 2020 in the checked material is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: oudedagsreserve addition maximum 2020. Art. 3.68 lid 1 as valid on 1 January 2020 gives € 9.218 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in two places (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The statute is the primary source and prevails. A corrected FISIN page or a Belastingdienst statement would settle it fully.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: the lookback window of the second test of the urencriterium for 2020. Art. 3.6 gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute prevails.
- Conflict, settled: the startersaftrek prior-years condition. The reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Art. 3.76 lid 3 requires that the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst words the same test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).
- Corrected item, not an open point: the startersaftrek is not a separate part of the art. 3.74 list of the ondernemersaftrek, but a raise of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Corrected item, not an open point: for 2020 the stakingsaftrek on an afneming of the oudedagsreserve sits in art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b, not in a transitional article, which only came in on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
