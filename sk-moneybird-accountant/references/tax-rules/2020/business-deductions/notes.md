## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked material contains no 2019 figures for this topic, so this file cannot give an old-to-new comparison for 2020. The points below state what the sources say about the 2020 position.
- 2020 is the first year in this knowledge base, so there is no previous-year file to compare with.
- The old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) could still be added to in 2020. Art. 3.67 to 3.72 were in force (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The addition percentage was 9,44% and the maximum was € 9.218 (art. 3.68 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Those articles lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition is possible from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- In 2020 the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) still covered profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) under art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which gives the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) a maximum value of 46% (art. 2.10 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). No other year's percentage is stated in the 2020 material.
- For 2020 the second test (tweede toets) of the hours criterion (urencriterium) is waived for anyone who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, so for 2015 to 2019 (art. 3.6 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Two 2020 FISIN misprints differ from the statute and are listed in section 7: the old-age reserve maximum of € 9.214 against € 9.218 and the lookback window of 2014 to 2018 against 2015 to 2019.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices to make the hours of the entrepreneur and of a working partner plausible. Non-billable hours count, standby time does not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- The entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) are applied in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) in the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming), in the order profit, then entrepreneur's allowance, then SME profit exemption (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- The unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) and its later offset are set by the tax inspector (inspecteur) in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar), so record the amount and check the decision (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and the partner counts as unpaid, so record such pay as a non-deductible item and check whether the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands apply (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition is optional, needs the hours criterion (urencriterium) and an age below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) at the start of the year, and is worked out in the profit and loss account (winst en verliesrekening) and balance sheet (balans). FISIN 2020 defines the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) for a decrease as equity (eigen vermogen) plus old-age reserve, without the equalisation reserve (egalisatiereserve) and reinvestment reserve (herinvesteringsreserve). A decrease is added to profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).
- The taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is part of the income on which the 2020 Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag) of 5,45% is levied, up to € 57.232. No contribution is levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve into an annuity (lijfrente) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The S&O deduction (aftrek) requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion, so keep the declaration (verklaring) and the hour records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- For a sole trader who also owns a BV: the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) and the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) do not apply if the business is continued under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) and that started in the calendar year or one of the five recent years (art. 3.76 lid 3 and 3.78a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- No value for 2020 in the checked material is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition maximum 2020. Art. 3.68 lid 1 as valid on 1 January 2020 gives € 9.218 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints € 9.214 in two places (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The statute is the primary source and prevails. A corrected FISIN page or a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) statement would settle it fully.
- Conflict, settled by the statute: the lookback window of the second test (tweede toets) of the hours criterion (urencriterium) for 2020. Art. 3.6 gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute prevails.
- Conflict, settled: the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) prior-years condition. The reading "not an entrepreneur in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Art. 3.76 lid 3 requires that the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) applied at most twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the same test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).
- Corrected item, not an open point: the starter's deduction is not a separate part of the art. 3.74 list of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), but a raise of the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) in art. 3.76 lid 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Corrected item, not an open point: for 2020 the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) on a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve sits in art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b, not in a transitional article, which only came in on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
