## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1 (Calculated example): self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) at a profit of € 60.000

Facts: profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) € 60.000, the entrepreneur meets the hours criterion (urencriterium), is below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) at the start of the year and does not qualify for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek).

- Step 1: self-employed person's deduction € 7.030. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not bite because € 7.030 is less than the profit of € 60.000.
- Step 2: profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000 - € 7.030 = € 52.970.
- Step 3: SME profit exemption 14% of € 52.970 = € 7.415,80.
- Step 4: taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 52.970 - € 7.415,80 = € 45.554,20.
- Check: the total reduction is € 7.030 + € 7.415,80 = € 14.445,80, and € 60.000 - € 14.445,80 = € 45.554,20.

### Example 2 (Calculated example): starter with a profit of € 20.000

Facts: profit as an entrepreneur € 20.000, meets the hours criterion, was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, had the self-employed person's deduction applied at most twice in that period, below the AOW age at the start of the year.

- Step 1: self-employed person's deduction € 7.030.
- Step 2: starter's deduction € 2.123, so the raise brings the deduction (aftrek) to € 7.030 + € 2.123 = € 9.153, which is less than the profit of € 20.000.
- Step 3: profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000 - € 9.153 = € 10.847.
- Step 4: SME profit exemption 14% of € 10.847 = € 1.518,58.
- Step 5: taxable profit from business: € 10.847 - € 1.518,58 = € 9.328,42.
- Check: € 10.847 × 0,14 = € 1.518,58 and € 10.847 - € 1.518,58 = € 9.328,42.

### Example 3 (Calculated example): profit below the self-employed person's deduction

Facts: profit as an entrepreneur € 4.000, meets the hours criterion, no starter's deduction.

- Step 1: the self-employed person's deduction is not more than the profit, so the deduction for 2020 is € 4.000, not € 7.030.
- Step 2: the amount cut off is € 7.030 - € 4.000 = € 3.030 of unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek), set by decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar) and to be offset in the next nine calendar years, oldest first, at most by the amount by which each later year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction.
- Step 3: profit after the entrepreneur's allowance is € 0, so the SME profit exemption is 14% of € 0 = € 0,00.
- Step 4: the year counts as a year in which the self-employed person's deduction was applied for the starter's deduction count. Art. 3.76 lid 5 also counts a year in which the cap sets the deduction to nil.

### Example 4 (Official example, FISIN 2020 section 8.4.3): carry-forward of unused self-employed person's deduction

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) example: in 2018 the profit was € 5.000 against a right of € 7.280, so € 2.280 was unused. In 2019 there was a loss of € 25.000, so the whole € 7.280 of that year was unused, giving a balance (saldo) of € 2.280 + € 7.280 = € 9.560. In 2020 the profit of € 100.000 minus € 7.030 gives € 92.970, minus the € 9.560 gives € 83.410 and a balance of € 0. The € 7.280 is the 2018 and 2019 self-employed person's deduction as printed in the source example, not the 2020 amount of € 7.030 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 5 (Official example, FISIN 2020 section 7.7.1.3): forced decrease of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

Assets (activa) € 6.000, equity (eigen vermogen) minus € 1.000, old-age reserve € 4.000 and accounts payable (crediteuren) € 3.000. The business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) is € 6.000 - € 3.000 = € 3.000, which equals minus € 1.000 + € 4.000. The reserve of € 4.000 is higher than the business capital of € 3.000, so the reserve decreases by € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000, and that € 1.000 is added to profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### Example 6 (Calculated example): deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) at 1.300 hours

Facts: profit € 60.000, meets the hours criterion, the partner works 1.300 hours in the business without pay.

- Step 1: 1.300 hours is at least 1.225 and below 1.750, so the band is 3%.
- Step 2: 3% of € 60.000 = € 1.800,00 deduction for assisting partner.
- Variation: exactly 875 hours would fall in the 2% band, because the band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" 875, and 2% of € 60.000 = € 1.200,00. Exactly 1.750 hours falls in the 4% band, and 4% of € 60.000 = € 2.400,00.
- Variation: if the partner were paid € 4.500, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and the partner counts as unpaid, and the bands still apply.

### Example 7 (Calculated example): discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) with earlier use

Facts: profit on the cessation (staking) of one whole business € 10.000, discontinuation deduction enjoyed in an earlier year € 1.500.

- Step 1: the maximum of € 3.630 is reduced by the earlier € 1.500, giving € 3.630 - € 1.500 = € 2.130.
- Step 2: the deduction equals the cessation profit, at most € 2.130, so the discontinuation deduction is € 2.130.
- Variation: had the earlier use been the full € 3.630, the maximum would be reduced to nil and there would be no discontinuation deduction this year.
- Variation: had the cessation profit been € 2.000 with no earlier use, the deduction would be € 2.000.

### Example 8 (Calculated example): starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Facts: was not an entrepreneur in one or more of 2015 to 2019, has a WIA benefit in 2020, works 900 hours in the business, profit € 30.000, below the AOW age at the start of the year, no tax-neutral return (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv).

- Step 1: 900 hours is at least 800 and not more than 1.225, so the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) is met and the hours criterion of 1.225 hours is not met.
- Step 2: the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, so the amount is € 12.000.
- Step 3: the amount is not more than the profit of € 30.000, so the deduction is € 12.000.
- Step 4: profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 30.000 - € 12.000 = € 18.000.
- Step 5: SME profit exemption, no hours criterion needed: 14% of € 18.000 = € 2.520,00.
- Step 6: taxable profit from business: € 18.000 - € 2.520,00 = € 15.480,00.
- Follow-on years: one earlier year of use gives € 8.000, two years give € 4.000, and after more than two uses there is no deduction and FISIN prints € 0. Each amount is capped at the profit.

### Example 9 (Calculated example): S&O deduction with the starter increase

Facts: profit € 100.000, has an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from RVO, 600 hours of S&O work, meets the hours criterion, was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period.

- Step 1: base amount € 12.980.
- Step 2: starter increase € 6.494, so the total S&O deduction is € 12.980 + € 6.494 = € 19.474.
- Step 3: profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 100.000 - € 19.474 = € 80.526.
- Step 4: SME profit exemption 14% of € 80.526 = € 11.273,64.
- Step 5: taxable profit from business: € 80.526 - € 11.273,64 = € 69.252,36.
- Check: € 80.526 × 0,14 = € 11.273,64 and € 80.526 - € 11.273,64 = € 69.252,36.

### Example 10 (Calculated example): old-age reserve addition

Facts: meets the hours criterion, below the AOW age at the start of the year, profit for the addition € 60.000, no pension premiums charged to that profit, business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end € 80.000, old-age reserve at the start of the year € 20.000, and the entrepreneur chooses to make the addition.

- Step 1: 9,44% of € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
- Step 2: the maximum of € 9.218 is not exceeded, so € 5.664,00 stands.
- Step 3: the cap is the business capital at year end minus the old-age reserve at the start: € 80.000 - € 20.000 = € 60.000, so this cap does not bite either.
- Step 4: the addition is € 5.664,00 and the reserve at year end is € 20.000 + € 5.664,00 = € 25.664,00.
- Variation: at a profit of € 120.000, 9,44% is € 11.328,00, which is above the maximum, so the addition is capped at € 9.218.
- Variation: at a profit of € 60.000 but with business capital at year end of € 21.000 against a reserve at the start of € 20.000, the headroom is € 21.000 - € 20.000 = € 1.000, so the addition is capped at € 1.000.
- Variation: a negative outcome of the calculation gives a nil addition.

### Example 11 (Calculated example): rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

Facts: taxable box 1 income after the deductions € 58.507 and deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) of € 20.000, being the entrepreneur's allowance plus the SME profit exemption.

- Step 1: taxable box 1 income plus the deductions: € 58.507 + € 20.000 = € 78.507.
- Step 2: the amount by which that sum exceeds the start of the top bracket: € 78.507 - € 68.507 = € 10.000.
- Step 3: 3,5% of € 10.000 = € 350,00. This is the base amount under art. 2.10 lid 2.
- Step 4: the cap is 3,5% of the deductions: 3,5% of € 20.000 = € 700,00. The € 350,00 is below the cap, so the rate adjustment is € 350,00.
- Step 5: the maximum value of the deductions is 46% in total, being 49,50% minus 3,5%.
- Variation: with taxable box 1 income of € 108.507 and the same € 20.000 of deductions, the sum is € 128.507, the excess is € 128.507 - € 68.507 = € 60.000, and 3,5% of € 60.000 = € 2.100,00. The cap of € 700,00 then applies, so the rate adjustment is € 700,00.

### Example 12 (Calculated example): SME profit exemption in a loss year

Facts: loss as an entrepreneur € 10.000, meets the hours criterion.

- Step 1: the self-employed person's deduction is capped at the profit. With a loss the deduction is nil, and the € 7.030 is unused self-employed person's deduction. Under art. 3.76 lid 5 this year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count, because the cap set the deduction to nil.
- Step 2: the SME profit exemption is 14% of the amount after the entrepreneur's allowance, so 14% of minus € 10.000 = minus € 1.400,00.
- Step 3: the fiscal loss becomes € 10.000 + € 1.400,00 = € 11.400,00. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls this enlargement of the loss disadvantageous in a loss year.

### Example 13 (Calculated example): Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet)

Facts: taxable profit from business € 40.000, no other contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen).

- Step 1: the rate by assessment for 2020 is 5,45%.
- Step 2: 5,45% of € 40.000 = € 2.180,00.
- Variation: at contribution income above the maximum of € 57.232, the contribution is at most 5,45% of € 57.232 = € 3.119,14, rounded down.
- Note: no contribution is levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve into an annuity (lijfrente).

### Example 14 (Official examples): part-year working and standby time

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: a business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. The same page: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so 1.225 - 936 = 289 extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).
