## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction) is a per-onderneming (per business) deduction, claimed by choice in the aangifte (tax return), on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) in the calendar year ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For 2020 the table in art. 3.41 lid 2 has five bands. An investment of € 2.400 or less gives no KIA. From € 2.400 up to € 58.238 the KIA is 28% of the investment. From € 58.238 up to € 107.848 the KIA is a fixed € 16.307. From € 107.848 up to € 323.544 the KIA is € 16.307 minus 7,56% of the part of the investment above € 107.848. Above € 323.544 the KIA is nil ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The table looks at the total investment of the year, not at each asset separately, but each asset must still pass the rules of art. 3.45 and the art. 3.44 payment cap where the asset is not yet in use.

### KIA in a samenwerkingsverband (art. 3.41 lid 3)

In a samenwerkingsverband (partnership, for example a vof, a maatschap or a cv) art. 3.41 lid 3 adds the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments, applies the table to that sum, and multiplies the result by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot (partner) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst (profit), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)).

### KIA per-asset minimum and KIA-only exclusion (art. 3.45 lid 2)

An asset with an investment amount below € 450 does not count for the KIA ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The same lid excludes for the KIA assets that are intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The € 450 minimum sits in art. 3.45 and not in art. 3.41, which is a corrected article reference.

### Excluded assets (art. 3.45 lid 1, lid 3 and lid 5)

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes from every investeringsaftrek assets intended mainly for use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (a rule to prevent double taxation), gronden (land) including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (soil improvement), woonhuizen (dwellings) and woonschepen (houseboats), personenauto's (passenger cars) as defined in art. 3 van de Wet BPM 1992, vaartuigen (vessels) as in art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, effecten (securities), vorderingen (receivables), goodwill, vergunningen (permits), ontheffingen (exemptions) and concessies (concessions), and dieren (animals) ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.45 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Lid 3 makes an exception for the MIA: for the MIA lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn MIA. Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.45 lid 3 and lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The same amounts and the same list appear in every checked version from 2020 to 2027.

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum and exclusions (art. 3.45 lid 4)

For the EIA and the MIA an asset with an investment amount below € 2.500 is excluded (art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b), and so are assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (onderdeel a) ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.45 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). FISIN 2020 gives the minimum as € 2.500 per bedrijfsmiddel (business asset) ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (art. 3.42 lid 4)

Art. 3.42 lid 4 sets the maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA at € 124.000.000 per year, pro rata in a samenwerkingsverband ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). FISIN 2020 prints the EIA as "45% van maximaal € 124.000.000" and the MIA as "niet meer dan € 124.000.000" ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). Separately from this statutory maximum, RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. That RVO page states no year, so the 2020 position is not confirmed in the checked sources ([RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### EIA percentage (art. 3.42 lid 3)

The energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction) is 45% of the investment in an asset that is on the Energielijst ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The asset must be new, see the new-asset subsection below ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The EIA can be combined with the KIA on the same asset ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### MIA percentages (art. 3.42a lid 3)

The milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction) has three categories. For 2020 categorie I gives 36%, categorie II gives 27% and categorie III gives 13,5% ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The asset must be on the Milieulijst ([RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I (45%), A or D to categorie II (36%) and B or E to categorie III (27%), and the statute then reads 45%, 36% and 27% ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). That 2022 mapping is not the 2020 position, and the checked sources do not print the 2020 code mapping.

### New-asset condition for the EIA and the MIA

Only new assets qualify for the EIA and the MIA. The test is "niet eerder gebruikt" (not used before). A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as voortbrengingskosten (production costs) ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)).

### Combination rules for the EIA, MIA, Vamil and KIA

The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset. Each of them can be combined with the KIA. No MIA is allowed on costs that already received EIA, and Vamil remains possible on those costs. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). An EIA asset must be on the Energielijst, a MIA and Vamil asset must be on the Milieulijst ([RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### MIA and Vamil maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year (RVO)

RVO states that the MIA and Vamil have a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum ([RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The RVO page does not state a year for that figure, so it cannot be tied to 2020 on the checked material. Alongside it, art. 3.42 lid 4 gives a statutory EIA and MIA maximum of € 124.000.000 for the year ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Vamil (art. 3.31 lid 1)

The costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven" (may be depreciated at will for 75%), and the rest follows the normal schedule ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that were not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). RVO states that the total tax benefit of MIA and Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### RVO notification deadline for the EIA, MIA and Vamil

An EIA, MIA or Vamil investment must be notified to the Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO, Netherlands Enterprise Agency) within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order for aanschafkosten (acquisition costs), and within 3 months after the quarter in which voortbrengingskosten were made ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO ondernemers](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) are notified together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). FISIN 2020 says "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)." ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (art. 3.44)

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (lid 1). At staking (termination) of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking (lid 2). The rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). FISIN 2020 phrases the cap as "tot het bedrag dat in het kalenderjaar is betaald" ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The 2020 text of art. 3.44 matches this rule. The article reference was corrected.

### Excluded obligations, including an asset bought from an own BV (art. 3.46)

No investeringsaftrek applies to obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam (legal entity) and that lichaam (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 (lid 2), which is the ontheffing (exemption) route ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" (the BV of which you hold at least one third of the shares) gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (in principle no right to the investment deduction) ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The 2020 text of art. 3.46 was read and matches.

### Ordinary depreciation (art. 3.30)

Yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffingskosten or voortbrengingskosten (acquisition or production costs) that is attributable to the year (lid 1). Per year the depreciation is at most 10% of the cost for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 years for goodwill and 5 years for other assets (lid 2). Voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging (lid 3). Voorwerpen van geringe waarde (low-value items) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (lid 4) ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The same text holds in 2020 to 2027 ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Bodemwaarde for buildings (art. 3.30a)

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde (floor value), and at most for the difference (lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden (appurtenances) are one bedrijfsmiddel, while detachable werktuigen (tools) are separate (lid 2). For 2020 the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (investment building) and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another (own use) building (lid 3). The WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking (decision) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (lid 4) ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). From 2024 there is one bodemwaarde, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold (art. 3.47 lid 1)

If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than € 2.400, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to profit ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47 lid 1](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)). The 2020 statute reads € 2.400. FISIN 2020 prints "€ 2.300 of minder". The statute prevails and the 2020 conflict is settled to € 2.400 ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling term and undoing of the investment (art. 3.47 lid 2 and lid 6)

The bijtelling applies only to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken (lid 2). Lid 3 treats as a disposal withdrawing the good from the onderneming, changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (third-party use, KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (non-resident use, EIA and MIA), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2°. In those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair market value) counts as the transfer price (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding (reduction, refund or compensation), counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7) ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst page says that renting out counts for the KIA but not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap (URWA art. 8)

Art. 3.34 van de Wet IB 2001 is the statutory basis for willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship. URWA art. 8 allows willekeurige afschrijving "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen" ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Wet IB 2001 art. 3.34](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). For 2020 that maximum is the top of the KIA table, € 323.544 ([Wet IB 2001 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). An earlier reading used the end of the 28% band, € 58.238. The corrected reading is the top of the table. This rests on reading the cross-reference in URWA art. 8, because no primary page prints the cap as a number ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving conditions (URWA art. 7, 9 and 12)

URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered into or voortbrengingskosten were made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek (increased self-employed deduction, the startersaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3 applies. Lid 2 also covers the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst as ondernemer (profit as an entrepreneur) ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The Belastingdienst adds that the scheme is open to an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, that the startersaftrek conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply ([Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). The art. 3.38 period is five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs (art. 9) and ten years for the zeeschepen (sea-going vessels) of art. 10 (art. 12). Herwaardering to the normal book value, with the difference as winst, is required within 5 years (10 for zeeschepen) if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf (forestry business) ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving

URWA art. 13 to 15 contain a temporary willekeurige afschrijving scheme for calendar year 2023, which is not this year's law ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). For 2020 the checked sources name no temporary scheme. The Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year, and the URWA text opened for this check lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). An absence cannot be confirmed on one page, so this remains UNVERIFIED.
