# Box 3 sparen en beleggen 2020

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2020 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

In 2020 box 3 tax is calculated with the old band method (oude berekening) over three bands of the grondslag sparen en beleggen (basis for savings and investments). The box 3 rate is 30% and the values are measured on the peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2020. The 2020 law is enacted. Later, the Wet rechtsherstel box 3 (restoration of rights) of 21 December 2022 can recalculate a 2020 assessment that was not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, but only when the recalculation gives a lower voordeel. The Hoge Raad ruling of 25 June 2026 confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 have no right to herstel of paid box 3 tax over that period.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Peildatum
The box 3 values for 2020 are measured on the peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2020. The Belastingdienst FISIN 2020 page states that the values are on the peildatum 1 January 2020. In the old band method the actual composition of the assets does not matter for the band calculation. The value on the peildatum determines the rendementsgrondslag (return basis). For the Wet rechtsherstel recalculation, art. 3 uses the banktegoeden (bank balances), overige bezittingen (other assets) and schulden (debts) at 1 January. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Heffingsvrij vermogen
The heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free capital) for 2020 is € 30.846 per person. With a fiscal partner it is € 61.692. The 2020 statute art. 5.5 says "Het heffingvrije vermogen bedraagt € 30.846". FISIN 2020 agrees. The Belastingdienst page on heffingsvrij vermogen states that the amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. For the actual-return calculation the Hoge Raad determined that no account may be taken of the heffingsvrij vermogen. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.5), and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement.

### Schuldendrempel
The schuldendrempel (debt threshold) for 2020 is € 3.100 per person and € 6.200 with a whole-year partner. Only the part of total debts above the drempel is deductible. The statute art. 5.3 lid 3 says "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...". The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. The Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return. The drempel doubles for whole-year partners and a joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.3 lid 3), and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.

### Box 3 tarief
The box 3 rate for 2020 is 30%. This is in art. 2.13. The rate applies to the voordeel (benefit) from sparen en beleggen (savings and investments). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 2.13) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening.

### Rendementsklasse percentages
The old band method uses two rendementsklassen (return classes). Klasse I is savings and klasse II is investments. For 2020 klasse I is 0,07% and klasse II is 5,28%. Art. 5.2 lid 1 was replaced by Stcrt. 2020, 20572 with effect from 1 January 2020. These percentages are not applied directly to the actual assets. They are mixed in the bands. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.2 lid 1).

### Band 1
Band 1 covers the part of the grondslag sparen en beleggen up to € 72.797. The Belastingdienst page writes the same limit as "tot € 72.798". The statute writes the limit as "niet meer dan" € 72.797 and the page writes "tot" € 72.798. Both describe the same band. In this band 67% is rendementsklasse I and 33% is rendementsklasse II. The average return is 1,789%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01.

### Band 2
Band 2 covers the part of the grondslag sparen en beleggen from € 72.797 to € 1.005.572. The page writes € 72.798 to € 1.005.573. In this band 21% is rendementsklasse I and 79% is rendementsklasse II. The average return is 4,185%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.

### Band 3
Band 3 covers the part of the grondslag sparen en beleggen above € 1.005.572. The page writes above € 1.005.573. In this band 0% is rendementsklasse I and 100% is rendementsklasse II. The return is 5,28%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.

### Rechtsherstel category percentages
For the Wet rechtsherstel (restoration of rights) recalculation the category percentages for 2020 are bank 0,04%, other assets 5,28%, and schulden 2,74%. These are the same figures in the law table. Art. 3 of the Wet rechtsherstel: the voordeel is the effective rendementspercentage times the grondslag sparen en beleggen. The effective percentage is the rendement divided by the rendementsgrondslag. The rendement is column I times banktegoeden plus column II times overige bezittingen minus column III times schulden at 1 January. Art. 3 lid 4: whole-year partners use the joint figures. For 2020 the percentages are as stated. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Rechtsherstel scope 2020
The Wet rechtsherstel applies to IB/PVV (income tax and national insurance contributions) assessments for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022. This is art. 1 lid 2. Art. 1 lid 3: it applies only where it leads to a lower voordeel. Art. 2: the voordeel is set at the outcome of art. 3, not below nil. Art. 5: extra persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) follows the partners' chosen division of specific care costs or gifts, with an ambtshalve-vermindering (ex officio reduction) request for another split. Art. 6: the double-taxation relief is recomputed. Art. 7: in force the day after the issue date of the Staatsblad (Government Gazette) of 27 December 2022, so in force 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support. Mass objectors get rechtsherstel for 2017 to 2020 (MvT, memorie van toelichting or explanatory memorandum). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html, and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.

### Most favourable method
For an assessment in scope of the Wet rechtsherstel the category recalculation applies only if it is lower, and the Belastingdienst applies the most favourable method. The Belastingdienst states: "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

### Kerstarrest 24 December 2021
Hoge Raad 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The memorie van toelichting to the Wet rechtsherstel describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP (First Protocol to the EVRM, European Convention on Human Rights), in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM": the forfaitaire (fictitious) system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Forfaits are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban. The claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return stays UNVERIFIED, because hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 again on 2026-10-11. The MvT stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on actual return for the rechtsherstel but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.

### Hoge Raad 6 June 2024
The Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024.

### Vrijstelling groene beleggingen
Art. 5.13: groene beleggingen (green investments) are not counted as bezittingen up to the stated amount. For 2020 the amount is € 59.477. With a whole-year partner it is € 118.954. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The checked material records the ordering rule from the 2023 text: the exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The 2020 text ordering is not stated in the checked material. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.13), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen.

### Korting groene beleggingen
Art. 8.19 lid 2: the korting voor groene beleggingen (green investment tax credit) is 0,7% of the exempt amount on the peildatum for 2020. This rate applies from 2020 to 2024. The korting is computed automatically. It is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 8.19 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen.

### Actual-return reporting 2020
For 2020 the werkelijke rendement (actual return) reporting route is an online form in Mijn Belastingdienst ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"). It is only available with a letter. The Belastingdienst states: "Voor 2017 tot en met 2020 zijn alle brieven verstuurd." Without a letter you may not report. A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call) and several forms via the BelastingTelefoon 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. The content rules for the actual return are: income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen is taken into account ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value (property valuation) and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte.

### Tegenbewijsregeling inside Wet rechtsherstel
The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 (counter-evidence scheme, law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195) added art. 6a to the Wet rechtsherstel. Art. IV works back to 1 January 2017 with its own form and six-week rule. Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the inspecteur (tax inspector), in deviation from Awb (Algemene wet bestuursrecht) arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus vermogensaanwas (capital growth). The law entered into force on 19 July 2025 by the besluit (decree) of 14 July 2025 (Stb. 2025, 196). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html.

### Hoge Raad 25 June 2026
Taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". The Rijksoverheid timeline now confirms this. The "or objected late" part is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED. hogeraad.nl still returns HTTP 403. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples were found in the checked material for 2020. All examples below are calculated examples.

**Calculated example 1: old band method with a single person.**
Inputs: banktegoeden € 200.000,00, overige bezittingen € 0,00, schulden € 10.000,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person, so heffingsvrij vermogen € 30.846,00 and schuldendrempel € 3.100,00.
Step 1: deductible debt = € 10.000,00 - € 3.100,00 = € 6.900,00.
Step 2: rendementsgrondslag = € 200.000,00 - € 6.900,00 = € 193.100,00.
Step 3: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 193.100,00 - € 30.846,00 = € 162.254,00.
Step 4: band 1 = € 72.797,00. Band 2 = € 162.254,00 - € 72.797,00 = € 89.457,00.
Step 5: band 1 voordeel = € 72.797,00 × 1,789% = € 1.302,34, rounded.
Step 6: band 2 voordeel = € 89.457,00 × 4,185% = € 3.743,78, rounded.
Step 7: total voordeel = € 1.302,34 + € 3.743,78 = € 5.046,12.
Step 8: box 3 tax = 30% × € 5.046,12 = € 1.513,84, rounded.

**Calculated example 2: whole year partners.**
Inputs: joint banktegoeden € 200.000,00, joint schulden € 10.000,00. Whole year partners, so joint heffingsvrij vermogen € 61.692,00 and joint schuldendrempel € 6.200,00.
Step 1: deductible joint debt = € 10.000,00 - € 6.200,00 = € 3.800,00.
Step 2: joint rendementsgrondslag = € 200.000,00 - € 3.800,00 = € 196.200,00.
Step 3: joint grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 196.200,00 - € 61.692,00 = € 134.508,00.
Step 4: the partners may divide the joint grondslag in any way that totals 100%.

**Calculated example 3: rechtsherstel recalculation.**
Inputs: banktegoeden € 100.000,00, overige bezittingen € 100.000,00, schulden € 0,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person.
Step 1: bank return = € 100.000,00 × 0,04% = € 40,00.
Step 2: other asset return = € 100.000,00 × 5,28% = € 5.280,00.
Step 3: debt return = € 0,00 × 2,74% = € 0,00.
Step 4: rendement = € 40,00 + € 5.280,00 - € 0,00 = € 5.320,00.
Step 5: rendementsgrondslag = € 200.000,00 - € 0,00 = € 200.000,00.
Step 6: effective percentage = € 5.320,00 / € 200.000,00 = 0,0266 = 2,66%.
Step 7: grondslag sparen en beleggen = € 200.000,00 - € 30.846,00 = € 169.154,00.
Step 8: rechtsherstel voordeel = € 169.154,00 × 2,66% = € 4.499,50, rounded.
Step 9: tax under rechtsherstel = 30% × € 4.499,50 = € 1.349,85.
Step 10: old band method for the same assets: band 1 = € 72.797,00. Band 2 = € 169.154,00 - € 72.797,00 = € 96.357,00. Band 1 voordeel = € 72.797,00 × 1,789% = € 1.302,34, rounded. Band 2 voordeel = € 96.357,00 × 4,185% = € 4.032,54, rounded down. Total old voordeel = € 1.302,34 + € 4.032,54 = € 5.334,88. Old tax = 30% × € 5.334,88 = € 1.600,46, rounded down.
Step 11: comparison. The rechtsherstel voordeel of € 4.499,50 is lower than the old voordeel of € 5.334,88. Under art. 1 lid 3 the category recalculation applies only if it gives a lower voordeel, so the rechtsherstel amount would be used if the assessment is in scope and the taxpayer objected.

**Calculated example 4: green investments.**
Inputs: green investments € 50.000,00 on 1 January 2020. Single person.
Step 1: the 2020 exemption cap is € 59.477,00.
Step 2: the exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January, so the exempt amount is € 50.000,00.
Step 3: the korting is 0,7% of the exempt amount = 0,7% × € 50.000,00 = € 350,00.
Second part: with a whole year partner and green investments of € 130.000,00, the cap is € 118.954,00. The exempt amount is € 118.954,00. The korting is 0,7% × € 118.954,00 = € 832,68, rounded.

**Calculated example 5: actual return.**
Inputs: bank interest € 200,00, value increase of overige bezittingen € 1.000,00, interest paid on a box 3 debt € 50,00. Single person.
Step 1: actual return = € 200,00 + € 1.000,00 - € 50,00 = € 1.150,00.
Step 2: no heffingsvrij vermogen is subtracted.
Step 3: if the total were negative, it would be € 0,00 and could not be carried to another year.
Step 4: under the tegenbewijsregeling, if this actual return is lower than the fictitious voordeel, the income is the actual return minus the persoonsgebonden aftrek. The actual return is at least nil. If there is no persoonsgebonden aftrek, the voordeel is € 1.150,00. At the 2020 box 3 rate of 30%, the tax is 30% × € 1.150,00 = € 345,00.
For 2020 the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst can only be used if you received a letter. The Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount, so you never pay more than the fictitious calculation.

- The 2020 green investment exemption is € 59.477 and € 118.954 with a whole-year partner. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 5.13) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the value of banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden on the peildatum 1 January 2020. These values determine the rendementsgrondslag for the 2020 box 3 return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_.
- Box 3 is reported in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) for 2020. The Belastingdienst computes the schuldendrempel in the return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.
- For a whole-year fiscal partner, the heffingsvrij vermogen doubles to € 61.692 and the schuldendrempel doubles to € 6.200. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). The partners may divide the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen in any way that totals 100%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel.
- For green investments, record the value on 1 January 2020. The exemption is up to € 59.477, or € 118.954 with a whole-year partner. The korting is 0,7% of the exempt amount and is computed automatically. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen.
- For actual-return reporting, if you have a letter, use the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst. Keep records of income, value changes, and the two deductible cost types. No deadline is stated on these pages. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement.
- If you did not object for 2020, the Hoge Raad ruling of 25 June 2026 means no herstel of paid box 3 tax for 2017 to 2020. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3.
- The Wet rechtsherstel recalculation for 2020 applies only to assessments not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 and only if it gives a lower voordeel. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return stays UNVERIFIED. hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. This would be settled by the ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl.
- The "or objected late" part of the Hoge Raad 25 June 2026 ruling is not on the Rijksoverheid timeline and stays UNVERIFIED. This would be settled by the ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl, which returned HTTP 403 for hogeraad.nl.
- Band limit presentation conflict: the statute writes "meer dan / niet meer dan" with € 72.797 and € 1.005.572. The Belastingdienst page writes "tot / vanaf" with € 72.798 and € 1.005.573. Both describe the same bands. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen.
- Wet rechtsherstel entry date conflict: statute art. 7 says the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. The Rijksoverheid timeline says "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3. The statute is the legal date.
- The checked material records the art. 5.13 lid 2 ordering rule from the 2023 text. The 2020 text ordering of the green exemption over green overige bezittingen and green banktegoeden is not stated in the checked material. This would be settled by the 2020 text of art. 5.13 on wetten.overheid.nl.
