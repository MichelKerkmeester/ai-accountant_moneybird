## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2020

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag)

For 2020 the income-dependent Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag) is 5,45% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This channel applies to a sole trader with profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland). It also covers income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage (loon) from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes (loonheffing) such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente) is not included in this channel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The Zvw assessment comes with the income tax assessment (aanslag inkomstenbelasting) or the provisional income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, UNVERIFIED for 2020). Exceptions are military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). The calculation is the rate of 5,45% applied to the contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) up to € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Withheld health insurance contribution (ingehouden Zvw-bijdrage, zorgverzekering)

The withheld health insurance contribution (ingehouden Zvw-bijdrage) is 5,45% in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). It is withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits (uitkeringen) with payroll taxes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An entrepreneur who also receives such income has this contribution withheld. The withheld amount uses up the € 57.232 maximum contribution income, because the maximum applies once per person across channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The rate is the same as the rate by assessment and lower than the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw employer levy

For 2020 the employer levy is 6,70% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This levy is paid by the employer or benefit agency over a wage from employment (loon uit loondienst), the DGA wage (wage of a director and substantial shareholder, directeur-grootaandeelhouder) when insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people insured under the health insurance fund scheme (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This is the employer channel and not the rate an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) pays on profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) by assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). If the sole trader also has a wage from employment (loon uit loondienst), the employer pays the 6,70% and that wage uses up the maximum contribution income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum applies once per person across channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Maximum contribution income

For 2020 the maximum contribution income is € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum applies once per person across channels. Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, UNVERIFIED for 2020). For a sole trader the base is capped at € 57.232 before the 5,45% rate is applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). If a wage from employment or a benefit (uitkering) already used part of the maximum, only the remaining headroom is available for the profit (winst) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Maximum once per person across channels

The maximum contribution income applies once per person across the three channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The channels are the employer levy, the withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) and the contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag), whether provisional or final (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom for the other channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An official example on the 2020 page shows a wage of € 40.000 with employer levy plus freelance income of € 20.000. The page says the remaining base for the assessment is € 17.214 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material states that arithmetic gives € 17.232. See section 7 for this conflict. A second official example shows a wage of € 60.000 plus freelance income of € 15.000. The wage exceeds the € 57.232 maximum, so no Zvw contribution is due over the freelance income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw base for profit (winst)

This item is UNVERIFIED for 2020. Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines taxable profit (belastbare winst) as profit (winst) minus entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) minus SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 version). So the Zvw base for an entrepreneur with profit from business is the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, both read in the 2026 version only). The 2020 versions of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 were not read, so this rule is UNVERIFIED for 2020. The base is set at no less than nil and no more than the yearly maximum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, UNVERIFIED for 2020). A co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption, as stated on the 2026 FISIN page for profits from business activities (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). This point comes from a 2026 page and is UNVERIFIED for 2020.

### Old-age reserve conversion into an annuity

The contribution by assessment covers profit from the Netherlands but not the conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, UNVERIFIED for 2020). When a sole trader buys an annuity with a matching decrease of the old-age reserve, that amount is not in the Zvw base. The rest of the taxable profit (belastbare winst) from business remains in the base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).

### Reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB) at AOW age

https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 (Tabel 77) footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB) (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2). The amount is not in the checked sources. No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2020 the rate for a self-employed person (zelfstandige) at AOW age is unknown. See section 7.

### Zvw assessment timing

The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For a sole trader the same return process covers the income and the Zvw contribution. The checked material does not give a separate Zvw filing deadline (aangiftetermijn). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms the assessment timing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic (checked material, Zvw declaration and deadlines). This item is Verified for the assessment timing and UNVERIFIED for the deadline.
