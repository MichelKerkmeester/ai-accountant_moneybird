## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Calculated example: Zvw on winst below the maximum

A sole trader has a belastbare winst uit onderneming of € 30.000. The Zvw base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2020.

Step 1: The base is € 30.000.
Step 2: € 30.000 is below the maximum € 57.232, so the full amount is used.
Step 3: The contribution is 5,45% × € 30.000 = € 1.635,00.

The rate and maximum are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. The base rule is from https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 versions, UNVERIFIED for 2020.

### Calculated example: Zvw on winst above the maximum

A sole trader has a belastbare winst uit onderneming of € 60.000. The Zvw base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2020.

Step 1: The base is € 60.000.
Step 2: The maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 57.232, so the base is capped at € 57.232. The excess is € 60.000 - € 57.232 = € 2.768.
Step 3: The contribution is 5,45% × € 57.232 = € 3.119,14.

The rate and maximum are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. The base rule is from https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 versions, UNVERIFIED for 2020.

### Calculated example: employer rate compared with the rate by aanslag

A sole trader compares € 30.000 of winst uit onderneming with € 30.000 of loon uit loondienst.

Step 1: By aanslag the rate is 5,45%. The contribution is 5,45% × € 30.000 = € 1.635,00.
Step 2: The werkgeversheffing rate is 6,70%. The employer levy is 6,70% × € 30.000 = € 2.010,00.
Step 3: The difference is € 2.010,00 - € 1.635,00 = € 375,00.

The rates are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. The employer pays the 6,70% on loon uit loondienst. The sole trader pays the 5,45% by aanslag on winst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Official example: loon plus freelance income

https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet shows loon of € 40.000 with werkgeversheffing plus freelance income of € 20.000. The page says the bijdrage by aanslag is 5,45% over € 17.214 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

Recheck with the section 2 figures: The maximum is € 57.232. The loon is € 40.000. The arithmetic in the checked material gives € 17.232. 5,45% × € 17.232 = € 939,14. 5,45% × the printed € 17.214 = € 938,16. See section 7 for the difference between the printed € 17.214 and the arithmetic € 17.232.

### Official example: no headroom left

https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet shows loon of € 60.000 plus freelance income of € 15.000. The loon is above the maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 57.232. The page says no bijdrage is due over the freelance income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum applies once per person across channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Calculated example: oudedagsreserve conversion excluded

A sole trader has a belastbare winst uit onderneming of € 50.000. Of that amount, € 10.000 is the addition under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve. The exclusion rule is UNVERIFIED for 2020.

Step 1: The Zvw base is € 50.000 - € 10.000 = € 40.000.
Step 2: € 40.000 is below the maximum € 57.232.
Step 3: The contribution is 5,45% × € 40.000 = € 2.180,00.

The exclusion is from https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, UNVERIFIED for 2020. The rate and maximum are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
