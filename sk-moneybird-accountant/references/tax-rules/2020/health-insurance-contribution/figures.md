## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet) under the Health Insurance Act for an entrepreneur in tax year 2020. The rate paid by assessment (aanslag) is 5,45% and the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 57.232. The employer rate, the employer levy (werkgeversheffing), is 6,70%. The 2020 law is enacted. The rule that the Zvw base for an entrepreneur with profit from business is the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) is UNVERIFIED for 2020 because only the 2026 statutory versions were read.
