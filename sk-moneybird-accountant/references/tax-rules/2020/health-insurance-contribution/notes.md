## 2. CHANGES FROM 2019

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The checked sources do not include tax year 2019 for this topic. A 2019 to 2020 comparison is not possible from the checked material.
- For 2020 the enacted values are 5,45% by assessment (aanslag), 5,45% withheld (ingehouden), 6,70% employer levy (werkgeversheffing) and a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The cross-year rule that the maximum applies once per person across channels is stated for 2020 to 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material does not state whether this rule began in 2020.
- The 2020 Zvw figures are enacted (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) that forms the Zvw base after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 versions, UNVERIFIED for 2020).
- Record income that already bore a Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet), such as a wage from employment (loon uit loondienst), a pension or an annuity (lijfrente), because it uses up the € 57.232 maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag) for 2020 is 5,45% of the base up to € 57.232 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The Zvw assessment comes with the income tax assessment (aanslag inkomstenbelasting) or the provisional income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity is not in the assessment base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The checked material does not give a 2020 Zvw filing deadline (aangiftetermijn). That deadline belongs to the procedure topic (checked material, Zvw declaration and deadlines).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for profit (winst): UNVERIFIED for 2020. The rule comes from https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, both read in the 2026 version only. Reading the 2020 versions of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 would settle it.
- Zvw declaration and filing deadline (aangiftetermijn): UNVERIFIED. The topic file states the same return (aangifte) is used, a provisional Zvw assessment (voorlopige aanslag Zvw) exists and the Zvw follows the income tax (IB) filing timeline. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms the Zvw assessment comes with the income tax assessment (aanslag inkomstenbelasting), final or provisional. The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic. Reading a primary source on the 2020 filing deadline would settle it.
- Zvw at state pension age (AOW-leeftijd): https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 (Tabel 77) footnote 2 states self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read. That is inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. The amount is not in the checked sources. Reading the 2020 Zvw statute and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 would settle it.
- Official example conflict: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet prints € 17.214 as the remaining base when the wage is € 40.000 and freelance income is € 20.000. The checked material states that arithmetic gives € 17.232. Both values are in the checked material. Both relate to the maximum € 57.232 from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. Reading the underlying calculation or a corrected page would settle it.
- Source URLs: the checked material records the Zvw sources by identifier, for example https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01. It does not include the exact primary URLs for those pages. This file uses the identifiers. Fetching the exact URLs would settle the citation.
