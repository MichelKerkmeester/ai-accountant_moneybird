## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The structure of the annual allowance (jaarruimte) stayed the same as in 2021: 13,3% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by factor A (6,27) and by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve), with a 7-year look-back. [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Maximum annual allowance: € 13.236 (2021) to € 13.570 (2022). [Ministry 2022 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf).
- Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of the contribution base: € 112.189 to € 114.866. [art. 3.127, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Franchise of the contribution base: € 12.672 to € 12.837. [art. 3.127, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window: still 7 years, but the window moved from the annual allowance of 2014 to and including 2020 (2021) to 2015 to and including 2021 (2022). [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum, younger group: € 7.489 to € 7.587. Older group: € 14.785 to € 14.978. [art. 3.127, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- The boundary between the two groups moved: in 2021 the Ministry of Finance (ministerie van financiën) table used younger than 56 years and 4 months (56 jaar en 4 maanden) on 1 January 2021 and FISIN 2021 used born after 31 August 1964. In 2022 the Ministry of Finance table uses younger than 56 years and 7 months (56 jaar en 7 maanden) on 1 January 2022 and FISIN 2022 states the higher amount for born before 1 September 1965, see section 7. [Ministry 2022 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap: € 22.443 to € 22.735. [art. 3.125, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 3.125, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maxima: tier a € 474.517 to € 480.686, tier b € 237.267 to € 240.352, tier c € 118.640 to € 120.183. [art. 3.129, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente): € 4.547 to € 4.607. Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid): € 42.460 to € 43.012. [art. 3.133, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 3.133, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- FOR addition (toevoeging) maximum: € 9.395 to € 9.632, the percentage 9,44% stayed the same. 2022 is the last year of an addition. From 2023 art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps an existing FOR under the old rules. [art. 3.68, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 3.68, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Payment deadline for the FOR conversion and the profit from business discontinuation conversion: before 1 July 2022 to before 1 July 2023, per art. 3.130 lid 2. [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Unchanged in 2022: the 13,3% percentage, the 6,27 factor A multiplier, the 17% catch-up allowance percentage cap, the age limit of not yet state pension age (AOW) at the start of the year, the bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) maximum of € 63.288, art. 3.128 as the FOR conversion article, the 1-year and 2-year annuity deadlines, and the AOV rules with the four exclusions (section 15.4 is identical to 2021). [art. 3.127, text 2021-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record every annuity premium (lijfrentepremie) with its payment date. Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts a premium in the year of payment or set-off (verrekening), and art. 3.130 lid 2 lets a payment before 1 July 2023 count in 2022 for the FOR conversion and the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion. [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Record per policy whether the benefits (uitkeringen) are periodic or a lump sum. A periodic AOV premium is fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 c. A lump-sum policy is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and its payout belongs in box 3. [art. 3.124, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- Record the FOR addition (toevoeging) and the FOR decrease (afname). For 2022 an addition of 9,44% of the profit (winst) up to € 9.632 is still possible if the hours criterion (urencriterium) is met and the taxpayer has not yet reached the state pension age at the start of the year. This is the last year of the addition. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. [art. 3.67, 3.68 and 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Keep a schedule of unused annual allowance (jaarruimte) per year from 2015 to 2021. The catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) is requested in the tax return (aangifte), the oldest unused year is used first, and the 17% and euro caps apply. The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Book the AOV premium as an expenditure for other income provisions (uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen) in the box 1 return, not as a business cost of the business (onderneming). Periodic benefits later are income from former employment (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking). [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- A mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium goes in as a business cost of the business, with a restriction. [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Watch the dates on existing annuities (lijfrentes). Convert or start within 1 full calendar year after the start date (ingangsdatum), and start a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) within 2 full calendar years after the year of death. Extension is possible on request in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Keep the start date on file for each policy. The FISIN 2022 deadlines (a start date in 2021 means 31 December 2022, a start date in 2022 means 31 December 2023) are tested on that date. [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

**Open points and conflicts for 2022.**

- Age boundary for the higher catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte). FISIN 2022 states the higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965. The Ministry of Finance 2022 table uses 56 years and 7 months (56 jaar en 7 maanden) or older on 1 January 2022. The law uses "ten hoogste tien jaar lager dan de in dat kalenderjaar geldende pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". The point is not fully settled because the state pension age of 2022 was not fetched from the General Old Age Pensions Act (Algemene Ouderdomswet, AOW). Settling source: art. 7a AOW as valid on 1 January 2022 on wetten.overheid.nl. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window. FISIN 2022 prints "2014 tot en met 2021", which is eight years. Art. 3.127 lid 2 of the 2022-01-01 text reads "zeven jaar", so the window is 2015 to and including 2021. The law text prevails. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap for 2022. FISIN 2022 prints € 22.443 in its insurance (verzekering) row and € 22.735 in its account (rekening) row. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c and the Ministry tables give € 22.735. Settled in favour of € 22.735. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.125, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).
- Note on verification. The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) of € 13.570 was derived and UNVERIFIED in earlier research. The Ministry tables confirm it, so section 2 marks it Verified. Sources: [Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf).

**Proposed and later-year items in the checked material, outside tax year 2022.**

- Basic Disability Insurance (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) for Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), bill 36 912. This is proposed, not 2022 law. Docket status on 2026-10-11: "In afwachting nota nav verslag". Bill art. 16 gives a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks (weken). The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) gives a benefit of 70% of the base (grondslag), with the base at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so at most the minimum wage. The premium is set yearly by the Employee Insurance Agency (Uitvoeringsinstituut Werknemersverzekeringen, UWV) and estimated at about 5,4% of the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) up to the maximum base. The MvT examples use a maximum base of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit (winst), and UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes (loonheffing). Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force (inwerkingtreding) is by royal decree (koninklijk besluit), and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16 (104 weken), settled at 104 weeks (weken). Sources: [dossier 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html), [document 2026D13315, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).
- Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027). Proposed, not adopted on 2026-10-11. Artikel LVII proposes no indexation of the income cap of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensionable salary (pensioengevend loon) at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) would stay € 137.800. A limited inflation correction of 48% of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the annuity maxima in the explanatory memorandum, being the maximum for annuity provisions (lijfrentevoorzieningen, art. 3.125), for an account and investment right (beleggingsrecht), the maximum premiums taken into account (in aanmerking te nemen premies, art. 3.127), the profit from business discontinuation maximum (art. 3.129) and the maximum for negative expenditures. The checked material also notes that the income cap was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (amendment (amendement) Grinwis c.s., Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90). Source: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet). Proposed and adjacent, with an internet consultation (internetconsultatie) until 29 October 2026, a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) leading to a "veilige haven" (safe harbour), and an intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) in January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension. Source: [rijksoverheid news item](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- Later-year correction on the 30% annual allowance percentage. The 30% runs from the Future Pensions Act (Wtp) with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2023, per the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page. The research tied Stcrt. 2023, 34571 and Stb. 2025, 431 to that change, but the 2023-07-01 text shows that Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6 (back to 1 January 2023) and Stb. 2025, 431 (in force (in werking) 1 January 2026) only rewrites the partner pension (partnerpensioen) wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Later-year correction on the pension accrual reduction. From the 2024-01-01 text the factor A reduction changes to the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964, also for accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2 gives for a fixed premium per year of service (dienstjaar, art. 38r) the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434 (retroactive to 1 July 2023): A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the net pension (nettopensioen) premium divided by the net factor (nettofactor). The research gave the older formula A = (1 - (X - Y) / X) x B. Sources: [art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10a.25, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Later-year conflict on the tier a age wording of art. 3.129. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (state pension age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. The law text governs. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).
- Later years, for completeness of the cross-year material. The FOR maximum of 2020 has a conflict between the law (€ 9.218) and FISIN 2020 (€ 9.214), settled at € 9.218. The temporary old-age annuity cap of 2026 has a conflict between € 27.192 and € 26.781, settled at € 27.192. The location of the FOR transition from 2023 is art. 10a.29 (not art. 3.127). The 2027 law text exists at wetten.overheid.nl with the 2026 amounts still in place. Sources: [art. 3.68, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Ministry 2020 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf), [art. 3.125, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 3.127, text 2027-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
