## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the income-provision rules for tax year 2022 for a self-employed person (ZZP) sole trader, and for a reader who may also own a private limited company (BV): the annual allowance (jaarruimte, the annual room for deductible annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies)) and the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte, the catch-up room for unused annual allowance), the conditions for annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) and annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), the conversion of profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) of art. 3.129, the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve, FOR) and the deductibility of disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premiums. The 2022 values come from the text of the Income Tax Act (Wet inkomstenbelasting) valid on 1 January 2022 and from the FISIN 2022 chapter of the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), so the law for this year is enacted. Two features of 2022 matter most: it is the last year in which a FOR addition (dotatie) is possible, and it is the last year in which the annual allowance is reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve). Section 7 lists the open points and conflicts in the checked material, including the age boundary for the higher catch-up allowance.
