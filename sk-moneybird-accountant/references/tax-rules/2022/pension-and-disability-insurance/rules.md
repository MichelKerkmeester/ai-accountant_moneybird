## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

This item applies to a taxpayer with a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year, so the 2022 deduction looks at 2021. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets that taxpayer deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to 13,3% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag, premium base), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual and, in 2022, also by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve, FOR, lid 1 and lid 4 sub b). The 13,3% holds in the texts of 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01. The 30% rate belongs to the texts from 2023-07-01 and later and does not apply to 2022. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte)

For 2022 the maximum annual allowance is € 13.570. The outcome of the percentage calculation is capped at this amount, which the Ministry of Finance tables print for 2022. The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. Premiums above the annual allowance are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed. Sources: [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of the contribution base (premiegrondslag)

For 2022 the income cap is € 114.866. This is the first-mentioned amount (eerstvermelde bedrag) of art. 3.127 lid 3. The income components of the previous calendar year are counted up to this amount. Art. 10.2b indexes the income cap with the contract wage development factor (contractloonontwikkelingsfactor). In the 2020 text that is lid 2, in the 2024 to 2027 texts lid 1. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10.2b, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 10.2b, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Franchise of the contribution base (premiegrondslag)

For 2022 the franchise is € 12.837. This is the last-mentioned amount (laatstvermelde bedrag) of art. 3.127 lid 3, subtracted after the cap. In the 2020 text the franchise follows art. 10.1. In the 2024 to 2027 texts art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the state pension (AOW) benefit for married persons without supplement (toeslag) plus holiday allowance (vakantieuitkering) times 100/75. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10.2b, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 10.2b, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Factor A multiplier

The multiplier for 2022 is 6,27. The reduction for pension accrual of art. 3.127 lid 4 is 6,27 times (keer) the growth (aangroei) of the yearly pension entitlement from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). This applies when the taxpayer accrues pension, for example through employment. From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction becomes the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor, with transitional rules in art. 10a.25. For 2022 the 6,27 formula holds. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10a.25, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Contribution base (premiegrondslag) composition

Art. 3.127 lid 3 sums over the previous calendar year (2021 for the 2022 deduction): (a) profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the addition to and the withdrawal from the old-age reserve (vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve) and before the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), (b) taxable wage (belastbaar loon), (c) taxable income from other work (belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and (d) taxable periodic payments and benefits in kind (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen). The sum is counted up to the income cap and then reduced by the franchise. Art. 3.127 lid 5 adds that on (partial) cessation (staking) the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Age limit for the annual allowance (jaarruimte)

At the start of the calendar year the taxpayer must not yet have reached the state pension age (AOW age). That is the rule in the 2022 text, and FISIN 2022 uses the same wording. From the Future Pensions Act (Wtp) text of 2023-07-01, applied to all of 2023, the limit becomes five years above the state pension age. For 2022 the older rule stands. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Look-back window for the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte)

Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows unused annual allowance of the preceding period to be used on request in the tax return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. The period is "zeven jaar" (seven years) in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts, so for 2022 the window covers the annual allowance of 2015 to and including 2021. From the 2023-07-01 text the period is "tien jaar" (ten years). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page advises using the catch-up allowance before the annual allowance so that older unused allowance (ruimte) does not lapse, and states that unused 2016 room lapses in 2027. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2022 prints the window as "2014 tot en met 2021", which does not match the law, see section 7.

### Catch-up allowance percentage cap

For 2020 to 2022 the catch-up allowance is at most 17% of the contribution base and at most a euro amount. That euro amount is raised to a higher amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the state pension age of that year. From the 2023-07-01 text there is one flat euro maximum, with no percentage and no age split. In 2022 both the 17% and the euro limit apply, so the lower of the two binds. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 2023-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Age boundary for the higher catch-up allowance

The law describes the boundary as an age at the start of the year of at most ten years below the state pension age of that year. The Ministry of Finance 2022 table uses 56 years and 7 months (56 jaar en 7 maanden) or older on 1 January 2022 for the higher amount. FISIN 2022 states the higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965, which looks shifted one year from 2021 without the state pension age change. The checked material does not fetch the state pension age of 2022 from the General Old Age Pensions Act (Algemene Ouderdomswet, AOW), so this boundary is not fully settled, see section 7. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, younger group

For 2022 the lower amount is € 7.587. It applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is more than ten years below the state pension age. The Ministry of Finance table describes this group as younger than 56 years and 7 months (56 jaar en 7 maanden) on 1 January 2022. The amount can be further limited by the 17% cap and by the unused annual allowance. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, older group

For 2022 the higher amount is € 14.978. It applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the state pension age. The Ministry of Finance table uses 56 years and 7 months (56 jaar en 7 maanden) or older on 1 January 2022. FISIN 2022 states this higher amount for a taxpayer born before 1 September 1965, see section 7. The amount can be further limited by the 17% cap and by the unused annual allowance. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Annuity types (lijfrentevormen) and old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente)

Three forms exist: annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), annuity account (lijfrenterekening) and annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht). For an old-age annuity (art. 3.125 lid 1 a) the first instalment (termijn) must be at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches state pension age plus five, and payments run until death. The FISIN example: state pension age in 2030 means a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. For an annuity account or annuity investment right the benefit (uitkering) must run at least 20 years, plus the number of years the benefit starts before the state pension year, so a start at 63 with state pension age 67 means 24 years. Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente, art. 3.125 lid 1 b) for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30. For an account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) it must run at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. An annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind) is deductible without a pension shortfall, per art. 3.124 lid 1 b. Sources: [art. 3.124 and 3.125, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

For 2022 the cap is € 22.735 per year. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c requires at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of state pension age and at the latest in the year of state pension age plus five, with a yearly amount up to the statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the benefits (uitkeringen) run at least 20 years. FISIN 2022 prints € 22.443 in its insurance (verzekering) row and € 22.735 in its account (rekening) row. The law text and the Ministry of Finance table give € 22.735, see section 7. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

The cap is € 63.288 per year. The bridge annuity is only for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. The benefits (uitkeringen) end at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching state pension age, or when a pension (pensioen) starts. The yearly maximum of € 63.288 appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

For 2022 the tier a maximum is € 480.686. Art. 3.129 allows a deduction of annuity premiums up to the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst, profit on cessation), capped at the tier maximum minus already built provisions. Those provisions are the value of pension rights charged to profit, the amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years and earlier art. 3.129 amounts. The elements that count "from 2023" are the old-age reserve (FOR) at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13, so they do not apply to 2022. Tier a applies when the age at cessation is at most five years below the state pension age, or the taxpayer is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity starting within six months, or the cessation happens by death. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

For 2022 the tier b maximum is € 240.352. Tier b applies when the age at cessation is at most fifteen years below the state pension age, or the annuity starts immediately. The same cap mechanics apply: deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at € 240.352 minus the already built provisions listed in the tier a subsection. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

For 2022 the tier c maximum is € 120.183, for the remaining cases. The same cap mechanics apply: deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at € 120.183 minus the already built provisions listed in the tier a subsection. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity

For 2022 the amount is € 4.607. Commutation of a small annuity in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount, set in art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d. Source: [art. 3.133, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

For 2022 the cap is € 43.012. Art. 3.133 lid 9 states that commutation is not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached the state pension age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap. If the alternative is higher, the test is the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment (termijn). Source: [art. 3.133, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### FOR addition (toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve)

For 2022 the addition (toevoeging) is 9,44% of the profit (winst), at most € 9.632, per art. 3.68. The addition is reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit and cannot exceed the growth of the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen). Access requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet state pension age at the start of the year, per art. 3.67. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. 2022 is the last year of an addition. From 2023 no addition is possible and an existing FOR stays under the old rules, per art. 10a.29 lid 1. In 2022 the addition also reduces the annual allowance. Sources: [art. 3.67, 3.68 and 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### FOR decrease (afname)

Art. 3.70 lid 1 provides the decrease (afname) of the FOR. The taxpayer may choose an amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or the decrease follows from the excess over the business assets at cessation, at state pension age on 1 January, or when the hours criterion fails in this year and the last year. FISIN 2022 repeats the example with € 3.000 against € 4.000. Sources: [art. 3.70, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022, winst en verliesrekening en balans](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### FOR to annuity conversion article

The FOR to annuity (lijfrente) conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022, up to the decrease under art. 3.70 lid 1 a. In the 2022 text art. 3.127 lid 8 is the order in council (AMvB) rule on pension accrual data, not the conversion right. The converted premium may count in the year itself when it is paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2), so before 1 July 2023 for 2022. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.128, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### AOV premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen)

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind for disability, illness or accident (periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer himself an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen). The premium is fully deductible and the article holds no allowance (ruimte) cap. FISIN states "volledig aftrekken" (deduct in full). Four categories are not deductible: premiums that the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and Work and Income (Capacity for Work) Act (WIA) premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Health Insurance Act (Zvw) premiums. For an entrepreneur (ondernemer) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an expenditure for other income provisions (uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen). Periodic benefits later are income from former employment (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as a prepayment (voorheffing). Art. 3.124 lid 1 b allows a deduction without a pension shortfall for an annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind). Sources: [art. 3.124, text 2022-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.124, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the rule of thumb that a premium that is not deductible means the payout is not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium

A mandatory occupational or industry-wide pension (beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). It is deductible as a business cost of the business, with a restriction. Source: [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Sectoral pension fund (sectoraal pensioenfonds) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen)

A self-employed person may join the pension fund of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible. The trial (proef) runs until 1 July 2028. Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimental provision (experimenteerbepaling) for self-employed persons' pension schemes inside the pension definitions. Sources: [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij), [art. 10a.26, text 2024-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Payment deadline for the FOR or the profit from business discontinuation conversion

Art. 3.130 lid 1 counts premiums in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend). Art. 3.130 lid 2 allows, on choice, a premium for the profit from business discontinuation conversion (art. 3.129), the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) to be counted in the year itself when it is paid within six months after the year end. For 2022 that means payment before 1 July 2023. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.130, text 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29, text 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Annuity start date (ingangsdatum) term

If an annuity stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, a change of terms, commutation or unblocking (deblokkering), the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due. An annuity that reaches its start date (ingangsdatum) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2022: a start date in 2021 means convert or start by 31 December 2022, a start date in 2022 means convert or start by 31 December 2023. From FISIN 2023 the term is described as the end date (einddatum). Extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). A refund of a deducted premium is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest, for an annuity only when it is undone within 30 days. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Survivor annuity term

For a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) the term is 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2022: death in 2021 means start by 31 December 2023. From FISIN 2023 the wording is "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren" (the year of death plus the 2 calendar years), with the same outcome. Extension on request is possible in special circumstances. Sources: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
