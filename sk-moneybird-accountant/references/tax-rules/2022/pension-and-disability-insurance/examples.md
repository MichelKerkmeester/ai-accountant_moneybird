## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All inputs in the calculated examples are invented and use round amounts. Every statutory amount used comes from section 2 or from the 2021 figures in section 5. The annual allowance (jaarruimte) examples assume a pension shortfall (pensioentekort), no net pension premiums (nettopensioenpremies) and a taxpayer who has not yet reached the state pension age (AOW age) at the start of 2022. Products are shown before rounding, and each rounding step says rounded or rounded down.

**Calculated example: annual allowance with a profit of € 60.000,00 (no pension accrual, no FOR addition).**

- Input: profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no other income components, no pension accrual and no FOR addition (dotatie).
- Step 1, cap at the income cap (aftoppingsgrens, art. 3.127 lid 3): min(€ 60.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 47.163,00 contribution base (premiegrondslag).
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, reductions: pension accrual € 0,00 and FOR addition € 0,00.
- Step 5, maximum check: € 6.272,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Annual allowance 2022: € 6.272,68.

**Calculated example: annual allowance with pension accrual (factor A).**

- Input: the same profit (winst) over 2021 of € 60.000,00, the same contribution base of € 47.163,00 and no FOR addition as above. The growth (aangroei) of the yearly pension entitlement is € 1.000,00.
- Reduction for pension accrual (factor A): 6,27 x € 1.000,00 = € 6.270,00, exact.
- Annual allowance before the reduction: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Annual allowance after the reduction: € 6.272,6790 - € 6.270,00 = € 2,68, rounded (exact € 2,6790).
- The maximum of € 13.570,00 does not bind.

**Calculated example: annual allowance with a FOR addition over 2021.**

- Input: the same profit over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no pension accrual and a FOR addition over 2021.
- Step 1, FOR addition over 2021: 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Step 2, cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2021 cap of € 9.395,00. This is the 2021 cap, because the annual allowance 2022 uses the FOR addition of the previous calendar year (2021), not the 2022 cap.
- Step 3, contribution base: the profit is taken before the FOR addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), so the base stays € 47.163,00 and 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, subtract the addition: € 6.272,6790 - € 5.664,00 = € 608,68, rounded (exact € 608,6790).
- Step 5, maximum check: € 608,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Annual allowance 2022: € 608,68.

**Calculated example: annual allowance at a high income (income cap).**

- Input: income components over 2021 of € 150.000,00, no pension accrual and no FOR addition.
- Step 1: min(€ 150.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 114.866,00.
- Step 2: € 114.866,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 102.029,00 contribution base.
- Step 3: 13,3% x € 102.029,00 = € 13.569,86, rounded (exact € 13.569,8570).
- Step 4, maximum check: € 13.569,86 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00, so the maximum does not bind.
- Annual allowance 2022: € 13.569,86.

**Calculated example: catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) cap and oldest-first use.**

- Input: contribution base of € 50.000,00, unused annual allowance 2016 of € 5.000,00 and unused annual allowance 2017 of € 3.000,00. The taxpayer's age at the start of 2022 is more than ten years below the state pension age, so the younger-group maximum of € 7.587,00 applies and not the older-group maximum of € 14.978,00. Both years lie in the seven-year look-back window of 2015 to and including 2021.
- Percentage limit: 17% x € 50.000,00 = € 8.500,00, exact.
- Younger-group maximum: min(€ 8.500,00, € 7.587,00) = € 7.587,00.
- Oldest unused year first, on request in the tax return (aangifte, art. 3.127 lid 2): € 5.000,00 from 2016 plus € 2.587,00 of the € 3.000,00 from 2017 = € 7.587,00.
- Catch-up allowance 2022: € 7.587,00.

**Calculated example: profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion.**

- Input: profit from business discontinuation of € 200.000,00 and already built provisions of € 80.000,00. The taxpayer is more than five years but at most fifteen years below the state pension age at the cessation date (staking date) and does not meet a tier a ground, so the tier b maximum of art. 3.129 applies.
- Tier b maximum: € 240.352,00.
- Cap after the provisions: € 240.352,00 - € 80.000,00 = € 160.352,00.
- Deductible premiums for the conversion: min(€ 200.000,00, € 160.352,00) = € 160.352,00.
- If a tier a ground applied instead, the tier a maximum of € 480.686,00 minus € 80.000,00 gives a cap of € 400.686,00, and the full profit from business discontinuation of € 200.000,00 would be deductible. The example therefore states the age range that places the taxpayer in tier b.
- A payment made before 1 July 2023 may on choice still count in 2022 (art. 3.130 lid 2).

**Calculated example: FOR addition (toevoeging) over 2022.**

- Input: profit (winst) over 2022 of € 60.000,00, no pension premiums charged to the profit, enough growth of the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), the hours criterion (urencriterium) is met and the taxpayer has not yet reached the state pension age at the start of 2022.
- 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2022 cap of € 9.632,00 (art. 3.68).
- Addition: € 5.664,00. 2022 is the last year in which a FOR addition is possible.
- Interaction: this addition does not reduce the annual allowance 2022. The annual allowance 2022 is reduced by the FOR addition over 2021, see the example above.

**Calculated example: AOV premium.**

- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with periodic benefits (uitkeringen): fully deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, so the deduction is € 2.400,00.
- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with a lump-sum payout: not deductible, and the later payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

**Calculated example: commutation (afkoop) for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid).**

- Input: the taxpayer meets the ministerial conditions for long-term disability and has not yet reached the state pension age, and receives a commutation of € 40.000,00 in 2022.
- 2022 cap: € 43.012,00.
- € 40.000,00 is below € 43.012,00, so the commutation is not a prohibited act under art. 3.133 lid 9 and counts as an annuity instalment (lijfrente termijn).

**Calculated example: temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente).**

- Assume the benefits (uitkeringen) run for at least five years and start in the allowed window, not before the year of state pension age and at the latest in the year of state pension age plus five. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment.
- Yearly benefit (uitkering) of € 20.000,00: below the 2022 cap of € 22.735,00, so the benefit stays within the cap of the temporary old-age annuity of art. 3.125 lid 1 c.
- Yearly benefit of € 25.000,00: above the cap. FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years.

**Official example: FISIN 2022 deadlines for the start date (ingangsdatum) and for survivor annuities (nabestaanden).**

- Start date in 2021: convert or start by 31 December 2022.
- Start date in 2022: convert or start by 31 December 2023.
- Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) after a death in 2021: start by 31 December 2023.
- Source: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

**Official example: FISIN 2022 FOR decrease (afname).**

- The taxpayer chooses a decrease (afname) of € 3.000,00, while € 4.000,00 is available.
- Source: [FISIN 2022, winst en verliesrekening en balans](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

**Official example: old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) start and duration.**

- AOW age in 2030: start at the latest on 31 December 2035 (art. 3.125 lid 1 a).
- Start at 63 with AOW age 67: the benefit (uitkering) starts 4 years before the AOW year, so an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht) must run 20 + 4 = 24 years.
- Source: [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
