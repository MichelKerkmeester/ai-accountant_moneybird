## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All inputs in the calculated examples are invented and use round amounts. Every statutory amount used comes from section 2 or from the 2021 figures in section 5. The jaarruimte examples assume a pensioentekort, no nettopensioenpremies and a taxpayer who has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2022. Products are shown before rounding, and each rounding step says rounded or rounded down.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with a profit of € 60.000,00 (no pension accrual, no FOR dotatie).**

- Input: winst uit onderneming over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no other income components, no pension accrual and no FOR dotatie.
- Step 1, cap at the aftoppingsgrens (art. 3.127 lid 3): min(€ 60.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 47.163,00 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, reductions: pension accrual € 0,00 and FOR dotatie € 0,00.
- Step 5, maximum check: € 6.272,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 6.272,68.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with pension accrual (factor A).**

- Input: the same winst over 2021 of € 60.000,00, the same premiegrondslag of € 47.163,00 and no FOR dotatie as above. The aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement is € 1.000,00.
- Reduction for pension accrual (factor A): 6,27 x € 1.000,00 = € 6.270,00, exact.
- Jaarruimte before the reduction: 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Jaarruimte after the reduction: € 6.272,6790 - € 6.270,00 = € 2,68, rounded (exact € 2,6790).
- The maximum of € 13.570,00 does not bind.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte with a FOR dotatie over 2021.**

- Input: the same winst over 2021 of € 60.000,00, no pension accrual and a FOR dotatie over 2021.
- Step 1, FOR dotatie over 2021: 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Step 2, cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2021 cap of € 9.395,00. This is the 2021 cap, because the jaarruimte 2022 uses the FOR dotatie of the previous calendar year (2021), not the 2022 cap.
- Step 3, premiegrondslag: the winst is taken before the FOR dotatie and before the ondernemersaftrek, so the base stays € 47.163,00 and 13,3% x € 47.163,00 = € 6.272,68, rounded (exact € 6.272,6790).
- Step 4, subtract the dotatie: € 6.272,6790 - € 5.664,00 = € 608,68, rounded (exact € 608,6790).
- Step 5, maximum check: € 608,68 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 608,68.

**Calculated example: jaarruimte at a high income (aftoppingsgrens).**

- Input: income components over 2021 of € 150.000,00, no pension accrual and no FOR dotatie.
- Step 1: min(€ 150.000,00, € 114.866,00) = € 114.866,00.
- Step 2: € 114.866,00 - € 12.837,00 = € 102.029,00 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3: 13,3% x € 102.029,00 = € 13.569,86, rounded (exact € 13.569,8570).
- Step 4, maximum check: € 13.569,86 is below the maximum of € 13.570,00, so the maximum does not bind.
- Jaarruimte 2022: € 13.569,86.

**Calculated example: reserveringsruimte cap and oldest-first use.**

- Input: premiegrondslag of € 50.000,00, unused jaarruimte 2016 of € 5.000,00 and unused jaarruimte 2017 of € 3.000,00. The taxpayer's age at the start of 2022 is more than ten years below the AOW age, so the younger-group maximum of € 7.587,00 applies and not the older-group maximum of € 14.978,00. Both years lie in the seven-year look-back window of 2015 tot en met 2021.
- Percentage limit: 17% x € 50.000,00 = € 8.500,00, exact.
- Younger-group maximum: min(€ 8.500,00, € 7.587,00) = € 7.587,00.
- Oldest unused year first, on request in the aangifte (art. 3.127 lid 2): € 5.000,00 from 2016 plus € 2.587,00 of the € 3.000,00 from 2017 = € 7.587,00.
- Reserveringsruimte 2022: € 7.587,00.

**Calculated example: stakingswinst conversion.**

- Input: stakingswinst of € 200.000,00 and already built provisions of € 80.000,00. The taxpayer is more than five years but at most fifteen years below the AOW age at the staking date and does not meet a tier a ground, so the tier b maximum of art. 3.129 applies.
- Tier b maximum: € 240.352,00.
- Cap after the provisions: € 240.352,00 - € 80.000,00 = € 160.352,00.
- Deductible premiums for the conversion: min(€ 200.000,00, € 160.352,00) = € 160.352,00.
- If a tier a ground applied instead, the tier a maximum of € 480.686,00 minus € 80.000,00 gives a cap of € 400.686,00, and the full stakingswinst of € 200.000,00 would be deductible. The example therefore states the age range that places the taxpayer in tier b.
- A payment made before 1 July 2023 may on choice still count in 2022 (art. 3.130 lid 2).

**Calculated example: FOR toevoeging over 2022.**

- Input: winst over 2022 of € 60.000,00, no pension premiums charged to the winst, enough growth of the ondernemingsvermogen, the urencriterium is met and the taxpayer has not yet reached the AOW age at the start of 2022.
- 9,44% x € 60.000,00 = € 5.664,00, exact.
- Cap check: € 5.664,00 is below the 2022 cap of € 9.632,00 (art. 3.68).
- Toevoeging: € 5.664,00. 2022 is the last year in which a FOR toevoeging is possible.
- Interaction: this toevoeging does not reduce the jaarruimte 2022. The jaarruimte 2022 is reduced by the FOR dotatie over 2021, see the example above.

**Calculated example: AOV premium.**

- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with periodic uitkeringen: fully deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen under art. 3.124 lid 1 c, so the deduction is € 2.400,00.
- Premium of € 2.400,00 for a policy with a lump-sum payout: not deductible, and the later payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

**Calculated example: afkoop bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid.**

- Input: the taxpayer meets the ministerial conditions for langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid and has not yet reached the AOW age, and receives an afkoop of € 40.000,00 in 2022.
- 2022 cap: € 43.012,00.
- € 40.000,00 is below € 43.012,00, so the afkoop is not a prohibited act under art. 3.133 lid 9 and counts as a lijfrente termijn.

**Calculated example: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente.**

- Assume the uitkeringen run for at least five years and start in the allowed window, not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment.
- Yearly uitkering of € 20.000,00: below the 2022 cap of € 22.735,00, so the uitkering stays within the cap of the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente of art. 3.125 lid 1 c.
- Yearly uitkering of € 25.000,00: above the cap. FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years.

**Official example: FISIN 2022 deadlines for the ingangsdatum and for nabestaanden.**

- Ingangsdatum in 2021: convert or start by 31 December 2022.
- Ingangsdatum in 2022: convert or start by 31 December 2023.
- Nabestaandenlijfrente after a death in 2021: start by 31 December 2023.
- Source: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

**Official example: FISIN 2022 FOR afname.**

- The taxpayer chooses an afname of € 3.000,00, while € 4.000,00 is available.
- Source: [FISIN 2022, winst en verliesrekening en balans](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

**Official example: oudedagslijfrente start and duration.**

- AOW age in 2030: start at the latest on 31 December 2035 (art. 3.125 lid 1 a).
- Start at 63 with AOW age 67: the uitkering starts 4 years before the AOW year, so a lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht must run 20 + 4 = 24 years.
- Source: [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
