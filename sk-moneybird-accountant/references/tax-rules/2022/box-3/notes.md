## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen): € 50.000 per person and € 100.000 with a fiscal partner in 2021, to € 50.650 and € 101.300 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Return class I (rendementsklasse I): 0,03% in 2021 to -0,01% in 2022. Return class II: 5,69% in 2021 to 5,53% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Band 1: the upper limit moves from € 50.000 in 2021 (page: up to € 50.001) to € 50.650 in 2022 (page: up to € 50.651), and the average drops from 1,898% to 1,818% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Band 2: the limits move from € 50.000 and € 950.000 in 2021 (page: € 50.001 to € 950.001) to € 50.650 and € 962.350 in 2022 (page: € 50.651 to € 962.351), and the average drops from 4,501% to 4,366% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Band 3: the threshold moves from € 950.000 to € 962.350, and the percentage drops from 5,69% to 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) category percentages: 0,01% for bank balances (banktegoeden), 5,69% for other assets (overige bezittingen) and 2,46% for debts (schulden) in 2021, to 0,00%, 5,53% and 2,28% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen): € 60.429 and € 120.858 in 2021, to € 61.215 and € 122.430 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_).
- Box 3 rate (tarief): 31% in 2021 and 31% in 2022, so unchanged. The 2020 rate was 30% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Debt threshold (schuldendrempel): € 3.200 and € 6.400 in 2021 and in 2022, so unchanged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_).
- Green investment credit (korting groene beleggingen): 0,7% in 2021 and 0,7% in 2022, so unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- New in 2022: the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, issued on 27 December 2022 and in force on 28 December 2022, retroactive to 1 January 2017. It governs the recalculation for the assessments for 2021 and 2022 and for the 2017 to 2020 assessments that were not irrevocable on 24 December 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- The Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) of 24 December 2021 (ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) falls at the end of 2021 and is the reason for the 2022 law. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3).
- Most favourable method: for 2021 and 2022 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes the old method and the category method and applies the more favourable one, so no change between 2021 and 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).
- Restoration of rights scope: all IB/PVV assessments for 2021 and all IB/PVV assessments for 2022, so no change (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Actual-return reporting: the separate online form may be filed without a letter in 2021 to 2024, so 2022 follows the same route as 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The 2022 box 3 return and the recalculation need the values at 1 January of the bank balances (banktegoeden), the other assets (overige bezittingen) and the debts (schulden), because art. 3 of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) uses the amounts at 1 January (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Only the debts above the debt threshold (schuldendrempel) of € 3.200 count, or € 6.400 with a whole-year partner, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes the threshold (drempel) in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel).
- Whole-year partners: tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) € 101.300, threshold € 6.400 and the partners divide the joint base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen). A joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s), and whole-year partners use the joint figures in the recalculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).
- Green investments: record the balance on 1 January and the exemption applied. The credit (korting) of 0,7% of the exempt amount is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen).
- If the actual return was lower than the fictitious benefit (voordeel), the online form in the My Tax and Customs Administration portal (Mijn Belastingdienst) can be used for 2022, and for 2021 to 2024 the form may always be filed without a letter. Paper forms run via 0800 - 023 01 07 and 0800 - 0543 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen).
- The form and the return use the income and value changes of the year, netted, with a negative total at € 0 and without the tax-free allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement).
- From 2025 the actual-return report is made in the income tax return itself (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).
- The box 3 benefit is part of the IB/PVV return for 2022 and is taxed at 31%, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies the most favourable method to the final assessment (definitieve aanslag) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- The checked 2022 sources do not cover BV-specific box 3 rules or the treatment of a sole trader's business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) in box 3.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The checked 2022 figures are all enacted and Verified, and the year's list of values records no open points. The points below still matter for 2022 work.

- UNVERIFIED. The claim that the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) itself ordered restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) on the basis of the actual return. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) describes the Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM" and says the legislator chose a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories instead (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html). The Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11, so the ruling text could not be checked. What would settle it: the text of ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963 on hogeraad.nl.
- Conflict, settled by the statute text. The entry date of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3). Art. 7 of Stb. 2022, 533 gives the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The Government of the Netherlands timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking" (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The statute governs the legal date. The Check tag for this item is Corrected.
- Conflict, settled by art. 4 and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page. The 2022 category percentages. The table in Stb. 2022, 533 as published prints 0,01% for column I and 2,46% for column III (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 4 orders these 2022 percentages to be replaced within two months after 2022 by ministerial regulation on DNB data for January to November 2022. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration percentages page gives the operative 2022 values: 0,00% for bank balances, 5,53% for other assets and 2,28% for debts (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend). The replacement regulation itself is not in the checked sources.
- Wording difference for the debt component. Art. 3 as given in the checked sources describes the return as column I x bank balances plus column II x other assets minus column III x debts (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The threshold page says only the part of the total debts above the threshold is deductible in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The two descriptions can give different outcomes when the debts are below or just above the threshold. What would settle it: the full text of art. 3 together with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration 2022 calculation page for the category method, which are not both in the checked sources.
