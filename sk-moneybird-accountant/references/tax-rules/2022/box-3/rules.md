## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)

For 2022 the tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is € 50.650 for one person and € 101.300 with a fiscal partner. The statute valid on 1 January 2022 and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page both give these amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen).

The return base (rendementsgrondslag) minus the tax-free allowance gives the base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen), the amount that the bands or the category calculation use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The first band ends at € 50.650, the same amount as the single-person tax-free allowance for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the partners' share of the joint base for savings and investments under art. 2.17 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Whole-year partners use the joint figures in the recalculation (art. 3 lid 4 of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

For 2022 the debt threshold is € 3.200 for one person and € 6.400 with a whole-year partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

Only the part of the total debts above the threshold counts in box 3, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes this in the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The statute places the threshold (drempel) in art. 5.3 lid 3, written as "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...", and the amount is indexed under art. 10.1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

The debts above the threshold are subtracted together with the rest of the assets in the return base, and they enter the return with the debt percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). With a whole-year partner the threshold doubles, and a joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel).

### Box 3 rate (tarief)

The box 3 rate for 2022 is 31% (art. 2.13, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The rate applies to the return from savings and investments (voordeel uit sparen en beleggen) under both methods (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rate is the same as in 2021, while 2020 used 30% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Reference date (peildatum)

The values in the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act calculation are those at 1 January, the reference date (peildatum). Art. 3 defines the return (rendement) with the bank balances (banktegoeden), the other assets (overige bezittingen) and the debts (schulden) at 1 January (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The exemption for green investments also never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Return class percentages (rendementsklasse)

For 2022 the return classes in art. 5.2 lid 1 are return class I (klasse I) at -0,01% and return class II (klasse II) at 5,53% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). Return class I is the savings class and return class II is the investments class. The old method mixes the two classes per band, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration prints the band outcomes as averages of 1,818%, 4,366% and 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Band 1

The first band covers the part of the base for savings and investments up to € 50.650. The statute writes the upper limit as "niet meer dan € 50.650" and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes "tot € 50.651". Both describe the same band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). In this band the fictitious return is 67% return class I and 33% return class II, printed as an average of 1,818% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The parts of the base in the three bands are each multiplied by their band percentage, and the parts are added to give the old-method benefit (voordeel).

### Band 2

The second band runs from € 50.650 to € 962.350 in the statute. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes € 50.651 to € 962.351 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The mix is 21% return class I and 79% return class II, an average of 4,366% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Band 3

Above € 962.350 the whole amount is return class II, so 0% return class I and 100% return class II, and the percentage is 5,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen).

### Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3), issue and entry

The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, Kamerstuk 36 203, issued on 27 December 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 7 puts the law in force on the day after the issue date of the Bulletin of Acts and Decrees (Staatsblad), with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017, so it is in force on 28 December 2022. The Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). Section 7 records this conflict.

The law reacts to the Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest), Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) to the bill describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM", because the flat-rate system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Flat rates are allowed, but they must approach reality as closely as possible (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The MvT says the legislator did not choose a levy on the actual return for the restoration of rights, but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html).

### Restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) category percentages 2022

For 2022 the operative category percentages for the recalculation are 0,00% for bank balances, 5,53% for other assets and 2,28% for debts (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend).

The law as published printed 0,01% in column I and 2,46% in column III. Art. 4 orders the 2022 percentages of columns I and III to be replaced within two months after 2022 by ministerial regulation on DNB data for January to November 2022. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration percentages page gives the replaced, operative values (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend). Section 7 records this conflict.

The calculation under art. 3 runs through these amounts (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). First the return: column I x the bank balances plus column II x the other assets minus column III x the debts at 1 January. Then the return base: the assets minus the debts above the threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel). The effective rate of return (rendementspercentage) is the return divided by the return base. The benefit is that percentage times the base for savings and investments, which is the return base after the tax-free allowance (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen). Art. 2 sets the benefit at the outcome of art. 3, not below nil. Art. 3 lid 4 makes whole-year partners use the joint figures (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 3 is given in the checked sources with column III x debts, while only the debts above the threshold count in box 3. Section 7 records this wording difference.

### Restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) scope 2022

Art. 1 lid 2 of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act makes the law apply to IB/PVV assessments (income tax and national insurance contributions assessments) for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 that were not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). For 2022 the law applies to all IB/PVV assessments for 2022, without the condition that the assessment was not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Art. 1 lid 3 adds that the law applies only where it leads to a lower benefit (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html).

Art. 5 lets the extra personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) follow the partners' chosen division of specific care costs or gifts, with an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) request for another split. Art. 6 recomputes the double-taxation relief (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). Mass objectors get restoration of rights for 2017 to 2020, as the explanatory memorandum states (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html).

### Most favourable method

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the box 3 income under the old band method and under the category method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rule also holds when the taxpayer already had restoration of rights (rechtsherstel) for the period 2017 to 2022: "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." That means: even if you had restoration of rights for the period 2017 to 2022, we apply the most favourable method (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen). The rate on either outcome is 31% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

Green investments are not counted as assets up to € 61.215 in 2022, or € 122.430 with a whole-year partner (art. 5.13, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_). The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The 2023 text of art. 5.13 lid 2 applies the exemption first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The checked sources do not state that ordering rule for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Green investment credit (korting groene beleggingen)

Art. 8.19 lid 2 gives a credit for green investments of 0,7% of the exempt amount on the reference date (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The rate is 0,7% for 2020 to 2024, so also for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the credit automatically, and it is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen).

### Actual-return reporting 2022

For 2022 the actual return (werkelijk rendement) can be reported on the online form "Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven" in the My Tax and Customs Administration portal (Mijn Belastingdienst) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement). For 2021 to 2024 the form may always be filed without a letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call), and several forms via the Tax Administration helpline (BelastingTelefoon) 0800 - 0543 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement). The pages state no deadline (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen). From 2025 the report goes in the income tax return itself (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).

The actual return is income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No tax-free allowance is applied: "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen." That means the Supreme Court of the Netherlands has ruled that the tax-free allowance may not be taken into account there. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the official property valuation (WOZ value) and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte).

The Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195, in force on 19 July 2025 by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html). Art. IV puts art. 6a in the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act with the same form and six-week rule for further documents, working back to 1 January 2017, so it also covers the 2022 restoration of rights (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html). The Supreme Court of the Netherlands 6 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705) held that the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act and the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3) still conflict with the EVRM when the flat-rate return is higher than the actual return (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3).
