## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Income tax return deadline: the 2021 return was due before 1 May 2022, the 2022 return before 1 May 2023 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230305/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)).
- Income tax return with deferral (uitstel): from before 1 September 2022 for the 2021 return to before 1 September 2023 for the 2022 return ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Final assessment (definitieve aanslag): from 31 December 2024 for 2021, or 30 April 2025 with 4 months deferral, to 31 December 2025 for 2022, or 30 April 2026 with 4 months deferral ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Tax interest on the tax year: for 2021 the interest ran from 1 July 2022 at 4% with 6% for days from 1 July 2023, for 2022 it runs from 1 July 2023 at 6% with 7,5% for days in 2024 ([2021 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [2022 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The rate for 2021 and 2022 itself is 4% in both years.
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2021 until 31 December 2026, tax year 2022 until 31 December 2027 ([2021 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen), [2022 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).
- VAT dates: the tables roll forward by one year, for example Q1 2021 30 April 2021 to Q1 2022 30 April 2022, and the yearly return from 31 March 2022 for 2021 to 31 March 2023 for 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)). The monthly deadline 31 January 2022 for the December 2021 period appears in both years' tables.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens): unchanged at € 48 and € 15 in the current higher-than wording in both 2021 and 2022, with € 49 and € 16 in the older wording for both years ([2022 and 2021 table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete): € 68 in both 2021 and 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210331/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220224/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). The 2026 amount of € 82 does not apply to 2022.
- Income tax penalties (boetes): unchanged at default fine (verzuimboete) € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757 and payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514 in both 2021 and 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210423/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
- Payment terms and record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht): unchanged from 2021, the Collection of State Taxes Act 1990 (Invorderingswet 1990) art. 9 and AWR art. 52 lid 4 texts being the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 ([Invorderingswet](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01), [AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- The rule for the filing period, deferral of 4 months, objection (bezwaar), additional assessment (navordering) and registration at the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) did not change between 2021 and 2022 in the checked material.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the records (administratie) for at least 7 years under AWR art. 52 lid 4, with 10 years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data, and start the count when an item (gegeven) is no longer current ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).
- The record-retention obligation covers a sole trader with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) under AWR art. 52 lid 2 ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- Record every VAT period (tijdvak) with its return and payment on the 2022 date, because both must be received by that date ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).
- Correct a 2022 VAT quarter with a supplementary return (suppletie) before 1 April 2023 to avoid tax interest over the period from 1 January 2023 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
- Book the paid or received provisional assessment amounts and settle them in the 2022 income tax return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)).
- Change the provisional assessment when the expected profit changes, using the profits from business activities (Winst uit onderneming) screen ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)).
- Send the 2022 income tax return before 1 May 2023, or request deferral before the filing date, and remember that deferral usually means tax interest ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)).
- A voluntary correction of the return within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
- Objection (bezwaar) against an assessment goes within 6 weeks, and against a provisional income tax assessment only a change is possible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)).
- Keep the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) on paper when other data carriers (gegevensdragers) are converted to another form ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- Register the business in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) within the two-week window around the start, then the Chamber of Commerce data go to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration and no separate registration is needed ([Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The tax year 2022 material states no open points of its own for 2022 beyond the cross-year items. The cross-year points are these.

- Conflict, assessment threshold and refund threshold 2022. The current page prints € 48 and € 15 and words the test as higher than. The captures up to 2023 printed € 49 and € 16 and worded the test as equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan). Both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is a reading of the two captures rather than a sentence on either page. A Belastingdienst statement on the rounding, or a page that prints both forms, would settle it.
- Conflict, the deferral channel. The form page says deferral can also be requested online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May, giving deferral to 1 September, and only for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). The deferral page says online is not possible. Both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte. Read in full, the deferral page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form by post is the route. A single Belastingdienst page stating both routes would settle it.
- Conflict, the Chamber of Commerce term for the VAT number. The Chamber of Commerce page carries two weeks for the VAT number and ten working days (werkdagen), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states registration takes at most 10 working days. Both URLs: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/. Ten working days equals two calendar weeks, so the two wordings agree. A single wording on both pages would settle it.
- Not in force for VAT, the interest stop (rentestop). The interest stop extension to requested supplementary assessments, ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). A later Dutch Tax and Customs Administration statement that the interest stop is feasible for VAT would settle it. This does not change the 2022 VAT figures.
- No date for the collective decisions on the tax interest mass objection. The mass-objection page is updated on 25 February 2026 and gives no date for the collective decisions themselves ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage)). The collective decisions themselves would settle it. The 25 February 2026 date is the update date of the page, not the decision date.
- The AWR articles used here were checked in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions, which the material states carry the same text. No 1 January 2022 version URL is in the checked material, so the article text for 2022 rests on that same-text statement.
