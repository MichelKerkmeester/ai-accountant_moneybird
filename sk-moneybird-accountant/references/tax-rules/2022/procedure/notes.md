## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- IB return deadline: the 2021 return was due before 1 May 2022, the 2022 return before 1 May 2023 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230305/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)).
- IB return with uitstel: from before 1 September 2022 for the 2021 return to before 1 September 2023 for the 2022 return ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Definitive aanslag: from 31 December 2024 for 2021, or 30 April 2025 with 4 months uitstel, to 31 December 2025 for 2022, or 30 April 2026 with 4 months uitstel ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Belastingrente on the tax year: for 2021 the rente ran from 1 July 2022 at 4% with 6% for days from 1 July 2023, for 2022 it runs from 1 July 2023 at 6% with 7,5% for days in 2024 ([2021 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [2022 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The rate for 2021 and 2022 itself is 4% in both years.
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2021 until 31 December 2026, tax year 2022 until 31 December 2027 ([2021 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen), [2022 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).
- Btw dates: the tables roll forward by one year, for example Q1 2021 30 April 2021 to Q1 2022 30 April 2022, and the yearly return from 31 March 2022 for 2021 to 31 March 2023 for 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)). The monthly deadline 31 January 2022 for the December 2021 period appears in both years' tables.
- Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens: unchanged at € 48 and € 15 in the current higher-than wording in both 2021 and 2022, with € 49 and € 16 in the older wording for both years ([2022 and 2021 table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Btw aangifteverzuimboete: € 68 in both 2021 and 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210331/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220224/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). The 2026 amount of € 82 does not apply to 2022.
- IB boetes: unchanged at verzuimboete € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757 and betaalverzuimboete up to € 5.514 in both 2021 and 2022 ([2021 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210423/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [2022 archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
- Payment terms and bewaarplicht: unchanged from 2021, the Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 and AWR art. 52 lid 4 texts being the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 ([Invorderingswet](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01), [AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- The rule for the filing period, uitstel of 4 months, bezwaar, navordering and registration at KVK did not change between 2021 and 2022 in the checked material.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the administratie for at least 7 years under AWR art. 52 lid 4, with 10 years for onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data, and start the count when a gegeven is no longer actual ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).
- The bewaarplicht covers a sole trader with winst uit onderneming under AWR art. 52 lid 2 ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- Record every btw tijdvak with its return and payment on the 2022 date, because both must be received by that date ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).
- Correct a 2022 btw quarter with a suppletie before 1 April 2023 to avoid belastingrente over the period from 1 January 2023 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
- Book the paid or received voorlopige aanslag amounts and settle them in the 2022 income tax return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)).
- Change the voorlopige aanslag when the expected profit changes, using the Winst uit onderneming screen ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)).
- Send the 2022 income tax return before 1 May 2023, or request uitstel before the filing date, and remember that uitstel usually means belastingrente ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)).
- A voluntary correction of the return within 2 years generally gives no boete ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
- Bezwaar against an aanslag goes within 6 weeks, and against a voorlopige aanslag IB only a change is possible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)).
- Keep the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten on paper when other gegevensdragers are converted to another form ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- Register the business in the Handelsregister within the two-week window around the start, then the KVK data go to the Belastingdienst and no separate registration is needed ([Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The tax year 2022 material states no open points of its own for 2022 beyond the cross-year items. The cross-year points are these.

- Conflict, aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2022. The current page prints € 48 and € 15 and words the test as higher than. The captures up to 2023 printed € 49 and € 16 and worded the test as gelijk aan of meer dan (equal to or more than). Both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is a reading of the two captures rather than a sentence on either page. A Belastingdienst statement on the rounding, or a page that prints both forms, would settle it.
- Conflict, the uitstel channel. The form page says uitstel can also be requested online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May, giving uitstel to 1 September, and only for someone who received an aangiftebrief. The uitstel page says online is not possible. Both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte. Read in full, the uitstel page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form by post is the route. A single Belastingdienst page stating both routes would settle it.
- Conflict, the KVK term for the btw number. The KVK page carries two weeks for the btw-number and ten werkdagen, and the Belastingdienst states registration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Both URLs: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/. Ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks, so the two wordings agree. A single wording on both pages would settle it.
- Not in force for btw, the rentestop. The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen, ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). A later Belastingdienst statement that the rentestop is executable for btw would settle it. This does not change the 2022 btw figures.
- No date for the collective decisions on the belastingrente mass objection. The mass-objection page is updated on 25 February 2026 and gives no date for the collective decisions themselves ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage)). The collective decisions themselves would settle it. The 25 February 2026 date is the update date of the page, not the decision date.
- The AWR articles used here were checked in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions, which the material states carry the same text. No 1 January 2022 version URL is in the checked material, so the article text for 2022 rests on that same-text statement.
