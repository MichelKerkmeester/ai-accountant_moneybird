## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Income tax (IB) return 2022 filing deadline

The 2022 income tax return had to arrive before 1 May 2023 ([archived Belastingdienst page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230305/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). The general rule is that the return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief, the letter that invites the return), often 1 May ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). The archived page for the 2022 return also records that filing before 1 April gave a message before 1 July ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230305/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). AWR art. 9 lid 1 provides that for assessment taxes (aanslagbelastingen, taxes set by assessment) the return is filed within a term set by the tax inspector (inspecteur) of at least one month after the invitation, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Income tax filing period 2022 opens

The filing period opens on 1 March, so the 2022 return could be filed from 1 March 2023 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). The deadline itself is the date in the filing invitation letter, for the 2022 return before 1 May 2023 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230305/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)).

### Income tax return 2022 with deferral (uitstel)

With a granted deferral the 2022 return was due before 1 September 2023 ([archived definitive aanslag page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Deferral gives 4 months extra on top of the normal filing date ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The deferral shifts the deadline, not the start of tax interest (belastingrente).

### Deferral request for the income tax return

Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September, and deferral usually means tax interest ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and the fiscal partner must request separately ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). So the interaction with tax interest is direct: using the extra 4 months keeps the interest period running from 1 July after the tax year.

### Deferral channel for the income tax return

Deferral can be requested online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May for someone who received a filing invitation letter, giving deferral to 1 September ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The deferral page states that online is not possible, and read in full that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Both are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text, so the working reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, and the paper form otherwise.

### Filing invitation letter and AWR art. 9 filing term

The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter, often 1 May ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). AWR art. 9 lid 1: for assessment taxes the return is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the invitation. AWR art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions. The text is the same in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Filing without an invitation for tax year 2022

Without a filing invitation letter a return can still be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). For tax year 2022 that ran until 31 December 2027. This route is separate from the final assessment period (definitieve aanslagtermijn) and from the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn).

### Final assessment 2022 latest

The official example for 2022: a return filed before 1 May 2023 gives the final assessment (definitieve aanslag) at the latest 31 December 2025. With 4 months deferral the return is due before 1 September 2023 and the assessment at the latest 30 April 2026 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). The same source notes that a provisional assessment does not stop this term.

### Final assessment period

AWR art. 11 lid 3: the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Assessment threshold and refund threshold 2022

Tax is only due if the amount is higher than the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For 2022 the current table prints € 48 and € 15. Under the older wording, used on the page up to the 2023 captures, the amounts were € 49 and € 16 with equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is a reading of the two captures and not a sentence on the page itself. The same page states that monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment before year end count toward the threshold, that a paid provisional assessment does not count, and that a refund received after filing does not count.

### Income tax penalties 2022

For 2022 the default fine (verzuimboete, failure-to-file penalty) for income tax was € 385, up to a maximum of € 5.514 ([archived Belastingdienst page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). Not requesting an invitation to file a return could lead to a penalty of € 2.757, and the payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) was up to € 5.514 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### Income tax penalty route and penalty for gross negligence or intent

After the income tax deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen) to respond. Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances (toeslagen) and the provisional assessment, and no return at all gives an ex officio assessment (ambtshalve aanslag, an assessment made without a return) plus a default fine ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag). The rates are 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), 75% and 150% for box 3 and 100% and 300% for aggravated cases ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). No penalty applies when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. For income tax and corporate income tax the request for a return must be made within 6 months after the tax debt arose, with 2 weeks leniency (coulance) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### Provisional assessment 2022

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return, and changing it is not mandatory ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Reasons to change it include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and discontinuation of the business (stakingswinst, the profit on ending the business) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)).

### Changing the expected profit

Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the profits from business activities (Winst uit onderneming) screen ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Amounts paid or received are settled in the income tax return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). So a change does not replace the return, it only changes the pre-payments or pre-refunds.

### Deadline to request or change the provisional assessment

A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)). With a filing invitation letter the option runs until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after filing ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)).

### Payment terms for assessments

Collection of State Taxes Act 1990 (Invorderingswet 1990) art. 9 gives the payment terms, and the text is the same on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([Invorderingswet 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01), [Invorderingswet 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01)). An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) is payable 1 month after, and a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. A provisional income tax assessment dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, the first one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date ([Invorderingswet 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01)).

### Tax interest for income tax and VAT 2022

The tax interest (belastingrente) for income tax and VAT was 4% for the whole of 2022 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). This sits inside the period from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023 in which the rate for all taxes except corporate income tax and allowances was 4%.

### Tax interest on tax year 2022 income tax

Tax interest on the tax year 2022 income tax runs from 1 July 2023 at 6% ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). For days in 2024 the rate is 7,5%, which follows from the rate table. The interest period starts on 1 July after the tax year even when deferral was granted.

### Tax interest rate table and rate-setting method

The rate table for all taxes except corporate income tax and allowances: 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 (for income tax the reduction started 1 July 2020), 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. For corporate income tax, withholding tax (bronbelasting), minimum tax and solidarity contribution the addition is 5,5 points, minimum 5,5% ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The 2022 rate of 4% was not set under this method, which only applies from 1 January 2024.

### Corporate income tax interest over 2022 and the mass objection

After the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the corporate income tax table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The mass objection (massaal bezwaar) on the rate was granted for corporate income tax and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT and the other taxes ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage)). This matters for a BV with a tax interest charge over 2022, not for the sole trader's income tax or VAT.

### Tax interest on income tax

Tax interest on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral, and ends 6 weeks after the assessment date. When the assessment follows the return unchanged it ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. There is no interest when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).

### Tax interest day count

The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, and the result is rounded down to whole euros ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).

### Tax interest on VAT

Tax interest on VAT is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). For late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments, where the period ends at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### VAT tax interest on 2022 quarters

The official example: quarterly returns 2022 with an error found later. A supplementary return (suppletie) before 1 April 2023 gives no tax interest. One after 1 April 2023 gives interest from 1 January 2023 to 14 days after the supplementary assessment date ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). This makes the 3-month rule concrete for the 2022 quarters.

### VAT filing and payment period

AWR art. 10 lid 2: a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1: the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables put return and payment together on one date.

### VAT monthly dates 2022

For a monthly return the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives the last day of the month after the period: December 2021 31 January 2022, January 2022 28 February 2022, February 2022 31 March 2022, March 2022 30 April 2022, April 2022 31 May 2022, May 2022 30 June 2022, June 2022 31 July 2022, July 2022 31 August 2022, August 2022 30 September 2022, September 2022 31 October 2022, October 2022 30 November 2022, November 2022 31 December 2022, December 2022 31 January 2023 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)). Deferral is not available on request for these returns in the normal course of business.

### VAT quarterly dates 2022

For a quarterly return the dates are Q4 2021 31 January 2022, Q1 2022 30 April 2022, Q2 2022 31 July 2022, Q3 2022 31 October 2022 and Q4 2022 31 January 2023 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### VAT yearly return 2022

A yearly return for 2022 was due by 31 March 2023 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Software submission and receipt of the VAT return

Software can submit the return from the 24th of the month before the period ends ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). The return and the payment must both be received by the date in the table, so filing alone is not enough ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).

### Deferral for a VAT return

Deferral for a VAT return is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties, a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit)) and is requested in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and a penalty remain possible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)). The normal route for a late or wrong return is a supplementary return within the 3-month window described above.

### VAT filing default penalty 2022

For 2022 there were 7 calendar days leniency (kalenderdagen coulance, a 7 calendar day grace period) after the deadline, and then a penalty of € 68 ([archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220224/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). The leniency is part of the enforcement practice, not of the statutory payment term.

### Additional assessment on a new fact and the error route

AWR art. 16 lid 1 allows an additional assessment (navordering) on a new fact, but not if the fact was known or reasonably knowable to the inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2 also allows it when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (voorlopige teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (voorlopige verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably knowable (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), and always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The 30% test is a lower bound, so a smaller shortfall can still be an evident error on the facts.

### Additional assessment period

The additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) is 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral. For an error-based additional assessment it is only 2 years after the assessment or decision. For income or assets held or arising abroad the term is 12 years. If in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by 6 months ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). The term runs alongside the final assessment period and the 5-year filing-without-invitation rule.

### Additional assessment official example

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, additional assessment possible until 31 May 2022 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). The example shows that the 5 years are counted from 1 January after the tax year and then extended by the deferral duration.

### Voluntary income tax correction

A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). This is the practical route when a filed 2022 return turns out to be wrong.

### Objection period, form, motivation and acknowledgement

An objection (bezwaar) must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if asked for in the objection. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. No objection is possible against a provisional income tax assessment, only a change ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). So the provisional assessment route is the change described above, and the objection route is for the final or other assessments.

### Request for an (ex officio) reduction

After the objection period a request for an (ex officio) reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) is possible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)). It can be made online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The material gives assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request goes by letter.

### Record-retention obligation (AWR art. 52)

AWR art. 52 lid 4: persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier, except for the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The text is the same in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Retention periods and basic records

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table: basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records are the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). The 7-year AWR term is the floor, the longer terms apply where the table says so.

### Registration in the Commercial Register

Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1: the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business ([Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). Other required statements are made within one week after the fact ([Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). The window applies per business, so a second business activity can trigger a new statement.

### Chamber of Commerce number, identity check and appointments

The Chamber of Commerce issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately when registering within that window ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). This allows the identity check to be done before the business starts.

### Registration with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration

The Chamber of Commerce passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other forms get a legal entity identification number (RSIN), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Where Chamber of Commerce registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)).
