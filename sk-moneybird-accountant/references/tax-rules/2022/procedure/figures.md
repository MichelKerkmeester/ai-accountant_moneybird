## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2022 for a sole trader and for a private limited company (BV) owner: the income tax filing deadline and deferral (uitstel), the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration duties. The dates that matter most for the 2022 income tax return are the filing deadline before 1 May 2023 and the deferral date before 1 September 2023, while the VAT (btw) deadlines for 2022 periods run to 31 January 2023 for the last period. The tax interest for 2022 is 4% all year, and the final assessment for 2022 could be set until 31 December 2025, or 30 April 2026 with deferral. The tax year 2022 items used in this file are enacted law, and the 2022 check material records no open points beyond the cross-year items.
