## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1: KIA on a single investment (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys one machine in 2022 for € 60.000,00 and takes it into use in 2022. The machine is the only investment of the year, so the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) is € 60.000,00. That total is above € 59.939,00 and not above € 110.998,00, so the third band of the KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2) applies.

- Step 1: total investment = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2: € 60.000,00 is above € 59.939,00 and not above € 110.998,00.
- Step 3: KIA = the fixed amount of the band = € 16.784,00.

Four variations on the same facts:

- Total investment € 2.000,00: the amount is in the first band, so KIA = € 0,00.
- Total investment € 40.000,00: the amount is above € 2.400,00 and up to € 59.939,00, so KIA = 28% × € 40.000,00 = € 11.200,00.
- Total investment € 200.000,00: the amount is above € 110.998,00 and up to € 332.994,00, so KIA = € 16.784,00 minus 7,56% × (€ 200.000,00 minus € 110.998,00) = € 16.784,00 minus 7,56% × € 89.002,00 = € 16.784,00 minus € 6.728,55 (rounded) = € 10.055,45, rounded down to € 10.055,00.
- Total investment € 350.000,00: the amount is above € 332.994,00, so KIA = € 0,00.

The per-asset minimum of € 450,00 (art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b) works as follows. If the € 60.000,00 machine is bought together with one small tool of € 400,00, the tool is below € 450,00 and does not count in the KIA base. The base stays € 60.000,00 and the KIA stays € 16.784,00.

### Example 2: KIA in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) (Calculated example)

A general partnership (vof) has two partners (vennoten) and an equal profit split (winstverdeling). The partnership invests € 100.000,00 in business assets in 2022. Neither partner invests outside the partnership. For the KIA (art. 3.41 lid 3) the investments of the partnership and the partner's own investments are added, the KIA table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the partner's own investment amount (investeringsbedrag) divided by the sum. The partner's own investment amount is his share in the partnership investment. With the equal profit split that share is € 50.000,00.

- Step 1: sum = € 100.000,00 plus € 0,00 = € 100.000,00.
- Step 2: € 100.000,00 is above € 59.939,00 and not above € 110.998,00, so the table outcome is the fixed band amount of € 16.784,00.
- Step 3: the partner's own investment amount divided by the sum = € 50.000,00 divided by € 100.000,00 = 1/2.
- Step 4: KIA for this partner = € 16.784,00 × 1/2 = € 8.392,00.

The other partner applies the same table outcome and the same 1/2 factor to his own share.

### Example 3: EIA with KIA (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a new machine in 2022 for € 100.000,00. The machine is on the Energy List (Energielijst), it is new (niet eerder gebruikt), it is in use before the end of 2022, it is not excluded and the investment is reported to the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) within 3 months. The machine is the only investment of 2022.

- Step 1: EIA = 45,5% (art. 3.42 lid 3) × € 100.000,00 = € 45.500,00.
- Step 2: the investment is below the maximum investment amount of € 128.000.000,00 (art. 3.42 lid 4), so the full € 100.000,00 counts.
- Step 3: the KIA on the same total investment of € 100.000,00 is the fixed band amount of € 16.784,00.
- Step 4: total deduction = € 45.500,00 plus € 16.784,00 = € 62.284,00.

For an investment of € 140.000.000,00 the EIA is calculated over at most the maximum investment amount of € 128.000.000,00: 45,5% × € 128.000.000,00 = € 58.240.000,00. The KIA would be € 0,00, because the total investment is above € 332.994,00.

### Example 4: MIA in the three categories (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a new asset in 2022 for € 100.000,00. The asset is on the Environmental List (Milieulijst) and it is new (niet eerder gebruikt). From 1 January 2022 the first letter of the Environmental List code sets the category and the MIA percentage (art. 3.42a lid 3).

- Code starting with F or G, category (categorie) I: MIA = 45% × € 100.000,00 = € 45.000,00.
- Code starting with A or D, category II: MIA = 36% × € 100.000,00 = € 36.000,00.
- Code starting with B or E, category III: MIA = 27% × € 100.000,00 = € 27.000,00.

With the KIA of € 16.784,00 on the same investment (the total investment of 2022 is € 100.000,00), the category I combination gives € 45.000,00 plus € 16.784,00 = € 61.784,00. An EIA may not be claimed on the same asset.

### Example 5: Per-asset minimums for the KIA, EIA and MIA (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys three assets in 2022: asset A of € 10.000,00, which is on the Environmental List, asset B of € 2.400,00 and asset C of € 400,00.

- Asset A: above € 450,00 for the KIA, so it counts in the KIA base. It is also above € 2.500,00 for the EIA and MIA, so the minimum does not exclude it.
- Asset B: above € 450,00 for the KIA, so it counts in the KIA base. It is below € 2.500,00 for the EIA and MIA, so it does not count for those.
- Asset C: below € 450,00 for the KIA, so it does not count in the KIA base. It is also below € 2.500,00 for the EIA and MIA, so it does not count for those.

The KIA base is € 10.000,00 plus € 2.400,00 = € 12.400,00. The KIA on that base is 28% × € 12.400,00 = € 3.472,00.

The € 450,00 minimum is in art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b and the € 2.500,00 minimum is in art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b. Meeting a per-asset minimum is not enough for an EIA or MIA. The asset must also meet the other conditions. It must be new (niet eerder gebruikt) and it must be on the Energy List for the EIA or on the Environmental List for the MIA.

### Example 6: Vamil (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) in 2022 for € 100.000,00. The Environmental List code starts with A and the asset is new (niet eerder gebruikt). Vamil allows 75% arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) (art. 3.31).

- Step 1: arbitrary depreciation in 2022 = 75% × € 100.000,00 = € 75.000,00.
- Step 2: book value at the end of 2022 = € 100.000,00 minus € 75.000,00 = € 25.000,00.
- Step 3: the remaining € 25.000,00 follows the normal schedule. Under art. 3.30 lid 2 the ordinary depreciation on this asset is at most 20% × € 100.000,00 = € 20.000,00 per year, so the remaining € 25.000,00 cannot be written off in one year.

### Example 7: Ordinary depreciation (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a machine in 2022 for € 60.000,00 and also has € 60.000,00 of goodwill.

- Machine: the art. 3.30 lid 2 cap is 20% per year, so the yearly maximum is 20% × € 60.000,00 = € 12.000,00. Writing the machine off takes at least 5 years.
- Goodwill: the art. 3.30 lid 2 cap is 10% per year, so the yearly maximum is 10% × € 60.000,00 = € 6.000,00. Writing the goodwill off takes at least 10 years.

### Example 8: Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) (Calculated example)

A sole trader buys a machine in 2022 for € 40.000,00 and claims the KIA on a total investment of € 40.000,00. The total is above € 2.400,00 and up to € 59.939,00, so the KIA percentage taken is 28%, and the KIA is 28% × € 40.000,00 = € 11.200,00. The machine is sold in 2024 for € 15.000,00.

- Step 1: the total transfer prices in 2024 are € 15.000,00, above the art. 3.47 lid 1 threshold of € 2.400,00, so the disinvestment addition applies.
- Step 2: the disposal must be within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment. The five years from the start of 2022 cover 2022 to 2026, so the 2024 disposal falls within the period.
- Step 3: disinvestment addition = the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) percentage taken × the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) = 28% × € 15.000,00 = € 4.200,00.
- Step 4: art. 3.47 lid 2 caps the amount at the investment amount over which the deduction (aftrek) was taken, € 40.000,00. The transfer price of € 15.000,00 is below that cap, so the cap does not reduce the amount.

The € 4.200,00 is added to the profit of 2024, the year of the disposal.

### Example 9: Minimum value (bodemwaarde) (Calculated example)

A sole trader owns a building that he uses in his own business, so it is not an investment building (gebouw ter belegging). The book value is € 450.000,00 in 2022 and the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for 2022 is € 400.000,00.

- Step 1: for this building the minimum value is 50% × € 400.000,00 = € 200.000,00 (art. 3.30a lid 3).
- Step 2: depreciation is allowed only while the book value is above the minimum value, and at most by the difference. For the book value of € 450.000,00 the room above the minimum value is € 450.000,00 minus € 200.000,00 = € 250.000,00.
- Step 3: if the same building were an investment building, the minimum value would be the full WOZ value of € 400.000,00, and the room above the minimum value would be € 450.000,00 minus € 400.000,00 = € 50.000,00.
- Step 4: a detachable machine (werktuig) in the building is a separate business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and is not part of the building for this rule (art. 3.30a lid 2).

### Example 10: Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) (Official example)

This Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) example runs from 2021 into 2022. A starter meets the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) conditions and buys a truck on 1 July 2021 for € 150.000,00. The truck is a business asset that qualifies for investment deduction. In 2021 € 60.000,00 is arbitrarily depreciated and in 2022 € 80.000,00. The book value at the end of 2025 is € 10.000,00 against a normal book value of € 60.000,00. The truck is rented out from 10 January 2026, which falls outside the five years after 1 January 2021, so no revaluation (herwaardering) applies. If the rental had started within that period, the difference would have been added to profit: € 60.000,00 minus € 10.000,00 = € 50.000,00.

URWA art. 8 caps the joint costs at the maximum amount over which KIA can be obtained, that is the top of the KIA table of art. 3.41 lid 2. The truck was bought in 2021, so the 2021 cap of € 328.721,00 is the cap for this truck. The investment of € 150.000,00 is within the cap. The € 80.000,00 of arbitrary depreciation in 2022 is depreciation on the 2021 investment, not a new cost in 2022, so the 2022 cap of € 332.994,00 does not apply to this truck.
