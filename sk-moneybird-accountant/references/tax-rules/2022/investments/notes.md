## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table: nil up to € 2.400 in both years, but the rest of the table was indexed. The end of the 28% band moved from € 59.170 to € 59.939. The fixed amount moved from € 16.568 to € 16.784. The third amount moved from € 109.574 to € 110.998. The top amount moved from € 328.721 to € 332.994 ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- MIA percentages: 36% (category (categorie) I) / 27% (category II) / 13,5% (category III) in the 2021 text of art. 3.42a lid 3, to 45% (I) / 36% (II) / 27% (III) from 1 January 2022 ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel)). The FISIN 2022 chapter still prints the old 36% / 27% / 13,5%, see section 7 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Environmental List (Milieulijst) code mapping: from 1 January 2022 codes starting with F or G map to category I (45%), A or D to category II (36%) and B or E to category III (27%). Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (investeringsbedrag): € 126.000.000 for 2021 to € 128.000.000 for 2022 ([FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap: € 328.721 for 2021 to € 332.994 for 2022, following the top of the KIA table ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- EIA percentage: 45,5% in both years, unchanged ([FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- KIA per-asset minimum: € 450 in both years, unchanged ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- EIA and MIA per-asset minimum: € 2.500 in both years, unchanged ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Vamil: 75% arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) in both years, unchanged ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Ordinary depreciation caps: goodwill 10% and other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) 20% per year in both years, unchanged ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold: € 2.400 in both years, unchanged. FISIN 2021 prints "€ 2.400 of minder" ([FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde): WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building in both years, unchanged ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving): none found for 2021 and none found for 2022, both UNVERIFIED absences. The 2023 scheme was set in December 2022 and covers 2023 costs only ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Claim the KIA in the tax return (aangifte) on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) of the calendar year. It is a deduction per business (per-onderneming) and it is claimed by choice ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Keep per asset the acquisition or production costs (aanschaffings- of voortbrengingskosten), the date the obligation was entered or the order was placed, the date the asset was taken into use and the payments made in the year. Art. 3.44 lid 1 caps the deduction at the payments made when the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year and moves the excess to later years ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Record the payment and use dates that art. 3.47 lid 6 needs. Undoing an investment includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount (investeringsbedrag) within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Report an EIA, MIA or Vamil asset to the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order, and within 3 months after the quarter in which production costs (voortbrengingskosten) were made. Keep the notification and the RVO decision with the purchase file. Keep the Environmental List (Milieulijst) version that belongs to the signing or order date, because that version governs ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).
- Book the yearly depreciation within the art. 3.30 caps and keep the minimum value (bodemwaarde) check for buildings in the file, because the book value above the minimum value (bodemwaarde) is the maximum ([art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Book the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) as profit in the year of the disposal when the transfer prices in that calendar year exceed € 2.400 and the disposal is within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment. Also record withdrawing an asset from the business (onderneming), changes in destination and undoings as disposals ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).
- Buying an asset from a BV in which the client holds at least one third of the shares gives in principle no right to investment deduction (investeringsaftrek). The same applies to obligations with household members, blood or in-law relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) or their household members, and heirs to an estate the asset belongs to. The Minister can disapply art. 3.46 lid 1, which is the exemption route (ontheffing) ([art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- For a private limited company (BV) the same Environmental List code mapping and the same MIA category percentages are published on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page for the corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) changes ([Belastingdienst Vpb](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) 2022: none found, so the value is UNVERIFIED as an absence. One page cannot confirm an absence. The 2022 end-of-year scheme (Eindejaarsregeling 2022) sets the 2023 scheme and names 2009, 2010, 2011 and partly (deels) 2013 as comparable years, and the URWA 2025 text lists no 2020, 2021 or 2022 scheme ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Reading the URWA text valid on 1 January 2022 and the end-of-year scheme for 2021 and 2022 would settle it.
- MIA percentages 2022, source conflict. The FISIN 2022 chapter prints 36% / 27% / 13,5% ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). Art. 3.42a lid 3 in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, the 2022 Environmental List (Milieulijst) in force (in werking) on 1 January 2022 and the Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) news item about Tax Plan 2022 (Belastingplan 2022) all give 45% / 36% / 27% from 1 January 2022 ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel)). The conflict is settled to 45% / 36% / 27% from 1 January 2022. The FISIN 2022 chapter is the losing source.
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) cap, UNVERIFIED: the € 332.994 for 2022 is a reading of the URWA art. 8 cross-reference to the maximum amount in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table over which KIA can be obtained. No primary page prints the cap as a number, so the amount rests on reading a cross-reference ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- EIA and MIA access for a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker): the rule that these persons cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages, so it is UNVERIFIED ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)).
- MIA and Vamil ceiling of € 25 million (miljoen) per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year: the RVO page states the amount but does not state a year, unless the Environmental List (Milieulijst) sets a lower maximum, so the year is unknown ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). An RVO page or regulation that names 2022 would settle it.
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde) 2021 and 2022: no longer inferred. Both year texts were read directly and both print the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) rule for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and the 50% rule for another building, so there is no open point here ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
