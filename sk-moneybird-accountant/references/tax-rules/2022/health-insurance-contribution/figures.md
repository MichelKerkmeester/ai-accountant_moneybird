## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

The income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet) is a health-care contribution charged on income. For a sole trader in 2022, the contribution is assessed at 5,50% of taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) in a (provisional) assessment (aanslag), up to a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 59.706. The employer rate for the same year is 6,75%, and a withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) of 5,50% applies to certain benefits (uitkeringen). The 2022 figures are enacted and verified on the FISIN Zvw page, but the statutory base rule for profit was read only in the 2026 version of Zvw art. 43 and is therefore UNVERIFIED for 2022.
