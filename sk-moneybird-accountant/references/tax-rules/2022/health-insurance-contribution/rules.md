## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag)

A sole trader pays the 5,50% contribution by way of a (provisional) assessment. The assessment channel covers business profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland), income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage from a foreign employer as stated on the 2020 to 2024 pages, pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic benefits (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes (loonheffing) such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente) is not part of this channel. The Zvw assessment is issued together with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Exceptions are military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum contribution income applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked sources do not state the 2022 Zvw filing deadline. That belongs to the procedure topic and is UNVERIFIED here (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen)

The maximum contribution income for 2022 is € 59.706 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). The maximum applies once per person across the assessment, employer health insurance contribution (Zvw werkgeversheffing) and withholding channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). If wage or a benefit (uitkering) already used part of the maximum, only the remaining headroom is available for the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The 2022 version of Zvw art. 43 was not read, so the statutory maximum rule is tied to the 2026 text and is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).

### Withheld Zvw contribution (ingehouden bijdrage)

The withheld contribution for 2022 is 5,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). It is withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits with payroll taxes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For a sole trader it matters only when such income exists alongside the profit. The withheld contribution uses up the single maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). No separate action for the profit itself is stated in the checked sources.

### Employer health insurance contribution (Zvw werkgeversheffing)

The employer health insurance contribution for 2022 is 6,75% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Employers or benefit agencies pay it on wages from employment (loon uit loondienst), director and substantial shareholder (dga) wages when the recipient is insured under the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand) benefits, early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people insured under the health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The rate is higher than the 5,50% assessment rate for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The employer or benefit agency pays this contribution, not the entrepreneur by assessment. For 2022 the employer health insurance contribution is marked Corrected, because an old value was not found in this run (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw base for profit (winst)

Art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under section 3.2 of the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001), except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines taxable profit as profit (winst) minus the entrepreneur's allowance minus the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). For a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) the Zvw base is therefore the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The base is capped by art. 43 lid 3 at nil on the low side and at the yearly maximum on the high side (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). The 2022 version of Zvw art. 43 was not read, so this base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). The checked FISIN Zvw pages do not mention state pension age (AOW age). https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB), and no state pension age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). A 2022 source on the state pension age treatment would settle that point.

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction for 2022 is € 6.310, or € 3.155 at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore before the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The conditions such as the hours criterion (urencriterium) belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The state pension age amount is lower (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The starter's deduction for 2022 is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is an extra entrepreneur's allowance for a starter who meets the conditions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore before the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs for 2022 is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000, then € 0, capped at the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance for a starter who is incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore before the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner for 2022 is 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4% over the same hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It applies when a partner works in the business and meets the hours test (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore before the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction for 2022 has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It applies on cessation of the business and is part of the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore before the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption for 2022 is 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked cross-year rule states that it is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and MKB). The taxable profit is the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, so the Zvw base is after this 14% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The 2022 version of Zvw art. 43 was not read, so the interaction with the Zvw base is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).

### Addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

The addition to the old-age reserve for 2022 is 9,44% of the profit, with a maximum of € 9.632 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The cross-year rule states that the yearly maximum is reduced by the occupational pension contribution (beroepspensioenpremie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). It applies to an entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and is below state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). 2022 is the last year of additions to the fiscal old-age reserve (fiscale oudedagsreserve, FOR), because since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). The addition reduces the profit and therefore the Zvw base. When the reserve decreases, the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 is excluded from the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).
