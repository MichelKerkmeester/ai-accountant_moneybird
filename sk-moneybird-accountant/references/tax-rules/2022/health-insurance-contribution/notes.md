## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag): 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Withheld Zvw contribution (ingehouden bijdrage): 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 58.311 in 2021 to € 59.706 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Employer health insurance contribution (Zvw werkgeversheffing): 7,00% in 2021 to 6,75% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The 2022 value is marked Corrected.
- Zvw base for profit (winst): no change in the checked sources. Both years use the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), read in the 2026 version and UNVERIFIED for both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022. At state pension age (AOW age) € 3.335 in 2021 to € 3.155 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek): € 2.123 in 2021 and € 2.123 in 2022. At state pension age € 1.062 in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid): € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000, then € 0 in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek): 1,25%, 2%, 3%, 4% in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): maximum € 3.630 in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 14% in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): 9,44% in both years. Maximum € 9.395 in 2021 to € 9.632 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). 2022 is the last year of additions to the fiscal old-age reserve (fiscale oudedagsreserve, FOR), because since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Maximum applies once per person across channels: no change stated in the checked sources (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Zvw at state pension age: no 2022 change found. The checked FISIN Zvw pages do not mention state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The amount to record for the Zvw assessment (aanslag) is the taxable profit from business after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. The 2022 version of the statutory base rule was not read, so this is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).
- Record the profit, each entrepreneur's allowance item and the SME profit exemption separately, because they determine the Zvw base. The checked 2022 sources do not list specific Zvw records beyond this calculation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The Zvw assessment is issued together with the (provisional) income tax assessment (aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked sources do not state a separate 2022 Zvw filing deadline. That belongs to the procedure topic and is UNVERIFIED here.
- If wage or a benefit (uitkering) already bore Zvw, record it because it uses up the single maximum of € 59.706. Only the remaining headroom can be charged on the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The employer health insurance contribution of 6,75% is a payroll entry for an employer. It is not an entry for a sole trader's own profit. The assessment rate for the profit is 5,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for profit 2022: UNVERIFIED. The 2022 version of Zvw art. 43 was not read. The rule used here comes from the 2026 version and from https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reading the 2022 version of Zvw art. 43 would settle it.
- Zvw declaration and deadlines 2022: UNVERIFIED. The checked material states that the Zvw assessment (aanslag) is issued together with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The separate filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic. A 2022 primary source on the Zvw filing timeline would settle it.
- Zvw at state pension age 2022: UNVERIFIED in part. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). No state pension age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). A 2022 source on the state pension age treatment of the Zvw base would settle it.
- 2022 employer health insurance contribution: the checked value is 6,75% and is marked Corrected because an old value was not found in this run (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). No conflicting value appears in the checked material.
- No other open points or conflicts for 2022 in the checked material.
