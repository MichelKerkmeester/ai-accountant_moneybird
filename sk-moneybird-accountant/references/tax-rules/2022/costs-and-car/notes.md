## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten): € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 1).
- Zero-emission reduction for a car first admitted in the year: a 2021 admission got 10% off capped € 4.000, rendered as 12% up to € 40.000. A 2022 admission gets 6% off capped € 2.100, rendered as 16% up to € 35.000. A car first admitted in 2021 keeps the reduction that applied at first admission for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (art. 3.20 lid 11). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Training expenses (scholingsuitgaven): the personal deduction (persoonsgebonden aftrek) ends. In 2021 art. 6.27 to 6.30 still applied with a threshold of € 250 and a maximum of € 15 000 outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). In 2022 art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed per 01-01-2022 (vervallen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), and the Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) took effect on 1 January 2022 by Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
- The STAP budget starts on 1 January 2022 as the non-fiscal replacement, with a maximum of € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) and six application windows per calendar year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).
- Performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case: the route continues under art. 10a.16 for study years up to and including 2014/2015. FISIN 2022 gives the caps € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for mbo, € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for hbo or wo, and the € 250 threshold (drempel) per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- Unchanged from 2021 to 2022: mileage allowance (kilometrevergoeding) € 0,19 per km, private-use addition for a car (bijtelling auto) 22%, pre-2017 car at least 25%, youngtimer 35% of market value (WEV) for cars more than 15 years, cap of € 1.500 on travel and accommodation for congresses and courses, relocation household contents plus € 7750, private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets) 7%, workspace at home 0%, telephone subscription at home 0%, private computers and equipment 0%, fines (geldboetes) 0% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- The text of art. 3.14 and art. 3.16 is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. The only change is art. 3.16 lid 14 from 1 January 2025, which is after 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Under the small businesses scheme (KOR, kleineondernemersregeling) no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need an adjustment (herziening), the low van motor vehicle tax rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per entrepreneur (ondernemer) covering all sub-numbers (subnummers) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Keep receipts and invoices for purchases and costs, and keep them for purchases made before the start of the business too (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Keep proof of travel costs, for example the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).
- Keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another record for the at most 500 private km. A certified trip registration system is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).
- The 80% election for mixed costs is made in the return, so note the choice for the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 5).
- Private use of a car or bicycle in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) and belongs in the profit calculation (winstberekening) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.20 and art. 3.20a, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
- Record study costs as business costs only when they maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). The personal route no longer exists for 2022. A performance grant deduction is claimed in the return year in which the diploma period (diplomatermijn) has passed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- For a BV record the wage total and the mixed costs per body, because the corporate income tax (Vpb) variant applies per body and only once in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), at the parent (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.4).
