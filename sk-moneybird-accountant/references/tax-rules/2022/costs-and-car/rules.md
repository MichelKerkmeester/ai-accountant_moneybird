## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel)

Costs connected with food (voedsel), drinks (drank), stimulants (genotmiddelen such as tobacco), representation expenses (representatie, including receptions, festive gatherings and entertainment) and congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips (studiereizen) and the like are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) up to a yearly threshold (drempel). For 2022 the threshold is € 4.800 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Travel and accommodation related to these items count as part of them (lid 2). The restriction does not apply when the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself in relation to these costs (lid 4). Food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including gratuities (fooien). Drinks cover coffee, tea, milk and soft drinks (frisdrank). Stimulants cover cigarettes and cigars. Food, drinks and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.3). The fixed amount is not time-proportioned, so it is not reduced when the enterprise starts during the calendar year and not increased when a financial year (boekjaar) runs longer than 12 months (same decree (besluit), section 3.1). Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals the restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wage in kind (loon in natura) (same decree, section 3.2). The threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only.

### 3.2 Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The same 80% applies to people receiving income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk) (resultaatgenieters). The choice is made in the return, so it is a per-year decision and not a bookkeeping entry. Compare the two routes before filing, because the better route depends on the total amount of these costs. Example 1 in section 4 shows both routes with the 2022 figures.

### 3.3 Travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf) for congresses and courses

Travel and accommodation for courses and training for study and professional purposes (cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep) and for congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Travel and accommodation also count among the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) items under art. 3.15 lid 2, so it interacts with the € 4.800 threshold or with the 80% election (same URL and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### 3.4 Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets (privévermogen) or is rented privately, such as a car (auto), motorcycle (motor) or bicycle (fiets), are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For 2022 that amount is € 0,19 per km. The amount is all-in, so fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), tolls and parking cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer for this rule (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Keep a kilometre record, because the deduction follows the business kilometres that are actually driven.

### 3.5 Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (werkelijke kosten) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview. This route sits next to the € 0,19 per km rule of 3.4, which applies to a private or privately rented vehicle (gehuurd vervoermiddel) and not to public transport.

### 3.6 Relocation (verhuizing) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

Moving costs are the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Costs of housing outside the place of residence are deductible for at most two years. The rule is unchanged in 2020 to 2027.

### 3.7 Car asset classification (vermogensetikettering)

Asset classification (vermogensetikettering) decides whether the car sits in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) or in the private assets (privévermogen). A car is mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen) when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres (privékilometers) and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately the car is compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). All other cases are freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) within reasonable bounds. The taxpayer can revise the choice (keuze herzien) for freely classifiable assets until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of choice becomes irrevocable, and afterwards only in a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use (privégebruik) of an asset in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

### 3.8 Private-use addition for a car (bijtelling auto), standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private use (lid 3). With a trip registration (rittenregistratie, mileage log) or another way of showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked. The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. Car means a passenger car (personenauto) or a van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 onderdeel a), for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled. A declaration of exclusively business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (lid 6 to 10). The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, described by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) as the original catalogue value including VAT and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). The RDW carries it for most cars. For an old car, market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) replaces the catalogue price.

### 3.9 Private-use addition for a car, first admission (eerste toelating) up to and including 31 December 2016

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. The rule does not affect the 35% rule for old cars of 3.10.

### 3.10 Youngtimer (old car)

When the car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, 35% of market value (waarde in het economische verkeer, WEV) is the withdrawal (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The age threshold is 15 years in 2022. The WEV replaces the catalogue price for an old car (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. The 25% minimum for pre-2017 cars does not touch this 35% rule (art. 10a.4).

### 3.11 Zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto), first admission 2022

For a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, and the reduction is capped in euros (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For a car first admitted in 2022 the reduction is 6% off capped at € 2.100, which the checked material renders as 16% up to € 35.000. Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (lid 11). After those 60 months the rules then in force (in werking) apply.

### 3.12 Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets)

When a bicycle (including an e-bike or a speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Availability for commuting counts as private availability. Lid 5, added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.20a, the note on retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) is also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### 3.13 VAT correction for private use of a car (privégebruik auto) (btw-correctie privéauto) without conclusive mileage records (zonder sluitende kilometeradministratie)

VAT (btw) on the use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting counts as private use for VAT, although it is business for income tax (inkomstenbelasting). In a private limited company (BV) the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, dga) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) and counts as an employee for this purpose. Without conclusive mileage records all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### 3.14 Small purchases (kleine aankopen) and one-year costs (eenjarige kosten)

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example small office supplies (kantoorartikelen), professional literature (vakliteratuur) or car maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. For computers and other equipment (apparatuur) the classification rules of art. 3.16 lid 3 decide the deductible part, see 3.22.

### 3.15 Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) abolished

Up to and including 2021 training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) were deductible as a personal deduction (persoonsgebonden aftrek) above a threshold of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (buiten standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30 in the 2021 text, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Study expenses are tuition fees (lesgeld, collegegeld), course fees (cursusgeld), examination fees (examengeld), doctorate costs (promotiekosten) and required study materials (leermiddelen) excluding computers. There was no deduction when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study. The Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree of 12 July 2021 (Besluit van 12 juli 2021), Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed per 01-01-2022 (vervallen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) rules in sections 3.16 to 3.18. Study costs (studiekosten) stay deductible as business costs (bedrijfskosten) when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers). Travel and accommodation for courses falls under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f.

### 3.16 Performance grant cap, mbo

When the diploma period (diplomatermijn, usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount that could not be deducted earlier because of the performance grant (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). For mbo study years the cap is € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for a part year. When the actual performance grant was lower, the lower amount applies.

### 3.17 Performance grant cap, hbo or wo

For hbo or wo study years the cap in the same case is € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for a part year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). When the actual performance grant was lower, the lower amount applies. The amounts are applied per study year, and the deduction is claimed in the return year in which the diploma period has passed.

### 3.18 Threshold in the performance grant case (prestatiebeurszaak)

The € 250 threshold applies per person in the performance grant case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten). The official example on the FISIN 2022 page subtracts the threshold once from the total of the study years, see section 4 example 12.

### 3.19 STAP budget

The STAP-budget is the non-fiscal replacement of the abolished deduction. Art. 29 of the scheme (regeling) links its start to the Abolition Act, and Stb. 2021, 349 states that it applies from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The maximum subsidy is € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). The checked material marks this value as Corrected: the maximum is per training and not per year. There are six application windows per calendar year. The scheme is marked lapsed per 01-01-2024 (Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024).

### 3.20 Workspace at home (werkruimte thuis)

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not part of the business assets are not deductible unless the workspace is a separate part of the home according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig) and an income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). With another workspace outside the home the combined profit, wage and income from other activities must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is classified as a business asset (bedrijfsmiddel). The official factors indicating that the workspace is separate include its own entrance or stairway (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the ability to rent the space to a third party (derde). A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not separate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints "Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen". The 2022 statute has no lid 14. That limit on costs of an owned home in the business assets was added from 1 January 2025 and does not apply to 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).

### 3.21 Home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis)

Telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The overview page prints "Telefoonabonnement thuis 0%". The practical rule is that a private telephone subscription is not deductible but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible but the call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints "Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100%" for that business part (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.22 Private computers and equipment (apparatuur)

Costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and similar equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). For a computer the income-tax treatment follows asset classification. Equipment and instruments (apparatuur en instrumenten) that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible. A capital asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business must be classified as compulsory private assets, one used 90% or more must be classified as mandatory business assets and one used in between can be classified as freely classifiable assets within reasonable bounds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.23 Literature except professional literature

Literature (literatuur) except professional literature is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Professional literature is deductible and, under the € 450 rule of 3.14, a purchase under € 450 is deducted in one go.

### 3.24 Clothing except work clothing (werkkleding)

Clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel c, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Work clothing is defined in lid 5 and is deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive). Personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) such as a visit to the hairdresser (kapper) is not deductible, while work clothing is.

### 3.25 Personal care (persoonlijke verzorging)

Costs of personal care are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel d, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Health costs such as a pedicure or health insurance (zorgverzekering) are not deductible as business costs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.26 Partner compensation (vergoeding partner)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11).

### 3.27 Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen)

Costs connected with fines imposed by a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten), comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines are not deductible (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Also not deductible are costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a penalty order became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and penalty payments under section 5.3.2 Awb (onderdeel i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Traffic fines (verkeersboeten) under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints "Geldboetes 0%". Costs made before the fine is imposed are not costs connected with the imposed fine and stay deductible, while costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment, the relevant point is whether it has been forfeited (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted are excluded, and the decree gives the example of a conviction for possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten) but not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, sections 1.1 and 2).

### 3.28 Mixed costs for entities subject to corporate income tax (Vpb) with employees

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wage Tax Act (Wet LB) sense in the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The rule applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.4). The fixed amount that is compared with the 0,4% is the art. 3.15 amount of € 4.800 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
