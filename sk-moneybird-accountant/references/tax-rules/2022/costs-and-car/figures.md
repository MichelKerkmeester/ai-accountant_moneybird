## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the deduction of business costs (bedrijfskosten) and the car, bicycle and travel rules for a Dutch sole trader in tax year 2022, plus the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) rules that also apply to a private limited company (BV). The most important change in 2022 is the end of the personal deduction for training costs (persoonsgebonden aftrek voor scholingsuitgaven) on 1 January 2022, with the non-fiscal STAP budget as its replacement. Study costs (studiekosten) stayed deductible as business costs only when they maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). The threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) rose to € 4.800, and a zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto) first admitted in 2022 gets a reduction of 6% capped at € 2.100. The 2022 rules in the checked material are enacted law, verified in the statute text valid on 1 January 2022.
