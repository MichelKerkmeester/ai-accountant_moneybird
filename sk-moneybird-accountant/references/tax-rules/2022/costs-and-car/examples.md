## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All input amounts below are invented round amounts. The rates and thresholds are from section 2. Every product is written with two decimals before rounding. No step below rounds.

### Example 1: Mixed costs, the threshold against the 80% election

**Calculated example.**

Case A, € 9.000,00 of mixed costs (gemengde kosten) in 2022.
- Threshold route: € 9.000,00 minus € 4.800,00 = € 4.200,00 deductible.
- Election route: 80% of € 9.000,00 = € 7.200,00 deductible.
- The election route deducts more.

Case B, € 30.000,00 of mixed costs in 2022.
- Threshold route: € 30.000,00 minus € 4.800,00 = € 25.200,00 deductible.
- Election route: 80% of € 30.000,00 = € 24.000,00 deductible.
- The threshold route deducts more.

Case C, € 3.000,00 of mixed costs in 2022.
- Threshold route: the threshold (drempel) of € 4.800,00 is not reached, so nothing is deductible.
- Election route: 80% of € 3.000,00 = € 2.400,00 deductible.
- The election route deducts more.

### Example 2: Travel and accommodation for congresses and courses

**Calculated example.**

A congress in 2022 costs € 1.200,00 in travel and accommodation, and a second congress costs € 800,00.
- Total travel and accommodation: € 1.200,00 plus € 800,00 = € 2.000,00.
- Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f makes travel and accommodation not deductible above € 1.500,00 per year.
- If attendance is not necessary, the excess is not deductible: € 2.000,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 500,00 not deductible. The remaining € 1.500,00 is treated as mixed costs under art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 2. With no other mixed costs, the threshold route gives € 0,00 because € 1.500,00 is below the threshold of € 4.800,00. The 80% election gives 80% of € 1.500,00 = € 1.200,00.
- Art. 3.16 lid 8 lifts the € 1.500,00 cap when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary.
- If attendance is necessary, the full € 2.000,00 is mixed costs. With no other mixed costs, the threshold route gives € 0,00 because € 2.000,00 is below the threshold of € 4.800,00. The 80% election gives 80% of € 2.000,00 = € 1.600,00.

### Example 3: Mileage allowance and public transport

**Calculated example.**

A private car is used for 4.500 business kilometres in 2022.
- 4.500 km times € 0,19 = € 855,00 deductible.
- Fuel, insurance, tolls and parking cannot be added separately, because the € 0,19 is all-in.
- A household member is treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). If the partner drives another 1.000 business kilometres for the same enterprise in that car, the same rate applies: 5.500 km times € 0,19 = € 1.045,00.
- A business train trip that is not travel for a congress or course costs € 240,00. Public transport (openbaar vervoer) is deductible at 100% of the actual costs, so € 240,00.

### Example 4: Relocation (verhuizing)

**Calculated example.**

Moving the household contents (inboedel) costs € 1.200,00.
- Deduction for the move: € 1.200,00 plus € 7.750,00 = € 8.950,00.
- Double accommodation outside the place of residence can be added for at most two years.

### Example 5: Private-use addition (bijtelling) 22% with the car costs cap

**Calculated example.**

A car in the business assets has a catalogue price including VAT and bpm of € 30.000,00 and is used privately for more than 500 km.
- Withdrawal (onttrekking) before the car costs (autokosten) cap: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the withdrawal at the total car costs including depreciation. If those costs are € 5.200,00, the withdrawal is € 5.200,00.
- With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) showing 480 private km, the withdrawal is nil.

### Example 6: Pre-2017 car, minimum 25%

**Calculated example.**

A car first admitted in 2015 has a value of € 20.000,00.
- Art. 10a.4 sets the minimum percentage of art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a at 25% instead of 22% for this car.
- Withdrawal before the car costs cap: 25% of € 20.000,00 = € 5.000,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the withdrawal at the total car costs including depreciation.
- If those costs are € 4.000,00, the withdrawal is € 4.000,00.
- If those costs are € 5.500,00, the withdrawal is € 5.000,00.

### Example 7: Zero-emission car first admitted in 2022

**Calculated example.**

Car A, value € 40.000,00, first admitted in 2022, 0 g CO2 per km.
- Reduction: 6% of € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00. The cap is € 2.100,00, so the reduction is € 2.100,00.
- Withdrawal before the car costs cap: 22% of € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00 minus € 2.100,00 = € 6.700,00.
- Check with the other rendering: 16% of € 35.000,00 = € 5.600,00 plus 22% of € 5.000,00 = € 1.100,00 gives € 6.700,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the withdrawal at the total car costs including depreciation.

Car B, value € 30.000,00, first admitted in 2022, 0 g CO2 per km.
- Reduction: 6% of € 30.000,00 = € 1.800,00, below the cap of € 2.100,00.
- Withdrawal before the car costs cap: 22% of € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00 minus € 1.800,00 = € 4.800,00.
- Check with the other rendering: 16% of € 30.000,00 = € 4.800,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the withdrawal at the total car costs including depreciation.

### Example 8: Youngtimer

**Calculated example.**

A car of the business was first taken into use more than 15 years ago. The market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer, WEV) is € 8.000,00.
- Withdrawal before the car costs cap: 35% of € 8.000,00 = € 2.800,00.
- Art. 3.20 lid 4 caps the withdrawal at the total car costs including depreciation. If those costs are € 2.000,00, the withdrawal is € 2.000,00.

### Example 9: Private-use addition for a bicycle (fiets)

**Calculated example.**

A business e-bike with a recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) of € 2.200,00 is also available privately.
- Withdrawal before the costs-borne cap: 7% of € 2.200,00 = € 154,00.
- If the costs borne are € 100,00, the withdrawal is capped at € 100,00.
- If the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the withdrawal is nil (art. 3.20a lid 5, retroactive to 1 January 2020).

### Example 10: VAT correction (btw-correctie) on a private car

**Calculated example.**

A private car with a catalogue price including VAT and bpm of € 30.000,00 is used for business, and there are no conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie).
- Year-end correction: 1,5% of € 30.000,00 = € 450,00 in rubriek 1d (rubriek 1d).
- Part year, six months: € 450,00 divided by 2 = € 225,00.
- The correction is never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance.

### Example 11: The € 450 rule and classification of computers

**Calculated example.**

- A laptop of € 400,00 is deducted in one go in the year of purchase.
- A laptop of € 1.200,00 used for several years is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account.
- A laptop used 95% for business is mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen).
- A laptop used 90% or more privately is compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen) and 0% deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### Example 12: Performance grant (prestatiebeurs), mbo

**Official example**, copied from FISIN 2022, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten.

Mbo from August 2012 to July 2016, the diploma period (diplomatermijn) ends in August 2022. Per study year the deduction is the lower of the fixed amount and the actual performance grant.

| Study year | Fixed amount | Performance grant | Lower amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | € 706,00 | € 333,00 | € 333,00 |
| 2 | € 1.693,00 | € 800,00 | € 800,00 |
| 3 | € 1.693,00 | € 800,00 | € 800,00 |
| 4 | € 988,00 | € 467,00 | € 467,00 |

- Total of the lower amounts: € 333,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 467,00 = € 2.400,00.
- Minus the threshold (drempel) per person: € 2.400,00 minus € 250,00 = € 2.150,00 deductible.

### Example 13: Performance grant, part-year hbo or wo

**Calculated example.**

An hbo study year falls only partly within the case, five months. Assume the actual performance grant is at least the cap.
- Cap: 5 months times € 203,59 = € 1.017,95.
- Minus the threshold per person: € 1.017,95 minus € 250,00 = € 767,95 deductible.
- If the actual performance grant is lower than € 1.017,95, that lower amount applies before the threshold.

### Example 14: STAP budget maximum

**Calculated example.**

A training (scholing) costs € 1.200,00 including VAT.
- The STAP budget maximum is € 1.000,00 including VAT per training.
- The lower of € 1.200,00 and € 1.000,00 is € 1.000,00.
- There are six application windows per calendar year.

### Example 15: Mixed costs in a private limited company (BV)

**Calculated example.**

A BV has employees. The wage total is € 1.500.000,00.
- 0,4% of € 1.500.000,00 = € 6.000,00, which is higher than € 4.800,00, so the threshold is € 6.000,00.
- Mixed costs of € 10.000,00, threshold route: € 10.000,00 minus € 6.000,00 = € 4.000,00.
- Election route at 73,5%: 0,735 times € 10.000,00 = € 7.350,00.
- The election route deducts € 7.350,00.

A second BV has employees and a wage total of € 1.000.000,00.
- 0,4% of € 1.000.000,00 = € 4.000,00, which is lower than € 4.800,00, so the fixed threshold of € 4.800,00 applies.

### Example 16: Items at 0%

**Calculated example.**

- A traffic fine under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) of € 300,00: € 300,00 times 0% = € 0,00 deductible (art. 3.14 lid 1).
- A private telephone subscription (telefoonabonnement) of € 40,00 per month: € 40,00 times 0% = € 0,00 deductible, while the business calls made through it are deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a).
- For a workspace in a private home, a bedroom furnished as an office is not separate (zelfstandig), so the workspace costs are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 1 unless the workspace is a separate part and the income test is met. The same test applies under art. 3.16 lid 13 to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is business assets. The restriction for an owned home in the business assets that serves as main residence (hoofdverblijf) was added only from 1 January 2025 by art. 3.16 lid 14.
- Partner compensation (vergoeding partner) of € 4.000,00 is below € 5.000,00 and is not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 4).
