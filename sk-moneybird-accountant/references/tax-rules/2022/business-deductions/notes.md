## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age (AOW age): € 3.335 in 2021 to € 3.155 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Research and development deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 13.188 in 2021 to € 13.360 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- S&O starter increase: € 6.598 in 2021 to € 6.684 in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition maximum: € 9.395 in 2021 to € 9.632 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021 to 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10 lid 2).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 43% in 2021 to 40% in 2022, being 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10).
- Second-test lookback window: 2016 to 2020 for 2021 to 2017 to 2021 for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6).
- 2022 is the last year with art. 3.67 to 3.72 in force, so 2022 is the last year for additions to the old-age reserve. The articles lapsed on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Unchanged in 2022, same value as 2021: hours criterion (urencriterium) 1.225 hours, reduced hours criterion 800 hours, starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123, starter's deduction at state pension age € 1.062, starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, SME profit exemption 14%, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands 1,25% to 4%, discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) € 3.630 maximum and the old-age reserve addition percentage 9,44% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Health insurance contribution (Zvw): 2021 had a self-assessed rate of 5,75% and a maximum contribution income of € 58.311 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material gives no 2022 Zvw figures, see section 7.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record all hours worked on the business, including non-billable work such as quotes, administration and the business website, because the hours criterion (urencriterium) is tested at 1.225 hours in the calendar year and the hours must be made plausible with evidence such as an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Keep the decision (beschikking) for the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) and the carry-forward balance (saldo) in the records, because the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets both in a decision open to objection (bezwaar) and the offset runs over the next nine calendar years, oldest first (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 6 to 8, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- Record an addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) as a movement in a fiscal reserve. The percentage base is the profit before the addition, the addition is capped at € 9.632 for 2022, and 2022 is the last year for additions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.68 and 3.71, and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Record the annuity (lijfrente) premium or deposit for converting the reserve, because it must be paid or made in 2022 or before 1 July 2023 and the amount is added to the 2022 profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Do not deduct pay to the partner below € 5.000. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) treats such a partner as working without pay for the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), and the pay is no income for the partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.16 lid 4, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Apply the order profit, then entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), then SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) on the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming). The SME profit exemption needs no hours criterion, and in a loss year it enlarges the loss (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.2 and 3.79a, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Keep a record of the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) used in earlier years, because the € 3.630 maximum is reduced by it, not below nil, and an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation (staking) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.79, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) raises the tax by 9,5% of the excess of taxable box 1 income plus the deductions over € 69.398, at most 9,5% of those deductions, so the deductions have a maximum value of 40% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- FISIN 2022 prints the lookback window for the second test as "20167 tot en met 2021". This is a misprint. Art. 3.6 gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2017 to 2021, and FISIN 2021 prints 2016 to 2020 for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6). The statute settles it.
- FISIN 2022 prints "20223" in the sentence on the annuity (lijfrente) deadline. The intended year is 2022, with a deadline of 1 July 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The page context of the 2022 return settles it.
- The Zvw interaction is verified in the checked material for 2020 and 2021 only. The 2022 self-assessed Zvw rate and maximum contribution income are unknown here (verified years: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The FISIN 2022 Zvw page or the Zvw statute for 2022 would settle it.
- FISIN 2022 does not state that 2022 is the last year for additions to the old-age reserve. The 2023 statute settles it, because art. 3.67 to 3.72 read "Vervallen per 01-01-2023" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Proposed, not in force in 2022: bill 37 022 would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs) from 1 January 2029, and the Act on fiscal stimulation of start-ups and scale-ups (Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups) would delete the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) three calendar years after its cuts take effect (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf).
- Apart from the points above, the checked material lists no open points for 2022.
