## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All examples use the 2022 figures unless stated otherwise.

### Example 1, Official example: carry-forward of the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)

Official example: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek (FISIN 2022, 8.4.3)

The example assumes the entrepreneur meets the hours criterion (urencriterium) in each year, as art. 3.76 lid 1 requires.

- 2020: profit € 5.000 against a self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 7.030. Unused: € 7.030 minus € 5.000 = € 2.030.
- 2021: a loss of € 25.000, so there is no profit to offset. Unused: € 6.670. Running balance (saldo): € 2.030 plus € 6.670 = € 8.700.
- 2022: profit € 100.000. Step 1: € 100.000 minus € 6.310 = € 93.690. Step 2: € 93.690 minus the balance of € 8.700 = € 84.990. Remaining balance: € 0.

The € 7.030 and € 6.670 are the self-employed person's deduction amounts of 2020 and 2021. The 2022 amount is € 6.310.

### Example 2, Official example: hours in a part year

Official examples:
- Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst): https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium
- The Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK): https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium

- Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period.
- Chamber of Commerce: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days (rounded), and at 8 hours a day 117 x 8 = 936,00 hours. Extra hours needed to reach the 1.225-hour test: 1.225 minus 936 = 289 hours.

### Example 3, Calculated example: second test (tweede toets) for a starter

Inputs: the taxpayer became an entrepreneur on 1 January 2020 and worked 1.300 hours on the business in 2022. The lookback window for 2022 is 2017 to 2021. The years 2017, 2018 and 2019 were non-entrepreneur years, so the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. The second test of art. 3.6 lid 1 is met. The 1.300 hours are above 1.225, so the hours criterion is met. The mainly (grotendeels) test does not need to be checked.

### Example 4, Calculated example: self-employed person's deduction capped at the profit, non-starter

Inputs: profit € 4.000, entrepreneur below state pension age (AOW age), not a starter, meets the hours criterion.

- Self-employed person's deduction on paper: € 6.310.
- Cap under art. 3.76 lid 5: not more than the profit, so € 4.000.
- Unused self-employed person's deduction: € 6.310 minus € 4.000 = € 2.310. This is set by decision (beschikking) and offset in the next nine calendar years, oldest first.
- Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 4.000 minus € 4.000 = € 0.
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 14% of € 0 = € 0,00.
- Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 0.

### Example 5, Calculated example: starter with a profit of € 60.000

Inputs: profit € 60.000, entrepreneur below state pension age, meets the hours criterion, starter who meets the art. 3.76 lid 3 conditions, no other income.

- Self-employed person's deduction plus starter's deduction (startersaftrek): € 6.310 plus € 2.123 = € 8.433.
- Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 minus € 8.433 = € 51.567.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 51.567 = € 7.219,38.
- Taxable profit from business: € 51.567 minus € 7.219,38 = € 44.347,62.
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) check: € 44.347,62 plus € 8.433 plus € 7.219,38 = € 60.000,00, which does not exceed € 69.398, so no rate adjustment applies.

### Example 6, Calculated example: starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Inputs: the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a WIA benefit in 2022, worked 900 hours in 2022 and is below state pension age at the start of the year. The 900 hours are at least 800 and not more than 1.225, so the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) is met and the normal hours criterion is not.

- First use, profit € 20.000: the amount is € 12.000, below the profit, so the deduction is € 12.000.
- Second use, applied once before, profit € 9.000: the amount is € 8.000, below the profit, so the deduction is € 8.000.
- Third use, applied twice before, profit € 3.000: the amount is € 4.000, capped at the profit, so the deduction is € 3.000.
- Fourth use: the statute has no tier above two earlier uses, so there is no deduction.

### Example 7, Calculated example: S&O deduction with the starter's increase (startersverhoging)

Input: an entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion, has an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), makes at least 500 hours of S&O work and was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years with the deduction applied at most twice.

- Base amount: € 13.360.
- Starter increase: € 6.684.
- Total: € 13.360 plus € 6.684 = € 20.044.

### Example 8, Calculated example: deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

Inputs: profit € 60.000, entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion, partner works 1.300 hours without pay.

- 1.300 hours falls in the 1.225 to under 1.750 band, so 3%.
- Deduction for assisting partner: 3% of € 60.000 = € 1.800,00.
- Boundary variant: exactly 1.225 hours also falls in the 3% band, because the band starts at "gelijk aan of meer dan" 1.225.
- Pay variant: the partner is paid € 4.000, which is below € 5.000, so the pay is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and the partner counts as working without pay. If that partner worked 900 hours, 900 falls in the 875 to under 1.225 band, so 2% of € 60.000 = € 1.200,00.

### Example 9, Calculated example: discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

Inputs: profit on the cessation (staking) of a whole business, no earlier discontinuation deduction.

- Cessation profit € 10.000, no earlier use: the deduction equals the profit, at most € 3.630, so € 3.630.
- Cessation profit € 10.000, an earlier discontinuation deduction of € 1.000: maximum € 3.630 minus € 1.000 = € 2.630, so the deduction is € 2.630.
- Cessation profit € 3.000, no earlier use: the deduction equals the profit, so € 3.000.

### Example 10, Calculated example: addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve)

Inputs: profit before the addition € 60.000, no pension premiums, the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end exceeds the old-age reserve at the start of the year by € 25.000, entrepreneur below state pension age who meets the hours criterion. This is the last year in which an addition is possible.

- Addition: 9,44% of € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
- € 5.664,00 is below the maximum of € 9.632 and below the € 25.000 increase in the business capital, so the addition is € 5.664,00.
- Second variant: profit before the addition € 120.000, with the same € 25.000 increase in the business capital. 9,44% of € 120.000 = € 11.328,00, which is above the maximum, so the addition is capped at € 9.632. The € 9.632 is also below the € 25.000 increase.

### Example 11, Calculated example: rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

Inputs: profit € 100.000, entrepreneur below state pension age, meets the hours criterion, not a starter, no other income.

- Self-employed person's deduction: € 100.000 minus € 6.310 = € 93.690.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 93.690 = € 13.116,60. Taxable profit from business: € 93.690 minus € 13.116,60 = € 80.573,40.
- Deductions: € 6.310 plus € 13.116,60 = € 19.426,60.
- Taxable box 1 income plus the deductions: € 80.573,40 plus € 19.426,60 = € 100.000,00.
- Excess over € 69.398: € 100.000 minus € 69.398 = € 30.602. 9,5% of € 30.602 = € 2.907,19.
- Cap: 9,5% of € 19.426,60 = € 1.845,53, rounded.
- Rate adjustment: the lower of the two, so € 1.845,53.
- Maximum value of the deductions at 40%: € 19.426,60 x 40% = € 7.770,64.
