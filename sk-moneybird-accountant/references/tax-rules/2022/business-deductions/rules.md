## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

Who is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged by the Belastingdienst per activity, separately from the btw. A btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst looks at more conditions for the inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The Belastingdienst weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer. Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk, the profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). Art. 3.4 defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations, and art. 3.5 counts the zelfstandig beroep as an onderneming (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.4 and 3.5). Medegerechtigden (persons entitled to a profit share) get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74).

### 3.1 Parts of the ondernemersaftrek

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek. The startersaftrek is not a separate list item. It is a verhoging (increase) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3. The article text is identical in every version from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74). Medegerechtigden get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit, and a person who is not an IB-ondernemer has no right to the zelfstandigenaftrek at all.

### 3.2 Urencriterium: 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). KVK gives an example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium). All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The text of art. 3.6 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027.

### 3.3 Second test waived: not an ondernemer in 2017 to 2021

The 1.225 hours are not the only test. Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires either (a) that the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is grotendeels (largely) spent on the businesses, or (b) that the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6). For 2022 the lookback window is 2017 to 2021. So the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. The same text applies in 2020 and 2021 and stays unchanged to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6). FISIN 2022 prints the window as "20167 tot en met 2021", a typo for 2017 to 2021, see section 7.

### 3.4 Verlaagd urencriterium: 800 hours

Art. 3.78a lid 3 gives the verlaagd urencriterium (reduced hours test): the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78a lid 3). The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, not of the other parts of the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.5 Pregnancy in the urencriterium: 16 weeks

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.6 Samenwerkingsverband with connected persons and the 70% reading

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (partnership) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and an ondermaatschap as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.7 Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310

Art. 3.76 lid 1: the zelfstandigenaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium. Lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year, which is € 6.310 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76). Lid 5: the aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (non-realised self-employed person's deduction), and if the cap sets the aftrek to nil the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count (same source). Lid 6: the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection). Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar (same source). The Belastingdienst describes the same carry-forward on its page on verrekenen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### 3.8 Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 3.155

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year, the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4). For 2022 the halved zelfstandigenaftrek is € 3.155 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Startersaftrek: € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the zelfstandigenaftrek is raised by the startersaftrek, € 2.123 for 2022, if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, tax-free return from a private limited company) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (same source). The Belastingdienst adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.10 Startersaftrek at AOW age: € 1.062

At the AOW age at the start of the year the startersaftrek is halved together with the rest of the aftrek under art. 3.76 lid 4, which gives € 1.062 for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.11 Startersaftrek prior-years condition

The prior-years test is: not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, with the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The Belastingdienst words the test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.12 Startersaftrek at most 3 times in the first 5 years

The Belastingdienst states that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). A year in which the profit cap sets the zelfstandigenaftrek to nil still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for this count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5).

### 3.13 Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a: the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance. Amount: € 12.000 if not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if applied for one year, € 4.000 if applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then, and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### 3.14 Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 13.360

Art. 3.77 gives the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D deduction) as part of the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74, with a base amount of € 13.360 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.77). The amounts are indexed per year. The article applies 2020 to 2027.

### 3.15 S&O starter increase: € 6.684

The base amount is raised by a starter increase of € 6.684 for 2022 for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.77 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.16 S&O conditions

FISIN adds that the S&O aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.17 MKB-winstvrijstelling: 14%

Art. 3.79a: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 14% of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.79a). Art. 3.2: belastbare winst uit onderneming is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling (same source, art. 3.2). No urencriterium is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none. No vrijstelling applies on profit as medegerechtigde. In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing, see 3.24 and 3.25. The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, but the checked sources verify that interaction for 2020 and 2021 only (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente.

### 3.18 Meewerkaftrek bands

Art. 3.78: the meewerkaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.78). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.78 lid 2, with the same text in the 2020 to 2027 versions, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.19 Partner pay below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.16). The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.20 Stakingsaftrek: € 3.630 maximum

Art. 3.79: the stakingsaftrek applies to profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.79). For a business continued under doorschuiving (tax-free transfer) on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming (decrease) of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29). The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and that there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.21 Oudedagsreserve addition percentage: 9,44%

Art. 3.67: the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year kan (may) add to the oudedagsreserve, so the addition is optional, and FISIN 2020 words it as "U kunt elk jaar aan de oudedagsreserve toevoegen, maar dit is niet verplicht" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.67, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The urencriterium is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68: the addition is 9,44% of the profit as ondernemer, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the ondernemingsvermogen (business capital) at the end of the year exceeds the oudedagsreserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by AMvB, lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pensioenrichtleeftijd changes, published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.67 to 3.72). 2022 is the last year with art. 3.67 to 3.72 in force, because those articles lapsed on 1 January 2023, printed as "Vervallen per 01-01-2023" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). FISIN 2022 itself does not say that 2022 is the last addition year, see section 7.

### 3.22 Oudedagsreserve addition maximum: € 9.632

Art. 3.68 lid 1 caps the addition at € 9.632 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.68, and FISIN 2022 agrees). Art. 3.69 lets the addition be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year, where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from a lijfrente formed from an oudedagsreserve, capped at the ondernemingsvermogen at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71 defines ondernemingsvermogen as the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the oudedagsreserve, corrected for a terugkeerreserve. Art. 3.72 covers more than one business. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.23 Oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente: deadline 1 July 2023

For converting the oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente (annuity), the premium must be paid or the deposit made in 2022 or before 1 July 2023, and the amount is added to the 2022 profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The page prints "20223", a typo for 2022, see section 7. The decrease rules that applied to a reserve on 31 December 2022 are kept in force by art. 10a.29: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year, and decreases are added to profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 10a.29). Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return (same source). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### 3.24 Tariefsaanpassing: 9,5% above € 69.398

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that reduce the tax base), expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10). For 2022 the percentage is 9,5% and the threshold is € 69.398 (same source, art. 2.10 lid 2).

### 3.25 Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling: 40%

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing percentage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 2.10). For 2022 that is 49,50% minus 9,5%, so 40% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10).
