## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

Whether someone is an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes is judged by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) per activity, separately from VAT (btw). A VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration looks at more conditions for income tax (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, business risk (ondernemersrisico) and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), the profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). Art. 3.4 defines the entrepreneur as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations, and art. 3.5 counts the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) as a business (onderneming) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.4 and 3.5). Persons entitled to a share (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) or SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) on their profit as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74).

### 3.1 Parts of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the research and development deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and the discontinuation deduction. The starter's deduction is not a separate list item. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3. The article text is identical in every version from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74). Persons entitled to a share get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction or SME profit exemption on their profit as a co-entitled party, and a person who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes has no right to the self-employed person's deduction at all.

### 3.2 Hours criterion (urencriterium): 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) gives an example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium). All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The text of art. 3.6 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027.

### 3.3 Second test waived: not an entrepreneur in 2017 to 2021

The 1.225 hours are not the only test. Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires either (a) that the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses, or (b) that the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.6). For 2022 the lookback window is 2017 to 2021. So the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. The same text applies in 2020 and 2021 and stays unchanged to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6). FISIN 2022 prints the window as "20167 tot en met 2021", a typo for 2017 to 2021, see section 7.

### 3.4 Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium): 800 hours

Art. 3.78a lid 3 gives the reduced hours criterion: the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78a lid 3). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs, not of the other parts of the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.5 Pregnancy in the hours criterion: 16 weeks

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.6 Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons and the 70% reading

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.7 Self-employed person's deduction: € 6.310

Art. 3.76 lid 1: the self-employed person's deduction applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion. Paragraph 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below state pension age (AOW age) at the start of the year, which is € 6.310 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76). Lid 5: the deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. The amount cut off by this cap is the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek), and if the cap sets the deduction to nil the year still counts as a year with the self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count (same source). Paragraph 6: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Paragraph 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Paragraph 8: the offset is also set by decision open to objection (same source). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes the same carry-forward on its page on offsetting (verrekenen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### 3.8 Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age: € 3.155

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at state pension age at the start of the year, the deduction is 50% of the amount under paragraphs 2 and 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4). For 2022 the halved self-employed person's deduction is € 3.155 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Starter's deduction: € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the self-employed person's deduction is raised by the starter's deduction, € 2.123 for 2022, if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (tax-neutral return from a private limited company, geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) if that business was started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (same source). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.10 Starter's deduction at state pension age: € 1.062

At state pension age at the start of the year the starter's deduction is halved together with the rest of the deduction under art. 3.76 lid 4, which gives € 1.062 for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.11 Starter's deduction prior-years condition

The prior-years test is: not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, with the self-employed person's deduction applied at most twice in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.12 Starter's deduction at most 3 times in the first 5 years

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). A year in which the profit cap sets the self-employed person's deduction to nil still counts as a year with the self-employed person's deduction for this count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5).

### 3.13 Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Art. 3.78a: the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong scheme, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached state pension age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from a disability or accident insurance policy (invaliditeits- of ongevallenverzekering). Amount: € 12.000 if not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if applied for one year, € 4.000 if applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then, and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### 3.14 Research and development deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 13.360

Art. 3.77 gives the research and development deduction (R&D deduction) as part of the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74, with a base amount of € 13.360 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.77). The amounts are indexed per year. The article applies 2020 to 2027.

### 3.15 S&O starter increase: € 6.684

The base amount is raised by a starter's increase (startersverhoging) of € 6.684 for 2022 for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.77 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.16 S&O conditions

FISIN adds that the S&O deduction requires an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.17 SME profit exemption: 14%

Art. 3.79a: the SME profit exemption is 14% of the total profit as an entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.79a). Art. 3.2: taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, so the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption (same source, art. 3.2). No hours criterion is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none. No exemption (vrijstelling) applies on profit as a co-entitled party. In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing), see 3.24 and 3.25. The taxable profit from business is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, but the checked sources verify that interaction for 2020 and 2021 only (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente).

### 3.18 Deduction for assisting partner bands

Art. 3.78: the deduction for assisting partner applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.78). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.78 lid 2, with the same text in the 2020 to 2027 versions, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation, profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.19 Partner pay below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.16). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.20 Discontinuation deduction: € 3.630 maximum

Art. 3.79: the discontinuation deduction applies to profit realized from or on the cessation of one or more entire businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by a discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.79). For a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That subparagraph (onderdeel) was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and that there is no deduction on profit as a co-entitled party (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.21 Old-age reserve addition percentage: 9,44%

Art. 3.67: the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and has not reached state pension age at the start of the calendar year may add to the old-age reserve, so the addition is optional, and FISIN 2020 words it as "U kunt elk jaar aan de oudedagsreserve toevoegen, maar dit is niet verplicht" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.67, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The hours criterion is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68: the addition is 9,44% of the profit as an entrepreneur, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of the year exceeds the old-age reserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. An order in council (algemene maatregel van bestuur, AMvB) may change the percentage. For each year that the pension reference age (pensioenrichtleeftijd) changes, the percentage is lowered by 0,36 percentage point. The change must be published at least a year in advance. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.67 to 3.72). 2022 is the last year with art. 3.67 to 3.72 in force, because those articles lapsed on 1 January 2023, printed as "Vervallen per 01-01-2023" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). FISIN 2022 itself does not say that 2022 is the last addition year, see section 7.

### 3.22 Old-age reserve addition maximum: € 9.632

Art. 3.68 lid 1 caps the addition at € 9.632 for 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.68, and FISIN 2022 agrees). Art. 3.69 lets the addition be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year, where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from an annuity formed from an old-age reserve, capped at the business capital at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71 defines business capital as the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the old-age reserve, corrected for a reversion reserve (terugkeerreserve). Art. 3.72 covers more than one business. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### 3.23 Old-age reserve into an annuity: deadline 1 July 2023

For converting the old-age reserve into an annuity, the premium must be paid or the deposit made in 2022 or before 1 July 2023, and the amount is added to the 2022 profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The page prints "20223", a typo for 2022, see section 7. The decrease rules that applied to a reserve on 31 December 2022 are kept in force by art. 10a.29: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the business capital at year end if the business or part of it was discontinued in the year, state pension age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year, and decreases are added to profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 10a.29). Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return (same source). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### 3.24 Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 9,5% above € 69.398

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10). For 2022 the percentage is 9,5% and the threshold is € 69.398 (same source, art. 2.10 lid 2).

### 3.25 Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption: 40%

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 2.10). For 2022 that is 49,50% minus 9,5%, so 40% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 2.10).
