## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Enforcement regime: unchanged. The handhavingsmoratorium with the aanwijzing route was in force in 2021 and in 2022, including the rule that kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.
- Webmodule: in 2021 the pilot ran from 11 January 2021, for 6 months, and ended on 10 July 2021. In 2022 the pilot had ended, the online pilot environment stayed active, with no rechtskracht during or after the pilot. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Policy: in 2021 the zevende voortgangsbrief (seventh progress letter) of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 and left decisions on the webmodule and the moratorium to the next cabinet. In 2022 the Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the kabinetsreactie of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen handhaving and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- New in 2022: the achtste voortgangsbrief of 16 December 2022 named legislation for 1 January 2025, webmodule development, and a rechtsvermoeden tied to an uurtarief with no effect outside arbeidsrecht. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Modelovereenkomsten: unchanged in 2022. The stop on new assessments (6 September 2024), the withdrawal of the vrije vervanging approval (14 August 2023) and the end of use (31 December 2029) all date from after 2022. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- Loondienst test and the opt-out for fictieve dienstbetrekkingen: unchanged. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html.
- Case law: HR 6 November 2020 already applied in 2021. The Deliveroo ruling of 24 March 2023 came after 2022. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the contract, any approved modelovereenkomst and any webmodule outcome with the administration, and record how the work is actually done. An approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst only if the parties work as described, and the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- Keep the administratie for 7 years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 (Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen, General Tax Act) says administratieplichtigen (persons with an administration duty) keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (profit from business) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (result from other activities). The text is the same in the version for 1 January 2020 and the version for 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
- Basisgegevens for the administration include the grootboek (general ledger), debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie (debtors and creditors records), voorraadadministratie (stock records), in- and verkoopadministratie (purchase and sales records), loonadministratie (payroll records), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- If the Belastingdienst gives an aanwijzing in a non-kwaadwillend case, one of the two follow-up routes is to process the relationship as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months. That puts the pay in the payroll records and the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.
- A modelovereenkomst or a webmodule outcome says nothing by itself about ondernemerschap for the IB return or the btw position. The webmodule is not a legal decision and gives no rights, and not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.
- After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action at the opdrachtnemer for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie. Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. The richtlijnen behind this exist since 2023, so this is context for years from 2023, not a 2022 rule. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

Open points:

- VBAR amount for the first application. Artikel III lid 2 of the VBAR law says the applicable amount for the first application after entry into force is given by ministeriële regeling. That regeling was not found on 11 October 2026, so the amount for the first application period is unknown. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. What would settle it: publication of the ministeriële regeling.
- HR Uber date and ECLI. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. What would settle it: a fetched copy of the ruling.

Proposed or not applicable to 2022:

- The 2022 policy aim to lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier (coalition agreement 10 January 2022, kabinetsreactie 24 June 2022) and the achtste voortgangsbrief of 16 December 2022 were proposed policy in 2022. The moratorium end became fact on 1 January 2025. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.
- VBAR bill 36 783: adopted-not-yet-in-force on 11 October 2026, in force on 31 December 2026, and not law in 2022. Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html.
- BAZ bill 36 912: proposed, and on 11 October 2026 awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.
- Zelfstandigenwet: proposed, consultation open until 29 October 2026, intended January 2028. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers.
- The boetebeleid for 2025 to 2027 is enforcement policy, not statute. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- The ambition to integrate DBA enforcement into regular handhaving from 2027 is an ambition in the 2026 plan. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

Settled, not open:

- € 36 against € 38: both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, based on the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 (€ 14,71). Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026: both true. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.
- Regular DBA handhaving start date: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026, and the 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026 and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

No open conflicts between the sources for 2022.
