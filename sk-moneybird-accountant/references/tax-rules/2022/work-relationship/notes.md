## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Enforcement regime: unchanged. The enforcement moratorium with the instruction (aanwijzing) route was in force in 2021 and in 2022, including the rule that cases in bad faith (kwaadwillend) could get correction obligations, supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and fines (boetes). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.
- Web module: in 2021 the pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021. In 2022 the pilot had ended, the online pilot environment stayed active, with no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Policy: in 2021 the seventh progress letter (zevende voortgangsbrief) of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 and left decisions on the web module and the moratorium to the next cabinet. In 2022 the Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the cabinet response (kabinetsreactie) of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen enforcement (handhaving) and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- New in 2022: the eighth progress letter (achtste voortgangsbrief) of 16 December 2022 named legislation for 1 January 2025, web module development and a legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) tied to an hourly rate (uurtarief) with no effect outside labour law (arbeidsrecht). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten): unchanged in 2022. The stop on new assessments (6 September 2024), the withdrawal of the free substitution (vrije vervanging) approval (14 August 2023) and the end of use (31 December 2029) all date from after 2022. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- Employment test and the opt-out for deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen): unchanged. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html.
- Case law: Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) 6 November 2020 already applied in 2021. The Deliveroo ruling of 24 March 2023 came after 2022. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the contract, any approved model agreement and any web module outcome with the administration, and record how the work is actually done. An approved model agreement means no employment (loondienst) only if the parties work as described, and the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- Keep the records (administratie) for 7 years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 (General Tax Act, Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen) says persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). The text is the same in the version for 1 January 2020 and the version for 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
- Basic records (basisgegevens) for the administration include the general ledger (grootboek), debtors and creditors records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- If the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives an instruction in a case without bad faith, one of the two follow-up routes is to process the relationship as an employment relationship in the payroll tax return, usually within 3 months. That puts the pay in the payroll records and the payroll taxes at the client (opdrachtgever). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.
- A model agreement or a web module outcome says nothing by itself about entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap) for the income tax return or the VAT position. The web module is not a legal decision and gives no rights, and not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax or VAT. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.
- After payroll tax corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action at the contractor (opdrachtnemer) for earlier years aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the payroll tax correction. The guidelines (richtlijnen) behind this exist since 2023, so this is context for years from 2023, not a 2022 rule. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

Open points:

- VBAR amount for the first application. Artikel III lid 2 of the VBAR law says the applicable amount for the first application after entry into force is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). That regulation was not found on 11 October 2026, so the amount for the first application period is unknown. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. What would settle it: publication of the ministerial regulation.
- HR Uber date and ECLI. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. What would settle it: a fetched copy of the ruling.

Proposed or not applicable to 2022:

- The 2022 policy aim to lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier (coalition agreement 10 January 2022, cabinet response (kabinetsreactie) of 24 June 2022) and the eighth progress letter (achtste voortgangsbrief) of 16 December 2022 were proposed policy in 2022. The moratorium end became fact on 1 January 2025. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.
- VBAR bill 36 783: adopted-not-yet-in-force on 11 October 2026, in force on 31 December 2026 and not law in 2022. Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html.
- BAZ bill 36 912: proposed, and on 11 October 2026 awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): proposed, consultation open until 29 October 2026, intended January 2028. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers.
- The penalty policy (boetebeleid) for 2025 to 2027 is enforcement policy, not statute. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- The ambition to integrate DBA enforcement into regular enforcement (handhaving) from 2027 is an ambition in the 2026 plan. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

Settled, not open:

- € 36 against € 38: both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, based on the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 (€ 14,71). Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- BAZ report date (verslag), 27 May against 29 May 2026: both are correct. The written contribution for the report (inbreng verslag) was submitted on 27 May and the report (verslag, 36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.
- Regular enforcement start date: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026, and the 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026 and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

No open conflicts between the sources for 2022.
