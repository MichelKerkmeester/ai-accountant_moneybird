## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

The Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet DBA, Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) is the Act of 3 February 2016. It entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, declaration of the work relationship). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

One correction matters here. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

For 2022 the framework applied to each engagement. Filing or deadline step: no separate filing follows from the law itself. The qualification of an engagement is tested when the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) asks or when a party goes to court, and the enforcement route of 3.5 sits on top of it.

### 3.2 Employment test (loondiensttoets)

Employment (loondienst) has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform the work personally (persoonlijke arbeid), and pay for the work. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

When this test is weighed, the courts look among others at the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding (inbedding) in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur (ondernemer). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Who it applies to: every client (opdrachtgever) and contractor. One limit matters for the books. Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. So a good outcome on this test is not a statement of entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap) for the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) or the VAT position.

### 3.3 Persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden)

The employment page lists the deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). Among them are contractors for work (aannemers van werk) and helpers, agents, artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees, interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors working through an intermediary (intermediair). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

A person treated as an employee (gelijkgestelde) is usually someone who works 2 or more days a week for the same client and earns at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (minimumloon). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. Who it applies to: a self-employed person without employees (ZZP, zelfstandige zonder personeel) with one client can meet this test. Exception: home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing, see 3.4. Interaction: this deeming rule sits next to the general employment test of 3.2 on the same page.

### 3.4 Opt-out from the deemed employment relationship

The Decree of 8 April 2016, Stb. 2016, 165 lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship for home workers, persons treated as employees and artists by agreement. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

Conditions: the exclusion is made in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. Consequence: the contractor then has no entitlement to ZW (Ziektewet, sickness benefit), WIA (Work and Income (Capacity for Work) Act (Wet werk en inkomen naar arbeidsvermogen), disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering)) and WW (Unemployment Insurance Act (werkloosheidswet), unemployment benefit). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

Interaction: the opt-out is for the listed deemed employment relationships. The general employment test of 3.2 remains a separate test, and the enforcement route of 3.5 is separate again. Deadline step: the written exclusion must be in the contract before the first payment, so it cannot be added after the work has started and pay has flowed.

### 3.5 Enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium)

Until 1 January 2025 the enforcement moratorium applied. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. In a case without bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration first gave an instruction (aanwijzing): reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Bad faith (kwaadwillend) means deliberately creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment (opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan), knowing or able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) cases could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and fines (boetes). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Background dates: the second restriction of the moratorium exception (enforcement when instructions are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020) and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were both announced in the third progress letter (derde voortgangsbrief) of 24 June 2019. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Deadline step: the 3 months to follow the instruction is the practical term. The correction obligation, if it comes, runs from the date of the instruction itself.

### 3.6 Policy 2022 (beleid)

The Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the cabinet response (kabinetsreactie) of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen enforcement (handhaving) and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

The eighth progress letter (achtste voortgangsbrief) of 16 December 2022 named legislation for 1 January 2025, web module (webmodule) development, a legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) tied to an hourly rate (uurtarief) with no effect outside labour law (arbeidsrecht), and a moratorium lifted at the latest (uiterlijk) 1 January 2025. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Status: this was proposed policy in 2022. It became fact later, see 3.17. No filing step follows from a policy letter.

### 3.7 Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten)

Who: a client and a contractor who want certainty in advance about the qualification of the work. Condition: working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment (loondienst), but only if the parties work as described. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.

Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer. The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.

The 2025 tranche enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) adds that the model agreements are honoured to the end of 2029 after the Van Oostenbruggen motion (motie Van Oostenbruggen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. The approval of the model agreements for free substitution (modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.

For 2022: the effect applied, and the stop, the withdrawal and the 2029 end date all fall after 2022. Interaction: see 3.9 for the Deliveroo ruling and 3.2 for the fact test that decides the qualification.

### 3.8 Web module for the assessment of the employment relationship (beoordeling arbeidsrelatie)

The web module is for clients with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking), an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

It is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a bv (besloten vennootschap, private limited company), for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon), or for a foreign contractor. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active, with no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with a client declaration (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. In 2022 the environment was available after the pilot, still without legal force. No filing step follows from a web module outcome.

### 3.9 Case law on the employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst)

Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of the intention of the parties (partijbedoeling). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Supreme Court 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), contains a catalogue of circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) that names integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), and free substitution not as an absolute element. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

For 2022: the 2020 ruling applied. The Deliveroo ruling came on 24 March 2023, after the 2022 tax year. Interaction: the free substitution approval of model agreements was withdrawn after Deliveroo, see 3.7. No filing step follows from a court ruling.

### 3.10 HR Uber

HR Uber (preliminary questions, prejudiciële vragen): no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on enforcement (handhaving). Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid.

Open: the date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. They are UNVERIFIED, see section 7. This ruling falls outside 2022 in any case.

### 3.11 VBAR bill 36 783

This is not 2022 law. In 2022 the bill did not yet exist. The internet consultation (internetconsultatie) of the original Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) (the bill for a legal presumption of an employment contract based on an hourly rate, rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van een uurtarief) started on 6 October 2023, with intended start 1 July 2025. It went to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024, with the critical advice published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and on 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer). The memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part. The House of Representatives adopted it on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Senate on 16 June 2026 by a sitting and standing vote (zitten en opstaan). It became the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026, and enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Decree of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone of in force (In werking) before 1 January 2027. Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html.

Effect: the worker (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Status on 11 October 2026: adopted but not yet in force. For 2022 it is not applicable. Deadline step: none for the 2022 administration.

### 3.12 VBAR legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden)

New art. 7:610aa BW, the Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek). Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract. Lid 2: the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment (SZW, Sociale Zaken en Werkgelegenheid) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 of the Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, WML), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html.

Effect: the worker can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Conditions and exceptions: the private exception of lid 3 is the only exception named in the checked sources. For 2022 this did not apply, the rules apply from 31 December 2026. The € 36 is the base text at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, see 3.13.

### 3.13 VBAR hourly rate (uurtarief)

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains the € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (excl. btw) at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html.

Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at the reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/.

So € 36 is the statutory base text at the 2025 reference date and € 38 is the indexed amount at the 2026 reference date. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers. This point is settled, both amounts are right for their own reference date, see section 7. For 2022 none of this applied.

### 3.14 VBAR first application (eerste toepassing)

Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation. That ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) was not found on 11 October 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED.

Practical effect: do not assume that either € 36 or € 38 is the amount for the first application period. See section 7 for what would settle it. For 2022 this did not apply.

### 3.15 BAZ bill 36 912

Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), bill 36 912 (a proposed insurance scheme for self-employed persons, zelfstandigen). The bill was submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026. A technical briefing (technische briefing) followed on 13 May 2026, the written contribution for the report (inbreng verslag) on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the report (verslag) was published on 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 11 October 2026 the bill was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

Docket summary: about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period (wachttijd) of about one year, an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

The link to the low hourly rate presumption: the € 36 calculation includes a 25% surcharge for disability insurance and pension, while bill 36 912 is a separate proposed arrangement for self-employed persons. The checked sources do not state a direct legal link between the two. Status proposed, and for 2022 it is not applicable.

### 3.16 Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet)

Proposed. The internet consultation was open until 29 October 2026, with intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) January 2028. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers.

Two tests: the self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets), covering self-invoicing, registration with the Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK), number of clients and provisions against business risks (ondernemersrisico's), and the work relationship test (werkrelatietoets), covering freedom to organise the work, own account and risk. Meeting both and working that way gives a safe harbour (veilige haven). Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers. For 2022 not applicable, and on 11 October 2026 still proposed.

### 3.17 End of the moratorium (einde moratorium)

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions (aanwijzingen) are given. False self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Interaction with 2022: in 2022 the moratorium of 3.5 was in force, so this regime did not yet apply. The eighth progress letter of 16 December 2022 already named 1 January 2025 as the end, see 3.6.

### 3.18 Penalty policy (boetebeleid) 2025 to 2027

2025: no default penalties (verzuimboetes) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent (opzet) for the qualification of employment relationships (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266). For corrections over 2025 this stays so. 2026: no default penalties, penalties for gross negligence or intent possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections over 2026. 2027: the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing, no default penalties, in principle starting with a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek)) lapse on 1 January 2027. This is enforcement policy, not statute. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. For 2022 this is not applicable.

### 3.19 Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving)

The ambition to integrate enforcement into regular enforcement: the 2025 tranche plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. Check Corrected, see section 7. For 2022 this is a later development.

### 3.20 Interaction between income tax and VAT (doorwerking IH en OB)

Guidelines (richtlijnen) on the knock-on effect to income tax (IH, inkomstenbelasting) and VAT (OB, omzetbelasting) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the payroll taxes at the client. After payroll tax corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action at the contractor for earlier years aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the payroll tax correction. A contractor in regular income tax or VAT supervision has the most recent open profit return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. For 2022: the guidelines date from 2023, so they are not a 2022 rule. In 2022 the moratorium of 3.5 governed the approach.

### 3.21 Preliminary consultation (vooroverleg)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes a position (standpunt) on a complete request for preliminary consultation (verzoek vooroverleg). The position gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The target period (streeftermijn) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A preliminary consultation checklist exists. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. For 2022: the checked sources place this in the enforcement plans for 2025 and 2026.
