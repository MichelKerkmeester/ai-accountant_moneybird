## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the employment relationship part of the 2022 rules: the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet DBA, Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) and the employment test (loondiensttoets), the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) with the instruction (aanwijzing) route, model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) and the web module for the assessment of the employment relationship (Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie). The year's rules were enacted and in force (in werking), and the main 2022 development was policy: the Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the eighth progress letter (achtste voortgangsbrief) of 16 December 2022 announced stronger enforcement (handhaving) and the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium) per 1 January 2025 or earlier. The later instruments named in the checked sources, the VBAR bill with the low hourly rate legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) and the BAZ bill, were not law in 2022, because the VBAR internet consultation (internetconsultatie) started on 6 October 2023 and the BAZ bill was submitted on 24 March 2026.
