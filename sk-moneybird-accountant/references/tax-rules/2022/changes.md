# Changes tax year 2022

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2022 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. Unique to 2022

- The corona bijzonder uitstel build-up window closed on 31 March 2022 and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 October 2022 with the first term due on 31 October 2022. [btw](btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing rose from 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021 to 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The below-AOW first rate fell from 37,10% to 37,07% and the top boundary moved from € 68.507 to € 69.398. [inkomstenbelasting](inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022. [zvw](zvw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The gemengde kosten drempel rose from € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 gets 6% off capped at € 2.100, so 16% up to € 35.000, against 10% off capped at € 4.000 for a 2021 admission. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 is the last year for an addition to the oudedagsreserve, with a maximum of € 9.632 against € 9.395 in 2021. [pensioen-en-aov](pensioen-en-aov/notes.md#5-changes)
