# Changes tax year 2022

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2022 topic files records. ## 1. OVERVIEW

This note lists what changed for tax year 2022. Section 2 holds the changes unique to 2022 and section 3 the generated table of every change by topic.

---

## 2. UNIQUE TO 2022

- The corona special deferral (bijzonder uitstel) build-up window closed on 31 March 2022 and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 October 2022 with the first instalment due on 31 October 2022. [VAT](vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022. [entrepreneur's allowance](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) rose from 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021 to 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022. [entrepreneur's allowance](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The first rate below state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) fell from 37,10% to 37,07% and the top boundary moved from € 68.507 to € 69.398. [income tax](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022. [Zvw](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) rose from € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022. [costs and car](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto) first admitted in 2022 gets 6% off capped at € 2.100, so 16% up to € 35.000, against 10% off capped at € 4.000 for a 2021 admission. [costs and car](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 is the last year for an addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve), with a maximum of € 9.632 against € 9.395 in 2021. [pension and AOV](pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
