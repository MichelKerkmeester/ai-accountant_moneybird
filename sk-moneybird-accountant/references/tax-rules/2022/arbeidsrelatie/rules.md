## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

The Wet DBA (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, the Act on deregulating the assessment of work relationships) is the Wet van 3 februari 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, declaration of the work relationship). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

One correction matters here. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking (deemed employment relationship) for thuiswerkers (home workers), gelijkgestelden (persons treated as employees) and artiesten (artists) by agreement. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

For 2022 the Wet DBA framework applied to each engagement. Filing or deadline step: no separate filing follows from the law itself. The qualification of an engagement is tested when the Belastingdienst asks or when a party goes to court, and the enforcement route of 3.5 sits on top of it.

### 3.2 Loondiensttoets

Loondienst (employment) has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid (personal work), and pay for the work. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

When this test is weighed, the courts look among others at the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer (contractor) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Who it applies to: every opdrachtgever (client) and opdrachtnemer. One limit matters for the books. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer (entrepreneur) for income tax or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. So a good outcome on this test is not a statement of ondernemerschap for the IB return or the btw position.

### 3.3 Gelijkgestelden

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen (deemed employment relationships). Among them are aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden, stagiairs (interns), thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair (intermediary). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

A gelijkgestelde is usually someone who works 2 or more days a week for the same client and earns at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. Who it applies to: a ZZP (zelfstandige zonder personeel, self-employed person without employees) with one client can meet this test. Exception: thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing, see 3.4. Interaction: this deeming rule sits next to the general loondienst test of 3.2 on the same page.

### 3.4 Opt-out fictieve dienstbetrekking

The Besluit van 8 april 2016, Stb. 2016, 165 lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

Conditions: the exclusion is made in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. Consequence: the opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW (Ziektewet, sickness benefit), WIA (Wet werk en inkomen naar arbeidsvermogen, disability benefit) and WW (Werkloosheidswet, unemployment benefit). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

Interaction: the opt-out is for the listed fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. The general loondienst test of 3.2 remains a separate test, and the enforcement route of 3.5 is separate again. Deadline step: the written exclusion must be in the contract before the first payment, so it cannot be added after the work has started and pay has flowed.

### 3.5 Handhavingsmoratorium

Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium (enforcement moratorium) applied. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. In a non-kwaadwillend (not in bad faith) case the Belastingdienst (the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration) first gave an aanwijzing (instruction): reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige (self-employed person), or process it as a dienstbetrekking (employment relationship) in the loonaangifte (payroll return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting (correction obligation) applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan (deliberately creating or continuing a situation of evident apparent self-employment), knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (additional payroll tax assessments) and boetes (fines). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Background dates: the second restriction of the moratorium exception (enforcement when aanwijzingen are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020) and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were both announced in the derde voortgangsbrief (third progress letter) of 24 June 2019. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Deadline step: the 3 months to follow the aanwijzing is the practical term. The correctieverplichting, if it comes, runs from the date of the aanwijzing itself.

### 3.6 Beleid 2022

The Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the kabinetsreactie (cabinet response) of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen handhaving and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

The achtste voortgangsbrief (eighth progress letter) of 16 December 2022 named legislation for 1 January 2025, webmodule development, a rechtsvermoeden (legal presumption) tied to an uurtarief (hourly rate) with no effect outside arbeidsrecht (employment law), and a moratorium lifted uiterlijk (at the latest) 1 January 2025. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

Status: this was proposed policy in 2022. It became fact later, see 3.17. No filing step follows from a policy letter.

### 3.7 Modelovereenkomsten

Who: an opdrachtgever and an opdrachtnemer who want certainty in advance about the qualification of the work. Condition: working under an approved modelovereenkomst (model agreement) means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.

Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.

The tranche 2025 enforcement plan adds that the modelovereenkomsten are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging (free replacement of the worker) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.

For 2022: the effect applied, and the stop, the withdrawal and the 2029 end date all fall after 2022. Interaction: see 3.9 for the Deliveroo ruling and 3.2 for the fact test that decides the qualification.

### 3.8 Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon (legal person) such as a bv (besloten vennootschap, private limited company), for a bemiddelaar (intermediary) or tussenpersoon (agent), or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active, with no rechtskracht (legal force) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring (client declaration) as one outcome. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. In 2022 the environment was available after the pilot, still without legal force. No filing step follows from a webmodule outcome.

### 3.9 Case law arbeidsovereenkomst

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (the intention of the parties). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), contains an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding (embedding) and ondernemerschap (entrepreneurship), and vrije vervanging not as an absolute element. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

For 2022: the 2020 ruling applied. The Deliveroo ruling came on 24 March 2023, after the 2022 tax year. Interaction: the vrije vervanging approval of modelovereenkomsten was withdrawn after Deliveroo, see 3.7. No filing step follows from a court ruling.

### 3.10 HR Uber

HR Uber (prejudiciële vragen, preliminary questions): no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten (points of view), so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid.

Open: the date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. They are UNVERIFIED, see section 7. This ruling falls outside 2022 in any case.

### 3.11 VBAR wetsvoorstel 36 783

This is not 2022 law. In 2022 the bill did not yet exist. The internet consultation of the original VBAR (the bill for a rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van een uurtarief, a legal presumption of an employment contract based on an hourly rate) started on 6 October 2023, with intended start 1 July 2025. It went to the Raad van State (Council of State) on 21 June 2024, with the critical advice published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend (submitted) on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and on 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. The nota van wijziging (memorandum of amendment) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking (clarification) part. The Tweede Kamer adopted it on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (sitting and standing vote). It became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026, and enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan (Recovery and Resilience Plan) milestone of in force before 1 January 2027. Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html.

Effect: the werkende (worker) can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst (employment contract). It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Status on 11 October 2026: adopted but not yet in force. For 2022 it is not applicable. Deadline step: none for the 2022 administration.

### 3.12 VBAR rechtsvermoeden

New art. 7:610aa BW (Burgerlijk Wetboek, Civil Code). Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW (Sociale Zaken en Werkgelegenheid, Social Affairs and Employment) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag (WML, the Minimum Wage Act), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling (ministerial regulation). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html.

Effect: the werkende can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Conditions and exceptions: the private exception of lid 3 is the only exception named in the checked sources. For 2022 this did not apply, the rules apply from 31 December 2026. The € 36 is the base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, see 3.13.

### 3.13 VBAR uurtarief

The memorie van toelichting (explanatory memorandum) explains the € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag (surcharge) for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw (excluding VAT) at peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html.

Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/.

So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers. This point is settled, both amounts are right for their own peildatum, see section 7. For 2022 none of this applied.

### 3.14 VBAR eerste toepassing

Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. That ministeriële regeling was not found on 11 October 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED.

Practical effect: do not assume that either € 36 or € 38 is the amount for the first application period. See section 7 for what would settle it. For 2022 this did not apply.

### 3.15 BAZ wetsvoorstel 36 912

BAZ, bill 36 912 (a proposed insurance scheme for zelfstandigen, self-employed persons). The bill was ingediend on 24 March 2026. A technische briefing (technical briefing) followed on 13 May 2026, the inbreng verslag (input for the report) on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the verslag was published on 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 11 October 2026 the bill was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag (memorandum in response to the report), with no debate and no vote. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

Docket summary: about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

The link to the low hourly rate presumption: the € 36 calculation includes a 25% opslag for AOV and pension, while bill 36 912 is a separate proposed arrangement for zelfstandigen. The checked sources do not state a direct legal link between the two. Status proposed, and for 2022 it is not applicable.

### 3.16 Zelfstandigenwet

Proposed. The internet consultation was open until 29 October 2026, with beoogde inwerkingtreding (intended entry into force) January 2028. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers.

Two tests: the zelfstandigentoets (self-employment test: self-invoicing, KVK registration with the Kamer van Koophandel, number of opdrachtgevers, provisions against ondernemersrisico's) and the werkrelatietoets (work relationship test: freedom to organise the work, own account and risk). Meeting both and working that way gives a veilige haven (safe harbour). Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers. For 2022 not applicable, and on 11 October 2026 still proposed.

### 3.17 Einde moratorium

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid (apparent self-employment) can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

Interaction with 2022: in 2022 the moratorium of 3.5 was in force, so this regime did not yet apply. The achtste voortgangsbrief of 16 December 2022 already named 1 January 2025 as the end, see 3.6.

### 3.18 Boetebeleid 2025 to 2027

2025: no verzuimboetes (default fines) and no vergrijpboetes (intent fines) for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266). For correcties over 2025 this stays so. 2026: no verzuimboetes, vergrijpboetes possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. 2027: the remaining parts of the zachte landing (soft landing, no verzuimboetes, in principle starting with a bedrijfsbezoek, a business visit) lapse on 1 January 2027. This is enforcement policy, not statute. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. For 2022 this is not applicable.

### 3.19 Reguliere handhaving

The ambition to integrate DBA enforcement into regular handhaving: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. Check Corrected, see section 7. For 2022 this is a later development.

### 3.20 Doorwerking IH en OB

Richtlijnen doorwerking (guidelines on the knock-on effect to) IH (inkomstenbelasting, income tax) and OB (omzetbelasting, VAT) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action at the opdrachtnemer for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie (qualification at source). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte (profit return) or tijdvakken (periods) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. For 2022: the richtlijnen date from 2023, so they are not a 2022 rule. In 2022 the moratorium of 3.5 governed the approach.

### 3.21 Vooroverleg

The Belastingdienst takes a standpoint on a complete verzoek vooroverleg (request for preliminary consultation). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn (target term) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. For 2022: the checked sources place this in the enforcement plans for 2025 and 2026.
