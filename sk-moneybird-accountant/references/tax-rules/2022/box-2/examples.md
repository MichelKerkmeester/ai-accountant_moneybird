## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Every product below is written with two decimals. No product in these examples needs rounding, so no step is marked rounded or rounded down.

### Official example: an unoffset loss converted into a tax credit (belastingkorting) (FISIN 2022)

Source: [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang). Shares were sold in 2021 with an unoffset loss of € 20.000. On request the loss is converted in 2023 into a tax credit of € 5.380,00 at the 26,9% of art. 4.53 lid 2, and the remainder can be used until 2030. Arithmetic: 26,9% × € 20.000 = € 5.380,00.

### Calculated example: dividend of € 100.000

Inputs: a substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) receives a dividend of € 100.000 in 2022 and has no deductible costs.

Step 1, the dividend is part of the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen), so box 2 income is € 100.000.

Step 2, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

Dividend tax withheld by the company is not deducted but credited as a prepayment (voorheffing), so the amount to pay is € 26.900,00 minus that prepayment. No dividend tax rate is in the checked sources, so no prepayment amount is computed here.

### Calculated example: sale of shares with notary costs and selling costs

Inputs: purchase price € 100.000, notary costs on the purchase € 1.500, sale price € 250.000, selling costs € 2.500.

Step 1, acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs): € 100.000 + € 1.500 = € 101.500.

Step 2, transfer price (overdrachtsprijs): € 250.000 − € 2.500 = € 247.500.

Step 3, disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel): € 247.500 − € 101.500 = € 146.000.

Step 4, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 146.000 = € 39.274,00.

### Calculated example: offset of a 2022 loss

Inputs: a loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) of € 20.000 arises in 2022 and there is no box 2 income in 2021.

Step 1, the loss is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment.

Step 2, it offsets box 2 income of 2021 and, if not used, of 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2027 and 2028, in the order the losses arose.

Step 3, assume box 2 income of € 30.000 in 2023. Then € 20.000 of the loss offsets it and € 30.000 − € 20.000 = € 10.000 remains taxable.

Step 4, box 2 tax for 2023: 26,9% × € 10.000 = € 2.690,00, because the one flat rate of 26,9% still applies in 2023.

### Calculated example: flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) for a vbi

Inputs: value at the start of 2022 € 200.000, percentage 5,53%, no other regular benefits from those shares, no deductible costs, shares held for the whole year.

Step 1, flat-rate benefit: 5,53% × € 200.000 = € 11.060,00.

Step 2, box 2 income is € 11.060,00.

Step 3, box 2 tax: 26,9% × € 11.060 = € 2.975,14.

If the shares were held for part of the year, the flat-rate benefit is time-apportioned. It is also reduced by other regular benefits from those shares and never falls below nil.

### Calculated example: excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) as proposed on 25 March 2022

The excessive borrowing fiction is not in force in 2022, so no amount is due for 2022. This example illustrates the bill as it stood after the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) of 25 March 2022.

Inputs: debts from the own private limited company (BV) of € 800.000 at the end of the year and a maximum of € 700.000.

Step 1, excess: € 800.000 − € 700.000 = € 100.000.

Step 2, the box 2 rate is 26,9% in 2022 and in 2023. At that rate the excess would give 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

The law entered into force on 1 January 2023, the first year of the fiction. This is a calculation of the bill, not of a 2022 rule.

### Calculated example: customary salary (gebruikelijk loon)

Inputs: the most comparable wage is € 60.000, the highest wage within the body is € 52.000, the fixed amount is € 48.000 and the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is more than € 5.000.

Step 1, 75% of the comparable wage: 75% × € 60.000 = € 45.000,00.

Step 2, the highest of the three amounts: € 45.000, € 52.000 and € 48.000, so € 52.000.

Step 3, the wage is at least € 52.000.

If the wage for the work for the body and all connected bodies together had been not more than € 5.000, art. 12a would not apply at all.

### Calculated example: ODV with a negative market rate

Inputs: ODV at the start of 2022 € 200.000, market rate -0,410%.

Step 1, increase: -0,410% × € 200.000 = -€ 820,00.

Step 2, balance at the end of 2022: € 200.000,00 − € 820,00 = € 199.180,00.

A negative market rate must also be applied.

### Calculated example: division between fiscal partners

Inputs: fiscal partners for the whole year, box 2 income € 100.000, division 50/50.

Step 1, partner 1: € 50.000, box 2 tax 26,9% × € 50.000 = € 13.450,00.

Step 2, partner 2: € 50.000, box 2 tax 26,9% × € 50.000 = € 13.450,00.

Step 3, total € 26.900,00, which equals 26,9% × € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.

Any other division is allowed as long as the total is 100%, and with a single flat rate in 2022 the total tax in this example does not change.
