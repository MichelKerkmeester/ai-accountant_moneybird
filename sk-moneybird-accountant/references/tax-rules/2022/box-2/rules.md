## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 rate (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

For 2022 there is a single rate of 26,9% on the whole box 2 income and no bracket ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). FISIN 2022 states 26,90% ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The same one-rate structure held from 2020, with 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023. From 1 January 2024 a two-bracket table applies, with 24,5% up to the bracket boundary and 33% above it in 2024, lowered to 31% from 2025 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The second bracket was announced in Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) ([rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024)). The rate applies per taxpayer ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

The tax credit on an unoffset loss is 26,9% in 2022, unchanged from 2021 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.53 lid 2 prints 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts. From the 2024 text the percentage is the one in the first line of the fourth column of the art. 2.12 table, which is 24,5% ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Lid 1 allows the conversion on request if the taxpayer and the partner had no substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) in the calendar year and the year before. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death ([art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang), offset period (art. 4.49)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose ([art. 4.49, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). The loss is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment and is open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)). The text of art. 4.49 is the same in the 2020 to 2027 versions ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (belastingdienst) states that the loss is "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).

### Tax credit period and further conditions (art. 4.53 lid 4)

Lid 3: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit by a decision (beschikking) that is open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Knowledge group position (Kennisgroep standpunt) KG:003:2023:8, published on 26 September 2023 and updated on 22 July 2026, tests the condition for the tax year and not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block the conversion, and an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([Kennisgroep](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)).

### Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the 5% test (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a, or (e) in the 2022 text holds a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit ([art. 4.6, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub e is new in the 2022 text and is not in the 2021 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). In sub d the word "tot" of the 2021 text reads "om" in the 2022 text. Sub f is not part of the 2022 text, it was added later by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) (Stb. 2025, 445), retroactive to 1 January 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Drag-along and carry-along rules (art. 4.7 to 4.10)

Once a taxpayer has a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company are also treated as part of the substantial interest under the drag-along rule (meesleepregeling, art. 4.9). Shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but the taxpayer's partner, or a blood relative or relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner, has one also form part of a substantial interest under the carry-along rule (meetrekregeling, art. 4.10). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner), plus their fiscal partners ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The text of art. 4.7 to 4.10 is identical in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11) and deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) (art. 4.16)

Shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases) ([art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. The art. 4.11 text is the same in the 2020 to 2027 versions. Art. 4.16 lists as a sale: purchase (inkoop) of shares, redemption (afkoop) and purchase of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by universal succession, or a transfer of the shares by universal succession or by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) that are no longer attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The art. 4.16 text is identical in the 2020 to 2027 versions ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Box 2 income of a minor child

The taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

The 5% test uses the wording "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (art. 4.6). Common income components (gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen) of the taxpayer and the partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return (art. 2.17 lid 2). With fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) may be divided, and "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own rate applies to his share ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). No primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two partners, so no combined figure is given here.

### Income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

Income from a substantial interest is regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (art. 4.12). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions. Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit from a vbi (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling, exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling)) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling) (sub e), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing fiction (excessief lenen) (sub f) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap) is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen). Salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The benefit on a transfer is the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) minus the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs). The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Selling costs reduce the transfer price. Special cases are inheritance, gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching 5% during the year and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs). At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Interest on a loan taken to buy the shares is deductible. Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted but credited as a prepayment (voorheffing) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14 lid 1)

The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned. For 2022 the percentage is 5,53% ([art. 4.14 lid 1, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). The percentage is a yearly value, 5,69% in 2021 and 5,53% in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### Inherited substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are not counted as box 2 income on request, up to that amount, and are deducted from the acquisition price ([art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction, which sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). The art. 4.12a text is identical in the 2020 to 2027 versions.

### Segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen, APV) loss deferral

For segregated private assets the loss is deferred and offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rollover routes on request for gift, inheritance and emigration are reported but not verified for 2022, see section 7 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Excessive Borrowing from the Own Company Act (Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap)

The Act of 21 December 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022) is cited as the Excessive Borrowing from the Own Company Act and enters into force on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). Nothing in it applies to 2022. For the years from 2023 the fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the related-persons rule (verbonden personen) in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Under the later texts the maximum is € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, the amount is measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value, and art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 indexation list, so that maximum is not indexed ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the threshold (drempel) "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).

### Excessive borrowing maximum in the bill after the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging)

The third memorandum of amendment of 25 March 2022 set the excessive borrowing maximum at € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1, following the coalition agreement ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)). The bill had proposed € 500.000. The Grinwis c.s. amendment (Amendement Grinwis c.s.) (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles ([Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The checked 2023 text prints € 700.000 in art. 4.14a and the 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Transitional owner-occupied home (eigen woning) debt (eigenwoningschuld) rule (art. 10a.23 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 4.14a lid 6 excludes an owner-occupied home debt to the extent a mortgage right (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company. Art. 10a.23 adds that an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The date 31 December 2022 is the link between the 2022 records and the rules that start on 1 January 2023.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). In 2022 the fixed amount is € 48.000. The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and that is known to the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) and the tax inspector. Lower wage: if the withholding agent demonstrates that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), the comparable wage applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower. The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023).

### Comparable-wage test for the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon)

In the 2020 to 2022 texts the wage from the most comparable employment counts for 75% ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). From the 2023 text it counts for 100% ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The art. 12a rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year. This is lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts and lid 4 from 2023 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Start-up rule for the customary salary (S&O)

For a body with an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage (minimumloon) of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de minimis ceiling (de-minimisplafond) ([art. 12a lid 3, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)). The rule is absent from the 2023 text and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rule could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2022 could run to 2024.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) and box 2

The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit. The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate (marktrente) for 2022

The old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, the average of the monthly U yields (U-rendementen) of the preceding year). For 2022 the rate is -0,410%, the December 2021 value ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)). A negative market rate must also be applied ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)). In the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p ([2020 Wet IB text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) instalments (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The ODV can be used tax-neutral (geruisloos) for an annuity (lijfrente), an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or an annuity investment right (beleggingsrecht) (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. From Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)).

### Pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB) commutation and conversion window (Stb. 2017, 115)

Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wage Tax (loonbelasting) Act text shows art. 38o and art. 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38p lid 5 was later filled again by Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, which is the conversion after the instalments have started described above. The commutation and conversion window ran from 1 April 2017 to 31 December 2019, and V&A 17-007 is marked lapsed (vervallen) ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). The published Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under art. 12 of the Advisory Referendum Act (Wet raadgevend referendum), the consolidated text of Stb. 2017, 115 shows "Geldend van 01-04-2017", and the leniency (coulance) decision of 22 March 2017 let directors and substantial shareholders (dga's) adjust until 30 June 2017 and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). In 2022 the commutation and conversion routes are closed. A remaining pension in own management keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially, and "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

### Acquisition price after PEB waiving (art. 10a.18 lid 3 Wet IB 2001)

Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 10a.18 lid 2 values the ODV in the company with the market rate of art. 38p. Payouts from a pension in own management are income from former employment in box 1.

### Non-residents (art. 7.5)

Art. 7.5 confirms the base for a non-resident. The base is a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) in a Netherlands-established company that is not part of an enterprise ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). A statement that a 2025 decision (beschikking) for partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect could not be confirmed on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.
