## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.6): the 2021 text has sub a to d only, the 2022 text adds sub e, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Substantial interest (art. 4.6 sub d): the word "tot" of the 2021 text became "om" in the 2022 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14 lid 1): 5,69% in 2021 to 5,53% in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964): € 47.000 in 2021 to € 48.000 in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).
- Old-age obligation (ODV) market rate (marktrente): -0,382% for 2021 (December 2020 value) to -0,410% for 2022 (December 2021 value) ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- Start-up rule for the customary salary: the text is unchanged at 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage, but a first use in 2021 could run to 2023 and a first use in 2022 could run to 2024, because the rule could be used at most 3 years and is absent from the 2023 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen): not in force in 2021 and still not in force in 2022, but on 21 December 2022 the law was enacted (Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued 27 December 2022) and it enters into force on 1 January 2023, and the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) of 25 March 2022 set the maximum at € 700.000 in the bill ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)).
- Unchanged in 2022: the box 2 rate of 26,9% with no bracket, the tax credit of 26,9% (art. 4.53 unchanged from 2021), the loss offset period of the previous year and the six following years, the € 5.000 customary salary threshold and the 75% comparable-wage test ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 12a 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Box 2 income lands in the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) return for the year as regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (art. 4.12) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Record a dividend from the private limited company (BV) as a regular benefit, and treat dividend tax on it as a prepayment (voorheffing), not as a cost ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Keep the purchase documents for the shares, including notary costs, for the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) and the sale documents for the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs), because selling costs reduce it ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- A salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon) and is tested against the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) of art. 12a, not against box 2 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Interest on money lent by the shareholder to the company is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- When a loss arises, keep the assessment and the separate loss decision (beschikking), because the loss offsets box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years and the decision is open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).
- For a later conversion of an unoffset loss into a tax credit (belastingkorting), the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), and the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit by a decision open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)).
- Declare the box 2 income of minor children with the parent until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For the company: no excessive borrowing fiction in the 2022 accounts, because the law takes effect on 1 January 2023. Note whether an owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld) on the home existed on 31 December 2022 and whether a mortgage right (recht van hypotheek) was given to the company, because art. 10a.23 uses that date for the exclusion ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- For a remaining pension in own management (PEB): index the accrued rights every year and value the provision actuarially every year if the scheme provided for indexation, because not indexing yearly "kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).
- Increase the old-age obligation (ODV) each year with the market rate, -0,410% for 2022 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- The division of the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) between fiscal partners is chosen in the return ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The act that inserted art. 4.6 sub e is not identified in the checked sources. Only the text change between the 1 January 2021 and the 1 January 2022 versions of art. 4.6 is known ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). What would settle it: the amending act and the Bulletin of Acts and Decrees issue behind the 2022 text of art. 4.6.
- Proposed: the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) maximum in the bill after the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) of 25 March 2022 is € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1 ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)). The bill had proposed € 500.000, and the Grinwis c.s. amendment (Amendement Grinwis c.s.) (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles ([Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The checked 2023 text prints € 700.000 in art. 4.14a while the 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Nothing of this applies to 2022. What would settle the threshold for each year: the Bulletin of Acts and Decrees text of Stb. 2022, 531 and the consolidated Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) text per year ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- UNVERIFIED: the rollover (doorschuiving) list on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. It was not re-fetched on a primary page in the check ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). What would settle it: the 2022 consolidated texts of the rollover articles named in art. 4.11 (art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 and 4.41) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- UNVERIFIED: no primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two fiscal partners. Art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer and only the freedom to divide is stated ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). What would settle it: a primary page with a combined amount, which the check did not find.
- UNVERIFIED: the statement that a 2025 decision (beschikking) for partial non-resident tax liability (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect as art. 7.5 for a non-resident. It was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages ([art. 7.5, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). What would settle it: the text of the decision and a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page on partial non-resident tax liability.
- Reasoning without a source: the statement that the customary salary reduces the residual profit available for a dividend. Only the box 1 character of the wage and the art. 12a test are verified ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Proposed for a later year: the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from the art. 4.12a facility from 1 January 2027. This is not a 2022 rule ([memorie van toelichting](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)).
