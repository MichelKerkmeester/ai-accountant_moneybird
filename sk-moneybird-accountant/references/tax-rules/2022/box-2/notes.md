## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.6): the 2021 text has sub a to d only, the 2022 text adds sub e, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.6 sub d): the word "tot" of the 2021 text became "om" in the 2022 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14 lid 1): 5,69% in 2021 to 5,53% in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).
- Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964): € 47.000 in 2021 to € 48.000 in 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).
- ODV marktrente: -0,382% for 2021 (December 2020 value) to -0,410% for 2022 (December 2021 value) ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- Start-up rule gebruikelijk loon: the text is unchanged at 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage, but a first use in 2021 could run to 2023 and a first use in 2022 could run to 2024, because the rule could be used at most 3 years and is absent from the 2023 text ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Excessief lenen: not in force in 2021 and still not in force in 2022, but on 21 December 2022 the law was enacted (Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued 27 December 2022) and it enters into force on 1 January 2023, and the derde nota van wijziging of 25 March 2022 set the maximum at € 700.000 in the bill ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)).
- Unchanged in 2022: the box 2 rate of 26,9% with no bracket, the belastingkorting of 26,9% (art. 4.53 unchanged from 2021), the loss offset period of the previous year and the six following years, the € 5.000 gebruikelijk loon threshold and the 75% comparable-loon test ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 12a 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Box 2 income lands in the inkomstenbelasting return for the year as reguliere voordelen minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen, minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (art. 4.12) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Record a dividend from the BV as a reguliere voordeel, and treat dividendbelasting on it as a voorheffing, not as a cost ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Keep the purchase documents for the shares, including notary costs, for the verkrijgingsprijs, and the sale documents for the overdrachtsprijs, because selling costs reduce it ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- A salary from the BV is box 1 loon and is tested against the gebruikelijk loon of art. 12a, not against box 2 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Interest on money lent by the shareholder to the BV is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- When a loss arises, keep the assessment and the separate loss beschikking, because the loss offsets box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years and the beschikking is open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).
- For a later conversion of an unoffset loss into a belastingkorting, the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang, and the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking open to objection ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)).
- Declare the box 2 income of minor children with the parent until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For the BV: no excessief-lenen fiction in the 2022 accounts, because the law takes effect on 1 January 2023. Note whether an eigenwoningschuld on the home existed on 31 December 2022 and whether a recht van hypotheek was given to the company, because art. 10a.23 uses that date for the exclusion ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- For a remaining PEB: index the accrued rights every year and value the provision actuarially every year if the scheme provided for indexation, because not indexing yearly "kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).
- Increase the ODV each year with the market rate, -0,410% for 2022 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- The division of the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang between fiscal partners is chosen in the return ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The act that inserted art. 4.6 sub e is not identified in the checked sources. Only the text change between the 1 January 2021 and the 1 January 2022 versions of art. 4.6 is known ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). What would settle it: the amending act and the Staatsblad issue behind the 2022 text of art. 4.6.
- Proposed: the excessief-lenen maximum in the bill after the derde nota van wijziging of 25 March 2022 is € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1 ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)). The bill had proposed € 500.000, and amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles ([Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The checked 2023 text prints € 700.000 in art. 4.14a while the 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Nothing of this applies to 2022. What would settle the threshold for each year: the Staatsblad text of Stb. 2022, 531 and the consolidated Wet IB 2001 text per year ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- UNVERIFIED: the doorschuiving list on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. It was not re-fetched on a primary page in the check ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). What would settle it: the 2022 consolidated texts of the rollover articles named in art. 4.11 (art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 and 4.41) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- UNVERIFIED: no primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two fiscal partners. Art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer and only the freedom to divide is stated ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). What would settle it: a primary page with a combined amount, which the check did not find.
- UNVERIFIED: the statement that a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht has the same effect as art. 7.5 for a non-resident. It was not found on the fetched Belastingdienst pages ([art. 7.5, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). What would settle it: the text of the beschikking and a Belastingdienst page on partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht.
- Reasoning without a source: the statement that the gebruikelijk loon reduces the residual profit available for a dividend. Only the box 1 character of the loon and the art. 12a test are verified ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Proposed for a later year: the Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from the art. 4.12a facility from 1 January 2027. This is not a 2022 rule ([memorie van toelichting](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)).
