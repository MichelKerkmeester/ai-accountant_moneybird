## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the box 1 bands and rates for 2022, the state pension age (AOW age) variants, the general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting), the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting), the income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting), the rate adjustment for deductions (tariefsaanpassing) and the route from profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable box 1 income. The published first-bracket rate combines the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) with the national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) below state pension age, while the statutory table separates those components. The 2022 figures in this file are enacted. The income-related health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) exists alongside the income tax and its calculation belongs to the Zvw reference.
