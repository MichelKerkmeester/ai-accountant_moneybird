## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Every example below uses the section 2 figures. Official examples are copied from the linked source page. Calculated examples are built here from the section 2 figures.

### Box 1 below state pension age (AOW age)

**Calculated example.** Assume a box 1 income of € 80.000 for a taxpayer below state pension age.

1. The published first band runs to € 69.398 at 37,07%.
2. The amount above that boundary is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602.
3. That amount falls in the 49,50% band.
4. Tax plus national insurance contribution before credits: 37,07% × € 69.398 = € 25.725,8386 and 49,50% × € 10.602 = € 5.247,99, together € 30.973,8286.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening

### Box 1 for a full year at state pension age

**Calculated example.** Assume a box 1 income of € 80.000 for a taxpayer born on or after 1 January 1946 who was at state pension age for the whole year.

1. The first band runs to € 35.472 at 19,17%.
2. The second band runs from € 35.472 to € 69.398. Its width is € 69.398 minus € 35.472 = € 33.926, at 37,07%.
3. The amount above € 69.398 is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602, at 49,50%.
4. Tax plus national insurance contribution before credits: 19,17% × € 35.472 = € 6.799,9824, 37,07% × € 33.926 = € 12.576,3682 and 49,50% × € 10.602 = € 5.247,99, together € 24.624,3406.

For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 the first band ends at € 36.409 instead. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10a

### First-bracket rate in the year state pension age is reached

**Calculated example.** The 2022 monthly rates run from 19,17% in January to 35,57% in December. The difference between the highest and the lowest monthly rate is 35,57% minus 19,17% = 16,40 percentage points, applied to the first bracket (eerste schijf) of € 35.472. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening

### National insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling

**Calculated example.** Assume a taxpayer below state pension age with box 1 income of € 80.000, insured for the full year.

1. The national insurance contributions of 27,65% are charged over the contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to the ceiling of € 35.472.
2. 27,65% × € 35.472 = € 9.808,008.

For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 the ceiling is € 36.409. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

**Calculated example.** Assume a taxable box 1 income after deductions of € 60.000 and qualifying deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) of € 20.000.

1. Add those deductions back for the threshold test: € 60.000 plus € 20.000 = € 80.000.
2. The amount above € 69.398 is € 80.000 minus € 69.398 = € 10.602.
3. The increase is 9,5% × € 10.602 = € 1.007,19.
4. The cap is 9,5% × € 20.000 = € 1.900.
5. The lower amount applies, so the illustrative rate adjustment is € 1.007,19.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK)

**Calculated example.** Assume a taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) of € 40.000 for a taxpayer below state pension age.

1. Income above the phase-out point (afbouwpunt) of € 21.317: € 40.000 minus € 21.317 = € 18.683.
2. Reduction: 6,007% × € 18.683 = € 1.122,28781.
3. AHK before statutory rounding: € 2.888 minus € 1.122,28781 = € 1.765,71219.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) below state pension age

**Calculated example.** Assume an earned income (arbeidsinkomen) of € 20.000 for a taxpayer below state pension age.

1. The second band starts at € 10.350 with a fixed amount of € 470.
2. Income in that band: € 20.000 minus € 10.350 = € 9.650.
3. Addition: 28,461% × € 9.650 = € 2.746,4865.
4. AK before rounding: € 470 plus € 2.746,4865 = € 3.216,4865.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Employment tax credit for a full year at state pension age

**Calculated example.** Assume an earned income of € 20.000 for a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year.

1. The second band starts at € 10.350 with a fixed amount of € 244.
2. Income in that band: € 20.000 minus € 10.350 = € 9.650.
3. Addition: 14,718% × € 9.650 = € 1.420,287.
4. AK before rounding: € 244 plus € 1.420,287 = € 1.664,287.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK)

**Calculated example.** Assume an earned income of € 20.000 and that the taxpayer meets the IACK conditions, including the child test.

1. Income above € 5.219: € 20.000 minus € 5.219 = € 14.781.
2. 11,45% × € 14.781 = € 1.692,4245.
3. This is below the maximum of € 2.534.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Payout of unused tax credits (heffingskortingen) to a partner

**Official example.** Born 3 February 1963, € 2.888 times 6,667% is € 193. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

**Official example.** Earned income € 10.000, AK € 455, and the page states that 6,667% gives € 31. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

**Official example.** Born before 1 January 1963: income € 4.000, tax € 1.482, AHK € 2.888, unused € 1.406 paid out against a partner who owes € 10.907 net. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

**Official example.** Part-year contribution liability (premieplicht): AK € 184, income-tax part (belastingdeel) € 47, contribution part (premiedeel) € 138, of which one third is € 46, total € 93. The page uses 9,45% here where it elsewhere uses 9,42%, see section 7. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Payout income limits

**Calculated example.** The 2022 payout income limits are compared against the taxpayer's own box 1 income and the partner's income.

1. Own box 1 income is compared with € 8.885 for people born before 1963, or with € 523 for people born later.
2. Partner income is compared with € 23.700, or € 30.400 at state pension age, or € 25.605 where the partner receives more tax credits.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### IACK payout percentage

No example can be built. The 2022 page states no IACK-specific payout percentage, so the payout percentage for the IACK is unknown and UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen

### Income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen)

**Calculated example.** The below-state-pension-age first-bracket rate can be rebuilt from its components.

1. 17,90% plus 0,10% plus 9,65% = 27,65%.
2. 9,42% plus 27,65% = 37,07%.

This confirms the published combined first-bracket rate. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen

### Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable box 1 income

**Calculated example.** Assume an eligible entrepreneur has a profit of € 60.000, qualifies for the € 6.310 self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), has no other entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and makes no addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve).

1. Profit after the self-employed person's deduction: € 60.000 minus € 6.310 = € 53.690.
2. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 14% × € 53.690 = € 7.516,60.
3. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 53.690 minus € 7.516,60 = € 46.173,40.
4. With no other income or loss to offset, this amount is the business-profit component of the taxable income from work and home.

This example stops before the box 1 tax table and the tax credits. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2

### Entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) values relevant to business profit

**Calculated example.** Assume a profit of € 60.000, a self-employed person's deduction of € 6.310 and a starter's deduction (startersaftrek) of € 2.123.

1. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 6.310 plus € 2.123 = € 8.433.
2. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 minus € 8.433 = € 51.567.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

**Calculated example.** Assume a profit after the entrepreneur's allowance of € 53.690.

1. SME profit exemption: 14% × € 53.690 = € 7.516,60.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling

### Addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

**Calculated example.** Assume a profit of € 60.000 and an entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (urencriterium) and is below state pension age.

1. Addition: 9,44% × € 60.000 = € 5.664.
2. This is below the 2022 maximum of € 9.632, so the addition would be € 5.664 before any reduction for the occupational pension (beroepspensioen) contribution (beroepspensioenpremie).

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans
