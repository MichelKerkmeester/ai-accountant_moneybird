## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below state pension age (AOW age)

For a taxpayer below state pension age, the published box 1 table has 37,07% up to € 69.398 and 49,50% above that amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). The statutory table in art. 2.10 lid 1 holds only the income-tax part and reads 9,42% up to € 35.472, then € 3.341 plus 37,07% up to € 69.398, then € 15.917 plus 49,50% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The published 37,07% equals the 9,42% income-tax part (belastingdeel) plus the national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) of 27,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The first statutory band is income tax (inkomstenbelasting) only and ends at the contribution ceiling. Above the ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the published combined rate, which is why the published table shows a single combined rate up to the top boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The tax on the box 1 balance (saldo) is computed under art. 2.10 lid 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Box 1 for a full year at state pension age

For a taxpayer who was at state pension age for the whole of 2022 and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first rate is 19,17% up to € 35.472, then 37,07% up to € 69.398, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946, art. 2.10a gives a first boundary of € 36.409 with fixed amounts of € 3.429 and € 15.658 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10a). The lower state pension age first-bracket rate follows from the AOW premium not being due from state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). The checked pages state only that the AOW premium stops at state pension age. Whether the Anw (survivor benefit) and Wlz (long-term care) parts continue is not stated as a rule in the checked pages and stays an inference, see section 7.

### First-bracket rate in the year state pension age is reached

In the year in which state pension age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it and rises by about 1,49 points per month from the state pension age rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2022 the monthly rates are January 19,17%, February 20,66%, March 22,15%, April 23,64%, May 25,13%, June 26,62%, July 28,12%, August 29,81%, September 31,10%, October 32,59%, November 34,08% and December 35,57% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening). The first bracket (eerste schijf) runs up to € 35.472 and the second and third brackets keep their normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening).

### National insurance contributions ceiling

The national insurance contributions of 27,65% are charged over the contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to a ceiling. For 2022 that ceiling is € 35.472, or € 36.409 for a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The ceiling equals the end of the first statutory band (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The premium components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). The checked page states that the AOW premium stops at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

When deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) have been deducted, the tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is raised by 9,5% of the amount by which the taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus those deductions exceeds the top bracket boundary of € 69.398. The increase is capped at 9,5% of those deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). Above the boundary, deductions are therefore worth at most 40% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). Those deductions are the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), namely maintenance obligations (onderhoudsverplichtingen) including spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie), weekend expenses for disabled people (weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten), deductible gifts (aftrekbare giften), specific healthcare costs (specifieke zorgkosten) and the remaining personal deductions from earlier years. They also include the owner-occupied home (eigen woning) costs deduction, namely mortgage interest (hypotheekrente), periodic ground rent (periodieke erfpacht), right of superficies (opstal) or long leasehold (beklemming), costs of taking out a mortgage (kosten afsluiten hypotheek) and residual debt costs (restschuldkosten), the tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten), namely the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), the research and development deduction, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), and the exemption for making assets available (vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and is shown on the assessment (aanslag) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The 2022 box 1 page names the rate adjustment but prints no percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening).

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK)

The AHK is granted to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The phase-out base in 2022 is the taxable income from work and home (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer below state pension age the AHK is € 2.888 minus 6,007% of the box 1 income above € 21.317 and reaches nil at € 69.398 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year the AHK is € 1.494 minus 3,106% above € 21.317 and reaches nil at € 69.398 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The phase-out points (afbouwpunten) for the AHK and AK are linked to the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon) ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301), footnote to Table 1).

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) below state pension age

The AK is granted over the earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) the earned income includes the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption and excludes the part earned as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or profit-sharing creditor (winstdelende schuldeiser) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The 2022 table below state pension age has four bands: 4,541% up to € 10.350, then € 470 plus 28,461% up to € 22.356, then € 3.887 plus 2,610% up to € 36.649, then € 4.260 minus 5,860% up to € 109.346, and nil above € 109.346 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The amount of the table at each band boundary is the starting amount for the next step (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Where the AK cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and national insurance contribution, the unused part can be paid out to the lowest-earning partner (minstverdienende partner) under the payout rules below (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Employment tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year, the AK table has its own percentages: 2,348% up to € 10.350, then € 244 plus 14,718% up to € 22.356, then € 2.011 plus 1,349% up to € 36.649, then € 2.204 minus 3,030% up to € 109.346, and nil above € 109.346 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For 2020 to 2023 the checked pages give no rule or amount for the tax credits (heffingskortingen) in the year in which state pension age is reached, so that point is UNVERIFIED for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK)

For a taxpayer below state pension age the IACK is 11,45% of the earned income above € 5.219, up to a maximum of € 2.534, reached at € 27.350 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year the rate is 5,911% and the maximum is € 1.452 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The IACK is applied only in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) and not through the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The partner test on the IACK page that covers 2024 to 2026 is that the taxpayer has no fiscal partner (fiscaal partner), or a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or a lower earned income than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### IACK child and co-parent (co-ouder) conditions

For 2022 the child must have been born after 31 December 2009 and must have been registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). A co-parent qualifies when the child spends at least 156 days in 2022 with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The IACK page covering 2024 to 2026 adds that qualifying children are own children, stepchildren, adopted children, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child, and that a foster child (pleegkind) qualifies only when it is raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a foster care allowance (pleegvergoeding). Residents of the EU, EEA (EER), Switzerland or the BES islands can qualify, and a death of the taxpayer or the child before the 6 months still gives the credit (korting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting). The same page describes roughly equal care with at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated when co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### Payout of unused tax credits to a partner

The payout to the lowest-earning partner was phased down (afgebouwd) since 2009: 80% was withheld in 2020, 86⅔% in 2021 and 93⅓% in 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). In 2022, 93⅓% was withheld and 6,667% was paid out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Conditions are that the partners were fiscal partners for more than 6 months and that the partner owes enough tax. Only the part that cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and national insurance contribution is paid out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The phase-down ends in 2023, and no AHK phase-down applies to people born before 1 January 1963 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Payout income limits

For 2022 the limits are an own box 1 income of € 8.885 for people born before 1963, although the page also prints € 8.845 once, or € 523 for people born later. The partner limits are income above € 23.700, or € 30.400 at state pension age, or € 25.605 where the partner receives more tax credits. The under-21 rule applies to people born after 31 December 2000 and uses € 446 per quarter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The € 8.885 and € 8.845 values conflict, see section 7.

### IACK payout percentage

The 2022 page states no IACK-specific payout percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED. Do not apply the general payout percentage to the IACK without a source that states it.

### Income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen)

Each tax credit splits into an income-tax part (the income-tax part of the first-bracket rate) and contribution components (premiedelen) for AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen). For 2022 the income-tax part is 9,42% and the total with the contribution components is 37,07% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). A taxpayer who is not insured for one of the schemes loses that part, and a part-year contribution liability (premieplicht) gives a time-proportional contribution part (premiedeel) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen).

### Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable box 1 income

For income to qualify as profits from business activities, the business must be run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer must be able to enter into binding agreements for it and must be liable for its debts. Registration in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) alone does not decide the tax classification. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (ondernemersrisico) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Taxable income from work and home includes the taxable profit from business and is reduced by losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen) (te verrekenen verliezen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) are taken out of the profit first. These include the forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), the agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the debt waiver profit exemption (vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst) for the part above losses available for offset, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business termination payments (vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (Waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, and exemptions for benefits under the final levy (eindheffing), mobility projects (mobiliteitsproject) and peak-avoidance projects (spitsmijdenproject) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). A co-entitled party or a lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). The SME profit exemption is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The box 1 balance is taxed by art. 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the rate adjustment, and the tax credits reduce the income tax and the national insurance contributions through an income-tax part and contribution components (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference, see section 7. The checked material read the definitions in arts. 3.1 and 3.2 in the 2026 version of Wet IB 2001.

### Entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) values relevant to business profit

The 2022 entrepreneur's allowance values are the self-employed person's deduction € 6.310, or € 3.155 at state pension age, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 and then € 0, capped at the profit, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the applicable hour bands and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) with a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked material does not give the hour-band boundaries for the deduction for assisting partner or the full eligibility conditions for these reliefs (faciliteiten), so those details are not in this file. The self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for assisting partner, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs, the discontinuation deduction and the SME profit exemption are among the deductions that reduce the tax base for the rate adjustment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

### SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption is 14% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance for 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). It reduces the taxable profit from business (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The SME profit exemption is also one of the deductions that reduce the tax base for the rate adjustment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

### Addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve)

In 2022 an entrepreneur who met the hours criterion (urencriterium) and was below state pension age could add 9,44% of the profit to an old-age reserve, up to a maximum of € 9.632, reduced by the occupational pension (beroepspensioen) contribution (beroepspensioenpremie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). 2022 was the last year of additions. Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). Reduction or release of the reserve follows on buying an annuity (lijfrente) with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen), on cessation, on reaching state pension age on 1 January, on failing the hours criterion in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
