## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Below state pension age (AOW age), the published first rate changed from 37,10% up to € 68.507 to 37,07% up to € 69.398, and the top rate stayed 49,50%. The statutory first boundary changed from € 35.129 to € 35.472, the income-tax part from 9,45% to 9,42%, and the fixed amounts from € 3.319 and € 15.702 to € 3.341 and € 15.917. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.

- For a full year at state pension age, the first rate changed from 19,20% to 19,17%, the first boundary from € 35.129 to € 35.472 for people born on or after 1 January 1946, the pre-1946 boundary from € 35.941 to € 36.409, and the middle boundary from € 68.507 to € 69.398. The pre-1946 fixed amounts changed from € 3.396 and € 15.477 to € 3.429 and € 15.658. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.

- The monthly first-bracket rates in the state pension age transition year changed: January 19,20% to 19,17%, February 20,69% to 20,66%, March 22,18% to 22,15%, April 23,67% to 23,64%, May 25,16% to 25,13%, June 26,65% to 26,62%, July 28,15% to 28,12%, August 29,84% to 29,81%, September 31,13% to 31,10%, October 32,62% to 32,59%, November 34,11% to 34,08% and December 35,60% to 35,57%. The first bracket changed from € 35.129 to € 35.472. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening.

- The national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling changed from € 35.129 to € 35.472, or from € 35.941 to € 36.409 for people born before 1 January 1946. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen.

- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) changed from 6,5% to 9,5%. The maximum value of deductions above the top bracket boundary changed from 43% to 40%, and the boundary changed from € 68.507 to € 69.398. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2.

- The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) below state pension age changed from € 2.837 to € 2.888, the phase-out rate from 5,977% to 6,007%, the phase-out point from € 21.043 to € 21.317 and the nil point from € 68.507 to € 69.398. For a full year at state pension age the amount changed from € 1.469 to € 1.494 and the rate from 3,093% to 3,106%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) below state pension age bands changed from € 10.108 at 4,581%, € 21.835 at € 463 plus 28,771%, € 35.652 at € 3.837 plus 2,663% and € 105.736 at € 4.205 minus 6% to € 10.350 at 4,541%, € 22.356 at € 470 plus 28,461%, € 36.649 at € 3.887 plus 2,610% and € 109.346 at € 4.260 minus 5,860%. For a full year at state pension age the bands changed from € 10.108 at 2,371%, € 21.835 at € 240 plus 14,890%, € 35.652 at € 1.987 plus 1,378% and € 105.736 at € 2.178 minus 3,105% to € 10.350 at 2,348%, € 22.356 at € 244 plus 14,718%, € 36.649 at € 2.011 plus 1,349% and € 109.346 at € 2.204 minus 3,030%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The income-related combination tax credit (IACK) threshold changed from € 5.153 to € 5.219 and the rate stayed 11,45%. The maximum changed from € 2.815 at € 29.739 to € 2.534 at € 27.350. For a full year at state pension age the rate changed from 5,926% to 5,911% and the maximum from € 1.458 to € 1.452. The child birth cutoff changed from after 31 December 2008 to after 31 December 2009. The co-parent (co-ouder) test continued to require at least 156 days with each parent in a repeating pattern, now tested for 2022. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The payout phase-down changed from 86⅔% withheld and 13⅓% paid to 93⅓% withheld and 6,667% paid. The AHK phase-down (afbouw) remained absent for people born before 1 January 1963. The phase-down ends in 2023. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The payout income limits changed. Own box 1 income changed from € 8.170 for people born before 1963 or € 1.024 for people born later to € 8.885 or € 523, with the 2022 page also printing € 8.845 once for the earlier-born group. The partner limit changed from € 24.650, or € 29.350 at state pension age, to € 23.700, or € 30.400 at state pension age, with the 2022 page also giving € 25.605 where the partner receives more tax credits. The under-21 cutoff changed from born after 31 December 1999 to born after 31 December 2000, and the quarterly amount changed from € 439 to € 446. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) changed from € 6.670 to € 6.310, or from € 3.335 to € 3.155 at state pension age. The starter's deduction (startersaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs, the deduction for assisting partner percentages and the discontinuation deduction were unchanged in the checked tables. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

- The old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) maximum changed from € 9.395, reduced by the occupational pension contribution (beroepspensioenpremie), to € 9.632. The addition rate stayed 9,44%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Record the profit and the steps to the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming), including the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), any exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen), losses available for offset (te verrekenen verliezen) and the deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten). Keep the calculations that support the amounts entered in the income tax return. The checked material does not state a filing deadline or a record-retention period for this topic. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.

The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) and the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) are applied automatically in the return and can also be applied through the payroll. The IACK is claimed in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) and is not applied through the payroll. The rate adjustment is applied automatically and appears on the assessment (aanslag). The payout of unused tax credits (heffingskortingen) to a partner depends on the partner's tax and on the payout income limits. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.

An income-related health insurance contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) exists for 2022 on top of the income tax. Its rates and base belong in the Zvw reference. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The 2022 payout income limit for people born before 1963 is printed as both € 8.885 and € 8.845 on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen. A corrected primary page or an official correction would settle it.

- No IACK-specific payout percentage is stated for 2022 on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. A primary source that states the 2022 IACK payout rule would settle it.

- The tax credits in the year in which state pension age is reached: the 2022 page gives no rule or amount, and the checked material marks 2020 to 2023 UNVERIFIED on this point. A 2022 primary instruction for the transition year would settle it. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The 2022 part-year contribution liability (premieplicht) official example uses 9,45% for the income-tax part while the 2022 table uses 9,42%. A corrected example or an official explanation would settle which rate belongs in that example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The statement that the Anw (survivor benefit) and Wlz (long-term care) premiums continue at state pension age is an inference from the state pension age first-bracket rates. The checked pages state only that the AOW premium stops at state pension age. An explicit 2022 age-specific premium breakdown would settle it. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.

- The full order of the last steps of the calculation, rate adjustment then tax credits, is not printed as one statement on a checked page. It rests on the structure of art. 2.10. A primary source that states the full sequence would settle it. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen.

- The definitions in arts. 3.1 and 3.2 of Wet IB 2001 are cited from the 2026 version of the law in the checked material. Reading the 2022 text would settle the exact 2022 wording. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
