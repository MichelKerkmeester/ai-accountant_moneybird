## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA, small-scale investment deduction) is one deduction per onderneming (business), not per asset. It is claimed by choice in the aangifte (tax return) and it is calculated on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen (business assets) in the calendar year ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

The 2022 table has five bands. Nil on a total investment up to € 2.400. 28% of the investment on a total above € 2.400 up to € 59.939. A fixed € 16.784 on a total above € 59.939 up to € 110.998. € 16.784 minus 7,56% of the part above € 110.998 on a total above € 110.998 up to € 332.994. Nil above € 332.994 ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

Only assets that are not excluded count towards the total. Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes, for every investeringsaftrek, assets intended mainly for certain uses. Those uses include assets used in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (a treaty for the avoidance of double taxation), gronden (land) including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering, woonhuizen (dwellings) and woonschepen, personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992, vaartuigen as meant in art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, effecten, vorderingen, goodwill, vergunningen, ontheffingen and concessies, and dieren (animals) ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For the KIA only, art. 3.45 lid 2 adds two more exclusions. On this list are assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (onderdeel a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (onderdeel b). Art. 3.45 lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

In a samenwerkingsverband (a partnership such as a vof or maatschap) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment divided by the sum ([art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that each firmant or vennoot (partner) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)).

Art. 3.44 lid 1 limits the deduction when the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year. The deduction is then taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years. Lid 2 gives the full investeringsaftrek in the year of staking (cessation) of the onderneming. This payment cap applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

A KIA asset can also carry the EIA or the MIA, because the EIA and the MIA can each be combined with the KIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### KIA per-asset minimum (€ 450)

The € 450 is a per-asset floor for the KIA. A bedrijfsmiddel with an investment amount below € 450 is excluded from the KIA base, so it does not count in the yearly total ([art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount is stated per asset, not for the year as a whole. The same € 450 sits in art. 3.45 in the 2020 to 2027 versions, so it is unchanged in 2022 ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### EIA percentage (45,5%)

The energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA, energy investment deduction) is 45,5% of the investment amount in 2022 ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

Only new assets qualify. The asset must be "niet eerder gebruikt", so a second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed oneself can qualify as voortbrengingskosten (production costs). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km. The EIA asset must be on the Energielijst (energy list) ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset. No MIA on costs that already received EIA, while Vamil remains possible on those costs. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

The EIA is not open for obligations listed in art. 3.46. No investeringsaftrek applies to obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (onderdeel b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (onderdeel c), and a person holding at least one third of a lichaam and that lichaam (onderdeel d). The Minister can disapply art. 3.46 lid 1 under lid 2, which is the ontheffing route ([art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). FISIN phrases the last point as: buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

The investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months, and the per-asset minimum of € 2.500 of art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b applies ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (€ 128.000.000)

For 2022 the maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 128.000.000 ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The amount is one of the two amounts in art. 3.42 lid 4 that the year files track, next to the EIA percentage. For the MIA and Vamil the RVO page adds a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum. That RVO page does not state a year for the € 25 miljoen ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). See section 7.

### MIA percentages (45%, 36%, 27%)

The milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA, environmental investment deduction) has three categories in 2022. Categorie I gives 45%, categorie II gives 36% and categorie III gives 27% ([https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)).

From 1 January 2022 the Milieulijst maps codes starting with F or G to categorie I (45%), codes starting with A or D to categorie II (36%) and codes starting with B or E to categorie III (27%). The Milieulijst 2022 is in force on 1 January 2022 ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)).

Rijksoverheid states the step-up as: "De percentages worden vanaf 1 januari 2022 verhoogd van 13,5%, 27% en 36% naar respectievelijk 27%, 36% en 45%" ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel)). The FISIN 2022 chapter still prints 36% / 27% / 13,5%. The statute, the Milieulijst 2022 and the Rijksoverheid news item all give 45% / 36% / 27% from 1 January 2022, and the same mapping is on the Belastingdienst page for the vennootschapsbelasting changes ([Belastingdienst Vpb](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). Section 7 records the FISIN 2022 discrepancy.

The MIA conditions follow the EIA pattern. Only new assets qualify, the asset must be on the Milieulijst, the minimum is € 2.500 per asset and the Milieulijst valid on the date of signing the koopovereenkomst or placing the order applies. The MIA cannot be combined with the EIA for one asset, and it can be combined with the KIA ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

Art. 3.45 lid 3 relaxes the exclusion list for the MIA. For the MIA, art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn the MIA ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

MIA and Vamil together can net out to a real benefit. RVO: "Uw totale belastingvoordeel (MIA en Vamil) kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (€ 2.500)

Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b excludes assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 from the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.45 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). RVO phrases the floor for its own procedure as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a adds a further exclusion for the EIA and the MIA: assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises. Art. 3.45 lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 4 onderdeel a ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Vamil percentage (75%, codes A, B, C and F)

The Vamil (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, random depreciation for environmental investments) lets the costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel be depreciated at will for 75%. Art. 3.31 reads that those costs "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", and the rest follows the normal depreciation schedule ([art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the bodemwaarde limit, does not apply to these assets ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

Only assets not yet common in the Netherlands, not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil ([art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)).

Vamil can be combined with the EIA, because Vamil remains possible on costs that already received EIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The Vamil asset must be reported to RVO within 3 months, and the Milieulijst valid on the date of signing the koopovereenkomst or placing the order applies ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### RVO notification deadline (3 months)

For aanschafkosten (acquisition costs) the investment must be reported within 3 months after signing the koopovereenkomst (purchase agreement) or placing the order. For voortbrengingskosten (production costs) the report is due within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Milieu-advieskosten (environmental advice costs) go with those costs under the same deadlines. The Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date applies ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026 all state: "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Ordinary depreciation caps (10% and 20%)

Art. 3.30 lid 1 makes the yearly depreciation the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten (acquisition or production costs) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2 caps the yearly amount at 10% of the cost for goodwill and at 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for other assets. Lid 3 allows voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa (intangible assets) to be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging. Lid 4 writes voorwerpen van geringe waarde (small items) that are normally treated as current costs off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging ([art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The same text stands in the 2020 to 2027 versions of art. 3.30, so 2022 has no separate regime ([art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

The 20% cap is a yearly maximum on the cost, not a required percentage. Random depreciation taken under the Vamil or under the starters rule comes on top of this schedule for the part that is depreciated at will ([art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For buildings the art. 3.30a bodemwaarde limits the deduction further.

### Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold (€ 2.400)

Art. 3.47 lid 1: if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold of € 2.400, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to profit ([art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

Lid 2 limits the bijtelling to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken. Lid 3 treats three situations as a disposal: withdrawing the good from the onderneming (onderdeel a), changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, KIA) or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, EIA and MIA) (onderdeel b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (onderdeel c). Lid 4 counts the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair value) as the transfer price in those cases. Lid 5 counts undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding (a reduction, refund or compensation), as a disposal for that amount. Lid 6 defines undoing to include not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

The threshold of € 2.400 is the same in the 2020 to 2027 versions of art. 3.47 lid 1, so 2022 carries the same amount ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap (€ 332.994)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001, which allows willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship ([WIB art. 3.34](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3, the startersaftrek, applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst als ondernemer (profit as an entrepreneur). Art. 8 allows the random depreciation for the joint amount of those costs in the calendar year up to the maximum amount in the table of art. 3.41 lid 2 over which KIA can be obtained, and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground. Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 sets ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10 ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).

For 2022 the cap is the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table, € 332.994 ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

The Belastingdienst adds the practical conditions: the scheme is open for an eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, the startersaftrek conditions must be met, only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek count, the choice of assets is free, and the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply ([Belastingdienst starters](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

If the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf, a herwaardering (revaluation) to the normal book value takes place within 5 years, or 10 years for zeeschepen, and the difference is winst ([Belastingdienst starters](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

### Bodemwaarde (WOZ-waarde and 50% of the WOZ-waarde)

Art. 3.30a lid 1 allows depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde, and at most for the difference. Lid 2 treats a building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden as one bedrijfsmiddel, with detachable werktuigen (machines) as separate assets. Lid 3 sets the bodemwaarde for 2022 at the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging (investment building) and at 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another building, which is an own-use building. From 2024 the law has one bodemwaarde, the WOZ-waarde of the building. Lid 4 defines the WOZ-waarde as the value set by beschikking under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2021 and 2022 texts both print the 50% rule, so 2022 follows the two-value system ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2026 text also has lid 5 to lid 10 on co-owned buildings, ground owned by an unrelated party and related persons, which do not change the 2022 rule ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

The Vamil assets of art. 3.31 are outside art. 3.30a lid 1, so the bodemwaarde does not block their random depreciation ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving (none found for 2022)

No temporary willekeurige afschrijving scheme for 2022 was found, so the 2022 value is unknown and the absence is UNVERIFIED ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). The Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting sets the 2023 scheme and says it is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013", and names no other year ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).

The 2023 scheme itself sits in URWA art. 13 to 15. Art. 13 lid 1 covers assets not used before, with obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in calendar year 2023 and taken into use before 1 January 2026, art. 13 lid 2 lists exclusions, art. 14 allows at most 50% of the costs once in the year of the obligation or the costs, and art. 15 ends the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 on 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Those rules attach to 2023 costs and are therefore not available for a 2022 investment.
