## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Official example: loon and freelance income

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The contribution amount below is arithmetic on the official base.

- Loon (wage): € 40.000.
- Freelance income: € 20.000.
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen: € 59.706.
- Headroom after the loon: € 59.706 - € 40.000 = € 19.706.
- The 5,50% applies to € 19.706.
- Contribution: € 19.706 × 5,50% = € 1.083,83.

### Example 2. Official example: no contribution when the loon uses the full maximum

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

- Loon: € 60.000.
- Freelance income: € 15.000.
- The loon of € 60.000 is above the maximum of € 59.706.
- The freelance income therefore gets no Zvw bijdrage by aanslag.

### Example 3. Calculated example: profit of € 60.000 with zelfstandigenaftrek

**Calculated example** from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 6.310 = € 53.690.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 53.690 × 14% = € 7.516,60.
- Belastbare winst: € 53.690 - € 7.516,60 = € 46.173,40.
- Zvw base: € 46.173,40. This is below the maximum of € 59.706.
- Zvw bijdrage: € 46.173,40 × 5,50% = € 2.539,54.

### Example 4. Calculated example: profit of € 100.000 and the maximum

**Calculated example** from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 100.000.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 100.000 - € 6.310 = € 93.690.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 93.690 × 14% = € 13.116,60.
- Belastbare winst: € 93.690 - € 13.116,60 = € 80.573,40.
- Zvw base: € 80.573,40 before the cap. The maximum is € 59.706.
- Zvw bijdrage: € 59.706 × 5,50% = € 3.283,83.

### Example 5. Calculated example: profit of € 60.000 with zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek

**Calculated example** from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310.
- Startersaftrek: € 2.123.
- Total ondernemersaftrek: € 6.310 + € 2.123 = € 8.433.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 8.433 = € 51.567.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 51.567 × 14% = € 7.219,38.
- Belastbare winst: € 51.567 - € 7.219,38 = € 44.347,62.
- Zvw bijdrage: € 44.347,62 × 5,50% = € 2.439,12.

### Example 6. Calculated example: employer rate against the aanslag rate

**Calculated example** from the section 2 figures.

- Gross wage: € 40.000.
- Werkgeversheffing at 6,75%: € 40.000 × 6,75% = € 2.700.
- If the same € 40.000 were a profit base by aanslag at 5,50%: € 40.000 × 5,50% = € 2.200.
- Difference: € 2.700 - € 2.200 = € 500.
- The employer rate is paid by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie, not by the entrepreneur by aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
