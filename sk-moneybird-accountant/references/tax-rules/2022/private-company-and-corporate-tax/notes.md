## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Corporate income tax (Vpb) first bracket: € 245.000 at 15% in 2021 moved to € 395.000 at 15% in 2022. Above the bracket: € 36.750 plus 25% in 2021 moved to € 59.250 plus 25,8% in 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking, art. 15b): 30% in 2021 moved to 20% in 2022. The threshold (drempel) of € 1.000.000 is unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening, art. 20): 2021 was one year back and six years forward, with the loss fixed by a decision (beschikking). From 2022 it is one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Art. 31 allocation (toerekening): the 2021 text states that a financial year straddling 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changed again. The 2022 text states the same for a financial year straddling 1 January 2023. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon): the standard amount (normbedrag) moved from € 47.000 in 2021 to € 48.000 in 2022. The comparable wage stays at 75% in 2022, and 2022 is the last year on that basis. The € 5.000 floor is unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.
- Start-up exception to the customary salary rule (startersaftrek gebruikelijk loon): a first use in 2021 could run 3 years. 2022 is the last year of first use, and the conditions for a 2022 start must still be met in 2024. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Zvw: the contribution (bijdrage) moved from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022, the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) from € 58.311 to € 59.706, and the employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing) from 7,00% to 6,75%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Box 2: 26,9% in 2021 and 26,9% in 2022, unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Dividend tax (dividendbelasting): 15% and a grossing up (brutering) of 100/85 in both years, unchanged. The 2021 values come from the version in force (geldend) 01-01-2020 to and including (t/m) 31-12-2021, and the 2022 values from the 2022-01-01 version. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01.
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): the rate follows the highest art. 22 rate, so 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Innovation box (innovatiebox): the fraction 9/H, the flat rate (forfait) of 25% with a maximum of € 25.000 and the nexus multiplier (nexusvermenigvuldiger) of 1,3 are the same in 2021 and 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) and fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid): the 5% participation threshold and the 95% ownership requirement (bezitseis) are unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds: unchanged in 2022, and stated as the values before financial year 2024. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): € 3.630 in both 2021 and 2022. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Correction carried in this file: the dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3, and it can be read in the dated texts. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01.
- Recorded for completeness: the employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw) values for 2020 to 2023 are 6,70%, 7,00%, 6,75% and 6,68%, from the FISIN pages of each year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and the 2020, 2021 and 2023 equivalents.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the DGA wage in the payroll records (loonadministratie). The DGA is employed by (in dienst van) his own company for wage tax (loonbelasting), national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) and Zvw, so the BV withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). For an uninsured DGA record no employee insurance premiums (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw), and record the Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw) of 5,50% withheld on the net wage up to € 59.706 and paid through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For an insured DGA record the employer health insurance contribution of 6,75% paid by the BV. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Keep the evidence for the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test, including the comparable wage used for the 75% escape and the highest wage in the body and in connected bodies. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Record a dividend with the proceeds (opbrengst), the 15% dividend tax and the dividend statement (dividendnota), and note the day the dividend is made available, because the return and payment are due within 1 month after that day. A fully exempt participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) usually needs no return unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration invites one. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.
- File the corporate income tax return for a 2022 calendar financial year before 1 June 2023. Request an online extension from 1 March to 1 June 2023, which runs to 1 November 2023. For a financial year not coinciding with the calendar year use the 5 month term and a paper request with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Keep the records (administratie) for seven years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) VAT records for nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Data may move to another data carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.
- Deposit the annual accounts (jaarrekening) within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as unadopted. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.
- After the switch from a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) to a BV, use the new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer) with EU suppliers at once, and request a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) when there are employees. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.
- For a fiscal unity the subsidiary (dochter) files no separate return, and losses of one company can offset profits of another. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The contents of the Decree on profit splitting, emergency repair and article 10a, Stcrt. 2022, 11028, were not read. Only its citation in the 2024 Consolidated Decree (Verzamelbesluit 2024) is checked, where it is named as withdrawn. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html. Reading the 2022 decree itself would settle the conditions it contained for 2022.
- The art. 12bc threshold amount and the full definition of T in the K/T formula are not in the checked material. The 2022 text of art. 12bb and 12bc would settle both. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.
- The 2022 WML amount and the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA are not in the checked material, so the 108% floor of the start-up exception to the customary salary rule (startersaftrek gebruikelijk loon) has no euro value in this file. The 2022 WML and WVA texts would settle it. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- The Zvw withholding route for an uninsured DGA is checked in the Handbook for Payroll Taxes 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1 and in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table for 2024 to 2026, not in a 2022 dated source. The FISIN 2022 page gives the 2022 percentages. A 2022 Handbook for Payroll Taxes would settle the 2022 route. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- The tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) conditions in this file come from the Decree of 24 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 37092, which replaced the decree of 30 June 2010, no. DGB 2010/3599M. The 2010 text was not read. Reading the 2010 decree in its 2022 wording would settle which conditions read that way in 2022. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
- The Handbook for Payroll Taxes 2025 figure of 5,26% up to € 75.864 in this file is a 2025 figure and not a 2022 value. The 2022 values are on the FISIN 2022 page. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- No open conflict between sources remains for 2022. The art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 correction and the Zvw withholding route are settled in the checked dated texts and the Handbook.
