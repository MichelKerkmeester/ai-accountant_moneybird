### Example 1: Corporate income tax (Vpb) on € 60.000 and on € 500.000 (Calculated example)
Invented inputs. Profit € 60.000 for 2022.
Step 1: € 60.000 is below € 395.000, so the whole amount falls in the first bracket (eerste schijf).
Step 2: 15% x € 60.000 = € 9.000,00.
Now the profit is € 500.000.
Step 3: the part above € 395.000 is € 500.000 minus € 395.000 = € 105.000.
Step 4: 25,8% x € 105.000 = € 27.090,00.
Step 5: the column III amount is 15% x € 395.000 = € 59.250,00.
Step 6: total tax = € 59.250,00 + € 27.090,00 = € 86.340,00.
Source for the rates: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 2: Financial year (boekjaar) from 1 July 2021 to 30 June 2022 (Calculated example)
Invented financial year (boekjaar) and invented profit of € 300.000 for the whole financial year. Art. 31 applies because the financial year starts before and ends after the change of art. 22 on 1 January 2022.
Step 1: X = 184 days (1 July 2021 to 31 December 2021), Y = 181 days (1 January 2022 to 30 June 2022), B = 365 days.
Step 2: To is the 2021 tax on € 300.000. The 2021 first bracket runs to € 245.000 at 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. The part above the bracket is € 300.000 minus € 245.000 = € 55.000. 25% x € 55.000 = € 13.750,00. To = € 36.750,00 + € 13.750,00 = € 50.500,00.
Step 3: Tn is the 2022 tax on € 300.000. € 300.000 is below € 395.000, so Tn = 15% x € 300.000 = € 45.000,00.
Step 4: (184/365) x € 50.500 = € 25.457,53 (rounded down). (181/365) x € 45.000 = € 22.315,07 (rounded).
Step 5: total tax = € 25.457,53 + € 22.315,07 = € 47.772,60.
Sources: 2021 rate https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, 2022 rate https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01, formula https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 3: Art. 15b interest limitation (Calculated example)
Invented: an adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) of € 600.000 and a net interest balance (saldo aan renten) of € 1.400.000.
Step 1: 20% x € 600.000 = € 120.000,00.
Step 2: the threshold (drempel) is the higher of € 120.000,00 and € 1.000.000, so € 1.000.000.
Step 3: € 1.400.000 minus € 1.000.000 = € 400.000.
Step 4: € 400.000 of the net interest balance is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) in 2022. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) change pages state that non-deductible interest carries forward.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025.

### Example 4: Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (Calculated example)
Invented: a profit before set-off of € 2.400.000 for 2022 and an older loss of € 2.000.000.
Step 1: the per year ceiling is € 1.000.000 plus 50% x (€ 2.400.000 minus € 1.000.000).
Step 2: 50% x € 1.400.000 = € 700.000,00. Ceiling = € 1.000.000,00 + € 700.000,00 = € 1.700.000,00.
Step 3: € 1.700.000 of the € 2.000.000 loss is set off.
Step 4: taxable profit (belastbare winst) = € 2.400.000 minus € 1.700.000 = € 700.000.
Step 5: remaining loss = € 2.000.000 minus € 1.700.000 = € 300.000. That amount goes forward, with the oldest losses first.
Carry-back part: with an invented 2022 loss of € 50.000 and a 2021 profit of € 80.000, the loss is set off against 2021 and leaves € 30.000 of 2021 profit.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Example 5: Innovation box (innovatiebox) (Calculated example)
Invented: a taxpayer that meets the conditions for the innovation box.
Fraction (breuk) part of art. 12b, invented: a profit of € 600.000 before the innovation box, of which € 200.000 is a qualifying benefit (voordeel).
Step 1: the fraction is 9/H, and H is the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2022.
Step 2: the benefit enters the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) at (9/25,8) x € 200.000 = € 69.767,44 (rounded down).
Step 3: taxable amount = € 600.000 minus € 200.000 + € 69.767,44 = € 469.767,44.
Step 4: the part above € 395.000 is € 469.767,44 minus € 395.000 = € 74.767,44. 25,8% x € 74.767,44 = € 19.290,00 (rounded).
Step 5: corporate income tax = € 59.250,00 + € 19.290,00 = € 78.540,00.
Step 6: the profit that stays in the normal regime is € 600.000 minus € 200.000 = € 400.000. That already exceeds € 395.000, so the fraction amount is taxed at the top rate. The effective rate (effectieve tarief) on the benefit is then 9%, the rate that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states: 9% x € 200.000,00 = € 18.000,00. That 9% follows from the fraction at the top rate and is not a separate rate.
Flat rate (forfait) part of art. 12bd, invented: the taxpayer chooses the flat rate, the profit before art. 12b is € 80.000 and that profit is positive.
Step 7: 25% x € 80.000 = € 20.000,00, which is below the maximum of € 25.000.
Step 8: the benefit of € 20.000 enters the taxable amount at (9/25,8) x € 20.000 = € 6.976,74 (rounded down).
Step 9: taxable amount = € 80.000 minus € 20.000 + € 6.976,74 = € 66.976,74.
Step 10: € 66.976,74 is below € 395.000, so corporate income tax = 15% x € 66.976,74 = € 10.046,51 (rounded down), which is € 10.047 when rounded to whole euros. The 9% is not a separate rate, the benefit is taxed through the fraction in the taxable amount.
Step 11: if the profit before art. 12b were € 120.000, 25% x € 120.000 = € 30.000,00 and the flat rate would be capped at € 25.000.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Example 6: Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) (Calculated example)
Invented: BV A holds 100% of BV B and receives a dividend of € 200.000. The holding of 100% is above the 5% threshold (drempel) of art. 13 lid 2, so the benefit from the participation (deelneming) is left out of the profit under art. 13 lid 1. No corporate income tax is due on the € 200.000. The payment is a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder, so it is usually exempt from dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withholding, and no return is needed unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration invites one.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Example 7: Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) (Calculated example)
Invented: BV A has a profit of € 300.000 and BV B a loss of € 100.000, the 95% ownership requirement (bezitseis) and the other conditions for a fiscal unity are met, and the two form a fiscal unity.
Step 1: tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, so the combined result is € 300.000 minus € 100.000 = € 200.000.
Step 2: € 200.000 is below € 395.000, so corporate income tax = 15% x € 200.000 = € 30.000,00.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Example 8: Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) (Calculated example)
Invented: the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) is € 80.000, so 75% x € 80.000 = € 60.000,00. The highest wage of the employees of the body or of related bodies is € 120.000. The standard amount (normbedrag) is € 48.000.
Step 1: the main rule gives the highest of € 60.000,00, € 120.000 and € 48.000, so € 120.000.
Step 2: the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that this highest amount of € 120.000 exceeds 75% of the comparable wage of € 60.000,00.
Step 3: the escape applies and the wage becomes 75% of the comparable wage, that is 75% x € 80.000 = € 60.000,00, which is above the standard amount of € 48.000, so the wage is € 60.000.
Second part: if the comparable wage were € 40.000, 75% x € 40.000 = € 30.000,00. The highest amount is at least the standard amount of € 48.000, which exceeds € 30.000,00, so the escape applies. Under art. 12a lid 2 the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when the comparable wage is lower than the standard amount. € 40.000 is lower than € 48.000, so the wage is € 40.000.
Third part: if the wage for all work in the body and related bodies is not more than € 5.000, the rule does not apply at all.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.

### Example 9: Start-up exception to the customary salary rule (startersaftrek gebruikelijk loon) (Calculated example)
No euro example can be computed here, because the checked material gives neither the 2022 WML amount nor the 2022 start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA. The counts are clear: a first use in 2022 can run at most 3 years, so 2022, 2023 and 2024, and the conditions must still be met in 2024. The de minimis ceiling (de-minimisplafond) is established by Regulation (EU) nr. 1407/2013.

### Example 10: Zvw for the DGA (Calculated example)
Invented wage of € 50.000.
Case A, an insured director and substantial shareholder (verzekerde DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder): the BV pays the employer health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw) of 6,75%. 6,75% x € 50.000 = € 3.375,00.
Case B, an uninsured DGA: the BV withholds the Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw) of 5,50% from the net wage (nettoloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). 5,50% x € 50.000 = € 2.750,00.
At an invented wage of € 70.000 the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 59.706 applies.
Case A: 6,75% x € 59.706 = € 4.030,16 (rounded).
Case B: 5,50% x € 59.706 = € 3.283,83.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Example 11: Box 2 and dividend tax (dividendbelasting) (Calculated example)
Invented: the BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to its DGA.
Step 1: dividend tax 15% x € 100.000 = € 15.000,00 withheld. Net received = € 85.000,00.
Step 2: box 2, 26,9% x € 100.000 = € 26.900,00.
Step 3: credit for the dividend tax withheld = € 15.000,00.
Step 4: additional income tax (inkomstenbelasting) = € 26.900,00 minus € 15.000,00 = € 11.900,00.
Step 5: if the dividend is made available on an invented day of 15 June 2022, the dividend tax return and payment are due within 1 month after that day, so by 15 July 2022.
Grossing-up (brutering) variant: if the BV bears the dividend tax and pays € 85.000 net, the proceeds (opbrengst) is € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000,00.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Example 12: Share buyback (inkoop van eigen aandelen) (Calculated example)
Invented: the buyback price is € 120.000 and the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares is € 50.000.
Step 1: the difference is € 120.000 minus € 50.000 = € 70.000.
Step 2: dividend tax 15% x € 70.000 = € 10.500,00.
Step 3: if the company bears the tax, the proceeds is grossed up by 100/85. € 70.000 x 100/85 = € 82.352,94 (rounded down). 15% x € 82.352,94 = € 12.352,94 (rounded down). The art. 4c facility for listed companies would switch the grossing off.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Example 13: Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) (Calculated example)
Invented: a Dutch BV pays € 400.000 of interest to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie). The rate is the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2022. 25,8% x € 400.000 = € 103.200,00.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01.

### Example 14: Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size class and deposit (Calculated example)
Invented first case: two consecutive balance sheet dates (opeenvolgende balansdata) with assets of € 400.000, net turnover (netto-omzet) of € 500.000 and 8 employees. The thresholds for a financial year before 2024 are used.
Step 1: micro needs at most € 350.000 assets (not met, € 400.000 is more), at most € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, both met.
Step 2: two of the three micro criteria are met on both dates, so the company is micro.
Invented second case: assets of € 8.000.000, net turnover of € 14.000.000 and 40 employees on both dates.
Step 3: micro is not met on assets or net turnover. Small is not met on assets (above € 6.000.000) or net turnover (above € 12.000.000). Medium is met on all three criteria, so the company is medium.
Deposit part: a financial year ending 31 December 2022 must be deposited at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends, so at the latest 31 December 2023, and within eight days after adoption (vaststelling).
Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.

### Example 15: Filing and extension dates (Calculated example)
A BV with a calendar financial year 2022 files its corporate income tax return (aangifte vennootschapsbelasting) before 1 June 2023. With an online extension, which applies only to a calendar financial year, the deadline moves to 1 November 2023, and the request runs from 1 March 2023 to 1 June 2023. A short financial year (kort boekjaar) ending 31 December 2022 files before 1 June 2023, and a short financial year ending 30 September 2022 files before 1 April 2023. For a financial year not coinciding with the calendar year (gebroken boekjaar) the term is 5 months after its end, and a paper request within 5 months with reasons is needed for an extension.
Term note: a 2022 record is kept for seven years under art. 52 AWR. Immovable property (onroerende zaken) records run nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records ten years after the year of the transaction.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01.

### Example 16: Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (Calculated example)
Invented: profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) of € 120.000.
Step 1: the discontinuation deduction is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime.
Step 2: € 120.000 minus € 3.630 = € 116.370,00 that remains as profit from business discontinuation.
An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. No box 1 rate is computed here, because the checked material for this topic does not give the 2022 box 1 rates.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen.

### Example 17: Tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) (Calculated example)
Invented: the fiscal book values of the contributed business total € 150.000 and the paid-up amount is € 200.000.
Step 1: the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values, so € 150.000.
Step 2: the rounding credit is at most 5% of the paid-up amount. 5% x € 200.000 = € 10.000,00, which is below the ceiling of € 30.000.
Counts: a sale of the shares within 3 years after the contribution is presumed part of a transfer plan unless the contributor proves otherwise, and the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) must be received within 9 months after the last full financial year for effect from the first day of the next financial year.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.

### Example 18: Request timing for art. 3.65, KG:212:2022:1 (Official example)
The known case: retroactivity to 1 January 2021 with the request received on 15 December 2021 and incorporation on 1 February 2022. The request was timely. The position (standpunt) adds that a request can be filed until the 2022 income tax and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) assessment (IB/PVV) is irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) is reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment decides. Adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023.
Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.
