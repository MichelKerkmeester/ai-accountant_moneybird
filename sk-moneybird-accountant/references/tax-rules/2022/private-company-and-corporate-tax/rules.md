## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) rate (Vpb-tarief, art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
A private limited company (BV) established in the Netherlands is a taxpayer for corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting). In the text valid on 1 January 2022 the first € 395.000 of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) carries 15%. Above € 395.000 the tax is € 59.250 plus 25,8% of the part above that amount. Art. 22 is built as a column I amount, a column III amount and a column IV percentage, so the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount above the column I amount. Every year from 2020 to 2026 has its own series in the dated text, so the 2022 figures must be read in the 2022 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Financial year (boekjaar) that crosses 1 January 2022 or 1 January 2023 (art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969)
A financial year (boekjaar) that starts before and ends on or after the date on which a change of art. 22 takes effect is split. The tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the number of days before the change, Y the number of days from the change, B all days of the financial year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31, checked in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Two straddles concern this year. A financial year that straddles 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changed again (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01). A financial year that straddles 1 January 2023 is apportioned as well (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01). For the part from 1 January 2023 the new series is up to and including (t/m) € 200.000 at 19%, above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01).

### Generic interest deduction (renteaftrek) limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking, art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)
The net interest balance (saldo aan renten) is the interest costs on loans minus the interest income. In 2022 that balance is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) in so far as it exceeds the higher of 20% of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021 and 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) change pages state 24,5% for 2025 and 2026 and say that non-deductible interest carries forward. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening, art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)
For 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by a decision (beschikking). From 2022 a loss can be set off against one preceding year and then against unlimited following years, but in each year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### Innovation box (innovatiebox), fraction (breuk, art. 12b Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-developed qualifying intangible asset (zelfgemaakt kwalificerend immaterieel activum) (immaterieel activum) count for 7/H in the 2020 text and 9/H in the texts from 2021 to 2026, where H is the highest art. 22 rate. For 2022 that rate is 25,8%, so the effective rate (effectieve tarief) on the advantage is 9%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states that the effective corporate income tax rate for these profits is 9%. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovation box, flat rate (forfait, art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years, and who chooses the flat rate, sets the qualifying advantage at 25% of the profit before art. 12b. The maximum is € 25.000, and the flat rate applies only when that profit is positive. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01.

### Innovation box, conditions and nexus (art. 12ba en 12bb Wet Vpb 1969)
A smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten) from R&D assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and the net turnover (netto-omzet) over those five years stays below € 250.000.000, in art. 12ba lid 2, the same in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Other taxpayers also need an access ticket under art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Brands (merken), logos and comparable assets are excluded under art. 12ba lid 6. Under art. 12bb the qualifying part is K/T, where K is the qualifying expenditure (kwalificerende uitgaven) multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. The checked material does not give the art. 12bc amount or the full definition of T. See section 7. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01.

### Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling, art. 13 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2 creates a participation from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, and a connected body is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party (derde). A tax-neutral contribution (geruisloze inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met. For VAT a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur (ondernemer) and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity (btw-eenheid), but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.

### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid, art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The parent company (moeder) must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital (nominaal gestort kapitaal) of the subsidiary (dochter), carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights (statutaire stemrechten) and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the parent company is a bv, nv (public limited company), mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie), or a comparable foreign form, that the subsidiary is a bv or nv, that the financial years and profit rules match, and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. The subsidiary keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the parent company at once. A subjective exemption (subjectieve vrijstelling) of one subsidiary ends it for that subsidiary only, under art. 15 lid 10. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Fiscal unity, policy decree (beleidsbesluit) 2022
The Decree on profit splitting (winstsplitsing), emergency repair (spoedreparatie) and article 10a was published as Stcrt. 2022, 11028. The 2024 Consolidated Decree (verzamelbesluit) names it as withdrawn. The text of the 2022 decree was not read in the checked material, so its conditions for 2022 are not stated here. See section 7. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon), standard amount (normbedrag, art. 12a lid 1 Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of three amounts. They are the wage from the most comparable employment (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), the highest wage of the employees of the body or of related bodies, and the standard amount. The standard amount for 2022 is € 48.000. It is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001, in lid 9 in 2020 to 2022 and in lid 7 from 2023. The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 (Handboek Loonheffingen 2025) adds that the test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen), including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) who hold shares. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Customary salary, comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) at 75%
For 2020 to 2022 the comparable wage counts at 75%, and 2022 is the last year on that basis. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 reads for 2020 to 2022 as follows. If the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the standard amount. From 2023 the rule reads that if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 notes that a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Customary salary, lower limit of € 5.000
The customary salary rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and related bodies is not more than € 5.000. The condition sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01.

### Start-up exception to the customary salary rule (startersaftrek gebruikelijk loon, art. 12a lid 3 en 4 Wet LB 1964)
For a body with an R&D declaration for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the statute for 2020 to 2022 sets the wage at least at 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if that is made plausible, within the de minimis ceiling (de-minimisplafond) of Regulation (EU) nr. 1407/2013. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the de minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and that an R&D declaration for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use in 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the Handbook 2025 allows a starting company to set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML (statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon)) for the hours worked (gewerkte uren), and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. The 2022 WML amount and the 2022 start-up percentage are not in the checked material, so the 108% floor has no euro value here. See section 7. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) for a non-insured DGA (niet-verzekerde DGA)
A DGA is employed by (in dienst van) his own company (eigen vennootschap) for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). For him the company pays no employee insurance premiums (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (zvw-werkgeversheffing), and instead withholds the income-related Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) from his net wage (nettoloon) up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For 2022 the FISIN page gives a contribution of 5,50% and a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 59.706. Each FISIN page says that DGA wage falls under the employer health insurance contribution only if he is insured for the employee insurance schemes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.

### Employer health insurance contribution for an insured DGA
A DGA who is insured for the employee insurance schemes falls under the employer health insurance contribution, paid by the BV. For 2022 the FISIN page gives 6,75%. The FISIN pages give 6,70% for 2020, 7,00% for 2021, 6,75% for 2022 and 6,68% for 2023. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

### Zvw route (zvw-route) for a non-insured DGA
The route in the books is a correction point. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding on net wage (inhouding op nettoloon) or by assessment (aanslag). The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, says the BV withholds the contribution on the net wage. This file follows the Handbook for the payroll treatment. The Handbook 2025 prints 5,26% up to € 75.864, which is its own 2025 figure and not a 2022 value. The 2022 values are on the FISIN 2022 page. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### Box 2 rate (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
For 2022 the box 2 rate is a single 26,9%. Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are indexed each year with the table correction factor. The rate is read in the text valid on 1 January 2022. A DGA who receives a dividend from his BV pays this rate and credits the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) that the BV withheld. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Dividend tax (dividendbelasting), rate and grossing up (brutering, art. 5 en art. 6 lid 1 Wet DB 1965)
The tax is 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst), in every version read from 2020 to 2025. Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company withholds. If the company bears the tax itself, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the proceeds by 100/85. Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividend statement (dividendnota) once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend, holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration invites one. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting), and bodies credit it in the corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/.

### Return and payment of dividend tax
The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Dividend stripping (art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965)
The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text already holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01.

### Share buyback (inkoop van eigen aandelen)
Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares. That also holds for an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies (beursgenoteerde vennootschappen) applies. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), rate (art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021)
The tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version, in art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividend tax. With art. 22 that gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. For 2022 the rate on interest and royalties is therefore 25,8%, following the 2022 art. 22 top rate. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Conditional withholding tax, scope (toepassingsbereik)
The tax hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie), meaning a profit tax below 9% or a place on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions (niet-coöperatieve jurisdicties), and abuse cases. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening), size classes (grootteklassen) and classification (indeling)
A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance sheet dates (opeenvolgende balansdata) it meets two of the three criteria. The statute in Book 2 of the Civil Code (burgerlijk wetboek) (Boek 2 BW) art. 395a, 396 and 397 sets micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small at € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50 employees, and medium at € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250 employees. Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raises these amounts to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million (miljoen) and € 15 million, and € 25 million and € 50 million, for financial years from 1 January 2024 and optionally from 1 January 2023. The employee counts are unchanged. This file uses the pre-2024 amounts for 2022. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/.

### Annual accounts, filing (deponering, art. 394 Boek 2 BW)
The board deposits the accounts within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted. Ondernemersplein states that there is no extension and that late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf). Ondernemersplein also states that from financial year 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. That last point is not a 2022 rule. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.

### Retention periods (bewaartermijnen, art. 52 AWR, art. 34a, 28rl en 28sj Wet OB 1968)
Bodies (lichamen) are required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss account (winst- en verliesrekening). The seven year term is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts. Records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten, under art. 34a Wet OB 1968. OSS records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction, under art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.

### Corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb), filing deadlines
A financial year equal to the calendar year must be filed before 1 June of the next year. A financial year not coinciding with the calendar year (gebroken boekjaar) must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short financial year (kort boekjaar) ending 31 December must be filed before 1 June, and a short financial year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. These pages were read for the current filing seasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen.

### Deferral (uitstel) of the corporate income tax return
An online extension is only for a calendar financial year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. For a financial year not coinciding with the calendar year, or for an extension of more than 5 months, a paper request is needed, filed within 5 months after the financial year ends and with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek, art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001)
Without the tax-neutral conversion facility a change of legal form (wijziging van rechtsvorm) is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays income tax on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The discontinuation deduction is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. Hidden reserves (stille reserves) realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity, under art. 3.129. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/.

### Tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting, art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The standard conditions (standaardvoorwaarden) in annex 1 of the Decree of 24 October 2025, published as Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025, include the point that selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed to be part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, so it is a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for income tax, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree, the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. A contribution into an existing company is allowed only when it already runs a business in the same line, and a contribution into a fiscal unity only through the parent company whose shares the contributor holds directly. If the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full financial year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next financial year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says to request at the office that handles the income tax, to register the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the accompanying form (Geleideformulier), and that the BV is a BV in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. The Decree of 24 October 2025 replaced the decree of 30 June 2010, no. DGB 2010/3599M, which was the text around 2022, and it lists more changed points, being 2.2.2.a for a rented-out business or profit right, 3.4 for the holding approval, 4.2.2 for lettered shares (letteraandelen) and art. 14a transfers, 6.1 for the reserves to settle, 8 for the Zvw credit, 12 for the eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad, 13.1.2 for the incidental tax advantage, 13.2 for the R&D approval and 15.3. The 2010 text was not read. See section 7. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm.

### Request under art. 3.65, term (KG:212:2022:1)
The inspector decides by a decision that is open to objection (bezwaar) and that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties, under onderdeel 14.2 of the old Decree. In the case the request can be filed until the 2022 income tax and national insurance contributions assessment (IB/PVV) is irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) is reported for 2021, and then the 2021 assessment decides. The position (standpunt) was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/.

### VAT fiscal unity (btw-fiscale eenheid), policy decree
The new legal form is a new business for VAT and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting) on business real estate is usually not due on the change. A VAT fiscal unity under art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically interconnected, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A decision, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability (hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid). Financial integration means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands. The Policy Decree on VAT liability and fiscal unity (Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025". This is not a 2022 rule and is noted for context.
