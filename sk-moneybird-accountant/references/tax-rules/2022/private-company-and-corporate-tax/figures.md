## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting, Vpb) for 2022 has a first bracket of 15% up to € 395.000 and 25,8% above it, read in the text valid on 1 January 2022, and these 2022 figures are enacted law. Two large switches in the same year matter for a private limited company (BV) owner, the generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) falls from 30% to 20%, and loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) becomes one year back with unlimited forward set-off, capped at € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000 per year. For the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag) is € 48.000, and 2022 is the last year in which the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) counts at 75%. This file also covers the dividend tax (dividendbelasting), the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), the innovation box (innovatiebox) and the switch from a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) to a private limited company.
