## 5. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Gemengde kosten drempel: € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 1).
- Zero-emission reduction for a car first admitted in the year: a 2021 admission got 10% off capped € 4.000, rendered as 12% up to € 40.000. A 2022 admission gets 6% off capped € 2.100, rendered as 16% up to € 35.000. A car first admitted in 2021 keeps the reduction that applied at first admission for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (art. 3.20 lid 11). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- Scholingsuitgaven: the persoonsgebonden aftrek ends. In 2021 art. 6.27 to 6.30 still applied with a drempel of € 250 and a maximum of € 15 000 outside the standaardstudieperiode (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). In 2022 art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), and the Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven took effect on 1 January 2022 by Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
- STAP-budget starts on 1 January 2022 as the non-fiscal replacement, with a maximum of € 1.000 including btw per scholing and six application windows per calendar year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).
- Prestatiebeurs case: the route continues under art. 10a.16 for study years up to and including 2014/2015. FISIN 2022 gives the caps € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for mbo, € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for hbo or wo, and the € 250 drempel per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- Unchanged from 2021 to 2022: kilometrevergoeding € 0,19 per km, bijtelling auto 22%, pre-2017 car at least 25%, youngtimer 35% of the WEV for cars more than 15 years, cap of € 1.500 on travel and stay for congressen en cursussen, verhuizing inboedel plus € 7750, bijtelling fiets 7%, werkruimte thuis 0%, telefoonabonnement thuis 0%, private computers en apparatuur 0%, geldboetes 0% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- The text of art. 3.14 and art. 3.16 is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. The only change is art. 3.16 lid 14 from 1 January 2025, which is after 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Deduct costs excluding btw when the btw is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the btw may not be deducted, deduct the costs including btw in the IB return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Under the KOR (kleineondernemersregeling, small business scheme) no btw is charged or paid, no btw on business costs and investments is deducted (also not btw paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need herziening (revision), the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- Keep receipts and invoices for purchases and costs, and keep them for purchases made before the start of the business too (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Keep proof of travel costs, for example the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).
- Keep a rittenregistratie or another record for the at most 500 private km. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, though trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).
- The 80% election for gemengde kosten is made in the return, so note the choice for the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 5).
- Private use of a car or fiets in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking and belongs in the winstberekening (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, art. 3.20 and art. 3.20a, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
- Record study costs as business costs only when they maintain existing vakkennis. The persoonsgebonden route no longer exists for 2022. A prestatiebeurs deduction is claimed in the return year in which the diplomatermijn has passed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).
- For a BV record the wage total and the gemengde kosten per body, because the Vpb variant applies per body and only once in a fiscale eenheid, at the parent (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.4).
