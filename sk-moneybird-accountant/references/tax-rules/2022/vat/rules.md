## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2022

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard, reduced and zero rates

Article 9 lid 1 of the Turnover Tax Act 1968 (Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968) sets the tax at 21 percent. Article 9 lid 2 sets 9 percent for the goods and services listed in Table I (Tabel I) and nil for those listed in Table II (Tabel II), in both cases only if the conditions set by order in council (algemene maatregel van bestuur) are met. The identical text was read in the versions of 1 January 2020, 1 July 2021, 1 January 2022, 1 January 2023, 1 January 2024, 1 January 2025 and 1 January 2026, so 2022 uses 21 percent, 9 percent and the nil rate. A nil-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, while an exempt supply does not. [Wet OB art. 9, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).

### Table I goods at 9%

The 9 percent list for goods covers foodstuffs (voedingsmiddelen), water, horticultural products (sierteeltproducten), medicines and medical devices (geneesmiddelen en hulpmiddelen), art, collectibles and antiques (kunst, verzamelvoorwerpen en antiek) and books and periodicals (boeken en periodieken). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration links the VAT Explanatory Decree Table 1 (Besluit Omzetbelasting Toelichting Tabel 1, BWBR0052086, 2026-01-01) as the explanation of the list. The current pages carry no year, so the dated post history of the list rests on the instruments named in this file. [Belastingdienst, goods at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw).

### Table I services at 9%

The 9 percent list for services covers the repair of bicycles, of shoes and leather goods and of clothing and household linen, hairdressers, certain work on homes, camping, access to cultural and recreational events and facilities, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, e-books comparable to a 9 percent physical publication, and access to news websites and apps unless they are mainly advertising, video or audible music. The page also names related services, including the delivery of goods (het opleveren van goederen), selling food in hospitality (horeca), renting and repairing medical aids, lending books and lending art by the artist himself. [Belastingdienst, services at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/).

### Electronic publications and news access

Item b.21 of Table I applies from 1 January 2020 to the electronic supply or lending of publications and to access to news websites. The decree of 31 March 2022, nr. 2022-6334 in the Explanatory Decree Table I settles that start date, so the 9 percent rate on qualifying electronic publications is part of the 2022 rules. [Besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html).

### Accommodation (logies)

Accommodation (logies) was in the 9 percent list in 2022. The Act of 29 October 2025 (Stb. 2025, 339) removes item b.11 from Table I with effect from 1 January 2026, so the rate goes to 21 percent from that later date, including for gas, electricity, water, sanitary, laundry and parking facilities tied to the accommodation, while a separately offered breakfast or pool and attraction access stays at 9 percent. That change does not apply to 2022. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html), [Belastingdienst, btw en logies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies).

### Zero rate conditions for an intra-EU goods supply

The zero rate is not an exemption. A zero-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, an exempt supply does not. For an intra-EU supply of goods the nil rate needs three conditions: proof from the records that the goods went to another EU country, a buyer with a valid VAT ID of another member state, and an ICP statement (opgaaf ICP) filed on time, correctly and completely. A late or wrong ICP statement can cost the zero rate and draw a fine. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Exemptions (Wet OB art. 11 lid 1)

Article 11 lid 1 onderdelen a to w were read in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions with the same headings: immovable property supply and letting (with exceptions), care in institutions, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups (koepelvrijstelling, VAT cost-sharing exemption), fundraising and childcare. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration list adds investment gold (beleggingsgoud) and canteens (kantines), and states that the exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies carry no right to deduct input VAT. [Wet OB art. 11, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, vrijstellingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).

### KOR turnover ceiling

The small businesses scheme (KOR) as it applied in 2022 comes from the Act on the modernisation of the small businesses scheme (Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling, Act of 19 December 2018), whose Articles I and II entered into force on 1 January 2020. Article 25 lid 1 applies to an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a fixed establishment (vaste inrichting) there. If turnover in the Netherlands in a calendar year is no more than € 20.000, the entrepreneur may choose the exemption for supplies in that and the following calendar years. [Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0041782), [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR turnover test year

Old Article 25 lid 1 tests the turnover in a calendar year (de omzet in een kalenderjaar) and no subsection names the preceding calendar year. The 1 January 2020, 1 July 2021, 1 January 2022, 1 January 2023 and 1 January 2024 versions were checked and none contains a preceding-year test. A prior-year overrun mattered before 2025 only through the three-year lock in lid 8. From 1 January 2025 the prior year counts, because art. 25a lid 5 requires the prior-year turnover in the notification (melding) and lid 9 bars the year after an overrun. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [art. 25a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### KOR notice, minimum participation and re-entry

Article 25 lid 6 requires the notice at the latest four weeks before the tax period (uiterlijk vier weken voorafgaand aan het belastingtijdvak). The inspector (inspecteur) refuses the option by a decision (beschikking) when the conditions will plausibly not be met. Lid 7 keeps participation in place for at least three years after the start, and re-entry after a revocation is possible only three years later. Lid 8 provides that on exceeding the ceiling the exemption ends from the crossing supply onward, and that re-entry is barred for three years after the overrun. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR invoice and deduction rule

Article 25 lid 4 states that a KOR participant does not deduct input VAT and does not charge VAT on the invoice. This is the mirror image of the exemption and it is the reason the KOR is only attractive when little input VAT is at stake. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).

### KOR special conditions from the 2020 handbook

The Handbook for Entrepreneurs (Handboek Ondernemen) 2020 (Handboek Ondernemen 2020) adds practical KOR conditions. Legal persons (bv, nv, stichting, vereniging) may join the KOR. A participant has no option for taxed letting under the KOR. Adjustment (herziening) stays out when the total is less than € 500 a year for investment goods used before that year. Deregistration is possible only per the first day of a return period (aangiftetijdvak). [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### KOR registration threshold without notice

The decree of 10 June 2021, nr. 2021-12740 allows an entrepreneur who is not yet registered and has an annual turnover (jaaromzet) of at most € 1.800 to apply the KOR without the usual notice, retroactive to 1 January 2020. From 1 January 2025 art. 25b Wet OB and art. 25 of the Implementing Regulation on turnover tax 1968 (Uitvoeringsbeschikking omzetbelasting 1968) set the amount and raise it from € 1.800 to € 2.200 (decree of 21 November 2024, nr. 2024-29602). Art. 25b also requires that the entrepreneur has not registered with the inspector or the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) and is not obliged to, and does not use the exemption in other member states. [Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html), [Besluit van 21 november 2024, nr. 2024-29602](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html), [art. 25b, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).

### VAT return periods

Quarterly filing is the usual period. Monthly or yearly filing can be requested by letter to the tax office (belastingkantoor). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration replies within 6 weeks and the change starts at the start of the new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

### Monthly return trigger

The Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 states that a business that usually pays more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter has a one-month period. The Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026 and the current period-change page no longer state that trigger, and no 2022 text was found, so whether the trigger applied in 2022 is not established. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

### Filing and payment term

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states that a period return is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period (binnen een door de inspecteur gestelde termijn van ten minste een maand na het einde van het tijdvak). AWR art. 19 lid 1 states that payment is due within one month after the end of that period (binnen één maand na het einde van dat tijdvak). The same text appears in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions, so the 2022 term is one month after the period. [AWR art. 10 and art. 19, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### Return deadlines for period returns and the yearly return

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that monthly and quarterly returns must be filed at the latest on the last day of the month after the quarter or the month (uiterlijk op de laatste dag van de maand die volgt op het kwartaal of de maand). The yearly return is due before 1 April of the next year (vóór 1 april van het eerstvolgende jaar), which is the same last day as 31 March. The filing date (aangiftedatum) is the day the return is received, and filing and payment share one date. Extension is granted only in the case of a calamity (als er sprake is van een calamiteit). [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Published 2022 return-date table

No 2022 version of the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration return-date table was found. The rule that fixes the dates is the last day of the month following the period for period returns and 31 March for the yearly return. A dated 2022 table would settle the exact scheduled dates. [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Yearly return VAT limit

The condition reads less than € 1.883 VAT a year. The amount appears in the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 and the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026, and the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names no year and says nothing on indexation. No 2022 text was found, so the amount for 2022 is UNVERIFIED. [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Other yearly return conditions

The yearly return also requires that there is no Article 23 permit (vergunning artikel 23), that the business is a natural person or a partnership of natural persons only (civil-law partnership (maatschap) or general partnership (vof)), and that each of four intra-EU categories stays below € 10.000 a year for each of these activities separately (voor elk van deze activiteiten afzonderlijk): intra-Community supplies, intra-Community services, intra-Community acquisitions and purchased intra-Community services. The conditions are identical in the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 and the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Supplementary return correction window and timing

UBOB art. 15 in the version valid for 2022 requires correction of returns of the past five calendar years (de afgelopen vijf kalenderjaren), before the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector knows, as soon as possible in the manner indicated by the inspector (zo spoedig mogelijk op de door de inspecteur aangegeven wijze). Failure to correct is a violation (overtreding), and the power to impose a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete) lapses five years after the end of the year. The later change that adds an eight-week outer limit and drops the as-soon-as-possible wording entered into force on 1 January 2025, so it is not the 2022 rule. [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024, Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html), [UBOB art. 15a, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01).

### Supplementary return amount boundary

The amount boundary for correcting a small error in the next return is not established for 2022. The Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 says a correction of less than € 1.000 can go in the next return, the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026 says a correction of € 1.000 or less (een correctie van € 1.000 of minder), and the year wording for 2021 to 2025 stays open. The current page states that a correction of at most € 1000 can go in the next return, that more than € 1.000 requires the form within 8 weeks, and that corrections are possible up to and including 5 years after the year. [Belastingdienst, aangifte corrigeren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### EU distance-sales threshold and option

From 1 July 2021 the € 10.000 threshold applies to the combined qualifying distance sales of goods and digital services, only for a supplier established in one member state, and only when the total in the current calendar year is not higher than € 10 000 (in het lopende kalenderjaar niet hoger is dan € 10 000) and the previous calendar year also stayed within it. Over the threshold, foreign VAT applies from the moment of overrun, and Dutch VAT can resume from 1 January of the next year (vanaf 1 januari van het jaar daarna) after a full calendar year at or below € 10.000. The goods rule covers goods sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country where the supplier arranges or pays for the transport, excluding margin goods, new or nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital services to consumers count toward the threshold and are measured excluding VAT. Below the threshold the supplier may opt for foreign VAT and the choice holds for at least 2 years (minimaal 2 jaar). There is no threshold when the supplier has a fixed establishment in another EU country, and a warehouse alone is not one. The Union scheme (Unieregeling) is optional and gives one return per quarter for all EU distance sales. Foreign input VAT cannot be reclaimed through it. [Wet OB art. 6k, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Besluit of 28 May 2021, Stb. 2021, 261](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, btw bij goederen naar EU-particulieren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

### OSS returns, corrections and records

The Union scheme notification (Unieregeling melding) is made once per quarter in the month after the quarter, by the last day of that month, so the Q3 notification is made in October by 31 October. A nil notification (melding) is also required. The scheme can be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For a first supply the registration must arrive on the 10th day of the month after the month of supply (op de 10e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand van de levering). Three consecutive quarters without a notification give exclusion for 2 years. A notification cannot be resubmitted, corrections go in a later notification at the latest within 3 years (uiterlijk binnen 3 jaar) after the original due date, and records are kept 10 years. Amounts are in euro, converted at the ECB rate of the last day of the quarter, and paid in one payment for all states. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

### Import scheme cap

The import scheme (invoerregeling) is filed monthly and applies only up to € 150 per consignment (zending). Three consecutive missed months give an exclusion for 2 years. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

### EU KOR

The EU KOR was not in force in 2022. The Act of 10 July 2023 (Stb. 2023, 278) brings it into force on 1 January 2025, with a transition provision from 1 October 2024. It therefore plays no part in the 2022 rules. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html).

### ICP statement deadline and period rules

The ICP statement (opgaaf ICP) reports intra-EU supplies of goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. Its total equals rubriek 3b of the return for the same period. Goods go in the period of the invoice date, services in the period the service was supplied. No ICP statement is filed for a period with nothing to report, corrections go in the next return, and the form allows at most 100 lines per section (rubriek). The deadline for 2022 was not found. A later guide states monthly reporting for goods, quarterly only when goods supplies stay at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and in each of the previous 4 quarters, a domestic deadline of within 1 month after the end of the chosen period (binnen 1 maand na afloop van het gekozen tijdvak), a two-monthly option (tweemaandelijks), and 31 January of the next year for both the quarterly Q4 return and the yearly return. Those period details come from a later guide, so their 2022 applicability is not separately established. Call-off stock conditions in that guide are an agreement with a known buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, own transport, no establishment in the arrival state, supply within 12 months of arrival, a register and listing per buyer in the return of the period the transport began. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, tijdvak opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).

### Reverse-charge electronics threshold

The reverse-charge scheme (verleggingsregeling) of art. 12 Wet OB shifts the levy from the supplier to the customer in the cases listed in art. 12 lid 2 and 3 and in the order in council cases of lid 5, in order to create more safeguards for its collection (ten einde voor de inning daarvan meer waarborgen te scheppen). Division E (Afdeling E) of the VAT Implementing Decree 1968 (Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968) holds arts. 24b, 24ba and 24bb. Art. 24b covers subcontracting (onderaanneming) and staff lending in construction and shipbuilding, with the invoice note btw verlegd (VAT reverse-charged). Art. 24ba covers further cases including certain immovable property, gold, emission rights, gas and electricity certificates and electronics. Art. 24bb covers old materials, waste and scrap. For mobile phones, chips before end product, game consoles, tablets and laptops the rule applies only for a total amount of € 10 000 or more per delivery per type of good (voor een totaalbedrag van € 10 000 of meer per levering per soort goed). The supplier invoices with the note VAT reverse-charged, the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate, and remains responsible for the invoice (blijft wel verantwoordelijk). The customer reports the VAT in rubriek 2 (rubriek 2) for domestic reverse charges or rubriek 4 for supplies from abroad and deducts it when the goods or services are used for a taxed activity. For subcontracting the exceptions are work carried out for more than half in the own workshop, staff lent for such work and subordinate work on sold goods, and disputes go to the main contractor's tax office (belastingkantoor). [Wet OB art. 12, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [UBOB Afdeling E, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, wanneer btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [Belastingdienst, hoe werkt btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen), [Belastingdienst, uitzonderingen onderaanneming en personeel uitlenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/bij_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen/uitzonderingen_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen).

### Invoice issue date

An invoice must be issued for every supply to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. A private customer normally needs no invoice, with exceptions on a separate page. The invoice is issued by the 15th day of the month after the month in which you supplied (de 15e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand waarin u hebt geleverd). The handbook example is a supply on 10 April with the invoice due by 15 May. VAT is due at the latest in the period in which the customer paid. [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).

### Invoice content

A full invoice contains the full name and address of both parties (a trade name is allowed when it is registered with the address at the Chamber of Commerce (KVK), and a PO box alone is not enough), the supplier's VAT ID with NL, the Chamber of Commerce number when registered, the invoice date, a unique consecutive number, the kind and quantity of the goods or the kind and scope of the services, the date of supply or prepayment, and the amounts excluding VAT per rate with the unit price, the rate and the VAT amount. A fiscal unity uses the VAT ID of the supplying part. A digital invoice needs customer agreement and assurance of the authenticity of origin, the content and the legibility (de echtheid van herkomst, de inhoud en de leesbaarheid), and there is no general obligation to send digital invoices. [Belastingdienst, factuureisen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/).

### Simplified invoice threshold

A simplified invoice may be used at most € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice. It contains the issue date, name and address, the goods or services, the VAT or the data to calculate it, and for a correction the reference to the original invoice. It cannot be used for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or reverse-charged supplies in a state where the supplier is not established. [Belastingdienst, vereenvoudigde factuur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur).

### Compulsory cash accounting

The cash accounting scheme (kasstelsel) is compulsory for listed trades such as shops, market traders, hairdressers and hospitality (horeca) and others, and can be requested when more than 80 percent of turnover goes to consumers. The 2026 handbook list adds web shops (webshops). Under the cash accounting scheme VAT is accounted for when the customer pays. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Last return of the year

Private use by the entrepreneur includes use by family. There are two situations, mixed use (a car, a business computer, painting a partly private building) and withdrawal of goods for exclusively private use (an onttrekking). Staff private use often limits deduction under the BUA. The last return of the year (Q4 or December) settles private use of goods and services, the car, the pro rata of taxed and exempt turnover, and personnel provisions, gifts and business gifts above € 227. The 2020 and 2026 handbooks also name gas, water and electricity, fictitious services (fictieve diensten) and adjustments (herzieningen). A last return that was already filed is corrected with a supplementary VAT return (Suppletie btw). [Belastingdienst, privégebruik](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Belastingdienst, laatste btw-aangifte van het jaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Business car private use

The VAT Decree on private use of a car and the application of the BUA (Besluit Omzetbelasting, heffing privégebruik auto en toepassing BUA, 25 June 2020, in force from 04-07-2020 to the present) replaces the older car decision. Without records the private use is 2,7% of the catalogue price including VAT and tax on passenger cars and motorcycles (bpm) (van de catalogusprijs, inclusief btw en bpm), for cars in the business assets and cars obtained in another way (op een andere wijze), capped at the VAT deducted in the year plus one fifth of the VAT on purchase (1/5 deel van de btw on purchase). The rate is 1,5 percent when no purchase VAT could be deducted, and 1,5 percent also applies after the end of the fourth year following the year in which the entrepreneur started using the car (na afloop van het vierde jaar volgende op het jaar waarin de ondernemer de auto is gaan gebruiken). The handbooks describe that moment as more than 5 years in use, with the year of purchase counting as a full year. Commuting is private use, exempt use reduces the amount proportionally, a part year is pro rata and the amount goes in the last return of the year. [Besluit privégebruik auto](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst, privégebruik auto van de zaak](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### BUA threshold and exceptions

The Decree on the exclusion of input VAT deduction (Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting 1968, BUA, one version in force from 23-02-2019 to the present) excludes input VAT deduction for maintaining a certain standard of living (het voeren van een zekere staat), business gifts and other gifts, staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport, with making a car available excluded from that list. Art. 1 lid 3 carve-outs cover food and drink for staff, licensed coach transport (besloten busvervoer) for commuting, a commuting bicycle of at most € 749 including VAT (no bicycle in the year or two before, and not 50 percent or more of travel days otherwise covered), and outplacement. Art. 2 limits the exclusion to the VAT owed on a charged consideration. Art. 3 handles staff meals charged below cost plus 25 percent, where deduction is excluded up to 9 percent of the difference (tot 9 percent van het verschil). Art. 4 lid 1 keeps provisions to the same person whose total cost is not more than € 227 per financial year (niet meer bedraagt dan € 227 per boekjaar) outside the exclusion. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Belastingdienst, drempelbedrag personeelsvoorzieningen, giften en relatiegeschenken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### BUA own contribution

An own contribution by the recipient could reduce the amount tested against the € 227 threshold under the explanation that applied in 2022. The decree of 6 October 2023, nr. 2023-20692 (Stcrt. 2023, 28124) ends that website explanation with effect from 1 January 2024 (met ingang van 1 januari 2024). The change therefore does not alter the 2022 position. The 2026 handbook adds that gifts are hit when the recipient would have less than 30 percent deduction. [Besluit van 6 oktober 2023, Stcrt. 2023, 28124](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).

### Corona special deferral and repayment

The corona special deferral (bijzonder uitstel) covered tax debts built up from 12 March 2020 to and including 31 March 2022 (van 12 maart 2020 tot en met 31 maart 2022). The built-up debt is repaid from 1 October 2022 to 1 October 2027 (van 1 oktober 2022 tot 1 oktober 2027), with the first instalment due on 31 October 2022 and 60 monthly instalments. Quarterly payment is possible, and one pause of up to 6 months or 2 quarters is allowed. Extension to 84 months is available only for a scheme of at least € 10.000. New tax debts had to be paid on time again from 1 April 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

### General B2B e-invoicing duty

No general B2B e-invoicing duty for 2022 could be verified. The current government guidance states that suppliers to central government must send e-invoices and that municipalities, provinces and water boards may require them, with Peppol named as the network. That guidance does not settle the 2022 general B2B position. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).
