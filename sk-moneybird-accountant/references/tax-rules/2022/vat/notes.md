## 2. CHANGES FROM 2021

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The corona special deferral (bijzonder uitstel) ran through 2021 as an open deferral. In 2022 the build-up window closed on 31 March 2022, new tax debts had to be paid on time again from 1 April 2022, and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 October 2022 with the first instalment due on 31 October 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).
- The rates did not change. The standard rate stayed at 21%, the reduced rate stayed at 9% for Table I (Tabel I) and the zero rate stayed nil (nihil) for Table II (Tabel II). Accommodation (logies) stayed at 9%. [Wet OB, version 1 July 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html).
- The KOR rules did not change. The ceiling stayed at € 20.000, the test stayed on the current calendar year only, and the notice, three-year participation and three-year re-entry rules stayed the same. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 July 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01).
- The supplementary return (suppletie) timing did not change. The rule remained as soon as possible (zo spoedig mogelijk) under the 2020 text of UBOB art. 15. The eight-week outer limit only arrived on 1 January 2025. [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024, Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html).
- The EU distance-sales threshold of € 10.000 was already in force from 1 July 2021 and stayed unchanged in 2022. MOSS had already lapsed on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop (eenloketsysteem) had taken its place. [Besluit of 28 May 2021, Stb. 2021, 261](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT rate used, the amounts per rate, VAT charged and input VAT deducted. Issue invoices to entrepreneurs and to legal persons that are not entrepreneurs by the 15th day of the month after the month of supply, and retain the invoice details the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration requires. [Belastingdienst, facturen maken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Belastingdienst, factuureisen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).
- Record reverse-charged purchases and sales with the note VAT reverse-charged (btw verlegd), the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate. Report domestic reverse charges in rubriek 2 (rubriek 2) and reverse charges from abroad in rubriek 4, and deduct input VAT only where the taxable use permits it. [Belastingdienst, hoe werkt btw verleggen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).
- Reconcile intra-EU goods and services in the return and the ICP statement (opgaaf ICP). The ICP statement total equals rubriek 3b for the same period. Retain evidence for the zero rate, including transport records and the buyer's VAT ID. The 2022 ICP statement deadline was not established in the checked material. [Belastingdienst, opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).
- Track whether KOR turnover stays within the current-year ceiling of € 20.000. A KOR participant does not charge VAT and does not deduct input VAT, so the choice needs to be made with the input VAT position in view. Keep records that support the Dutch turnover test and the date a supply crosses the ceiling, if that occurs. [Wet OB art. 25, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf).
- File and pay by the deadline for the assigned period, which is the last day of the month following the period for monthly and quarterly filers. The yearly return is due by 31 March of the following year. If a filed return needs correction, use the supplementary return process that applied in 2022. The exact 2022 small-correction boundary is unknown. [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [UBOB art. 15, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01).
- In the last return of the year, settle private use, the business car, the pro rata of taxed and exempt turnover, personnel provisions and the gifts above € 227. If the last return has already been filed, use a supplementary VAT return (Suppletie btw). [Belastingdienst, laatste btw-aangifte van het jaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar).
- For One Stop Shop transactions, file the separate quarterly notification (melding), including a nil notification (melding), and keep the specified records for 10 years. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through the One Stop Shop notification. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
- For a debt built up under the corona special deferral, record the repayment terms in the 60-month schedule from 1 October 2022 to 1 October 2027 and the first instalment due on 31 October 2022. [Belastingdienst, betalingsregeling voor opgebouwde belastingschuld door bijzonder uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/betalenenontvangen/content/betalingsregeling-voor-opgebouwde-belastingschuld-door-bijzonder-uitstel).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Yearly return VAT limit for 2022:** the amount of less than € 1.883 a year is stated in the Handbook for Entrepreneurs (Handboek Ondernemen) 2020 and the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026, and the current page names no year. No 2022 text was found, so the 2022 wording is UNVERIFIED. A 2022 Handbook for Entrepreneurs would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).
- **Other yearly return conditions for 2022:** the conditions (no Article 23 permit (vergunning artikel 23), natural person or partnership of natural persons only, less than € 10.000 a year per intra-EU category) are identical in the 2020 and 2026 handbooks. No 2022 handbook text was found, so the 2022 wording of the whole condition set is not confirmed from a 2022 publication. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).
- **Monthly return trigger:** the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 gives more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter as a monthly-return trigger. The Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026 and the current period-change page no longer state it. Whether it applied in 2022 is not established. A 2022 handbook or a dated administrative instruction would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, wijziging aangiftetijdvak btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).
- **Published 2022 return-date table:** no 2022 table was found, so the scheduled 2022 dates are UNVERIFIED. An archived Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table for 2022 would settle them. [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Supplementary return amount boundary for 2022:** unknown. The Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2020 says less than € 1.000 (minder dan € 1.000) may go in the next return, the Handbook for Entrepreneurs 2026 says € 1.000 or less (€ 1.000 of minder), and the wording for 2021 to 2025 stays open. A 2022 handbook would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, aangifte corrigeren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren).
- **ICP statement deadline and detailed period rules for 2022:** the deadline was not found. The later guide describes monthly goods periods, quarterly reporting only at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and each of the previous 4 quarters, a 1-month domestic deadline and a two-monthly option (tweemaandelijks), but it does not establish the 2022 deadline or confirm the 2022 wording. A 2022 guide would settle both. [Belastingdienst, toelichting digitale opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, tijdvak opgaaf ICP](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp).
- **General B2B e-invoicing duty for 2022:** not verified. The current government guidance covers e-invoicing to central government and the option for other public bodies, and does not settle the 2022 general B2B position. A dated 2022 primary source or archived official guidance would settle it. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).
- **One Stop Shop inactivity:** the claim that participation ends after two years without supplies while the notifications (meldingen) continue does not appear on the checked Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page and stays UNVERIFIED. A primary rule or Dutch Tax and Customs Administration guidance stating that condition would settle it. [Belastingdienst, melden via het eenloketsysteem](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
- **Table I (Tabel I) list pages carry no year:** the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration goods and services pages for the 9 percent rate carry no year, so the exact 2022 version of those lists is not separately dated on the pages. The post history for electronic publications rests on the decree of 31 March 2022, nr. 2022-6334 and the accommodation item rests on Stb. 2025, 339. [Belastingdienst, goods at 9 percent](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Besluit van 31 maart 2022, nr. 2022-6334](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html).
- **ViDA EU act dates:** the checked material records 11 and 25 March 2025 as the EU adoption dates and 14 April 2025 as the entry into force of the ViDA acts, but these dates were not found on a Dutch primary page and stay UNVERIFIED. No 2022 rule depends on them. [Kamerbrief 26 juni 2025, Kamerstuk 22112, nr. 4090](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-22112-4090.html).
- **Future legislation, no effect on 2022:** the Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) news item of 11 September 2026 describes e-invoicing (e-facturatie) from 1 July 2030 and reporting from 1 July 2031 for national B2B, with a bill expected before summer 2027 and KOR businesses exempt from e-invoicing and reporting (vrijgesteld van e-facturatie en -rapportage). Bill 36.920 on the ViDA single VAT registration is adopted-not-yet-in-force, with entry into force on 1 January 2027. Neither affects 2022. [Rijksoverheid, 11 September 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven), [Eerste Kamer, bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn).
- **Resolved KOR turnover test conflict:** a general summary described the € 20.000 ceiling as applying to both the application year and the preceding year. For 2020 to 2024 the law tests the turnover in a calendar year (de omzet in een kalenderjaar) and no subsection names the preceding year, so the preceding-year test is wrong for 2022. That test belongs to the rules from 1 January 2025 under art. 25a lid 5 and lid 9. [Wet OB, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- **Resolved yearly return deadline wording:** before 1 April (vóór 1 april) and 31 March put the last day in the same place, so there is no conflict. Both identify 31 March as the last day for the yearly return. [Belastingdienst, waar moet u aan denken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst, uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Resolved MOSS replacement:** MOSS lapsed on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop (eenloketsysteem) took its place, so 2022 uses the one-stop shop. [Belastingdienst, EU btw-melding](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/).
