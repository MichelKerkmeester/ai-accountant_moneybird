## 2027

Path: [2027/changes.md](2027/changes.md)

- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 1.200 in 2026 to € 900 in 2027 and the amount for those at state pension age (AOW) from € 600 to € 450. [Income tax (inkomstenbelasting)](2027/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) fell from 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 to 11,91% above € 79.569 in the 2027 text. [entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)](2027/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2027 gets 2% off capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000, against 4% off capped at € 1.200 in 2026. [Costs and the car (kosten en auto)](2027/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- New from 1 January 2027 is a pseudo-final levy (pseudo-eindheffing) of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately. [Costs and the car](2027/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution (bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag) goes from 4,85% in 2026 to 5,02% in 2027 (proposed, a budget estimate until the ministerial regulation sets the rate). [Zvw contribution](2027/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The income cap (aftoppingsgrens) on the contribution base (premiegrondslag) stays € 137.800 in 2026 and € 137.800 in the 2027 text, with a proposed freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032. [Pension and disability insurance (pensioen en AOV)](2027/pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) lapse, so default penalties (verzuimboetes) are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. [Procedure](2027/procedure/notes.md#5-changes)
- The VBAR entered into force on 31 December 2026, so the legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) applies throughout 2027. [employment relationship (arbeidsrelatie)](2027/work-relationship/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2026

Path: [2026/changes.md](2026/changes.md)

- Accommodation (logies) moved from 9% to 21% from 1 January 2026. [VAT](2026/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- In 2026 there are no default penalties (verzuimboetes), but penalties for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboetes) are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026. [employment relationship](2026/work-relationship/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026 and the state pension age (AOW age) amount from € 1.235 to € 600. [income tax](2026/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) fell from 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025 to 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026. [business deductions](2026/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) rose from € 57.684 per person in 2025 to € 59.357 in 2026. [box-3](2026/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2026 statute still prints € 0,23 per km, but the applied amount for 2026 is € 0,25 per km, retroactive to 1 January 2026. [costs and car](2026/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2026 gets 4% off capped at € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000. [costs and car](2026/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution assessed (bijdrage bij aanslag) fell from 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026. [health insurance contribution](2026/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2025

Path: [2025/changes.md](2025/changes.md)

- The small businesses scheme (KOR) changed from the current-calendar-year test with a minimum participation and re-entry period to the 2025 rules for a business established in the Netherlands, with turnover information for the preceding and current calendar years. [VAT (btw)](2025/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply to employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties), no instructions (aanwijzingen) are given and false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen) and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). [employment relationship](2025/work-relationship/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 3.750 in 2024 to € 2.470 in 2025. [entrepreneur's allowance](2025/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) fell from 13,31% in 2024 to 12,7% in 2025. [entrepreneur's allowance](2025/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The box 1 table below state pension age (AOW) became three brackets, 35,82% up to € 38.441, 37,48% to € 76.817 and 49,50% above. [income tax](2025/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) rose from € 57.000 per person in 2024 to € 57.684 in 2025. [box 3](2025/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2025 gets 5% off capped at € 1.500, so 17% up to € 30.000. [costs and car](2025/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution (zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025. [Zvw](2025/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2024

Path: [2024/changes.md](2024/changes.md)

- Box 2 moved from one rate of 26,9% in 2023 to two brackets in 2024, 24,5% up to € 67.000 and 33% above € 67.000. [box-2](2024/box-2/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 5.030 to € 3.750 and the AOW-age amount from € 2.515 to € 1.875. [income tax (inkomstenbelasting)](2024/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) fell from 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024. [entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)](2024/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2024 the own contribution of an employee is no longer subtracted when total BUA costs are tested against € 227 per recipient. [VAT (btw)](2024/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,21 in 2023 to € 0,23 in 2024. [costs and car (kosten en auto)](2024/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The EIA percentage fell from 45,5% in 2023 to 40% in 2024. [investments (investeringen)](2024/investments/notes.md#5-changes)
- The box 3 rate rose from 32% in 2023 to 36% in 2024. [box-3](2024/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024. [Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage)](2024/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2023

Path: [2023/changes.md](2023/changes.md)

- The qualifying solar-panel zero rate began on 1 January 2023. [VAT (btw)](2023/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. [Entrepreneur's allowance](2023/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 was the last year for an addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), because the articles lapsed on 1 January 2023 and from 2023 no addition is possible. [entrepreneur's allowance](2023/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) rose from 9,5% above € 69.398 to 12,57% above € 73.031 and the maximum value of a deduction fell from 40% to 36,93%. [Income tax](2023/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The old band method was replaced from 1 January 2023 by the category method of the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) box 3. [Box 3](2023/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023. [Costs and car](2023/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) rose from € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. [Pension and disability insurance](2023/pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023. [Zvw](2023/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2022

Path: [2022/changes.md](2022/changes.md)

- The corona special deferral (bijzonder uitstel) build-up window closed on 31 March 2022 and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 October 2022 with the first instalment due on 31 October 2022. [VAT](2022/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022. [entrepreneur's allowance](2022/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) rose from 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021 to 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022. [entrepreneur's allowance](2022/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The first rate below state pension age (AOW) fell from 37,10% to 37,07% and the top boundary moved from € 68.507 to € 69.398. [income tax](2022/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution (bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022. [Zvw](2022/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) rose from € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022. [costs and car](2022/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 gets 6% off capped at € 2.100, so 16% up to € 35.000, against 10% off capped at € 4.000 for a 2021 admission. [costs and car](2022/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 is the last year for an addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), with a maximum of € 9.632 against € 9.395 in 2021. [pension and AOV](2022/pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2021

Path: [2021/changes.md](2021/changes.md)

- From 1 July 2021 the e-commerce package introduced the € 10.000 EU distance-sales threshold for qualifying goods and digital services together. [VAT](2021/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 7.030 in 2020 to € 6.670 in 2021. [entrepreneur's allowance](2021/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) rose from 3,5% above € 68.507 in 2020 to 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021. [entrepreneur's allowance](2021/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The combined first rate for those below state pension age fell from 37,35% to 37,10% and the first statutory boundary moved from € 34.712 to € 35.129. [income tax](2021/income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) rose from 5,45% in 2020 to 5,75% in 2021. [zvw](2021/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) rose from € 30.846 per person in 2020 to € 50.000 in 2021. [box-3](2021/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zero-emission reduction for a car first admitted in 2021 is 10% off capped at € 4.000, so 12% up to € 40.000. [costs and car](2021/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) rose from € 12.986 in 2020 to € 13.236 in 2021. [pension and disability insurance](2021/pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2020

Path: [2020/changes.md](2020/changes.md)

- The new KOR replaced the old VAT-based reduction from 1 January 2020, with a Dutch calendar-year turnover ceiling of € 20.000. [btw](2020/vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which caps the value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) at 46%. [ondernemersaftrek](2020/business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is € 30.846 per person and € 61.692 with a fiscal partner. [box-3](2020/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 Zvw rate by assessment (aanslag) is 5,45% and the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 57.232. [zvw](2020/health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 KIA table gives 28% of the investment up to € 58.238 and a fixed € 16.307 up to € 107.848, with a € 450 per-asset minimum. [investeringen](2020/investments/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2020 gets a reduction of 14% off with a cap of € 6.300, so 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. [kosten-en-auto](2020/costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) for 2020 is € 12.986. [pensioen-en-aov](2020/pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- The second restriction of the moratorium exception applies from 1 January 2020, when enforcement also follows if instructions (aanwijzingen) are not followed within a reasonable term. [arbeidsrelatie](2020/work-relationship/notes.md#5-changes)
