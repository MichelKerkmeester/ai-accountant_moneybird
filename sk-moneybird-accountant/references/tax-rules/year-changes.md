# Year changes

Generated overview of the changes in every tax year. Each section names the year note and repeats its authored bullets. The note owns the wording, so one edit moves both files.

## 2027

Path: [2027/changes.md](2027/changes.md)

- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 1.200 in 2026 to € 900 in 2027 and the AOW-age amount from € 600 to € 450. [inkomstenbelasting](2027/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing fell from 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 to 11,91% above € 79.569 in the 2027 text. [ondernemersaftrek](2027/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2027 gets 2% off capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000, against 4% off capped at € 1.200 in 2026. [kosten-en-auto](2027/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- New from 1 January 2027 is a pseudo-eindheffing of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately. [kosten-en-auto](2027/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag goes from 4,85% in 2026 to 5,02% in 2027 (proposed, a budget estimate until the ministerial regulation sets the rate). [zvw](2027/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag stays € 137.800 in 2026 and € 137.800 in the 2027 text, with a proposed freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032. [pensioen-en-aov](2027/pensioen-en-aov/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse, so verzuimboetes are possible again and the default bedrijfsbezoek start ends. [procedure](2027/procedure/notes.md#5-changes)
- The VBAR entered into force on 31 December 2026, so the rechtsvermoeden applies throughout 2027. [arbeidsrelatie](2027/arbeidsrelatie/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2026

Path: [2026/changes.md](2026/changes.md)

- Logies moved from 9% to 21% from 1 January 2026. [btw](2026/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- In 2026 there are no verzuimboetes, but vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026. [arbeidsrelatie](2026/arbeidsrelatie/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026 and the AOW-age amount from € 1.235 to € 600. [inkomstenbelasting](2026/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing fell from 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025 to 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026. [ondernemersaftrek](2026/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The heffingsvrij vermogen rose from € 57.684 per person in 2025 to € 59.357 in 2026. [box-3](2026/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2026 statute still prints € 0,23 per km, but the applied amount for 2026 is € 0,25 per km, retroactive to 1 January 2026. [kosten-en-auto](2026/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2026 gets 4% off capped at € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000. [kosten-en-auto](2026/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026. [zvw](2026/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2025

Path: [2025/changes.md](2025/changes.md)

- The KOR changed from the current-calendar-year test with a minimum participation and re-entry period to the 2025 rules for a business established in the Netherlands, with turnover information for the preceding and current calendar years. [btw](2025/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply to work relationships, no aanwijzingen are given and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. [arbeidsrelatie](2025/arbeidsrelatie/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 3.750 in 2024 to € 2.470 in 2025. [ondernemersaftrek](2025/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The MKB-winstvrijstelling fell from 13,31% in 2024 to 12,7% in 2025. [ondernemersaftrek](2025/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The below-AOW box 1 table became three brackets, 35,82% up to € 38.441, 37,48% to € 76.817 and 49,50% above. [inkomstenbelasting](2025/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The heffingsvrij vermogen rose from € 57.000 per person in 2024 to € 57.684 in 2025. [box-3](2025/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2025 gets 5% off capped at € 1.500, so 17% up to € 30.000. [kosten-en-auto](2025/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025. [zvw](2025/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2024

Path: [2024/changes.md](2024/changes.md)

- Box 2 moved from one rate of 26,9% in 2023 to two brackets in 2024, 24,5% up to € 67.000 and 33% above € 67.000. [box-2](2024/box-2/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 5.030 to € 3.750 and the AOW-age amount from € 2.515 to € 1.875. [inkomstenbelasting](2024/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The MKB-winstvrijstelling fell from 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024. [ondernemersaftrek](2024/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2024 the own contribution of an employee is no longer subtracted when total BUA costs are tested against € 227 per recipient. [btw](2024/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,21 in 2023 to € 0,23 in 2024. [kosten-en-auto](2024/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The EIA percentage fell from 45,5% in 2023 to 40% in 2024. [investeringen](2024/investeringen/notes.md#5-changes)
- The box 3 rate rose from 32% in 2023 to 36% in 2024. [box-3](2024/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024. [zvw](2024/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2023

Path: [2023/changes.md](2023/changes.md)

- The qualifying solar-panel zero rate began on 1 January 2023. [btw](2023/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. [ondernemersaftrek](2023/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 was the last year for an addition to the oudedagsreserve, because the articles lapsed on 1 January 2023 and from 2023 no addition is possible. [ondernemersaftrek](2023/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing rose from 9,5% above € 69.398 to 12,57% above € 73.031 and the maximum value of a deduction fell from 40% to 36,93%. [inkomstenbelasting](2023/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The old band method was replaced from 1 January 2023 by the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3. [box-3](2023/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023. [kosten-en-auto](2023/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum jaarruimte rose from € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. [pensioen-en-aov](2023/pensioen-en-aov/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023. [zvw](2023/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2022

Path: [2022/changes.md](2022/changes.md)

- The corona bijzonder uitstel build-up window closed on 31 March 2022 and repayment of the built-up debt started on 1 October 2022 with the first term due on 31 October 2022. [btw](2022/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 6.670 in 2021 to € 6.310 in 2022. [ondernemersaftrek](2022/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing rose from 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021 to 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022. [ondernemersaftrek](2022/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The below-AOW first rate fell from 37,10% to 37,07% and the top boundary moved from € 68.507 to € 69.398. [inkomstenbelasting](2022/inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,75% in 2021 to 5,50% in 2022. [zvw](2022/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The gemengde kosten drempel rose from € 4.700 in 2021 to € 4.800 in 2022. [kosten-en-auto](2022/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2022 gets 6% off capped at € 2.100, so 16% up to € 35.000, against 10% off capped at € 4.000 for a 2021 admission. [kosten-en-auto](2022/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 is the last year for an addition to the oudedagsreserve, with a maximum of € 9.632 against € 9.395 in 2021. [pensioen-en-aov](2022/pensioen-en-aov/notes.md#5-changes)

## 2020

Path: [2020/changes.md](2020/changes.md)

- The new KOR replaced the old VAT-based reduction from 1 January 2020, with a Dutch calendar-year turnover ceiling of € 20.000. [btw](2020/btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing for 2020 is 3,5% above € 68.507, which caps the value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling at 46%. [ondernemersaftrek](2020/ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 heffingsvrij vermogen is € 30.846 per person and € 61.692 with a fiscal partner. [box-3](2020/box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 Zvw rate by aanslag is 5,45% and the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 57.232. [zvw](2020/zvw/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2020 KIA table gives 28% of the investment up to € 58.238 and a fixed € 16.307 up to € 107.848, with a € 450 per-asset minimum. [investeringen](2020/investeringen/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2020 gets a reduction of 14% off with a cap of € 6.300, so 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. [kosten-en-auto](2020/kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum jaarruimte for 2020 is € 12.986. [pensioen-en-aov](2020/pensioen-en-aov/notes.md#5-changes)
- The second restriction of the moratorium exception applies from 1 January 2020, when enforcement also follows if aanwijzingen are not followed within a reasonable term. [arbeidsrelatie](2020/arbeidsrelatie/notes.md#5-changes)
