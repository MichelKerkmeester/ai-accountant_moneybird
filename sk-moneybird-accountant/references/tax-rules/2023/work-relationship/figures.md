## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

In 2023 the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) was in force and the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) still applied, so in a case without bad faith (non-kwaadwillend), the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing) instead of correcting straight away (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). The two legal markers of the year were the Deliveroo ruling of the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) on 24 March 2023, which put integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap) in the catalogue of circumstances and treated free substitution (vrije vervanging) as no absolute element, and the withdrawal of the approval of the model agreements for free substitution on 14 August 2023 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) was still in internet consultation from 6 October 2023, with an intended entry into force of 1 July 2025, so the low hourly rate presumption was proposed law in 2023 and not in force (same URL). The interaction guidelines (richtlijnen doorwerking) for income tax (IH) and VAT (OB) (guidelines on how payroll tax corrections at the client carry over to income tax and VAT for the contractor) exist since 2023 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
