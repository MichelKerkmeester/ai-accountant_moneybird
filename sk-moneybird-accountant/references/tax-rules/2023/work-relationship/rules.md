## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties is the Wet van 3 februari 2016 and entered into force on 1 May 2016. It replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, a prior declaration about the status of the work) (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). In 2023 this law was the framework for the question whether work is done in loondienst (employment) or by a zelfstandige (self-employed person). The law gives no fixed checklist, the qualification follows from the facts that the courts weigh, see 3.2 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). One common mix-up: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement, see 3.4 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). Working outside loondienst does not by itself make the opdrachtnemer (contractor) an ondernemer for income tax or VAT (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### 3.2 Loondienst test

Loondienst has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid (personal work), and pay for the work (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur (same URL). Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT (same URL). So the arbeidsrelatie answer and the fiscal ondernemerschap answer are two separate questions.

### 3.3 Fictieve dienstbetrekkingen

Loondienst can also follow from a fictieve dienstbetrekking (deemed employment) even where the ordinary test points the other way. The loondienst page lists aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten (agents), artiesten and beroepssporters (artists and professional athletes), gelijkgestelden (persons equated with employees), stagiairs (interns), thuiswerkers (home workers) and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair (intermediary) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The gelijkgestelden category is often relevant for a ZZP with one large client. It applies when the person usually works 2 or more days a week for the same client and earns at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (same URL). Both parts of that test must be met for the category to fit (same URL).

### 3.4 Opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten

Thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded from the fictieve dienstbetrekking by agreement. The exclusion must be in writing in the contract and must be made before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW (sickness, disability and unemployment benefits) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The legal basis is the Besluit van 8 april 2016, Stb. 2016, 165 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). The timing condition is strict, a clause added after the first payment does not meet the stated condition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). For the fiscal side, using this opt-out does not make the opdrachtnemer an ondernemer for income tax or VAT (same URL).

### 3.5 DBA enforcement regime 2023

Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). In a case that is not kwaadwillend the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing instead of correcting at once. The aanwijzing asks the parties to reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or to process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing is not followed, a correctieverplichting (correction obligation) applies from the date of the aanwijzing (same URL). Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan (deliberately creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment), knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (additional payroll tax assessments) and boetes (fines) (same URL). In 2023 the practical route was therefore: first an aanwijzing with a term, then corrections running from the aanwijzing date if nothing changed. A Handhavingsplan was made for 2023 per the 2026 plan (same URL and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### 3.6 End of the moratorium

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid (false self-employment) can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). For a 2023 relationship this means a later correction reaches back to 1 January 2025 at the earliest, or further back in a kwaadwillend case or when an aanwijzing from before 2025 was not followed. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The tranche 2025 plan had stated integration from 1 January 2026 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). This is enforcement policy, not statute. The boetebeleid (fine policy) by year: 2025 no verzuimboetes (default fines) and no vergrijpboetes (fines for intent or gross negligence) for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and for correcties over 2025 this stays so. 2026 no verzuimboetes, vergrijpboetes possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing (soft landing, no verzuimboetes and in principle starting with a bedrijfsbezoek) lapse on 1 January 2027 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). All of this is later than tax year 2023.

### 3.7 Case law on partijbedoeling

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (what the parties intended their relationship to be) in qualifying the work relationship (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). This ruling was part of the framework in 2023.

### 3.8 Deliveroo ruling

HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), held that the maaltijdbezorgers (meal deliverers) are werknemers (employees). The ruling contains an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding and ondernemerschap as elements to weigh, and vrije vervanging is no absolute element but part of the catalogue (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). This is settled case law. The status of unknown in the earlier research is corrected to Corrected in the figures table. The ruling led to the withdrawal of the approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging on 14 August 2023, see 3.9 (same URL). A later development, not part of tax year 2023: a ruling on platform work (Uber) holds that there is no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten, so ondernemerschap is a full criterion, and the government states this has no effect on handhaving (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The exact date and ECLI of that later ruling are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.9 Modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst (model contract) means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described in it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). From that date those specific modelovereenkomsten no longer carry the approval. This is the 2023 event in this topic. The general stop on new modelovereenkomsten came later, see 3.10.

### 3.10 Modelovereenkomsten stop and use-up-to date

Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty), because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done (same URL). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). For tax year 2023 this whole item is later than the year. The condition that is relevant to 2023 work stays the same: the modelovereenkomst protects only if the parties work exactly as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### 3.11 Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie is meant for opdrachtgevers (clients) with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/). It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon (legal person) such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar (intermediary) or tussenpersoon (agent), or for a foreign opdrachtnemer (same URL). The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021, the online pilot environment stayed active, and there was no rechtskracht (legal force) during or after the pilot (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring (client statement) as one outcome (same URL). In 2023 the tool existed, and its outcome did not bind the Belastingdienst.

### 3.12 VBAR internet consultation

The internet consultation of the original VBAR started on 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025, mits uitvoerbaar (provided it is executable). Status proposed (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The later path, none of it law in 2023: the bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend (submitted) on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. The nota van wijziging (memorandum of amendment) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking (clarification) part. The Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 with all parties except FVD, and the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (voice vote). The Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, was published on 29 June 2026 and entered into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026. That date was chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone, in force before 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). In tax year 2023 none of this was in force.

### 3.13 VBAR rule

The enacted rule is new art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most the hourly amount is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst (employment contract). Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling (ministerial regulation) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). The effect is that the werkende (the worker) can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove that there is no arbeidsovereenkomst (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule (same URL). In 2023 this was proposed law at most and not in force.

### 3.14 VBAR hourly threshold, statutory text

The memorie van toelichting (explanatory memorandum) explains the € 36 in the bill text. The build-up at peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2025: 120% of the WML (wettelijk minimumloon, statutory minimum wage) of € 14,06 per hour, plus a 25% opslag (mark-up) for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance for the self-employed) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours. That gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The WML used for 1 January 2025 is € 14,06 per hour (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025). Artikel III lid 2 provides that the amount for the first application after entry into force is given by ministeriële regeling. That regeling was not found on 2026-10-11, see section 7 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### 3.15 VBAR hourly threshold, indexed

The WML rose to € 14,71 per hour on 1 January 2026 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026). Under the lid 2 mechanism every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering). This € 38 (peildatum 1 januari 2026) is what the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. In tax year 2023 neither amount was in force.

### 3.16 Richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB

The richtlijnen for the doorwerking (carry-over effect) to inkomstenbelasting (income tax) and omzetbelasting (VAT) for the opdrachtnemer exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The focus is on the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie (the qualification of the income source). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns (same PDF and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### 3.17 BAZ, bill 36 912

The BAZ appears as a separate bill, 36 912, proposed. It was ingediend on 24 March 2026, a technische briefing followed on 13 May 2026, the inbreng verslag was delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026. On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote. The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). The bill is later than tax year 2023 and changes nothing in the 2023 rules. It is included here because the same discussion about zelfstandigheid names it.

### 3.18 IB return 2023 filing deadline

The income tax return for 2023 must arrive before 1 May 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### 3.19 IB return 2023 with uitstel

A granted uitstel (deferral) gives until before 1 September 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### 3.20 Definitive aanslag 2023 latest

The definitive aanslag (definitive assessment) for 2023 follows no later than 31 December 2026 when the return is filed before 1 May 2024, and no later than 30 April 2027 with 4 months uitstel (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
