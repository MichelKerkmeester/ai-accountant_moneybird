## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA enforcement regime: unchanged. In 2022 the handhavingsmoratorium was in force with the aanwijzing route (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). In 2023 it was still in force with the same aanwijzing route, and a Handhavingsplan was made for 2023 per the 2026 plan (same URL and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2022 the achtste voortgangsbrief of 16 December 2022 announced a rechtsvermoeden tied to an uurtarief, with no effect outside arbeidsrecht, legislation for 1 January 2025 and the moratorium lifted uiterlijk 1 January 2025 (proposed, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). In 2023 the internet consultation of the original VBAR opened on 6 October 2023, with an intended entry into force of 1 July 2025, mits uitvoerbaar (proposed, same URL). So the 2022 announcement became a concrete consultation draft in 2023.
- Cabinet aim: the Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the kabinetsreactie of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen handhaving and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier (proposed, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The enforcement sources for 2023 record the moratorium as still in force (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Case law: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746 (partijbedoeling), was already there in 2022 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). New in 2023 was HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), with the omstandighedencatalogus and vrije vervanging as no absolute element (same URL).
- Modelovereenkomsten: new in 2023, the approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The stop on new modelovereenkomsten since 6 September 2024 and the use-up-to date of 31 December 2029 came after 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- Richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB: new in 2023, they exist since 2023 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the whole qualification story, not only the label. The qualification depends on how the work is actually done, which is the reason the Belastingdienst gives for stopping new modelovereenkomsten (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). For a 2023 file, store the written contract or modelovereenkomst, the invoice trail, and the facts that show how work and working hours are set, where the commercial risk sits and how the opdrachtnemer acts as an entrepreneur (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- If an opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden or artiesten is used, keep the written clause that was agreed before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Keep every aanwijzing and the reaction to it. The usual term is 3 months and the correctieverplichting runs from the date of the aanwijzing if the aanwijzing is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Note the doorwerking rules when a correction lands at the opdrachtgever. In principle no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie, with earlier years meaning tax years before the year of the LH correction. In regular IH or OB supervision the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken are examined, with attention to future returns (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Report the 2023 result of the onderneming in the income tax return for 2023. The return must arrive before 1 May 2024, and with a granted uitstel before 1 September 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- No loondienst does not make the opdrachtnemer an ondernemer for income tax or VAT, so the qualification of the winst uit onderneming is a separate step with its own records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Keep the gegevensdragers for seven years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 has the same text in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The seven-year term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, and basisgegevens include the grootboek, the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, the voorraadadministratie, the in- and verkoopadministratie and the loonadministratie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- The definitive aanslag for 2023 follows by 31 December 2026 at the latest, or by 30 April 2027 with 4 months uitstel (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

**Proposed or not yet law for 2023**

- VBAR. In 2023 it was an internet consultation only, started 6 October 2023, intended entry into force 1 July 2025, mits uitvoerbaar (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The later stages, none of them 2023 law: to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024, critical advice published 11 November 2024, bill 36 783 ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer, nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) that removed the verduidelijking part, Tweede Kamer adoption on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), Eerste Kamer adoption on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan, Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, entry into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone (in force before 1 January 2027). Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/.
- BAZ, bill 36 912. Proposed. Ingediend 24 March 2026, technische briefing 13 May 2026, inbreng verslag 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, verslag published 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 2026-10-11 awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, no debate and no vote. Docket summary: about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, waiting period about one year, opt-out with a qualifying private policy (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). This bill is later than tax year 2023 and changes nothing in the 2023 law.

**UNVERIFIED**

- The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 for the HR Uber ruling come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it. The substance of that ruling is stated by the government, no rangorde between the gezichtspunten, so ondernemerschap is a full criterion, and the government says it has no effect on handhaving (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

**Unknown**

- The ministeriële regeling under artikel III lid 2 VBAR that sets the amount for the first application after entry into force was not found on 2026-10-11. Publication in the Staatscourant or on wetten.overheid.nl would settle it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

**Conflicts, now resolved**

- € 36 against € 38. Both are right. € 36 is the statutory text, based on € 35,43 unrounded at peildatum 1 January 2025 (120% of the WML of € 14,06 plus the opslagen, times 1,5). € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 (€ 14,71), the peildatum the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein use. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- Regular DBA handhaving start date. The tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The 2026 plan decides.
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA. It is not the Wet DBA but the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force 1 May 2016. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026. Both are true. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Deciding source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

**Policy, not statute**

- The DBA boetebeleid by year and the move to regular handhaving are enforcement policy, not statute (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
