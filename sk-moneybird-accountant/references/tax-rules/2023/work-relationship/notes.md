## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA enforcement regime: unchanged. In 2022 the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) was in force with the instruction (aanwijzing) route (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). In 2023 it was still in force with the same instruction route, and an enforcement plan (Handhavingsplan) was made for 2023 per the 2026 plan (same URL and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2022 the eighth progress letter (voortgangsbrief) of 16 December 2022 announced a legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) tied to an hourly rate (uurtarief), with no effect outside labour law (arbeidsrecht), legislation for 1 January 2025 and the moratorium lifted by 1 January 2025 at the latest (proposed, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). In 2023 the internet consultation of the original VBAR opened on 6 October 2023, with an intended entry into force of 1 July 2025, provided it is feasible (uitvoerbaar) (proposed, same URL). So the 2022 announcement became a concrete consultation draft in 2023.
- Cabinet aim: the Rutte IV coalition agreement of 10 January 2022 and the cabinet response (kabinetsreactie) of 24 June 2022 aimed to strengthen enforcement (handhaving) and lift the moratorium per 1 January 2025 or earlier (proposed, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The enforcement sources for 2023 record the moratorium as still in force (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Case law: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746 (intention of the parties (partijbedoeling)), was already there in 2022 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). New in 2023 was HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), with the catalogue of circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) and free substitution (vrije vervanging) as no absolute element (same URL).
- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten): new in 2023, the approval of the model agreements for free substitution was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The stop on new model agreements since 6 September 2024 and the use-up-to date of 31 December 2029 came after 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- Interaction guidelines (richtlijnen doorwerking) for income tax (IH) and VAT (OB): new in 2023, they exist since 2023 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the whole qualification story, not only the label. The qualification depends on how the work is actually done, which is the reason the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) gives for stopping new model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). For a 2023 file, store the written contract or model agreement (modelovereenkomst), the invoice trail, and the facts that show how work and working hours are set, where the commercial risk sits and how the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts as an entrepreneur (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- If an opt-out for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) or artists (artiesten) is used, keep the written clause that was agreed before the first wage payment. The contractor then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Keep every instruction (aanwijzing) and the reaction to it. The usual term is 3 months and the correction obligation (correctieverplichting) runs from the date of the instruction if the instruction is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Note the interaction (doorwerking) rules when a correction lands at the client (opdrachtgever). In principle no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie), with earlier years meaning tax years before the year of the LH correction. In regular IH or OB supervision the most recent open return ((winst)aangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) are examined, with attention to future returns (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Report the 2023 result of the business (onderneming) in the income tax return for 2023. The return must arrive before 1 May 2024, and with a granted deferral (uitstel) before 1 September 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- No employment (loondienst) does not make the contractor an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax or VAT, so the qualification of the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) is a separate step with its own records (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 has the same text in the 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026 versions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The seven-year term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual, and basic records (basisgegevens) include the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie) and the payroll records (loonadministratie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- The final assessment (definitieve aanslag) for 2023 follows by 31 December 2026 at the latest, or by 30 April 2027 with 4 months deferral (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

**Proposed or not yet law for 2023**

- VBAR. In 2023 it was an internet consultation only, started 6 October 2023, intended entry into force 1 July 2025, provided it is feasible (uitvoerbaar) (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The later stages, none of them 2023 law: to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024, critical advice published 11 November 2024, bill 36 783 submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer), memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) that removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part, House of Representatives adoption on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), Senate adoption on 16 June 2026 by a vote by sitting and standing (zitten en opstaan), Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, entry into force on 31 December 2026 by the Decree of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone (in force before 1 January 2027). Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/.
- BAZ, bill 36 912. Proposed. Submitted 24 March 2026, a technical briefing (technische briefing) 13 May 2026, contribution to the debate on the report (inbreng verslag) 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, report published 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 2026-10-11 awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), no debate and no vote. Docket summary: about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, waiting period about one year, opt-out with a qualifying private policy (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). This bill is later than tax year 2023 and changes nothing in the 2023 law.

**UNVERIFIED**

- The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 for the HR Uber ruling come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it. The substance of that ruling is stated by the government, no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap) is a full criterion, and the government says it has no effect on handhaving (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

**Unknown**

- The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under Article III, subsection 2, VBAR that sets the amount for the first application after entry into force was not found on 2026-10-11. Publication in the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) or on the laws site (wetten.overheid.nl) would settle it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

**Conflicts, now resolved**

- € 36 against € 38. Both are right. € 36 is the statutory text, based on € 35,43 unrounded at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025 (120% of the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML) of € 14,06 plus the surcharges, times 1,5). € 38 is the same amount indexed to the minimum wage of 1 January 2026 (€ 14,71), the reference date the Senate, the Government of the Netherlands (rijksoverheid.nl) and the government business portal (Ondernemersplein) use. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- Regular DBA enforcement start date. The tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) aims to integrate from 2027 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The 2026 plan decides.
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA. It is not the Wet DBA but the Decree of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force 1 May 2016. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- BAZ report date 27 May against 29 May 2026. Both are true. The contribution to the debate was delivered on 27 May and the report (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Deciding source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912.

**Policy, not statute**

- The DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid) by year and the move to regular handhaving are enforcement policy, not statute (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
