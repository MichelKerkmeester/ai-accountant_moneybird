## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example: applying the employment (loondienst) elements to a 2023 engagement.**
An contractor (opdrachtnemer) does the bookkeeping for one client in 2023. Step 1, check the three elements. Employer authority (werkgeversgezag): the client sets the working hours and can direct the contractor to other tasks. Personal work (persoonlijke arbeid): the work must be done by the contractor in person. Pay: the client pays for the work. All three elements are present in this invented case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Step 2, weigh the factors. Points toward employment: the work is embedded in the client's organisation and the client sets how and when the work is done. Points away: the contractor carries commercial risk, works for several clients and can act as an entrepreneur. Step 3, the checked material gives no fixed score, the qualification follows from the whole picture (same URL). No arithmetic.

**Calculated example: the instruction (aanwijzing) route for a 2023 relationship.**
A sole trader works for one client through 2023. Early in 2023 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) concludes that the relationship has the elements of employment and that there is no bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Step 1, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration first gives an instruction instead of correcting at once. Step 2, the parties usually have 3 months to reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or to process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte). If the instruction is dated 15 March 2023, that term runs to about 15 June 2023. Step 3, if the instruction is not followed, the correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applies from the date of the instruction, so from 15 March 2023 (same URL). No euro amounts are used here because the checked material gives none for the 2023 correction itself.

**Calculated example: the use-up-to date of an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst).**
Suppose an approved model agreement is valid on 6 September 2024 and states a different validity date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). Step 1, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration rule gives approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 a use-up-to date of 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Step 2, 31 December 2029 applies, on the condition that the parties work exactly as the agreement describes (same URL and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). No arithmetic.

**Calculated example: from the unrounded amount to € 36 and to € 38.**
The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) builds the statutory amount from the WML at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The explanatory memorandum's method takes 120% of the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML) of € 14,06 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025), adds a 25% surcharge (opslag) for disability insurance for the self-employed (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, AOV) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, and multiplies by 1,5 for non-billable hours. The combined factor is 1,20 × 1,40 × 1,50 = 2,52. Step 1, € 14,06 × 2,52 = € 35,43, rounded down. That is the unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (excl. btw) at the reference date 1 January 2025 in the explanatory memorandum. Step 2, rounded up to whole euros: € 36,00, the statutory text (same URL). Step 3, index the same unrounded amount to reference date 1 January 2026 with the WML of € 14,71 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026). The index factor is € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,04623. € 35,43 × 1,04623 = € 37,07, rounded. That is the approximately € 37,07 in the checked material. Step 4, rounded up to whole euros: € 38,00. The € 38 itself is stated on the primary pages (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and same WML page).

**Official example: final assessment (definitieve aanslag) 2023.**
A return for 2023 filed before 1 May 2024 gives a final assessment no later than 31 December 2026. With deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2024 it is no later than 30 April 2027 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
