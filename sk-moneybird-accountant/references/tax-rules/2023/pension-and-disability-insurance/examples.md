## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1: Annual allowance (jaarruimte) with a profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) over 2022 of € 60.000, no pension accrual in 2022, no FOR, no income from the other three components of art. 3.127 lid 3.

- Step 1, cap the income at the income cap (aftoppingsgrens): min(€ 60.000, € 128.810) = € 60.000.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000 minus € 13.646 = € 46.354.
- Step 3, apply the 30% percentage: € 46.354 x 30% = € 13.906,20.

The annual allowance 2023 is € 13.906,20. An annuity premium (lijfrentepremie) paid in 2023 up to that amount is deductible when the other conditions are met. A premium above that amount is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), and the excess is taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed. Source of the method and the figures: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Example 2: Annual allowance at the maximum, profit of € 200.000 (Calculated example)

Inputs: profits from business activities over 2022 of € 200.000, no pension accrual.

- Step 1: min(€ 200.000, € 128.810) = € 128.810.
- Step 2: € 128.810 minus € 13.646 = € 115.164.
- Step 3: € 115.164 x 30% = 34.549,2.

The Ministry table prints the maximum annual allowance 2023 as € 34.550. Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This is the rounding open point in section 7. Use the printed € 34.550 as the ceiling. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf).

### Example 3: Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) with unused years (Calculated example)

Inputs: unused annual allowance of € 5.000 from 2019 and € 3.000 from 2021, annual allowance 2023 of € 13.906,20 from example 1, and annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) paid in 2023 of € 20.000.

- Step 1, list the unused years oldest first: 2019 with € 5.000, then 2021 with € 3.000.
- Step 2, total the catch-up allowance: € 5.000 plus € 3.000 = € 8.000.
- Step 3, test the flat maximum: € 8.000 is below € 38.000, so the whole € 8.000 fits.
- Step 4, total the deduction allowance: € 13.906,20 plus € 8.000 = € 21.906,20.
- Step 5, apply the premiums: € 20.000 is below € 21.906,20, so the full € 20.000 is deductible.
- Step 6, the remainder: € 21.906,20 minus € 20.000 = € 1.906,20 of the 2023 annual allowance stays unused and remains available for a later year within the ten-year window.

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) advises using the catch-up allowance before the annual allowance, so the older 2019 and 2021 allowance (ruimte) is used first and does not lapse. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 4: Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) within and above the cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: two policies, each with instalments (termijnen) starting in the state pension (AOW) year and running six years.

- Case A, yearly benefit of € 20.000. This is below the cap of € 24.168, so the cap is respected and the premiums are deductible within the annual allowance and catch-up allowance.
- Case B, yearly benefit of € 30.000. This is above the cap of € 24.168, so the premiums are deductible only if the benefits (uitkeringen) run at least 20 years. Six years is not 20 years, so the premiums of case B are not deductible.

The term of six years meets the minimum of five years in both cases, and a start in the state pension year is allowed because the start may not be before the year of state pension age. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 5: Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: a pre-2006 annuity balance (lijfrentetegoed) and a planned yearly payment of € 70.000.

- Step 1, take the yearly maximum: € 63.288.
- Step 2, compare the planned payment: € 70.000 minus € 63.288 = € 6.712 above the cap.
- Step 3, a planned payment of € 60.000 stays within the cap, because € 60.000 is below € 63.288.

The product is only for balances built up before 2006, and it ends at choice in the year of turning 65, on reaching the state pension age or on starting a pension (pensioen). Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 6: Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion in tier c (Calculated example)

Inputs: profit from business discontinuation of € 200.000, taxpayer aged 50 at cessation (staking), no annuity (lijfrente) starting immediately, so the case falls in tier c with a maximum of € 127.755. Already built provisions are earlier deductions under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 of € 40.000, a FOR at the start of 2023 of € 30.000 and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 of € 10.000.

- Step 1, total the provisions: € 40.000 plus € 30.000 plus € 10.000 = € 80.000.
- Step 2, subtract the provisions from the tier maximum: € 127.755 minus € 80.000 = € 47.755.
- Step 3, test the profit from business discontinuation: the deduction is also never more than the profit from business discontinuation of € 200.000, so the binding cap is € 47.755.

A tier a or tier b amount would require the age, disability or immediate-start conditions of those tiers, which this taxpayer does not meet. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 7: Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente) and the disability cap (Calculated example)

Inputs: two situations.

- Case A, a yearly benefit of € 4.000. This is below € 4.898, so the small-annuity exception (kleine lijfrente) of art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d applies and the commutation in one payment is allowed.
- Case B, a yearly benefit of € 6.000 with long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) and a taxpayer below the state pension age. The small-annuity exception does not apply here. The disability cap of € 45.722 applies, so a commutation received in the year of € 45.000 stays within the cap, since € 45.722 minus € 45.000 = € 722 of room remains. The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment (termijn).

The alternative cap of the average contribution base (premiegrondslag) plus franchise of the year and the previous year applies only when it is higher than € 45.722. Sources: [art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01) and [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Example 8: FOR transition in the annual allowance (Calculated example)

Inputs: profits from business activities over 2022 of € 60.000, a FOR at the start of 2023 of € 30.000, no decrease (afneming) of the FOR in 2023, an addition (toevoeging) over 2022 of € 9.000 and a decrease at the end of 2022 of € 3.000.

- Step 1, fix part a of the contribution base under art. 10a.29 lid 10: the profit before a decrease of the FOR and before the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) is € 60.000.
- Step 2, subtract the franchise: € 60.000 minus € 13.646 = € 46.354.
- Step 3, apply 30%: € 46.354 x 30% = € 13.906,20.
- Step 4, compute the excess of the previous year's addition over the decrease at the end of that year: € 9.000 minus € 3.000 = € 6.000.
- Step 5, reduce the annual allowance: € 13.906,20 minus € 6.000 = € 7.906,20.

The annual allowance 2023 is € 7.906,20 for this FOR holder. The reduction applies for 2023 and 2024. Source: [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Example 9: AOV premium with periodic benefits against a lump-sum policy (Calculated example)

Inputs: two policies, each with a premium of € 3.600 in 2023, both covering disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer.

- Policy A pays periodic benefits (periodieke uitkeringen). The premium is fully deductible with no allowance cap, so the deduction is € 3.600 and the later benefits are taxed as income from a former employment relationship (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) with payroll taxes (loonheffing) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing).
- Policy B pays a lump sum. The premium is not deductible, so the deduction is € 0. The later payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

The rule of thumb is that a premium which is not deductible means a payout which is not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen) and [Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Example 10: Deadlines from FISIN 2023 (Official example)

- An end date (einddatum) in 2022 means convert or start the annuity by 31 December 2023. An end date in 2023 means convert or start by 31 December 2024.
- A death in 2022 means start the survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) by 31 December 2024.
- With state pension age in 2030 the old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) must start at the latest on 31 December 2035.
- For an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht) the term is at least 20 years plus the number of years the benefit starts before the state pension year, so a start at 63 with state pension age 67 means 20 plus 4 = 24 years.

Source: [FISIN 2023, uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
