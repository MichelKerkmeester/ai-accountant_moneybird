## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

The percentage applies to a taxpayer who has a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets that taxpayer deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to a percentage of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). For 2023 the percentage is 30% in the text of 1 July 2023. The text of 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3% ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on the Future Pensions Act (Wtp) states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" and confirms that the annuity (lijfrente) widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so the whole of 2023 uses 30% ([Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The calculation for a self-employed person (zelfstandige) without pension accrual is 30% x (min(income of the previous year, € 128.810) minus € 13.646). The income of the previous year matters because the allowance (ruimte) of a year rests on the income of the year before. Up to the text of 1 January 2023 the outcome was also reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve) under lid 1 and lid 4 sub b. From the text of 1 July 2023 lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 adds its own contribution base and reduction rules for 2023 and 2024 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Maximum annual allowance

The maximum for 2023 is € 34.550. It applies when the income of the previous year is at or above the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of € 128.810, so that the whole capped base minus the franchise is used. The Ministry table for 2024 prints € 34.550 and carries the footnote that the 2023 amounts changed retroactively for the Wtp ([Ministry, belangrijkste wijzigingen belastingen 2024](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 30% x (€ 128.810 minus € 13.646) = 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This is the one Corrected value in this file. The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Maximum annual allowance under the pre-Wtp rules

The amount of € 15.317 comes from the Ministry table of 9 January 2023 and rests on the 13,3% percentage that the text of art. 3.127 valid on 1 January 2023 still had ([Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-a585ce5bd38ddc5d9e2adb1fd6f796a7cbaaa775/pdf)). The retroactive Wtp changes superseded it, so a 2023 return uses the ceiling of € 34.550 and not € 15.317. The € 15.317 row is kept here only so a reader who finds the older table can place it.

### Income cap for the contribution base

The income cap for 2023 is € 128.810. Art. 3.127 lid 3 builds the contribution base as the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming), (b) taxable wages (belastbaar loon), (c) taxable income from other work (belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and (d) taxable periodic payments (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen) and benefits in kind (verstrekkingen), counted up to the first-mentioned amount (eerstvermelde bedrag, the income cap) and then reduced by the last-mentioned amount (laatstvermelde bedrag, the franchise) ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The amount is the same in the text of 1 January 2023 and in both Ministry tables. The wording of part a differs between the texts. In the 2020 to 1 January 2023 texts it is profits from business activities before an addition to and a decrease of the old-age reserve (voor toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve) and before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek). From the 1 July 2023 text it is profits from business activities before the entrepreneur's allowance. For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 keeps the profit before a decrease (afneming) of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, before an addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Art. 3.127 lid 5 gives one relief: on (partial) cessation (staking) the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. The amounts counted are the amounts of the previous calendar year, so the 2023 room rests on 2022 income.

### Franchise for the contribution base

The franchise for 2023 is € 13.646 ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). It is subtracted after the cap of € 128.810 is applied to the four income components of art. 3.127 lid 3. The subtraction happens once, on the capped total, and not per income component. The same income year applies as for the income cap, so the 2023 franchise is subtracted from 2022 income.

### Factor A multiplier

The multiplier for 2023 is 6,27. In the 2023 texts, art. 3.127 lid 4 reduces the outcome of lid 1 by 6,27 times the growth (aangroei) of the yearly pension entitlement from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor) ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The 2023 annual allowance rests on pension accrual in 2022. The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25 and not in art. 3.127. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964 (the old norms), and that old text also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023, so accrual in the first half of 2023 stays under the old reduction rule for later annual allowance. From the text of 1 January 2024 the reduction covers the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus the net pension premium divided by the net factor. For a fixed premium per year of service (dienstjaar) under art. 38r the formula is A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the net pension premium divided by the net factor, and that formula applies with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 July 2023 ([art. 10a.25, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### Look-back window

The window for 2023 is ten years, covering the annual allowance of 2013 to 2022. Art. 3.127 lid 2 says that unused annual allowance of the preceding period may be used on request in the return (aangifte), oldest unused year first ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The text of 1 January 2023 said "zeven jaar", the text of 1 July 2023 says "tien jaar". FISIN 2023 prints the window as 2013 to 2022 ([FISIN 2023, uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration advises using the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) before the annual allowance, so that older unused allowance does not lapse. The same page notes that unused 2016 room lapses in 2027, which shows the ten-year window in practice ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Catch-up allowance maximum

The maximum for 2023 is € 38.000 flat. The text of art. 3.127 lid 2 valid from 1 July 2023 names one flat euro maximum, with no percentage and no age split ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). FISIN 2023 and the Ministry table for 2024 agree ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). The maximum applies to the part of the deduction that comes from unused annual allowance of 2013 to 2022. That part must be requested in the return and is used oldest year first, before the annual allowance of 2023.

### Catch-up allowance under the pre-Wtp rules

The amounts of € 8.065 and € 15.922 come from the text of art. 3.127 lid 2 valid on 1 January 2023 and from the Ministry table of 9 January 2023 ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). That text capped the catch-up allowance at 17% of the contribution base and at a euro amount, raised to the higher amount of € 15.922 for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year was at most ten years below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) of that year. The Wtp replaced both amounts with the flat € 38.000, retroactively for 2023, so a 2023 return uses € 38.000.

### Age limit annual allowance

At the start of the year, the taxpayer must not yet have reached an age five years above the state pension age. The text of art. 3.127 valid from 1 July 2023, applied to all of 2023, states this, and FISIN 2023 uses the same wording ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The text of 1 January 2023 still said that the taxpayer must not yet have reached the state pension age at the start of the calendar year ([art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) may be paid up to 5 years after the state pension age ([Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The age test is done at the start of the year, so a taxpayer who passes the limit during the year still fails the test for that year.

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

The cap for 2023 is € 24.168 per year. It applies to the temporary old-age annuity of art. 3.125 lid 1 c. The conditions are at least five years of instalments (termijnen), a start not before the year of state pension age and at the latest in the year of state pension age plus five, and a yearly amount up to the statutory cap. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment ([art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). FISIN 2023 adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the benefits (uitkeringen) run at least 20 years ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The cap also appears in the Ministry tables ([Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf)). The regular old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) of art. 3.125 lid 1 a has its first instalment (termijn) at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches state pension age plus five and pays until death. FISIN 2023 gives the example that with state pension age in 2030 the start must be at the latest on 31 December 2035. For an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht) the term is at least 20 years plus the number of years the benefit (uitkering) starts before the state pension year.

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

The cap for 2023 is € 63.288 per year ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The bridge annuity is only available for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, on reaching the state pension age or on starting a pension (pensioen). The same amount of € 63.288 appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text, so the FISIN chapters are the working source. The cap is a yearly maximum per year of payment, so a planned yearly payment above it is not covered by this product.

### Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

The tier a maximum for 2023 is € 510.970 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at a tier maximum minus the already built provisions. Tier a applies when the taxpayer is at the most five years below the state pension age at the moment of cessation, or is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity starting within six months, or ceases by death. FISIN 2023 describes tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches 2023 because the state pension age was 66 years and 10 months ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The law keeps the wording "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". The provisions that reduce the tier maximum are the value of pension rights charged to profit, the amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts and, from 2023, the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

The tier b maximum for 2023 is € 255.495 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Tier b applies when the taxpayer is at the most fifteen years below the state pension age, or when the annuity starts immediately. The same provision deduction applies as in tier a, so the effective room is the tier amount minus the already built provisions.

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

The tier c maximum for 2023 is € 127.755 ([art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Tier c covers all other cases. The deduction is never more than the profit from business discontinuation itself, and never more than the tier amount minus the already built provisions. The payment can on choice be counted in the year of cessation when it is made within six months after the year end, which for 2023 means before 1 July 2024 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente)

The amount for 2023 is € 4.898. Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows commutation of a small annuity in one payment when the yearly amount stays below this figure ([art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). The Ministry table for 2024 agrees. The background rule is that an annuity which stops meeting the conditions, for example through gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation or unblocking (deblokkering), is taxed on its value and in most cases carries revision interest (revisierente). The small-annuity exception (kleine lijfrente) is the route that lets a small policy end in one payment without a prohibited act ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The cap for 2023 is € 45.722 ([art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes commutation not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached the state pension age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap of € 45.722 or, if that is higher, within the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment (termijn) for the tax rules. All three conditions must hold, so the ministerial disability conditions and the state pension age limit are tested as well as the amount.

### FOR addition

No addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve, toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve) is possible from 1 January 2023 ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The Ministry 2023 report section "Uitfasering oudedagsreserve" agrees with the phase-out. Up to 2022 an addition required the hours criterion (urencriterium) and that the taxpayer had not yet reached the state pension age at the start of the year, which is art. 3.67. Art. 3.68 set the addition as a percentage of the profit (winst), capped at a euro amount, reduced by the pension premiums charged to the profit and not above the growth of business equity plus the old-age reserve (ondernemingsvermogen) ([art. 3.67 and 3.68, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). An existing FOR stays under the old rules. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70 to 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows a decrease (afname) of the FOR by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the business equity plus the old-age reserve at cessation, at the state pension age at the start of the year, or when the hours criterion fails this year and last year. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the business (onderneming) and takes the FOR over on request ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).

### FOR transition in the annual allowance

The transition sits in art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11, not in art. 3.127, and it applies for 2023 and 2024 ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For a taxpayer with a FOR the contribution base uses the profit before a decrease of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, before an addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance. For 2023 and 2024 the annual allowance is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the decrease at the end of that previous year. The deduction for a FOR to annuity (lijfrente) conversion is art. 10a.29 lid 12, up to the decrease under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a. Art. 10a.29 lid 13 counts the FOR in the profit from business discontinuation provisions that reduce the art. 3.129 tier maximum. A reader who finds the transition described under art. 3.127 lid 10 and 11 should use the art. 10a.29 location instead.

### AOV premium with periodic benefits

Such premiums are fully deductible. Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c treats premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind in connection with disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) as the taxpayer's expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen). The article contains no allowance cap and FISIN 2023 says "volledig aftrekken" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Four exclusions apply. Not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) are premiums that the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Zvw (Health Insurance Act, zorgverzekeringswet) premiums. The four exclusions and section 15.4 of FISIN are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. For an entrepreneur (ondernemer) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" (income from a former employment) with payroll taxes withheld as prepayment (voorheffing). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives the rule of thumb that a premium which is not deductible means a payout which is not taxed, and the reverse ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

Such a premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). This covers policies that pay a lump sum, such as capital insurance policies. The rule of thumb applies here in the other direction. Because the premium is not deductible, the payout stays outside box 1 and is only a box 3 asset. A taxpayer who switches treatment between the two policy types breaks the link between deduction and taxation, so the policy type decides the whole treatment ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

### Payment deadline FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion

Premiums count in the year in which they are paid or offset (verrekend) under art. 3.130 lid 1 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Premiums for the profit from business discontinuation conversion of art. 3.129, for the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, for the FOR conversion of art. 3.128 may on choice be counted in the year itself when they are paid within six months after the year end, under art. 3.130 lid 2. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. For 2023 the deadline for these choices is a payment before 1 July 2024. Two further limits apply. Premiums above the allowance are never deductible, but they are taken into account when the benefit is taxed. The maximum applies to all annuity products together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Annuity end date (einddatum) term

An annuity that reaches its end date must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). FISIN 2023 uses the word end date where FISIN 2022 used start date (ingangsdatum). The official examples are that an end date in 2022 means convert or start by 31 December 2023 and an end date in 2023 by 31 December 2024. An extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). The three product forms are the annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), the annuity savings account and the annuity investment right. For the old-age annuity of art. 3.125 lid 1 a the first term must be at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches state pension age plus five, and the payments run until death ([art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01)). If an annuity stops meeting the conditions, for example through gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation or unblocking, the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest is due. A refund of a deducted premium is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest, and for an annuity only if the undoing happens within 30 days ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) term

A survivor annuity must start within the year of death plus 2 calendar years. FISIN 2023 words the deadline that way, and the outcome is the same as the 2022 wording of 2 full calendar years ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The official example is that a death in 2022 means a start by 31 December 2024. An extension can be requested in special circumstances. The product conditions of art. 3.125 lid 1 b are that for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner the survivor annuity ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and that for an annuity savings account or annuity investment right the term is at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. An annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind) is deductible without a pension shortfall under art. 3.124 lid 1 b ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
