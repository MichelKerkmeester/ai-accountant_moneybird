## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage: 13,3% in 2022 to 30% for 2023, with the text of 1 July 2023 and retroactive effect for the whole of 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Maximum annual allowance: € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. The 2023 table of 9 January 2023 still printed € 15.317 for 2023, and that pre-Wtp amount was superseded retroactively. Sources: [Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf), [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf).
- Look-back window: 7 years for 2022, annual allowance 2015 to 2021, to 10 years for 2023, annual allowance 2013 to 2022. Source: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte): 17% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag) with € 7.587 for the younger group and € 14.978 for the older group in 2022 to a flat € 38.000 in 2023. The 2023 text of 1 January 2023 still had € 8.065 or € 15.922, superseded retroactively. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age limit annual allowance: not yet state pension (AOW) age in 2022 to not yet the age five years above the state pension age for 2023, with the 1 January 2023 text still on the old wording. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) for the contribution base: € 114.866 in 2022 to € 128.810 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Franchise for the contribution base: € 12.837 in 2022 to € 13.646 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Factor A multiplier: 6,27 in both years. What changed is the surrounding rule. Accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023 keeps the old lid 4 rule for later annual allowance. Source: [art. 10a.25, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap: € 22.735 in 2022 to € 24.168 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap: € 63.288 in 2022 and € 63.288 in 2023, unchanged. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maxima: tier a from € 480.686 to € 510.970, tier b from € 240.352 to € 255.495, tier c from € 120.183 to € 127.755. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente): € 4.607 in 2022 to € 4.898 in 2023. Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid): € 43.012 in 2022 to € 45.722 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.133, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- FOR addition (toevoeging): 9,44% of the profit (winst), at most € 9.632, in 2022, the last year of an addition, to none possible from 1 January 2023. An existing FOR stays under the old rules via art. 10a.29 lid 1. Sources: [art. 3.68, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- FOR to annuity (lijfrente) conversion deduction: art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 to art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023. The six-month payment option moves too, to art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.128, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Contribution base part a: the 2020 to 1 January 2023 wording "winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek" changes to "winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek" from 1 July 2023, with art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 for FOR holders in 2023 and 2024. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Reduction in lid 1: the addition (dotatie) reduction for the old-age reserve under lid 1 and lid 4 sub b is gone from the text of 1 July 2023, which names only the pension accrual reduction. Source: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Annuity deadline wording: FISIN 2022 uses start date (ingangsdatum), FISIN 2023 uses end date (einddatum). Survivors (Nabestaanden) deadline wording: 2 full calendar years in 2022 becomes the year of death plus 2 calendar years in 2023, with the same outcome. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Payment deadline for the conversion options: before 1 July 2023 for 2022 to before 1 July 2024 for 2023. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- AOV premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen): fully deductible, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, in both 2022 and 2023, unchanged. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record AOV premiums for a policy with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in box 1, and not as a business cost of the business (onderneming). They are fully deductible with no allowance (ruimte) cap ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Do not deduct premiums for AOV policies that pay a lump sum. Keep those policies in the box 3 records, because the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Book a mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium for an entrepreneur (ondernemer) as a business cost with a restriction, and not as an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie) ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book annuity premiums in the year in which they are paid or offset (verrekend). For the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion and the FOR conversion a payment before 1 July 2024 may on choice be counted in 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Keep the premium records and the room calculation together. Premiums above the allowance are never deductible, they are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed, and the maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep a per-year schedule of unused annual allowance (jaarruimte) for 2013 to 2022, because the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) must be requested in the return (aangifte) and the oldest unused year is used first ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Record an existing FOR under the old rules. No addition (toevoeging) can be made in 2023. Record the FOR to annuity (lijfrente) conversion deduction under art. 10a.29 lid 12, up to the decrease (afname) under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Keep the policy documents with the end date (einddatum) and the death date records for a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente). The conversion or start must happen within 1 full calendar year, and the survivors (nabestaanden) start deadline is the year of death plus 2 calendar years. An extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden) ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Periodic AOV benefits arrive as income from a former employment relationship (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) with payroll taxes (loonheffing) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing), so record them together with the withheld payroll taxes ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2023, rounding. The Ministry table prints € 34.550. Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 30% x (€ 128.810 minus € 13.646) = 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This file follows the Ministry figure of € 34.550. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) calculation (hulpmiddel) would show the rounding the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses. URLs: [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf) and [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Two law texts for 2023. The text of art. 3.127 valid on 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3%, with a maximum annual allowance of € 15.317, a catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) of € 8.065 or € 15.922, a seven-year look-back and an age limit of not yet state pension (AOW) age. The text valid from 1 July 2023 reads 30%, with € 34.550, € 38.000, ten years and state pension age plus five. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" and confirms the annuity (lijfrente) widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so this file follows the Wtp values for the whole of 2023. URLs: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Proposed items that do not apply to 2023. Proposed bill 36 912, the Basic Insurance for Self-Employed Persons (Incapacity) Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), had docket status "In afwachting nota nav verslag" on 2026-10-11. Bill art. 16 proposes a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks (weken), the explanatory memorandum (MvT) proposes a benefit of 70% of the tax base (grondslag) with the tax base at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so at most the minimum wage, and a premium set yearly by UWV estimated at about 5,4% of profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) up to the maximum tax base. The explanatory memorandum's examples use a maximum tax base of € 38.000 and about € 171 gross per month, and assume 1 January 2030 for entry into force by royal decree (koninklijk besluit). The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit, and UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes (loonheffing). Private policies keep the current tax treatment. The docket summary says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16. URLs: [dossier 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html), [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).
- Proposed Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) items. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of art. 3.127 lid 3 at the start of 2027 to 2032, so it would stay € 137.800, and a limited inflation correction of 48% of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, giving 1,01248, for the annuity maxima. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11. URL: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Adjacent proposed track. The Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) had an internet consultation (internetconsultatie) until 29 October 2026, with a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) leading to a "veilige haven", and an intended entry into force (inwerkingtreding) of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension. URL: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
