## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Bijdrage Zvw by aanslag: 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen: € 59.706 in 2022 to € 66.956 in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Ingehouden bijdrage: 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Werkgeversheffing: 6,75% in 2022 (Corrected) to 6,68% in 2023 (Corrected) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. At AOW age: € 3.155 in 2022 to € 2.515 in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Oudedagsreserve: 2022 was the last year in which an addition was possible, at 9,44% of the profit with a maximum of € 9.632 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). From 2023 no new oudedagsreserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Unchanged in 2023: startersaftrek € 2.123 (€ 1.062 at AOW age), startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 then € 0, meewerkaftrek 1,25%, 2%, 3%, 4%, stakingsaftrek maximum € 3.630 and MKB-winstvrijstelling 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The Zvw base rule, belastbare winst after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, is recorded in the same way for 2022 and 2023, but both years rest on the 2026 versions of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 and are UNVERIFIED for the year itself (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- For the winst of the ondernemer, the Zvw contribution is not withheld during the year. It is assessed with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The figures that determine it are the same figures as in the inkomstenbelasting return: the winst, the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Keep track of income that already bore a Zvw contribution in 2023, such as loon, benefits or pension, because the € 66.956 maximum applies once per person and that income shrinks the headroom for the winst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- No new oudedagsreserve can be formed from 2023. An existing reserve stays on the balance sheet, and a decrease leads to an addition under art. 3.70 lid 2 that is excluded from the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- In a BV, the BV owes the werkgeversheffing of 6,68% over the DGA loon when the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The declaration route and the filing deadline for 2023 are not confirmed in the checked sources, see section 7.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer: UNVERIFIED for 2023. The rule, belastbare winst uit onderneming after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, rests on Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 read in their 2026 versions only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reading both articles in the versions valid on 1 January 2023 would settle it.
- Zvw at AOW age: verified in part. No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 footnote 2 states that zelfstandigen and gepensioneerden pay the verlaagde IAB. UNVERIFIED.
- Aangifte and deadlines for 2023: UNVERIFIED. The checked sources confirm only that the aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic.
- Werkgeversheffing 2023: 6,68% is marked Corrected (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked sources record no old value, so no second value can be shown as a conflict.
- Whether any heffingskorting (tax credit) reduces the Zvw contribution is not stated in the checked sources. The heffingskortingen are described as reducing the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- Heffingskortingen in the AOW transition year 2023: the checked 2023 sources record no rule.
