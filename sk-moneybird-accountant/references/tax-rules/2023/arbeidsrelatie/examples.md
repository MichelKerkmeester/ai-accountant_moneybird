## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example: applying the loondienst elements to a 2023 engagement.**
An opdrachtnemer does the bookkeeping for one client in 2023. Step 1, check the three elements. Werkgeversgezag: the client sets the working hours and can direct the opdrachtnemer to other tasks. Persoonlijke arbeid: the work must be done by the opdrachtnemer in person. Pay: the client pays for the work. All three elements are present in this invented case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Step 2, weigh the factors. Points toward loondienst: the work is embedded in the client's organisation and the client sets how and when the work is done. Points away: the opdrachtnemer carries commercial risk, works for several clients and can act as an entrepreneur. Step 3, the checked material gives no fixed score, the qualification follows from the whole picture (same URL). No arithmetic.

**Calculated example: the aanwijzing route for a 2023 relationship.**
A sole trader works for one client through 2023. Early in 2023 the Belastingdienst concludes that the relationship has the elements of loondienst and that there is no kwaadwillendheid (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Step 1, the Belastingdienst first gives an aanwijzing instead of correcting at once. Step 2, the parties usually have 3 months to reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or to process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte. If the aanwijzing is dated 15 March 2023, that term runs to about 15 June 2023. Step 3, if the aanwijzing is not followed, the correctieverplichting applies from the date of the aanwijzing, so from 15 March 2023 (same URL). No euro amounts are used here because the checked material gives none for the 2023 correction itself.

**Calculated example: the use-up-to date of an approved modelovereenkomst.**
Suppose an approved modelovereenkomst is valid on 6 September 2024 and states a different validity date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). Step 1, the Belastingdienst rule gives approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 a use-up-to date of 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Step 2, 31 December 2029 applies, on the condition that the parties work exactly as the agreement describes (same URL and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). No arithmetic.

**Calculated example: from the unrounded amount to € 36 and to € 38.**
The memorie van toelichting builds the statutory amount from the WML at peildatum 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The MvT method takes 120% of the WML of € 14,06 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025), adds a 25% opslag for AOV and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, and multiplies by 1,5 for non-billable hours. The combined factor is 1,20 × 1,40 × 1,50 = 2,52. Step 1, € 14,06 × 2,52 = € 35,43, rounded down. That is the unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025 in the MvT. Step 2, rounded up to whole euros: € 36,00, the statutory text (same URL). Step 3, index the same unrounded amount to peildatum 1 January 2026 with the WML of € 14,71 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026). The index factor is € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,04623. € 35,43 × 1,04623 = € 37,07, rounded. That is the about € 37,07 in the checked material. Step 4, rounded up to whole euros: € 38,00. The € 38 itself is stated on the primary pages (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and same WML page).

**Official example: definitive aanslag 2023.**
A return for 2023 filed before 1 May 2024 gives a definitive aanslag no later than 31 December 2026. With uitstel to 1 September 2024 it is no later than 30 April 2027 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
