## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet, bijdrage bij aanslag): 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 59.706 in 2022 to € 66.956 in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Withheld health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (ingehouden bijdrage, ingehouden bijdrage zvw, ingehouden zvw-bijdrage): 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw): 6,75% in 2022 (Corrected) to 6,68% in 2023 (Corrected) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. At state pension age (AOW age): € 3.155 in 2022 to € 2.515 in 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): 2022 was the last year in which an addition was possible, at 9,44% of the profit with a maximum of € 9.632 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). From 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Unchanged in 2023: starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123 (€ 1.062 at state pension age), starter's deduction on occupational disability € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 then € 0, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) 1,25%, 2%, 3%, 4%, discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) maximum € 3.630 and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The Zvw base rule, taxable profit (belastbare winst) from business after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption, is recorded in the same way for 2022 and 2023, but both years rest on the 2026 versions of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 and are UNVERIFIED for the year itself (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- For the profit of the entrepreneur, the Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) is not withheld during the year. It is assessed with the provisional income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The figures that determine it are the same figures as in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting): the profit, the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Keep track of income that already bore a Zvw contribution in 2023, such as wage, benefits or pension, because the € 66.956 maximum applies once per person and that income shrinks the headroom for the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- No new old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) can be formed from 2023. An existing reserve stays on the balance sheet, and a decrease leads to an addition under art. 3.70 lid 2 that is excluded from the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- In a private limited company (BV), the company owes the employer health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw) of 6,68% over the wage of a director and substantial shareholder (DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder) when the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The declaration route and the filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) for 2023 are not confirmed in the checked sources, see section 7.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur: UNVERIFIED for 2023. The rule, taxable profit (belastbare winst) from business after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), rests on Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 read in their 2026 versions only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reading both articles in the versions valid on 1 January 2023 would settle it.
- Zvw at state pension age: verified in part. No state-pension-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). UNVERIFIED.
- Return and deadlines (aangiftetermijn) for 2023: UNVERIFIED. The checked sources confirm only that the Zvw assessment comes with the provisional income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic.
- Employer health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw) 2023: 6,68% is marked Corrected (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked sources record no old value, so no second value can be shown as a conflict.
- Whether any tax credit (heffingskorting) reduces the Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) is not stated in the checked sources. The tax credits (heffingskortingen) are described as reducing the income tax and the national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) through an income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- Tax credits in the state pension transition year 2023: the checked 2023 sources record no rule.
