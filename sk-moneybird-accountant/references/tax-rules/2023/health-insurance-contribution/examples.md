## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Official example, loon plus freelance income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)

Facts: loon of € 40.000 and freelance income of € 30.000 in 2023.

Step 1: the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 66.956 (section 2).
Step 2: the loon already bore a Zvw contribution, so it uses up headroom. Headroom is € 66.956 minus € 40.000 = € 26.956.
Step 3: the source states that the 5,43% applies to € 26.956.
Step 4: the bijdrage is € 26.956 x 5,43% = € 1.463,71 (the multiplication is arithmetic added here, the source prints the € 26.956 base, not the euro outcome).

### Example 2. Official example, no headroom left (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)

Facts: loon of € 70.000 and freelance income of € 15.000 in 2023.

Step 1: the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 66.956.
Step 2: € 70.000 is above € 66.956, so the loon already exceeds the maximum.
Step 3: remaining headroom is € 0.
Step 4: the source states that no bijdrage is due on the freelance income.

### Example 3. Calculated example, profit of € 60.000, below AOW age

Facts: winst uit onderneming of € 60.000, no loon, below AOW age.

Step 1: winst = € 60.000.
Step 2: zelfstandigenaftrek = € 5.030. Winst after the ondernemersaftrek = € 60.000 minus € 5.030 = € 54.970.
Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling = 14% x € 54.970 = € 7.695,80.
Step 4: belastbare winst = € 54.970 minus € 7.695,80 = € 47.274,20.
Step 5: € 47.274,20 is below the maximum of € 66.956, so the bijdrage-inkomen is € 47.274,20.
Step 6: bijdrage = € 47.274,20 x 5,43% = € 2.566,99.

### Example 4. Calculated example, profit of € 100.000, the maximum applies

Facts: winst uit onderneming of € 100.000, no loon, below AOW age.

Step 1: winst = € 100.000.
Step 2: zelfstandigenaftrek = € 5.030. Winst after the ondernemersaftrek = € 100.000 minus € 5.030 = € 94.970.
Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling = 14% x € 94.970 = € 13.295,80.
Step 4: belastbare winst = € 94.970 minus € 13.295,80 = € 81.674,20.
Step 5: € 81.674,20 is above the maximum of € 66.956, so the bijdrage-inkomen is capped at € 66.956.
Step 6: bijdrage = € 66.956 x 5,43% = € 3.635,71.

### Example 5. Calculated example, AOW age, profit of € 40.000

Facts: winst uit onderneming of € 40.000, no loon, AOW age.

Step 1: winst = € 40.000.
Step 2: zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age = € 2.515. Winst after the ondernemersaftrek = € 40.000 minus € 2.515 = € 37.485.
Step 3: MKB-winstvrijstelling = 14% x € 37.485 = € 5.247,90.
Step 4: belastbare winst = € 37.485 minus € 5.247,90 = € 32.237,10.
Step 5: € 32.237,10 is below the maximum of € 66.956, so the bijdrage-inkomen is € 32.237,10.
Step 6: bijdrage = € 32.237,10 x 5,43% = € 1.750,47.
Note: the checked sources state no separate AOW-age rate or AOW-age exemption for the Zvw contribution itself, see section 7.

### Example 6. Calculated example, BV pays the werkgeversheffing over DGA loon

Facts: a BV pays DGA loon of € 50.000 in 2023 and the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen.

Step 1: the werkgeversheffing rate is 6,68% (section 2).
Step 2: € 50.000 x 6,68% = € 3.340 owed by the BV.
Step 3: contrast with the aanslag rate. The same € 50.000 as bijdrage-inkomen by aanslag would bear 5,43%, which is € 50.000 x 5,43% = € 2.715.
Step 4: € 50.000 is below the maximum of € 66.956 in both cases, so no cap applies.
