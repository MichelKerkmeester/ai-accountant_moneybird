## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen)

Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). For 2023 the maximum is € 66.956 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum applies once per person across all Zvw channels. Income that already bore a Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) therefore uses up the headroom for income that is still to be assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The 2023 page gives the example of a wage (loon) of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 30.000, where the 5,43% is applied to € 26.956 only (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.2 Zvw contribution by assessment (bijdrage bij aanslag)

An entrepreneur with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) pays the contribution by assessment or provisional assessment (aanslag of voorlopige aanslag), meaning by provisional or final assessment (definitieve aanslag). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, and for 2023 the rate is 5,43% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The assessment channel covers business profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland), but not the conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente), income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes (loonheffing) such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The Zvw assessment comes with the provisional income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The declaration route and the filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) for 2023 are not confirmed in the checked sources, see section 7. The checked sources list these exceptions to the assessment channel: military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Because the maximum applies once per person across channels, any other income that already bore a contribution reduces the headroom for the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.3 Withheld health insurance (zorgverzekering) contribution (ingehouden bijdrage Zvw)

The contribution is withheld from pension and annuity (lijfrente) income and from other benefits (uitkeringen) that carry payroll taxes (loonheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2023 the rate is also 5,43% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An entrepreneur normally does not meet this channel through the business, but income that already bore this contribution uses up the same € 66.956 maximum when the profit is assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.4 Employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw)

The employer health insurance contribution is paid by the employer or benefit agency on wages from employment (loon uit loondienst) and on a director and substantial shareholder's (DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder) wages when the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). It is also paid on social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people who were insured under the health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2023 the rate is 6,68% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, Corrected). The entrepreneur's rate by assessment is 5,43% in the same year, so the employer rate is the higher of the two (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The channels share one maximum. The maximum contribution income of € 66.956 applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a Zvw contribution reduces the headroom for the income that is still to be assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). In a private limited company (BV) the employer health insurance contribution is owed over the DGA wage when the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.5 Zvw contribution base (Zvw-grondslag) for a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer)

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines taxable profit as the profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance minus the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur is therefore the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Both sources were read in their 2026 versions only, so the rule is UNVERIFIED for 2023, see section 7. Art. 43 lid 3 then sets the contribution income at nil or more and at no more than the yearly maximum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). A person entitled to a share of the profit (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (FISIN winst uit onderneming). Profits from business activities require that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. Registration in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) alone does not decide the question (FISIN winst uit onderneming).

### 3.6 Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction for 2023 is € 5.030. At state pension age (AOW age) it is € 2.515 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). An entrepreneur who qualifies deducts it from the profit. The entrepreneur's allowance comes off the profit before the SME profit exemption under art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001, so the self-employed person's deduction lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The checked 2023 sources give the amounts only. The qualifying conditions belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic.

### 3.7 Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The starter's deduction for 2023 is € 2.123. At state pension age it is € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is listed on the same FISIN entrepreneur's allowance page as the self-employed person's deduction and, like the other entrepreneur's allowance items, it lowers the profit that forms the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The checked 2023 sources give the amount only.

### 3.8 Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The 2023 amounts are € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, then € 0. The deduction is capped at the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It sits in the entrepreneur's allowance and therefore lowers the Zvw base the same way as the self-employed person's deduction (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.9 Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the same hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked 2023 sources do not state the hour bands themselves. The item is on the FISIN entrepreneur's allowance page and reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption, so it lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.10 Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction for 2023 has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is an entrepreneur's allowance item and lowers the Zvw base through the same art. 3.2 route (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.11 SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption for 2023 is 14% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is deducted after the entrepreneur's allowance under art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001, so it lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). A person entitled to a share of the profit or lender does not get the SME profit exemption (FISIN winst uit onderneming).

### 3.12 Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

From 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed. An existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). For the Zvw base the interaction is that Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). A decrease of the reserve therefore does not feed the contribution income. The conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity is also not part of the profit channel for the Zvw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The FOR page lists the events that reduce or release the reserve, including buying an annuity with a matching reserve decrease, the reserve exceeding the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen), cessation, reaching state pension age on 1 January, failing the hours criterion (urencriterium) in both the current and the prior year, and death unless a continuing partner takes the reserve over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
