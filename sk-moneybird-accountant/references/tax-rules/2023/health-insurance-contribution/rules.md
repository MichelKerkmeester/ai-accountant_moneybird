## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Maximum bijdrage-inkomen

Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the bijdrage-inkomen at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). For 2023 the maximum is € 66.956 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The maximum applies once per person across all Zvw channels. Income that already bore a Zvw contribution therefore uses up the headroom for income that is still to be assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The 2023 page gives the example of loon of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 30.000, where the 5,43% is applied to € 26.956 only (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.2 Bijdrage Zvw by aanslag

An ondernemer with winst uit onderneming (profit from business) pays the bijdrage by (voorlopige) aanslag, meaning by provisional or final assessment. Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, and for 2023 the rate is 5,43% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The aanslag channel covers winst uit Nederland, but not the conversion of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente, inkomsten uit overig werk, loon from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign uitkeringsinstantie and periodieke uitkeringen without loonheffing such as partneralimentatie (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The declaration route and the filing deadline for 2023 are not confirmed in the checked sources, see section 7. The checked sources list these exceptions to the aanslag channel: militairen in active service or on buitengewoon verlof with retention of military income, deelvissers where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Because the maximum applies once per person across channels, any other income that already bore a contribution reduces the headroom for the winst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.3 Ingehouden bijdrage Zvw

The ingehouden bijdrage (withheld contribution) is withheld from pension, lijfrente (annuity) and other uitkeringen that carry loonheffing (wage tax) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2023 the rate is also 5,43% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An ondernemer normally does not meet this channel through the business, but income that already bore this bijdrage uses up the same € 66.956 maximum when the winst is assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.4 Werkgeversheffing Zvw

The werkgeversheffing (employer levy) is paid by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie over loon uit loondienst (wages from employment), DGA loon when the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (employee insurance schemes), bijstand (social assistance), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people who were insured under the ziekenfonds in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2023 the rate is 6,68% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, Corrected). The entrepreneur's rate by aanslag is 5,43% in the same year, so the employer rate is the higher of the two (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The channels share one maximum. The maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 66.956 applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a Zvw contribution reduces the headroom for the income that is still to be assessed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). In a BV the werkgeversheffing is owed over the DGA loon when the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.5 Zvw-grondslag for a winst-ondernemer

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines belastbare winst as the winst minus the ondernemersaftrek minus the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer is therefore the profit after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Both sources were read in their 2026 versions only, so the rule is UNVERIFIED for 2023, see section 7. Art. 43 lid 3 then sets the bijdrage-inkomen at nil or more and at no more than the yearly maximum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). A medegerechtigde (person entitled to a share of the profit) or geldverstrekker (financier) gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling (FISIN winst uit onderneming). Winst uit onderneming requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. Registration in the Handelsregister (commercial register) alone does not decide the question (FISIN winst uit onderneming).

### 3.6 Zelfstandigenaftrek

The zelfstandigenaftrek for 2023 is € 5.030. At AOW age (state pension age) it is € 2.515 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). An ondernemer who qualifies deducts it from the winst. The ondernemersaftrek comes off the winst before the MKB-winstvrijstelling under art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001, so the zelfstandigenaftrek lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The checked 2023 sources give the amounts only. The qualifying conditions belong to the ondernemersaftrek topic.

### 3.7 Startersaftrek

The startersaftrek for 2023 is € 2.123. At AOW age it is € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is listed on the same FISIN ondernemersaftrek page as the zelfstandigenaftrek and, like the other ondernemersaftrek items, it lowers the winst that forms the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The checked 2023 sources give the amount only.

### 3.8 Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

The 2023 amounts are € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, then € 0. The deduction is capped at the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It sits in the ondernemersaftrek and therefore lowers the Zvw base the same way as the zelfstandigenaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.9 Meewerkaftrek

The meewerkaftrek is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the same hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked 2023 sources do not state the hour bands themselves. The item is on the FISIN ondernemersaftrek page and reduces the winst before the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so it lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.10 Stakingsaftrek

The stakingsaftrek for 2023 has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is an ondernemersaftrek item and lowers the Zvw base through the same art. 3.2 route (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).

### 3.11 MKB-winstvrijstelling

The MKB-winstvrijstelling for 2023 is 14% of the winst after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is deducted after the ondernemersaftrek under art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001, so it lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker does not get the MKB-winstvrijstelling (FISIN winst uit onderneming).

### 3.12 Oudedagsreserve

From 2023 no new oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) can be formed. An existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). For the Zvw base the interaction is that Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). A decrease of the reserve therefore does not feed the bijdrage-inkomen. The conversion of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente is also not part of the winst channel for the Zvw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The FOR page lists the events that reduce or release the reserve, including buying a lijfrente with a matching reserve decrease, the reserve exceeding the ondernemingsvermogen (business assets), cessation, reaching AOW age on 1 January, failing the urencriterium (hours test) in both the current and the prior year, and death unless a continuing partner takes the reserve over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
