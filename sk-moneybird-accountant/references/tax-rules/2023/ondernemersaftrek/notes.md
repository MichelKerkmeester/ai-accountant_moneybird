## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at the AOW age: € 3.155 in 2022 to € 2.515 in 2023 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 13.360 in 2022 to € 14.202 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- S&O starter increase: € 6.684 in 2022 to € 7.106 in 2023 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Tariefsaanpassing: 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022 to 12,57% above € 73.031 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling: 40% in 2022 to 36,93% in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Second-test lookback window: 2017 to 2021 in 2022 to 2018 to 2022 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). FISIN 2022 printed "20167 tot en met 2021", a misprint against the statute, which gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2017 to 2021.
- Oudedagsreserve: 2022 was the final year with art. 3.67 to 3.72 in force, so the final year for additions, with an addition of 9,44% of the profit and a maximum of € 9.632 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so from 2023 no addition is possible ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). An existing reserve continues under art. 10a.29.
- Stakingsaftrek: the part for profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve left art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b on 1 January 2023 and now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The maximum of € 3.630 is unchanged.
- New in 2023: art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11, with the premiegrondslag in 2023 and 2024 taken before addition to the reserve and the jaarruimte for 2023 and 2024 reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Unchanged from 2022: urencriterium 1.225 hours, verlaagd urencriterium 800 hours, startersaftrek € 2.123 and € 1.062, the startersaftrek prior-years condition, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, MKB-winstvrijstelling 14%, meewerkaftrek bands 1,25% to 4% with the same hour bands, and stakingsaftrek maximum € 3.630 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Judge IB-ondernemer status per activity before claiming anything, separately from the btw registration ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)).
- Keep an hours record. Hours must be made plausible with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).
- Apply the order profit, then ondernemersaftrek, then MKB-winstvrijstelling when the belastbare winst uit onderneming is computed (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The belastbare winst uit onderneming is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, and that is the amount that enters the inkomstenbelasting.
- Record the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek when the profit cap cuts the aftrek. The inspecteur sets it in a beschikking open to bezwaar, and it is offset in the next nine calendar years ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).
- Keep both beschikkingen in the records, the one for the niet-gerealiseerde amount and the one for the offset, because both are open to bezwaar (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Record a partner's hours for the meewerkaftrek, and treat pay to the partner below € 5.000 as not deductible and as working without pay (art. 3.16 lid 4) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).
- For an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, keep the reserve on the balance, add every decrease to profit, and keep the lijfrente premiums that are deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (art. 10a.29) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve may be counted for the year by choice in the return, and premiums paid within six months after the year end may also be counted for that year by choice (art. 10a.29 lid 12 and 14) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Keep the S&O-verklaring from RVO and the hours record of the S&O work, because the aftrek depends on at least 500 hours of S&O work and on the urencriterium ([Belastingdienst FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Keep the evidence for the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, because that aftrek depends on the right to a listed benefit and on the verlaagd urencriterium (art. 3.78a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
