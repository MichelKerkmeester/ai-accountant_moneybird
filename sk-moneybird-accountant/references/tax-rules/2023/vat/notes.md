## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The qualifying solar-panel zero rate began on 1 January 2023. The checked material does not provide a separate 2022 solar-panel rate for comparison. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)
- The small businesses scheme (KOR) reform was published in 2023 and scheduled to take effect in 2025. It was therefore adopted but not yet in force for 2023. The old current-calendar-year test remained applicable in 2023, as it was in 2022. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html) · [2022 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01) · [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)
- The 21%, 9%, and zero-rate structure did not change from 2022. [2022 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01) · [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)
- The EU distance-sales threshold remained € 10.000 in 2023. It had applied since 1 July 2021. [Distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)
- The checked 2022 and 2023 materials both state the annual VAT return deadline as before 1 April of the following year, meaning by 31 March. [2022 VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [2023 VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
- The yearly-return VAT limit, VAT correction return amount boundary (suppletie), ICP deadline, and exact date table are not established for either year in the checked year-specific material. See the open points below. [2022 VAT return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw) · [2023 VAT return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the rates used, exempt turnover, input VAT deductions, and any zero-rated intra-EU supply. For the zero rate, retain evidence of transport to another EU country, the buyer’s valid VAT ID, and a correct ICP filing. [ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- Reconcile the VAT return to the sales and purchase records for its period. Report domestic reverse-charge transactions in section 2 and foreign reverse-charge transactions in section 4. Deduct reverse-charged VAT only when the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)
- Report relevant intra-EU supplies and services in the VAT return and ICP. The ICP total should match rubriek 3b for the same period. Record goods by invoice period and services by service period. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp)
- If using OSS, submit the relevant quarterly return, including a nil return when required, and retain the OSS records for the stated retention period. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through the Union-scheme return. [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- If using the small businesses scheme (KOR), do not charge VAT on invoices and do not deduct input VAT. Keep records of Dutch turnover and monitor supplies that count toward the ceiling. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [KOR turnover conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)
- Settle private use, the business car, taxable and exempt turnover ratios, and relevant staff provisions and gifts in the last VAT return of the year. Use a VAT correction return (suppletie) if that return has already been filed and needs correction. [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar) · [Suppletie rules](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01)
- Issue required invoices by the 15th day of the month after delivery. Record invoice number, supply date, rate, VAT amount, and the required customer and supplier details. [Invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Annual-return VAT limit:** less than € 1.883 is listed, but its application to 2023 is UNVERIFIED. The checked handbooks establish the amount for 2020 and 2026, not a 2023 publication. A 2023 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) publication or dated handbook would settle this. [Return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw) · [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Annual-return EU conditions:** the checked handbooks state less than € 10.000 for each of four EU activities, but a 2023 publication was not checked. A 2023 dated handbook or official publication would settle year-specific application. [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Exact 2023 filing dates:** the published 2023 date table was not found. A dated 2023 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration schedule would settle the exact dates. [Due-date page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
- **VAT correction return amount boundary (suppletie):** not established for 2023. The 2020 handbook says less than € 1.000 can be included in the next return, while 2026 guidance says € 1.000 or less. Those values do not settle the 2023 rule. A 2023 handbook or publication would settle it. [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf) · [Correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)
- **ICP deadline:** not found for 2023. A dated 2023 ICP publication would settle it. [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- **B2B e-invoicing duty:** the checked source reports no general B2B duty but does not verify that absence for 2023. A 2023 Dutch rule or official publication would settle the point. [Government e-invoicing guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)
- **Reduced-rate category history:** the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages do not state a year. The 2023 rate structure is verified, but the exact 2023 history of each category on those pages needs dated Table I (Tabel I) text for any borderline case. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [9% goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw) · [9% services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)
- **Solar flat rate (forfait) intermediate bands:** the checked material provides the first and last bands only. Use the dated 2023 decision schedule to confirm an intermediate band. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)
- **OSS inactivity:** the claim that 2 years without supplies ends participation while returns continue is UNVERIFIED. The checked OSS page does not confirm it. [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- **small businesses scheme (KOR) turnover-test conflict, resolved:** one baseline stated that both the year of application and the year before were tested. The 2023 statute tests turnover in a calendar year and does not name the preceding year. The prior-year test appears in the later 2025 law and the current small businesses scheme page. This later wording does not change the 2023 rule. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01) · [Current KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)
- **Annual-return deadline wording, resolved:** “before 1 April” and “by 31 March” agree. [VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
