## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard rate

The standard rate is 21%. Apply it where the supply is not covered by a reduced rate, zero rate, or exemption. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services listed in Table I (Tabel I). The current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages list food, water, flowers and plants, medicines and aids, art, collectibles and antiques, books and periodicals. They also list services such as bicycle, shoe, leather-goods, clothing and household-linen repairs, hairdressing, certain work on homes, camping, cultural and recreational access, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, qualifying electronic publications, and certain news websites and apps. The pages also identify related services such as supplying goods, selling food in hospitality, renting or repairing medical aids, lending books, and an artist lending their own art. The pages do not state a year, so check the applicable 2023 Table I text for a borderline category. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [9% goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw) · [9% services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to goods and services in Table II (Tabel II) when the applicable conditions are met. A zero-rated supply is still a taxable supply, so input VAT can generally remain deductible. An exempt supply does not carry that deduction right. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [Belastingdienst guidance on the ICP statement](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

For an intra-EU supply of goods, retain evidence that the goods went to another EU country, verify that the buyer has a valid VAT ID from another member state, and file the ICP statement correctly and on time. A late or incorrect ICP statement can put the zero rate at risk. [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Accommodation (Logies)

Accommodation was subject to the 9% rate in 2023. The later legislation preserved the reduced rate for 2023 while changing the treatment of accommodation from a later date. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Exemptions

Article 11 exemptions cover specified supplies and services, including certain dealings with immovable property, care, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money and securities, fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal services, public broadcasting, education, certain lectures, services by composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods, organs and blood, member services, cost-sharing groups, fundraising, and childcare. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration also lists investment gold and canteens and says exemptions apply to imports. Confirm the conditions for the specific exemption. A business making exempt supplies cannot deduct input VAT attributable to those supplies. [Article 11 of the VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) · [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Solar panels on or near homes

From 1 January 2023, the supply and installation of qualifying solar panels on or near homes is subject to the zero rate. Covered items include panels, cables, PV distributors, mounting materials, optimizers and inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The zero rate does not cover, among other things, multifunction panels, solar heat collectors, panels on non-residential business or public buildings, roof reinforcement, cable milling, replacement of the entire group cabinet, smart meters, bird or snow guards, separately purchased replacement inverters, heat pumps, or batteries. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home are taxed at 21% when the home is supplied too. If only the panels are supplied and installed, the panels qualify for the zero rate. The June decision applies from the start of 2023. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html) · [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen)

The decision gives a VAT flat rate (forfait) for solar-panel owners. The checked material specifies the first and last capacity bands, but not the intermediate bands. Do not infer an intermediate amount from the endpoints. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### small businesses scheme (KOR) ceiling and turnover test

The 2023 small businesses scheme allowed an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands, or with a fixed establishment there, to choose exemption if Dutch turnover in the calendar year was no more than € 20.000. In 2023 the statutory test referred to turnover in the calendar year itself. The prior-year test belongs to the later small businesses scheme legislation. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

For the turnover calculation, the checked guidance includes Dutch-taxed supplies at any rate, including zero-rated supplies, domestic reverse-charged supplies, and certain exempt supplies relating to immovable property, means of payment, securities, and financial or insurance services. Other exempt turnover does not count. Private-use VAT, sales of business investment goods, purchases reverse-charged to the business, supplies taxed abroad, and intra-community acquisitions are excluded. Before joining, measure relevant turnover excluding VAT. During small businesses scheme participation, use the total amount charged. The small businesses scheme applies across the entrepreneur’s sub-numbers. [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Current KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)

A small businesses scheme participant does not charge VAT on invoices and cannot deduct input VAT. Exceeding the ceiling removes the exemption from the supply that crosses the ceiling onward. The old rules also restricted re-entry after revocation or an overrun. Legal persons could also join under the checked 2020 handbook. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### small businesses scheme notice, minimum participation, and re-entry

Under the 2023 rules, notify the tax office at least 4 weeks before the intended tax period. Participation had to last at least 3 years. After voluntary revocation, re-entry was restricted for 3 years. After exceeding the turnover ceiling, re-entry was also restricted for 3 years. If the ceiling is crossed, tax applies from the crossing supply onward. [2022 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01) · [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

### small businesses scheme registration threshold without notice

An entrepreneur not yet registered for VAT could apply for the small businesses scheme without the usual notice if annual turnover was no more than € 1.800 and the decision’s other conditions were met. From 2025, the statutory threshold changed. That later threshold does not replace the 2023 amount. [2021 decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### small businesses scheme reform published in 2023

The 2023 reform was adopted but was not in force for the 2023 tax year. It was scheduled to start on 1 January 2025, with transition provisions from 1 October 2024. The amended regime tests the prior calendar year as well as the year of participation and changes other eligibility and notification rules. Apply the old Article 25 rules to 2023. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### VAT return period

Quarterly filing is the usual period. An entrepreneur can ask by letter for monthly or yearly filing. The tax office replies within 6 weeks and a change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a monthly period being imposed. [Changing a VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

A yearly return is available only if all applicable conditions are met. These include a yearly VAT amount below the relevant limit, no Article 23 permit (vergunning artikel 23), the required legal form, and limits on each of the specified intra-EU activities. The checked handbooks for 2020 and 2026 state these conditions, but the 2023 limit and its application were not confirmed in a 2023 publication. Do not assume eligibility based only on a current return-period page. [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook for businesses 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf) · [Changing a VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

### Period return and payment deadline

A period return must be filed within the term set by the inspector, which must be at least 1 month after the end of the period. Payment is due within 1 month after the end of that period. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the month following the return period. Filing and payment share a date. An extension is available only in a calamity. [General State Taxes Act, article 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01) · [General State Taxes Act, article 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01) · [VAT return and payment guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Annual VAT return deadline

The yearly return is due before 1 April of the following year, meaning by 31 March. Filing and payment use the same date. [VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Published 2023 filing-date table

The checked material did not locate a published table of exact 2023 VAT filing dates. The general deadline rules above are supported, but the dated table remains unverified. [Belastingdienst due-date page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### VAT correction return timing and correction period

Use a VAT correction return to correct earlier VAT returns for the past 5 calendar years. The 2023 rule required correction as soon as possible, in the way specified by the inspector, before the taxpayer knew or should have suspected that the inspector already knew. The later 8-week rule does not govern 2023. [UBOB 2020, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01) · [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html)

### VAT correction return amount boundary

The checked material does not establish the 2023 amount at which a correction could be made in the next return rather than by a separate VAT correction return. Keep the correction rule separate from later guidance until a 2023 source is confirmed. [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

### Reverse charge

The reverse-charge mechanism shifts the VAT reporting obligation from supplier to customer in specified cases. It applies, for example, to certain construction subcontracting and staff lending, waste and scrap, certain property transactions, gold, emission rights, gas and electricity certificates, and specified electronics. For mobile phones, chips before end-product, game consoles, tablets, and laptops, the threshold is measured per delivery and per type of good. The supplier still remains responsible for correct invoicing. An invoice must state “btw verlegd,” the customer’s VAT ID, and the consideration per rate. The customer reports domestic reverse charges in rubriek 2 and foreign reverse charges in rubriek 4, and may deduct the VAT when the purchase is used for taxable activity. [VAT Act, article 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) · [UBOB, articles 24b to 24bb](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01) · [When to reverse charge](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/) · [How reverse charge works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

For subcontracting, exceptions include work done mostly in the subcontractor’s own workshop, staff lent for that kind of work, and subordinate work on goods sold. Disputes are addressed to the main contractor’s tax office. [Subcontracting exceptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/bij_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen/uitzonderingen_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen)

### EU distance sales and OSS

The EU-wide distance-sales threshold applies only to a supplier established in one member state. It covers qualifying goods sent to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country where the supplier arranged or paid for transport, and relevant digital services. Margin goods, nearly new means of transport, and assembly supplies are excluded from the described goods rule. Measure the threshold excluding VAT. If turnover stays below it, the supplier may opt to use foreign VAT, with the choice lasting at least 2 years. Once the threshold is exceeded, foreign VAT applies from the supply that crosses it. Dutch VAT resumes from 1 January of the year after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The Union scheme is optional and allows one quarterly return for EU distance sales. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through that return. A fixed establishment in another EU country removes the threshold. [Distance-sales and OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren) · [EU VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)

### OSS deadlines and retention

The Union scheme uses a quarterly return in the month after the quarter, due by the last day of that month. A nil return is also required. It can generally be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For an earlier first supply, registration must arrive by the deadline shown in the table. Corrections go into a later return. Keep OSS records for the stated retention period. The import scheme is monthly and is limited to qualifying consignments. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### OSS inactivity claim

The checked source does not establish the claim that two years without supplies ends participation if returns are still being filed. Treat that claim as unverified. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### ICP reporting

Report intra-EU goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country, and call-off stock transfers in the ICP statement. The total should match rubriek 3b (rubriek 3b) for the same period. Goods belong in the period of the invoice date. Services belong in the period the service was supplied. Do not file an ICP statement for a period with nothing to report. Correct errors in the next ICP statement. Goods may be reported quarterly only if the goods threshold is met for the current and previous quarters in the table. The checked material does not establish the 2023 ICP filing deadline. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp) · [ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp) · [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

For B2B services supplied to a customer in another EU country, the service is generally taxed where the customer is established. Invoice without VAT, mark the invoice “btw verlegd,” check the customer’s VAT ID, and report the transaction in the VAT return and ICP. Some services are exceptions. The checked guidance excludes certain exempt or zero-rated services in the customer’s country, OSS services, property-related services, passenger transport, and admission to certain events from the ICP list. [EU B2B services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen) · [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Private use and the last return

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur’s family. It can involve mixed-use items such as a car, a business computer, or a building used partly privately, and goods withdrawn for exclusively private use. Staff private use can restrict deductions under the BUA. The last VAT return of the year settles private use, the business car, the taxable and exempt turnover ratio, and relevant personnel provisions, gifts, and business gifts above the BUA limit. It can also include utilities, fictitious services, and revisions of earlier deductions. Correct an already-filed last return by filing a VAT correction return (suppletie). [Private use guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik) · [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Business-car private use

If records do not establish actual private use, the flat rate is based on the catalogue price including VAT and BPM. The 2,7% calculation is capped by the VAT deducted in the year plus the stated share of purchase VAT. Use 1,5% if no purchase VAT could be deducted, and after the specified period following the year the car first came into use. Commuting is private use. Reduce the amount proportionally for exempt use and calculate a part-year amount pro rata. Settle the amount in the last return of the year. [Private-use car decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04) · [Belastingdienst car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak)

### BUA and own contributions

The BUA restricts deduction for certain staff facilities, business gifts, and other gifts. It also concerns staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure, and private transport, subject to exceptions. The decision provides carve-outs for specified staff food, licensed commuting bus transport, qualifying commuting bicycles, and outplacement. It also provides that costs for a person within the per-person limit are outside the exclusion. The 2023 decision ended the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration website explanation that let an own contribution reduce the tested amount from the following year. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23) · [2023 decision on own contributions](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### Invoicing

Issue an invoice for supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. An invoice is generally not needed for a private customer, subject to exceptions. Issue the invoice by the 15th day of the month after delivery. The invoice must include the parties’ names and addresses, the required VAT ID and registration details, invoice date, unique consecutive number, description and quantity or scope, supply or prepayment date, amounts excluding VAT by rate, unit prices, rate, and VAT amount. A simplified invoice is allowed only within the stated ceiling or for a correction invoice and has its own content rules. Digital invoicing requires customer agreement and assurance of authenticity, content, and readability. The cash accounting scheme (kasstelsel) is compulsory for specified trades and can apply on request where the stated share of turnover is to consumers. [Invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/) · [Invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen) · [Simplified invoices](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)
