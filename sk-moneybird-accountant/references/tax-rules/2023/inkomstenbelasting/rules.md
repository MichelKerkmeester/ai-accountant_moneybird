## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

For a taxpayer below AOW age, the published table applies 36,93% up to € 73.031 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The statutory income-tax schedule separately shows 9,28% up to € 37.149, then € 3.447 plus 36,93% through € 73.031, then € 16.698 plus 49,50% above it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The 36,93% published rate includes the applicable national insurance premiums within the premium ceiling described below (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen).

### Box 1 full-year AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a person who is at AOW age for the full year and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the published rates are 19,03% up to € 37.149, 36,93% to € 73.031 and 49,50% above that amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The first rate is lower because the AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). The checked material treats the continuation of Anw and Wlz premiums at AOW age as an inference, not as a verified stated rule.

### Box 1 full-year AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

A person at AOW age for the full year and born before 1 January 1946 uses the higher first boundary of € 38.703. The published rates are 19,03% up to that boundary, then 36,93% to € 73.031 and 49,50% above it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10a). The statutory table amounts shown for this variant are € 3.591 and € 16.268 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10a).

### Box 1 in the AOW transition year

If a taxpayer reaches AOW age during 2023, the first-bracket rate depends on the month in which AOW age is reached. The applicable first-bracket rate runs from 19,03% for January to 35,43% for December, and the first bracket ends at € 37.149 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The second and third bracket rates remain the normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1).

### Income-tax and social insurance components

The first statutory band's income-tax part is 9,28%. The national insurance premium rate is 27,65%, made up of AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). The published combined rate in the first bracket adds the income-tax part and applicable premiums (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). At AOW age, the AOW premium stops. The checked material does not verify as a stated rule whether the Anw and Wlz parts continue at that age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling

The national insurance premiums are charged on premie-inkomen up to the ceiling of € 37.149. For people born before 1 January 1946, the ceiling is € 38.703 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). The first statutory bracket boundary and premium ceiling therefore depend on the birth-date variant in the table.

### Tariefsaanpassing

When a taxpayer deducts grondslagverminderende posten (items that reduce the taxable base), art. 2.10 lid 2 raises the tax calculated under lid 1 by 12,57% of the amount by which taxable Box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds € 73.031. The increase cannot exceed 12,57% of the deductions themselves (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). This makes deductions above the top bracket boundary worth at most 36,93%, calculated as 49,50% minus 12,57% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

The listed posts include persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions), costs for an owner-occupied home, ondernemersfaciliteiten (entrepreneur reliefs) and the exemption for making assets available to another party (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The entrepreneur reliefs include zelfstandigenaftrek, R&D deductions, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

### Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

The algemene heffingskorting (general tax credit) is available to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the tax return and can also be applied by an employer or benefit agency through payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). Below AOW age, the maximum is € 3.070. It is reduced by 6,095% of Box 1 taxable income above € 22.660 and reaches nil at € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).

For 2023, the phase-out base is taxable income from work and home. The Toelichting M-biljet 2023 calculation aid uses worldwide income in Box 1 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).

### Algemene heffingskorting for a full year at AOW age

For a full year at AOW age, the maximum general tax credit is € 1.583. It is reduced by 3,141% of income above € 22.660 and reaches nil at € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The ordinary table does not establish the amount for someone who reaches AOW age during 2023. That transition-year point remains unverified below.

### Arbeidskorting below AOW age

The arbeidskorting (employment tax credit) is based on arbeidsinkomen. For an income-tax entrepreneur, arbeidsinkomen includes business profit before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. It excludes the part earned as a medegerechtigde (co-entitled party) or winstdelende schuldeiser (profit-sharing creditor) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

The credit builds through the first three bands, then falls by 6,510% from the fourth-band amount. It is nil above € 115.295 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The bands and starting amounts are in the figures table.

### Arbeidskorting for a full year at AOW age

For a full year at AOW age, the arbeidskorting uses the separate rates and amounts in the figures table. The Toelichting M-biljet 2023 confirms a maximum of € 2.604 and a top-band reduction of 3,355% above € 37.691, until the credit becomes nil above € 115.295 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The source conflict about the top band is resolved in section 7.

### Higher arbeidskorting applied by an employer

If an employer applied a higher arbeidskorting and arbeidsinkomen is not above € 37.691, the higher amount counts up to € 5.052. The corresponding limit is € 2.604 if AOW age was reached before 2023 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The checked 2023 material does not provide additional conditions for this rule.

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting below AOW age

The inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting (income-dependent combination credit, or IACK) is 11,45% of arbeidsinkomen above € 5.547, up to a maximum of € 2.694 at € 29.076 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). It is claimed in the tax return or through a provisional assessment, not through payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

The IACK generally requires a qualifying child under 12 on 1 January and that the child be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The child may be the taxpayer's own, step, adopted, a spouse's child from an earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child. A pleegkind (foster child) qualifies only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a pleegvergoeding (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The checked material also identifies residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES as potentially eligible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

The income and partner test requires no fiscal partner, a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner. With equal incomes, only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6-month period is complete still allows the credit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

For co-ouders, the 2023 page requires at least 78 days in a 6-month period, in a repeating pattern with each parent (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting for a full year at AOW age

For a full year at AOW age, the IACK rate is 5,90%, with a maximum of € 1.389 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The child and partner conditions still apply as described above (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Heffingskortingen in the year AOW age is reached

The checked 2023 heffingskortingen sources provide no rule or amount for the year in which AOW age is reached. The transition-year treatment of the general tax credit, arbeidskorting and IACK is therefore unverified for 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen of heffingskortingen

Each heffingskorting has a belastingdeel (income-tax part) and premiedelen (national insurance premium parts). For 2023 the belastingdeel is 9,28%, and the premium components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). A taxpayer who is not insured for one of these schemes does not receive that part of the credit. For part-year premium liability, the premium part is time-proportional (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

### Payment of unused heffingskortingen to a lower-earning partner

Payment of unused credits to a lower-earning partner is subject to conditions. The same fiscal partnership must have lasted more than 6 months, the partner must owe enough tax, and only the part that cannot be offset against the taxpayer's own tax and premiums can be paid (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

From 2023, the AK and IACK can no longer be paid out to the partner. The AHK payment ended for people born in 1963 or later. It remains possible for people born before 1 January 1963, who were not subject to the phase-out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The 2023 page lists an own Box 1 income reference of € 10.705 and a partner income threshold of € 28.580, or € 32.480 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Winst uit onderneming flow

Winst uit onderneming (business profit) requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and that the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide the tax classification. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

First remove vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen (exempt profit components). The checked material lists the forest and agricultural exemptions, the applicable waiver-of-debt exemption, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business-termination compensation, Waz benefits, certain forest and nature subsidies, and specified final-levy, mobility-project and peak-avoidance exemptions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

The taxable business profit is then profit reduced by ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated on profit after ondernemersaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker (lender) receives neither ondernemersaftrek nor MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

Belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning (taxable income from work and home) is income from work and home, including taxable business profit, reduced by losses that can be carried forward or offset (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). The Box 1 table applies to that balance. Tariefsaanpassing may then increase the tax, and heffingskortingen reduce the tax and premium amounts through their belastingdeel and premiedelen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The checked material says that the exact sequence of the final credit steps is an inference from the article structure, as recorded in section 7.

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

The zelfstandigenaftrek (self-employed deduction) is € 5.030, or € 2.515 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of ondernemersaftrek and reduces profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). Detailed eligibility tests belong to the ondernemersaftrek topic.

### Startersaftrek

The startersaftrek (start-up deduction) is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of ondernemersaftrek and is applied before the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

The startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (start-up deduction for disability) is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 and then € 0 under its staged scale. The deduction is capped at profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Meewerkaftrek

The meewerkaftrek (deduction for a partner's work in the business) is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the applicable hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked material does not set out the hour bands here.

### Stakingsaftrek

The stakingsaftrek (business-cessation deduction) has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

The MKB-winstvrijstelling (SME profit exemption) is 14% of profit after ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). It reduces the taxable business profit that enters Box 1. It is also included in the list of deductions subject to tariefsaanpassing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

### Oudedagsreserve

No new oudedagsreserve (retirement reserve) additions can be formed from 2023. An existing reserve may remain on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). It therefore does not create a new 2023 addition to the business-profit calculation.
