## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example: carry-forward of the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)

**Official example** ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

- 2021: profit € 5.000,00 against a self-employed person's deduction of € 6.670,00, so € 1.670,00 was unused and became an unused self-employed person's deduction.
- 2022: a loss of € 25.000,00 against a self-employed person's deduction of € 6.310,00, so € 6.310,00 was unused. The balance (saldo) becomes € 1.670,00 plus € 6.310,00 = € 7.980,00.
- 2023: profit € 100.000,00 minus € 5.030,00 gives € 94.970,00. The profit exceeds the self-employed person's deduction of 2023, so the carry-forward can be used up to € 7.980,00. The 2023 base becomes € 94.970,00 minus € 7.980,00 = € 86.990,00 and the balance becomes € 0,00.

### Official example: part-year start and the hours criterion (urencriterium)

**Official example** ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). A webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur, and the taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours are not recalculated to the part of the year.

### Official example: eight months from 1 May

**Official example** ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium)). A start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days. 117 working days times 8 hours = 936,00 hours, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours.

### Official example: standby time

**Official example** ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). A webhosting owner who is reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend and fixes one outage of 4 hours counts only the 4 hours, not the standby time.

### Official example: broken book year ending in 2023

**Official example** ([Kennisgroepen Belastingdienst](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220232-doteren-aan-de-fiscale-oudedagsreserve-bij-een-gebroken-boekjaar-eindigend-in-belastingjaar-2023/)). KG:212:2023:2 answers five cases about a broken book year ending in tax year 2023 and states per case whether adding (doteren) to the fiscal old-age reserve (fiscale oudedagsreserve) in 2023 is possible. An addition is possible over a broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 and ends in 2023.

### Calculated example: profit € 60.000 with the hours criterion, no starter

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. A sole trader meets the hours criterion, is below state pension age (AOW-leeftijd), is not a starter, and has no other box 1 income.

1. Profit: € 60.000,00.
2. Self-employed person's deduction: € 5.030,00. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000,00 minus € 5.030,00 = € 54.970,00.
3. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 14% of € 54.970,00 = € 7.695,80.
4. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 54.970,00 minus € 7.695,80 = € 47.274,20.
5. Total deductions: € 5.030,00 plus € 7.695,80 = € 12.725,80.
6. Taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus the deductions: € 47.274,20 plus € 12.725,80 = € 60.000,00. This is below € 73.031,00, so no rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) applies in this example.

### Calculated example: profit € 60.000, first-year starter

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. Same facts as above, but the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of 2018 to 2022 and the self-employed person's deduction was used at most twice. Assume no other box 1 income.

1. Self-employed person's deduction plus starter's deduction (startersaftrek): € 5.030,00 plus € 2.123,00 = € 7.153,00.
2. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000,00 minus € 7.153,00 = € 52.847,00.
3. SME profit exemption: 14% of € 52.847,00 = € 7.398,58.
4. Taxable profit from business: € 52.847,00 minus € 7.398,58 = € 45.448,42.
5. Total deductions: € 7.153,00 plus € 7.398,58 = € 14.551,58.
6. Taxable income from work and home plus the deductions: € 45.448,42 plus € 14.551,58 = € 60.000,00. This is below € 73.031,00, so no rate adjustment applies in this example.

### Calculated example: profit € 60.000 without the hours criterion

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The taxpayer works fewer than 1.225 hours, so there is no entrepreneur's allowance, but the SME profit exemption still applies because it has no hours criterion. Assume no other box 1 income.

1. Profit: € 60.000,00.
2. SME profit exemption: 14% of € 60.000,00 = € 8.400,00.
3. Taxable profit from business: € 60.000,00 minus € 8.400,00 = € 51.600,00.
4. Total deductions: € 8.400,00.
5. Taxable income from work and home plus the deductions: € 51.600,00 plus € 8.400,00 = € 60.000,00. This is below € 73.031,00, so no rate adjustment applies in this example.

### Calculated example: starter's deduction and a low profit

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The taxpayer is a starter with the hours criterion and a profit of € 6.000,00.

1. Self-employed person's deduction plus starter's deduction: € 5.030,00 plus € 2.123,00 = € 7.153,00.
2. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 6.000,00 minus € 7.153,00 = minus € 1.153,00.
3. Because the taxpayer qualifies for the starter's deduction, the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction may exceed the profit. The excess of € 1.153,00 is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)).

### Calculated example: deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The partner works 1.300 hours in the business without pay, the profit is € 60.000,00 and the hours criterion is met. For clarity this example uses only the deduction for assisting partner and not the self-employed person's deduction.

1. 1.300 hours falls in the band from 1.225 to under 1.750 hours, so the percentage is 3%.
2. Deduction for assisting partner: 3% of € 60.000,00 = € 1.800,00.
3. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000,00 minus € 1.800,00 = € 58.200,00.
4. SME profit exemption: 14% of € 58.200,00 = € 8.148,00.
5. Taxable profit from business: € 58.200,00 minus € 8.148,00 = € 50.052,00.

### Calculated example: discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The taxpayer ceases a whole business with a profit of € 10.000,00.

1. The discontinuation deduction equals the profit, at most € 3.630,00, so the deduction is € 3.630,00.
2. If € 1.000,00 of discontinuation deduction was used in an earlier year, the maximum becomes € 3.630,00 minus € 1.000,00 = € 2.630,00, and the deduction is € 2.630,00.
3. If the cessation profit were € 2.000,00 and the reduced maximum were € 2.630,00, the deduction would be € 2.000,00, because the deduction cannot exceed the profit.

### Calculated example: starter's deduction on occupational disability (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, does not meet the hours criterion but meets the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium), has a right to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, and is below state pension age (AOW age).

1. First use, profit € 9.000,00. The tier is € 12.000,00, but the deduction is capped at the profit, so the deduction is € 9.000,00.
2. A later year in which the deduction was applied once before, profit € 20.000,00. The tier is € 8.000,00, which is below the profit, so the deduction is € 8.000,00.
3. A later year in which the deduction was applied twice before, profit € 20.000,00. The tier is € 4.000,00, which is below the profit, so the deduction is € 4.000,00.
4. If the deduction was applied three times before, there is no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction.

### Calculated example: rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) at a profit of € 100.000

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. A sole trader meets the hours criterion, is below state pension age (AOW age), and has no other box 1 income.

1. Profit: € 100.000,00.
2. Self-employed person's deduction: € 5.030,00. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 100.000,00 minus € 5.030,00 = € 94.970,00.
3. SME profit exemption: 14% of € 94.970,00 = € 13.295,80.
4. Taxable profit from business: € 94.970,00 minus € 13.295,80 = € 81.674,20.
5. Total deductions: € 5.030,00 plus € 13.295,80 = € 18.325,80.
6. Taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus the deductions: € 81.674,20 plus € 18.325,80 = € 100.000,00.
7. Excess over the start of the top bracket: € 100.000,00 minus € 73.031,00 = € 26.969,00.
8. 12,57% of € 26.969,00 = € 3.390,0033, rounded down to € 3.390,00.
9. Cap: 12,57% of € 18.325,80 = € 2.303,55306, rounded down to € 2.303,55.
10. Rate adjustment: the lower of the two amounts, so € 2.303,55.
11. Check against the maximum value: 36,93% of € 18.325,80 = € 6.767,71794, rounded to € 6.767,72. This equals 49,50% of € 18.325,80 = € 9.071,271, rounded down to € 9.071,27, minus the rate adjustment of € 2.303,55 = € 6.767,72.

### Calculated example: forced decrease of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The old-age reserve was € 20.000,00 on 1 January 2023, the hours criterion was not met in 2023 and not met in 2022, and the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of 2023 is € 12.000,00.

1. No choice decrease is assumed, because there are no annuity (lijfrente) premiums deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in this example.
2. The reserve of € 20.000,00 is more than the business capital of € 12.000,00 at year end.
3. Forced decrease: € 20.000,00 minus € 12.000,00 = € 8.000,00.
4. The decrease of € 8.000,00 is added to the profit of 2023.

### Calculated example: annuity premium (lijfrentepremie) and the choice for the year

**Calculated example.** Built from the section 2 figures. The taxpayer deducts € 4.000,00 of annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in 2023.

1. The taxpayer chooses a decrease of the reserve of € 4.000,00, which is the maximum, because the decrease is at most the premiums deducted.
2. The decrease of € 4.000,00 is added to the profit of 2023.
3. A decrease of € 6.000,00 is not possible in this example, because it exceeds the premiums deducted.
4. If part of the premium were paid in the first six months of 2024, that part can still count for 2023 by choice in the return.
