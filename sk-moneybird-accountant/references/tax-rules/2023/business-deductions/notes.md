## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age (AOW-leeftijd): € 3.155 in 2022 to € 2.515 in 2023 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Research and development work deduction (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 13.360 in 2022 to € 14.202 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- S&O starter increase: € 6.684 in 2022 to € 7.106 in 2023 ([FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 9,5% above € 69.398 in 2022 to 12,57% above € 73.031 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 40% in 2022 to 36,93% in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Second-test lookback window: 2017 to 2021 in 2022 to 2018 to 2022 in 2023 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). FISIN 2022 printed "20167 tot en met 2021", a misprint against the statute, which gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2017 to 2021.
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): 2022 was the final year with art. 3.67 to 3.72 in force, so the final year for additions, with an addition of 9,44% of the profit and a maximum of € 9.632 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so from 2023 no addition is possible ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). An existing reserve continues under art. 10a.29.
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): the part for profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve left art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b on 1 January 2023 and now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The maximum of € 3.630 is unchanged.
- New in 2023: art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11, with the contribution base (premiegrondslag) in 2023 and 2024 taken before addition to the reserve and the annual allowance (jaarruimte) for 2023 and 2024 reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease ([2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Unchanged from 2022: hours criterion (urencriterium) 1.225 hours, reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) 800 hours, starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123 and € 1.062, the starter's deduction prior-years condition, starter's deduction on occupational disability € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, SME profit exemption 14%, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands 1,25% to 4% with the same hour bands, and discontinuation deduction maximum € 3.630 ([2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Judge entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes (IB-ondernemer) status per activity before claiming anything, separately from the VAT (btw) registration ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)).
- Keep an hours record. Hours must be made plausible with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).
- Apply the order profit, then entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), then SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) when the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is computed (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The taxable profit from business is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, and that is the amount that enters the income tax (inkomstenbelasting).
- Record the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) when the profit cap cuts the deduction. The tax inspector (inspecteur) sets it in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar), and it is offset in the next nine calendar years ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).
- Keep both decisions in the records, the one for the unused amount and the one for the offset, because both are open to objection (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Record a partner's hours for the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), and treat pay to the partner below € 5.000 as not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and as working without pay (art. 3.16 lid 4) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).
- For an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) that existed on 31 December 2022, keep the reserve on the balance, add every decrease to profit, and keep the annuity (lijfrente) premiums that are deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) (art. 10a.29) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) for converting the reserve may be counted for the year by choice in the return, and premiums paid within six months after the year end may also be counted for that year by choice (art. 10a.29 lid 12 and 14) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Keep the S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) and the hours record of the S&O work, because the deduction depends on at least 500 hours of S&O work and on the hours criterion (urencriterium) ([Belastingdienst FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Keep the evidence for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) on occupational disability, because that deduction depends on the right to a listed benefit and on the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) (art. 3.78a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
