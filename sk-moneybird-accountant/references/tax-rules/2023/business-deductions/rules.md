## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Urencriterium (1.225 hours)

The urencriterium (hours criterion) is the entry condition for the zelfstandigenaftrek, the startersaftrek, the meewerkaftrek and the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk. It only matters for someone who is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting (IB-ondernemer). Who counts as an IB-ondernemer is judged by the Belastingdienst per activity, separately from the btw. Someone who is a btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/)). Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer, and someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work), where the profit is calculated the same way but there is no right to the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)). The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the zelfstandig beroep (independent profession) counts as an onderneming (art. 3.5) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

The test itself has two elements. There must be at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer, and either the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is grotendeels (mainly) spent on the businesses, or the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (art. 3.6 lid 1) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The text of art. 3.6 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). The Belastingdienst example is a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. The KVK example is a start on 1 May, which leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium)).

Which hours count: all hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Tweede toets en de vijfjaarstoets

The second element of the art. 3.6 lid 1 test, the grotendeels test, falls away for anyone who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. It comes back only after five straight ondernemer years ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 the lookback window is 2018 to 2022, as the Belastingdienst FISIN 2023 handbook prints it ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The other year handbooks print their own five-year windows around the same statutory rule, for example 2021: 2016 to 2020, 2024: 2019 to 2023, 2025: 2020 to 2024 and 2026: 2021 to 2025 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Verlaagd urencriterium (800 hours)

The verlaagd urencriterium (reduced hours criterion) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (art. 3.78a lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid. It is used when the ordinary urencriterium is not met, so it is the band between 800 hours and 1.225 hours. It gives no right to the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek, the startersaftrek or the MKB-winstvrijstelling ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Zwangerschap, 16 weken tellen als gewerkte uren

If the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, the work counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity and confinement leave) for employees (art. 3.6 lid 5) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks, and the FISIN 2023 handbook repeats that 16-week rule ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Samenwerkingsverband met verbonden personen

Hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (cooperation arrangement) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not (art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and an ondermaatschap (sub-partnership) as the example for (b) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek (€ 5.030)

The zelfstandigenaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium (art. 3.76 lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age (state pension age) at the start of the year, which is € 5.030 for 2023. At the AOW age at the start of the year the aftrek is 50% of the amounts under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too (lid 4). The aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek (lid 5). The amount cut off by this cap is the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (non-realised self-employed person's allowance). If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count. The inspecteur (tax inspector) sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection) (lid 6). It is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first (lid 7). The offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar (lid 8) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).

The zelfstandigenaftrek is one of the five parts of the ondernemersaftrek in art. 3.74, so it is deducted from the profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek op AOW-leeftijd (€ 2.515)

At the AOW age at the start of the calendar year the zelfstandigenaftrek is 50% of the normal amount, so € 2.515 instead of € 5.030 for 2023, and the startersaftrek is halved as well, to € 1.062 instead of € 2.123 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The halving follows from art. 3.76 lid 4, which refers to the amounts under lid 2 and lid 3 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Startersaftrek (€ 2.123)

The zelfstandigenaftrek is raised by the startersaftrek of € 2.123 if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period (art. 3.76 lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, a tax-free return from a private limited company) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Belastingdienst adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)).

The startersaftrek is not a separate item in the art. 3.74 list of the ondernemersaftrek. It is a verhoging (increase) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Startersaftrek op AOW-leeftijd (€ 1.062)

At the AOW age at the start of the year the startersaftrek is € 1.062, which is 50% of € 2.123 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The halving of the startersaftrek follows from art. 3.76 lid 4 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Startersaftrek, de voorwaarde over eerdere jaren

The condition is a combined lookback: the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied at most twice in that period (art. 3.76 lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which means the same as the statute, and FISIN 2023 states the same prior-years condition ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). For 2023 the five preceding calendar years are 2018 to 2022. A year in which the profit cap set the zelfstandigenaftrek to nil still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for this count ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

This part of the ondernemersaftrek applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (occupational disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year (art. 3.78a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 that started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance (disability or accident insurance). The amount is € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The text and the amounts are identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (€ 14.202)

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (research and development deduction, S&O) is a part of the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.74) and is set in art. 3.77. For 2023 the amount is € 14.202 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst FISIN 2023 handbook agrees with that amount ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The amounts are indexed per year, so every year has its own base amount. The deduction is a base amount plus a possible starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### S&O startersverhoging (€ 7.106)

For 2023 the base amount of € 14.202 can be raised by the S&O starter increase of € 7.106 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The starter increase uses the same lookback structure as the startersaftrek: the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Aftrek S&O, de voorwaarden

The Belastingdienst FISIN handbook adds three conditions to the S&O aftrek: it requires an S&O-verklaring (S&O declaration) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium ([Belastingdienst FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The S&O-verklaring is the document from RVO that must be held before the aftrek can be claimed, and the 500 hours are hours of S&O work, next to the 1.225 hours of the urencriterium for the business as a whole.

### MKB-winstvrijstelling (14%)

The MKB-winstvrijstelling is a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.79a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 the percentage is 14%. Belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit from business) is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (art. 3.2), so the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. No urencriterium is needed, because the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). There is no vrijstelling on profit as a medegerechtigde (profit-sharing partner). Medegerechtigden also get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek or stakingsaftrek on their medegerechtigde profit ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing (rate adjustment) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)).

### Meewerkaftrek

The meewerkaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay (art. 3.78) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. This settles the boundary hours: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 hours in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Partnerbeloning onder € 5.000

Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit (art. 3.16 lid 4) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). This interacts directly with the meewerkaftrek, because the partner then counts as an unpaid partner for the bands above.

### Stakingsaftrek (€ 3.630)

The stakingsaftrek applies to profit made with or on the staking (cessation) of one or more whole businesses (art. 3.79) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under doorschuiving (tax-free transfer) on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and that there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek)).

### Oudedagsreserve, geen toevoegingen meer vanaf 2023

Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition to the oudedagsreserve is possible from 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The afneming (decrease) rules are: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenses for income provisions) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen (business capital) at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (huwelijksgemeenschap, death, transfer to partner), and lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve. Lid 10 and 11 state that for the premiegrondslag in 2023 and 2024 the profit is taken before addition to the reserve, and that the jaarruimte (annual pension room) for 2023 and 2024 is reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Oudedagsreserve in een gebroken boekjaar eindigend in 2023

A knowledge group decision of the Belastingdienst, KG:212:2023:2 of 9 May 2023, answers five cases about a broken book year with the answer per case whether doteren (adding) to the fiscale oudedagsreserve in 2023 is possible ([Kennisgroepen Belastingdienst](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220232-doteren-aan-de-fiscale-oudedagsreserve-bij-een-gebroken-boekjaar-eindigend-in-belastingjaar-2023/)). An addition is possible over a broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 and ends in 2023, which matches art. 10a.29 lid 1 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Oudedagsreserve, lijfrentepremies en de zesmaandstermijn

Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year. Lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). So a premium paid between 1 January 2024 and 1 July 2024 can still be chosen for 2023, and the choice is made in the return for 2023.

### Tariefsaanpassing (12,57% above € 73.031)

If grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the tax base), expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 that percentage is 12,57% and the start of the top bracket is € 73.031. The effect is that the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are worth less than the top rate for a taxpayer whose income is in or above the top bracket.

### Maximum value of the aftrekken (36,93%)

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing percentage of 12,57%, so 36,93% ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). That rate equals the second-bracket rate. Practically, the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling reduce the tax by at most 36,93% of the amount deducted ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
