## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Hours criterion (urencriterium) (1.225 hours)

The hours criterion (urencriterium) is the entry condition for the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) and the research and development deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk). It only matters for someone who is an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer, inkomstenbelasting). Who counts as an entrepreneur for income tax purposes is judged by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) per activity, separately from the VAT (btw). Someone who is a VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/)). Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, and someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), where the profit is calculated the same way but there is no right to the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)). The statute defines the entrepreneur (ondernemer) as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (onderneming) (art. 3.5) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

The test itself has two elements. There must be at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer), and either the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses, or the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (art. 3.6 lid 1) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The text of art. 3.6 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example is a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. The Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) example is a start on 1 May, which leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium)).

Which hours count: all hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Second test (tweede toets) and the five-year test (vijfjaarstoets)

The second element of the art. 3.6 lid 1 test, the mainly (grotendeels) test, falls away for anyone who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. It comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 the lookback window is 2018 to 2022, as the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration FISIN 2023 handbook prints it ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The other year handbooks print their own five-year windows around the same statutory rule, for example 2021: 2016 to 2020, 2024: 2019 to 2023, 2025: 2020 to 2024 and 2026: 2021 to 2025 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) (800 hours)

The reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (art. 3.78a lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). It is a condition only of the starter's deduction on occupational disability (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid). It is used when the ordinary hours criterion is not met, so it is the band between 800 hours and 1.225 hours. It gives no right to the ordinary self-employed person's deduction, the starter's deduction or the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Pregnancy (zwangerschap), 16 weeks count as hours worked (gewerkte uren)

If the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, the work counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the maternity and confinement leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees (art. 3.6 lid 5) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks, and the FISIN 2023 handbook repeats that 16-week rule ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with related persons (verbonden personen)

Hours for a business that is part of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with related persons (verbonden persoon) do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between unrelated persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a related person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not (art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Related persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a related person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) as the example for (b) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) (€ 5.030)

The self-employed person's deduction applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (art. 3.76 lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below state pension age (AOW age) at the start of the year, which is € 5.030 for 2023. At state pension age at the start of the year the deduction is 50% of the amounts under lid 2 and lid 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too (lid 4). The deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction (lid 5). The amount cut off by this cap is the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count. The tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar) (lid 6). It is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first (lid 7). The offset is also set by decision open to objection (lid 8) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).

The self-employed person's deduction is one of the five parts of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) in art. 3.74, so it is deducted from the profit before the SME profit exemption is calculated ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age (AOW age) (€ 2.515)

At state pension age at the start of the calendar year the self-employed person's deduction is 50% of the normal amount, so € 2.515 instead of € 5.030 for 2023, and the starter's deduction is halved as well, to € 1.062 instead of € 2.123 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The halving follows from art. 3.76 lid 4, which refers to the amounts under lid 2 and lid 3 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek) (€ 2.123)

The self-employed person's deduction is raised by the starter's deduction of € 2.123 if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period (art. 3.76 lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (tax-neutral return from a private limited company (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv)) if that business started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)).

The starter's deduction is not a separate item in the art. 3.74 list of the entrepreneur's allowance. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Starter's deduction at state pension age (AOW age) (€ 1.062)

At state pension age at the start of the year the starter's deduction is € 1.062, which is 50% of € 2.123 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The halving of the starter's deduction follows from art. 3.76 lid 4 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Starter's deduction, the earlier-years condition (voorwaarde over eerdere jaren)

The condition is a combined lookback: the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the self-employed person's deduction was applied at most twice in that period (art. 3.76 lid 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which means the same as the statute, and FISIN 2023 states the same prior-years condition ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). For 2023 the five preceding calendar years are 2018 to 2022. A year in which the profit cap set the self-employed person's deduction to nil still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for this count ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

This part of the entrepreneur's allowance applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached state pension age at the start of the year (art. 3.78a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 that started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), and a periodic benefit from a disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The text and the amounts are identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Research and development work deduction (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) (€ 14.202)

The research and development work deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, S&O) is a part of the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.74) and is set in art. 3.77. For 2023 the amount is € 14.202 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration FISIN 2023 handbook agrees with that amount ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The amounts are indexed per year, so every year has its own base amount. The deduction is a base amount plus a possible starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### S&O starter's increase (startersverhoging) (€ 7.106)

For 2023 the base amount of € 14.202 can be raised by the S&O starter increase of € 7.106 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The starter increase uses the same lookback structure as the starter's deduction: the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### S&O deduction, the conditions (voorwaarden)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration FISIN handbook adds three conditions to the S&O deduction: it requires an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion ([Belastingdienst FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The S&O declaration is the document from RVO that must be held before the deduction can be claimed, and the 500 hours are hours of S&O work, next to the 1.225 hours of the hours criterion for the business as a whole.

### SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (14%)

The SME profit exemption is a percentage of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.79a) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 the percentage is 14%. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (art. 3.2), so the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. No hours criterion is needed, because the statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). There is no exemption (vrijstelling) on profit as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde). Such persons also get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner or discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) on their co-entitlement profit ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)).

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay (art. 3.78) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. This settles the boundary hours: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 hours in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Partner remuneration (partnerbeloning) under € 5.000

Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) from profit (art. 3.16 lid 4) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). This interacts directly with the deduction for assisting partner, because the partner then counts as an unpaid partner for the bands above.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (€ 3.630)

The discontinuation deduction applies to profit made with or on the cessation (staking) of one or more whole businesses (art. 3.79) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving, tax-deferred transfer) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That part was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later discontinuation, and that there is no deduction on profit as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek)).

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), no more additions (toevoegingen) from 2023

Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition to the old-age reserve (toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve) is possible from 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force (in werking) for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last financial year (boekjaar) not coinciding with the calendar year (broken book year, gebroken boekjaar) that started before 1 January 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The reduction (afneming) rules are: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity (lijfrente) premiums deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end if the business or part of it ceased (staking) in the year, state pension age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (community of property, death, transfer to partner), and lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve. Lid 10 and 11 state that for the contribution base (premiegrondslag) in 2023 and 2024 the profit is taken before addition to the reserve, and that the annual allowance (jaarruimte) for 2023 and 2024 is reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Old-age reserve in a financial year not coinciding with the calendar year (broken book year) ending in 2023

A knowledge group (kennisgroep) decision of the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, KG:212:2023:2 of 9 May 2023, answers five cases about a broken book year with the answer per case whether adding (doteren) to the fiscal old-age reserve (fiscale oudedagsreserve) in 2023 is possible ([Kennisgroepen Belastingdienst](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220232-doteren-aan-de-fiscale-oudedagsreserve-bij-een-gebroken-boekjaar-eindigend-in-belastingjaar-2023/)). An addition is possible over a broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 and ends in 2023, which matches art. 10a.29 lid 1 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Old-age reserve, annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) and the six-month period (zesmaandstermijn)

Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year. Lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). So a premium paid between 1 January 2024 and 1 July 2024 can still be chosen for 2023, and the choice is made in the return for 2023.

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (12,57% above € 73.031)

If deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2023 that percentage is 12,57% and the start of the top bracket is € 73.031. The effect is that the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption are worth less than the top rate for a taxpayer whose income is in or above the top bracket.

### Maximum value of the deductions (aftrekken) (36,93%)

The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage of 12,57%, so 36,93% ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). That rate equals the second-bracket rate. Practically, the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption reduce the tax by at most 36,93% of the amount deducted ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
