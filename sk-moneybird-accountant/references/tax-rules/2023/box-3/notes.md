## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- System: the old band method with rendementsklassen I and II in art. 5.2 lid 1 applies to 2020 to 2022. From 1 January 2023 the Overbruggingswet box 3 applies the category method to everyone. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen
- Box 3 tarief: 31% in 2022 to 32% in 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 50.650 per person and € 101.300 with fiscal partner in 2022 to € 57.000 per person and € 114.000 with fiscal partner in 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.200 per person and € 6.400 with whole-year partner in 2022 to € 3.400 per person and € 6.800 with whole-year partner in 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01
- Category percentages: 2022 was bank 0,00%, other 5,53%, debts 2,28% to 2023 bank 0,92%, other 6,17%, debts 2,46%. The published law text printed 0,01% / 5,69% / 2,46%, replaced for 2023 under art. 10.6ter. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 61.215 and € 122.430 with whole-year partner in 2022 to € 65.072 and € 130.144 with whole-year partner in 2023. The art. 5.13 lid 2 order rule (first green overige bezittingen, then green banktegoeden) is now in the statute from the 2023 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% in 2022 and 0,7% in 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- New in 2023: peildatumarbitrage, art. 5.24, with a three-month window around 1 January and a business-reasons exception. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- New in 2023: no negative voordeel. Art. 4.14 lid 1 floors the forfaitaire voordeel at nil and art. 5.2 sets a negative rendement at nil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- Tegenbewijsregeling: statutory effect from 1 January 2023 for 2023, and 1 January 2017 inside the Wet rechtsherstel. The law itself is in force from 19 July 2025. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- Most favourable method: for 2021 and 2022 the Belastingdienst compares the old method and the current method, also after rechtsherstel for 2017 to 2022. That comparison is a 2021 and 2022 rule, not a 2023 rule. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen
- Reporting route unchanged for 2023: the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, and the return may always be filed without a letter for 2021 to 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The box 3 calculation uses the values on 1 January 2023. Step 1 takes banktegoeden x 0,92%, overige bezittingen x 6,17% and (schulden minus drempel) x 2,46%. Step 2 takes bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel). Keep the 1 January 2023 balance statements, the bank and investment overviews, the debt statements and the WOZ or valuation records with the records of the year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- The Belastingdienst computes the deductible part of the debts in the return, so the drempel of € 3.400 per person (€ 6.800 with a whole-year partner) does not need to be entered manually. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Box 3 assets and debts land on the income tax return (IB/PVV) for 2023, not on the business profit. The box 3 result is separate from the winst uit onderneming of the sole trader. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- When partners divide the grondslag sparen en beleggen, the two parts must total 100%. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- A partner rule for the joint calculation stands in art. 5.2 lid 5. For 2023 the allowance doubles with a fiscal partner. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- For green investments, record the value on 1 January 2023 and keep the statements that show the green status. The exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden, and the exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. The korting of 0,7% of the exempt amount is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- If the actual return is lower than the forfaitaire voordeel, the taxpayer can make that plausible. For 2023 the route is the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, and it may be filed without a letter. The form comes from the inspecteur and further documents can follow up to six weeks after the form is sent. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- For the actual return, keep the records of income and value changes over the calendar year. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- For a BV, the BV's own balance sheet does not carry box 3. Box 3 applies to the person who holds the assets or debts. Shares in a BV are overige bezittingen at 6,17% for a holder in box 3, unless the substantial-interest rules of box 2 apply. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023
- The three-month window around 1 January 2023 can catch transactions around the peildatum. Transactions within "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum" are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- No Moneybird menu paths are given here. The tax return and the actual-return form are filed with the Belastingdienst. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The 2026 calculation page uses 3 decimals for the aandeel, which differs from the 2023 and 2025 pages that use 2 decimals. This does not affect 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- The 2027 and 2028 texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028". For 2023 this changes nothing. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- For the tegenbewijsregeling inside the Wet rechtsherstel, art. IV works back to 1 January 2017, and the law itself entered into force on 19 July 2025. For 2023 the statutory effect runs from 1 January 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- The Hoge Raad of 6 June 2024 held that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. For 2023, the effect on the return is via the tegenbewijsregeling route. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- No 2023 open point is in the checked material.
