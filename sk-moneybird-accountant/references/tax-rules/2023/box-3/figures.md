## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers box 3 (savings and investments, sparen en beleggen) for the 2023 income tax year of a sole trader and for anyone who also owns a private limited company (BV). For 2023 the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3) replaced the old band method with a category method for everyone, with three category percentages, a rate of 32% and a tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) of € 57.000 per person. 2023 is a fully enacted year. The rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling), which lets a taxpayer prove a lower actual return, takes statutory effect from 1 January 2023, but the law that introduced it entered into force on 19 July 2025.
