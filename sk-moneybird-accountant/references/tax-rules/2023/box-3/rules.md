## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### System

2023 is the first year of the category method (categorieënmethode) of the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3). 2020 to 2022 used the old band method with return classes I and II (rendementsklassen I and II) in art. 5.2 lid 1, from 1 January 2023 the category method applies to everyone. Art. II of the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, states "Deze wet treedt in werking met ingang van 1 januari 2023". https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement The Actual Return Box 3 Act (Wet werkelijk rendement box 3) (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box

For 2021 and 2022 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes box 3 income under the old method and the current method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment. "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe." https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen That most-favourable comparison is a 2021 and 2022 rule. The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) keeps covering 2017 to 2022, not 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html

Background. Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) to the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM": the flat-rate system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Flat rates (forfaits) are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The explanatory memorandum (MvT) stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on the actual return for the restoration of rights but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html

### Box 3 rate (tarief)

The box 3 rate for 2023 is 32%, enacted. It applies to the benefit (voordeel) from savings and investments (sparen en beleggen) after the six-step calculation below. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

### Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)

The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is € 57.000 per person for 2023, and € 114.000 with a fiscal partner (fiscaal partner). The allowance doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint tax base (grondslag) allocated under art. 2.17. The 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint return base (rendementsgrondslag) and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

The tax-free allowance is subtracted in step 3 of the six-step calculation, after the return base is reduced by the deductible part of the debts. It is not used in the rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling), because "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

Only the part of total debts above the debt threshold (schuldendrempel) is deductible, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes it in the return. Statute: art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

For 2023 the threshold (drempel) is € 3.400 per person and € 6.800 with a whole-year partner. The debt threshold doubles for whole-year partners and a joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel

### Category percentages

Bank balances (banktegoeden) 0,92%, other assets (overige bezittingen) 6,17%, debts 2,46%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

The fixed category percentages are set under art. 10.6ter. The bank percentage (lid 2) is set after the year, with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January, at "de door twaalf te delen som" of the Dutch Central Bank (DNB, De Nederlandsche Bank) average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. So it is an average (sum divided by twelve), not a twelve-month sum. The debt percentage (lid 4) uses the same method on the Dutch Central Bank average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The other-assets percentage (lid 3) is replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

The law as published printed 0,01% / 5,69% / 2,46% in art. 5.2 lid 2. These were replaced for 2023 by the figures in the 2023 row above. So the 1 January 2023 text of the statute printed 0,01% bank, replaced after the year under art. 10.6ter. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

Category percentage series 2017 to 2025, as savings (spaargeld) / investments and other assets (beleggingen en andere bezittingen) / debts (schulden): 2017 0,25 / 5,39 / 3,43, 2018 0,12 / 5,38 / 3,20, 2019 0,08 / 5,59 / 3,00, 2020 0,04 / 5,28 / 2,74, 2021 0,01 / 5,69 / 2,46, 2022 0,00 / 5,53 / 2,28, 2023 0,92 / 6,17 / 2,46, 2024 1,44 / 6,04 / 2,61, 2025 1,37 / 5,88 / 2,70 (all %). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Share (aandeel) rounding

For 2023 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration rounding instruction for step 4 is "Rond af op 2 decimalen achter de komma". The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year: 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages, 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

### Six-step category calculation

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration method runs in six steps. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023

1. Taxable return (belastbaar rendement) = bank balances x bank percentage plus other assets x other percentage minus (debts minus threshold) x debt percentage.
2. Return base (rendementsgrondslag) = assets minus (debts minus threshold).
3. Base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen) = return base minus tax-free allowance. Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%.
4. Share (aandeel) = base / return base x 100, rounded to 2 decimals for 2023.
5. Benefit (voordeel) = taxable return x share.
6. Tax = rate x benefit.

Interactions. This six-step method replaced the band calculation of 2020 to 2022. The full list of category percentages shows the sequence from 2017 to 2025 and confirms the 2023 row. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

Art. 5.13: green investments are not counted as assets (bezittingen) up to the stated amount, doubled for whole-year partners. For 2023 that amount is € 65.072, or € 130.144 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 lid 2 (from the 2023 text): the exemption applies first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Credit for green investments (korting voor groene beleggingen)

Art. 8.19 gives a green investment credit (korting voor groene beleggingen) of 0,7% (2020 to 2024) of the exempt amount on the reference date (peildatum). It is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 For 2023 the rate is 0,7%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Rebuttal scheme start

The rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling) applies from 1 January 2023. Art. V: arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023, art. IV to 1 January 2017. Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit (voordeel), the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. The law itself, Stb. 2025, 195, entered into force (in werking trad) on 19 July 2025 by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Form and six-week rule

Art. 5.25 lid 6: a form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur), in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Actual-return reporting 2023

For 2020 to 2024 the actual return is reported on the online form in My Tax Administration portal (Mijn Belastingdienst) ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07, one form per call, and several forms via the Tax Information Line (BelastingTelefoon) 0800 - 0543. For 2021 to 2024 it may always be filed without a letter. From 2025 it is reported in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). No deadline is stated on these pages. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte

Content rules. Income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. Costs are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. No tax-free allowance applies. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen

### Reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage) rule (art. 5.24)

New art. 5.24 (reference date arbitrage): transactions around 1 January within "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum" are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

### Floor on a negative return

Art. 4.14 lid 1 is amended so the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) is at least nil. In art. 5.2 a negative return (rendement) is set at nil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) after a 2023 recalculation

Art. III: personal deductions after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024 follow the partners' chosen split. A different split goes by an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) request. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Restoration of Rights Act scope

The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act keeps covering 2017 to 2022. Art. 1 lid 2: applies to IB/PVV assessments for 2017, 2018, 2019 or 2020 not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021, and to the assessments for 2021 and 2022. So it does not cover 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html
