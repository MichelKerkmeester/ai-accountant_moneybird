# BTW 2023

**Rule:** These values hold for tax year 2023 only. Respect the Status and Check tags. This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers Dutch VAT (omzetbelasting, or BTW) for a sole trader, including general VAT rules that also matter to a BV. The main practical points are choosing the correct rate, keeping taxable and exempt turnover separate, and reporting VAT in the right return and period. The core rules for 2023 are enacted. A KOR reform published during 2023 was scheduled to start in 2025, so the existing KOR rules still govern 2023. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [2023 KOR reform](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard rate

The standard rate is 21%. Apply it where the supply is not covered by a reduced rate, zero rate, or exemption. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services listed in Tabel I. The current Belastingdienst pages list food, water, flowers and plants, medicines and aids, art, collectibles and antiques, books and periodicals. They also list services such as bicycle, shoe, leather-goods, clothing and household-linen repairs, hairdressing, certain work on homes, camping, cultural and recreational access, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, qualifying electronic publications, and certain news websites and apps. The pages also identify related services such as supplying goods, selling food in hospitality, renting or repairing medical aids, lending books, and an artist lending their own art. The pages do not state a year, so check the applicable 2023 Tabel I text for a borderline category. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [9% goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw) · [9% services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to goods and services in Tabel II when the applicable conditions are met. A zero-rated supply is still a taxable supply, so input VAT can generally remain deductible. An exempt supply does not carry that deduction right. [Article 9 of the 2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [Belastingdienst guidance on the ICP return](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

For an intra-EU supply of goods, retain evidence that the goods went to another EU country, verify that the buyer has a valid VAT ID from another member state, and file the ICP return correctly and on time. A late or incorrect ICP return can put the zero rate at risk. [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Logies

Logies was subject to the 9% rate in 2023. The later legislation preserved the reduced rate for 2023 while changing the treatment of logies from a later date. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Exemptions

Article 11 exemptions cover specified supplies and services, including certain dealings with immovable property, care, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money and securities, fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal services, public broadcasting, education, certain lectures, services by composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods, organs and blood, member services, cost-sharing groups, fundraising, and childcare. The Belastingdienst also lists investment gold and canteens and says exemptions apply to imports. Confirm the conditions for the specific exemption. A business making exempt supplies cannot deduct input VAT attributable to those supplies. [Article 11 of the VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) · [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Solar panels on or near homes

From 1 January 2023, the supply and installation of qualifying solar panels on or near homes is subject to the zero rate. Covered items include panels, cables, PV distributors, mounting materials, optimizers and inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The zero rate does not cover, among other things, multifunction panels, solar heat collectors, panels on non-residential business or public buildings, roof reinforcement, cable milling, replacement of the entire group cabinet, smart meters, bird or snow guards, separately purchased replacement inverters, heat pumps, or batteries. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home are taxed at 21% when the home is supplied too. If only the panels are supplied and installed, the panels qualify for the zero rate. The June decision applies from the start of 2023. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html) · [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen)

The decision gives a VAT forfait for solar-panel owners. The checked material specifies the first and last capacity bands, but not the intermediate bands. Do not infer an intermediate amount from the endpoints. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### KOR ceiling and turnover test

The 2023 KOR allowed an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands, or with a fixed establishment there, to choose exemption if Dutch turnover in the calendar year was no more than € 20.000. In 2023 the statutory test referred to turnover in the calendar year itself. The prior-year test belongs to the later KOR legislation. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

For the turnover calculation, the checked guidance includes Dutch-taxed supplies at any rate, including zero-rated supplies, domestic reverse-charged supplies, and certain exempt supplies relating to immovable property, means of payment, securities, and financial or insurance services. Other exempt turnover does not count. Private-use VAT, sales of business investment goods, purchases reverse-charged to the business, supplies taxed abroad, and intra-community acquisitions are excluded. Before joining, measure relevant turnover excluding VAT. During KOR participation, use the total amount charged. The KOR applies across the entrepreneur’s sub-numbers. [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Current KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)

A KOR participant does not charge VAT on invoices and cannot deduct input VAT. Exceeding the ceiling removes the exemption from the supply that crosses the ceiling onward. The old rules also restricted re-entry after revocation or an overrun. Legal persons could also join under the checked 2020 handbook. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR notice, minimum participation, and re-entry

Under the 2023 rules, notify the tax office at least 4 weeks before the intended tax period. Participation had to last at least 3 years. After voluntary revocation, re-entry was restricted for 3 years. After exceeding the turnover ceiling, re-entry was also restricted for 3 years. If the ceiling is crossed, tax applies from the crossing supply onward. [2022 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01) · [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01)

### KOR registration threshold without notice

An entrepreneur not yet registered for VAT could apply for the KOR without the usual notice if annual turnover was no more than € 1.800 and the decision’s other conditions were met. From 2025, the statutory threshold changed. That later threshold does not replace the 2023 amount. [2021 decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### KOR reform published in 2023

The 2023 reform was adopted but was not in force for the 2023 tax year. It was scheduled to start on 1 January 2025, with transition provisions from 1 October 2024. The amended regime tests the prior calendar year as well as the year of participation and changes other eligibility and notification rules. Apply the old Article 25 rules to 2023. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### VAT return period

Quarterly filing is the usual period. An entrepreneur can ask by letter for monthly or yearly filing. The tax office replies within 6 weeks and a change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a monthly period being imposed. [Changing a VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

A yearly return is available only if all applicable conditions are met. These include a yearly VAT amount below the relevant limit, no vergunning artikel 23, the required legal form, and limits on each of the specified intra-EU activities. The checked handbooks for 2020 and 2026 state these conditions, but the 2023 limit and its application were not confirmed in a 2023 publication. Do not assume eligibility based only on a current return-period page. [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook for businesses 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf) · [Changing a VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

### Period return and payment deadline

A period return must be filed within the term set by the inspector, which must be at least 1 month after the end of the period. Payment is due within 1 month after the end of that period. The Belastingdienst says monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the month following the return period. Filing and payment share a date. An extension is available only in a calamity. [General State Taxes Act, article 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01) · [General State Taxes Act, article 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01) · [VAT return and payment guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Annual VAT return deadline

The yearly return is due before 1 April of the following year, meaning by 31 March. Filing and payment use the same date. [VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

The checked material did not locate a published table of exact 2023 VAT filing dates. The general deadline rules above are supported, but the dated table remains unverified. [Belastingdienst due-date page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Suppletie timing and correction period

Use a suppletie to correct earlier VAT returns for the past 5 calendar years. The 2023 rule required correction as soon as possible, in the way specified by the inspector, before the taxpayer knew or should have suspected that the inspector already knew. The later 8-week rule does not govern 2023. [UBOB 2020, article 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01) · [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html)

### Suppletie amount boundary

The checked material does not establish the 2023 amount at which a correction could be made in the next return rather than by a separate suppletie. Keep the correction rule separate from later guidance until a 2023 source is confirmed. [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

### Reverse charge

The reverse-charge mechanism shifts the VAT reporting obligation from supplier to customer in specified cases. It applies, for example, to certain construction subcontracting and staff lending, waste and scrap, certain property transactions, gold, emission rights, gas and electricity certificates, and specified electronics. For mobile phones, chips before end-product, game consoles, tablets, and laptops, the threshold is measured per delivery and per type of good. The supplier still remains responsible for correct invoicing. An invoice must state “btw verlegd,” the customer’s VAT ID, and the consideration per rate. The customer reports domestic reverse charges in rubriek 2 and foreign reverse charges in rubriek 4, and may deduct the VAT when the purchase is used for taxable activity. [VAT Act, article 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01) · [UBOB, articles 24b to 24bb](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01) · [When to reverse charge](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/) · [How reverse charge works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

For subcontracting, exceptions include work done mostly in the subcontractor’s own workshop, staff lent for that kind of work, and subordinate work on goods sold. Disputes are addressed to the main contractor’s tax office. [Subcontracting exceptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/bij_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen/uitzonderingen_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen)

### EU distance sales and OSS

The EU-wide distance-sales threshold applies only to a supplier established in one member state. It covers qualifying goods sent to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country where the supplier arranged or paid for transport, and relevant digital services. Margin goods, nearly new means of transport, and assembly supplies are excluded from the described goods rule. Measure the threshold excluding VAT. If turnover stays below it, the supplier may opt to use foreign VAT, with the choice lasting at least 2 years. Once the threshold is exceeded, foreign VAT applies from the supply that crosses it. Dutch VAT resumes from 1 January of the year after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The Union scheme is optional and allows one quarterly return for EU distance sales. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through that return. A fixed establishment in another EU country removes the threshold. [Distance-sales and OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren) · [EU VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)

### OSS deadlines and retention

The Union scheme uses a quarterly return in the month after the quarter, due by the last day of that month. A nil return is also required. It can generally be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For an earlier first supply, registration must arrive by the deadline shown in the table. Corrections go into a later return. Keep OSS records for the stated retention period. The import scheme is monthly and is limited to qualifying consignments. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### OSS inactivity claim

The checked source does not establish the claim that two years without supplies ends participation if returns are still being filed. Treat that claim as unverified. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### ICP reporting

Report intra-EU goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country, and call-off stock transfers in the ICP return. The total should match rubriek 3b for the same period. Goods belong in the period of the invoice date. Services belong in the period the service was supplied. Do not file an ICP return for a period with nothing to report. Correct errors in the next ICP return. Goods may be reported quarterly only if the goods threshold is met for the current and previous quarters in the table. The checked material does not establish the 2023 ICP filing deadline. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp) · [ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp) · [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

For B2B services supplied to a customer in another EU country, the service is generally taxed where the customer is established. Invoice without VAT, mark the invoice “btw verlegd,” check the customer’s VAT ID, and report the transaction in the VAT return and ICP. Some services are exceptions. The checked guidance excludes certain exempt or zero-rated services in the customer’s country, OSS services, property-related services, passenger transport, and admission to certain events from the ICP list. [EU B2B services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen) · [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Private use and the last return

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur’s family. It can involve mixed-use items such as a car, a business computer, or a building used partly privately, and goods withdrawn for exclusively private use. Staff private use can restrict deductions under the BUA. The last VAT return of the year settles private use, the business car, the taxable and exempt turnover ratio, and relevant personnel provisions, gifts, and business gifts above the BUA limit. It can also include utilities, fictitious services, and revisions of earlier deductions. Correct an already-filed last return by suppletie. [Private use guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik) · [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Business-car private use

If records do not establish actual private use, the forfait is based on the catalogue price including VAT and BPM. The 2,7% calculation is capped by the VAT deducted in the year plus the stated share of purchase VAT. Use 1,5% if no purchase VAT could be deducted, and after the specified period following the year the car first came into use. Commuting is private use. Reduce the amount proportionally for exempt use and calculate a part-year amount pro rata. Settle the amount in the last return of the year. [Private-use car decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04) · [Belastingdienst car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak)

### BUA and own contributions

The BUA restricts deduction for certain staff facilities, business gifts, and other gifts. It also concerns staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure, and private transport, subject to exceptions. The decision provides carve-outs for specified staff food, licensed commuting bus transport, qualifying commuting bicycles, and outplacement. It also provides that costs for a person within the per-person limit are outside the exclusion. The 2023 decision ended the Belastingdienst website explanation that let an own contribution reduce the tested amount from the following year. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23) · [2023 decision on own contributions](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### Invoicing

Issue an invoice for supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. An invoice is generally not needed for a private customer, subject to exceptions. Issue the invoice by the 15th day of the month after delivery. The invoice must include the parties’ names and addresses, the required VAT ID and registration details, invoice date, unique consecutive number, description and quantity or scope, supply or prepayment date, amounts excluding VAT by rate, unit prices, rate, and VAT amount. A simplified invoice is allowed only within the stated ceiling or for a correction invoice and has its own content rules. Digital invoicing requires customer agreement and assurance of authenticity, content, and readability. The kasstelsel is compulsory for specified trades and can apply on request where the stated share of turnover is to consumers. [Invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/) · [Invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen) · [Simplified invoices](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur) · [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Electronic invoices

The checked source states that suppliers to central government must send e-invoices and that municipalities, provinces, and water boards may require them. It does not verify whether there was no general B2B e-invoicing duty in 2023. [Government e-invoicing guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### VAT rates and the difference between zero-rated and exempt supplies

**Calculated example.** Assume an invented net sale of € 100,00.

1. At 21%, output VAT is € 100,00 × 21% = € 21,00. The total invoice is € 100,00 + € 21,00 = € 121,00.
2. At 9%, output VAT is € 100,00 × 9% = € 9,00. The total invoice is € 100,00 + € 9,00 = € 109,00.
3. At 0%, output VAT is € 100,00 × 0% = € 0,00. The total is € 100,00.
4. If the purchase VAT attributable to a zero-rated supply is € 21,00, it can generally remain deductible. If the same input is attributable to an exempt supply, the deduction is not available.

The example uses the rates in Article 9. Whether a supply qualifies for a rate or exemption depends on its legal category and conditions. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [Exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Solar-panel official examples

**Official example.** The 2023 decision gives an example for loose panels rated 2.800 Wp. VAT on the invoice is € 800,00. Subtract the € 60,00 forfait: € 800,00 - € 60,00 = € 740,00 refund. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

**Official example.** The decision also gives an example for integrated panels rated 2.800 Wp. VAT on the invoice is € 1.500,00. One third is deductible: € 1.500,00 ÷ 3 = € 500,00. Subtract the € 20,00 forfait: € 500,00 - € 20,00 = € 480,00 refund. The decision does not specify the year for these examples. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### KOR ceiling

**Calculated example.** Assume invented Dutch turnover of € 18.000,00 before an additional supply.

1. Add a supply of € 2.000,00: € 18.000,00 + € 2.000,00 = € 20.000,00. The total is at the ceiling.
2. Instead add a supply of € 2.001,00: € 18.000,00 + € 2.001,00 = € 20.001,00. The ceiling is exceeded by € 1,00: € 20.001,00 - € 20.000,00 = € 1,00.
3. Under the 2023 rule, the supply that crosses the ceiling is taxable, and the exemption no longer applies from that supply onward.

The example assumes all amounts count toward KOR turnover under the relevant rules. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [KOR turnover conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)

### Return deadline

**Calculated example.** Assume a quarterly VAT period ends on 30 June.

1. The period ends on 30 June.
2. The following month is July.
3. The monthly and quarterly filing rule makes the deadline the last day of the following month. In this example, that is 31 July.

This demonstrates the general deadline rule. It does not establish a separate published 2023 date table. [General State Taxes Act, article 19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Reverse charge for specified electronics

**Calculated example.** Assume a qualifying delivery of 10 laptops at € 1.000,00 each.

1. Calculate the delivery total: 10 × € 1.000,00 = € 10.000,00.
2. The total reaches the specified reverse-charge threshold for that type of good, which is € 10.000,00 or more per delivery per type of good.
3. The supplier issues the invoice with "btw verlegd" and the buyer accounts for VAT under the applicable rules.

This example assumes the goods and parties meet the reverse-charge conditions. [UBOB, article 24ba](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### EU distance-sales threshold

**Calculated example.** Assume an eligible supplier has € 9.000,00 of relevant cross-border sales in the current calendar year and had not exceeded the threshold in the preceding calendar year.

1. Add € 1.000,00 of further relevant sales: € 9.000,00 + € 1.000,00 = € 10.000,00. This does not exceed the € 10.000,00 threshold because the rule allows a total not higher than € 10.000,00.
2. Add € 1,00 more: € 10.000,00 + € 1,00 = € 10.001,00. The threshold is exceeded by € 1,00: € 10.001,00 - € 10.000,00 = € 1,00.
3. Foreign VAT applies from the supply that crosses the threshold. The Union scheme can be used to report qualifying cross-border sales through a single return.

This example assumes the supplier and supplies are within the rule's scope. [Distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### OSS missed returns

**Calculated example.** Assume a Union-scheme participant misses three consecutive quarterly returns.

1. Count the missed returns in sequence: first, second, third.
2. The sequence reaches three consecutive missed quarterly returns.
3. The guidance says this can result in exclusion for 2 years.

The example applies the published sequence and consequence. [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### Business-car private use

**Calculated example.** Assume an invented catalogue price of € 20.000,00 and no records establishing actual private use. Assume the example year is within the first four years after the year the car was first used, so the 2,7% forfait applies.

1. Multiply the catalogue price by the 2,7% forfait: € 20.000,00 × 2,7% = € 540,00.
2. Calculate the cap for that period. Use invented deductible VAT of € 600,00 for the year and purchase VAT of € 1.000,00. One fifth of purchase VAT is € 1.000,00 ÷ 5 = € 200,00. The cap is € 600,00 + € 200,00 = € 800,00.
3. The forfait amount of € 540,00 is below the cap of € 800,00. The amount before any proportional adjustment is therefore € 540,00.
4. After the first four years, the 1/5 addition no longer applies. The cap is then the VAT deducted in the year, here € 600,00. The 1,5% forfait applies after that period.

The actual return amount can change for exempt use or a part-year period. [Private-use car decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

### BUA gifts and bicycle

**Calculated example.** Assume invented gifts to one person cost € 200,00 in the book year.

1. Compare the total with the € 227,00 limit: € 227,00 - € 200,00 = € 27,00. The total is € 27,00 below the limit, so the amount stays outside the BUA exclusion.
2. If the gifts instead total € 230,00, the total exceeds the limit by € 3,00: € 230,00 - € 227,00 = € 3,00. The exception does not apply to that total.

For the commuting-bicycle exception, an invented bicycle cost of € 700,00 including VAT is below the € 749,00 limit. The other statutory conditions still apply. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Invoice deadline

**Official example.** The handbook example says that goods delivered on 10 April should be invoiced by 15 May. The delivery date determines the following-month invoice deadline. [Handbook for businesses 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the rates used, exempt turnover, input VAT deductions, and any zero-rated intra-EU supply. For the zero rate, retain evidence of transport to another EU country, the buyer’s valid VAT ID, and a correct ICP filing. [ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- Reconcile the VAT return to the sales and purchase records for its period. Report domestic reverse-charge transactions in rubriek 2 and foreign reverse-charge transactions in rubriek 4. Deduct reverse-charged VAT only when the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)
- Report relevant intra-EU supplies and services in the VAT return and ICP. The ICP total should match rubriek 3b for the same period. Record goods by invoice period and services by service period. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp)
- If using OSS, submit the relevant quarterly return, including a nil return when required, and retain the OSS records for the stated retention period. Foreign input VAT is not reclaimed through the Union-scheme return. [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- If using the KOR, do not charge VAT on invoices and do not deduct input VAT. Keep records of Dutch turnover and monitor supplies that count toward the ceiling. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [KOR turnover conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)
- Settle private use, the business car, taxable and exempt turnover ratios, and relevant staff provisions and gifts in the last VAT return of the year. Use suppletie if that return has already been filed and needs correction. [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar) · [Suppletie rules](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01)
- Issue required invoices by the 15th day of the month after delivery. Record invoice number, supply date, rate, VAT amount, and the required customer and supplier details. [Invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Annual-return VAT limit:** less than € 1.883 is listed, but its application to 2023 is UNVERIFIED. The checked handbooks establish the amount for 2020 and 2026, not a 2023 publication. A 2023 Belastingdienst publication or dated handbook would settle this. [Return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw) · [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Annual-return EU conditions:** the checked handbooks state less than € 10.000 for each of four EU activities, but a 2023 publication was not checked. A 2023 dated handbook or official publication would settle year-specific application. [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Exact 2023 filing dates:** the published 2023 date table was not found. A dated 2023 Belastingdienst schedule would settle the exact dates. [Due-date page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
- **Suppletie amount boundary:** not established for 2023. The 2020 handbook says less than € 1.000 can be included in the next return, while 2026 guidance says € 1.000 or less. Those values do not settle the 2023 rule. A 2023 handbook or publication would settle it. [Handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf) · [Handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf) · [Correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)
- **ICP deadline:** not found for 2023. A dated 2023 ICP publication would settle it. [Digital ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- **B2B e-invoicing duty:** the checked source reports no general B2B duty but does not verify that absence for 2023. A 2023 Dutch rule or official publication would settle the point. [Government e-invoicing guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)
- **Reduced-rate category history:** the current Belastingdienst pages do not state a year. The 2023 rate structure is verified, but the exact 2023 history of each category on those pages needs dated Tabel I text for any borderline case. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [9% goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw) · [9% services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)
- **Solar forfait intermediate bands:** the checked material provides the first and last bands only. Use the dated 2023 decision schedule to confirm an intermediate band. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)
- **OSS inactivity:** the claim that 2 years without supplies ends participation while returns continue is UNVERIFIED. The checked OSS page does not confirm it. [OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- **KOR turnover-test conflict, resolved:** one baseline stated that both the year of application and the year before were tested. The 2023 statute tests turnover in a calendar year and does not name the preceding year. The prior-year test appears in the later 2025 law and the current KOR page. This later wording does not change the 2023 rule. [2023 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01) · [2025 VAT Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01) · [Current KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)
- **Annual-return deadline wording, resolved:** “before 1 April” and “by 31 March” agree. [VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/) · [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
