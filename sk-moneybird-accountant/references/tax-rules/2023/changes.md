# Changes tax year 2023

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2023 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This note lists what changed for tax year 2023. Section 2 holds the changes unique to 2023 and section 3 the generated table of every change by topic.

---

## 2. UNIQUE TO 2023

- The qualifying solar-panel zero rate began on 1 January 2023. [VAT (btw)](vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. [Entrepreneur's allowance](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 was the last year for an addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), because the articles lapsed on 1 January 2023 and from 2023 no addition is possible. [entrepreneur's allowance](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) rose from 9,5% above € 69.398 to 12,57% above € 73.031 and the maximum value of a deduction fell from 40% to 36,93%. [Income tax](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The old band method was replaced from 1 January 2023 by the category method of the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) box 3. [Box 3](box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023. [Costs and car](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) rose from € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. [Pension and disability insurance](pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023. [Zvw](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
