# Changes tax year 2023

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2023 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. Unique to 2023

- The qualifying solar-panel zero rate began on 1 January 2023. [btw](vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 6.310 in 2022 to € 5.030 in 2023. [ondernemersaftrek](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- 2022 was the last year for an addition to the oudedagsreserve, because the articles lapsed on 1 January 2023 and from 2023 no addition is possible. [ondernemersaftrek](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing rose from 9,5% above € 69.398 to 12,57% above € 73.031 and the maximum value of a deduction fell from 40% to 36,93%. [inkomstenbelasting](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The old band method was replaced from 1 January 2023 by the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3. [box-3](box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023. [kosten-en-auto](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum jaarruimte rose from € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. [pensioen-en-aov](pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,50% in 2022 to 5,43% in 2023. [zvw](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
