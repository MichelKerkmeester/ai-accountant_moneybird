## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Jaarruimte percentage: 13,3% in 2022 to 30% for 2023, with the text of 1 July 2023 and retroactive effect for the whole of 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Maximum jaarruimte: € 13.570 in 2022 to € 34.550 in 2023. The 2023 table of 9 January 2023 still printed € 15.317 for 2023, and that pre-Wtp amount was superseded retroactively. Sources: [Ministry 2023 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-acc574eb-1c51-43d6-a2ac-69b39bcb6d35/pdf), [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf).
- Look-back window: 7 years for 2022, jaarruimte 2015 to 2021, to 10 years for 2023, jaarruimte 2013 to 2022. Source: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Reserveringsruimte: 17% of the premiegrondslag with € 7.587 for the younger group and € 14.978 for the older group in 2022 to a flat € 38.000 in 2023. The 2023 text of 1 January 2023 still had € 8.065 or € 15.922, superseded retroactively. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age limit jaarruimte: not yet AOW age in 2022 to not yet the age five years above the AOW age for 2023, with the 1 January 2023 text still on the old wording. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag: € 114.866 in 2022 to € 128.810 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Franchise premiegrondslag: € 12.837 in 2022 to € 13.646 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Factor A multiplier: 6,27 in both years. What changed is the surrounding rule. Accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023 keeps the old lid 4 rule for later jaarruimte. Source: [art. 10a.25, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap: € 22.735 in 2022 to € 24.168 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.125, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.125, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap: € 63.288 in 2022 and € 63.288 in 2023, unchanged. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Stakingswinst maxima: tier a from € 480.686 to € 510.970, tier b from € 240.352 to € 255.495, tier c from € 120.183 to € 127.755. Sources: [art. 3.129, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.129, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- Afkoop kleine lijfrente: € 4.607 in 2022 to € 4.898 in 2023. Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid: € 43.012 in 2022 to € 45.722 in 2023. Sources: [art. 3.133, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.133, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01).
- FOR toevoeging: 9,44% of the winst, at most € 9.632, in 2022, the last year of a toevoeging, to none possible from 1 January 2023. An existing FOR stays under the old rules via art. 10a.29 lid 1. Sources: [art. 3.68, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction: art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 to art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023. The six-month payment option moves too, to art. 10a.29 lid 14. Sources: [art. 3.128, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Premiegrondslag part a: the 2020 to 1 January 2023 wording "winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek" changes to "winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek" from 1 July 2023, with art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 for FOR holders in 2023 and 2024. Sources: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Reduction in lid 1: the dotatie reduction for the oudedagsreserve under lid 1 and lid 4 sub b is gone from the text of 1 July 2023, which names only the pension accrual reduction. Source: [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Lijfrente deadline wording: FISIN 2022 uses ingangsdatum, FISIN 2023 uses einddatum. Nabestaanden deadline wording: 2 full calendar years in 2022 becomes the year of death plus 2 calendar years in 2023, with the same outcome. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Payment deadline for the conversion options: before 1 July 2023 for 2022 to before 1 July 2024 for 2023. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen: fully deductible, art. 3.124 lid 1 c, in both 2022 and 2023, unchanged. Source: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record AOV premiums for a policy with periodic uitkeringen as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen in box 1, and not as a business cost of the onderneming. They are fully deductible with no ruimte cap ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Do not deduct premiums for AOV policies that pay a lump sum. Keep those policies in the box 3 records, because the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Book a mandatory beroepspensioen premium for an ondernemer as a business cost with a restriction, and not as a lijfrentepremie ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book lijfrentepremies in the year in which they are paid or verrekend. For the stakingswinst conversion and the FOR conversion a payment before 1 July 2024 may on choice be counted in 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Keep the premium records and the ruimte calculation together. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible, they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep a per-year schedule of unused jaarruimte for 2013 to 2022, because the reserveringsruimte must be requested in the aangifte and the oldest unused year is used first ([art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Record an existing FOR under the old rules. No toevoeging can be made in 2023. Record the FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction under art. 10a.29 lid 12, up to the afname under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a ([art. 10a.29, text 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Keep the policy documents with the einddatum and the death date records for a nabestaandenlijfrente. The conversion or start must happen within 1 full calendar year, and the nabestaanden start deadline is the year of death plus 2 calendar years. An extension can be requested in bijzondere omstandigheden ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Periodic AOV benefits arrive as inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking with loonheffing withheld as voorheffing, so record them together with the withheld loonheffing ([Belastingdienst, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Maximum jaarruimte 2023, rounding. The Ministry table prints € 34.550. Plain arithmetic on the same figures gives 30% x (€ 128.810 minus € 13.646) = 34.549,2, so the printed figure sits slightly above the plain arithmetic outcome. This file follows the Ministry figure of € 34.550. A Belastingdienst calculation (hulpmiddel) would show the rounding the Belastingdienst uses. URLs: [Ministry 2024 table](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf) and [art. 3.127, text 1 July 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Two law texts for 2023. The text of art. 3.127 valid on 1 January 2023 still reads 13,3%, with a maximum jaarruimte of € 15.317, a reserveringsruimte of € 8.065 or € 15.922, a seven-year look-back and an age limit of not yet AOW age. The text valid from 1 July 2023 reads 30%, with € 34.550, € 38.000, ten years and AOW age plus five. The Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" and confirms the lijfrente widening applies retroactively from 1 January 2023, so this file follows the Wtp values for the whole of 2023. URLs: [art. 3.127, text 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127) and [Belastingdienst, nieuwe regels voor pensioenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).
- Proposed items that do not apply to 2023. The BAZ bill (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, 36 912) is proposed and had docket status "In afwachting nota nav verslag" on 2026-10-11. Bill art. 16 proposes a wachttijd of 104 weken, the MvT proposes a benefit of 70% of the grondslag with the grondslag at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so at most the minimumloon, and a premium set yearly by UWV estimated at about 5,4% of winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag. The MvT examples use a maximum grondslag of € 38.000 and about € 171 gross per month, and assume 1 January 2030 for entry into force by koninklijk besluit. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, and UWV benefits would carry loonheffing. Private policies keep the current tax treatment. The docket summary says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16. URLs: [dossier 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html), [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).
- Proposed Belastingplan 2027 items. Article LVII proposes no indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3 at the start of 2027 to 2032, so it would stay € 137.800, and a limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, for the lijfrente maxima. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11. URL: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Adjacent proposed track. The Zelfstandigenwet had an internetconsultatie until 29 October 2026, with a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets leading to a "veilige haven", and a beoogde inwerkingtreding of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension. URL: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
