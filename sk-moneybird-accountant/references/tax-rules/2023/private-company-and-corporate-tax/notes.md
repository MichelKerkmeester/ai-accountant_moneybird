## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Corporate income tax (Vpb) first bracket (eerste schijf): 2022 up to and including € 395.000 at 15% to 2023 up to and including € 200.000 at 19%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Corporate income tax above the bracket: 2022 € 59.250 plus 25,8% to 2023 € 38.000 plus 25,8%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Art. 31 apportionment applies to a book year straddling 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01
- Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) is unchanged from 2022. One year back (één jaar terug), unlimited forward, per year € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Art. 15b percentage is 20% in 2022 and 2023. The threshold is € 1.000.000 in both years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Innovation box (innovatiebox) fraction is 9/H in 2022 and 2023. The flat rate (forfait) is 25% with a maximum of € 25.000 in both years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Participation (deelneming) threshold is 5% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) ownership requirement (bezitseis) is at least 95% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag): 2022 € 48.000 to 2023 € 51.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Customary salary comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon): 2022 75% to 2023 100%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Customary salary minimum floor is € 5.000 in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- Start-up relief: in 2022 it was the last year of first use and could run to 2024. In 2023 it lapsed for new cases. A 2021 starter continues in 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw): 2022 5,50% to 2023 5,43%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): 2022 € 59.706 to 2023 € 66.956. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw: 2022 6,75% to 2023 6,68%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Box 2 rate is 26,9% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is 15% in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) is 25,8% on interest and royalties in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- Dividend stripping rule is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 in 2022 and 2023. The research had art. 4 lid 3 and unknown. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds: in 2022 the old values applied. For financial year (boekjaar) 2023 the old values apply by default, and the raised values are optional from 1 January 2023. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) is at most € 3.630 in 2022 and 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01
- Arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) 2023 allows up to 50% of qualifying new assets, with costs made in 2023 and use before 1 January 2026. The checked 2022 material does not state a 2022 figure, so no old-to-new comparison can be made from the checked sources. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023
- No more payment discount (betalingskorting) for paying a provisional corporate income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag Vpb) in one sum from 1 January 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023
- Customary salary escape: in 2022 still on the 75% basis with the € 48.000 floor, in 2023 the comparable wage itself. A settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made on the old basis keeps its 100% wage for its term. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Zvw route (zvw-route) for an uninsured director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA): corrected to the BV withholding the contribution on net wage (nettoloon), not an assessment (aanslag). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Dividend stripping article: corrected from art. 4 lid 3 to art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- The Decree on profit splitting (winstsplitsing), emergency repair (spoedreparatie) and Article 10a, Stcrt. 2022, 11028, is named as withdrawn by the 2024 Consolidated Decree (verzamelbesluit). https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) wage through the payroll records (loonadministratie). The BV withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). For an uninsured director and substantial shareholder, withhold the Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw) on net wage (nettoloon) and pay it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For an insured director and substantial shareholder (verzekerde dga), the BV pays the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
- Keep the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test in the records, including the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), the highest wage in connected bodies and the standard amount (normbedrag) of € 51.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01
- File the corporate income tax (Vpb) return for a calendar book year 2023 before 1 June 2024. Online extension to 1 November 2024 can be requested from 1 March to 1 June 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting
- Record a dividend decision and a dividend statement (dividendnota). File and pay dividend tax (dividendbelasting) within 1 month after the dividend is made available. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen
- For a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend), no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert
- For a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/
- Deposit the annual accounts (jaarrekening) within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/
- Keep records for seven years. Keep immovable property (onroerende zaken) records for nine years following the year use began. Keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01
- For the arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving), record the qualifying asset, the 2023 costs and the use before 1 January 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023
- For a tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting), register the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the Accompanying form (Geleideformulier). The request goes to the office that handles the income tax (inkomstenbelasting). The BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm
- The new legal form (rechtsvorm) may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer). Tell EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm
- Non-deductible interest under art. 15b carries forward. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025
- Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) follows the order in which losses arose. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01
- For 2023 the corporate income tax rate (vpb-tarief) is 19% up to € 200.000 and € 38.000 plus 25,8% above that amount. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for 2023.

The checked 2023 material records no UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown value for 2023. Two items are marked Corrected and are explained in section 3. The dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3 as the research said. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 The Zvw route (zvw-route) for an uninsured director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) is that the BV withholds the Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw) on net wage (nettoloon), not that the director and substantial shareholder pays it by assessment (aanslag). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf
