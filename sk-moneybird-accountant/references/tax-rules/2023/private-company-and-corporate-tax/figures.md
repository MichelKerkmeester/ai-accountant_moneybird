## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the Dutch corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) for a BV in 2023, plus the main related rules for a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder), dividend tax (dividendbelasting), the conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), the annual accounts (jaarrekening) and the switch from a sole trader to a BV. The 2023 rate is 19% up to a taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) of € 200.000 and € 38.000 plus 25,8% above that amount. The customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) comparable wage moves to 100% in 2023. The law for 2023 is enacted.
