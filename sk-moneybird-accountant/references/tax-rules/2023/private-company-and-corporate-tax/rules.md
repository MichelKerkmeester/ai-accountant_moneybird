## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Corporate income tax (Vpb) rate
The rate applies to a BV and other bodies (lichamen) subject to corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting). Article 22 sets the table. For 2023 the first bracket (eerste schijf) runs up to and including € 200.000 at 19%. For the part of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rates page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting The rate interacts with loss offsetting (verliesverrekening), the art. 15b interest deduction limitation (renteaftrekbeperking), the innovation box (innovatiebox) and the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling). The result goes on the corporate income tax return (aangifte vennootschapsbelasting).

### Art. 31 apportionment for a book year straddling 1 January 2023
This applies when a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect. For this file the relevant date is 1 January 2023. The tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 The 2022 material states the art. 31 apportionment applies to a book year straddling 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 Report the apportioned tax in the Corporate income tax return.

### Loss offsetting
This applies to a BV with a loss. From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and unlimited following years. Per year the set-off is only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. In 2020 and 2021 the rule was different, one preceding year and six following years, after the inspector fixes the loss by decision (beschikking). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 The limit interacts with the Corporate income tax rate (vpb-tarief) because only the remaining profit is taxed.

### Generic interest deduction limitation (Generieke renteaftrekbeperking) percentage
Article 15b lid 1 applies to a BV with a balance of interest (saldo aan renten). The balance is interest costs on loans minus interest income. The balance is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. For 2023 the percentage is 20%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 Non-deductible interest carries forward. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025

### Generic interest deduction limitation threshold
The threshold is € 1.000.000. The taxpayer compares 20% of the adjusted profit with € 1.000.000 and uses the higher amount. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01 If 20% is lower, € 1.000.000 applies. If 20% is higher, that higher amount applies.

### Innovation box
This applies to a taxpayer with advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset (kwalificerend immaterieel activum). The advantages count for 9/H, H being the highest art. 22 rate, so the effective rate (effectieve tarief) is 9% in 2023. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox A smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring). Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten, aanschafkosten) from S&O assets in the year and four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover (netto-omzet) over those five years below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2). Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Trademarks, logos and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb gives a K/T part, K the qualifying expenditure (kwalificerende uitgaven) multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. The art. 12bd flat rate (forfait) is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive and the taxpayer made a qualifying asset in the year or two preceding years and chooses it. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01

### Participation (Deelneming) threshold
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation out of the profit. A participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (nominaal gestort kapitaal) (art. 13 lid 2). This is identical in every text 2020 to 2026. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies with a 1/3 interest. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Fiscal unity (Fiscale eenheid) ownership requirement (bezitseis)
A fiscal unity requires the mother to hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights (statutaire stemrechten) and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The mother can be a bv, nv, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie) or a comparable foreign form. The daughter must be a bv or nv. Both must have the same book years and profit rules and be effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption (subjectieve vrijstelling) of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html

### Customary salary standard amount (normbedrag)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking) (100% from 2023), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the standard amount of € 51.000. The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 7 from 2023). The Payroll Tax Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage (loon) for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party (derde), and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) who hold shares. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Customary salary comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon)
The comparable wage is 100% from 2023. In 2020 to 2022 it was 75%. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 changed. From 2023, if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. In 2020 to 2022, if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) made plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage was 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when that was lower than the standard amount. A settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Customary salary minimum floor
The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This is lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01

### Start-up relief
Statute 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4): for a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) No 1407/2013. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the de-minimis condition did not apply to a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA), and an R&D declaration for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. The Payroll Tax Handbook 2025 states the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the handbook 2025: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked (gewerkte uren), and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw)
A director and substantial shareholder who is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) falls under the income-related contribution Zvw (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw). The BV withholds the contribution from the net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For 2023 the contribution is 5,43% and the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 66.956. The Payroll Tax Handbook 2025 gives 5,26% up to € 75.864 for 2025, but the 2023 FISIN page gives 5,43% and € 66.956. Corrected: the research said the uninsured director and substantial shareholder pays the contribution himself by assessment (aanslag). The handbook says the BV withholds it on net wage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the director and substantial shareholder pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding from net wage (inhouding op nettoloon) or by assessment. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/

### Maximum contribution income
The maximum contribution income for 2023 is € 66.956. The contribution Zvw and the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw are calculated up to this amount. Each FISIN page states that the wage (loon) of a director and substantial shareholder falls under the employer levy only if the director and substantial shareholder is insured for the employee insurance schemes. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

### Employer levy Zvw
For 2023 the employer levy Zvw is 6,68%. A director and substantial shareholder who is insured for the employee insurance schemes falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV. A director and substantial shareholder who is not insured pays no employer levy and instead the BV withholds the contribution Zvw. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

### Box 2 rate
The box 2 rate for 2023 is 26,9% flat. Box 2 applies to income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang). Individuals credit the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01

### Dividend tax
Dividend tax is 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst) (art. 5 Wet DB 1965). If the company bears the tax, the proceeds are multiplied by 100/85 (art. 6 lid 1). The shareholder is the taxpayer and the company withholds (art. 1). Once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due (art. 9 lid 1). The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return, bodies in the corporate income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

### Participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) and investment dividend (beleggingsdividend)
A participation dividend, a holding of 5% or more, to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption. No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. An investment dividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return, bodies in the corporate income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert

### Buy-back of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)
Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting)
The conditional withholding tax of the Conditional Withholding Tax Act (Wet bronbelasting 2021) applies in 2023 to interest and royalties. Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. With the 2023 top rate this is 25,8%. It hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie) where the profit tax is below 9% or which is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions (niet-coöperatieve jurisdicties), and abuse cases. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividend tax). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/

### Dividend stripping rule
The dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds
A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. For financial year (boekjaar) 2023 the default values are micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000, fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000, fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million and € 15 million, € 25 million and € 50 million, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts are unchanged. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html

### Annual accounts deposit
The board deposits the annual accounts within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. the government business portal (Ondernemersplein) states there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf). From financial year 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/ and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)
Without the tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) facility, a change of legal form (wijziging van rechtsvorm) is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays income tax (inkomstenbelasting) on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The discontinuation deduction of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630, once in a lifetime, and an extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. Hidden reserves (stille reserves) realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html

### Arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) 2023
For 2023 the arbitrary depreciation allows up to 50% of qualifying new assets, with costs made in 2023 and the asset in use before 1 January 2026. The facility excludes buildings, intangibles, assets mainly for rent and non-zero-emission passenger cars. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

### Corporate income tax return and extension
For a book year equal to the calendar year, file the Corporate income tax return before 1 June of the next year. For a broken book year, file within 5 months after its end. For a short book year ending 31 December, file before 1 June. For a short book year ending in another month, file before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting

### Record-keeping obligation (administratieplicht) and retention period (bewaartermijn)
Art. 52 AWR makes bodies required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires them to keep the records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement (resultatenrekening). Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 requires records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) to be kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 require OSS records to be kept for ten years after the year of the transaction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

### Tax-neutral conversion
Art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001 lets a sole trader convert to a BV on request without a cessation. The whole business must be contributed, the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for income tax, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree (Besluit), the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscal unity only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the request goes to the office that handles the income tax, the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) is registered with the form Geleideformulier, and the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm

### Decree on tax-neutral conversion 2025
The Decree on tax-neutral conversion is dated 24 October 2025, published in Government Gazette (Staatscourant) 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025, and in force (in werking) the day after publication. It replaces the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M. The decree lists changes at 2.2.2.a, 3.4, 4.2.2, 6.1, 8, 12, 13.1.2, 13.2 and 15.3. The consolidated version is BWBR0051693 and was not re-read. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html

### Request timing for art. 3.65
The inspector decides by decision open to objection (bezwaar) that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties (onderdeel 14.2 of the old Besluit). In the case the request can be filed until the 2022 income tax and national insurance contributions (IB/PVV) assessment is irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) is reported for 2021, then the 2021 assessment decides. The position (standpunt) was adopted 27-06-2022 and published 12-01-2023. https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/

### VAT (btw) for a BV
The new legal form is a new business for VAT and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which must go to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. A VAT fiscal unity (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968) arises by operation of law (van rechtswege) once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A decision, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability (hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid). Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands. The Policy Decree (beleidsbesluit) on VAT liability and VAT fiscal unity (belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, states it enters into force on 1 July 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html

### Director and substantial shareholder salary, payroll taxes (loonheffing), employee insurance schemes and Zvw
A director and substantial shareholder is employed by his own company (eigen vennootschap, in dienst van) for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes. A director and substantial shareholder who alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes. For him the company pays no premiums for the employee insurance schemes and no employer levy Zvw, and instead withholds the income-related contribution Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage and pays it through the payroll tax return. A director and substantial shareholder who is insured falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV.
