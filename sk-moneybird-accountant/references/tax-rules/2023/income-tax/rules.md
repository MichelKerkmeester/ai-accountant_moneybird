## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below state pension age (AOW age)

For a taxpayer below state pension age (AOW-leeftijd), the published table applies 36,93% up to € 73.031 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The statutory income-tax schedule separately shows 9,28% up to € 37.149, then € 3.447 plus 36,93% through € 73.031, then € 16.698 plus 49,50% above it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The 36,93% published rate includes the applicable national insurance premiums within the premium ceiling described below (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen).

### Box 1 full-year AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a person who is at AOW age for the full year and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the published rates are 19,03% up to € 37.149, 36,93% to € 73.031 and 49,50% above that amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The first rate is lower because the AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). The checked material treats the continuation of Anw and Wlz premiums at AOW age as an inference, not as a verified stated rule.

### Box 1 full-year AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

A person at AOW age for the full year and born before 1 January 1946 uses the higher first boundary of € 38.703. The published rates are 19,03% up to that boundary, then 36,93% to € 73.031 and 49,50% above it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10a). The statutory table amounts shown for this variant are € 3.591 and € 16.268 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10a).

### Box 1 in the AOW transition year

If a taxpayer reaches AOW age during 2023, the first-bracket rate depends on the month in which AOW age is reached. The applicable first-bracket rate runs from 19,03% for January to 35,43% for December, and the first bracket (eerste schijf) ends at € 37.149 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening). The second and third bracket rates remain the normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1).

### Income-tax and social insurance components

The first statutory band's income-tax part (belastingdeel) is 9,28%. The national insurance premium rate is 27,65%, made up of AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). The published combined rate in the first bracket adds the income-tax part and applicable premiums (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). At AOW age, the AOW premium stops. The checked material does not verify as a stated rule whether the Anw and Wlz parts continue at that age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### National insurance premiums (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling

The national insurance premiums are charged on contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to the ceiling of € 37.149. For people born before 1 January 1946, the ceiling is € 38.703 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen). The first statutory bracket boundary and premium ceiling therefore depend on the birth-date variant in the table.

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

When a taxpayer deducts deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), art. 2.10 lid 2 raises the tax calculated under lid 1 by 12,57% of the amount by which taxable Box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds € 73.031. The increase cannot exceed 12,57% of the deductions themselves (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). This makes deductions above the top bracket boundary worth at most 36,93%, calculated as 49,50% minus 12,57% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

The listed posts include personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), costs for an owner-occupied home (eigen woning), tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) and the exemption for making assets available (vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen) to another party (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The entrepreneur reliefs include the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), R&D deductions, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction on occupational disability (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) below state pension age (AOW age)

The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) is available to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) and can also be applied by an employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) through payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). Below state pension age (AOW age), the maximum is € 3.070. It is reduced by 6,095% of Box 1 taxable income above € 22.660 and reaches nil at € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).

For 2023, the phase-out base is taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning). The explanatory notes M form 2023 (Toelichting M-biljet 2023) calculation aid uses worldwide income in Box 1 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).

### General tax credit for a full year at state pension age (AOW age)

For a full year at state pension age, the maximum general tax credit is € 1.583. It is reduced by 3,141% of income above € 22.660 and reaches nil at € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The ordinary table does not establish the amount for someone who reaches state pension age during 2023. That transition-year point remains unverified below.

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) below state pension age (AOW age)

The employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) is based on earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an income-tax entrepreneur, earned income includes business profit before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). It excludes the part earned as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) or profit-sharing creditor (winstdelende schuldeiser) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

The credit builds through the first three bands, then falls by 6,510% from the fourth-band amount. It is nil above € 115.295 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The bands and starting amounts are in the figures table.

### Employment tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For a full year at state pension age, the employment tax credit uses the separate rates and amounts in the figures table. The explanatory notes M form 2023 confirm a maximum of € 2.604 and a top-band reduction of 3,355% above € 37.691, until the credit becomes nil above € 115.295 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The source conflict about the top band is resolved in section 7.

### Higher employment tax credit applied by an employer

If an employer applied a higher employment tax credit and earned income is not above € 37.691, the higher amount counts up to € 5.052. The corresponding limit is € 2.604 if state pension age was reached before 2023 (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf). The checked 2023 material does not provide additional conditions for this rule.

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting) below state pension age (AOW age)

The income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, or IACK) is 11,45% of earned income above € 5.547, up to a maximum of € 2.694 at € 29.076 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). It is claimed in the tax return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not through payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

The IACK generally requires a qualifying child under 12 on 1 January and that the child be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The child may be the taxpayer's own child, stepchild or adopted child, a spouse's child from an earlier relationship or a child of a fiscal partner (fiscaal partner). A foster child (pleegkind) qualifies only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a foster care allowance (pleegvergoeding) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The checked material also identifies residents of the EU, EEA, Switzerland or BES as potentially eligible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

The income and partner test is met if the taxpayer has no fiscal partner, has had one for 6 months or less, or has lower earned income than the partner. With equal incomes, only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). If the taxpayer or child dies before the 6-month period is complete, the credit is still allowed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

For co-parents (co-ouders), the 2023 page requires at least 78 days in a 6-month period, in a repeating pattern with each parent (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Income-related combination tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For a full year at state pension age, the IACK rate is 5,90%, with a maximum of € 1.389 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The child and partner conditions still apply as described above (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Tax credits (heffingskortingen) in the year state pension age is reached

The checked 2023 tax credit sources provide no rule or amount for the year in which state pension age is reached. The transition-year treatment of the general tax credit, employment tax credit and IACK is therefore unverified for 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Income-tax part (belastingdeel) and premium parts (premiedelen) of tax credits

Each tax credit has an income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance premium parts (premiedelen). For 2023 the income-tax part is 9,28%, and the premium components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). A taxpayer who is not insured for one of these schemes does not receive that part of the credit. For part-year premium liability, the premium part is time-proportional (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

### Payment of unused tax credits to a lower-earning partner

Payment of unused credits to a lower-earning partner is subject to conditions. The same fiscal partnership must have lasted more than 6 months, the partner must owe enough tax, and only the part that cannot be offset against the taxpayer's own tax and premiums can be paid (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen through https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).

From 2023, the employment tax credit (AK) and IACK can no longer be paid out to the partner. The general tax credit (AHK) payment ended for people born in 1963 or later. It remains possible for people born before 1 January 1963, who were not subject to the phase-out (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The 2023 page lists an own Box 1 income reference of € 10.705 and a partner income threshold of € 28.580, or € 32.480 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Flow of profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming)

Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) require that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and that the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide the tax classification. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (ondernemersrisico) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

First remove exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen). The checked material lists the forest and agricultural exemptions, the applicable debt waiver profit (kwijtscheldingswinst) exemption, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business termination payments (bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen), WAZ benefits (waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, and specified final levy (eindheffing), mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

The taxable business profit is then profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. The SME profit exemption is calculated on profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). A person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) receives neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

Taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) is income from work and home, including taxable business profit, reduced by losses that can be carried forward or offset (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). The Box 1 table applies to that balance. The rate adjustment may then increase the tax, and tax credits reduce the tax and premium amounts through their income-tax part and premium parts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen). The checked material says that the exact sequence of the final credit steps is an inference from the article structure, as recorded in section 7.

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction is € 5.030, or € 2.515 at state pension age (AOW age) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and reduces profit before the SME profit exemption is calculated (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). Detailed eligibility tests belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic.

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age (AOW age) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and is applied before the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The starter's deduction on occupational disability (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 and then € 0 under its staged scale. The deduction is capped at profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the applicable hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked material does not set out the hour bands here.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) is 14% of profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). It reduces the taxable business profit that enters Box 1. It is also included in the list of deductions subject to the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

No new old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) additions can be formed from 2023. An existing reserve may remain on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). It therefore does not create a new 2023 addition to the business-profit calculation.
