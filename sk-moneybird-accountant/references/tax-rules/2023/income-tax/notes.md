## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The below-state-pension-age published Box 1 rate changed from 37,07% up to € 69.398 to 36,93% up to € 73.031. The top rate remained 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- For a full year at state pension age and birth on or after 1 January 1946, the first rate changed from 19,17% up to € 35.472 to 19,03% up to € 37.149. The next rate changed from 37,07% to 36,93%, and its boundary moved from € 69.398 to € 73.031. The top rate remained 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening).
- For a full year at state pension age and birth before 1 January 1946, the first boundary changed from € 36.409 to € 38.703. The rates changed from 19,17%, 37,07% and 49,50% to 19,03%, 36,93% and 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10a).
- The transition-year first-bracket rates changed month by month: January 19,17% to 19,03%, February 20,66% to 20,52%, March 22,15% to 22,01%, April 23,64% to 23,50%, May 25,13% to 24,99%, June 26,62% to 26,48%, July 28,12% to 27,98%, August 29,81% to 29,47%, September 31,10% to 30,96%, October 32,59% to 32,45%, November 34,08% to 33,94%, and December 35,57% to 35,43% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening).
- The national insurance premium ceiling changed from € 35.472 to € 37.149, or from € 36.409 to € 38.703 for people born before 1 January 1946 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/premie_volksverzekeringen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen).
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) increased from 9,5% to 12,57%. The maximum value of deductions above the top boundary decreased from 40% to 36,93% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).
- The below-state-pension-age general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) changed from € 2.888, reduced by 6,007% above € 21.317 and nil from € 69.398, to € 3.070, reduced by 6,095% above € 22.660 and nil from € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).
- The full-year state-pension-age general tax credit changed from € 1.494, reduced by 3,106% above € 21.317 and nil from € 69.398, to € 1.583, reduced by 3,141% above € 22.660 and nil from € 73.031 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The below-state-pension-age employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) bands changed from up to € 10.350 at 4,541%, to € 22.356 at € 470 plus 28,461%, to € 36.649 at € 3.887 plus 2,610%, and to € 109.346 at € 4.260 minus 5,860%, to the 2023 bands in section 2 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).
- The full-year state-pension-age employment tax credit bands changed from up to € 10.350 at 2,348%, to € 22.356 at € 244 plus 14,718%, to € 36.649 at € 2.011 plus 1,349%, and to € 109.346 at € 2.204 minus 3,030%, to the 2023 bands in section 2. The corrected 2023 maximum is € 2.604 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).
- The IACK threshold changed from € 5.219 to € 5.547. The below-state-pension-age maximum changed from € 2.534 at € 27.350 to € 2.694 at € 29.076. The full-year state-pension-age rate changed from 5,911% to 5,90%, and its maximum changed from € 1.452 to € 1.389 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The qualifying child birth-date test changed from a child born after 31 December 2009 to a child born after 31 December 2010. The co-parent (co-ouder) test changed from at least 156 days in 2022 with each parent in a repeating pattern to at least 78 days in a 6-month period in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- Partner payment of unused credits changed from 93⅓% withheld and 6,667% paid to no employment tax credit (AK) or IACK payout from 2023. The general tax credit (AHK) payment ended for people born in 1963 or later, while it remains possible for people born before 1 January 1963 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The listed own-income reference for partner payment changed from € 8.885 for people born before 1963 or € 523 for later-born people to € 10.705. The listed partner threshold changed from € 23.700, or € 30.400 at AOW age, to € 28.580, or € 32.480 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The income-tax part (belastingdeel) changed from 9,42% to 9,28%, and the total first-bracket rate changed from 37,07% to 36,93% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) changed from € 6.310, or € 3.155 at state pension age, to € 5.030, or € 2.515 at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The last year for new old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) additions was 2022. The 2022 addition was 9,44% up to € 9.632, while no new additions could be formed from 2023 and an existing reserve could remain (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Track business profit, exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen), eligible entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) and any losses that affect taxable Box 1 income. These items determine the taxable business profit and taxable income described in section 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and art. 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Record whether the income qualifies as profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming). Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not determine that classification. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) considers the business relationship and risk factors listed in section 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).
- The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) and employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) may be applied through the return and payroll. The IACK is applied in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
- The checked sources for this topic do not state a filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) or a record-retention period.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **UNVERIFIED, tax credits (heffingskortingen) in the state pension transition year:** The checked 2023 sources give no rule or amount for the year state pension age is reached. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen would need to be supplemented with a 2023 transition-year rule or amount to settle this point.
- **UNVERIFIED, Anw and Wlz at state pension age:** The checked source states that the AOW premium stops at state pension age. The continuation of Anw and Wlz is inferred from the state-pension-age rates, not stated as a verified rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening).
- **Corrected conflict, general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) phase-out point (afbouwpunt):** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen prose once prints € 22.060, while its table and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf give € 22.660. Use € 22.660, supported by the table and the explanatory notes M form (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).
- **Corrected conflict, full-year state-pension-age employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) top band:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen prints € 2.374 minus 3,355% for the top band. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf identifies € 2.374 as the start of the preceding band and gives a top-band amount of € 2.604 minus 3,355%, with a maximum of € 2.604. Use the explanatory notes M form result (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf).
- **Resolved source conflict, end of partner payment:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen says the payment ends in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen say it ends from 2023. Use 2023, as confirmed by the later year pages and the 2023 table.
- **Inference, final order of the tax calculation (belastingberekening):** The checked material establishes the Box 1 balance, the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and the credit components. It does not state the full sequence of the final credit steps in one place. The order described in section 3 is an inference from the article structure and tax credit pages (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).
