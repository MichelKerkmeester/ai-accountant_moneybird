## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Business profit flowing into Box 1

**Calculated example.** Assume an income-tax entrepreneur qualifies for a € 5.030 self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) and has € 60.000 profit before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek). No other entrepreneur deductions, exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) or loss offsets apply.

1. Profit after the self-employed person's deduction: € 60.000 - € 5.030 = € 54.970.
2. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): € 54.970 × 14% = € 7.695,80.
3. Taxable business profit: € 54.970 - € 7.695,80 = € 47.274,20.
4. With no other Box 1 income or losses, taxable Box 1 income is € 47.274,20.

The rates and deduction values used are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling. The Box 1 treatment is described in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and art. 3.2.

### Box 1 below state pension age (AOW age)

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable Box 1 income of € 80.000 and a taxpayer below state pension age (AOW age).

1. Income through the first published boundary: € 73.031 × 36,93% = € 26.970,3483.
2. Income above that boundary: € 80.000 - € 73.031 = € 6.969.
3. Tax on that amount: € 6.969 × 49,50% = € 3.449,655.
4. Total using the published rates: € 26.970,3483 + € 3.449,655 = € 30.420,0033.

This uses the published Box 1 rates in https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening. The arithmetic is shown before any assessment rounding.

### Box 1 for a full year at state pension age

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable Box 1 income of € 40.000 and a person born on or after 1 January 1946 who is at state pension age for the full year.

1. First band: € 37.149 × 19,03% = € 7.069,4547.
2. Remaining income: € 40.000 - € 37.149 = € 2.851.
3. Second band tax: € 2.851 × 36,93% = € 1.052,8743.
4. Total using the published rates: € 7.069,4547 + € 1.052,8743 = € 8.122,329.

For a person born before 1 January 1946, the first boundary is € 38.703. The rates and boundaries are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening.

### State pension transition-year first rate

**Calculated example.** Assume a taxpayer reaches state pension age (AOW age) in September 2023. The first-bracket rate is 30,96% up to € 37.149. The second and third rates remain the normal rates. This is a table lookup from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening.

### National insurance premiums (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling

**Calculated example.** Assume a person below state pension age has € 40.000 contribution income (premie-inkomen). The premium ceiling is € 37.149.

1. Premium income within the ceiling: € 37.149.
2. Income above the ceiling: € 40.000 - € 37.149 = € 2.851.
3. That € 2.851 is above the stated premium ceiling.

The premium rate and ceiling are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen. This example only demonstrates the ceiling.

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable Box 1 income after a deduction is € 74.000 and the deduction is € 1.000.

1. Income before the deduction: € 74.000 + € 1.000 = € 75.000.
2. Amount above the top boundary: € 75.000 - € 73.031 = € 1.969.
3. 12,57% of € 1.969 is € 247,5033.
4. The cap is 12,57% of € 1.000, or € 125,70.
5. Use the lower amount, € 125,70, as the adjustment.
6. The deduction's maximum effective value is € 1.000 × 49,50% - € 125,70 = € 369,30, or 36,93%.

This uses the statutory rule in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2.

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume Box 1 taxable income of € 30.000 and a taxpayer below state pension age (AOW age).

1. Income above the phase-out point (afbouwpunt): € 30.000 - € 22.660 = € 7.340.
2. Reduction: € 7.340 × 6,095% = € 447,373.
3. Credit before assessment rounding: € 3.070 - € 447,373 = € 2.622,627.

For a full-year state-pension-age taxpayer at the same income:

1. Reduction: € 7.340 × 3,141% = € 230,5494.
2. Credit before assessment rounding: € 1.583 - € 230,5494 = € 1.352,4506.

The figures are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf.

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume earned income (arbeidsinkomen) of € 30.000 and a taxpayer below state pension age (AOW age).

1. The relevant band is above € 23.201 and up to € 37.691.
2. Amount above € 23.201: € 30.000 - € 23.201 = € 6.799.
3. Credit: € 4.605 + (€ 6.799 × 3,085%) = € 4.605 + € 209,74915 = € 4.814,74915.

For a full-year state-pension-age taxpayer with the same earned income:

1. The same band applies.
2. Credit: € 2.374 + (€ 6.799 × 1,589%) = € 2.374 + € 108,03611 = € 2.482,03611.

The band values are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf.

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume earned income of € 20.000 and that all IACK conditions are met.

1. Income above the threshold: € 20.000 - € 5.547 = € 14.453.
2. Below state pension age (AOW age): € 14.453 × 11,45% = € 1.654,8685.
3. For a full year at state pension age: € 14.453 × 5,90% = € 852,727.

Both results are below their applicable maximums. The rates and limits are from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen.

### Payment of unused general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) to a partner

**Official example.** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen gives an example in which an eligible taxpayer born before 1 January 1963 has no income, the partner owes enough tax, and € 3.070 can be paid out. The payment depends on the partner and offset conditions stated in section 3.
