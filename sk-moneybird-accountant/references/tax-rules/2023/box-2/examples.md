## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Example 1, Official example, loss converted into a belastingkorting ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang)).** FISIN 2023 gives a verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang of € 20.000 that arose in 2021, a belastingkorting of € 5.380 in 2023 and a remainder usable until 2030. Check with the section 2 figures: the belastingkorting is 26,9% of the unoffset loss, so € 20.000 × 0,269 = € 5.380,00, rounded to whole euros € 5.380. The belastingkorting is a credit against box 1 tax, so it is not deducted from the € 20.000 loss. Any remainder is the part of the € 5.380 belastingkorting not yet used against box 1 tax. Art. 4.53 lid 4 allows use in the year of the korting and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Both limits give 2030 here: the seven following years of the 2023 korting end in 2030, and the ninth year after the loss year 2021 is 2030 (2021 + 9 = 2030). This matches the remainder period in FISIN 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

**Example 2, Calculated example, dividend at the 2023 rate.** Invented inputs: a BV pays a dividend of € 60.000 in 2023 to its sole shareholder, who has an aanmerkelijk belang in the BV, there are no deductible costs and no other box 2 items. Step 1: box 2 income is € 60.000. Step 2: the 2023 box 2 rate is 26,9%, so the tax is € 60.000 × 0,269 = € 16.140,00, rounded to whole euros € 16.140. Step 3: dividendbelasting is not deducted from the income but credited as voorheffing, so the € 16.140 is reduced by the voorheffing in the aanslag ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Example 3, Official example, excessief lenen in 2023 ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)).** The Belastingdienst page gives for 2023 a drempel of € 700.000 and a loan at 31 December 2023 of € 800.000, so the fictief regulier voordeel is € 800.000 − € 700.000 = € 100.000. FISIN 2025 gives a debt of € 900.000 in 2023 against the € 700.000 maximum, so the fictief regulier voordeel is € 900.000 − € 700.000 = € 200.000.

**Example 4, Calculated example, excessief lenen with a smaller debt.** Invented inputs: the taxpayer and the partner together owe the BV € 750.000 at 31 December 2023, no amounts were taxed earlier, and the taxpayer still has the aanmerkelijk belang at the end of the year. Step 1: the maximum is € 700.000 and applies to the taxpayer and the partner together. Step 2: the positive amount is the debt above the maximum, € 750.000 − € 700.000 = € 50.000. Step 3: the € 50.000 is a fictief regulier voordeel in box 2, and at the 2023 rate of 26,9% the tax on it is € 50.000 × 0,269 = € 13.450,00, rounded to whole euros € 13.450. The loan itself stays a loan in full ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).

**Example 5, Calculated example, forfaitair voordeel.** Invented inputs: the value of the shares in the vbi at 1 January 2023 is € 100.000, other reguliere voordelen from those shares in 2023 are € 1.000, and the shares are held the whole year. Step 1: the yearly percentage for 2023 is 6,17%. Step 2: € 100.000 × 0,0617 = € 6.170,00, rounded to whole euros € 6.170. Step 3: the forfaitair voordeel is reduced by the other reguliere voordelen, so € 6.170,00 − € 1.000,00 = € 5.170,00, rounded to whole euros € 5.170, and the reduction never brings it below nil. Because the shares are held the whole year, no time apportionment applies ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

**Example 6, Calculated example, gebruikelijk loon.** Invented inputs: the BV has one directeur-grootaandeelhouder, the loon from the most comparable dienstbetrekking is € 55.000 and an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role in it, the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies is € 52.000, and the fixed amount for 2023 is € 51.000. Step 1: the 2023 comparable-loon test is 100% of the most comparable loon, so 100% of € 55.000 = € 55.000,00, rounded to whole euros € 55.000. Step 2: the gebruikelijk loon is the highest of € 55.000, € 52.000 and € 51.000, so € 55.000. The rule does not apply at all if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000, so no minimum applies at a total of € 4.800 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Example 7, Calculated example, loss offset in time.** Invented inputs: a verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang of € 30.000 arises in 2023, there is no box 2 income in 2022 and there is box 2 income of € 18.000 in 2024. Step 1: the loss offsets box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, so 2022 and 2024 to 2029 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Step 2: the € 18.000 is offset in 2024. Step 3: € 30.000 − € 18.000 = € 12.000 remains usable in 2025 to 2029.

**Example 8, Calculated example, ODV increase for 2023.** Invented inputs: the ODV balance at 1 January 2023 is € 100.000. Step 1: the market rate for the yearly increase in 2023 is 0,699%. Step 2: the increase is € 100.000 × 0,00699 = € 699,00, rounded to whole euros € 699 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

**Example 9, Calculated example, inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility.** Invented inputs: the taxpayer inherited shares, box 2 income of € 20.000 was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, and within 24 months after the death the taxpayer receives reguliere voordelen of € 15.000. Step 1: on request the € 15.000 is not counted as box 2 income, because it does not exceed the € 20.000 that was taken into account with the deceased. Step 2: the same € 15.000 is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs, so the verkrijgingsprijs falls by € 15.000 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
