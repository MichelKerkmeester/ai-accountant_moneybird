## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Example 1, Official example, loss converted into a tax credit (belastingkorting) ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang)).** FISIN 2023 gives a loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) of € 20.000 that arose in 2021, a tax credit of € 5.380 in 2023 and a remainder usable until 2030. Check with the section 2 figures: the tax credit is 26,9% of the unoffset loss, so € 20.000 × 0,269 = € 5.380,00, rounded to whole euros € 5.380. The tax credit is a credit against box 1 tax, so it is not deducted from the € 20.000 loss. Any remainder is the part of the € 5.380 tax credit not yet used against box 1 tax. Art. 4.53 lid 4 allows use in the year of the credit (korting) and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Both limits give 2030 here: the seven following years of the 2023 credit end in 2030, and the ninth year after the loss year 2021 is 2030 (2021 + 9 = 2030). This matches the remainder period in FISIN 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

**Example 2, Calculated example, dividend at the 2023 rate.** Invented inputs: a private limited company (BV) pays a dividend of € 60.000 in 2023 to its sole shareholder, who has a substantial interest in the company, there are no deductible costs and no other box 2 items. Step 1: box 2 income is € 60.000. Step 2: the 2023 box 2 rate is 26,9%, so the tax is € 60.000 × 0,269 = € 16.140,00, rounded to whole euros € 16.140. Step 3: dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted from the income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing), so the € 16.140 is reduced by the prepayment in the assessment (aanslag) ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Example 3, Official example, excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) in 2023 ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)).** The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives for 2023 a threshold (drempel) of € 700.000 and a loan at 31 December 2023 of € 800.000, so the fictitious regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) is € 800.000 − € 700.000 = € 100.000. FISIN 2025 gives a debt of € 900.000 in 2023 against the € 700.000 maximum, so the fictitious regular benefit is € 900.000 − € 700.000 = € 200.000.

**Example 4, Calculated example, excessive borrowing with a smaller debt.** Invented inputs: the taxpayer and the partner together owe the private limited company € 750.000 at 31 December 2023, no amounts were taxed earlier, and the taxpayer still has the substantial interest at the end of the year. Step 1: the maximum is € 700.000 and applies to the taxpayer and the partner together. Step 2: the positive amount is the debt above the maximum, € 750.000 − € 700.000 = € 50.000. Step 3: the € 50.000 is a fictitious regular benefit in box 2, and at the 2023 rate of 26,9% the tax on it is € 50.000 × 0,269 = € 13.450,00, rounded to whole euros € 13.450. The loan itself stays a loan in full ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).

**Example 5, Calculated example, flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel).** Invented inputs: the value of the shares in the exempt investment institution (vbi, vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) at 1 January 2023 is € 100.000, other regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) from those shares in 2023 are € 1.000, and the shares are held the whole year. Step 1: the yearly percentage for 2023 is 6,17%. Step 2: € 100.000 × 0,0617 = € 6.170,00, rounded to whole euros € 6.170. Step 3: the flat-rate benefit is reduced by the other regular benefits, so € 6.170,00 − € 1.000,00 = € 5.170,00, rounded to whole euros € 5.170, and the reduction never brings it below nil. Because the shares are held the whole year, no time apportionment applies ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

**Example 6, Calculated example, customary salary (gebruikelijk loon).** Invented inputs: the BV has one director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder), the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking) is € 55.000 and a substantial interest plays no role in it, the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies is € 52.000, and the fixed amount for 2023 is € 51.000. Step 1: the 2023 comparable-wage test is 100% of the most comparable wage, so 100% of € 55.000 = € 55.000,00, rounded to whole euros € 55.000. Step 2: the customary salary is the highest of € 55.000, € 52.000 and € 51.000, so € 55.000. The rule does not apply at all if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000, so no minimum applies at a total of € 4.800 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

**Example 7, Calculated example, loss offset in time.** Invented inputs: a loss from a substantial interest of € 30.000 arises in 2023, there is no box 2 income in 2022 and there is box 2 income of € 18.000 in 2024. Step 1: the loss offsets box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, so 2022 and 2024 to 2029 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Step 2: the € 18.000 is offset in 2024. Step 3: € 30.000 − € 18.000 = € 12.000 remains usable in 2025 to 2029.

**Example 8, Calculated example, old-age obligation (ODV) increase for 2023.** Invented inputs: the old-age obligation balance at 1 January 2023 is € 100.000. Step 1: the market rate (marktrente) for the yearly increase in 2023 is 0,699%. Step 2: the increase is € 100.000 × 0,00699 = € 699,00, rounded to whole euros € 699 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

**Example 9, Calculated example, inherited substantial interest facility.** Invented inputs: the taxpayer inherited shares, box 2 income of € 20.000 was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, and within 24 months after the death the taxpayer receives regular benefits of € 15.000. Step 1: on request the € 15.000 is not counted as box 2 income, because it does not exceed the € 20.000 that was taken into account with the deceased. Step 2: the same € 15.000 is written off on the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs), so the acquisition price falls by € 15.000 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
