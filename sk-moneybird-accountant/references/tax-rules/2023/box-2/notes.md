## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen): not in force (in werking) in 2022, in force from 1 January 2023. The new fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, with art. 4.14a to 4.14c. The 2023 text sets the maximum at € 700.000 plus amounts taxed earlier ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). In 2022 the act was already issued but not yet in force ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- Excessive borrowing threshold (drempel) history: the bill proposed € 500.000, the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) of 25 March 2022 replaced that by € 700.000, and the Grinwis c.s. amendment (amendement Grinwis c.s.) in the adopted text replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000, which the 2024 to 2027 texts print ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html), [wetten.overheid.nl 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).
- Owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld): art. 10a.23, new for 2023, also excludes an owner-occupied home (eigen woning) debt that existed on 31 December 2022 without a mortgage to the company ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).
- Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debts: from 1 January 2023 the debts from a civil-law joint liability for a debt of a partnership are left out, numbered lid 4 and 5 in the 2023 text, inserted retroactively by the 2025 Tax Plan (Belastingplan 2025) ([Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html), [wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount: € 48.000 in 2022 to € 51.000 in 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Customary salary comparable-wage test: 75% in 2022 to 100% in 2023. Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. F names "het afschaffen van de doelmatigheidsmarge in het gebruikelijk loon per 2023" (the removal of the efficiency margin in the customary salary from 2023) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Customary salary € 5.000 threshold: art. 12a lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, art. 12a lid 4 from 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Start-up rule (S&O): present in the 2022 text, absent from the 2023 text. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) says "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen". Earlier users could use it at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 to 2024 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14): 5,53% in 2022 to 6,17% in 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate (marktrente): -0,410% for 2022 to 0,699% for 2023 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).
- FISIN 2023 lists two kinds of benefits (regular, disposal), while FISIN 2025 and 2026 list three, which matches the new fictitious regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Unchanged from 2022: the 26,9% box 2 rate, the 26,9% tax credit (belastingkorting), the loss offset period of the previous year and the six following years, and the art. 4.6 definition with sub a to e ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Borrowing from the private limited company (BV): keep a list of all debts of the taxpayer and the partner to the company or companies at 31 December, at nominal value. The fictitious regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) is measured against one € 700.000 maximum for the two together. Receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, and debts at several companies are added ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).
- The private limited company side: the loan stays a loan in full. The company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays corporate income tax (Vpb) on it, and the other terms stay. The company does not withhold dividend tax (dividendbelasting) on the part taxed as box 2 income ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).
- Dividends: dividend tax is not deducted from the box 2 income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Shares: notary costs are added to the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs). Interest on a loan to buy the shares is deductible. Interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap) is box 1 income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen), not box 2 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Salary: salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon), and the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) is declared as income from employment (box 1), not as a box 2 benefit (voordeel) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Losses: a loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment and shown separately on it, open to objection. Keep the decision with the records ([Belastingdienst, verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)).
- Inherited shares: the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) that are exempt under art. 4.12a are written off on the acquisition price, so adjust the acquisition price record ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- Minor children: the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) of minor children must be declared by the taxpayer until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB) and old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV): if the scheme provides for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. Failing to index can be seen as waiving (prijsgeven) ([Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). The old-age obligation is increased each year by the market rate (marktrente), 0,699% for 2023 ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The rollover list (doorschuiving, rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration) is UNVERIFIED for 2023. The rollover list was not re-fetched on a primary page in the check. The 10-year APV term is Verified. Sources: [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: a primary statute text or Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on the rollover articles.
- The statement that a 2025 decision (beschikking) for partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect for a non-resident as art. 7.5 was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages. UNVERIFIED. Source: [wetten.overheid.nl, art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). What would settle it: the decision text or a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page on partial non-resident liability.
- FISIN 2023 works its excessive-borrowing example into 2024 with a threshold (drempel) of € 800.000 (€ 700.000 plus € 100.000) and € 50.000 taxed, written before the 2024 cut to € 500.000. Under the 2024 law the 2024 threshold is € 600.000. The FISIN 2023 example is outdated for 2024. The 2023 step of that example matches the 2023 statute. Sources: [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- The act that inserted art. 4.6 sub e was not identified. Only the text change between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 versions is known. Source: [wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). What would settle it: the amending act for art. 4.6 sub e.
- No primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two partners together. The rule of art. 2.17 lid 2 allows any division as long as the total is 100%, and art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer. UNVERIFIED for a summed amount. Sources: [wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: a primary page or form that prints a summed amount.
- Proposed for 2027: the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the fictitious regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from the art. 4.12a inherited facility from 1 January 2027. Not adopted on 2026-10-11. Source: [eerstekamer.nl](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).
