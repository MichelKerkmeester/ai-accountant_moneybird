## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Box 2 rate (art. 2.12)

For 2023 there is one flat rate: 26,9%. The text of art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 valid on 1 January 2023 prints that rate ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)), and FISIN 2023 states 26,90% ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

One flat rate applied from 2020 to 2023: 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). From 2024 a two-bracket table applies ([wetten.overheid.nl 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The table applies per taxpayer. Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

Who and how: the rate applies to the income from a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) as a whole, including the fictitious regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) from 2023 and the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or foreign investment company. Because the rate applies per taxpayer, each fiscal partner bears the rate on his own share of any divided box 2 income ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.2 Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the 5% test (art. 4.6), drag-along, carry-along and deemed interest

The taxpayer has a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) if he, alone or together with his partner ("al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner"), directly or indirectly meets one of these tests ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)):

- sub a, he holds at least 5% of the issued capital
- sub b, he has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital
- sub c, he holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds
- sub d, he is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a
- sub e, he has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit

The 2023 text has sub a to e, the same as 2022. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, and in sub d "tot" became "om" in the same period ([wetten.overheid.nl 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub f was added later, by the Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) (Stb. 2025, 445), with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, so it does not apply to 2023 ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html), [wetten.overheid.nl 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

Drag-along rule (meesleepregeling, art. 4.9): once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company also belong to it. Carry-along rule (meetrekregeling, art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line (aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The texts of art. 4.7 to 4.10 are identical in every year 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11): shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to constitute a substantial interest if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. The text is identical 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The vbi status is a deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) case listed by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, not part of the art. 4.11 list. For segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) the loss deferral runs to a third-party sale, death or 10 years, and FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rollover (doorschuiving) list is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.3 Income from a substantial interest (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

The income is regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus the deductible costs, plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions. Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)):

- sub a, the flat-rate benefit from an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company
- sub b to d, certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction
- sub e, bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling)
- sub f, from 2023 the excessive-borrowing fiction

Not box 2: interest on a receivable on the own company is income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon). Deductible is interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs). Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted from the income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel): the benefit is the transfer price minus the acquisition price. The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Special cases (inheritance, gift with or without rollover, reaching 5% during the year, the non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs)) are as the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes. At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

Deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) (art. 4.16): the statute lists as a sale the buy-back (inkoop) of shares, commutation (afkoop) and buy-back of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of segregated private assets no longer attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.4 Partner and minor children

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner", so a holding can be a substantial interest through the partner ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

Fiscal partners may divide the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) under art. 2.17 lid 2. With a fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest may be divided in the proportion chosen each year in the return. FISIN 2026 says: "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own rate applies to his share ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). No primary page states a summed two-partner amount, that part is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.5 Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14)

Who: a taxpayer with shares in an exempt investment institution (vbi, vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company. The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of those shares at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned. The percentage for 2023 is 6,17%, printed in the 2023 text of art. 4.14 lid 1 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The percentage was 5,53% in 2022 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). When the shares start to yield the flat-rate benefit, that is a deemed disposal under art. 4.16 ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.6 Excessive borrowing, entry into force and placement

The Act of 21 December 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022), the Excessive Borrowing from the Own Company Act, is cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap" and is in force from 1 January 2023 (Artikel III of the act) ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ("het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)", debts meaning all civil-law debt relations and obligations), the amount rule in art. 4.14a ("Fictief regulier voordeel"), the related-person (verbonden persoon) rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.13, 4.14a to 4.14c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### 3.7 Excessive borrowing, the maximum and the mechanism (art. 4.14a)

Lid 1: the positive amount equals the total debts above the maximum. A negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. Lid 2: the maximum in the 2023 text is € 700.000, plus amounts taxed earlier. Lid 5: if the taxpayer no longer has a substantial interest at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount. Lid 6: an owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld) is excluded to the extent a mortgage right (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company. Lid 7, inserted by the 2025 Tax Plan (Belastingplan 2025) (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023: double counting of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debt among several participants is removed ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html)).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds: receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, debts at several companies are added, and the threshold (drempel) "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).

Threshold history: the bill proposed € 500.000. The third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) (Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced € 500.000 by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, 4.14b and 4.14c lid 1, following the coalition agreement ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html)). The Grinwis c.s. amendment (Amendement Grinwis c.s.) (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles ([Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c ([wetten.overheid.nl 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For 2023 itself the operative figure is € 700.000, printed in the 2023 text.

### 3.8 Excessive borrowing, measurement (art. 4.14a lid 3 and 4)

The debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly (lid 3). The debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value (lid 4) ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### 3.9 Excessive borrowing, related persons (verbonden personen) (art. 4.14b)

Debts of a related person (verbonden persoon) (a blood relative or a relative by marriage in the direct line (aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner) to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 700.000 in the 2023 text, applied to that person and his partner together. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### 3.10 Excessive borrowing, immigration (art. 4.14c)

A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 700.000 in the 2023 text ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). From 2024 the floor is € 500.000 ([wetten.overheid.nl 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.11 Excessive borrowing, partnership exception

Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a related person for a debt of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) (a general partnership (vof), a limited partnership (cv), a civil-law partnership (maatschap) or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out. This was inserted by the 2025 Tax Plan (Belastingplan 2025) (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023, as lid 6 and 7 in the general numbering and numbered lid 4 and 5 in the 2023 text. Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) (Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" retroactive to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html), [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)).

### 3.12 Owner-occupied home debt exclusion (art. 4.14a lid 6 and art. 10a.23)

Art. 4.14a lid 6 excludes an owner-occupied home debt to the extent a mortgage right on the home was given to the company. The transitional article 10a.23 adds that an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### 3.13 Consequences of excessive borrowing

The loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays corporate income tax (Vpb, vennootschapsbelasting) on it, and the other terms stay. The company does not withhold dividend tax (dividendbelasting) on the part taxed as box 2 income. Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold. A negative fictitious regular benefit is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.14 Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon), fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount. In 2023 the fixed amount is € 51.000 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the table correction factor (lid 7 from 2023, lid 9 in the 2020 text) ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.15 Customary salary, comparable-wage test

In the 2023 text the test in (a) is 100% of the wage from the most comparable employment relationship. In the 2020 to 2022 texts it was 75% ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. F names "het afschaffen van de doelmatigheidsmarge in het gebruikelijk loon per 2023" (the removal of the efficiency margin in the customary salary from 2023) in the material for this item. The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and which is known to the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) and the tax inspector (inspecteur). A lower wage is possible: if the withholding agent makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage, the comparable wage applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower. The customary salary is declared as income from employment (box 1), not as a box 2 benefit (voordeel). The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The checked sources do not state that the customary salary reduces the residual profit available for a dividend.

### 3.16 Customary salary, the € 5.000 threshold

The rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year. This is lid 4 in the 2023 text, lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts ([wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (not per business) ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.17 Customary salary, the start-up rule

For a body with an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring, R&D declaration) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applied, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling. This rule is in the 2020 to 2022 texts and absent from the 2023 text ([wetten.overheid.nl 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says: "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" (from 2023 this exception has lapsed for new cases). It could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 to 2024 ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.18 Loss offset (art. 4.49), including losses from 2018 or earlier

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose. The loss is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection. For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (to be offset over a longer period, in the coming 9 years) ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)). The text of art. 4.49 is identical 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.19 Conversion into a tax credit (belastingkorting) (art. 4.53)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. Lid 2: the percentage is 26,25% in the 2020 text and 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage". Lid 3: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit (korting) by a decision (beschikking) open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner ([wetten.overheid.nl 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest ([Belastingdienst, verlies omzetten in belastingkorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Knowledge group position (Kennisgroepstandpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block it, and ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([Kennisgroepstandpunt KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)).

### 3.20 Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the acquisition price. This is what art. 4.12a carries. It does not carry the excessive-borrowing fiction. The text is identical 2020 to 2027 ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the fictitious regular benefit from this facility from 1 January 2027, see section 7 ([eerstekamer.nl](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)).

### 3.21 PEB, ODV and the 2023 market rate (marktrente)

Background. Art. 38n lid 1 Wet LB 1964 keeps the old pension rules (as at 31 December 2016) for pension in own management (PEB, pensioen in eigen beheer). Art. 38n lid 2 allowed waiving (prijsgeven) of the part of the claim above the fiscal balance sheet value (fiscale balanswaarde), on condition the claim was at the same moment wholly commuted (afgekocht) or tax-neutrally (geruisloos) converted into an old-age obligation (ODV, oudedagsverplichting). Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also a former partner who kept part of the claim. Art. 38o: on commutation (afkoop) the taxable wage is the surrender value (afkoopwaarde) minus 34,5% of the fiscal balance sheet value (capped by reference to the 2015 balance), 25% from 1 January 2018 and 19,5% from 1 January 2019. Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual. Artikel VI: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html), [wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen) states that commutation or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019" ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). So for 2023 those routes are closed and only a remaining PEB or an ODV exists.

Start date. The consolidated text on wetten.overheid.nl (BWBR0039380) reads "Geldend van 01-04-2017", with listed parts retroactive to 1 January 2017, while the published Stb. 2017, 115 Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under art. 12 of the Advisory Referendum Act (Wet raadgevend referendum) ([wetten.overheid.nl BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). The leniency decision (coulancebesluit) of 22 March 2017 (Stcrt. 2017, 18118) let directors and substantial shareholders adjust "uiterlijk tot en met 30 juni 2017" (no later than 30 June 2017) and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

Remaining PEB. A remaining PEB keeps the old rules (art. 38n). If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences ([Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).

Old-age obligation (ODV) (art. 38p). The old-age obligation is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Wage Tax Implementing Regulation 2011 (Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011), the average of the monthly U rates (U-rendementen) of the preceding year). For 2023 the rate is 0,699%, the December 2022 value ([Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)). A negative market rate must also be applied. The old-age obligation can be used tax-neutrally for an annuity (lijfrente), an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or an annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension age (AOW age) and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Added by Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020: under ministerial conditions the old-age obligation can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5) ([wetten.overheid.nl 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [wetten.overheid.nl 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), [Stcrt. 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)).

Pension in own management and box 2 (art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001). Lid 3 says the acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. Lid 2 says that in the company the old-age obligation is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. Pension in own management payouts are income from former employment (box 1) ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 10a.18](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)).

### 3.22 Non-residents (art. 7.5)

Art. 7.5 confirms the base for a non-resident: a substantial interest in a Netherlands-established company, not part of an enterprise ([wetten.overheid.nl, art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The research statement that a 2025 decision for partial non-resident tax liability (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages and stays UNVERIFIED, see section 7.
