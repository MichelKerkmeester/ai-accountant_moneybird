## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the deduction of business costs (zakelijke kosten) and the car rules for a sole trader in tax year 2023. It runs through the threshold (drempel) for mixed costs (gemengde kosten) such as food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen) and representation expenses (representatie) against the 80% election, the mileage allowance for a private vehicle (kilometervergoeding privévervoermiddel), the private-use addition (bijtelling), the profit addition for private use (privégebruik) of a business asset (bedrijfsmiddel), the workspace, telephone, computer and fine exclusions, and the study costs (studiekosten) position. The most important 2023 figures are the threshold of € 5.100, the mileage allowance of € 0,21 per kilometre, the 22% standard private-use addition with the 35% youngtimer rule and the zero-emission reduction of 6% capped at € 1.800 for cars first registered in 2023. All 2023 amounts are enacted law, read in the statute text valid on 1 January 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
