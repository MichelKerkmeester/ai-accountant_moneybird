## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel): € 4.800 in 2022 to € 5.100 in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding): € 0,19 per km in 2022 to € 0,21 per km in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Zero-emission (zero-emissie) private-use addition for cars first registered in the year: 2022 gave 6% off capped at € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000, and 2023 gives 6% off capped at € 1.800, which is 16% up to € 30.000 (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- A zero-emission car first registered in 2022 keeps the 2022 reduction for 60 months from the first day of the month after its first registration, so in 2023 the 2022 amounts still apply to that car (art. 3.20 lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek): 2022 was the first year of the abolition of the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) for study expenses (scholingsuitgaven), and art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). 2023 continues that position, nil except the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).
- STAP-budget: started on 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In 2023 the maximum stays € 1.000 including VAT (btw) per training (scholing) and 2023 is the last year of the scheme, because the scheme is marked lapsed per 01-01-2024 (Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).
- Unchanged from 2022 to 2023: the € 1.500 cap on travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf) for conferences and courses, relocation costs (verhuiskosten) of the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting) for at most two years, the 22% standard private-use addition, the 25% minimum for cars first registered up to 31 December 2016, the 35% youngtimer rule with the 15 year threshold, the 7% private-use addition for the bicycle (fiets), the workspace 0% rule with the art. 3.16 lid 1 exceptions, the 0% for a telephone subscription at home (telefoonabonnement thuis) and for private computers and equipment (apparatuur), and the 0% for fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The 2023 statute keeps the same rule set as 2022, so apart from the indexed and adjusted amounts above there is no substantive change in this topic (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep receipts and invoices for every cost. A purchase under € 450 or a cost that covers one year is deducted at once, and a purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Book the mixed costs posts together. The € 5.100 threshold applies to the yearly total of those posts, and the choice for the 80% alternative is made in the return (art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Book costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte), and including VAT when no VAT return is filed or when the small businesses scheme (KOR) applies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/).
- A car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) lands in the profit as a withdrawal (onttrekking) of at least 22% of the value, or 35% for an old car, capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation, and nil when at most 500 private km are shown with a trip registration (rittenregistratie) (art. 3.20, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- A bicycle (fiets) of the business that is available privately lands in the profit as 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs), capped at the costs borne, and nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Business travel by public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig) is booked at the actual costs, with the ticket or the public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview as the record (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).
- For a private car used for business, without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) deduct all VAT during the year and correct 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d (rubriek 1d) at year end, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).
- Record relocation costs as the household contents cost plus € 7750 in the year of the move, and double accommodation for at most two years (art. 3.17 lid 1 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Fines (geldboetes), penalty payments (dwangsommen) and related costs never belong in the deductible cost lines (art. 3.14, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- The personal study cost deduction goes into the income tax return only in the performance grant case, with the € 250 threshold per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).
- For a van (bestelauto) that is almost exclusively suited for goods, a declaration of exclusively business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (art. 3.20 lid 6 to 10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).
- Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they were made for the work (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for 2023.

One scope note outside the 2023 figures. The Wet Vpb 1969 variant of the threshold for mixed costs in the cross-year checked material (0,4% of the employees' wage total when higher than the fixed amount, election at 73,5%, per body, once at the parent company in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid)) is marked Verified for 2020 and 2026 only, and the checked sources do not state the 2023 text. So those two BV percentages are not confirmed for 2023 in this file. Reading art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 in the version valid on 1 January 2023 would settle it, in the same statute series that the checked sources used for 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).
