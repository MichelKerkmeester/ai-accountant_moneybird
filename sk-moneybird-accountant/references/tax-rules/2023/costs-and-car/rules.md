## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten)

The threshold (drempel) applies to a sole trader and also to a recipient of other income (resultaatgenieter). Costs connected with food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), representation expenses (representatie, including receptions (recepties), festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak)) and conferences (congressen), seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and similar events are not deductible up to a yearly amount of € 5.100 in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods and services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). Food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including tips (fooien), drink covers coffee, tea, milk and soft drinks (frisdrank), and stimulants covers cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation includes receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation. Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals to staff the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wage in kind (loon in natura) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, sections 3.2 and 3.3). The threshold is not time-proportioned: it is not reduced when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either (same decree (besluit), section 3.1). For a BV, art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 replaces the fixed amount with 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher and sets the election at 73,5% instead of 80%, applied per body, and only once at the parent company inside a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).

### 3.2 Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The same 80% applies to recipients of other income. The choice covers the same posts as the threshold in 3.1, so it is one yearly choice over the total of those posts. Which route gives more depends on the total cost level, and the comparison is worked out in section 4. For a BV the election percentage is 73,5% (art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01).

### 3.3 Costs of a mixed nature (kosten met een gemengd karakter)

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive). Examples with only a business part or with a restriction are rent (huur) and maintenance (onderhoud) of premises split into a business and a private part, telephone costs (telefoonkosten), a car of the business, conferences, study trips, symposia, seminars and excursions, business lunches and dinners, receptions and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business such as a driving licence (rijbewijs), health costs such as a pedicure and health insurance (zorgverzekering), and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser. Work clothing (werkkleding) is deductible.

### 3.4 Travel and accommodation costs (reis- en verblijfkosten) for conferences and courses

Travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession and for conferences, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and similar events are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. The same travel and stay also count as one of the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) posts of art. 3.15 lid 1 through art. 3.15 lid 2, so both rules have to be tested. Study costs (studiekosten) themselves stay deductible as a business cost only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers).

### 3.5 Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, so a car, motorcycle or bicycle (fiets), are limited to a fixed € 0,21 per business kilometre in 2023 (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The amount is all-in: fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll and parking cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed. A vehicle of the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) instead follows the balance sheet and, for private use, the withdrawal rules of 3.9 to 3.14.

### 3.6 Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview. This route sits next to the € 0,21 mileage allowance in 3.5, which applies to a private or privately rented vehicle.

### 3.7 Relocation costs (verhuiskosten)

Moving costs for the enterprise are the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The rule is unchanged in every year 2020 to 2027.

### 3.8 Double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

Under the same provision, the cost of housing outside the place of residence is deductible for at most two years (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The rule is unchanged from 2020 to 2027.

### 3.9 Private-use addition for a car (bijtelling auto), standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal (onttrekking) per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year, and commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private (lid 3). Car (auto) means a passenger car (personenauto) or a van (bestelauto), except a van that is almost exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private kilometres times the actual cost per kilometre are settled instead. For such a van a declaration of exclusive business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (lid 6 to 10). The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, described by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) as the original catalogue value including VAT and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). The RDW site carries this value for most cars. For an old car the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) replaces it. The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto) in the business assets is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price.

### 3.10 Car first registered up to and including 31 December 2016

For a car first registered on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars in 3.11, because the two minimums apply to different cars.

### 3.11 Youngtimer

The 35% regime applies to a car first taken into use more than 15 years ago (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The threshold is 15 years in 2023. For an old car the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer, WEV) replaces the catalogue price. When the threshold is crossed during the year the year is split by month. The 35% works on the WEV, while the 25% minimum of art. 10a.4 works on cars first registered up to 31 December 2016 that are not old cars.

### 3.12 Zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto), first registered in 2023

For a car with 0 g CO2 per kilometre the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). For a car first registered in 2023 the reduction is 6% with a maximum of € 1.800, which amounts to 16% up to € 30.000. Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead, and the 1 kilowatt peak test is the statute text for 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### 3.13 Fixed percentage from first registration

The reduction that applied at the first registration of the car stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after the first registration (art. 3.20 lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). After those 60 months the rules then in force apply. A zero-emission car first registered in 2022 therefore keeps the 2022 reduction of 6% capped at € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000, during 2023 and the rest of its 60 month period.

### 3.14 500 km rule (500 km regel)

A car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year (art. 3.20 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another way of showing at most 500 private kilometres the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), although trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto). Commuting is not private use for this test (lid 3).

### 3.15 Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets)

When a bicycle (fiets) of the business, including an e-bike or a speed pedelec that also runs on muscle power, is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Availability for commuting counts as private availability. A new subsection 5 (lid 5), added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, so that outcome is also open for 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.16 Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek)

In 2023 the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) for study costs are nil, except for the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case in 3.17 to 3.19 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). Up to and including 2021 study expenses (scholingsuitgaven) were deductible above a threshold (drempel) of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Act on the Abolition of the Fiscal Deduction for Study Expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree of 12 July 2021 (Besluit van 12 juli 2021), Stb. 2021, 349, and in the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). Study costs stay deductible as a business cost when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only serve to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis), and costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, although the boundary is hard to draw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for study expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.17 Performance grant (prestatiebeurs) for vocational education (mbo)

When the diploma period (diplomatermijn), usually 10 years, has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). For vocational education the cap is € 1.693 per study year and € 141,09 per month for a part year. When the actual performance grant was lower, the lower amount applies. FISIN 2023 gives an official example, worked out in section 4. The case is verified for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 in FISIN (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten).

### 3.18 Performance grant for higher professional education (hbo) or university education (wo)

The same case applies with the caps for higher professional education and university education of € 2.443 per study year and € 203,59 per month for a part year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten). When the actual performance grant was lower than the cap, the lower amount applies.

### 3.19 Threshold in the performance grant case

The € 250 threshold applies per person in the performance grant case (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).

### 3.20 STAP-budget

The STAP-budget was the non-fiscal replacement for the abolished study cost deduction and took effect at the same moment as the abolition, 1 January 2022. Art. 29 of the scheme (regeling) links its start to the Abolition Act, and Stb. 2021, 349 states that it applies from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The maximum subsidy is € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). Corrected: the research said at most € 1.000 per year, the scheme text says per training. The scheme is marked lapsed per 01-01-2024 (Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024), so 2023 is the last year of the scheme.

### 3.21 STAP application windows (aanvraagvensters)

There are six application windows per calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).

### 3.22 Workspace at home (werkruimte thuis)

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not part of the business assets are not deductible unless the workspace is a self-contained part of the home in the ordinary meaning (naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig) and an income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit, wage and income from other activities (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is part of the business assets. The official factors for self-contained are an own entrance (opgang), own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party, so a loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints Workspace at home 0%, with exceptions. The lid 14 limit for an owned home in the business assets that serves as main residence (hoofdverblijf) applies from 1 January 2025 and does not change 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).

### 3.23 Telephone subscription at home (telefoonabonnement thuis)

Telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The practical rule is that a private telephone subscription is not deductible but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible but call costs for private calls on it are not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). The overview page prints Telephone subscription at home 0% and Business telephone calls at home 100%. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.16 lid 11).

### 3.24 Clothing (kleding), literature (literatuur) and personal care (persoonlijke verzorging)

Not deductible: literature except professional literature (vakliteratuur) (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b), clothing except work clothing (werkkleding) (onderdeel c) with work clothing defined in lid 5, and personal care (onderdeel d) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The overview page keeps the same line and names a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business as not deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.25 Private computers (privécomputers) and equipment (apparatuur)

Costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other similar equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) are not deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Equipment and instruments that do not belong to the business assets are therefore 0% deductible. Whether a laptop or computer belongs to the business follows asset classification (vermogensetikettering), see 3.29.

### 3.26 Payment to the partner (betaling aan de partner)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11).

### 3.27 Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen)

Not deductible are costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c), costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a penalty order became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under section 5.3.2 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb) (onderdeel i) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Traffic fines (verkeersboeten) under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines, and the overview page prints Fines (geldboetes) 0% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1). Timing: costs made before the fine is imposed are not costs relating to the imposed fine and stay deductible, costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible, and for a penalty payment the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring) (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught. Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum or settled by a settlement (transactie) or a penalty order, are excluded (same decree, section 2).

### 3.28 Asset (bedrijfsmiddel) under € 450

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example small office supplies (kleine kantoorartikelen), professional literature or maintenance of the car (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they were made for the work, and receipts and invoices must be kept.

### 3.29 Asset classification (vermogensetikettering) of assets

Assets used 10% or less for business are compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). Assets used 90% or more for business are mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). Assets used between those levels are freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) within reasonable bounds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once. A choice to treat an asset as freely classifiable can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only if a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid) applies. Private use of an asset in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule, which the knowledge group (Kennisgroep) applies to the youngtimer change that takes effect on 1 January 2027 (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/).

### 3.30 Asset classification of a car

A car must be classified as a mandatory business asset when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. A car used 90% or more privately must be classified as a compulsory private asset. In all other cases, it is a freely classifiable asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). Once the car is in the business assets, private use is settled with the withdrawal of 3.9 to 3.14.

### 3.31 VAT (BTW) on the private car (privéauto)

VAT on the use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, although it is business for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting). In a private limited company (BV) the director and substantial shareholder (DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) but counts as an employee for this purpose. Without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance.

### 3.32 Cost base (kostengrondslag) and VAT

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, which is the small businesses scheme (KOR) case, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted, also not VAT paid in another EU country, earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening), the low motor vehicle tax (mrb) rate for vans stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per entrepreneur covering all sub-numbers (subnummers) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The KOR threshold history by year belongs to the VAT topic.
