## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

The calculated examples use invented round amounts. Every step uses only the section 2 figures. Every product is written with two decimals before rounding. No step in these examples requires rounding.

### Example 1: mixed costs (gemengde kosten), threshold against 80% (Calculated example)

Costs for food, drink and representation expenses (voedsel, drank en representatie) of € 8.000,00 in 2023.

- Threshold (drempel) route: € 8.000,00 minus € 5.100,00 = € 2.900,00 deductible.
- 80% route: 80% x € 8.000,00 = € 6.400,00 deductible.
- Difference: € 6.400,00 minus € 2.900,00 = € 3.500,00, so the 80% election is better at this cost level (art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 2: crossover point of the two routes (Calculated example)

Same posts, € 25.500,00 in 2023.

- Threshold route: € 25.500,00 minus € 5.100,00 = € 20.400,00.
- 80% route: 80% x € 25.500,00 = € 20.400,00.
- The two routes are equal here, so € 25.500,00 is the crossover point. Below € 25.500,00 the 80% election gives more, above it the threshold route gives more.

### Example 3: kilometre allowance (Calculated example)

A privately owned car drives 6.000 business kilometres in 2023.

- Deduction: 6.000 x € 0,21 = € 1.260,00.
- Fuel, insurance, toll and parking of € 2.100,00 cannot be deducted on top, because the amount is all-in. The deduction stays € 1.260,00 (art. 3.15 lid 6, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 4: public transport (openbaar vervoer) and taxi (Calculated example)

Train tickets for business trips of € 940,00 and a business taxi ride of € 60,00.

- Deduction: 100% x € 940,00 = € 940,00, plus 100% x € 60,00 = € 60,00, together € 1.000,00.
- The taxpayer keeps the tickets and the public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview as proof (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### Example 5: congress cap (Calculated example)

A taxpayer visits a conference (congres) abroad that is not necessary because of the nature of the work. Travel and stay cost € 1.850,00.

- Cap: € 1.850,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 350,00 is not deductible.
- The remaining € 1.500,00 is part of the mixed costs posts of art. 3.15 and is subject to the threshold or the 80% election (art. 3.16 lid 2 f and lid 8, and art. 3.15 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 6: relocation costs (verhuiskosten) (Calculated example)

Moving the household contents (inboedel) to a new business location costs € 2.500,00.

- Deduction: € 2.500,00 plus € 7.750,00 = € 10.250,00 (art. 3.17 lid 1 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 7: private-use addition (bijtelling) capped at the car costs (autokosten) (Calculated example)

A car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) has a value (waarde) of € 32.000,00, was first taken into use in 2019 and is used privately above 500 km. Total car costs including depreciation are € 5.500,00.

- Standard withdrawal (onttrekking): 22% x € 32.000,00 = € 7.040,00.
- The withdrawal is capped at the total car costs of € 5.500,00. The amount taken into account is € 5.500,00 (art. 3.20 lid 1 a and lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 8: the 500 km rule (Calculated example)

A trip registration (rittenregistratie) shows 460 private kilometres and 31.000 business kilometres in 2023.

- Private use is at most 500 km a year, so the withdrawal is nil and no private-use addition is added.
- Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) does not count as private use for this test. With a certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) the registration is assumed conclusive (sluitend) (art. 3.20 lid 1 and lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 9: zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto) first registered in 2023 (Calculated example)

A car with 0 g CO2 per kilometre, first registered in 2023, catalogue price € 40.000,00.

- Standard: 22% x € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00.
- Reduction for first registration in 2023: 6% x € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00, capped at € 1.800,00, so € 1.800,00.
- Withdrawal: € 8.800,00 minus € 1.800,00 = € 7.000,00.
- Control through the 16% up to € 30.000,00 reading: 16% x € 30.000,00 = € 4.800,00 plus 22% x € 10.000,00 = € 2.200,00, together € 7.000,00 (art. 3.20 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 10: zero-emission car first registered in 2022, used in 2023 (Calculated example)

The same car, catalogue price € 40.000,00, but first registered in 2022, so the 2022 reduction stays fixed for 60 months.

- Standard: 22% x € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00.
- Reduction: 6% x € 40.000,00 = € 2.400,00, capped at € 2.100,00, which is 16% up to € 35.000,00, so € 2.100,00.
- Withdrawal: € 8.800,00 minus € 2.100,00 = € 6.700,00.
- Control: 16% x € 35.000,00 = € 5.600,00 plus 22% x € 5.000,00 = € 1.100,00, together € 6.700,00 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 11: bicycle (fiets) of the business (Calculated example)

An e-bike of the business has a recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) of € 2.400,00 and is also used privately. The business bore the full € 2.400,00.

- Withdrawal: 7% x € 2.400,00 = € 168,00, capped at the costs borne of € 2.400,00, so € 168,00 (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 12: performance grant (prestatiebeurs) for vocational education (mbo) (Official example)

FISIN 2023 example. A vocational education programme ran from August 2013 to July 2017 and the diploma period (diplomatermijn) ends in August 2023. The fixed amounts are € 706,00, € 1.693,00 and € 988,00 against actual performance grant amounts of € 333,00, € 800,00 and € 467,00. For each period the actual performance grant was lower than the cap, so the lower amounts are used.

- Base: € 333,00 plus € 800,00 plus € 467,00 = € 1.600,00.
- Deduction after the threshold: € 1.600,00 minus € 250,00 = € 1.350,00 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten).

### Example 13: STAP-budget (Calculated example)

A training (scholing) costs € 1.200,00 including VAT (btw) and is claimed in one of the six application windows.

- Maximum subsidy: € 1.000,00 including VAT per training.
- If the maximum is granted, € 1.000,00 of the € 1.200,00 is covered and € 1.200,00 minus € 1.000,00 = € 200,00 remains for the taxpayer (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01).

### Example 14: workspace at home (werkruimte thuis) (Calculated example)

A taxpayer works from a loose desk in the living room and spends € 1.800,00 on a desk, a chair and a cabinet (kast).

- The workspace is not self-contained, so the costs are not deductible and € 0,00 of the € 1.800,00 is deducted.
- With its own entrance (opgang), own sanitary facilities and a space that could be rented to a third party, and with the income test met, the space would be self-contained and the costs would fall under the workspace rules (art. 3.16 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Example 15: telephone subscription at home (telefoonabonnement thuis) (Calculated example)

A private telephone subscription costs € 360,00 for the year and business calls through it cost € 75,00.

- The subscription of € 360,00 is not deductible.
- The € 75,00 of business calls is deductible (art. 3.16 lid 2 a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### Example 16: the € 450 rule (Calculated example)

Assume the laptop and printer belong to the business assets. A taxpayer buys a laptop of € 1.200,00 for the business and a printer of € 350,00. Both are used longer than a year.

- The laptop of € 1.200,00 is depreciated, taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account.
- The printer of € 350,00 is deducted in one go in the year of purchase.
- If the VAT on both is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte), the amounts are taken excluding VAT. Under the small businesses scheme (KOR), they are taken including VAT (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### Example 17: VAT (btw) correction on the private car (Calculated example)

A private car used for business has a catalogue price including VAT and bpm of € 30.000,00. There is no conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie), so all VAT is deducted during the year.

- Annual correction (jaarcorrectie): 1,5% x € 30.000,00 = € 450,00 in rubriek 1d.
- If only € 400,00 of VAT was deducted on use and maintenance during the year, the correction cannot exceed € 400,00 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### Example 18: fine (geldboete) (Calculated example)

The car-rental company passes on a traffic fine (verkeersboete) under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) of € 340,00 for a business trip.

- The € 340,00 is not deductible and cannot be booked as a business cost (art. 3.14 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1).
