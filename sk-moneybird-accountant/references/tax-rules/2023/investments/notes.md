## 5. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table, nil band: up to € 2.400 in 2022, up to € 2.600 in 2023 ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- KIA table, end of the 28% band: € 59.939 in 2022, € 63.716 in 2023 ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- KIA table, fixed amount and its ceiling: € 16.784 up to € 110.998 in 2022, € 17.841 up to € 117.991 in 2023 ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- KIA table, top amount: € 332.994 in 2022, € 353.973 in 2023 ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- KIA per-asset minimum: € 450 in both years, no change ([art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- EIA percentage: 45,5% in both years, no change ([art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount: € 128.000.000 in 2022, € 136.000.000 in 2023 ([art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The FISIN 2023 EIA sentence still printed € 128.000.000, see section 7.
- MIA percentages: 45%, 36% and 27% in both years, set from 1 January 2022 and unchanged for 2023 ([art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Rijksoverheid Belastingplan 2022](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel)).
- EIA and MIA per-asset minimum: € 2.500 in both years, no change ([art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).
- Vamil: 75% willekeurig in both years, no change ([art. 3.31](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Ordinary depreciation caps: goodwill 10% and other bedrijfsmiddelen 20% per year in both years, no change ([art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold: € 2.400 in 2022, € 2.600 in 2023 ([art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [FISIN 2022](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Bodemwaarde: WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another building in both years, no change ([art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). 2023 is the last year of the 50% rule, from 2024 one bodemwaarde applies, the WOZ-waarde.
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap: € 332.994 in 2022, € 353.973 in 2023, both read from the top of the KIA table through the URWA art. 8 cross-reference ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Temporary willekeurige afschrijving: new for assets with obligations or voortbrengingskosten in 2023, at most 50% once in the investment year and use before 1 January 2026 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). For 2022 no temporary scheme was found, marked unknown and UNVERIFIED, and the URWA text checked lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme either, an absence that one page cannot confirm ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The KIA is claimed by choice in the aangifte over the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen of the calendar year, per onderneming, so the annual investment list has to be complete and split per asset ([art. 3.41](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Keep the koopovereenkomst or the order date. The EIA, MIA and Vamil must be reported to RVO within 3 months after signing or ordering, and the Milieulijst valid on that date governs the application ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).
- The EIA must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).
- When an asset is not in use at the end of the year, only the amount paid enters the deduction, and the excess follows in later years. Note the full deduction at staking in the year of staking ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Track every disposal of an asset within five years after the start of the investment year, because the desinvesteringsbijtelling adds the same percentage as the investeringsaftrek taken over the transfer price. Withdrawal from the onderneming, a change of destination, a received vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding and staking within the term can count as a disposal ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).
- Monitor the two art. 3.47 lid 6 tests, being at least 25% of the investment amount paid within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and use within three years after the start of the investment year ([art. 3.47 lid 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- Buying an asset from your own BV in which you hold at least one third of the shares gives in principle no investeringsaftrek ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- For assets under the temporary 2023 scheme, record the date the asset was taken into use, since use before 1 January 2026 is required, and only 2023 costs count ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).
- Keep the WOZ-beschikking of the year in the building file, because building depreciation is capped by the bodemwaarde, which is the WOZ-waarde of the calendar year of the depreciation ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).
- These items land in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting for the sole trader, and the same 2023 figures are printed on the Belastingdienst page about the 2023 vennootschapsbelasting changes for the BV ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- EIA and MIA for a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker: UNVERIFIED. The rule that a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched RVO and Belastingdienst pages ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). What would settle it: the statutory text of [art. 3.42 and art. 3.42a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) or an RVO page that names which persons can use the EIA and the MIA.
- Settled conflict, EIA and MIA maximum investment amount 2023: € 136.000.000 against € 128.000.000. [art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and the [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023) page ("€ 136 miljoen") give € 136.000.000. The [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) chapter prints € 128.000.000 in its EIA sentence and € 136.000.000 in its MIA sentence. The 2023 value is settled at € 136.000.000 on the statute text and the Vpb page, and the FISIN EIA sentence is the losing source.
- Settled conflict, MIA percentages 2023: [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), the [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html) in force on 1 January 2022 and the [Rijksoverheid Belastingplan 2022](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel) news give 45%, 36% and 27%, with the increase "vanaf 1 januari 2022 verhoogd van 13,5%, 27% en 36% naar respectievelijk 27%, 36% en 45%". The [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) chapter prints 36%, 27% and 13,5%. The 2023 value is settled at 45%, 36% and 27%, and the FISIN chapter is the losing source. 2023 itself brought no MIA change.
- Reading of a cross-reference, not a printed number, UNVERIFIED: the starters willekeurige afschrijving cap of € 353.973 for 2023 rests on the [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0) wording "het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", read with the top of the KIA table ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). No primary page prints the cap as a number. What would settle it: a page that states the cap amount for the year directly.
- Year not stated on the page: the € 25 miljoen maximum per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil comes from the current RVO page, which does not state a year in its text ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). What would settle it: the Milieulijst or an RVO page for 2023 that prints the maximum for the year.
- Temporary willekeurige afschrijving before 2023: UNVERIFIED (absence). No temporary scheme was found for 2020, 2021 or 2022 ([Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), [URWA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The URWA text checked lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme, and the Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year. An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. What would settle it: a year page for 2020, 2021 or 2022 that names such a scheme, or a statement that none existed.
