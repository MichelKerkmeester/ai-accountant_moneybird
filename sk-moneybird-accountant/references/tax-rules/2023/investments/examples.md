## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for 2023 was found in the checked sources. Every example below is a `Calculated example` built from the figures in section 2.

### Calculated example: small-scale investment allowance (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek, KIA) in the 28% band

A sole trader invests € 60.000,00 in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in 2023. Each asset clears the € 450,00 floor and the whole amount qualifies for the small-scale investment allowance.

Step 1: € 60.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the second band applies.

Step 2: 28% of € 60.000,00 = 0,28 x € 60.000,00 = € 16.800,00.

Step 3: € 16.800,00 is lower than the fixed amount of € 17.841,00, so the small-scale investment allowance is € 16.800,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: small-scale investment allowance in the fixed band

The same sole trader invests € 100.000,00 instead.

Step 1: € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the fixed band applies.

Step 2: the small-scale investment allowance is € 17.841,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: small-scale investment allowance in the fourth band

A sole trader invests € 120.000,00.

Step 1: € 120.000,00 is above € 117.991,00 and at or below € 353.973,00, so the fourth band applies.

Step 2: the part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00.

Step 3: 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88.

Step 4: € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12. The small-scale investment allowance is € 17.689,12.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: no small-scale investment allowance above the top of the table

A sole trader invests € 360.000,00.

Step 1: € 360.000,00 is above € 353.973,00, so the table gives nil. The small-scale investment allowance is nil.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: small-scale investment allowance in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband)

A vof invests € 80.000,00 and one partner (vennoot) invests € 40.000,00 privately. Both amounts qualify for the small-scale investment allowance.

Step 1: the sum is € 80.000,00 plus € 40.000,00 = € 120.000,00.

Step 2: the table on € 120.000,00 gives € 17.841,00 minus 7,56% of € 2.009,00. The part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00. 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88. The table result is € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12.

Step 3: the own share is € 40.000,00 divided by € 120.000,00 = 1/3.

Step 4: the partner's small-scale investment allowance is € 17.689,12 x 1/3 = € 5.896,373333..., rounded down € 5.896,37.

Rule source: [art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: the € 450 floor in the small-scale investment allowance base

A sole trader invests in several business assets in 2023. The assets that clear the € 450,00 floor together come to € 50.000,00.

Step 1: art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b leaves every asset with an investment amount below € 450,00 out of the small-scale investment allowance base.

Step 2: the small-scale investment allowance base here is € 50.000,00. € 50.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the 28% band applies.

Step 3: 28% of € 50.000,00 = 0,28 x € 50.000,00 = € 14.000,00. The small-scale investment allowance is € 14.000,00.

Step 4: an asset below the floor does not enter the small-scale investment allowance base.

Source: [art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: Energy Investment Allowance (energie-investeringsaftrek, EIA) together with small-scale investment allowance

A sole trader buys a new machine of € 100.000,00 that is on the Energy List (Energielijst) and above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the Energy Investment Allowance is 45,5% of € 100.000,00 = 0,455 x € 100.000,00 = € 45.500,00.

Step 2: the same € 100.000,00 also enters the small-scale investment allowance. € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the small-scale investment allowance is € 17.841,00.

Step 3: the total investment deductions are € 45.500,00 plus € 17.841,00 = € 63.341,00.

Step 4: the Environmental Investment Allowance (milieu-investeringsaftrek, MIA) cannot be added on top of the Energy Investment Allowance, because Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance cannot be combined for one asset.

Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### Calculated example: Environmental Investment Allowance in the three categories

A trader buys three new assets of € 50.000,00 each, all on the Environmental List (Milieulijst) and each above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the asset with code F or G falls in category I (categorie I), Environmental Investment Allowance = 45% of € 50.000,00 = 0,45 x € 50.000,00 = € 22.500,00.

Step 2: the asset with code A or D falls in category II, Environmental Investment Allowance = 36% of € 50.000,00 = 0,36 x € 50.000,00 = € 18.000,00.

Step 3: the asset with code B or E falls in category III, Environmental Investment Allowance = 27% of € 50.000,00 = 0,27 x € 50.000,00 = € 13.500,00.

Step 4: the total Environmental Investment Allowance is € 22.500,00 plus € 18.000,00 plus € 13.500,00 = € 54.000,00.

Source: [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, Vamil) on a designated asset

A trader buys a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) of € 100.000,00 that has not been used before and is above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: 75% of € 100.000,00 = 0,75 x € 100.000,00 = € 75.000,00 may be depreciated at will.

Step 2: the remaining € 25.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) 2023

A trader buys a new machine in 2023 for € 40.000,00. The machine has not been used before. The obligation is entered in 2023 and the trader takes the machine into use on 1 November 2023, so it is in use at 31 December 2023.

Step 1: the asset is not used before, the obligation is in 2023, and the machine is in use before 1 January 2026. These conditions are met.

Step 2: the arbitrary depreciation is allowed once, for at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation. 50% of € 40.000,00 = 0,50 x € 40.000,00 = € 20.000,00 in 2023.

Step 3: because the machine is in use at 31 December 2023, the payment cap for an asset not yet in use does not reduce the deduction. If the machine were not in use at 31 December 2023, the deduction would be capped at the amount paid in 2023.

Step 4: the remaining € 20.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

### Calculated example: disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling)

A trader buys an asset in 2023 for € 20.000,00 and takes a small-scale investment allowance of 28% = 0,28 x € 20.000,00 = € 5.600,00. This is the only investment that year. The trader sells the asset later in 2023 for a transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) of € 15.000,00. The transfer price is above the yearly threshold of € 2.600,00, and the sale is within five years after the start of 2023.

Step 1: the disinvestment addition percentage equals the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) percentage taken, 28%.

Step 2: 28% of € 15.000,00 = 0,28 x € 15.000,00 = € 4.200,00.

Step 3: the transfer price of € 15.000,00 is below the investment amount (investeringsbedrag) of € 20.000,00 over which deduction (aftrek) was taken, so the whole € 4.200,00 is added to profit.

Source: [art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: minimum value (bodemwaarde) on a building

A trader owns a building with a WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) of € 300.000,00 and a book value of € 350.000,00.

Step 1: for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) the minimum value is the WOZ value of € 300.000,00. Depreciation may not bring the book value below that minimum value, so the total remaining amount above the minimum value is € 350.000,00 minus € 300.000,00 = € 50.000,00.

Step 2: for another building, such as an own-use building, the minimum value is 50% of € 300.000,00 = 0,50 x € 300.000,00 = € 150.000,00. The total remaining amount above that minimum value is € 350.000,00 minus € 150.000,00 = € 200.000,00.

Source: [art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

A trader buys a machine for € 30.000,00 and goodwill for € 30.000,00.

Step 1: the machine may be depreciated at 20% per year at most, so 0,20 x € 30.000,00 = € 6.000,00 per year, which means at least 5 years.

Step 2: the goodwill may be depreciated at 10% per year at most, so 0,10 x € 30.000,00 = € 3.000,00 per year, which means at least 10 years.

Source: [art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: start-up arbitrary depreciation within the cap

A starter with the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) invests € 80.000,00 in assets that qualify for investment deduction.

Step 1: the yearly cap is € 353.973,00 of total costs, read from the top of the small-scale investment allowance table.

Step 2: € 80.000,00 is below € 353.973,00, so the whole € 80.000,00 of costs stays within the cap.

Step 3: the starter may choose freely which assets to depreciate at will, and the € 2.600,00 minimum total investment of the small-scale investment allowance table does not apply.

Sources: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).
