## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for 2023 was found in the checked sources. Every example below is a `Calculated example` built from the figures in section 2.

### Calculated example: KIA in the 28% band

A sole trader invests € 60.000,00 in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2023. Each asset clears the € 450,00 floor and the whole amount qualifies for the KIA.

Step 1: € 60.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the second band applies.

Step 2: 28% of € 60.000,00 = 0,28 x € 60.000,00 = € 16.800,00.

Step 3: € 16.800,00 is lower than the fixed amount of € 17.841,00, so the KIA is € 16.800,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in the fixed band

The same sole trader invests € 100.000,00 instead.

Step 1: € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the fixed band applies.

Step 2: the KIA is € 17.841,00.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in the fourth band

A sole trader invests € 120.000,00.

Step 1: € 120.000,00 is above € 117.991,00 and at or below € 353.973,00, so the fourth band applies.

Step 2: the part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00.

Step 3: 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88.

Step 4: € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12. The KIA is € 17.689,12.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: no KIA above the top of the table

A sole trader invests € 360.000,00.

Step 1: € 360.000,00 is above € 353.973,00, so the table gives nil. The KIA is nil.

Table source: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

A vof invests € 80.000,00 and one vennoot invests € 40.000,00 privately. Both amounts qualify for the KIA.

Step 1: the sum is € 80.000,00 plus € 40.000,00 = € 120.000,00.

Step 2: the table on € 120.000,00 gives € 17.841,00 minus 7,56% of € 2.009,00. The part above € 117.991,00 is € 120.000,00 minus € 117.991,00 = € 2.009,00. 7,56% of € 2.009,00 = 0,0756 x € 2.009,00 = € 151,8804, rounded down € 151,88. The table result is € 17.841,00 minus € 151,88 = € 17.689,12.

Step 3: the own share is € 40.000,00 divided by € 120.000,00 = 1/3.

Step 4: the vennoot's KIA is € 17.689,12 x 1/3 = € 5.896,373333..., rounded down € 5.896,37.

Rule source: [art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: the € 450 floor in the KIA base

A sole trader invests in several bedrijfsmiddelen in 2023. The assets that clear the € 450,00 floor together come to € 50.000,00.

Step 1: art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b leaves every asset with an investment amount below € 450,00 out of the KIA base.

Step 2: the KIA base here is € 50.000,00. € 50.000,00 is above € 2.600,00 and at or below € 63.716,00, so the 28% band applies.

Step 3: 28% of € 50.000,00 = 0,28 x € 50.000,00 = € 14.000,00. The KIA is € 14.000,00.

Step 4: an asset below the floor does not enter the KIA base.

Source: [art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: EIA together with KIA

A sole trader buys a new machine of € 100.000,00 that is on the Energielijst and above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the EIA is 45,5% of € 100.000,00 = 0,455 x € 100.000,00 = € 45.500,00.

Step 2: the same € 100.000,00 also enters the KIA. € 100.000,00 is above € 63.716,00 and at or below € 117.991,00, so the KIA is € 17.841,00.

Step 3: the total investment deductions are € 45.500,00 plus € 17.841,00 = € 63.341,00.

Step 4: the MIA cannot be added on top of the EIA, because EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset.

Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### Calculated example: MIA in the three categories

A trader buys three new assets of € 50.000,00 each, all on the Milieulijst and each above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: the asset with code F or G falls in categorie I, MIA = 45% of € 50.000,00 = 0,45 x € 50.000,00 = € 22.500,00.

Step 2: the asset with code A or D falls in categorie II, MIA = 36% of € 50.000,00 = 0,36 x € 50.000,00 = € 18.000,00.

Step 3: the asset with code B or E falls in categorie III, MIA = 27% of € 50.000,00 = 0,27 x € 50.000,00 = € 13.500,00.

Step 4: the total MIA is € 22.500,00 plus € 18.000,00 plus € 13.500,00 = € 54.000,00.

Source: [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: Vamil on a designated asset

A trader buys a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel of € 100.000,00 that has not been used before and is above the € 2.500,00 floor.

Step 1: 75% of € 100.000,00 = 0,75 x € 100.000,00 = € 75.000,00 may be depreciated at will.

Step 2: the remaining € 25.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023

A trader buys a new machine in 2023 for € 40.000,00. The machine has not been used before. The obligation is entered in 2023 and the trader takes the machine into use on 1 November 2023, so it is in use at 31 December 2023.

Step 1: the asset is not used before, the obligation is in 2023, and the machine is in use before 1 January 2026. These conditions are met.

Step 2: the random depreciation is allowed once, for at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation. 50% of € 40.000,00 = 0,50 x € 40.000,00 = € 20.000,00 in 2023.

Step 3: because the machine is in use at 31 December 2023, the payment cap for an asset not yet in use does not reduce the deduction. If the machine were not in use at 31 December 2023, the deduction would be capped at the amount paid in 2023.

Step 4: the remaining € 20.000,00 follows the ordinary depreciation rules.

Source: [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

### Calculated example: desinvesteringsbijtelling

A trader buys an asset in 2023 for € 20.000,00 and takes a KIA of 28% = 0,28 x € 20.000,00 = € 5.600,00. This is the only investment that year. The trader sells the asset later in 2023 for a transfer price of € 15.000,00. The transfer price is above the yearly threshold of € 2.600,00, and the sale is within five years after the start of 2023.

Step 1: the bijtelling percentage equals the investeringsaftrek percentage taken, 28%.

Step 2: 28% of € 15.000,00 = 0,28 x € 15.000,00 = € 4.200,00.

Step 3: the transfer price of € 15.000,00 is below the investment amount of € 20.000,00 over which aftrek was taken, so the whole € 4.200,00 is added to profit.

Source: [art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: bodemwaarde on a building

A trader owns a building with a WOZ-waarde of € 300.000,00 and a book value of € 350.000,00.

Step 1: for a gebouw ter belegging the bodemwaarde is the WOZ-waarde of € 300.000,00. Depreciation may not bring the book value below that bodemwaarde, so the total remaining amount above the bodemwaarde is € 350.000,00 minus € 300.000,00 = € 50.000,00.

Step 2: for another building, such as an own-use building, the bodemwaarde is 50% of € 300.000,00 = 0,50 x € 300.000,00 = € 150.000,00. The total remaining amount above that bodemwaarde is € 350.000,00 minus € 150.000,00 = € 200.000,00.

Source: [art. 3.30a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

A trader buys a machine for € 30.000,00 and goodwill for € 30.000,00.

Step 1: the machine may be depreciated at 20% per year at most, so 0,20 x € 30.000,00 = € 6.000,00 per year, which means at least 5 years.

Step 2: the goodwill may be depreciated at 10% per year at most, so 0,10 x € 30.000,00 = € 3.000,00 per year, which means at least 10 years.

Source: [art. 3.30 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Calculated example: starters willekeurige afschrijving within the cap

A starter with the startersaftrek invests € 80.000,00 in assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek.

Step 1: the yearly cap is € 353.973,00 of total costs, read from the top of the KIA table.

Step 2: € 80.000,00 is below € 353.973,00, so the whole € 80.000,00 of costs stays within the cap.

Step 3: the starter may choose freely which assets to depreciate at will, and the € 2.600,00 minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply.

Sources: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).
