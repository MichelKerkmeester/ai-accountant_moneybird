## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the investment deductions and depreciation rules for tax year 2023: the small-scale investment allowance (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), the Energy Investment Allowance (energie-investeringsaftrek), the Environmental Investment Allowance (milieu-investeringsaftrek), the arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen, Vamil), ordinary depreciation, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) for buildings, the arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for start-ups and the temporary 2023 scheme, and the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling). The most important 2023 points are the wider small-scale investment allowance (KIA) table (nil up to € 2.600 and a top of € 353.973), the Energy Investment Allowance (EIA) at 45,5%, the Environmental Investment Allowance (MIA) at 45%, 36% and 27%, the arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments at 75% arbitrary depreciation, and a temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) of at most 50% for assets with 2023 costs that are in use before 1 January 2026. The 2023 rules are enacted law, checked against the 2023 statute text and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages. 2023 is the last year in which the minimum value of an own-use building is 50% of the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde), from 2024 one minimum value applies.
