## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Small-scale investment allowance (KIA) table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The small-scale investment allowance is a deduction per business (onderneming), claimed by choice in the return (aangifte), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year 2023 ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). The table has five bands. A total up to € 2.600 gives no small-scale investment allowance. A total above € 2.600 and up to € 63.716 gives 28% of the investment. A total above € 63.716 and up to € 117.991 gives the fixed amount of € 17.841. A total above € 117.991 and up to € 353.973 gives € 17.841 minus 7,56% of the part above € 117.991. A total above € 353.973 gives no small-scale investment allowance ([art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The same 2023 figures are printed on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page about the 2023 corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) changes, which states that investments can get Environmental Investment Allowance (MIA) and small-scale investment allowance together, that Environmental Investment Allowance and Energy Investment Allowance (EIA) cannot be combined and that the Energy Investment Allowance must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The small-scale investment allowance cannot be used for the assets excluded by art. 3.45, and each separate asset must clear the € 450 floor ([art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Small-scale investment allowance window as printed by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrases the 2023 small-scale investment allowance window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). An investment total below € 2.601 or above € 353.973 therefore gives no small-scale investment allowance. This window matches the table, which opens above € 2.600 and closes at € 353.973.

### Small-scale investment allowance per-asset minimum

Art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes assets with an investment amount below € 450 from the small-scale investment allowance ([art. 3.45 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The same amount appears in the FISIN chapter for 2023 ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). An asset below the floor therefore does not enter the small-scale investment allowance base, while it does stay part of the ordinary business assets and keeps its own depreciation schedule.

### Small-scale investment allowance in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) (art. 3.41 lid 3)

In a partnership the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum ([art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration small-scale investment allowance page adds that each firm partner (firmant) or partner (vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the profit (winst), including any excess profit (overwinst), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). The pro rata outcome is the amount each participant claims in the return.

### Assets excluded from every investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (art. 3.45 lid 1)

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes, for every investment deduction, assets intended mainly for use in a business whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting) (onderdeel a), land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering) (onderdeel b), dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen) (onderdeel c), passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (onderdeel d), vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (onderdeel e), securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies) (onderdeel f), and animals (dieren) (onderdeel g) ([art. 3.45 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). These exclusions hold for the small-scale investment allowance, the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance.

### Further exclusions (art. 3.45 lid 2, lid 3, lid 4, lid 5)

Subsection 2 adds two KIA-only exclusions, being assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (part a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (part b). Subsection 3 disapplies subsection 1 parts c and d for the Environmental Investment Allowance, so dwellings and passenger cars can still earn Environmental Investment Allowance. Subsection 4 adds two exclusions for the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance, being assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (part a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (part b). Subsection 5 lets the Minister disapply subsection 1 part a under 2° and subsection 4 part a ([art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). RVO phrases the Environmental Investment Allowance and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments (Vamil) floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### Energy Investment Allowance percentage

The Energy Investment Allowance is 45,5% of the investment amount for 2023 ([art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The asset must be on the Energy List (Energielijst), and the Energy Investment Allowance must be reported to RVO in advance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). The Energy Investment Allowance can be combined with the small-scale investment allowance for the same asset, but not with the Environmental Investment Allowance ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance maximum investment amount

The 2023 maximum investment amount for the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance is € 136.000.000 ([art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration prints "€ 136 miljoen" on the 2023 Corporate income tax page ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The FISIN chapter for 2023 prints € 128.000.000 in its Energy Investment Allowance sentence and € 136.000.000 in its Environmental Investment Allowance sentence ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The 2023 value is settled at € 136.000.000 on the statute text and the Corporate income tax page, see section 7.

### Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance per-asset minimum

Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b sets a floor of € 2.500 per asset for the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance ([art. 3.45 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). RVO applies the same floor to the arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments with the phrase "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).

### Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance conditions

Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify for the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, as can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer, as production costs (voortbrengingskosten). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). Environmental Investment Allowance is not available for costs that already received Energy Investment Allowance, and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments remains possible in that case. Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance cannot be combined for one asset. The Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance can each be combined with the small-scale investment allowance. The Energy Investment Allowance asset must be on the Energy List and the Environmental Investment Allowance and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments asset on the Environmental List ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). RVO states a maximum of € 25 million (miljoen) per asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for Environmental Investment Allowance and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). A rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use Energy Investment Allowance or Environmental Investment Allowance was not found on the pages checked, see section 7.

### Environmental Investment Allowance percentages and Environmental List codes

From 1 January 2022 the Environmental List maps codes starting with F or G to category (categorie) I with 45%, codes starting with A or D to category II with 36%, and codes starting with B or E to category III with 27% ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). These percentages also hold for 2023, so 2023 brought no Environmental Investment Allowance change. The FISIN chapter for 2023 prints 36%, 27% and 13,5%, which is the losing source, see section 7.

### Arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments (Vamil)

The costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", and the rest follows the normal schedule ([art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets not yet common in the Netherlands, not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated ([art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). RVO states that the total tax benefit of Environmental Investment Allowance and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### RVO notification deadline for Energy Investment Allowance, Environmental Investment Allowance and arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments

Apply within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order for acquisition costs (aanschafkosten), within 3 months after the quarter in which production costs were made, and for environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Environmental List valid on the signing or order date applies ([RVO MIA en Vamil](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The FISIN chapters for 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)", and the 2023 Corporate income tax page states that the Energy Investment Allowance must be reported to RVO in advance ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### Excluded obligations (art. 3.46)

There is no investment deduction for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (part a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (part b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (part c), and a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam) and that entity (part d). The Minister can disapply subsection 1 under subsection 2, which is the exemption (ontheffing) route ([art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (art. 3.44)

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (subsection 1). At cessation (staking) of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation (subsection 2). This applies to the small-scale investment allowance, the Energy Investment Allowance and the Environmental Investment Allowance ([art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold

The 2023 threshold for the disinvestment addition is € 2.600 ([art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage taken is added to profit (subsection 1). This applies only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken (subsection 2). Subsection 3 treats as disposal the withdrawal of the good from the business (part a), a change of destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a for the small-scale investment allowance or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a for the Energy Investment Allowance and Environmental Investment Allowance (part b), and a change bringing it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (part c). In those cases the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) counts as transfer price (subsection 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a reduction (vermindering), refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), counts as a disposal for that amount (subsection 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (subsection 6). The Minister may disapply subsection 3 part c and subsection 6 (subsection 7) ([art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the small-scale investment allowance, not for the Energy Investment Allowance or the Environmental Investment Allowance, and adds cessation of part or all of the business within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Ordinary depreciation caps

Yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten) or production costs attributable to the year (subsection 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost is allowed for goodwill and at most 20% for other business assets, so at least 10 and 5 years (subsection 2). Production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) may be written off at once in the year of production (voortbrenging) (subsection 3). Objects of small value (voorwerpen van geringe waarde) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or production (subsection 4) ([art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Minimum value for buildings

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the minimum value, and at most for the difference (subsection 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) are one asset, and detachable machinery (werktuigen) are separate (subsection 2). For 2023 the minimum value is the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another, own-use building (subsection 3). 2023 is the last year of the 50% rule, from 2024 one minimum value applies, the WOZ value of the building (gebouw). The WOZ value is the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (subsection 4) ([art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)).

### Start-up arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) (URWA art. 7 to 9)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001, arbitrary depreciation on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship ([art. 3.34](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), applies. Subsection 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Subsection 3 limits the scheme to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit (winst) as an entrepreneur (ondernemer). Art. 8 allows arbitrary depreciation "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 sets ten years for the sea-going vessels (zeeschepen) of art. 10 ([URWA art. 7 to 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the scheme is for a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), civil-law partnership (maatschap), cv or vof, that the starter's deduction conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investment deduction count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the small-scale investment allowance table does not apply ([Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). A revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value, with the difference as profit, must take place within 5 years, or 10 years for sea-going vessels, if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf) ([Belastingdienst startende ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). The cap amount for 2023 is € 353.973, the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table, read from the URWA art. 8 cross-reference. This is a reading of a cross-reference, no primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7.

### Temporary arbitrary depreciation 2023 (URWA art. 13 to 15)

Art. 13 lid 1 designates assets not used before, for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in calendar year 2023 and which are taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026. Subsection 2 excludes buildings (gebouwen), ships (schepen), aircraft (vliegtuigen), mopeds (bromfietsen), motorcycles (motorrijwielen), passenger cars except for commercial road transport (beroepsvervoer over de weg) or 0 grams CO2 per km, intangible assets, animals, assets mainly made available to third parties with short rental to successive hirers excepted, public roads and paths, and assets already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. Subsection 3 moves the 1 January 2026 date by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14 allows the arbitrary depreciation once, at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15 sets the end of the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 at 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year, and that the scheme covers both the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) and the corporate income tax ([Belastingdienst Vpb 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)).
