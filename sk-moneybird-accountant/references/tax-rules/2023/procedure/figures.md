## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2023 for a sole trader and for a BV where a rule applies to a BV: the income tax filing deadline and deferral (uitstel), the VAT (btw) filing and payment calendar, the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and registration at KVK and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst). The law for 2023 is enacted, so every Status tag below is enacted. The two 2023 events with the widest effect are the tax interest rate moving from 4% to 6% on 1 July 2023 and the new interest stop (rentestop) for requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) from 1 January 2023, which is not yet in force for VAT. The return for tax year 2023 is filed in 2024, so the filing deadline, the final assessment (definitieve aanslag) window and the tax interest on tax year 2023 all run in 2024 and later.
