## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Official example, tax interest (belastingrente) when the 2023 return arrives after 1 May

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 3 May 2024 with € 1.000 to pay. The assessment (aanslag) is dated 4 December 2024 and takes the return over unchanged.

Steps:
1. The tax interest period starts on 1 July 2024.
2. Because the assessment follows the return unchanged, the period ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. That is 13 September 2024.
3. The 19-week point is earlier than 6 weeks after the assessment date, so the period ends on 13 September 2024.
4. Under the 30/360 count, July has 30 days, August has 30 days and September has 13 days. Total 73 days.
5. Tax interest is 73/360 x 7,5% x € 1.000 = € 15,21, rounded down to € 15.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, same facts with an earlier assessment

Facts: same return and same amount, but the assessment is dated 27 June 2024.

Steps:
1. The tax interest period starts on 1 July 2024.
2. The assessment follows the return unchanged, so the period ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt. That cap is 13 September 2024.
3. Six weeks after the assessment date is 9 August 2024, which is earlier than the cap. So the period ends on 9 August 2024.
4. Under the 30/360 count, July has 30 days and August has 9 days. Total 39 days.
5. Tax interest is 39/360 x 7,5% x € 1.000 = € 8,13, rounded down to € 8.
6. An earlier assessment lowers the tax interest because it shortens the period.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, the assessment deviates from the return

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 28 April 2024 with too low an income. The assessment of € 2.500 extra is dated 11 December 2024.

Steps:
1. Tax interest is charged because the assessment deviates from the return, even though the return was filed before 1 May 2024.
2. The period starts on 1 July 2024.
3. The period ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, on 22 January 2025.
4. Under the 30/360 count, the period has 180 days in 2024 and 22 days in 2025, so 202 days.
5. The rate is 7,5% for 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025.
6. 180 days at 7,5%: 180/360 x 7,5% x € 2.500 = € 93,75, rounded down to € 93.
7. 22 days at 6,5%: 22/360 x 6,5% x € 2.500 = € 9,93, rounded down to € 9.
8. Total tax interest is € 93 + € 9 = € 102.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, no tax interest

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed on 30 April 2024 and is taken over unchanged.

Steps:
1. No tax interest is charged because the return arrived before 1 May 2024 and the assessment follows it unchanged.
2. Filing early removes the tax interest risk even though the period would otherwise have started on 1 July 2024.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Official example, definitive assessment 2023

Facts: the return for 2023 is filed before 1 May 2024.

Steps:
1. The definitive assessment can be set until 31 December 2026 at the latest.
2. The deferral (uitstel) from 1 May 2024 to 1 September 2024 is 4 months.
3. The term is extended by the duration of the deferral, so the latest date becomes 30 April 2027.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### Official example, record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) term start

Facts: the records (administratie) contains a 4-year lease.

Steps:
1. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual.
2. The lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### Calculated example, additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) for a 2023 return with deferral

Facts: the return for tax year 2023 is filed with deferral, so the deadline is 1 September 2024 instead of 1 May 2024.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. The 5-year additional assessment period runs from the moment the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) counts from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2024 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
2. 5 years from 1 January 2024 runs to 31 December 2028.
3. The deferral from 1 May 2024 to 1 September 2024 is 4 months, and the term is extended by the duration of the deferral.
4. 31 December 2028 plus 4 months is 30 April 2029.

This matches the method of the official example, where tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral gave 31 May 2022 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). An error-based additional assessment (navordering) has a shorter term of 2 years after the assessment or decision ([AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Calculated example, payment schedule of a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) dated in the tax year

Facts: a provisional assessment for 2023 is dated 15 March 2023.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. Months remaining after the month of the date (March) are April to December, which is 9 months ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. The number of equal monthly instalments (termijnen) is therefore 9.
3. The first instalment (termijn) is one month after the date, so 15 April 2023.

### Calculated example, payment schedule of a provisional assessment dated before the tax year

Facts: a provisional assessment for 2023 is dated 20 December 2022.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. The first instalment falls at the end of January 2023.

### Calculated example, payment term of an assessment

Facts: an income tax assessment (aanslag IB) is dated 1 September 2024.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. An assessment is payable 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet) ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
2. 6 weeks is 42 days.
3. 1 September 2024 plus 29 days is 30 September 2024, and 13 more days brings the end at 13 October 2024.

### Calculated example, VAT (btw) deadlines in 2023

Facts: three different filers.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. A quarterly filer: the Q2 2023 return and payment must both be received by 31 July 2023 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

A monthly filer: March 2023 by 30 April 2023 and April 2023 by 31 May 2023 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

A yearly filer: the 2023 return by 31 March 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### Calculated example, KVK registration window

Facts: a business starts on 1 June 2023.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. The window is two weeks, starting one week before and ending one week after the start of the business ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)).
2. One week before 1 June 2023 is 25 May 2023.
3. One week after 1 June 2023 is 8 June 2023.
4. So the statement (opgave) must be made between 25 May 2023 and 8 June 2023. KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately within that window ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)).

### Calculated example, VAT tax interest on a 2023 correction

Facts: an error is found in a 2023 VAT period.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. Interest on a VAT supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) runs from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2024 for a 2023 period ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
2. It ends 14 days after the date of the supplementary assessment ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
3. A voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year gives no tax interest, so a VAT correction return (suppletie) filed before 1 April 2024 avoids tax interest for a 2023 period ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
4. A supplementary assessment dated 15 November 2024 therefore carries tax interest over 1 January 2024 to 29 November 2024, at the rate for 2024 of 7,5% ([rate](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The checked VAT source does not state the 30/360 day count for VAT, so no euro amount is worked out here.

### Calculated example, income tax default fine (verzuimboete) for 2023

Facts: the 2023 return is not filed after the formal demand (aanmaning) term.

Steps, built from the section 2 figures:
1. The route is a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand with 10 working days (werkdagen), then the default fine ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)).
2. The 2023 default fine is € 385, with a maximum of € 5.514 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
