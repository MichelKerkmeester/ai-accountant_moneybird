## 2. CHANGES FROM 2022

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Tax interest (Belastingrente) rate: 2022 had 4% for the whole year. In 2023 the rate is 4% to 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).
- Tax interest per tax year: tax interest on the tax year 2022 starts 1 July 2023 at 6%, tax interest on the tax year 2023 starts 1 July 2024 at 7,5% and moves to 6,5% from 1 January 2025 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).
- New in 2023: the interest stop (rentestop) for requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) applies from 1 January 2023, tax interest ends at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, and the extension is not in force for VAT (btw) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).
- Assessment threshold (Aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens): 2022 was € 48 and € 15 in the higher than wording and € 49 and € 16 under the older wording. For 2023 it is € 51 and € 16 in the higher than wording and € 52 and € 17 under the older wording ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- income tax (IB) filing deadline per year: the 2022 return was due before 1 May 2023 with deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2023, the 2023 return is due before 1 May 2024 with deferral to 1 September 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Definitive assessment (aanslag) window: for 2022 the latest dates were 31 December 2025, or 30 April 2026 with deferral. For 2023 they are 31 December 2026, or 30 April 2027 with deferral ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2022 could be filed until 31 December 2027, tax year 2023 until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).
- Request for a reduction (verzoek om vermindering): for the assessment 2023 the online route is open until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).
- VAT monthly dates: 2022 ran from December 2021 with 31 January 2022 through December 2022 with 31 January 2023. 2023 runs from December 2022 with 31 January 2023 through December 2023 with 31 January 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).
- VAT quarterly dates: 2022 ran from Q4 2021 with 31 January 2022 through Q4 2022 with 31 January 2023. 2023 runs from Q4 2022 with 31 January 2023 through Q4 2023 with 31 January 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).
- VAT yearly return: the 2022 return was due by 31 March 2023, the 2023 return by 31 March 2024 ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).
- Unchanged between 2022 and 2023: the VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) is € 68 in both years ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220224/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)), and the income tax penalties (boetes) are € 385, up to € 5.514, € 2.757 and up to € 5.514 in both years ([2022 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [2023 source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Diarise the filing date from the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). The filing period opens on 1 March and the 2023 return is due before 1 May 2024 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), [source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Request deferral (uitstel) before the filing date if the records (administratie) is not ready. It gives 4 months extra to 1 September 2024 and usually means tax interest (belastingrente) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)).
- File and pay each VAT (btw) period by the date in the table, return and payment together, and note the 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance) and the € 68 penalty (boete) for 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).
- Book the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) instalments (termijnen) as payments during 2023. The amounts paid or received are settled in the return for 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). The payment rhythm follows Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9 ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).
- Keep the records 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years and One Stop Shop data 10 years, with the term starting when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). The seven-year rule follows art. 52 lid 4 AWR ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).
- Keep the assessment notices (aanslagbiljetten) and diarise 6 weeks after the assessment (aanslag) date for an objection (bezwaar) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)).
- Treat a 2023 correction as an additional assessment (navordering) risk for 5 years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, with 12 years for income or assets abroad, while a voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
- Register the business (onderneming) with KVK within the two-week window and let KVK pass the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst). Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen) ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).
- For a BV the same AWR and Collection of State Taxes Act terms apply to taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen), and the checked material names Corporate income tax (Vpb) in the 6-month term for requesting an invitation to file a return ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

### Wording of the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and the refund threshold (teruggaafgrens)

The current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold, and prints € 51 and € 16 for 2023 ([current page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). The April 2023 capture uses equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and prints € 52 and € 17 for 2023 ([April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading in the checked material, so this is a wording difference rather than a value conflict. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page or law text that states the 2023 threshold (grens) in one fixed wording would settle it fully.

### KVK term for the VAT number (btw-number)

The KVK page carries both wordings for the term in which the VAT number arrives, two weeks and ten working days (werkdagen) ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). Ten working days equals two calendar weeks, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that registration in its administration takes at most 10 working days ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). The two do not differ in substance. A single rewording of the KVK page would settle the presentation.

### Interest stop (rentestop) not in force for VAT (btw)

The interest stop extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). A technical change that makes the rule feasible for VAT would settle it. Until then a requested VAT supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) does not get the 10-week end point.

No other 2023 value in this file is marked UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown in the checked material.
