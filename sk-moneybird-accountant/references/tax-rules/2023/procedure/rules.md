## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2023

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Income tax filing period (aangifteperiode inkomstenbelasting)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) opens the income tax filing period on 1 March each year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). The return for tax year 2023 is the return filed in 2024, and its deadline is the date printed in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) and set out in the next subsection. The filing period runs from 1 March in the filing year to that deadline.

### Income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) 2023 filing deadline (aangiftetermijn)

The return for tax year 2023 must arrive before 1 May 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). The general rule is that the return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter, which is often 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). With a granted deferral (uitstel) the 2023 return is due before 1 September 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### Deferral rules for the income tax return

Deferral must be requested before the filing date (aangiftedatum), in the annual period before 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, which moves a 1 May deadline to 1 September ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The form can be used only for the first deferral request ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) must request deferral separately ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente) is charged, even when the return is later taken over unchanged ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). Art. 9 lid 2 AWR gives the tax inspector (inspecteur) the power to extend the filing term under conditions, which is the statutory basis for this deferral ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Deferral channel

Deferral can also be requested online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May, and that route gives deferral to 1 September and is open only to someone who received a filing invitation letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The deferral page says online is not possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Read in full, that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Both texts are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text, so the practical reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date and the paper form otherwise.

### AWR art. 9 filing term

For taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the tax inspector of at least one month after the invitation ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Art. 9 lid 2 lets the tax inspector extend that term under conditions ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The text of art. 9 is the same in the version valid on 1 January 2020 and the version valid on 1 January 2026 ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### Definitive assessment (aanslag) 2023

The definitive assessment for tax year 2023 can be set until 31 December 2026 at the latest when the return is filed before 1 May 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). With deferral to 1 September 2024 the latest date is 30 April 2027 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). These are the official examples on the page. Filing the return does not end the period, the period only limits the moment the assessment can be imposed.

### Definitive assessment term

Art. 11 lid 3 AWR says the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For tax year 2023 that counting produces the two dates in the previous subsection.

### Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2023

The current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For 2023 the current page prints € 51 and € 16 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Pages up to the 2023 captures used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold (grens) one euro higher, so € 52 and € 17 for 2023 ([April 2023 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, which is the reading in the checked material. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before year end count toward the threshold, a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### Filing without an invitation, tax year 2023

Without a filing invitation letter a return can still be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). For tax year 2023 that date is 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). This route matters when no filing invitation letter arrived but a refund or a correction is still wanted.

### Objection (bezwaar)

Objection is possible within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The motivation can follow later if the objection asks for that ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). There is no objection against a provisional assessment income tax, only a change ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). After the objection term a request for an ex officio reduction (verzoek om ambtshalve vermindering) is possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

### Request for a reduction of the assessment (verzoek om vermindering aanslag) 2023

A request for a reduction can be filed online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)). For the assessment 2023 the online route is open until 31 December 2028 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)). Otherwise the request goes by letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

### Provisional assessment

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return, and changing it is not mandatory ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Reasons to change include buying a home, a change in mortgage interest (hypotheekrente), higher or lower income, a change in business profit and profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). An entrepreneur changes the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Paid or received amounts are settled in the return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after the return is filed ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)).

### Tax interest rates

For all taxes except Corporate income tax (Vpb) and allowances (toeslagen) the rate is 4% for the period to 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). Amounts over tax year 2023 that are still open in later years follow the later rates, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). So a 2023 debt can carry two or more rates across its life, and the period is split at each rate change.

### Tax interest on tax year 2023 income tax

Tax interest on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The period starts on 1 July after the tax year, also when deferral was granted ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged it ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). For an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). For tax year 2023 the rate is 7,5% from 1 July 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). There is no tax interest when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).

### Tax interest on VAT (btw)

Tax interest on VAT is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). On late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). On a supplementary assessment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). No tax interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or for a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### Interest stop (rentestop) for requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen)

The interest stop extension to requested supplementary assessments applies from 1 January 2023, so tax interest ends at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). The extension is not yet in force (in werking) for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### Additional assessment (navordering) AWR art. 16

Art. 16 lid 1 AWR allows an additional assessment on a new fact, which cannot be used if it was known or reasonably apparent to the tax inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw) ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text as on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). Subsection 2 adds the case where a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (voorlopige teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (voorlopige verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and the case of an error (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), which is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Subsection 3 sets the term at 5 years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by deferral, and for an error-based (fout) additional assessment at 2 years after the assessment or decision ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Subsection 4 sets 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Subsection 5 extends the term by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the official example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, where additional assessment was still possible until 31 May 2022 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).

### Voluntary correction

A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)). For a 2023 return that means a correction made in time reduces the risk of both an additional assessment and a penalty.

### Payment terms

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9 sets the payment terms, with the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01)). An assessment (aanslag) is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet) ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) is payable 1 month after its date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) is payable 14 days after its date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). An income tax provisional assessment dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, with the first instalment (termijn) one month after the date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)).

### VAT filing and payment rule

Art. 10 lid 2 AWR sets the VAT filing term at a term chosen by the tax inspector of at least one month after the end of the period (tijdvak) ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Art. 19 lid 1 AWR sets the payment at one month after the end of the period ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give, for every year, the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together for monthly and quarterly filers, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). Return and payment must both be received by that date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).

### VAT software submission

Software can submit a VAT return (btw-aangifte) from the 24th of the month before the period ends ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). That opening date is earlier than the deadline and does not change the deadline itself.

### VAT monthly dates 2023

For monthly filers the 2023 table gives the following last days for return and payment together: December 2022 on 31 January 2023, January on 28 February 2023, February on 31 March 2023, March on 30 April 2023, April on 31 May 2023, May on 30 June 2023, June on 31 July 2023, July on 31 August 2023, August on 30 September 2023, September on 31 October 2023, October on 30 November 2023, November on 31 December 2023 and December 2023 on 31 January 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### VAT quarterly dates 2023

For quarterly filers the 2023 table gives Q4 2022 on 31 January 2023, Q1 2023 on 30 April 2023, Q2 2023 on 31 July 2023, Q3 2023 on 31 October 2023 and Q4 2023 on 31 January 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### VAT yearly return 2023

A yearly VAT return for 2023 must be filed and paid by 31 March 2024 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte)).

### VAT deferral

Deferral for a VAT return (btw-aangifte) is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), meaning a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit) ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)). The request goes in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)). Even when deferral is granted, a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and a penalty remain possible ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)).

### VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2023

For 2023 there are 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance) and then a penalty of € 68 for a missing or late VAT return ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). The cross-year rule keeps € 68 for 2020 to 2024 and raises it to € 82 for 2025 and 2026 ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).

### Income tax penalties (ib boetes) 2023

The 2023 amounts are a default fine (verzuimboete) of € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, and a payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) of up to € 5.514 ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). The source lists them in this form and does not split them further for this file.

### Income tax missed deadline route

After the filing deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen) to comply ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can also stop allowances and the provisional assessment ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Filing no return at all gives an ex officio (ambtshalve) assessment plus a default fine ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty (vergrijpboete) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent, also with an additional assessment ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)).

### Penalty for gross negligence or intent and no-penalty rules

The penalty is 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), in box 3 75% and 150%, and in aggravated cases 100% and 300% ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). No penalty is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). A return can be requested within 6 months after the tax debt arose for income tax and Corporate income tax, with 2 weeks leniency ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### Record-retention obligation (Bewaarplicht)

Art. 52 lid 4 AWR requires persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) to keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), same text as on 1 January 2020, [AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01)). Subsection 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Subsection 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager), except for the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). A sole trader with records (administratie) for 2023 therefore falls under the seven-year rule in all these forms.

### Record-retention obligation terms

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). Basic records are the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable (debiteuren) records (debiteurenadministratie) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), the payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).

### KVK registration returns (opgaven)

Under art. 20 lid 1 Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). Other required returns are made within one week after the fact ([source](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). For a business that starts in 2023 this window is the trigger for both the KVK registration and, through the data transfer, the tax registration.

### KVK practical

KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce (kamer van koophandel) number (KVK-nummer) immediately when registration happens within the two-week window ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead ([source](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). This lets a starter arrange the identity check and appointment before the start date and still register within the window.

### Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration

KVK passes the registration data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration is needed ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used ([source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).
