#### Box 1 table structure and the national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen)

Verified. Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) art. 2.10 lid 1 holds only the income-tax part. The first statutory band is income tax only (9,70% in 2020, 9,45% in 2021, 9,42% in 2022, 9,28% in 2023, 9,32% in 2024, 8,17% in 2025, 8,10% in 2026). The national insurance contributions of 27,65% (AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10%, Wlz 9,65%) are charged over the contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to a ceiling that equals the end of that first band. The published combined first-bracket rate is the income-tax part plus 27,65%. Above the premium ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the combined rate, which is why 2020 to 2024 show one combined rate up to the top boundary. The ceiling is € 34.712 (2020), € 35.129 (2021), € 35.472 (2022), € 37.149 (2023), € 38.098 (2024), € 38.441 (2025) and € 38.883 (2026), and for people born before 1 January 1946 it is € 35.375, € 35.941, € 36.409, € 38.703, € 40.021, € 40.502 and € 41.123. Art. 2.10a holds the table for people born before 1 January 1946 with that higher first boundary. Once the AOW age is reached no AOW premium is due, which is why the AOW-age first-bracket rate is lower. #### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (art. 2.10 lid 2)

Verified. When deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) have been deducted, the tax under lid 1 is raised by a percentage of the amount by which the taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus those items (posten) exceeds the top bracket boundary, capped at that percentage of the deducted items. The percentage is 3,5% (2020), 6,5% (2021), 9,5% (2022), 12,57% (2023), 12,53% (2024), 12,02% (2025), 11,94% (2026) and 11,91% in the enacted 2027 text. The effective deduction value is 49,50% minus the percentage. The items are the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (Maintenance obligations (onderhoudsverplichtingen) including spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie), weekend expenses for disabled people (weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten), deductible (aftrekbare) gifts (giften), specific healthcare costs (specifieke zorgkosten), remaining personal deductions from preceding years (restant persoonsgebonden aftrek voorgaande jaren)), the deduction for costs of an owner-occupied home (aftrek kosten eigen woning) (mortgage interest (hypotheekrente), periodic ground rent (periodieke erfpacht), right of superficies (opstal) of long leasehold (beklemming), costs of taking out a mortgage (afsluiten hypotheek), residual debt costs (restschuldkosten)), the tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) (self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), deduction for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek), SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling)) and the exemption (vrijstelling) for making available (terbeschikkingstelling) of assets (bezittingen). It is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (aanslag). #### Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) into box 1

Verified in part. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit (winst) as entrepreneur (ondernemer) reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Taxable income from work and home is the income from work and home (inkomen uit werk en woning) reduced by losses (verliezen) to be offset (verrekenen), and that income (inkomen) includes the taxable profit from business (art. 3.1). The SME profit exemption is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and MKB). Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) are taken out of the profit first (Forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the debt waiver profit exemption (kwijtscheldingswinst) for the part above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, compensation (vergoedingen) for business cessation (bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, benefits under final levy (eindheffing), mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions (vrijstellingen)), and a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). The box 1 balance (saldo) is taxed by art. 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the rate adjustment, and the tax credits (heffingskortingen) reduce the income tax and the national insurance contributions through an income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen) (the yearly FISIN heffingskortingen pages, for example https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference.

#### Aggregate income (verzamelinkomen)

Verified. Aggregate income is the sum of the taxable income from work and home, the taxable income from a substantial interest (belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) and the taxable (belastbaar) income from savings and investments (sparen en beleggen), minus te to offset personal deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18). It is the AHK phase-out base from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026).

#### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting)

Verified. Granted to every taxpayer, applied automatically in the return and by the employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) in the payroll. The phase-out base is the taxable income from work and home in 2020 to 2024 and the aggregate income from 2025. The phase-out runs from the phase-out point (afbouwpunt) to the top bracket boundary, where the credit (korting) reaches nil. The AHK and AK phase-out points (afbouwpunten) are linked to the statutory (wettelijk) minimum wage (minimumloon) ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) footnote to Tabel 1). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

#### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting)

Verified. Granted over the earned income (arbeidsinkomen), which for an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) includes the profits from business activities before the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption and excludes the part earned as co-entitled party or profit-sharing (winstdelende) creditor (schuldeiser) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen). The table has four bands: a build-up percentage, two further build-up steps, then a phase-out of 6% (2020, 2021), 5,860% (2022) or 6,510% (2023 to 2026) to nil. From 2024 the year tables state that if the employer applied a higher AK and the earned income is not above the third-band boundary, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) takes over the employer's amount up to the maximum. #### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting)

Verified. 11,45% of the earned income above a threshold, up to a maximum. Applied only in the return or through a provisional assessment, not in the payroll. Child under 12 on 1 January. Through 2024 the child must be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (2024 also accepts 6 months in the same household under a Government Gazette (Staatscourant) approval). From 2025 the child must belong to the household for at least 6 months. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months still gives the credit. No fiscal partner, or one for 6 months or less, or a lower earned income than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner. Qualifying children are own, step, adopted, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child, and a foster child (pleegkind) only when raised and maintained as own and not for a foster care allowance (pleegvergoeding). Residents of the EU, EEA (EER), Switzerland or BES can qualify. #### Zvw income-related contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage)

Verified. Three channels. The employer levy (werkgeversheffing) is paid by the employer or benefit agency over wages from employment (loondienst), DGA wage (loon) when insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for health insurance fund (ziekenfonds)-insured people of 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits. The withheld (ingehouden) contribution is withheld from pension, annuity (lijfrente) and other benefits (uitkeringen) with payroll taxes (loonheffing). The contribution by (voorlopige) assessment covers profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland) (not the conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente)), income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage (loon) from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign benefit agency and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes such as spousal maintenance. The assessment Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) assessment income tax (inkomstenbelasting). The maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom. Exceptions: Military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

#### Zvw base for an entrepreneur for income tax purposes

Corrected (the topic file left this open). Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business determined under division (afdeling) 3.2 Income Tax Act 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve. Art. 3.2 Income Tax Act 2001 defines taxable profit (belastbare winst) as profit minus entrepreneur's allowance minus SME profit exemption. So the Zvw base for an entrepreneur whose income is business profit (winst-ondernemer) is profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. Art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation. Art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages. #### Entrepreneur for income tax purposes (ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting)

Verified. Profits from business activities requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide. #### Old-age reserve

Verified. Additions of 9,44% of the profit with a yearly maximum reduced by occupational pension contribution (beroepspensioenpremie), for an entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (urencriterium) and is below AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). Reduction or release follows on buying an annuity with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), on cessation, on reaching the AOW age on 1 January, on failing the hours criterion in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (FOR, as summarised on fetch).
