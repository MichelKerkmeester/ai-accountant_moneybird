#### Rate structure 21, 9 and 0 percent (Verified)
Art. 9 lid 1 Turnover Tax Act (Wet OB): "De belasting bedraagt 21 percent." Art. 9 lid 2: 9 percent for goods and services in Table I (Tabel I), nil (nihil) for those in Table II (Tabel II) "mits is voldaan aan bij algemene maatregel van bestuur vast te stellen voorwaarden". The identical text was read in the 2020-01-01, 2021-07-01, 2022-01-01, 2023-01-01, 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01 and 2026-01-01 versions. Years 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

#### Accommodation (logies) from 9 to 21 percent on 1 January 2026, other reduced posts kept (Verified, added)
Stb. 2025, 339 (Act of 29 October 2025, Act on the retention of the reduced VAT rate for culture, media and sport (Wet behoud verlaagd btw-tarief op cultuur, media en sport)) amends the Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) so that "In tabel I vervalt onderdeel b, post 11" from 1 January 2026, and limits the transitional rule to "het verstrekken van logies". The Tax Plan 2025 had also scheduled other Table I posts (culture, media, sport) to lapse on 1 January 2026. Stb. 2025, 339 withdrew that, so those posts stay at 9 percent in 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration confirms 21 percent "vanaf 1 januari 2026", also for 2025 payments for stays in 2026 or later and for single-use vouchers for 2026 stays, and also for gas, electricity, water, sanitary, laundry and parking facilities tied to accommodation. Separately offered breakfast or pool and attraction access stays at 9 percent. #### Zero rate is not an exemption (Verified)
A 0 percent supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, an exempt supply does not. For an intra-EU supply of goods the 0 percent rate needs three conditions: proof from the records that the goods went to another EU country, a buyer with a valid VAT ID of another member state, and a statement (opgaaf) ICP filed "op tijd, juist en volledig". A late or wrong ICP can cost the 0 percent rate and draw a fine. Years 2020 to 2027 (2024 guide and the 2026 handbook). Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration list adds investment gold (beleggingsgoud) and canteens (kantines) and says the exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies carry no right to deduct. #### Return (aangifte) periods (Verified)
Quarterly is the usual period, monthly or yearly on request by letter to the tax office (belastingkantoor), reply within 6 weeks, the change starts at the start of the new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. The Handbook (handboek) 2020 adds "Betaalt u doorgaans meer dan € 15.000 btw per kwartaal aan ons, dan is uw aangiftetijdvak 1 maand." The Handbook 2026 and the current page no longer state that trigger. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf

#### Filing and payment term (Verified)
AWR art. 10 lid 2: a period return is filed "binnen een door de inspecteur gestelde termijn van ten minste een maand na het einde van het tijdvak". AWR art. 19 lid 1: payment "binnen één maand na het einde van dat tijdvak". Same text in the 2020-01-01 and 2026-01-01 versions. #### Old KOR to 31 December 2019 (Verified)
Art. 25 lid 1 (2019-01-01 version): for a natural person living or established in the Netherlands (or with a permanent establishment (vaste inrichting) there) whose VAT after deduction is "niet hoger is dan € 1883", the VAT is reduced by "2,5 maal het verschil tussen € 1883" and the VAT due, at most to nil. Lid 3: on request exemption (ontheffing) of administrative obligations. The € 1.345 full-relief point is not printed in the statute. It follows from the formula (2,5 x (1.883 minus x) equals x at x = 1.345). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2019-01-01

#### KOR 2020 to 2024, old art. 25 (Verified, baseline Corrected)
Act on the modernisation of the small businesses scheme (Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling) (Wet van 19 december 2018): Art. IV lid 1 general entry into force 1 June 2019, Art. IV lid 2 Articles I and II "met ingang van 1 januari 2020", Art. III the 20 November 2019 notice date. Art. 25 lid 1: an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with "aldaar een vaste inrichting" "van wie de omzet in een kalenderjaar in Nederland niet meer bedraagt dan € 20.000" may choose the exemption for supplies "in dat en volgende kalenderjaren". No lid mentions the preceding calendar year (checked in the 2020, 2021-07-01, 2022, 2023 and 2024 versions). Lid 6: notice "uiterlijk vier weken voorafgaand aan het belastingtijdvak", refusal by decision (beschikking) when the conditions are plausibly not met. Lid 7: "ten minste voor drie jaren na aanvang", re-entry three years after revocation. Lid 8: on exceeding, no exemption from the crossing supply onward, re-entry three years after the overrun. Lid 4: no input VAT deduction and no VAT on the invoice. Handbook 2020 adds: legal persons (bv, nv, foundation (stichting), association (vereniging)) may join, no option for taxed letting under the KOR, adjustment (herziening) stays out when the total is less than € 500 a year for investment goods used before that year, and deregistration only per the first day of a return period (aangiftetijdvak). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0041782, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-4774.html, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf

#### KOR from 2025, art. 25 to 25e (Verified)
Act of 10 July 2023 (Stb. 2023, 278) in force 1 January 2025, its Art. II (transition) from 1 October 2024. Art. 25: annual turnover in the Netherlands (jaaromzet in Nederland) and in the Union (de Unie) defined as totals of consideration "gedurende een kalenderjaar", lid 2 excludes business-used immovable property and depreciable movable assets. Art. 25a lid 1: "Een ondernemer die in Nederland is gevestigd en van wie de jaaromzet in Nederland niet meer bedraagt dan € 20.000" may choose the exemption. Lid 2: established in the Union but not in the Netherlands, Union turnover at most € 100.000. Lid 3: not for new means of transport sent to another member state or business-used immovable property. Lid 4: no deduction and no mention of VAT on the invoice in any way. Lid 5: start from the first tax period "minimaal vier weken na ontvangst van de melding", the notification (melding) states turnover of "het voorafgaande kalenderjaar" and of the current year to the notice. Lid 7: revocation takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter "minimaal vier weken na ontvangst van de wederopzegging", re-entry after "het kalenderjaar waarin de vrijstelling is beëindigd en het daaropvolgende kalenderjaar". Lid 9: exceeding € 20.000 (or € 100.000 Union) excludes the exemption also "in het daaropvolgende kalenderjaar", and the crossing supply is taxed "gedurende het resterende kalenderjaar en het daaropvolgende kalenderjaar". Same amounts in the 2026-01-01 version. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

#### KOR turnover-test conflict with the baseline (settled)
The baseline row "turnover of at most € 20.000 in the year of application and the year before" for 2020 to 2027 is Corrected for 2020 to 2024. Old art. 25 lid 1 tests "de omzet in een kalenderjaar" and no lid names the preceding year, in every dated version from 2020 to 2024. The Council of State (Raad van State) advice and further report (nader rapport) on the 2020 KOR speak of the expected turnover in the year itself ("verwacht in het jaar 2020 de omzetgrens van € 20.000 niet te overschrijden"). A prior-year overrun mattered before 2025 only through the three-year lock of lid 8. From 1 January 2025 the prior year counts: art. 25a lid 5 requires prior-year turnover in the notification and lid 9 bars the year after an overrun. The transition law barred 2025 entry when 2024 Dutch turnover "meer dan € 20.000 bedraagt". The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KOR page and Handbook 2026 now state the limit "voor het kalenderjaar waarin u zich aanmeldt, én voor het kalenderjaar daarvóór". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-4774.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden

#### KOR turnover that counts (Verified for 2025 to 2026, current page)
Counted: supplies taxed with Dutch VAT, including 0 percent (export, intra-EU), domestic supplies reverse-charged to the customer, and exempt supplies relating to immovable property, means of payment and securities, and financial and insurance services in the Netherlands. Other exempt turnover does not count. Not counted: VAT on private use, sale of business-used investment goods, domestic supplies reverse-charged to you as the buyer, supplies taxed in another country, intra-community acquisitions. Before joining the turnover is measured excluding VAT, during participation it is the total amount charged. All subnummers are added and the KOR applies to all of them. Handbook 2020 gives the same principle in short form for 2020 (all Dutch taxed turnover whatever the rate, also when reverse-charged, plus some exempt supplies). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf

#### KOR registration threshold without notice (Verified, added)
Decree of 10 June 2021, nr. 2021-12740: an entrepreneur not yet registered "met een jaaromzet van maximaal € 1.800" may apply the KOR without the notice, retroactive to 1 January 2020. From 1 January 2025 art. 25b Turnover Tax Act and art. 25 Implementing regulation (uitvoeringsbeschikking) VAT 1968 set the amount, "verhoogd van € 1.800 naar € 2.200" (besluit van 21 november 2024, nr. 2024-29602). Art. 25b also requires that the entrepreneur has not registered with the inspector or the KVK and is not obliged to, and does not use the exemption in other member states. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01

#### KOR practice from 2025 (Verified)
Apply only after receiving the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) and VAT-id. The registration (aanmelding) must arrive "uiterlijk 4 weken vóór de ingangsdatum", quarterly filer cut-offs 3 March (1 April), 2 June (1 July), 2 September (1 October), 3 December (1 January), a late registration starts the next quarter, returns continue until the definitive start date, confirmation within 8 weeks (weken). Voluntary deregistration (afmelding) via My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) Zakelijk "alleen met ingang van de 1e dag van uw aangiftetijdvak", at least 4 weeks ahead, no re-entry for "de rest van het kalenderjaar waarin u bent afgemeld, en het jaar erop", confirmation within 8 weeks. Paper forms are no longer handled. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor

#### EU KOR from 2025 (Verified)
Art. 25c: prior notification and an individual number with suffix "EX", start on the date the inspector issues or confirms the EX number, issued "binnen 35 werkdagen", not when Union turnover exceeds € 100.000 in the previous or current year (then a beschikking) or when the other state reports its threshold exceeded or art. 288 bis conditions unmet, and not where more time is needed against fraud. Ending takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter, or the first day of the second month of the next quarter when the information arrives in the last month of a quarter. Art. 25d: notice contains name, activity, legal form, address, the member states, previous-year totals in the Netherlands and each other state (two years for states that extended the period under art. 288 bis), and current-year totals to the notice. Art. 25e: quarterly totals per state ("0" if none) "binnen één maand te rekenen vanaf het einde van het kalenderkwartaal", and notice "binnen 15 werkdagen" when Union turnover passes € 100.000 with supplies from the start of the quarter. #### ICP statement (Opgaaf ICP) (Verified)
Report intra-EU supplies and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. The total equals rubriek 3b for the same period. Goods in the period of the invoice date, services in the period the service was supplied. No ICP for a period with nothing to report, corrections in the next ICP, at most 100 lines per section on the form. Goods: monthly, quarterly only when goods supplies stay at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and in each of the previous 4 quarters, yearly with a permit (vergunning). Crossing in month 1 gives 3 monthly ICPs, in month 2 two (first two months, then the last), in month 3 one quarterly ICP and monthly afterwards. Quarterly again after 4 consecutive quarters at or below the threshold, from the fifth quarter if that also stays below. Services: monthly, quarterly or yearly (permit (vergunning)), the € 50.000 test does not apply to services. Goods and services may be split or combined (combined follows the goods rules). 2024 guide: a yearly ICP only for natural persons and partnerships of natural persons, domestic deadline "binnen 1 maand na afloop van het gekozen tijdvak", including an every two months (tweemaandelijks) option, quarterly Q4 and the yearly ICP both due 31 January of the next year. Businesses not established in the Netherlands have 2 months. Call-off stock conditions (2024 guide): agreement with a known buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, own transport, no establishment in the arrival state, supply within 12 months of arrival, a register, and listing per buyer in the ICP of the period the transport began. #### Distance sales, € 10.000 threshold and OSS from 1 July 2021 (Verified)
The Act implementing the electronic commerce directives (Wet implementatie richtlijnen elektronische handel) "treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2021" (Besluit van 28 mei 2021, Stb. 2021, 261). "De mini One Stop Shop-regeling (MOSS-regeling) is per 1 juli 2021 vervallen" and the one-stop shop (eenloketsysteem) took its place. Art. 6k lid 1: the € 10.000 threshold applies only to a supplier established in one member state, the total "in het lopende kalenderjaar niet hoger dan € 10 000" and also not exceeded in the previous calendar year, lid 2 applies the normal rules from the moment of overrun. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: goods sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country with transport arranged or paid by the supplier, excluding margin goods, (nearly) new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital services to consumers count toward the threshold, measured excluding VAT. Below the threshold the supplier may opt for foreign VAT, the choice holds "minimaal 2 jaar". Above it foreign VAT from the overrun, Dutch VAT again "vanaf 1 januari van het jaar daarna" after a full calendar year at or below € 10.000. No threshold with a permanent establishment in another EU country (a warehouse alone is not one). The Union scheme is optional, one return per quarter for all EU distance sales, foreign input VAT cannot be reclaimed through it. #### OSS mechanics (Verified, one research item UNVERIFIED)
Union scheme: one notification per quarter in the month after, by the last day of that month (Q3 by 31 October), also a nil notification. Usable from the first day of the quarter after registration, for a first supply the registration must arrive "op de 10e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand van de levering". Three consecutive quarters without a notification gives exclusion for 2 years. Euro, ECB rate of the last day of the quarter, one payment for all states. Import scheme: monthly, maximum € 150 per consignment (zending), three consecutive missed months gives 2-year exclusion. #### BUA (Verified, gap closed)

Art. 2: no exclusion up to the VAT owed on a charged consideration. Art. 3: staff meals charged below cost plus 25 percent, deduction excluded "tot 9 percent van het verschil". #### Invoicing (Verified)
Invoice every supply to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur, no invoice needed for private customers (with exceptions on a separate page). Issue by "de 15e dag van de maand die volgt op de maand waarin u hebt geleverd". Handbook example: delivered on 10 April, invoice by 15 May. VAT is due at the latest in the period the customer paid. Fiscal unity: the VAT ID of the supplying part. Simplified invoice at most € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice, with issue date, name and address, the goods or services, the VAT or data to calculate it, and the reference to the original, not for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or reverse-charged supplies in a state where the supplier is not established. Digital invoice: customer agreement and assurance of "de echtheid van herkomst, de inhoud en de leesbaarheid", no general obligation. Cash accounting scheme (kasstelsel) is compulsory for listed trades (shops including web shops (webshops) in 2026, market traders, hairdressers, hospitality and others) and on request when more than 80 percent goes to consumers.
