## 2. SETTLED SOURCE CONFLICTS

Where two sources disagreed and a primary source settles it, the year files use the settled value below.

### VAT (BTW)

- KOR turnover test, baseline against the research: for 2020 to 2024 only the current calendar year counts (old art. 25 lid 1 "omzet in een kalenderjaar", no preceding-year wording in the 2020, 2021-07-01, 2022, 2023 and 2024 versions). The baseline "year of application and the year before" is right only from 2025 (art. 25a lid 5 and 9). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01
- KOR 2020 transition source: the research called stcrt-2019-4774 an official transition explanation. It is the Advice of the Council of State (Advies Raad van State) with further report (nader rapport) of 1 February 2019 on the Act on the modernisation of the small businesses scheme (Wet modernisering kleineondernemersregeling, KOR). Its dates and examples stand. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-4774.html
- Yearly return deadline "vóór 1 april" against "31 maart": no conflict, both put the last day at 31 March. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums
- MOSS replacement: MOSS lapsed on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop (eenloketsysteem) took its place. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/
- Supplementary return (suppletie) boundary 2020: "minder dan € 1.000" (Handboek 2020). 2026: "€ 1.000 of minder" (Handboek 2026). 2021 to 2025 stay open. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf
- Start of the 8-week supplementary return term (baseline UNVERIFIED): 1 January 2025, Art. XXIII and XXIV of the End-of-year decree (eindejaarsbesluit) 2024. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html
- Car cap (baseline "capped in the first years"): the cap is the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT for four years after the year of first use, then the VAT deducted in the year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04
- Car 1,5 percent "after 4 years" against "more than 5 years in use, purchase year counts": the same rule. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04
- € 227 BUA amount location: BUA art. 4 lid 1, version applicable 23-02-2019 to date (t/m heden). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23
- € 1.883 yearly return limit for 2024 (baseline "not found"): not settled for 2024 because the 2024 handbook URL returns 404, settled for 2020 and 2026. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf
- Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) KOR amendment (research "unknown"): the bill text contains no KOR change. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- Reverse charge (verlegging) article range: Division (afdeling) E is arts. 24b, 24ba and 24bb, not "24 to 24bb". https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01

### Entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek) prior-years condition (2020 to 2023 in the research). Settled: not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, with the self-employed person's deduction applied at most twice in that period. The deepseek-3 reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) wording "niet elk jaar ondernemer" means the same as the statute. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek
- Old-age reserve maximum 2020: € 9.218 (statute art. 3.68 lid 1 valid on 1 January 2020). FISIN 2020 still prints € 9.214 in two places. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01
- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age 2024: € 1.875 (art. 3.76 lid 2 € 3.750 and lid 4 50%). FISIN 2024 prints € 2.123 in the same sentence, a misprint. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- SME profit exemption 2024: 13,31% (art. 3.79a valid on 1 January 2024 and the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration MKB page). FISIN 2024 prints 14%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Self-employed person's deduction 2025: € 2.470 (art. 3.76 valid on 1 January 2025 and the FISIN 2025 table). The € 2.740 appears only in the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 carry-forward examples. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Starter's deduction 2027 and 2028: three layers kept apart. Enacted 2027 text € 2.123 (adopted-not-yet-in-force), bill 37 022 € 10 in 2027 and abolition from 1 January 2028 (proposed), Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) statement not matching either. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) and discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) 2027: enacted text 1,25% to 4% and € 3.630 (adopted-not-yet-in-force) against the cuts in the Act on fiscal stimulation of start-ups and scale-ups (Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups) (proposed). The research gap on the carrying bill is closed: its Artikel I parts (onderdelen) B and C carry the cuts, Artikel II the abolition. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf
- Deduction for assisting partner boundary hours: the statute bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" to "minder dan", so 875, 1.225 and 1.750 hours fall in the higher band. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.78 lid 2)
- Old-age reserve percentage 2020 to 2022: 9,44% in each statute version valid on 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022, so no order-in-council (AMvB) change applied. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01
- Second-test lookback windows: FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 prints "20167 tot en met 2021". The statute gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6)
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) 2027: the enacted text holds 11,91% (maximum value 37,59%), the Tax Plan 2027 figure is 38,16% (proposed). Both layers kept. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf

- EIA and MIA maximum 2023: € 136.000.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4 and the Vpb-2023 page) against € 128.000.000 (FISIN 2023 EIA sentence, while its MIA sentence already says € 136.000.000). Settled to € 136.000.000.

Settled to the top of the table on the URWA art. 8 wording "maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0) read with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KIA window. This rests on reading a cross-reference, no primary page prints the cap as a number.
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde) 2021 and 2022: inferred in the research, now read directly in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (50% rule for own use still present).
- 2027 EIA: not settled, see 2027 open points.

### Costs and the car

- Kilometre allowance 2026, € 0,23 against € 0,25. Settled: the statute prints € 0,23 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.15 lid 6 and 3.17 lid 1 b). The Policy decree (beleidsbesluit) on fiscal measures (maatregelen) in response to the energy shock (energieschok) of 17 May 2026, nr. 2026-8260, Stcrt. 2026, 18302, approves € 0,25 "structureel en met terugwerkende kracht tot en met 1 januari 2026", and the decree lapses on 1 January 2027 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies € 0,25 for 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026). Use € 0,25 for 2026. The research word "structureel" and the baseline "for 2026 only" are both partly right: the approval is meant to be structural but its own legal life ends on 1 January 2027, and from 2027 € 0,25 depends on Tax Plan 2027.
- Youngtimer age 2027, 25 against 17 years. Settled by layer, not by value: 25 years is the enacted 2027 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 2028 is the Tax Plan 2027 proposal with transitional rules in art. XLVII (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The bill is not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022). The research claim that the CPB note describes "a further gradual raise to 25 years" is not supported. The bill moves away from 25.
- Zero-emission 2020, "8% up to € 45.000" against "14% off capped € 6.300". Same regime: the 2020 statute prints 14% with a maximum of € 6300 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Only hydrogen (waterstof) was uncapped in 2020.
- Old-car minimum, 35% against 25%. Both apply to different cars: 35% of WEV for old cars (art. 3.20 lid 1 b), at least 25% instead of 22% for a car first admitted by 31 December 2016 (art. 10a.4) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Solar-cell exemption from the zero-emission cap. The 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page says at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek). The statute says 1 kilowatt peak only in the 2021 to 2023 texts and from the 2024 text requires watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by watt-hour (wattuur) per km of at least 7 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). The statute decides.
