#### Vpb rate table and how the tax is computed (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
The tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag above the column I amount. Series read in each dated text: 2020 t/m € 200.000 16,5%, above that € 33.000 plus 25%. 2021 t/m € 245.000 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. 2022 t/m € 395.000 15%, above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%. 2023 to 2026 t/m € 200.000 19%, above that € 38.000 plus 25,8%. The Belastingdienst tarieven page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified.

#### Book year that straddles a change of art. 22 (art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969)
If a year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, the tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The research named no article. The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified.

#### Filing the Vpb return and extension
Book year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. Broken book year: within 5 months after its end. Short book year ending 31 December: before 1 June, ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension (only for a calendar book year) runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. Verified (current page, added).

#### Gebruikelijkloonregeling (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (75% of it in 2020 to 2022, 100% from 2023), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, (c) the normbedrag. Normbedrag per dated text: € 46.000 (2020), € 47.000 (2021), € 48.000 (2022), € 51.000 (2023), € 56.000 (2024), € 56.000 (2025), € 58.000 (2026), € 58.000 in the future text for 2027. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (lid 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year). The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022, lid 7 from 2023). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 adds that the test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling, that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Gebruikelijk loon escape (art. 12a lid 2)
2020 to 2022: if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the normbedrag, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the normbedrag. From 2023: if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The Handboek 2025 notes a vaststellingsovereenkomst made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Verified.

#### Start-up relief and lower wages for starters and loss makers
Statute 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4): for a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013. Belastingdienst: the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years (conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start). Handboek 2025: the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy (Handboek 2025): a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. Verified.

#### DGA salary, loonheffing, werknemersverzekeringen and Zvw
A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. A DGA who (alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity) can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. For him the company pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw, and instead withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen (Handboek 2025: 5,26% up to € 75.864). A DGA who is insured falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV. Corrected: the research said the uninsured DGA pays the bijdrage himself by aanslag. The Handboek says the BV withholds it on net wage. The Belastingdienst table only says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag. Checked for 2025 in the Handboek and for 2024 to 2026 in the table.

#### Dividendbelasting (Wet DB 1965)
Art. 5: the tax is 15% of the opbrengst, in every version read (2020 version geldend 2020 to 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and the 2025 version geldend t/m heden). Art. 6 lid 1: if the company bears the tax, the opbrengst is multiplied by 100/85. Art. 1: the shareholder is the taxpayer, the company withholds. Art. 9 lid 1: once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota is due. Belastingdienst: a deelnemingsdividend (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. A beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting, bodies in the aangifte Vpb. Verified.

#### Dividend stripping (uiteindelijk gerechtigde)
The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3 as the research said. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Corrected.

#### Inkoop van eigen aandelen
Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. Verified.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

#### Conditionele bronbelasting (Wet bronbelasting 2021)
Art. 4.1: the tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version (first version with the levy articles). Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividendbelasting). It hits payments to a related body (qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (profit tax below 9% or on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. Verified.

#### Fiscale eenheid Vpb (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. Identical wording in every text 2020 to 2026. Belastingdienst adds: mother a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie (or a comparable foreign form), daughter a bv or nv, same book years and profit rules, both effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). Verified.

#### Deelnemingsvrijstelling and holding structures
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2: a deelneming exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies (1/3 interest). A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when the conditions there are met. For btw a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. #### Innovatiebox (art. 12b to 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 7/H (2020 text) and 9/H (2021 to 2026 texts), H being the highest art. 22 rate, so an effective 7% in 2020 and 9% from 2021. Belastingdienst: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring. Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover over those five years below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, same 2020 and 2026). Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb: K/T part, K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. Art. 12bd forfait: for a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or two preceding years and chooses it, 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Verified.

#### Switch from eenmanszaak to BV, geruisloze omzetting (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. Standard conditions (Besluit 2025, annex 1): selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise (this is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban). The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the voorovereenkomst is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. Belastingdienst: request at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. Verified.

#### BTW for a BV
The new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. Btw fiscale eenheid (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968): it arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. #### Jaarrekening: size classes and deposit
A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. Statute 2020 (Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397): micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000, fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000, fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 (5 March 2024) raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts unchanged. Deposit (art. 394): within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein: no extension, late filing is a criminal offence, from boekjaar 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR. Verified.
