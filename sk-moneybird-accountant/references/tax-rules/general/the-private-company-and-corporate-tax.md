#### Vpb rate table and how the tax is computed (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
The tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable (belastbaar) amount (bedrag) above the column I amount. Series read in each dated text: 2020 up to (t/m) € 200.000 16,5%, above that € 33.000 plus 25%. 2021 up to € 245.000 15%, above that € 36.750 plus 25%. 2022 up to € 395.000 15%, above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%. 2023 to 2026 up to € 200.000 19%, above that € 38.000 plus 25,8%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration's rates page (tarieven) states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified.

#### Book year that straddles a change of art. 22 (art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969)
If a year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, the tax is (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. The research named no article. The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and 2026 texts. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified.

#### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)
2020 and 2021: a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixes it by decision (beschikking). From 2022: one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. The research did not cover this. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified (added).

#### Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)
The interest balance (saldo aan renten) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. The balance (saldo) is interest costs on loans minus interest income. Percentage per dated text: 30% in 2020 and 2021, 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024, 24,5% in 2025 and 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration change pages state 20% for 2024, 24,5% for 2025 and 24,5% for 2026, and say non-deductible interest carries forward. Years 2020 to 2026. Verified.

#### Filing the Vpb return and extension
Book year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. Broken book year: within 5 months after its end. Short book year ending 31 December: before 1 June, ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension (only for a calendar book year) runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. Verified (current page, added).

#### Customary wage scheme (gebruikelijkloonregeling) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking) (75% of it in 2020 to 2022, 100% from 2023), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, (c) the standard amount (normbedrag). Standard amount per dated text: € 46.000 (2020), € 47.000 (2021), € 48.000 (2022), € 51.000 (2023), € 56.000 (2024), € 56.000 (2025), € 58.000 (2026), € 58.000 in the future text for 2027. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (lid 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year). The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022, lid 7 from 2023). The Handbook (handboek) Payroll taxes (loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) escape (art. 12a lid 2)
2020 to 2022: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the standard amount. From 2023: if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The Handbook 2025 notes a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. Verified.

#### Start-up relief and lower wages for starters and loss makers
Statute 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4): for a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (verordening) (EU) nr. 1407/2013. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an R&D declaration for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years (conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start). Handbook 2025: the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy (Handboek 2025): a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. Verified.

#### DGA salary, payroll taxes, employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) and Zvw
A DGA is in service (dienst) of his own company for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes. A DGA who (alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity) can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes. For him the company pays no premiums for the employee insurance schemes (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw, and instead withholds the income-related Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen) (Handboek 2025: 5,26% up to € 75.864). A DGA who is insured falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV. Corrected: the research said the uninsured DGA pays the contribution himself by assessment (aanslag). The Handbook says the BV withholds it on net wage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table only says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding (inhouding) on net wage (nettoloon) or by assessment. Checked for 2025 in the Handbook and for 2024 to 2026 in the table.

#### Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) (Wet DB 1965)
Art. 5: the tax is 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst), in every version read (2020 version applicable 2020 to 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and the 2025 version applicable to date (t/m heden)). Art. 6 lid 1: if the company bears the tax, the proceeds are multiplied by 100/85. Art. 1: the shareholder is the taxpayer, the company withholds. Art. 9 lid 1: once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling), and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting), bodies in the corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb). Verified.

#### Dividend stripping (beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde))
The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3 as the research said. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner when the proceeds benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Corrected.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01

#### Purchase of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)
Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. Verified.
Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

#### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) (Wet bronbelasting 2021)
Art. 4.1: the tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Corporate Income Tax Act 1969. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version (first version with the levy articles). Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividend tax). It hits payments to a related body (qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (profit tax below 9% or on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. Verified.

#### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) Vpb (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. Identical wording in every text 2020 to 2026. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds: mother a bv, nv, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie) (or a comparable foreign form), daughter a bv or nv, same book years and profit rules, both effectively established in the Netherlands. The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10). Verified.

#### Participation exemption and holding structures
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. Art. 13 lid 2: a participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure the customary salary test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies (1/3 interest). A tax-neutral (geruisloze) contribution (inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Decree (besluit) tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met. For VAT (btw) a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur (ondernemer) and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. Verified.

#### Innovation box (innovatiebox) (art. 12b to 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 7/H (2020 text) and 9/H (2021 to 2026 texts), H being the highest art. 22 rate, so an effective 7% in 2020 and 9% from 2021. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring). Smaller means advantages plus acquisition costs from R&D assets in the year and four preceding years below € 37.500.000 and net turnover over those five years below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, same 2020 and 2026). Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Trademarks (merken), logos and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb: K/T part, K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. Art. 12bd flat rate (forfait): for a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or two preceding years and chooses it, 25% of the profit before art. 12b, maximum € 25.000, only when that profit is positive. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Verified.

#### Switch from sole proprietorship to BV, tax-neutral conversion (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. Standard conditions (Decree 2025 (Besluit 2025), Annex 1): selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise (this is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban). The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree, the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscal unity only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: request at the office that handles the income tax, register the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the Accompanying form (Geleideformulier), the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. Verified.

#### VAT for a BV
The new legal form is a new business for VAT and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. VAT fiscal unity (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968): it arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. #### Annual accounts (jaarrekening): size classes and deposit
A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. Statute 2020 (Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397): micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6.000.000, € 12.000.000, fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000, € 40.000.000, fewer than 250. Stb. 2024, 52 (5 March 2024) raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million (miljoen) and € 15 million, € 25 million and € 50 million, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts unchanged. Deposit (art. 394): within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein: no extension, late filing is a criminal offence, from financial year (boekjaar) 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR. Verified.

#### Record-keeping obligation (administratieplicht) and retention period (bewaartermijn)
Art. 52 AWR: lid 1 sets the record-keeping obligation and lid 2 names who carries it, entities (lichamen) (onderdeel a) and natural persons who run a business or practise an independent profession or who enjoy profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) (onderdeel b), so a sole trader (ZZP) owner is required to keep records (administratieplichtig). The records are kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. Identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts. The research could not find the basis for longer terms. Art. 34a Turnover Tax Act 1968: records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept for nine years following the year in which use began (in effect ten). Art. 28rl and 28sj Turnover Tax Act 1968: OSS records ten years after the year of the transaction. Verified.
