#### Entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) status is its own test
Verified. Who is an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) is judged by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) per activity, separately from the VAT (btw). Someone who is a VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration looks at more conditions for the income tax. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), the profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek). The statute defines the entrepreneur as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (onderneming) (art. 3.5). The fetched pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, they say only that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration makes its own assessment after registration. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.4 and 3.5)

#### What the entrepreneur's allowance consists of
Corrected. Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction (aftrek) for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek). The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is not a separate list item. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3. The research listed the starter's deduction as its own part. The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Bill 37 022 would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) from 1 January 2029 (proposed), and the start-ups bill would delete parts (onderdelen) c and d (meewerkaftrek and stakingsaftrek after renumbering) three calendar years after its cuts take effect (proposed). Persons entitled to a share (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction or SME profit exemption on their co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) profit. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.74) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Hours criterion (urencriterium), 1.225 hours and the second test
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 1: at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur, and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is "grotendeels" spent on the businesses, or (b) the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. So the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur (non-ondernemer) year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. The text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. The FISIN handbooks print the lookback window per year (2021: 2016 to 2020, 2023: 2018 to 2022, 2024: 2019 to 2023, 2025: 2020 to 2024, 2026: 2021 to 2025). FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 prints "20167 tot en met 2021", both misprints against the statute, which gives 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Hours criterion, no pro-rating for a part year
Verified. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. KVK example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium

#### Hours criterion, which hours count and proof
Verified. All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Years 2020 to 2027.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium

#### Hours criterion, partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) for (b). Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium

#### Pregnancy and the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium)
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. The reduced hours criterion (art. 3.78a lid 3) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6 lid 5 and 3.78a lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Self-employed person's deduction, conditions, profit cap and carry-forward
Verified. Art. 3.76: the self-employed person's deduction applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below the AOW age at the start of the year. Lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year, the deduction is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too. Lid 5: the deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. The amount cut off by this cap is unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek), and if the cap sets the deduction to nil the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count. Lid 6: the inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by decision open to objection. The text is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.76) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek

#### Starter's deduction, conditions
Verified. Art. 3.76 lid 3: the self-employed person's deduction is raised by the starter's deduction if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969) (tax-neutral return from a BV (geruisloze terugkeer uit een BV)) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute. Years 2020 to 2027 (enacted text), with the 2027 and 2028 proposals under 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek

#### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs
Verified. Art. 3.78a: for the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. Not for an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from a disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance. Amount: € 12.000 if not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if applied for one year, € 4.000 if applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then (FISIN prints € 0). The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.78a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026

#### Deduction for research and development work
Verified. Part of the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.74, art. 3.77). A base amount plus a starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period. FISIN adds that it requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from RVO, at least 500 hours of R&D work and the hours criterion. Amounts are indexed per year (see the year tables). Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.77) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### SME profit exemption and the order of application
Verified. Art. 3.79a: the SME profit exemption is a percentage of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance. Art. 3.2: taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. So the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. No hours criterion is needed (the statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none). No exemption (vrijstelling) on profit as co-entitled party. In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing). Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling

#### Rate adjustment on the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption
Verified. Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) (expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Percentages per year are in the year tables. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 2.10)

#### Deduction for assisting partner
Verified. Art. 3.78: for the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. This settles the boundary-hour gap: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. Hours must be made plausible. Text identical 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.78 and 3.16) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek

#### Discontinuation deduction
Corrected (the oudedagsreserve link moved in 2023). Art. 3.79: the discontinuation deduction applies to profit made with or on the cessation of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022. The research cited art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b for all years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and there is no deduction on profit as co-entitled party.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 (art. 3.79 and 10a.29) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek

#### Old-age reserve additions, 2020 to 2022
Verified. Art. 3.67: the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year "kan" add to the old-age reserve, so it is optional (FISIN 2020: "U kunt elk jaar aan de oudedagsreserve toevoegen, maar dit is niet verplicht"). The hours criterion is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68: the addition is 9,44% of the profit as entrepreneur, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of the year exceeds the old-age reserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by an order in council (AMvB), lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pension reference age (pensioenrichtleeftijd) changes, published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years. Art. 3.69: the addition can be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year (where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply) and by taxable periodic benefits from an annuity (lijfrente) formed from an old-age reserve, capped at the business assets at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71: the business asset value is the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the old-age reserve, corrected for a reversion reserve (terugkeerreserve). Art. 3.72 covers more than one business. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (FISIN 2020).

#### Old-age reserve from 2023, end of additions and the reduction rules
Corrected (the legal basis). Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition is possible from 2023. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023. The research cited art. 3.70 directly for 2023 to 2027. The reduction rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the business assets at year end if the business or part of it ceased in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Art. 10a.29 lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (huwelijksgemeenschap, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the discontinuation deduction for a reduction after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (Belastingdienst). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027.

#### Zvw interaction
Verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The taxable profit from business is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income. FISIN 2020 states the contribution is not levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve into an annuity. Wet Zvw art. 43 itself was not reopened. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic.
