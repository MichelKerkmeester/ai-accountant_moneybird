#### IB-ondernemer status is its own test
Verified. Who is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged by the Belastingdienst per activity, separately from the btw. Someone who is a btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst looks at more conditions for the inkomstenbelasting. The Belastingdienst weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer. Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk, the profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek. The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the zelfstandig beroep counts as an onderneming (art. 3.5). The fetched pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an IB-ondernemer, they say only that the Belastingdienst makes its own assessment after registration. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.4 and 3.5)

#### What the ondernemersaftrek consists of
Corrected. Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek. The startersaftrek is not a separate list item. It is a verhoging of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3. The research listed the startersaftrek as its own part. The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Bill 37 022 would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) from 1 January 2029 (proposed), and the start-ups bill would delete onderdelen c and d (meewerkaftrek and stakingsaftrek after renumbering) three calendar years after its cuts take effect (proposed). Medegerechtigden get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.74) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Urencriterium, 1.225 hours and the second test
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 1: at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer, and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is "grotendeels" spent on the businesses, or (b) the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. So the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. The text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. The FISIN handbooks print the lookback window per year (2021: 2016 to 2020, 2023: 2018 to 2022, 2024: 2019 to 2023, 2025: 2020 to 2024, 2026: 2021 to 2025). FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 prints "20167 tot en met 2021", both misprints against the statute, which gives 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Urencriterium, no pro-rating for a part year
Verified. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. KVK example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium

#### Urencriterium, which hours count and proof
Verified. All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Years 2020 to 2027.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium

#### Urencriterium, samenwerkingsverband with connected persons
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and an ondermaatschap for (b). Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium

#### Pregnancy and the verlaagd urencriterium
Verified. Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof for employees. The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. The verlaagd urencriterium (art. 3.78a lid 3) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6 lid 5 and 3.78a lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### Zelfstandigenaftrek, conditions, profit cap and carry-forward
Verified. Art. 3.76: the zelfstandigenaftrek applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium (lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year. Lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year, the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too. Lid 5: the aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, and if the cap sets the aftrek to nil the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count. Lid 6: the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking open to bezwaar. Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar. The text is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.76) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek

#### Startersaftrek, conditions
Verified. Art. 3.76 lid 3: the zelfstandigenaftrek is raised by the startersaftrek if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Belastingdienst adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The Belastingdienst words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute. Years 2020 to 2027 (enacted text), with the 2027 and 2028 proposals under 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek

#### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid
Verified. Art. 3.78a: for the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. Not for an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance. Amount: € 12.000 if not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if applied for one year, € 4.000 if applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then (FISIN prints € 0). The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.78a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026

#### Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk
Verified. Part of the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.74, art. 3.77). A base amount plus a starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period. FISIN adds that it requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium. Amounts are indexed per year (see the year tables). Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.77) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

#### MKB-winstvrijstelling and the order of application
Verified. Art. 3.79a: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek. Art. 3.2: belastbare winst uit onderneming is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. So the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. No urencriterium is needed (the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none). No vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde. In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling

#### Tariefsaanpassing on the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling
Verified. Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Percentages per year are in the year tables. Years 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 2.10)

#### Meewerkaftrek
Verified. Art. 3.78: for the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. This settles the boundary-hour gap: exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening, profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. Hours must be made plausible. Text identical 2020 to 2027.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.78 and 3.16) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek

#### Stakingsaftrek
Corrected (the oudedagsreserve link moved in 2023). Art. 3.79: the stakingsaftrek applies to profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under doorschuiving on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022. The research cited art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b for all years. The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 (art. 3.79 and 10a.29) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek

#### Oudedagsreserve additions, 2020 to 2022
Verified. Art. 3.67: the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year "kan" add to the oudedagsreserve, so it is optional (FISIN 2020: "U kunt elk jaar aan de oudedagsreserve toevoegen, maar dit is niet verplicht"). The urencriterium is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68: the addition is 9,44% of the profit as ondernemer, at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the ondernemingsvermogen at the end of the year exceeds the oudedagsreserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by AMvB, lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pensioenrichtleeftijd changes, published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years. Art. 3.69: the addition can be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year (where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply) and by taxable periodic benefits from a lijfrente formed from an oudedagsreserve, capped at the ondernemingsvermogen at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Art. 3.71: ondernemingsvermogen is the book value of the business capital minus reserves under art. 3.53 other than the oudedagsreserve, corrected for a terugkeerreserve. Art. 3.72 covers more than one business. A negative calculation gives a nil addition (FISIN 2020).

#### Oudedagsreserve from 2023, end of additions and the afneming rules
Corrected (the legal basis). Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition is possible from 2023. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023. The research cited art. 3.70 directly for 2023 to 2027. The afneming rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Art. 10a.29 lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (huwelijksgemeenschap, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the stakingsaftrek for an afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (Belastingdienst). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027.

#### Zvw interaction
Verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income. FISIN 2020 states the contribution is not levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente. Wet Zvw art. 43 itself was not reopened. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic.
