#### System per year (Verified)
2020 to 2022 use the old band method (rendementsklassen I and II in art. 5.2 lid 1). From 1 January 2023 the Overbruggingswet box 3 (law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, art. II: "Deze wet treedt in werking met ingang van 1 januari 2023") applies the category method to everyone. The 2027 and 2028 texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028".

#### Old band method 2020 to 2022 (Verified)
The grondslag sparen en beleggen is split over three bands. Band 1 is 67% rendementsklasse I (savings) and 33% klasse II (investments). Band 2 is 21% and 79%. Band 3 is 0% and 100%. The statute writes the limits as "meer dan / niet meer dan" (2020: € 72.797 and € 1.005.572, 2021: € 50.000 and € 950.000, 2022: € 50.650 and € 962.350). The Belastingdienst page writes the same limits one euro higher as "tot / vanaf" (€ 72.798, € 1.005.573, € 50.001, € 950.001, € 50.651, € 962.351). Both describe the same bands. Rates 30% (2020), 31% (2021, 2022) in art. 2.13.

#### How the category percentages are fixed, art. 10.6ter (Corrected)
Bank percentage (lid 2): after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January, set at "de door twaalf te delen som" of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. So it is an average (sum divided by twelve), not a twelve-month sum as the research wrote. Debt percentage (lid 4): same method on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. Other-assets percentage (lid 3): replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. The statute text in force therefore carries last year's bank and debt figures (2025 text 0,92% / 2,46%, 2026 text 1,44% / 2,61%, 2027 text 1,37% / 2,70%) until the regulation replaces them.

#### Six-step category calculation (Verified)
Step 1 belastbaar rendement = banktegoeden x bank % plus overige bezittingen x other % minus (schulden minus drempel) x debt %. Step 2 rendementsgrondslag = bezittingen minus (schulden minus drempel). Step 3 grondslag sparen en beleggen = rendementsgrondslag minus heffingsvrij vermogen, partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 aandeel = grondslag / rendementsgrondslag x 100. Step 5 voordeel = belastbaar rendement x aandeel. Step 6 tax = rate x voordeel. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year: 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages, 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages (see the 2026 open point).
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

#### Partner rule (Verified)
The heffingsvrij vermogen doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint grondslag allocated under art. 2.17. The 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint rendementsgrondslag and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values. The schuldendrempel doubles for whole-year partners and a joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s).

#### Schuldendrempel (Verified)
Only the part of total debts above the drempel is deductible, and the Belastingdienst computes it in the return. Statute: art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1.

#### Groene beleggingen (Corrected for 2027)
Art. 5.13: green investments are not counted as bezittingen up to the stated amount, doubled for whole-year partners. Art. 5.13 lid 2 (from the 2023 text): the exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen and only the remaining room to green banktegoeden. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. Art. 8.19: korting voor groene beleggingen of 0,7% (2020 to 2024) and 0,1% (2025 to 2027) of the exempt amount on the peildatum, computed automatically, split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. Belastingplan 2026 (Stb. 2025, 444) set the 2027 amount at € 200 (€ 400 for partners), excluded it from the 2027 indexation (art. XLV), and moved the repeal of afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 to 1 January 2028. The research said both facilities lapse completely on 1 January 2027. That is what the Rijksoverheid page says, but the enacted 2027 text and Stb. 2025, 444 say otherwise.

#### Own use of real estate, art. 5.27a, from 2026 (Verified, source Corrected)
The statute sets the benefit at the economic rent value per year and excludes days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable. The fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value (peildatum 1 January of the previous year) times the usable days is a Belastingdienst calculation method on its werkelijk-rendement page. #### Content rules for the actual return (Verified)
Income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Belastingdienst uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation.

A novelle to bill 36 748 extends the vermogenswinstbelasting to all financial instruments from 2028 (about 90% of assets with value development), with a full switch for the other assets in 2030. Box 3 heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027. A heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028. The forfait for overige bezittingen, including rent and own-use benefits, up by 1,5 percentage point in 2027. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The Eerste Kamer dossier lists the letter under 37.020. The number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page.
