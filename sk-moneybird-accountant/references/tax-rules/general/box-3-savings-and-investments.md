#### System per year (Verified)
2020 to 2022 use the old band method (rendementsklassen I and II in art. 5.2 lid 1). From 1 January 2023 the Box 3 Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet box 3) (law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, art. II: "Deze wet treedt in werking met ingang van 1 januari 2023") applies the category method (categorieënmethode) to everyone. The 2027 and 2028 texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Actual Return Box 3 Act (Wet werkelijk rendement box 3) (bill 36 748) is not law on 2026-10-11. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028".

#### Old band method 2020 to 2022 (Verified)
The tax base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen) is split over three bands. Band 1 is 67% return class (rendementsklasse) I (savings) and 33% class II (investments). Band 2 is 21% and 79%. Band 3 is 0% and 100%. The statute writes the limits as "meer dan / niet meer dan" (2020: € 72.797 and € 1.005.572, 2021: € 50.000 and € 950.000, 2022: € 50.650 and € 962.350). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes the same limits one euro higher as "tot / vanaf" (€ 72.798, € 1.005.573, € 50.001, € 950.001, € 50.651, € 962.351). Both describe the same bands. Rates 30% (2020), 31% (2021, 2022) in art. 2.13.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01

#### Most favourable method (meest gunstige methode) (Verified)
For 2021 and 2022 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes box 3 income under the old method and the current method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment. "Ook als u over de periode 2017-2022 rechtsherstel hebt gehad, passen wij de meest gunstige methode toe."

#### Box 3 Bridge Act (Verified)
Law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, in force 1 January 2023. New art. 5.2: benefit (voordeel) = effective rate of return (rendementspercentage) x tax base for savings and investments, a negative return is set at nil. Art. 4.14 lid 1 is amended so the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) is at least nil. New art. 5.24 on reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage): transactions around 1 January within "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum" are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them. New art. 10.6ter for the yearly percentages. The law as published printed 0,01% / 5,69% / 2,46% in art. 5.2 lid 2. These were replaced for 2023 by the figures in the 2023 row.

#### How the category percentages are fixed, art. 10.6ter (Corrected)
Bank percentage (lid 2): after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January, set at "de door twaalf te delen som" of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. So it is an average (sum divided by twelve), not a twelve-month sum as the research wrote. Debt percentage (lid 4): same method on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. Other-assets percentage (lid 3): replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. The statute text in force therefore carries last year's bank and debt figures (2025 text 0,92% / 2,46%, 2026 text 1,44% / 2,61%, 2027 text 1,37% / 2,70%) until the regulation replaces them.

#### Six-step category calculation (Verified)
Step 1 taxable return (belastbaar rendement) = bank balances x bank % plus other assets x other % minus (debts minus threshold) x debt %. Step 2 return base = assets minus (debts minus threshold). Step 3 tax base for savings and investments = return base minus tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen), partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 share (aandeel) = tax base (grondslag) / return base x 100. Step 5 benefit (voordeel) = taxable return x share. Step 6 tax = rate x benefit. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year: 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages, 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages (see the 2026 open point).
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

#### Partner rule (Verified)
The tax-free allowance doubles with a fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint tax base allocated under art. 2.17. The 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint return base and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values. The debt threshold (schuldendrempel) doubles for whole-year partners and a joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s).
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel

#### Debt threshold (Verified)
Only the part of total debts above the threshold is deductible, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes it in the return. Statute: art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1.

#### Green investments (groene beleggingen) (Corrected for 2027)
Art. 5.13: green investments are not counted as assets up to the stated amount, doubled for whole-year partners. Art. 5.13 lid 2 (from the 2023 text): the exemption applies first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. Art. 8.19: credit (korting) for green investments of 0,7% (2020 to 2024) and 0,1% (2025 to 2027) of the exempt amount on the reference date (peildatum), computed automatically, split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance. Tax Plan 2026 (Belastingplan 2026) (Stb. 2025, 444) set the 2027 amount at € 200 (€ 400 for partners), excluded it from the 2027 indexation (art. XLV), and moved the repeal of division (afdeling) 5.3 and art. 8.19 to 1 January 2028. The research said both facilities lapse completely on 1 January 2027. That is what the Government of the Netherlands page says, but the enacted 2027 text and Stb. 2025, 444 say otherwise.

#### Own use of real estate, art. 5.27a, from 2026 (Verified, source Corrected)
The statute sets the benefit at the economic rent value per year and excludes days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable. The fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde, peildatum 1 January of the previous year) times the usable days is a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calculation method on its actual return (werkelijk-rendement) page. It is not in the text of Stb. 2025, 195, which the research cited for it.

#### Content rules for the actual return (Verified)
Income plus value changes over the calendar year, changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No tax-free allowance ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation.
