#### Box 2 rate structure (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
One flat rate in 2020 to 2023: 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023. From 2024 a two-bracket table: 24,5% up to the bracket boundary and a higher rate above it, 33% in 2024 and 31% from 2025. The table states the tax at the top of bracket 1 in column III. The table applies per taxpayer. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Origin of the two brackets and of the 33% and 31% rates
The second bracket was announced in Belastingplan 2023 and took effect on 1 January 2024 (rijksoverheid.nl: "Deze maatregel is in het Belastingplan 2023 aangekondigd", 24,5% over the first € 67.000 per person, 33% above). The 33% itself came from Belastingplan 2024: the amendement Van der Lee c.s. raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024, and Belastingplan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025. Years 2024 to 2025. Verified (the 33% origin was missing from the research).

#### Aanmerkelijk belang, the 5% test (art. 4.6)
The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a. The text is not the same across the years, contrary to the research. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts (a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969, 2022 numbering, with at least 5% of its profit). Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025 (a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights). In sub d "tot" became "om" in the same period. Years 2020 to 2027. Corrected (the research said the 2020 and 2027 texts read the same).
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html

#### Soorten aandelen, omvang kapitaal, meesleep and meetrek (art. 4.7 to 4.10)
Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 (checked year by year, not only at the endpoints). Meesleepregeling (art. 4.9): once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that company also belong to it. Meetrekregeling (art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang. The Belastingdienst page names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.11)
Shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. Text identical 2020 to 2027. Corrected (the research described art. 4.11 as the vbi route and said the list was not read. The vbi status is a fictieve vervreemding case listed by the Belastingdienst, not the art. 4.11 list).

#### Partner and minor children
The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (art. 4.6). The Belastingdienst states the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children, until the child turns 18. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Fiscal partners may divide box 2 income (art. 2.17 lid 2)
Gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen of the taxpayer and partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return. FISIN 2026: with fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own first bracket applies to his share. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified (rule). The summed figure stays UNVERIFIED.

#### Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12) and reguliere voordelen (art. 4.13)
Income is reguliere voordelen minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen, minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (art. 4.12). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions (Belastingdienst). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief-lenen fiction (sub f). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available. Salary from the company is box 1 loon. Deductible: interest on a loan to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs. Dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Forfaitair voordeel for vbi and foreign investment companies (art. 4.14)
The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, time-apportioned. The percentage is a yearly value, listed in each year block. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified (added, the research did not give the percentages).

#### Vervreemdingsvoordeel, verkrijgingsprijs and onzakelijke prijs
The voordeel is the overdrachtsprijs minus the verkrijgingsprijs. The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Special cases (inheritance, gift with or without doorschuiving, reaching 5% during the year, onzakelijke prijs) are as the Belastingdienst describes. At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)
The statute lists as a sale: inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen, becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie, shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. #### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)
If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs. This is what art. 4.12a carries. It does not carry the excessief-lenen fiction. Text identical 2020 to 2027. Verified. The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from this facility from 1 January 2027 (proposed, see 2027).

#### Loss offset (art. 4.49) and the older-loss rule
A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose. The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection. For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar". Art. 4.49 text identical 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Conversion of an unoffset loss into a belastingkorting (art. 4.53)
Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death. Lid 2: 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", so 24,5% in 2024 to 2027. Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner. The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang. Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block it, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified. The FISIN 2025 and 2026 statement "De korting is 26,9%" is overruled by the statute (see Resolved conflicts).

#### Excessief lenen, maximum and mechanism (art. 4.14a)
Lid 1: positive amount equals total debts above the maximum. Negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. Lid 2: maximum € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier. Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Lid 4: measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Lid 5 (missing in the research): if the taxpayer no longer has an aanmerkelijk belang at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount. Lid 6: an eigenwoningschuld is excluded to the extent a recht van hypotheek on the home was given to the company. Art. 10a.23 (transitional, missing in the research as an article): an eigenwoningschuld existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded. Lid 7 (Belastingplan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023): double counting of a samenwerkingsverband debt among several participants is removed. Belastingdienst: receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, debts at several companies are added, the drempel "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)". Years 2023 to 2027. Verified.

#### Excessief lenen, threshold history
The bill proposed € 500.000. The derde nota van wijziging (Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced € 500.000 by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, 4.14b and 4.14c lid 1, following the coalition agreement. Amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles. The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c. Years 2023 to 2027. Verified.

#### Excessief lenen, samenwerkingsverband exception (art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5)
Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a verbonden person for a debt of a samenwerkingsverband (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out. Inserted by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023 as lid 6 and 7 (numbered lid 4 and 5 for 2023). Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference «zesde lid» to «vierde lid» retroactive to 1 January 2023, not for 2024. #### Excessief lenen, verbonden personen and immigration (art. 4.14b, 4.14c)
Debts of a verbonden person (blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner) to a company where the taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 700.000 (2023) or € 500.000 (from 2024), applied to that person and his partner together. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally. A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 700.000 (2023 text) or € 500.000 (from 2024). Years 2023 to 2027. Verified.

#### Gebruikelijk loon (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies, (c) a fixed amount. Lower loon: if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable loon, the comparable loon applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower. The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur. € 5.000 rule: the rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, lid 4 from 2023). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming". The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023). Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### End of pensioen in eigen beheer (PEB)
Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen, Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules (as at 31 December 2016) for a PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed prijsgeven of the part of the claim above the fiscale balanswaarde, on condition the claim was at the same moment wholly afgekocht or geruisloos converted into an oudedagsverplichting. Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also a former partner who kept part of the claim. Art. 38o: on afkoop the taxable loon is the afkoopwaarde minus 34,5% of the fiscale balanswaarde (capped by reference to the 2015 balance), 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual. Artikel VI: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]". The Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen: afkoop or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked vervallen. Years 2017 to 2020. Verified.

#### Oudedagsverplichting (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)
The ODV is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year). It can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente, lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Added (Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020): under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5). A negative market rate must also be applied. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.
