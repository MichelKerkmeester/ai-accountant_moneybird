#### Box 2 rate structure (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
A single rate in 2020 to 2023: 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023. From 2024 a two-bracket table: 24,5% up to the bracket boundary and a higher rate above it, 33% in 2024 and 31% from 2025. The table states the tax at the top of bracket 1 in column III. The table applies per taxpayer. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the 5% test (art. 4.6)
The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a. The text is not the same across the years, contrary to the research. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts (a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969), 2022 numbering, with at least 5% of its profit). Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2025 (a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights (stemrechten)). In sub d "tot" became "om" in the same period. Years 2020 to 2027. Corrected (the research said the 2020 and 2027 texts read the same).

#### Classes of shares (soorten aandelen), size of capital (omvang kapitaal), drag-along (meesleep) and carry-along (meetrek) (art. 4.7 to 4.10)
Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 (checked year by year, not only at the endpoints). Drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) (art. 4.9): once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company also belong to it. Carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) (art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or a relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner), plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11)
Shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. Text identical 2020 to 2027. #### Income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.12) and regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) (art. 4.13)
Income is regular benefits minus deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (art. 4.12). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions (Belastingdienst). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (sub e), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction (sub f). Interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap) is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen). Salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon). Deductible: interest on a loan to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs). Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted but credited as prepayment (voorheffing). Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) for exempt investment institutions (vbi) and foreign investment companies (art. 4.14)
The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, time-apportioned. The percentage is a yearly value, listed in each year block. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified (added, the research did not give the percentages).

#### Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel), acquisition price and non-arm's-length price (verkrijgingsprijs en onzakelijke prijs)
The benefit (voordeel) is the transfer price minus the acquisition price. The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Special cases (inheritance, gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching 5% during the year, non-arm's-length price) are as the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes. At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Deemed disposal (art. 4.16)
The statute lists as a sale: Purchase (inkoop) of shares, commutation (afkoop) and purchase of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares that are no longer attributed to a segregated private asset pool (afgezonderd particulier vermogen), and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. #### Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)
If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the acquisition price. This is what art. 4.12a carries. It does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction. Text identical 2020 to 2027. Verified. The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from this facility from 1 January 2027 (proposed, see 2027).

#### Loss offset (art. 4.49) and the older-loss rule
A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose. The loss is set by decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection. For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar". Art. 4.49 text identical 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### Conversion of an unoffset loss into a tax credit (belastingkorting) (art. 4.53)
Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. Lid 2: 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", so 24,5% in 2024 to 2027. Lid 3: the inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit (korting) by decision open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without substantial interest. Knowledge group (kennisgroep) position (standpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block it, and ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified. The FISIN 2025 and 2026 statement "De korting is 26,9%" is overruled by the statute (see Resolved conflicts).

#### Excessive borrowing, maximum and mechanism (art. 4.14a)
Lid 1: positive amount equals total debts above the maximum. Negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. Lid 2: maximum € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier. Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Lid 4: measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Lid 5 (missing in the research): if the taxpayer no longer has a substantial interest at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount. Lid 6: an owner-occupied home (eigen woning) debt (eigenwoningschuld) is excluded to the extent a right of mortgage (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company. Art. 10a.23 (transitional, missing in the research as an article): an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded. Lid 7 (Tax Plan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023): double counting of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debt among several participants is removed. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, debts at several companies are added, the threshold "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)". Years 2023 to 2027. Verified.

#### Excessive borrowing, threshold history
The bill proposed € 500.000. #### Excessive borrowing, related persons (verbonden personen) and immigration (art. 4.14b, 4.14c)
Debts of a related person (verbonden persoon), a blood relative or a relative by marriage in the direct line (aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 700.000 (2023) or € 500.000 (from 2024), applied to that person and his partner together. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally. A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 700.000 (2023 text) or € 500.000 (from 2024). Years 2023 to 2027. Verified.

#### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment, 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies, (c) a fixed amount. Lower wage: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), the comparable wage applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower. The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and is known to the withholding agent and the inspector. € 5.000 rule: the rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, lid 4 from 2023). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming". The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023). Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.

#### End of pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer) (PEB)
Act on the phasing out of pension in own management and other fiscal pension measures (Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen), Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules (as at 31 December 2016) for a PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed the waiver (prijsgeven) of the part of the claim above the fiscal balance sheet value (fiscale balanswaarde), on condition the claim was at the same moment wholly commuted (afgekocht) or tax-neutrally (geruisloos) converted into an old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting). Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also a former partner who kept part of the claim. Art. 38o: on commutation the taxable wage (loon) is the surrender value (afkoopwaarde) minus 34,5% of the fiscal balance sheet value (capped by reference to the 2015 balance), 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual. Artikel VI: from 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]". The Central Pension Contact Point (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen): Commutation or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked lapsed (vervallen). Years 2017 to 2020. Verified.

#### Old-age obligation (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)
The ODV is increased each year by a market rate (marktrente) set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Wage Tax Implementing Regulation 2011 (Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011), the average of the monthly U-returns (rendementen) of the preceding year). It can be used tax-neutrally (geruisloos) for an annuity (lijfrente), annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Added (Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020): under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5). A negative market rate must also be applied. Years 2020 to 2027. Verified.
