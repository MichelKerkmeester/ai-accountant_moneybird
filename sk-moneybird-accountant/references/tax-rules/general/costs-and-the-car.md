#### Gemengde kosten drempel and 80% keuze (art. 3.15)
Costs connected with voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen, representatie (including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke are not deductible up to a yearly drempel. The related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No onttrekking is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (lid 5). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters. The drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Drempel by statute: € 4.700 (2020, 2021), € 4.800 (2022), € 5.100 (2023), € 5.600 (2024), € 5.700 (2025, 2026, and the 2027 base text). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.15, art. 10.1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026

#### Content of the gemengde-kosten posts
Voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien. Drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank. Genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken generally fall under representatie. Relatiegeschenken are a subcategory of representatiekosten per the wetsgeschiedenis of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Verified (current page, no year label, and the 2020 beleidsbesluit). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.3)

#### Drempel not time-proportioned
The art. 3.15 lid 1 amount is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a boekjaar longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either. Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.1)

#### Worker meals
Food costs that qualify as loonkosten are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura. Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.2)

#### Vpb variant of the drempel (for a BV)
Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscale eenheid does it apply once, at the parent. Verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 8 lid 5), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.4)

#### Congress, course and study-trip travel cap (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8)
Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep, and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 f and lid 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### Other fixed exclusions in art. 3.16 (telefoon, kleding, literatuur, private apparatuur, partner)
Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home (lid 2 a), literatuur except vakliteratuur (lid 2 b), kleding except werkkleding (lid 2 c, werkkleding defined in lid 5), persoonlijke verzorging (lid 2 d), costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur (lid 3), and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5000 (lid 4). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.16)

#### Telefoon (practical rule)
A private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100%. Verified (current pages, no year label, statutory basis art. 3.16 lid 2 a unchanged 2020 to 2027). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### Laptop and computer
The income-tax treatment follows vermogensetikettering. Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3). A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen, 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, in between is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once. Verified (current pages, statutory basis unchanged 2020 to 2027). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3)

#### € 450 rule and depreciation
A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred (kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur, onderhoud van de auto). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. Verified (current page, no year label). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten

#### Keuze herzien and heretikettering after a change in the law
A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag for the year of the choice is irrevocable, afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid. Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking. A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The Kennisgroep applies this to the youngtimer change, revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Verified. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/ (KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026)

#### Mixed character costs
Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction: huur and onderhoud of a pand split into business and private parts, telefoonkosten, a car of the business, congressen, studiereizen, symposia, seminars en excursies, zakenlunches en diners, recepties and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all: a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (rijbewijs), health costs (pedicure, zorgverzekering), personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding is deductible. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive

#### Werkruimte thuis (art. 3.16 lid 1, 13 and from 2025 lid 14)
Costs of a workspace including its inrichting in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met. With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig. Overview page: Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen. From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The MvT calls this a codification of case law: for a non-zelfstandige workspace in such a home the huurderslasten (inrichting such as meubilair, stoffering, behang- en schilderwerk, and gas, water en licht) are not deductible. Corrected for 2025 onward: the Belastingdienst page still presents the second route (owner and home in ondernemingsvermogen) without this limit. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.16), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html (MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning

#### Geldboetes and dwangsommen (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdelen c, d, e, i)
Not deductible: costs connected with geldboeten from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten, EU fines (c), costs connected with a misdrijf for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (d) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable (e), and dwangsommen under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. Overview page: Geldboetes 0%. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.14), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 1.1)

#### Timing rule for costs around a fine or dwangsom
Costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring. Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught. Verified. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/ (published 11-03-2024)

#### Misdrijf scope
Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted (or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking) are excluded. Example in the besluit: convicted only for possession of hennepplanten, not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 2)

#### Studiekosten as a business cost
Deductible when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis. Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs. The boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f. Verified (current pages, no year label, the 2022 abolition removed only the persoonsgebonden route). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### End of the studiekostenaftrek (persoonsgebonden aftrek)
Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven (lesgeld, cursusgeld, collegegeld, examengeld, promotiekosten, required leermiddelen excluding computers) were deductible above a drempel of € 250 and, outside the standaardstudieperiode, up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30). No deduction when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study. The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349. In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022. Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 (the prestatiebeurs case). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.16)

#### Niet-omgezette prestatiebeurs exception
When the diplomatermijn (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the prestatiebeurs. Caps: € 1.693 per study year (mbo) and € 2.443 per study year (hbo or wo), part-year € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo). When the actual prestatiebeurs was lower, the lower amount applies. The € 250 drempel applies per person. Verified for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 in FISIN. For 2026 FISIN narrows the case to an extended diplomatermijn (maximum 15 years), so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten

#### STAP-budget
The non-fiscal replacement took effect at the same moment as the abolition (art. 29 of the regeling links its start to the Wet afschaffing, and Stb. 2021, 349 states it applies from 1 January 2022). Maximum subsidy € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7), six application windows per calendar year. The regeling is marked "Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024". Corrected: the research said at most € 1.000 per year, the regeling text says per scholing. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html

#### Kilometre allowance for a private vehicle (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b)
Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (auto, motor, fiets) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The amount is all-in: brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). Statute: € 0,19 (2020, 2021, 2022), € 0,21 (2023), € 0,23 (2024, 2025, 2026 and the 2027 text). For 2026 an approving beleidsbesluit allows € 0,25 retroactively to 1 January 2026. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026

#### Openbaar vervoer, taxi en vliegtuig
Business travel by openbaar vervoer, taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### Verhuizing and dubbele huisvesting (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a)
Moving costs: the cost of moving the inboedel plus € 7750. Housing outside the place of residence: for at most two years. Unchanged 2020 to 2027. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.17)

#### Auto: vermogensetikettering
A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately it is verplicht privévermogen. All other cases are keuzevermogen. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel

#### Auto: bijtelling base, 500 km rule and cap (art. 3.20)
When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year (lid 1 a), or 35% when the car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago (lid 1 b). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer is not private (lid 3). With a rittenregistratie or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, though trips can still be checked. The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation. Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private km x the actual cost per km is settled. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible (lid 6 to 10). Age threshold: 15 years (2020 to 2025), 16 years (2026), 25 years in the enacted 2027 text. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto

#### Auto: waarde van de auto
The waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Belastingdienst describes as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer replaces it. The statute (art. 3.20 lid 5 b) keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 (2026) and 25 (2027 text). The Belastingdienst applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026. Belastingplan 2027 would repair lid 5 b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. A laadpaal for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 5), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html

#### Auto: zero-emission reduction (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11)
The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (lid 11). After that the rules then in force apply. Cars on waterstof are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text (panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek, battery without lead), and from the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in wattpiek divided by consumption in wattuur per km is at least 7. By year of first admission: 2020 14% off capped € 6300 (8% up to € 45.000), 2021 10% off capped € 4.000 (12% up to € 40.000), 2022 6% off capped € 2.100 (16% up to € 35.000), 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800 (16% up to € 30.000), 2025 5% off capped € 1.500 (17% up to € 30.000), 2026 4% off capped € 1.200 (18% up to € 30.000), 2027 text 2% off capped € 600 (20% up to € 30.000). Verified (statute). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026

#### Auto: pre-2017 cars (art. 10a.4)
For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22%. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.4)

#### Fiets of the business (art. 3.20a)
When a fiets (including an e-bike or speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address. Verified. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a, note on terugwerkende kracht also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts)

#### BTW on a private car used for business
VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet. Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, though it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA is not the btw-ondernemer, he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto

#### KOR and the income-tax cost base
Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted (the KOR case), deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need herziening, the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers. Verified (current pages, the KOR threshold history by year belongs to the BTW topic).
