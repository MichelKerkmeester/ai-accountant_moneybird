#### Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) and the 80% election (keuze) (art. 3.15)
Costs connected with food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), representation expenses (representatie) (including receptions (recepties), festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak)) and conferences (congressen), seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and the like are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) up to a yearly threshold (drempel). The related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (lid 5). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters). The threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Threshold by statute: € 4.700 (2020, 2021), € 4.800 (2022), € 5.100 (2023), € 5.600 (2024), € 5.700 (2025, 2026, and the 2027 base text). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.15, art. 10.1) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026

#### Content of the mixed costs (gemengde-kosten) posts
Food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including gratuities (fooien, tips). Drink covers coffee (koffie), tea (thee), milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank). Stimulants cover cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation expenses includes receptions (recepties), and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses. Business gifts are a subcategory of representation expenses (representatiekosten) per the legislative history (wetsgeschiedenis) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Verified (current page, no year label, and the 2020 policy decree (beleidsbesluit)). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.3)

#### Threshold not time-proportioned
The art. 3.15 lid 1 amount is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either. Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.1)

#### Worker meals
Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wages in kind (loon in natura). Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.2)

#### Vpb variant of the threshold (for a BV)
Under art. 8 lid 5 Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969) the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent. Verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 8 lid 5), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.4)

#### Conferences (congres), course and study-trip travel cap (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8)
Travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession (studie en beroep), and for conferences (congressen), seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 f and lid 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### Other fixed exclusions (overige vaste uitsluitingen) in art. 3.16 (telephone, clothing, literature, private equipment, partner)
Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: Telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home (lid 2 a), literature except professional literature (vakliteratuur) (lid 2 b), clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) (lid 2 c, werkkleding defined in lid 5), personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) (lid 2 d), costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other similar equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) (lid 3), and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5000 (lid 4). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.16)

#### Telephone (practical rule)
A private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis) 0% and business telephone calls at home (zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis) 100%. Verified (current pages, no year label, statutory basis art. 3.16 lid 2 a unchanged 2020 to 2027). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### Laptop and computer
The income-tax treatment follows asset classification (vermogensetikettering). Equipment and instruments (apparatuur en instrumenten) that do not belong to the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) are 0% deductible (art. 3.16 lid 3). A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business is classified as part of the compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). One used 90% or more is classified as part of the mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). One in between is freely classifiable (keuzevermogen) within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once. Verified (current pages, statutory basis unchanged 2020 to 2027). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3)

#### € 450 rule and depreciation
A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred (small office supplies (kleine kantoorartikelen), professional literature, maintenance (onderhoud) of the car). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. Verified (current page, no year label). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten

#### Revise the choice (keuze herzien) and reclassification (heretikettering) after a change in the law
A choice to classify an asset as freely classifiable can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable. After that, it can be revised only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use (privégebruik) of an asset held as a business asset is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The Knowledge group (kennisgroep) applies this to the youngtimer change, revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Verified. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/ (KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026)

#### Mixed character costs
Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction: Rent (huur) and maintenance of premises (pand) split into business and private parts, telephone costs (telefoonkosten), a car of the business, conferences (congressen), study trips, symposia, seminars and excursions, business lunches and dinners (diners), receptions (recepties) and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all: a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (rijbewijs), health costs (pedicure, health insurance (zorgverzekering)), personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser (kapper). Work clothing is deductible. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive

#### Work space at home (werkruimte thuis) (art. 3.16 lid 1, 13 and from 2025 lid 14)
Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not classified as a business asset are not deductible unless the workspace is, according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting), an independent (zelfstandig) part of the home and an income test is satisfied. With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit (winst), wages (loon) and income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is included in the business assets. The official factors for independence (zelfstandig) are its own entrance or stairway (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the ability to rent the space to a third party (derde). A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not independent (zelfstandig). Overview page: work space at home 0%, with exceptions (uitzonderingen). From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home classified as a business asset and serving as the main residence (hoofdverblijf) are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The MvT calls this a codification of case law: for a non-independent (non-zelfstandige) workspace in such a home the tenant's costs (huurderslasten), including fixtures and fittings such as furniture (meubilair), soft furnishings (stoffering), wallpaper and painting work (behang- en schilderwerk), plus gas, water and electricity (gas, water en licht), are not deductible. Corrected for 2025 onward: the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page still presents the second route (home classified as a business asset (ondernemingsvermogen)) without this limit. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.16), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html (MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning

#### Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen) (art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdelen c, d, e, i)
Costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary penalties (tuchtrechtelijke boeten), EU fines (c), costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (d) or for which a penal order (strafbeschikking) became irrevocable (e), and penalty payments under division (afdeling) 5.3.2 Awb (i) are not deductible. The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fines (verkeersboeten) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. Overview page: Fines 0%. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.14), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 1.1)

#### Timing rule for costs around a fine or dwangsom
Costs made before the fine (geldboete) is imposed are not costs that relate to the imposed fine and stay deductible in that case. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught. Verified. Source: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/ (published 11-03-2024)

#### Criminal offence scope
Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted (or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by settlement (transactie) or penal order) are excluded. Example in the decree: convicted only for possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten), not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Verified. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 2)

#### Study costs (studiekosten) as a business cost
Deductible when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs (zakelijke kosten). The boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f. Verified (current pages, no year label, the 2022 abolition removed only the personal deduction route (persoonsgebonden aftrek)). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten

#### End of the study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek), a personal deduction (persoonsgebonden aftrek)
Up to and including 2021 training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) (Tuition fees (lesgeld), course fees (cursusgeld), tuition fees (collegegeld), examination fees (examengeld), doctorate costs (promotiekosten), required study materials (leermiddelen) excluding computers) were deductible above a threshold (drempel) of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (buiten standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30). No deduction when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study. The Abolition of the Fiscal Deduction for Training Expenses Act (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree of 12 July 2021, Stb. 2021, 349. In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022. Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 (the performance grant case). Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.16)

#### Non-converted performance grant (niet-omgezette prestatiebeurs) exception
When the diploma period (diplomatermijn) (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant. Caps: € 1.693 per study year (mbo) and € 2.443 per study year (hbo or wo), part-year € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo). When the actual performance grant was lower, the lower amount applies. The € 250 threshold applies per person. Verified for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 in FISIN. For 2026 FISIN narrows the case to an extended diploma period (maximum 15 years), so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten

#### STAP-budget
The non-fiscal replacement took effect at the same moment as the abolition (art. 29 of the scheme (regeling) links its start to the Act on the Abolition of the Fiscal Deduction for Training Expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven), and Stb. 2021, 349 states it applies from 1 January 2022). Maximum subsidy € 1.000 including VAT (btw) per training (scholing) (art. 7), six application windows per calendar year. The scheme is marked "Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024". #### Kilometre allowance for a private vehicle (privévervoermiddel) (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b)
Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (car, motorcycle, bicycle) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The amount is all-in: Fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll (tol) and parking (parkeren) cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). Statute: € 0,19 (2020, 2021, 2022), € 0,21 (2023), € 0,23 (2024, 2025, 2026 and the 2027 text). For 2026 an approving policy decree allows € 0,25 retroactively to 1 January 2026. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026

#### Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)
Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (werkelijke kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview. Verified (current page). #### Relocation (verhuizing) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting) (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a)
Moving costs: the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750. Housing outside the place of residence: for at most two years. Unchanged 2020 to 2027. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.17)

#### Car: asset classification
A car is a mandatory business asset when used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres (privékilometers) a year. When used 90% or more privately it is a compulsory private asset. All other cases are freely classifiable assets. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel

#### Car: private-use addition (bijtelling) base, 500 km rule and cap (art. 3.20)
When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year (lid 1 a), or 35% when the car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago (lid 1 b). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private (lid 3). With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked. The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. A car means a passenger car (personenauto) or a van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private km x the actual cost per km is settled. A declaration of exclusively business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (lid 6 to 10). Age threshold: 15 years (2020 to 2025), 16 years (2026), 25 years in the enacted 2027 text. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto

#### Car: value of the car (waarde van de auto)
The value (waarde) is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) replaces it. The statute (art. 3.20 lid 5 b) keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 (2026) and 25 (2027 text). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) would repair lid 5 b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto) in the business assets is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 5), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html

#### Car: zero-emission reduction (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11)
The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission (lid 11). After that the rules then in force (in werking) apply. Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text (panels of at least 1 kilowatt-peak (kilowattpiek), a battery without lead), and from the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7. By year of first admission: 2020 14% off capped € 6300 (8% up to € 45.000), 2021 10% off capped € 4.000 (12% up to € 40.000), 2022 6% off capped € 2.100 (16% up to € 35.000), 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800 (16% up to € 30.000), 2025 5% off capped € 1.500 (17% up to € 30.000), 2026 4% off capped € 1.200 (18% up to € 30.000), 2027 text 2% off capped € 600 (20% up to € 30.000). Verified (statute). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026

#### Car: pre-2017 cars (art. 10a.4)
For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22%. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Verified. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.4)

#### Bicycle (fiets) of the business (art. 3.20a)
When a bicycle (including an e-bike or speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for commuting counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is parked no more than incidentally at the home address. Verified. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a, note on retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts)

#### VAT on a private car (privéauto) used for business
VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, though it is business for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting). In a BV the DGA is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer), he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Verified (current page). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto

#### KOR and the income-tax cost base (kostengrondslag)
Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted (the KOR case), deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening), the reduced motor vehicle tax (mrb) rate for vans (bestelauto) stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per entrepreneur (ondernemer) covering all subnumbers (subnummers). Verified (current pages, the KOR threshold history by year belongs to the VAT topic).
