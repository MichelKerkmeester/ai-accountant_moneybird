#### KIA structure and table (art. 3.41)
Verified. The KIA is a per-onderneming deduction, claimed by choice in the aangifte, on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year. Five bands: nil up to the first threshold, 28% of the investment up to the second amount, a fixed amount up to the third amount, then the fixed amount minus 7,56% of the part above the third amount up to the top amount, nil above the top amount. The yearly amounts are in each year block and match the research for every year 2020 to 2026. The Belastingdienst year pages phrase the window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" (2023) and "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236" (2026). Years 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

#### KIA samenwerkingsverband (art. 3.41 lid 3)
Verified. In a samenwerkingsverband the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum. The Belastingdienst KIA page adds that each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and another reasonable key is allowed if applied consistently. Years 2020 to 2027. #### Excluded assets and per-asset minimums (art. 3.45)
Corrected (article reference). The research placed the € 450 and € 2.500 minimums in art. 3.41 and 3.42. They are in art. 3.45. Lid 1 excludes, for every investeringsaftrek, assets intended mainly for certain uses including use in an onderneming whose profit falls under a regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting (onderdeel a), gronden including the ground under buildings except grondverbetering (b), woonhuizen and woonschepen (c), personenauto's as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (d), vaartuigen of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), effecten, vorderingen, goodwill, vergunningen, ontheffingen and concessies (f) and dieren (g). Lid 2 adds for the KIA only: assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (b). Lid 3: for the MIA lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply, so woonhuizen and personenauto's can still earn MIA. Lid 4 adds for the EIA and MIA: assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (b). Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. RVO phrases the MIA/Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag". Years 2020 to 2027, same amounts in every version. #### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (art. 3.44)
Corrected (article reference, the rule itself holds). Lid 1: if the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years. Lid 2: at staking of the onderneming the full investeringsaftrek is taken in the year of staking. Applies to KIA, EIA and MIA. Years 2020 to 2027. #### EIA and MIA conditions
Verified. Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Milieulijst requirements can qualify, as can (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed oneself, as voortbrengingskosten. RVO treats a vehicle as new if used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km. No MIA on costs that already received EIA, Vamil remains possible. EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset. EIA and MIA can each be combined with the KIA. The EIA asset must be on the Energielijst, the MIA and Vamil asset on the Milieulijst. RVO states a maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year for MIA/Vamil, unless the Milieulijst sets a lower maximum (current RVO page, year not stated). Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The research rule that a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages (UNVERIFIED). Years 2020 to 2027. #### Vamil (art. 3.31)
Verified. The costs of a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", the rest follows the normal schedule. #### Desinvesteringsbijtelling (art. 3.47)
Verified. Lid 1: if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investeringsaftrek percentage taken is added to profit. Lid 2: only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which aftrek was taken. Lid 3 treats as disposal: (a) withdrawing the good from the onderneming, (b) changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, EIA and MIA), (c) a change bringing it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2°. Lid 4: in those cases the waarde in het economische verkeer counts as transfer price. Lid 5: undoing an investment, or receiving a vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding, counts as disposal for that amount. Lid 6: undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7: the Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6. The Belastingdienst page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for EIA or MIA, and adds staking of (part of) the onderneming within the term. Years 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

#### Ordinary depreciation (art. 3.30)
Verified. Lid 1: yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten attributable to the year. Lid 2: per year at most 10% of the cost for goodwill and at most 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, so at least 10 and 5 years. Lid 3: voortbrengingskosten of immateriële activa may be written off at once in the year of voortbrenging. Lid 4: voorwerpen van geringe waarde normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging. Same text in 2020 to 2027. #### Building depreciation and bodemwaarde (art. 3.30a)
Verified. Lid 1: depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the bodemwaarde, at most the difference. Lid 2: a building, its parts, the ground under it and aanhorigheden are one bedrijfsmiddel, detachable werktuigen separate. Lid 3 bodemwaarde: 2020 to 2023 the WOZ-waarde for a gebouw ter belegging and 50% of the WOZ-waarde for another (own use) building, from 2024 one bodemwaarde "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw". Lid 4: the WOZ-waarde is the value set by beschikking under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text also has lid 5 (co-owned building pro rata), lid 6 (ground owned by an unrelated party reduces the WOZ-waarde) and lid 7 to 10 (related persons and verbonden lichaam). The 2021 and 2022 texts were opened this time and both print the 50% rule. #### Willekeurige afschrijving for starters (URWA art. 7 to 9)
Corrected (article numbers and the cap amount). Statutory basis art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001 (willekeurige afschrijving on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship). URWA art. 7 lid 1: designated are assets for which obligations were entered or voortbrengingskosten made in a calendar year in which the verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the startersaftrek) applies. Lid 2: also the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3: only for costs made in an onderneming from which the taxpayer earns winst as ondernemer. Art. 8: willekeurige afschrijving is allowed "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Art. 9: the art. 3.38 period is five years from the start of the year of the obligation or costs. Art. 12: ten years for the zeeschepen of art. 10. The research cited "art. 7 and 10", the right articles are 7, 8 and 9 (and 12 for zeeschepen). The Belastingdienst adds: eenmanszaak, maatschap, cv or vof, startersaftrek conditions met, only assets that qualify for investeringsaftrek, free choice of assets, and the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Herwaardering to the normal book value (the difference is winst) within 5 years (10 for zeeschepen) if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the bosbedrijf. Cap amount: the URWA wording names the maximum investment amount over which KIA can be obtained, which is the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table (2026: € 398.236, the Belastingdienst window "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236"). The baseline instead used the end of the 28% band (€ 58.238 for 2020, € 69.765 for 2024, € 71.683 for 2026). This is a reading of a cross-reference, the pages do not print the cap as a number. #### Temporary willekeurige afschrijving 2023 (URWA art. 13 to 15)
Corrected (article numbers, the research cited art. 11). Art. 13 lid 1: assets not used before, obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in calendar year 2023, taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026. Lid 2 exclusions: gebouwen, schepen, vliegtuigen, bromfietsen, motorrijwielen, personenauto's except for beroepsvervoer over de weg or 0 gram CO2 per km, immateriële activa, dieren, assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Lid 3: the 1 January 2026 date moves by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14: once, at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation or costs. Art. 15: the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 ends 31 December 2025. The Belastingdienst adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year. IB and Vpb both.
