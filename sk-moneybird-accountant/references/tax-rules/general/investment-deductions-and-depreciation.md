#### KIA structure and table (art. 3.41)
Verified. The KIA is a per business (per-onderneming) deduction, claimed by choice in the return (aangifte), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year. Five bands: nil up to the first threshold, 28% of the investment up to the second amount, a fixed amount up to the third amount, then the fixed amount minus 7,56% of the part above the third amount up to the top amount, nil above the top amount. The yearly amounts are in each year block and match the research for every year 2020 to 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) year pages phrase the window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" (2023) and "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236" (2026). Years 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

#### KIA partnership (samenwerkingsverband) (art. 3.41 lid 3)
Verified. In a partnership the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KIA page adds that each firm partner (firmant) or partner (vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)profit (winst), and another reasonable key is allowed if applied consistently. Years 2020 to 2027. #### Excluded assets and per-asset minimums (art. 3.45)
Corrected (article reference). The research placed the € 450 and € 2.500 minimums in art. 3.41 and 3.42. They are in art. 3.45. Lid 1 excludes, for every investment deduction (investeringsaftrek), assets intended mainly for certain uses including use in a business (onderneming) whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting) (onderdeel a), land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering) (b), dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen) (c), passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (d), vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies) (f) and animals (dieren) (g). Lid 2 adds for the KIA only: assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (b). Lid 3: for the MIA lid 1 parts (onderdelen) c and d do not apply, so dwellings and passenger cars (personenauto's) can still earn MIA. Lid 4 adds for the EIA and MIA: assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (b). Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. RVO phrases the MIA/Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag". Years 2020 to 2027, same amounts in every version. #### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use (art. 3.44)
Corrected (article reference, the rule itself holds). Lid 1: if the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years. Lid 2: at cessation (staking) of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation. Applies to KIA, EIA and MIA. Years 2020 to 2027. #### EIA and MIA conditions
Verified. Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, as can (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed oneself, as production costs (voortbrengingskosten). RVO treats a vehicle as new if used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km. No MIA on costs that already received EIA, Vamil remains possible. EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset. EIA and MIA can each be combined with the KIA. The EIA asset must be on the Energy List (Energielijst), the MIA and Vamil asset on the Environmental List. RVO states a maximum of € 25 million (miljoen) per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for MIA/Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum (current RVO page, year not stated). Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The research rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages (UNVERIFIED). Years 2020 to 2027. #### Vamil (art. 3.31)
Verified. The costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", the rest follows the normal schedule. #### Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) (art. 3.47)
Verified. Lid 1: if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage taken is added to profit. Lid 2: only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken. Lid 3 treats as disposal: (a) withdrawing the good from the business, (b) changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, EIA and MIA), (c) a change bringing it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2°. Lid 4: in those cases the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) counts as transfer price. Lid 5: undoing an investment, or receiving a reduction (vermindering), refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), counts as disposal for that amount. Lid 6: undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7: the Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for EIA or MIA, and adds cessation of (part of) the business within the term. Years 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

#### Ordinary depreciation (art. 3.30)
Verified. Lid 1: yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition or production costs (aanschaffings- of voortbrengingskosten) attributable to the year. Lid 2: per year at most 10% of the cost for goodwill and at most 20% for other business assets, so at least 10 and 5 years. Lid 3: Production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) may be written off at once in the year of production (voortbrenging). Lid 4: Objects of small value (voorwerpen van geringe waarde) normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or production. Same text in 2020 to 2027. #### Building depreciation and minimum value (art. 3.30a)
Verified. Lid 1: depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the minimum value, at most the difference. Lid 2: a building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) are one business asset (bedrijfsmiddel), detachable machinery (werktuigen) separate. Lid 3 minimum value: 2020 to 2023 the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another (own use) building, from 2024 one minimum value "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw". Lid 4: the WOZ value is the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text also has lid 5 (co-owned building pro rata), lid 6 (ground owned by an unrelated party reduces the WOZ value) and lid 7 to 10 (related persons and affiliated entities (verbonden lichaam)). The 2021 and 2022 texts were opened this time and both print the 50% rule. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

#### Arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for starters (URWA art. 7 to 9)
Corrected (article numbers and the cap amount). Statutory basis art. 3.34 Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) (Arbitrary depreciation on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship). URWA art. 7 lid 1: designated are assets for which obligations were entered or production costs made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the starter's deduction (startersaftrek)) applies. Lid 2: also the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3: only for costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as entrepreneur (ondernemer). Art. 8: arbitrary depreciation is allowed "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Art. 9: the art. 3.38 period is five years from the start of the year of the obligation or costs. Art. 12: ten years for the sea-going vessels (zeeschepen) of art. 10. The research cited "art. 7 and 10", the right articles are 7, 8 and 9 (and 12 for sea-going vessels (zeeschepen)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds: Sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), civil-law partnership (maatschap), limited partnership (cv) or general partnership (vof), starter's deduction conditions met, only assets that qualify for investment deduction, free choice of assets, and the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value (the difference is profit (winst)) within 5 years (10 for sea-going vessels) if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf). Cap amount: the URWA wording names the maximum investment amount over which KIA can be obtained, which is the top of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table (2026: € 398.236, the Belastingdienst window "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236"). The baseline instead used the end of the 28% band (€ 58.238 for 2020, € 69.765 for 2024, € 71.683 for 2026). This is a reading of a cross-reference, the pages do not print the cap as a number. #### Temporary arbitrary depreciation 2023 (URWA art. 13 to 15)
Corrected (article numbers, the research cited art. 11). Art. 13 lid 1: assets not used before, obligations entered or production costs made in calendar year 2023, taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026. Lid 2 exclusions: Buildings (gebouwen), ships (schepen), aircraft (vliegtuigen), mopeds (bromfietsen), motorcycles (motorrijwielen), passenger cars (personenauto's) except for commercial road transport (beroepsvervoer over de weg) or 0 gram CO2 per km, intangible assets, animals (dieren), assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Lid 3: the 1 January 2026 date moves by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14: once, at most 50% of the costs, in the year of the obligation or costs. Art. 15: the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 ends 31 December 2025. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year. IB and Vpb both.
