#### Enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) (2020 to 2024)
Verified. Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied. In a not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) case the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing): reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally creating or continuing (opzettelijk laten ontstaan of voortbestaan) a situation of evident false self-employment (evidente schijnzelfstandigheid), knowing or able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Acting in bad faith cases could lead to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary assessments for payroll taxes (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and penalties (boetes). The second restriction of the moratorium exception (enforcement when instructions (aanwijzingen) are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020) and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were both announced in the third progress letter (derde voortgangsbrief) of 24 June 2019. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

#### End of the moratorium and the phase-in model (ingroeimodel) (2025 to 2030)
Verified. Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions are given. False self-employment can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. #### Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) (2020 to 2029)
Verified. Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Reason given: Model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motion (motie) Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. The approval of the model agreements free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding (excl). VAT (btw) at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. This is the € 38 (peildatum 1 januari 2026) that the Senate, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state. So € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 2026. The ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. The arithmetic for 1 January 2026 is mine from the MvT method and the WML figures, the € 38 itself is stated on the primary pages. #### Income tax filing deadline and deferral (uitstel) (2020 to 2027)
Verified. The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and the fiscal partner must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). AWR art. 9 lid 1: for taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the inspector (inspecteur) of at least one month after the invitation, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions (same text in 2020 and 2026). #### Additional assessment (navordering) (2020 to 2027)
Verified. AWR art. 16 (same in 2020 and 2026 texts). Lid 1: a new fact, not usable if known or reasonably apparent to the inspector unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2: also when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3: 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral, and for an error-based additional assessment only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4: 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5: extension by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, additional assessment possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). #### Payment terms of assessments (2020 to 2027, added)
Verified. Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. A provisional assessment IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, the first one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. #### Provisional assessment (2020 to 2027)
Verified. An estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing is not mandatory. Reasons include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst). #### Tax interest on income tax (2020 to 2027)
Verified. Charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if filed before 1 May. Period from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No interest (rente) when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. 30 days per month, 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. #### VAT filing and payment deadline (2020 to 2027, added)
Verified. AWR art. 10 lid 2: a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1: the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give for every year the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together (monthly and quarterly) and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Deferral for a VAT return is available only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit), and must be requested in writing from the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment and penalty remain possible. VAT failure to file (aangifteverzuim): 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance), then a penalty of € 68 (2020 to 2024) or € 82 (2025, 2026). #### Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) (2020 to 2027)
Verified. AWR art. 52 lid 4 (same in 2020 and 2026): Persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table: Basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual (a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more). Basic records: general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable and payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. #### KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration (2020 to 2027)
Verified. Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1: the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required returns (opgaven) within one week after the fact. KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately when registering within that window, an identity check is possible up to three months ahead, appointments up to eight weeks ahead. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Start-up notification (opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister)) is used.
