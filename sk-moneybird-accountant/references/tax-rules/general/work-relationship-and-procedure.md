#### Handhavingsmoratorium (2020 to 2024)
Verified. Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing: reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes. The second restriction of the moratorium exception (enforcement when aanwijzingen are not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020) and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were both announced in the derde voortgangsbrief of 24 June 2019. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file

#### End of the moratorium and the ingroeimodel (2025 to 2030)
Verified. Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Verified. Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. #### VBAR hourly amount, € 36 against € 38 (settled)
Verified and settled. The memorie van toelichting explains € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag for AOV and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. This is the € 38 (peildatum 1 januari 2026) that the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. The arithmetic for 1 January 2026 is mine from the MvT method and the WML figures, the € 38 itself is stated on the primary pages. #### Income tax filing deadline and uitstel (2020 to 2027)
Verified. The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. #### Navordering (2020 to 2027)
Verified. AWR art. 16 (same in 2020 and 2026 texts). Lid 1: a new fact, not usable if known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw. Lid 2: also when a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and for a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar, always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3: 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and for a fout-based navordering only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4: 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5: extension by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Belastingdienst page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, navordering possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

#### Payment terms of assessments (2020 to 2027, added)
Verified. Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, the first one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. #### Voorlopige aanslag (2020 to 2027)
Verified. An estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing is not mandatory. Reasons include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst). #### Belastingrente on income tax (2020 to 2027)
Verified. Charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if filed before 1 May. Period from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. No rente when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. 30 days per month, 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. #### Bewaarplicht (2020 to 2027)
Verified. AWR art. 52 lid 4 (same in 2020 and 2026): administratieplichtigen keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. #### KVK and Belastingdienst registration (2020 to 2027)
Verified. Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1: the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven within one week after the fact. KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when registering within that window, an identity check is possible up to three months ahead, appointments up to eight weeks ahead. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used.
