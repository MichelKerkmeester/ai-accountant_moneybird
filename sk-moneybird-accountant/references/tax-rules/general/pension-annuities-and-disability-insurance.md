#### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) rate, pension shortfall (pensioentekort) condition and formula (Verified)
Years 2020 to 2027. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pension shortfall in the previous calendar year deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to a percentage of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual. The percentage is 13,3% in the texts of 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01, and 30% in the texts of 2023-07-01, 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01 and 2027-01-01. Up to 2022 the outcome is also reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve) (lid 1 and lid 4 sub b). From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023". Formula for a self-employed person without pension accrual: percentage x (min(income of previous year, income cap (aftoppingsgrens)) minus franchise). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

#### Retroactivity notes on art. 3.127 (Corrected)
The research tied "Stcrt. 2023, 34571 back to 01-01-2023 and Stb. 2025, 431 back to 01-07-2023" to the 30% change. The 2023-07-01 text shows that Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6 (back to 1 January 2023), and Stb. 2025, 431 (in force 1 January 2026) only rewrites the partner pension (partnerpensioen) wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023 ("partnerpensioen bij overlijden op of na pensioendatum ... voor zover deze ingelegde premies het gevolg zijn van de toeneming van de diensttijd in dat kalenderjaar"). The 30% itself runs from the Wtp with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023 per the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration Wtp page. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

#### Age window (Verified)
2020 to 2022 (and the 2023-01-01 text): at the start of the calendar year the taxpayer has not yet reached the AOW age. From the Wtp (2023-07-01 text, applied to all of 2023) to 2027: the taxpayer has not yet reached the age five years above the AOW age. FISIN 2023 to 2026 say "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd". The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page for 2026 adds that a taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the annual allowance 2026 but may still use the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Catch-up allowance look-back (Verified)
Art. 3.127 lid 2: unused annual allowance of the preceding period may be used on request in the return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. The period is "zeven jaar" in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts and "tien jaar" from the 2023-07-01 text. FISIN windows: 2013 to 2019 (2020), 2014 to 2020 (2021), 2014 to 2021 (2022, see the open point in 2022), 2013 to 2022 (2023), 2014 to 2023 (2024), 2015 to 2024 (2025), 2016 to 2025 (2026). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page advises using catch-up allowance before annual allowance so older unused allowance (ruimte) does not lapse, and says unused 2016 allowance lapses in 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Catch-up allowance cap (Verified)
2020 to 2022: at most 17% of the contribution base and at most a euro amount, raised to a higher euro amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. From the 2023-07-01 text: one flat euro maximum, no percentage and no age split. Sources: the art. 3.127 lid 2 texts per year and the FISIN chapters.

#### Contribution base composition (Verified)
Art. 3.127 lid 3: the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before addition (toevoeging) to and reduction of the old-age reserve (afneming van de oudedagsreserve) and before the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) (2020 to 2023-01-01 text) or profits from business activities before the entrepreneur's allowance (from the 2023-07-01 text), (b) taxable wages (belastbaar loon), (c) taxable income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and (d) taxable periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) and benefits in kind (verstrekkingen), counted up to the first-mentioned (eerstvermelde) amount (bedrag) (aftoppingsgrens) and then reduced by the last-mentioned (laatstvermelde) amount (franchise). Lid 5: on (partial) cessation (staking) the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

#### Reduction for pension accrual, art. 3.127 lid 4 and art. 10a.25 (Corrected)
Texts 2020 to 2023-07-01: 6,27 times (keer) the growth of the annual pension entitlement (aangroei van het jaarlijkse pensioenrecht) from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). From the 2024-01-01 text: the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension, excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wage Tax Act 1964 (Wet LB 1964), plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor. The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Lid 1: for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wage Tax Act 1964 (old norms) the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 applies, and that also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2: for a fixed premium per year of service (dienstjaar) (art. 38r) the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434 (retroactive to 1 July 2023) is A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the net pension premium divided by the net factor. The research gave the older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)

#### Indexation of the art. 3.127 amounts (Corrected, article number filled)
Art. 10.2b. In the 2020 text art. 10.2b lid 2 indexes the income cap with the contract wage development factor (contractloonontwikkelingsfactor), the franchise follows art. 10.1, and art. 10.2b lid 1 derives the FOR maximum from the art. 3.127 lid 3 amounts times the FOR percentage. In the 2024 to 2027 texts art. 10.2b (titled "Contractloonontwikkelingscorrectie, percentage jaarruimte en drempelbedrag") has lid 1 income cap x contract wage development factor, lid 3 the annual allowance percentage set each year equal to the art. 18a lid 1 Wage Tax Act 1964 percentage, and lid 4 the franchise set at the AOW benefit for married persons without allowance (toeslag) plus holiday allowance (vakantietoeslag) x 100/75. The Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) says the income cap was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (Amendment (amendement) Grinwis c.s., Parliamentary papers (kamerstukken) II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90). #### Deduction timing and the allowance limit (Verified)
Premiums count in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend) (art. 3.130 lid 1). Premiums for the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion (art. 3.129), the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Premiums above the allowance are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed. The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Annuity products and benefit conditions (Verified)
Three forms: Annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening), annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht). Old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) (art. 3.125 lid 1 a): first instalment (termijn) at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, payments until death. FISIN example: AOW age in 2030, start at the latest on 31 December 2035. Account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht): at least 20 years, plus the number of years the benefit starts before the AOW year (start at 63 with AOW age 67 means 24 years). Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) (art. 3.125 lid 1 b): for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner it ends at death or at the latest at age 30, for an account or investment right at least 20 years or for children under 30 at least 5 years. Annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind): deductible without pension shortfall (art. 3.124 lid 1 b). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.124 and 3.125), FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) (Verified)
Art. 3.125 lid 1 c: at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN adds that above the cap premiums are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years. Caps: € 22.089 (2020), € 22.443 (2021), € 22.735 (2022), € 24.168 (2023), € 26.463 (2024), € 26.781 (2025), € 27.192 (2026). Sources: art. 3.125 per year, FISIN chapters, Ministry tables.

#### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) (Verified for 2020 to 2026)
Only for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. Ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age or starting a pension (pensioen). Yearly maximum € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. Sources: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

#### Profit from business discontinuation to annuity, art. 3.129 (Verified, tier wording Corrected for 2024 to 2026)
Deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions (value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13). Tier a: age at most five years below the AOW age at cessation, or at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with annuity starting within six months, or cessation by death. Tier b: age at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or annuity starting immediately. Tier c: other cases. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen , https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

#### Losing annuity status, start date (ingangsdatum) and survivors (nabestaanden) deadlines (Verified)
If an annuity stops meeting the conditions (gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation (afkoop), unblocking (deblokkering)) the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due. An annuity that reaches its start date (FISIN 2023 onward: einddatum) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. Survivor annuity: 2 full calendar years after the year of death (FISIN 2023 onward: "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", same outcome). Extension on request in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). Refunds of a deducted premium are negative expenditure (negatieve uitgaven) without revision interest, for an annuity only if undone within 30 days. Sources: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

#### Commutation exceptions, art. 3.133 (Verified, cap amounts filled)
Commutation of a small annuity in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount (art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d). Lid 9: Commutation is not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term incapacitated for work (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment. Amounts per year in the year blocks. Sources: art. 3.133 per year.

#### AOV premium deductibility (Verified)
Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c: premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in respect of (ter zake van) disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer are expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen), with no allowance cap in the article (FISIN: "volledig aftrekken"). Not deductible: premiums the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies paying a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Zvw premiums. The four exclusions and section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. As entrepreneur (ondernemer) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing). A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. Rule of thumb on the page: premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.124), FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

#### Mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) for entrepreneurs (ondernemers) (Verified)
A mandatory occupational or industry-wide pension contribution (beroeps- of bedrijfstakpensioenpremie) is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). It is deductible as a business expense, subject to a restriction. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) link (Corrected article references)
Access to a FOR addition requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year. That is art. 3.67, not art. 3.68. Art. 3.68: addition 9,44% of the profit (winst), capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit, and not above the growth of business capital (ondernemingsvermogen). Art. 3.70 lid 1: Decrease (afname) by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over business assets at cessation, AOW age at the start of the year, or failing the hours criterion this year and last year. The FOR-annuity conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 (not art. 3.127 lid 8, which is the order-in-council rule (AMvB) on pension accrual data in those texts) and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023. From 2023 no addition is possible, an existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1), and art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 (not art. 3.127 lid 10 and 11) add the transition for the annual allowance: for a taxpayer with a FOR the contribution base uses the profit before reduction (afneming) of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, before addition and before entrepreneur's allowance, and for 2023 and 2024 the annual allowance is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the year-end reduction. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. #### BAZ, Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen), bill 36 912 (Verified as proposed)
Docket status on 2026-10-11: "In afwachting nota nav verslag". Submitted (ingediend) 24 March 2026, no advisory process (adviestraject) by the Fundamental rights (grondrechten) committee (2 April), contribution date (inbrengdatum) set on 7 April for 20 May, technical briefing (technische briefing) 13 May, postponement request 15 May (Neijenhuis, D66, with VVD, GroenLinks-PvdA, CDA, JA21), contribution to the debate (inbreng verslag) on 27 May 2026 ("Inbreng geleverd"), Report document 36 912 nr. 5 published 29 May 2026. Bill art. 16: waiting period (wachttijd) 104 weeks (weken). MvT: benefit 70% of the tax base (grondslag), tax base at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so at most the minimum wage. Premium set yearly by UWV, estimated at about 5,4% of profits from business activities up to the maximum tax base. The MvT examples use a maximum tax base of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit, and UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes. Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force by royal decree (koninklijk besluit), and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16 (104 weken).
