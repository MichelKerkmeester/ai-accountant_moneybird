#### Jaarruimte rate, pensioentekort condition and formula (Verified)
Years 2020 to 2027. Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pensioentekort in the previous calendar year deduct lijfrentepremies up to a percentage of the premiegrondslag, reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual. The percentage is 13,3% in the texts of 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01, and 30% in the texts of 2023-07-01, 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01 and 2027-01-01. Up to 2022 the outcome is also reduced by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve (lid 1 and lid 4 sub b). From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. The Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023". Formula for a self-employed person without pension accrual: percentage x (min(income of previous year, aftoppingsgrens) minus franchise). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

The 30% itself runs from the Wtp with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023 per the Belastingdienst Wtp page. #### Age window (Verified)
2020 to 2022 (and the 2023-01-01 text): at the start of the calendar year the taxpayer has not yet reached the AOW age. From the Wtp (2023-07-01 text, applied to all of 2023) to 2027: the taxpayer has not yet reached the age five years above the AOW age. FISIN 2023 to 2026 say "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd". The Belastingdienst page for 2026 adds that a taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the jaarruimte 2026 but may still use the reserveringsruimte 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen , https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Reserveringsruimte look-back (Verified)
Art. 3.127 lid 2: unused jaarruimte of the preceding period may be used on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first. The period is "zeven jaar" in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts and "tien jaar" from the 2023-07-01 text. FISIN windows: 2013 to 2019 (2020), 2014 to 2020 (2021), 2014 to 2021 (2022, see the open point in 2022), 2013 to 2022 (2023), 2014 to 2023 (2024), 2015 to 2024 (2025), 2016 to 2025 (2026). The Belastingdienst page advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse, and says unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Reserveringsruimte cap (Verified)
2020 to 2022: at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount, raised to a higher euro amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. From the 2023-07-01 text: one flat euro maximum, no percentage and no age split. Sources: the art. 3.127 lid 2 texts per year and the FISIN chapters.

#### Premiegrondslag composition (Verified)
Art. 3.127 lid 3: the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) winst uit onderneming vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve en vóór de ondernemersaftrek (2020 to 2023-01-01 text) or winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek (from the 2023-07-01 text), (b) belastbaar loon, (c) belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden and (d) belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen, counted up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (aftoppingsgrens) and then reduced by the laatstvermelde bedrag (franchise). Lid 5: on (partial) staking the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 , https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

#### Reduction for pension accrual, art. 3.127 lid 4 and art. 10a.25 (Corrected)
Texts 2020 to 2023-07-01: 6,27 keer the aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. From the 2024-01-01 text: the premiums ingelegd in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen and partnerpensioen, excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Lid 1: for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964 (old norms) the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 applies, and that also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2: for a fixed premium per dienstjaar (art. 38r) the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434 (retroactive to 1 July 2023) is A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor. The research gave the older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.127 and art. 10a.25), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)

#### Indexation of the art. 3.127 amounts (Corrected, article number filled)
Art. 10.2b. In the 2020 text art. 10.2b lid 2 indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor, the franchise follows art. 10.1, and art. 10.2b lid 1 derives the FOR maximum from the art. 3.127 lid 3 amounts times the FOR percentage. #### Deduction timing and the ruimte limit (Verified)
Premiums count in the year they are paid or verrekend (art. 3.130 lid 1). Premiums for the stakingswinst conversion (art. 3.129), the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Lijfrente products and uitkering conditions (Verified)
Three forms: lijfrenteverzekering, lijfrenterekening, lijfrentebeleggingsrecht. Oudedagslijfrente (art. 3.125 lid 1 a): first termijn at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, payments until death. FISIN example: AOW age in 2030, start at the latest on 31 December 2035. Rekening or beleggingsrecht: at least 20 years, plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year (start at 63 with AOW age 67 means 24 years). Nabestaandenlijfrente (art. 3.125 lid 1 b): for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner it ends at death or at the latest at age 30, for a rekening or beleggingsrecht at least 20 years or for children under 30 at least 5 years. Lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind: deductible without pensioentekort (art. 3.124 lid 1 b). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.124 and 3.125), FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente (Verified)
Art. 3.125 lid 1 c: at least five years of termijnen, starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN adds that above the cap premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. Caps: € 22.089 (2020), € 22.443 (2021), € 22.735 (2022), € 24.168 (2023), € 26.463 (2024), € 26.781 (2025), € 27.192 (2026). Sources: art. 3.125 per year, FISIN chapters, Ministry tables.

#### Overbruggingslijfrente (Verified for 2020 to 2026)
Only for lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006. Ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age or starting a pensioen. Yearly maximum € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. Sources: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

#### Stakingswinst to lijfrente, art. 3.129 (Verified, tier wording Corrected for 2024 to 2026)
Deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions (value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13). Tier a: age at most five years below the AOW age at staking, or at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt with lijfrente starting within six months, or staking by death. Tier b: age at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or lijfrente starting immediately. Tier c: other cases. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen , https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

#### Losing lijfrente status, ingangsdatum and nabestaanden deadlines (Verified)
If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions (gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop, deblokkering) the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente is due. A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum (FISIN 2023 onward: einddatum) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. Nabestaandenlijfrente: 2 full calendar years after the year of death (FISIN 2023 onward: "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", same outcome). Extension on request in bijzondere omstandigheden. Refunds of a deducted premium are negatieve uitgaven without revisierente, for a lijfrente only if undone within 30 days. Sources: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

#### Afkoop exceptions, art. 3.133 (Verified, cap amounts filled)
Afkoop of a small lijfrente in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount (art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d). Lid 9: afkoop is not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer or gerechtigde is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn. Amounts per year in the year blocks. Sources: art. 3.133 per year.

#### AOV premium deductibility (Verified)
Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c: premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer are uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen, with no ruimte cap in the article (FISIN: "volledig aftrekken"). Not deductible: premiums the employer takes into account for loonheffing, mandatory Ziektewet and WIA premiums, policies paying a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen, and Zvw premiums. The four exclusions and section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. As ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing. A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. Rule of thumb on the page: premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.124), FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

#### Mandatory beroepspensioen for ondernemers (Verified)
A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming with a restriction. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

#### Oudedagsreserve link (Corrected article references)
Access to a FOR toevoeging requires the urencriterium and not yet AOW age at the start of the year. That is art. 3.67, not art. 3.68. Art. 3.68: toevoeging 9,44% of the winst, capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst, and not above the growth of ondernemingsvermogen. Art. 3.70 lid 1: afname by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over ondernemingsvermogen at staking, AOW age at the start of the year, or failing the urencriterium this year and last year. The FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 (not art. 3.127 lid 8, which is the AMvB rule on pension accrual data in those texts) and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023. From 2023 no toevoeging is possible, an existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1), and art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 (not art. 3.127 lid 10 and 11) add the transition for the jaarruimte: for a taxpayer with a FOR the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek, and for 2023 and 2024 the jaarruimte is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the year-end afneming. On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.67, 3.68, 3.70, 3.128), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve

#### Zelfstandigen in a sectoraal pensioenfonds (Verified)
A self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds of the branch if the fund allows it, premiums are then deductible, and the proef runs until 1 July 2028. Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimenteerbepaling zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions.
