#### Box 1 table structure and the premie volksverzekeringen

Verified. Wet IB 2001 art. 2.10 lid 1 holds only the income-tax part. The first statutory band is income tax only (9,70% in 2020, 9,45% in 2021, 9,42% in 2022, 9,28% in 2023, 9,32% in 2024, 8,17% in 2025, 8,10% in 2026). The premie volksverzekeringen of 27,65% (AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10%, Wlz 9,65%) is charged over the premie-inkomen up to a ceiling that equals the end of that first band. The published combined first-bracket rate is the income-tax part plus 27,65%. Above the premium ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the combined rate, which is why 2020 to 2024 show one combined rate up to the top boundary. The ceiling is € 34.712 (2020), € 35.129 (2021), € 35.472 (2022), € 37.149 (2023), € 38.098 (2024), € 38.441 (2025) and € 38.883 (2026), and for people born before 1 January 1946 it is € 35.375, € 35.941, € 36.409, € 38.703, € 40.021, € 40.502 and € 41.123. Art. 2.10a holds the table for people born before 1 January 1946 with that higher first boundary. Once the AOW age is reached no AOW premium is due, which is why the AOW-age first-bracket rate is lower. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/premie_volksverzekeringen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen. The https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen page states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age. #### Tariefsaanpassing (art. 2.10 lid 2)

Verified. When grondslagverminderende posten have been deducted, the tax under lid 1 is raised by a percentage of the amount by which the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning plus those posten exceeds the top bracket boundary, capped at that percentage of the deducted posten. The percentage is 3,5% (2020), 6,5% (2021), 9,5% (2022), 12,57% (2023), 12,53% (2024), 12,02% (2025), 11,94% (2026) and 11,91% in the enacted 2027 text. The effective deduction value is 49,50% minus the percentage. The posten are the persoonsgebonden aftrek (onderhoudsverplichtingen including partneralimentatie, weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten, aftrekbare giften, specifieke zorgkosten, restant persoonsgebonden aftrek voorgaande jaren), the aftrek kosten eigen woning (hypotheekrente, periodieke erfpacht, opstal of beklemming, kosten afsluiten hypotheek, restschuldkosten), the ondernemersfaciliteiten (zelfstandigenaftrek, aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek, mkb-winstvrijstelling) and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen. It is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag. #### Winst uit onderneming into box 1

Verified in part. Belastbare winst uit onderneming is the winst as ondernemer reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning is the inkomen uit werk en woning reduced by te verrekenen verliezen, and that inkomen includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming (art. 3.1). The MKB-winstvrijstelling is computed on the winst after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and MKB). Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen are taken out of the winst first (bosbouwvrijstelling, landbouwvrijstelling, vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst for the part above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies, benefits under eindheffing, mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject vrijstellingen), and a medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). The box 1 saldo is taxed by art. 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the tariefsaanpassing, and the heffingskortingen reduce the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen (the yearly FISIN heffingskortingen pages, for example https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference.

#### Verzamelinkomen

Verified. Verzamelinkomen is the sum of the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning, the belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang and the belastbaar inkomen uit sparen en beleggen, minus te verrekenen persoonsgebonden aftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18). It is the AHK phase-out base from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026).

#### Algemene heffingskorting

Verified. Granted to every taxpayer, applied automatically in the return and by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie in the payroll. The phase-out base is the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning in 2020 to 2024 and the verzamelinkomen from 2025. The phase-out runs from the afbouwpunt to the top bracket boundary, where the korting reaches nil. The AHK and AK afbouwpunten are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) footnote to Tabel 1). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301).

#### Arbeidskorting

Verified. Granted over the arbeidsinkomen, which for an IB-ondernemer includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling and excludes the part earned as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen). The table has four bands: a build-up percentage, two further build-up steps, then a phase-out of 6% (2020, 2021), 5,860% (2022) or 6,510% (2023 to 2026) to nil. From 2024 the year tables state that if the employer applied a higher AK and the arbeidsinkomen is not above the third-band boundary, the Belastingdienst takes over the employer's amount up to the maximum. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024 to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.

#### AOW transition year for the heffingskortingen

Verified for 2024 to 2026. The ordinary AHK, AK and IACK tables do not apply in the year in which the AOW age is reached. The amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag of that year in Mijn Belastingdienst. A rounding difference of a few euros between the table and the aanslag can occur and the aanslag is correct. #### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

Verified. 11,45% of the arbeidsinkomen above a threshold, up to a maximum. Applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in the payroll. Child under 12 on 1 January. Through 2024 the child must be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months (2024 also accepts 6 months in the same household under a Staatscourant approval). From 2025 the child must belong to the household for at least 6 months. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months still gives the korting. No fiscal partner, or one for 6 months or less, or a lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner. Qualifying children are own, step, adopted, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child, and a pleegkind only when raised and maintained as own and not for a pleegvergoeding. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES can qualify. #### Zvw inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage

Verified. Three channels. The werkgeversheffing is paid by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie over loon uit loondienst, DGA loon when insured for the werknemersverzekeringen, bijstand, early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for ziekenfonds-insured people of 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits. The ingehouden bijdrage is withheld from pension, lijfrente and other uitkeringen with loonheffing. The bijdrage by (voorlopige) aanslag covers winst uit Nederland (not the conversion of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente), inkomsten uit overig werk, loon from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign uitkeringsinstantie and periodieke uitkeringen without loonheffing such as partneralimentatie. The aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting. The maximum bijdrage-inkomen applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom. Exceptions: militairen in active service or on buitengewoon verlof with retention of military income, deelvissers where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

#### Zvw base for an IB-ondernemer

Corrected (the topic file left this open). Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve. Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines belastbare winst as winst minus ondernemersaftrek minus MKB-winstvrijstelling. So the Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer is the profit after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Art. 43 lid 3 sets the bijdrage-inkomen at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation. Art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages. #### Ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting

Verified. Winst uit onderneming requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. #### Oudedagsreserve

Verified. Additions of 9,44% of the profit with a yearly maximum reduced by beroepspensioenpremie, for an ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and is below AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). Since 2023 no new oudedagsreserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). Reduction or release follows on buying a lijfrente with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the ondernemingsvermogen, on cessation, on reaching the AOW age on 1 January, on failing the urencriterium in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (FOR, as summarised on fetch).
