### Example 1: Definitive assessment (aanslag) 2021 (Official example)
This example is copied from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- Return before 1 May 2022: the definitive assessment comes at the latest 31 December 2024.
- Return with 4 months deferral (uitstel), so before 1 September 2022: the assessment comes at the latest 30 April 2025.
No calculation is needed. The source gives both dates.

### Example 2: Threshold example (Official example)
This example is on the same Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- A provisional refund of € 100 is received.
- The entitlement is € 80.
- The difference is € 100,00 - € 80,00 = € 20,00.
- € 20,00 is not higher than the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) of € 48, so nothing has to be paid back.
- The source gives the result as a nil assessment (nihilaanslag).

### Example 3: Income tax tax interest (belastingrente) for 2021 (Calculated example)
Inputs are round and invented. The rate, the period and the day count come from section 2.
- Assume a tax due of € 10.000 for tax year 2021.
- Assume the return is filed on 1 June 2022, so it arrives on or after 1 May 2022.
- Assume the assessment date is 31 October 2022.
- Assume the assessment does not follow the return unchanged, so the 19-week rule for an unchanged return does not apply.
- The interest period runs from 1 July 2022 to 6 weeks after the assessment date.
- 6 weeks x 7 days = 42 days. 31 October 2022 plus 42 days is 12 December 2022.
- Days from 1 July 2022 to 12 December 2022 with 30 days per month: 5 x 30 + 12 = 162 days.
- The calculation uses 360 days per year.
- Rate: 4%.
- Interest = € 10.000 x 4% x 162 / 360.
- € 10.000 x 0,04 = € 400,00.
- € 400,00 x 162 = € 64.800,00.
- € 64.800,00 / 360 = € 180,00.
- Rounded down to whole euros: € 180.
Source for the rate, period and day count: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### Example 4: VAT tax interest for 2021 (Calculated example)
Inputs are round and invented. The rate, the 14 days and the day count come from section 2.
- Assume a VAT supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for tax year 2021 with a principal of € 5.000.
- Assume the supplementary assessment date is 15 October 2022.
- The interest period runs from 1 January 2022 to 14 days after the supplementary assessment date.
- 14 days after 15 October 2022 is 29 October 2022.
- Days from 1 January 2022 to 29 October 2022 with 30 days per month: 9 x 30 + 29 = 299 days.
- The calculation uses 360 days per year.
- Rate: 4%.
- Interest = € 5.000 x 4% x 299 / 360.
- € 5.000 x 0,04 = € 200,00.
- € 200,00 x 299 = € 59.800,00.
- € 59.800,00 / 360 = € 166,11 (rounded to two decimals).
- Rounded down to whole euros: € 166.
Source for the rate, period and day count: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting

### Example 5: Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) term (Calculated example)
The rule is in section 2. A 4-year lease is kept for the duration of the lease and then 7 more years.
- Lease duration: 4 years.
- Extra record-retention obligation: 7 years.
- Total: 4 + 7 = 11 years.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### Example 6: Chamber of Commerce registration window (Calculated example)
The rule is in section 2. Assume the business starts on 1 June 2021.
- One week before 1 June 2021 is 25 May 2021.
- One week after 1 June 2021 is 8 June 2021.
- The registration window runs from 25 May 2021 to 8 June 2021.
- Registering within that window means Chamber of Commerce issues the Chamber of Commerce number (kvk-nummer) immediately.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/
