## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 IB return 2021 and deferral (uitstel)
The income tax return (inkomstenbelasting) for tax year 2021 must arrive before 1 May 2022. The filing period opens on 1 March 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen.
Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
The form can be used only for the first deferral request. The fiscal partner must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting.
The channel is settled. Online in My Tax and Customs Administration (mijn belastingdienst) until 1 May, giving deferral to 1 September, only for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). The deferral page says online is not possible. Read in full, the deferral page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte.
AWR art. 9 lid 1: for taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the invitation. AWR art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions. The same text appears in the 2020 and 2026 versions. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Interaction: deferral usually means tax interest. See 3.10.

### 3.2 Definitive assessment (aanslag) 2021
AWR art. 11 lid 3: the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. The same text appears in the 2020 and 2026 versions. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For tax year 2021: the definitive assessment comes at the latest 31 December 2024. With 4 months deferral the return is due before 1 September 2022 and the assessment comes at the latest 30 April 2025. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
Added rule: if within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Interaction: this is the normal assessment term. Additional assessment (navordering) under AWR art. 16 can go further. See 3.5.

### 3.3 Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2021
The assessment threshold and refund threshold decide whether a small amount is collected or paid. The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For 2021 the live page prints € 48 and € 15 in that wording. Captures up to 2023 used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold one euro higher, € 49 and € 16 for 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. That is the reading in the checked material. The conflict is in section 7.
Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment before year end count toward the threshold. A paid provisional assessment does not count. A refund received after filing does not count. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
Official example: a provisional refund of € 100 against an entitlement of € 80 gives a nil assessment (nihilaanslag). Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### 3.4 Filing without invitation for tax year 2021
Without a filing invitation letter a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2021 that is until 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen.
This route is separate from the normal filing deadline. It lets a taxpayer file without an invitation.

### 3.5 Additional assessment 2021
AWR art. 16 lid 1: an additional assessment needs a new fact. A fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably apparent to the inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Lid 2: additional assessment is also possible when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund or provisional loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) was incorrectly offset, and for a mistake (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar). That is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Source: same URLs.
Lid 3: the term is 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral. For a mistake-based additional assessment the term is only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Source: same URLs.
Lid 4: the term is 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Source: same URLs.
Lid 5: the term is extended by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral gives additional assessment until 31 May 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag.
A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). Source: same additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) URL.
Interaction: the definitive assessment term in 3.2 is 3 years. Additional assessment can go beyond it under art. 16.

### 3.6 Payment terms of assessments
Collection of State Taxes Act 1990 art. 9 sets the payment terms. The same text appears on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01.
An assessment is collectable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment is collectable 1 month after its date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) is collectable 14 days after its date. Source: same URLs.
A provisional assessment IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date. The first instalment is one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. Source: same URLs.

### 3.7 Objection (bezwaar) and request for reduction
Objection must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment. For income tax it can be made online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if asked for in the objection. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar.
There is no objection against a provisional assessment IB. Only a change is possible. Source: same eisen-bezwaar URL.
After the objection term a request for an ex officio reduction (verzoek om ambtshalve vermindering) is possible. Online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The sources give assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise by letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting.
For assessment 2021 the exact last online date is not in the checked sources. See section 7.

### 3.8 Provisional assessment
A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen.
Changing is not mandatory. Reasons include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst)). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. Source: same URL.
A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after filing. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen.
Interaction: no objection against a provisional assessment IB, only a change. See 3.7. Payment terms are in 3.6.

### 3.9 Tax interest rates 2021
Tax interest is charged on certain taxes. For 2021 the rate is 4% all year for income tax and VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
The rate table gives the history. All taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen): 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020. For the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started 1 July 2020. Then 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025, 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: same URL.
From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Vpb, withholding tax, minimum tax (minimumbelasting) and solidarity contribution (solidariteitsbijdrage) plus 5,5 points, minimum 5,5%. Source: same URL. This method is after 2021, so it does not set the 2021 rate.
After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT and the other taxes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage. This is after 2021 and does not change the 2021 rate.

### 3.10 Tax interest on tax year 2021 income tax
Tax interest on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting.
The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. Source: same URL.
For an additional assessment the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. Source: same URL.
No interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. Source: same URL.
The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: same URL.
For tax year 2021 the rate is 4% from 1 July 2022. The source note says 6% applies to days from 1 July 2023. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and the 2021 value row.

### 3.11 Tax interest on VAT
Tax interest on VAT is charged on a supplementary assessment and on late payment. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting.
Late payment: the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. Supplementary assessment: the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. Source: same URL.
No interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. Source: same URL.
The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) (end at most 10 weeks after receipt) applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible. Source: same URL. This is after 2021 and does not change 2021.
For 2021 the rate is 4% all year.

### 3.12 VAT filing and payment deadlines 2021
AWR art. 10 lid 2: a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1: the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give for every year the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, for monthly and quarterly returns, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums.
Monthly 2021: Dec 2020 31 Jan 2021, Jan 28 Feb, Feb 31 Mar, Mar 30 Apr, Apr 31 May, May 30 Jun, Jun 31 Jul, Jul 31 Aug, Aug 30 Sep, Sep 31 Oct, Oct 30 Nov, Nov 31 Dec 2021, Dec 2021 31 Jan 2022. Quarterly 2021: Q4 2020 31 January 2021, Q1 30 April 2021, Q2 31 July 2021, Q3 31 October 2021, Q4 31 January 2022. Yearly return 2021 by 31 March 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums.
Deferral for a VAT return is only possible in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit). It must be requested in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment and penalty remain possible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte.
Interaction: late filing or late payment can lead to a penalty. See 3.13.

### 3.13 Penalties (boetes) 2021
For income tax, after the deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days. Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine (verzuimboete). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. No return at all gives an ex officio (ambtshalve) assessment plus default fine. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment. No penalty is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. Requesting a return: within 6 months after the tax debt arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks grace period (coulance). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
Amounts for 2021: default fine IB € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210423/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
Penalty rates for gross negligence or intent: 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), box 3 75% and 150%, aggravated 100% and 300%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
For VAT failure to file (aangifteverzuim) the rule is 7 calendar days grace period, then a penalty of € 68. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210331/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.
Interaction: deferral usually means tax interest. See 3.10.

### 3.14 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)
AWR art. 52 lid 4: persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Source: same URLs.
Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). Source: same URLs.
The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table: basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
The term starts when the item is no longer current. A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Source: same URL.
Basic records include general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: same URL.

### 3.15 Chamber of Commerce and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration
Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1: the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16.
Other required statements (opgaven) are made within one week after the fact. Source: same URL.
Chamber of Commerce issues the Chamber of Commerce number (kvk-nummer) immediately when registering within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead. Appointments up to eight weeks ahead. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/.
Chamber of Commerce passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number, other forms get a legal entity identifier, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.
Where Chamber of Commerce registration is not possible, the form Start-up notification (opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister)) is used. Source: same Dutch Tax and Customs Administration URL.
