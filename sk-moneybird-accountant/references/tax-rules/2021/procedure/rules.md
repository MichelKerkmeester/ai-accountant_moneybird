## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 IB return 2021 and uitstel
The income tax return (inkomstenbelasting) for tax year 2021 must arrive before 1 May 2022. The filing period opens on 1 March 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen.
Uitstel (extension) must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, to 1 September 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
The form can be used only for the first uitstel request. The fiscal partner must request separately. Uitstel usually means belastingrente (tax interest). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting.
The channel is settled. Online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May, giving uitstel to 1 September, only for someone who received an aangiftebrief (filing invitation letter). The uitstel page says online is not possible. Read in full, the uitstel page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both are current Belastingdienst text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte.
AWR art. 9 lid 1: for aanslagbelastingen the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the invitation. AWR art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The same text appears in the 2020 and 2026 versions. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Interaction: uitstel usually means belastingrente. See 3.10.

### 3.2 Definitive aanslag 2021
AWR art. 11 lid 3: the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld (tax debt) arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. The same text appears in the 2020 and 2026 versions. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For tax year 2021: the definitive aanslag comes at the latest 31 December 2024. With 4 months uitstel the return is due before 1 September 2022 and the aanslag comes at the latest 30 April 2025. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
Added rule: if within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Interaction: this is the normal assessment term. Navordering under AWR art. 16 can go further. See 3.5.

### 3.3 Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2021
The aanslaggrens (assessment threshold) and teruggaafgrens (refund threshold) decide whether a small amount is collected or paid. The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For 2021 the live page prints € 48 and € 15 in that wording. Captures up to 2023 used gelijk aan of meer dan (equal to or more than) and printed each grens one euro higher, € 49 and € 16 for 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. That is the reading in the checked material. The conflict is in section 7.
Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens. A paid voorlopige aanslag does not count. A refund received after filing does not count. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
Official example: a voorlopige teruggaaf of € 100 against an entitlement of € 80 gives a nihilaanslag. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### 3.4 Filing without invitation for tax year 2021
Without an aangiftebrief a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2021 that is until 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen.
This route is separate from the normal filing deadline. It lets a taxpayer file without an invitation.

### 3.5 Navordering 2021
AWR art. 16 lid 1: a navordering (additional assessment) needs a new fact. A fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw (in bad faith). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Lid 2: navordering is also possible when a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and for a fout (mistake) that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar (reasonably recognizable). That is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Source: same URLs.
Lid 3: the term is 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel. For a fout-based navordering the term is only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Source: same URLs.
Lid 4: the term is 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Source: same URLs.
Lid 5: the term is extended by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Belastingdienst example: tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel gives navordering until 31 May 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag.
A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Source: same navorderingsaanslag URL.
Interaction: the definitive aanslag term in 3.2 is 3 years. Navordering can go beyond it under art. 16.

### 3.6 Payment terms of assessments
Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 sets the payment terms. The same text appears on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01.
An aanslag (assessment) is invorderbaar (collectable) 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet (assessment notice). A navorderingsaanslag is collectable 1 month after its date. A naheffingsaanslag is collectable 14 days after its date. Source: same URLs.
A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen (instalments) as months remain after the month of the date. The first instalment is one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. Source: same URLs.

### 3.7 Bezwaar and verzoek om vermindering
Bezwaar (objection) must be made within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. For income tax it can be made online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if asked for in the bezwaar. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar.
There is no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag IB. Only a change is possible. Source: same eisen-bezwaar URL.
After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering (request for an ex officio reduction) is possible. Online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The sources give aanslag 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise by letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting.
For aanslag 2021 the exact last online date is not in the checked sources. See section 7.

### 3.8 Voorlopige aanslag
A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen.
Changing is not mandatory. Reasons include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst, cessation profit). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming (business profit) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. Source: same URL.
A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with an aangiftebrief until the filing date or the uitstel date, and no longer after filing. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen.
Interaction: no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag IB, only a change. See 3.7. Payment terms are in 3.6.

### 3.9 Belastingrente rates 2021
Belastingrente (tax interest) is charged on certain taxes. For 2021 the rate is 4% all year for income tax and btw. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
The rate table gives the history. All taxes except Vpb and toeslagen: 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020. For the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started 1 July 2020. Then 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025, 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: same URL.
From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB herfinancieringsrente (refinancing rate) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Vpb, bronbelasting, minimumbelasting and solidariteitsbijdrage plus 5,5 points, minimum 5,5%. Source: same URL. This method is after 2021, so it does not set the 2021 rate.
After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, btw and the other taxes. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage. This is after 2021 and does not change the 2021 rate.

### 3.10 Belastingrente on tax year 2021 income tax
Belastingrente on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting.
The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. Source: same URL.
For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. Source: same URL.
No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. Source: same URL.
The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: same URL.
For tax year 2021 the rate is 4% from 1 July 2022. The source note says 6% applies to days from 1 July 2023. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and the 2021 value row.

### 3.11 Belastingrente on btw
Belastingrente on btw is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting.
Late payment: the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. Naheffingsaanslag: the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. Source: same URL.
No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. Source: same URL.
The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen (end at most 10 weeks after receipt) applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable. Source: same URL. This is after 2021 and does not change 2021.
For 2021 the rate is 4% all year.

### 3.12 VAT filing and payment deadlines 2021
AWR art. 10 lid 2: a return for a tijdvak (period) is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak. AWR art. 19 lid 1: the tax is paid within one month after the end of the tijdvak. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
The Belastingdienst tables give for every year the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, for monthly and quarterly returns, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums.
Monthly 2021: Dec 2020 31 Jan 2021, Jan 28 Feb, Feb 31 Mar, Mar 30 Apr, Apr 31 May, May 30 Jun, Jun 31 Jul, Jul 31 Aug, Aug 30 Sep, Sep 31 Oct, Oct 30 Nov, Nov 31 Dec 2021, Dec 2021 31 Jan 2022. Quarterly 2021: Q4 2020 31 January 2021, Q1 30 April 2021, Q2 31 July 2021, Q3 31 October 2021, Q4 31 January 2022. Yearly return 2021 by 31 March 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums.
Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is only possible in uitzonderlijke situaties (exceptional situations, an ernstige calamiteit or serious calamity). It must be requested in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date. A naheffingsaanslag and boete remain possible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte.
Interaction: late filing or late payment can lead to a boete. See 3.13.

### 3.13 Boetes 2021
For income tax, after the deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering (reminder), then an aanmaning (demand) with 10 werkdagen (working days). Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete (default penalty). The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen (allowances) and the voorlopige aanslag. No return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag (ex officio assessment) plus verzuimboete. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete (intent penalty), also with a navorderingsaanslag. No boete is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief. Requesting a return: within 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks coulance (grace period). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
Amounts for 2021: verzuimboete IB € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, betaalverzuimboete (payment default penalty) up to € 5.514. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210423/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
Vergrijpboete percentages: 25% for grove schuld (gross negligence), 50% for opzet (intent), box 3 75% and 150%, aggravated 100% and 300%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
For btw aangifteverzuim the rule is 7 kalenderdagen (calendar days) coulance, then a boete of € 68. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210331/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.
Interaction: uitstel usually means belastingrente. See 3.10.

### 3.14 Bewaarplicht
AWR art. 52 lid 4: administratieplichtigen (persons required to keep records) keep the gegevensdragers (data carriers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (business profit) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (income from other activities). Source: same URLs.
Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans (balance sheet) and staat van baten en lasten (statement of income and expenses). Source: same URLs.
The Belastingdienst table: basisgegevens (basic records) 7 years, onroerende zaken (immovable property) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
The term starts when the gegeven (item) is no longer actual. A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Source: same URL.
Basisgegevens include grootboek (general ledger), debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie (debtors and creditors records), voorraadadministratie (inventory records), in- and verkoopadministratie (purchase and sales records), loonadministratie (payroll records), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: same URL.

### 3.15 KVK and Belastingdienst registration
Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1: the opgave (notification) for the first inschrijving (registration) of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16.
Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact. Source: same URL.
KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when registering within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead. Appointments up to eight weeks ahead. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/.
KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. An eenmanszaak (sole proprietorship) is registered with the bsn (citizen service number), other forms get an RSIN (legal entity identifier), and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer (VAT number) from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.
Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Source: same Belastingdienst URL.
