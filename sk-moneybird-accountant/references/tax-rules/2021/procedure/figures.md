## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the filing and procedure rules for tax year 2021 for a Dutch sole trader (self-employed person, zelfstandige, ZZP) and, where relevant, a BV. The most important dates are the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) before 1 May 2022, deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2022, and the definitive assessment (aanslag) at the latest 31 December 2024 or 30 April 2025 with deferral. Tax interest (belastingrente) is 4% all year for income tax and VAT. The VAT return and payment (btw-aangifte en betaling) dates (betaaldatums) are monthly or quarterly as listed in section 2. The law for 2021 is enacted.
