## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- IB return 2020 deadline: before 8 May 2021, because the 1 May 2021 date was moved one week due to a technical failure (storing) (Stcrt. 2021, 24144). IB return 2021 deadline: before 1 May 2022. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html and https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen.
- IB return 2020 with deferral (uitstel): before 1 September 2021. IB return 2021 with deferral: before 1 September 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Definitive assessment (aanslag) 2020 latest: 31 December 2023, with 4 months deferral 30 April 2024. Definitive assessment 2021 latest: 31 December 2024, with 4 months deferral 30 April 2025. Source: same archived page.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2020: € 47 and € 15 (higher than), equal to € 48 and € 16 under the older wording. 2021: € 48 and € 15 (higher than), equal to € 49 and € 16 under the older wording. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and the live page.
- Filing without invitation, tax year 2020: until 31 December 2025. 2021: until 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen.
- Tax interest (belastingrente) IB 2020: 4% to 30 June 2020, 0,01% from 1 July to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. 2021: 4% all year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- Tax interest VAT 2020: 4% to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June to 30 September 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020. 2021: 4% all year. Source: same rate table.
- Tax interest on tax year 2020 IB: from 1 July 2021 at 4%. 2021: from 1 July 2022 at 4%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting.
- VAT monthly dates 2020: Dec 2019 31 Jan 2020, Jan 29 Feb, Feb 31 Mar, Mar 30 Apr, Apr 31 May, May 30 Jun, Jun 31 Jul, Jul 31 Aug, Aug 30 Sep, Sep 31 Oct, Oct 30 Nov, Nov 31 Dec 2020, Dec 2020 31 Jan 2021. 2021: Dec 2020 31 Jan 2021, Jan 28 Feb, Feb 31 Mar, Mar 30 Apr, Apr 31 May, May 30 Jun, Jun 31 Jul, Jul 31 Aug, Aug 30 Sep, Sep 31 Oct, Oct 30 Nov, Nov 31 Dec 2021, Dec 2021 31 Jan 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201101/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
- VAT quarterly dates 2020: Q4 2019 31 January 2020, Q1 30 April 2020, Q2 31 July 2020, Q3 31 October 2020, Q4 31 January 2021. 2021: Q4 2020 31 January 2021, Q1 30 April 2021, Q2 31 July 2021, Q3 31 October 2021, Q4 31 January 2022. Source: same archived VAT date pages.
- VAT yearly return 2020: by 31 March 2021. 2021: by 31 March 2022. Source: same archived VAT date pages.
- VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2020: € 68 after 7 calendar days grace period (coulance). 2021: € 68. No change. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20210331/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte.
- IB penalties (boetes) 2020: default fine (verzuimboete) € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514. 2021: same. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20201120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete and https://web.archive.org/web/20210423/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete.
- Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) 2020: 7 years, AWR art. 52 lid 4. 2021: unchanged from 2020. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.
- Payment terms 2020: assessment 6 weeks, additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month, supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days, provisional assessment in monthly instalments (termijnen). 2021: unchanged from 2020. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the records so the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) can be met. Keep basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office (belastingkantoor). The term starts when the item is no longer current. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- File the IB return 2021 before 1 May 2022, or request deferral (uitstel) before the filing date to get to 1 September 2022. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- File and pay VAT by the monthly or quarterly dates in section 2. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20210820/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte.
- Register the business with Chamber of Commerce within the two-week window. Chamber of Commerce passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/.
- Record the provisional assessment payments or refunds, because they are settled in the return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen.
- A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag.
- For VAT, no interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting.
- If records are converted to another data carrier (gegevensdrager), keep the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten), because AWR art. 52 lid 5 excepts those. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2021 conflict. The live page prints € 48 and € 15 under the "higher than" wording. Captures up to 2023 print € 49 and € 16 under the "gelijk aan of meer dan" wording. The checked material's reading is that for whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold, but that reading is not a primary statement. What would settle it: a primary page that states the exact wording and values for 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Exact last date for an online request for reduction for assessment (aanslag) 2021. The rule is fewer than 5 years after the tax year. The sources give assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. The exact 2021 date is not in the checked sources. What would settle it: a primary page that states the date for assessment 2021. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting.
- Whether the deferral (uitstel) request deadline for the 2020 return also moved to 8 May 2021 is not stated on a primary page that was opened. This affects filing in calendar 2021. What would settle it: a primary page that states the deferral request deadline for the 2020 return. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html.
- For the 2020 return, the 2021 definitive assessment page still uses before 1 May 2021 in its example, while the deadline page and the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) decree (besluit) give 8 May 2021. Both are Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text. The 8 May date is the operative one for 2021 per the decree. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-24144.html and https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
