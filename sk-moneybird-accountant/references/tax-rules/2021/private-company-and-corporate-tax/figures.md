## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting, Vpb) for a BV in 2021, the DGA (director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder)) wage rules, dividend tax (dividendbelasting), the conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) and the switch from a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) to a BV. The 2021 Vpb rate is 15% for the first € 245.000 of the taxable amount and € 36.750 plus 25% above that bracket, and the innovation box (innovatiebox) effective rate is 9%, up from 7% in 2020. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 All stated 2021 values are enacted. The only Corrected items are the dividend stripping article in the Wet DB 1965 and the Zvw route for an uninsured DGA.
