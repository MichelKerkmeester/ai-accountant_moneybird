## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Vpb first bracket: 2020 had up to and including € 200.000 at 16,5%, 2021 has up to and including € 245.000 at 15%. The amount above the bracket changed from € 33.000 plus 25% in 2020 to € 36.750 plus 25% in 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01
- Innovation box (innovatiebox) fraction: 2020 had 7/H with an effective 7%, 2021 to 2026 have 9/H with an effective 9%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag): € 46.000 in 2020 to € 47.000 in 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01
- Zvw: the contribution changed from 5,45% in 2020 to 5,75% in 2021, the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) from € 57.232 to € 58.311, and the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) for an insured DGA from 6,70% to 7,00%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet
- Box 2 rate: 26,25% in 2020 to 26,9% flat in 2021 (and 2021 to 2023). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): no levy in 2020, the 2020 version holds only headings and amending chapter 7. The 2021 version is the first version with the levy articles, it hits interest and royalties and, with art. 22, the rate is 25% in 2021. Dividends are not in scope in 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01
- Art. 31 apportionment: a 2021 book year that straddles 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changes again on that date. A 2020 book year that straddled 1 January 2021 was apportioned for the same reason. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01
- Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening): unchanged in 2021 at 1 year back and 6 years forward. From 2022 a loss is set off one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01
- Art. 15b: unchanged in 2021 at 30% of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and a € 1.000.000 threshold. The percentage drops to 20% in 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01
- Participation (deelneming) threshold and fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) ownership requirement (bezitseis): unchanged at 5% and at least 95%. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds: unchanged for 2021 at micro € 350.000 and € 700.000, small € 6 million and € 12 million, medium € 20 million and € 40 million. Stb. 2024, 52 raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million and € 15 million, and € 25 million and € 50 million, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts are unchanged. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): at most € 3.630 in both 2020 and 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01
- Start-up relief: the statute minimum of 108% of twelve times the WML amount continues in 2021 (statute 2020 to 2022). A first use in 2021 may run at most 3 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang
- Dividend tax (dividendbelasting): 15% and the 100/85 gross-up are unchanged. The 2020 version of the Wet DB 1965 applies 2020 to 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- Dividend stripping: art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 in both years, corrected from art. 4 lid 3. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

A BV records the DGA wage in its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). For a DGA who is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), the BV withholds the income-related Zvw contribution on the net wage up to the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For 2021 that is 5,75% up to € 58.311. An insured DGA falls under the employer Zvw levy of 7,00% paid by the BV. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

File the corporate income tax return. A calendar book year is filed before 1 June of the next year, a broken book year within 5 months after its end, a short book year ending 31 December before 1 June and a short book year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension to 1 November is requested from 1 March to 1 June and is only for a calendar book year. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. These rules come from the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting

Record a dividend statement (dividendnota) once the proceeds (opbrengst) is paid, credited, settled or handed over. The dividend tax (dividendbelasting) return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A fully exempt participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) needs no return unless invited. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. The shareholder credits the withheld tax in the income tax return or, for a body, in the corporate income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

Deposit the annual accounts (jaarrekening) within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as such. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/

Keep the records for seven years. Records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept nine years following the year in which use began. OSS records are kept ten years after the year of the transaction. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

For a tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting), register the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the Accompanying form (Geleideformulier) at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting. The BV is a company in formation (bv in oprichting) until the notarial deed. The new legal form may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once, and a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm

In a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) the subsidiary files no separate return and the results of the companies are combined in one Vpb return. The subsidiary remains in existence and losses of one company offset profits of another. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The Decree (besluit) tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) of 30 June 2010, in force through 4 November 2025, was not read. The 2021 wording of the nine-month and three-year conditions in this file therefore rests on the successor Decree of 24 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 37092. Reading Stcrt. 2010, 10512 would settle the 2021 text. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html
- Corrected: the dividend stripping rule is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not art. 4 lid 3. The rule can be read in the dated texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01
- Corrected route for the Zvw contribution of an uninsured DGA: the Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 paragraph 8.2.2 states that the BV withholds the contribution on net wage and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) table says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding on net wage or by assessment (aanslag). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/
- Proposed law with no effect on 2021: Tax Plan 2027 (belastingplan 2027) (bill 37 022) changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j in Article XIX, and deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 in Article XX. It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- The 2026-01-01 text of the Wet Vpb 1969 notes that changes to art. 22 without an entry date exist. No amount or date for those changes is given, and they do not affect the 2021 rate. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01
- No further conflicts for 2021.
