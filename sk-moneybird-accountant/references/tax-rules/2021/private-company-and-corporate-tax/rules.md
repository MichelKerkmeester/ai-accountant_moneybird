## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) rate

A BV that is subject to Vpb pays tax on its taxable amount. Art. 22 computes the tax as the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount above the column I amount. For 2021 the series is up to and including € 245.000 at 15%, and above that € 36.750 plus 25%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The tax on a taxable amount of exactly € 245.000 is therefore € 36.750. The tax is reported in the corporate income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen

### 3.2 Rates for a book year that straddles 1 January 2022

Art. 31 applies when a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect. The tax is then (X/B × To) + (Y/B × Tn). X is the number of days before the change, Y the number of days from the change, B all days of the book year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The formula sits in art. 31 in both the 2020 and the 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 A 2021 book year that straddles 1 January 2022 is apportioned because art. 22 changes again on that date. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The 2022 series is up to and including € 395.000 at 15%, and above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01

### 3.3 Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking)

The generic interest deduction limitation of art. 15b lid 1 applies to a Vpb taxpayer with a net interest expense. The net interest expense is interest costs on loans minus interest income. It is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 30% of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) change pages state that non-deductible interest carries forward. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026 The percentage drops to 20% in 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01

### 3.4 Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening)

For 2020 and 2021 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixes it by decision. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Set-off follows the order in which losses arose. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 From 2022 a loss is set off one year back and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 A 2021 loss that carries into 2022 therefore meets the new per-year cap.

### 3.5 Innovation box (innovatiebox) fraction

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H. H is the highest art. 22 rate, so the effective rate is 9% from 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%." https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox Brands, logos and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox

### 3.6 Innovation box flat rate (forfait)

Art. 12bd gives a flat-rate benefit to a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses it. The flat-rate benefit is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000, and it applies only when that profit is positive. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.7 Innovation box smaller taxpayer

A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring (R&D declaration). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years are below € 37.500.000 and that net turnover over those five years is below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.8 Nexus multiplier

Art. 12bb applies a K/T part. K is the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3. The outcome is capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 No amount for that threshold is stated in the sources used here.

### 3.9 Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling)

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. A participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (art. 13 lid 2). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies (1/3 interest). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A tax-neutral contribution (geruisloze inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html For VAT a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html

### 3.10 Fiscal unity

The parent company must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the subsidiary, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the parent company is a BV, NV, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or a foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as a housing corporation (woningcorporatie) or a comparable foreign form, that the subsidiary is a BV or NV, that both have the same book years and profit rules and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ The subsidiary remains in existence but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/ and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the parent company at once. Subjective exemption of one subsidiary ends it for that subsidiary only (art. 15 lid 10). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01

### 3.11 Customary salary standard amount (normbedrag)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies and (c) the standard amount. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 For 2021 the standard amount is € 47.000. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.12 Customary salary comparable wage

In 2021 the comparable wage counts at 75%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 The escape of art. 12a lid 2 for 2020 to 2022 reads: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the wage is 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the standard amount. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 From 2023 the rule reads: if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 notes that a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.13 Customary salary floor

The customary salary rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (lid 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01

### 3.14 Start-up relief for starters (startersfaciliteit)

Statute 2020 to 2022, art. 12a lid 3 and 4: for a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the start-up percentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage is at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (verordening) (EU) nr. 1407/2013. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and that an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf Separate policy in the Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf

### 3.15 Zvw for a DGA

A DGA is employed by his own company for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A DGA who (alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity) can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). For him the company pays no employee insurance contributions and no employer Zvw levy, and instead withholds the income-related Zvw contribution from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf A DGA who is insured falls under the employer Zvw levy paid by the BV. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet For 2021 the contribution is 5,75%, the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 58.311 and the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) is 7,00%. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet The FISIN page says DGA wage falls under the employer levy only if he is insured for the employee insurance schemes. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet

### 3.16 Zvw route for an uninsured DGA

The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025, paragraph 8.2.2, states that for the uninsured DGA the BV withholds the contribution from the net wage and pays it through the payroll tax return. https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf This corrects the statement that the uninsured DGA pays the contribution himself by assessment (aanslag). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding on net wage or by assessment. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/

### 3.17 Box 2 rate

The box 2 rate for 2021 is 26,9% flat. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the table correction factor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 An individual credits the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld on a dividend in the income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert

### 3.18 Dividend tax

Art. 5 sets the tax at 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst), in every version read. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Art. 9 lid 1 requires a dividend statement (dividendnota) once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless invited. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return, bodies in the corporate income tax return. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/

### 3.19 Grossing up (brutering)

Art. 6 lid 1 provides that if the company bears the tax, the proceeds are multiplied by 100/85. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares on a buyback of own shares, also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html

### 3.20 Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting)

Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividend tax), so dividends are not in scope for 2021. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 The levy hits payments to a related body (qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (profit tax below 9% or on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 With art. 22 this gives 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/

### 3.21 Dividend stripping rule

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01

### 3.22 Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size classes

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 The 2021 thresholds are micro at most € 350.000 assets and € 700.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 6 million and € 12 million and fewer than 50 employees, and medium € 20 million and € 40 million and fewer than 250 employees. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html These are the values stated as the values before financial year (boekjaar) 2024. Stb. 2024, 52 raises them to € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million and € 15 million, and € 25 million and € 50 million, for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023. Employee counts are unchanged. For 2021 the old values apply. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html and https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/

### 3.23 Annual accounts deposit

Art. 394 requires deposit within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01 Ondernemersplein states that there is no extension and that late filing is a criminal offence. From financial year 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/

### 3.24 Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

Without the tax-neutral conversion facility, a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays IB on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen The discontinuation deduction of art. 3.79 is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/ Hidden reserves realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129). https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html

### 3.25 Retention periods (bewaartermijnen)

Art. 52 AWR makes entities required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement. The wording is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 requires records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) to be kept for nine years following the year in which use began. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 require OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01

### 3.26 Corporate income tax return deadlines

Book year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. Broken book year: file within 5 months after its end. Short book year ending 31 December: before 1 June. Short book year ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen Online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting These are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.27 Tax-neutral conversion

Art. 3.65 provides that on request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination.
