## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example. Loss from 2021 converted into a belastingkorting.** FISIN 2022 and FISIN 2023 describe shares sold in 2021 with an unoffset loss of € 20.000. The taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang afterwards and requests conversion in 2023. Step 1: the loss is € 20.000. Step 2: the korting is 20.000 x 26,9% = € 5.380. Step 3: the remainder is usable until 2030. This fits art. 4.53 lid 4, the loss year 2021 plus nine years is 2030. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Dividend of € 60.000 from the own BV.** The BV pays a gross dividend of € 60.000 in 2021 and there are no deductible costs. Step 1: the dividend is a reguliere voordeel of € 60.000. Step 2: the belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang is € 60.000. Step 3: box 2 tax is 60.000 x 26,9% = € 16.140. Any dividendbelasting withheld is not deducted from the dividend but credited as voorheffing. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Sale of shares.** The shares are sold for € 200.000. The verkrijgingsprijs is € 120.000, notary costs included. Step 1: the vervreemdingsvoordeel is 200.000 minus 120.000 = € 80.000. Step 2: box 2 tax is 80.000 x 26,9% = € 21.520. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Onzakelijke prijs.** Shares are sold to a family member for € 20.000, while a third party would pay € 35.000. At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer applies to both sides. Step 1: the overdrachtsprijs is € 35.000. Step 2: if the verkrijgingsprijs is € 20.000, the voordeel is 35.000 minus 20.000 = € 15.000. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. Forfaitair voordeel.** At the start of 2021 the shares in a vbi are worth € 100.000 and there are no other reguliere voordelen from those shares. Step 1: the forfaitair voordeel is 100.000 x 5,69% = € 5.690. Step 2: box 2 tax on this part is 5.690 x 26,9% = € 1.530,61. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example. Loss offset against the previous year.** A loss of € 20.000 arises in 2021. Box 2 income in 2020 was € 12.000. Step 1: offset against 2020: 20.000 minus 12.000 = € 8.000 remaining. Step 2: the € 8.000 is carried to 2022 to 2027, the six following years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example. Gebruikelijk loon.** The most comparable loon is € 80.000. The highest loon of the employees is € 50.000. The fixed amount is € 47.000. Step 1: 75% of € 80.000 = € 60.000. Step 2: the highest of € 60.000, € 50.000 and € 47.000 is € 60.000. The loon is therefore at least € 60.000 and it is declared in box 1, not in box 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

**Calculated example. ODV with a negative market rate.** The ODV balance at the start of 2021 is € 100.000. The marktrente for 2021 is -0,382%. Step 1: the yearly increase is 100.000 x -0,382% = -€ 382. Step 2: the balance at the end of 2021 is 100.000 minus 382 = € 99.618. A negative market rate must also be applied. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.

**Calculated example. Excessief lenen does not apply in 2021.** A loan from the own BV of € 600.000 is outstanding in 2021. The excessief-lenen rules are not in force in 2021, so these rules add no box 2 income for 2021. The measure enters into force on 1 January 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.

**Calculated example. Inherited aanmerkelijk belang.** Shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer. Within 24 months after the death the heir receives a reguliere voordeel of € 10.000. On request the € 10.000 is not counted as box 2 income and is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs, up to the amount that was taxed with the deceased. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
