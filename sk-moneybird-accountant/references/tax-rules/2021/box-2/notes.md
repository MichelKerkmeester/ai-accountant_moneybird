## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 2 rate: 26,25% in 2020 to 26,9% in 2021, a rise of 0,65 point. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss: 26,25% in the 2020 text of art. 4.53 lid 2 to 26,9% in the 2021 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) for an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) (vbi) or foreign investment company: 5,28% in 2020 to 5,69% in 2021. The 1 January 2020 text printed 5,33%, replaced retroactively to 1 January 2020 by Stcrt. 2020, 20572, so the standing 2020 figure is 5,28%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount: € 46.000 in 2020 to € 47.000 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01.
- ODV market rate (marktrente): -0,107% for 2020 to -0,382% for 2021. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.
- Art. 4.6: no change in 2021, the text still has sub a to d. Sub e appears in the 1 January 2022 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01.
- PEB: commutation (afkoop) and ODV conversion remain closed in 2021, as in 2020, because Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115 ended them from 1 January 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html.
- Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen): still not in force (in werking) in 2021, it enters into force on 1 January 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.
- Loss offset: unchanged, the previous year and six following years, with nine following years for losses from 2018 or earlier. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Start-up customary salary: unchanged in 2021. A first use in 2021 could run to 2023 and the rule is absent from the 2023 text. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record a received dividend as a regular benefit (regulier voordeel) for box 2. Deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) reduce that income. Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld is not a cost, it is credited as a prepayment (voorheffing). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Record the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) and the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of sold shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price, selling costs reduce the transfer price, and interest on a loan taken to buy the shares is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Keep the decision (beschikking) that sets the loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang). It is issued together with the assessment, shown separately on it and open to objection. The loss is offset in the order the losses arose, against the previous year and the six following years. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.
- Request conversion of an unoffset loss only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest. In 2021 the credit is 26,9% and it reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- The customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) is a wage for the payroll records (loonadministratie) and goes to box 1, not to box 2. The withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) must be able to make plausible the amounts used in the test. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Divide the box 2 income of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) in the return, any split, as long as the total is 100%. Declare the box 2 income of minor children in the parent's return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- In 2021 the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) rules put nothing in the box 2 return, because they are not in force (in werking). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html.
- For a remaining PEB the company must index the accrued rights each year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision each year actuarially. The ODV is increased each year with the market rate (marktrente), -0,382% for 2021. PEB payouts are income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/ and https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The FISIN 2021 substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) chapter URL returned 404, so the 2021 rate rests on the statute and on FISIN 2021 tax calculation (belastingberekening). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening.
- The rollover (doorschuiving) list for gift, inheritance and emigration is UNVERIFIED. It was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The 10-year APV term is Verified. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.
- The statement that a decision (beschikking) for partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect as a substantial interest for a non-resident was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages and is UNVERIFIED. Art. 7.5 itself is Verified. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- No primary page states a summed box 2 amount for two partners. The division rule and the 100% limit are Verified. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- The statement that the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) reduces the residual profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement. The rule that the wage is box 1 income is Verified. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.
- Proposed and not adopted, and outside 2021: the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from the art. 4.12a facility from 1 January 2027. Source: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf.
