## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 rate (tarief) (art. 2.12)

Taxable income from a substantial interest (belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) is taxed at one flat rate of 26,9% in 2021. There is no bracket split in 2021. Art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each fiscal partner pays the rate on his own share of the income from the substantial interest. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. FISIN 2021 belastingberekening also states 26,90%: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening. The two-bracket table with a higher rate above the boundary exists only from 2024 and does not apply here: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01.

### Income from a substantial interest (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

The income from a substantial interest is the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs, plus the disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang. Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds further groups. Sub a is the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company. Sub b to d are certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction. Sub e is bonus shares from an investment institution. Sub f, the excessive borrowing fiction, exists from 2023 and not in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html. Four further points. Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 benefit but income from making assets available. Salary from the company is box 1 wage income. Deductible is interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price. Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted but credited as a prepayment (voorheffing). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel), acquisition price and non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs)

The disposal gain is the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) minus the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs). The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. Special cases are inheritance, a gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching the 5% threshold during the year and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs). At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) (art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists the cases that count as a disposal even when no disposal takes place. The list is purchase (inkoop) of shares, commutation (afkoop) and purchase of profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen), liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of shares that carry a deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) under art. 4.40. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years. Losses are offset in the order in which they arose. The loss is set by decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment and is open to objection. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.

### Losses from 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) states the longer carry-forward period: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar". Such a loss is offset over nine following years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1.

### Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53 lid 2)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and his partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a tax credit. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. Lid 2 sets the percentage, 26,9% in the 2021 text. Lid 3: the inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit by decision (beschikking), open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces the box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting. Knowledge group position (kennisgroepstandpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block the conversion, and an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment: https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/.

### Substantial interest threshold (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly meets one of the grounds. Sub a: at least 5% of the issued capital. Sub b: rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital. Sub c: profit-sharing certificates for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds. Sub d: at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a. The 2021 text has sub a to d only. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, covering a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01. Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, covering a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html. In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang. The drag-along rule (meesleepregeling, art. 4.9) and the carry-along rule (meetrekregeling, art. 4.10) widen the interest. Once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company also belong to it. Shares in a company in which the taxpayer has no substantial interest but in which his partner or a blood relative or a relative by marriage in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or the partner does have one also belong to a substantial interest. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners. Art. 4.7 to 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang. Art. 4.11 adds a deemed substantial interest for shares or profit-sharing certificates that fall outside the other articles when art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. The text of art. 4.11 is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a are a deemed disposal under art. 4.16 and not an art. 4.11 case. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. For a non-resident art. 7.5 limits the Dutch base to a substantial interest in a company established in the Netherlands that is not part of an enterprise. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Minor children

The taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest of his minor children, until the child turns 18. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Division of box 2 income between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Common income components (gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen) of the taxpayer and his partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return. FISIN 2026 states that with a fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest may be divided and that "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner applies his own rate to his share. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang. The 5% test is satisfied "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner". Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount, see section 7.

### Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount. The same amount is written off on the acquisition price of the shares. This is what art. 4.12a carries. The facility does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from this facility from 1 January 2027, see section 7: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf.

### Flat-rate benefit for an exempt investment institution (vbi) or foreign investment company (art. 4.14 lid 1)

The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year. It is reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, it is never below nil, and it is time-apportioned. For 2021 the percentage is 5,69%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

### Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) from the own company

Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 was issued on 27 December 2022 and enters into force on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html. In 2021 the fiction is therefore not in force, so a loan from the own BV does not create box 2 income under these rules in 2021. From 2023 the fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, the amount rule in art. 4.14a, the related-persons (verbonden personen) rule in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01. In the 2023 text the maximum is € 700.000, from the 2024 text it is € 500.000. Debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. The debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the threshold (drempel) never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (art. 12a lid 1 sub c)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage is at least the highest of three amounts. Sub a is the wage from the most comparable employment. Sub b is the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies. Sub c is a fixed amount, € 47.000 for 2021. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. If the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds 75% of the comparable wage, the comparable wage applies, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) (lid 9 in 2020, lid 7 from 2023). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit. The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Customary salary comparable-wage test (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and which is known to the withholding agent and the inspector. The test is 75% of the wage from that employment in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text. For 2021 the test is 75%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### Customary salary de-minimis (art. 12a lid 6)

The customary-salary rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year. This is lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts (lid 4 from 2023). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Start-up customary salary (art. 12a lid 3 and 4)

For a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the increased starter percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01. The Check tag Corrected rests on the old description "the minimum wage". The statute says 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage.

### Start-up rule maximum duration

The start-up rule could be used at most 3 years. A first use in 2021 could run to 2023. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen", and the rule is absent from the 2023 text. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### PEB commutation (afkoop) and ODV conversion

Act on the phasing out of pension in own management and other fiscal pension measures (Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen), Stb. 2017, 115 (given 8 March 2017, issued 27 March 2017), governs the end of pension in own management (PEB, pension in the own company). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules as at 31 December 2016 for a remaining PEB. Art. 38n lid 2 allowed waiving (prijsgeven) of the part of the claim above the fiscal balance sheet value (fiscale balanswaarde), on the condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly commuted (afgekocht) or converted on a tax-neutral basis (geruisloos) into an old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV). Lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, also a former partner who kept part of the claim. Art. 38o set the taxable wage on commutation at the surrender value (afkoopwaarde) minus 34,5% of the fiscal balance sheet value, then 25% from 1 January 2018 (Artikel IV) and 19,5% from 1 January 2019 (Artikel V). Art. 38q blocked these routes for 2016 accrual above 125% of the 2015 accrual. Artikel VI ends all of this. From 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]". The Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen) states commutation or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019". So in 2021 there is no commutation and no ODV conversion. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2021-01-01 and https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/. A remaining PEB keeps the old rules (art. 38n). If the scheme provided for indexation the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/. Art. 10a.18 lid 3 of the Wet IB 2001 keeps the acquisition price of the shares unchanged for the 2017, 2018 and 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. PEB payouts are income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) (box 1). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html.

### ODV market rate (marktrente) for the 2021 increase

The old-age obligation is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation. Art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011 uses the average of the monthly U yields (U-rendementen) of the preceding year. For the 2021 increase the market rate is -0,382%. Source: https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/. A negative market rate must also be applied. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. In the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. The ODV can be used on a tax-neutral basis for an annuity (lijfrente), an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or an annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht). It can also be paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01. Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5). This was added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01.

### APV loss deferral term

The APV rules defer the loss until a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang. The rollover list for gift, inheritance and emigration was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check and is UNVERIFIED, see section 7. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang.
