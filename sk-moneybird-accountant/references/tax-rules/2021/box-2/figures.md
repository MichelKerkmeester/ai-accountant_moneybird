## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers box 2 and the substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) rules for tax year 2021. The year has one flat rate of 26,9%, with no brackets. The 5% test of art. 4.6 still has four grounds only, sub a to d, and the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction for loans from the own BV is not in force, it starts on 1 January 2023. The rules for 2021 are enacted law.
