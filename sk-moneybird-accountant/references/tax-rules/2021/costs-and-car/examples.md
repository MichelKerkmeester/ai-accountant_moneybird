## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example 1, mixed costs (gemengde kosten), threshold (drempel) against the 80% election.** Suppose a sole trader has € 6.000,00 of mixed costs in 2021, made up of business lunches (zakenlunches) and receptions (recepties). Step 1, threshold route: the first € 4.700,00 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), so the deduction is € 6.000,00 - € 4.700,00 = € 1.300,00. Step 2, 80% election: the taxpayer elects in the return to deduct 80% of the costs, 80% × € 6.000,00 = € 4.800,00. Step 3, compare: the election gives the higher deduction here, € 4.800,00 against € 1.300,00. With costs of € 3.000,00 the threshold route gives € 0,00 and the election gives 80% × € 3.000,00 = € 2.400,00, so the election is again better. The election is made in the return of the year (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 2, conference (congres) travel cap and the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) threshold.** Travel and stay of € 2.200,00 for a conference in 2021. Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f makes the part above € 1.500,00 not deductible, so € 2.200,00 - € 1.500,00 = € 700,00 is not deductible. The remaining € 1.500,00 is travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf) that counts in the mixed costs items under art. 3.15 lid 2. With no other mixed costs, the threshold route gives € 0,00 because € 1.500,00 is below the € 4.700,00 threshold. The 80% election gives 80% × € 1.500,00 = € 1.200,00, so the election is better here. If the nature of the work makes attendance necessary, the art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f cap does not apply, and the full € 2.200,00 goes into the art. 3.15 items. The threshold route then still gives € 0,00, while the 80% election gives 80% × € 2.200,00 = € 1.760,00 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 3, mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding).** 9.000 business kilometres with a private car (privéauto) in 2021: 9.000 × € 0,19 = € 1.710,00 deductible. Fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll (tol) and parking (parkeren) cannot be added on top. If the same trips were made by train, the actual ticket cost would be deductible as public transport (openbaar vervoer) (art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 4, relocation costs (verhuiskosten).** Moving the household contents (inboedel) costs € 900,00, so the deduction is € 900,00 + € 7.750,00 = € 8.650,00 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Official example, private-use addition (bijtelling) 1.** The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page gives a car with a catalogue price (catalogusprijs) of € 30.000,00. The addition is 22% × € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00. Against € 10.000,00 of costs, € 10.000,00 - € 6.600,00 = € 3.400,00 remains as business costs (bedrijfskosten) (Official example, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto). The page carries no year label and uses the 22% rate.

**Official example, private-use addition 2.** The same page gives a second example of the cap at the actual costs (werkelijke kosten). The calculated addition is € 11.000,00 and the car costs (autokosten) are € 9.000,00. Under art. 3.20 lid 4 the withdrawal (onttrekking) is capped at the costs borne, so the withdrawal is € 9.000,00 (Official example, same page, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto).

**Calculated example 5, standard private-use addition with the lid 4 cap.** Car in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), catalogue price (catalogusprijs) € 25.000,00, private use (privégebruik) above 500 km. 22% × € 25.000,00 = € 5.500,00 withdrawal. If the total car costs including depreciation are only € 4.000,00, the withdrawal is capped at € 4.000,00 (art. 3.20 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 6, pre-2017 car (auto).** A car first admitted in 2015 and still driven in 2021. Value (waarde) € 20.000,00, private use above 500 km. The minimum of art. 10a.4 is 25%, so the withdrawal is 25% × € 20.000,00 = € 5.000,00. The standard 22% would have given 22% × € 20.000,00 = € 4.400,00, so the minimum bites (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 7, youngtimer.** A car first used more than 15 years ago, market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) € 8.000,00. 35% × € 8.000,00 = € 2.800,00 withdrawal (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 8, zero-emission first admission 2021.** Zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto) first admitted in 2021, value € 40.000,00. 10% × € 40.000,00 = € 4.000,00, which is exactly the cap. 22% × € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00 minus € 4.000,00 = € 4.800,00, equal to 12% × € 40.000,00 = € 4.800,00.
Same car with a value of € 60.000,00: 12% × € 40.000,00 = € 4.800,00 plus 22% × € 20.000,00 = € 4.400,00, total € 9.200,00. Control by the other route: 22% × € 60.000,00 = € 13.200,00 minus the capped reduction of € 4.000,00 = € 9.200,00.
A car on hydrogen (waterstof) is uncapped, so a € 60.000,00 car on hydrogen first admitted in 2021 gives 22% × € 60.000,00 = € 13.200,00 minus 10% × € 60.000,00 = € 6.000,00, so € 7.200,00. A car with integrated solar panels is uncapped only when the panels meet the 2021 conditions, at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a lead-free battery (loodvrije accu). If those conditions are met, the same € 60.000,00 car gives the same € 7.200,00. If they are not met, the capped reduction applies and the outcome is € 13.200,00 - € 4.000,00 = € 9.200,00 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 9, private-use addition bicycle (fiets).** E-bike of the business with a recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) of € 2.500,00, also used privately: 7% × € 2.500,00 = € 175,00 withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne. If the bicycle is stalled at the home address only incidentally, the withdrawal is nil (art. 3.20a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 10, training expenses (scholingsuitgaven).** Training expenses of € 3.200,00 in 2021, no right to student finance (studiefinanciering). Threshold € 250,00, so € 3.200,00 - € 250,00 = € 2.950,00 deduction as personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). The € 15.000,00 maximum outside the standard study period (buiten standaardstudieperiode) is not reached (art. 6.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 11, the 0% items.** A room in a home that is not business capital, used as a workspace, not independent (zelfstandig) in the sense of art. 3.16 lid 1, with € 2.400,00 of costs in 2021: the deduction is € 0,00. A private telephone subscription of € 480,00: € 0,00 deductible, while business calls made through the same line are deductible. A laptop that is left in private assets (privévermogen) and used 30% for business: 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 3. A Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fine (verkeersboete) of € 340,00: € 0,00 deductible under art. 3.14, while costs made before the penalty (boete) was imposed stay deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

**Calculated example 12, laptop in the business capital.** A laptop used 80% for business is within the freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) range and can be brought into the business capital within reasonable bounds, and one used 90% or more is mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). On the balance sheet it is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450,00 is expensed at once (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
