## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the deduction of business costs (bedrijfskosten) and the car rules for a sole trader, plus the BV variant of the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) rule, in tax year 2021. The law for 2021 is enacted, and the checked statute text is the version valid on 1 January 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The year is almost a copy of 2020, and the one real change is the zero-emission private-use addition (bijtelling) for cars first admitted in 2021, where the reduction drops to 10% with a cap of € 4.000, so 12% up to € 40.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Two other facts shape the year: the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) of € 4.700 with the alternative of deducting 80%, and 2021 as the last year of the training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) as personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), with the abolition taking effect on 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
