## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zero-emission private-use addition (bijtelling), the one real change. A car first admitted in 2020 got a reduction of 14% with a cap of € 6300, so 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. A car first admitted in 2021 gets a reduction of 10% with a cap of € 4.000, so 12% up to € 40.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Solar panels. The uncapped treatment for a car with integrated solar panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead starts with the 2021 text. In the 2020 text only hydrogen (waterstof) was uncapped (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Everything else in this topic is the same in 2021 as in 2020: the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) of € 4.700, the 80% election, the € 1.500 travel cap, the € 0,19 kilometre allowance, the relocation costs (verhuiskosten) of the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750, the 22% standard, the 25% minimum for pre-2017 cars, the 35% youngtimer rule, the 7% bicycle (fiets) rule, the € 250 threshold (drempel), the € 15 000 maximum, and the 0% items for home workspace (werkruimte thuis), home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis), private computers and fines (geldboetes) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Training expenses (scholingsuitgaven), no change in 2021. The regime was still in force and 2021 is its last year. The Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree (Besluit) of 12 July 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
- Bicycle, a later change with retroactive effect. A lid 5 was added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, so the nil withdrawal (onttrekking) for a bicycle stalled no more than incidentally at the home address also governs 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Pre-2017 cars kept the reduction rules of their first registration for 60 months in both years (art. 10a.4 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax (IB) return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Under the small businesses scheme (KOR, kleineondernemersregeling) no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening), the low motor vehicle tax (mrb) rate for a van (bestelauto) stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per entrepreneur (ondernemer) covering all sub-numbers (subnummers) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The small businesses scheme threshold history by year belongs to the VAT topic.
- The mixed costs election is made in the return (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- The private-use addition (bijtelling) is a withdrawal (onttrekking), not a business cost. Private use of an asset in the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
- Keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) if the 500 km route is used. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed conclusive (sluitend), but trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).
- The value (waarde) of the car is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) including btw and bpm, and the RDW site carries it for most cars (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto).
- Keep receipts and invoices for costs, and record a move as the household contents (inboedel) costs plus € 7750 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- VAT on a private car used for business: VAT on use and maintenance is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) counts as private use for the VAT, though it is business for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting). In a BV the director and substantial shareholder (DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer), he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT (btw) and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).
- On the balance sheet, a business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once, and € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated with the residual value (restwaarde) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The 2021 material records no open points for the year itself. The points below come from the surrounding checked material.

- Wet Vpb 1969 art. 8 lid 5, the BV variant of the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) rule. The statute was checked for 2020 and 2026 only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The 2021 text of art. 8 lid 5 was not read for this file, so the exact 2021 fixed amount in the Vpb rule is not confirmed here. Reading the 2021 Wet Vpb text would settle it. The 2021 threshold (drempel) for the IB is € 4.700 from the 2021 statute text.
- Performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case, amounts for 2021. The per study year caps of € 1.693 (mbo) and € 2.443 (hbo or wo), and the part-year amounts of € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo), are verified in FISIN for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, not for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten). The 2021 amounts are unknown in this material. The FISIN page for 2021 would settle it. FISIN 2026 narrows the case to an extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) of at most 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten). The 2020 FISIN study costs (studiekosten) page under the slug aftrek_studiekosten did not return content.
- STAP budget, corrected conflict. The research said at most € 1.000 per year, the scheme (regeling) text says a maximum of € 1.000 including btw per training (scholing) (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). The start date is settled as 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). This does not affect 2021.
- Pages without a year label. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages for the € 450 rule, for public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig), for the telephone rule, for the mixed character of costs, and for study costs as a business cost carry no year label in the checked material. Their statutory basis in art. 3.14 and art. 3.16 is the same in every text from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Reading the pages for 2021 specifically would settle their year label.
- Home workspace (werkruimte thuis), owner route, corrected for later years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page allows a deduction for a non-independent (non-zelfstandig) workspace when the home is owned and in the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen). From 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 bars costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). This restriction is not part of the 2021 text, so for 2021 the page and the statute do not conflict.
- Solar-cell threshold. The 2021 to 2023 texts use at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek), and from the 2024 text the test is the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km of at least 7 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). For 2021 the 1 kilowatt peak test governs.
