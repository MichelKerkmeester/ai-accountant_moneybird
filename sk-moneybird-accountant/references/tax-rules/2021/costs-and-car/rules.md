## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Gemengde kosten drempel (art. 3.15 lid 1)

Art. 3.15 lid 1 restricts the deduction of costs connected with voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen (food, drink and stimulants), representatie (representation, including recepties, feestelijke bijeenkomsten en vermaak) and congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke. These posts are not deductible up to a yearly drempel (threshold), and the 2021 text prints € 4.700 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The amount is the same in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Travel and stay relating to these posts count as part of them (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No onttrekking (withdrawal) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4).

Content of the posts: voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien, drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank, and genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken generally fall under representatie, a subcategory of representatiekosten per the wetsgeschiedenis of art. 8a Wet IB 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.3).

The drempel is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a boekjaar longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.1). Food costs that qualify as loonkosten (wage costs) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.2).

Indexation: the drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only. The kilometre amount in lid 6 is therefore not indexed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

For a BV: under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount, and the election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscale eenheid does it apply once, at the parent. The statute was checked in this material for 2020 and 2026, see section 7 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, art. 8 lid 5).

### Keuze in plaats van de drempel (art. 3.15 lid 5)

Instead of the drempel the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The election is made in the return, so the taxpayer uses the route that gives the higher deduction for the year. Worked example 1 in section 4 shows both routes.

### Reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen (art. 3.16 lid 2 f en lid 8)

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep (courses and education for study and profession), and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary (lid 8). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst overview page also lists these costs as restricted (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, page carries no year label).

Interaction: under art. 3.15 lid 2 the related travel and stay count as part of the gemengde kosten posts, so the same amounts also pass through the € 4.700 drempel or the 80% election (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Kilometervergoeding (art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 b)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (auto, motor, fiets) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre, and the 2021 text prints € 0,19 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The amount is all-in: brandstof, verzekering, tol en parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. The same € 0,19 applies in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Other travel: business travel by openbaar vervoer (public transport), taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs, and the costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, page carries no year label).

### Verhuiskosten (art. 3.17 lid 1 a)

Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a covers the costs of moving: the cost of moving the inboedel (household effects) plus € 7750 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Housing outside the place of residence (dubbele huisvesting) is deductible for at most two years. The text is unchanged in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Bijtelling auto, standaard (art. 3.20 lid 1 a)

Art. 3.20 lid 1 a: when a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer (commuting) is not private for this test (lid 3). With a rittenregistratie (trip log) or another way of showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem (certified trip registration system) is assumed to be sluitend (watertight), though trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto (statement of business-only use) is possible (lid 6 to 10).

Vermogensetikettering (asset allocation): a car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen (mandatory business assets) when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately it is verplicht privévermogen, and all other cases are keuzevermogen (choice assets) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

Waarde van de auto: the waarde is the catalogusprijs within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Belastingdienst describes as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer (fair market value) replaces it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). When the age threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month (same page). A laadpaal (charging point) for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Bijtelling auto, pre-2017 auto (art. 10a.4)

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b)

Art. 3.20 lid 1 b: when a car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, 35% of the waarde is the onttrekking. In 2021 the threshold is 15 years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The threshold is 15 years in the 2020 to 2025 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For such an old car the waarde is the waarde in het economische verkeer, and in the 2021 text art. 3.20 lid 5 b also uses the words meer dan vijftien jaar (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Zero-emission, eerste toelating 2021 (art. 3.20 lid 2 en lid 11)

Art. 3.20 lid 2: for a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros. For a car first admitted in 2021 the reduction is 10% and the cap is € 4.000, so the effective rate is 12% up to a value of € 40.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Cars on waterstof (hydrogen) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Art. 3.20 lid 11: the reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force apply. For a car first admitted in 2021 the 60 months end during 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Bijtelling fiets (art. 3.20a)

Art. 3.20a: when a fiets of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (consumer advice price) is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne. A fiets includes an e-bike or a speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power. Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, note on terugwerkende kracht also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### Scholingsuitgaven drempel (art. 6.30)

Up to and including 2021 scholingsuitgaven (study expenses) were a persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deduction): lesgeld, cursusgeld, collegegeld, examengeld, promotiekosten, and required leermiddelen and beschermingsmiddelen, excluding computer equipment (art. 6.27 and 6.28, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). They were deductible above a drempel of € 250 (art. 6.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). There was no deduction when there was a right to studiefinanciering for the study (art. 6.27 and 6.28).

End of the regime: the Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the prestatiebeurs case.

Replacement: the STAP-budget took effect at the same moment, on 1 January 2022. Maximum subsidy € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7) with six application windows per calendar year, and the regeling is marked Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The STAP-budget does not exist in 2021.

Business costs: separate from the persoonsgebonden aftrek, studiekosten are deductible as a business cost when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, pages carry no year label).

Prestatiebeurs case: when the diplomatermijn (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the prestatiebeurs into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the prestatiebeurs. The € 250 drempel applies per person. The per study year caps and monthly amounts are verified in FISIN for 2022 to 2025, not for 2021, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten).

### Scholingsuitgaven maximum buiten de standaardstudieperiode (art. 6.30)

Art. 6.30: outside the standaardstudieperiode (standard study period) the deduction was at most € 15 000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same maximum applied in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The maximum ended together with the drempel on 1 January 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Werkruimte thuis (art. 3.16 lid 1)

Art. 3.16 lid 1: costs of a workspace including its inrichting (furnishing) in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht (lease right) is ondernemingsvermogen.

The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints Werkruimte thuis 0%, met uitzonderingen (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Note: the restriction of art. 3.16 lid 14 for an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen dates from 1 January 2025 and is not part of the 2021 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### Telefoonabonnement thuis (art. 3.16 lid 2 a)

Art. 3.16 lid 2 a: telefoonabonnementen (telephone subscriptions) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Practical rule: a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Art. 3.16 lid 11 treats persons in the household as the taxpayer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Privécomputers en apparatuur (art. 3.16 lid 3)

Art. 3.16 lid 3: costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur, and beeldapparatuur, are not deductible. Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

The income-tax treatment follows vermogensetikettering. A bedrijfsmiddel used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen, 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, and in between is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

The € 450 rule on its own page: a purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred (kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur, onderhoud van de auto). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten, page carries no year label).

Keuze herzien (revising the choice): a keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag for the year of the choice is irrevocable, afterwards only on a bijzondere omstandigheid. Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking. A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/, KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026).

Other fixed exclusions in the same article, unchanged in every year 2020 to 2027: literatuur except vakliteratuur (lid 2 b), kleding except werkkleding (lid 2 c, with werkkleding defined in lid 5), persoonlijke verzorging (lid 2 d), and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5000 (lid 4) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Mixed character costs: costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction are huur and onderhoud of a pand split into business and private parts, telefoonkosten, a car of the business, congressen, studiereizen, symposia, seminars en excursies, zakenlunches en diners, recepties and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all: a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (rijbewijs), health costs (pedicure, zorgverzekering), personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding is deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### Geldboetes (art. 3.14 lid 1 c)

Art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdelen c, d, e and i. Not deductible: costs connected with geldboeten (fines) from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen, bestuurlijke boeten and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten, EU fines (c), costs connected with a misdrijf for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (d) or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable (e), and dwangsommen under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Wahv verkeersboeten (traffic fines) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints Geldboetes 0% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1).

Timing: costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring. Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/, published 11-03-2024).

Misdrijf scope: only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted (or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking) are excluded. Example in the besluit: convicted only for possession of hennepplanten, not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 2).
