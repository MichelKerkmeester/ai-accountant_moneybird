## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Threshold for mixed costs (Gemengde kosten drempel) (art. 3.15 lid 1)

Art. 3.15 lid 1 restricts the deduction of costs connected with food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), representation expenses (representatie), including receptions, festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak), and congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and similar (dergelijke). These items are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) up to a yearly threshold (drempel), and the 2021 text prints € 4.700 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The amount is the same in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Travel and stay relating to these items count as part of them (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4).

Content of the items: food (voedsel) covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including gratuities (fooien), drink covers coffee (koffie), tea (thee), milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank), and stimulants (genotmiddelen) covers cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation expenses includes receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation, a subcategory of representation expenses (representatiekosten) per the legislative history (wetsgeschiedenis) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.3).

The threshold is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, Decree (besluit) of 23 April 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.1). Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not salary in kind (loon in natura) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 3.2).

Indexation: the threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists only the first paragraph (eerste lid) of art. 3.15. The kilometre amount in lid 6 is therefore not indexed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

For a BV: under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount, and the election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent. The statute was checked in this material for 2020 and 2026, see section 7 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, art. 8 lid 5).

### Election instead of the threshold (Keuze in plaats van de drempel) (art. 3.15 lid 5)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The election is made in the return, so the taxpayer uses the route that gives the higher deduction for the year. Worked example 1 in section 4 shows both routes.

### Travel and accommodation (Reis en verblijf) for congresses and courses (art. 3.16 lid 2 f en lid 8)

Travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession (studie en beroep), and for congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and similar, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary (lid 8). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) overview page also lists these costs as restricted (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, page carries no year label).

Interaction: under art. 3.15 lid 2 the related travel and stay count as part of the mixed costs items, so the same amounts also pass through the € 4.700 threshold or the 80% election (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Mileage allowance (Kilometervergoeding) (art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 b)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets (privévermogen) or is rented privately (car (auto), motorcycle (motor), bicycle (fiets)) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre, and the 2021 text prints € 0,19 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The amount is all-in: fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll (tol) and parking (parkeren) cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. The same € 0,19 applies in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

Other travel: business travel by public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig) is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (werkelijke kosten), and the costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, page carries no year label).

### Relocation costs (Verhuiskosten) (art. 3.17 lid 1 a)

Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a covers the costs of moving: the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Double accommodation outside the place of residence (dubbele huisvesting) is deductible for at most two years. The text is unchanged in the 2020 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### Private-use addition (Bijtelling) for a car, standard (standaard) (art. 3.20 lid 1 a)

Art. 3.20 lid 1 a: when a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value (waarde) of the car is a withdrawal per year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The car is deemed available for private use (privégebruik) unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private for this test (lid 3). With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another way of showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Car means passenger car (personenauto) or van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods (lid 5 a), for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled. A statement of exclusively business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (lid 6 to 10).

Asset classification (Vermogensetikettering): a car is mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen) when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres (privékilometers) and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately it is compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen), and all other cases are freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

Value of the car (waarde van de auto): the value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT (btw) and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the fair market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) replaces it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). When the age threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month (same page). A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car (zero-emissieauto) in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Private-use addition car, pre-2017 car (art. 10a.4)

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22% (art. 10a.4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Youngtimer (art. 3.20 lid 1 b)

Art. 3.20 lid 1 b: when a car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, 35% of the value is the withdrawal. In 2021 the threshold is 15 years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The threshold is 15 years in the 2020 to 2025 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For such an old car the value is the market value, and in the 2021 text art. 3.20 lid 5 b also uses the Dutch statutory phrase "meer dan vijftien jaar" (more than fifteen years) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Zero-emission, first admission (eerste toelating) 2021 (art. 3.20 lid 2 en lid 11)

Art. 3.20 lid 2: for a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros. For a car first admitted in 2021 the reduction is 10% and the cap is € 4.000, so the effective rate (effectieve tarief) is 12% up to a value of € 40.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Art. 3.20 lid 11: the reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force (in werking) apply. For a car first admitted in 2021 the 60 months end during 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Private-use addition bicycle (art. 3.20a)

Art. 3.20a: when a bicycle of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne. A bicycle includes an e-bike or a speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power. Availability for commuting counts as private availability (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, note on retroactive effect also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### Training expenses (Scholingsuitgaven) threshold (art. 6.30)

Up to and including 2021 training expenses were personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek): tuition fees (lesgeld), course fees (cursusgeld), tuition fees (collegegeld), examination fees (examengeld), doctorate costs (promotiekosten), and required study materials (leermiddelen) and protective equipment (beschermingsmiddelen), excluding computer equipment (art. 6.27 and 6.28, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). They were deductible above a threshold of € 250 (art. 6.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). There was no deduction when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study (art. 6.27 and 6.28).

End of the regime: the Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree of 12 July (juli) 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case.

Replacement: the STAP budget took effect at the same moment, on 1 January 2022. Maximum subsidy € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) (art. 7) with six application windows per calendar year, and the scheme (regeling) is marked lapsed per 01-01-2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). The STAP budget does not exist in 2021.

Business costs (bedrijfskosten): separate from the personal deductions, study costs (studiekosten) are deductible as a business cost when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten, pages carry no year label).

Performance grant case (prestatiebeurszaak): when the diploma period (diplomatermijn) (usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant. The € 250 threshold applies per person. The per study year caps and monthly amounts are verified in FISIN for 2022 to 2025, not for 2021, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten).

### Training expenses maximum outside the standard study period (buiten standaardstudieperiode) (art. 6.30)

Art. 6.30: outside the standard study period the deduction was at most € 15 000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same maximum applied in 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The maximum ended together with the threshold on 1 January 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).

### Home workspace (Werkruimte thuis) (art. 3.16 lid 1)

Art. 3.16 lid 1: costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not business capital are not deductible unless the workspace is, according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting), an independent (zelfstandig) part of the home and an income test is satisfied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit (winst), salary and income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is business capital.

The official factors for independent use are its own entrance or stairway (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the possibility of renting the space to a third party (derde). A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not independent (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning). The overview page prints Home workspace 0%, with exceptions (uitzonderingen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Note: the restriction of art. 3.16 lid 14 for an owned home in the business capital dates from 1 January 2025 and is not part of the 2021 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### Home phone subscription (Telefoonabonnement thuis) (art. 3.16 lid 2 a)

Art. 3.16 lid 2 a: telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Practical rule: a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Home phone subscription 0% and Business telephone calls (zakelijke telefoongesprekken) at home 100% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Art. 3.16 lid 11 treats persons in the household as the taxpayer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Private computers (Privécomputers) and equipment (apparatuur) (art. 3.16 lid 3)

Art. 3.16 lid 3: costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other similar equipment, and video equipment (beeldapparatuur), are not deductible. Equipment and instruments (apparatuur en instrumenten) that do not belong to the business capital are 0% deductible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

The income-tax treatment follows asset classification. A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business is compulsory private assets, 90% or more is mandatory business assets, and in between is freely classifiable assets within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

The € 450 rule on its own page: a purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred (small office supplies (kleine kantoorartikelen), professional literature (vakliteratuur), maintenance of the car (onderhoud van de auto)). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten, page carries no year label).

Revise the choice (Keuze herzien): a freely classifiable assets choice can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, afterwards only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use of an asset in the business capital is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/, KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026).

Other fixed exclusions (overige vaste uitsluitingen) in the same article, unchanged in every year 2020 to 2027: literature (literatuur) except professional literature (lid 2 b), clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) (lid 2 c, with work clothing defined in lid 5), personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) (lid 2 d), and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5000 (lid 4) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

Mixed character costs: costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction are rent (huur) and maintenance of a building split into business and private parts, telephone costs (telefoonkosten), a car of the business, congresses, study trips, symposia, seminars and excursions, business lunches and dinners, receptions and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all: a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business, a driving licence (rijbewijs) for example, health costs such as a pedicure and health insurance (zorgverzekering) and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser (kapper). Work clothing is deductible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### Fines (Geldboetes) (art. 3.14 lid 1 c)

Art. 3.14 lid 1 parts (onderdelen) c, d, e and i. Not deductible: the costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten), EU fines (c), costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (d) or for which a penal order (strafbeschikking) became irrevocable (e), and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fines (verkeersboeten) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints Fines 0% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 1.1).

Timing: costs made before the fine (geldboete) is imposed are not costs that relate to the imposed fine and stay deductible. The costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/, published 11-03-2024).

Criminal offence scope: only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted (or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or penal order) are excluded. Example in the decree: convicted only for possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten), not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html, section 2).
