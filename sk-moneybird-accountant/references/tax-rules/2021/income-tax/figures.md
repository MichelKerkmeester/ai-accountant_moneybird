## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2021 box 1 rates, the variants for people at state pension age (AOW-leeftijd), the tax credits (heffingskortingen), being the general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting), the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) and the income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting), the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and the route from profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable income. The ordinary below-AOW table applies 37,10% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that amount. AOW age changes the first-bracket rate and the tax credit (heffingskorting) tables, and the rate adjustment of 6,5% limits the value of a deduction in the top bracket to 43%. The 2021 rules in this file are enacted. A separate Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) also exists on profit, and its calculation belongs in the Zvw file for 2021.
