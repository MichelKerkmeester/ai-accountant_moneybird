## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

For a taxpayer below AOW age the published 2021 table applies 37,10% up to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening). The statute separates the first band into income tax (inkomstenbelasting) only at 9,45% up to € 35.129, then a fixed € 3.319 plus 37,10% up to € 68.507, then € 15.702 plus 49,50% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) of 27,65% are charged over the contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to a ceiling that equals the end of that first band, € 35.129 in 2021, and above the contribution ceiling the statutory income-tax rate equals the combined rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen).

### Box 1 for a whole year at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer who is at AOW age for the whole year and was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first-bracket rate is 19,20% up to € 35.129, then 37,10% to € 68.507 and 49,50% above that boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening). Once the AOW age is reached no AOW premium is due, which is why the AOW-age first-bracket rate is lower (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen). That page states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age, and the same file notes that the continuation of the Anw and Wlz premiums at AOW age is inference from the AOW-age rates and stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule.

### Box 1 for a whole year at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer born before 1 January 1946 the first-bracket boundary is € 35.941 instead of € 35.129. The rates above it are 37,10% to € 68.507 and 49,50% above. Article 2.10a of the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001, Wet IB 2001) holds this table, and the statute works with the fixed amounts € 3.396 and € 15.477 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10a).

### First-bracket rate in the month AOW age is reached

In the year in which the AOW age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it and rises by about 1,49 points per month from the AOW-age rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). The 2021 monthly rates run from 19,20% in January to 35,60% in December, in the order January 19,20%, February 20,69%, March 22,18%, April 23,67%, May 25,16%, June 26,65%, July 28,15%, August 29,84%, September 31,13%, October 32,62%, November 34,11%, December 35,60%. The first bracket (eerste schijf) ends at € 35.129 and brackets (schijven) 2 and 3 keep the normal rates (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### National insurance contributions ceiling and components

The contribution ceiling is € 35.129, or € 35.941 for a person born before 1 January 1946 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen). The standard components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%, together 27,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen). The AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

When deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) have been deducted, the tax under article 2.10 lid 1 is raised by a percentage of the amount by which the taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus those items exceeds the top bracket boundary, capped at that percentage of the deducted items. For 2021 the percentage is 6,5%, so a deduction that falls in the top bracket is worth at most 43%, being 49,50% minus 6,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).

The items are the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (maintenance obligations (onderhoudsverplichtingen) including spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie), weekend expenses for disabled people (weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten), deductible gifts (aftrekbare giften), specific healthcare costs (specifieke zorgkosten) and the remaining personal deductions from preceding years), the owner-occupied home (eigen woning) costs (mortgage interest (hypotheekrente), periodic ground rent (periodieke erfpacht), right of superficies (opstal) or long leasehold (beklemming), costs of taking out a mortgage (kosten afsluiten hypotheek) and residual debt costs (restschuldkosten)), the tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) (the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), the deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling)) and the exemption for making assets available (vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (aanslag) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The 2021 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) calculation page names the rate adjustment but prints no percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) below AOW age

The general tax credit is granted to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). For a taxpayer below AOW age the maximum is € 2.837. The phase-out base in 2021 is the taxable income from work and home. The credit is reduced by 5,977% of that income above € 21.043 and reaches nil at € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The phase-out points (afbouwpunten) of the general tax credit and the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) are linked to the statutory (wettelijk) minimum wage (minimumloon) ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).

### General tax credit for a whole year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the maximum general tax credit is € 1.469. It is reduced by 3,093% of the phase-out base above € 21.043 and reaches nil at € 68.507. The phase-out base remains the taxable income from work and home (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) below AOW age

The employment tax credit is granted over the earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) the earned income includes the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption and excludes the part earned as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or a profit-sharing (winstdelende) creditor (schuldeiser) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen). The 2021 below-AOW table builds the credit in three bands at 4,581%, then € 463 plus 28,771%, then € 3.837 plus 2,663%, and phases it out in the final band at 6% until it is nil above € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Employment tax credit for a whole year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the employment tax credit uses the separate rates and fixed amounts, being 2,371% up to € 10.108, then € 240 plus 14,890%, then € 1.987 plus 1,378%, and finally € 2.178 minus 3,105% until the credit is nil above € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting)

The IACK is 11,45% of the earned income above € 5.153 up to a maximum of € 2.815, reached at an earned income of € 29.739. For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the rate is 5,926% and the maximum is € 1.458 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The IACK is applied only in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) and not in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting). The taxpayer qualifies with no fiscal partner (fiscaal partner), with a fiscal partner for 6 months or less, or with a lower earned income than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner qualifies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).

### IACK child and co-parent (co-ouder) test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2008 and be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months. For a co-parent the child must spend at least 156 days in 2021 with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Payout of unused tax credits (heffingskortingen) to a lower-earning partner

In 2021, 86⅔% of the unused credit was withheld and 13⅓% could be paid. The payout is limited to the part of the credit that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and premiums, the partners must have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months and the partner must owe enough tax (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The phase-down (afbouw) has run since 2009 and ends in 2023. The employment tax credit and IACK payouts lapsed from 2023 and the general tax credit payout lapsed from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later, while it continues for people born before 1 January 1963, who were never in the phase-down (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen).

### Payout income limits

The payout is subject to the own-income limits of € 8.170 for a person born before 1963 and € 1.024 for a person born later, and to the partner income limit of € 24.650, or € 29.350 at AOW age. No payout applies to someone born after 31 December 1999 who is maintained by parents with at least € 439 per quarter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### IACK payout percentage

The checked 2021 source states no separate payout percentage for the IACK. The general 86⅔% phase-down figure is not confirmed as the IACK-specific percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen)

Each tax credit (heffingskorting) splits into an income-tax part (belastingdeel, the income-tax part, 9,45% in 2021) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen) for AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%, together 37,10%. Someone not insured for one scheme loses that part, and a part-year contribution liability (premieplicht) gives a time-proportional contribution part (premiedeel) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Tax credits in the year AOW age is reached

The checked 2021 page states no rule or amount for the tax credits in the year in which the AOW age is reached. This stays UNVERIFIED for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).

### Self-employed person's deduction

The self-employed person's deduction reduces business profit for a taxpayer who meets the conditions, including the hours criterion (urencriterium). The 2021 amount is € 6.670, or € 3.335 at AOW age. Eligibility detail belongs to the entrepreneur's allowance topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The starter's deduction is an additional entrepreneur's allowance of € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

This deduction for an entrepreneur with a disability decreases over the years of use from € 12.000 to € 8.000, then € 4.000 and then € 0, and it is capped at the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner is a deduction for a partner who works in the business, at 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% of the profit over the applicable hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The checked 2021 material does not state the hour-band thresholds.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction has a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption is 14% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and is part of the route from profit to taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition

An entrepreneur (ondernemer) who meets the hours criterion and is below AOW age may add 9,44% of the profit to the old-age reserve, with a yearly maximum of € 9.395 reduced by the occupational pension contribution (beroepspensioenpremie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). The addition reduces the profit that flows into box 1.

### Profits from business activities and taxable income from work and home

A business is assessed for income tax by weighing whether it is run for the taxpayer's account, whether the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and whether the taxpayer is liable for its debts, and Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide the question. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration also weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (ondernemersrisico) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

Taxable profit from business is the profit (winst) as entrepreneur reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). Taxable income from work and home is the income from work and home reduced by losses to be offset (te verrekenen verliezen), and that income includes the taxable profit from business (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1). The SME profit exemption is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The complete sequence was read in the 2026 version of the statute and the 2021 entrepreneur's allowance page, so the 2021 statutory wording was not read for this sequence.

A co-entitled party or lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen)

Exempt profit components are taken out of the profit first. The listed categories are the forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), the agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the debt waiver profit (kwijtscheldingswinst) exemption for the part above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, compensation (vergoedingen) for business cessation (bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (WAZ-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies and benefits under the final levy (eindheffing), mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions (vrijstellingen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### Box 1 balance (saldo), rate adjustment and tax credits in sequence

The box 1 balance is taxed by article 2.10 lid 1, then lid 2 adds the rate adjustment, and the tax credits reduce the income tax and the national insurance contributions through an income-tax part and national insurance contribution components (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen). The full sequence as one statement is not printed on any page read, so the order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference.
