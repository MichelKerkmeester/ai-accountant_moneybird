## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The below-AOW combined first rate changed from 37,35% to 37,10%. The first statutory boundary changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129. The top rate stayed 49,50% and the € 68.507 boundary stayed the same. The income-tax part changed from 9,70% to 9,45%, while the premium components stayed AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- For a whole year at AOW age the first rate changed from 19,45% to 19,20%. The first boundary changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129 for people born on or after 1 January 1946, and from € 35.375 to € 35.941 for people born before 1 January 1946. The middle rate changed from 37,35% to 37,10% and the top rate stayed 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- The monthly first-bracket transition rates changed from January through December as follows: 19,45% to 19,20%, 20,94% to 20,69%, 22,43% to 22,18%, 23,92% to 23,67%, 25,41% to 25,16%, 26,90% to 26,65%, 28,40% to 28,15%, 30,09% to 29,84%, 31,38% to 31,13%, 32,87% to 32,62%, 34,36% to 34,11% and 35,85% to 35,60% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/belastingberekening and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- The national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling changed from € 34.712 to € 35.129, and for people born before 1 January 1946 from € 35.375 to € 35.941 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/premie_volksverzekeringen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/premie_volksverzekeringen).
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) changed from 3,5% to 6,5%. The maximum value of a deduction in the top bracket fell from 46% to 43% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2).
- The below-AOW general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) changed from € 2.711 to € 2.837. Its phase-out percentage changed from 5,672% to 5,977% and its phase-out point changed from € 20.711 to € 21.043. Its nil point stayed € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The whole-year AOW general tax credit changed from € 1.413 to € 1.469. Its phase-out percentage changed from 2,954% to 3,093% and its phase-out point changed from € 20.711 to € 21.043. Its nil point stayed € 68.507 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The below-AOW employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) schedule changed from up to € 9.921 at 2,812%, then € 279 plus 28,812% to € 21.430, then € 3.595 plus 1,656% to € 34.954 and then € 3.819 minus 6% to € 98.604, to up to € 10.108 at 4,581%, then € 463 plus 28,771% to € 21.835, then € 3.837 plus 2,663% to € 35.652 and then € 4.205 minus 6% to € 105.736. The nil point moved from € 98.604 to € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The whole-year AOW employment tax credit schedule changed from up to € 9.921 at 1,464%, then € 146 plus 15,004% to € 21.430, then € 1.873 plus 0,862% to € 34.954 and then € 1.989 minus 3,124% to € 98.604, to up to € 10.108 at 2,371%, then € 240 plus 14,890% to € 21.835, then € 1.987 plus 1,378% to € 35.652 and then € 2.178 minus 3,105% to € 105.736. The nil point moved from € 98.604 to € 105.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The IACK threshold changed from € 5.072 to € 5.153 and the maximum from € 2.881 at € 30.233 to € 2.815 at € 29.739. The whole-year AOW rate changed from 5,963% to 5,926% and the whole-year AOW maximum from € 1.502 to € 1.458 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The IACK child birth cutoff changed from after 31 December 2007 to after 31 December 2008. The 2021 co-parent (co-ouder) test states at least 156 days with each parent in a repeating pattern (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The payout share changed from 80% withheld and 20% paid to 86⅔% withheld and 13⅓% paid (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The payout own-income limits changed from € 7.855 to € 8.170 for people born before 1963, and from € 1.440 to € 1.024 for people born in 1963 or later. The partner limit changed from € 23.790 to € 24.650, and from € 28.390 to € 29.350 at AOW age. The under-21 birth cutoff changed from after 31 December 1998 to after 31 December 1999 and the quarterly parental amount changed from € 433 to € 439 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) changed from € 7.030 to € 6.670, and its AOW-age amount from € 3.515 to € 3.335. The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) stayed € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age. The SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) stayed 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) maximum changed from € 9.214 in 2020 to € 9.395 in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Reconcile the business profit to the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) and to any losses used in the box 1 calculation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and art. 3.2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Keep the earned income (arbeidsinkomen) base for the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) in view, because it includes the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption and excludes income as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or a profit-sharing (winstdelende) creditor (schuldeiser) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen).

The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) and employment tax credit can be applied in the return and in the payroll, the IACK is claimed in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), and the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).

The checked sources do not state a bookkeeping retention period or a filing deadline for this topic. An income-related Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) is also charged, and its calculation belongs in the Zvw file for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The IACK-specific payout percentage for 2021 is unknown. The checked 2021 page states no separate percentage, and the general 86⅔% phase-down (afbouw) figure is not confirmed for the IACK (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The amount and the calculation rule for the tax credits (heffingskortingen) in the year in which the AOW age is reached are UNVERIFIED for 2021. The checked 2021 page states no rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The statement that the Anw and Wlz premiums continue at AOW age is inference from the AOW-age first-bracket rates and stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule. The premium page states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/belastingberekening).
- The route from profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) and taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) was read in the 2026 version of the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet inkomstenbelasting 2001) and on the 2021 entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) page. The 2021 statute versions of articles 3.1 and 3.2 were not read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The order in which the box 1 tax, the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and the tax credits are applied is an inference from the structure of article 2.10, because no checked page prints the full sequence as one statement (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The 2021 deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) source gives the percentage schedule but the checked material does not state the hour-band thresholds (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The official general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) payout example page carries an arithmetic slip of € 1.484 against € 1.489. The checked material does not provide the inputs that would settle it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen).
- The Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur (winstondernemer) is documented as the taxable profit from business after entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling), but only the 2026 version of Zvw art. 43 was read, so the 2021 wording is UNVERIFIED. That calculation belongs to the Zvw file (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- In the previous-year comparison, the 2020 old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) maximum is given as € 9.214 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans) and as € 9.218 in the baseline citing the 2020 statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.68). Reading article 3.68 of the 2020 statute would settle it.
