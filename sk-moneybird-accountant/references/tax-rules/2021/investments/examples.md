## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All inputs below are invented and round. Calculated examples are built from the figures in section 2, with every step written out.

### Official example: starters willekeurige afschrijving on a truck

`Official example` (the second example on the Belastingdienst page, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). A truck is bought on 1 July 2021 for € 150.000. In 2021 € 60.000 is randomly depreciated and in 2022 € 80.000. Together that is € 60.000 + € 80.000 = € 140.000 of the € 150.000 cost, below the € 328.721 yearly cap. At the end of 2025 the book value is € 10.000 against a normal book value of € 60.000. The truck is rented out from 10 January 2026. That date falls outside 5 years after 1 January 2021, so no herwaardering applies.

### Calculated example: KIA in the 28% band

Input: total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in 2021 is € 50.000, each asset at least € 450, no excluded assets.
Step 1: € 50.000 is more than € 2.400 and less than € 59.170.
Step 2: the table gives 28% of the investment.
Step 3: KIA = 28% × € 50.000 = € 14.000.

At the edges: a total investment of € 2.000 gives KIA nil, because it stays below € 2.400. A total investment of € 400.000 gives KIA nil, because it is above € 328.721.

### Calculated example: KIA in the phase-out band

Input: total investment in 2021 is € 150.000.
Step 1: € 150.000 is more than € 109.574 and less than € 328.721, so the fourth band applies.
Step 2: the part above € 109.574 is € 150.000 minus € 109.574 = € 40.426.
Step 3: 7,56% of € 40.426 is 0,0756 × € 40.426 = € 3.056,2056, rounded to € 3.056,21.
Step 4: KIA = € 16.568 minus € 3.056,21 = € 13.511,79.

### Calculated example: KIA in a samenwerkingsverband

Input: the samenwerkingsverband invests € 60.000 in 2021 and the taxpayer's own investments are € 20.000.
Step 1: the sum is € 60.000 + € 20.000 = € 80.000.
Step 2: € 80.000 is more than € 59.170 and less than € 109.574, so the table amount for the sum is € 16.568.
Step 3: the taxpayer's own share is € 16.568 × (€ 20.000 / € 80.000) = € 16.568 × 0,25 = € 4.142.

### Calculated example: EIA at 45,5%

Input: one new machine on the Energielijst, bought in 2021, cost € 100.000, above the € 2.500 minimum and far below the € 126.000.000 maximum.
Step 1: EIA = 45,5% × € 100.000.
Step 2: 0,455 × € 100.000 = € 45.500.

### Calculated example: MIA in each categorie

Input: three assets on the Milieulijst of € 100.000 each, one per categorie.
Step 1: categorie I gives 36% × € 100.000 = € 36.000.
Step 2: categorie II gives 27% × € 100.000 = € 27.000.
Step 3: categorie III gives 13,5% × € 100.000 = € 13.500.

### Calculated example: KIA and MIA on the same asset

Input: total 2021 investments are € 40.000. One Milieulijst asset in categorie II costs € 30.000, and a machine without EIA, MIA or Vamil costs € 10.000.
Step 1: the KIA runs on the total of € 40.000. That is more than € 2.400 and less than € 59.170, so 28% × € 40.000 = € 11.200.
Step 2: the MIA runs on the € 30.000 asset in categorie II, so 27% × € 30.000 = € 8.100.
Step 3: the two deductions together are € 11.200 + € 8.100 = € 19.300.

### Calculated example: Vamil at 75%

Input: a designated milieu-bedrijfsmiddel bought in 2021 for € 60.000. The taxpayer chooses Vamil and no MIA.
Step 1: 75% of € 60.000 = € 45.000 may be depreciated at will in 2021.
Step 2: the remaining cost is € 60.000 minus € 45.000 = € 15.000, which follows the normal schedule.
Step 3: the ordinary cap for other bedrijfsmiddelen is 20% of the cost per year, so 20% × € 60.000 = € 12.000 per year.
Step 4: writing off € 15.000 at € 12.000 per year takes at least 2 years, for example € 12.000 in the next year and € 3.000 in the year after.

### Calculated example: ordinary depreciation caps

Input A: a machine costing € 24.000. The cap is 20% × € 24.000 = € 4.800 per year, so at least 5 years (5 × € 4.800 = € 24.000).
Input B: goodwill costing € 50.000. The cap is 10% × € 50.000 = € 5.000 per year, so at least 10 years (10 × € 5.000 = € 50.000).
Input C: voorwerpen van geringe waarde that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition.

### Calculated example: bodemwaarde

Input A: an own use building with a WOZ-waarde for 2021 of € 500.000. The bodemwaarde is 50% × € 500.000 = € 250.000. The book value is € 300.000, so the maximum depreciation for the year is € 300.000 minus € 250.000 = € 50.000.
Input B: a gebouw ter belegging with a WOZ-waarde of € 500.000. The bodemwaarde is the full WOZ-waarde of € 500.000. The book value is € 520.000, so the maximum depreciation for the year is € 520.000 minus € 500.000 = € 20.000.

### Calculated example: desinvesteringsbijtelling

Input: a machine bought in 2021 for € 20.000, on which a KIA of 28% was taken, so € 5.600. The machine is sold in a later year within the five-year term, say 2023, for € 12.000.
Step 1: the disposal is within five years after 1 January 2021, so the bijtelling applies (art. 3.47 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).
Step 2: the transfer price of € 12.000 is more than the € 2.400 threshold.
Step 3: the bijtelling is 28% × € 12.000 = € 3.360, added to the profit of the year of disposal.
Step 4: the cap of the investment amount over which aftrek was taken is € 20.000, and € 3.360 stays below it.

### Calculated example: payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Input: a machine is ordered in 2021 for € 100.000. In 2021 € 40.000 is paid and the machine is not in use on 31 December 2021.
Step 1: art. 3.44 lid 1 gives a deduction over the payments made, so the base for 2021 is € 40.000, not € 100.000.
Step 2: the excess is € 100.000 minus € 40.000 = € 60.000 and follows in later years.
Step 3: if the € 40.000 base falls in the 28% KIA band, the KIA for 2021 is 28% × € 40.000 = € 11.200.

### Calculated example: starters willekeurige afschrijving cap

Input: assets with obligations entered in 2021, a year in which the startersaftrek applies, total costs € 350.000.
Step 1: the yearly cap is the top of the 2021 KIA table, € 328.721.
Step 2: willekeurige afschrijving is possible up to € 328.721.
Step 3: the remainder is € 350.000 minus € 328.721 = € 21.279, which follows the normal schedule.

### Calculated example: RVO notification deadline

Input: the order for an Energielijst asset is placed on 15 March 2021.
Step 1: the notification must be made within 3 months after placing the order.
Step 2: 15 March 2021 plus 3 months is 15 June 2021.
