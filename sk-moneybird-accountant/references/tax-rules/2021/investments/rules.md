## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The KIA (small-scale investment allowance (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek)) is a deduction per business, claimed by choice in the tax return, on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2021 the table has five bands (art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). An investment up to € 2.400 gives no KIA. An investment from € 2.400 up to € 59.170 gives 28% of the investment. An investment from € 59.170 up to € 109.574 gives the fixed amount of € 16.568. An investment from € 109.574 up to € 328.721 gives € 16.568 minus 7,56% of the part above € 109.574. An investment above € 328.721 gives no KIA.

In a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by that sum (art. 3.41 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) adds that each partner in a firm or partnership takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)profit, and that another reasonable key is allowed if applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

Two limits work on the base before the table is applied. An asset with an investment amount below € 450 counts for nothing (art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), and an asset that is not in use at the end of the calendar year counts only for the payments made (art. 3.44 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44, see the payment cap subsection below).

### KIA per-asset minimum (€ 450)

A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) with an investment amount below € 450 does not count for the KIA (art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same lid 2 also excludes, for the KIA only, assets that are intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties.

For every investment deduction (investeringsaftrek), art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for certain uses. The list is: use in a business whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting) in onderdeel a, land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering) in b, dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen) in c, passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 in d, vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b in e, securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies) in f, and animals in g (art. 3.45, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The Minister may disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a (lid 5). The same amounts hold in every version from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### EIA percentage (45,5%)

The EIA (energy investment allowance (energie-investeringsaftrek)) is 45,5% of the investment for 2021 (art. 3.42 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The scheme is administered by RVO, and the asset must be on the Energy List (Energielijst) (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that have not been used before. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, as can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer, as production costs (voortbrengingskosten). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). EIA and MIA cannot be combined for one asset, and there is no MIA on costs that already received EIA, while Vamil remains possible on such an asset. EIA can be combined with the KIA on the same asset. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). The € 2.500 per-asset minimum applies, the maximum investment amount of € 126.000.000 applies, and the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months (the details are in the subsections below). The rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages and is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (€ 126.000.000)

For 2021 the EIA and the MIA have a maximum investment amount of € 126.000.000 (art. 3.42 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The allowance percentage is applied within that amount. RVO states a further maximum of € 25 million per business asset and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). That RVO page does not state the year of the € 25 million maximum, so for 2021 the amount is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### MIA percentages (36%, 27% and 13,5%)

For 2021 the MIA (environmental investment allowance (milieu-investeringsaftrek)) percentages are 36% for category I, 27% for category II and 13,5% for category III (art. 3.42a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must be new and must be on the Environmental List. MIA and Vamil can be combined on the same asset, MIA and EIA cannot (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Under art. 3.45 lid 3 the MIA can still be earned on dwellings and passenger cars, which art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d exclude from the other investment allowances (investeringsaftrekken) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Under art. 3.45 lid 4 the € 2.500 minimum applies and assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises are excluded, unless the Minister disapplies that onderdeel a (see below).

The increase of the MIA percentages to 45% for category I, 36% for category II and 27% for category III takes effect on 1 January 2022, so it does not apply to a 2021 investment (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). The Environmental List 2022 regulation that maps codes starting with F or G to category I, A or D to category II and B or E to category III belongs to those 2022 rules too and is in force from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). Which code in the 2021 Environmental List maps to which category (categorie) is not in the checked sources (section 7).

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (€ 2.500)

An asset with an investment amount below € 2.500 earns no EIA and no MIA (art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same lid 4 onderdeel a excludes assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises, unless the Minister disapplies that onderdeel (lid 5). RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The checked FISIN 2020 chapter phrased the EIA and MIA minimum as an amount per business asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute ties the amount to the investment in the asset, while RVO ties the floor to the application.

### Excluded obligations, including assets bought from an own BV (art. 3.46)

No investment deduction is given for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam) and that entity (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 (lid 2), which is the exemption (ontheffing) route (art. 3.46, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46). The FISIN chapter phrases buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" as giving "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). For a sole trader who also holds at least one third of a BV, an asset bought from that BV therefore in principle gives no investment deduction, with the ministerial exemption as the exception route. The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Vamil percentage (75% at will)

The costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", and the rest follows the normal depreciation schedule (art. 3.31 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, have not been used before and materially reduce environmental harm can be designated, and the asset must be on the Environmental List (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). RVO states: "Uw totale belastingvoordeel (MIA en Vamil) kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Ordinary depreciation caps (goodwill 10%, other 20%)

Yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten) or production costs attributable to the year (art. 3.30 lid 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost may be depreciated for goodwill and at most 20% for other business assets, so at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for other business assets (lid 2). Production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) may be written off at once in the year of production (lid 3). Low value items that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or production (lid 4). The same text holds in 2020 to 2027 (art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The caps run next to the investment allowances. The KIA, EIA and MIA lower the profit, and Vamil changes the depreciation schedule by allowing 75% of the cost to be depreciated at will while the remaining part follows the normal schedule.

### Minimum value (WOZ value (woz-waarde), 50% for own use)

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the minimum value, and at most up to the difference (art. 3.30a lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) count as one business asset, while detachable machinery (werktuigen) count separately (lid 2). For 2021 the minimum value is the WOZ value for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another, own use, building (lid 3). The WOZ value is the value set by decision under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation (lid 4). The 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 texts have these two floor values (bodemwaarden), and from 2024 there is one minimum value, the WOZ value of the building. The 2021 and 2022 texts were read directly and both print the 50% rule (art. 3.30a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Vamil assets are not subject to the minimum value limit (see the Vamil subsection above).

### Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) (threshold € 2.400)

If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices of more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage taken is added to the profit (art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The threshold for 2021 is € 2.400, and FISIN 2021 prints the threshold from the other side as "€ 2.400 of minder". The disinvestment addition (bijtelling) applies only for disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken (lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).

Lid 3 treats as a disposal: withdrawing the good from the business (a), changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, for the KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, for the EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). In those cases the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) counts as the transfer price (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a reduction, refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or MIA, and adds cessation (staking) of (part of) the business within the term (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling). The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use and cessation (art. 3.44)

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (lid 1). At cessation of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation (lid 2). The rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA (art. 3.44, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44). The FISIN 2020 chapter phrases the same rule as "tot het bedrag dat in het kalenderjaar is betaald" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The rule holds in the 2020 to 2027 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) (cap € 328.721)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 of the Income Tax Act 2001 for arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.34). URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as entrepreneur. Art. 8 allows arbitrary depreciation for the joint amount of those costs in the calendar year up to the maximum amount in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table over which KIA can be obtained, and not for assets already subject to arbitrary depreciation on another ground. For 2021 that maximum is the top of the KIA table, € 328.721 (URWA art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, read with https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2). Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs, and art. 12 sets ten years for the sea-going vessels (zeeschepen) of art. 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the scheme is for a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), civil-law partnership (maatschap), limited partnership or general partnership, that the starter's deduction conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investment deduction can be used, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). Revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value, with the difference counted as profit, applies within 5 years (10 years for sea-going vessels) if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer). The cap of € 328.721 rests on reading the URWA art. 8 cross-reference to the top of the KIA table, and no primary page prints the cap as a number (section 7).

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil (3 months)

An investment is reported within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order for acquisition costs (aanschafkosten), and within 3 months after the quarter in which production costs were made (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). Environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines, and the Environmental List valid on the signing or order date applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). FISIN 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The notification goes to RVO, and the deduction itself is claimed in the tax return.

### MIA and Vamil maximum per business asset and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per business asset and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The page does not state the year of that maximum, so for 2021 the amount is UNVERIFIED (section 7).

### Temporary arbitrary depreciation (none found for 2021)

No temporary arbitrary depreciation scheme was found for 2021 (status unknown, check UNVERIFIED). The End-of-year scheme (eindejaarsregeling) 2022 explanation says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html). The URWA 2025 text lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. The 2023 scheme itself sits in URWA art. 13 to 15, covers assets with obligations entered or production costs made in calendar year 2023 that are taken into use before 1 January 2026, and allows at most 50% of the costs to be depreciated at will, so it is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).
