## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table: 2020 had nil up to € 2.400, 28% to € 58.238, € 16.307 to € 107.848, € 16.307 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 107.848 up to € 323.544 and nil above € 323.544 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2). For 2021 the 28% band ends at € 59.170, the fixed amount is € 16.568, the phase-out starts above € 109.574 and the table ends at € 328.721 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap: € 323.544 in 2020 to € 328.721 in 2021, both being the top of the KIA table of the year (URWA art. 8 read with the KIA table, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- EIA percentage: 45% in 2020 to 45,5% in 2021, with the maximum moving from € 124.000.000 to € 126.000.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3 and lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- MIA percentages: unchanged at 36% for category I, 27% for category II and 13,5% for category III in both 2020 and 2021. The increase to 45%, 36% and 27% takes effect on 1 January 2022 and does not touch a 2021 investment (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel).
- KIA per-asset minimum: € 450 in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b).
- EIA and MIA per-asset minimum: € 2.500 in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b).
- Vamil: 75% depreciate at will (willekeurig afschrijven) in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1).
- Ordinary depreciation caps: goodwill 10% per year and other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) 20% per year in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2).
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde): the WOZ value (woz-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building in both years. The 2021 text was read directly and prints the 50% rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3).
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold: € 2.400 in both years. For 2020 the statute printed € 2.400 while FISIN 2020 printed "€ 2.300 of minder", settled to the statute. FISIN 2021 prints "€ 2.400 of minder", which matches the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- RVO notification: within 3 months in FISIN 2020 to 2026, unchanged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Temporary arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving): none found in 2020 and none found in 2021. The precedents named for the 2023 scheme are 2009, 2010, 2011 and partly 2013, and the URWA 2025 text lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) the date of the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or the order, the cost, the amount paid in the year and the date the asset is taken into use. The 3-month RVO notification runs from the signing or order date, the Environmental List (Milieulijst) valid on that date applies, and the 25% within twelve months and three-year use tests in art. 3.47 lid 6 use the obligation date and the investment year (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47).
- Keep the RVO notification and the RVO decision with the investment records. The deduction itself is claimed in the tax return, and the KIA is a per business (per-onderneming) deduction claimed by choice on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). FISIN 2021 repeats that the investment is reported to RVO within 3 months (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Mark per asset which scheme is used: KIA alone, KIA with EIA, KIA with MIA, or MIA with Vamil. EIA and MIA cannot be combined on one asset, and MIA is not available on costs that already received EIA (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).
- When an asset is not yet in use at year end, record the payments made, because the 2021 deduction is limited to those payments (art. 3.44, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44).
- Watch disposals, rental and cessation (staking) within five years after the start of the investment year. Transfer prices above € 2.400 in a calendar year lead to a disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) at the percentage of the deduction (aftrek) taken, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page counts rental for the KIA only (art. 3.47 lid 1 and lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Sole trader: the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) is claimed in the income tax return, as a deduction per business on the total investment in business assets in the calendar year (art. 3.41, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Sole trader with a BV: when the taxpayer holds at least one third of a BV, an obligation between the taxpayer and that BV gives in principle no investment deduction, and the exemption (ontheffing) route of art. 3.46 lid 2 is the exception (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Temporary arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for 2021: unknown. No temporary scheme was found for 2021. The End-of-year scheme (eindejaarsregeling) 2022 explanation says the 2023 scheme is "op hoofdlijnen gelijk aan de regeling die gold voor de jaren 2009, 2010, 2011 en (deels) 2013" and names no other year (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html), and the URWA 2025 text lists no 2020 or 2021 scheme (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). An absence cannot be confirmed on one page. A scheme in a 2021 ministerial regulation or an announcement of the Minister would settle it. The 2023 scheme itself sits in URWA art. 13 to 15, applies to assets with obligations entered or production costs (voortbrengingskosten) made in calendar year 2023 that are taken into use before 1 January 2026, and allows at most 50% of the costs to be depreciated at will, so it is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving) cap, UNVERIFIED: the € 328.721 rests on reading the URWA art. 8 cross-reference to the maximum amount in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table over which KIA can be obtained, which is the top of the KIA table. The pages do not print the cap as a number (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0). The cap has also been read as the end of the 28% band of the KIA table, which gives € 58.238 for 2020 and € 59.170 for 2021, and that reading is not what the URWA wording says. A primary text that states the maximum amount for arbitrary depreciation as a number would settle it.
- The rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use EIA or MIA was not found on the fetched pages and is UNVERIFIED (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). A primary text on the EIA or MIA entitlement of a co-entitled party or a financier would settle it.
- RVO maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil: the RVO page states the amount but not the year, so for 2021 it is UNVERIFIED (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). A 2021 version of the RVO page or the Environmental List (Milieulijst) would settle it.
- 2021 Environmental List codes: the code-letter mapping of the Environmental List (F or G to category I, A or D to category II, B or E to category III) is stated for the Environmental List 2022, in force from 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html). Which 2021 Environmental List code maps to category I, II or III is not in the checked sources, while the 2021 percentages are 36%, 27% and 13,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3). The Environmental List 2021 would settle it.
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) 2020 conflict, resolved: the law gave € 2.400 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1) against € 2.300 on the FISIN 2020 page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), settled to € 2.400. FISIN 2021 prints "€ 2.400 of minder", so 2021 has no conflict on this threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1).
- MIA percentages 2022 and 2023 conflict, resolved and outside 2021: the statute and the Environmental List 2022 give 45%, 36% and 27% from 1 January 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel), while the FISIN 2022 and 2023 chapters kept 36%, 27% and 13,5%. The 2021 text still reads 36%, 27% and 13,5%, which is the enacted 2021 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3).
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde): the 2026 text has further lids 5 to 10 on co-owned buildings, ground owned by an unrelated party and related persons and related entity (verbonden lichaam) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a). Whether the 2021 text contains those lids is not stated in the checked sources. Reading the 2021 text would settle it. For 2021 the two floor values (bodemwaarden) of lid 3 were read directly (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3).
