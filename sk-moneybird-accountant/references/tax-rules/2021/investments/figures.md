## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2021 investment allowances, the KIA (small-scale investment allowance (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek)), the EIA (energy investment allowance (energie-investeringsaftrek)), the MIA (environmental investment allowance (milieu-investeringsaftrek)) and the Vamil, plus the per-asset exclusions and minimums, the payment cap, the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) and the depreciation rules for business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) of a sole trader who may also own a BV. What matters most in 2021 is the KIA table with a top of € 328.721, the EIA of 45,5%, the MIA percentages still at 36%, 27% and 13,5%, the 3-month RVO notification for EIA, MIA and Vamil, and arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (starters willekeurige afschrijving), whose yearly cap equals the top of the KIA table. The year is fully enacted law. The values come from the Income Tax Act 2001 as it read for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) and from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) FISIN 2021 chapter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), and the cross-year rules of the checked sources apply in full (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
