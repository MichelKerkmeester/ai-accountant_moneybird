## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Official example 1: FISIN 2021 8.4.3, niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

- 2019 profit € 5.000 against a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek of € 7.280. The taxpayer does not qualify for the startersaftrek, so the aftrek is not more than the profit. € 5.000 is used and the unused amount is € 7.280 - € 5.000 = € 2.280.
- 2020 loss of € 25.000. The whole zelfstandigenaftrek of € 7.030 is unused. Saldo: € 2.280 + € 7.030 = € 9.310.
- 2021 profit € 100.000 minus € 6.670 = € 93.330. The offset is at most the amount by which the profit exceeds the zelfstandigenaftrek, so the full € 9.310 is offset. € 93.330 - € 9.310 = € 84.020. Saldo unused: € 0.
- FISIN 2021 prints "€ 7.30" once for the 2020 amount. That is a typo for € 7.030.

### Official example 2: Belastingdienst, start on 1 July
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

- A webhosting business started on 1 July gives 6 months as an ondernemer.
- The taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours are not pro-rated to the part of the year.

### Official example 3: KVK, start on 1 May
Official example. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium.

- A start on 1 May leaves eight months.
- That is about 117 working days, rounded.
- At 8 hours a day: 117 × 8 = 936,00 hours.
- 936,00 hours is less than 1.225 hours, and the requirement is not pro-rated for the part of the year, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours.

### Calculated example 4: Zelfstandigenaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000. Urencriterium met. No startersaftrek.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.670.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 6.670 = € 53.330.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 14% of € 53.330 = € 7.466,20.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 53.330 - € 7.466,20 = € 45.863,80.

### Calculated example 5: Startersaftrek
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000. Urencriterium met. Starter under art. 3.76 lid 3.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek plus startersaftrek: € 6.670 + € 2.123 = € 8.793.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 8.793 = € 51.207.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 14% of € 51.207 = € 7.168,98.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 51.207 - € 7.168,98 = € 44.038,02.

### Calculated example 6: Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 5.000. Verlaagd urencriterium met, so the regular urencriterium is not met. First use. Right to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering. Not AOW age at the start of the year. Not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years.
- Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid: € 12.000, capped at the profit of € 5.000, so € 5.000.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 5.000 - € 5.000 = € 0.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 14% of € 0 = € 0,00.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 0.

### Calculated example 7: Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 50.000. Urencriterium met. S&O-verklaring and at least 500 S&O hours. Other parts ignored.
- Base S&O-aftrek: € 13.188.
- Profit after base: € 50.000 - € 13.188 = € 36.812.
- MKB: 14% of € 36.812 = € 5.153,68.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 36.812 - € 5.153,68 = € 31.658,32.
- If the S&O starter increase also applies, not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and the aftrek applied at most twice: € 13.188 + € 6.598 = € 19.786.
- Profit after total S&O: € 50.000 - € 19.786 = € 30.214.
- MKB: 14% of € 30.214 = € 4.229,96.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 30.214 - € 4.229,96 = € 25.984,04.

### Calculated example 8: Meewerkaftrek
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 40.000. Urencriterium met. Partner works 1.000 hours unpaid.
- Band: 875 to under 1.225 hours gives 2%.
- Meewerkaftrek: 2% of € 40.000 = € 800,00.
- Profit after this part: € 40.000 - € 800,00 = € 39.200,00. Other parts are ignored here.

### Calculated example 9: Stakingsaftrek
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Staking profit: € 10.000. No earlier stakingsaftrek.
- Stakingsaftrek: at most € 3.630, so € 3.630.
- Profit after stakingsaftrek: € 10.000 - € 3.630 = € 6.370.
- If earlier stakingsaftrek was € 1.000, the maximum is € 3.630 - € 1.000 = € 2.630.
- Stakingsaftrek then: € 2.630. Profit after: € 10.000 - € 2.630 = € 7.370.

### Calculated example 10: Oudedagsreserve addition
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit before addition to or decrease of the oudedagsreserve: € 60.000. Urencriterium met. Below AOW age. No pension premiums charged to profit. Ondernemingsvermogen at year end: € 50.000. Oudedagsreserve at start: € 0.
- If the taxpayer chooses to add: 9,44% of € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
- Yearly maximum: € 9.395, not reached.
- Cap on increase in ondernemingsvermogen: € 50.000 - € 0 = € 50.000, not reached.
- Addition: € 5.664,00.
- If profit before addition is € 150.000: 9,44% of € 150.000 = € 14.160,00. The yearly maximum caps it at € 9.395. Addition: € 9.395.

### Official example 11: Oudedagsreserve decrease
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

- FISIN 2021 repeats the same balance as 2020. Ondernemingsvermogen: € 3.000. Oudedagsreserve: € 4.000.
- Excess: € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000.
- The reserve decreases by € 1.000.

### Calculated example 12: Tariefsaanpassing
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 100.000. Urencriterium met. No startersaftrek. No other box 1 income or deductions.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 6.670. Profit after: € 100.000 - € 6.670 = € 93.330.
- MKB: 14% of € 93.330 = € 13.066,20.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 93.330 - € 13.066,20 = € 80.263,80.
- Deductions: € 6.670 + € 13.066,20 = € 19.736,20.
- Taxable box 1 income plus deductions: € 80.263,80 + € 19.736,20 = € 100.000.
- Excess over € 68.507: € 100.000 - € 68.507 = € 31.493.
- 6,5% of € 31.493 = € 2.047,045, rounded to € 2.047,05.
- Cap: 6,5% of € 19.736,20 = € 1.282,853, rounded down to € 1.282,85.
- Tariefsaanpassing: € 1.282,85, the lower amount.

### Calculated example 13: Zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek at AOW age
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 20.000. AOW age at the start of 2021. Urencriterium met. Starter under art. 3.76 lid 3.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 3.335.
- Startersaftrek at AOW age: € 1.062.
- Total: € 3.335 + € 1.062 = € 4.397.
- Profit after ondernemersaftrek: € 20.000 - € 4.397 = € 15.603.
- MKB: 14% of € 15.603 = € 2.184,42.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 15.603 - € 2.184,42 = € 13.418,58.

### Calculated example 14: Samenwerkingsverband 70% threshold
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Taxpayer works 1.000 hours in a samenwerkingsverband with a connected person.
- 750 of those hours are supporting work.
- 750 / 1.000 = 0,75, that is 75%.
- 75% is 70% or more, so the Belastingdienst reads the work as mainly supporting.
- Whether the hours count for the urencriterium also depends on the rest of the art. 3.6 lid 2 test. This example does not decide the rest of that test.
