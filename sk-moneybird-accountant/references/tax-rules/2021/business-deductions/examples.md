## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Official example 1: FISIN 2021 8.4.3, unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

- 2019 profit € 5.000 against a right to the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 7.280. The taxpayer does not qualify for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), so the deduction (aftrek) is not more than the profit. € 5.000 is used and the unused amount is € 7.280 - € 5.000 = € 2.280.
- 2020 loss of € 25.000. The whole self-employed person's deduction of € 7.030 is unused. Balance (saldo): € 2.280 + € 7.030 = € 9.310.
- 2021 profit € 100.000 minus € 6.670 = € 93.330. The offset is at most the amount by which the profit exceeds the self-employed person's deduction, so the full € 9.310 is offset. € 93.330 - € 9.310 = € 84.020. Balance unused: € 0.
- FISIN 2021 prints "€ 7.30" once for the 2020 amount. That is a typo for € 7.030.

### Official example 2: Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), start on 1 July
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

- A webhosting business started on 1 July gives 6 months as an entrepreneur (ondernemer).
- The taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours are not pro-rated to the part of the year.

### Official example 3: KVK, start on 1 May
Official example. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium.

- A start on 1 May leaves eight months.
- That is about 117 working days, rounded.
- At 8 hours a day: 117 × 8 = 936,00 hours.
- 936,00 hours is less than 1.225 hours, and the requirement is not pro-rated for the part of the year, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours.

### Calculated example 4: Self-employed person's deduction and SME profit exemption
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000. Hours criterion (urencriterium) is met. No starter's deduction.
- Self-employed person's deduction: € 6.670.
- Profit after entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000 - € 6.670 = € 53.330.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 53.330 = € 7.466,20.
- Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 53.330 - € 7.466,20 = € 45.863,80.

### Calculated example 5: Starter's deduction
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 60.000. Hours criterion is met. Starter under art. 3.76 lid 3.
- Self-employed person's deduction plus starter's deduction: € 6.670 + € 2.123 = € 8.793.
- Profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 8.793 = € 51.207.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 51.207 = € 7.168,98.
- Taxable profit from business: € 51.207 - € 7.168,98 = € 44.038,02.

### Calculated example 6: Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 5.000. Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) is met, so the regular hours criterion is not met. First use. Right to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering). Not AOW age at the start of the year. Not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years.
- Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs: € 12.000, capped at the profit of € 5.000, so € 5.000.
- Profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 5.000 - € 5.000 = € 0.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 0 = € 0,00.
- Taxable profit from business: € 0.

### Calculated example 7: Deduction for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk)
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 50.000. Hours criterion is met. R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) and at least 500 S&O hours. Other parts ignored.
- Base S&O deduction: € 13.188.
- Profit after base: € 50.000 - € 13.188 = € 36.812.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 36.812 = € 5.153,68.
- Taxable profit from business: € 36.812 - € 5.153,68 = € 31.658,32.
- If the S&O starter increase also applies, not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and the deduction applied at most twice: € 13.188 + € 6.598 = € 19.786.
- Profit after total S&O: € 50.000 - € 19.786 = € 30.214.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 30.214 = € 4.229,96.
- Taxable profit from business: € 30.214 - € 4.229,96 = € 25.984,04.

### Calculated example 8: Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 40.000. Hours criterion is met. Partner works 1.000 hours unpaid.
- Band: 875 to under 1.225 hours gives 2%.
- Deduction for assisting partner: 2% of € 40.000 = € 800,00.
- Profit after this part: € 40.000 - € 800,00 = € 39.200,00. Other parts are ignored here.

### Calculated example 9: Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Cessation (staking) profit: € 10.000. No earlier discontinuation deduction.
- Discontinuation deduction: at most € 3.630, so € 3.630.
- Profit after discontinuation deduction: € 10.000 - € 3.630 = € 6.370.
- If earlier discontinuation deduction was € 1.000, the maximum is € 3.630 - € 1.000 = € 2.630.
- Discontinuation deduction then: € 2.630. Profit after: € 10.000 - € 2.630 = € 7.370.

### Calculated example 10: Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit before addition to or decrease of the old-age reserve: € 60.000. Hours criterion is met. Below AOW age. No pension premiums charged to profit. Business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end: € 50.000. Old-age reserve at start: € 0.
- If the taxpayer chooses to add: 9,44% of € 60.000 = € 5.664,00.
- Yearly maximum: € 9.395, not reached.
- Cap on increase in business capital: € 50.000 - € 0 = € 50.000, not reached.
- Addition: € 5.664,00.
- If profit before addition is € 150.000: 9,44% of € 150.000 = € 14.160,00. The yearly maximum caps it at € 9.395. Addition: € 9.395.

### Official example 11: Old-age reserve decrease
Official example. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

- FISIN 2021 repeats the same balance as 2020. Business capital: € 3.000. Old-age reserve: € 4.000.
- Excess: € 4.000 - € 3.000 = € 1.000.
- The reserve decreases by € 1.000.

### Calculated example 12: Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 100.000. Hours criterion is met. No starter's deduction. No other box 1 income or deductions.
- Self-employed person's deduction: € 6.670. Profit after: € 100.000 - € 6.670 = € 93.330.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 93.330 = € 13.066,20.
- Taxable profit from business: € 93.330 - € 13.066,20 = € 80.263,80.
- Deductions: € 6.670 + € 13.066,20 = € 19.736,20.
- Taxable box 1 income plus deductions: € 80.263,80 + € 19.736,20 = € 100.000.
- Excess over € 68.507: € 100.000 - € 68.507 = € 31.493.
- 6,5% of € 31.493 = € 2.047,045, rounded to € 2.047,05.
- Cap: 6,5% of € 19.736,20 = € 1.282,853, rounded down to € 1.282,85.
- Rate adjustment: € 1.282,85, the lower amount.

### Calculated example 13: Self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction at AOW age
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Profit: € 20.000. AOW age at the start of 2021. Hours criterion is met. Starter under art. 3.76 lid 3.
- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age: € 3.335.
- Starter's deduction at AOW age: € 1.062.
- Total: € 3.335 + € 1.062 = € 4.397.
- Profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000 - € 4.397 = € 15.603.
- SME profit exemption: 14% of € 15.603 = € 2.184,42.
- Taxable profit from business: € 15.603 - € 2.184,42 = € 13.418,58.

### Calculated example 14: Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) 70% threshold
Calculated example from the section 2 figures.

- Taxpayer works 1.000 hours in a partnership with a connected person.
- 750 of those hours are supporting work.
- 750 / 1.000 = 0,75, that is 75%.
- 75% is 70% or more, so the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads the work as mainly supporting.
- Whether the hours count for the hours criterion also depends on the rest of the art. 3.6 lid 2 test. This example does not decide the rest of that test.
