## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 IB-ondernemer status
An IB-ondernemer (income tax entrepreneur) is judged separately from a btw-ondernemer (VAT entrepreneur). The Belastingdienst looks per activity at whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer. Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek. The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations under art. 3.4, and the zelfstandig beroep counts as an onderneming under art. 3.5. The checked pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an IB-ondernemer. They say the Belastingdienst makes its own assessment after registration. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.4 and 3.5).

### 3.2 Ondernemersaftrek components
Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek. The startersaftrek is not a separate list item. It is a verhoging of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3. Medegerechtigden (profit-entitled persons) get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit. The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.74 and 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.3 Urencriterium
Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer. The taxpayer must also meet one of two further tests. Test (a) is that the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is grotendeels (mainly) spent on the businesses. Test (b) is that the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. The second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years. The urencriterium is needed for the zelfstandigenaftrek, the meewerkaftrek, the S&O-aftrek and the oudedagsreserve addition. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.4 Urencriterium second test, 2021 lookback
For 2021 the five preceding calendar years are 2016 to 2020. The second test is waived if the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of 2016 to 2020. FISIN 2021 prints this window as 2016 to 2020. If the taxpayer was an ondernemer in each of 2016 to 2020, the grotendeels test in art. 3.6 lid 1 onderdeel a still applies. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6).

### 3.5 Urencriterium, no pro-rating for a part year
Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example is a webhosting business started on 1 July. That means 6 months as an ondernemer and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. KVK gives a start on 1 May. That leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium.

### 3.6 Urencriterium, hours that count and proof
All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.7 Urencriterium, samenwerkingsverband mainly supporting threshold
Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4 says hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (cooperation arrangement) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang counts under (b). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more. The Belastingdienst example for (a) is a dentist and dental assistant partnership. The example for (b) is an ondermaatschap. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.8 Verlaagd urencriterium
The verlaagd urencriterium (reduced hours criterion) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid. It applies to a taxpayer who does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78a lid 3).

### 3.9 Pregnancy rule
Art. 3.6 lid 5 says that if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof for employees. The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. The rule helps the taxpayer meet the urencriterium. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6 lid 5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.10 Zelfstandigenaftrek
Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the zelfstandigenaftrek to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium. Lid 2 sets the amount for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year at € 6.670 for 2021. The aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. The amount cut off by this cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (unrealised self-employed person's deduction). The aftrek is deducted from profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Filing: claim it in the box 1 return. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 1, 2 and 5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.11 Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age
Art. 3.76 lid 4 gives an ondernemer who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3. For 2021 the zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age is € 3.335. The startersaftrek is halved too, to € 1.062. The taxpayer must still meet the urencriterium. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).

### 3.12 Zelfstandigenaftrek profit cap and carry-forward
Art. 3.76 lid 5 caps the aftrek at the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek. If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count. Lid 6 says the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection). Lid 7 says the amount is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years. The raise is at most the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, and the oldest amounts are offset first. Lid 8 says the offset is also set by a beschikking open to bezwaar. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 5 to 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek.

### 3.13 Startersaftrek
Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the zelfstandigenaftrek by the startersaftrek of € 2.123 for 2021 if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period. It requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek. The Belastingdienst adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer. The combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit. The excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The startersaftrek does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek.

### 3.14 Startersaftrek at AOW age
At the AOW age at the start of the year, art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the startersaftrek. For 2021 the amount is € 1.062. The taxpayer must meet the urencriterium and the other startersaftrek conditions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).

### 3.15 Startersaftrek prior-years condition
The prior-years condition is that the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied at most twice in that period. For 2021 the five preceding calendar years are 2016 to 2020. The Belastingdienst words the test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting". That wording means the same as the statute. The reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3).

### 3.16 Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid
Art. 3.78a gives the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid to an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, who has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, who does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and who has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years. Each amount is not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then. FISIN prints € 0 for that case. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026.

### 3.17 Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk
The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk is part of the ondernemersaftrek under art. 3.74 and art. 3.77. The base amount for 2021 is € 13.188. FISIN adds that it requires an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium. The amount is indexed per year. The aftrek reduces profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.77) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.18 S&O starter increase
The S&O starter increase for 2021 is € 6.598. It applies to an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period. It is an increase on top of the base amount. If the taxpayer is not a starter under this test, the increase does not apply. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.77).

### 3.19 MKB-winstvrijstelling
Art. 3.79a gives the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption) as a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek. For 2021 the percentage is 14%. Art. 3.2 says the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable profit from business) is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling. No urencriterium is needed. There is no vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde. In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit is capped through the tariefsaanpassing. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.

### 3.20 Meewerkaftrek bands
Art. 3.78 gives the meewerkaftrek (co-working partner's deduction) to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening, profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek.

### 3.21 Meewerkaftrek partner pay limit
Art. 3.16 lid 4 says pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay. Such pay is no income for the partner. If the partner is paid at or above € 5.000, the pay may be deductible, but the meewerkaftrek does not apply because the partner is not working without pay. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 4). The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.22 Stakingsaftrek
Art. 3.79 gives the stakingsaftrek (cessation deduction) on profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses. The aftrek equals that profit, at most € 3.630 for 2021. The maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under doorschuiving on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap under art. 3.59 lid 2, on death under art. 3.62 or to ondernemers under art. 3.63, the aftrek applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That rule is in force for 2021. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.

### 3.23 Stakingsaftrek lifetime limit
The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime. An unused remainder can be used at a later staking. There is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.

### 3.24 Oudedagsreserve addition percentage
Art. 3.67 lets the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year add to the oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve). The word "kan" makes the addition optional. The urencriterium is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit as ondernemer for 2021. The addition is at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the ondernemingsvermogen (business capital) at the end of the year exceeds the oudedagsreserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by AMvB, lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pensioenrichtleeftijd changes, published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.67 and 3.68) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

### 3.25 Oudedagsreserve addition maximum
The oudedagsreserve addition is at most € 9.395 for 2021. It is also capped at the increase in the ondernemingsvermogen at year end over the reserve at the start of the year. A negative calculation gives a nil addition. The addition can be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from a lijfrente formed from an oudedagsreserve, capped at the ondernemingsvermogen at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.68 and 3.69) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

### 3.26 Tariefsaanpassing
Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3 says that if grondslagverminderende posten are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. The posten expressly include the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. For 2021 the percentage is 6,5% and the start of the top bracket is € 68.507. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).

### 3.27 Maximum value of ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling
The maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 43% for 2021. This is 49,50% minus 6,5%. The tariefsaanpassing enforces this cap. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).

### 3.28 Zvw self-assessed rate
The self-assessed Zvw contribution rate is 5,75% for 2021. The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### 3.29 Zvw maximum contribution income
The maximum contribution income for the self-assessed Zvw contribution is € 58.311 for 2021. FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the omzetting of an oudedagsreserve into a lijfrente. The checked Zvw interaction covers 2020 and 2021. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
