## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) status
An entrepreneur for income tax purposes is judged separately from a VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) looks per activity at whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek). The statute defines the entrepreneur (ondernemer) as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations under art. 3.4, and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (onderneming) under art. 3.5. The checked pages do not literally say that KVK registration alone does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. They say the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration makes its own assessment after registration. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/ and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.4 and 3.5).

### 3.2 Entrepreneur's allowance (Ondernemersaftrek) components
Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek). The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is not a separate list item. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3. Co-entitled parties (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction or SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) on their co-entitlement (medegerechtigdheid) profit. The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.74 and 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.3 Hours criterion (Urencriterium)
Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur. The taxpayer must also meet one of two further tests. Test (a) is that the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses. Test (b) is that the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. The second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur (non-ondernemer) year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. The hours criterion is needed for the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for assisting partner, the S&O deduction and the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.4 Hours criterion second test, 2021 lookback
For 2021 the five preceding calendar years are 2016 to 2020. The second test is waived if the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of 2016 to 2020. FISIN 2021 prints this window as 2016 to 2020. If the taxpayer was an entrepreneur in each of 2016 to 2020, the mainly test in art. 3.6 lid 1 onderdeel a still applies. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6).

### 3.5 Hours criterion, no pro-rating for a part year
Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example is a webhosting business started on 1 July. That means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. KVK gives a start on 1 May. That leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium.

### 3.6 Hours criterion, hours that count and proof
All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.7 Hours criterion, partnership (samenwerkingsverband) mainly supporting threshold
Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4 says hours for a business that is part of a partnership with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example for (a) is a dentist and dental assistant partnership. The example for (b) is a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.8 Reduced hours criterion (Verlaagd urencriterium)
The reduced hours criterion is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs. It applies to a taxpayer who does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78a lid 3).

### 3.9 Pregnancy rule
Art. 3.6 lid 5 says that if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. The rule helps the taxpayer meet the hours criterion. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6 lid 5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.

### 3.10 Self-employed person's deduction
Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the self-employed person's deduction to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion. Lid 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below the AOW age at the start of the year at € 6.670 for 2021. The deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. The amount cut off by this cap is unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). The deduction is deducted from profit before the SME profit exemption. Filing: claim it in the box 1 return. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 1, 2 and 5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.11 Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age
Art. 3.76 lid 4 gives an entrepreneur who has reached the AOW age at the start of the year 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3. For 2021 the self-employed person's deduction at AOW age is € 3.335. The starter's deduction is halved too, to € 1.062. The taxpayer must still meet the hours criterion. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).

### 3.12 Self-employed person's deduction profit cap and carry-forward
Art. 3.76 lid 5 caps the deduction at the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count. Lid 6 says the inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Lid 7 says the amount is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years. The raise is at most the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, and the oldest amounts are offset first. Lid 8 says the offset is also set by a decision open to objection. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 5 to 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek.

### 3.13 Starter's deduction
Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the self-employed person's deduction by the starter's deduction of € 2.123 for 2021 if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period. It requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that it is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur. The combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit. The excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The starter's deduction does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 in a tax-neutral return from a BV (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) if that business started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek.

### 3.14 Starter's deduction at AOW age
At the AOW age at the start of the year, art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the starter's deduction. For 2021 the amount is € 1.062. The taxpayer must meet the hours criterion and the other starter's deduction conditions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).

### 3.15 Starter's deduction prior-years condition
The prior-years condition is that the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, and the self-employed person's deduction was applied at most twice in that period. For 2021 the five preceding calendar years are 2016 to 2020. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting". That wording means the same as the statute. The reading "not an entrepreneur in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3).

### 3.16 Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs
Art. 3.78a gives the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs to an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, who has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, who does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and who has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from a disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years. Each amount is not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then. FISIN prints € 0 for that case. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026.

### 3.17 Deduction for research and development work
The deduction for research and development work is part of the entrepreneur's allowance under art. 3.74 and art. 3.77. The base amount for 2021 is € 13.188. FISIN adds that it requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion. The amount is indexed per year. The deduction reduces profit before the SME profit exemption. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.77) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### 3.18 S&O starter increase
The S&O starter increase for 2021 is € 6.598. It applies to an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period. It is an increase on top of the base amount. If the taxpayer is not a starter under this test, the increase does not apply. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.77).

### 3.19 SME profit exemption
Art. 3.79a gives the SME profit exemption as a percentage of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance. For 2021 the percentage is 14%. Art. 3.2 says the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. The order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. No hours criterion is needed. There is no exemption on profit as co-entitled party. In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous. The benefit is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.2 and 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.

### 3.20 Deduction for assisting partner bands
Art. 3.78 gives the deduction for assisting partner to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.78) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek.

### 3.21 Deduction for assisting partner partner pay limit
Art. 3.16 lid 4 says pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay. Such pay is no income for the partner. If the partner is paid at or above € 5.000, the pay may be deductible, but the deduction for assisting partner does not apply because the partner is not working without pay. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 4). The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.22 Discontinuation deduction
Art. 3.79 gives the discontinuation deduction on profit made with or on the cessation of one or more whole businesses. The deduction equals that profit, at most € 3.630 for 2021. The maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) under art. 3.59 lid 2, on death under art. 3.62 or to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) under art. 3.63, the deduction applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a decrease (afneming) of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That rule is in force for 2021. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.

### 3.23 Discontinuation deduction lifetime limit
The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime. An unused remainder can be used at a later cessation. There is no deduction on profit as co-entitled party. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.

### 3.24 Old-age reserve addition percentage
Art. 3.67 lets the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year add to the old-age reserve. The word "kan" makes the addition optional. The hours criterion is not required for the extra addition of art. 3.69 lid 1 onderdeel b. Art. 3.68 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit as entrepreneur for 2021. The addition is at most the yearly maximum, reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and at most the amount by which the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of the year exceeds the old-age reserve at the start of the year. Profit here is profit before addition to and decrease of the reserve, plus those pension premiums, without profit covered by a double-tax relief. The percentage can be changed by order in council (AMvB), lowered by 0,36 point per year that the pension reference age (pensioenrichtleeftijd) changes, published at least a year ahead. The statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all print 9,44%, so no change was made in those years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.67 and 3.68) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

### 3.25 Old-age reserve addition maximum
The old-age reserve addition is at most € 9.395 for 2021. It is also capped at the increase in the business capital at year end over the reserve at the start of the year. A negative calculation gives a nil addition. The addition can be raised by the amount of a forced decrease in the previous year where art. 3.72 lid 3 did not apply, and by taxable periodic benefits from an annuity (lijfrente) formed from an old-age reserve, capped at the business capital at year end minus the reserve at the start of the year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.68 and 3.69) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

### 3.26 Rate adjustment
Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3 says that if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. The deductions expressly include the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption. For 2021 the percentage is 6,5% and the start of the top bracket is € 68.507. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).

### 3.27 Maximum value of entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption
The maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is 43% for 2021. This is 49,50% minus 6,5%. The rate adjustment enforces this cap. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).

### 3.28 Zvw self-assessed rate
The self-assessed Zvw contribution rate is 5,75% for 2021. The taxable profit from business is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.

### 3.29 Zvw maximum contribution income
The maximum contribution income for the self-assessed Zvw contribution is € 58.311 for 2021. FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity. The checked Zvw interaction covers 2020 and 2021. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
