## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) for tax year 2021. The 2021 figures are enacted law. The main changes from 2020 are a lower self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), a higher deduction for research and development work (S&O-aftrek), a higher old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) maximum, a higher rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and a higher Zvw rate. The rules for the hours criterion (urencriterium) and the components of the entrepreneur's allowance are otherwise the same as in 2020.
