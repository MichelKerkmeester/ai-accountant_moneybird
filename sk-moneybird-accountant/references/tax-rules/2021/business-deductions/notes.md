## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 7.030 in 2020 to € 6.670 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age: € 3.515 in 2020 to € 3.335 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Deduction for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 12.980 in 2020 to € 13.188 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- S&O starter increase: € 6.494 in 2020 to € 6.598 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition maximum: € 9.218 in 2020 to € 9.395 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. FISIN 2020 printed € 9.214 twice, the statute amount € 9.218 prevails for 2020.
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 3,5% above € 68.507 in 2020 to 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling): 46% in 2020 to 43% in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Zvw self-assessed rate: 5,45% in 2020 to 5,75% in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Zvw maximum contribution income: € 57.232 in 2020 to € 58.311 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Hours criterion (urencriterium) second test lookback window: 2015 to 2019 in 2020 to 2016 to 2020 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek. FISIN 2020 printed 2014 to 2018, a misprint against the statute.
- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age is 50% of the normal amount in both years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).
- Unchanged in 2021: hours criterion 1.225 hours, reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) 800 hours and not more than 1.225, pregnancy rule 16 weeks, starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123, starter's deduction at AOW age € 1.062, starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, SME profit exemption 14%, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4%, deduction for assisting partner partner pay limit € 5.000, discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) € 3.630, old-age reserve addition percentage 9,44%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Rule changes: the checked 2021 material says art. 3.6, 3.74, 3.78, 3.78a, 3.79, 3.79a and 3.67 to 3.72 read the same as in 2020 apart from amounts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b on the discontinuation deduction for an old-age reserve decrease (afneming) after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) is still in force in 2021, as in 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business so the 1.225-hour hours criterion (urencriterium) can be made plausible. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) names an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- Include non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- If the work is in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons, record the hours and the nature of the work. Mainly supporting work is 70% or more under the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reading and can be excluded under art. 3.6 lid 2. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6).
- If the taxpayer is pregnant, record the leave period. Non-worked hours count as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- For the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), record the partner's hours and any pay. Pay below € 5.000 is not deductible and the partner is treated as unpaid. Such pay is no income for the partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek.
- For the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) and the S&O starter increase, keep records of whether the taxpayer was an entrepreneur (ondernemer) in each of the five preceding calendar years and whether the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) was applied at most twice. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek.
- For the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek), keep the decisions (beschikkingen) open to objection (bezwaar) and the profit figures, because the unused amount is set by decision (beschikking) and offset in the next nine calendar years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek.
- For the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), record the addition, the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end and the reserve at the start of the year. The addition is optional and capped by the yearly maximum and by the increase in business capital. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.67 and 3.68).
- For the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek), record the profit on the cessation (staking) of whole businesses and earlier discontinuation deduction. The € 3.630 maximum is reduced by earlier use and is available only once in a lifetime. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.
- The entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) are applied in the box 1 return. The order is profit, then entrepreneur's allowance, then SME profit exemption. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.2, 3.74 and 3.79a).
- No hours criterion is needed for the SME profit exemption. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) of 6,5% above € 68.507 raises the tax on the deductions and caps their maximum value at 43%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).
- The taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is part of the income for the self-assessed Zvw contribution up to € 58.311, at a rate of 5,75% for 2021. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- If the taxpayer is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), the profit is reported as income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk) and there is no right to the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No open points for 2021. One known misprint in an official example is recorded for completeness.

- Known misprint: FISIN 2021 8.4.3 prints "€ 7.30" once for the 2020 unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) amount. The same example uses € 7.030 for the 2020 right, and € 2.280 + € 7.030 = € 9.310. The intended amount is € 7.030. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek. This misprint does not change any 2021 figure.
- The 2021 lookback window in FISIN 2021 is 2016 to 2020, which matches the five preceding calendar years in art. 3.6. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6). This is not an open conflict.
- The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) prior-years condition is settled in the checked material. The reading "not an entrepreneur in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3). This is not an open point for 2021.
