## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 7.030 in 2020 to € 6.670 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 3.515 in 2020 to € 3.335 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 12.980 in 2020 to € 13.188 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- S&O starter increase: € 6.494 in 2020 to € 6.598 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Oudedagsreserve addition maximum: € 9.218 in 2020 to € 9.395 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01. FISIN 2020 printed € 9.214 twice, the statute amount € 9.218 prevails for 2020.
- Tariefsaanpassing: 3,5% above € 68.507 in 2020 to 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Maximum value of ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling: 46% in 2020 to 43% in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Zvw self-assessed rate: 5,45% in 2020 to 5,75% in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Zvw maximum contribution income: € 57.232 in 2020 to € 58.311 in 2021. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Urencriterium second test lookback window: 2015 to 2019 in 2020 to 2016 to 2020 in 2021. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.6) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek. FISIN 2020 printed 2014 to 2018, a misprint against the statute.
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age is 50% of the normal amount in both years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 4).
- Unchanged in 2021: urencriterium 1.225 hours, verlaagd urencriterium 800 hours and not more than 1.225, pregnancy rule 16 weeks, startersaftrek € 2.123, startersaftrek at AOW age € 1.062, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, MKB-winstvrijstelling 14%, meewerkaftrek bands 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4%, meewerkaftrek partner pay limit € 5.000, stakingsaftrek € 3.630, oudedagsreserve addition percentage 9,44%. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.
- Rule changes: the checked 2021 material says art. 3.6, 3.74, 3.78, 3.78a, 3.79, 3.79a and 3.67 to 3.72 read the same as in 2020 apart from amounts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.
- Art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b on the stakingsaftrek for an oudedagsreserve afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving is still in force in 2021, as in 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business so the 1.225-hour urencriterium can be made plausible. The Belastingdienst names an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- Include non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- If the work is in a samenwerkingsverband with connected persons, record the hours and the nature of the work. Mainly supporting work is 70% or more under the Belastingdienst reading and can be excluded under art. 3.6 lid 2. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6).
- If the taxpayer is pregnant, record the leave period. Non-worked hours count as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium.
- For the meewerkaftrek, record the partner's hours and any pay. Pay below € 5.000 is not deductible and the partner is treated as unpaid. Such pay is no income for the partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek.
- For the startersaftrek and the S&O starter increase, keep records of whether the taxpayer was an ondernemer in each of the five preceding calendar years and whether the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied at most twice. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek.
- For the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek, keep the beschikkingen open to bezwaar and the profit figures, because the unused amount is set by beschikking and offset in the next nine calendar years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek.
- For the oudedagsreserve, record the addition, the ondernemingsvermogen at year end and the reserve at the start of the year. The addition is optional and capped by the yearly maximum and by the increase in ondernemingsvermogen. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.67 and 3.68).
- For the stakingsaftrek, record the profit on the staking of whole businesses and earlier stakingsaftrek. The € 3.630 maximum is reduced by earlier use and is available only once in a lifetime. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek.
- The ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling are applied in the box 1 return. The order is profit, then ondernemersaftrek, then MKB-winstvrijstelling. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.2, 3.74 and 3.79a).
- No urencriterium is needed for the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.79a) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.
- The tariefsaanpassing of 6,5% above € 68.507 raises the tax on the deductions and caps their maximum value at 43%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 2.10).
- The belastbare winst uit onderneming is part of the income for the self-assessed Zvw contribution up to € 58.311, at a rate of 5,75% for 2021. Detail belongs to the Zvw topic. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- If the taxpayer is not an IB-ondernemer, the profit is reported as inkomsten uit overig werk and there is no right to the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No open points for 2021. One known misprint in an official example is recorded for completeness.

- Known misprint: FISIN 2021 8.4.3 prints "€ 7.30" once for the 2020 unused zelfstandigenaftrek amount. The same example uses € 7.030 for the 2020 right, and € 2.280 + € 7.030 = € 9.310. The intended amount is € 7.030. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek. This misprint does not change any 2021 figure.
- The 2021 lookback window in FISIN 2021 is 2016 to 2020, which matches the five preceding calendar years in art. 3.6. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.6). This is not an open conflict.
- The startersaftrek prior-years condition is settled in the checked material. The reading "not an ondernemer in each of the past 5 years" is wrong. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 (art. 3.76 lid 3). This is not an open point for 2021.
