## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

For 2021, Box 3 savings and investments (sparen en beleggen) taxes a fictitious return on the assets and debts held on the reference date (peildatum) of 1 January 2021. The year runs on the old band method with three bands over the savings and investments base (grondslag sparen en beleggen) and a rate of 31%. The Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) of the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) of 24 December 2021 (ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) falls in this year, and the later Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) brings every 2021 IB/PVV assessment into scope of a category recalculation, with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) applying the most favourable of the two methods. All 2021 values in the checked material are enacted. The restoration of rights law itself was enacted on 21 December 2022 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017.
