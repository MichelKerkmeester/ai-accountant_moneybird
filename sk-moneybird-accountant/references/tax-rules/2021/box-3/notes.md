## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 30.846 per person becomes € 50.000, and € 61.692 becomes € 100.000 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [art. 5.5 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 5.5 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.100 becomes € 3.200 per person, and € 6.200 becomes € 6.400 with a whole-year partner ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)).
- Box 3 tarief: 30% becomes 31% ([art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Rendementsklasse percentages: klasse I 0,07% becomes 0,03%, klasse II 5,28% becomes 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Band 1: the statute limit of € 72.797 becomes € 50.000, the page limit "tot € 72.798" becomes "tot € 50.001", and the average return 1,789% becomes 1,898% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [2020 statute](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [2021 statute](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).
- Band 2: € 72.797 to € 1.005.572 becomes € 50.000 to € 950.000, the page band € 72.798 to € 1.005.573 becomes € 50.001 to € 950.001, and the average return 4,185% becomes 4,501% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Band 3: the threshold above € 1.005.572 becomes above € 950.000, and the return 5,28% becomes 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- Rechtsherstel category percentages, applied per year by the later law: bank 0,04% becomes 0,01%, other 5,28% becomes 5,69%, and schulden 2,74% becomes 2,46% ([Belastingdienst, met welke percentages is het fictief rendement berekend](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend), [Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 59.477 becomes € 60.429, and € 118.954 becomes € 120.858 with a whole-year partner ([art. 5.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 5.13 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)).
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% in 2020 and 0,7% in 2021, so no change in this year ([art. 8.19 lid 2 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- Actual-return reporting: for 2020 the form could only be filed with a letter ("Voor 2017 tot en met 2020 zijn alle brieven verstuurd"), while for 2021 it may always be filed without a letter ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)).
- Scope of the later Wet rechtsherstel: for 2020 only assessments not yet irrevocable on 24 December 2021 are in scope, while for 2021 all assessments are in scope ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- New in this year: the Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963, falls in 2021 and set the rechtsherstel process in motion ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html), [Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the box 3 assets and debts at 1 January 2021. The category recalculation of art. 3 of the Wet rechtsherstel uses the banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden at 1 January ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)).
- Debts count only above the schuldendrempel of € 3.200, or € 6.400 for whole-year partners. The Belastingdienst computes the drempel in the return, so the records only need to show the total debts on the peildatum ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Keep the 1 January valuation of green investments. The exemption of € 60.429 (€ 120.858 for whole-year partners) never exceeds that value, and the 0,7% korting is computed on the exempt amount and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [art. 8.19 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- The most favourable of the old method and the category method is applied automatically on the definitive assessment, so the return itself needs no method choice ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)).
- For 2021 the actual return can always be reported through the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst without a letter, and further documents can follow up to six weeks after the form is sent ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen), [Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).
- The box 3 voordeel lands on the IB/PVV return for 2021. The checked pages state no filing deadline for the actual-return form ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Whether the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return is UNVERIFIED. The memorie van toelichting describes a breach of art. 1 EP with art. 14 EVRM and a recalculation in three categories, and hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3. The ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl would settle it.
- Entry date of the Wet rechtsherstel box 3. The statute art. 7 says the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html). The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking" (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The text of the statute settles it.
- Whether the Hoge Raad ruling of 25 June 2026 also covers late objectors is UNVERIFIED. The part about non-objectors for 2017 to 2020 is confirmed by the Rijksoverheid timeline (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3). The "or objected late" part is not on that page. The ruling text on hogeraad.nl or rechtspraak.nl would settle it, and hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11.
- The rounding instruction for step 4 of the category calculation in 2021 is not in the checked sources. The six-step method is Verified, but the checked Belastingdienst pages give the rounding only for 2023 to 2026, namely 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages and 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026). The 2021 rounding would have to be confirmed on a 2021 page.
- No deadline for the 2021 actual-return form is stated on the checked pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement).
