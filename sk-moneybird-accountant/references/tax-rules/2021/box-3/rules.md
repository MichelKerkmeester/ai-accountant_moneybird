## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)

The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) for 2021 is € 50.000 per person and € 100.000 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [art. 5.5, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The allowance is deducted from the return base (rendementsgrondslag) to give the savings and investments base (grondslag sparen en beleggen), so a single person pays box 3 tax only on wealth above € 50.000 on the reference date (peildatum) of 1 January 2021. The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership, with the joint base allocated under art. 2.17 ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)). In the actual-return route the allowance is not applied, because the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) has determined that no account may be taken of the tax-free allowance in that calculation ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### 3.2 Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

Only the part of the total box 3 debts above € 3.200 is deductible. With a whole-year partner the debt threshold (schuldendrempel) is € 6.400 ([FISIN 2021, bezittingen en schulden box 3](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_), [art. 5.3 lid 3, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The statute phrases this as debts of which the total "daarvan meer bedraagt dan" the threshold, meaning the threshold is not deductible itself ([art. 5.3 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the threshold in the return, so the taxpayer does not apply it manually ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). A joint threshold requires both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 5.3 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). In both the band method and the category recalculation the threshold is subtracted from the debts before the return is computed.

### 3.3 Box 3 rate (tarief)

The box 3 rate (tarief) for 2021 is 31% ([art. 2.13, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The rate is applied to the benefit from savings and investments (voordeel uit sparen en beleggen), whichever method produces that benefit. In 2020 the rate was 30%, so 2021 is the first year of the 31% rate ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 2.13 as at 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).

### 3.4 Return class percentages (rendementsklassen)

Art. 5.2 lid 1 splits the fictitious return into two return classes (rendementsklassen), class I (klasse I) for savings at 0,03% and class II (klasse II) for investments at 5,69% ([art. 5.2 lid 1 as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The two percentages are not applied per asset in this method. They are mixed per band, as sections 3.5 to 3.7 show.

### 3.5 Band 1

The first band covers the part of the savings and investments base up to € 50.000 and is treated as 67% return class I and 33% return class II, an average return of 1,898% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes the same limit from below as "tot € 50.001". The statute writes the limits as "meer dan / niet meer dan" and gives € 50.000 for 2021. Both descriptions cover the same band ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen), [art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.6 Band 2

The second band covers the savings and investments base from € 50.000 to € 950.000 and is treated as 21% return class I and 79% return class II, an average return of 4,501% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page writes the same band as € 50.001 to € 950.001. The statute limit for 2021 is € 950.000 ([art. 5.2 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.7 Band 3

The third band covers the savings and investments base above € 950.000 and is treated as 0% return class I and 100% return class II, so the full 5,69% ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). The bands are applied to the savings and investments base, that is the return base after the tax-free allowance. The benefit from the three bands is added and taxed at 31% ([art. 2.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.8 Restoration of rights category percentages

The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) recalculates the 2021 benefit with three category percentages, bank balances (banktegoeden) 0,01%, other assets (overige bezittingen) 5,69% and debts (schulden) 2,46% ([Belastingdienst, met welke percentages is het fictief rendement berekend](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend), [Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 3 of that law multiplies the bank balances by the column I percentage, the other assets by column II and the deductible debts by column III, all at 1 January of the year ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). These percentages differ from the return class percentages of 2021 and are used only in the recalculation.

### 3.9 Restoration of rights scope 2021

Art. 1 lid 2 of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act brings all IB/PVV assessments for 2021 into scope ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 1 lid 3 applies the law only where it leads to a lower benefit, and art. 2 sets the benefit at the outcome of art. 3 with a floor of nil ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 3 lid 4 provides that whole-year partners use the joint figures ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). The recalculation is therefore built on the assets and debts on 1 January 2021, split into the three categories.

### 3.10 Most favourable method

For 2021 and 2022 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes box 3 income under the old method and the current method and applies the more favourable one to the definitive assessment. This also applies if the taxpayer had a restoration of rights over the period 2017 to 2022 ([Belastingdienst, oude berekening box 3-inkomen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/oude-berekening-box-3-inkomen)). There is no election to make, the comparison is made by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration. Section 4 shows both computations for the same taxpayer, once with the category method winning and once with the band method winning.

### 3.11 Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

Green investments (groene beleggingen) are not counted as assets (bezittingen) up to € 60.429 in 2021. The amount is doubled to € 120.858 for whole-year partners ([art. 5.13, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01), [FISIN 2021](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)). The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January ([Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The text of art. 5.13 lid 2 from 2023 orders the exemption first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances, the ordering in the 2021 text is not in the checked material ([art. 5.13, text from 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).

### 3.12 Green investment credit (korting groene beleggingen)

Art. 8.19 gives the green investment credit (korting voor groene beleggingen) of 0,7% of the exempt amount on the reference date ([art. 8.19 lid 2, law text as at 1 January 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)). The 0,7% applies for 2020 to 2024 ([Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)). The credit is computed automatically and is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

### 3.13 Actual-return reporting 2021

For 2021 the taxpayer may always file the online form Opgaaf werkelijk rendement in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst), without a letter. The page states "Voor 2021 tot en met 2024 mag u altijd het formulier Opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen" ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)). Without a letter the form asks "Nee" to the letter question and the amount needed is the "Voordeel sparen en beleggen", three amounts with a whole-year partner ([Belastingdienst, zonder brief formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement invullen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)). A paper form is available via 0800 - 023 01 07, one form per call, and several forms via the Tax Information Line (BelastingTelefoon) 0800 - 0543 ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)). The checked pages state no deadline for the 2021 form ([Belastingdienst, formulier opgaaf werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)). The route for 2021 runs through art. 6a of the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act, added by art. IV of the Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3), which works back to 1 January 2017, with a form provided by the inspector (inspecteur) and up to six weeks for further documents after the form is sent ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). If the taxpayer makes the lower actual return plausible, the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), and the actual return is at least nil ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The actual return is income plus value changes over the calendar year, gains and losses in one year are netted, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year, costs are not deductible except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality and interest paid on a box 3 debt, and the report is optional with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration using the most favourable amount ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst, werkelijk rendement belastingaangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)). The taxpayer never pays more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst, wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### 3.14 Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) 24 December 2021

The Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) is the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963 ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html), [Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) to the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act describes the ruling as a breach of "artikel 1 EP, in samenhang met artikel 14 EVRM", because the flat-rate system (forfaitaire system) from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Flat rates (forfaits) are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). The Government of the Netherlands timeline confirms the ruling and names the right to property and the discrimination ban ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). The explanatory memorandum stresses that the legislator did not choose a levy on the actual return for the restoration of rights, but a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). The claim that the Supreme Court itself ordered a restoration of rights on the basis of the actual return is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.15 Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3), entry into force

The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533, issued on 27 December 2022, Kamerstuk 36 203 ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Art. 7 puts the law in force on the day after the issue date of the Bulletin of Acts and Decrees (Staatsblad), so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017 ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). The Government of the Netherlands timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking", which the statute does not support, see section 7 ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). Art. 5 lets an extra personal deduction follow the partners' chosen division of specific healthcare costs or gifts, with an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) request for another split, and art. 6 recomputes the double-taxation relief ([Stb. 2022, 533](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html)). Mass objectors get a restoration of rights for 2017 to 2020 ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html)). For 2021 the scope is every assessment, see section 3.9.

### 3.16 Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3)

The Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. It entered into force by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, so on 19 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). Art. IV works back to 1 January 2017 and puts art. 6a in the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act with the same form and six-week rule, which is the route for a 2021 assessment ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The explanatory note (nota van toelichting) of Stb. 2025, 196 names HR 6 June 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705, HR 14 June 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857, HR 2 August 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084, and HR 20 December 2024, ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788 ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). In the 6 June 2024 ruling the Supreme Court held that the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act and the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) still conflict with the EVRM when the flat-rate return is higher than the actual return ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)). On 25 June 2026 the Supreme Court held that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". Whether late objectors are covered as well is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED ([Rijksoverheid, tijdlijn rechtsherstel box 3](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).
