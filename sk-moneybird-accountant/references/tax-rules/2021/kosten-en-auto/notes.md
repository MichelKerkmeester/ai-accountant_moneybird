## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zero-emission bijtelling, the one real change. A car first admitted in 2020 got a reduction of 14% with a cap of € 6300, so 8% up to € 45.000 and 22% above. A car first admitted in 2021 gets a reduction of 10% with a cap of € 4.000, so 12% up to € 40.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Solar panels. The uncapped treatment for a car with integrated solar panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead starts with the 2021 text. In the 2020 text only waterstof was uncapped (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Everything else in this topic is the same in 2021 as in 2020: the gemengde kosten drempel of € 4.700, the 80% election, the € 1.500 travel cap, the € 0,19 kilometre allowance, the verhuiskosten of the inboedel plus € 7750, the 22% standard, the 25% minimum for pre-2017 cars, the 35% youngtimer, the 7% fiets, the € 250 drempel, the € 15 000 maximum, and the 0% items for werkruimte thuis, telefoonabonnement thuis, private computers and geldboetes (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Scholingsuitgaven, no change in 2021. The regime was still in force and 2021 is its last year. The Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).
- Fiets, a later change with retroactive effect. A lid 5 was added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, so the nil onttrekking for a fiets stalled no more than incidentally at the home address also governs 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Pre-2017 cars kept the reduction rules of their first registration for 60 months in both years (art. 10a.4 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).
- Under the KOR (kleineondernemersregeling, small business scheme) no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need herziening, the low bestelauto mrb rate stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The KOR threshold history by year belongs to the BTW topic.
- The gemengde kosten election is made in the return (art. 3.15 lid 5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- The bijtelling is an onttrekking, not a business cost. Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).
- Keep a rittenregistratie if the 500 km route is used. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed sluitend, but trips can still be checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).
- The waarde of the car is the catalogusprijs including btw and bpm, and the RDW site carries it for most cars (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto).
- Keep receipts and invoices for costs, and record a move as the inboedel costs plus € 7750 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- BTW on a private car used for business: VAT on use and maintenance is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet. Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, though it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA is not the btw-ondernemer, he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).
- On the balance sheet, a bedrijfsmiddel with an aanschafwaarde under € 450 is expensed at once, and € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated with the restwaarde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The 2021 material records no open points for the year itself. The points below come from the surrounding checked material.

- Wet Vpb 1969 art. 8 lid 5, the BV variant of the gemengde kosten rule. The statute was checked for 2020 and 2026 only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The 2021 text of art. 8 lid 5 was not read for this file, so the exact 2021 fixed amount in the Vpb rule is not confirmed here. Reading the 2021 Wet Vpb text would settle it. The 2021 drempel for the IB is € 4.700 from the 2021 statute text.
- Prestatiebeurs case, amounts for 2021. The per study year caps of € 1.693 (mbo) and € 2.443 (hbo or wo), and the part-year amounts of € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo), are verified in FISIN for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, not for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/aftrek_studiekosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten). The 2021 amounts are unknown in this material. The FISIN page for 2021 would settle it. FISIN 2026 narrows the case to an extended diplomatermijn of at most 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten). The 2020 FISIN studiekosten page under the slug aftrek_studiekosten did not return content.
- STAP-budget, corrected conflict. The research said at most € 1.000 per year, the regeling text says a maximum of € 1.000 including btw per scholing (art. 7, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01). The start date is settled as 1 January 2022 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html). This does not affect 2021.
- Pages without a year label. The Belastingdienst pages for the € 450 rule, for openbaar vervoer, taxi and vliegtuig, for the telephone rule, for the mixed character of costs, and for studiekosten as a business cost carry no year label in the checked material. Their statutory basis in art. 3.14 and art. 3.16 is the same in every text from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Reading the pages for 2021 specifically would settle their year label.
- Werkruimte thuis, owner route, corrected for later years. The Belastingdienst page presents deduction for a non-zelfstandig workspace when the home is owned and in the ondernemingsvermogen. From 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 bars costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, and the MvT names the huurderslasten (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). This restriction is not part of the 2021 text, so for 2021 the page and the statute do not conflict.
- Solar-cell threshold. The 2021 to 2023 texts use at least 1 kilowattpiek, and from the 2024 text the test is the panel power in wattpiek divided by consumption in wattuur per km of at least 7 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). For 2021 the 1 kilowattpiek test governs.
