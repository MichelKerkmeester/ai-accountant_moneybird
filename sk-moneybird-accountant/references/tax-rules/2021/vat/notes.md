## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The 21%, 9% and zero rates continued from 2020 into 2021. The 2020 and 2021 statutory versions show the same rate structure. [2020 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)
- The KOR ceiling remained € 20.000, and the 2021 rule still tested the current calendar year only. The preceding-year test described in later guidance does not apply to the old 2021 Article 25 rule. [2020 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)
- From 1 July 2021, the e-commerce package introduced the € 10.000 EU distance-sales threshold for qualifying goods and digital services together. [Wet implementatie richtlijnen elektronische handel](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-261.html), [Belastingdienst, goods to private customers in the EU](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)
- MOSS ended on 1 July 2021 and the one-stop shop (eenloketsysteem) took its place. Before the change, MOSS applied to B2C digital services. [Belastingdienst, changes to digital services and VAT reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)
- A 2021 approval allowed certain entrepreneurs not yet registered to apply for the KOR without the usual notice if annual turnover was no more than € 1.800. It applied retroactively to 1 January 2020, so it did not introduce a new effective date for 2021. [Besluit van 10 juni 2021, nr. 2021-12740](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html)
- The checked material says the KOR, rate and supplementary return rules of 2020 continued unchanged in 2021, except for the e-commerce changes described above. [2021 Wet op de omzetbelasting](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [UBOB, 2020 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT return (btw-aangifte), including invoices, the rate applied, and any reverse-charge treatment. An invoice record should show the required parties, dates, consecutive invoice number, description, taxable amounts per rate, rate and VAT amount. [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)
- Issue invoices by the 15th day of the month after supply when an invoice is required. A customer must agree to digital invoices, and the supplier must preserve authenticity of origin, content integrity and readability. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)
- Reconcile output VAT, deductible input VAT (voorbelasting) and reverse-charged amounts to the VAT return. For domestic reverse charge, the customer reports in rubriek 2. For reverse charge from abroad, the customer reports in rubriek 4 and deducts the VAT only where the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Belastingdienst, how reverse charging works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)
- For intra-EU goods, retain transport evidence and the customer’s VAT ID (btw-id), and file the ICP correctly and on time to support the zero rate. Report intra-EU goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country, and call-off stock (afroepvoorraad) transfers in the ICP. The ICP total equals rubriek 3b for the same period. [Belastingdienst, ICP reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [Belastingdienst, ICP reporting period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)
- For OSS supplies, keep the records required by the scheme and file a return even when there is nothing to report. Make corrections in a later return and retain records for the stated retention period (bewaartermijn). [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- Put the annual private-use adjustments in the last VAT return of the year. Review private use (privégebruik) of goods and services, the business car, the taxable and exempt turnover (vrijgestelde omzet) ratio, and staff provisions, gifts and business gifts above the BUA threshold. Also review gas, water and electricity, deemed services (fictieve diensten) and adjustment (herziening) where relevant. If the last return has already been filed, correct it with a supplementary return. [Belastingdienst, private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Belastingdienst, last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)
- If using the KOR, do not charge VAT on invoices and do not deduct input VAT. Keep turnover records that allow the business to monitor the calendar-year ceiling. Legal persons, including a BV, could use the 2021 KOR, but confirm which entity made each supply and incurred each cost. [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)
- File and pay within the applicable period. The exact official 2021 date table and exact 2021 ICP deadline were not found in the checked sources. [AWR, filing and payment terms](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, VAT due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **2021 VAT due-date calendar: unknown.** The current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) date table does not contain an archived 2021 table. An archived 2021 official table would settle the specific dates. [Belastingdienst, VAT due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
- **Yearly VAT-return limit: UNVERIFIED for 2021.** Less than € 1.883 is recorded in the 2020 and 2026 handbooks, but the checked materials do not establish the 2021 amount. A 2021 handbook or dated official instruction would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Other yearly-return conditions: UNVERIFIED for 2021.** The 2020 and 2026 handbooks require a natural person or a partnership of natural persons, no Article 23 licence, and less than € 10.000 for each of four intra-EU activities separately. The 2021-specific guidance was not found. A 2021 handbook would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Monthly-return trigger: UNVERIFIED for 2021.** The 2020 handbook says more than € 15.000 VAT per quarter. The 2026 handbook and current page no longer state that trigger. A 2021 handbook or dated official policy would settle whether it applied in 2021. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Supplementary return small-correction boundary: UNVERIFIED for 2021.** The 2020 handbook says “less than € 1.000”. The 2026 handbook says “€ 1.000 or less”. The current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page says a maximum of € 1.000. These are different boundary wordings. A 2021 handbook or dated official instruction would settle which wording applied. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst, correcting a return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)
- **ICP filing deadline (aangiftetermijn): unknown for 2021.** The checked explanation is not a 2021 guide. The 2021 ICP instructions or official calendar would settle the deadline. [Belastingdienst, digital ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- **Historical detail of the full Table I (tabel i) list: UNVERIFIED.** The checked Dutch Tax and Customs Administration goods and services lists are current and do not state their year. The 2021 statute establishes the 9% rate for Table I, and the specific checked instruments establish the 2021 treatment of accommodation (logies) and electronic publications. A 2021 version of Table I and its applicable amendments would settle the full historical list. [Belastingdienst, goods at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Belastingdienst, services at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/), [Wet op de omzetbelasting, Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2021-07-01)
- **OSS inactivity claim: UNVERIFIED.** A claim that two years without supplies ends participation while returns are still filed is not stated on the checked Dutch Tax and Customs Administration OSS page. The applicable 2021 legal provision or official instruction would settle it. [Belastingdienst, OSS VAT return](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- **General B2B e-invoicing duty: UNVERIFIED for 2021.** The current government page says suppliers to central government must send e-invoices and local authorities may require them, but it does not establish the 2021 position. A dated 2021 government source would settle the historical rule. [Business.gov.nl, e-invoicing to government organisations](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)
