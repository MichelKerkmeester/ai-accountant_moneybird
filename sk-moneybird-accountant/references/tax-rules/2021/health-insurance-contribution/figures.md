## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers only the income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet) that an entrepreneur pays over the profit, its rate of 5,75% for 2021 and the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 58.311. It also covers who pays through the assessment (aanslag), how the base follows the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) and how the rate differs from the 7,00% employer levy (werkgeversheffing) that an employer pays. The 2021 rates and the maximum are enacted and verified in the checked sources. One element stays UNVERIFIED for 2021: the base rule rests on Zvw art. 43 read in the 2026 version, because the 2021 version of that article was not read.
