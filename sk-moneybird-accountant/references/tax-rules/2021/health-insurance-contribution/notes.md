## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) and withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage): 5,45% in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to 5,75% in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 57.232 in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to € 58.311 in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Zvw employer levy (werkgeversheffing): 6,70% in 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, also the employer levy series 2020 to 2024) to 7,00% in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Base rule: the checked material states the same rule for both years, taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling), and marks it UNVERIFIED in both years because only the 2026 texts of Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 were read (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). No change can be confirmed from the checked material.
- Channels, the once-per-person maximum and its exceptions: the checked material states the same for 2020 and 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The entrepreneur's own Zvw contribution is billed by assessment (aanslag), and the Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Record the billed amount as a separate liability from the income tax entry when the assessment is received.
- The base is the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, 2026 versions, UNVERIFIED for 2021). Keep the profit (winst) calculation so that the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 6.670 and the SME profit exemption of 14% for 2021 are visible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 58.311 applies once per person, so when there is also salary (loon) from employment (loondienst) or a benefit (uitkering) that already bore a contribution, only the headroom is charged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The 2021 percentages are set by ministerial regulation under Zvw art. 45 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, 2026 version).
- Filing route and deadline: the checked Zvw sources do not state the 2021 declaration route or dates. The assessment follows the income tax route per https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, and the deadline belongs to the procedure topic. This is listed as UNVERIFIED in section 7.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for the profit entrepreneur (winstondernemer), UNVERIFIED for 2021. The rule, taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling), with the art. 3.70 lid 2 addition for an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) decrease excluded, rests on Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 as read in the 2026 version only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reading the text valid on 1 January 2021 of Zvw art. 43 and of Wet IB 2001 afdeling 3.2 would settle it.
- Declaration and deadlines, UNVERIFIED. The checked 2021 material does not state whether the same return is used or which deadlines apply. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms that the Zvw assessment (aanslag) comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting), and the filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic. The 2021 procedure pages would settle it.
- AOW age, no stated rule. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table (tabel) 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). No AOW-age exemption or reduction appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. The 2021 text of Zvw art. 41 to 45 would settle it.
- Rates and maximum for 2021: no conflict between the checked sources. All 2021 values come from https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and are marked Verified.
