## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Example 1. Official example (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), the multiplication written out here.**
Salary (loon) € 40.000 plus freelance income € 20.000.
Step 1: maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 58.311.
Step 2: the salary of € 40.000 already bore the employer levy (werkgeversheffing), so it uses up € 40.000 of the headroom.
Step 3: remaining headroom is € 58.311 minus € 40.000 = € 18.311.
Step 4: the freelance income of € 20.000 is above the remaining headroom, so the 5,75% applies to € 18.311 only.
Step 5: the contribution is € 18.311 times 5,75% = € 1.052,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 2. Official example (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).**
Salary € 60.000 plus freelance income € 15.000.
Step 1: the salary of € 60.000 is above the maximum of € 58.311, so the headroom is € 0.
Step 2: no Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag) is charged over the freelance income. The page states this gives no contribution.

**Example 3. Calculated example.**
Profit (winst) € 60.000, entrepreneur below AOW age.
Step 1: profit is € 60.000.
Step 2: self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) € 6.670, so profit after entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) is € 60.000 minus € 6.670 = € 53.330.
Step 3: SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) is 14% of € 53.330 = € 7.466,20.
Step 4: taxable profit (belastbare winst) is € 53.330 minus € 7.466,20 = € 45.863,80. This is below the maximum of € 58.311, so the full amount is the contribution income.
Step 5: the contribution is € 45.863,80 times 5,75% = € 2.637,17 rounded to the cent.

**Example 4. Calculated example.**
Profit (winst) € 100.000, entrepreneur below AOW age.
Step 1: € 100.000 minus € 6.670 = € 93.330.
Step 2: 14% of € 93.330 = € 13.066,20.
Step 3: taxable profit is € 93.330 minus € 13.066,20 = € 80.263,80.
Step 4: the maximum contribution income is € 58.311, so the base is capped at € 58.311.
Step 5: the contribution is € 58.311 times 5,75% = € 3.352,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 5. Calculated example.**
Taxable profit of € 20.000 plus salary of € 50.000 that already bore the employer levy.
Step 1: headroom is € 58.311 minus € 50.000 = € 8.311.
Step 2: the taxable profit of € 20.000 is above the headroom, so the contribution is charged over € 8.311.
Step 3: the contribution is € 8.311 times 5,75% = € 477,88 rounded to the cent.

**Example 6. Calculated example, employer rate against the other channels.**
Salary € 40.000.
Step 1: employer levy is 7,00% of € 40.000 = € 2.800,00.
Step 2: the 5,75% rate over the same € 40.000 = € 2.300,00.
Step 3: difference is € 2.800,00 minus € 2.300,00 = € 500,00.
