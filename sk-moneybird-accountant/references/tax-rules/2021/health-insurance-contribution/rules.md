## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) by (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag), 5,75%

The entrepreneur who is not in employment (loondienst) pays the income-dependent Zvw contribution by (provisional) assessment. This channel covers the profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland), which does not include the conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente), plus income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), salary (loon) from a foreign employer per the 2020 to 2024 pages, pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes (loonheffing) such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The withheld Zvw contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) is also 5,75% in 2021 and is withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits (uitkeringen) with payroll taxes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, in the article text read in the 2026 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, see section 7).

At AOW age (state pension age) https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table (tabel) 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). No AOW-age exemption or reduction appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), and the FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age. This is noted in section 7.

### Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen), € 58.311

The maximum contribution income of € 58.311 applies once per person across all channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom, so only the amount up to the maximum is charged. Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation, in the article text read in the 2026 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, see section 7). The checked material lists these exceptions: military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw employer levy (werkgeversheffing), 7,00%

The employer levy is paid by the employer or benefit agency over salary from employment, director and substantial shareholder (DGA) salary when insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people insured with a health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). It is levied on the employer, while the withheld contribution is withheld from the income of the insured person and the entrepreneur pays by assessment. For 2021 the employer rate is 7,00% and the rate for the withheld contribution and the assessment is 5,75% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The same maximum contribution income of € 58.311 applies across the channels, so salary that already bore the employer levy uses up the headroom for the assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw base for profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming)

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 in turn defines taxable profit (belastbare winst) as profit minus entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) minus SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). So the Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur (winstondernemer) is the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Both texts were read in the 2026 version only, so this base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2021 and is listed in section 7.

Interaction with the old-age reserve: the 2021 addition is 9,44% of the profit, with a maximum of € 9.395 reduced by the occupational pension contribution (beroepspensioenpremie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans). An addition to the reserve lowers the profit and thereby the Zvw base, while a decrease that is added under art. 3.70 lid 2 is excluded from the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), € 6.670

Interaction only. The self-employed person's deduction for 2021 is € 6.670, or € 3.335 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption and so lowers the taxable profit that forms the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The conditions, among them the hours criterion (urencriterium), belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic, see section 8.

### SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling), 14%

Interaction only. The SME profit exemption for 2021 is 14% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is applied after the entrepreneur's allowance in the step from profit to taxable profit, so it lowers the Zvw base as well (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The detailed conditions belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic, see section 8.
