## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- **Moratorium end date.** 2020: the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 was announced in the third progress letter of 24 June 2019, and the Sixth Progress Letter (zesde voortgangsbrief) of 16 November 2020 put the restart of enforcement at the earliest 1 October 2021 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: the seventh progress letter of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 and left the decisions on the web module (webmodule) and the moratorium to the next cabinet (same source).
- **Web module status.** 2020: not yet live, with the pilot expected to start on 11 January 2021 (Sixth Progress Letter of 16 November 2020, same source). 2021: the pilot ran from 11 January 2021 to 10 July 2021, the online environment stayed active and there was no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot (same source and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).
- **Enforcement exception.** From 1 January 2020: enforcement also when instructions (aanwijzingen) were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020 (third progress letter of 24 June 2019, https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: this stayed in force through the extension (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- **Legislative pause.** 2020: the fifth progress letter (vijfde voortgangsbrief) of 18 June 2020 stated that the cabinet stopped working out new legislation (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). 2021: the checked sources state that the decisions on the web module and the moratorium were left to the next cabinet (seventh progress letter of 20 September 2021, same source).
- **Case law.** 2020: HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on intention of the parties (partijbedoeling) was decided in that year (same source). 2021: that ruling applies in the 2021 test, and the later Deliveroo and Uber rulings do not apply to 2021 (same source).
- **Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten).** 2020: the 2020 file records approved model agreements in use with no 2020-specific change found, marked UNVERIFIED (same source). 2021: the checked material again states no 2021-specific change, and the next changes are the withdrawal of the free substitution (vrije vervanging) approval on 14 August 2023 and the stop on new assessments from 6 September 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- **VBAR and BAZ.** Neither existed in 2020 or 2021. The VBAR internet consultation started on 6 October 2023 and the BAZ bill was submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the signed agreement (overeenkomst), the approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) if one is used, and a record of how the work is actually done. Working under an approved model agreement means no employment (loondienst) only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- If a home worker (thuiswerker), person treated as an employee (gelijkgestelde) or artist (artiest) is used and the parties want to exclude the deemed employment relationship, the written exclusion must be in the contract before the first payment of pay. The consequence is no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW for the contractor (opdrachtnemer) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- If the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) gives an instruction (aanwijzing), keep the instruction and the follow-up in the file. The relationship must be reshaped or processed in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months, and a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) runs from the date of the instruction if it is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- The profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) of the sole trader are part of the income tax return for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). The return had to arrive before 1 May 2022 and the filing period opened on 1 March (Status enacted, Check Corrected, https://web.archive.org/web/20220401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). With deferral (uitstel) the date was before 1 September 2022 (Status enacted, Check Corrected, https://web.archive.org/web/20220309/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Without a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) the 2021 return could still be filed until 31 December 2026 (Status enacted, Check Verified, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).
- Keep the records for seven years (AWR art. 52 lid 4, same text in the 2020 and 2026 versions, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). AWR art. 52 lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden).
- A web module (webmodule) outcome is not a legal decision and gives no rights, so keep the contract and the facts as the basis of the file (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).
- Not being in employment does not make the contractor an entrepreneur for income tax or VAT, so registration and returns follow their own tests (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) 2021.** The checked material contains no 2021-specific event for model agreements. The 2020 file recorded the same gap as UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). A 2021 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page or a 2021 progress letter (voortgangsbrief) that lists a model agreements change would settle whether anything changed in 2021.
- **Uber ruling date.** The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling, so they are UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The ruling text on rechtspraak.nl or the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) site would settle the date and the ECLI.
- **2/5 of the minimum wage for persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) in 2021.** The condition is at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), but the checked sources give no 2021 minimum wage, so no 2021 euro amount is computed here. A 2021 WML source would settle it.
- **VBAR first-application amount.** The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). Publication of that scheme would settle it.
- **BAZ status.** The BAZ bill 36 912 is proposed. On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). A nota and a vote would settle its status. It did not exist in tax year 2021.
- **VBAR status for 2021.** The VBAR was adopted on 18 June 2026 as Stb. 2026, 158 and enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). It does not apply to tax year 2021.
- **Resolved, not open: € 36 against € 38.** Both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, built from the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 at € 14,71 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- **Resolved, not open: BAZ report dates 27 May against 29 May 2026.** Both are correct. The committee input (inbreng) was delivered on 27 May 2026 and the report (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- **Resolved, not open: Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA.** It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree of 8 April 2016 on the opt-out for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees and artists (artiesten) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).
- The 2021 material states no further open points beyond the cross-year items listed here.
