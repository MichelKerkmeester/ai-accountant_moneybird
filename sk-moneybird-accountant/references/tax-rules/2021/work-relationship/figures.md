## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

In 2021 the Wet DBA (Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties)) was in force and the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) still applied, so a case that was not acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) first got an instruction (aanwijzing) instead of a direct correction. The web module for the assessment of employment relationships ran as a pilot from 11 January 2021 to 10 July 2021 and had no legal force (rechtskracht), and the moratorium was extended at least to 1 October 2021 by the seventh progress letter of 20 September 2021, which left the decisions on the web module (webmodule) and the moratorium to the next cabinet. The 2021 rules are enacted law and enforcement policy, while the VBAR hourly rate presumption and the BAZ bill in section 2 are later items that did not apply to tax year 2021.
