## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Wet DBA

The Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) is the Act of 3 February 2016 and entered into force on 1 May 2016 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). It replaced the VAR, the earlier work-relationship declaration (same source). The name refers to the assessment of work relationships. In 2021 the substantive question is still the ordinary test for employment (loondienst), set out in the next subsection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). A source that is often mixed up: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). It is the Decree of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement (same source).

### Employment test

The test applies to every work relationship where employment is at issue for the 2021 rules (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Employment has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform (personal) work (arbeid), and pay for the work (same source). The courts weigh, among other factors, the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur (same source). There is no calculation. The test is a weighing of the facts of the case (same source). Exception and interaction: not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax or VAT, so the work-relationship qualification and the qualification as entrepreneur for income tax or VAT are separate questions (same source). The outcome of this test is what the enforcement route of the moratorium acts on, and an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) supports the no-employment position only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen) and the written opt-out

The employment page lists the deemed employment relationships, among them contractors for work and helpers, agents, artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees, interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors (opdrachtnemers) working through an intermediary (intermediair) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). For persons treated as employees the page gives the condition of usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (same source). Exception: home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay (same source). The consequence of the exclusion is that the contractor has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW (same source). ZW is the Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet), WIA is the Work and Income (Capacity for Work) Act and WW is the Unemployment Insurance Act (Werkloosheidswet). The exclusion is the subject of the Decree of 8 April 2016, Staatsblad 2016, 165, which is not the Wet DBA itself (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). The timing rule is strict: the exclusion must be written in the contract before the first payment of pay (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### Enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium)

In 2021 the enforcement moratorium applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). The moratorium is enforcement policy, not statute (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). In a case that was not acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing) instead of correcting directly (same enforcement source). The instruction says to reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or to process it as an employment relationship in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months (same source). If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith means intentionally creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment (opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan), knowing or able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Acting in bad faith cases could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), payroll tax supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and penalties (boetes). The second restriction of the moratorium exception applied from 1 January 2020: enforcement also when instructions (aanwijzingen) were not followed within a reasonable term, for periods from 1 January 2020, announced in the third progress letter of 24 June 2019 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 was also announced in that brief (same source). Later context, not for 2021: the moratorium applied until 1 January 2025, and since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply with no instructions, so false self-employment can lead directly to correction obligations and payroll tax supplementary assessments (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed, in which case up to 5 years back, and only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years (same source and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Deadline step: the follow-up on the instruction must usually be done within 3 months, and the correction obligation starts on the date of the instruction if it is not followed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Moratorium extension 2021

The seventh progress letter of 20 September 2021 extended the moratorium at least to 1 October 2021 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The same brief left the decisions on the web module (webmodule) and the moratorium to the next cabinet (same source). In 2020 the Sixth Progress Letter (zesde voortgangsbrief) of 16 November 2020 had put the restart of enforcement at the earliest 1 October 2021 (same source). Effect: through 2021 the instruction route of the previous subsection stayed in force (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). For the file this means no direct correction for a case that was not acting in bad faith during 2021 before an instruction.

### Web module for the assessment of employment relationships

The web module is for clients (opdrachtgevers) with a business in the Netherlands (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/). It gives three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside employment relationship, an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible (same source). It is not a legal decision and gives no rights (same source). Limits: it is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a BV, for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon), or for a foreign contractor. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended on 10 July 2021 (same source and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). There was no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot (same sources). The online pilot environment stayed active after the pilot (same sources). The pilot design used a points system with a client declaration (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Interaction: an outcome can inform the parties, but because it is not a legal decision and gives no rights it does not by itself establish the qualification (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/).

### Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten)

Working under an approved model agreement means no employment, but only if the parties work as described (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). For 2021 this route was in use and the checked material states no 2021-specific change (same source and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Later changes, not for 2021: the approval of the model agreements free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed, and approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done (same source). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motion (motie) Van Oostenbruggen (Parliamentary paper (kamerstuk) 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Interaction with 2021: the condition that the parties work as described applies in 2021, so the bookkeeping should record the actual way of working (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Case law on the employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst)

For 2021 the relevant ruling in the checked material is HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of intention of the parties (partijbedoeling) (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The checked material gives the subject of the ruling, not its full holding. Later rulings, not for 2021: HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), with a Catalogue of Circumstances (Omstandighedencatalogus) that names integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), and free substitution not as an absolute element (same source). HR Uber (preliminary questions): no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion (same source). The government states this has no effect on enforcement (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 for the Uber ruling are UNVERIFIED, see section 7 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### VBAR bill status

Not applicable to tax year 2021. The internet consultation of the original VBAR (legal presumption of an employment contract based on an hourly rate) ran from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering). The bill went to the Council of State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024 (same source). Bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and on 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) (same source and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783). The notice of amendment of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering). The House of Representatives adopted the bill on 21 April 2026, all parties except FVD, and the Senate on 16 June 2026 by a vote taken by sitting and standing. It became the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). It enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Decree of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). For tax year 2021 none of this applied, so the 2021 qualification uses the ordinary employment test and the moratorium described above (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### VBAR low hourly rate presumption

Not applicable to tax year 2021. New art. 7:610aa BW: lid 1 someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). Lid 2 the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount (same source). Lid 3 the presumption is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business (same source). Article II provides an evaluation within 5 years (same source). Article III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) (same source). Effect: the worker (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client (opdrachtgever) must prove there is no employment contract (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule (same source). The amount: the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains € 36 as 120% of the WML (minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon)) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV (disability insurance (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering)) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, which gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025). Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026 is stated on the Senate page, on rijksoverheid.nl and on Ondernemersplein (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). The ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). The build-up of the € 36 and the indexation to € 38 are in section 4.

### BAZ bill 36 912

Not applicable to tax year 2021. Bill 36 912 was submitted on 24 March 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). A technical briefing followed on 13 May 2026 (same source). The written contribution to the debate was made on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May 2026 (same source). The report was published on 29 May 2026 as 36 912, nr. 5 (same source). On 2026-10-11 the bill was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote (same source). The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy (same source). Status: proposed (same source). For 2021 the BAZ bill did not exist (same source).
