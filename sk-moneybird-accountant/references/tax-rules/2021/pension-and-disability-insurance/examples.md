## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example 1: annual allowance (jaarruimte) on a profit of € 60.000.**
Facts: 2020 profits from business activities of € 60.000, being the amount before the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and before any addition to or withdrawal from the old-age reserve, with no taxable wage, no taxable income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden), no taxable periodic payments and benefits in kind (periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen), no pension accrual, no FOR addition.
- Step 1, cap at the income cap (aftoppingsgrens): min(€ 60.000, € 112.189) = € 60.000.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 60.000 - € 12.672 = € 47.328 contribution base (premiegrondslag).
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.328 = € 6.294,62, rounded down.
Outcome: the 2021 deduction for annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) is at most € 6.294,62, if the premiums are paid in 2021 and the product conditions are met. The maximum annual allowance of € 13.236 does not bind here.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf.

**Calculated example 2: the maximum annual allowance.**
Facts: taxable wage 2020 of € 120.000 and no other contribution base components, no pension accrual, no FOR addition.
- Step 1, cap at the income cap: min(€ 120.000, € 112.189) = € 112.189.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 112.189 - € 12.672 = € 99.517 contribution base.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 99.517 = € 13.235,76, rounded down.
Outcome: the highest possible annual allowance before rounding to whole euros is € 13.235,76. The Ministry table publishes the maximum as € 13.236, rounded to the nearest euro.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf.

**Calculated example 3: annual allowance with a FOR addition.**
Facts: 2020 profits from business activities of € 60.000, being the amount before the entrepreneur's allowance and before any addition to or withdrawal from the old-age reserve, with a FOR addition over 2020 of 9,44% x € 60.000 = € 5.664,00 (below the € 9.395 cap), no reduction, no pension accrual, and the growth of business capital allows the full addition.
- Step 1, part a of the contribution base is the profit before additions to or withdrawals from the old-age reserve (winst vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve): € 60.000.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 60.000 - € 12.672 = € 47.328 contribution base.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.328 = € 6.294,62, rounded down.
- Step 4, subtract the FOR addition: € 6.294,62 - € 5.664,00 = € 630,62.
Outcome: at most € 630,62 of annuity premiums is deductible in 2021. The addition of € 5.664,00 is the 9,44% amount for a profit of € 60.000, so the € 9.395 cap cannot be reached at this profit level.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example 4: catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte), three situations.**
Situation a, younger group. Facts: born after 31 August 1964, contribution base 2020 of € 40.000, unused annual allowance 2014 to 2020 of € 10.000.
- Step 1: 17% x € 40.000 = € 6.800,00.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 7.489.
- Step 3: the lower of € 6.800,00 and € 7.489 is € 6.800,00.
- Step 4: the unused annual allowance of € 10.000 is higher than € 6.800,00, so it does not bind.
Outcome: catch-up allowance at most € 6.800,00. Here the 17% limit binds.
Situation b, younger group with a larger base. Facts: same group, contribution base 2020 of € 47.328.
- Step 1: 17% x € 47.328 = € 8.045,76.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 7.489.
- Step 3: the lower of € 8.045,76 and € 7.489 is € 7.489.
Outcome: catch-up allowance at most € 7.489. Here the euro cap binds. The unused annual allowance 2014 to 2020 must be at least € 7.489 for this amount to be available.
Situation c, older group. Facts: born before 1 September 1964, contribution base 2020 of € 99.517.
- Step 1: 17% x € 99.517 = € 16.917,89.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 14.785.
- Step 3: the lower of € 16.917,89 and € 14.785 is € 14.785.
Outcome: catch-up allowance at most € 14.785. Here the euro cap binds. The unused annual allowance 2014 to 2020 must be at least € 14.785 for this amount to be available.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 5: temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) at and above the cap.**
Facts: a temporary old-age annuity with five yearly instalments (termijnen), starting in the year of state pension age.
- Situation a: the yearly benefit (uitkering) is € 22.443, exactly at the 2021 cap of € 22.443 per year, so the product meets the cap condition.
- Situation b: the yearly benefit is € 25.000, above the 2021 cap of € 22.443. Above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years. Five instalments do not meet that condition, so the premiums are not deductible.
Outcome: the cap is judged per year at the time of premium payment. At or below € 22.443 the five year product fits, above € 22.443 the 20 year condition decides.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 6: profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion in two tiers.**
Situation a, tier a. Facts: cessation (staking) in 2021 at age 66, the 2021 state pension age is 66 years and 4 months, so the age is 4 months below the state pension age and at most five years below the state pension age, profit from business discontinuation of € 200.000, no already built provisions (no pension rights charged to profit, no FOR at the start of the year, no earlier annuity premium deductions, no earlier conversions).
- Step 1: the deduction can never exceed the profit from business discontinuation, so the first boundary is € 200.000.
- Step 2: the tier a maximum is € 474.517.
- Step 3: the lower of € 200.000 and € 474.517 is € 200.000.
Outcome: at most € 200.000 is deductible, if the premium is paid in 2021 or, on choice, before 1 July 2022.
Situation b, tier c. Facts: a taxpayer who meets neither the tier a nor the tier b condition, profit from business discontinuation of € 150.000, no already built provisions.
- Step 1: the tier c maximum is € 118.640.
- Step 2: the lower of € 118.640 and € 150.000 is € 118.640.
Outcome: at most € 118.640 is deductible.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 7: commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (lijfrente) and the long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) route.**
- Small annuity: commutation in one payment is allowed for a small annuity with a yearly benefit below the 2021 amount of € 4.547. A yearly benefit of € 4.400 stays below it, so this exception applies. A yearly benefit of € 4.800 is above it, so this exception does not apply.
- Long-term disability: a commutation of € 40.000 in 2021 by a taxpayer who is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) and has not yet reached the state pension age stays within the basic cap of € 42.460. A higher average of the contribution base plus franchise for 2021 and 2020 could raise that cap.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example 8: AOV premium with periodic benefits.**
Facts: an entrepreneur pays € 1.800 in 2021 for a policy that gives rights to periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) on disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ongeval). Art. 3.124 lid 1 c puts no allowance cap on this item, so the full € 1.800 is deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and it is not a business cost. The annual allowance and the catch-up allowance do not limit it.
Variant: if the same policy paid a lump sum, the € 1.800 would not be deductible and a later payout would count in box 3.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers.

**Official example 9: FOR decrease (afname).**
The FISIN 2021 example repeats the 2020 example. The business capital (ondernemingsvermogen, equity plus the old-age reserve (FOR)) is € 3.000 and the FOR is € 4.000, so the FOR must decrease by € 1.000. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

**Official example 10: start date (ingangsdatum) and survivors (nabestaanden) deadlines.**
A start date in 2020 means the annuity must be converted or started by 31 December 2021. A start date in 2021 means by 31 December 2022. A death in 2020 means the survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) must start by 31 December 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Official example 11: old-age annuity and the 24 years.**
An old-age annuity with a state pension age in 2030 must start at the latest on 31 December 2035. A account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) that starts at 63 with a state pension age of 67 runs at least 20 + 4 = 24 years. A child who is 21 at death runs at least 5 years and stops at the latest after 9 years, and a child who is 27 at death stops after 3 years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.
