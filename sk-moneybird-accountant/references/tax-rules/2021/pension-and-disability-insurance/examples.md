## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example 1: jaarruimte on a winst of € 60.000.**
Facts: winst uit ondernemen 2020 of € 60.000, being the amount before the ondernemersaftrek and before any toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve, no belastbaar loon, no belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden, no belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen, no pension accrual, no FOR toevoeging.
- Step 1, cap at the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 60.000, € 112.189) = € 60.000.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 60.000 - € 12.672 = € 47.328 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.328 = € 6.294,62, rounded down.
Outcome: the 2021 deduction for lijfrentepremies is at most € 6.294,62, if the premiums are paid in 2021 and the product conditions are met. The maximum jaarruimte of € 13.236 does not bind here.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf.

**Calculated example 2: the maximum jaarruimte.**
Facts: belastbaar loon 2020 of € 120.000 and no other premiegrondslag components, no pension accrual, no FOR toevoeging.
- Step 1, cap at the aftoppingsgrens: min(€ 120.000, € 112.189) = € 112.189.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 112.189 - € 12.672 = € 99.517 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 99.517 = € 13.235,76, rounded down.
Outcome: the highest possible jaarruimte before rounding to whole euros is € 13.235,76. The Ministry table publishes the maximum as € 13.236, rounded to the nearest euro.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf.

**Calculated example 3: jaarruimte with a FOR toevoeging.**
Facts: winst uit ondernemen 2020 of € 60.000, being the amount before the ondernemersaftrek and before any toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve, a FOR toevoeging over 2020 of 9,44% x € 60.000 = € 5.664,00 (below the € 9.395 cap), no afneming, no pension accrual, and the growth of ondernemingsvermogen allows the full toevoeging.
- Step 1, part a of the premiegrondslag is the winst vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve: € 60.000.
- Step 2, reduce by the franchise: € 60.000 - € 12.672 = € 47.328 premiegrondslag.
- Step 3, apply the percentage: 13,3% x € 47.328 = € 6.294,62, rounded down.
- Step 4, subtract the FOR toevoeging: € 6.294,62 - € 5.664,00 = € 630,62.
Outcome: at most € 630,62 of lijfrentepremies is deductible in 2021. The toevoeging of € 5.664,00 is the 9,44% amount for a winst of € 60.000, so the € 9.395 cap cannot be reached at this winst level.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example 4: reserveringsruimte, three situations.**
Situation a, younger group. Facts: born after 31 August 1964, premiegrondslag 2020 of € 40.000, unused jaarruimte 2014 to 2020 of € 10.000.
- Step 1: 17% x € 40.000 = € 6.800,00.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 7.489.
- Step 3: the lower of € 6.800,00 and € 7.489 is € 6.800,00.
- Step 4: the unused jaarruimte of € 10.000 is higher than € 6.800,00, so it does not bind.
Outcome: reserveringsruimte at most € 6.800,00. Here the 17% limit binds.
Situation b, younger group with a larger base. Facts: same group, premiegrondslag 2020 of € 47.328.
- Step 1: 17% x € 47.328 = € 8.045,76.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 7.489.
- Step 3: the lower of € 8.045,76 and € 7.489 is € 7.489.
Outcome: reserveringsruimte at most € 7.489. Here the euro cap binds. The unused jaarruimte 2014 to 2020 must be at least € 7.489 for this amount to be available.
Situation c, older group. Facts: born before 1 September 1964, premiegrondslag 2020 of € 99.517.
- Step 1: 17% x € 99.517 = € 16.917,89.
- Step 2: the euro cap for this group is € 14.785.
- Step 3: the lower of € 16.917,89 and € 14.785 is € 14.785.
Outcome: reserveringsruimte at most € 14.785. Here the euro cap binds. The unused jaarruimte 2014 to 2020 must be at least € 14.785 for this amount to be available.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 5: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente at and above the cap.**
Facts: a tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente with five yearly termijnen, starting in the year of AOW age.
- Situation a: the yearly uitkering is € 22.443, exactly at the 2021 cap of € 22.443 per year, so the product meets the cap condition.
- Situation b: the yearly uitkering is € 25.000, above the 2021 cap of € 22.443. Above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. Five termijnen do not meet that condition, so the premiums are not deductible.
Outcome: the cap is judged per year at the time of premium payment. At or below € 22.443 the five year product fits, above € 22.443 the 20 year condition decides.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 6: stakingswinst conversion in two tiers.**
Situation a, tier a. Facts: staking in 2021 at age 66, the 2021 AOW age is 66 years and 4 months, so the age is 4 months below the AOW age and at most five years below the AOW age, stakingswinst of € 200.000, no already built provisions (no pension rights charged to profit, no FOR at the start of the year, no earlier lijfrentepremie deductions, no earlier conversions).
- Step 1: the deduction can never exceed the stakingswinst, so the first boundary is € 200.000.
- Step 2: the tier a maximum is € 474.517.
- Step 3: the lower of € 200.000 and € 474.517 is € 200.000.
Outcome: at most € 200.000 is deductible, if the premium is paid in 2021 or, on choice, before 1 July 2022.
Situation b, tier c. Facts: a taxpayer who meets neither the tier a nor the tier b condition, stakingswinst of € 150.000, no already built provisions.
- Step 1: the tier c maximum is € 118.640.
- Step 2: the lower of € 118.640 and € 150.000 is € 118.640.
Outcome: at most € 118.640 is deductible.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Calculated example 7: afkoop of a small lijfrente and the langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid route.**
- Small lijfrente: afkoop in one payment is allowed for a small lijfrente with a yearly uitkering below the 2021 amount of € 4.547. A yearly uitkering of € 4.400 stays below it, so this exception applies. A yearly uitkering of € 4.800 is above it, so this exception does not apply.
- Langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid: an afkoop of € 40.000 in 2021 by a taxpayer who is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt and has not yet reached the AOW age stays within the basic cap of € 42.460. A higher average of the premiegrondslag plus franchise for 2021 and 2020 could raise that cap.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01.

**Calculated example 8: AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen.**
Facts: an ondernemer pays € 1.800 in 2021 for a policy that gives rights to periodieke uitkeringen on invaliditeit, ziekte or ongeval. Art. 3.124 lid 1 c puts no ruimte cap on this item, so the full € 1.800 is deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and it is not a business cost. The jaarruimte and the reserveringsruimte do not limit it.
Variant: if the same policy paid a lump sum, the € 1.800 would not be deductible and a later payout would count in box 3.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers.

**Official example 9: FOR afname.**
The FISIN 2021 example repeats the 2020 example. The ondernemingsvermogen (eigen vermogen plus FOR) is € 3.000 and the FOR is € 4.000, so the FOR must decrease by € 1.000. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans.

**Official example 10: ingangsdatum and nabestaanden deadlines.**
An ingangsdatum in 2020 means the lijfrente must be converted or started by 31 December 2021. An ingangsdatum in 2021 means by 31 December 2022. A death in 2020 means the nabestaandenlijfrente must start by 31 December 2022. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.

**Official example 11: oudedagslijfrente and the 24 years.**
An oudedagslijfrente with an AOW age in 2030 must start at the latest on 31 December 2035. A rekening or beleggingsrecht that starts at 63 with an AOW age of 67 runs at least 20 + 4 = 24 years. A child who is 21 at death runs at least 5 years and stops at the latest after 9 years, and a child who is 27 at death stops after 3 years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen.
