## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2021

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

Art. 3.127 lid 1 gives a taxpayer with a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year a deduction for annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to a percentage of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In the text valid on 1 January 2021 that percentage is 13,3% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The test looks at the previous calendar year, so the 2021 deduction is built on income and pension accrual of 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). In 2021 the outcome is also reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve), the same structure as 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The rule that the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 keeps applying to accrual under the old pension norms only becomes relevant from 2023 and is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Maximum annual allowance

The Ministry of Finance (ministerie van financiën) table of expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) states a maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2021 of € 13.236 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf). This ceiling binds when the income of 2020 reaches the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf). The same amount also appears in the Ministry table for 2022 as the comparison value (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf). Premiums above the allowance are never deductible, but they are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is later taxed, and the maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Income cap contribution base

Art. 3.127 lid 3 sums four components over the previous calendar year and counts them up to the first-mentioned (eerstvermelde) amount, the income cap (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The four components are the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before additions to or withdrawals from the old-age reserve and before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek), the taxable wage (belastbaar loon), the taxable income from other work (belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and the taxable periodic payments and benefits in kind (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). For 2021 the income cap is € 112.189 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). Income above that amount is ignored (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). On a (partial) cessation (staking) the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year, which is the lid 5 option (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Franchise contribution base

After the cap, art. 3.127 lid 3 reduces the sum by the last-mentioned (laatstvermelde) amount, the franchise, which is € 12.672 for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). Only the part of the 2020 income above the franchise counts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). For a 2020 income at or below the franchise the contribution base does not exceed nil, so no annual allowance arises (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Factor A multiplier

Art. 3.127 lid 4 sub a reduces the annual allowance by 6,27 times the growth of the yearly pension entitlement from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). This is the reduction for pension accrual, called factor A (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). A self-employed person (zelfstandige) without pension accrual has no reduction under this item, so for that person only the FOR addition reduces the 2021 outcome (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The method changes later: from the 2024-01-01 text the reduction is built on the premiums actually contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus the net pension premiums divided by the net factor (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). That later method does not apply to 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Look-back window catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte)

Art. 3.127 lid 2 lets unused annual allowance of the preceding period be used on request in the tax return, oldest unused year first (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The period is "zeven jaar" in the text valid on 1 January 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The FISIN chapter for 2021 describes the window as annual allowance 2014 to 2020, so unused annual allowance of 2013 can no longer be used in the 2021 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) advises using catch-up allowance before annual allowance so that older unused allowance does not lapse (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). The ten-year window only starts with the text of 1 July 2023 and is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance percentage cap

Art. 3.127 lid 2 for 2020 to 2022 caps the catch-up allowance at 17% of the contribution base and at a euro amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). Both limits apply at the same time, so the lower of the two binds (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The flat single euro maximum without a percentage only starts with the text of 1 July 2023 and is not a 2021 rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, younger group

The euro limit of the catch-up allowance is € 7.489 for a taxpayer who does not fall in the higher group (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). FISIN 2021 describes this group as born after 31 August 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The Ministry table describes the same group as younger than 56 years and 4 months on 1 January 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The higher euro amount is for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) of that year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Catch-up allowance maximum, older group

The higher euro limit is € 14.785 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). It applies to a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the state pension age of that year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). FISIN 2021 describes this group as born before 1 September 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The 17% limit of the previous item still applies on top of this euro amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Age limit annual allowance

In 2021 the taxpayer must not yet have reached the state pension age at the start of the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The FISIN 2021 chapter carries the same wording (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The wider window, where the taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the state pension age, starts with the Wtp text used from 1 July 2023 and does not apply to 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). The special rule that a taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the annual allowance but may still use the catch-up allowance is stated for 2026 only and is not a 2021 rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c allows a temporary old-age annuity with at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of state pension age and at the latest in the year of state pension age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The 2021 cap is € 22.443 per year, and art. 3.125, FISIN 2021 and the Ministry table for 2021 agree on that amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). FISIN adds that above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

A bridge annuity is only possible for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The yearly maximum is € 63.288, printed on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026, so also for 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction of annuity premiums up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The provisions that reduce the cap are the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). For 2021 the tier a maximum is € 474.517, per art. 3.129 in the text of 1 January 2021, FISIN 2021 and the Ministry table for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Tier a applies when the age at cessation is at most five years below the state pension age at cessation, or when the taxpayer is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) and the annuity (lijfrente) starts within six months, or when the cessation happens by death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The law test is relative to the state pension age and keeps the wording "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

Tier b applies when the age at cessation is at most fifteen years below the state pension age, or when the annuity starts immediately (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2021 tier b maximum is € 237.267, per art. 3.129 in the text of 1 January 2021, FISIN 2021 and the Ministry table for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same subtraction of already built provisions described under tier a applies to this cap (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

Tier c covers the other cases (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2021 tier c maximum is € 118.640, per art. 3.129 in the text of 1 January 2021, FISIN 2021 and the Ministry table for 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The same subtraction of already built provisions described under tier a applies to this cap (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The deduction can never exceed the profit from business discontinuation itself (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Commutation of a small annuity (afkoop kleine lijfrente)

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows a small annuity to be commuted in a single payment when its annual benefit is below the threshold (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2021 that amount is € 4.547, per art. 3.133 lid 2 d in the text of 1 January 2021 and the Ministry table for 2021, which lists it as "afkoop lijfrente vrij van revisierente" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). A commutation above this small-annuity threshold follows the normal rules for losing the annuity status (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Art. 3.133 lid 9 does not treat commutation as a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached the state pension age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment (termijn) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2021 the cap in art. 3.133 lid 9 c is € 42.460 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).

### FOR addition

Art. 3.68 sets the addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) at 9,44% of the profit, capped at a euro amount of € 9.395 for 2021, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit, and never above the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). Access to a FOR addition requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and that the taxpayer has not yet reached the state pension age at the start of the year, which is art. 3.67 and not art. 3.68 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows a decrease (afname) by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the business capital at cessation, at state pension age at the start of the year, or when the hours criterion fails both this year and last year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). A FOR addition of the previous year still reduces the annual allowance in 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127). A FOR annuity conversion is deducted under art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022, and the converted amount must be added to the profit. FISIN 2021 links the payment to "in 2021 of vóór 1 juli 2022" and the addition to the profit (bijtelling) "in 2022", while the law allows the payment within six months after the year end to count in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the business and takes the reserve over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### Mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium

A mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies). It is deductible as a business cost of the business, with a restriction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### AOV premium with periodic benefits

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c treats premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind (periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) in respect of disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) for the taxpayer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The article puts no allowance cap on this item and FISIN states the premiums can be "volledig aftrekken" (fully deducted), so the annual allowance does not limit them (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). Not deductible (niet aftrekbaar): premiums the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Health Insurance Act (Zorgverzekeringswet, Zvw) premiums (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). These four exclusions and section 15.4 of the FISIN chapter are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026, so 2021 repeats them unchanged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). As an entrepreneur the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A policy that pays a lump sum does not qualify (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and it counts in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states the rule of thumb: a premium that is not deductible means the payout is not taxed, and the reverse (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### Payment deadline FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion

Art. 3.130 lid 1 makes premiums count in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Art. 3.130 lid 2 lets premiums for the profit from business discontinuation conversion (art. 3.129), the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) be counted in the year itself on choice when they are paid within six months after the year end (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). For 2021 that option runs to before 1 July 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). FISIN 2021 links the payment to "in 2021 of vóór 1 juli 2022" but prints the addition to the profit "in 2022", which is inconsistent year wording on the page. The law allows the payment within six months after the year end to count in 2021 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Annuity start date (ingangsdatum) term

There are three annuity forms: the annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), the annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) and the annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). For an old-age annuity under art. 3.125 lid 1 a the first instalment must be paid at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches the state pension age plus five, with payments until death, and an account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) must run at least 20 years plus the number of years the benefit starts before the state pension year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). FISIN 2021 repeats the example that a state pension age in 2030 means starting at the latest on 31 December 2035, and that starting at 63 with a state pension age of 67 gives a minimum of 20 + 4 = 24 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). If an annuity stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation or unblocking (deblokkering), the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). An annuity that reaches its start date must be converted or started within one full calendar year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). Refunds of a deducted premium are negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest, and for an annuity only if the refund is undone within 30 days (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### Survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) term

A survivor annuity must start within two full calendar years after the year of death, with an extension on request in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). Art. 3.125 lid 1 b limits a survivor annuity for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner to death or at the latest age 30, and for an account or investment right to at least 20 years, or for children under 30 to at least 5 years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). FISIN 2021 repeats the examples that a child who is 21 at death runs at least 5 years and stops at the latest after 9 years, and that a child who is 27 at death stops after 3 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen). An annuity for a disabled adult child or grandchild (meerderjarig (klein)kind) is deductible without a pension shortfall (pensioentekort), under art. 3.124 lid 1 b (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
