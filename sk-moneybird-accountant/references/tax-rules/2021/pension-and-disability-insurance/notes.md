## 2. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage stays 13,3%, unchanged from 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Maximum annual allowance rises from € 12.986 to € 13.236 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).
- Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) contribution base (premiegrondslag) rises from € 110.111 to € 112.189 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Franchise contribution base rises from € 12.472 to € 12.672 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Factor A multiplier stays 6,27, unchanged from 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window stays seven years and moves forward by one year, from annual allowance 2013 to 2019 for 2020 to annual allowance 2014 to 2020 for 2021. Unused 2013 room lapses (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) percentage cap stays 17% and both the percentage and the euro cap still apply together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Catch-up allowance maximum for the younger group rises from € 7.371 to € 7.489. FISIN changes the birth date in its wording from born after 31 August 1963 to born after 31 August 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Catch-up allowance maximum for the older group rises from € 14.552 to € 14.785. FISIN changes the birth date from born before 1 September 1963 to born before 1 September 1964 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age limit for the annual allowance stays "not yet state pension age at the start of the year" (AOW-leeftijd) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap rises from € 22.089 to € 22.443 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap stays € 63.288, unchanged, and it still applies only to annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum tier a rises from € 467.044 to € 474.517, tier b from € 233.530 to € 237.267, tier c from € 116.771 to € 118.640 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Commutation of a small annuity (afkoop kleine lijfrente) rises from € 4.475 to € 4.547 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) rises from € 41.791 to € 42.460 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- FOR addition stays 9,44% and the euro cap rises from € 9.218 to € 9.395 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2020 the FISIN page printed € 9.214 against the law and Ministry table figure of € 9.218, which was settled for € 9.218 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- The FOR conversion stays with art. 3.128 and still requires the converted amount to be added to the profit. FISIN 2021 repeats the conversion description from 2020 with the year wording shifted (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- AOV rules are unchanged. Section 15.4 of FISIN 2021, including the four exclusions and the lump-sum exclusion, is identical to 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The six-month payment option moves with the year, from before 1 July 2021 for 2020 premiums to before 1 July 2022 for 2021 premiums (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The start date (ingangsdatum) term (termijn) stays one full calendar year and the survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) term stays two full calendar years, with the official examples shifting one year forward (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The structure of the annual allowance is the same as 2020: 13,3% minus factor A minus the FOR addition, on the contribution base of the previous year, with the FOR wording in onderdeel a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- One 2020 sentence is dropped: FISIN 2020 said that when an annuity stops meeting the conditions and has not started paying, the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid. This sentence does not appear on the 2021 page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the AOV premium separately from business costs. As an entrepreneur the premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen", claimed in the box 1 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- Keep the policy documents that show the benefits are periodic. A policy that pays a lump sum gives no deduction and its payout counts in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) count in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend). Premiums for the FOR conversion, the cessation (staking) option and the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion may still be counted in 2021 if paid before 1 July 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Record the FOR addition of 9,44% of the profit, at most € 9.395, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit and never above the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Record a decrease (afname) of the FOR correctly. The decrease can be a chosen amount up to the annuity (lijfrente) premiums taken into account, or the excess over the business capital at cessation, at state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) at the start of the year, or when the hours criterion (urencriterium) fails this year and last year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- On a FOR annuity conversion the deduction runs through art. 3.128 and the converted amount must be added to the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Premiums above the allowance are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed, and the maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Keep a year-by-year schedule of unused annual allowance (jaarruimte). The catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) must be requested in the tax return, is used oldest unused year first, and in 2021 covers the years 2014 to 2020. Use catch-up allowance before annual allowance so older room does not lapse (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- A mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium and is deductible as a business cost of the business, with a restriction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Watch the start date (ingangsdatum) of every annuity. An annuity that reaches its start date must be converted or started within one full calendar year, a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) within two full calendar years after the year of death (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Report periodic AOV benefits in box 1. They are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No 2021 value in this file is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown, and the checked material records no unresolved conflict for 2021 itself. The points below are wording issues and forward-looking items that are not 2021 law.

- Look-back window: the law text of 1 January 2021 reads "zeven jaar" and FISIN 2021 gives annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2014 to 2020. A ten-year reading for 2021 is wrong (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- FISIN 2021 year wording on the FOR and profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion: the page links the payment to "in 2021 of vóór 1 juli 2022" and the addition to the profit (bijtelling) to "in 2022". Art. 3.130 lid 2 lets a payment within six months after the year end count in 2021. Settled in favour of the law (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Profit from business discontinuation cap reductions: the art. 3.129 breakdown adds the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 to the reduction list only from 2023, while the FISIN 2020 description mentions the FOR at the start of the year among the amounts deducted from the profit from business discontinuation. What would settle it: the art. 3.129 text of 1 January 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Tier a ages: FISIN chapters 2023 to 2026 print "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 with a state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) of 66 years and 10 months, and the ODB 2026 sheet prints "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. The law keeps the relative test "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". No absolute tier a age is printed in the 2021 sources, so for 2021 only the relative test applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).
- Proposed, not 2021 law: the Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), bill 36 912. Docket status on 11 October 2026: "In afwachting nota nav verslag". Bill art. 16 gives a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) describes a benefit of 70% of the base (grondslag), with the base at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so at most the minimum wage. The premium would be set yearly by UWV, estimated at about 5,4% of profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) up to the maximum base. The MvT examples use a maximum base of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit, UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes (loonheffing), and private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force would be by royal decree, and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16. Settled for 104 weeks by bill art. 16 lid 1 and MvT 5.3.1 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 , [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Proposed, not 2021 law: Tax Plan 2027 (belastingplan 2027) article LVII would stop the indexation of the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensionable salary (pensioengevend loon) at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the income cap would stay € 137.800 for those years. A limited inflation correction of 48% of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the annuity (lijfrente) maxima listed in the MvT: the maximum for annuity provisions (lijfrentevoorzieningen) in art. 3.125, the maximum for account (rekening) and investment right (beleggingsrecht), the maximum premiums to be taken into account in art. 3.127, the profit from business discontinuation maximum in art. 3.129 and the maximum for negative expenditure. Not adopted on 11 October 2026. The memorandum also notes that the income cap was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (amendment (amendement) Grinwis c.s., Parliamentary papers (kamerstukken) II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90) (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Later rule, not 2021 law: a self-employed person may join the pension fund (pensioenfonds) of the branch if the fund allows it, the premiums are then deductible, and the test (proef) runs until 1 July 2028, with art. 10a.26 keeping the experimental provision (experimenteerbepaling) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) pension schemes inside the pension definitions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
