## 5. CHANGES FROM 2020

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Jaarruimte percentage stays 13,3%, unchanged from 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Maximum jaarruimte rises from € 12.986 to € 13.236 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-04947996-d528-4eac-afb0-6aa936dc577e/pdf).
- Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag rises from € 110.111 to € 112.189 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Franchise premiegrondslag rises from € 12.472 to € 12.672 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Factor A multiplier stays 6,27, unchanged from 2020 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Look-back window stays seven years and moves forward by one year, from jaarruimte 2013 to 2019 for 2020 to jaarruimte 2014 to 2020 for 2021. Unused 2013 ruimte lapses (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Reserveringsruimte percentage cap stays 17% and both the percentage and the euro cap still apply together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Reserveringsruimte maximum for the younger group rises from € 7.371 to € 7.489. FISIN changes the birth date in its wording from born after 31 August 1963 to born after 31 August 1964 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Reserveringsruimte maximum for the older group rises from € 14.552 to € 14.785. FISIN changes the birth date from born before 1 September 1963 to born before 1 September 1964 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Age limit for the jaarruimte stays "not yet AOW age at the start of the year" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap rises from € 22.089 to € 22.443 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap stays € 63.288, unchanged, and it still applies only to lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Stakingswinst maximum tier a rises from € 467.044 to € 474.517, tier b from € 233.530 to € 237.267, tier c from € 116.771 to € 118.640 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Afkoop kleine lijfrente rises from € 4.475 to € 4.547 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid rises from € 41.791 to € 42.460 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- FOR toevoeging stays 9,44% and the euro cap rises from € 9.218 to € 9.395 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01). For 2020 the FISIN page printed € 9.214 against the law and Ministry table figure of € 9.218, which was settled for € 9.218 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-423637a0-c7a6-4123-a683-148a64e343fa/pdf).
- The FOR conversion stays with art. 3.128 and still requires the converted amount to be added to the winst. FISIN 2021 repeats the conversion description from 2020 with the year wording shifted (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- AOV rules are unchanged. Section 15.4 of FISIN 2021, including the four exclusions and the lump-sum exclusion, is identical to 2020 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The six-month payment option moves with the year, from before 1 July 2021 for 2020 premiums to before 1 July 2022 for 2021 premiums (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The ingangsdatum termijn stays one full calendar year and the nabestaandenlijfrente termijn stays two full calendar years, with the official examples shifting one year forward (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- The structure of the jaarruimte is the same as 2020: 13,3% minus factor A minus the FOR dotatie, on the premiegrondslag of the previous year, with the FOR wording in part a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).
- One 2020 sentence is dropped: FISIN 2020 said that when a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions and has not started paying, the taxed value is at least the total premiums paid. This sentence does not appear on the 2021 page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the AOV premium separately from business costs. As ondernemer the premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen", claimed in the box 1 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).
- Keep the policy documents that show the uitkeringen are periodic. A policy that pays a lump sum gives no deduction and its payout counts in box 3 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Lijfrentepremies count in the year they are paid or verrekend. Premiums for the FOR conversion, the staking option and the stakingswinst conversion may still be counted in 2021 if paid before 1 July 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Record the FOR toevoeging of 9,44% of the winst, at most € 9.395, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst and never above the growth of the ondernemingsvermogen (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01).
- Record an afname of the FOR correctly. The afname can be a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at staking, at AOW age at the start of the year, or when the urencriterium fails this year and last year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01).
- On a FOR lijfrente conversion the deduction runs through art. 3.128 and the converted amount must be added to the winst (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Keep a year-by-year schedule of unused jaarruimte. The reserveringsruimte must be requested in the aangifte, is used oldest unused year first, and in 2021 covers the years 2014 to 2020. Use reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older ruimte does not lapse (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie and is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming, with a restriction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).
- Watch the ingangsdatum of every lijfrente. A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum must be converted or started within one full calendar year, a nabestaandenlijfrente within two full calendar years after the year of death (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Report periodic AOV benefits in box 1. They are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No 2021 value in this file is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown, and the checked material records no unresolved conflict for 2021 itself. The points below are wording issues and forward-looking items that are not 2021 law.

- Look-back window: the law text of 1 January 2021 reads "zeven jaar" and FISIN 2021 gives jaarruimte 2014 to 2020. A ten-year reading for 2021 is wrong (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- FISIN 2021 year wording on the FOR and stakingswinst conversion: the page links the payment to "in 2021 of vóór 1 juli 2022" and the bijtelling on the winst to "in 2022". Art. 3.130 lid 2 lets a payment within six months after the year end count in 2021. Settled in favour of the law (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Stakingswinst cap reductions: the art. 3.129 breakdown adds the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 to the reduction list only from 2023, while the FISIN 2020 description mentions the FOR at the start of the year among the amounts deducted from the stakingswinst. What would settle it: the art. 3.129 text of 1 January 2021 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).
- Tier a ages: FISIN chapters 2023 to 2026 print "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 with an AOW age of 66 years and 10 months, and the ODB 2026 sheet prints "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. The law keeps the relative test "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". No absolute tier a age is printed in the 2021 sources, so for 2021 only the relative test applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).
- Proposed, not 2021 law: the BAZ (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen), bill 36 912. Docket status on 11 October 2026: "In afwachting nota nav verslag". Bill art. 16 gives a wachttijd (waiting period) of 104 weken. The memorie van toelichting describes a benefit of 70% of the grondslag, with the grondslag at most 142,86% of the minimumloon, so at most the minimumloon. The premium would be set yearly by UWV, estimated at about 5,4% of winst uit onderneming up to the maximum grondslag. The MvT examples use a maximum grondslag of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not from the winst, UWV benefits would carry loonheffing, and private policies keep the current tax treatment. Entry into force would be by koninklijk besluit, and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with bill art. 16. Settled for 104 weken by bill art. 16 lid 1 and MvT 5.3.1 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 , [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Proposed, not 2021 law: Belastingplan 2027 article LVII would stop the indexation of the aftoppingsgrens of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensioengevend loon at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the aftoppingsgrens would stay € 137.800 for those years. A limited inflation correction of 48% of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, giving 1,01248, would also apply to the lijfrente maxima listed in the MvT: the maximum for lijfrentevoorzieningen in art. 3.125, the maximum for rekening and beleggingsrecht, the maximum in aanmerking te nemen premies in art. 3.127, the stakingswinst maximum in art. 3.129 and the maximum for negatieve uitgaven. Not adopted on 11 October 2026. The memorie also notes that the aftoppingsgrens was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (amendement Grinwis c.s., Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90) (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).
- Later rule, not 2021 law: a self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds of the branch if the fund allows it, the premiums are then deductible, and the proef (pilot) runs until 1 July 2028, with art. 10a.26 keeping the experimenteerbepaling for zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
