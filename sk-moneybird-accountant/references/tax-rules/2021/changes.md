# Changes tax year 2021

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2021 topic files records. ---

## 1. Unique to 2021

- From 1 July 2021 the e-commerce package introduced the € 10.000 EU distance-sales threshold for qualifying goods and digital services together. [btw](vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 7.030 in 2020 to € 6.670 in 2021. [ondernemersaftrek](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing rose from 3,5% above € 68.507 in 2020 to 6,5% above € 68.507 in 2021. [ondernemersaftrek](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- The below-AOW combined first rate fell from 37,35% to 37,10% and the first statutory boundary moved from € 34.712 to € 35.129. [inkomstenbelasting](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag rose from 5,45% in 2020 to 5,75% in 2021. [zvw](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The heffingsvrij vermogen rose from € 30.846 per person in 2020 to € 50.000 in 2021. [box-3](box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zero-emission reduction for a car first admitted in 2021 is 10% off capped at € 4.000, so 12% up to € 40.000. [kosten-en-auto](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The maximum jaarruimte rose from € 12.986 in 2020 to € 13.236 in 2021. [pensioen-en-aov](pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
