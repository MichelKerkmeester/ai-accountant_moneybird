## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table, old to new: 2023 was nil up to € 2.600, 28% up to € 63.716, € 17.841 up to € 117.991, € 17.841 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 117.991 up to € 353.973 and nil above € 353.973. 2024 is nil up to € 2.800, 28% up to € 69.765, € 19.535 up to € 129.194, € 19.535 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 129.194 up to € 387.580 and nil above € 387.580. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) phrased the 2023 window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" on the [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- EIA percentage, € 45,5% in 2023 to 40% in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.42 lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount, € 136.000.000 in 2023 to € 149.000.000 in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 4 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [art. 3.42 lid 4 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- MIA percentages, no change in 2024: 2023 was already 45% (category I), 36% (category II) and 27% (category III), and 2024 repeats those three percentages. The step from 36% / 27% / 13,5% to 45% / 36% / 27% took effect on 1 January 2022, not in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.42a lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.42a lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html).
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold, € 2.600 in 2023 to € 2.800 in 2024. Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 1 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.47 lid 1 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Starters arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap, € 353.973 in 2023 to € 387.580 in 2024, following the top of the KIA table. Sources: [URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.41 lid 2 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde), old to new: 2023 was the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building. From 1 January 2024 there is one minimum value, the WOZ value of the building, and the 50% rule for own use is gone, except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value until 3 years after first use ([building depreciation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand)). Sources: [art. 3.30a lid 3 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.30a lid 3 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Unchanged from 2023: the KIA per-asset minimum of € 450, the EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500, the Vamil percentage of 75% at will (willekeurig), and the ordinary depreciation caps of 10% for goodwill and 20% for other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen). Sources: [art. 3.45, art. 3.31 and art. 3.30 in the 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 3.45, art. 3.31 and art. 3.30 in the 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Still running in 2024 from the 2023 temporary scheme: costs for which the obligation was entered or production costs (voortbrengingskosten) were made in 2023 can be depreciated at will once, at most 50%, in the year of the obligation or the costs, and the asset must be taken into use before 1 January 2026, with that date moving by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. The scheme covers income tax and corporate income tax. Sources: [URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022 toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the investment per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) with the date of the obligation, the acquisition costs (aanschafkosten) or production costs (voortbrengingskosten) and the payments made in the year, because an asset that is not in use at the year end carries the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) only over the payments made and the excess follows in later years, and because at cessation (staking) the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation. Sources: [art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Claim the KIA in the tax return (aangifte) itself, as a per business (per-onderneming) choice on the total investment in the calendar year. Source: [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).
- Keep the RVO notification with the signed purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or the order confirmation, because the EIA, MIA and Vamil must be reported to RVO within 3 months and the Environmental List (Milieulijst) valid on the signing or order date applies. Sources: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).
- Keep proof that an asset is new ("niet eerder gebruikt"), because a second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset earns no EIA and no MIA, while adaptation costs and self-installed parts can qualify as production costs. Source: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).
- Keep the WOZ decision (WOZ beschikking) for a building, because depreciation stops at the minimum value (bodemwaarde) and the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) of the calendar year of the depreciation counts. Sources: [art. 3.30a lid 3 and 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Track disposals, rentals and cessation for five years after the investment year. Transfer prices above € 2.800 in one year add an amount to the profit at the investment deduction percentage that was taken, rental counts for the KIA and not for the EIA or MIA, and cessation of (part of) the business (onderneming) within the term also counts. Sources: [art. 3.47](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Do not claim investment deduction on an asset bought from a BV in which at least one third of the shares is held, because that obligation is excluded in principle. Sources: [art. 3.46 lid 1 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- For a BV, the same investment deduction instruments appear on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) pages, including the yearly KIA, EIA and MIA changes. Source: [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- Keep the depreciation schedule per asset for the normal book value, because the starters arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) must be corrected through a revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf) within five years, or ten years for sea-going vessels (zeeschepen). Sources: [URWA art. 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Unknown: the RVO maximum of € 25 million (miljoen) per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil. The RVO page at https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden states the ceiling and the exception for a lower maximum in the Environmental List (Milieulijst), but it does not print a year, so the value is not confirmed for 2024 specifically. The same page family prints the combined MIA and Vamil benefit as "ruim 14%" of the investment amount on https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, also without a year. What would settle it: a year-stamped RVO page for 2024 or the Environmental List for 2024.
- UNVERIFIED: the rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use the EIA or MIA. It was not found on the fetched pages https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia. What would settle it: the full EIA and MIA conditions text on those pages, or a policy statement on the point.
- Open caveat on the starters arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) cap of € 387.580, UNVERIFIED. The amount is read from the cross-reference in URWA art. 8 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0) to the maximum amount over which KIA can be obtained in the art. 3.41 lid 2 table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). No fetched page prints the cap as a number. What would settle it: a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) or URWA page that prints the maximum in figures.
- No further conflict between sources was found for 2024.
