## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### KIA table (art. 3.41 lid 2)

The KIA is a deduction from the profit, granted per business (onderneming). A taxpayer with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) claims it by choice in the tax return (aangifte), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) carries the same table on its corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) pages, so a BV meets the same instrument. The deduction is calculated on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year, not per asset. The 2024 table has five bands. Nil up to € 2.800 of investment. 28% of the investment from € 2.800 up to € 69.765. A fixed € 19.535 from € 69.765 up to € 129.194. From € 129.194 up to € 387.580 the fixed amount of € 19.535 minus 7,56% of the part above € 129.194. Nil above € 387.580. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

In a partnership (samenwerkingsverband, such as a general partnership (vof) or civil-law partnership (maatschap)) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the outcome is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that each partner (firmant or vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)profit (winst), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently. Sources: [art. 3.41 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

Several assets never earn the KIA. Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for use in a business whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (a treaty for the avoidance of double taxation, regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting), land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering), dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen), passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992, vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies), and animals (dieren). For the KIA only, art. 3.45 lid 2 adds assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties, and assets with an investment amount below € 450. Art. 3.45 lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. Source: [art. 3.45](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

No investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) is granted on obligations between the taxpayer and members of the same household, blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members, heirs to an estate the asset belongs to, or a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam) and that entity. The Minister can disapply this. FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" (the BV of which you are a shareholder for at least one third) gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (in principle no right to investment deduction). Sources: [art. 3.46](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years. At cessation (staking) of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation. This cap applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. Sources: [art. 3.44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### KIA per-asset minimum (€ 450)

Art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b excludes from the KIA business assets whose investment amount stays below € 450. The test is per asset. FISIN 2024 prints the same € 450. Such an asset can still be depreciated under the ordinary rules of art. 3.30, and a low-value item (voorwerp van geringe waarde) that is normally treated as a current cost is written off at once in the year of acquisition. Sources: [art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.30 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### EIA percentage (40%)

The EIA is available to a taxpayer who invests in a new business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) that appears on the Energy List (Energielijst). For 2024 the percentage is 40% of the investment amount. Only new assets ("niet eerder gebruikt") qualify. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as production costs (voortbrengingskosten). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km. The EIA cannot be combined with the MIA for one asset, and it can be combined with the KIA. Where an asset already received EIA, no MIA follows on those costs, although Vamil remains possible. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The per-asset minimum of € 2.500 and the maximum of € 149.000.000 apply, and the investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

One research rule could not be confirmed this year: that a co-entitled party (a person entitled to a share of the profit, medegerechtigde) or a lender (a provider of money, geldverstrekker) cannot use the EIA or MIA. The fetched pages do not state it. It is listed in section 7 as UNVERIFIED. Source of the failed check: [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (€ 149.000.000)

Art. 3.42 lid 4 sets the maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA at € 149.000.000 for 2024, and FISIN 2024 prints the same amount. The checked sources do not state whether this ceiling runs per asset, per year, or over the combined EIA and MIA base, so no calculation here goes near it. A separate ceiling of € 25 million (miljoen) per business asset and per applicant per year is stated by RVO for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum. Sources: [art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### MIA percentages (45% / 36% / 27%)

The MIA applies to a new business asset that appears on the Environmental List, with an investment amount of at least € 2.500 and within the maximum amounts. From 1 January 2022 the Environmental List maps codes starting with F or G to category (categorie) I (45%), codes starting with A or D to category II (36%) and codes starting with B or E to category III (27%). For 2024 there is no MIA change and the three percentages are again 45%, 36% and 27%. The MIA can be combined with the KIA and with the Vamil. The MIA cannot be combined with the EIA for one asset. Art. 3.45 lid 3 provides that for the MIA the exclusions of lid 1 parts (onderdelen) c and d do not apply, so dwellings and passenger cars can still earn MIA. Art. 3.45 lid 4 excludes, for the EIA and MIA, assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises, and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500. Sources: [art. 3.42a lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Milieulijst 2022, in force 1 January 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum (€ 2.500)

Art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b excludes business assets from the EIA and the MIA whose investment amount stays below € 2.500. FISIN 2024 gives the same € 2.500. RVO phrases the floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (at least € 2.500 per application), so the statute ties the amount to the asset and RVO to the application. Sources: [art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### Vamil percentage (75% willekeurig)

Art. 3.31 lid 1 provides that the costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven" (may be depreciated at will for 75 percent by the taxpayer). The rest of the cost follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that have not been used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil. RVO states that the total tax benefit of MIA and Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (can rise net to over 14% of the investment amount), and that page does not state a year. Sources: [art. 3.31 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html), [Belastingdienst vennootschapsbelasting 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023), [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).

### Ordinary depreciation caps (10% and 20%)

Art. 3.30 lid 1 makes the yearly depreciation the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition or production costs that is attributable to the year. Lid 2 caps the yearly depreciation at 10% of the cost for goodwill and at 20% of the cost for other business assets, which means at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for the other assets. Lid 3 allows production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) to be written off at once in the year of production (voortbrenging). Lid 4 writes low-value objects that are normally treated as current costs off at once in the year of acquisition or production. The same text applies in every year 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.30](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Minimum value for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1 allows depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the minimum value, and never for more than the difference. Lid 2 treats the building, its parts, the ground under it and the appurtenances (aanhorigheden) as one business asset, with detachable machinery (werktuigen) as separate assets. Lid 3 gives the 2024 rule: one minimum value, the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) of the building. From 2020 up to and including 2023 the rule was the WOZ value for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building, so the 50% rule for own use is gone from 1 January 2024, except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value until 3 years after first use. Lid 4 takes the WOZ value as the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text of the article also has lid 5 to 10 on co-owned buildings, ground owned by an unrelated party and related persons and a related entity (verbonden lichaam), which the 2024 version as read does not show. Sources: [art. 3.30a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2027 text of the article](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold (€ 2.800)

Art. 3.47 lid 1 adds to the profit, when in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of those transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage that was taken. For 2024 the threshold is € 2.800. FISIN 2024 gives the same amount and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration disinvestment addition page confirms it with "in 2024 was dit € 2.800". Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### Disinvestment addition term (five years)

Art. 3.47 lid 2 limits the disinvestment addition (bijtelling) to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken. Lid 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the business (a), a change of destination that brings the asset under art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, for the KIA) or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, for the EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings the asset under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). Lid 4 counts the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) as the transfer price in those cases. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or MIA, and adds cessation of (part of) the business within the term. Sources: [art. 3.47 lid 2 to 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### Disinvestment addition, investment undone

Art. 3.47 lid 5 treats the undoing of an investment, and the receipt of a reduction (vermindering), refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), as a disposal for that amount. Lid 6 brings under undoing the case that at least 25% of the investment amount has not been paid within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and the case that the asset has not been taken into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6. Source: [art. 3.47 lid 5 to 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Starters arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap (€ 387.580)

The statutory basis is art. 3.34 Wet IB 2001, which allows arbitrary depreciation on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship. URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), starter's allowance) applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits the scheme to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (ondernemer). URWA art. 8 allows the arbitrary depreciation "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. The top of the 2024 KIA table is € 387.580. The checked material marks this cap as UNVERIFIED because it is read from the cross-reference and no fetched page prints the cap as a number, see section 7. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the scheme is open in a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), civil-law partnership, limited partnership (cv) or general partnership, that the starter's deduction conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investment deduction count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Sources: [URWA art. 7 and 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [art. 3.34 and art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

### Starters arbitrary depreciation, revaluation (herwaardering) period (five or ten years)

If the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf) within the term, the asset is revalued to its normal book value and the difference is profit. URWA art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs, and art. 12 sets ten years for the sea-going vessels (zeeschepen) of art. 10. Sources: [URWA art. 9 and 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Belastingdienst page on willekeurige afschrijving for the starting entrepreneur](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil (3 months)

The investment is notified to RVO within 3 months after the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) is signed or the order for acquisition (aanschafkosten) is placed, and within 3 months after the quarter in which production costs were incurred. Environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Environmental List that is valid on the signing or order date applies. FISIN 2020 to 2026 all state "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)." Sources: [RVO MIA and Vamil for entrepreneurs](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### MIA and Vamil maximum per business asset and per applicant per year (€ 25 miljoen)

RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per business asset and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum. The fetched RVO page does not state a year, so the amount carries Status unknown in the figures table and is listed in section 7. Source: [RVO conditions](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).
