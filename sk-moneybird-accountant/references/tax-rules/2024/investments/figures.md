## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) in its four forms, the KIA (small-scale investment deduction, kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), the EIA (energy investment deduction, energie-investeringsaftrek), the MIA (environmental investment deduction, milieu-investeringsaftrek) and the Vamil (arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments, willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen), together with the ordinary and arbitrary depreciation of business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in tax year 2024. It is written for a sole trader and notes where the checked sources describe the same instruments for a BV. The 2024 law is enacted. The headline change for 2024 is the minimum value (bodemwaarde) for buildings: the 50% rule for a building in own use is gone from 1 January 2024 and one minimum value applies, the full WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) of the building, except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value until 3 years after first use.
