## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Income tax return deadline: 2023 before 1 May 2024, with deferral (uitstel) before 1 September 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 before 1 May 2025, with deferral before 1 September 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Final assessment (definitieve aanslag): 2023 at the latest 31 December 2026, or 30 April 2027 with deferral (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 at the latest 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with deferral (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens): 2023 € 51 and € 16 in the higher-than wording, with € 52 and € 17 under the older equal to or more than (gelijk-aan-of-meer-dan) wording (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 € 56 and € 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2023 until 31 December 2028 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). Tax year 2024 until 31 December 2029 (same page).
- Request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering) online: assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). The checked sources do not state the date for assessment 2024, see section 7.
- Tax interest (belastingrente) rate: 4% until 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). 7,5% in 2024, and from 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for the following 1 January (same page).
- Tax interest on a tax year: tax year 2023 at 7,5% from 1 July 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). Tax year 2024 at 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Interest stop (rentestop) for requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen): applies from 1 January 2023, ends the interest (rente) at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, and is still not in force for VAT (btw) in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).
- Income tax penalties (boetes): 2023 default fine (verzuimboete) € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). 2024 the same amounts (https://web.archive.org/web/20240301/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).
- VAT default fine: € 68 in 2023 and € 68 in 2024, so no change. The € 82 applies from 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20240225/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).
- VAT monthly dates: the December 2022 period was due 31 January 2023 and the December 2023 period 31 January 2024. The January 2023 period was due 28 February 2023, the January 2024 period 29 February 2024 (2024 is a leap year). The other monthly dates follow the same last-day-of-the-next-month pattern (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).
- VAT quarterly dates: 2023 fourth quarter 2022 on 31 January 2023, first quarter 30 April 2023, second quarter 31 July 2023, third quarter 31 October 2023, fourth quarter 31 January 2024. 2024 fourth quarter 2023 on 31 January 2024, first quarter 30 April 2024, second quarter 31 July 2024, third quarter 31 October 2024, fourth quarter 31 January 2025 (same two archived pages).
- VAT yearly return: 2023 by 31 March 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte). 2024 by 31 March 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).
- Payment terms: the checked material states the same Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, so no change is recorded for 2024. The checked material gives no 2023 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01).
- Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and the KVK registration window: the checked material gives no 2023 values for these items, so no change can be reported.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Register the business (onderneming) with KVK inside the two-week window, one week before to one week after the start of the business. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), so no separate registration is needed. Record the start date, the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) and the legal entity identifier (RSIN) where the form is not a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- Keep the records (administratie) as basic records (basisgegevens) for 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data for 10 years, with the term starting when the item (gegeven) is no longer current (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Record the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) and its date. The 2024 income tax return is due before 1 May 2025, or before 1 September 2025 with deferral (uitstel), and deferral must be requested before the filing date (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Record the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) payments and refunds. They are settled in the 2024 return. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment before year end count toward the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens), a paid provisional assessment and a refund received after filing do not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- File the VAT (btw) return and pay together by the dates in section 2. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- Record the date of each assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). Payment is due 6 weeks after that date for an assessment (aanslag), and objection (bezwaar) must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).
- Book tax interest (belastingrente) when the 2024 income tax return is late or the assessment deviates from the return. It runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026. The rate during 2024 itself was 7,5% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Note the VAT interest (rente) start of 1 January after the tax year, and the 3-month window with no interest for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Online end date of the request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering) for an assessment (aanslag) 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is fewer than 5 years after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). The stated examples are assessment 2020 until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028 (same page). Applying the rule to 2024 lands on 31 December 2029, which is also the stated final date for filing the 2024 return without an invitation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) statement for the 2024 assessment year would settle it.
- End date of the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) for tax year 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is 5 years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by deferral (uitstel) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The stated example is tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example for tax year 2024 would settle it.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) wording. The current page uses higher than (hoger dan) with € 56 and € 18 for 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Captures up to 2023 used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold (grens) one euro higher, such as € 52 and € 17 for 2023 in the April 2023 capture (https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The checked material marks the equivalence of the two wordings for whole-euro amounts as its own reading, not as a statement from the source. Two of the three standing examples on the live page also print a € 20 assessment, an amount below the 2024 assessment threshold of € 56, and the material says the examples are unchanged since the 2023 capture. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration explanation of the current wording, or updated examples, would settle it.
- Deferral channel, settled. The form page states that online deferral in My Tax and Customs Administration (mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May gives deferral to 1 September and is only for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The deferral page states that online is not possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Read in full, the second page covers the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Settled reading: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May filing date, paper form otherwise.
- KVK and VAT number term, settled. One wording is two weeks, the other ten working days (werkdagen) (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). Ten working days equals two calendar weeks and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states registration takes at most 10 working days, so the two wordings agree.
- Carry-over of a late change to the 2024 provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) is not stated. The checked page states that a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). It does not give the corresponding rule for a change to the 2024 provisional assessment. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration statement for 2024 would settle it.
- No official tax interest (belastingrente) example for tax year 2024 is printed in the checked material. The examples at 7,5% are listed under 2023 because they concern the 2023 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). Section 4 therefore uses calculated examples for 2024.
