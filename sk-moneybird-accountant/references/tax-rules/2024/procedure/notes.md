## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Income tax return deadline: 2023 before 1 May 2024, with uitstel before 1 September 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 before 1 May 2025, with uitstel before 1 September 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Definitive aanslag: 2023 at the latest 31 December 2026, or 30 April 2027 with uitstel (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 at the latest 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with uitstel (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens: 2023 € 51 and € 16 in the higher-than wording, with € 52 and € 17 under the older gelijk-aan-of-meer-dan wording (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). 2024 € 56 and € 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2023 until 31 December 2028 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). Tax year 2024 until 31 December 2029 (same page).
- Verzoek om vermindering online: aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). The checked sources do not state the date for aanslag 2024, see section 7.
- Belastingrente rate: 4% until 30 June 2023 and 6% from 1 July 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). 7,5% in 2024, and from 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for the following 1 January (same page).
- Belastingrente on a tax year: tax year 2023 at 7,5% from 1 July 2024 and 6,5% from 1 January 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). Tax year 2024 at 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Rentestop for requested naheffingsaanslagen: applies from 1 January 2023, ends the rente at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, and is still not in force for btw in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).
- Income tax boetes: 2023 verzuimboete € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, betaalverzuimboete up to € 5.514 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230403/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). 2024 the same amounts (https://web.archive.org/web/20240301/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).
- Btw verzuimboete: € 68 in 2023 and € 68 in 2024, so no change. The € 82 applies from 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20240225/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).
- Btw monthly dates: the December 2022 period was due 31 January 2023 and the December 2023 period 31 January 2024. The January 2023 period was due 28 February 2023, the January 2024 period 29 February 2024 (2024 is a leap year). The other monthly dates follow the same last-day-of-the-next-month pattern (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).
- Btw quarterly dates: 2023 fourth quarter 2022 on 31 January 2023, first quarter 30 April 2023, second quarter 31 July 2023, third quarter 31 October 2023, fourth quarter 31 January 2024. 2024 fourth quarter 2023 on 31 January 2024, first quarter 30 April 2024, second quarter 31 July 2024, third quarter 31 October 2024, fourth quarter 31 January 2025 (same two archived pages).
- Btw yearly return: 2023 by 31 March 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20230127/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte). 2024 by 31 March 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).
- Payment terms: the checked material states the same Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, so no change is recorded for 2024. The checked material gives no 2023 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01).
- Bewaarplicht and the KVK registration window: the checked material gives no 2023 values for these items, so no change can be reported.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Register the onderneming with KVK inside the two-week window, one week before to one week after the start of the business. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. Record the start date, the KVK-nummer and the RSIN where the form is not an eenmanszaak (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- Keep the administratie as basisgegevens for 7 years, onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data for 10 years, with the term starting when the gegeven is no longer actual (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Record the aangiftebrief and its date. The 2024 income tax return is due before 1 May 2025, or before 1 September 2025 with uitstel, and uitstel must be requested before the filing date (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Record the voorlopige aanslag payments and refunds. They are settled in the 2024 return. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens, a paid voorlopige aanslag and a refund received after filing do not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- File the btw return and pay together by the dates in section 2. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- Record the date of each aanslagbiljet. Payment is due 6 weeks after that date for an aanslag, and bezwaar must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).
- Book belastingrente when the 2024 income tax return is late or the aanslag deviates from the return. It runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026. The rate during 2024 itself was 7,5% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Note the btw rente start of 1 January after the tax year, and the 3-month window with no rente for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Online end date of the verzoek om vermindering for an aanslag 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is fewer than 5 years after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). The stated examples are aanslag 2020 until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028 (same page). Applying the rule to 2024 lands on 31 December 2029, which is also the stated final date for filing the 2024 return without an invitation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). A Belastingdienst statement for the 2024 assessment year would settle it.
- End date of the navorderingstermijn for tax year 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The stated example is tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). A Belastingdienst example for tax year 2024 would settle it.
- Aanslaggrens wording. The current page uses hoger dan with € 56 and € 18 for 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Captures up to 2023 used gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, such as € 52 and € 17 for 2023 in the April 2023 capture (https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The checked material marks the equivalence of the two wordings for whole-euro amounts as its own reading, not as a statement from the source. Two of the three standing examples on the live page also print a € 20 aanslag, an amount below the 2024 aanslaggrens of € 56, and the material says the examples are unchanged since the 2023 capture. A Belastingdienst explanation of the current wording, or updated examples, would settle it.
- Uitstel channel, settled. The form page states that online uitstel in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May gives uitstel to 1 September and is only for someone who received an aangiftebrief (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The uitstel page states that online is not possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Read in full, the second page covers the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Settled reading: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May filing date, paper form otherwise.
- KVK and btw-number term, settled. One wording is two weeks, the other ten werkdagen (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). Ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks and the Belastingdienst states registration takes at most 10 werkdagen, so the two wordings agree.
- Carry-over of a late change to the 2024 voorlopige aanslag is not stated. The checked page states that a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). It does not give the corresponding rule for a change to the 2024 voorlopige aanslag. A Belastingdienst statement for 2024 would settle it.
- No official belastingrente example for tax year 2024 is printed in the checked material. The examples at 7,5% are listed under 2023 because they concern the 2023 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). Section 4 therefore uses calculated examples for 2024.
