## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Official example: the final assessment (aanslag) for tax year 2024

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) example for the 2024 return: filing before 1 May 2025 gives a final assessment at the latest on 31 December 2027. With deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2025 the latest date is 30 April 2028 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Example 2. Official example: what counts toward the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens)

The final-assessment page prints three standing examples (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag):

1. A provisional (voorlopige) refund (teruggaaf) of € 100 was received and € 80 was due, so € 20 too much was received. That € 20 is below the threshold (grens) and the outcome is a nil assessment (nihilaanslag).
2. A provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) of € 100 was paid and € 120 was due, so € 20 remains and the outcome is a € 20 assessment.
3. A refund of € 100 was received after filing and € 80 was due, so the outcome is a € 20 to be paid (te betalen) assessment, because a refund received after filing does not count toward the threshold.

Calculated check against the 2024 threshold. Step 1: the assessment threshold for 2024 is € 56 (section 2). Step 2: the amount in the examples is € 20. Step 3: € 20 is lower than € 56, so € 20 is not higher than the assessment threshold and the higher-than test is not met. The checked material notes that the examples are unchanged since the 2023 capture and does not reconcile examples 2 and 3 with the 2024 threshold of € 56, see section 7.

### Example 3. Calculated example: tax interest (belastingrente) on a 2024 return filed after 1 May, assessment unchanged

Inputs: the 2024 return is filed on 3 May 2025. The assessment is dated 4 December 2025 and follows the return unchanged. The amount to pay is € 1.000.

Step 1: interest (rente) is charged because the return arrives on or after 1 May 2025 (section 3.13).
Step 2: the interest period starts on 1 July 2025.
Step 3: the end is the earlier of 6 weeks after the assessment date and 19 weeks after receipt of the return. 4 December 2025 plus 42 days (6 weeks) = 15 January 2026. 3 May 2025 plus 133 days (19 weeks) = 13 September 2025. The earlier date is 13 September 2025.
Step 4: days from 1 July 2025 to 13 September 2025 in the 30/360 count: 30 days for July plus 30 days for August plus 13 days for September = 73 days.
Step 5: the rate on tax year 2024 is 6,5% from 1 July 2025.
Step 6: 73 / 360 × 6,5% × € 1.000 = 0,2027778 × € 65 = € 13,18, rounded down to whole euros = € 13.

The tax interest is € 13.

### Example 4. Calculated example: tax interest where the assessment deviates, across the rate change

Inputs: the 2024 return is filed on 28 April 2025 with too low an income. An extra assessment of € 2.500 is dated 11 December 2025.

Step 1: interest is charged because the assessment deviates from the return, even though the return was filed before 1 May.
Step 2: the period runs from 1 July 2025 to 6 weeks after the assessment date. 11 December 2025 plus 42 days = 22 January 2026.
Step 3: days: 1 July 2025 to 1 January 2026 is six months = 180 days, plus 22 days in January = 202 days.
Step 4: the 2025 part runs at 6,5%. 180 / 360 × 6,5% × € 2.500 = 0,5 × € 162,50 = € 81,25, rounded down = € 81.
Step 5: the 2026 part runs at 5%. 22 / 360 × 5% × € 2.500 = 0,0611111 × € 125 = € 7,64, rounded down = € 7.
Step 6: total € 81 + € 7 = € 88.

### Example 5. Official example and calculated example: additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn)

Official example: tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, additional assessment (navordering) possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

Calculated example for 2024, following that pattern. Step 1: take the tax debt (belastingschuld) at the end of the tax year, 31 December 2024. Step 2: 5 years after that date is 31 December 2029. Step 3: the deferral for the 2024 return runs from 1 May 2025 to 1 September 2025, a duration of 4 months, and the term is extended by the duration of the deferral. Step 4: 31 December 2029 plus 4 months = 30 April 2030. The checked material does not state an end date for tax year 2024, see section 7.

### Example 6. Calculated example: payment term (termijn) and provisional assessment instalments (termijnen)

Inputs: an assessment for tax year 2024 dated 15 September 2025 with € 3.000 to pay, an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) dated 15 September 2025, and a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) dated 15 September 2025.

- Assessment: payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). 15 September 2025 plus 42 days = 27 October 2025.
- Additional assessment: 1 month after the date = 15 October 2025.
- Supplementary assessment: 14 days after the date = 29 September 2025.

Calculated example for a provisional assessment. A provisional assessment for 2024 is dated 15 March 2024 and the amount is € 2.700. Step 1: month of the date = March 2024. Step 2: months remaining after March in 2024 = April to December = 9. Step 3: 9 equal monthly instalments of € 2.700 / 9 = € 300 each. Step 4: the first term is one month after the date = 15 April 2024, the last = 15 December 2024.

### Example 7. Calculated example: objection (bezwaar) timeline on the same assessment

Inputs: the assessment for tax year 2024 is dated 15 September 2025.

Step 1: objection must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment. 15 September 2025 plus 42 days = 27 October 2025.
Step 2: if the objection is filed on 27 October 2025, acknowledgement follows within 3 weeks. 27 October 2025 plus 21 days = 17 November 2025.
Step 3: the payment of the same assessment is due 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice, the same 27 October 2025 (section 3.9).

### Example 8. Calculated example: VAT (btw) quarterly deadline and default fine (verzuimboete)

Inputs: a quarterly filer with the period January to March 2024 (first quarter 2024). The return and the payment are both due 30 April 2024 (section 2).

- Variant A: the return is received on 1 May 2024. That is 1 calendar day (kalenderdag) after the deadline and inside the 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) of leniency (coulance), so no default fine.
- Variant B: the return is received on 10 May 2024. That is 10 calendar days after the deadline and outside the 7 calendar days of leniency, so the default fine is € 68.

The VAT interest period for late payment starts on 1 January after the tax year, so a payment on 10 May 2024 lies before that start and no interest period runs.

### Example 9. Calculated example: filing without an invitation and request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering) for 2024

Filing without an invitation. Step 1: a return for tax year 2024 can be filed up to 5 years back. Step 2: 2024 plus 5 years = 2029. Step 3: the final date is 31 December 2029 (stated in the checked material, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).

Request for reduction. Step 1: the checked rule is online if fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. Step 2: the stated example is assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Step 3: applying the same rule to assessment 2024 gives 2024 plus 5 = 2029, so 31 December 2029. The checked sources do not print this 2024 date, see section 7.

### Example 10. Calculated example: KVK registration window

Inputs: the business starts on 1 May 2024. Step 1: the two-week window starts one week before the start. 1 May 2024 minus 7 days = 24 April 2024. Step 2: the window ends one week after the start. 1 May 2024 plus 7 days = 8 May 2024. So the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) is made between 24 April 2024 and 8 May 2024. Registering inside the window means the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) is issued immediately. Other required statements (opgaven) are made within 1 week after the fact, so a change on 1 May 2024 is reported by 8 May 2024.

### Example 11. Official example: record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) term start

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example is a 4-year lease. The item (gegeven) is no longer current after those 4 years, so it is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Step 1: 4 + 7 = 11 years in total (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
