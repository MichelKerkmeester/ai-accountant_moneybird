## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Income tax return 2024, filing deadline

The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). That date is often 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). For tax year 2024 the deadline is 1 May 2025 and deferral (uitstel) could be requested until 1 May 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). AWR art. 9 lid 1 states that for taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the inspector (inspecteur) of at least one month after the invitation, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions. The checked material finds the same wording in the 1 January 2020 text and the 1 January 2026 text of the AWR (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### 3.2 Income tax return 2024 with deferral

A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, so the 2024 return is due before 1 September 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.3 Deferral request

Deferral can be requested online in My Tax and Customs Administration (mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May, giving deferral to 1 September, and this online route is for someone who received a filing invitation letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The deferral page states that online is not possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Read in full, that second page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both texts are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text, so the reading is: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May filing date, paper form otherwise.

### 3.4 Filing period opens

The filing period opens on 1 March (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). A return can be filed from that date. The checked material gives no different opening date for tax year 2024.

### 3.5 Final assessment (definitieve aanslag) 2024 latest

AWR art. 11 lid 3 says the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For tax year 2024 the latest date is 31 December 2027, and with 4 months deferral 30 April 2028 (same page).

### 3.6 Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2024

The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For 2024 these amounts are € 56 and € 18 (same page). Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before year end count toward the threshold (grens), a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not (same page and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Pages up to the 2023 captures used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold one euro higher. The capture of 28 March 2025 prints € 47 and € 15 for 2020, € 48 and € 15 for 2021 and 2022, and € 51 and € 16 for 2023 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For whole-euro amounts the two wordings describe the same threshold, which the checked material marks as its own reading.

### 3.7 Filing the 2024 return without an invitation

Without a filing invitation letter a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). For tax year 2024 that is until 31 December 2029 (same page).

### 3.8 Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn)

AWR art. 16 lid 1 allows an additional assessment (navordering) on a new fact. The fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2 also allows additional assessment when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional (voorlopige) refund (teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3 sets the term at 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral, and for an error-based additional assessment only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4 sets 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months when in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example is tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, where additional assessment is possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). The checked material does not state an end date for tax year 2024, see section 7.

### 3.9 Payment term (termijn) of an assessment

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 carries the same text on 1 January 2020, 1 January 2024, 1 January 2025 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01). An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after (same article).

### 3.10 Payment instalments (termijnen) of a provisional assessment

A provisional assessment for the income tax dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments as months remain after the month of the date, and the first term is one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date (Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01).

### 3.11 Provisional assessment 2024, request and change

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, a higher or lower income, a change in business profit, and stopping the business, which produces profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after filing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). That page states that a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. It does not state the corresponding rule for a change to the 2024 provisional assessment, see section 7.

### 3.12 Tax interest rate 2024

For 2024 the rate for all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen) is 7,5% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). The rate history in the checked material runs from 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 (for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) the reduction started 1 July 2020), 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (same page). From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. For Vpb, bronbelasting, minimum tax (minimumbelasting) and solidarity contribution (solidariteitsbijdrage) the addition is 5,5 points, minimum 5,5% (same page). After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. A mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT (btw) and the other taxes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). That page gives no date for the collective decisions, its update date is 25 February 2026.

### 3.13 Tax interest on tax year 2024 income tax

Interest (rente) is charged when the 2024 return arrives on or after 1 May 2025, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). The period runs from 1 July 2025, also when deferral was granted (same page). It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return (same page). For an additional assessment the period runs from 1 July 2025 to 1 month after its date (same page). No interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged (same page). On tax year 2024 the rate is 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### 3.14 Tax interest income tax, day count

The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.15 Tax interest on VAT

Interest is charged on a supplementary assessment and on late payment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting). For late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen), which ends the interest at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible (same page). The 7,5% rate of 2024 is the rate for VAT in that year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### 3.16 Objection period (bezwaartermijn)

Objection (bezwaar) must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar). The motivation can follow later if the objection asks for that. Acknowledgement follows within 3 weeks. There is no objection against a provisional assessment for the income tax, only a change (same page). After the objection term an ex officio request for reduction (verzoek om ambtshalve vermindering) is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.17 Request for reduction

After the objection term an ex officio request for reduction (verzoek om ambtshalve vermindering) is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). Online is possible only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The checked material gives assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request goes by letter. For an assessment 2024 the checked sources do not print the online end date, see section 7.

### 3.18 Income tax penalties (boetes) 2024

After the deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine (verzuimboete). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. No return at all gives an ex officio assessment plus default fine. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment. No penalty is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. The time limit for requesting an invitation to file a return is 6 months after the tax debt arose for the income tax and Vpb, with a leniency (coulance) of 2 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). For 2020 to 2024 the amounts are default fine income tax € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 2.757, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 5.514. The penalty is 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), box 3 75% and 150%, aggravated 100% and 300% (https://web.archive.org/web/20240301/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### 3.19 VAT default fine 2024

For a VAT tax return (aangifte) there are 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) of leniency, then a penalty of € 68 in 2020 to 2024 or € 82 in 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte). For 2024 the amount is € 68 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240225/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).

### 3.20 VAT monthly dates 2024

AWR art. 10 lid 2 says a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1 says the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, and return and payment must both be received by that date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums). The monthly dates for 2024 are: December 2023 period 31 January 2024, January period 29 February 2024, February period 31 March 2024, March period 30 April 2024, April period 31 May 2024, May period 30 June 2024, June period 31 July 2024, July period 31 August 2024, August period 30 September 2024, September period 31 October 2024, October period 30 November 2024, November period 31 December 2024, December 2024 period 31 January 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.21 VAT quarterly dates 2024

The quarterly dates for 2024 are: fourth quarter 2023 on 31 January 2024, first quarter 30 April 2024, second quarter 31 July 2024, third quarter 31 October 2024, fourth quarter 31 January 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.22 VAT yearly return 2024

A yearly return for 2024 is due by 31 March 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.23 VAT submission window

Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### 3.24 Deferral for a VAT return

Deferral for a VAT return is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit), and must be requested in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment and a penalty remain possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte).

### 3.25 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4 says persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records are the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), the payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### 3.26 KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 says the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required statements (opgaven) are made within one week after the fact (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately when the registration is made within that window, an identity check is possible up to three months ahead, and appointments up to eight weeks ahead (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other forms get a legal entity identifier (RSIN), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Start-up notification (opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister)) is used (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
