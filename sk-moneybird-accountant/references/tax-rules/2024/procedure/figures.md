## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2024 for a sole trader, the filing deadlines and deferral (uitstel) for income tax and VAT (btw), the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar) and request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht), and the KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration duties. All items for 2024 in the checked material are enacted. The 2024 income tax return is filed in 2025, with 1 May 2025 as the deadline and 1 September 2025 with deferral, and the definitive assessment (aanslag) can be set until 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with deferral. The tax interest rate during 2024 is 7,5%, the first year in which the rate is set once a year, and interest (rente) on the 2024 income tax runs at 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026.
