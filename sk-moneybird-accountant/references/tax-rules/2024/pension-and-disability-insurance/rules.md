## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Jaarruimte percentage

Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year deduct lijfrentepremies up to 30% of the premiegrondslag, reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2024-01-01 text ties the percentage to art. 18a lid 1 Wet LB 1964 through art. 10.2b lid 3. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) The Belastingdienst Wtp page states that the 30% applies with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij Up to 2022 the outcome was also reduced by dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve. From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies Premiums count in the year they are paid or verrekend (art. 3.130 lid 1). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130)

### Maximum jaarruimte

The maximum jaarruimte for 2024 is € 36.077. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf The figure is confirmed by the Ministry tables. The 2024 open points state that no open point remains for this maximum. The maximum is the top of the jaarruimte formula and applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

Art. 3.127 lid 3 counts the income components up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (aftoppingsgrens, cap) of € 137.800. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 In the 2024 to 2027 texts art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) The 2024 amount is € 137.800 in the 2024-01-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

### Franchise premiegrondslag

Art. 3.127 lid 3 reduces the counted income by the laatstvermelde bedrag (franchise, threshold) of € 17.545. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 In the 2024 text art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the AOW benefit for married persons without toeslag plus vakantieuitkering x 100/75, which explains the jump from 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) A taxpayer with no pension accrual uses percentage x (min(income of previous year, aftoppingsgrens) minus franchise). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

### Premiegrondslag composition

Art. 3.127 lid 3 uses the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) winst uit onderneming vóór de ondernemersaftrek (from the 2023-07-01 text), (b) belastbaar loon, (c) belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden and (d) belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen, counted up to the eerstvermelde bedrag and reduced by the laatstvermelde bedrag. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The texts 2020 to 2023-01-01 also subtracted toevoeging aan and afneming van de oudedagsreserve in part (a). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 Lid 5 gives a staking option. On (partial) staking the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 set the premiegrondslag on the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, also vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### Pension accrual reduction

From the 2024-01-01 text art. 3.127 lid 4 subtracts the premiums ingelegd in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen and partnerpensioen, excluding a compensatiepremie under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2020 to 2023-07-01 texts used 6,27 keer the aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2024 wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25) The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964 (old norms), and that also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25) For the jaarruimte 2024 the Belastingdienst hulpmiddel assumes a pension scheme that existed on 30 June 2023 and was not yet adapted to the Wtp. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/hulpmiddel-lijfrentepremie-2016-en-daarna

### Transitional formula art. 10a.25 lid 2

Art. 10a.25 lid 2 gives the formula for a fixed premium per dienstjaar under art. 38r Wet LB 1964, A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the nettopensioen premium divided by the nettofactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25) The formula was amended by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023. The older formula was A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B. The checked material gives the formula but does not define X, Y and B. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)

### FOR transition in the jaarruimte

Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 apply for 2023 and 2024, the last year being 2024. For a taxpayer with a FOR the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, also vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek. For 2023 and 2024 the jaarruimte is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the year-end afneming. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) For the 2024 jaarruimte the previous year is 2023 and from 1 January 2023 no FOR toevoeging is possible, so the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the year-end afneming cannot be positive. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve An existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### Look-back window

Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows unused jaarruimte of the preceding period to be used on request in the aangifte, oldest unused year first. The period is 10 years from the 2023-07-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The FISIN 2024 window is the jaarruimte 2014 to 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The Belastingdienst page advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse, and says unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Reserveringsruimte maximum

Art. 3.127 lid 2 sets one flat maximum of € 41.608 for the unused jaarruimte that may be used in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 From the 2023-07-01 text there is no percentage and no age split. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2020 to 2022 texts had at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount, raised for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 FISIN 2024 and the Ministry tables agree with € 41.608. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Age limit jaarruimte

Art. 3.127 in the 2024-01-01 text requires that at the start of the calendar year the taxpayer has not yet reached the age five years above the AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 FISIN 2023 to 2026 say "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The 2020 to 2022 texts and the 2023-01-01 text required that the taxpayer had not yet reached the AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The Belastingdienst Wtp page states that lijfrente premiums may be paid up to 5 years after AOW age. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

### Lijfrente products and uitkering conditions

Three forms exist, the lijfrenteverzekering, the lijfrenterekening and the lijfrentebeleggingsrecht. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies An oudedagslijfrente (art. 3.125 lid 1 a) must have its first termijn at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, with payments until death. FISIN gives the example of AOW age in 2030 and a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen For a rekening or beleggingsrecht the term is at least 20 years, plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year. Starting at 63 with AOW age 67 means 24 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen A nabestaandenlijfrente (art. 3.125 lid 1 b) for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, for a rekening or beleggingsrecht at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.125) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen A lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind is deductible without a pensioentekort (art. 3.124 lid 1 b). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.124) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c allows a tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente with at least five years of termijnen, starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125) For 2024 the cap is € 26.463 per year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125) FISIN adds that above the cap premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

An overbruggingslijfrente is only for lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age or starting a pensioen. The yearly maximum is € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at € 559.483 for tier a minus already built provisions. The already built provisions are the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129) Tier a applies to a taxpayer whose age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or who is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt with the lijfrente starting within six months, or where the staking is by death. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf For 2024 follow the law age wording. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129) A payment before 1 July 2025 may on choice be counted in 2024 (art. 3.130 lid 2). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

Tier b has a maximum of € 279.752 minus the already built provisions. It applies when the taxpayer's age at staking is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or when the lijfrente starts immediately. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) The deduction is still capped at the stakingswinst. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

Tier c covers other cases and has a maximum of € 139.885 minus the already built provisions. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) The same provisions list and the same stakingswinst cap apply. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows afkoop of a small lijfrente in one payment below a yearly amount. For 2024 that amount is € 5.364. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133) If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions (gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop, deblokkering) the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente is due. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes afkoop not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer or gerechtigde is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The 2024 cap is € 50.063. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133) The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133)

### Lijfrente einddatum termijn

A lijfrente that reaches its einddatum must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2024 gives the official examples. Einddatum 2023 means by 31 December 2024 and einddatum 2024 by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Extension on request is possible in bijzondere omstandigheden. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Nabestaandenlijfrente termijn

A nabestaandenlijfrente must start within 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2023 onward writes it as "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", with the same outcome. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Official example. Death in 2023 means start by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Extension on request is possible in bijzondere omstandigheden. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Negatieve uitgaven reversal window

Refunds of a deducted premium are negatieve uitgaven without revisierente, for a lijfrente only if the refund is undone within 30 days. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Payment deadline FOR or stakingswinst conversion

Premiums count in the year they are paid or verrekend (art. 3.130 lid 1). Premiums for the stakingswinst conversion (art. 3.129), the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29) For 2024 the deadline is before 1 July 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The FISIN 2024 stakingswinst line still says "Zet u vóór 1 juli 2024 de stakingswinst om" next to a payment by 1 July 2025, a page inconsistency. The law (art. 3.130 lid 2) gives six months after the year end. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130)

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen, with no ruimte cap in the article. FISIN says "volledig aftrekken". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for loonheffing, mandatory Ziektewet and WIA premiums, policies paying a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen, and Zvw premiums. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers As ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers The rule of thumb on the page is that premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers The rule of thumb applies. Premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### Mandatory beroepspensioen premium for ondernemers

A mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming with a restriction. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies This differs from the AOV premium, which is an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and not a business cost. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### Sectoraal pensioenfonds for zelfstandigen

A self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible. The proef runs until 1 July 2028. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimenteerbepaling zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.26)

### FOR toevoeging

Access to a FOR toevoeging requires the urencriterium (hours test) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year. That is art. 3.67. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.67) Art. 3.68 sets the toevoeging at 9,44% of the winst, capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst, and not above the growth of ondernemingsvermogen. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.68) Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows afname by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over ondernemingsvermogen at staking, AOW age at the start of the year, or failing the urencriterium this year and last year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.70) From 2023 no toevoeging is possible. An existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) The FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023, up to the afname under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.128) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve The FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions count in the stakingswinst provisions (art. 10a.29 lid 13). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### BAZ consultation premium

The 11 June 2024 consultation proposed a premium of about 6,5% of winst, up to about € 195 per month. The € 195 is based on the 2024 minimumloon. Status proposed. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie This design was later replaced, see 2025. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie

### BAZ consultation design

The 11 June 2024 consultation proposed a wachttijd of 1 year, a benefit of 70% of winst up to the minimumloon, until AOW age, and an opt-out with minimum conditions.
