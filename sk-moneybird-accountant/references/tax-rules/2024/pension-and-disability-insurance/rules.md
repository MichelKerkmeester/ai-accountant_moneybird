## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to 30% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2024-01-01 text ties the percentage to art. 18a lid 1 Wet LB 1964 through art. 10.2b lid 3. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page states that the 30% applies with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) from 1 January 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij Up to 2022 the outcome was also reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve). From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 Premiums above the allowance (ruimte) are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed. The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies Premiums count in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend) (art. 3.130 lid 1). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130)

### Maximum annual allowance

The maximum annual allowance for 2024 is € 36.077. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf The figure is confirmed by the Ministry tables. The 2024 open points state that no open point remains for this maximum. The maximum is the top of the annual allowance formula and applies to all annuity products together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Income cap on the contribution base (aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag)

Art. 3.127 lid 3 counts the income components up to the first-mentioned amount, the income cap (aftoppingsgrens), of € 137.800. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 In the 2024 to 2027 texts art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes the income cap with the contract wage development factor (contractloonontwikkelingsfactor). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) The 2024 amount is € 137.800 in the 2024-01-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

### Franchise on the contribution base (franchise premiegrondslag)

Art. 3.127 lid 3 reduces the counted income by the last-mentioned amount (laatstvermelde bedrag), the franchise or threshold, of € 17.545. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 In the 2024 text art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the state pension (AOW) benefit for married persons without allowance (toeslag) plus holiday allowance (vakantieuitkering) x 100/75, which explains the jump from 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b) A taxpayer with no pension accrual uses percentage x (min(income of previous year, income cap) minus franchise). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

### Contribution base composition

Art. 3.127 lid 3 uses the sum over the previous calendar year of (a) profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) (from the 2023-07-01 text), (b) taxable wage (belastbaar loon), (c) taxable income from other work (belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and (d) taxable periodic payments and benefits in kind (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) (in natura), counted up to the first-mentioned amount (eerstvermelde bedrag) and reduced by the last-mentioned amount (laatstvermelde bedrag). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The texts 2020 to 2023-01-01 also subtracted additions to and reductions of the old-age reserve (toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve) in part (a). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 Lid 5 gives a cessation (staking) option. On (partial) cessation the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 For a taxpayer with a FOR, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 set the contribution base on the profit (winst) before a reduction of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, also before an addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### Pension accrual reduction

From the 2024-01-01 text art. 3.127 lid 4 subtracts the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2020 to 2023-07-01 texts used 6,27 times the growth of the annual pension entitlement (aangroei van het jaarlijkse pensioenrecht) from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2024 wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25) The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Art. 10a.25 lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964 (old norms), and that also applies to accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25) For the annual allowance 2024 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aid (hulpmiddel) assumes a pension scheme that existed on 30 June 2023 and was not yet adapted to the Wtp. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/hulpmiddel-lijfrentepremie-2016-en-daarna

### Transitional formula art. 10a.25 lid 2

Art. 10a.25 lid 2 gives the formula for a fixed premium per year of service (dienstjaar) under art. 38r Wet LB 1964, A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the net pension premium divided by the net factor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25) The formula was amended by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023. The older formula was A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B. The checked material gives the formula but does not define X, Y and B. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)

### FOR transition in the annual allowance

Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 apply for 2023 and 2024, the last year being 2024. For a taxpayer with a FOR the contribution base uses the profit before a reduction of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, also before an addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance. For 2023 and 2024 the annual allowance is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the reduction at the end of the year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) For the 2024 annual allowance the previous year is 2023 and from 1 January 2023 no FOR addition is possible, so the excess of the previous year's addition over the reduction at the end of the year cannot be positive. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve An existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### Look-back window

Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows unused annual allowance of the preceding period to be used on request in the tax return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. The period is 10 years from the 2023-07-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The FISIN 2024 window is the annual allowance 2014 to 2023. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page advises using catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) before annual allowance so older unused allowance does not lapse, and says unused 2016 allowance lapses in 2027. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Catch-up allowance maximum

Art. 3.127 lid 2 sets one flat maximum of € 41.608 for the unused annual allowance that may be used in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 From the 2023-07-01 text there is no percentage and no age split. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The 2020 to 2022 texts had at most 17% of the contribution base and at most a euro amount, raised for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 FISIN 2024 and the Ministry tables agree with € 41.608. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Age limit annual allowance

Art. 3.127 in the 2024-01-01 text requires that at the start of the calendar year the taxpayer has not yet reached the age five years above the AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 FISIN 2023 to 2026 say "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The 2020 to 2022 texts and the 2023-01-01 text required that the taxpayer had not yet reached the AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration Wtp page states that annuity (lijfrente) premiums may be paid up to 5 years after AOW age. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

### Annuity products and benefit conditions

Three forms exist, the annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), the annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) and the annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies An old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) (art. 3.125 lid 1 a) must have its first instalment (termijn) at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, with payments until death. FISIN gives the example of AOW age in 2030 and a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen For an account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) the term is at least 20 years, plus the number of years the benefit starts before the AOW year. Starting at 63 with AOW age 67 means 24 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen A survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) (art. 3.125 lid 1 b) for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, for an account or investment right at least 20 years, or for children under 30 at least 5 years. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.125) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen An annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind) is deductible without a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) (art. 3.124 lid 1 b). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.124) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The maximum applies to all annuity products together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c allows a temporary old-age annuity with at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125) For 2024 the cap is € 26.463 per year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125) FISIN adds that above the cap premiums are deductible only if the benefits (uitkeringen) run at least 20 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

A bridge annuity is only for annuity balances (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age or starting a pension (pensioen). The yearly maximum is € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at € 559.483 for tier a minus already built provisions. The already built provisions are the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129) Tier a applies to a taxpayer whose age at cessation is at most five years below the AOW age, or who is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity starting within six months, or where the cessation is by death. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf For 2024 follow the law age wording. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129) A payment before 1 July 2025 may on choice be counted in 2024 (art. 3.130 lid 2). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

Tier b has a maximum of € 279.752 minus the already built provisions. It applies when the taxpayer's age at cessation is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or when the annuity starts immediately. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) The deduction is still capped at the profit from business discontinuation. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

Tier c covers other cases and has a maximum of € 139.885 minus the already built provisions. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) The same provisions list and the same profit from business discontinuation cap apply. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Commutation of a small annuity (afkoop kleine lijfrente)

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows commutation of a small annuity in one payment below a yearly amount. For 2024 that amount is € 5.364. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133) If an annuity stops meeting the conditions (gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation (afkoop), unblocking (deblokkering)) the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Commutation cap for long-term incapacity for work (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Art. 3.133 lid 9 makes commutation not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term incapacitated for work (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The 2024 cap is € 50.063. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133) The commutation then counts as an annuity instalment. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133)

### Annuity end date (einddatum) term

An annuity that reaches its end date must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2024 gives the official examples. End date 2023 means by 31 December 2024 and end date 2024 by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Extension on request is possible in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Survivor annuity term

A survivor annuity must start within 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2023 onward writes it as "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", with the same outcome. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Official example. Death in 2023 means start by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Extension on request is possible in special circumstances. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Negative expenditure (negatieve uitgaven) reversal window

Refunds of a deducted premium are negative expenditure without revision interest, for an annuity only if the refund is undone within 30 days. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Payment deadline FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion

Premiums count in the year they are paid or offset (art. 3.130 lid 1). Premiums for the profit from business discontinuation conversion (art. 3.129), the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29) For 2024 the deadline is before 1 July 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen The FISIN 2024 profit from business discontinuation line still says "Zet u vóór 1 juli 2024 de stakingswinst om" next to a payment by 1 July 2025, a page inconsistency. The law (art. 3.130 lid 2) gives six months after the year end. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130)

### AOV premium with periodic benefits

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits for disability (invaliditeit), illness or accident (ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen), with no allowance cap in the article. FISIN says "volledig aftrekken". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen Not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) are premiums the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies paying a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Health Insurance Act (Zorgverzekeringswet, Zvw) premiums. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers As entrepreneur (ondernemer) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers The rule of thumb on the page is that premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible, the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers The rule of thumb applies. Premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### Mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium for entrepreneurs (ondernemers)

A mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). It is deductible as a business cost of the business (onderneming) with a restriction. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies This differs from the AOV premium, which is an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not a business cost. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### Sectoral pension fund (sectoraal pensioenfonds) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen)

A self-employed person (zelfstandige) may join the pension fund of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible. The pilot (proef) runs until 1 July 2028. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimental provision for self-employed persons pension schemes (experimenteerbepaling zelfstandigenpensioenregelingen) inside the pension definitions. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.26)

### FOR addition

Access to a FOR addition requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year. That is art. 3.67. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.67) Art. 3.68 sets the addition at 9,44% of the profit, capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit, and not above the growth of business capital (ondernemingsvermogen). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.68) Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows decrease (afname) by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at cessation, AOW age at the start of the year, or failing the hours criterion this year and last year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.70) From 2023 no addition is possible. An existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) The FOR-annuity conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023, up to the decrease under the old art. 3.70 lid 1 a. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.128) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29) On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve The FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions count in the profit from business discontinuation provisions (art. 10a.29 lid 13). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

### BAZ consultation premium

The 11 June 2024 consultation proposed a premium of about 6,5% of profit, up to about € 195 per month. The € 195 is based on the 2024 minimum wage (minimumloon). Status proposed. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie This design was later replaced, see 2025. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie

### BAZ consultation design

The 11 June 2024 consultation proposed a waiting period (wachttijd) of 1 year, a benefit of 70% of profit up to the minimum wage, until AOW age, and an opt-out with minimum conditions.
