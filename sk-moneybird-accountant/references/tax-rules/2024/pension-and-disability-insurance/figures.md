## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the annual allowance (jaarruimte) and catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) for 2024, the annuity product caps, the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion, the AOV (disability insurance, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premium deduction and the proposed BAZ consultation. Tax year 2024 is enacted law. The annual allowance percentage is 30%, the income cap (aftoppingsgrens) is € 137.800, the franchise is € 17.545, the maximum annual allowance is € 36.077 and the catch-up allowance maximum is € 41.608. FOR addition (toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve) is no longer possible and 2024 is the last year of the FOR transition in the annual allowance. The BAZ consultation of 11 June 2024 is proposed only and was later replaced, see 2025.
