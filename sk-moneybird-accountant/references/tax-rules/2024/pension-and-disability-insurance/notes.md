## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage. 30% in the 2023-07-01 text and 30% in the 2024-01-01 text. The 2023-01-01 text still read 13,3%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page states the 30% applies with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij
- Maximum annual allowance. € 34.550 in 2023 to € 36.077 in 2024. The 2023 figure was changed retroactively for the Wtp. The pre-Wtp 2023 table printed € 15.317. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-a585ce5bd38ddc5d9e2adb1fd6f796a7cbaaa775/pdf
- Income cap on the contribution base (aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag). € 128.810 in 2023 to € 137.800 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Franchise on the contribution base (franchise premiegrondslag). € 13.646 in 2023 to € 17.545 in 2024. The 2024 text sets the franchise at the AOW benefit x 100/75 through art. 10.2b lid 4, which explains the jump. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b)
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum. € 38.000 flat in 2023 to € 41.608 in 2024. The flat shape without a percentage and without an age split was already in the 2023-07-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Look-back window. Annual allowance 2013 to 2022 in 2023 to annual allowance 2014 to 2023 in 2024. Both are 10 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Age limit annual allowance. Not yet AOW age plus 5 years at the start of the year in the 2023-07-01 and 2024-01-01 texts. The 2023-01-01 text still used not yet AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Pension accrual reduction. The 2023-07-01 text used 6,27 times the growth of the annual pension entitlement (aangroei van het jaarlijkse pensioenrecht) from additional service time (extra diensttijd) plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). The 2024-01-01 text uses the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension (partnerpensioen), excluding an art. 38s Wet LB 1964 compensation premium (compensatiepremie), plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor. The wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Transitional formula art. 10a.25 lid 2. The older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B was corrected by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023 to A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- FOR transition in the annual allowance. Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 apply to 2023 and 2024, so 2024 is the last year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- FOR addition. None possible from 1 January 2023, unchanged in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap. € 24.168 in 2023 to € 26.463 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.125) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125)
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap. € 63.288 in both 2023 and 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maxima. Tier a € 510.970 in 2023 to € 559.483 in 2024, tier b € 255.495 to € 279.752, tier c € 127.755 to € 139.885. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.129) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)
- Commutation of a small annuity (afkoop kleine lijfrente). € 4.898 in 2023 to € 5.364 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.133) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d)
- Commutation cap for long-term incapacity for work (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid). € 45.722 in 2023 to € 50.063 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.133) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133 lid 9)
- Payment deadline FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion. Before 1 July 2024 in 2023 to before 1 July 2025 in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Annuity end date (einddatum) term and survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) term. 1 full calendar year and the year of death plus 2 calendar years, the same in 2023 and 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- AOV premium treatment. Fully deductible with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) and not deductible with a lump sum, unchanged in 2023 and 2024. The four exclusions and FISIN section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- BAZ. The 11 June 2024 internet consultation (internetconsultatie) introduced a proposed premium of about 6,5% of profit (winst), up to about € 195 per month, with a waiting period (wachttijd) of 1 year, a benefit of 70% of profit up to the minimum wage (minimumloon), until AOW age and an opt-out with minimum conditions. This design was later replaced, see 2025. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the AOV premium for a policy with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen), not as a business cost. It is fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Do not deduct an AOV premium for a lump-sum policy. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Record a mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) premium as a business cost of the business (onderneming) with the restriction in the source. It is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- Record annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend) (art. 3.130 lid 1). For the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion and the FOR conversion, a payment before 1 July 2025 may on choice be counted in 2024 (art. 3.130 lid 2 and art. 10a.29 lid 14). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Keep the payment date, the policy type and the benefit (uitkering) conditions. Premiums above the allowance (ruimte) are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit is taxed, and the maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- Keep the 2023 income data and the 2023 pension accrual data because the annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2024 uses the previous calendar year and is reduced by premiums contributed (ingelegd) for old-age and partner pension (ouderdoms- en partnerpensioen), excluding an art. 38s compensation premium (compensatiepremie), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- For the annual allowance 2024 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) aid (hulpmiddel) assumes a pension scheme that existed on 30 June 2023 and was not yet adapted to the Wtp. Keep that assumption with the calculation. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/hulpmiddel-lijfrentepremie-2016-en-daarna
- FOR holders. Keep the FOR balance at the start of 2024 and any reduction (afneming), because the contribution base (premiegrondslag) for 2024 uses the profit (winst) before a reduction of the FOR and 2024 is the last year of that transition (art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- Do not record a BAZ premium or benefit for 2024. The 11 June 2024 design is proposed only. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- Record periodic AOV benefits as income from former employment (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers
- Claim the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) in the tax return (aangifte) and use it before the annual allowance so older unused allowance does not lapse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- For an annuity (lijfrente) with end date (einddatum) in 2024, convert or start it by 31 December 2025. For a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) where the death was in 2023, start it by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Use before 1 July 2025 for the FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion. The FISIN 2024 profit from business discontinuation line also prints "vóór 1 juli 2024", but the law gives six months after the year end and the page itself lists 1 July 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130 lid 2)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- BAZ consultation premium. About 6,5% of profit (winst), up to about € 195 per month. Status proposed, from the internet consultation (internetconsultatie) of 11 June 2024. Not enacted for 2024 and later replaced, see 2025. Source https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- BAZ consultation design. Waiting period (wachttijd) 1 year, benefit 70% of profit up to minimum wage (minimumloon), until AOW age, opt-out with minimum conditions. Status proposed, same source. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- Formula variables. The checked material gives the art. 10a.25 lid 2 formula A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z but does not define X, Y and B. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25). Reading art. 10a.25 lid 2 together with the art. 38r Wet LB 1964 definitions would settle this.
- Conflict on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) payment wording in FISIN 2024. The profit from business discontinuation line says "Zet u vóór 1 juli 2024 de stakingswinst om", while the same page gives a payment by 1 July 2025 and the law (art. 3.130 lid 2) gives six months after the year end. Values: 1 July 2024 against 1 July 2025. Sources https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130 lid 2). The law text settles the 2024 deadline at before 1 July 2025.
- Conflict on the profit from business discontinuation tier a age wording. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. Sources https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf. The law text settles the 2024 wording at at most five years below the AOW age.
- Resolved correction on art. 10a.25 lid 2. The older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B was corrected by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023 to A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Resolved correction on the pension accrual reduction wording. The 2024 wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431, so the 2024-01-01 text is the one to use. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Resolved placement of the FOR rules. The FOR transition lives in art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 and the FOR conversion deduction in art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023, not in art. 3.127. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2024. No open point remains. The Ministry table confirms € 36.077. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf
