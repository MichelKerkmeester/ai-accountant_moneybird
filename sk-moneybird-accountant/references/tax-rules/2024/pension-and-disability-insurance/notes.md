## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Jaarruimte percentage. 30% in the 2023-07-01 text and 30% in the 2024-01-01 text. The 2023-01-01 text still read 13,3%. The Belastingdienst Wtp page states the 30% applies with retroactive effect from 1 January 2023. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij
- Maximum jaarruimte. € 34.550 in 2023 to € 36.077 in 2024. The 2023 figure was changed retroactively for the Wtp. The pre-Wtp 2023 table printed € 15.317. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-a585ce5bd38ddc5d9e2adb1fd6f796a7cbaaa775/pdf
- Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag. € 128.810 in 2023 to € 137.800 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Franchise premiegrondslag. € 13.646 in 2023 to € 17.545 in 2024. The 2024 text sets the franchise at the AOW benefit x 100/75 via art. 10.2b lid 4, which explains the jump. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b)
- Reserveringsruimte maximum. € 38.000 flat in 2023 to € 41.608 in 2024. The flat shape without a percentage and without an age split was already in the 2023-07-01 text. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Look-back window. Jaarruimte 2013 to 2022 in 2023 to jaarruimte 2014 to 2023 in 2024. Both are 10 years. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Age limit jaarruimte. Not yet AOW age plus 5 years at the start of the year in the 2023-07-01 and 2024-01-01 texts. The 2023-01-01 text still used not yet AOW age. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Pension accrual reduction. The 2023-07-01 text used 6,27 keer the aangroei of the yearly pension entitlement from extra diensttijd plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. The 2024-01-01 text uses the premiums ingelegd in the previous year for ouderdomspensioen and partnerpensioen, excluding an art. 38s Wet LB 1964 compensatiepremie, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. The wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Transitional formula art. 10a.25 lid 2. The older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B was corrected by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023 to A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- FOR transition in the jaarruimte. Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 apply to 2023 and 2024, so 2024 is the last year. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- FOR toevoeging. None possible from 1 January 2023, unchanged in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap. € 24.168 in 2023 to € 26.463 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.125) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.125)
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap. € 63.288 in both 2023 and 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Stakingswinst maxima. Tier a € 510.970 in 2023 to € 559.483 in 2024, tier b € 255.495 to € 279.752, tier c € 127.755 to € 139.885. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.129) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.129)
- Afkoop kleine lijfrente. € 4.898 in 2023 to € 5.364 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.133) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d)
- Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid. € 45.722 in 2023 to € 50.063 in 2024. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01 (art. 3.133) and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 3.133 lid 9)
- Payment deadline FOR or stakingswinst conversion. Before 1 July 2024 in 2023 to before 1 July 2025 in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Lijfrente einddatum termijn and nabestaandenlijfrente termijn. 1 full calendar year and the year of death plus 2 calendar years, the same in 2023 and 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- AOV premium treatment. Fully deductible with periodic uitkeringen and not deductible with a lump sum, unchanged in 2023 and 2024. The four exclusions and FISIN section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- BAZ. The 11 June 2024 internetconsultatie introduced a proposed premium of about 6,5% of winst, up to about € 195 per month, with a wachttijd of 1 year, a benefit of 70% of winst up to the minimumloon, until AOW age and an opt-out with minimum conditions. This design was later replaced, see 2025. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the AOV premium for a policy with periodic uitkeringen as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen, not as a business cost. It is fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Do not deduct an AOV premium for a lump-sum policy. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Record a mandatory beroepspensioen premium as a business cost of the onderneming with the restriction in the source. It is not a lijfrentepremie. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- Record lijfrentepremies in the year they are paid or verrekend (art. 3.130 lid 1). For the stakingswinst conversion and the FOR conversion, a payment before 1 July 2025 may on choice be counted in 2024 (art. 3.130 lid 2 and art. 10a.29 lid 14). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Keep the payment date, the policy type and the uitkering conditions. Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- Keep the 2023 income data and the 2023 pension accrual data because the jaarruimte 2024 uses the previous calendar year and is reduced by premiums ingelegd for ouderdoms- and partnerpensioen, excluding an art. 38s compensatiepremie, plus nettopensioen premiums divided by the nettofactor. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- For the jaarruimte 2024 the Belastingdienst hulpmiddel assumes a pension scheme that existed on 30 June 2023 and was not yet adapted to the Wtp. Keep that assumption with the calculation. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/hulpmiddel-lijfrentepremie-2016-en-daarna
- FOR holders. Keep the FOR balance at the start of 2024 and any afneming, because the premiegrondslag for 2024 uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and 2024 is the last year of that transition (art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- Do not record a BAZ premium or benefit for 2024. The 11 June 2024 design is proposed only. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- Record periodic AOV benefits as inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers
- Claim the reserveringsruimte in the aangifte and use it before the jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- For a lijfrente with einddatum in 2024, convert or start it by 31 December 2025. For a nabestaandenlijfrente where the death was in 2023, start it by 31 December 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Use before 1 July 2025 for the FOR or stakingswinst conversion. The FISIN 2024 stakingswinst line also prints "vóór 1 juli 2024", but the law gives six months after the year end and the page itself lists 1 July 2025. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130 lid 2)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- BAZ consultation premium. About 6,5% of winst, up to about € 195 per month. Status proposed, from the internetconsultatie of 11 June 2024. Not enacted for 2024 and later replaced, see 2025. Source https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- BAZ consultation design. Wachttijd 1 year, benefit 70% of winst up to minimumloon, until AOW age, opt-out with minimum conditions. Status proposed, same source. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie
- Formula variables. The checked material gives the art. 10a.25 lid 2 formula A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z but does not define X, Y and B. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25). Reading art. 10a.25 lid 2 together with the art. 38r Wet LB 1964 definitions would settle this.
- Conflict on the stakingswinst payment wording in FISIN 2024. The stakingswinst line says "Zet u vóór 1 juli 2024 de stakingswinst om", while the same page gives a payment by 1 July 2025 and the law (art. 3.130 lid 2) gives six months after the year end. Values: 1 July 2024 against 1 July 2025. Sources https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.130 lid 2). The law text settles the 2024 deadline at before 1 July 2025.
- Conflict on the stakingswinst tier a age wording. FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023 (AOW age 66 years and 10 months). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd", and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. Sources https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf. The law text settles the 2024 wording at at most five years below the AOW age.
- Resolved correction on art. 10a.25 lid 2. The older formula A = (1 - (X-Y)/X) x B was corrected by Stb. 2024, 434 with retroactive effect to 1 July 2023 to A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Resolved correction on the pension accrual reduction wording. The 2024 wording was amended back to 1 July 2023 by Stb. 2025, 431, so the 2024-01-01 text is the one to use. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.25)
- Resolved placement of the FOR rules. The FOR transition lives in art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 and the FOR conversion deduction in art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023, not in art. 3.127. Source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)
- Maximum jaarruimte 2024. No open point remains. The Ministry table confirms € 36.077. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf
