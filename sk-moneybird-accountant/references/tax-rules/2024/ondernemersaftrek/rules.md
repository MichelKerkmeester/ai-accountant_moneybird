## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### IB-ondernemer status

Who is an ondernemer for the inkomstenbelasting is judged by the Belastingdienst per activity, separately from the btw (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). Someone who is a btw-ondernemer is not automatically an IB-ondernemer, because the Belastingdienst looks at more conditions for the inkomstenbelasting (same source). The Belastingdienst weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts (same source). Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source (same source). Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an IB-ondernemer (same source).

KVK registration is not the test. The checked pages say that the Belastingdienst makes its own assessment after registration (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). A page that literally says that KVK registration alone cannot make someone an IB-ondernemer is not in the checked material.

The statute defines the ondernemer as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the zelfstandig beroep (independent profession) counts as an onderneming (art. 3.5) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Someone who is not an IB-ondernemer reports the profit as inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the zelfstandigenaftrek or the investeringsaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The MKB-winstvrijstelling applies only to profit as ondernemer (art. 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Urencriterium, 1.225 hours and the second test

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an ondernemer, and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is "grotendeels" (largely) spent on the businesses, or (b) the taxpayer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 1). The second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-ondernemer year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight ondernemer years (same source). For 2024 the lookback window for test (b) is 2019 to 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6). FISIN prints this window per year, and the 2024 printing follows the statute's five preceding calendar years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an ondernemer. The Belastingdienst example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an ondernemer, and the taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The KVK example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day that is 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage (same source). Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (same source).

The urencriterium is a condition of the zelfstandigenaftrek (art. 3.76 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), of the meewerkaftrek (art. 3.78, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and, per FISIN, of the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Urencriterium, samenwerkingsverband with connected persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a samenwerkingsverband (working partnership) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a samenwerkingsverband between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the samenwerkingsverband relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an ondernemer but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household (same source). A samenwerkingsverband with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) counts under (b) (same source). The Belastingdienst reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and an ondermaatschap as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6).

### Urencriterium, pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity and childbirth leave) for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5).

### Verlaagd urencriterium

Art. 3.78a lid 3: the verlaagd urencriterium (reduced hours criterion) is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a lid 3). The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). In the checked material it appears as a condition only of the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (same source). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### Parts of the ondernemersaftrek

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the zelfstandigenaftrek, the aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, the meewerkaftrek, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid and the stakingsaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.74). The startersaftrek is not a separate list item. It is a verhoging (increase) of the zelfstandigenaftrek in art. 3.76 lid 3 (same source). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74).

Medegerechtigden (profit-sharing partners) get no zelfstandigenaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling on their medegerechtigde profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The ondernemersaftrek reduces the profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

Art. 3.76: the zelfstandigenaftrek (self-employed person's allowance) applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium (lid 1) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 1). For 2024 the amount is € 3.750 for an ondernemer below the AOW age at the start of the year (lid 2) (same source, art. 3.76 lid 2). At the AOW age at the start of the year the aftrek is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the startersaftrek is halved too. The amount for 2024 is then € 1.875 (lid 4) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4). FISIN 2024 agrees with € 3.750 and prints € 2.123 for the AOW age amount in the same sentence, which is a misprint against the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

The aftrek is not more than the profit, except for an ondernemer who qualifies for the startersaftrek (lid 5) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5). The amount cut off by this cap is niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek (unrealised self-employed person's allowance). If the cap sets the aftrek to nil, the year still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for the startersaftrek count (same source). The text of art. 3.76 is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.76).

### Niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek

Lid 6: the inspecteur sets the niet-gerealiseerde amount in a beschikking (decision) open to bezwaar (objection) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 6). Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the zelfstandigenaftrek of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's zelfstandigenaftrek, oldest first (same source, art. 3.76 lid 7). Lid 8: the offset is also set by beschikking open to bezwaar (same source, art. 3.76 lid 8). The Belastingdienst describes the same carry-forward and notes the beschikking route (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek). For a starter the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, and that excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### Startersaftrek

For 2024 the startersaftrek (starter's allowance) is € 2.123 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). At the AOW age at the start of the year it is € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), because art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the lid 2 and lid 3 amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4).

The startersaftrek raises the zelfstandigenaftrek and is not a separate item in art. 3.74. It requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit, and the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (same source).

### Startersaftrek, prior-years condition

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the zelfstandigenaftrek is raised by the startersaftrek if the ondernemer was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). For 2024 FISIN prints the five preceding years as 2019 to 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The startersaftrek does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv, a silent return from a BV) where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3).

The Belastingdienst adds that the startersaftrek is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an ondernemer, that it requires a right to the zelfstandigenaftrek, and that the combined zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek can exceed the profit with the excess as a loss (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The Belastingdienst words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting". That wording means the same as the statute (same source and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). A year in which the profit cap set the zelfstandigenaftrek to nil still counts as a year with zelfstandigenaftrek for this count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5).

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

Art. 3.78a applies to the ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to an arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering (disability benefit) or to arbeidsondersteuning under the Wajong, does not meet the urencriterium but does meet the verlaagd urencriterium, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a). It does not apply to an ondernemer who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before (same source). The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from an invaliditeit or ongeval insurance (same source).

For 2024 the amounts are € 12.000 if the aftrek was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a). The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no aftrek then (same source). The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk

The aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk (R&D allowance) is part of the ondernemersaftrek (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.77). It is a base amount plus a starter increase for an ondernemer who was not an ondernemer in one or more of the five preceding years and had the aftrek applied at most twice in that period (same source). For 2024 the base amount is € 15.551 (same source) and the starter increase is € 7.781 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

FISIN adds that the aftrek requires an S&O-verklaring (R&D certificate) from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The amounts are indexed per year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.77).

### Meewerkaftrek

Art. 3.78: the meewerkaftrek (co-working partner's allowance) applies to the ondernemer who meets the urencriterium and whose partner works in the business without any pay (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78). For 2024 the bands are 1,25% for 525 to under 875 hours, 2% for 875 to under 1.225 hours, 3% for 1.225 to under 1.750 hours and 4% from 1.750 hours, each of the profit (same source).

The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 hours in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.78 lid 2). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by onteigening (expropriation), profit from (partial) staking including staking by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78). Hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). The text is identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78).

### Pay to the partner

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.16 lid 4, text identical 2020 to 2027). The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). That means the meewerkaftrek can apply while the pay is left out of the profit (same source).

### Stakingsaftrek

Art. 3.79: the stakingsaftrek (cessation allowance) applies to profit made with or on the staking of one or more whole businesses (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by stakingsaftrek enjoyed in earlier years (same source). The Belastingdienst adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and that there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from an afneming (decrease) of the oudedagsreserve after a geruisloze doorschuiving or omzetting (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29).

### Stakingsaftrek, own-account period for a continued business

For a business continued under doorschuiving on dissolution of a huwelijksgemeenschap (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to ondernemers (art. 3.63), the stakingsaftrek applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### Oudedagsreserve additions

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition is possible from 2023 and none in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an oudedagsreserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 (same sources).

The afneming (decrease) rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the lijfrente premiums deducted as uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the ondernemingsvermogen at year end if the business or part of it was staked in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the urencriterium was not met in the year and the previous year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 10a.29). Decreases are added to profit (same source). Lid 12 and 14: the taxpayer may deduct lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return (same source). Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (huwelijksgemeenschap, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the stakingsaftrek for an afneming after a geruisloze doorschuiving (same source). On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

For the premiegrondslag and the jaarruimte, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 state that for 2023 and 2024 the profit is taken before addition to the reserve, and the jaarruimte for 2023 and 2024 is reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 10a.29).

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

Art. 3.79a: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is a percentage of the total profit as ondernemer from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the ondernemersaftrek (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79a). The percentage for 2024 is 13,31% (same source). The Belastingdienst MKB page agrees (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). FISIN 2024 prints 14%, which is a misprint against the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Art. 3.2: the belastbare winst uit onderneming (taxable business profit) is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so the order is profit, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.2). No urencriterium is needed. The statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none (same source and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). There is no vrijstelling on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). In a loss year the vrijstelling enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling is capped through the tariefsaanpassing (art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Tariefsaanpassing

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the tax base, expressly the ondernemersaftrek of art. 3.74 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling) are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3). For 2024 the percentage is 12,53% and the threshold is € 75.518 (same source). The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage (same source). The article exists in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 2.10).

### Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling

For 2024 the maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling together is 36,97%, being the top rate of 49,50% minus the tariefsaanpassing percentage of 12,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). In the checked wording, every euro of these deductions is worth at most 36,97% of its amount (same source and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10).
