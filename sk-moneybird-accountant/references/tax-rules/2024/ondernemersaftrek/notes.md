## 5. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 5.030 in 2023 to € 3.750 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 2.515 in 2023 to € 1.875 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 14.202 in 2023 to € 15.551 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- S&O starter increase: € 7.106 in 2023 to € 7.781 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Tariefsaanpassing: 12,57% above € 73.031 in 2023 to 12,53% above € 75.518 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10).
- Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling: 36,93% in 2023 to 36,97% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Lookback window for the second test of the urencriterium and for the startersaftrek condition: 2018 to 2022 in 2023 to 2019 to 2023 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Unchanged in 2024: the urencriterium of 1.225 hours, the verlaagd urencriterium of 800 hours, the startersaftrek of € 2.123 and € 1.062 at AOW age, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid of € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, the meewerkaftrek bands of 1,25% to 4%, the partner pay threshold of € 5.000 and the stakingsaftrek maximum of € 3.630 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- No rule changes: the 2024 material states the same rules as 2023, including art. 10a.29 for the old oudedagsreserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Oudedagsreserve: no addition is possible in 2024, the same as from 1 January 2023. For 2023 the checked material records an addition over a broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 and ended in 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220232-doteren-aan-de-fiscale-oudedagsreserve-bij-een-gebroken-boekjaar-eindigend-in-belastingjaar-2023/). The 2024 material records no such addition for 2024.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling are inkomstenbelasting items for the 2024 return. The order is profit as ondernemer, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling, which together give the belastbare winst uit onderneming (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Keep the records that make the urencriterium hours plausible, for example an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Keep the records that make the partner's hours plausible for the meewerkaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit and is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- If the urencriterium was not met, check whether the taxpayer is an IB-ondernemer at all. Someone who is not reports the profit under inkomsten uit overig werk, with no ondernemersaftrek and no schemes such as the investeringsaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/).
- The niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek and its offset are set by beschikking open to bezwaar, so watch for that beschikking (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- Keep a record of stakingsaftrek used in earlier years. The 2024 maximum of € 3.630 is reduced by earlier stakingsaftrek, and an unused remainder can be used at a later staking (art. 3.79, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).
- Lijfrente premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for 2024 by choice in the return, up to the decrease of the old oudedagsreserve (art. 10a.29 lid 12 and 14, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, text the same 2023 to 2027). On death the old reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- For an owner who did a geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 in 2024 or in 2019 to 2023, there is no startersaftrek and no startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (art. 3.76 lid 3 and art. 3.78a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for 2024.

Two FISIN 2024 printings differ from the 2024 statute and are settled in favour of the statute:

- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: the statute gives € 1.875 under art. 3.76 lid 2 and lid 4 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), while FISIN 2024 prints € 2.123 in the same sentence (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute text settles the value.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: the statute gives 13,31% under art. 3.79a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and the Belastingdienst MKB page agrees (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), while FISIN 2024 prints 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute text settles the value.
