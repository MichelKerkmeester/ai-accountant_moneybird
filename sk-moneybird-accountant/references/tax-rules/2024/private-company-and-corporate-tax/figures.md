## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) for tax year 2024 and the rules that come with a BV, including the rate table, loss offsetting (verliesverrekening), the generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking), the innovation box (innovatiebox), the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling), the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), the customary wage scheme (gebruikelijk loonregeling) for a director and substantial shareholder (DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder), Zvw, box 2, dividend tax (dividendbelasting), the conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), the annual accounts (jaarrekening) size classes and the record-keeping obligation (administratieplicht). The law for 2024 is enacted and every value below carries its Check tag. What matters most in 2024: the Vpb rates stay at 19% up to € 200.000 and 25,8% above it, the art. 15b percentage stays at 20%, the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag) rises to € 56.000, box 2 becomes a two bracket rate, the conditional withholding tax now also covers dividends, and the annual accounts size thresholds are raised for book years from 1 January 2024. The open points for this year sit in section 7.
