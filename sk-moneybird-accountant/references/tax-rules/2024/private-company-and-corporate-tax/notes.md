## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Gebruikelijk loon normbedrag: € 51.000 in 2023 to € 56.000 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).
- Start-up relief gebruikelijk loon: in 2023 lapsed for new cases with 2021 starters continuing, in 2024 only 2022 starters continue and it is the last year for them, and the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- Bijdrage Zvw: 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum bijdrage-inkomen Zvw: € 66.956 in 2023 to € 71.628 in 2024 (same two FISIN pages).
- Werkgeversheffing Zvw for an insured DGA: 6,68% in 2023 to 6,57% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Box 2: a flat 26,9% in 2023 to t/m € 67.000 at 24,5% and above that € 16.415 plus 33% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Conditionele bronbelasting: 25,8% on interest and royalties in 2023, and from the 2024 version dividends enter the levy (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividendbelasting) at the same 25,8% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01).
- Uiteindelijk gerechtigde: the 2023 text to the 2024 text, which now reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01).
- Jaarrekening size thresholds: the old values applied by default in boekjaar 2023 with the raised values optional, and from boekjaar 2024 the raised values apply (micro € 350.000 and € 700.000 to € 450.000 and € 900.000, small € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 to € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, medium € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 to € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01).
- Fiscale eenheid: a new verzamelbesluit, Besluit van 2 april 2024, nr. 2024-186206, Stcrt. 2024, 11814, published 25-04-2024 and in force the day after publication, replaces the decision of 2010 and the 2022 winstsplitsing decision (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html).
- Btw-eenheid: the Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, was published on 10 December 2024 in Stcrt. 2024, 38545 and is in force from 1 July 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Unchanged against 2023: the Vpb rates of 19% and € 38.000 plus 25,8%, the art. 15b percentage of 20% and its € 1.000.000 threshold, the verliesverrekening cap of € 1.000.000 plus 50%, the innovatiebox fraction of 9/H and its forfait of 25% with a € 25.000 maximum, the deelneming threshold of 5%, the fiscale eenheid bezitseis of 95%, the dividendbelasting of 15% and the stakingsaftrek of at most € 3.630 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The BV runs the DGA wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. For a DGA who is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen the BV withholds the Zvw bijdrage on the net wage at 5,32% up to a maximum bijdrage-inkomen of € 71.628 and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. For an insured DGA the BV pays the werkgeversheffing Zvw of 6,57% (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Record the gebruikelijk loon test in the payroll file: the normbedrag of € 56.000, the comparable wage and the highest wage in connected bodies, tested on the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- On a dividend, issue the dividendnota when the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, file the dividendbelasting return and pay within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available, and credit the withheld tax in the aangifte Vpb (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen).
- Conditionele bronbelasting on a dividend is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividendbelasting withheld on the same payment is credited (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/).
- In a fiscale eenheid the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/).
- Keep the records for seven years (art. 52 AWR). Records on onroerende zaken for nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records for ten years. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the paper profit and loss statement (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).
- Deposit the annual accounts within eight days after the vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts and mark them as unadopted. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From boekjaar 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- File the Vpb return for a book year equal to the calendar year before 1 June of the next year, or request the online extension to 1 November from 1 March to 1 June (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting).
- On a switch to a BV: register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, request the conversion at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, expect a new btw-identificatienummer to pass on to EU suppliers and a new loonheffingennummer when there are employees, and note that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Micro size thresholds. Stb. 2024, 52 art. 1 raises art. 2:395a Boek 2 BW to € 450.000 and € 900.000, and KVK applies those amounts. The consolidated Boek 2 BW at 2025-01-01 and 2026-07-01 still prints € 350.000 and € 700.000 in art. 395a, while it prints the raised amounts in art. 396 and 397. The Nota van toelichting says the AMvB basis for 395a was missing and was to be added to art. 2:398 lid 4, which the 2026-07-01 text now does. The settled value used in this file is € 450.000 and € 900.000. A KVK or Justitie statement on the legal effect for boekjaar 2024 would close the point (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html).
- Large class turnover bound. KVK prints more than € 51 mln, Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 miljoen. Settled at € 50 miljoen, recorded here because the two sources differ (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/).
- Koepelvrijstelling and BUA. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. The beleidsbesluit does not cover them and the Wet OB article and the BUA were not opened in the sources for this file (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Zvw route for an uninsured DGA. The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 paragraph 8.2.2 says the BV withholds the bijdrage on the net wage (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), while the Belastingdienst table says the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/). This file follows the Handboek on the withholding route. A dated Belastingdienst statement on the collection route would settle it.
- Filing and deposit deadlines. The aangifte, uitstel and deponering steps come from current Belastingdienst and Ondernemersplein pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. The sources for this file have no 2024 dated text for those deadlines (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- Geruisloze omzetting version. The art. 3.65 conditions in this file are those of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 24 October 2025, published in Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication. The sources for this file do not contain the text of the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, that it replaces, so the 2024 conversion conditions in that older text are not verified here (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html).
