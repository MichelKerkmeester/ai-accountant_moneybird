## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag): € 51.000 in 2023 to € 56.000 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).
- Start-up relief customary salary: in 2023 lapsed for new cases with 2021 starters continuing, in 2024 only 2022 starters continue and it is the last year for them, and the Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw): 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) Zvw: € 66.956 in 2023 to € 71.628 in 2024 (same two FISIN pages).
- Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw for an insured DGA: 6,68% in 2023 to 6,57% in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Box 2: a flat 26,9% in 2023 to up to and including € 67.000 at 24,5% and above that € 16.415 plus 33% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): 25,8% on interest and royalties in 2023, and from the 2024 version dividends enter the levy (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividendbelasting) at the same 25,8% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01).
- Beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde): the 2023 text to the 2024 text, which now reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01).
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds: the old values applied by default in financial year (boekjaar) 2023 with the raised values optional, and from financial year 2024 the raised values apply (micro € 350.000 and € 700.000 to € 450.000 and € 900.000, small € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 to € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen, medium € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 to € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01).
- Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid): a new consolidated decree (verzamelbesluit), Decree of 2 April 2024, nr. 2024-186206, Stcrt. 2024, 11814, published 25-04-2024 and in force (in werking) the day after publication, replaces the decision of 2010 and the 2022 profit splitting (winstsplitsing) decision (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html).
- VAT fiscal unity (btw-eenheid): the policy decree (beleidsbesluit) on tax liability (belastingplicht) and VAT fiscal unity (btw-eenheid) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, was published on 10 December 2024 in Stcrt. 2024, 38545 and is in force from 1 July 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Unchanged against 2023: the Vpb rates of 19% and € 38.000 plus 25,8%, the art. 15b percentage of 20% and its € 1.000.000 threshold, the loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) cap of € 1.000.000 plus 50%, the innovation box (innovatiebox) fraction of 9/H and its flat rate (forfait) of 25% with a € 25.000 maximum, the participation (deelneming) threshold of 5%, the fiscal unity ownership requirement (bezitseis) of 95%, the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) of 15% and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) of at most € 3.630 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2023-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The BV runs the DGA wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). For a DGA who is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) the BV withholds the Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw) on the net wage (nettoloon) at 5,32% up to a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 71.628 and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For an insured DGA the BV pays the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw of 6,57% (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/).
- Record the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test in the payroll file: the standard amount (normbedrag) of € 56.000, the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) and the highest wage in connected bodies, tested on the wage (loon) for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).
- On a dividend, issue the dividend statement (dividendnota) when the proceeds (opbrengst) is paid, credited, settled or handed over, file the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) return and pay within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available, and credit the withheld tax in the tax return (aangifte) Vpb (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen).
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) on a dividend is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividend tax withheld on the same payment is credited (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/).
- In a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/).
- Keep the records for seven years (art. 52 AWR). Records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) for nine years following the year in which use began, and OSS records for ten years. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the paper profit and loss statement (resultatenrekening) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).
- Deposit the annual accounts (jaarrekening) within eight days after the adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts and mark them as unadopted. There is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf). From financial year (boekjaar) 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- File the Vpb return for a financial year equal to the calendar year before 1 June of the next year, or request the online extension to 1 November from 1 March to 1 June (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting).
- On a switch to a BV: register the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the Accompanying form (Geleideformulier), request the conversion at the office that handles the income tax (inkomstenbelasting), expect a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer) to pass on to EU suppliers and a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) when there are employees, and note that the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Micro size thresholds. Stb. 2024, 52 art. 1 raises art. 2:395a Boek 2 BW to € 450.000 and € 900.000, and Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) applies those amounts. The consolidated Boek 2 BW at 2025-01-01 and 2026-07-01 still prints € 350.000 and € 700.000 in art. 395a, while it prints the raised amounts in art. 396 and 397. The explanatory note (Nota van toelichting) says the AMvB basis for 395a was missing and was to be added to art. 2:398 lid 4, which the 2026-07-01 text now does. The settled value used in this file is € 450.000 and € 900.000. A KVK or Justice (Justitie) statement on the legal effect for financial year (boekjaar) 2024 would close the point (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html).
- Large class (grote klasse) turnover bound. KVK prints more than € 51 mln, Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 million (miljoen). Settled at € 50 million, recorded here because the two sources differ (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/).
- VAT cost-sharing exemption (koepelvrijstelling) and BUA. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. The policy decree (beleidsbesluit) does not cover them and the Turnover Tax Act 1968 (Wet OB) article and the BUA were not opened in the sources for this file (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html).
- Zvw route (zvw-route) for an uninsured DGA. The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 paragraph 8.2.2 says the BV withholds the contribution (bijdrage) on the net wage (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), while the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) table says the DGA pays the contribution (bijdrage) himself, collected by withholding (inhouding) from net wage (nettoloon) or by assessment (aanslag) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/). This file follows the Handbook on the withholding route. A dated Dutch Tax and Customs Administration statement on the collection route would settle it.
- Filing and deposit deadlines. The tax return (aangifte), deferral (uitstel) and filing (deponering) steps come from current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration and Ondernemersplein pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons. The sources for this file have no 2024 dated text for those deadlines (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).
- Tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) version. The art. 3.65 conditions in this file are those of the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) of 24 October 2025, published in Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and in force (in werking) the day after publication. The sources for this file do not contain the text of the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, that it replaces, so the 2024 conversion conditions in that older text are not verified here (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html).
