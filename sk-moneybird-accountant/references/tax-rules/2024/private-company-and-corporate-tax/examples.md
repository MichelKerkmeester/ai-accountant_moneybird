## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Vpb on a taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) of € 60.000, € 200.000 and € 300.000 (Calculated example)

- € 60.000: below € 200.000, so only the first bracket (eerste schijf) applies. 19% of € 60.000 = € 11.400.
- € 200.000: exactly the bracket, so 19% of € 200.000 = € 38.000.
- € 300.000: € 38.000 plus 25,8% of (€ 300.000 - € 200.000 = € 100.000). 25,8% of € 100.000 = € 25.800. Total € 38.000 + € 25.800 = € 63.800.

Figures from section 2. Source for the bracket values: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Art. 15b: an interest balance (saldo aan renten) of € 1.500.000 (Calculated example)

- Case 1, adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) € 4.000.000. 20% of € 4.000.000 = € 800.000. The higher of € 800.000 and € 1.000.000 is € 1.000.000. The balance of € 1.500.000 exceeds € 1.000.000 by € 500.000, so € 500.000 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) and € 1.000.000 is deductible.
- Case 2, adjusted profit € 8.000.000. 20% of € 8.000.000 = € 1.600.000. The higher of € 1.600.000 and € 1.000.000 is € 1.600.000. The balance of € 1.500.000 stays below € 1.600.000, so the whole € 1.500.000 is deductible.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) with a 2024 profit of € 1.400.000 (Calculated example)

A loss of € 1.200.000 is carried forward from an earlier year. The 2024 cap is € 1.000.000 plus 50% of (€ 1.400.000 - € 1.000.000 = € 400.000) = € 1.000.000 + € 200.000 = € 1.200.000. The full loss of € 1.200.000 fits inside the cap, so the taxable amount is € 1.400.000 - € 1.200.000 = € 200.000. Vpb = 19% of € 200.000 = € 38.000. If the carried-forward loss had been € 1.500.000, only € 1.200.000 could have been set off in 2024 and € 300.000 would carry forward.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Innovation box (innovatiebox) with a benefit (voordeel) of € 100.000 (Calculated example)

A BV has € 400.000 of other profit and a € 100.000 advantage from a self-made qualifying intangible asset (kwalificerend immaterieel activum).

- Without the innovation box: taxable amount € 500.000. Vpb = € 38.000 + 25,8% of (€ 500.000 - € 200.000 = € 300.000) = € 38.000 + € 77.400 = € 115.400.
- With the innovation box: the benefit counts for 9/25,8 of € 100.000 = € 34.883,72. Taxable amount € 400.000 + € 34.883,72 = € 434.883,72. Vpb = € 38.000 + 25,8% of € 234.883,72 = € 38.000 + € 60.600,00 = € 98.600,00.
- The Vpb on the benefit itself is 25,8% of € 34.883,72 = € 9.000,00, which is 9% of € 100.000. The box saves € 115.400 - € 98.600 = € 16.800.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovation box flat rate (forfait) (Calculated example)

- Profit before art. 12b of € 80.000: 25% of € 80.000 = € 20.000. That is below the € 25.000 maximum, so the flat rate is € 20.000.
- Profit before art. 12b of € 200.000: 25% of € 200.000 = € 50.000, capped at € 25.000.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01.

### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) of a parent company (moeder) and a subsidiary (dochter) (Calculated example)

A parent company BV holds 100% of a subsidiary BV, which is more than the 95% requirement, and both ask for a fiscal unity. The subsidiary makes a loss of € 100.000 and the parent company a profit of € 250.000. Tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, so the taxable amount is € 250.000 - € 100.000 = € 150.000. Vpb = 19% of € 150.000 = € 28.500.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) and the escape (Calculated example)

- Case 1: comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) € 50.000, highest wage in the body or connected bodies € 70.000, standard amount (normbedrag) € 56.000. The highest of the three amounts is € 70.000, which exceeds the comparable wage, so the wage is the comparable wage of € 50.000.
- Case 2: comparable wage € 60.000, highest other wage € 40.000, standard amount € 56.000. The highest amount is € 60.000, so the wage is at least € 60.000.
- Case 3: the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is € 5.000 in total. That is not more than the € 5.000 floor, so the customary wage (gebruikelijk loon) scheme (gebruikelijk loonregeling) does not apply.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01.

### Zvw for an uninsured and an insured DGA (Calculated example)

- Uninsured DGA, wage € 71.628, which is exactly the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen). Contribution = 5,32% of € 71.628 = € 3.810,61. A wage of € 90.000 gives the same amount, because the maximum contribution income is € 71.628.
- Insured DGA, wage € 71.628. Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) = 6,57% of € 71.628 = € 4.705,96, paid by the BV.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/.

### Box 2 and dividend tax (dividendbelasting) on a dividend of € 100.000 (Calculated example)

A BV pays a dividend of € 100.000 to an individual DGA.

- Dividend tax: 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000 withheld.
- Box 2: the first € 67.000 at 24,5% = € 16.415, and the remaining € 100.000 - € 67.000 = € 33.000 at 33% = € 10.890. Box 2 tax = € 16.415 + € 10.890 = € 27.305.
- Credit: € 27.305 - € 15.000 = € 12.305 remains to be paid in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting).

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01.

### Dividend tax gross-up and buyback of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen) (Calculated example)

- Gross-up: the company bears the tax on an agreed net amount of € 85.000. Art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the proceeds (opbrengst) by 100/85: € 85.000 x 100/85 = € 100.000. Dividend tax is 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000, leaving € 85.000 net.
- Buyback of own shares: the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on the shares is € 20.000 and the buyback price is € 120.000. The difference is € 120.000 - € 20.000 = € 100.000. Dividend tax = 15% of € 100.000 = € 15.000.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) on interest and royalties (Official example)

Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page example: interest of € 100.000 gross, 25,8% withheld = € 25.800, net € 74.200. Royalties borne by the payer: a net of € 100.000 needs about € 134.771 gross. Check of the second amount: 25,8% of € 134.771 = € 34.771, and € 134.771 - € 34.771 = € 100.000.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) (Calculated example)

A holding BV holds 100% of a subsidiary, which is above the 5% participation (deelneming) threshold, and receives a dividend of € 200.000. The advantage is left out of the profit under art. 13 lid 1. The dividend is a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder, so it is usually exempt from dividend tax withholding and no return is needed for the fully exempt payment unless the holding is invited to file.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size class (Calculated example)

A BV has a balance sheet total of € 400.000, a net turnover (netto-omzet) of € 850.000 and 7 employees on two consecutive balance dates. € 400.000 is at most € 450.000, € 850.000 is at most € 900.000 and 7 is fewer than 10, so it meets all three micro criteria and is a micro legal person.

Figures from section 2. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.

### Taxable contribution (ruisende inbreng) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (Calculated example)

A change of legal form (wijziging van rechtsvorm) without the art. 3.65 facility is a cessation. With a profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) of € 60.000 the discontinuation deduction is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime: € 60.000 - € 3.630 = € 56.370 remains as profit from business discontinuation. The income tax rate is not in this file, so no IB is computed here.

Figures from section 2. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen.
