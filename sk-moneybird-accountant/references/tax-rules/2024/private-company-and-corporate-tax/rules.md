## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting), first bracket (eerste schijf) (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

Corporate income tax (Vpb) for a 2024 financial year is 19% on the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) up to and including € 200.000. Art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 is built as columns: the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount above the column I amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rates page (tarieven) states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting). The art. 22 series prints the same 19% and 25,8% for 2023 to 2026, so no change of art. 22 falls inside a 2024 financial year. Where a financial year does start before and end on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 apportions the tax as (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01).

### Corporate income tax, above the first bracket (boven de eerste schijf) (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

For the part of the taxable amount above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8% of that part (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration rates page (tarieven) states 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting). The top rate also sets the rate of the conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), because art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 takes the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01).

### Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)

The interest balance (saldo aan renten, interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. For 2024 the percentage is 20% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021, 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024 and 24,5% in 2025 and 2026 (each year from https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration change pages for 2025 and 2026 state 20% for 2024, 24,5% for 2025 and 24,5% for 2026, and say that non-deductible interest carries forward (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026).

### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)

From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and carried forward without a time limit, but in each year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). For 2020 and 2021 the older rule applies: a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixes the loss by decision (beschikking) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01).

### Innovation box (innovatiebox), fraction (breuk) (art. 12b Wet Vpb 1969)

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H of the amount, H being the highest art. 22 rate, which is 25,8% for 2024. The effective rate on those profits is 9%. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox). Brands (merken), logo's and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Art. 12bb gives a K/T part, K being the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01).

### Innovation box, smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for the innovation box. Smaller means that the advantages plus the acquisition costs from R&D assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000, and that the net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, same in the 2020 and 2026 texts, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01). A taxpayer who is not smaller also needs an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b.

### Innovation box, flat rate (forfait) (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

For a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years and who chooses the flat rate, the amount is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000. The flat rate applies only when that profit is positive (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox).

### Participation (deelneming), threshold (drempel) (art. 13 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

Art. 13 lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969 leaves the advantages from a participation out of the profit. A participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, with a 1/3 interest as the connected test (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). A tax-neutral contribution (geruisloze inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html).

### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), ownership requirement (bezitseis) (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)

For a fiscal unity the mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01). Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the mother is a bv, nv, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or a foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie) (or a comparable foreign form), that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both must have the same financial years and profit rules and that both must be effectively established in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/). The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption of one daughter ends the fiscal unity for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10).

### Consolidated Decree on fiscal unity (verzamelbesluit fiscale eenheid)

The consolidated decree on fiscal unity (verzamelbesluit fiscale eenheid) is the Decree of 2 April 2024, nr. 2024-186206, published in Stcrt. 2024, 11814 on 25-04-2024 and in force the day after publication. It replaces the decision of 2010 and the 2022 profit splitting (winstsplitsing) decision. The decree works through examples on loss attribution, among them a sub-subsidiary (kleindochter) and a hived-off Y BV (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html). Option rights: the fiscal unity ends when the option holder exercises and the mother drops below 95%, even with an immediate offer duty (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html).

### Customary salary, standard amount (normbedrag) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies and (c) the standard amount, which is € 56.000 for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01). The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. The Handbook for Payroll Taxes (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Customary salary, comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon)

Amount (a) is 100% of the wage from the most comparable employment relationship from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). In 2020 to 2022 it was 75% of that wage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). For 2024 the 100% test holds (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).

### Customary salary, lower limit (ondergrens)

The customary wage scheme (gebruikelijk loonregeling) does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and in connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. This floor sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01).

### Customary salary, escape (art. 12a lid 2 Wet LB 1964)

From 2023, if the highest of the three amounts exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). In 2020 to 2022 the escape worked on the 75% basis: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) made plausible that the highest amount exceeded 75% of the comparable wage, the wage was 75% of the comparable wage, but at least the standard amount, or the comparable wage itself when that is lower than the standard amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The Handbook for Payroll Taxes 2025 notes that a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).

### Start-up relief for customary salary

In the statute of 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4 Wet LB 1964) a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies could use a wage of at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount (WML, statutory minimum wage), or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) nr. 1407/2013 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA, and an R&D declaration for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, with the conditions still met in 2023 for a 2021 start and in 2024 for a 2022 start. So in 2024 only starters who first used the relief for 2022 continue, and 2024 is the last year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The Handbook for Payroll Taxes 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025. Separate policy in the same Handboek: a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf).

### Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw)

A DGA is employed by his own company for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw (Health Insurance Act, Zorgverzekeringswet), so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). A DGA who, alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). For him the company pays no employee insurance premiums (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw, and the BV withholds the income-related Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen) (Handbook for Payroll Taxes 2025 paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf). For 2024 the contribution (bijdrage) is 5,32% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) Zvw

The maximum contribution income for 2024 is € 71.628. A wage above that amount does not raise the contribution (bijdrage) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Employer levy Zvw (insured DGA)

A DGA who is insured for the employee insurance schemes falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV. For 2024 that is 6,57% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/). The FISIN page for 2024 says that DGA wage falls under the employer levy only if the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Box 2, first bracket (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

Box 2 is the tax on the income from a substantial interest. For 2024 the first bracket runs to and including € 67.000 at 24,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). In 2023 the box 2 rate was a flat 26,9%, and in 2021 to 2023 that flat rate applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Box 2, above the first bracket (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

Above € 67.000 the box 2 tax is € 16.415 plus 33% of the part above € 67.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the table correction factor. Individuals credit the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld on a dividend in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting), bodies credit it in the Vpb return (aangifte Vpb) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/).

### Dividend tax (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 puts the tax at 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst) in every version read, from the 2020 version to the 2025 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01). Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. Art. 6 lid 1: if the company bears the tax, the proceeds are multiplied by 100/85. Art. 9 lid 1: once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend, a holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling), and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the company is invited to file. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen). On a buyback of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen) the dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback, where the company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85 unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html).

### Dividend tax, tax return and dividend statement

The return and the payment of dividend tax are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. Once the proceeds is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement is due (art. 9 lid 1 Wet DB 1965) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01). A participation dividend to a Dutch shareholder needs no return when it is fully exempt, unless the company is invited to file. A return is also needed when a qualification decision is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return, bodies in the Vpb return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/).

### Conditional withholding tax (art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021)

The tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969, so 25,8% for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01). Interest and royalties are in scope from the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01). Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividend tax, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01). The levy hits payments to a related body (a qualifying interest, always above 50% of the voting rights) in a low-tax jurisdiction (a profit tax below 9% or a place on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions) and abuse cases. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. Withholding tax (bronbelasting) on dividends is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividend tax withheld on the same payment is credited (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/).

### Beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde), dividend stripping (art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965)

The dividend stripping rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner when the proceeds benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text already holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01).

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening), size classes (grootteklassen) (Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397)

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. From Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 the criteria for financial years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, are: micro at most € 450.000 assets, at most € 900.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 million (miljoen), € 15 million and fewer than 50 employees, medium € 25 million, € 50 million and fewer than 250 employees. The employee counts are unchanged. The old statute values of 2020 were € 350.000 and € 700.000 for micro, € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 for small and € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 for medium (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/). Section 7 records an open conflict on the micro amounts in art. 395a.

### Annual accounts, large class (grote klasse)

The large class is above € 50 million net turnover (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html). The KVK table prints more than € 51 mln, while Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 million. The settled value is € 50 million (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01, https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/).

### Annual accounts, filing (deponering) (art. 394 Boek 2 BW)

The board deposits the annual accounts within eight days after the adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01). Ondernemersplein: there is no extension and late filing is a criminal offence. From financial year (boekjaar) 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/).

### VAT fiscal unity (btw-eenheid), policy decree (beleidsbesluit)

The VAT (btw) fiscal unity of art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A decision (beschikking), on request or given by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial intertwining means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The Policy Decree on tax liability and the VAT fiscal unity (Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, was published in Stcrt. 2024, 38545 on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html). For a change of legal form to a BV: the new legal form is a new business for VAT and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which must go to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change. For VAT, a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur (ondernemer) and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm).

### Tax-neutral conversion (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not to have ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets must be placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for the profit determination. The contributor may be credited for the IB, the national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree, the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. A contribution into an existing company is possible only when it already runs a business in the same line, and into a fiscal unity only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Selling the shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. Retroactivity: if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full financial year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next financial year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: request at the office that handles the income tax, register the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the Accompanying form (Geleideformulier), and the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm). A request remains timely until failing to file it would have irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties (onderdeel 14.2 of the old Besluit). In the published case the request could be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment was irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) was reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment is decisive. Position (standpunt) adopted 27-06-2022, published 12-01-2023 (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/). The version checked here is the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) of 24 October 2025, published in Stcrt. 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication, which replaces the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M. Its substantive changes against the old text include 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right that amounts to co-entitlement (medegerechtigdheid)), 3.4 (the holding approval widened to an indirect holding and to a new or activity-free daughter), 4.2.2 (lettered shares (letteraandelen) besides cumulative preference shares (cumulatief preferente aandelen), plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw contribution credit), 12 (a new eighth standard condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (the incidental tax advantage clarified), 13.2 (R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) in the pre-preceding period (voor-voorperiode)) and 15.3 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html). A consolidated version is BWBR0051693, which the sources for this file did not re-read.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001)

Without the art. 3.65 facility a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays IB on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The discontinuation deduction is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. Hidden reserves (stille reserves) realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Vpb tax return and deferral (uitstel)

A financial year equal to the calendar year: file before 1 June of the next year. A broken financial year: file within 5 months after its end. A short financial year ending 31 December: file before 1 June, and one ending in another month: before 1 April of the next calendar year. Online extension is only for a calendar financial year and runs to 1 November, requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken financial year or for more than 5 months, must be filed within 5 months after the financial year ends and must give reasons. The pages for these steps are current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting).

### Retention period for records (bewaartermijn administratie)

Art. 52 AWR (Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen) makes entities (lichamen) required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the paper profit and loss statement. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968: records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept for nine years following the year in which the use began, in effect ten years. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968: OSS records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction. The AWR wording is identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01).
