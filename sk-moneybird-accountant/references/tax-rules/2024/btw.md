## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This reference covers VAT for a Dutch sole trader, including a sole trader who may also own a BV, and the general VAT rules that affect domestic and cross-border transactions. The main rate structure, the old KOR and the ordinary filing rules applied in 2024. The old KOR turnover test used the current calendar year only, and 2024 was its final year before the new KOR rules took effect.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard VAT rate

The standard rate is 21% for supplies that do not qualify for the reduced rate, the zero rate or an exemption. The rate structure is set out in Article 9 of the Wet OB. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)

### Reduced VAT rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services listed in Tabel I. The listed goods include food, water, horticultural products, medicines and aids, art, collectibles and antiques, books and periodicals. Listed services include bicycle, shoe, leather-goods, clothing and household-linen repairs, hairdressing, certain work on homes, camping, cultural and recreational access, performing artists, sport and bathing, and passenger transport. The list also covers comparable electronic publications, news websites and apps unless they mainly carry advertising, video or audible music. The Belastingdienst also identifies related services such as supplying goods, selling food in hospitality, renting or repairing medical aids, lending books and lending art by the artist. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [goods at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [services at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)

Electronic supply or lending of publications and access to news websites qualified from 1 January 2020 under post b.21 of Tabel I. [Toelichting Tabel I, decision of 31 March 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to goods and services in Tabel II if the conditions set by general regulation are met. A zero-rated supply is still a taxable supply for input VAT purposes. This differs from an exempt supply. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

For an intra-EU supply of goods, the seller needs records showing the goods went to another EU country, the buyer’s valid VAT ID from another member state, and an opgaaf ICP that is timely, correct and complete. A late or incorrect ICP can cost the seller the zero rate and can lead to a fine. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Logies

In 2024, logies fell under the 9% rate. The later change to the rate for logies takes effect from 1 January 2026, so it does not change the 2024 rate. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Solar panels

The zero rate applies to the supply and installation of covered solar panels on or near homes from 1 January 2023. Covered items include integrated and non-integrated panels, cables, a PV-verdeler, mounting materials, optimizers and inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The 21% rate applies to items such as multifunctional panels, solar collectors for heat, panels on non-residential or public buildings, roof reinforcement, milling cables into walls, replacing a whole groupenkast, a smart meter, bird or snow guards, goods bought separately from the installation, heat pumps and batteries. Integrated panels on a new-build home are at 21% when supplied with the home, and at 0% when only the panels are supplied and installed. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html), [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen)

### VAT exemptions

Article 11, paragraph 1, parts a to w lists exemptions. These include certain supplies and letting of immovable property, care, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, universal postal services, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst also lists investment gold and canteens, and notes that the exemptions also apply to imports. The exemption depends on the particular legal conditions for the activity. [Wet OB, 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemption list](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Input VAT on zero-rated and exempt supplies

A business can deduct input VAT attributable to a zero-rated supply, subject to the ordinary deduction conditions. It cannot deduct input VAT attributable to exempt supplies. Where a business has both taxed and exempt turnover, it must account for the proportion attributable to each in the final return of the year. [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst exemption list](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Conditions for 0% intra-EU goods supply

The seller must retain proof of transport to another EU country, check that the buyer has a valid VAT ID from another member state, and report the transaction correctly in the ICP. These requirements apply together. A seller that fails to file a timely, correct and complete ICP risks losing the zero rate. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### KOR turnover ceiling

The 2024 KOR applied to an entrepreneur established in the Netherlands or with a fixed establishment there whose Dutch turnover in the calendar year did not exceed € 20.000. For 2024, the law tested turnover in the current calendar year only. It did not make prior-year turnover an entry condition. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)

The old KOR permitted no VAT deduction and no VAT amount on the invoice. A legal person such as a BV could also choose the KOR according to the Belastingdienst handbook. If the entrepreneur exceeded the ceiling, the exemption ended from the supply that crossed it. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR territorial eligibility and legal form

The old KOR required establishment in the Netherlands or a fixed establishment there. The handbook explains that legal persons, including a BV, could use the scheme. A sole trader and a BV should therefore assess their own VAT position and turnover separately in light of the rules applicable to each entrepreneur. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR notice, minimum participation and re-entry

A KOR notice had to reach the Belastingdienst at least four weeks before the tax period in which the exemption was to start. Participation lasted at least three years after commencement. After revocation, or after exceeding the ceiling, re-entry was not available for three years. On an overrun, ordinary VAT rules applied from the supply that crossed the threshold. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)

The handbook states that the KOR was unavailable for an option to tax letting of immovable property. It also says that an entrepreneur could leave the scheme only from the first day of an aangiftetijdvak. [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR without prior VAT registration notice

An entrepreneur not yet registered for VAT, with annual turnover of at most € 1.800, could apply the KOR without the usual notice. The policy decision applied this procedure retroactively to 1 January 2020. [Decision of 10 June 2021](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-30899.html)

### KOR investment-goods adjustment

Under the handbook’s explanation of the old KOR, herziening for investment goods used before the year was left out when the total adjustment was less than € 500 per year. [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Transition to the 2025 KOR

The transition provisions took effect on 1 October 2024. A participant in the old KOR was treated as having notified the Belastingdienst for the new scheme unless they opted out by 3 December 2024. A new transition notice also had to be made by that date. An entrepreneur whose Dutch turnover in 2024 exceeded € 20.000 was barred from entering the new KOR in 2025. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)

### Normal VAT return period

Quarterly filing was the usual period. An entrepreneur could request monthly or yearly filing by letter to the tax office. The Belastingdienst was to reply within six weeks, and a change began at the start of a new period. Late filing or payment could result in a monthly period being imposed. [Belastingdienst, change of VAT return period](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

A handbook for an earlier year stated that quarterly VAT payable above € 15.000 required monthly filing. The checked sources do not establish whether that trigger applied in 2024. See section 7. [Belastingdienst handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Yearly VAT return conditions

The checked handbooks for earlier and later years describe a yearly return for a natural person or a partnership of natural persons, without an Article 23 permit, when annual VAT is below € 1.883 and each listed intra-EU activity is below € 10.000. The yearly period also has conditions for intra-EU supplies, services, acquisitions and services received. The exact 2024 handbook could not be verified, so the 2024 amounts and conditions remain UNVERIFIED. [Belastingdienst handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst handbook 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Periodic return and payment deadline

A periodic VAT return is due within a period set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the tax period. Payment is due within one month after the period. The Belastingdienst states that monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the month after the month or quarter. A yearly return is due before 1 April of the following year, which puts the 2024 yearly return deadline on 31 March 2025. The filing date is the day the return is received. Filing and payment share the same due date. Extension is available only in the event of a calamity. [AWR, 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst VAT dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Published calendar of 2024 VAT dates

The checked current VAT date page did not provide a separate published calendar for 2024. The general deadline rule still applies, but a year-specific date table was not verified. [Belastingdienst VAT dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Invoice issue deadline

An entrepreneur must invoice supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. An invoice is generally not needed for a private customer, subject to exceptions. The invoice must be issued by the 15th day of the month after the month of supply. The handbook gives an example of a supply on 10 April invoiced by 15 May. [Belastingdienst invoice rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Belastingdienst handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

The invoice must include the supplier’s and customer’s names and addresses, a VAT ID with NL where required, the KVK number where registered, the invoice date, a unique consecutive number, a description and quantity or scope, the date of supply or prepayment, the amounts excluding VAT per rate, the unit price, the rate and the VAT amount. For a fiscal unity, the invoice uses the VAT ID of the supplying part. [Belastingdienst invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

VAT is due no later than the period in which the customer pays. Digital invoicing requires the customer’s agreement and assurance of authenticity of origin, content integrity and readability. [Belastingdienst invoice rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/)

### Simplified invoice

A simplified invoice may be used for an invoice of at most € 100 including VAT, or for a correction invoice. It must contain the issue date, name and address, the goods or services, the VAT amount or information needed to calculate it, and a reference to the original invoice for a correction. The simplified form is not available for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or reverse-charged supplies in a state where the supplier is not established. [Belastingdienst simplified-invoice rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur)

### BUA threshold and own contribution

The Besluit uitsluiting aftrek omzetbelasting, or BUA, excludes deduction for certain staff provisions, gifts and business gifts. It also covers certain housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport. From 1 January 2024, test the total cost per recipient against € 227 per book year without subtracting that recipient’s own contribution. A deduction exclusion does not apply up to the VAT charged to the recipient. [BUA, version applying from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [decision ending subtraction of the own contribution](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### BUA commuting bicycle exception

The BUA does not exclude deduction for a commuting bicycle costing at most € 749 including VAT when the stated conditions are met. The person must not have received a bicycle in the year or two years before, and other commuting transport must not have covered at least half of their travel days. The BUA also has carve-outs for food and drink for staff, licensed closed bus transport for commuting, and outplacement. [BUA, version applying from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### BUA staff meals

For staff meals charged below cost plus 25%, the deduction excluded is up to 9% of the difference. Apply the rule to the relevant staff meals and charge made. [BUA, version applying from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Private-use car adjustment

Private use includes commuting. Without records, the adjustment is 2,7% of the catalogue price including VAT and BPM. The 1,5% rate applies when no purchase VAT could be deducted, and after the fourth year following the year the car first came into use. The amount is capped at the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of the VAT on purchase during the relevant period. The adjustment is reduced proportionally for exempt use and calculated pro rata for a part-year. Report it in the last return of the year. [Decision on VAT for private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Belastingdienst private-use car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak)

### Last VAT return of the year

The last return of the year settles private use of goods and services, a business car, the proportion of taxed and exempt turnover, and staff provisions, gifts and business gifts above the BUA threshold. The handbooks also identify utilities, fictitious services and investment-goods adjustments. Correct an already-filed final return by submitting a suppletie. [Belastingdienst final VAT return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### Suppletie correction period and timing

Correct VAT returns for the past five calendar years as soon as possible, using the method specified by the inspector, if the taxpayer knows or should suspect the inspector does not already know. The 2024 return period can be corrected through the end of 2029. The statutory eight-week deadline for reporting a discovered error starts on 1 January 2025 and does not set the 2024 timing rule. [UBOB, 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html)

### Suppletie small-correction boundary

The 2024 edition of the handbook is no longer published and the only archived copy is an 11-page extract without the VAT chapter, so the exact 2024 boundary for reporting a small correction in the next return rather than by suppletie could not be confirmed. See section 7. [Handbook page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/handboek-ondernemen), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/index)

### Reverse-charge legal provisions

The general rules are in Article 12 of the Wet OB. They shift the tax to the customer in specified cases, including cases involving suppliers not established in the Netherlands. The detailed domestic categories are in UBOB Articles 24b, 24ba and 24bb. The supplier remains responsible for applying the rule correctly. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [UBOB, 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst reverse-charge cases](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/)

Cases include foreign suppliers, subcontracting and staff lending in construction, shipbuilding, cleaning and gardening, waste and scrap, specified electronics, immovable property, execution sales, gas and electricity certificates, gold, emission rights and certain telecom services. In construction and staff lending, exceptions include work done more than half in the supplier’s own workshop, staff lent for that work and subordinate work on goods sold. The parties can direct disputes about subcontracting to the main contractor’s tax office. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge cases](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [subcontracting exceptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/bij_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen/uitzonderingen_onderaanneming_en_personeel_uitlenen)

For the specified electronics, the threshold is € 10.000 or more per delivery per type of good. The supplier invoices without VAT and states “btw verlegd”, the customer’s VAT ID and the consideration per rate. [UBOB, 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst reverse-charge invoicing](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

The customer reports domestic reverse-charged VAT in rubric 2, and VAT charged to the customer from abroad in rubric 4. The customer may deduct it if the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### Intra-EU B2B services

For a general B2B service to a customer in another EU country, VAT is usually due in the customer’s country. Invoice without VAT, mark “btw verlegd”, check the customer’s VAT ID, report the service on the VAT return and include it in the ICP. Exceptions apply, including some services connected with immovable property, passenger transport and admission to events. Some services exempt or zero-rated in the customer’s country and OSS services are not listed in the ICP. [Belastingdienst services to EU customers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen), [ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### ICP goods threshold and frequency

Report intra-EU supplies of goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. The ICP total must agree with rubric 3b for the same return period. Goods are reported in the period of the invoice date, and services in the period in which the service was supplied. No ICP is due for a period with nothing to report. Corrections go in the next ICP. The form permits at most 100 lines per rubric. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp)

Goods are normally reported monthly. Quarterly filing is allowed while goods supplies stay at or below € 50.000 in the quarter and in each of the previous four quarters. If the threshold is exceeded in the first month, file for each of the three months. If it is exceeded in the second month, report the first two months and then the final month. If it is exceeded in the third month, file quarterly for that period and monthly afterwards. After four consecutive quarters at or below the threshold, quarterly filing can resume from the fifth quarter if that quarter also stays below it. [Belastingdienst ICP periods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)

Services may be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with a permit. The € 50.000 goods test does not apply to services. Goods and services can be reported separately or together. A combined report follows the goods rules. The 2024 guide gives a domestic deadline within one month after the chosen period, including a two-monthly option. For 2024, quarterly ICP deadlines were 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January of the next year. A yearly ICP required a permit and was available to natural persons and partnerships of natural persons, with a 31 January deadline. Businesses not established in the Netherlands had two months. [2024 ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Call-off stock

The call-off stock simplification requires an agreement with a known buyer, the buyer’s VAT ID, transport by the supplier, no establishment in the arrival state and a register. Identify the buyer in the ICP for the period the transport began. The goods must be supplied within 12 months of arrival. [2024 ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### EU distance-sales threshold

The € 10.000 threshold applies to qualifying intra-EU distance sales of goods and digital services to consumers. For goods, the seller arranges or pays for transport from the Netherlands to a consumer in another EU country. Margin goods, nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies are excluded from the described goods rule. The threshold applies only when the supplier is established in one member state and was not exceeded in either the current or previous calendar year. Measure it excluding VAT. [Wet OB, 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

Below the threshold, the seller can opt for the VAT rate of the customer’s country. The choice lasts at least two years. If the threshold is exceeded, apply the foreign rate from the transaction that crosses it. Dutch VAT can apply again from 1 January after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The threshold does not apply where the supplier has a fixed establishment in another EU country. A warehouse alone is not a fixed establishment. [Belastingdienst distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

### OSS and import scheme

The Union scheme uses one return per quarter for covered EU distance sales and services. File by the last day of the month after the quarter, including a nil return. It can be used from the first day of the quarter after registration. For a first supply, the registration must arrive by the 10th day of the month after the month of supply. Three consecutive missed returns lead to exclusion for two years. Do not resubmit a return. Put corrections in a later return within three years of the original due date. Keep the OSS records for ten years. Returns use euros and the European Central Bank rate for the last day of the quarter. [Belastingdienst one-stop-shop guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

The import scheme is monthly and covers consignments of at most € 150. Three consecutive missed monthly returns lead to exclusion for two years. The claim that two years without supplies ends participation while returns are still being filed is UNVERIFIED and is not used here. [Belastingdienst one-stop-shop guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### National B2B e-invoicing duty

The checked value for 2024 says there was no national B2B e-invoicing duty, but that value is UNVERIFIED. Suppliers to central government must send e-invoices. Municipalities, provinces and water boards may require them, and Peppol is named as a channel. [Government business portal on e-invoicing](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/)

### EU KOR

The EU KOR was not in force in 2024. The published law took effect from 2025. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### VAT rates on domestic sales

**Calculated example.** A sale of goods subject to the standard rate has a net price of € 1.000.

1. Net amount: € 1.000.
2. VAT at 21%: € 1.000 × 21% = € 210.
3. Invoice total: € 1.000 + € 210 = € 1.210.

For a qualifying 9% supply with the same net amount, VAT is € 1.000 × 9% = € 90, and the invoice total is € 1.000 + € 90 = € 1.090. For a qualifying zero-rated supply, VAT is € 1.000 × 0% = € 0. The applicable rates and conditions are set out in section 2. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)

### Exempt versus zero-rated purchase

**Calculated example.** A business buys an input used only for a taxable zero-rated supply. The input price is € 1.000 and its invoice shows € 210 input VAT at 21%.

1. Output VAT on the zero-rated sale is calculated at 0%.
2. Input VAT attributable to that zero-rated supply remains deductible, subject to the deduction conditions.
3. If the same input is used only for an exempt supply, the € 210 is not deductible.

This example illustrates the difference between a zero rate and an exemption. [Belastingdienst handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### KOR overrun

**Official example.** Turnover is € 19.750 on 15 September 2024. A € 500 advance received the next day raises 2024 turnover above € 20.000. The ordinary VAT rules apply from 16 September, and the entire € 500 advance is taxed. [Belastingdienst KOR opt-out guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor)

### Solar panels

**Calculated example.** A home installation that qualifies for the zero rate has a net price of € 5.000.

1. Net amount: € 5.000.
2. VAT at 0%: € 5.000 × 0% = € 0.
3. Invoice total: € 5.000 + € 0 = € 5.000.

The example assumes the supply and installation meet the covered conditions. It does not apply to items or installations subject to 21%. [Decision of 16 June 2023](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### Private-use car

**Calculated example.** Assume a business car has a catalogue price of € 30.000 including VAT and BPM. No private-use records are available, and the 2,7% rate applies.

1. € 30.000 × 2,7% = € 810.
2. Compare the result with the applicable cap, which is based on VAT deducted in the year and the relevant purchase VAT.
3. Report no more than the applicable cap in the last return of the year.

The example does not determine the cap because the amount of VAT deducted and the car’s use history are not specified. [Decision on VAT for private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

### BUA gifts and staff provisions

**Official example.** A gift costs € 220, an employee contributes € 100, and a Christmas package costs € 80. Input VAT deducted on the combined € 300 is € 63. VAT due on the € 100 contribution is € 21. The remaining repayment is € 63 − € 21 = € 42. For 2024, compare the total cost per recipient with the € 227 BUA threshold without subtracting the contribution. [Belastingdienst BUA threshold and own-contribution guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken), [BUA decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### Invoice deadline

**Official example.** If the supply is delivered on 10 April, the invoice is due by 15 May. The invoice should state the amount excluding VAT, the applicable rate and the VAT amount. [Belastingdienst handbook 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

### Reverse charge

**Calculated example.** A domestic reverse-charged service has a net amount of € 1.000 and is subject to 21%.

1. Supplier invoices € 1.000 without VAT and marks the invoice “btw verlegd”.
2. Customer calculates VAT: € 1.000 × 21% = € 210.
3. Customer reports the € 210 as reverse-charged VAT.
4. If the service is used for taxable activity and ordinary deduction conditions are met, the customer may deduct the same € 210.

The supplier must still apply the reverse-charge rule correctly. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### EU distance sales

**Calculated example.** A supplier established in one member state has qualifying distance sales and digital services of € 9.000 in the current calendar year and then makes another qualifying sale of € 2.000.

1. The first amount is € 9.000.
2. Add the next sale: € 9.000 + € 2.000 = € 11.000.
3. The total is above the € 10.000 threshold.
4. The foreign VAT rule applies from the transaction that crosses the threshold.

This assumes the previous-year test and the other threshold conditions are met. [Belastingdienst distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- From 1 January 2024, an employee’s own contribution is no longer subtracted when testing total BUA costs against € 227 per recipient. In 2023, the own contribution could reduce the tested amount. [2023 BUA decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html), [BUA decision ending subtraction from 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)
- The KOR ceiling and current-year turnover test remained the same. The 2024 test used the current calendar year only. The old regime’s minimum participation and re-entry rules also remained in place. [Wet OB, 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)
- The new KOR law had been published in 2023 but was not yet in force for 2024. Its transition provisions took effect on 1 October 2024, ahead of the new rules taking effect in 2025. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)
- The 21%, 9% and zero-rate structure did not change from 2023. The 0% solar-panel rule began in 2023 and continued in 2024. [Wet OB, 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [solar-panel decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

Apply the BUA test per recipient without reducing the tested amount by the employee’s own contribution. [Belastingdienst final-return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [BUA decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

Sources: [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- **Yearly return deadline wording is consistent.** “Before 1 April” and “31 March” put the deadline on the same final day. Sources: [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst VAT dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Car adjustment descriptions are consistent.** The 1,5% rate is described as applying after the fourth year following first use, and also as applying after more than five years in use when the year of purchase counts as a full year. The cap is the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT during the relevant period. Source: [Decision on VAT for private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).
- **BUA threshold location is settled.** The € 227 threshold is total cost per recipient per book year under Article 4. From 1 January 2024, the own contribution does not reduce the amount tested. Sources: [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [decision ending subtraction from 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).
- **Reverse-charge article range is corrected.** The detailed UBOB provisions are Articles 24b, 24ba and 24bb. Article 24 has lapsed, and Article 24a belongs to a different section. Source: [UBOB, 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01).
