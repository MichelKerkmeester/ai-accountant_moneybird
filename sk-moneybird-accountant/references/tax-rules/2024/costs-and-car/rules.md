## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel)

This applies to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) and recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters) with costs for food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), representation expenses (representatie), including receptions, festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak), and conferences (congressen), seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and similar events. These costs are not deductible up to € 5.600 for 2024, so only the part above € 5.600 counts as costs ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 1, via art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Travel and accommodation that belong to these cost categories count as part of them ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Exception: the restriction does not apply when the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of the turnover ([art. 3.15 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The amount is not time-proportioned when the enterprise starts during the calendar year, and it is not increased for a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food (voedsel) covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including tips (fooien), drink covers coffee (koffie), tea (thee), milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank), stimulants (genotmiddelen) covers cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren), and food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals to staff the restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wages in kind (loon in natura) ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The threshold (drempel) is indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 3.15 en art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.2 Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities ([art. 3.15 lid 5, gelezen met de wetteksten 2020 tot en met 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). With this election the € 5.600 threshold does not apply. The choice is made in the return, so a sole trader can compare both outcomes and pick the higher deduction for 2024.

### 3.3 Corporate income tax (Vpb): replacement of the threshold and optional percentage (keuzeprocent)

For a BV the art. 3.15 restriction applies under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 when the body has one or more employees in the sense of the Wet LB in the year ([Wet Vpb 1969, art. 8 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [Wet Vpb 1969, versie 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The fixed amount of € 5.600 is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The checked material verifies this text in the 2020 and 2026 versions of the Wet Vpb 1969 ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [Wet Vpb 1969, 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.4 Travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf) for conferences and courses (cursussen)

Travel and accommodation for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study (studie) and profession (beroep) and for conferences, seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and similar events are not deductible above € 1.500 per year, so at most € 1.500 counts as costs ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f en lid 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Exception: the cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary ([same article](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f en lid 8, alle jaargangen 2020 tot en met 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Interaction: the same travel and accommodation count as part of the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) under art. 3.15 lid 2, so they also fall within the € 5.600 threshold or the 80% choice ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.5 Mileage allowance for a private vehicle (kilometervergoeding privévervoermiddel)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (car, motorcycle (motor), bicycle (fiets)) are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. For 2024 the amount is € 0,23 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The amount is all-in, so fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), tol and parking (parkeren) cannot be deducted separately ([art. 3.15 lid 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Household members are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.15 lid 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 first (eerste) lid only ([art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.6 Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

### 3.7 Relocation (verhuizing) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

Moving costs for the enterprise are the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7.750 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Costs of housing outside the place of residence, double accommodation, are deductible for at most two years ([art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The amounts and the two-year period are unchanged from 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.17, jaargangen 2020 tot en met 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.8 Work space at home (werkruimte thuis)

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) are not deductible unless the workspace is a self-contained (zelfstandig) part of the home according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting) and an income test is satisfied ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). With another workspace outside the home the combined profit (winst), wage (loon) and income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace they must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it ([art. 3.16 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Art. 3.16 lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is business assets ([art. 3.16 lid 13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The official factors for self-containment are an own entrance or stairway (opgang), own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained ([Belastingdienst, werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). The overview page prints Work space at home 0%, with exceptions (uitzonderingen) ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). 2024 is the last year without art. 3.16 lid 14. From 1 January 2025 that lid bars costs connected with an owned home in the business assets that serves as main residence (hoofdverblijf) when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private, and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) for a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workspace ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, versie 2025-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [MvT Belastingplan 2025, sectie 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)).

### 3.9 Home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis)

Telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home are not deductible ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Practical rule: a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). The overview page prints Home phone subscription 0% and Business telephone calls (zakelijke telefoongesprekken) at home 100% ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.10 Other fixed exclusions (overige vaste uitsluitingen)

Not deductible in 2024: literature (literatuur) except professional literature (vakliteratuur) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)), clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel c, met werkkleding omschreven in lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)), personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)), and the costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other such equipment (apparatuur) and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) ([art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). So private computers and equipment are 0% deductible. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.11 Laptop and computer, labelling (etikettering)

The income-tax treatment of a laptop or computer follows asset classification (vermogensetikettering). Equipment and instruments (instrumenten) that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business must be classified as part of the compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). When used 90% or more for business, it must be classified as part of the mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). Between those thresholds, it is freely classifiable (keuzevermogen) within reasonable bounds ([Belastingdienst, vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). The asset-classification choice can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use of an asset in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule ([Kennisgroepstandpunt KG:213:2026:1, 6 maart 2026](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/)).

### 3.12 Small purchase (kleine aanschaf)

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example small office supplies (kleine kantoorartikelen), professional literature and maintenance (onderhoud) of the car ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Receipts and invoices must be kept ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).

### 3.13 Remuneration (beloning) partner

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5.000 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.16 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.14 Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen)

Not deductible under art. 3.14 lid 1 of the Wet inkomstenbelasting: costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines (onderdeel c), costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a penal order (strafbeschikking) became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and penalty payments under section (afdeling) 5.3.2 General Administrative Law Act (Awb) (onderdeel i) ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.14 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.14, jaargangen 2020 tot en met 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fines (verkeersboeten) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The overview page prints Fines 0% ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Timing rule: costs made before the fine (geldboete) is imposed do not relate (houden verband) to the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment (dwangsom) the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught ([Kennisgroepstandpunt, gepubliceerd 11 maart 2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)). Criminal offence scope: only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by a transaction (transactie) or penal order, are excluded ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### 3.15 Car, asset classification (vermogensetikettering)

A car must be classified as part of the mandatory business assets when used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. If used 90% or more privately, it must be classified as part of the compulsory private assets. All other cases are freely classifiable assets ([Belastingdienst, vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

### 3.16 Private-use addition (bijtelling) car, standard (standaard)

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year ([Belastingdienst, privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private ([art. 3.20 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself, so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation ([art. 3.20 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Car means passenger car (personenauto) or van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods ([art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)), for which the private kilometres times the actual cost per kilometre are settled. A declaration of exclusive business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible ([art. 3.20 lid 6 tot en met lid 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.17 Car from before 2017

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 10a.4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months ([art. 10a.4, jaargangen 2020 tot en met 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars ([art. 10a.4 en art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.18 Youngtimer

For a car first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, 35% of the value of the car is a withdrawal per year instead of 22% ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For 2024 the age threshold is 15 years ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b en lid 5 onderdeel b, jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). This is the youngtimer rule. For an old car the value (waarde) is the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer), see 3.20.

### 3.19 Private use of a car up to 500 km per year

With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another way of showing at most 500 private kilometres, the withdrawal is nil ([Belastingdienst, privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)).

### 3.20 Value of the car

The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes it as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT (btw) and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued, and the RDW site carries it for most cars ([Belastingdienst, waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). For an old car the market value replaces it ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)), and when the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car in the business assets is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price ([Belastingdienst, waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)).

### 3.21 Zero-emission, first admission (eerste toelating) 2024

For a car with 0 g CO2 per km the 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value, capped in euros ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For a car first admitted in 2024 the reduction is 6% of the value with a maximum of € 1.800, which means 16% up to € 30.000 ([art. 3.20 lid 2, jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.22 Zero-emission, hydrogen (waterstof), solar cells (zonnecellen) and fixed period (periode)

Cars on hydrogen are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped, and from the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 2, jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission, and after that the rules then in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.23 Private-use addition bicycle

When a bicycle (fiets, including an e-bike or speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20a, jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Availability for commuting counts as private availability ([art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). A lid 5 added with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address ([art. 3.20a, versie 2026-01-01, met de terugwerkende kracht die ook in de teksten 2020 tot en met 2025 staat](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### 3.24 VAT on the private car (privéauto) in the business

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet) ([Belastingdienst, zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, although it is business for the income tax (inkomstenbelasting). In a BV the DGA (director and substantial shareholder) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) but counts as an employee for this purpose ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).

### 3.25 Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek)

Costs of a study are deductible as a business cost when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw ([Belastingdienst, studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers)). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f ([Belastingdienst, overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten) and [Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) for training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) existed up to and including 2021: deductible above a threshold of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15.000, with no deduction when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 6.27 tot en met 6.30, jaargang 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Act on the abolition of the tax deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Decree (Besluit) of 12 July 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, jaargang 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 10a.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). For 2024 the deduction is nil except in the performance grant case ([FISIN 2024, aftrek studiekosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)).

### 3.26 Performance grant, caps and threshold

When the diploma period (diplomatermijn, usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant ([FISIN 2024, aftrek studiekosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)). Caps: € 1.693 per study year (mbo) and € 2.443 per study year (hbo or wo), part-year € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo). When the actual performance grant was lower, the lower amount applies. The € 250 threshold applies per person ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)). The case is verified in FISIN for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 ([FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)).

### 3.27 STAP-budget

The STAP-budget was the non-fiscal replacement for the training deduction (scholingsaftrek) and took effect at the same moment as the abolition of that deduction. Art. 29 of the scheme (regeling) links its start to the abolition Act, and Stb. 2021, 349 states it applies from 1 January 2022 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). The maximum subsidy was € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) (art. 7) and there were six application windows per calendar year, and the scheme is marked lapsed per 01-01-2024 ([Regeling STAP-budget, versie 2023-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01)). There is therefore no STAP subsidy for 2024, and this does not restore the fiscal study cost deduction ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, jaargang 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) and [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)).

### 3.28 KOR and the cost base (kostengrondslag)

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, in the KOR case (small businesses scheme, kleineondernemersregeling), deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted, and VAT paid in another EU country is not deducted either, earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening), the low motor vehicle tax (mrb) rate for vans stays available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter, and exit is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year. The choice is per entrepreneur (ondernemer) and covers all sub-numbers (subnummers) ([Belastingdienst, kleineondernemersregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)). The checked material notes that the KOR threshold history by year belongs to the VAT topic ([same page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
