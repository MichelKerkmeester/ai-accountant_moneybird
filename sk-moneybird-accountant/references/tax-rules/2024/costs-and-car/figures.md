## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the deductibility of business costs and the car rules for tax year 2024 for a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) and, where noted, a BV. The most important 2024 figures are the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) of € 5.600, the € 0,23 kilometre allowance for a private vehicle, the 22% private-use addition (bijtelling) for a car that is available for private use, and the 6% zero-emission reduction capped at € 1.800 for a car first admitted in 2024. The fiscal study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) no longer exists except in the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case, and the STAP-budget scheme (regeling) is marked lapsed per 1 January 2024. All 2024 values in this file are enacted and Verified, and the law for 2024 is in force.
