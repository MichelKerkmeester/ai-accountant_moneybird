## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel): € 5.100 in 2023 to € 5.600 in 2024 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 1 en art. 10.1, jaargang 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).
- Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding): € 0,21 in 2023 to € 0,23 in 2024 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 6 en art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, jaargang 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).
- Zero-emission solar cells (zonnecellen): in the 2023 text (tekst) solar cells with a power (vermogen) of at least (ten minste) 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead (accu zonder lood) were uncapped (onbeperkt). From (vanaf) the 2024 text the test (toets) is that the panel capacity (paneelvermogen) in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by the consumption (verbruik) in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 2, jaargang 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).
- STAP-budget: 2023 still had a maximum of € 1.000 including VAT (inclusief btw) per training (scholing), and 2023 was the last year (jaar) of the scheme (regeling). From 01-01-2024 the scheme has lapsed (vervallen) ([Regeling STAP-budget, versie 2023-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01)).
- New (nieuw) in 2024: the knowledge group position (kennisgroepstandpunt) of 11 March 2024 on the deduction (aftrek) of costs (kosten) and expenses (lasten) connected with (in verband met) fines (geldboeten) and penalty payments (dwangsommen) ([Kennisgroepstandpunt, 11 maart 2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).
- Unchanged (ongewijzigd) from 2023 to 2024: the 80% election (keuze) and optional percentage (keuzeprocent) for the corporate income tax (Vpb), the € 1.500 cap for travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf), € 7.750 relocation costs (verhuiskosten) and the two years for double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting), 0% for a work space (werkruimte) at home (thuis), a home phone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis), private computers and fines (geldboetes), the 10% and 90% labelling (etikettering) thresholds, the € 450 rule (regel), the € 5.000 partner threshold (partnergrens), the 22% standard private-use addition (standaardbijtelling), at least 25% for a car from before 2017, 35% youngtimer with a threshold (grens) of 15 years, the 7% private-use addition for a bicycle (fietsbijtelling), the 6% zero-emission credit (korting) with a maximum of € 1.800 and 16% up to € 30.000, the FISIN caps and threshold (drempel) for the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) (€ 1.693 and € 141,09 for mbo, € 2.443 and € 203,59 for hbo or wo, € 250), and the nil result (resultaat) of the study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) except in the performance grant case (prestatiebeurszaak) ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, jaargang 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aftrek_studiekosten) and [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record every business cost with the receipt or invoice, and keep receipts and invoices ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Record costs excluding VAT (btw) when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). Under the KOR record the costs including VAT in the IB return ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten) and [Belastingdienst, kleineondernemersregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- Record the mixed costs (gemengde kosten) posts separately, because the € 5.600 threshold (drempel) or the 80% choice applies, and make that choice in the return ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).
- Record the business and private parts of a mixed cost separately. Costs with a dominant personal character are not deductible at all ([Belastingdienst, zakelijk of privé](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive)).
- Keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or other evidence for the car if the withdrawal (onttrekking) should be nil at up to 500 private kilometres. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed conclusive (sluitend) ([Belastingdienst, privégebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)).
- Keep a kilometre administration for the VAT on a private car, or apply the 1,5% year-end correction in rubriek 1d ([Belastingdienst, zakelijk gebruik van de privéauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Private use of an asset in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) and belongs in the books as such ([Belastingdienst, vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).
- Record a purchase under € 450 as a cost of the year and put a business asset of € 450 or more that lasts longer than a year on the balance sheet with depreciation ([Belastingdienst, welke kosten mag ik aftrekken](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Put the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) deduction in the IB return for the year in which the diploma period (diplomatermijn) ends, subject to the € 250 threshold per person ([FISIN 2024, aftrek studiekosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten)).
- Record fines and penalty payments (dwangsommen) as non-deductible and keep the date of imposition, because costs before the fine stay deductible and costs after it that relate to the fine do not ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.14 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [Kennisgroepstandpunt, 11 maart 2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).
- For a BV with one or more employees in the Wet LB sense, apply the corporate income tax (Vpb) variant, the higher of € 5.600 and 0,4% of the wage total, with an optional percentage (keuzeprocent) of 73,5% ([Wet Vpb 1969, art. 8 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, sectie 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No value in section 2 is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown, and the checked material lists no open points for 2024. The following points need care.

- Solar cells (zonnecellen). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on the 2026 private-use addition (bijtelling) says at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) for the uncapped solar exemption ([Belastingdienst, bijtelling privégebruik auto 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026)). The statute says 1 kilowatt peak only in the 2021 to 2023 texts and requires from the 2024 text a panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km of at least 7 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, art. 3.20 lid 2, jaargang 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). For 2024 the statute decides, so this file uses the ratio test of at least 7.
- Work space at home (werkruimte thuis), owner route. For 2024 the statute contains no art. 3.16 lid 14 ([Wet inkomstenbelasting, jaargang 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page offers a deduction for a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workspace when the home is owned and in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) ([Belastingdienst, werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)), while the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) for Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) describes lid 14, effective from 2025, as a codification of case law and names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) for a non-self-contained workspace ([MvT Belastingplan 2025, sectie 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). The checked material does not state how the owner route stood in 2024. The underlying case law or a published position (standpunt) would settle it.
- Wet Vpb 1969, art. 8 lid 5. The 0,4% of the wage total and the 73,5% optional percentage (keuzeprocent) are verified in the 2020 and 2026 texts of the statute ([Wet Vpb 1969, versie 2020-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [versie 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The 2024 text of the Wet Vpb 1969 was not read separately in the checked material. Reading the 2024 text of art. 8 lid 5 would settle it.
- KOR turnover threshold (omzetgrens). The € 20.000 is verified on the current KOR page, and the checked material notes that the KOR threshold history by year belongs to the VAT (BTW) topic ([Belastingdienst, kleineondernemersregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)). The 2024-specific threshold history is not in this file. The VAT topic file for 2024 would settle it.
- Practical pages without a year label. The € 450 rule, the 10% and 90% labelling (etikettering) thresholds, the 100% for public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig), the telephone (telefoon) percentages and the 1,5% VAT correction come from Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages that carry no year label. The checked material marks them Verified on those pages and notes that the statutory basis is unchanged from 2020 to 2027 where it states this.
