## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This reference covers VAT for a Dutch sole trader, including a sole trader who may also own a BV, and the general VAT rules that affect domestic and cross-border transactions. The main rate structure, the old small businesses scheme (KOR) and the ordinary filing rules applied in 2024. The old KOR turnover test used the current calendar year only, and 2024 was its final year before the new KOR rules took effect.
