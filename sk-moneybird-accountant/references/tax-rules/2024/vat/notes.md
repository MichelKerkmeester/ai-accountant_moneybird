## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- From 1 January 2024, an employee’s own contribution is no longer subtracted when testing total BUA costs against € 227 per recipient. In 2023, the own contribution could reduce the tested amount. [2023 BUA decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html), [BUA decision ending subtraction from 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)
- The KOR ceiling and current-year turnover test remained the same. The 2024 test used the current calendar year only. The old regime’s minimum participation and re-entry rules also remained in place. [Wet OB, 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01)
- The new KOR law had been published in 2023 but was not yet in force for 2024. Its transition provisions took effect on 1 October 2024, ahead of the new rules taking effect in 2025. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)
- The 21%, 9% and zero-rate structure did not change from 2023. The 0% solar-panel rule began in 2023 and continued in 2024. [Wet OB, 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2023-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [solar-panel decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase invoices with the required parties, dates, descriptions, amounts by VAT rate and VAT amounts. Issue invoices to other entrepreneurs and legal persons by the 15th day of the month after supply. [Belastingdienst invoice rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)
- Separate zero-rated turnover from exempt turnover. Keep the records needed to establish eligibility for the zero rate, including transport evidence and the buyer’s valid VAT ID for an intra-EU goods supply. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- If using the KOR, do not charge VAT on invoices and do not deduct input VAT. Track Dutch turnover against the 2024 current-year ceiling. Keep the notice and any opt-out or transition records. [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html)
- File and pay for the assigned period by the applicable deadline. Report domestic reverse-charge transactions in rubric 2 and reverse-charge VAT from abroad in rubric 4. Deduct reverse-charged VAT only when the purchase is used for taxable activity. [Belastingdienst return and payment guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)
- Reconcile the ICP with rubric 3b for the same period. Record intra-EU goods by invoice date and services in the period supplied. File no ICP for a period with nothing to report. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp)
- In the last return of the year, account for private use, the business car, mixed taxable and exempt turnover, relevant staff provisions, gifts and investment-goods adjustments. Apply the BUA test per recipient without reducing the tested amount by the employee’s own contribution. [Belastingdienst final-return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [BUA decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)
- Correct an earlier VAT return by suppletie within the applicable correction period and as soon as possible. The 2024 small-correction boundary is not verified. [UBOB, 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)
- For the Union OSS scheme, retain the records for the required retention period and file a nil return when there is no amount to report. A return cannot be resubmitted, so put corrections in a later return within the allowed period. [Belastingdienst one-stop-shop guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Yearly VAT return amount and 2024 conditions: UNVERIFIED.** The 2024 edition of the handbook is no longer published and the only archived copy is an 11-page extract without the VAT chapter. The value below € 1.883 and the related yearly-return conditions appear in the checked 2020 and 2026 handbooks, but that does not verify the exact 2024 wording. A complete copy of the 2024 edition or the matching FISIN 2024 page would settle it. Sources: [Handbook page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/handboek-ondernemen), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/index), [2020 handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [2026 handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).
- **Suppletie small-correction boundary: UNVERIFIED.** The 2024 edition of the handbook is no longer published and the only archived copy is an 11-page extract without the VAT chapter. A complete copy of that edition or the matching FISIN 2024 page would settle whether the 2024 rule allowed a small correction in the next return and the exact boundary. Sources: [Handbook page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/handboek-ondernemen), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/index).
- **Monthly filing trigger: UNVERIFIED for 2024.** The 2020 handbook says that quarterly VAT payable above € 15.000 triggers a monthly period. The current Belastingdienst page and 2026 handbook no longer state that trigger. A dated 2024 handbook or decision would settle whether it applied in 2024. Sources: [2020 handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [current period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [2026 handbook](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).
- **Published calendar of 2024 VAT dates: unknown.** No year-specific date table was found in the checked source. The general return and payment deadline is verified. A 2024 dated calendar would settle the missing date table. Source: [Belastingdienst VAT dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **National B2B e-invoicing duty in 2024: UNVERIFIED.** The checked value says none, but the available government business page establishes duties for central-government suppliers and possible requirements by other public bodies. A 2024 primary source specifically addressing private B2B invoicing would settle the value. Source: [Government business portal](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).
- **KOR turnover-test conflict is settled.** A baseline description said that both the application year and preceding year were tested. For 2024, Article 25 tested the current calendar year only and did not name the preceding year. The preceding-year test belongs to the rules from 2025. Sources: [Wet OB, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [Wet OB, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- **Yearly return deadline wording is consistent.** “Before 1 April” and “31 March” put the deadline on the same final day. Sources: [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst VAT dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- **Car adjustment descriptions are consistent.** The 1,5% rate is described as applying after the fourth year following first use, and also as applying after more than five years in use when the year of purchase counts as a full year. The cap is the VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT during the relevant period. Source: [Decision on VAT for private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04).
- **BUA threshold location is settled.** The € 227 threshold is total cost per recipient per book year under Article 4. From 1 January 2024, the own contribution does not reduce the amount tested. Sources: [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [decision ending subtraction from 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).
- **Reverse-charge article range is corrected.** The detailed UBOB provisions are Articles 24b, 24ba and 24bb. Article 24 has lapsed, and Article 24a belongs to a different section. Source: [UBOB, 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01).
