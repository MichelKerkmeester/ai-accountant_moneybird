## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### System 2024

Tax year 2024 uses the category method of the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) box 3, which applies to everyone from 1 January 2023. The law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, brought the category method in place of the old band method of 2020 to 2022. The 2024 calculation follows six steps. Step 1 sets the taxable return (belastbaar rendement) at bank balances (banktegoeden) times the bank percentage, plus other assets (overige bezittingen) times the other-assets percentage, minus the deductible part of the debts (schulden) times the debt percentage. Step 2 sets the return base (rendementsgrondslag) at assets (bezittingen) minus the deductible debts. Step 3 sets the tax base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen) at the return base minus the tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen). Partners may divide that tax base in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 sets the share (aandeel) at the tax base divided by the return base times 100. Step 5 sets the benefit (voordeel) at the taxable return times the share. Step 6 sets the tax at the box 3 rate times the benefit. A negative return is set at nil. The flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) is at least nil under art. 4.14 lid 1 and art. 5.2 lid 2. Art. 5.24 addresses reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage). Transactions around 1 January within a three-month period that starts before and ends after the reference date (peildatum) are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons lay behind them.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Reference date

The reference date for tax year 2024 is 1 January 2024. The values of bank balances, other assets and debts on that date enter the six-step calculation. The same date is the reference point for the green-investment exemption and the green investments credit (korting groene beleggingen). The reference date arbitrage rule of art. 5.24 uses a three-month window around 1 January. Transactions in that window are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons lay behind them.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Tax-free allowance

The tax-free allowance is € 57.000 per person for 2024. It doubles to € 114.000 with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint tax base allocated under art. 2.17. The amount reduces the return base to the tax base for savings and investments in step 3. It does not apply to the actual-return route. The Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) has determined that the actual return does not take the tax-free allowance into account.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Box 3 rate (tarief)

The box 3 rate for 2024 is 36%. It applies to the benefit from step 5. The tax is 36% of the benefit. The rate is in the statute and is confirmed on the 2024 calculation page. The rate interacts with the tax-free allowance and the category percentages. It does not apply to the return base directly, only to the benefit.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

The debt threshold for 2024 is € 3.700 per person. It is € 7.400 with a whole-year partner. Only the part of total debts above the threshold (drempel) is deductible. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes the threshold in the return. Statute art. 5.3 lid 3 writes the rule as "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...". The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. A joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns. The threshold enters step 1 and step 2. It reduces the deductible debt before the debt percentage is applied and before the return base is set.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01

### Bank percentage

The bank percentage for 2024 is 1,44%. It applies to bank balances. The percentage is fixed after the year under art. 10.6ter lid 2 of the statute. The method is the sum of the Dutch Central Bank (DNB) average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice, divided by twelve. So it is an average, not a twelve-month sum. The regulation of 7 March 2025, published on 14 March 2025, replaced the 0,92% figure and applied back to 1 January 2024. The 1 January 2024 text of the statute therefore carried 0,92% until the regulation replaced it. The bank percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Other-assets percentage

The other-assets percentage for 2024 is 6,04%. It applies to other assets. The figure is in the 1 January 2024 text of the statute. Under art. 10.6ter lid 3 the percentage is replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation. The other-assets percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage for 2024 is 2,61%. It applies to the deductible debt, so only the part of the debts above the threshold. The percentage is fixed after the year under art. 10.6ter lid 4. The method is the same as for the bank percentage, applied to the Dutch Central Bank average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The eleven monthly figures from 1 January to 30 November count, November is counted twice, and the sum is divided by twelve. The regulation of 7 March 2025 replaced 2,46% and applied back to 1 January 2024. The debt percentage enters step 1.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend

### Share rounding

The share in step 4 is rounded to 3 decimals on the 2024 calculation page. The page's examples also use 3 decimals. The rounding matters because the benefit in step 5 is the taxable return times the share. For 2024 the taxpayer uses 3 decimals.

Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

The green investment exemption for 2024 is € 71.251 per person. It is € 142.502 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 excludes green investments from the assets up to that amount. The exemption applies first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The exemption interacts with step 1 and step 2. Exempt green investments do not enter the bank balances or other assets. The green investments credit is then calculated on the exempt amount.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Green investments credit

The green investments credit for 2024 is 0,7% of the exempt amount on the reference date. Art. 8.19 gives the rate. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the credit automatically. For a whole-year partner the credit is split between the partners in the same ratio as the green balance. The 2024 rate of 0,7% applies in the period 2020 to 2024. The credit is a tax credit (heffingskorting), so it reduces the tax rather than the benefit. It interacts with the green investment exemption, because the exempt amount is the base.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen

### Actual-return reporting 2024

The rebuttal scheme (tegenbewijsregeling) of section (afdeling) 5.6 applies for 2024 under art. 5.25 and 5.26. If the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit, the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). The actual return is at least nil. Whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Art. 5.26 defines the actual return as all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus increase in value (vermogensaanwas). Income plus value changes over the calendar year count, including changes during the year. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No tax-free allowance is used. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation. The form is the form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur) under art. 5.25 lid 6, in deviation from General Administrative Law Act (Awb) arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Further documents can be sent up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8 says lids 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. For 2024 the route is the online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst), "Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven". The 2021 to 2024 years may always be filed without a letter. Paper forms are available via 0800 - 023 01 07 for one form per call, and several forms via the Tax information line (BelastingTelefoon) 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. If a recalculation for 2024 leads to personal deductions, it follows the partners' chosen split. Another split requires an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve-vermindering) request.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte

### Supreme Court of the Netherlands 6 June 2024

On 6 June 2024 the Supreme Court of the Netherlands ruled that the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) and the Bridge Act still conflict with the ECHR (EVRM) when the flat-rate return is higher than the actual return. The cases are ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income. The ruling interacts with the rebuttal scheme. A taxpayer who can make the lower actual return plausible can use that lower amount. The Supreme Court of the Netherlands ruling does not change the 2024 rates or thresholds in this file.
