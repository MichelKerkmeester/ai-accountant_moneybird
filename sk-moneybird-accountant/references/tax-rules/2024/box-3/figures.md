## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Tax year 2024 uses the enacted category method (categorieënmethode) of the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) box 3. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) calculates a fictitious return on bank balances (banktegoeden), other assets (overige bezittingen) and debts (schulden), and taxes the part above the tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) at 36%. For 2024 the percentages are 1,44% for bank balances, 6,04% for other assets and 2,61% for debts. Green investments (groene beleggingen) get a separate exemption and a credit (korting), and a taxpayer may report the actual return (werkelijk rendement) when it is lower than the fictitious benefit (voordeel), also after the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 6 June 2024.
