## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- System: the category method of the Bridge Act (Overbruggingswet) box 3 stays in force. It applied from 1 January 2023 and continues in 2024. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- Box 3 rate (tarief): 32% in 2023 to 36% in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen): unchanged at € 57.000 per person and € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Debt threshold (schuldendrempel): € 3.400 per person and € 6.800 with a whole-year partner in 2023 to € 3.700 per person and € 7.400 in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Bank percentage: 0,92% in 2023 to 1,44% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html
- Other-assets percentage: 6,17% in 2023 to 6,04% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Debt percentage: 2,46% in 2023 to 2,61% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html
- Share (aandeel) rounding: 2 decimals in 2023 to 3 decimals in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen): € 65.072 per person and € 130.144 with a whole-year partner in 2023 to € 71.251 and € 142.502 in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Green investments credit (korting groene beleggingen): unchanged at 0,7% in 2023 and 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Actual-return reporting: the 2024 route stays the online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) and may be filed without a letter, as in 2023. The Rebuttal Scheme Act box 3 (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) later placed the 2023 and 2024 rebuttal scheme in art. 5.25 and 5.26 with effect from 1 January 2023. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) 6 June 2024: a new ruling in the 2024 tax year. It holds that the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) and the Bridge Act still conflict with the ECHR (EVRM) when the flat-rate return (forfaitair rendement) is higher than the actual return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- Reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage): art. 5.24 with the three-month window around 1 January continues from 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the values of bank balances (banktegoeden), other assets (overige bezittingen) and debts (schulden) on 1 January 2024. The six-step calculation uses those values. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Record the green investments on 1 January 2024 and separate green other assets from green bank balances. The exemption applies first to green other assets. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes the debt threshold (schuldendrempel) in the return. Only the part of total debts above € 3.700 per person or € 7.400 with a whole-year partner is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- The box 3 income and the 36% tax land on the income tax return. The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is € 57.000 per person or € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- For the actual-return route for 2024, use the online form in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). The route may be used without a letter. Paper forms are available via 0800 - 023 01 07 for one form per call, and several forms via 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement
- Keep documents that support the actual return. If the tax inspector (inspecteur) sends the form, further documents can be sent up to six weeks after the form is sent. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If a 2024 recalculation leads to personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), the partners' chosen split applies. A different split requires an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) request. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 6 June 2024 affects the box 3 income, a letter follows. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
