## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- System: the category method of the Overbruggingswet box 3 stays in force. It applied from 1 January 2023 and continues in 2024. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- Box 3 tarief: 32% in 2023 to 36% in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Heffingsvrij vermogen: unchanged at € 57.000 per person and € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.400 per person and € 6.800 with a whole-year partner in 2023 to € 3.700 per person and € 7.400 in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Bank percentage: 0,92% in 2023 to 1,44% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html
- Other-assets percentage: 6,17% in 2023 to 6,04% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Debt percentage: 2,46% in 2023 to 2,61% in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html
- Aandeel rounding: 2 decimals in 2023 to 3 decimals in 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 65.072 per person and € 130.144 with a whole-year partner in 2023 to € 71.251 and € 142.502 in 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Korting groene beleggingen: unchanged at 0,7% in 2023 and 2024. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- Actual-return reporting: the 2024 route stays the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst and may be filed without a letter, as in 2023. The Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3 later placed the 2023 and 2024 tegenbewijsregeling in art. 5.25 and 5.26 with effect from 1 January 2023. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- Hoge Raad 6 June 2024: a new ruling in the 2024 tax year. It holds that the Wet rechtsherstel and the Overbruggingswet still conflict with the EVRM when the forfaitaire return is higher than the actual return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- Peildatumarbitrage: art. 5.24 with the three-month window around 1 January continues from 2023. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the values of banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden on 1 January 2024. The six-step calculation uses those values. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
- Record the green investments on 1 January 2024 and separate green overige bezittingen from green banktegoeden. The exemption applies first to green overige bezittingen. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- The Belastingdienst computes the schuldendrempel in the return. Only the part of total debts above € 3.700 per person or € 7.400 with a whole-year partner is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- The box 3 income and the 36% tax land on the income tax return. The heffingsvrij vermogen is € 57.000 per person or € 114.000 with a fiscal partner. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- For the actual-return route for 2024, use the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst. The route may be used without a letter. Paper forms are available via 0800 - 023 01 07 for one form per call, and several forms via 0800 - 0543. No deadline is stated on these pages. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement
- Keep documents that support the actual return. If the inspecteur sends the form, further documents can be sent up to six weeks after the form is sent. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If a 2024 recalculation leads to persoonsgebonden aftrek, the partners' chosen split applies. A different split requires an ambtshalve-vermindering request. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If the Hoge Raad ruling of 6 June 2024 affects the box 3 income, a letter follows. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024
