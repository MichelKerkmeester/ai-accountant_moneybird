## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

The published combined rate is 36,97% up to € 75.518, then 49,50%. In the statutory calculation, the first income-tax band is 9,32% up to € 38.098, with premie volksverzekeringen charged separately up to that ceiling. Above the ceiling, the income-tax rate is 36,97%, which produces the same combined rate through the next boundary. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### Box 1 at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer at AOW age who was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first-bracket rate is 19,07% up to € 38.098. The next rate is 36,97% up to € 75.518, followed by 49,50%. The lower first-bracket rate reflects that the AOW premium is no longer due once AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen)

### Box 1 at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer at AOW age born before 1 January 1946, the first boundary is € 40.021. The rates are 19,07% to that boundary, 36,97% up to € 75.518, then 49,50%. The higher boundary is set out in art. 2.10a. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 2.10a)

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

In the year AOW age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month in which that age is reached. The 2024 monthly rates are listed in section 2. The first bracket ends at € 38.098. The later brackets keep the ordinary rates. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening)

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling

The 27,65% premie volksverzekeringen rate applies to premie-inkomen up to € 38.098, the end of the first bracket. The maximum premie is € 10.534. The rate comprises AOW, Anw and Wlz premiedelen. AOW premium stops at AOW age. The supplied material does not state the continuing Anw and Wlz rule as a standalone rule, so that point is treated as an inference from the AOW-age rates. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen)

### Tariefsaanpassing

When qualifying grondslagverminderende posten are deducted, the tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 12,53% of the amount by which taxable Box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds € 75.518. The increase is capped at 12,53% of the deducted amount. As a result, deductions in this situation are worth at most 36,97%. The covered deductions include persoonsgebonden aftrek, aftrek kosten eigen woning, ondernemersfaciliteiten and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment. (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, TA)

### Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

The algemene heffingskorting (general tax credit) is available to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the tax return. An employer or benefit agency can also apply it through payroll. For 2024, the phase-out base is taxable income from work and home. The credit starts to reduce above € 24.812 and reaches nil at the top-bracket boundary. FISIN prints the nil point as € 75.518, while the year table prints € 75.519. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Algemene heffingskorting for a full year at AOW age

The year table gives a maximum credit of € 1.735 and a phase-out rate of 3,421% above € 24.812. FISIN states 3,420%. The credit reaches nil at the top-bracket boundary. Section 7 records this unresolved rate difference. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Arbeidskorting below AOW age

The arbeidskorting (employment tax credit) is based on arbeidsinkomen. For an IB-ondernemer, this includes winst uit onderneming before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. It excludes income earned as a medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser. The 2024 table builds the credit through its bands, then phases it out by 6,510% to nil. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Arbeidskorting for a full year at AOW age

The separate whole-year AOW table has lower build-up rates and a 3,358% phase-out in its top band. The band figures are in section 2. The ordinary arbeidskorting tables do not apply in the year AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)

### Arbeidskorting taken over from employer

If an employer applied a higher arbeidskorting and arbeidsinkomen is not above € 39.958, the Belastingdienst takes over the employer's higher amount up to the stated maximum. The maximum is € 5.532 below AOW age and € 2.854 for a full year at AOW age. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

The inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting (income-dependent combination credit) is 11,45% of arbeidsinkomen above € 6.073, up to the maximum in section 2. It is applied through the tax return or a provisional assessment, not through payroll. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### IACK for a full year at AOW age

For a full year at AOW age, the IACK rate is 5,90% and the maximum is € 1.521. The ordinary credit tables do not apply in the year AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)

### IACK child and residence test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2011 and must be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months. A Staatscourant approval also accepts a child who lived in the same household for 6 months. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months does not remove the credit. A qualifying child can be the taxpayer's own, step or adopted child, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship, or a fiscal partner's child. A pleegkind qualifies only when raised and maintained as one's own and no pleegvergoeding is received. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES can qualify. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting)

### IACK co-ouder test

A co-ouder must provide roughly equal care, with at least 156 days per calendar year in each household. If co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year, the days are prorated. From 1 April, the stated threshold is 117 days in each household. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen instead says 156 days in a period of 6 months. The calendar-year rule is used here based on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Payout of unused heffingskortingen

Only the algemene heffingskorting remains payable to a qualifying minstverdienende partner who was born before 1 January 1963. The partners must generally have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months, the partner must owe enough tax, and only the amount that cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and premie can be paid out. The more-than-6-month condition is not required if the partner died in 2024. The payout of arbeidskorting and IACK has ended. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Payout income references

The 2024 income references are € 12.995 for the taxpayer's own Box 1 income, and € 31.402 for the partner's income or € 35.375 when the partner is at AOW age. These are source references for the payout rules. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen

Each heffingskorting is split into a belastingdeel and premiedelen. The 2024 belastingdeel is 9,32%. The premiedelen are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. A person not insured for one scheme loses that scheme's part. If premieplicht applies for only part of the year, the relevant premiedeel is time-proportional. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints 9,28%, the 2023 belastingdeel. The 2024 value is 9,32% based on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### Winst uit onderneming and taxable Box 1 income

A business is treated as the taxpayer's winst uit onderneming when it is run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide the tax treatment. The Belastingdienst also weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))

Remove exempt profit components first. The stated examples include bosbouwvrijstelling, landbouwvrijstelling, the part of kwijtscheldingswinst above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies, eindheffing benefits, and mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject exemptions. A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker does not get ondernemersaftrek or MKB-winstvrijstelling. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))

Taxable winst uit onderneming is business profit reduced by ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated on profit after ondernemersaftrek. Taxable income from work and home includes taxable business profit and is reduced by losses available for offset. The sequence used here for the box 1 tax, tariefsaanpassing and heffingskortingen follows the structure of the cited rules. The supplied material does not state the entire sequence in one source, so that ordering is an inference. (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, MKB, the yearly FISIN heffingskortingen pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen))

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Each example is marked as an Official example or a Calculated example. The Official examples come from the source given with them. The Calculated examples use the 2024 figures in section 2 and invented inputs. All amounts are in euros and cents. Every product is written with two decimals. Where a product cannot be written exactly in two decimals, the step says "rounded down" or "rounded".

### Calculated example: Box 1 below AOW age

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 80.000,00, no deductions that trigger a tariefsaanpassing and no heffingskortingen.

1. Tax on the first € 75.518,00 at 36,97%: € 75.518,00 × 0,3697 = € 27.919,00 (rounded down).
2. Income above € 75.518,00: € 80.000,00 minus € 75.518,00 = € 4.482,00.
3. Tax on that amount at 49,50%: € 4.482,00 × 0,495 = € 2.218,59.
4. Tax before heffingskortingen: € 27.919,00 plus € 2.218,59 = € 30.137,59.

### Calculated example: Box 1 at AOW age

Assume a full year at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946, and a taxable income from work and home of € 40.000,00.

1. Tax on the first € 38.098,00 at 19,07%: € 38.098,00 × 0,1907 = € 7.265,29 (rounded).
2. Remaining income: € 40.000,00 minus € 38.098,00 = € 1.902,00.
3. Tax on that amount at 36,97%: € 1.902,00 × 0,3697 = € 703,17 (rounded).
4. Tax before heffingskortingen: € 7.265,29 plus € 703,17 = € 7.968,46.

### Calculated example: Box 1 at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

Assume a full year at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946, and a taxable income from work and home of € 40.000,00. The whole amount is below the € 40.021,00 first boundary.

1. Tax before heffingskortingen: € 40.000,00 × 0,1907 = € 7.628,00.

### Calculated example: AOW transition month

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 20.000,00 in the year in which AOW age is reached. That income is below the € 38.098,00 first-bracket boundary, so the whole amount falls in the first bracket.

1. If AOW age is reached in January, the first-bracket rate is 19,07%: € 20.000,00 × 0,1907 = € 3.814,00.
2. If AOW age is reached in December, the first-bracket rate is 35,47%: € 20.000,00 × 0,3547 = € 7.094,00.

The applicable rate depends on the month in which AOW age is reached.

### Calculated example: Tariefsaanpassing

Assume a grondslagverminderende post of € 1.000,00 (a deduction that lowers the base) and a taxable income from work and home of € 76.000,00 after that deduction. Under art. 2.10 lid 2, the tax under lid 1 is raised by 12,53% of the amount by which the taxable income plus the post exceeds € 75.518,00, capped at 12,53% of the post.

1. Taxable income plus the deduction: € 76.000,00 plus € 1.000,00 = € 77.000,00.
2. Amount above € 75.518,00: € 77.000,00 minus € 75.518,00 = € 1.482,00.
3. The base of the tariefsaanpassing is the lower of that amount and the deduction: € 1.000,00.
4. Tariefsaanpassing: € 1.000,00 × 0,1253 = € 125,30.
5. The deduction lowers the tax under lid 1 at the top rate of 49,50%, because the whole deduction lies above € 75.518,00: € 1.000,00 × 0,495 = € 495,00.
6. Benefit after the tariefsaanpassing: € 495,00 minus € 125,30 = € 369,70.
7. The deduction is worth 36,97% of € 1.000,00.

If the taxable income after the deduction is below € 75.518,00, the base is limited to the part of the deduction above € 75.518,00. With a taxable income of € 75.000,00, the base is € 75.000,00 plus € 1.000,00 minus € 75.518,00 = € 482,00, and the tariefsaanpassing is € 482,00 × 0,1253 = € 60,39 (rounded down).

### Calculated example: Winst uit onderneming

Assume an ondernemer below AOW age with a profit of € 13.750,00, an available zelfstandigenaftrek of € 3.750,00, no other ondernemersaftrek, no exempt profit components and no losses to offset.

1. Profit after the zelfstandigenaftrek: € 13.750,00 minus € 3.750,00 = € 10.000,00.
2. MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 10.000,00 × 0,1331 = € 1.331,00.
3. Taxable winst uit onderneming: € 10.000,00 minus € 1.331,00 = € 8.669,00.
4. That amount is included in the taxable income from work and home.

### Calculated example: Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 40.000,00.

1. Income above the € 24.812,00 phase-out point: € 40.000,00 minus € 24.812,00 = € 15.188,00.
2. Reduction: € 15.188,00 × 0,0663 = € 1.006,96 (rounded down).
3. Algemene heffingskorting: € 3.362,00 minus € 1.006,96 = € 2.355,04.

### Calculated example: Algemene heffingskorting for a full year at AOW age

Assume a full year at AOW age and a taxable income from work and home of € 40.000,00. The year table gives € 1.735,00, reduced above € 24.812,00 by 3,421%.

1. Income above € 24.812,00: € 40.000,00 minus € 24.812,00 = € 15.188,00.
2. Reduction: € 15.188,00 × 0,03421 = € 519,58 (rounded down).
3. Algemene heffingskorting: € 1.735,00 minus € 519,58 = € 1.215,42.

This example uses the 3,421% rate from the year table. The difference from the 3,420% rate on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen is set out in section 7.

### Official example: Payout of the algemene heffingskorting

A taxpayer born on 20 December 1962 with no income has the full algemene heffingskorting of € 3.362,00 paid out. The taxpayer had the same fiscal partner for more than 6 months and the partner owes enough tax. The payout is available only to people born before 1 January 1963.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen.

### Calculated example: Arbeidskorting below AOW age

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 30.000,00.

1. This is in the band from € 24.821,00 to € 39.957,00, so the arbeidskorting is € 5.158,00 plus 2,471% of the income above € 24.820,00.
2. Income above € 24.820,00: € 30.000,00 minus € 24.820,00 = € 5.180,00.
3. € 5.180,00 × 0,02471 = € 128,00 (rounded).
4. Arbeidskorting: € 5.158,00 plus € 128,00 = € 5.286,00.

### Calculated example: Arbeidskorting for a full year at AOW age

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 30.000,00.

1. This is in the band up to € 39.957,00, so the arbeidskorting is € 2.662,00 plus 1,275% of the income above € 24.820,00.
2. Income above € 24.820,00: € 30.000,00 minus € 24.820,00 = € 5.180,00.
3. € 5.180,00 × 0,01275 = € 66,05 (rounded).
4. Arbeidskorting: € 2.662,00 plus € 66,05 = € 2.728,05.

### Calculated example: Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 20.000,00 and that the child, residence and partner conditions are met.

1. Income above € 6.073,00: € 20.000,00 minus € 6.073,00 = € 13.927,00.
2. Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting: € 13.927,00 × 0,1145 = € 1.594,64 (rounded down).
3. This is below the maximum of € 2.950,00.

No calculated example is given for a full year at AOW age. The rate of 5,90% and the maximum of € 1.521,00 are stated, but the income above which the 5,90% rate applies is not, so that calculation is left out rather than guessed.

### Official example: IACK co-ouder test from 1 April

For an arrangement as co-ouder that starts on 1 April, 117 days in each household are required instead of the 156 days for a full year.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the profit calculation distinct from exempt profit components, ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The taxable business profit includes the relevant reductions, and it flows into taxable income from work and home. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, MKB)
- Record the basis for treating an activity as winst uit onderneming, including who runs it for their account, who can make binding agreements and who is liable for debts. The Belastingdienst also considers profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))
- The algemene heffingskorting is applied automatically in the return and may also be applied by an employer or benefit agency through payroll. The arbeidskorting may include business profit before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. The IACK is claimed through the return or a provisional assessment, not payroll. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)
- If AOW age is reached during 2024, calculate the AHK, AK and IACK through the 2024 online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag. The assessment is authoritative if its amount differs from a table by a few euros. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)
- The tariefsaanpassing is applied automatically and shown on the assessment. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen)
- The checked material does not state the record-retention period or the income tax filing deadline. It also does not provide BV-specific instructions for this file.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **AHK phase-out rate for a full year at AOW age:** the year table gives 3,421%, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives 3,420%. The arithmetic from € 1.735 divided by the difference between € 75.518 and € 24.812 is about 3,4217%. The year table is used in this file. The fetched text of Wet IB art. 8.10 was not reachable. The statutory text or an authoritative corrected table would settle the rate. Sources named in the supplied material: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen.
- **IACK maximum point:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024 gives a maximum of € 2.950 from € 31.839, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen says it is reached at € 31.837. Both state the same maximum. The statutory text or a corrected authoritative table would settle the exact point.
- **IACK co-ouder test:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen says 156 days in a period of 6 months. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting says at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated where co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year. This file follows https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting. A controlling official rule or corrected FISIN text would settle the difference.
- **Belastingdeel:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints 9,28%, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 give 9,32%. The 9,28% figure is the 2023 value. This file uses 9,32%.
- **AHK nil point:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 prints nil from € 75.519, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints nil at € 75.518. The sources describe the same rule, with the credit reaching nil at the top-bracket boundary. This file follows the year table.
- **Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek prints € 2.123, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024 gives 50% of € 3.750, or € 1.875.
