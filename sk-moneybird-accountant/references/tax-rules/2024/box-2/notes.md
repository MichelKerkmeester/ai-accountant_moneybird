## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 2 rate: 2023 had one rate of 26,9%. 2024 has two brackets, 24,5% up to € 67.000 and 33% above € 67.000, with € 16.415 tax at the top of bracket 1. [art. 2.12, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 2.12, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024) [Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)
- Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss: 2023 was 26,9%. 2024 is 24,5%. Art. 4.53 lid 2 now refers to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage". [art. 4.53, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.53, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)
- Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14): 2023 was 6,17%. 2024 is 6,04%. [art. 4.14, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.14, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
- Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) maximum: 2023 was € 700.000 plus amounts taxed earlier. 2024 is € 500.000 plus amounts taxed earlier. The amount also appears in art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c. [art. 4.14a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.14a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)
- Excessive borrowing measurement: 2023 was 31 December, nominal value, taxpayer and partner jointly. 2024 is the same. [art. 4.14a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.14a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
- Owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld) transitional rule: art. 10a.23 excluded an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without a mortgage. The rule applies in 2024. [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)
- Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) exception: inserted by Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023, so it applies in 2024. [Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html) [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount: 2023 was € 51.000. 2024 is € 56.000. [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) [art. 12a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)
- Customary salary comparable-wage test: 2023 was 100%. 2024 is 100%. No change from 2023. [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) [art. 12a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)
- Customary salary minimum threshold: 2023 was € 5.000. 2024 is € 5.000. [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) [art. 12a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)
- ODV market rate (marktrente): 2023 was 0,699%. 2024 is 2,657%. [Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)
- Start-up customary salary rule: absent from the 2023 text, closed for new cases. 2024 is the last possible year for users who started in 2022. [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)
- Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) definition: 2023 was 5%, sub a to e. 2024 is 5%, sub a to e. No change. [art. 4.6, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.6, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
- Loss offset period: 2023 was the previous year and the six following years. 2024 is the same. [art. 4.49, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.49, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

For a sole trader who owns a BV, the following records and entries matter in 2024. A dividend from the BV is a regular benefit (regulier voordeel) in box 2. Record the dividend decision and the dividend received. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) The BV withholds dividend tax (dividendbelasting). Record it as prepayment (voorheffing), not as a deduction. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)

Record the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) as box 1 wage (loon) on the payroll. It is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel). [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)

Measure debts to the BV at 31 December at nominal value. If the total exceeds € 500.000 plus amounts taxed earlier, the excess is a deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) in box 2. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt) The loan stays a loan. The BV keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays corporate income tax (Vpb) on it. No dividend tax is withheld on the taxed part. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt) Receivables on the company do not reduce the debts. Debts at several companies are added. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)

Keep records of loan agreements, balances at 31 December, interest charges, dividend decisions, notary costs for shares, and loss decisions (beschikkingen). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

For an unoffset loss, the loss is set by decision (beschikking) together with the assessment. The tax credit (belastingkorting) request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)

For an ODV or PEB in the BV, value the ODV with the market rate of 2,657% for 2024 and index the PEB if the scheme provides for indexation. PEB payouts are box 1 income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking). [Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/) [art. 10a.18](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. [art. 10a.18](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) [Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)

A non-resident declares only a substantial interest in a Netherlands company under art. 7.5. [art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for the 2024 values. [2024 values table](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

UNVERIFIED: The rollover (doorschuiving) list (rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration) was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The 10-year APV term is Verified. [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang) A primary page would settle the rollover list.

UNVERIFIED: The summed two-partner amount. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) A primary page would settle it.

UNVERIFIED: The 2025 partial non-resident tax liability decision (beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) statement. The 30%-rule decision statement was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages. [art. 7.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) A primary page would settle it.

Proposed: The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from the art. 4.12a facility from 1 January 2027. [Eerste Kamer, memorie van toelichting](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf) Not adopted on 2026-10-11.

Proposed: 2027 box 2 changes, a 29,2% second bracket, an excessive borrowing (excessief-lenen) phase-down and two technical measures. [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file) [Eerste Kamer, memorie van toelichting](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf) Not adopted on 2026-10-11.

Conflict resolved: Loss-conversion credit (korting) 2024 to 2026, 24,5% against 26,9%. Settled at 24,5%. Art. 4.53 lid 2 in the 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts refers to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", which is 24,5%. The live Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page, FISIN 2024 and the FISIN 2025 example agree. The 26,9% in FISIN 2025 (rule text) and FISIN 2026 is a stale handbook figure. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)

Conflict resolved: Placement of the excessive borrowing fiction, art. 4.12a against art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f. Settled at art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c. Art. 4.12a carries the inherited-belang facility. [art. 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)

Conflict resolved: PEB start date, 1 April 2017 against 1 July 2017. Settled at 1 April 2017 for the measures, with the leniency (coulance) period to 30 June 2017 and lapse on 1 July 2017. [BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01) [Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/) [Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html)

Conflict resolved: Art. 4.6 "unchanged 2020 to 2027" against the year texts. Sub e is in the 2022 text and sub f is from 2025 with retroactive effect. [art. 4.6, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)

Conflict resolved: "No box 2 change proposed for 2027" against the 29 September 2026 cabinet letter and the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 bill. Box 2 changes are proposed, none adopted on 2026-10-11. [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file) [Eerste Kamer, memorie van toelichting](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)

Conflict resolved: Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) against Tax Plan 2024 (Belastingplan 2024) as the source of the 2024 brackets. Both are right for different parts. Tax Plan 2023 introduced the second bracket and Tax Plan 2024 (the amendment (amendement) Van der Lee c.s.) set its rate at 33%. [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024) [Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)

Conflict resolved: The excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) threshold history. The bill proposed € 500.000, the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging) replaced it by € 700.000, and the amendment Grinwis c.s. replaced it by € 500.000. [Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html) [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html) [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)

Corrected: The start-up rule wording "the minimum wage" against the statute "108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage". [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01)

Outdated for 2024: FISIN 2023 works its excessive borrowing example into 2024 with a threshold (drempel) of € 800.000 and € 50.000 taxed. Under the 2024 law the 2024 threshold is € 600.000. [FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/aanmerkelijk_belang) [art. 4.14a, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
