## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Box 2 bracket 1

The 24,5% rate applies to box 2 income up to € 67.000. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The bracket applies per taxpayer. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Fiscal partners may divide the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) in any proportion that totals 100%. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) Each partner's own first bracket then applies to his or her share. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Box 2 income is the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (art. 4.12). [art. 4.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from a vbi (exempt investment institution, vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing (excessief-lenen) fiction (sub f). [art. 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Interest on a loan to buy the shares is deductible. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted but credited as prepayment (voorheffing). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)

A disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel) is the transfer price minus the acquisition price. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, notary costs included. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Special cases are inheritance, gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching 5% during the year, and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)

Deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding, art. 4.16) lists as a sale: buy-back (inkoop) of shares, commutation (afkoop) and buy-back of profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen), liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a. [art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment. [art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) The text is identical 2020 to 2027. [art. 4.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor). [art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

### 3.2 Tax at top of bracket 1

The tax at the top of bracket 1 is € 16.415. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) This is the amount in column III of the art. 2.12 table. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The table applies the second bracket above € 67.000. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### 3.3 Box 2 bracket 2

The 33% rate applies to box 2 income above € 67.000. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The second bracket was announced in Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) and took effect on 1 January 2024. [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024) The 33% itself came from Tax Plan 2024 (Belastingplan 2024). [Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html) The amendment (amendement) Van der Lee c.s. raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024. [Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html) Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025. [Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html) For 2024 the rate is 33%. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The bracket applies per taxpayer. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### 3.4 Tax credit (belastingkorting) on unoffset loss

Art. 4.53 converts an unoffset loss into a tax credit on request. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) Lid 1: the taxpayer and partner must have had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) Lid 2: from the 2024 text the percentage is "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", so 24,5% in 2024. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Lid 3: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit (korting) by decision (beschikking) open to objection. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) Lid 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without substantial interest. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting) Knowledge group (kennisgroep) position (standpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date. [KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/) A new substantial interest at the request does not block it. [KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/) Ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment. [KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/) The FISIN 2025 and 2026 statement "De korting is 26,9%" is overruled by the statute. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)

### 3.5 Loss offset period

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order the losses arose (art. 4.49). [art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The loss is set by decision together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1) For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier: "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar". [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1) A negative deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt) [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)

### 3.6 Substantial interest definition (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly:
(a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital,
(b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital,
(c) holds profit-sharing certificates for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds,
(d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a,
(e) has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit. [art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

For 2024 the text has sub a to e. [art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts. [art. 4.6, 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) [art. 4.6, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026) (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025. [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html) Sub f therefore does not apply to 2024. [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html) In sub d "tot" became "om" between those texts. [art. 4.6, 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) [art. 4.6, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)

Drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) (art. 4.9): once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company also belong to it. [art. 4.9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) Carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) (art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line (bloed- of aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest. [art. 4.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang) Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027. [art. 4.7 to 4.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11): shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (rollover cases). [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%. [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) On request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) The text is identical 2020 to 2027. [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) This is not the vbi route. [art. 4.11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Partner and minor children: the 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (art. 4.6). [art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the taxpayer must also declare the income from substantial interest of minor children, until the child turns 18. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)

Fiscal partners may divide box 2 income. Art. 2.17 lid 2 treats common income components (gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen) of the taxpayer and partner as divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return. [art. 2.17](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) FISIN 2026: with fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is." [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own first bracket applies to his share. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) No primary page states a summed two-partner amount. The summed figure stays UNVERIFIED. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)

Rollover and APV rules: the research lists rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration and the APV loss deferral (offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years). [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang) [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year APV term. [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang) The rollover list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The rollover list is UNVERIFIED. The 10-year APV term is Verified. [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)

### 3.7 Flat-rate benefit (art. 4.14)

The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, time-apportioned. [art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The percentage is a yearly value. [art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) For 2024 it is 6,04%. [art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) It applies to shares in an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company. [art. 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) The percentage can differ each year. [art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)

### 3.8 Excessive borrowing maximum and mechanism

Excess Borrowing from Own Companies Act (Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap), Act of 21 December 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued 27 December 2022, in force 1 January 2023 (Artikel III). [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html) The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ("het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)", debts meaning all civil-law debt relations and obligations). [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html) The amount rule is in art. 4.14a ("Fictief regulier voordeel"), the related-person rule (verbonden personen) in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c. [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)

Art. 4.14a lid 2: maximum € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) In 2023 the maximum was € 700.000. [art. 4.14a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) Lid 1: positive amount equals total debts above the maximum. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Lid 4: measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Lid 5: if the taxpayer no longer has a substantial interest at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) That releases a negative amount. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Lid 6: an owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld) is excluded to the extent a right of mortgage (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Art. 10a.23: an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded. [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html) Lid 7 (Tax Plan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023): double counting of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debt among several participants is removed. [Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html)

Partnership exception (art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5): debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a connected person for a debt of a partnership (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out. [art. 4.13, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) [art. 4.13, 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) It was inserted by Tax Plan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023. [Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html) Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" retroactive to 1 January 2023, not for 2024. [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)

Related persons (verbonden personen) (art. 4.14b): debts of a connected person (a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line of the taxpayer or partner) to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000 from 2024, applied to that person and his partner together. ### 3.10 Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount

Art. 12a Wet LB 1964 applies to an employee working for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest. [art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01) The wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies, (c) a fixed amount. [art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01) The fixed amount is € 56.000 for 2024. [art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01) The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the table correction factor. [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)

Lower wage: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage, the comparable wage applies. [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) In 2020 to 2022 the fixed amount applied as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower. [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01) The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and is known to the withholding agent and the tax inspector. [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)

Start-up rule: for a body with an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de-minimis ceiling. [art. 12a, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 12a, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01) The rule is absent from the 2023 text. [art. 12a, 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01) The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen", it could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 to 2024. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang) This is the last possible year for start-up customary salary users who started in 2022. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)

Interaction: the customary salary is declared as income from employment (box 1), not as a box 2 benefit. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) The statement that the wage reduces the residual profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement. ### 3.13 ODV market rate (marktrente) for 2024

[art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01) Each instalment is the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01) An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01) On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01) A negative market rate must also be applied. [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) [art. 38p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)
