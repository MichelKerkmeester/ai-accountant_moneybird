# Changes tax year 2024

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2024 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This note lists what changed for tax year 2024. Section 2 holds the changes unique to 2024 and section 3 the generated table of every change by topic.

---

## 2. UNIQUE TO 2024

- Box 2 moved from one rate of 26,9% in 2023 to two brackets in 2024, 24,5% up to € 67.000 and 33% above € 67.000. [box-2](box-2/notes.md#5-changes)
- The self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 5.030 to € 3.750 and the AOW-age amount from € 2.515 to € 1.875. [income tax (inkomstenbelasting)](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) fell from 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024. [entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2024 the own contribution of an employee is no longer subtracted when total BUA costs are tested against € 227 per recipient. [VAT (btw)](vat/notes.md#5-changes)
- The kilometre allowance rose from € 0,21 in 2023 to € 0,23 in 2024. [costs and car (kosten en auto)](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The EIA percentage fell from 45,5% in 2023 to 40% in 2024. [investments (investeringen)](investments/notes.md#5-changes)
- The box 3 rate rose from 32% in 2023 to 36% in 2024. [box-3](box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution by assessment (aanslag) fell from 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024. [Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage)](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
