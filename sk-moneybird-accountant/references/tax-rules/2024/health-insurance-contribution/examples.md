## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

The checked material contains no usable official 2024 example. The [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) repeats the 2023 examples (the 2023 amounts € 26.956 and wage (loon) € 70.000) with 2024 figures that do not fit the € 71.628 maximum, so that page is not usable as a 2024 example. Every example below is a Calculated example built from the section 2 figures.

### Example 1: profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

Assumptions. Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) of € 60.000, the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) applies, no other income, no AOW age.

Step 1. Profits from business activities: € 60.000.
Step 2. Entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000 minus € 3.750 = € 56.250.
Step 3. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 13,31% × € 56.250 = € 7.486,875, rounded € 7.486,88.
Step 4. Taxable profit (belastbare winst): € 56.250 minus € 7.486,875 = € 48.763,125, rounded € 48.763,13.
Step 5. Cap: € 48.763,13 is below € 71.628, so the full base carries the contribution.
Step 6. Zvw contribution (bijdrage): 5,32% × € 48.763,125 = € 2.594,198, rounded € 2.594,20.

The amount of € 2.594,20 is the Zvw contribution in the assessment (aanslag) channel ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

### Example 2: profit of € 100.000, cap reached (Calculated example)

Assumptions. Profits from business activities of € 100.000, the self-employed person's deduction applies, no other income, no AOW age.

Step 1. Profits from business activities: € 100.000.
Step 2. Entrepreneur's allowance: € 100.000 minus € 3.750 = € 96.250.
Step 3. SME profit exemption: 13,31% × € 96.250 = € 12.810,875, rounded € 12.810,88.
Step 4. Taxable profit: € 96.250 minus € 12.810,875 = € 83.439,125, rounded € 83.439,13.
Step 5. Cap: € 83.439,13 is above € 71.628, so the contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 71.628.
Step 6. Zvw contribution: 5,32% × € 71.628 = € 3.810,6096, rounded € 3.810,61.

The profit above the cap carries no further Zvw contribution ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

### Example 3: wage of € 70.000 plus profit of € 25.000 (Calculated example)

Assumptions. Wages from employment (loon uit loondienst) of € 70.000 that already bore a contribution, profits from business activities of € 25.000, the self-employed person's deduction applies, no AOW age.

Step 1. The wage of € 70.000 uses € 70.000 of the € 71.628 maximum.
Step 2. Headroom: € 71.628 minus € 70.000 = € 1.628.
Step 3. Entrepreneur's allowance: € 25.000 minus € 3.750 = € 21.250.
Step 4. SME profit exemption: 13,31% × € 21.250 = € 2.828,375, rounded € 2.828,38.
Step 5. Taxable profit: € 21.250 minus € 2.828,375 = € 18.421,625, rounded € 18.421,63.
Step 6. Cap: € 18.421,63 is above the € 1.628 headroom, so the assessment base is € 1.628.
Step 7. Zvw contribution: 5,32% × € 1.628 = € 86,6096, rounded € 86,61.

### Example 4: employer rate against assessment rate on € 40.000 (Calculated example)

Step 1. Employer levy (werkgeversheffing): 6,57% × € 40.000 = € 2.628,00.
Step 2. Assessment rate: 5,32% × € 40.000 = € 2.128,00.
Step 3. Difference: € 2.628,00 minus € 2.128,00 = € 500,00.

For the same € 40.000 of contribution income the employer channel carries € 500,00 more than the entrepreneur channel in 2024.
