## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag): 5,43% in 2023 to 5,32% in 2024 ([Zvw page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). In 2023 the withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) was also 5,43%. The checked material gives no 2024 figure for that channel.
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 66.956 in 2023 to € 71.628 in 2024 ([Zvw page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).
- Employer levy (werkgeversheffing): 6,68% in 2023 to 6,57% in 2024 ([Zvw page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). Both values are marked Corrected.
- Base for the profit (winst): the checked material gives the same description for 2023 and 2024, the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), and marks both UNVERIFIED because only the 2026 article text was read ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01)).
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 5.030 in 2023, or € 2.515 at AOW age, to € 3.750 in 2024, or € 1.875 at AOW age ([ondernemersaftrek page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024)). A lower deduction (aftrek) raises the Zvw base for the same profit.
- SME profit exemption: 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024 ([ondernemersaftrek page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). A lower exemption raises the Zvw base for the same profit.
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): no new additions since 2023 and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet. Unchanged in 2024 ([oudedagsreserve page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) over profit (winst) is not collected through a payroll. It is charged by assessment (aanslag), and the assessment Zvw comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) ([Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).
- The amount to expect follows the same profit steps as the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). Profit, minus the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), including the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 3.750, or € 1.875 at AOW age, minus the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) of 13,31%, then 5,32% over the result up to € 71.628 ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024), [MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), the last marked UNVERIFIED for 2024).
- An existing old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) can stay on the balance sheet. No new addition is made in 2024 ([oudedagsreserve page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The amount added to the profit under art. 3.70 lid 2 when the reserve decreases stays outside the Zvw base ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024).
- Wage (loon) and benefits (uitkeringen) that already bore the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) or the withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) use up the € 71.628 headroom of the same person before the profit is charged ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). Book those amounts next to the profit estimate so the split over the channels stays visible.
- The checked material states no separate Zvw records or administration rules for 2024. The 2024 filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) is not in the checked material for this file and belongs to the procedure topic, see section 7.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for the profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer): UNVERIFIED. This file uses the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), excluding the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve). [Zvw art. 43](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01) and [Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) were read in the 2026 version only, so the text valid for 2024 is not confirmed. Reading the Zvw text and the Wet IB 2001 text as valid on 1 January 2024 would settle it.
- Declaration and deadlines: UNVERIFIED, not re-opened in the checked material. The checked material states that the same return (aangifte) is used, that a (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag) Zvw exists and that the Zvw follows the IB filing timeline. The [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) confirms that the assessment (aanslag) Zvw comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting). The 2024 deadline belongs to the procedure topic. The 2024 return instructions and the [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) would settle it.
- Zvw at AOW age: verified in part. The [Tabel 77 Premieoverzicht Zvw](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf) footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB). No AOW-age exemption appears in [Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01) as read, and the Zvw pages on belastingdienst.nl do not mention the AOW age. The absence is inference from absence. The Zvw text and the budget table (begrotingstabel) for 2024 would settle it.
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) at AOW age 2024: € 2.123 ([ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)) against € 1.875 ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024), 50% of € 3.750). The checked material settles on € 1.875, deciding source [zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024).
- SME profit exemption 2024: 14% ([ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)) against 13,31% ([MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). The checked material settles on 13,31%, deciding source [MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) 2024: marked Corrected. The checked material records the corrected series for 2020 to 2024 as 6,70%, 7,00%, 6,75%, 6,68% and 6,57% ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). No earlier 2024 value is given.
- Withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) 2024: the checked material gives no 2024 percentage. In 2023 the withheld contribution was 5,43% ([Zvw page 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) would settle it.
- Official 2024 example: the [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) repeats the 2023 examples (the 2023 amounts € 26.956 and wage (loon) € 70.000) with 2024 figures that do not fit the € 71.628 maximum, so there is no usable official 2024 example. A corrected 2024 page would settle it.
