## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the income-dependent Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet), the health-insurance contribution, for an entrepreneur in 2024. The entrepreneur pays 5,32% by assessment (aanslag) on the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming), with contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) capped at € 71.628. An employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) pays the 6,57% employer levy (werkgeversheffing) on wage (loon) and benefits. The 2024 rules are enacted. The Zvw base for the profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) is UNVERIFIED for 2024 because only the 2026 article text was read, and the AOW-age treatment is only verified in part.
