## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Bijdrage Zvw bij (voorlopige) aanslag: 5,32%

The rate of 5,32% belongs to the Zvw contribution that is charged by (voorlopige) aanslag (assessment or provisional assessment) ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages for the year, which is how a yearly rate is fixed ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for the 2024 article text).

Who it applies to. The aanslag channel carries the contribution for an IB-ondernemer (sole trader for income tax) with winst uit Nederland (profit from the Netherlands), and for people with inkomsten uit overig werk (income from other work), loon from a foreign employer (the pages 2020 to 2024 cover this item), pension from a foreign uitkeringsinstantie and periodieke uitkeringen (periodic payments) without loonheffing (payroll tax) such as partneralimentatie (spousal maintenance) ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

How it is calculated. The rate is applied to the bijdrage-inkomen. For the winst-ondernemer that base is the belastbare winst uit onderneming after the ondernemersaftrek (entrepreneur's deduction) and the MKB-winstvrijstelling (small business profit exemption), capped at € 71.628 ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024, and [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

Filing step. The aanslag Zvw comes with the (voorlopige) aanslag inkomstenbelasting ([Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The checked material does not describe a separate Zvw return or a 2024 Zvw deadline, see section 7.

What is not in this channel. The checked material names winst uit Nederland as an aanslag item and notes that the conversion of an oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) into a lijfrente (annuity) is not covered by it ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)). The amount added to profit under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve also stays outside the base ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024).

Exceptions and corrections in the channel rules. Militairen (military personnel) in active service or on buitengewoon verlof (special leave) with retention of military income, deelvissers (share fishermen) where another party pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

### 3.2 Maximum bijdrage-inkomen Zvw: € 71.628

Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the bijdrage-inkomen at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for 2024). For 2024 that maximum is € 71.628 ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

The maximum is a cap on the income that can carry a contribution in one year. Below the cap the full base carries the contribution. Above the cap no further contribution is due. The cap applies once per person across all channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See section 3.8 for the interaction between loon, uitkeringen and winst.

### 3.3 Werkgeversheffing Zvw: 6,57%

The 6,57% werkgeversheffing is paid by the employer or the uitkeringsinstantie. It covers loon uit loondienst (wages from employment), DGA loon (director and major shareholder's salary) when the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen (employee insurance schemes), bijstand (social assistance), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people who were ziekenfonds-insured in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (disability and unemployment schemes) ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

How it differs from the entrepreneur rate. The werkgeversheffing is 6,57% and the aanslag rate is 5,32% ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The employer or uitkeringsinstantie pays the werkgeversheffing over loon and benefits, while the profit of an entrepreneur is not collected through a payroll and is charged by aanslag instead. An entrepreneur who also has loon uit loondienst meets both channels in the same year.

The 6,57% is marked Corrected. The checked material records the corrected series for 2020 to 2024 as 6,70%, 7,00%, 6,75%, 6,68% and 6,57% ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). No earlier 2024 value is given, see section 7.

The third channel is the ingehouden bijdrage (withheld contribution), withheld from pension, lijfrente and other uitkeringen that carry loonheffing ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The checked material gives no 2024 percentage for this channel, see section 7.

Interaction. Loon or an uitkering that already bore the werkgeversheffing or the ingehouden bijdrage uses up the € 71.628 headroom of the same person, so less room is left for the winst in the aanslag ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See the calculated example 3 in section 4.

### 3.4 Grondslag voor de winst uit onderneming

Who. The base applies to the winst-ondernemer, the person with winst uit onderneming (profit from a business). The Belastingdienst tests whether an activity is an onderneming. The business must be run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer must be able to make binding agreements for it and must be liable for its debts. Registration in the handelsregister (trade register) alone does not decide. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk ([page on winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming)).

The base. Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b brings the belastbare winst uit onderneming determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001 into the bijdrage-inkomen, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the oudedagsreserve ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for 2024).

Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines the belastbare winst as the winst minus the ondernemersaftrek minus the MKB-winstvrijstelling ([Wet IB 2001 valid on 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Zvw base for the winst-ondernemer is therefore the profit after the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling.

Calculation order. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is computed on the winst after the ondernemersaftrek ([ondernemersaftrek page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). So the order is winst, then the ondernemersaftrek, then the MKB-winstvrijstelling, then the cap of € 71.628, then the 5,32%.

Interaction. The ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling lower the box 1 income and the Zvw base in the same step. They do not change the 5,32% rate. The oudedagsreserve can no longer be increased in 2024, an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet, and a decrease that is added to the profit under art. 3.70 lid 2 stays outside the Zvw base ([oudedagsreserve page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve) and [Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), the latter UNVERIFIED for 2024).

### 3.5 Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 3.750, or € 1.875 at AOW age

Who. The zelfstandigenaftrek is an item of the ondernemersaftrek for the ondernemer who meets its conditions. The conditions themselves belong to the ondernemersaftrek topic of 2024.

The amount. For 2024 the zelfstandigenaftrek is € 3.750. At AOW age (state pension age) it is 50% of € 3.750, so € 1.875 ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024) and [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) prints € 2.123 for the AOW-age amount, which the checked material resolves to € 1.875, see section 7.

Carry-forward. Unused zelfstandigenaftrek can be carried forward for 9 years if the later profit is high enough ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024)).

Interaction with the Zvw. The zelfstandigenaftrek comes off the winst before the MKB-winstvrijstelling, so it lowers the Zvw base and with it the 5,32% contribution. It does not lower the rate.

### 3.6 MKB-winstvrijstelling: 13,31%

For 2024 the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 13,31% of the winst after the ondernemersaftrek ([MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). The [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) prints 14%, which the checked material resolves to 13,31%, see section 7.

Interaction with the Zvw. The MKB-winstvrijstelling is part of the step that turns the winst into the belastbare winst, so it lowers the Zvw base and the 5,32% contribution ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024).

### 3.7 Inkomsten in de aanslag Zvw

The checked material lists the aanslag items as winst uit Nederland, inkomsten uit overig werk, loon from a foreign employer (on the pages 2020 to 2024), pension from a foreign uitkeringsinstantie and periodieke uitkeringen without loonheffing such as partneralimentatie ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

These items are charged at the aanslag rate of 5,32% and are counted together under the maximum of € 71.628 per person ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See sections 3.2 and 3.8.

### 3.8 Maximum per person

The maximum applies once per person across all channels. Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

So the order of the channels matters for the split, not for the total. Loon and uitkeringen that already bore the werkgeversheffing or the ingehouden bijdrage reduce the room left for the winst in the aanslag. Once the € 71.628 is full, no further Zvw contribution is due on the remaining winst.

Two corrections work on the amount of the same person, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

### 3.9 Zvw and AOW age

The Volksgezondheid begroting 2027 Tabel 77 footnote 2 states that zelfstandigen (self-employed people) and gepensioneerden (pensioners) pay the verlaagde IAB, the lower income-dependent contribution ([Tabel 77 Premieoverzicht Zvw](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)).

No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, and the Zvw pages on belastingdienst.nl do not mention the AOW age ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version). That absence is inference from absence, so it is UNVERIFIED as a stated rule. The checked material does not state that the 5,32% changes when the AOW age is reached, and it does not give a separate AOW-age Zvw amount.
