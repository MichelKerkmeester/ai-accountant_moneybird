## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Contribution (bijdrage) Zvw by (provisional) assessment: 5,32%

The 5,32% rate applies to the Zvw contribution charged by a (provisional) assessment (aanslag) ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages for the year, which is how a yearly rate is fixed ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for the 2024 article text).

Who it applies to. The assessment channel carries the contribution for an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) with profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland), and for people with income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage (loon) from a foreign employer (the pages 2020 to 2024 cover this item), pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes (loonheffing) such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

How it is calculated. The rate is applied to the contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen). For the profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) that base is the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), capped at € 71.628 ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024, and [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

Filing step. The assessment Zvw comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting) ([Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The checked material does not describe a separate Zvw return or a 2024 Zvw deadline, see section 7.

What is not in this channel. The checked material names profit from the Netherlands as an assessment item and notes that the conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente) is not covered by it ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)). The amount added to profit under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve also stays outside the base ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024).

Exceptions and corrections in the channel rules. Military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, share fishermen (deelvissers) where another party pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution, and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

### 3.2 Maximum contribution income Zvw: € 71.628

Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for 2024). For 2024 that maximum is € 71.628 ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

The maximum is a cap on the income that can carry a contribution in one year. Below the cap the full base carries the contribution. Above the cap no further contribution is due. The cap applies once per person across all channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See section 3.8 for the interaction between wage, benefits (uitkeringen) and profit.

### 3.3 Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw: 6,57%

The 6,57% employer levy is paid by the employer or the benefit agency. It covers wages from employment (loon uit loondienst), DGA wage (director and major shareholder's salary) when the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people who were insured under a health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (disability and unemployment schemes) ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

How it differs from the entrepreneur rate. The employer levy is 6,57% and the assessment rate is 5,32% ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The employer or benefit agency pays the employer levy over wage and benefits, while the profit of an entrepreneur is not collected through a payroll and is charged by assessment instead. An entrepreneur who also has wages from employment meets both channels in the same year.

The 6,57% is marked Corrected. The checked material records the corrected series for 2020 to 2024 as 6,70%, 7,00%, 6,75%, 6,68% and 6,57% ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). No earlier 2024 value is given, see section 7.

The third channel is the withheld contribution, withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits that carry payroll taxes ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). The checked material gives no 2024 percentage for this channel, see section 7.

Interaction. Wage or a benefit (uitkering) that already bore the employer levy or the withheld contribution uses up the € 71.628 headroom of the same person, so less room is left for the profit in the assessment ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See the calculated example 3 in section 4.

### 3.4 Tax base (grondslag) for the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming)

Who. The base applies to the profit-entrepreneur, the person with profits from business activities. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) tests whether an activity is a business (onderneming). The business must be run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer must be able to make binding agreements for it and must be liable for its debts. Registration in the Commercial Register (handelsregister) alone does not decide. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk ([page on winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming)).

The base. Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b brings the taxable profit from business determined under section (afdeling) 3.2 Wet IB 2001 into the contribution income, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version, so UNVERIFIED for 2024).

Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines the taxable profit (belastbare winst) as the profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance minus the SME profit exemption ([Wet IB 2001 valid on 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Zvw base for the profit-entrepreneur is therefore the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption.

Calculation order. The SME profit exemption is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance ([ondernemersaftrek page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). So the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption, then the cap of € 71.628, then the 5,32%.

Interaction. The entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption lower the box 1 income and the Zvw base in the same step. They do not change the 5,32% rate. The old-age reserve can no longer be increased in 2024, an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet, and a decrease that is added to the profit under art. 3.70 lid 2 stays outside the Zvw base ([oudedagsreserve page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve) and [Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), the latter UNVERIFIED for 2024).

### 3.5 Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 3.750, or € 1.875 at AOW age

Who. The self-employed person's deduction is an item of the entrepreneur's allowance for the entrepreneur who meets its conditions. The conditions themselves belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic of 2024.

The amount. For 2024 the self-employed person's deduction is € 3.750. At AOW age (state pension age) it is 50% of € 3.750, so € 1.875 ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024) and [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) prints € 2.123 for the AOW-age amount, which the checked material resolves to € 1.875, see section 7.

Carry-forward. Unused self-employed person's deduction can be carried forward for 9 years if the later profit is high enough ([zelfstandigenaftrek 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024)).

Interaction with the Zvw. The self-employed person's deduction comes off the profit before the SME profit exemption, so it lowers the Zvw base and with it the 5,32% contribution. It does not lower the rate.

### 3.6 SME profit exemption: 13,31%

For 2024 the SME profit exemption is 13,31% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance ([MKB-winstvrijstelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). The [ondernemersaftrek page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) prints 14%, which the checked material resolves to 13,31%, see section 7.

Interaction with the Zvw. The SME profit exemption is part of the step that turns the profit into the taxable profit, so it lowers the Zvw base and the 5,32% contribution ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), UNVERIFIED for 2024).

### 3.7 Income (inkomsten) in the assessment Zvw

The checked material lists the assessment items as profit from the Netherlands, income from other work, wage from a foreign employer (on the pages 2020 to 2024), pension from a foreign benefit agency and periodic payments without payroll taxes such as spousal maintenance ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

These items are charged at the assessment rate of 5,32% and are counted together under the maximum of € 71.628 per person ([Zvw page 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). See sections 3.2 and 3.8.

### 3.8 Maximum per person

The maximum applies once per person across all channels. Income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

So the order of the channels matters for the split, not for the total. Wage and benefits that already bore the employer levy or the withheld contribution reduce the room left for the profit in the assessment. Once the € 71.628 is full, no further Zvw contribution is due on the remaining profit.

Two corrections work on the amount of the same person, a correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution and a reduction for the part of the year insured in the Netherlands ([Zvw page 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) to [Zvw page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)).

### 3.9 Zvw and AOW age

The Public Health (Volksgezondheid) budget (begroting) 2027, Table 77 footnote 2, states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB), the lower income-dependent contribution ([Tabel 77 Premieoverzicht Zvw](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)).

No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, and the Zvw pages on belastingdienst.nl do not mention the AOW age ([Zvw art. 43, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01), read in the 2026 version). That absence is inference from absence, so it is UNVERIFIED as a stated rule. The checked material does not state that the 5,32% changes when the AOW age is reached, and it does not give a separate AOW-age Zvw amount.
