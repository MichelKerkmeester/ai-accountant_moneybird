## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2024 for a sole trader, the filing deadlines and uitstel (deferral) for income tax and btw (VAT), the voorlopige aanslag (provisional assessment), belastingrente (tax interest), the navorderingstermijn (additional assessment period), bezwaar (objection) and verzoek om vermindering (request for reduction), the bewaarplicht (record-keeping duty), and the KVK and Belastingdienst registration duties. All items for 2024 in the checked material are enacted. The 2024 income tax return is filed in 2025, with 1 May 2025 as the deadline and 1 September 2025 with uitstel, and the definitive aanslag (assessment) can be set until 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with uitstel. The belastingrente rate during 2024 is 7,5%, the first year in which the rate is set once a year, and rente on the 2024 income tax runs at 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Income tax return 2024, filing deadline

The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief (invitation to file). That date is often 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). For tax year 2024 the deadline is 1 May 2025 and uitstel could be requested until 1 May 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). AWR art. 9 lid 1 states that for aanslagbelastingen (taxes collected by assessment) the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur (tax inspector) of at least one month after the invitation, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The checked material finds the same wording in the 1 January 2020 text and the 1 January 2026 text of the AWR (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01).

### 3.2 Income tax return 2024 with uitstel

A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, so the 2024 return is due before 1 September 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). The form can be used only for the first uitstel request, and the fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) must request separately. Uitstel usually means belastingrente (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.3 Uitstel request

Uitstel can be requested online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May, giving uitstel to 1 September, and this online route is for someone who received an aangiftebrief (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The uitstel page states that online is not possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Read in full, that second page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both texts are current Belastingdienst text, so the reading is: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May filing date, paper form otherwise.

### 3.4 Filing period opens

The filing period opens on 1 March (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). A return can be filed from that date. The checked material gives no different opening date for tax year 2024.

### 3.5 Definitive aanslag 2024 latest

AWR art. 11 lid 3 says the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld (tax debt) arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For tax year 2024 the latest date is 31 December 2027, and with 4 months uitstel 30 April 2028 (same page).

### 3.6 Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2024

The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For 2024 these amounts are € 56 and € 18 (same page). Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not (same page and https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Pages up to the 2023 captures used gelijk aan of meer dan (equal to or more than) and printed each grens one euro higher. The capture of 28 March 2025 prints € 47 and € 15 for 2020, € 48 and € 15 for 2021 and 2022, and € 51 and € 16 for 2023 (https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For whole-euro amounts the two wordings describe the same threshold, which the checked material marks as its own reading.

### 3.7 Filing the 2024 return without an invitation

Without an aangiftebrief a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). For tax year 2024 that is until 31 December 2029 (same page).

### 3.8 Navorderingstermijn

AWR art. 16 lid 1 allows a navordering (additional assessment) on a new fact. The fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw (in bad faith). Lid 2 also allows navordering when a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and for a fout (mistake) that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar (reasonably identifiable), always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3 sets the term at 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and for a fout-based navordering only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4 sets 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months when in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst example is tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering is possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete (same page). The checked material does not state an end date for tax year 2024, see section 7.

### 3.9 Payment termijn of an aanslag

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 carries the same text on 1 January 2020, 1 January 2024, 1 January 2025 and 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01). An aanslag is invorderbaar (collectible) 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after (same article).

### 3.10 Payment termijnen of a voorlopige aanslag

A voorlopige aanslag for the income tax dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen (instalments) as months remain after the month of the date, and the first termijn is one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date (Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01).

### 3.11 Voorlopige aanslag 2024, request and change

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen). Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, a higher or lower income, a change in business profit, and stopping the business (stakingswinst, the profit on ending the business). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming (business profit) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with an aangiftebrief until the filing date or the uitstel date, and no longer after filing (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). That page states that a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. It does not state the corresponding rule for a change to the 2024 voorlopige aanslag, see section 7.

### 3.12 Belastingrente rate 2024

For 2024 the rate for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen is 7,5% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). The rate history in the checked material runs from 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 (for the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started 1 July 2020), 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024, 6,5% in 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (same page). From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB herfinancieringsrente published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. For Vpb, bronbelasting, minimumbelasting and solidariteitsbijdrage the addition is 5,5 points, minimum 5,5% (same page). After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. A mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, btw and the other taxes (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). That page gives no date for the collective decisions, its update date is 25 February 2026.

### 3.13 Belastingrente on tax year 2024 income tax

Rente is charged when the 2024 return arrives on or after 1 May 2025, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). The period runs from 1 July 2025, also when uitstel was granted (same page). It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return (same page). For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July 2025 to 1 month after its date (same page). No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged (same page). On tax year 2024 the rate is 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### 3.14 Belastingrente income tax, day count

The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.15 Belastingrente on btw

Rente is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting). For late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a naheffingsaanslag from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen, which ends the rente at most 10 weeks after receipt of the request, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable (same page). The 7,5% rate of 2024 is the rate for btw in that year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

### 3.16 Bezwaartermijn

Bezwaar (objection) must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar). The motivation can follow later if the bezwaar asks for that. Acknowledgement follows within 3 weeks. There is no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag for the income tax, only a change (same page). After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### 3.17 Verzoek om vermindering

After the bezwaar term an (ambtshalve) verzoek om vermindering (request for reduction) is possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). Online is possible only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The checked material gives aanslag 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request goes by letter. For an aanslag 2024 the checked sources do not print the online end date, see section 7.

### 3.18 Income tax boetes 2024

After the deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering (reminder), then an aanmaning (formal demand) with 10 werkdagen (working days). Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete (default penalty). The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag. No return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag plus verzuimboete. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete (culpability penalty), also with a navorderingsaanslag. No boete is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief. The time limit for requesting a return is 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for the income tax and Vpb, with a coulance of 2 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). For 2020 to 2024 the amounts are verzuimboete income tax € 385, up to € 5.514, not requesting a return € 2.757, betaalverzuimboete up to € 5.514. The vergrijpboete is 25% for grove schuld (gross negligence), 50% for opzet (intent), box 3 75% and 150%, aggravated 100% and 300% (https://web.archive.org/web/20240301/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### 3.19 Btw verzuimboete 2024

For a btw aangifte there are 7 kalenderdagen (calendar days) of coulance, then a boete of € 68 in 2020 to 2024 or € 82 in 2025 and 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte). For 2024 the amount is € 68 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240225/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).

### 3.20 Btw monthly dates 2024

AWR art. 10 lid 2 says a return for a tijdvak (period) is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak. AWR art. 19 lid 1 says the tax is paid within one month after the end of the tijdvak (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, and return and payment must both be received by that date (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums). The monthly dates for 2024 are: December 2023 period 31 January 2024, January period 29 February 2024, February period 31 March 2024, March period 30 April 2024, April period 31 May 2024, May period 30 June 2024, June period 31 July 2024, July period 31 August 2024, August period 30 September 2024, September period 31 October 2024, October period 30 November 2024, November period 31 December 2024, December 2024 period 31 January 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.21 Btw quarterly dates 2024

The quarterly dates for 2024 are: fourth quarter 2023 on 31 January 2024, first quarter 30 April 2024, second quarter 31 July 2024, third quarter 31 October 2024, fourth quarter 31 January 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.22 Btw yearly return 2024

A yearly return for 2024 is due by 31 March 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte).

### 3.23 Btw submission window

Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### 3.24 Uitstel for a btw return

Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties (exceptional situations), such as an ernstige calamiteit (serious calamity), and must be requested in writing to the belastingkantoor (tax office) before the filing date. A naheffingsaanslag and a boete remain possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte).

### 3.25 Bewaarplicht

AWR art. 52 lid 4 says administratieplichtigen (persons with an administration duty) keep the gegevensdragers (data carriers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (business profit) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (income from other activities). Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst table gives basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basisgegevens are the grootboek (general ledger), the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie (debtors and creditors records), the voorraadadministratie (stock records), the in- and verkoopadministratie (purchase and sales records), the loonadministratie (payroll records), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### 3.26 KVK and Belastingdienst registration

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 says the opgave (notification) for the first inschrijving (registration) of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when the registration is made within that window, an identity check is possible up to three months ahead, and appointments up to eight weeks ahead (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer (VAT number) from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Official example: the definitive aanslag for tax year 2024

The Belastingdienst example for the 2024 return: filing before 1 May 2025 gives a definitive aanslag at the latest on 31 December 2027. With uitstel to 1 September 2025 the latest date is 30 April 2028 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).

### Example 2. Official example: what counts toward the aanslaggrens

The definitive-aanslag page prints three standing examples (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag):

1. A voorlopige teruggaaf of € 100 was received and € 80 was due, so € 20 too much was received. That € 20 is below the grens and the outcome is a nihilaanslag.
2. A voorlopige aanslag of € 100 was paid and € 120 was due, so € 20 remains and the outcome is a € 20 aanslag.
3. A refund of € 100 was received after filing and € 80 was due, so the outcome is a € 20 te betalen (to be paid) aanslag, because a refund received after filing does not count toward the grens.

Calculated check against the 2024 grens. Step 1: the aanslaggrens for 2024 is € 56 (section 2). Step 2: the amount in the examples is € 20. Step 3: € 20 is lower than € 56, so € 20 is not higher than the aanslaggrens and the higher-than test is not met. The checked material notes that the examples are unchanged since the 2023 capture and does not reconcile examples 2 and 3 with the 2024 grens of € 56, see section 7.

### Example 3. Calculated example: belastingrente on a 2024 return filed after 1 May, aanslag unchanged

Inputs: the 2024 return is filed on 3 May 2025. The aanslag is dated 4 December 2025 and follows the return unchanged. The amount to pay is € 1.000.

Step 1: rente is charged because the return arrives on or after 1 May 2025 (section 3.13).
Step 2: the rente period starts on 1 July 2025.
Step 3: the end is the earlier of 6 weeks after the aanslag date and 19 weeks after receipt of the return. 4 December 2025 plus 42 days (6 weeks) = 15 January 2026. 3 May 2025 plus 133 days (19 weeks) = 13 September 2025. The earlier date is 13 September 2025.
Step 4: days from 1 July 2025 to 13 September 2025 in the 30/360 count: 30 days for July plus 30 days for August plus 13 days for September = 73 days.
Step 5: the rate on tax year 2024 is 6,5% from 1 July 2025.
Step 6: 73 / 360 × 6,5% × € 1.000 = 0,2027778 × € 65 = € 13,18, rounded down to whole euros = € 13.

The belastingrente is € 13.

### Example 4. Calculated example: belastingrente where the aanslag deviates, across the rate change

Inputs: the 2024 return is filed on 28 April 2025 with too low an income. An extra aanslag of € 2.500 is dated 11 December 2025.

Step 1: rente is charged because the aanslag deviates from the return, even though the return was filed before 1 May.
Step 2: the period runs from 1 July 2025 to 6 weeks after the aanslag date. 11 December 2025 plus 42 days = 22 January 2026.
Step 3: days: 1 July 2025 to 1 January 2026 is six months = 180 days, plus 22 days in January = 202 days.
Step 4: the 2025 part runs at 6,5%. 180 / 360 × 6,5% × € 2.500 = 0,5 × € 162,50 = € 81,25, rounded down = € 81.
Step 5: the 2026 part runs at 5%. 22 / 360 × 5% × € 2.500 = 0,0611111 × € 125 = € 7,64, rounded down = € 7.
Step 6: total € 81 + € 7 = € 88.

### Example 5. Official example and calculated example: navorderingstermijn

Official example: tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, navordering possible until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag).

Calculated example for 2024, following that pattern. Step 1: take the belastingschuld at the end of the tax year, 31 December 2024. Step 2: 5 years after that date is 31 December 2029. Step 3: the uitstel for the 2024 return runs from 1 May 2025 to 1 September 2025, a duration of 4 months, and the term is extended by the duration of the uitstel. Step 4: 31 December 2029 plus 4 months = 30 April 2030. The checked material does not state an end date for tax year 2024, see section 7.

### Example 6. Calculated example: payment termijn and voorlopige aanslag termijnen

Inputs: an aanslag for tax year 2024 dated 15 September 2025 with € 3.000 to pay, a navorderingsaanslag dated 15 September 2025, and a naheffingsaanslag dated 15 September 2025.

- Aanslag: invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. 15 September 2025 plus 42 days = 27 October 2025.
- Navorderingsaanslag: 1 month after the date = 15 October 2025.
- Naheffingsaanslag: 14 days after the date = 29 September 2025.

Calculated example for a voorlopige aanslag. A voorlopige aanslag for 2024 is dated 15 March 2024 and the amount is € 2.700. Step 1: month of the date = March 2024. Step 2: months remaining after March in 2024 = April to December = 9. Step 3: 9 equal monthly termijnen of € 2.700 / 9 = € 300 each. Step 4: the first termijn is one month after the date = 15 April 2024, the last = 15 December 2024.

### Example 7. Calculated example: bezwaar timeline on the same aanslag

Inputs: the aanslag for tax year 2024 is dated 15 September 2025.

Step 1: bezwaar must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. 15 September 2025 plus 42 days = 27 October 2025.
Step 2: if the bezwaar is filed on 27 October 2025, acknowledgement follows within 3 weeks. 27 October 2025 plus 21 days = 17 November 2025.
Step 3: the payment of the same aanslag is due 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet, the same 27 October 2025 (section 3.9).

### Example 8. Calculated example: btw quarterly deadline and verzuimboete

Inputs: a quarterly filer with the period January to March 2024 (first quarter 2024). The return and the payment are both due 30 April 2024 (section 2).

- Variant A: the return is received on 1 May 2024. That is 1 kalenderdag after the deadline and inside the 7 kalenderdagen coulance, so no verzuimboete.
- Variant B: the return is received on 10 May 2024. That is 10 kalenderdagen after the deadline and outside the 7 kalenderdagen coulance, so the verzuimboete is € 68.

The btw rente period for late payment starts on 1 January after the tax year, so a payment on 10 May 2024 lies before that start and no rente period runs.

### Example 9. Calculated example: filing without an invitation and verzoek om vermindering for 2024

Filing without an invitation. Step 1: a return for tax year 2024 can be filed up to 5 years back. Step 2: 2024 plus 5 years = 2029. Step 3: the final date is 31 December 2029 (stated in the checked material, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen).

Verzoek om vermindering. Step 1: the checked rule is online if fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. Step 2: the stated example is aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Step 3: applying the same rule to aanslag 2024 gives 2024 plus 5 = 2029, so 31 December 2029. The checked sources do not print this 2024 date, see section 7.

### Example 10. Calculated example: KVK registration window

Inputs: the business starts on 1 May 2024. Step 1: the two-week window starts one week before the start. 1 May 2024 minus 7 days = 24 April 2024. Step 2: the window ends one week after the start. 1 May 2024 plus 7 days = 8 May 2024. So the opgave for the first inschrijving is made between 24 April 2024 and 8 May 2024. Registering inside the window means the KVK-nummer is issued immediately. Other required opgaven are made within 1 week after the fact, so a change on 1 May 2024 is reported by 8 May 2024.

### Example 11. Official example: bewaarplicht term start

The Belastingdienst example is a 4-year lease. The gegeven is no longer actual after those 4 years, so it is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Step 1: 4 + 7 = 11 years in total (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

- Payment terms: the checked material states the same Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, so no change is recorded for 2024. The checked material gives no 2023 value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01).
- Bewaarplicht and the KVK registration window: the checked material gives no 2023 values for these items, so no change can be reported.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Register the onderneming with KVK inside the two-week window, one week before to one week after the start of the business. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. Record the start date, the KVK-nummer and the RSIN where the form is not an eenmanszaak (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- Keep the administratie as basisgegevens for 7 years, onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data for 10 years, with the term starting when the gegeven is no longer actual (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Record the aangiftebrief and its date. The 2024 income tax return is due before 1 May 2025, or before 1 September 2025 with uitstel, and uitstel must be requested before the filing date (https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- Record the voorlopige aanslag payments and refunds. They are settled in the 2024 return. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens, a paid voorlopige aanslag and a refund received after filing do not (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- File the btw return and pay together by the dates in section 2. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- Record the date of each aanslagbiljet. Payment is due 6 weeks after that date for an aanslag, and bezwaar must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).
- Book belastingrente when the 2024 income tax return is late or the aanslag deviates from the return. It runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026. The rate during 2024 itself was 7,5% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Note the btw rente start of 1 January after the tax year, and the 3-month window with no rente for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Online end date of the verzoek om vermindering for an aanslag 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is fewer than 5 years after the tax year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). The stated examples are aanslag 2020 until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028 (same page). Applying the rule to 2024 lands on 31 December 2029, which is also the stated final date for filing the 2024 return without an invitation (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). A Belastingdienst statement for the 2024 assessment year would settle it.
- End date of the navorderingstermijn for tax year 2024 is not stated in the checked sources. The rule is 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). The stated example is tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, until 31 May 2022 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). A Belastingdienst example for tax year 2024 would settle it.
- Aanslaggrens wording. The current page uses hoger dan with € 56 and € 18 for 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Captures up to 2023 used gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, such as € 52 and € 17 for 2023 in the April 2023 capture (https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The checked material marks the equivalence of the two wordings for whole-euro amounts as its own reading, not as a statement from the source. Two of the three standing examples on the live page also print a € 20 aanslag, an amount below the 2024 aanslaggrens of € 56, and the material says the examples are unchanged since the 2023 capture. A Belastingdienst explanation of the current wording, or updated examples, would settle it.
- Uitstel channel, settled. The form page states that online uitstel in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May gives uitstel to 1 September and is only for someone who received an aangiftebrief (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). The uitstel page states that online is not possible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Read in full, the second page covers the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Settled reading: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May filing date, paper form otherwise.
- KVK and btw-number term, settled. One wording is two weeks, the other ten werkdagen (https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). Ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks and the Belastingdienst states registration takes at most 10 werkdagen, so the two wordings agree.
- Carry-over of a late change to the 2024 voorlopige aanslag is not stated. The checked page states that a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). It does not give the corresponding rule for a change to the 2024 voorlopige aanslag. A Belastingdienst statement for 2024 would settle it.
- No official belastingrente example for tax year 2024 is printed in the checked material. The examples at 7,5% are listed under 2023 because they concern the 2023 return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). Section 4 therefore uses calculated examples for 2024.
