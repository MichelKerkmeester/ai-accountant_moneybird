## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### No pro-rating of the 1.225 hours

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The Belastingdienst example: a webhosting business started on 1 July leaves 6 months as an ondernemer, and the taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours are not recalculated to the part of the year.

**Calculated example.** A start on 1 May 2024 leaves eight months. The period from 1 May to 31 December 2024 inclusive contains 245 days, which is exactly 35 weeks, so it contains 175 weekdays. At 8 hours a day, 175 x 8 = 1.400,00 hours. That is above 1.225 hours, so this illustration shows that a part-year start can still meet the urencriterium when enough hours are worked. The rule remains that the 1.225 hours are not pro-rated.

### Carry-forward of niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). FISIN 2024, section 8.4.3. The example has no startersaftrek, so the profit cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 applies.

- 2022: profit € 5.000 against a zelfstandigenaftrek of € 6.310. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 limits the aftrek to the profit, so the aftrek is € 5.000 and € 6.310 - € 5.000 = € 1.310 remains unused.
- 2023: a loss of € 25.000, so the cap sets the aftrek to € 0 and the whole zelfstandigenaftrek of € 5.030 remains unused. The saldo becomes € 1.310 + € 5.030 = € 6.340.
- 2024: profit € 100.000 minus the 2024 zelfstandigenaftrek of € 3.750 gives € 96.250. The offset of € 6.340 raises the 2024 zelfstandigenaftrek, so the profit after the ondernemersaftrek becomes € 96.250 - € 6.340 = € 89.910, before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The saldo becomes € 0.
- Check against art. 3.76 lid 7: the offset in 2024 is at most the amount by which the 2024 profit exceeds the 2024 zelfstandigenaftrek, which is € 100.000 - € 3.750 = € 96.250. The whole € 6.340 fits, so the full € 6.340 is offset and the saldo is € 6.340 - € 6.340 = € 0.

### Starter with a profit of € 60.000

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader below the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the urencriterium, was not an ondernemer in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period, and has no other income. Profit € 60.000.

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 3.750.
- Startersaftrek: € 2.123.
- Ondernemersaftrek: € 3.750 + € 2.123 = € 5.873.
- Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000 - € 5.873 = € 54.127.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 13,31% x € 54.127,00 = € 7.204,30, rounded down.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 54.127 - € 7.204,30 = € 46.922,70.
- Tariefsaanpassing: the grondslagverminderende posten are the ondernemersaftrek of € 5.873 and the MKB-winstvrijstelling of € 7.204,30. Taxable box 1 income plus these posten is € 46.922,70 + € 5.873 + € 7.204,30 = € 60.000,00, which is below € 75.518, so no tariefsaanpassing applies.

### Profit of € 120.000 and the cap on the tariefsaanpassing

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader below the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the urencriterium, was an ondernemer in all of 2019 to 2023, so no startersaftrek, and has no other income. Profit € 120.000.

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 3.750.
- Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 120.000 - € 3.750 = € 116.250.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 13,31% x € 116.250,00 = € 15.472,88, rounded.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 116.250 - € 15.472,88 = € 100.777,12.
- Grondslagverminderende posten: € 3.750 + € 15.472,88 = € 19.222,88.
- Taxable box 1 income plus the grondslagverminderende posten: € 100.777,12 + € 19.222,88 = € 120.000,00.
- Excess over the threshold of € 75.518: € 120.000 - € 75.518 = € 44.482.
- Tariefsaanpassing by the formula: 12,53% x € 44.482,00 = € 5.573,59, rounded down.
- Cap: 12,53% x € 19.222,88 = € 2.408,63, rounded.
- The lower of the two applies, so the tariefsaanpassing is € 2.408,63.
- Check against the maximum value: before the tariefsaanpassing the posten are worth 49,50% x € 19.222,88 = € 9.515,33, rounded. After the tariefsaanpassing of € 2.408,63 the value is € 9.515,33 - € 2.408,63 = € 7.106,70. This is 36,97% x € 19.222,88 = € 7.106,70, rounded. That matches the maximum value of 36,97% in section 2.

### Meewerkaftrek with a profit of € 80.000

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the ondernemer meets the urencriterium, the partner works in the business without pay for 1.300 hours, profit € 80.000.

- 1.300 hours falls in the band from 1.225 to under 1.750 hours, which carries 3%.
- Meewerkaftrek: 3% x € 80.000,00 = € 2.400,00.
- Boundary check from the statute wording: exactly 1.225 hours also falls in the 3% band, because the band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" 1.225 hours to "minder dan" 1.750 hours. Exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band.
- Variant on pay: if the partner were paid € 4.000, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible from the profit and the partner is treated as working without pay. The meewerkaftrek stays available and the € 4.000 is no income for the partner.

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid at four use levels

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the ondernemer is below the AOW age at the start of 2024, was not an ondernemer in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, has a right to a WIA benefit, and does not meet the urencriterium but meets the verlaagd urencriterium (for example 900 hours, which is at least 800 and not more than 1.225).

- Not applied in the five preceding years, profit € 3.000: the amount is € 12.000, which is more than the profit, so the aftrek is capped at € 3.000.
- Applied for one of the five preceding years, profit € 20.000: the amount is € 8.000, which is less than the profit, so the aftrek is € 8.000.
- Applied for two of the five preceding years, profit € 20.000: the amount is € 4.000, so the aftrek is € 4.000.
- Applied for three or more of the five preceding years: the statute gives no amount for this case, so the aftrek is € 0.

### Stakingsaftrek

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the staking of one whole business.

- Staking profit € 5.000, no earlier stakingsaftrek: the aftrek equals the staking profit, at most € 3.630, so the aftrek is € 3.630.
- Staking profit € 2.000, no earlier stakingsaftrek: the aftrek equals the staking profit, so the aftrek is € 2.000. The unused part of the maximum is € 3.630 - € 2.000 = € 1.630.
- Later staking with a profit of € 10.000, after € 2.000 of stakingsaftrek was used earlier: the maximum is € 3.630 - € 2.000 = € 1.630, so the aftrek is € 1.630.
- After the full € 3.630 was used earlier: the maximum is € 3.630 - € 3.630 = € 0, so there is no further stakingsaftrek.

### AOW age at the start of 2024

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader at the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the urencriterium, was not an ondernemer in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, and had the zelfstandigenaftrek applied at most twice in that period. Profit € 40.000.

- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 1.875 (section 2).
- Startersaftrek at AOW age: € 1.062 (section 2).
- Ondernemersaftrek: € 1.875 + € 1.062 = € 2.937.
- Profit after the ondernemersaftrek: € 40.000 - € 2.937 = € 37.063.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: 13,31% x € 37.063,00 = € 4.933,09, rounded.
- Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 37.063 - € 4.933,09 = € 32.129,91.

### Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the ondernemer has an S&O-verklaring from RVO, at least 500 hours of S&O work, meets the urencriterium, was not an ondernemer in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, and had this aftrek applied at most twice in that period.

- Base amount: € 15.551.
- Starter increase: € 7.781.
- Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 15.551 + € 7.781 = € 23.332.
- Interaction: this amount is part of the ondernemersaftrek, so it lowers the profit before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. With a profit of € 60.000 and this part alone, the profit after this aftrek would be € 60.000 - € 23.332 = € 36.668.

### MKB-winstvrijstelling without the urencriterium and in a loss year

**Calculated example.** No urencriterium is needed for the MKB-winstvrijstelling.

- Profit € 60.000, no urencriterium and no ondernemersaftrek: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 13,31% x € 60.000,00 = € 7.986,00, so the belastbare winst uit onderneming is € 60.000 - € 7.986,00 = € 52.014,00.
- Loss year, profit of minus € 10.000,00 and no ondernemersaftrek: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 13,31% x minus € 10.000,00 = minus € 1.331,00, so the belastbare winst uit onderneming is minus € 10.000,00 - (minus € 1.331,00) = minus € 8.669,00. The vrijstelling makes the loss smaller, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous.
