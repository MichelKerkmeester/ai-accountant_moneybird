## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### No pro-rating of the 1.225 hours

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) example: a webhosting business started on 1 July leaves 6 months as an entrepreneur (ondernemer), and the taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours are not recalculated to the part of the year.

**Calculated example.** A start on 1 May 2024 leaves eight months. The period from 1 May to 31 December 2024 inclusive contains 245 days, which is exactly 35 weeks, so it contains 175 weekdays. At 8 hours a day, 175 x 8 = 1.400,00 hours. That is above 1.225 hours, so this illustration shows that a part-year start can still meet the hours criterion (urencriterium) when enough hours are worked. The rule remains that the 1.225 hours are not pro-rated.

### Carry-forward of unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)

**Official example** (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). FISIN 2024, section 8.4.3. The example has no starter's deduction (startersaftrek), so the profit cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 applies.

- 2022: profit € 5.000 against a self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 6.310. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 limits the deduction (aftrek) to the profit, so the deduction is € 5.000 and € 6.310 - € 5.000 = € 1.310 remains unused.
- 2023: a loss of € 25.000, so the cap sets the deduction to € 0 and the whole self-employed person's deduction of € 5.030 remains unused. The balance (saldo) becomes € 1.310 + € 5.030 = € 6.340.
- 2024: profit € 100.000 minus the 2024 self-employed person's deduction of € 3.750 gives € 96.250. The offset of € 6.340 raises the 2024 self-employed person's deduction, so the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) becomes € 96.250 - € 6.340 = € 89.910, before the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). The balance becomes € 0.
- Check against art. 3.76 lid 7: the offset in 2024 is at most the amount by which the 2024 profit exceeds the 2024 self-employed person's deduction, which is € 100.000 - € 3.750 = € 96.250. The whole € 6.340 fits, so the full € 6.340 is offset and the balance is € 6.340 - € 6.340 = € 0.

### Starter with a profit of € 60.000

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader below the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the hours criterion, was not an entrepreneur in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, had the self-employed person's deduction applied at most twice in that period, and has no other income. Profit € 60.000.

- Self-employed person's deduction: € 3.750.
- Starter's deduction: € 2.123.
- Entrepreneur's allowance: € 3.750 + € 2.123 = € 5.873.
- Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 5.873 = € 54.127.
- SME profit exemption: 13,31% x € 54.127,00 = € 7.204,30, rounded down.
- Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 54.127 - € 7.204,30 = € 46.922,70.
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): the deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) are the entrepreneur's allowance of € 5.873 and the SME profit exemption of € 7.204,30. Taxable box 1 income plus these items (posten) is € 46.922,70 + € 5.873 + € 7.204,30 = € 60.000,00, which is below € 75.518, so no rate adjustment applies.

### Profit of € 120.000 and the cap on the rate adjustment

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader below the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the hours criterion, was an entrepreneur in all of 2019 to 2023, so no starter's deduction, and has no other income. Profit € 120.000.

- Self-employed person's deduction: € 3.750.
- Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 120.000 - € 3.750 = € 116.250.
- SME profit exemption: 13,31% x € 116.250,00 = € 15.472,88, rounded.
- Taxable profit from business: € 116.250 - € 15.472,88 = € 100.777,12.
- Deductions that reduce the tax base: € 3.750 + € 15.472,88 = € 19.222,88.
- Taxable box 1 income plus the deductions that reduce the tax base: € 100.777,12 + € 19.222,88 = € 120.000,00.
- Excess over the threshold of € 75.518: € 120.000 - € 75.518 = € 44.482.
- Rate adjustment by the formula: 12,53% x € 44.482,00 = € 5.573,59, rounded down.
- Cap: 12,53% x € 19.222,88 = € 2.408,63, rounded.
- The lower of the two applies, so the rate adjustment is € 2.408,63.
- Check against the maximum value: before the rate adjustment the items are worth 49,50% x € 19.222,88 = € 9.515,33, rounded. After the rate adjustment of € 2.408,63 the value is € 9.515,33 - € 2.408,63 = € 7.106,70. This is 36,97% x € 19.222,88 = € 7.106,70, rounded. That matches the maximum value of 36,97% in section 2.

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) with a profit of € 80.000

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the entrepreneur meets the hours criterion, the partner works in the business without pay for 1.300 hours, profit € 80.000.

- 1.300 hours falls in the band from 1.225 to under 1.750 hours, which carries 3%.
- Deduction for assisting partner: 3% x € 80.000,00 = € 2.400,00.
- Boundary check from the statute wording: exactly 1.225 hours also falls in the 3% band, because the band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" 1.225 hours to "minder dan" 1.750 hours. Exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band.
- Variant on pay: if the partner were paid € 4.000, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible from the profit and the partner is treated as working without pay. The deduction for assisting partner stays available and the € 4.000 is no income for the partner.

### Starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) at four use levels

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the entrepreneur is below the AOW age at the start of 2024, was not an entrepreneur in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, has a right to a WIA benefit, and does not meet the hours criterion but meets the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) (for example 900 hours, which is at least 800 and not more than 1.225).

- Not applied in the five preceding years, profit € 3.000: the amount is € 12.000, which is more than the profit, so the deduction is capped at € 3.000.
- Applied for one of the five preceding years, profit € 20.000: the amount is € 8.000, which is less than the profit, so the deduction is € 8.000.
- Applied for two of the five preceding years, profit € 20.000: the amount is € 4.000, so the deduction is € 4.000.
- Applied for three or more of the five preceding years: the statute gives no amount for this case, so the deduction is € 0.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the cessation (staking) of one whole business.

- Cessation profit € 5.000, no earlier discontinuation deduction: the deduction equals the cessation profit, at most € 3.630, so the deduction is € 3.630.
- Cessation profit € 2.000, no earlier discontinuation deduction: the deduction equals the cessation profit, so the deduction is € 2.000. The unused part of the maximum is € 3.630 - € 2.000 = € 1.630.
- Later cessation with a profit of € 10.000, after € 2.000 of discontinuation deduction was used earlier: the maximum is € 3.630 - € 2.000 = € 1.630, so the deduction is € 1.630.
- After the full € 3.630 was used earlier: the maximum is € 3.630 - € 3.630 = € 0, so there is no further discontinuation deduction.

### AOW age at the start of 2024

**Calculated example.** Inputs: a sole trader at the AOW age at the start of 2024, who meets the hours criterion, was not an entrepreneur in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, and had the self-employed person's deduction applied at most twice in that period. Profit € 40.000.

- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age: € 1.875 (section 2).
- Starter's deduction at AOW age: € 1.062 (section 2).
- Entrepreneur's allowance: € 1.875 + € 1.062 = € 2.937.
- Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 40.000 - € 2.937 = € 37.063.
- SME profit exemption: 13,31% x € 37.063,00 = € 4.933,09, rounded.
- Taxable profit from business: € 37.063 - € 4.933,09 = € 32.129,91.

### Deduction for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk)

**Calculated example.** Inputs: the entrepreneur has an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO), at least 500 hours of R&D (S&O) work, meets the hours criterion, was not an entrepreneur in 1 or more of 2019 to 2023, and had this deduction applied at most twice in that period.

- Base amount: € 15.551.
- Starter increase: € 7.781.
- Deduction for research and development work: € 15.551 + € 7.781 = € 23.332.
- Interaction: this amount is part of the entrepreneur's allowance, so it lowers the profit before the SME profit exemption. With a profit of € 60.000 and this part alone, the profit after this deduction would be € 60.000 - € 23.332 = € 36.668.

### SME profit exemption without the hours criterion and in a loss year

**Calculated example.** No hours criterion is needed for the SME profit exemption.

- Profit € 60.000, no hours criterion and no entrepreneur's allowance: the SME profit exemption is 13,31% x € 60.000,00 = € 7.986,00, so the taxable profit from business is € 60.000 - € 7.986,00 = € 52.014,00.
- Loss year, profit of minus € 10.000,00 and no entrepreneur's allowance: the SME profit exemption is 13,31% x minus € 10.000,00 = minus € 1.331,00, so the taxable profit from business is minus € 10.000,00 - (minus € 1.331,00) = minus € 8.669,00. The exemption (vrijstelling) makes the loss smaller, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous.
