## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) and the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) for tax year 2024, together with the hours criterion (urencriterium) that gates most of the entrepreneur's allowance. It also covers who counts as an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting, IB-ondernemer), because without that status there is no right to the entrepreneur's allowance and no exemption on profit as entrepreneur (ondernemer). The law for 2024 is enacted, with the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) at € 3.750 and the SME profit exemption at 13,31% as printed in the statute valid on 1 January 2024. Two amounts printed differently in FISIN 2024 are settled in favour of the statute and are listed in section 7.
