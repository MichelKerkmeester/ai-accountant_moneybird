## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) status

Whether someone is an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax purposes (inkomstenbelasting) is judged by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) per activity, separately from VAT (btw) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). Someone who is a VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration looks at more conditions for the income tax. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts (same source). Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source (same source). Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes.

Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) registration is not the test. The checked pages say that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration makes its own assessment after registration (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). A page that literally says that KVK registration alone cannot make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes is not in the checked material.

The statute defines the entrepreneur as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (onderneming) (art. 3.5) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports the profit as income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/). The SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) applies only to profit as entrepreneur (art. 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Hours criterion (urencriterium), 1.225 hours and the second test (tweede toets)

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur, and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is "grotendeels" (largely) spent on the businesses, or (b) the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 1). The second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur (non-ondernemer) year in the five-year lookback, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years (same source). For 2024 the lookback window for test (b) is 2019 to 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6). FISIN prints this window per year, and the 2024 printing follows the statute's five preceding calendar years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur, and the taxpayer must still make at least 1.225 hours in that period (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The KVK example: a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day that is 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). Standby time does not count. A webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage (same source). Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (same source).

The hours criterion is a condition of the self-employed person's deduction (art. 3.76 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), of the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) (art. 3.78, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and, per FISIN, of the deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Hours criterion, partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) but the taxpayer does not (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household (same source). A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b) (same source). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) as example for (b) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.6).

### Hours criterion, pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5).

### Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium)

Art. 3.78a lid 3: the reduced hours criterion is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a lid 3). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). In the checked material it appears as a condition only of the starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### Parts of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for research and development work, the deduction for assisting partner, the starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.74). The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is not a separate list item. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3 (same source). The article text is identical in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.74).

Persons entitled to a share (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction or SME profit exemption on their co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The entrepreneur's allowance reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption is calculated (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Self-employed person's deduction

Art. 3.76: the self-employed person's deduction applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (lid 1) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 1). For 2024 the amount is € 3.750 for an entrepreneur below the AOW age at the start of the year (lid 2) (same source, art. 3.76 lid 2). At the AOW age at the start of the year the deduction is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too. The amount for 2024 is then € 1.875 (lid 4) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4). FISIN 2024 agrees with € 3.750 and prints € 2.123 for the AOW age amount in the same sentence, which is a misprint against the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

The deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction (lid 5) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5). The amount cut off by this cap is unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count (same source). The text of art. 3.76 is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.76).

### Unused self-employed person's deduction

Lid 6: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 6). Lid 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first (same source, art. 3.76 lid 7). Lid 8: the offset is also set by decision open to objection (same source, art. 3.76 lid 8). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes the same carry-forward and notes the decision route (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek). For a starter the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, and that excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### Starter's deduction

For 2024 the starter's deduction is € 2.123 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). At the AOW age at the start of the year it is € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), because art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the lid 2 and lid 3 amounts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 4).

The starter's deduction raises the self-employed person's deduction and is not a separate item in art. 3.74. It requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, and the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years (same source).

### Starter's deduction, prior-years condition

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the self-employed person's deduction is raised by the starter's deduction if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). For 2024 FISIN prints the five preceding years as 2019 to 2023 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The starter's deduction does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969, a tax-neutral return from a bv (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv), where that started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit with the excess as a loss (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting". That wording means the same as the statute (same source and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 3). A year in which the profit cap set the self-employed person's deduction to nil still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for this count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.76 lid 5).

### Starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work

Art. 3.78a applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before (same source). The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), and a periodic benefit from disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance (same source).

For 2024 the amounts are € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year, and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78a). The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then (same source). The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 3.78a).

### Deduction for research and development work

The R&D deduction (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) is part of the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.77). It is a base amount plus a starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period (same source). For 2024 the base amount is € 15.551 (same source) and the starter increase is € 7.781 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

FISIN adds that the deduction requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), at least 500 hours of R&D (S&O) work and the hours criterion (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The amounts are indexed per year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.77).

### Deduction for assisting partner

Art. 3.78: the deduction for assisting partner applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78). For 2024 the bands are 1,25% for 525 to under 875 hours, 2% for 875 to under 1.225 hours, 3% for 1.225 to under 1.750 hours and 4% from 1.750 hours, each of the profit (same source).

The band runs from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 hours in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 hours in the 4% band (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.78 lid 2). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.78). Hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). The text is identical 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.78).

### Pay to the partner

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.16 lid 4, text identical 2020 to 2027). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). That means the deduction for assisting partner can apply while the pay is left out of the profit (same source).

### Discontinuation deduction

Art. 3.79: the discontinuation deduction applies to profit made with or on the cessation of one or more whole businesses (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years (same source). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and that there is no deduction on profit as co-entitled party (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a decrease (afneming) of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion (omzetting) (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65). That part was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29).

### Discontinuation deduction, own-account period for a continued business

For a business continued under rollover on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) (art. 3.63), the discontinuation deduction applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### Old-age reserve additions

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition is possible from 2023 and none in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force (in werking) for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 (same sources).

The decrease rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end if the business or part of it ceased (gestaakt) in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 10a.29). Decreases are added to profit (same source). Lid 12 and 14: the taxpayer may deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return (same source). Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (community of property, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the discontinuation deduction for a decrease after a tax-neutral rollover. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

For the contribution base (premiegrondslag) and the annual allowance (jaarruimte), art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 state that for 2023 and 2024 the profit is taken before addition to the reserve, and the annual allowance for 2023 and 2024 is reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the forced decrease (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 10a.29).

### SME profit exemption

Art. 3.79a: the SME profit exemption is a percentage of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.79a). The percentage for 2024 is 13,31% (same source). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration MKB page agrees (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). FISIN 2024 prints 14%, which is a misprint against the statute (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

Art. 3.2: the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, so the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 3.2). No hours criterion is needed. The statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none (same source and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). There is no exemption (vrijstelling) on profit as co-entitled party (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### Rate adjustment

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3). For 2024 the percentage is 12,53% and the threshold is € 75.518 (same source). The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage (same source). The article exists in every version 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, art. 2.10).

### Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption

For 2024 the maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption together is 36,97%, being the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage of 12,53% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling). In the checked wording, every euro of these deductions is worth at most 36,97% of its amount (same source and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10).
