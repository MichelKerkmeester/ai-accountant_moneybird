## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 5.030 in 2023 to € 3.750 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age (AOW-leeftijd): € 2.515 in 2023 to € 1.875 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 14.202 in 2023 to € 15.551 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- R&D (S&O) starter increase: € 7.106 in 2023 to € 7.781 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 14% in 2023 to 13,31% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 12,57% above € 73.031 in 2023 to 12,53% above € 75.518 in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, art. 2.10).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption: 36,93% in 2023 to 36,97% in 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Lookback window for the second test (tweede toets) of the hours criterion (urencriterium) and for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) condition: 2018 to 2022 in 2023 to 2019 to 2023 in 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Unchanged in 2024: the hours criterion of 1.225 hours, the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) of 800 hours, the starter's deduction of € 2.123 and € 1.062 at AOW age, the starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) of € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands of 1,25% to 4%, the partner pay threshold of € 5.000 and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) maximum of € 3.630 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- No rule changes: the 2024 material states the same rules as 2023, including art. 10a.29 for the old old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): no addition is possible in 2024, the same as from 1 January 2023. For 2023 the checked material records an addition over a broken book year that started before 1 January 2023 and ended in 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220232-doteren-aan-de-fiscale-oudedagsreserve-bij-een-gebroken-boekjaar-eindigend-in-belastingjaar-2023/). The 2024 material records no such addition for 2024.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) are income tax (inkomstenbelasting) items for the 2024 return. The order is profit as entrepreneur (ondernemer), then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption, which together give the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).
- Keep the records that make the hours criterion (urencriterium) hours plausible, for example an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Keep the records that make the partner's hours plausible for the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) from profit and is no income for the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- If the hours criterion was not met, check whether the taxpayer is an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer) at all. Someone who is not reports the profit under income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), with no entrepreneur's allowance and no schemes such as the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/).
- The unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) and its offset are set by decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar), so watch for that decision (art. 3.76 lid 6 and 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- Keep a record of discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) used in earlier years. The 2024 maximum of € 3.630 is reduced by earlier discontinuation deduction, and an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation (staking) (art. 3.79, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).
- Annuity (lijfrente) premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for 2024 by choice in the return, up to the decrease of the old old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) (art. 10a.29 lid 12 and 14, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, text the same 2023 to 2027). On death the old reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- For an owner who made a tax-neutral return (geruisloze terugkeer) from a bv under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 in 2024 or in 2019 to 2023, there is no starter's deduction (startersaftrek) and no starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) (art. 3.76 lid 3 and art. 3.78a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for 2024.

Two FISIN 2024 printings differ from the 2024 statute and are settled in favour of the statute:

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) at AOW age: the statute gives € 1.875 under art. 3.76 lid 2 and lid 4 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), while FISIN 2024 prints € 2.123 in the same sentence (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute text settles the value.
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): the statute gives 13,31% under art. 3.79a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) MKB page agrees (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), while FISIN 2024 prints 14% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The statute text settles the value.
