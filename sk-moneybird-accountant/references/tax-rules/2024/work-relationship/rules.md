## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties (Wet DBA) is the Wet van 3 februari 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, a prior declaration about the status of the work). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. Loondienst (employment) has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid (personal work) and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer (contractor) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer (entrepreneur) for income tax or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### 3.2 Staatsblad 2016, 165

Staatsblad 2016, 165 is the Besluit van 8 april 2016. It lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking (deemed employment) for thuiswerkers (home workers), gelijkgestelden (persons treated as employees) and artiesten (artists) by agreement. It is not the Wet DBA itself. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html. The loondienst page states that thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefit), WIA (disability benefit) and WW (unemployment benefit). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### 3.3 Fictieve dienstbetrekking gelijkgestelden

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. They include aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten (agents), artiesten and beroepssporters (professional athletes), gelijkgestelden, stagiairs (interns), thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair (intermediary). For a gelijkgestelde the page gives the usual test: 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. A fictieve dienstbetrekking can bring loonheffingen (payroll taxes) without a real employment contract. For thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten the written opt-out of section 3.2 can prevent that outcome. Source: same URL.

### 3.4 DBA enforcement regime 2024

In 2024 the Belastingdienst still worked under the handhavingsmoratorium. Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied, so 2024 is its last year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. In a non-kwaadwillend case the first step was the aanwijzing of section 3.5, not a direct correction. For the ZZP this means 2024 sits between the old aanwijzing route and the normal rules that started on 1 January 2025. Source: same URL.

### 3.5 Aanwijzing under the moratorium

In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing: reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige (self-employed person), or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen (additional payroll tax assessments) and boetes. Source: same URL. The moratorium and its exceptions are enforcement policy, not a change to the law itself. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.6 End of the moratorium

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid (false self-employment) can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. This change was announced during 2024 and does not alter the treatment of 2024 itself, which still ran under the moratorium. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.7 Lifting of the moratorium announced

The cabinet announced the end of the moratorium per 1 January 2025 in a Kamerbrief (letter to parliament) of 6 September 2024. A commissiedebat (parliamentary committee debate) followed on 12 September 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. This announcement is the reason 2024 is described as the last year of the moratorium.

### 3.8 Moties shaping the zachte landing

The moties of 1 October 2024 shaped the zachte landing (soft landing), the transition approach recorded in the tranche 2025 plan of section 3.10. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. The checked material gives no further detail on the content of these moties. The specific 2025 and later enforcement details that followed are outside this 2024 file.

### 3.9 Handhavingsplan 2024

The Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties 2024 was published on 28 February 2024, with a perspectiefnota (perspective note) and the memo richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB (guidelines for the knock-on effect to inkomstenbelasting and omzetbelasting). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action at the opdrachtnemer for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie (the qualification of the income source). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

### 3.10 Handhavingsplan tranche 2025

The tranche 2025 plan is versie 1.0 of 18 December 2024, sent to the Tweede Kamer that day, 28 pages. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. It records the transition to the normal rules from 1 January 2025, and it honours the modelovereenkomsten to the end of 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275) on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: same URL. The plan also stated integration of DBA supervision from 1 January 2026. The later 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

### 3.11 Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking

The Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking (decision and weighing framework for the private-law employment relationship) was published on 1 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The checked material gives no further description of its content. It was published in the last full year of the moratorium.

### 3.12 Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten. Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Source: same URL. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: same URL. The tranche 2025 plan confirms that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.

### 3.13 Modelovereenkomst vrije vervanging

The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging (free substitution) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. That withdrawal was the position during 2024 as well.

### 3.14 Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon (legal person) such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon (intermediary) or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months, ended on 10 July 2021, the online pilot environment stayed active, and there was no rechtskracht (legal force) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring as one outcome. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.15 Case law

The case law that applied in 2024 starts with HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of the partijbedoeling (the intent of the parties). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), added an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding (embedding) and ondernemerschap, and treats vrije vervanging not as an absolute element. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. In HR Uber (prejudiciële vragen, preliminary questions) there is no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten, so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.16 VBAR in preparation in 2024

The VBAR (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden) began with an internet consultation of the original bill from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2024 the bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the advice was published on 11 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The 2024 material records the advice as critical with a limited contribution, and notes that the kernactiviteiten (core activities) element was dropped after the consultation. Source: same URL. Nothing of the VBAR applied to tax year 2024.

### 3.17 VBAR hourly presumption

The bill creates a new art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst (employment contract). The € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum (reference date) 1 January 2025. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. Effect: the werkende can invoke the presumption, and the opdrachtgever must prove that there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/. This provision was not law in 2024. In 2024 the VBAR was only at the Raad van State stage.

### 3.18 VBAR amount build-up

The memorie van toelichting explains the € 36: 120% of the WML (wettelijk minimumloon, statutory minimum wage) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag (surcharge) for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours. That gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html. See section 4 for the arithmetic.

### 3.19 VBAR indexed amount

The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. This is the € 38 (peildatum 1 januari 2026) that the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/ and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11, see section 7. None of this applied in 2024.

### 3.20 WML at 1 January 2025

The WML was € 14,06 per hour on 1 January 2025. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025. That amount is the input for the unrounded € 35,43 and the € 36,00 of the VBAR presumption. The checked material does not state the WML amount that applied in 2024 itself.

### 3.21 WML at 1 January 2026

The WML was € 14,71 per hour on 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026. That amount is the input for the unrounded about € 37,07 and the € 38 of the VBAR indexed amount.

### 3.22 VBAR later enactment

Bill 36 783 was ingediend (submitted) on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. A nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part. The Tweede Kamer adopted it on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD) and the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan. The Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, was published on 29 June 2026 and entered into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026. That date was chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. Sources: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html. For tax year 2024 none of this applied.

### 3.23 IB return 2024 filing deadline

The income tax return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief (filing letter), often 1 May, and the filing period opens on 1 March. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen. Uitstel (deferral) must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May, and a granted uitstel gives 4 months extra. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting. For the 2024 return the filing deadline was 1 May 2025 and uitstel could be requested until 1 May 2025. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. AWR art. 9 lid 1 sets a term of at least one month after the invitation and lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend it under conditions. The text is the same in 2020 and 2026. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.

### 3.24 IB return 2024 with uitstel

With uitstel the 2024 return had to be filed before 1 September 2025. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. Uitstel usually means belastingrente (tax interest). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte. The form can be used only for the first uitstel request, and the fiscal partner must request separately. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte.

### 3.25 Definitive aanslag 2024 latest

The definitive aanslag (final assessment) for 2024 can be set until 31 December 2027. With 4 months uitstel the latest date is 30 April 2028. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. AWR art. 11 lid 3: the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld (tax debt) arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. The text is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### 3.26 Belastingrente rate 2024

The belastingrente rate for 2024 is 7,5% for the inkomstenbelasting and btw. It is the first year of annual rate setting. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB herfinancieringsrente (refinancing rate) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Source: same URL.

### 3.27 Belastingrente on tax year 2024 IB

Belastingrente on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting. For tax year 2024 the rate path is 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### 3.28 Payment terms 2024

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An aanslag is invorderbaar (collectible) 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after its date. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after its date. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01. These terms matter when a qualification is corrected and a payroll tax or income tax assessment follows.

### 3.29 Bewaarplicht

AWR art. 52 lid 4, the same in 2020 and 2026: administratieplichtigen keep the gegevensdragers (data carriers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming (profit from business) or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (income from other activities). Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans (balance sheet) and staat van baten en lasten (statement of income and expenses). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01. The Belastingdienst table gives basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual (a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more). Basisgegevens include the grootboek (ledger), debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie (debtors and creditors records), voorraadadministratie (stock records), in- and verkoopadministratie (purchase and sales records) and loonadministratie (payroll records). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
