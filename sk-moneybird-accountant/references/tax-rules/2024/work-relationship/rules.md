## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

The Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) (Wet DBA) is the Act of 3 February 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (a prior declaration about the status of the work, Verklaring arbeidsrelatie). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. Employment (loondienst) has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform personal work (persoonlijke arbeid) and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### 3.2 Staatsblad 2016, 165

Staatsblad 2016, 165 is the Decree of 8 April 2016. It lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. It is not the Wet DBA itself. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html. The employment page states that home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The contractor then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefit), WIA (disability benefit) and WW (unemployment benefit). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.

### 3.3 Deemed employment relationship for persons treated as employees (fictieve dienstbetrekking gelijkgestelden)

The employment page lists the deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). They include contractors for work (aannemers van werk) and helpers, agents (agenten), artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees, interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors (opdrachtnemers) working through an intermediary (intermediair). For a person treated as an employee (gelijkgestelde) the page gives the usual test: 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst. A deemed employment relationship can bring payroll taxes (loonheffingen) without a real employment contract. For home workers, persons treated as employees and artists the written opt-out of section 3.2 can prevent that outcome. Source: same URL.

### 3.4 DBA enforcement regime 2024

In 2024 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) still worked under the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium). Until 1 January 2025 the moratorium applied, so 2024 is its last year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. In a case not involving bad faith (non-kwaadwillend), the first step was the instruction (aanwijzing) of section 3.5, not a direct correction. For the ZZP this means 2024 sits between the old instruction route and the normal rules that started on 1 January 2025. Source: same URL.

### 3.5 Instruction under the moratorium

In a case not involving bad faith, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration first gave an instruction: reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally (opzettelijk) creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid), knowing or reasonably being able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Acting in bad faith cases could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) for payroll taxes and penalties (boetes). Source: same URL. The moratorium and its exceptions are enforcement policy, not a change to the law itself. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.6 End of the moratorium

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions (aanwijzingen) are given. False self-employment can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. This change was announced during 2024 and does not alter the treatment of 2024 itself, which still ran under the moratorium. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.7 Lifting of the moratorium announced

The cabinet announced the end of the moratorium per 1 January 2025 in a Parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 6 September 2024. A committee debate (commissiedebat) followed on 12 September 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. This announcement is the reason 2024 is described as the last year of the moratorium.

### 3.8 Motions (moties) shaping the soft landing (zachte landing)

The motions of 1 October 2024 shaped the soft landing, the transition approach recorded in the tranche 2025 plan of section 3.10. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. The checked material gives no further detail on the content of these motions. The specific 2025 and later enforcement details that followed are outside this 2024 file.

### 3.9 Enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) 2024

The Enforcement plan employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) 2024 was published on 28 February 2024, with an outlook memorandum (perspectiefnota) and the guidelines on the interaction between income tax and VAT (richtlijnen doorwerking IH en OB). Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The guidelines on the interaction between income tax and VAT have existed since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the payroll taxes at the client (opdrachtgever). After LH corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action at the contractor for earlier years aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. A contractor in regular income tax or VAT supervision has the most recent open profit tax return (aangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

### 3.10 Enforcement plan tranche 2025

The tranche 2025 plan is version (versie) 1.0 of 18 December 2024, sent to the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) that day, 28 pages. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf. It records the transition to the normal rules from 1 January 2025, and it honours the model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) to the end of 2029 after motion (motie) Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275) on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: same URL. The plan also stated integration of DBA supervision from 1 January 2026. The later 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.

### 3.11 Decision and assessment framework for private-law employment relationships (Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking)

The Decision and assessment framework for private-law employment relationships was published on 1 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The checked material gives no further description of its content. It was published in the last full year of the moratorium.

### 3.12 Model agreements

Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment, but only if the parties work as described. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten. Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Source: same URL. The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: same URL. The tranche 2025 plan confirms that they are honoured to end 2029 after motion Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.

### 3.13 Model agreement free substitution (vrije vervanging)

The approval of the model agreements free substitution was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. That withdrawal was the position during 2024 as well.

### 3.14 Web module assessment of the employment relationship (Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie)

The Web module assessment of the employment relationship is for clients (opdrachtgevers) with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside an employment relationship, an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a bv, for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon) or for a foreign contractor. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months, ended on 10 July 2021, the online pilot environment stayed active, and there was no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with a client declaration (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/ and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.

### 3.15 Case law

The case law that applied in 2024 starts with HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, on the role of the intention of the parties (partijbedoeling). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), added a Catalogue of Circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) that names integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), and treats free substitution not as an absolute element. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. In HR Uber (preliminary questions, prejudiciële vragen) there is no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on enforcement (handhaving). Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.16 VBAR in preparation in 2024

The VBAR (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden) began with an internet consultation of the original bill from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2024 the bill went to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024 and the advice was published on 11 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file. The 2024 material records the advice as critical with a limited contribution, and notes that the core activities (kernactiviteiten) element was dropped after the consultation. Source: same URL. Nothing of the VBAR applied to tax year 2024.

### 3.17 VBAR hourly presumption

The bill creates a new art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). The € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimum wage (minimumloon) and minimum holiday allowance (minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the presumption does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Article II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Article III lid 2: for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). Effect: the worker (werkende) can invoke the presumption, and the client must prove that there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/. This provision was not law in 2024. In 2024 the VBAR was only at the Council of State stage.

### 3.18 VBAR amount build-up

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains the € 36: 120% of the WML (statutory minimum wage, wettelijk minimumloon) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV (disability insurance, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours. That gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (excl. btw) at reference date 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html. See section 4 for the arithmetic.

### 3.19 VBAR indexed amount

The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. This is the € 38 (reference date 1 January (januari) 2026) that the Senate (Eerste Kamer), Government of the Netherlands website (rijksoverheid.nl) and Ondernemersplein state. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/ and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026. The ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11, see section 7. None of this applied in 2024.

### 3.20 WML at 1 January 2025

The WML was € 14,06 per hour on 1 January 2025. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025. That amount is the input for the unrounded € 35,43 and the € 36,00 of the VBAR presumption. The checked material does not state the WML amount that applied in 2024 itself.

### 3.21 WML at 1 January 2026

The WML was € 14,71 per hour on 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026. That amount is the input for the unrounded about € 37,07 and the € 38 of the VBAR indexed amount.

### 3.23 IB return 2024 filing deadline

The income tax return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), often 1 May, and the filing period opens on 1 March. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen. Deferral (uitstel) must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May, and a granted deferral gives 4 months extra. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting. For the 2024 return the filing deadline was 1 May 2025 and deferral could be requested until 1 May 2025. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. AWR art. 9 lid 1 sets a term of at least one month after the invitation and lid 2 lets the inspector (inspecteur) extend it under conditions. The text is the same in 2020 and 2026. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01.

### 3.24 IB return 2024 with deferral

With deferral the 2024 return had to be filed before 1 September 2025. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte. The form can be used only for the first deferral request, and the fiscal partner must request separately. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte.

### 3.25 Final assessment (definitieve aanslag) 2024 latest

The final assessment for 2024 can be set until 31 December 2027. With 4 months deferral the latest date is 30 April 2028. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag. AWR art. 11 lid 3: the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. The text is the same on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.

### 3.26 Tax interest rate 2024

The tax interest rate for 2024 is 7,5% for the income tax and VAT. It is the first year of annual rate setting. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Source: same URL.

### 3.27 Tax interest on tax year 2024 IB

Tax interest on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. No interest (rente) is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The day count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting. For tax year 2024 the rate path is 6,5% from 1 July 2025 and 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### 3.28 Payment terms 2024

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 has the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after its date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after its date. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01. These terms matter when a qualification is corrected and a payroll tax or income tax assessment follows.

### 3.29 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4, the same in 2020 and 2026: persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office (belastingkantoor). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current (a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more). Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie) and payroll records (loonadministratie). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
