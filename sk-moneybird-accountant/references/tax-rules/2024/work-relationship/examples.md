## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1. Official example, the VBAR amount build-up to € 36

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) gives the build-up of the € 36 at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025.

Step 1: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 is € 16,872 per hour.
Step 2: the 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV and pension plus the 15% surcharge for general costs is 40% of € 16,872, that is € 6,7488. € 16,872 + € 6,7488 = € 23,6208.
Step 3: times 1,5 for non-billable hours. € 23,6208 × 1,5 = € 35,4312.
Step 4: unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (excl. btw), rounded up to € 36,00.

Source for the method, the inputs and the outcomes: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html. Source for the WML of € 14,06: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025.

### Example 2. Calculated example, indexation to € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026

Same method, with the WML of € 14,71 at 1 January 2026 from section 2.

Step 1: 120% of € 14,71 = € 17,652.
Step 2: plus 40% of € 17,652 = € 7,0608 gives € 24,7128.
Step 3: times 1,5 = € 37,0692.
Step 4: unrounded about € 37,07, rounded up to € 38.

The checked material states the unrounded amount as about € 37,07 and the rounded amount as € 38. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026 and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering. The step-by-step arithmetic here is mine from those section 2 figures.

### Example 3. Calculated example, the € 36 threshold applied (not applicable to tax year 2024)

An contractor (opdrachtnemer) works for a business client at an invented hourly rate of € 30. € 30 is at most € 36, so under art. 7:610aa lid 1 BW the presumption of an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst) would apply, and the client (opdrachtgever) would have to prove that there is no employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). At an invented rate of € 40 per hour the rate is above € 36, so the presumption would not apply on the rate alone. If the client were a natural person not acting in a profession or business, lid 3 would exclude the presumption. The threshold and the rules are from section 2, the rates are invented inputs. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html. This provision was not in force (in werking) in 2024.

### Example 4. Calculated example, the instruction (aanwijzing) timeline in 2024

Suppose the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) gives an instruction on an invented date of 1 June 2024 in a case not involving bad faith (non-kwaadwillend). The usual 3 months to reshape the relationship or to process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) run to an invented date of 1 September 2024. If the instruction is not followed, the correction obligation (correctieverplichting) runs from the date of the instruction, so from 1 June 2024. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving.

### Example 5. Calculated example, a model agreement (modelovereenkomst) valid on 6 September 2024

Suppose an approved model agreement was valid on 6 September 2024 and its text states that it is valid until 31 December 2025. Under the policy it can still be used up to and including 31 December 2029, and only if the parties work as described. From 6 September 2024 no new model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) are assessed and none are renewed. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.

### Example 7. Calculated example, tax interest (belastingrente) on a 2024 return that is corrected

An invented 2024 return arrives on 10 November 2025 and the assessment deviates from the return with an invented € 1.000 extra tax. The assessment is dated 20 December 2025, so the interest (rente) period runs from 1 July 2025 to 6 weeks after the assessment date, 31 January 2026.

Step 1: day count by the 30/360 rule: 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2025 is 6 months = 180 days. 1 January 2026 to 31 January 2026 is 30 days. Total 210 days.
Step 2: 180 days at 6,5% on € 1.000: 180/360 × 6,5% × € 1.000 = € 32,50, rounded down to € 32.
Step 3: 30 days at 5% on € 1.000: 30/360 × 5% × € 1.000 = € 4,17, rounded down to € 4.
Step 4: total € 36.

Sources for the method and the rates: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.

### Example 8. Calculated example, payment terms

An invented supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for payroll taxes (loonheffingen) dated 10 March 2025 is collectible 14 days after its date, so on 24 March 2025. An invented additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) dated 1 October 2025 is collectible 1 month after its date, so on 1 November 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01.
