## 5. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA enforcement regime: 2023 ran under the handhavingsmoratorium with the aanwijzing route. 2024 is the last year of that moratorium, and the end per 1 January 2025 was announced on 6 September 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Modelovereenkomsten: in 2023 the approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023. In 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed from 6 September 2024, and agreements valid on that date can be used up to and including 31 December 2029. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- VBAR: the internet consultation started on 6 October 2023 with an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2024 the bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the advice was published on 11 November 2024, still proposed. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Handhavingsplan: 2023 had its own plan per the 2026 plan. The 2024 plan was published on 28 February 2024 with the perspectiefnota and the memo richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB, and the tranche 2025 plan followed on 18 December 2024 with 28 pages. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- Richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB: they exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.
- New in 2024: the Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking of 1 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- IB return: the 2023 return was due before 1 May 2024 and with uitstel before 1 September 2024. The 2024 return was due before 1 May 2025 and with uitstel before 1 September 2025. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Definitive aanslag: the 2023 aanslag could be set until 31 December 2026, with 4 months uitstel 30 April 2027. The 2024 aanslag can be set until 31 December 2027, with 4 months uitstel 30 April 2028. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Belastingrente on the tax year: the 2023 return ran from 1 July 2024 at 7,5% and 6,5% from 1 January 2025. The 2024 return runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- The aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens (€ 56 and € 18 in 2024) belong to the assessment mechanics and not to this work relationship file. They changed from the 2023 values, see the year overview.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the records that show the actual work relationship per opdrachtgever: the agreement, any modelovereenkomst, and evidence of how work and working hours are set, inbedding, who performs the work, how pay is set and paid, commercial risk and entrepreneurial activity. The qualification turns on the actual work, and a modelovereenkomst only helps if the parties work as described. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.
- If the opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden or artiesten is used, keep the written clause. It must be in the contract before the first payment of pay. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html.
- For a modelovereenkomst valid on 6 September 2024, record that it can be used up to and including 31 December 2029 even if it states another validity date, and that from 6 September 2024 no new assessments and no renewals take place. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- As a natural person with winst uit onderneming you are an administratieplichtige. Keep the gegevensdragers 7 years, from the moment the gegeven is no longer actual, unless another term applies (onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data 10 years). Basisgegevens include the grootboek, debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, voorraadadministratie, in- and verkoopadministratie and loonadministratie. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- The income from the work lands in the income tax return for 2024 as winst uit onderneming only if the person qualifies as an ondernemer. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.
- File the 2024 return before 1 May 2025, or ask uitstel before that date for a filing date before 1 September 2025. The definitive aanslag for 2024 can be set until 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with 4 months uitstel. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- A change in expected profit goes in the Winst uit onderneming screen of the voorlopige aanslag (provisional assessment). Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen.
- A late or deviating 2024 return can carry belastingrente from 1 July 2025 at 6,5%, then 5% from 1 January 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- If the Belastingdienst corrects the opdrachtgever's loonheffingen, the richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB mean in principle no targeted action at the opdrachtnemer for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie. Record the year of the LH correction, because earlier years means tax years before it. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- If the opdrachtgever is a bv, note that the Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- HR Uber date UNVERIFIED. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it.
- VBAR is proposed for the purpose of tax year 2024. In 2024 the bill was at the Raad van State stage, to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024, advice published 11 November 2024. The bill was introduced in 2025 and became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, in force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html. Nothing of it applied to 2024.
- The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 of the VBAR that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering. Publication in the Staatscourant would settle it.
- The € 36 against € 38 is settled, not a conflict. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, from an unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- BAZ, bill 36 912, is proposed. Ingediend on 24 March 2026. Technische briefing on 13 May 2026. Inbreng verslag delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May. Verslag published on 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote. The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912. The verslag date 27 May against 29 May is settled: inbreng delivered 27 May, verslag published 29 May. The bill is later than tax year 2024 and has no effect on it.
- Zelfstandigenwet is proposed. Internet consultation open until 29 October 2026, beoogde inwerkingtreding (intended entry into force) January 2028. The zelfstandigentoets (self-invoicing, KVK registration, number of opdrachtgevers, provisions against ondernemersrisico's) and the werkrelatietoets (freedom to organise the work, own account and risk) would give a veilige haven (safe harbour) when both are met and the work is done that way. Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers. The proposal is later than tax year 2024 and has no effect on it.
- Move to regular handhaving is a corrected point between two plans. The tranche 2025 plan of 18 December 2024 stated integration of DBA supervision from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. The later plan settles it.
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 is corrected, not a conflict. It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force on 1 May 2016. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- For tax year 2024 itself the checked material lists no open points beyond these cross-year items.
