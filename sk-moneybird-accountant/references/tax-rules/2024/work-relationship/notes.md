## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA enforcement regime: 2023 ran under the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) with the instruction (aanwijzing) route. 2024 is the last year of that moratorium, and the end per 1 January 2025 was announced on 6 September 2024. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten): in 2023 the approval of the model agreements free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023. In 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed from 6 September 2024, and agreements valid on that date can be used up to and including 31 December 2029. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- VBAR: the internet consultation started on 6 October 2023 with an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2024 the bill went to the Council of State (raad van State) on 21 June 2024 and the advice was published on 11 November 2024, still proposed. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- Enforcement plan (handhavingsplan): 2023 had its own plan per the 2026 plan. The 2024 plan was published on 28 February 2024 with the outlook memorandum (perspectiefnota) and the memo with guidelines on the interaction between income tax and VAT (richtlijnen doorwerking IH en OB), and the tranche 2025 plan followed on 18 December 2024 with 28 pages. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- Guidelines on the interaction between income tax and VAT: they have existed since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf.
- New in 2024: the Decision and assessment framework for private-law employment relationships (beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking) of 1 November 2024. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- IB return: the 2023 return was due before 1 May 2024 and with deferral (uitstel) before 1 September 2024. The 2024 return was due before 1 May 2025 and with deferral before 1 September 2025. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Definitive assessment (aanslag): the 2023 assessment could be set until 31 December 2026, with 4 months deferral 30 April 2027. The 2024 assessment can be set until 31 December 2027, with 4 months deferral 30 April 2028. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- Tax interest (belastingrente) on the tax year: the 2023 return ran from 1 July 2024 at 7,5% and 6,5% from 1 January 2025. The 2024 return runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- The assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) (€ 56 and € 18 in 2024) belong to the assessment mechanics and not to this work relationship file. They changed from the 2023 values, see the year overview.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the records that show the actual work relationship per client (opdrachtgever): the agreement, any model agreement (modelovereenkomst), and evidence of how work and working hours are set, integration (inbedding), who performs the work, how pay is set and paid, commercial risk and entrepreneurial activity. The qualification turns on the actual work, and a model agreement only helps if the parties work as described. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten.
- If the opt-out for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) or artists (artiesten) is used, keep the written clause. It must be in the contract before the first payment of pay. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html.
- For a model agreement valid on 6 September 2024, record that it can be used up to and including 31 December 2029 even if it states another validity date, and that from 6 September 2024 no new assessments and no renewals take place. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer.
- As a natural person with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) you are a person required to keep records (administratieplichtige). Keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) 7 years, from the moment the item (gegeven) is no longer current, unless another term applies (onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data 10 years). Basic records (basisgegevens) include the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie) and payroll records (loonadministratie). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren.
- The income from the work lands in the income tax return for 2024 as profits from business activities only if the person qualifies as an entrepreneur (ondernemer). Not being in employment (loondienst) does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax or VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst.
- File the 2024 return before 1 May 2025, or ask deferral (uitstel) before that date for a filing date before 1 September 2025. The definitive assessment (aanslag) for 2024 can be set until 31 December 2027, or 30 April 2028 with 4 months deferral. Sources: https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag.
- A change in expected profit goes in the Profits from business activities screen of the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag). Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen.
- A late or deviating 2024 return can carry tax interest (belastingrente) from 1 July 2025 at 6,5%, then 5% from 1 January 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente.
- If the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) corrects the client's payroll taxes (loonheffingen), the guidelines on the interaction between income tax and VAT (richtlijnen doorwerking IH en OB) mean in principle no targeted action at the contractor (opdrachtnemer) for earlier years aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Record the year of the LH correction, because earlier years means tax years before it. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf.
- If the client is a bv, note that the Web module assessment of the employment relationship (webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie) is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a bv. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- HR Uber date UNVERIFIED. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid. A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it.
- VBAR is proposed for the purpose of tax year 2024. In 2024 the bill was at the Council of State (Raad van State) stage, to the Council of State on 21 June 2024, advice published 11 November 2024. The bill was introduced in 2025 and became the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, in force on 31 December 2026 by Decree of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026. Sources: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html. Nothing of it applied to 2024.
- The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 of the VBAR that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering. Publication in the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) would settle it.
- The € 36 against € 38 is settled, not a conflict. € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, from an unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026.
- BAZ, bill 36 912, is proposed. Submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026. Technical (technische) briefing on 13 May 2026. Contribution to the report (inbreng verslag) delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May. The report was published on 29 May 2026 (36 912, nr. 5). On 2026-10-11 it was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote. The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912. The report date 27 May against 29 May is settled: contribution delivered 27 May, report published 29 May. The bill is later than tax year 2024 and has no effect on it.
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) is proposed. Internet consultation open until 29 October 2026, intended entry into force (inwerkingtreding) January 2028. The self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) (self-invoicing, KVK registration, number of clients (opdrachtgevers), provisions against business risk (ondernemersrisico's)) and the work relationship test (freedom to organise the work, own account and risk, werkrelatietoets) would give a safe harbour (veilige haven) when both are met and the work is done that way. Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers. The proposal is later than tax year 2024 and has no effect on it.
- Move to regular enforcement (handhaving) is a corrected point between two plans. The tranche 2025 plan of 18 December 2024 stated integration of DBA supervision from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf. The later plan settles it.
- Staatsblad 2016, 165 is corrected, not a conflict. It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree of 8 April 2016 on the opt-out for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten). The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force on 1 May 2016. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file.
- For tax year 2024 itself the checked material lists no open points beyond these cross-year items.
