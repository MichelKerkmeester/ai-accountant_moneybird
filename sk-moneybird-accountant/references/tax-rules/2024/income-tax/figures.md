## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers Box 1 rates, AOW-age variants, tax credits (heffingskortingen, AHK and AK), rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and how profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) enter taxable income. The 2024 rules covered here are enacted. Source differences and unresolved points are identified below.
