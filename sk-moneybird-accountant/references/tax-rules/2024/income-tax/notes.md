## 2. CHANGES FROM 2023

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The Box 1 below-AOW combined rate rose from 36,93% up to € 73.031 to 36,97% up to € 75.518. The top rate stayed 49,50%. The statutory first-band income-tax part changed from 9,28% to 9,32%. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
- For taxpayers at AOW age, the first-bracket rate rose from 19,03% to 19,07%. The first boundary for people born on or after 1 January 1946 rose from € 37.149 to € 38.098. For people born before 1 January 1946, it rose from € 38.703 to € 40.021. The upper boundary rose from € 73.031 to € 75.518. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)
- In the AOW transition year, the monthly first-bracket rates changed from January 19,03%, February 20,52%, March 22,01%, April 23,50%, May 24,99%, June 26,48%, July 27,98%, August 29,47%, September 30,96%, October 32,45%, November 33,94% and December 35,43% to January 19,07%, February 20,56%, March 22,05%, April 23,54%, May 25,03%, June 26,52%, July 28,02%, August 29,51%, September 31,00%, October 32,49%, November 33,99% and December 35,47%. The first-bracket boundary changed from € 37.149 to € 38.098. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening)
- The premie volksverzekeringen ceiling changed from € 37.149 to € 38.098. For people born before 1 January 1946, it changed from € 38.703 to € 40.021. The 27,65% rate did not change. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen)
- The tariefsaanpassing percentage fell from 12,57% to 12,53%. The maximum deduction value rose from 36,93% to 36,97%. The top-bracket boundary used in the rule changed from € 73.031 to € 75.518. (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2)
- Below AOW age, the algemene heffingskorting rose from € 3.070 to € 3.362. Its phase-out point changed from € 22.660 to € 24.812, and its phase-out rate from 6,095% to 6,630%. Its nil point changed from € 73.031 to the 2024 top-bracket boundary. For a full year at AOW age, the credit rose from € 1.583 to € 1.735 and the year-table phase-out rate changed from 3,141% to 3,421%. The 2024 FISIN page prints 3,420%. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)
- Below AOW age, the arbeidskorting bands changed from up to € 10.740 at 8,231%, then € 884 plus 29,861% to € 23.201, then € 4.605 plus 3,085% to € 37.691, then € 5.052 minus 6,510% to € 115.295, to the 2024 bands in section 2. For a full year at AOW age, the bands changed from up to € 10.740 at 4,241%, then € 457 plus 15,388% to € 23.201, then € 2.374 plus 1,589% to € 37.691, then € 2.604 minus 3,355% to € 115.295, to the 2024 bands in section 2. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)
- The amount the Belastingdienst can take over from an employer's higher arbeidskorting changed from a maximum of € 5.052 below AOW age and € 2.604 at AOW age, with the income boundary at € 37.691, to € 5.532 and € 2.854, with the boundary at € 39.958. (https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_m_biljet_2023_ib3271t31fd.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)
- The IACK income threshold changed from € 5.547 to € 6.073. The maximum rose from € 2.694 to € 2.950. The year-table maximum point changed from € 29.076 to the 2024 point in section 2. For a full year at AOW age, the maximum rose from € 1.389 to € 1.521. The 11,45% and 5,90% rates did not change. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)
- The IACK child birth cutoff changed from born after 31 December 2010 to born after 31 December 2011. The co-ouder test changed from at least 78 days in a period of 6 months in a repeating pattern to at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated where co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting)
- Payout income references changed from € 10.705 to € 12.995 for own Box 1 income, from € 28.580 to € 31.402 for the partner, and from € 32.480 to € 35.375 for a partner at AOW age. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 5.030 to € 3.750. At AOW age, it fell from € 2.515 to € 1.875. The MKB-winstvrijstelling fell from 14% to 13,31%. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, MKB)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the profit calculation distinct from exempt profit components, ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The taxable business profit includes the relevant reductions, and it flows into taxable income from work and home. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, MKB)
- Record the basis for treating an activity as winst uit onderneming, including who runs it for their account, who can make binding agreements and who is liable for debts. The Belastingdienst also considers profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))
- The algemene heffingskorting is applied automatically in the return and may also be applied by an employer or benefit agency through payroll. The arbeidskorting may include business profit before ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling. The IACK is claimed through the return or a provisional assessment, not payroll. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)
- If AOW age is reached during 2024, calculate the AHK, AK and IACK through the 2024 online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag. The assessment is authoritative if its amount differs from a table by a few euros. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)
- The tariefsaanpassing is applied automatically and shown on the assessment. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen)
- The checked material does not state the record-retention period or the income tax filing deadline. It also does not provide BV-specific instructions for this file.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **AHK phase-out rate for a full year at AOW age:** the year table gives 3,421%, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives 3,420%. The arithmetic from € 1.735 divided by the difference between € 75.518 and € 24.812 is about 3,4217%. The year table is used in this file. The fetched text of Wet IB art. 8.10 was not reachable. The statutory text or an authoritative corrected table would settle the rate. Sources named in the supplied material: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen.
- **IACK maximum point:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024 gives a maximum of € 2.950 from € 31.839, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen says it is reached at € 31.837. Both state the same maximum. The statutory text or a corrected authoritative table would settle the exact point.
- **IACK co-ouder test:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen says 156 days in a period of 6 months. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting says at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated where co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year. This file follows https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting. A controlling official rule or corrected FISIN text would settle the difference.
- **Belastingdeel:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints 9,28%, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 give 9,32%. The 9,28% figure is the 2023 value. This file uses 9,32%.
- **AHK nil point:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 prints nil from € 75.519, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints nil at € 75.518. The sources describe the same rule, with the credit reaching nil at the top-bracket boundary. This file follows the year table.
- **Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek prints € 2.123, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024 gives 50% of € 3.750, or € 1.875. This file uses € 1.875.
- **MKB-winstvrijstelling:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek prints 14%, while MKB gives 13,31%. This file uses 13,31%.
- **AHK payout end date:** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen says payout lapses in 2024. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/heffingskortingen say it lapses from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later, while people born before 1 January 1963 remain eligible. This file follows the corrected 2023 end date for the affected group.
- **Tax calculation sequence:** the supplied sources do not print the full sequence from taxable Box 1 income through tariefsaanpassing and heffingskortingen in one statement. The order described in section 3 follows the statutory structure and heffingskorting pages and is an inference.
- **Source URL traceability:** the supplied checked material gives source labels for most claims but does not include their literal URLs. Those labels are preserved here without guessed URLs. The exact Belastingdienst URL supplied for Winst uit onderneming is linked in the relevant sections. A primary-source URL list would settle the missing links.
