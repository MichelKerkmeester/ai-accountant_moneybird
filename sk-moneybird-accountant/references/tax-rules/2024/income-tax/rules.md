## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2024

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

The published combined rate is 36,97% up to € 75.518, then 49,50%. In the statutory calculation, the first income-tax band is 9,32% up to € 38.098, with national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) charged separately up to that ceiling. Above the ceiling, the income-tax rate is 36,97%, which produces the same combined rate through the next boundary. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### Box 1 at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer at AOW age who was born on or after 1 January 1946, the first-bracket rate is 19,07% up to € 38.098. The next rate is 36,97% up to € 75.518, followed by 49,50%. The lower first-bracket rate reflects that the AOW premium is no longer due once AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen)

### Box 1 at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For a taxpayer at AOW age born before 1 January 1946, the first boundary is € 40.021. The rates are 19,07% to that boundary, 36,97% up to € 75.518, then 49,50%. The higher boundary is set out in art. 2.10a. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 2.10a)

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

In the year AOW age is reached, the first-bracket rate depends on the month in which that age is reached. The 2024 monthly rates are listed in section 2. The first bracket ends at € 38.098. The later brackets keep the ordinary rates. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening)

### National insurance contributions ceiling

The 27,65% national insurance contributions rate applies to contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to € 38.098, the end of the first bracket. The maximum premium (premie) is € 10.534. The rate comprises AOW, Anw and Wlz national insurance contribution components (premiedelen). AOW premium stops at AOW age. The supplied material does not state the continuing Anw and Wlz rule as a standalone rule, so that point is treated as an inference from the AOW-age rates. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen)

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

When qualifying deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) are deducted, the tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 12,53% of the amount by which taxable Box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds € 75.518. The increase is capped at 12,53% of the deducted amount. As a result, deductions in this situation are worth at most 36,97%. The covered deductions include personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), the deduction for owner-occupied home costs (aftrek kosten eigen woning), tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) and the exemption (vrijstelling) for making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen). The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment. (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, TA)

### General tax credit (Algemene heffingskorting, AHK) below AOW age

The general tax credit is available to every taxpayer and is applied automatically in the tax return. An employer or benefit agency can also apply it through payroll. For 2024, the phase-out base is taxable income from work and home. The credit starts to reduce above € 24.812 and reaches nil at the top-bracket boundary. FISIN prints the nil point as € 75.518, while the year table prints € 75.519. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### General tax credit for a full year at AOW age

The year table gives a maximum credit of € 1.735 and a phase-out rate of 3,421% above € 24.812. FISIN states 3,420%. The credit reaches nil at the top-bracket boundary. Section 7 records this unresolved rate difference. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) below AOW age

The employment tax credit is based on earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), this includes profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). It excludes income earned as a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or profit-sharing creditor (winstdelende schuldeiser). The 2024 table builds the credit through its bands, then phases it out by 6,510% to nil. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2022/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Employment tax credit for a full year at AOW age

The separate whole-year AOW table has lower build-up rates and a 3,358% phase-out in its top band. The band figures are in section 2. The ordinary employment tax credit tables do not apply in the year AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)

### Employment tax credit taken over from employer

If an employer applied a higher employment tax credit and earned income is not above € 39.958, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) takes over the employer's higher amount up to the stated maximum. The maximum is € 5.532 below AOW age and € 2.854 for a full year at AOW age. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024)

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK)

The income-related combination tax credit is 11,45% of earned income above € 6.073, up to the maximum in section 2. It is applied through the tax return or a provisional assessment, not through payroll. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### IACK for a full year at AOW age

For a full year at AOW age, the IACK rate is 5,90% and the maximum is € 1.521. The ordinary credit tables do not apply in the year AOW age is reached. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)

### IACK child and residence test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2011 and must be registered at the taxpayer's address for at least 6 months. A Government Gazette (Staatscourant) approval also accepts a child who lived in the same household for 6 months. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months does not remove the credit. A qualifying child can be the taxpayer's own, step or adopted child, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship, or a fiscal partner's child. A foster child (pleegkind) qualifies only when raised and maintained as one's own and no foster care allowance (pleegvergoeding) is received. Residents of the EU, EEA, Switzerland or BES can qualify. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting)

### IACK co-parent (co-ouder) test

A co-parent must provide roughly equal care, with at least 156 days per calendar year in each household. If co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year, the days are prorated. From 1 April, the stated threshold is 117 days in each household. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen instead says 156 days in a period of 6 months. The calendar-year rule is used here based on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Payout of unused tax credits (heffingskortingen, AHK and AK)

Only the general tax credit remains payable to a qualifying lowest-earning partner (minstverdienende partner) who was born before 1 January 1963. The partners must generally have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months, the partner must owe enough tax, and only the amount that cannot be set off against the taxpayer's own tax and premium can be paid out. The more-than-6-month condition is not required if the partner died in 2024. The payout of employment tax credit and IACK has ended. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Payout income references

The 2024 income references are € 12.995 for the taxpayer's own Box 1 income, and € 31.402 for the partner's income or € 35.375 when the partner is at AOW age. These are source references for the payout rules. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen)

### Income-tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components

Each tax credit (heffingskorting) is split into an income-tax part and national insurance contribution components. The 2024 income-tax part is 9,32%. The national insurance contribution components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. A person not insured for one scheme loses that scheme's part. If contribution liability (premieplicht) applies for only part of the year, the relevant contribution part (premiedeel) is time-proportional. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen prints 9,28%, the 2023 income-tax part. The 2024 value is 9,32% based on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/heffingskortingen to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

### Profits from business activities and taxable Box 1 income

A business is treated as the taxpayer's source of profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) when it is run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide the tax treatment. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration also weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))

Remove exempt profit components first. The stated examples include forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the part of debt waiver profit (kwijtscheldingswinst) above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business termination payments (bedrijfsbeëindigingsvergoedingen), WAZ benefits (waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, final levy (eindheffing) benefits, and mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions. A co-entitled party or lender (geldverstrekker) does not get entrepreneur's allowance or SME profit exemption. ([Belastingdienst, Winst uit onderneming](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming))

Taxable profits from business activities is business profit reduced by entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption. The SME profit exemption is calculated on profit after entrepreneur's allowance. Taxable income from work and home includes taxable business profit and is reduced by losses available for offset. The sequence used here for the box 1 tax, rate adjustment and tax credits follows the structure of the cited rules. The supplied material does not state the entire sequence in one source, so that ordering is an inference. (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, MKB, the yearly FISIN tax credits pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen))

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The 2024 self-employed person's deduction is € 3.750. At AOW age, the amount is 50% of that figure, or € 1.875. Unused self-employed person's deduction can be carried forward for 9 years if later profit is high enough. The supplied 2024 material does not set out the full eligibility test for the deduction. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The 2024 starter's deduction is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age. The supplied material gives the amounts but not the full eligibility conditions. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024)

### Starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The starter's deduction for entrepreneurs incapacitated for work (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) follows the amounts listed in section 2 and is capped at profit. The supplied material does not state the full conditions for each amount. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner uses the stated percentages over the same hour bands. The supplied material does not list the hour-band thresholds. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction has a maximum of € 3.630. The supplied material does not state the full eligibility conditions. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)

### SME profit exemption

The SME profit exemption is 13,31% of profit after entrepreneur's allowance. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek prints 14%, which is treated as an error. (MKB, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

No new additions to the old-age reserve are allowed in 2024. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)

### Zvw contribution

The income-related Zvw contribution exists. Its rates, bases and calculation belong in the Zvw reference file. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)

### AOW transition year for tax credits

The ordinary AHK, AK and IACK tables do not apply in the year AOW age is reached. Use the online form Request or amendment of a provisional assessment (verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag) 2024 in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) to calculate the amounts. A difference of a few euros between a table calculation and the assessment can occur, and the assessment is correct. (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024)
