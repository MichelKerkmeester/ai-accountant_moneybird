## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Tax year 2024 is the first year of the two-bracket box 2 rate. A taxpayer pays 24,5% on box 2 income up to € 67.000 and 33% above that. The excessief lenen (excessive borrowing) maximum drops from € 700.000 in 2023 to € 500.000 in 2024. The forfaitair voordeel (notional benefit) for a vbi or foreign investment company is 6,04%, the belastingkorting (tax credit) on an unoffset loss is 24,5%, and the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) fixed amount is € 56.000. All 2024 values in this file are enacted. The file covers the aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest) definition, box 2 income and rates, loss offset and conversion, excessief lenen, gebruikelijk loon and the pension interactions.

### 3.1 Box 2 bracket 1

The 24,5% rate applies to box 2 income up to € 67.000. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) The bracket applies per taxpayer. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Fiscal partners may divide the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang in any proportion that totals 100%. [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) Each partner's own first bracket then applies to his or her share. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Box 2 income is the reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen (disposal gains), minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) (art. 4.12). [art. 4.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vbi (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling, exempt investment institution) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief-lenen fiction (sub f). [art. 4.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)

Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Salary from the company is box 1 loon. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Interest on a loan to buy the shares is deductible. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang) Dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing (withholding tax). [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)

[art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)

### 3.6 Aanmerkelijk belang definition (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly:
(a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital,
(b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital,
(c) holds winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds,
(d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a,
(e) has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit. [art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

For 2024 the text has sub a to e. [art. 4.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts. [art. 4.6, 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) [art. 4.6, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025. [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html) Sub f therefore does not apply to 2024. [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html) In sub d "tot" became "om" between those texts. [art. 4.6, 2021](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01) [art. 4.6, 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)

Meesleepregeling (art. 4.9): once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that company also belong to it. [art. 4.9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) Meetrekregeling (art. 4.10): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang. [art. 4.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang) Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027. ### 3.7 Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14)

### 3.8 Excessief lenen maximum and mechanism

Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap, Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, issued 27 December 2022, in force 1 January 2023 (Artikel III). [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html) The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ("het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)", debts meaning all civil-law debt relations and obligations). ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Calculated example 1: Box 2 rate on a profit distribution of € 100.000.**
Step 1: bracket 1 is 24,5% up to € 67.000.
Step 2: the tax at the top of bracket 1 is 24,5% of € 67.000 = € 16.415,00, matching column III of the art. 2.12 table.
Step 3: income above the bracket is € 100.000 minus € 67.000 = € 33.000.
Step 4: bracket 2 tax is 33% of € 33.000 = € 10.890,00.
Step 5: total box 2 tax is € 16.415,00 plus € 10.890,00 = € 27.305,00.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

**Calculated example 2: Partner division with € 200.000 box 2 income.**
Step 1: fiscal partners may divide box 2 income in any proportion, as long as the total is 100%.
Step 2: in this example the two partners divide € 200.000 equally, so each has € 100.000.
Step 3: art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each partner's own first bracket applies to his own share.
Step 4: for each partner, the part above bracket 1 is € 100.000 minus € 67.000 = € 33.000.
Step 5: for each partner, the bracket 1 tax is 24,5% of € 67.000 = € 16.415,00 and the bracket 2 tax is 33% of € 33.000 = € 10.890,00.
Step 6: each partner pays € 16.415,00 plus € 10.890,00 = € 27.305,00 on his own € 100.000.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)

**Calculated example 3: Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss of € 20.000.**
Step 1: the percentage is 24,5%.
Step 2: the korting is 24,5% of € 20.000 = € 4.900,00.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 4.53](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

**Official example: FISIN 2024 belastingkorting.**
A loss of € 20.000 from 2022 gives a korting of € 4.900,00 in 2024 at 24,5%. [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)

**Calculated example 4: Loss offset period.**
A loss of € 20.000 from 2024 is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years. That is 2023, 2025, 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030. The loss is used in the order the losses arose. [art. 4.49](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

**Official example: Belastingdienst loss page.**
Shares are sold in 2024. The loss is € 20.000. It is set in a 2024 assessment issued in 2026. It is converted from 2026 into € 4.900,00, with the remainder until 2033. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)

**Calculated example 5: Excessief lenen with a loan of € 600.000 at 31 December 2024.**
Step 1: the maximum is € 500.000 plus amounts taxed earlier. In this example no amounts were taxed earlier, so the maximum is € 500.000.
Step 2: the fictief regulier voordeel is € 600.000 minus € 500.000 = € 100.000.
Step 3: assuming the fictief regulier voordeel is the only box 2 income, the part above bracket 1 is € 100.000 minus € 67.000 = € 33.000.
Step 4: the bracket 1 tax is 24,5% of € 67.000 = € 16.415,00.
Step 5: the bracket 2 tax is 33% of € 33.000 = € 10.890,00.
Step 6: the total box 2 tax is € 16.415,00 plus € 10.890,00 = € 27.305,00.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 4.14a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) [art. 2.12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

**Official example: FISIN 2024 excessief lenen.**
In 2024 the drempel is € 500.000 and the loan is € 600.000, so € 100.000 is taxed. In 2025 the drempel is € 600.000 and the loan is € 650.000. In 2026 there is a negative fictief regulier voordeel of € 125.000. [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang)

**Official example: Belastingdienst excessief lenen page, 2024 step.**
In 2024 the drempel is € 600.000, the loan is € 850.000, and € 250.000 is taxed. [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)

**Calculated example 6: Gebruikelijk loon.**
Step 1: the most comparable loon is € 67.000, and the comparable-loon test is 100% of it.
Step 2: the highest employee loon is € 56.000.
Step 3: the fixed amount is € 56.000.
Step 4: the highest of the three is € 67.000, so the gebruikelijk loon is € 67.000.
Step 5: if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year, the rule does not apply.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)

**Calculated example 7: ODV marktrente.**
Step 1: the ODV at the start of 2024 is € 100.000.
Step 2: the market rate is 2,657%.
Step 3: the increase is 2,657% of € 100.000 = € 2.657,00.
Step 4: the balance at the end of 2024 is € 100.000 plus € 2.657,00 = € 102.657,00.
All figures come from section 2. [Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/)

**Calculated example 8: Forfaitair voordeel.**
Step 1: the value at the start of the year is € 100.000.
Step 2: the forfaitair voordeel is 6,04% of € 100.000 = € 6.040,00.
Step 3: other reguliere voordelen from those shares are € 2.000,00.
Step 4: the forfaitair voordeel after reduction is € 6.040,00 minus € 2.000,00 = € 4.040,00.
Step 5: if the shares were held for six months, the amount is time-apportioned, so € 4.040,00 times 6/12 = € 2.020,00.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 4.14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)

**Calculated example 9: Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility.**
Step 1: the verkrijgingsprijs is € 100.000.
Step 2: the shares were inherited and box 2 income of € 24.000 was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer.
Step 3: reguliere voordelen of € 24.000 are received within 24 months after the death.
Step 4: on request, the € 24.000 is not counted as box 2 income and is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs, up to the € 24.000 taken into account with the deceased.
Step 5: the remaining verkrijgingsprijs is € 100.000 minus € 24.000 = € 76.000.
All figures come from section 2. [art. 4.12a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)
