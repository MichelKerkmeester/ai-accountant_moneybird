## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1: Hours criterion (urencriterium) in a part year (Official example)

**Official example.** The Chamber of Commerce (kamer van koophandel, KVK) gives a start on 1 May, which leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed to reach 1.225 hours. Arithmetic as printed: 117 working days x 8 hours = 936 hours. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) gives a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year.

Source: [KVK, de magische 1.225 uur](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium) and [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Example 2: Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** A sole trader below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) meets the hours criterion, has no starter's deduction (startersaftrek) and no other deductions. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: profit € 60.000.
- Step 2: Self-employed person's deduction € 2.470.
- Step 3: profit after entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000 - € 2.470 = € 57.530.
- Step 4: SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 57.530: € 57.530 x 0,127 = € 7.306,31.
- Step 5: Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 57.530 - € 7.306,31 = € 50.223,69.
- Step 6: Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) test: the deductions are € 2.470 + € 7.306,31 = € 9.776,31 and the test amount is € 50.223,69 + € 9.776,31 = € 60.000. That is below € 76.817, so no rate adjustment with this income alone.

### Example 3: same profit at state pension age (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Same facts as Example 2, but the entrepreneur has reached the state pension age at the start of 2025 and meets the hours criterion.

- Step 1: Self-employed person's deduction € 1.235.
- Step 2: profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 1.235 = € 58.765.
- Step 3: SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 58.765: € 58.765 x 0,127 = € 7.463,155, rounded to € 7.463,16.
- Step 4: Taxable profit from business: € 58.765 - € 7.463,16 = € 51.301,84.

### Example 4: starter with profit of € 60.000 (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years, meets the hours criterion and the self-employed person's deduction was applied at most twice in that period. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: Self-employed person's deduction € 2.470 plus starter's deduction € 2.123 = € 4.593.
- Step 2: profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 4.593 = € 55.407.
- Step 3: SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 55.407: € 55.407 x 0,127 = € 7.036,689, rounded to € 7.036,69.
- Step 4: Taxable profit from business: € 55.407 - € 7.036,69 = € 48.370,31.

### Example 5: low profit with starter's deduction (startersaftrek) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Same starter as Example 4, but the profit is € 4.000.

- Step 1: Self-employed person's deduction € 2.470 plus starter's deduction € 2.123 = € 4.593.
- Step 2: the profit cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not apply to an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction, so the full € 4.593 is deducted: € 4.000 - € 4.593 = -€ 593.
- Step 3: the excess of € 593 is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years.

### Example 6: Unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) (Official example)

**Official example.** The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration carry-forward page prints: 2025 profit € 1.500 against a self-employed person's deduction of € 2.470. The deduction (aftrek) is limited to the profit, so € 1.500 is used and € 970 is unused (unused self-employed person's deduction): € 2.470 - € 1.500 = € 970. The page then prints for 2026: profit € 50.000 minus € 1.200 gives € 48.800, minus € 970 gives € 47.830 and the remainder of the unused amount is € 0.

Source: [Belastingdienst, verrekenen niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### Example 7: carry-forward across three years (Official example, with a printed typo)

**Official example.** FISIN 2025 section 8.4.3 prints: 2023 profit € 5.000 against € 5.030, so € 30 is unused (€ 5.030 - € 5.000 = € 30). 2024 shows a loss of € 25.000 and € 3.750 is unused, so the balance (saldo) is € 30 + € 3.750 = € 3.780. 2025 profit € 100.000 with a printed right of € 2.740 (which should be € 2.470) gives € 97.260 (€ 100.000 - € 2.740 = € 97.260), minus € 3.780 gives € 93.480 (€ 97.260 - € 3.780 = € 93.480).

Corrected calculation with the section 2 figure of € 2.470, not printed on the page: € 100.000 - € 2.470 = € 97.530, then € 97.530 - € 3.780 = € 93.750.

Source: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Example 8: Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The entrepreneur meets the hours criterion and the partner works in the business without pay for exactly 1.225 hours. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: exactly 1.225 hours falls in the 3% band, because the band runs from 1.225 hours to under 1.750 hours.
- Step 2: Deduction for assisting partner 3% of € 60.000: € 60.000 x 0,03 = € 1.800,00.
- Step 3: Entrepreneur's allowance total: € 2.470 + € 1.800,00 = € 4.270,00.
- Step 4: profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 4.270,00 = € 55.730,00.
- Step 5: SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 55.730: € 55.730 x 0,127 = € 7.077,71.
- Step 6: Taxable profit from business: € 55.730,00 - € 7.077,71 = € 48.652,29.

### Example 9: Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Case A: the profit on the cessation (staking) of the whole business is € 10.000 and € 1.000 of discontinuation deduction was used in an earlier year. The maximum is then € 3.630 - € 1.000 = € 2.630, which is below the € 10.000 profit, so the deduction is € 2.630. Case B: the profit on the cessation is € 2.000 and no discontinuation deduction was used before. The deduction equals the profit, so € 2.000, which is below the maximum of € 3.630.

### Example 10: Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years, has a right to a qualifying disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering), meets the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) but not the hours criterion, and is below the state pension age.

- First use, profit € 3.000: the amount is € 12.000, capped at the profit, so the deduction is € 3.000.
- Second use, profit € 50.000: the amount is € 8.000, below the profit, so the deduction is € 8.000.
- Third use, profit € 50.000: the amount is € 4.000, below the profit, so the deduction is € 4.000.
- Fourth use, profit € 50.000: the statute has no tier for more than two earlier uses, so the deduction is € 0.

### Example 11: Deduction for research and development work (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** The entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the S&O deduction applied at most twice in that period, so the S&O starter increase applies. The self-employed person's deduction was applied more than twice in that period, so no starter's deduction applies. The entrepreneur has an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), at least 500 hours of S&O work in 2025 and meets the hours criterion. Profit is € 60.000.

- Step 1: Self-employed person's deduction € 2.470.
- Step 2: Deduction for research and development work: base amount € 15.738 plus starter increase € 7.875 = € 23.613.
- Step 3: total entrepreneur's allowance: € 2.470 + € 23.613 = € 26.083.
- Step 4: profit after entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 - € 26.083 = € 33.917.
- Step 5: SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 33.917: € 33.917 x 0,127 = € 4.307,459, rounded to € 4.307,46.
- Step 6: Taxable profit from business: € 33.917 - € 4.307,46 = € 29.609,54.

### Example 12: Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** Use the taxpayer of Example 2, with deductions of € 9.776,31 (€ 2.470 entrepreneur's allowance plus € 7.306,31 SME profit exemption) and a taxable profit from business of € 50.223,69.

- Case A: if this is the only income, the test amount is € 50.223,69 + € 9.776,31 = € 60.000, below € 76.817, so the tax is not raised.
- Case B: if other box 1 income brings the test amount to € 81.817, the excess is € 81.817 - € 76.817 = € 5.000. The rise is 12,02% of € 5.000: € 5.000 x 0,1202 = € 601,00. The cap is 12,02% of € 9.776,31: € 9.776,31 x 0,1202 = € 1.175,112462, rounded down to € 1.175,11, so the rise of € 601,00 stands.
- Case C: if the test amount is € 90.000, the excess is € 90.000 - € 76.817 = € 13.183. The rise would be 12,02% of € 13.183: € 13.183 x 0,1202 = € 1.584,5966, rounded to € 1.584,60, which is above the cap of € 1.175,11, so the rise is limited to € 1.175,11.
- The maximum value of the deductions is 37,48%, which is 49,50% minus 12,02%.

### Example 13: Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) with a broken hours criterion (urencriterium) (Calculated example)

**Calculated example.** An existing old-age reserve is € 50.000 at the start of 2025 and the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at the end of 2025 are € 30.000. The hours criterion was not met in 2025 and not met in 2024. There is no cessation and the state pension age was not reached.

- Step 1: no addition to the reserve is possible in 2025, so on that count the reserve stays at € 50.000.
- Step 2: because the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year, the reserve decreases by the excess of the reserve over the business assets: € 50.000 - € 30.000 = € 20.000.
- Step 3: the decrease of € 20.000 is added to profit.
