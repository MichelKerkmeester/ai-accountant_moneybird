## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) for the entrepreneur for income tax purposes (ib-ondernemer) in tax year 2025. The main figures are the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 2.470, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) of € 2.123 and the SME profit exemption of 12,7%, all built on the hours criterion (urencriterium) of 1.225 hours. Tax year 2025 is enacted law and the rules are the same as 2024 apart from the indexed and reduced amounts. Additions to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) are not possible in 2025, an existing reserve stays in place under the transitional rules of art. 10a.29.
