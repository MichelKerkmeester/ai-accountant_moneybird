## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 3.750 in 2024 to € 2.470 in 2025. Source: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01). The FISIN 2025 table agrees.
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension (AOW) age: € 1.875 in 2024 to € 1.235 in 2025. Source: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) for the 2024 amount under the 50% rule of lid 4, and [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) for the 2025 amount.
- Deduction (aftrek) for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 15.551 in 2024 to € 15.738 in 2025, indexed. Source: [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).
- S&O starter increase: € 7.781 in 2024 to € 7.875 in 2025. Source: [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling): 13,31% in 2024 to 12,7% in 2025. Source: [art. 3.79a, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [art. 3.79a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 12,53% above € 75.518 in 2024 to 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025. Source: [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption: 36,97% in 2024 to 37,48% in 2025. Source: [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Lookback window for the second test (tweede toets) of art. 3.6: 2019 to 2023 in 2024 to 2020 to 2024 in 2025. Source: [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Unchanged in 2025: the hours criterion (urencriterium) of 1.225 hours, the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) of 800 hours, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) of € 2.123, the starter's deduction at state pension age of € 1.062, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) of € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands of 1,25% to 4%, the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) maximum of € 3.630 and the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) rule that no addition is possible while an existing reserve follows art. 10a.29. Source: [version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek) and [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours of the entrepreneur. An agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices make the 1.225 hours plausible, and only hours actually spent on the business count. Standby time does not count. Source: [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Record the hours of the partner when the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) is used, those hours must be made plausible. Source: [Belastingdienst, meewerkaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Keep the S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) with the records when the deduction (aftrek) for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) is used. Source: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Keep evidence for the entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes (IB-ondernemer) status, because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) weighs profit, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer bent u ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/).
- The entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) land in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting), in that order. The unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) and its later offset are each set by decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar), so keep those decisions and the carry-forward balance. Sources: [art. 3.2 and 3.79a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- Pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), so that pay stays in the profit in the books and the partner counts as working without pay. Source: [art. 3.16, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- For an existing old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), annuity (lijfrente) premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return, and on death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return. Sources: [art. 10a.29, version 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- For a private limited company (BV) owner who brings the business back to a sole trader under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969, note that the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) and the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) do not apply where that return started in the year or one of the five years before. Sources: [art. 3.76 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

None for 2025.

- The € 2.740 that the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 carry-forward examples print as the 2025 self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) is an example typo. The statute valid on 1 January 2025 and the FISIN 2025 table print € 2.470. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The Zvw interaction of the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is verified in the checked material for 2020 and 2021 only, so this file makes no Zvw statement for 2025. Sources: [FISIN 2020, inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet) and [FISIN 2021, inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The Zvw detail belongs to the Zvw topic, and a 2025 Zvw source would settle it.
