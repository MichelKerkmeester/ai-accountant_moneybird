## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes (IB-ondernemer) status

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) judges per activity whether someone is an entrepreneur for income tax. This test stands apart from the VAT. A VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) is not automatically an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, because the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration looks at more conditions for income tax. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts. Activities are judged one by one and only sufficiently connected activities form one source. Activities in the hobby or family sphere do not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes. Someone who is not an entrepreneur for income tax purposes reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk). The profit is calculated the same way, but there is no right to schemes such as the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek). The statute defines the entrepreneur as the taxpayer for whose account a business is run and who is directly bound for its obligations (art. 3.4), and the independent profession (zelfstandig beroep) counts as a business (art. 3.5). The fetched pages do not literally say that Chamber of Commerce (kamer van koophandel) registration alone does not make someone an entrepreneur for income tax purposes, they say that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration makes its own assessment after registration. Sources: [Belastingdienst, wanneer bent u ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein, wanneer bent u ondernemer](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/), [art. 3.4 and 3.5, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.2 What the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) consists of

Art. 3.74 lists five parts: the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction (aftrek) for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek). The starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is not a separate list item. It is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3. The article text of art. 3.74 is identical in every version 2020 to 2027. Persons entitled to a share (medegerechtigden), for example a limited partner, get no self-employed person's deduction, no deduction for assisting partner, no discontinuation deduction and no SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) on their profit as a person entitled to a share. For 2025 the list applies as printed and the 2025 amounts are in the subsections below. Sources: [art. 3.74, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.74, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2020, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 Hours criterion (urencriterium), 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur. On top of that hours test, one of two further tests must be met. Test (a): the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses. Test (b): the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in a weekend who counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives a webhosting business started on 1 July, which means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. The test is applied in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). Sources: [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.4 Second test (tweede toets) waived and the 2020 to 2024 lookback

The second test of art. 3.6 lid 1 falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur (non-ondernemer) year in the five-year lookback. It comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. FISIN 2025 prints the lookback window for 2025 as 2020 to 2024. For comparison, FISIN 2020 printed 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 printed "20167 tot en met 2021", both misprints against the statute, which gives the five preceding calendar years, so 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).

### 3.5 Hours criterion and a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) but the taxpayer does not. Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives a dentist and dental assistant partnership as example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) for (b). Sources: [art. 3.6, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, voorwaarden urencriterium](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### 3.6 Hours criterion and pregnancy

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks. Sources: [art. 3.6 lid 5, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN 2023, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.7 Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium)

The reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) in art. 3.78a lid 3 is the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800 hours. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs. Sources: [art. 3.78a lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.78a lid 3, version 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

### 3.8 Self-employed person's deduction, € 2.470

Art. 3.76 applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion (lid 1). Lid 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) at the start of the year. For 2025 that amount is € 2.470. Only an entrepreneur for income tax purposes qualifies, so not a person entitled to a share and not someone who reports income from other work. The amount of € 2.470 is printed in art. 3.76 valid on 1 January 2025 and the FISIN 2025 table agrees. Watch out: the FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 carry-forward examples print € 2.740, which is an example typo. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.9 Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age, € 1.235

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the state pension age at the start of the year, the deduction is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too. For 2025 the amount is € 1.235. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.10 Profit cap and the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)

Art. 3.76 lid 5: the deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. The amount cut off by this cap is the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count. Lid 6: the tax inspector sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Lid 7: the amount is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Lid 8: the offset is also set by decision open to objection. The text is the same 2020 to 2027 apart from the amounts. The decisions and the carry-forward balance belong in the records. Sources: [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst, verrekenen niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).

### 3.11 Starter's deduction, € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the self-employed person's deduction by the starter's deduction when the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period. For 2025 the amount is € 2.123. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 by means of a tax-neutral return of a business from a private limited company (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) that began in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute. Sources: [art. 3.76 lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.12 Starter's deduction at state pension age, € 1.062

At the state pension age at the start of the year the starter's deduction is halved under art. 3.76 lid 4. For 2025 the amount is € 1.062. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.13 Starter's deduction prior-years condition

The condition is that the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied at most twice in that period. FISIN 2025 prints this condition for 2025. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration wording "niet elk jaar ondernemer" means the same as the statute, so one single non-entrepreneur year in the five-year window is enough. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration also caps the use at 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur and lets a year in which the profit cap set the self-employed person's deduction to nil still count as a year with self-employed person's deduction. A tax-neutral return (geruisloze terugkeer) of a business from a private limited company (besloten vennootschap, BV) under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 that began in the year or one of the five preceding years blocks the starter's deduction. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76 lid 3, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek).

### 3.14 Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs

Art. 3.78a applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong scheme, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached the state pension age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong scheme, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) and a periodic benefit from a disability or accident insurance (invaliditeits- of ongevallenverzekering). The amount is € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.78a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.78a, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026).

### 3.15 Deduction for research and development work (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), € 15.738

The deduction for research and development work (speur- en ontwikkelingswerk) is part of the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77). For 2025 the base amount is € 15.738. FISIN adds that the deduction requires an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), at least 500 hours of S&O work and the hours criterion. The amounts are indexed per year. Sources: [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.16 S&O starter increase, € 7.875

On top of the base amount a starter increase of € 7.875 applies for 2025 to an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period. The same S&O declaration from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, the 500-hour S&O test and the hours criterion apply. Sources: [FISIN 2025, ondernemersaftrek en investeringsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.77, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 3.17 SME profit exemption, 12,7%

Art. 3.79a makes the SME profit exemption 12,7% of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance. Art. 3.2 says the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption. The order is therefore profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. No hours criterion is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page names none. There is no exemption on profit as person entitled to a share. In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing). Sources: [art. 3.2 and 3.79a, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### 3.18 Deduction for assisting partner bands

Art. 3.78 gives the deduction for assisting partner to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3% and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" (equal to or more than) the lower number to "minder dan" (less than) the upper number. Exactly 875 hours therefore falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. The hours of the partner must be made plausible. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.78, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.78 lid 2, version 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.78, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, meewerkaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.19 Pay to the partner below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. This links the pay rule to the deduction for assisting partner bands above. Sources: [art. 3.16, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.16, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, meewerkaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### 3.20 Discontinuation deduction, € 3.630

Art. 3.79 gives the discontinuation deduction on profit made with or on the cessation (staking) of one or more whole businesses. It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and there is no deduction on profit as person entitled to a share. Special cases: for a business continued under rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a marital community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap, art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (ondernemers) (art. 3.63), the deduction applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a decrease (afneming) of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) under art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65. That onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022. Sources: [art. 3.79, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.79, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 3.79 and 10a.29, version 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [Belastingdienst, stakingsaftrek](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### 3.21 Old-age reserve in 2025

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023 ("Vervallen per 01-01-2023"), so no addition to the old-age reserve (dotatie aan de oudedagsreserve) is possible from 2023, also not in 2025. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force (in werking) for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last broken book year that started before 1 January 2023. The decrease rules: the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity (lijfrente) premiums deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year. It also decreases by the excess over the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end if the business or part of it ceased during the year, the state pension age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business (community of property, death, transfer to partner), lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve and lid 7 keeps the discontinuation deduction for a decrease after a tax-neutral rollover. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return. The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027. For an old reserve the additions were ruled by art. 3.67 to 3.72 as they read on 31 December 2022, with an addition of 9,44% of the profit as entrepreneur up to a yearly maximum and reduced by pension premiums charged to that profit, and the statute texts of 1 January 2020, 2021 and 2022 all printed 9,44%. Sources: [art. 3.67 to 3.72 and 10a.29, version 1 January 2023](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [art. 10a.29, version 1 January 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.67 to 3.72, version 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 3.67 to 3.72, version 1 January 2022](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### 3.22 Rate adjustment, 12,02% above € 76.817

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. For 2025 the percentage is 12,02% and the start of the top bracket is € 76.817. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage. Sources: [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### 3.23 Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption (ondernemersaftrek en mkb-winstvrijstelling), 37,48%

For 2025 the maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is 37,48%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page prints this as 49,50% minus 12,02%. The rate adjustment of art. 2.10 is what delivers the cap. Sources: [Belastingdienst, MKB-winstvrijstelling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), [art. 2.10, version 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).
