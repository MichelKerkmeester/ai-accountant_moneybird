## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The KOR changed from the previous scheme, which tested turnover in the current calendar year, allowed a Dutch fixed establishment (vaste inrichting), and had a minimum participation and re-entry period, to the 2025 rules for a business established in the Netherlands. The 2025 notification requires turnover information for the preceding and current calendar years. The new scheme has no minimum term and uses the re-entry restriction described above. [2024 version of Article 25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [2025 version of Articles 25 to 25e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- The KOR registration threshold without notification rose from € 1.800 to € 2.200 from 1 January 2025. [Decision of 21 November 2024, no. 2024-29602](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html).
- The EU KOR became available from 1 January 2025. It was not in force (in werking) in 2024. [Stb. 2023, 278](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-278.html).
- Supplementary return (suppletie) timing changed from “as soon as possible” to a deadline of no later than eight weeks after discovering an error or omission. The eight-week rule applies from 1 January 2025. [Stb. 2024, 441](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html).
- The standard, reduced and zero rates remain 21%, 9% and nil (nihil) respectively. [2024 version of Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2024-01-01), [2025 version of Article 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01).
- The € 227 BUA threshold and the rule not to subtract an employee contribution were already in place for 2024. No change for 2025 is shown in the checked material. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Decision of 6 October 2023, no. 2023-20692](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html).
- The yearly-return VAT limit is not a confirmed old-to-new change. Its 2025 value is unverified, and the checked 2024 handbook URL is unavailable. See section 7. [2025 Belastingdienst return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales records (verkoopadministratie) that identify the supply, customer, date, consideration, VAT rate (btw-tarief) and VAT amount. Issue invoices to business customers and relevant legal persons by the stated deadline. Retain the required invoice details and make sure simplified invoices are used only where allowed. [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).
- Separate taxable, zero-rated and exempt turnover (vrijgestelde omzet) in the records. Track input VAT (voorbelasting) by the business use that supports deduction. Zero-rated turnover can retain input VAT deduction, while exempt turnover does not. Reconcile mixed taxable and exempt use in the final return. [Article 11, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/).
- Track KOR turnover across every activity and subnumber (subnummer). Keep the turnover calculation and the notification evidence, and flag the supply that would take the total above the ceiling. While participating, do not charge VAT or state VAT on invoices, and do not deduct input VAT. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden).
- Reconcile each return to sales invoices, purchase invoices, bank records and any reverse-charged transactions. Report domestic reverse charge in rubriek 2 and foreign reverse charge in rubriek 4. Deduct reverse-charged VAT (verlegde btw) only where the purchase supports taxable activity. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen).
- For intra-EU supplies and services, retain transport and VAT-ID evidence where required. Reconcile the ICP total to rubriek 3b. File an ICP only for a period with reportable activity and correct an error in the next ICP. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).
- File and pay by the applicable return deadline. The filing date (aangiftedatum) is when the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) receives the return. For a software return, observe the stated submission window. [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- In the final return of the year, settle private use (privégebruik), the business-car adjustment, taxable and exempt turnover apportionment, and BUA items. If the final return has already been filed, use a supplementary return (suppletie) for a correction. [Belastingdienst final return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren).
- Keep OSS records for the required retention period (bewaartermijn) and retain evidence for the country and rate of each reported supply. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Yearly return VAT limit, UNVERIFIED.** The checked 2025 material states less than € 1.883, but the 2025 handbook URL returned 404. The condition is otherwise supported by the checked 2020 and 2026 handbooks. A working 2025 handbook or a year-specific primary confirmation would settle whether the value applied unchanged in 2025. [Belastingdienst return-period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf).
- **Supplementary return (suppletie) amount boundary, UNVERIFIED.** The checked value says a correction of € 1.000 or less may go in the next return, but the cited January 2025 guide was not re-read. A checked copy of that guide or a year-specific Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) confirmation would settle the boundary. [January 2025 suppletie guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_suppletie_btw_ob1431t21pl.pdf).
- **ICP deadline, unknown and UNVERIFIED for 2025.** The checked ICP guide is for 2024 and does not verify the domestic 2025 deadline. A 2025 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration deadline page or guide would settle it. [2024 digital ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf).
- **KOR voluntary exit, conflict.** The statute says the first day of the next calendar quarter, at least four weeks after the notice is received. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration withdrawal page says the first day of the return period (aangiftetijdvak). For a quarterly filer the dates coincide. For a monthly filer they can differ, so the statutory wording is used here. A corrected Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page or an official clarification would settle the wording difference. [Article 25a, Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR withdrawal guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor).
- **ViDA EU acts dates, UNVERIFIED.** The checked EU page reports the adoption, publication and entry-into-force dates, but those dates were not confirmed on a listed primary Dutch source. The official EU legal acts or a Dutch primary source reproducing them would settle the dates. [EU Commission ViDA page](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en).
- **Domestic B2B e-invoicing duty, UNVERIFIED.** The checked government-business page says suppliers to central government must e-invoice and that certain other public bodies may require it. The statement that there is no general domestic B2B duty is not verified as a complete 2025 legal rule. A Dutch statute or official legal clarification would settle it. [Business.gov.nl government e-invoicing guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/e-invoicing-government-organisations/).
- **OSS participation after a prolonged period without supplies, UNVERIFIED.** The research claim that participation ends after a stated period without supplies while returns continue is not on the checked Dutch Tax and Customs Administration OSS page. A direct statutory provision or explicit Dutch Tax and Customs Administration guidance would settle it. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem).
- **Late VAT penalties, limited source scope.** The figures in the table are from a page for businesses established outside the Netherlands. That source does not establish the same penalty amounts for a Dutch-established sole trader. A primary source specifically covering domestic businesses would settle the domestic position. [Belastingdienst foreign-established business penalties](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte).
