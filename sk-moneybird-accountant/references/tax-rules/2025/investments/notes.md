## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table, every amount moves up. The window start goes from € 2.800 to € 2.900. The end of the 28% band goes from € 69.765 to € 70.602. The fixed amount goes from € 19.535 to € 19.769. The end of the fixed band goes from € 129.194 to € 130.744. The top of the table goes from € 387.580 to € 392.230 ([2024 text of art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text of art. 3.41 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (investeringsbedrag): € 149.000.000 in 2024 to € 151.000.000 in 2025 ([2024 text of art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [2025 text of art. 3.42 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold: € 2.800 in 2024 to € 2.900 in 2025 ([2024 text of art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling), [2025 text of art. 3.47 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Arbitrary depreciation for starters (starters willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap: € 387.580 in 2024 to € 392.230 in 2025. Both are read from the URWA art. 8 reference to the top of the KIA table ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [2024 KIA table](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 KIA table](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). No primary page prints the cap as a number.
- No change for 2025 in: the EIA percentage, 40% in both years, the MIA percentages, 45%, 36% and 27% in both years, with no MIA change in 2024 either, the Vamil percentage of 75%, the KIA per-asset minimum of € 450, the EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500, the ordinary depreciation caps of 10% and 20%, and the building minimum value (bodemwaarde), one minimum value equal to the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) with the transition exception for a qualifying own-use building taken into use before 2024, already in force (in werking) from 1 January 2024 ([2024 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 texts](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- The temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) 2023 scheme carries over into 2025. The use deadline is before 1 January 2026 and the period in which the asset must keep meeting the art. 13 conditions ends 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).
- The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) truck example for arbitrary depreciation for starters, published with the 2024 page, runs on into 2025 with € 80.000 arbitrary depreciation in that year ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Claim the KIA by choice in the tax return (aangifte), per business (per-onderneming), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year ([art. 3.41 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Record each investment per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) with the date of the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or the order, because the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) notification must go out within 3 months ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).
- Keep the Environmental List (Milieulijst) version that is valid on the signing or order date with the file ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).
- Keep payment records per asset for assets that are not yet in use, because the deduction is taken over the payments made until use ([art. 3.44, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).
- Keep proof that at least 25% of the investment amount (investeringsbedrag) was paid within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and that the asset was taken into use within three years after the start of the investment year, because failing either counts as a disposal ([art. 3.47 lid 3 to 6, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Book disposals within five years and apply the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) on the transfer prices above the € 2.900 threshold ([art. 3.47 lid 1 and lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).
- Depreciate at the art. 3.30 caps, goodwill at most 10% per year and other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) at most 20% per year, and keep a depreciation schedule ([art. 3.30 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- For a building, stop depreciation at the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde), the minimum value (bodemwaarde) ([art. 3.30a lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)), except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value until 3 years after first use ([building depreciation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand)).
- For a BV, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration covers the same investment deductions (investeringsaftrek) on its corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) and income tax (inkomstenbelasting) pages ([Vpb 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).
- Assets from the 2023 temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) scheme must be in use before 1 January 2026 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

The 2025 figures themselves are settled. The items below are the unknown values, UNVERIFIED points and the resolved conflict that touch 2025.

- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) 2025 amount: FISIN 2025 prints "€ 2.800 of minder" ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)) against € 2.900 in art. 3.47 lid 1 of the 2025 text ([art. 3.47 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The conflict is settled to € 2.900, the statute prevails. A corrected FISIN page would settle it fully.
- RVO maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for the MIA and the Vamil: the RVO page does not state a year, so it is marked unknown and UNVERIFIED for 2025 ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). A year-specific RVO page would settle it.
- RVO test for a vehicle counting as new, used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km: the RVO page does not state a year, so it is marked unknown and UNVERIFIED for 2025 ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)).
- RVO statement that the combined MIA and Vamil benefit can run up to just over 14% of the investment amount (investeringsbedrag): the RVO page does not state a year, so it is marked unknown and UNVERIFIED for 2025 ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).
- The research rule that a co-entitled party (medegerechtigde) or lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages, so it stays UNVERIFIED ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden), [Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)).
- Arbitrary depreciation for starters (starters willekeurige afschrijving) cap, UNVERIFIED: the URWA names the maximum amount in the KIA table over which KIA can be obtained, and no primary page prints the cap as a number ([URWA art. 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [2025 KIA table](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the reading gives € 392.230. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) or RVO page that prints the cap as a number would settle it.
