## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the 2025 rules for investment deductions and depreciation (investeringsaftrek en afschrijving): the small-scale investment deduction (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek, KIA), the Energy Investment Allowance (energie-investeringsaftrek, EIA), the Environmental Investment Allowance (milieu-investeringsaftrek, MIA), the Vamil (extra arbitrary depreciation for designated environmental assets), the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) and ordinary depreciation. The 2025 KIA table runs from € 2.900 to € 392.230 with a fixed € 19.769 in the middle band, the EIA is 40%, the MIA is 45%, 36% or 27%, the Vamil lets 75% of the cost be depreciated at will, the EIA and MIA maximum investment amount is € 151.000.000 and the disinvestment addition threshold is € 2.900. All 2025 law values here are enacted and verified against the 2025 text of the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001), the URWA and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages for 2025. A few figures on the RVO applicant pages come without a stated year and are marked unknown and UNVERIFIED in section 2 and section 7.
