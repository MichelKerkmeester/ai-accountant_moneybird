## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Small-scale investment deduction (KIA, kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), table

The KIA is a per-business (per-onderneming) deduction that is claimed by choice in the tax return (aangifte). It is taken on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year. The 2025 table in art. 3.41 lid 2 of the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) has five bands, read from the 2025 text ([art. 3.41 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

- Nil up to € 2.900.
- 28% of the investment for a total above € 2.900 up to € 70.602.
- A fixed € 19.769 for a total above € 70.602 up to € 130.744.
- € 19.769 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 130.744 for a total up to € 392.230.
- Nil above € 392.230.

An investment total under € 2.900 therefore gives no KIA, and a total above € 392.230 gives no KIA either. The deduction is optional, so the taxpayer may take less or skip it. The KIA can be combined with the EIA or with the MIA on the same asset. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined with each other for one asset ([Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) also covers the KIA on its corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting) pages, so the same figures come back in the corporate income tax setting ([Vpb 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)).

### KIA in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband)

For a partnership the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added. The table is applied to that sum. The outcome is then multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum ([art. 3.41 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KIA page adds that each partner (firmant or vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the profit share ((over)winst), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently ([Belastingdienst KIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia)). Pick one key and keep it for the year. The KIA itself remains a per-business deduction for each partner.

### KIA per-asset minimum of € 450

Art. 3.45 lid 2 adds two exclusions for the KIA on top of lid 1. Assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties are excluded (onderdeel a), and assets with an investment amount below € 450 are excluded (onderdeel b) ([art. 3.45, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The € 450 is a per-asset floor, not a yearly total. An asset below it drops out of the KIA base while the rest of the year keeps counting. A taxpayer with only assets below € 450 reaches no KIA at all.

### Payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Art. 3.44 lid 1: if the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments that were made. The excess follows in later years. Lid 2: at cessation (staking) of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation. The rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA ([art. 3.44, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). This matters for orders placed late in the year that are delivered or installed after the year end. Keep the payment record per asset so the split between this year and later years can be shown.

### EIA percentage of 40%

The EIA is 40% of the investment amount for a new asset on the Energy List (Energielijst) ([art. 3.42 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration EIA page states that in 2025 and 2026 the EIA is 40% ([Belastingdienst EIA page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia)). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that were not used before ("niet eerder gebruikt"). A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, and second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as production costs (voortbrengingskosten) ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The EIA cannot be combined with the MIA for one asset. The EIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The asset must be reported to RVO within the notification deadline.

### EIA and MIA maximum investment amount

The maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 151.000.000 ([art. 3.42 lid 4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). This caps the investment amount on which the EIA or the MIA can be taken. It sits far above the investments of a normal sole trader or small BV. The RVO pages add a separate maximum for the MIA and the Vamil per asset and per applicant per year, see that item below.

### MIA percentages and Environmental List (Milieulijst) categories

The MIA is 45% for category I, 36% for category II and 27% for category III ([art. 3.42a lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). From 1 January 2022 the Environmental List maps codes that start with F or G to category I, codes that start with A or D to category II and codes that start with B or E to category III ([Milieulijst 2022, in force 1 January 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). Only new assets qualify, the same test as for the EIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). No MIA is given on costs that already received the EIA, while the Vamil remains possible for those costs. The MIA can be combined with the KIA. The asset must be on the Environmental List and be reported to RVO within the deadline.

### EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500

For the EIA and the MIA, art. 3.45 lid 4 excludes assets that are made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (onderdeel a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (onderdeel b) ([art. 3.45, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). RVO phrases the floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (per application) ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). Lid 3 carves out an exception for the MIA: art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply to the MIA, so dwellings (woonhuizen) and passenger cars (personenauto's) can still earn the MIA. Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a.

### RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

Report the investment to the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order to acquire the asset (acquisition costs, aanschaffingskosten). For production costs (voortbrengingskosten) the deadline is within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers), [RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The Environmental List that is valid on the signing or order date applies, so keep that list version with the file. FISIN repeats the deadline for every year from 2020 to 2026 with the sentence "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### RVO maximum for MIA and Vamil

RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per asset and per applicant per year for the MIA and the Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The page does not state a year for this figure, so it cannot be tied to 2025 on the checked material, see section 7. The amount does not affect normal small investments.

### RVO test for a vehicle counting as new

RVO treats a vehicle as new if it has been used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km ([RVO voorwaarden](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden)). The page does not state a year for this test, see section 7. For the rest, the general new-asset test applies. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify for the EIA or the MIA, but costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List requirements can qualify ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)).

### Vamil

The costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven", so 75% of the cost may be depreciated at will in a chosen year ([art. 3.31 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The rest follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit for buildings, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that were not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes that start with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil ([Milieulijst 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html)). RVO speaks of the combined MIA and Vamil benefit, so the Vamil can sit on the same asset as the MIA ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts. The asset must be reported to RVO within the deadline.

### RVO statement on the combined MIA plus Vamil benefit

RVO states: "Uw totale belastingvoordeel (MIA en Vamil) kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers)). That is a statement about the combined net benefit of the MIA deduction and the Vamil acceleration, not a statutory rate. The page does not state a year, see section 7.

### Assets excluded from every investment deduction (investeringsaftrek)

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes, for the KIA, the EIA and the MIA alike, assets that are intended mainly for certain uses ([art. 3.45 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The list is:

- use in a business whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting), onderdeel a
- land (gronden) including the ground under buildings, except land improvement (grondverbetering), onderdeel b
- dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen), onderdeel c
- passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992, onderdeel d
- vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b, onderdeel e
- securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies), onderdeel f
- animals (dieren), onderdeel g

For the EIA and the MIA, lid 4 adds the assets made available mainly to non-residents or foreign enterprises and the € 2.500 floor. Lid 3 lifts the dwellings and passenger cars exclusion for the MIA only. Lid 5 gives the Minister a route to disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a.

### Own-BV and related-party exclusion

No investment deduction is given on obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (onderdeel a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (onderdeel b), heirs to an estate to which the asset belongs (onderdeel c), and a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam) and that entity (onderdeel d) ([art. 3.46, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Minister can disapply lid 1 under lid 2, which is the exemption (ontheffing) route. FISIN states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). A sole trader who holds one third or more of a BV and buys an asset from that BV therefore loses the investment deduction on that asset in principle. The cross-year rules apply in 2025 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold of € 2.900

Art. 3.47 lid 1: if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage that was taken is added to profit ([art. 3.47 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the threshold is € 2.900. FISIN 2025 prints "€ 2.800 of minder", and the statute prevails, see section 7 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The threshold is stated per calendar year.

### Disinvestment addition period and cap

The addition only applies to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment. The addition is never taken over more than the investment amount over which the deduction (aftrek) was taken ([art. 3.47 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Both limits apply together. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes the term on its disinvestment addition page ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Disinvestment addition deemed disposals and undone investments

Lid 3 treats as a disposal: withdrawing the good from the business (onderdeel a), changing its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, the KIA case) or art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents or foreign enterprises, the EIA and MIA case) (onderdeel b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (onderdeel c). Lid 4: in those cases the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) counts as the transfer price. Lid 5: undoing an investment, or receiving a reduction (vermindering), refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), counts as a disposal for that amount. Lid 6: undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7: the Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 ([art. 3.47 lid 3 to 6, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA and not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds cessation of (part of) the business within the term ([Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling)).

### Ordinary depreciation caps

Art. 3.30 lid 1: the yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition or production costs (aanschaffings- of voortbrengingskosten) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2: per year at most 10% of the cost for goodwill and at most 20% for other business assets, so at least 10 and 5 years. Lid 3: production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) may be written off at once in the year of production. Lid 4: items of small value (voorwerpen van geringe waarde) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or production ([art. 3.30, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The text is the same from 2020 through 2027. For a Vamil asset, 75% may be depreciated at will and the rest follows this normal schedule.

### Minimum value (bodemwaarde) for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1: depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the minimum value (bodemwaarde), and at most for the difference. Lid 2: a building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) are one business asset, while detachable machinery (werktuigen) are separate. Lid 3: for 2020 to 2023 the minimum value was the WOZ value for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another (own use) building. From 2024 there is one minimum value, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw", except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value until 3 years after first use ([art. 3.30a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Lid 4: the WOZ value is the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text adds lid 5 (co-owned building pro rata), lid 6 (ground owned by an unrelated party reduces the WOZ value) and lid 7 to lid 10 (related persons and related entities). For 2025 the one minimum value of the WOZ value applies.

### Arbitrary depreciation for starters (starters willekeurige afschrijving) yearly cap

Statutory basis: art. 3.34 of the Wet IB 2001 allows arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship ([art. 3.34, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In the URWA, the regulation that designates the assets, art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3 (the starter's deduction, startersaftrek) applies. Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 limits this to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur. Art. 8 allows the arbitrary depreciation for the part of the total costs in the calendar year that does not go above the maximum amount in the KIA table over which KIA can be obtained, and not for assets that were already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground ([URWA art. 7 to 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). For 2025 that cap reads as € 392.230, the top of the KIA table ([art. 3.41 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). No primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that this applies to sole proprietorships (eenmanszaak), civil-law partnerships (maatschap), limited partnerships (cv) and general partnerships (vof) when the starter's deduction conditions are met. Only assets that qualify for the investment deduction are eligible. The taxpayer may choose which eligible assets to depreciate, and the KIA minimum total investment does not apply ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)). Revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value, with any difference treated as profit, applies within 5 years, or 10 for sea-going vessels, if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf).

### Arbitrary depreciation for starters period

Art. 9 of the URWA sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs ([URWA art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Art. 12 sets ten years for the sea-going vessels of art. 10 ([URWA art. 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). The same 5-year and 10-year terms come back in the revaluation rule on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page ([Belastingdienst starters page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer)).

### Temporary arbitrary depreciation 2023

Art. 13 lid 1 of the URWA: assets not used before, with obligations entered or production costs made in calendar year 2023, taken into use by the taxpayer before 1 January 2026 ([URWA art. 13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0)). Lid 2 excludes buildings, vessels, aircraft, mopeds, motorcycles, passenger cars except for commercial transport by road or 0 gram CO2 per km, intangible assets, animals, assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, and assets already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. Lid 3: the 1 January 2026 date moves by the days justified when special circumstances delayed use. Art. 14: once, at most 50% of the costs, taken in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15: the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 ends 31 December 2025 ([URWA art. 13 to 15](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0), [Eindejaarsregeling 2022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset that is not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year ([Vpb 2023 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023)). The scheme applies to both income tax and corporate income tax. In 2025 the scheme is in its final window: assets from 2023 costs must be in use before 1 January 2026.
