## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte): € 36.077 in 2024 to € 35.798 in 2025. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-wijzigingen-belastingen-2024/20240108+-+Eindejaarsbericht+-+V1.2.pdf and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/dcbb490f-d406-4842-ac33-b5c397e19df0/file
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum: € 41.608 in 2024 to € 42.108 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Income cap (aftoppingsgrens): € 137.800 in 2024 to € 137.800 in 2025, not indexed in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf
- Franchise: € 17.545 in 2024 to € 18.475 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- Look-back window: 10 years, annual allowance 2014 to 2023 in 2024 to 10 years, annual allowance 2015 to 2024 in 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap: € 26.463 in 2024 to € 26.781 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap: € 63.288 in 2024 to € 63.288 in 2025, unchanged. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) tier a: € 559.483 in 2024 to € 566.197 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Profit from business discontinuation tier b: € 279.752 in 2024 to € 283.110 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Profit from business discontinuation tier c: € 139.885 in 2024 to € 141.564 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente): € 5.364 in 2024 to € 5.429 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid): € 50.063 in 2024 to € 50.664 in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- The old-age reserve (FOR, oudedagsreserve) transition in the annual allowance under art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 applied for the last time in 2024. It does not apply in 2025. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01
- BAZ proposed design: in 2024 the consultation version had a premium of about 6,5% of profit (winst), up to about € 195 per month, a waiting period (wachttijd) of 1 year, a benefit of 70% of profit up to the minimum wage (minimumloon), until state pension (AOW) age, and an opt-out with minimum conditions. In 2025 the Council of State (Raad van State) version has a premium of 5,4% of profit, at most € 171 gross per month, a waiting period of 2 years, every self-employed person (zelfstandige) up to state pension age insured, a benefit at most the minimum wage, a private policy as alternative with a yearly switch moment at the end of the calendar year, and no premium for someone whose WIA rights already reach minimum wage level. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state
- BAZ consultation responses: the 2024 consultation was on 11 June 2024. The 2025 Council of State version reports 2260 responses. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2024/06/11/verplichte-verzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-in-internetconsultatie and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state
- Payment deadline for an old-age reserve (FOR) or profit on discontinuation conversion: before 1 July 2025 in 2024 to before 1 July 2026 in 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Conversion deadlines (conversiedeadlines): in 2024 an end date (einddatum) 2023 meant by 31 December 2024, an end date 2024 by 31 December 2025 and a death in 2023 by 31 December 2025. In 2025 an end date 2024 means by 31 December 2025, an end date 2025 by 31 December 2026 and a death in 2024 by 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130)
- For an old-age reserve (FOR, oudedagsreserve) or profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion, the payment deadline for 2025 is before 1 July 2026. The premium may on choice be counted in 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130 lid 2)
- Record a disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) as expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen), not as a business cost. It is fully deductible with no allowance (ruimte) cap. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers
- Do not deduct a lump-sum disability insurance premium. The payout belongs in box 3. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers
- Keep the policy conditions and the end date (einddatum). An end date 2024 must be converted or started by 31 December 2025. An end date 2025 by 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Keep the death date for a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente). A death in 2024 means conversion or start by 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
- Claim unused annual allowance (jaarruimte) as catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) on request in the tax return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127
- The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- A mandatory occupational pension (beroepspensioen) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is deductible as a business cost with a restriction, not as an annuity premium. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies
- Periodic disability insurance benefits are taxed as income from previous employment (inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as a prepayment (voorheffing). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers
- BAZ is proposed, so no BAZ premium or benefit is recorded in 2025. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The BAZ values are proposed, not enacted. The Council of State (Raad van State) version states a premium of 5,4% of profit (winst), at most € 171 gross per month, a waiting period (wachttijd) of 2 years and 2260 consultation responses. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state. What would settle it: enactment of a BAZ law.
- The 2025 proposed BAZ waiting period of 2 years differs from later material. Bill art. 16 of the 2026 bill states 104 weeks (weken), and the docket summary still says "ongeveer één jaar". Source: [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912. What would settle it: the final enacted text. For 2025 the proposed value is 2 years.
- FISIN 2025 prints "€ 26.7813" in the temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) account row. The law text valid on 1 January 2025 states € 26.781. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. What would settle it: use the law text and a corrected FISIN page.
- FISIN 2025 describes the profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst) tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder". The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd". The checked material says the FISIN wording matches only 2023 and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129) and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf. What would settle it: apply the law's relative age test to the taxpayer's state pension (AOW) age. For 2025 the exact age equivalent is not stated in the checked material.
- The 2025 value table has no UNVERIFIED or unknown items. The maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) of € 35.798 is Verified by the Ministry table. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/dcbb490f-d406-4842-ac33-b5c397e19df0/file
