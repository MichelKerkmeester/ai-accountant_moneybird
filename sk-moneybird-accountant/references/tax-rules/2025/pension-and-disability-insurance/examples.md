## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Unless stated otherwise, the amounts below are the 2025 amounts. Official examples are taken from the Belastingdienst publication cited with them. Calculated examples use invented inputs with the published amounts.

### Calculated example: jaarruimte with a profit of € 60.000

Invented inputs: winst uit onderneming of € 60.000 in 2024, before the ondernemersaftrek, no pension accrual, no oudedagsreserve and an age below the age limit at the start of 2025.

Step 1: min(60.000, 137.800) = 60.000.
Step 2: 60.000 - 18.475 = 41.525.
Step 3: 30% x 41.525 = 12.457,50.

The jaarruimte for 2025 is € 12.457,50. This is below the maximum of € 35.798.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

### Calculated example: jaarruimte at the maximum

Invented inputs: winst uit onderneming of € 200.000 in 2024, before the ondernemersaftrek, no pension accrual, no oudedagsreserve and an age below the age limit at the start of 2025.

Step 1: min(200.000, 137.800) = 137.800.
Step 2: 137.800 - 18.475 = 119.325.
Step 3: 30% x 119.325 = 35.797,50. Rounded to whole euros this is € 35.798.

The rounded outcome equals the published maximum jaarruimte for 2025 of € 35.798, so this income reaches the maximum. The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/dcbb490f-d406-4842-ac33-b5c397e19df0/file

### Calculated example: reserveringsruimte from two earlier years

Invented inputs: unused jaarruimte 2015 of € 5.000 and unused jaarruimte 2016 of € 8.000, with a pensioentekort and an age below the age limit in 2025.

Step 1: 5.000 + 8.000 = 13.000.
Step 2: 13.000 is below the 2025 reserveringsruimte maximum of € 42.108.

Both years fall within the 2025 look-back window of 2015 to 2024. The taxpayer can use € 13.000 on request in the 2025 aangifte, oldest unused year first (2015 first). The Belastingdienst advises using the reserveringsruimte before the jaarruimte, so older unused ruimte does not lapse.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Calculated example: tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente above the cap

Invented input: a tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente with a yearly uitkering of € 30.000. The product runs at least five years and starts not earlier than the year of AOW age and not later than the year of AOW age plus five.

Step 1: 30.000 - 26.781 = 3.219.

The yearly uitkering is € 3.219 above the 2025 cap of € 26.781. The cap is judged at the time of premium payment. Above the cap the premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen run at least 20 years.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.125) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Calculated example: overbruggingslijfrente monthly amount

Invented input: a yearly amount of € 63.288.

Step 1: 63.288 / 12 = 5.274,00.

The yearly maximum of € 63.288 corresponds to € 5.274,00 per month. The cap is a yearly amount. This product is only for lijfrentetegoeden built up before 2006.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Calculated example: stakingswinst in tier a

Invented inputs: stakingswinst of € 600.000, an age at staking at most five years below the AOW age (tier a) and no already built provisions.

The maximum is the tier a amount minus the already built provisions, limited to the stakingswinst.

Step 1: 566.197 - 0 = 566.197.
Step 2: min(600.000, 566.197) = 566.197.

The maximum deduction for the stakingswinst conversion is € 566.197, the 2025 tier a amount. A premium for this conversion paid before 1 July 2026 may, on choice, still be counted in 2025.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Calculated example: stakingswinst in tier b

Invented inputs: stakingswinst of € 500.000, an age at staking at most fifteen years below the AOW age (tier b) and no already built provisions.

The maximum is the tier b amount minus the already built provisions, limited to the stakingswinst.

Step 1: 283.110 - 0 = 283.110.
Step 2: min(500.000, 283.110) = 283.110.

The maximum deduction for the stakingswinst conversion is € 283.110, the 2025 tier b amount.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.129)

### Calculated example: afkoop kleine lijfrente

Invented input: a lijfrente with a yearly uitkering of € 6.000.

Step 1: 6.000 - 5.429 = 571.

The yearly uitkering is € 571 above the 2025 amount of € 5.429, so this is not a small lijfrente and afkoop in one payment is not allowed under this exception. A yearly uitkering below € 5.429, for example € 5.000, would fall within the exception.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.133)

### Calculated example: afkoop bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

Invented inputs: the taxpayer is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under the ministerial conditions and has not yet reached AOW age, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year is € 60.000, and the afkoop received in the year is € 55.000.

The exception applies when the taxpayer is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under the ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within € 50.664 or, if higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The higher of the two amounts is the allowed maximum.

Step 1: the higher of 50.664 and 60.000 is 60.000.
Step 2: the afkoop of 55.000 is below 60.000, so it stays within the maximum.

The afkoop counts as a lijfrente termijn.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.133)

### Calculated example: AOV premium with periodic or lump-sum payout

Invented input: AOV premium of € 2.400.

The premium is not a business cost. With periodic uitkeringen the € 2.400 premium is fully deductible as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen, with no ruimte cap. With a lump-sum payout the same € 2.400 premium is not deductible, and the payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

### Calculated example: proposed BAZ premium

Invented input: winst uit onderneming of € 60.000.

The proposed premium is 5,4% of winst, at most € 171 gross per month.

Step 1: 5,4% x 60.000 = 3.240,00.
Step 2: 3.240,00 / 12 = 270,00.
Step 3: 270,00 is above the proposed maximum of € 171 gross per month, so the proposed premium is capped at € 171 gross per month.

This is the Raad van State version of the proposal and not an enacted premium.

Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state

### Official example: conversion deadlines

From FISIN 2025. A lijfrente that reaches its einddatum must be converted or started within one full calendar year. A lijfrente with einddatum 2024 must be converted or started by 31 December 2025. A lijfrente with einddatum 2025 must be converted or started by 31 December 2026. After a death in 2024 the nabestaandenlijfrente deadline is 31 December 2026, two full calendar years after the year of death.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen
