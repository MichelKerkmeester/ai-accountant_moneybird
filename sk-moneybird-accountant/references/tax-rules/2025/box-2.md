## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

For 2025 box 2 has two brackets: 24,5% over the first € 67.804 and 31% above that, with € 16.611 of tax at the top of bracket 1 in the art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 table. The second-bracket rate went down from 33% to 31% on 1 January 2025 through the Belastingplan 2025 measure "Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief". An aanmerkelijk belang (substantial shareholding) starts at 5% of the issued capital, alone or together with a fiscal partner, and art. 4.6 gained a sixth ground (sub f) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025 by Stb. 2025, 445. For a sole trader who also owns a BV, the same year's excessief lenen (excessive borrowing from the own company) maximum of € 500.000, the gebruikelijk loon (customary salary) fixed amount of € 56.000 and the ODV market rate of 2,602% sit next to the rate table. The 2025 rules described here are enacted law.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Box 2 bracket 1 (24,5% up to € 67.804)

Art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 puts the first € 67.804 of box 2 income of a taxpayer in bracket 1 at 24,5% ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The rate applies to a taxpayer with income from an aanmerkelijk belang, which is a holder of shares, winstbewijzen (profit-sharing certificates) or comparable rights who meets the 5% test of art. 4.6 (see 3.4). The two-bracket table applies from 2024 and the table applies per taxpayer ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor (indexation factor), so the boundary is an indexed amount ([art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst box 2 page and FISIN 2025 state the same 24,5% up to € 67.804 ([FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.2 Tax at the top of bracket 1 (€ 16.611)

The art. 2.12 table states € 16.611 as the tax at the top of bracket 1, in column III of the table ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). This figure is the tax on € 67.804 of box 2 income at 24,5% and is the anchor for the tax on income above the boundary: the part up to € 67.804 carries € 16.611 and only the excess is taxed at the second-bracket rate. Dividendbelasting (dividend withholding tax) that was withheld is not deducted from the income but credited as voorheffing (pre-levy) against the tax that follows from the table ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.3 Box 2 bracket 2 (31% above € 67.804)

Above € 67.804 the rate is 31% for 2025 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The second bracket was announced in Belastingplan 2023 and took effect on 1 January 2024 ([rijksoverheid.nl, belangrijkste belastingwijzigingen per 1 januari 2024](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024)). The 33% rate of 2024 came from the amendement Van der Lee c.s. in Belastingplan 2024, and Belastingplan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 percentage points to 31% from 1 January 2025 ([Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each fiscal partner has his own bracket 1 and his own 31% bracket over his own share of the income (see 3.8).

### 3.4 Aanmerkelijk belang, the 5% test (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds winstbewijzen for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts. The research statement that the 2020 and 2027 texts read the same is corrected by these year texts ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.5 Meesleepregeling and meetrekregeling (art. 4.9 and 4.10)

Meesleepregeling (pull-along rule): once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that company also belong to it. Meetrekregeling (carry-along rule): shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant (blood relative or relative by marriage) in the rechte lijn (direct line) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang. The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst, wanneer hebt u een aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027, checked year by year ([art. 4.9 and 4.10 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.6 Fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.11)

Shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied, so in rollover cases ([art. 4.11 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. The vbi (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) status is a fictieve vervreemding case listed by the Belastingdienst, not part of the art. 4.11 list.

### 3.7 Box 2 income of minor children

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (whether or not together with his partner) under art. 4.6 ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst, wanneer hebt u een aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). This rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.8 Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen (common income items) of the taxpayer and partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return ([art. 2.17 lid 2 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2026 states that with a fiscal partnership for the whole year the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (any split is allowed, as long as the total is 100%) ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own first bracket applies to his share. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount, so that figure is UNVERIFIED (see section 7).

### 3.9 Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

Income from an aanmerkelijk belang is reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen (disposal benefits), minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) ([art. 4.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company (sub a, see 3.10), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief-lenen fiction (sub f, see 3.16 to 3.19). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available. Salary from the company is box 1 loon. Deductible costs include interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs. Dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The placement of the excessief-lenen fiction was settled at art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c. Art. 4.12a carries a different facility, the inherited-belang facility (see 3.13) ([art. 4.12a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### 3.10 Forfaitair voordeel for a vbi or foreign investment company (5,88%)

The forfaitair voordeel of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, time-apportioned over the year ([art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the percentage is 5,88%. The percentage is set per year in the statute text, 6,04% in 2024 and 5,88% in 2025 ([art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). It applies to shares in a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company that meets the art. 4.14 conditions.

### 3.11 Vervreemdingsvoordeel, verkrijgingsprijs and onzakelijke prijs

The vervreemdingsvoordeel is the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price) minus the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price) ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included. Special cases such as inheritance, a gift with or without doorschuiving (rollover), reaching the 5% threshold during the year and an onzakelijke prijs (non-arm's-length price) are handled as the Belastingdienst describes. At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer (market value) applies to both sides. The rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.12 Fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists as a sale: inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen (liquidation payments), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie (purchase option), shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a ([art. 4.16 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.13 Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (24 months)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs ([art. 4.12a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessief-lenen fiction. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from this facility from 1 January 2027, a proposal that was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([memorie van toelichting, Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)).

### 3.14 Doorschuiving on request for gift, inheritance and emigration

The checked material lists rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. That list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check, so it is UNVERIFIED ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), see section 7).

### 3.15 APV loss deferral (10 years)

The APV (afgezonderd particulier vermogen, separated private wealth) rules defer the loss and allow offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year APV term ([FISIN 2020, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.16 Excessief lenen maximum (€ 500.000)

The Wet van 21 december 2022, Stb. 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022) is in force from 1 January 2023 and is cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap" ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f Wet IB 2001, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f as "het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)" (the excessive part of debts, the fictitious regular benefit), where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations. The amount rule is art. 4.14a ("Fictief regulier voordeel"), the verbonden-person rule is art. 4.14b and the immigration rule is art. 4.14c ([art. 4.14a to 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.14a lid 1: a positive amount equals the total debts above the maximum, and a negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier, or under a comparable foreign rule, and not yet reversed. Lid 2: the maximum is € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier, after € 700.000 in the 2023 text. Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 indexation list, so the € 500.000 is not indexed ([art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The threshold history: the bill proposed € 500.000, the derde nota van wijziging (Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced it by € 700.000, and amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), brought it back to € 500.000 ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c ([art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.17 Excessief lenen, measurement and floor

The debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value (art. 4.14a lid 4). If the taxpayer no longer has an aanmerkelijk belang at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount (lid 5). An eigenwoningschuld (own-home debt) is excluded to the extent a recht van hypotheek on the home was given to the company (lid 6). Art. 10a.23 adds that an eigenwoningschuld existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded ([art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the drempel (threshold) "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)). FISIN 2025 phrases the test as "op 31 december 2025 meer dan € 500.000" (more than € 500.000 on 31 December 2025) ([FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Consequences: the loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividendbelasting on the part taxed as box 2 income. Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold. A negative fictief regulier voordeel is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.18 Excessief lenen, verbonden personen and immigration (art. 4.14b and 4.14c)

Debts of a verbonden person (connected person, a blood or aanverwant in the rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner) to a company where the taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000 from 2024, applied to that person and his partner together ([art. 4.14b and 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally. A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 500.000 from 2024, after € 700.000 in the 2023 text ([art. 4.14b and 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.19 Excessief lenen, samenwerkingsverband exception

Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a verbonden person for a debt of a samenwerkingsverband (a vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out. This exception was inserted by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023, as lid 6 and 7 of art. 4.13, numbered lid 4 and 5 for 2023 ([Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html)). The same act removed the double counting of a samenwerkingsverband debt among several participants in art. 4.14a lid 7. Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). The 2025 text carries the exception in art. 4.13 lid 6 and 7 and art. 4.14a lid 7 ([art. 4.13 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.20 Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (€ 56.000)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the fixed amount is € 56.000, the same art. 12a text as 2024 ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (lid 7 from the 2023 text). The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.21 Gebruikelijk loon minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The gebruikelijk-loon rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, lid 4 from 2023) ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (not per company) ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.22 Gebruikelijk loon comparable loon (100% from 2023)

The loon from the most comparable employment counts at 75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts and at 100% from the 2023 text ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A lower loon is allowed if the inhoudingsplichtige (withholding agent) makes it plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable loon, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself is lower. The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and which is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur. For a body with an S&O-verklaring to which the verhoogd starterspercentage applied, the 2020 to 2022 texts had a start-up rule of at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable loon if lower, usable at most 3 years. The rule is absent from the 2023 text. The Belastingdienst states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" (from 2023 the exception has lapsed for new cases), so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.23 Belastingkorting on an unoffset box 2 loss (24,5%)

Art. 4.53 lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a belastingkorting (tax credit). A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death. Lid 2 gives 24,5% for 2024 to 2027 through the reference to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage" (the percentage in the first row of the fourth column of the art. 2.12 table), after 26,25% in the 2020 text and 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts. Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking (decision) open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang ([Belastingdienst, verlies omzetten in belastingkorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block it, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([Kennisgroepstandpunt KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)). FISIN 2025 states "De korting is 26,9%" in its rule text, which is overruled by the statute and by its own example (see section 7).

### 3.24 Loss offset period (previous year and 6 following years)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang (loss from an aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose ([art. 4.49 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, shown separately on the assessment, and is open to objection. For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (to be offset over a longer period, in the coming 9 years) ([Belastingdienst, verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)). The art. 4.49 text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.25 ODV marktrente for 2025 (2,602%)

The oudedagsverplichting (ODV, old-age obligation) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation, art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, being the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year ([art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the rate is 2,602% ([Stcrt. 2025 publication](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html)). The ODV can be used geruisloos (without recognition of gain) for a lijfrente, lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking (income from a former employment), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years ([art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into a lijfrente after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5, added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020). A negative market rate must also be applied.

### 3.26 End of pensioen in eigen beheer afkoop and omzetting

The Wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen, Stb. 2017, 115, ends the old routes ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules as at 31 December 2016 for a pensioen in eigen beheer (PEB). Art. 38n lid 2 allowed prijsgeven of the part of the claim above the fiscale balanswaarde on the condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly afgekocht or geruisloos converted into an ODV, lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, and art. 38o set the afkoop tax at 34,5% of the fiscale balanswaarde minus, 25% from 1 January 2018 and 19,5% from 1 January 2019. Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115 makes art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse from 1 January 2020, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (lapsed as of 01-01-2020) ([art. 38o Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen states that afkoop or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019" (from 1 April 2017 up to and including 31 December 2019), and its V&A 17-007 is marked vervallen (lapsed) ([CAP, V&A 17-007](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). On the start date, the consolidated text on wetten.overheid.nl (BWBR0039380) shows "Geldend van 01-04-2017" with listed parts retroactive to 1 January 2017, and the coulance decision of 22 March 2017 (Stcrt. 2017, 18118) let dga's adjust "uiterlijk tot en met 30 juni 2017" and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Belastingdienst phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). A remaining PEB keeps the old rules. If the scheme provided for indexation the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" (if the BV does not index annually, we can see this as a form of giving up the rights), with possible fiscal consequences ([Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. Art. 10a.18 lid 2: in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking in box 1 ([art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.27 Non-residents (art. 7.5)

A non-resident declares only an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands company, not part of an enterprise, and art. 7.5 confirms that base ([art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The statement in the research that a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht (partial non-resident taxpayer status, the 30%-rule ruling) has the same effect was not found on the fetched Belastingdienst pages and stays UNVERIFIED (see section 7).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### 4.1 Dividend inside bracket 1

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A dga receives a dividend of € 60.000 from his BV in 2025 and has no other box 2 income. The whole amount falls in bracket 1.

- Step 1: box 2 income is € 60.000.
- Step 2: € 60.000 is below the bracket boundary of € 67.804, so only 24,5% applies.
- Step 3: 60.000 × 0,245 = € 14.700,00.

Box 2 tax: € 14.700,00. Source for the rates: [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.2 Dividend across both brackets

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A dividend of € 100.000 in 2025.

- Step 1: bracket 1 covers € 67.804. Tax at the top of bracket 1 is € 16.611 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Multiplying € 67.804 by 24,5% gives € 16.611,98, rounded down to € 16.611. The statute prints € 16.611.
- Step 2: the excess is 100.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 32.196,00.
- Step 3: 32.196 × 0,31 = € 9.980,76.
- Step 4: 16.611,00 + 9.980,76 = € 26.591,76.

Box 2 tax: € 26.591,76.

### 4.3 Dividendbelasting withheld

`Official example` from the Belastingdienst page on inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang, with the subtraction written out.

Gross dividend € 10.000, 15% dividendbelasting withheld, so € 8.500 is received net.

- Step 1: withholding, 10.000 × 0,15 = € 1.500,00.
- Step 2: box 2 tax on the gross dividend, 10.000 × 0,245 = € 2.450,00.
- Step 3: credit, 2.450,00 − 1.500,00 = € 950,00.

The dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing. Source: [Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.4 Fiscal partners split the income (50/50 against 100/0)

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

Box 2 income of € 120.000, fiscal partners for the whole year.

Split 50/50:

- Step 1: 120.000 / 2 = € 60.000,00 per partner.
- Step 2: each amount is below € 67.804, so 24,5% applies to each share.
- Step 3: 60.000 × 0,245 = € 14.700,00 per partner.
- Step 4: 14.700,00 × 2 = € 29.400,00 together.

One partner takes all € 120.000:

- Step 1: bracket 1 tax, € 16.611,00.
- Step 2: excess, 120.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 52.196,00.
- Step 3: 52.196 × 0,31 = € 16.180,76.
- Step 4: 16.611,00 + 16.180,76 = € 32.791,76.

The difference follows from the rule that art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each partner's own bracket 1 applies to his share ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 4.5 Forfaitair voordeel for a vbi or foreign investment company

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

Shares in a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling with a value of € 200.000 at the start of 2025.

- Step 1: 200.000 × 0,0588 = € 11.760,00 forfaitair voordeel.
- Step 2: other reguliere voordelen from those shares of € 3.000 reduce it, 11.760,00 − 3.000,00 = € 8.760,00.
- Step 3: the outcome is never below nil, and here it is positive.

Source: [art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.6 Excessief lenen, fictief regulier voordeel

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

On 31 December 2025 the taxpayer and his partner together have € 600.000 of debts to the BV. No amounts were taxed earlier, so the maximum is € 500.000.

- Step 1: debts above the maximum, 600.000,00 − 500.000,00 = € 100.000,00 fictief regulier voordeel.
- Step 2: bracket 1 tax, € 16.611,00 on the first € 67.804.
- Step 3: excess, 100.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 32.196,00.
- Step 4: 32.196 × 0,31 = € 9.980,76.
- Step 5: 16.611,00 + 9.980,76 = € 26.591,76 box 2 tax.

The loan itself stays in full and the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 4.7 Loss converted into a belastingkorting

`Official example` from FISIN 2025.

A loss of € 20.000 arose in 2023. The korting in 2025 is € 4.900 and the remainder runs until 2032.

- Step 1: the conversion percentage for 2025 is 24,5%.
- Step 2: 20.000 × 0,245 = € 4.900,00.
- Step 3: the loss year 2023 plus 9 gives the last year, 2032.

Source: [FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.8 Korting on a larger unoffset loss

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

An unoffset loss of € 40.000 is converted on request in a year in which the taxpayer and his partner had no aanmerkelijk belang, and also had none in the preceding year.

- Step 1: the rate is 24,5%.
- Step 2: 40.000 × 0,245 = € 9.800,00 belastingkorting.

The korting is set by beschikking and reduces box 1 tax of the conversion year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Source: [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.9 Gebruikelijk loon

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A BV in which the dga holds 100% of the shares pays a salary. The most comparable employment pays € 60.000, the highest employee loon in the BV and connected bodies is € 45.000, and the fixed amount is € 56.000.

- Step 1: (a) comparable loon € 60.000 at 100% = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2: (b) highest employee loon € 45.000,00.
- Step 3: (c) fixed amount € 56.000,00.
- Step 4: the highest of the three is € 60.000,00, so the loon is at least € 60.000,00.

If the total loon for the work for the BV and all connected bodies is not more than € 5.000, the rule does not apply at all, for example a total of € 4.000 for one company. The loon is box 1 income from employment, not a box 2 voordeel. Sources: [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.10 ODV market rate

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

An ODV of € 100.000 at the start of 2025, with the 2025 market rate of 2,602%.

- Step 1: 100.000 × 0,02602 = € 2.602,00 increase.
- Step 2: 100.000,00 + 2.602,00 = € 102.602,00 balance after the increase.

A negative market rate must also be applied, which would lower the balance. Source: [Stcrt. 2025 publication](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01).

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 2 bracket 2 rate: 33% in 2024 to 31% in 2025. Belastingplan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered the rate by 2 percentage points from 1 January 2025 ([Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Bracket 1 boundary: € 67.000 in 2024 to € 67.804 in 2025, indexed under art. 10.1 lid 1 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Tax at the top of bracket 1: € 16.415 in 2024 to € 16.611 in 2025 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Aanmerkelijk belang definition: 5% with grounds sub a to e in 2024 to 5% with grounds sub a to f in 2025. Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025 ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)).
- Forfaitair voordeel for a vbi or foreign investment company: 6,04% in 2024 to 5,88% in 2025 ([art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- ODV marktrente: 2,657% for 2024 to 2,602% for 2025 ([Stcrt. 2025 publication](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [Box 2 en aanmerkelijk belang 2024](../2024/box-2.md)).
- Samenwerkingsverband exception in the excessief-lenen rules: Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) inserted the exception in art. 4.13 lid 6 and 7 and art. 4.14a lid 7 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023. In 2025 the internal reference repair by Stb. 2025, 445 applies, retroactive to 1 January 2023 and not for 2024 ([Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html), [Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)).
- Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount: € 56.000 in 2024 and € 56.000 in 2025, unchanged, the same art. 12a text ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)).
- Gebruikelijk loon start-up rule: 2024 was the last possible year for users who started in 2022, after the rule was absent from the 2023 text. From 2025 the exception cannot be used by anyone ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01)).
- Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss: 24,5% in 2024 and 24,5% in 2025, because art. 4.53 lid 2 already referred to the first-row percentage of the art. 2.12 table from the 2024 text ([art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Excessief lenen maximum: € 500.000 in 2024 and € 500.000 in 2025, unchanged ([art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Loss offset period: previous year and 6 following years in 2024 and 2025, unchanged ([art. 4.49 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 4.49 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Box 2 income is declared as inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang, separately from salary and from the making available of assets. Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 loon ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Record each dividend gross with the dividendbelasting withheld, since the dividendbelasting is not a cost but a voorheffing credited against the box 2 tax ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Add notary costs to the verkrijgingsprijs and subtract selling costs from the overdrachtsprijs. Record the interest on a loan taken to buy the shares as a deductible cost ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For excessief lenen, list all debts to the BV and to any other company with an aanmerkelijk belang at nominal value on 31 December. Receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, debts at several companies are added, and debts of the partner count once for the two together. Apply the samenwerkingsverband exception and the eigenwoningschuld exclusion where they fit ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- The taxed part of the loan stays a loan in full. The company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, and the company does not withhold dividendbelasting on the part taxed as box 2 income ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)).
- Record the gebruikelijk loon as box 1 loon from employment. The € 5.000 test looks at the total of the work for all companies, not per company ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- Keep the beschikking for a box 2 loss. The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and is open to objection. The belastingkorting is also set by beschikking open to objection, and the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang ([art. 4.49 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, verlies omzetten in belastingkorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)).
- A negative fictief regulier voordeel is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years, so record the year of the repayment and the taxed amounts of earlier years ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).
- For a remaining PEB, keep the yearly indexation and the yearly actuarial valuation. If the BV does not index annually the Belastingdienst can see this as a form of prijsgeven of the rights ([Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)).
- Do not increase the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares with the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2, and value the ODV in the company with the art. 38p market rate ([art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- The taxpayer also declares the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst, wanneer hebt u een aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Loss-conversion korting 24,5% against 26,9%. FISIN 2025 states "De korting is 26,9%" in its rule text, while the statute for 2025 gives 24,5%, FISIN 2024 states 24,5% and the FISIN 2025 example itself uses 24,5%. The statute decides, and the Belastingdienst may correct the handbook. Sources: [FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2024, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: a corrected FISIN text or a Kennisgroep publication.
- Doorschuiving list UNVERIFIED. The list of rollover cases for gift, inheritance and emigration comes from the research and was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The 10-year APV term is confirmed by FISIN 2020. Sources: [FISIN 2020, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang), [FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: the 2025 consolidated text of art. 4.17 and 4.17a to 4.17c on wetten.overheid.nl or a Belastingdienst page that lists the rollover cases.
- 30%-rule beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht UNVERIFIED. The statement that such a beschikking has the same effect as an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands company was not found on the fetched Belastingdienst pages. Art. 7.5 itself is verified. Source: [art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01). What would settle it: a Belastingdienst page on partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht or the text of the beschikking.
- Summed two-partner amount UNVERIFIED. The rule that fiscal partners may divide the box 2 income in any proportion summing to 100% is verified, but no primary page states a single summed amount for two partners, because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer. Source: [FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: a Belastingdienst page or a Kennisgroep publication that states a summed amount.
- The statement that the gebruikelijk loon reduces the residual profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement. Source: [FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). What would settle it: a primary page that states the interaction.
- Proposed for 2027, not for 2025. The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from the inherited-belang facility from 1 January 2027, and the 29 September 2026 cabinet letter proposes a 29,2% second-bracket rate and a phase-down of excessief lenen. Nothing of this was adopted on 2026-10-11. Sources: [memorie van toelichting, Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf), [letter of 29 September 2026](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). What would settle it: publication in the Staatsblad.
