## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Zvw contribution (zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag)

An entrepreneur who is Zvw-insured and receives business profit from the Netherlands (winst uit nederland) pays the income-dependent contribution by (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not through a payroll. Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, and the 2025 percentage for this channel is 5,26% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material does not state which return is used for the Zvw and does not state a separate deadline, see section 7.

### Maximum contribution income

The maximum contribution income for 2025 is € 75.864 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Art. 43 lid 3 keeps the contribution income between nil and that yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Income above the maximum carries no further contribution. The maximum applies once per person across all channels, so income that already carried a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For an entrepreneur this means that profit counts only up to the amount left after salary (loon), pension or benefit income that already bore a contribution (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Withheld health insurance contribution (ingehouden zvw-bijdrage)

The withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) is withheld from pension, annuity (lijfrente) and other benefits (uitkeringen) that carry payroll taxes (loonheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). In 2025 the rate is the same 5,26% as the assessment rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). It matters to an entrepreneur because it uses the one maximum together with the profit, so a pension that already carried the withheld contribution reduces the room left for the profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Employer health insurance contribution (zvw-werkgeversheffing)

The employer levy (werkgeversheffing) is paid by the employer or the benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) over salary from employment (loondienst), over director and substantial shareholder (DGA) salary when the director and substantial shareholder is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), over social assistance (bijstand), over early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for health insurance fund (ziekenfonds)-insured people of 2005, and over WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The rate is 6,51% in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). The difference from the 5,26% is who owes the contribution and over what income. The 6,51% is owed by the employer or benefit agency over salary and benefits, while the 5,26% is owed by the insured person by assessment over for example profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An entrepreneur with both salary and profit therefore sees the 6,51% on the salary and the 5,26% on the profit, with one shared maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Income charged by assessment

By (provisional) assessment the following income is charged: Business profit from the Netherlands, income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), salary from a foreign employer, pension from a foreign benefit agency and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll taxes such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity is expressly not covered (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material attributes salary from a foreign employer to the 2020 to 2024 pages of the FISIN Zvw series, and the 2025 listing is not confirmed, see section 7. The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Exceptions to the assessment

The checked material gives four exceptions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Military personnel (militairen) in active service, or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income, fall outside the assessment. Share fishermen (deelvissers) fall outside where another party pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip. Income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution is corrected. And the contribution is reduced for the part of the year in which the person was insured in the Netherlands. The last two matter to an entrepreneur with foreign income or with a year that is partly spent outside the Dutch insurance. Further conditions per exception are not in the checked material.

### Maximum across channels

The maximum contribution income applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet page gives the pattern: Salary € 40.000 plus freelance income € 50.000 leaves € 35.864 on which the 5,26% is charged, and salary € 80.000 plus freelance income € 15.000 leaves nothing because the salary alone is above the maximum. For the assessment this means that an entrepreneur first counts the income that already carried a contribution, such as salary, a pension or a benefit, and applies the 5,26% only to the remaining room (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer)

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 defines the taxable profit (belastbare winst) as the profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) minus the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) is therefore the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and after the SME profit exemption, not the gross profit. Whether there is profit from business activities at all follows from the entrepreneur test: the business must be run for the taxpayer's account, the taxpayer must be able to make binding agreements for it and must be liable for its debts, and Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). This base rule is UNVERIFIED for 2025 because the material was read in the 2026 version only, see section 7.

### Zvw base and an old-age reserve decrease

Art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b carves out the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve, so a release of the reserve does not enter the Zvw base even though it raises the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The assessment also does not cover the conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). The euro effect of the carve-out is limited to the reserve movements that arise from the release grounds in the FOR material.

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction is part of the entrepreneur's allowance, which is deducted from the profit before the SME profit exemption, so it lowers the Zvw base. The 2025 amount is € 2.470, or 50% (€ 1.235) at state pension (AOW) age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025 and the https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek table give € 2.470, and the https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek example that prints € 2.740 is a typo that is resolved against https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025 and the table. The conditions of the deduction (aftrek), such as the hours criterion (urencriterium), belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic and are not repeated here (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek)

The starter's deduction is part of the entrepreneur's allowance as well, so it also lowers the profit before the SME profit exemption and therefore the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The 2025 amounts are € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The conditions for the starter's deduction belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic.

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid)

This part of the entrepreneur's allowance runs through € 12.000, then € 8.000, then € 4.000 and then € 0 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption, so it reduces the Zvw base in the same way as the other parts (onderdelen) of the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Further conditions are not in the checked material and belong to the entrepreneur's allowance topic.

### Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek)

The deduction for assisting partner is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the same hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and therefore lowers the profit before the SME profit exemption and the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The hour bands themselves are not in the checked material.

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)

The discontinuation deduction has a maximum of € 3.630 in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance, so in the year of the cessation (staking) it lowers the profit before the SME profit exemption and the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Further conditions are not in the checked material.

### SME profit exemption

The SME profit exemption is 12,7% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance in 2025 (SME, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). It is applied after the entrepreneur's allowance, and because the Zvw base is the taxable profit from business, the SME profit exemption lowers the Zvw base too (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). A person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Some profit components are exempt before the calculation (berekening), such as the forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), the agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the debt waiver profit exemption (vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst) for the part above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, compensation (vergoedingen) for business cessation (bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies and benefits under the final levy (eindheffing), mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions (vrijstellingen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### Old-age reserve

Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed, and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). A decrease follows on buying an annuity with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the business assets, on cessation, on reaching the state pension age on 1 January, on failing the hours criterion in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). For the Zvw the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 on such a decrease stays out of the base, and the conversion into an annuity is not covered by the assessment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### Assessment Zvw timing

The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material confirms only this timing. That a (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag) Zvw exists as a separate instrument, that the same return (aangifte) is used and that the Zvw follows the income tax filing timeline is stated in the underlying topic file but was not re-opened, so it is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### Filing deadline for the Zvw contribution

The checked material gives no filing deadline for the Zvw contribution for 2025. The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), and the filing deadline itself belongs to the procedure topic, see section 7.

### Zvw at state pension age

https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 (Tabel 77) footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde iab) (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2). No state pension age (AOW) exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, but that is inference from absence and the article was read in the 2026 version only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). No percentage for the reduced income-related contribution for a self-employed person at state pension age is in the checked material, so the rate for that situation cannot be given here. Note that the entrepreneur's allowance amounts themselves drop to 50% at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025).
