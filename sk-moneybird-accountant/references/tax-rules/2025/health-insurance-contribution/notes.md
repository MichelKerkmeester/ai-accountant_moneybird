## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zvw contribution (zvw-bijdrage) by assessment (aanslag): 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 71.628 in 2024 to € 75.864 in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Employer health insurance contribution (zvw-werkgeversheffing): 6,57% in 2024 to 6,51% in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). The cross-year series for this rate is 6,70% (2020), 7,00% (2021), 6,75% (2022), 6,68% (2023), 6,57% (2024) and 6,51% (2025).
- Withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage): 5,26% in 2025, the same as the assessment rate. The checked material gives no separate 2024 value for the withheld rate, so no old-to-new line can be drawn for it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer: the rule itself is unchanged, the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling). The amounts that shape that base changed, see the next bullets (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 3.750 in 2024 to € 2.470 in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025).
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age: 50% of € 3.750, so € 1.875, in 2024, to 50% of € 2.470, so € 1.235, in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2024/ondernemersaftrek-2024/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025).
- SME profit exemption: 13,31% in 2024 to 12,7% in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek): unchanged at € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid): unchanged at € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000, then € 0 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek): unchanged at 1,25%, 2%, 3%, 4% over the same hour bands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): unchanged at a maximum of € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): no new additions in 2024 and no new additions in 2025 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the profit, the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) for the year, because the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001 is the Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Record which income in the year already carried a Zvw contribution, such as salary (loon), a pension, a benefit or a withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage), because the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) applies once per person across channels and that income uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Keep the balance sheet movement of any old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve), because the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 on a decrease is not part of the Zvw base and the conversion into an annuity (lijfrente) is not covered by the assessment (aanslag) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- No new old-age reserve can be added, and an existing reserve stands on the balance sheet until a release ground applies (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment, so the Zvw amount lands with the income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- For a year in which part of the year was insured outside the Netherlands, or in which a foreign statutory health contribution was paid, keep the evidence, because the contribution is reduced or corrected for that (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The Zvw filing deadline is not in the checked material and belongs to the procedure topic, see section 7.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Zvw base for a winst-ondernemer: UNVERIFIED for 2025. https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 were read in the 2026 version only, and the 2025 version was not read. Reading Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 in the version valid on 1 January 2025 on wetten.overheid.nl would settle it.
- Zvw base and an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) decrease: UNVERIFIED for 2025 for the same reason, the exception in art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b was read in the 2026 version only.
- Zvw declaration and deadlines: UNVERIFIED. That the same return (aangifte) is used, that a (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag) Zvw exists and that the Zvw follows the income tax filing timeline comes from the topic file and was not re-opened. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms only that the Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment. The procedure topic carries the deadline.
- Zvw at state pension age: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 (Tabel 77) footnote 2 records that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde iab), with no percentage and no URL in the checked material. No state pension age (AOW) exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, but that is inference from absence and the article was read in the 2026 version only. Reading Zvw art. 41 to 45 in the version valid on 1 January 2025 would settle both points.
- Salary (loon) from a foreign employer as assessment income: the checked material lists it on the FISIN Zvw pages of 2020 to 2024, and the 2025 listing is not confirmed. Reading https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet would settle it.
- No conflict between sources is open for 2025 in the checked material.
