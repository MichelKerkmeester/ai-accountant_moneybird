## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of Dutch tax for a self-employed person without employees (ZZP). It lists the filing and payment deadlines for income tax and VAT (btw), deferral (uitstel), the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration duties. For 2025 the key values are the income tax return deadline of 1 May 2026 with deferral to 1 September 2026, the tax interest of 6,5% in 2025 that drops to 5% from 1 January 2026, and the VAT supplementary return (suppletie) term of 8 weeks that applies since 1 January 2025. All values for tax year 2025 in this file are enacted and Verified.
