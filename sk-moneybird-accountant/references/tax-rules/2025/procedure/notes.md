## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Tax interest (belastingrente) for income tax (IB) and VAT (btw): 7,5% in 2024 to 6,5% in 2025, then 5% from 1 January 2026. The rate regime is set once a year since 1 January 2024, with 2024 as the first year at 7,5% ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).
- Tax interest on the tax year: tax year 2024 runs from 1 July 2025 at 6,5% and 5% from 1 January 2026, tax year 2025 runs from 1 July 2026 at 5% ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).
- Income tax (IB) return deadline: tax year 2024 was due 1 May 2025 with deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2025, tax year 2025 is due 1 May 2026 with deferral to 1 September 2026 ([archief 22 mei 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250522/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte), [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)).
- Final assessment (definitieve aanslag): tax year 2024 at the latest 31 December 2027 and, with 4 months deferral, 30 April 2028, tax year 2025 at the latest 31 December 2028 and, with 4 months deferral, 30 April 2029 ([archief 28 maart 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens): € 56 in 2024 to € 57 in 2025. Refund threshold (teruggaafgrens): € 18 in both 2024 and 2025 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Filing without an invitation: tax year 2024 until 31 December 2029, tax year 2025 until 31 December 2030 ([hoeveel jaar terug belastingaangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).
- VAT (btw) yearly return: 2024 by 31 March 2025, 2025 by 31 March 2026 ([archief 6 februari 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [uiterste aangifte en betaaldatums btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).
- VAT monthly and quarterly dates shift with the calendar. January 2024 was due 29 February 2024 and January 2025 is due 28 February 2025. The December 2025 period runs to 31 January 2026 ([archief 6 februari 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240206/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-+waar-moet-u-aan-denken/wanneer_btw_aangifte_doen/uiterste_inlever_en_betaaldatums_btw_aangifte), [archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).
- VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete): € 68 for 2020 to 2024 to € 82 for 2025 and 2026, still after 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) of leniency (coulance) ([u doet geen of te laat aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte), [archief 20 januari 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).
- Income tax default fine (verzuimboete): € 385 for 2020 to 2024 to € 469 for 2025 and 2026. The maximum moves from up to € 5.514 to up to € 6.709, not requesting a return from € 2.757 to € 3.354, and the payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) from up to € 5.514 to up to € 6.709 ([archief 1 maart 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240301/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [archief 4 maart 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250304/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
- VAT supplementary return (suppletie): since 1 January 2025 a VAT error is corrected within 8 weeks after discovering it ([boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
- Payment terms for an assessment (aanslag), additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) and supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) are unchanged at 6 weeks, 1 month and 14 days in 2024 and 2025 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2024-01-01), [Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- File and pay each VAT (btw) return by the last day of the month after the period (tijdvak), or by 31 March 2026 for the 2025 yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date ([uiterste aangifte en betaaldatums btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).
- Correct a VAT error within 8 weeks after discovering it to stay penalty-free (boetevrij) ([boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).
- Record the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) instalments (termijnen) as they are paid or received, because paid or received amounts are settled in the income tax return. A paid provisional assessment does not count toward the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens), monthly refunds received before year end do, and a refund received after filing does not ([in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen), [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Note the assessment (aanslag) date in the records. Objection (bezwaar) runs 6 weeks from it and tax interest (belastingrente) ends 6 weeks after it ([eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar), [belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).
- Request deferral (uitstel) for the income tax return before the filing date if the records are not ready, and note that deferral usually means tax interest ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting), [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)).
- Keep the records (administratie) for 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data for 10 years. The paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) must stay on paper ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).
- Register the business (onderneming) with the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) within the two-week window. The Chamber of Commerce passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), so a separate registration is not needed, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen) ([Handelsregisterwet 2007, 16 juli 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

No value in this file is UNVERIFIED, proposed or unknown. All 2025 values are enacted and Verified. The points below are conflicts between sources or places where a source does not state a detail.

- Deferral (uitstel) channel, two Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages. The form page says online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May gives deferral to 1 September for someone who received a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The deferral page says online is not possible ([kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Read in full, the deferral page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form is the route. Settled reading: online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise.
- Chamber of Commerce (KVK) term, two weeks against ten working days (werkdagen). Both wordings remain on the Chamber of Commerce page ([hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states registration takes at most 10 working days ([schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)). Ten working days equals two calendar weeks, so both wordings describe the same period.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) wording, higher than (hoger dan) against equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan). The current page prints € 57 and € 18 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)) and the pages up to the 2023 captures print each threshold (grens) one euro higher ([archief 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [archief 2023](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. The full text of the two versions would settle it completely.
- The mass objection (massaal bezwaar) page on the tax interest (belastingrente) rate states 25 February 2026 only as its update date and gives no date for the collective decisions ([massaal bezwaar voor de hoogte van het belastingrentepercentage](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage)). A published decision date would settle it.
- The interest stop (rentestop) extension for requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen), ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). A later Dutch Tax and Customs Administration statement on the technical execution would settle the start date.
- The provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) cut-off is printed as 15 October without the calendar year in the checked source ([tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)). The page text on the specific tax year would settle which year the date belongs to.
