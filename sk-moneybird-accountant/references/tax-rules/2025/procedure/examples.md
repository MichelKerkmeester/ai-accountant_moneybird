## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example, final assessment (definitieve aanslag) for tax year 2025** ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Step 1: the return is filed before 1 May 2026.
- Step 2: the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2026.
- Step 3: the final assessment follows at the latest on 31 December 2028.
- Step 4: with 4 months deferral (uitstel) the return is due 1 September 2026 and the assessment follows at the latest on 30 April 2029.

**Official example, how the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) works** ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- A provisional refund (voorlopige teruggaaf) of € 100 received against € 80 entitled gives € 20 below the threshold (grens), so the assessment is nil.
- A paid provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) of € 100 against € 120 due gives a € 20 assessment.
- A refund of € 100 received after filing against € 80 entitled gives a € 20 assessment payable (te betalen).

**Calculated example, the 2025 limits (grenzen).**
- Step 1: tax due is calculated at € 50. The assessment threshold is € 57. € 50 is not higher than € 57, so no assessment is imposed.
- Step 2: a refund is calculated at € 15. The refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) is € 18. € 15 is not higher than € 18, so no refund is paid. Figures from section 2 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

**Official example, additional assessment (navordering) for tax year 2016** ([navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
- Tax year 2016, 5 months of deferral. An additional assessment is possible until 31 May 2022.

**Calculated example, additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn) for tax year 2025.**
- Step 1: the 5-year term runs after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, counted as in the official example from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2026.
- Step 2: 5 years from that point ends on 31 December 2030.
- Step 3: the granted deferral of 4 months extends the term by 4 months, so the term ends on 30 April 2031.
- Step 4: an error-based additional assessment (fout) would run only 2 years after the assessment or decision, and income or assets abroad would give 12 years. Figures from section 2 ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).

**Calculated example, payment term and objection (bezwaar) term on the same assessment.**
- Step 1: an assessment is dated 1 June 2026.
- Step 2: the assessment is collectible (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after that date. 1 June 2026 plus 42 days is 13 July 2026.
- Step 3: an objection must also be filed within 6 weeks after the date. The objection term ends on 13 July 2026 as well. Figures from section 2 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01), [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)).

**Calculated example, provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) instalments (termijnen).**
- Step 1: a provisional income tax assessment is dated 15 March 2025 for an amount of € 4.500.
- Step 2: the months remaining after March 2025 are April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December, so 9 months.
- Step 3: € 4.500 divided by 9 is € 500 per monthly instalment.
- Step 4: the first instalment is one month after the date, so 15 April 2025, and the last one is 15 December 2025.
- Step 5: a provisional assessment dated before the tax year, for example 20 December 2024 for 2025, is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. € 2.400 divided by 12 is € 200 each. Figures from section 2 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01)).

**Official example, tax interest (belastingrente) split** ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).
- 22 days in 2025 at 6,5% on € 2.500 gives € 9 within a € 102 total. Check: € 2.500 times 6,5% is € 162,50 for a full year. € 162,50 times 22 and divided by 360 is € 9,93, rounded down to € 9.

**Calculated example, tax interest on income tax for tax year 2025.**
- Step 1: the return for tax year 2025 arrives after 1 May 2026, so interest is charged.
- Step 2: the period runs from 1 July 2026. The rate for tax year 2025 is 5% from 1 July 2026.
- Step 3: the assessment is dated 20 September 2026. The period ends 6 weeks after the assessment date. 20 September 2026 plus 42 days is 1 November 2026.
- Step 4: 1 July 2026 to 1 November 2026 is 4 months. At 30 days per month that is 120 days out of 360 days per year.
- Step 5: the tax due is € 12.000. € 12.000 times 5% is € 600 per year.
- Step 6: € 600 times 120 and divided by 360 is € 200. Rounded down to whole euros the interest is € 200. Figures from section 2 ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).

**Calculated example, tax interest on VAT (btw), period and the no-interest window.**
- Step 1: for a late payment over a 2025 period (tijdvak) the period runs from 1 January 2026 to the payment date.
- Step 2: a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year gives no interest. The tax year 2025 ends 31 December 2025, so a payment up to 31 March 2026 falls inside the window.
- Step 3: for a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) the period runs from 1 January 2026 to 14 days after its date. A supplementary assessment dated 15 March 2026 gives a period to 29 March 2026. Figures from section 2 ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

**Calculated example, VAT due date and the penalty (boete) leniency (coulance) for the March 2025 period.**
- Step 1: the March 2025 monthly period is due 30 April 2025.
- Step 2: the leniency covers 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) after that date, ending 7 May 2025.
- Step 3: after the leniency the penalty is € 82. Figures from section 2 ([archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [archief 20 januari 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).

**Calculated example, the income tax (IB) missed deadline route.**
- Step 1: the return for tax year 2025 is due 1 May 2026 and is not filed.
- Step 2: a reminder (herinnering) follows, then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen).
- Step 3: filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine (verzuimboete) of € 469. A return that is never filed gives an ex officio assessment (ambtshalve aanslag) plus the default fine. Figures from section 2 ([wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)).

**Calculated example, request for a reduction (verzoek om vermindering) for assessment 2025.**
- Step 1: the online route is open when fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year and the return was filed online.
- Step 2: the pattern on the page is assessment 2020 until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028.
- Step 3: for assessment 2025 the same pattern gives 2025 plus 5, so online until 31 December 2030. Figures from section 2 ([verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

**Official example, record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) for a 4-year lease** ([hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).
- A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.

**Calculated example, Chamber of Commerce (KVK) registration window.**
- Step 1: the business starts on 1 March 2025.
- Step 2: the window starts one week before the start, so 22 February 2025, and ends one week after the start, so 8 March 2025.
- Step 3: registering within that window gives the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Figures from section 2 ([Handelsregisterwet 2007, 16 juli 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).
