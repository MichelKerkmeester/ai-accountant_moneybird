## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example, definitive aanslag for tax year 2025** ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- Step 1: the return is filed before 1 May 2026.
- Step 2: the Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2026.
- Step 3: the definitive aanslag follows at the latest on 31 December 2028.
- Step 4: with 4 months uitstel the return is due 1 September 2026 and the aanslag follows at the latest on 30 April 2029.

**Official example, how the aanslaggrens works** ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).
- A voorlopige teruggaaf of € 100 received against € 80 entitled gives € 20 below the grens, so the aanslag is nil.
- A paid voorlopige aanslag of € 100 against € 120 due gives a € 20 aanslag.
- A refund of € 100 received after filing against € 80 entitled gives a € 20 te betalen aanslag.

**Calculated example, the 2025 grenzen.**
- Step 1: tax due is calculated at € 50. The aanslaggrens is € 57. € 50 is not higher than € 57, so no aanslag is imposed.
- Step 2: a refund is calculated at € 15. The teruggaafgrens is € 18. € 15 is not higher than € 18, so no refund is paid. Figures from section 2 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

**Official example, navordering for tax year 2016** ([navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).
- Tax year 2016, 5 months of uitstel. Navordering is possible until 31 May 2022.

**Calculated example, navorderingstermijn for tax year 2025.**
- Step 1: the 5-year term runs after the belastingschuld arose, counted as in the official example from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2026.
- Step 2: 5 years from that point ends on 31 December 2030.
- Step 3: the granted uitstel of 4 months extends the term by 4 months, so the term ends on 30 April 2031.
- Step 4: a fout-based navordering would run only 2 years after the assessment or decision, and income or assets abroad would give 12 years. Figures from section 2 ([AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).

**Calculated example, payment term and bezwaar term on the same aanslag.**
- Step 1: an aanslag is dated 1 June 2026.
- Step 2: the aanslag is collectible 6 weeks after that date. 1 June 2026 plus 42 days is 13 July 2026.
- Step 3: bezwaar must also be filed within 6 weeks after the date. The bezwaar term ends on 13 July 2026 as well. Figures from section 2 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01), [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)).

**Calculated example, voorlopige aanslag termijnen.**
- Step 1: a voorlopige aanslag IB is dated 15 March 2025 for an amount of € 4.500.
- Step 2: the months remaining after March 2025 are April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December, so 9 months.
- Step 3: € 4.500 divided by 9 is € 500 per monthly termijn.
- Step 4: the first termijn is one month after the date, so 15 April 2025, and the last one is 15 December 2025.
- Step 5: a voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year, for example 20 December 2024 for 2025, is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. € 2.400 divided by 12 is € 200 each. Figures from section 2 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01)).

**Official example, belastingrente split** ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).
- 22 days in 2025 at 6,5% on € 2.500 gives € 9 within a € 102 total. Check: € 2.500 times 6,5% is € 162,50 for a full year. € 162,50 times 22 and divided by 360 is € 9,93, rounded down to € 9.

**Calculated example, belastingrente on income tax for tax year 2025.**
- Step 1: the return for tax year 2025 arrives after 1 May 2026, so rente is charged.
- Step 2: the period runs from 1 July 2026. The rate for tax year 2025 is 5% from 1 July 2026.
- Step 3: the aanslag is dated 20 September 2026. The period ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date. 20 September 2026 plus 42 days is 1 November 2026.
- Step 4: 1 July 2026 to 1 November 2026 is 4 months. At 30 days per month that is 120 days out of 360 days per year.
- Step 5: the tax due is € 12.000. € 12.000 times 5% is € 600 per year.
- Step 6: € 600 times 120 and divided by 360 is € 200. Rounded down to whole euros the rente is € 200. Figures from section 2 ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)).

**Calculated example, belastingrente on btw, period and the no-rente window.**
- Step 1: for a late payment over a 2025 tijdvak the period runs from 1 January 2026 to the payment date.
- Step 2: a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year gives no rente. The tax year 2025 ends 31 December 2025, so a payment up to 31 March 2026 falls inside the window.
- Step 3: for a naheffingsaanslag the period runs from 1 January 2026 to 14 days after its date. A naheffingsaanslag dated 15 March 2026 gives a period to 29 March 2026. Figures from section 2 ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

**Calculated example, btw due date and the boete coulance for the March 2025 period.**
- Step 1: the March 2025 monthly period is due 30 April 2025.
- Step 2: the coulance covers 7 kalenderdagen after that date, ending 7 May 2025.
- Step 3: after the coulance the boete is € 82. Figures from section 2 ([archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [archief 20 januari 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).

**Calculated example, the IB missed deadline route.**
- Step 1: the return for tax year 2025 is due 1 May 2026 and is not filed.
- Step 2: a herinnering follows, then an aanmaning with 10 werkdagen.
- Step 3: filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete of € 469. A return that is never filed gives an ambtshalve aanslag plus the verzuimboete. Figures from section 2 ([wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)).

**Calculated example, verzoek om vermindering for aanslag 2025.**
- Step 1: the online route is open when fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year and the return was filed online.
- Step 2: the pattern on the page is aanslag 2020 until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028.
- Step 3: for aanslag 2025 the same pattern gives 2025 plus 5, so online until 31 December 2030. Figures from section 2 ([verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

**Official example, bewaarplicht of a 4-year lease** ([hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).
- A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.

**Calculated example, KVK registration window.**
- Step 1: the business starts on 1 March 2025.
- Step 2: the window starts one week before the start, so 22 February 2025, and ends one week after the start, so 8 March 2025.
- Step 3: registering within that window gives the KVK-nummer immediately, and the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Figures from section 2 ([Handelsregisterwet 2007, 16 juli 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16), [hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).
