## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) 2025 filing deadline

The income tax return for tax year 2025 must arrive before the date given in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), and that date is often 1 May, so for tax year 2025 it is 1 May 2026 ([Belastingdienst, wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen), [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). The filing period opens on 1 March ([wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen)). AWR art. 9 lid 1 says that for assessment taxes (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the inspector (inspecteur) of at least one month after the invitation, and art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions ([AWR, geldend op 1 januari 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### 3.2 Deferral (uitstel) of the income tax return 2025

Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). For tax year 2025 a timely request gives a deadline of 1 September 2026 ([kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). The form can be used only for the first deferral request. A fiscal partner must request separately. Deferral usually means tax interest ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)).

### 3.3 Deferral channel for the income tax (IB) return

Online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May gives deferral to 1 September, and this route is only for someone who received a filing invitation letter ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting)). The deferral page says online is not possible ([kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)). Read in full, the deferral page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise ([aanvraag uitstel aangifte inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting), [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte)).

### 3.4 Final assessment (definitieve aanslag) 2025 latest date

AWR art. 11 lid 3, with the same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026, says the power to set the assessment (aanslag) lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For tax year 2025 the official example says a return before 1 May 2026 gives the final assessment at the latest 31 December 2028, and with 4 months deferral the return is due 1 September 2026 and the assessment at the latest 30 April 2029 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### 3.5 Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2025

For 2025 the assessment threshold is € 57 and the refund threshold is € 18 ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Tax is only due if the amount is higher than the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before year end count toward the threshold (grens), a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). Pages up to the 2023 captures used the wording equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold one euro higher ([archief 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [archief 2023](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)). For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold ([definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [archief 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag), [archief 2023](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag)).

### 3.6 Tax return (aangifte) without an invitation, tax year 2025

Without a filing invitation letter a return can still be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year ([hoeveel jaar terug belastingaangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). For tax year 2025 that is 31 December 2030 ([hoeveel jaar terug belastingaangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)). The same rule stands on the 2022 capture of the page ([archief 2022](https://web.archive.org/web/20220408/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen)).

### 3.7 Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn)

AWR art. 16 ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Lid 1 requires a new fact, and a fact known or reasonably knowable to the inspector cannot be used unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2 allows an additional assessment (navordering) also when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (voorlopige teruggaaf) or provisional loss set-off (voorlopige verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably identifiable (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), always when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3 sets the term at 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral, and for an error-based additional assessment only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends the term by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, where an additional assessment is possible until 31 May 2022, and states that a voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete) ([navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag)).

### 3.8 Payment terms for assessments (betalingstermijnen van aanslagen)

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet 1990) art. 9, with the same text on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An assessment is collectible (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) is collectible 1 month after the date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) is collectible 14 days after the date. A provisional income tax (IB) assessment dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, with the first one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01), [Invorderingswet 1990, 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01), [Invorderingswet 1990, 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01)). The 2025 table confirms 6 weeks, 1 month and 14 days for 2025 ([Invorderingswet 1990, 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01)).

### 3.9 Objection period (bezwaartermijn) and request for a reduction (verzoek om vermindering)

An objection (bezwaar) must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment, for income tax online, by pdf form or by letter ([eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The motivation can follow later if the objection asks for it ([eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks ([eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar)). No objection is possible against a provisional income tax assessment, only a change ([eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar), [tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)). After the objection period a request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering, also made ex officio (ambtshalve) by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration itself) is possible, online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The page gives assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 online until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request goes by letter ([verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting)).

### 3.10 Provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) change and carry-over

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return ([in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, a change in mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business, which gives profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return ([in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen)). A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after filing. A change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026 ([tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen)).

### 3.11 Tax interest (belastingrente) percentage 2025

For all taxes except corporate income tax (Vpb) and allowances (toeslagen) the rate is 6,5% in 2025, and 5% applies from 1 January 2026 ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The interest on tax year 2025 income tax runs at 5% from 1 July 2026 ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). The earlier sequence was 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 with the reduction for income tax starting 1 July 2020, 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023 and 7,5% in 2024 ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, with a minimum of 4,5%. For corporate income tax, withholding tax (bronbelasting), minimum tax (minimumbelasting) and solidarity contribution (solidariteitsbijdrage) the addition is 5,5 points, with a minimum of 5,5% ([overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente)). After Supreme Court of the Netherlands (HR) 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the corporate income tax table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. The mass objection (massaal bezwaar) on the rate was granted for corporate income tax and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT (btw) and the other taxes. The 25 February 2026 date on that page is only its update date, the page gives no date for the collective decisions ([massaal bezwaar voor de hoogte van het belastingrentepercentage](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage)).

### 3.12 Tax interest on income tax

Interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No interest applies when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)). The count uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros ([belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting)).

### 3.13 Tax interest on VAT

Interest is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). For a late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No interest applies to a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or to a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)). The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments, ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible ([belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting)).

### 3.14 VAT (btw) final filing and payment dates 2025

AWR art. 10 lid 2 says a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1 says the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period ([AWR, geldend op 1 januari 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, for both monthly and quarterly filers, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. Return and payment must both be received by that date ([uiterste aangifte en betaaldatums btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). For 2025 the monthly dates are Dec 2024 31 Jan 2025, Jan 28 Feb 2025, Feb 31 Mar 2025, Mar 30 Apr 2025, Apr 31 May 2025, May 30 Jun 2025, Jun 31 Jul 2025, Jul 31 Aug 2025, Aug 30 Sep 2025, Sep 31 Oct 2025, Oct 30 Nov 2025, Nov 31 Dec 2025 and Dec 2025 31 Jan 2026. The quarterly dates are Q4 2024 31 Jan 2025, Q1 30 Apr 2025, Q2 31 Jul 2025, Q3 31 Oct 2025 and Q4 31 Jan 2026 ([archief 30 april 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250430/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).

### 3.15 VAT yearly return 2025

A yearly VAT (btw) return for 2025 is due by 31 March 2026 ([uiterste aangifte en betaaldatums btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)).

### 3.16 VAT submission window and deferral

Software can submit a VAT return from the 24th of the month before the period ends ([uiterste aangifte en betaaldatums btw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)). Deferral for a VAT return (btw-aangifte) is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), with a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit) named, in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date, and a supplementary assessment and penalty remain possible ([uitstel aanvragen btw-aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte)).

### 3.17 VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2025

There are 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) of leniency (coulance). After that the penalty (boete) is € 82 in 2025 ([archief 20 januari 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250120/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)). In 2020 to 2024 the amount was € 68 ([u doet geen of te laat aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte)).

### 3.18 VAT supplementary return (suppletie) term 2025

Since 1 January 2025 a VAT error is corrected within 8 weeks after discovering it ([boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). Penalty-free (boetevrij) correcting ends once the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration finds the error first ([boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### 3.19 Income tax (IB) penalties (boetes) 2025 and the missed deadline route

After the deadline a reminder (herinnering) follows, then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen) ([wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine (verzuimboete). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. A return that is never filed gives an ex officio assessment (ambtshalve aanslag, made by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration itself) plus a default fine. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment ([wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig)). No penalty applies when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. A return can be requested within 6 months after the tax debt arose for income tax and corporate income tax, with 2 weeks leniency ([boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). Amounts for 2025: default fine (verzuimboete) income tax € 469, up to € 6.709, not requesting a return € 3.354, payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 6.709, the same amounts on the 2026 page, which adds 5% of the open amount with a minimum of € 50 ([archief 4 maart 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250304/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete), [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)). Penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete): 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld), 50% for intent (opzet), 75% and 150% in box 3, aggravated 100% and 300% ([wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig), [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete)).

### 3.20 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4, with the same text in 2020 and 2026, says persons with an administration duty (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten) ([AWR 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives 7 years for basic records (basisgegevens), 10 years for immovable property (onroerende zaken), 10 years for One Stop Shop data and 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office for other data ([hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer actual. A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable and accounts payable records (debiteuren- en crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), the payroll records (loonadministratie) and data relevant for the taxation of third parties ([hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren)).

### 3.21 Chamber of Commerce (KVK) and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration

Commercial Register Act (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1: the notification (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required notifications (opgaven) go within one week after the fact ([Handelsregisterwet 2007, 16 juli 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16)). The Chamber of Commerce (KVK) issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately when registering within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead ([hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/)). The Chamber of Commerce passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other legal forms get a legal entity identifier (RSIN), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. Where Chamber of Commerce registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used ([schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/)).
