## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

Each example uses the figures from section 2. Products are written with two decimals. Where a step rounds, it says rounded.

**Corporate income tax (Vpb) on a taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) of € 60.000 (Calculated example).**
1. € 60.000,00 is at or below € 200.000,00, so only the 19% bracket applies.
2. Tax = 19% × € 60.000,00 = € 11.400,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
Uses the section 2 figures 19% and € 200.000.

**Corporate income tax on a taxable amount of € 300.000 (Calculated example).**
1. Part above € 200.000,00 = € 300.000,00 - € 200.000,00 = € 100.000,00.
2. 25,8% × € 100.000,00 = € 25.800,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
3. Tax = € 38.000,00 + € 25.800,00 = € 63.800,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
Check on the first bracket: 19% × € 200.000,00 = € 38.000,00, which matches the column III amount in section 2.
Uses the section 2 figures 19%, € 200.000, € 38.000 and 25,8%.

**Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) with a profit of € 3.000.000 and a carryforward loss of € 2.500.000 (Calculated example).**
1. The yearly cap is € 1.000.000,00 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000,00.
2. Profit above € 1.000.000,00 = € 3.000.000,00 - € 1.000.000,00 = € 2.000.000,00.
3. 50% × € 2.000.000,00 = € 1.000.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
4. Cap = € 1.000.000,00 + € 1.000.000,00 = € 2.000.000,00.
5. Set-off = € 2.000.000,00, so € 500.000,00 of the loss is carried forward.
6. Profit after set-off = € 3.000.000,00 - € 2.000.000,00 = € 1.000.000,00.
Uses the section 2 figures € 1.000.000 and 50%.

**Art. 15b with a net interest balance (saldo aan renten) of € 2.400.000 and an adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) of € 5.000.000 (Calculated example).**
1. 24,5% × € 5.000.000,00 = € 1.225.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
2. The higher of € 1.225.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00 is € 1.225.000,00.
3. Non-deductible = € 2.400.000,00 - € 1.225.000,00 = € 1.175.000,00.
4. Deductible = € 1.225.000,00.
Second case, adjusted profit € 3.000.000 and balance € 1.200.000:
5. 24,5% × € 3.000.000,00 = € 735.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
6. The threshold is the higher of € 735.000,00 and € 1.000.000,00, so € 1.000.000,00.
7. Non-deductible = € 1.200.000,00 - € 1.000.000,00 = € 200.000,00.
8. Deductible = € 1.000.000,00.
Uses the section 2 figures 24,5% and € 1.000.000.

**Innovation box (innovatiebox) flat rate (forfait) (Calculated example).**
1. A BV made a qualifying intangible asset in 2025 and has a profit before art. 12b of € 80.000,00. The profit is positive and the qualifying asset was made in the current year, so the flat rate may be chosen.
2. Flat rate = 25% × € 80.000,00 = € 20.000,00, below the maximum of € 25.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
3. Profit before art. 12b € 120.000,00 gives 25% × € 120.000,00 = € 30.000,00, capped at € 25.000,00.
4. The checked material states that the 9/H fraction gives an effective rate of 9%, with H = 25,8%.
Uses the section 2 figures 25%, € 25.000, 9/H and 25,8%.

**Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) (Calculated example).**
1. Comparable wage € 70.000,00, highest wage in the body and connected bodies € 45.000,00, standard amount (normbedrag) € 56.000,00.
2. The highest of the three is € 70.000,00, so the wage is at least € 70.000,00.
3. If the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is € 5.000,00, the rule does not apply, because that is not more than € 5.000,00.
Uses the section 2 figures € 56.000 and € 5.000.

**Zvw for a non-insured DGA (Calculated example).**
1. Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) € 75.864,00.
2. 5,26% × € 75.864,00 = € 3.990,45, rounded from € 3.990,4464, withheld on net wage.
3. With a gross wage of € 60.000,00 the withholding is 5,26% × € 60.000,00 = € 3.156,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
Uses the section 2 figures 5,26% and € 75.864.

**Box 2 and dividend tax (dividendbelasting) on a dividend of € 100.000 (Calculated example).**
1. Dividend tax withheld = 15% × € 100.000,00 = € 15.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
2. Box 2 tax = € 16.611,00 + 31% × (€ 100.000,00 - € 67.804,00).
3. € 100.000,00 - € 67.804,00 = € 32.196,00.
4. 31% × € 32.196,00 = € 9.980,76. Exact, no rounding needed.
5. Box 2 tax = € 16.611,00 + € 9.980,76 = € 26.591,76. Exact, no rounding needed.
6. Credit of the dividend tax = € 15.000,00.
7. Amount still to pay in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) = € 26.591,76 - € 15.000,00 = € 11.591,76.
Uses the section 2 figures € 67.804, € 16.611, 31% and 15%.

**Official example on a share buyback (inkoop van eigen aandelen) (Official example).**
Consolidated Dividend Tax Decree 2025, onderdeel 7: with a cash dividend shortfall of € 10 million, a taxed buyback of € 8,5 million plus the dividend tax of (100/85 × € 8,5 million × 15% =) € 1,5 million may make it up. This concerns the art. 4c buyback facility for listed companies.
1. € 8.500.000,00 × 100/85 = € 10.000.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
2. 15% × € 10.000.000,00 = € 1.500.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.

**Rounding credit (Calculated example).**
1. Paid-up amount € 100.000,00 gives 5% × € 100.000,00 = € 5.000,00, below € 30.000,00, so the credit is € 5.000,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
2. Paid-up amount € 800.000,00 gives 5% × € 800.000,00 = € 40.000,00, capped at € 30.000,00, so the credit is € 30.000,00.
Uses the section 2 figures 5% and € 30.000.

**Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (Calculated example).**
1. Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) € 50.000,00.
2. Discontinuation deduction at most € 3.630,00, once in a lifetime.
3. Taxable profit from business discontinuation = € 50.000,00 - € 3.630,00 = € 46.370,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
Uses the section 2 figure € 3.630.

**Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) (Calculated example).**
1. Mother profit € 100.000,00, daughter loss € 40.000,00, both inside one fiscal unity.
2. Combined profit = € 100.000,00 - € 40.000,00 = € 60.000,00.
3. Corporate income tax = 19% × € 60.000,00 = € 11.400,00. Exact, no rounding needed.
Uses the section 2 figures 19% and € 200.000.

**Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size class (Calculated example).**
1. On two consecutive balance dates: balance sheet total € 500.000,00, net turnover € 800.000,00, 8 employees.
2. Assets test for micro: € 500.000,00 is more than € 450.000,00, so it fails.
3. Turnover test for micro: € 800.000,00 is not more than € 900.000,00, so it passes.
4. Employee test for micro: 8 is fewer than 10, so it passes.
5. Two of three criteria are met, so the class is micro.
Second case:
6. On two consecutive balance dates: balance sheet total € 8.000.000,00, turnover € 10.000.000,00, 40 employees.
7. Assets test for small: € 8.000.000,00 is more than € 7.500.000,00, so it fails.
8. Turnover test for small: € 10.000.000,00 is not more than € 15.000.000,00, so it passes.
9. Employee test for small: 40 is fewer than 50, so it passes.
10. Two of three criteria are met, so the class is small.
Uses the section 2 figures € 450.000, € 900.000, 10, € 7,5 million, € 15 million and 50.

**Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) (Official example).**
Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) withholding tax page: interest of € 100.000,00 to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction.
1. 25,8% × € 100.000,00 = € 25.800,00. Exact, no rounding needed. Net = € 100.000,00 - € 25.800,00 = € 74.200,00.
2. A royalty that must net € 100.000,00 to the recipient: gross = € 100.000,00 ÷ (1 - 0,258) = € 100.000,00 ÷ 0,742 = € 134.770,89, rounded from € 134.770,8895, about € 134.771.
URL: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/

**Dividend tax gross-up (Calculated example).**
1. Proceeds (opbrengst) € 100.000,00 and the BV bears the tax.
2. 100/85 × € 100.000,00 = € 117.647,06, rounded from € 117.647,0588.
3. 15% × € 117.647,06 = € 17.647,06, rounded from € 17.647,0590.
Uses the section 2 figures 15% and 100/85.
