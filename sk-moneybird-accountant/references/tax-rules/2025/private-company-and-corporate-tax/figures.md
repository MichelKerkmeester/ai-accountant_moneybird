## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the private limited company (BV, besloten vennootschap) and corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting, Vpb) for tax year 2025. It sets out the corporate income tax rates (vpb-tarieven) and computation, loss offsetting (loss set-off, verliesverrekening), the generic interest deduction (renteaftrek) limit, the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) for a director and substantial shareholder (DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder), the Zvw route (zvw-route) for that DGA, box 2 and dividend tax (dividendbelasting), conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting), the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid), the innovation box (innovatiebox), the annual accounts (jaarrekening) and record-keeping duties, and the switch from a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) to a BV. What matters most in 2025 is that the generic interest deduction percentage rises from 20% to 24,5%, the customary wage (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag) stays at € 56.000, the start-up transitional right lapses for all starters, and a new Tax-neutral Conversion Decree (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) applies from the day after its publication on 4 November 2025. The law for 2025 is enacted, and the Check tags mark the corrected descriptions and the one UNVERIFIED item.
