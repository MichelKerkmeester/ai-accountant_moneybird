## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Vpb tarief (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 computes the tax as the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the belastbaar bedrag (taxable amount) above the column I amount. For 2025 the first € 200.000 is taxed at 19% and the part above € 200.000 at € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([tarieven vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting)). The same series runs for 2023 to 2026, so no change of art. 22 takes effect during 2025. Art. 31 still carries the split formula (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn) for a book year that straddles a change of art. 22, with X the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the old tax and Tn the new tax ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### Verliesverrekening (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969 lets a loss be set off against the profit of the preceding year and carried forward without a time limit, but per year the set-off is capped at € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000, and the set-off follows the order in which the losses arose ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). This is the rule from 2022 onward. In 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed the loss by beschikking ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01)).

### Generieke renteaftrekbeperking (art. 15b Wet Vpb 1969)
The saldo aan renten (net interest balance) is the interest costs on loans minus the interest income. Art. 15b lid 1 makes it non-deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 24,5% of the gecorrigeerde winst (adjusted profit) and € 1.000.000 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The percentage ran at 30% in 2020 and 2021, at 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024 and at 24,5% in 2025 and 2026, and the Belastingdienst states 24,5% for 2025 ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)). Interest that is not deductible carries forward ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)).

### Innovatiebox (art. 12b tot 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H of the profit, where H is the highest art. 22 rate, which gives an effective 9% in 2025 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring, others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b, and merken, logo's and comparable assets are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb uses the K/T part, with K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3 and capped at the whole advantage after the drempel of art. 12bc. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)).

### Innovatiebox forfait (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in one of the two preceding years and chooses the forfait may count 25% of the profit before art. 12b, up to a maximum of € 25.000, and only when that profit is positive ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)).

### Innovatiebox smaller taxpayer (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)
Smaller means that the advantages plus the acquisition costs from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and that the net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). A smaller taxpayer needs only an S&O-verklaring. Others also need the access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)).

### Innovatieboxbesluit 2025
The Innovatieboxbesluit 2025 is geldend van 25-07-2025 ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)). It describes a cost-related markup that is veelal 8% to 15% of the costs and keeps the box open after an art. 3.65 conversion. An official example in it is insurer Z with its in-house software under the cost-related method ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)).

### Deelneming (art. 13 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a deelneming (shareholding) out of the profit, and art. 13 lid 2 creates a deelneming from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). In a holding structure the gebruikelijk loon test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A geruisloze inbreng into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Besluit geruisloze omzetting when its conditions are met ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). For btw a pure holding that only holds shares is not an ondernemer and cannot join a btw fiscale eenheid, while a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).

### Fiscale eenheid (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, in identical wording in every text from 2020 to 2026 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The Belastingdienst adds that the mother is a bv, nv, onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, coöperatie, or a stichting or vereniging acting as woningcorporatie (or a comparable foreign form), that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both have the same book years and profit rules and are effectively established in the Netherlands, that the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and that losses of one company offset profits of another ([fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)). A drop below 95% ends the fiscale eenheid at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once ([Stcrt. 2024, 11814](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html)). Subjective exemption of one daughter ends the eenheid for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### Gebruikelijk loon (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds an aanmerkelijk belang (substantial interest), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking (wage from the most comparable employment), which counts at 100% from 2023, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the normbedrag, which is € 56.000 for 2025 ([Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). The Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 also gives € 56.000 ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The normbedrag is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. From 2023 the escape is that the wage becomes the loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking when the highest amount exceeds that comparable wage. A vaststellingsovereenkomst (agreed settlement) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The test wage is the loon for loonbelasting and premie volksverzekeringen including the car bijtelling ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Connected body (Handboek Loonheffingen 2025)
A connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party. The gebruikelijk loon rule applies the same way to the partner of the DGA and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Start-up transitional right
The statutory start-up relief in art. 12a lid 3 and 4 ran from 2020 to 2022. For a body with an S&O-verklaring for R&D to which the starterspercentage of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Verordening (EU) nr. 1407/2013 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Belastingdienst stated that the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA and that an S&O-verklaring for part of a year was enough for the whole year ([loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023, a first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, and the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Lower wage for a starting company
Separate policy allows a starting company to set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked, and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as an eenmanszaak. A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Zvw bedragen 2025
The inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw (income-related contribution under the Zorgverzekeringswet) is 5,26% for 2025, the werkgeversheffing Zvw (employer levy) is 6,51% and the maximum bijdrage-inkomen is € 75.864 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). DGA loon falls under the werkgeversheffing only if the DGA is insured for the werknemersverzekeringen ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### Zvw route for an uninsured DGA
A DGA is in dienst of his own company for loonbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the werknemersverzekeringen. For him the BV pays no premies werknemersverzekeringen and no werkgeversheffing Zvw and instead withholds the inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw from his net wage up to the maximum bijdrageloon and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen. In 2025 that withholding is 5,26% up to € 75.864 ([Handboek 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A DGA who is insured falls under the werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by the BV ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The Belastingdienst table only says that the DGA pays the bijdrage himself, collected by inhouding op nettoloon or by aanslag ([DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### Box 2 (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
Box 2 (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang, income from a substantial interest) taxes 2025 income up to € 67.804 at 24,5% and everything above at € 16.611 plus 31% (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001) ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are indexed each year with the tabelcorrectiefactor ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Dividendbelasting withheld on a dividend is credited by the individual in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Dividendbelasting (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)
Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 makes the dividendbelasting 15% of the opbrengst (proceeds), and art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer while the company withholds ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). If the company bears the tax, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the opbrengst by 100/85 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). A deelnemingsdividend (holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the deelnemingsvrijstelling, while a beleggingsdividend to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Dividendbelasting aangifte en betaling
Once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividendnota (dividend statement) is due (art. 9 lid 1 Wet DB 1965) ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available ([dividendbelasting aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen)). No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Belastingdienst invites one, but a return is needed when a kwalificatiebeschikking (qualification ruling) is applied ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)). Individuals credit the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting and bodies in the aangifte Vpb ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Inkoop van eigen aandelen
Dividendbelasting is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. The company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies ([Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)). The Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025 of 7 October 2025 replaces the 2022 decision ([Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)).

### Dividend stripping rule
The uiteindelijk gerechtigde (beneficial owner) rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the uiteindelijk gerechtigde when the opbrengst benefits someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). From the 2024 text the wording is "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd", and the 2025 text carries that wording ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).

### Conditionele bronbelasting tarief
Art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)). With the 2025 art. 22 rate of 25,8% the tariff is 25,8%, and the Belastingdienst states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/)). With art. 22 the levy gave 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)).

### Conditionele bronbelasting scope
The levy hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, which is always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction where the profit tax is below 9% or that is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, and abuse cases ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)). Interest and royalties are in scope from the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles, and dividends enter in the 2024 version through art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividendbelasting ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)). Bronbelasting on dividends is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividendbelasting withheld on the same payment is credited ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/)).

### Besluit geruisloze omzetting
The Besluit geruisloze omzetting of 24 October 2025 was published in Staatscourant 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and is in force the day after publication. It replaces the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The substantive points named in it are 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right qualifies when it amounts to medegerechtigdheid), 3.4 (the holding approval widened to indirect holding and to a new or activity-free daughter), 4.2.2 (letteraandelen besides cumulatief preferente aandelen, plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw bijdrage credit), 12 (a new eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (the incidental tax advantage clarified), 13.2 (an S&O approval in the voor-voorperiode) and 15.3 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The consolidated version is BWBR0051693 and was not re-read ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). A rented-out business generally does not qualify (HR 20 February 2004), unless the letting gives medegerechtigdheid that continues the entrepreneurship ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Rounding credit geruisloze omzetting
On a geruisloze omzetting the contributor may be credited for inkomstenbelasting, premie volksverzekeringen and, from the 2025 Besluit, the Zvw bijdrage materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. This is the fourth standard condition ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Share sale presumption
Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise. This is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Retroactivity window
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets must be placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination, and the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscale eenheid only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). If the voorovereenkomst is received by the Belastingdienst within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The Belastingdienst adds that the request goes to the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, that the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring is registered with the Geleideformulier, and that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)). A kennisgroep standpunt adopted on 27 June 2022 and published on 12 January 2023 states that a request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties ([KG:212:2022:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/)).

### Stakingsaftrek
Without the art. 3.65 facility a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays inkomstenbelasting on the stakingswinst (cessation profit), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm), [stakingswinst berekenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen)). The stakingsaftrek (cessation allowance) is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime, and an extra lijfrente premium deduction applies ([Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/)). Stille reserves realised by a direct onttrekking before a geruisloze omzetting do not get the stakingsaftrek but may go into a lijfrente (art. 3.129) ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/)).

### BTW-eenheid beleidsbesluit
The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Staatscourant 2024, 38545, was published on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The btw fiscale eenheid of art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A beschikking, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The new legal form is a new business for btw and may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees, and transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).

### BTW fiscale eenheid verwevenheid
Financial intertwining means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).

The beleidsbesluit does not cover them and the Wet OB 1968 article and the BUA were not opened, so the section 2 row is Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). ### Jaarrekening size classes
A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. For 2025 the classes are micro at most € 450.000 assets and € 900.000 net turnover with fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 miljoen and € 15 miljoen with fewer than 50 employees and medium € 25 miljoen and € 50 miljoen with fewer than 250 employees, with the large class above € 50 miljoen turnover ([Boek 2 BW, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raised the amounts to these levels for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, and the employee counts are unchanged from the statute text of 2020 ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01)).

### Jaarrekening deposit
Under art. 394 Boek 2 BW the board deposits the jaarrekening within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted ([Boek 2 BW, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). There is no extension, late filing is a criminal offence, and from boekjaar 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### SBR deposit
From boekjaar 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file the jaarrekening through SBR ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### Bewaartermijnen
Art. 52 AWR makes lichamen administratieplichtig and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier, except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement, and the text is identical in the 2020 and 2026 versions ([AWR, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Records on onroerende zaken are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten (art. 34a Wet OB 1968), and OSS records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction (art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968) ([Wet OB 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).

### Aangifte Vpb deadlines
A book year equal to the calendar year is filed before 1 June of the next year. A broken book year is filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending on 31 December is filed before 1 June, and a short book year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)).

### Uitstel aangifte Vpb
The online extension, which exists only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for an extension of more than 5 months, and it must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons ([uitstel aangifte vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)).
