## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Corporate income tax (Vpb) rate (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 computes the tax as the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) above the column I amount. For 2025 the first € 200.000 is taxed at 19% and the part above € 200.000 at € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([tarieven vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting)). The same series runs for 2023 to 2026, so no change of art. 22 takes effect during 2025. Art. 31 still carries the split formula (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn) for a financial year (boekjaar) that straddles a change of art. 22, with X the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the old tax and Tn the new tax ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969 lets a loss be set off against the profit of the preceding year and carried forward without a time limit, but per year the set-off is capped at € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000, and the set-off follows the order in which the losses arose ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). This is the rule from 2022 onward. In 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed the loss by decision (beschikking) ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01)).

### Generic interest deduction (renteaftrek) limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) (art. 15b Wet Vpb 1969)
The net interest balance (saldo aan renten) is the interest costs on loans minus the interest income. Art. 15b lid 1 makes it non-deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of 24,5% of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The percentage ran at 30% in 2020 and 2021, at 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024 and at 24,5% in 2025 and 2026, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 24,5% for 2025 ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)). Interest that is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) carries forward ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)).

### Innovation box (innovatiebox) (art. 12b tot 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset (kwalificerend immaterieel activum) count for 9/H of the profit, where H is the highest art. 22 rate, which gives an effective 9% in 2025 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). A smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring), others also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b, and brands, logo's and comparable assets are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb uses the K/T part, with K the qualifying expenditure (kwalificerende uitgaven) multiplied by 1,3 and capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)).

### Innovation box flat rate (forfait) (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)
A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in one of the two preceding years and chooses the flat rate (forfait) may count 25% of the profit before art. 12b, up to a maximum of € 25.000, and only when that profit is positive ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)).

### Innovation box for a smaller taxpayer (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)
Smaller means that the advantages plus the acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten, aanschafkosten) from R&D assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and that the net turnover (netto-omzet) over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 (art. 12ba lid 2, identical in the 2020 and 2026 texts) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D declaration. Others also need the access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)).

### Innovation Box Decree (Innovatieboxbesluit 2025)
The Innovation Box Decree 2025 is valid from 25-07-2025 ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)). It describes a cost-related markup that is usually 8% to 15% of the costs and keeps the box open after an art. 3.65 conversion. An official example in it is insurer Z with its in-house software under the cost-related method ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)).

### Participation (deelneming) (art. 13 Wet Vpb 1969)
Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (shareholding) out of the profit, and art. 13 lid 2 creates a participation from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital, identical in every text from 2020 to 2026 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A tax-neutral contribution (geruisloze inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Tax-neutral Conversion Decree (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) when its conditions are met ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). For VAT a pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity (btw fiscale eenheid), while a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).

### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)
The mother must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital (nominaal gestort kapitaal) of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights (statutaire stemrechten) and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer, in identical wording in every text from 2020 to 2026 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the mother is a bv, nv, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or a foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie) (or a comparable foreign form), that the daughter is a bv or nv, that both have the same financial years and profit rules and are effectively established in the Netherlands, that the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and that losses of one company offset profits of another ([fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)). A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once ([Stcrt. 2024, 11814](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html)). Subjective exemption (subjectieve vrijstelling) of one daughter ends the unity for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)
For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), which counts at 100% from 2023, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the standard amount (normbedrag), which is € 56.000 for 2025 ([Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 also gives € 56.000 ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001. The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000. From 2023 the escape is that the wage becomes the wage from the most comparable employment relationship when the highest amount exceeds that comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon). An agreed settlement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling) ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Connected body (Handboek Loonheffingen 2025)
A connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party (derde). The customary salary rule applies the same way to the partner of the DGA and to working relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) who hold shares ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Start-up transitional right
The statutory start-up relief in art. 12a lid 3 and 4 ran from 2020 to 2022. For a body with an R&D declaration to which the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applies, the wage was at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, or the lower comparable wage if made plausible, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) nr. 1407/2013 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration stated that the de-minimis condition did not apply to a DGA and that an R&D declaration for part of a year was enough for the whole year ([loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023, a first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, and the Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 states that the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 ([Handboek](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Lower wage for a starting company
Separate policy allows a starting company to set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the statutory minimum wage (WML, wettelijk minimumloon) for the hours worked (gewerkte uren), and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).

### Zvw amounts 2025
The income-related contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) for health insurance (zorgverzekering) under the Health Insurance Act (zorgverzekeringswet) is 5,26% for 2025, the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) is 6,51% and the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is € 75.864 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)). DGA wage falls under the employer levy only if the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### Zvw route (zvw-route) for a non-insured DGA (niet-verzekerde dga)
A DGA is in service of his own company (eigen vennootschap) for wage tax, national insurance contributions and the Health Insurance Act, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes. For him the BV pays no employee insurance premiums (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer levy and instead withholds the income-related Health Insurance Act contribution from his net wage (nettoloon) up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). In 2025 that withholding is 5,26% up to € 75.864 ([Handboek 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A DGA who is insured falls under the employer levy paid by the BV ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table only says that the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding from net wage (inhouding op nettoloon) or by assessment (aanslag) ([DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### Box 2 (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)
Box 2 (income from a substantial interest, inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) taxes 2025 income up to € 67.804 at 24,5% and everything above at € 16.611 plus 31% (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001) ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are indexed each year with the table correction factor ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld on a dividend is credited by the individual in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)
Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 makes the dividend tax 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst), and art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer while the company withholds ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). If the company bears the tax, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the proceeds by 100/85 ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). A participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend, holding of 5% or more) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling), while an investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Dividend tax return and payment
Once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due (art. 9 lid 1 Wet DB 1965) ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). The return and the payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available ([dividendbelasting aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen)). No return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration invites one, but a return is needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)). Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return and bodies in the corporate income tax return (aangifte vennootschapsbelasting, aangifte vpb) ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).

### Share buyback (inkoop van eigen aandelen)
Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. The company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies ([Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)). The Consolidated Dividend Tax Decree 2025 (Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025) of 7 October 2025 replaces the 2022 decision ([Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)).

### Dividend stripping rule
The beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). From the 2024 text the wording is "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd", and the 2025 text carries that wording ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).

### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) rate
Art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)). With the 2025 art. 22 rate of 25,8% the rate is 25,8%, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/)). With art. 22 the levy gave 25% in 2021 and 25,8% from 2022 ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)).

### Conditional withholding tax scope
The levy hits payments to a related body with a qualifying interest, which is always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie) where the profit tax is below 9% or that is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions (niet-coöperatieve jurisdicties), and abuse cases ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)). Interest and royalties are in scope from the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles, and dividends enter in the 2024 version through art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividend tax ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)). Withholding tax on dividends is withheld when the dividend is made available, and dividend tax withheld on the same payment is credited ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/)).

### Tax-neutral conversion decree (besluit geruisloze omzetting)
The Tax-neutral Conversion Decree of 24 October 2025 was published in Government Gazette (Staatscourant, Stcrt.) 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025 and is in force (in werking) the day after publication. It replaces the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The substantive points named in it are 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right qualifies when it amounts to co-entitlement (medegerechtigdheid)), 3.4 (the holding approval widened to indirect holding and to a new or activity-free daughter), 4.2.2 (lettered shares (letteraandelen) besides cumulative preference shares (cumulatief preferente aandelen), plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw contribution credit), 12 (a new eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (the incidental tax advantage clarified), 13.2 (an R&D approval in the pre-pre period) and 15.3 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The consolidated version is BWBR0051693 and was not re-read ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). A rented-out business generally does not qualify (Supreme Court of the Netherlands (hoge raad, HR) 20 February 2004), unless the letting gives co-entitlement that continues the entrepreneurship ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Rounding credit on a tax-neutral conversion
On a tax-neutral conversion the contributor may be credited for income tax, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree, the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. This is the fourth standard condition ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Share sale presumption
Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise. This is a rebuttable presumption, not an absolute sale ban ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### Retroactivity window
On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets must be placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination, and the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscal unity only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). If the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full financial year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next financial year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request goes to the office that handles the income tax, that the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) is registered with the accompanying form (Geleideformulier), and that the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)). A knowledge group (kennisgroep) position adopted on 27 June 2022 and published on 12 January 2023 states that a request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties ([KG:212:2022:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/)).

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek)
Without the art. 3.65 facility a change of legal form is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays income tax on the profit on discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm), [stakingswinst berekenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen)). The discontinuation deduction is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime, and an extra annuity premium deduction applies ([Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/)). Hidden reserves realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129) ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/)).

### VAT fiscal unity policy decree (btw-eenheid beleidsbesluit)
The Policy Decree on VAT liability and the VAT fiscal unity of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Staatscourant 2024, 38545, was published on 10 December 2024 and states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The VAT fiscal unity of art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968 arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A decision (beschikking), on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability (hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid) ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The new legal form (rechtsvorm) is a new business for VAT and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees, and transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).

### VAT fiscal unity and interdependence (verwevenheid)
Financial intertwining means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).

### VAT cost-sharing exemption (koepelvrijstelling) and BUA
No value is available here for the VAT cost-sharing exemption and the BUA. The policy decree does not cover them and the Wet OB 1968 article and the BUA were not opened, so the section 2 row is Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). See section 7.

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size classes (grootteklassen)
A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria. For 2025 the classes are micro at most € 450.000 assets and € 900.000 net turnover with fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 million and € 15 million with fewer than 50 employees and medium € 25 million and € 50 million with fewer than 250 employees, with the large class (grote klasse) above € 50 million turnover ([Boek 2 BW, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raised the amounts to these levels for financial years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, and the employee counts are unchanged from the statute text of 2020 ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01)).

### Annual accounts deposit
Under art. 394 Boek 2 BW the board deposits the annual accounts within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as unadopted ([Boek 2 BW, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). There is no extension, late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf), and from financial year 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### SBR deposit
From financial year 2025, with filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file the annual accounts through SBR ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### Retention periods (bewaartermijnen)
Art. 52 AWR makes bodies required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier, except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement (resultatenrekening), and the text is identical in the 2020 and 2026 versions ([AWR, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten (art. 34a Wet OB 1968), and One Stop Shop records are kept for ten years after the year of the transaction (art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968) ([Wet OB 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).

### Corporate income tax return deadlines
A financial year equal to the calendar year is filed before 1 June of the next year. A broken financial year is filed within 5 months after its end. A short financial year (kort boekjaar) ending on 31 December is filed before 1 June, and a short financial year ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)).

### Deferral of the corporate income tax return
The online extension, which exists only for a calendar financial year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken financial year or for an extension of more than 5 months, and it must be filed within 5 months after the financial year ends, with reasons ([uitstel aangifte vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)).
