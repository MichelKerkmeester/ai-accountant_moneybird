## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking): the art. 15b percentage goes from 20% in 2024 to 24,5% in 2025 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)). The threshold of € 1.000.000 stays the same.
- Zvw: the contribution (bijdrage) goes from 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025, the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) from € 71.628 to € 75.864, and the employer levy (werkgeversheffing) from 6,57% to 6,51% ([FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).
- Box 2: the first bracket goes from € 67.000 at 24,5% to € 67.804 at 24,5%, the column III amount from € 16.415 to € 16.611, and the rate above the bracket from 33% to 31% ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Start-up relief: in 2024 only the 2022 starters could still continue, in the last year of the facility. From 2025 the transitional right has lapsed for all starters ([loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).
- Tax-neutral conversion decree (besluit geruisloze omzetting): the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M is replaced by the Decree of 24 October 2025, published in Government Gazette (Staatscourant) 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025, in force the day after publication. The named changes include 2.2.2.a, 3.4, 4.2.2, 6.1, 8 with the Zvw contribution credit, 12 with the new eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad, 13.1.2, 13.2 and 15.3 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).
- The Consolidated Dividend Tax Decree 2025 of 7 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 34752, replaces the 2022 decision ([Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)).
- The Innovation Box Decree (Innovatieboxbesluit 2025) is valid from (geldend van) 25-07-2025 ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)).
- The Policy Decree on VAT liability and the VAT fiscal unity (beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, enters into force on 1 July 2025 ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).
- From financial year (boekjaar) 2025 all legal persons file the annual accounts (jaarrekening) through SBR, with filing from 1 January 2026 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).
- Unchanged from 2024 to 2025: the corporate income tax (Vpb) rates of 19% and € 38.000 plus 25,8%, the loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) rule, the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount of € 56.000, the € 5.000 floor, the 100% comparable wage, dividend tax (dividendbelasting) of 15%, conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) of 25,8%, the participation (deelneming) threshold of 5%, the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) shareholding requirement (bezitseis) of 95%, the innovation box (innovatiebox) 9/H and the € 25.000 flat rate (forfait), the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) of € 3.630 and the annual accounts (jaarrekening) size thresholds ([Wet Vpb 1969 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [Wet LB 1964 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [Wet DB 1965 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01), [Wet IB 2001 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html)).

Corrections carried into this 2025 file, not changes of law:

- Dividend stripping: the article is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not art. 4 lid 3 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).
- Zvw route for a non-insured DGA: the BV withholds the contribution (bijdrage) on net wage in the Handbook for Payroll Taxes, Handboek Loonheffingen 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, and the DGA does not pay it by assessment (aanslag) ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).
- Tax-neutral conversion decree (besluit geruisloze omzetting) citation: Stcrt. 2025, 37092, not Stcrt. 2025, 3709 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).
- In-force date of the VAT fiscal unity policy decree (btw-eenheid beleidsbesluit): 1 July 2025, as the decree itself states ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).
- Large class turnover bound: € 50 million per Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397, while the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) table prints more than € 51 million ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb): a financial year (boekjaar) equal to the calendar year is filed before 1 June of the next year, a broken financial year within 5 months after its end, and a short financial year ending 31 December before 1 June or ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)). The online extension runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June, and a paper request within 5 months after the financial year ends with reasons is needed for a broken financial year or for more than 5 months ([uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)).
- Payroll records (loonadministratie): the BV runs the DGA wage through its payroll records and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). The wage is at least € 56.000 or the higher of the comparable wage and the highest wage in connected bodies, the test wage includes the car private-use addition (bijtelling), and for a non-insured DGA the BV withholds 5,26% Zvw on net wage up to € 75.864 and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen) ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Wet LB 1964 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)).
- Dividend: record the dividend statement (dividendnota) once the proceeds (opbrengst) are paid, credited, settled or handed over, withhold 15%, and file and pay within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) of 5% or more to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt and needs no return unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) invites one. A qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) needs a return. The shareholder credits the withheld tax in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) or, for a body, in the corporate income tax return (aangifte Vpb) ([aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen), [als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): withhold 25,8% on interest, royalties and dividends to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction, and credit any dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld on the same payment ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/), [Wet bronbelasting 2021, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)).
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening): deposit within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the financial year (boekjaar) ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as unadopted. There is no extension, late filing is a criminal offence, and from financial year 2025 all legal persons file through SBR ([Boek 2 BW 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).
- Records: legal entities (lichamen) are required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and keep them for seven years, and data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement ([AWR 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten, and One Stop Shop records for ten years ([Wet OB 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).
- Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid): the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another ([fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)).
- Switch to a BV: request at the office that handles the income tax (inkomstenbelasting), register the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the accompanying form (Geleideformulier), and note that the BV is a bv in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. The new legal form may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once, and payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).
- Innovation box (innovatiebox): a smaller taxpayer needs an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring), and the K/T calculation uses the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, so the qualifying expenditure must be recorded ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox), [Wet Vpb 1969 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- VAT cost-sharing exemption (koepelvrijstelling) and BUA: Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED in section 2. The policy decree on VAT liability and the VAT fiscal unity (Stcrt. 2024, 38545) does not cover them, and the Wet OB 1968 article and the BUA were not opened ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). Reading the Wet OB 1968 article on the VAT cost-sharing exemption and the BUA would settle it.
- Conflict on the large class turnover bound: the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) table prints more than € 51 million ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)), while Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 million ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). Settled at € 50 million.
- Conflict on the micro thresholds: Stb. 2024, 52 art. 1 raises art. 395a to € 450.000 and € 900.000, and the Chamber of Commerce applies these ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)), while the consolidated Boek 2 BW at 2025-01-01 and at 2026-07-01 still prints € 350.000 and € 700.000 in art. 395a and prints the raised amounts in art. 396 and 397 ([Boek 2 BW 2025-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [Boek 2 BW 2026-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01)). The explanatory memorandum (Nota van toelichting) says the AMvB basis for art. 395a was missing and was to be added to art. 2:398 lid 4, which the 2026-07-01 text now does. The value used in section 2 is € 450.000 and € 900.000. A Chamber of Commerce or Justice (Justitie) statement on the legal effect for the financial year (boekjaar) would close it.
- The 2025 list of open points names no other unknown, proposed or UNVERIFIED value for the year.
