## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Box 2 bracket 1 (24,5% up to € 67.804)

Art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 puts the first € 67.804 of box 2 income of a taxpayer in bracket 1 at 24,5% ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The rate applies to a taxpayer with income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang), which is a holder of shares, profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) or comparable rights who meets the 5% test of art. 4.6 (see 3.4). The two-bracket table applies from 2024 and the table applies per taxpayer ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor), so the boundary is an indexed amount ([art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) box 2 page and FISIN 2025 state the same 24,5% up to € 67.804 ([FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.2 Tax at the top of bracket 1 (€ 16.611)

The art. 2.12 table states € 16.611 as the tax at the top of bracket 1, in column III of the table ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). This figure is the tax on € 67.804 of box 2 income at 24,5% and is the anchor for the tax on income above the boundary: the part up to € 67.804 carries € 16.611 and only the excess is taxed at the second-bracket rate. Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) that was withheld is not deducted from the income but credited as prepayment (voorheffing) against the tax that follows from the table ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.3 Box 2 bracket 2 (31% above € 67.804)

Above € 67.804 the rate is 31% for 2025 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The second bracket was announced in the Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) and took effect on 1 January 2024 ([rijksoverheid.nl, belangrijkste belastingwijzigingen per 1 januari 2024](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024)). The 33% rate of 2024 came from the Van der Lee c.s. amendment (amendement) in the Tax Plan 2024 (Belastingplan 2024), and the Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 percentage points to 31% from 1 January 2025 ([Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) has his own bracket 1 and his own 31% bracket over his own share of the income (see 3.8).

### 3.4 Substantial interest, the 5% test (art. 4.6)

The taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds profit-sharing certificates for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Sub f was added by the Other Fiscal Measures 2026 Act (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2025, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights (stemrechten) ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). In sub d the word "tot" became "om" between the 2021 and 2022 texts. The research statement that the 2020 and 2027 texts read the same is corrected by these year texts ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01)).

### 3.5 Drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) and carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) (art. 4.9 and 4.10)

The drag-along rule: once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that company also belong to it. The carry-along rule: shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts ([Belastingdienst, wanneer hebt u een aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.7, 4.8, 4.9 and 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027, checked year by year ([art. 4.9 and 4.10 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.6 Deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11)

Shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles are deemed to be one if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied, so in rollover cases ([art. 4.11 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. The exempt investment institution (vbi, vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) status is a deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding) case listed by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, not part of the art. 4.11 list.

### 3.7 Box 2 income of minor children

The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner" (whether or not together with his partner) under art. 4.6 ([art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest of minor children, until the child turns 18 ([Belastingdienst, wanneer hebt u een aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang)). This rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.8 Division between fiscal partners (art. 2.17 lid 2)

Common income components (gemeenschappelijke inkomensbestanddelen) of the taxpayer and partner are divided in the proportion they choose each year in the return ([art. 2.17 lid 2 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2026 states that with a fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang) may be divided, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (any split is allowed, as long as the total is 100%) ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, each partner's own first bracket (eerste schijf) applies to his share. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount, so that figure is UNVERIFIED (see section 7).

### 3.9 Income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.12 and 4.13)

Income from a substantial interest is regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) ([art. 4.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company (sub a, see 3.10), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling) (sub e), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction (sub f, see 3.16 to 3.19). Interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap) is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen). Salary from the company is box 1 salary (loon). Deductible costs include interest on a loan taken to buy the shares. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs). Dividend tax is not deducted but credited as prepayment ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The placement of the excessive borrowing fiction was settled at art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c. Art. 4.12a carries a different facility, the inherited substantial interest facility (see 3.13) ([art. 4.12a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html)).

### 3.10 Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) for an exempt investment institution or foreign investment company (5,88%)

The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a is a yearly percentage of the value of the shares at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, time-apportioned over the year ([art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the percentage is 5,88%. The percentage is set per year in the statute text, 6,04% in 2024 and 5,88% in 2025 ([art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). It applies to shares in an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company that meets the art. 4.14 conditions.

### 3.11 Disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel), acquisition price and non-arm's-length price (verkrijgingsprijs en onzakelijke prijs)

The disposal gain is the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) minus the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) ([Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)). The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included. Special cases such as inheritance, a gift with or without rollover (doorschuiving), reaching the 5% threshold during the year and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs) are handled as the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes. At a non-arm's-length price, the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides. The rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.12 Deemed disposal (art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists as a sale: share buyback (inkoop van aandelen), commutation (afkoop) and purchase of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of a segregated private assets fund (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a ([art. 4.16 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 counts as a liquidation payment. The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.13 Inherited substantial interest facility (24 months)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the acquisition price ([art. 4.12a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction. The text is identical 2020 to 2027. The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from this facility from 1 January 2027, a proposal that was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([memorie van toelichting, Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf)).

### 3.14 Rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration

The checked material lists rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration. That list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check, so it is UNVERIFIED ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), see section 7).

### 3.15 APV loss deferral (10 years)

The segregated private assets (APV, afgezonderd particulier vermogen) rules defer the loss and allow offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year APV term ([FISIN 2020, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang)). The rule holds for 2020 to 2027.

### 3.16 Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) maximum (€ 500.000)

The Wet van 21 december 2022, Stb. 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022) is in force (in werking) from 1 January 2023 and is cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap" ([Stb. 2022, 531](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html), [art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f Wet IB 2001, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f as "het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)" (the excessive part of debts, the fictitious regular benefit), where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations. The amount rule is art. 4.14a ("Fictief regulier voordeel"), the related-person rule is art. 4.14b and the immigration rule is art. 4.14c ([art. 4.14a to 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 4.14a lid 1: a positive amount equals the total debts above the maximum, and a negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier, or under a comparable foreign rule, and not yet reversed. Lid 2: the maximum is € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier, after € 700.000 in the 2023 text. Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 indexation list, so the € 500.000 is not indexed ([art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The threshold history: the bill proposed € 500.000, the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging, Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced it by € 700.000, and amendment Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), brought it back to € 500.000 ([Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html), [Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. A](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html)). The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c ([art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

### 3.17 Excessive borrowing, measurement and floor

The debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value (art. 4.14a lid 4). If the taxpayer no longer has a substantial interest at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount (lid 5). An owner-occupied home (eigen woning) debt (eigenwoningschuld) is excluded to the extent a mortgage right (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company (lid 6). Art. 10a.23 adds that an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage is also excluded ([art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the threshold (drempel) "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt)). FISIN 2025 phrases the test as "op 31 december 2025 meer dan € 500.000" (more than € 500.000 on 31 December 2025) ([FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang)). Consequences: the loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividend tax on the part taxed as box 2 income. Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold. A negative deemed regular benefit is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.18 Excessive borrowing, related persons (verbonden personen) and immigration (art. 4.14b and 4.14c)

Debts of a related person (verbonden persoon) to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000 from 2024, applied to that person and his partner together ([art. 4.14b and 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A related person is a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line (aanverwant in de rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally. A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 500.000 from 2024, after € 700.000 in the 2023 text ([art. 4.14b and 4.14c Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.19 Excessive borrowing, partnership (samenwerkingsverband) exception

Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a related person for a debt of a partnership (a general partnership (vof), limited partnership (cv), civil-law partnership (maatschap) or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out. This exception was inserted by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023, as lid 6 and 7 of art. 4.13, numbered lid 4 and 5 for 2023 ([Stb. 2024, 434](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html)). The same act removed the double counting of a partnership debt among several participants in art. 4.14a lid 7. The Other Fiscal Measures 2026 Act (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 ([Stb. 2025, 445](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html)). The 2025 text carries the exception in art. 4.13 lid 6 and 7 and art. 4.14a lid 7 ([art. 4.13 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.20 Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (€ 56.000)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the salary is at least the highest of (a) the salary from the most comparable employment, (b) the highest salary of the employees of the body or connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the fixed amount is € 56.000, the same art. 12a text as 2024 ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01)). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor (lid 7 from the 2023 text). The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.21 Customary salary minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The customary salary rule does not apply if the salary for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, lid 4 from 2023) ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (not per company) ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.22 Customary salary comparable salary (100% from 2023)

The salary from the most comparable employment counts at 75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts and at 100% from the 2023 text ([art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). A lower salary is allowed if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes it plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable salary, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable salary itself is lower. The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and which is known to the withholding agent and the tax inspector (inspecteur). For a body with an S&O declaration (S&O-verklaring) to which the increased start-up percentage (verhoogd starterspercentage) applied, the 2020 to 2022 texts had a start-up rule of at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage (minimumloon) of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January, or the comparable salary if lower, usable at most 3 years. The rule is absent from the 2023 text. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" (from 2023 the exception has lapsed for new cases), so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 ([Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)).

### 3.23 Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset box 2 loss (24,5%)

Art. 4.53 lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted on request into a tax credit (belastingkorting). A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. Lid 2 gives 24,5% for 2024 to 2027 through the reference to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage" (the percentage in the first row of the fourth column of the art. 2.12 table), after 26,25% in the 2020 text and 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts. Lid 3: the tax inspector sets the tax credit by decision (beschikking) open to objection. Lid 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner ([art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without substantial interest ([Belastingdienst, verlies omzetten in belastingkorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting)). Knowledge group (kennisgroep) position (kennisgroepstandpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block it, and ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment ([Kennisgroepstandpunt KG:003:2023:8](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/)). FISIN 2025 states "De korting is 26,9%" in its rule text, which is overruled by the statute and by its own example (see section 7).

### 3.24 Loss offset period (previous year and 6 following years)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose ([art. 4.49 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The loss is set by decision together with the assessment, shown separately on the assessment, and is open to objection. For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (to be offset over a longer period, in the coming 9 years) ([Belastingdienst, verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1)). The art. 4.49 text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### 3.25 Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate (marktrente) for 2025 (2,602%)

The old-age obligation (ODV, oudedagsverplichting) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation, art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, being the average of the monthly U-returns of the preceding year ([art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the rate is 2,602% ([Stcrt. 2025 publication](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html)). The ODV can be used on a tax-neutral basis (geruisloos) for an annuity (lijfrente), annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years ([art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) and at the latest two months after it. An earlier start lengthens the twenty years. On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs. Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5, added by Stb. 2022, 532 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020). A negative market rate must also be applied.

### 3.26 End of pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer) commutation and conversion (omzetting)

The Act on the phasing out of pension in own management and other fiscal pension measures (wet uitfasering pensioen in eigen beheer en overige fiscale pensioenmaatregelen), Stb. 2017, 115, ends the old routes ([Stb. 2017, 115](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html)). Art. 38n lid 1 keeps the old pension rules as at 31 December 2016 for a pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB). Art. 38n lid 2 allowed the waiving (prijsgeven) of the part of the claim above the tax balance sheet value (fiscale balanswaarde) on the condition that the claim was at the same moment wholly commuted (afgekocht) or converted on a tax-neutral basis into an ODV, lid 4 required the written consent of the partner, and art. 38o set the commutation tax at 34,5% of the tax balance sheet value minus, 25% from 1 January 2018 and 19,5% from 1 January 2019. Artikel VI of Stb. 2017, 115 makes art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapse from 1 January 2020, and the 2020 Wet LB text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (lapsed as of 01-01-2020) ([art. 38o Wet LB 1964, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen) states that commutation or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019" (from 1 April 2017 up to and including 31 December 2019), and its V&A 17-007 is marked lapsed (vervallen) ([CAP, V&A 17-007](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/)). On the start date, the consolidated text on wetten.overheid.nl (BWBR0039380) shows "Geldend van 01-04-2017" with listed parts retroactive to 1 January 2017, and the leniency (coulance) decision of 22 March 2017 (Stcrt. 2017, 18118) let DGAs (director and substantial shareholders) adjust "uiterlijk tot en met 30 juni 2017" and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which explains the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration phrase "Sinds 1 juli 2017 kunt u niet langer pensioen in eigen beheer opbouwen" ([BWBR0039380](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01), [Stcrt. 2017, 18118](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html), [Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). A remaining PEB keeps the old rules. If the scheme provided for indexation the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten" (if the private limited company (BV) does not index annually, we can see this as a form of giving up the rights), with possible fiscal consequences ([Belastingdienst, uitfaseren pensioen in eigen beheer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/)). Art. 10a.18 lid 3: the acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2. Art. 10a.18 lid 2: in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p. PEB payouts are income from former employment in box 1 ([art. 10a.18 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.27 Non-residents (art. 7.5)

A non-resident declares only a substantial interest in a Netherlands company, not part of an enterprise, and art. 7.5 confirms that base ([art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The statement in the research that a 2025 decision for partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht, the 30%-rule ruling) has the same effect was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages and stays UNVERIFIED (see section 7).
