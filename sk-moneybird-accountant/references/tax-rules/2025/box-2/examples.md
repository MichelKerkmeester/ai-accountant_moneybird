## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### 4.1 Dividend inside bracket 1

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, dga) receives a dividend of € 60.000 from his private limited company (BV) in 2025 and has no other box 2 income. The whole amount falls in bracket 1.

- Step 1: box 2 income is € 60.000.
- Step 2: € 60.000 is below the bracket boundary of € 67.804, so only 24,5% applies.
- Step 3: 60.000 × 0,245 = € 14.700,00.

Box 2 tax: € 14.700,00. Source for the rates: [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.2 Dividend across both brackets

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A dividend of € 100.000 in 2025.

- Step 1: bracket 1 covers € 67.804. Tax at the top of bracket 1 is € 16.611 ([art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Multiplying € 67.804 by 24,5% gives € 16.611,98, rounded down to € 16.611. The statute prints € 16.611.
- Step 2: the excess is 100.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 32.196,00.
- Step 3: 32.196 × 0,31 = € 9.980,76.
- Step 4: 16.611,00 + 9.980,76 = € 26.591,76.

Box 2 tax: € 26.591,76.

### 4.3 Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld

`Official example` from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on income from a substantial interest (inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang), with the subtraction written out.

Gross dividend € 10.000, 15% dividend tax withheld, so € 8.500 is received net.

- Step 1: withholding, 10.000 × 0,15 = € 1.500,00.
- Step 2: box 2 tax on the gross dividend, 10.000 × 0,245 = € 2.450,00.
- Step 3: credit, 2.450,00 − 1.500,00 = € 950,00.

The dividend tax is not deducted but credited as prepayment (voorheffing). Source: [Belastingdienst, inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.4 Fiscal partners split the income (50/50 against 100/0)

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

Box 2 income of € 120.000, fiscal partners for the whole year.

Split 50/50:

- Step 1: 120.000 / 2 = € 60.000,00 per partner.
- Step 2: each amount is below € 67.804, so 24,5% applies to each share.
- Step 3: 60.000 × 0,245 = € 14.700,00 per partner.
- Step 4: 14.700,00 × 2 = € 29.400,00 together.

One partner takes all € 120.000:

- Step 1: bracket 1 tax, € 16.611,00.
- Step 2: excess, 120.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 52.196,00.
- Step 3: 52.196 × 0,31 = € 16.180,76.
- Step 4: 16.611,00 + 16.180,76 = € 32.791,76.

The difference follows from the rule that art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each partner's own bracket 1 applies to his share ([FISIN 2026, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang), [art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 4.5 Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) for an exempt investment institution (vbi, vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or foreign investment company

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

Shares in an exempt investment institution with a value of € 200.000 at the start of 2025.

- Step 1: 200.000 × 0,0588 = € 11.760,00 flat-rate benefit.
- Step 2: other regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) from those shares of € 3.000 reduce it, 11.760,00 − 3.000,00 = € 8.760,00.
- Step 3: the outcome is never below nil, and here it is positive.

Source: [art. 4.14 lid 1 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.6 Excessive borrowing (excessief lenen), deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel)

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

On 31 December 2025 the taxpayer and his partner together have € 600.000 of debts to the private limited company. No amounts were taxed earlier, so the maximum is € 500.000.

- Step 1: debts above the maximum, 600.000,00 − 500.000,00 = € 100.000,00 deemed regular benefit.
- Step 2: bracket 1 tax, € 16.611,00 on the first € 67.804.
- Step 3: excess, 100.000,00 − 67.804,00 = € 32.196,00.
- Step 4: 32.196 × 0,31 = € 9.980,76.
- Step 5: 16.611,00 + 9.980,76 = € 26.591,76 box 2 tax.

The loan itself stays in full and the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan ([Belastingdienst, excessief lenen van bv beperkt](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt), [art. 4.14a Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 4.7 Loss converted into a tax credit (belastingkorting)

`Official example` from FISIN 2025.

A loss of € 20.000 arose in 2023. The tax credit in 2025 is € 4.900 and the remaining loss can be offset through 2032.

- Step 1: the conversion percentage for 2025 is 24,5%.
- Step 2: 20.000 × 0,245 = € 4.900,00.
- Step 3: the loss year 2023 plus 9 gives the last year, 2032.

Source: [FISIN 2025, aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.8 Credit on a larger unoffset loss

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

An unoffset loss of € 40.000 is converted on request in a year in which the taxpayer and his partner had no substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), and also had none in the preceding year.

- Step 1: the rate is 24,5%.
- Step 2: 40.000 × 0,245 = € 9.800,00 tax credit.

The credit is set by decision (beschikking) and reduces box 1 tax of the conversion year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Source: [art. 4.53 Wet IB 2001, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01).

### 4.9 Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon)

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

A private limited company in which the director and substantial shareholder holds 100% of the shares pays a salary. The most comparable employment pays € 60.000, the highest employee salary (loon) in the private limited company and connected bodies is € 45.000, and the fixed amount is € 56.000.

- Step 1: (a) comparable salary € 60.000 at 100% = € 60.000,00.
- Step 2: (b) highest employee salary € 45.000,00.
- Step 3: (c) fixed amount € 56.000,00.
- Step 4: the highest of the three is € 60.000,00, so the salary is at least € 60.000,00.

If the total salary for the work for the private limited company and all connected bodies is not more than € 5.000, the rule does not apply at all, for example a total of € 4.000 for one company. The salary is box 1 income from employment, not a box 2 benefit (voordeel). Sources: [art. 12a Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst, loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### 4.10 Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate (marktrente)

`Calculated example` from the section 2 figures.

An ODV of € 100.000 at the start of 2025, with the 2025 market rate of 2,602%.

- Step 1: 100.000 × 0,02602 = € 2.602,00 increase.
- Step 2: 100.000,00 + 2.602,00 = € 102.602,00 balance after the increase.

A negative market rate must also be applied, which would lower the balance. Source: [Stcrt. 2025 publication](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html), [art. 38p Wet LB 1964, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01).
