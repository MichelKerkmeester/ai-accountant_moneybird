## 5. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Generieke renteaftrekbeperking: the art. 15b percentage goes from 20% in 2024 to 24,5% in 2025 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025)). The threshold of € 1.000.000 stays the same.
- Zvw: the bijdrage goes from 5,32% in 2024 to 5,26% in 2025, the maximum bijdrage-inkomen from € 71.628 to € 75.864, and the werkgeversheffing from 6,57% to 6,51% ([FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).
- Box 2: the first bracket goes from € 67.000 at 24,5% to € 67.804 at 24,5%, the column III amount from € 16.415 to € 16.611, and the rate above the bracket from 33% to 31% ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Start-up relief: in 2024 only the 2022 starters could still continue, in the last year of the facility. From 2025 the transitional right has lapsed for all starters ([loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).
- Besluit geruisloze omzetting: the besluit of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M is replaced by the Besluit of 24 October 2025, published in Staatscourant 2025, 37092 on 4 November 2025, in force the day after publication. The named changes include 2.2.2.a, 3.4, 4.2.2, 6.1, 8 with the Zvw bijdrage credit, 12 with the new eighth condition for a shareholder resident abroad, 13.1.2, 13.2 and 15.3 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).
- The Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025 of 7 October 2025, Staatscourant 2025, 34752, replaces the 2022 decision ([Stcrt. 2025, 34752](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)).
- The Innovatieboxbesluit 2025 is geldend van 25-07-2025 ([BWBR0051317](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/)).
- The Beleidsbesluit belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Staatscourant 2024, 38545, published 10 December 2024, enters into force on 1 July 2025 ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).
- From boekjaar 2025 all legal persons file the jaarrekening through SBR, with filing from 1 January 2026 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).
- Unchanged from 2024 to 2025: the Vpb rates of 19% and € 38.000 plus 25,8%, the verliesverrekening rule, the gebruikelijk loon normbedrag of € 56.000, the € 5.000 floor, the 100% comparable wage, dividendbelasting of 15%, conditionele bronbelasting of 25,8%, the deelneming threshold of 5%, the fiscale eenheid bezitseis of 95%, the innovatiebox 9/H and the € 25.000 forfait, the stakingsaftrek of € 3.630 and the jaarrekening size thresholds ([Wet Vpb 1969 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01), [Wet LB 1964 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01), [Wet DB 1965 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01), [Wet IB 2001 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01) and [2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html)).

Corrections carried into this 2025 file, not changes of law:

- Dividend stripping: the article is art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not art. 4 lid 3 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).
- Zvw route for an uninsured DGA: the BV withholds the bijdrage on net wage in the Handboek Loonheffingen 2025, paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1, and the DGA does not pay it by aanslag ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)).
- Besluit geruisloze omzetting citation: Staatscourant 2025, 37092, not Staatscourant 2025, 3709 ([Stcrt. 2025, 37092](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).
- In-force date of the btw-eenheid beleidsbesluit: 1 July 2025, as the besluit itself states ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)).
- Large class turnover bound: € 50 miljoen per Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397, while the KVK table prints more than € 51 mln ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Aangifte Vpb: a book year equal to the calendar year is filed before 1 June of the next year, a broken book year within 5 months after its end, and a short book year ending 31 December before 1 June or ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)). The online extension runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June, and a paper request within 5 months after the book year ends with reasons is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months ([uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)).
- Loonadministratie: the BV runs the DGA wage through its loonadministratie and withholds loonheffing. The wage is at least € 56.000 or the higher of the comparable wage and the highest wage in connected bodies, the test wage includes the car bijtelling, and for an uninsured DGA the BV withholds 5,26% Zvw on net wage up to € 75.864 and pays it through the aangifte loonheffingen ([Handboek 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Wet LB 1964 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01)).
- Dividend: record the dividendnota once the opbrengst is paid, credited, settled or handed over, withhold 15%, and file and pay within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A deelnemingsdividend of 5% or more to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt and needs no return unless the Belastingdienst invites one. A kwalificatiebeschikking needs a return. The shareholder credits the withheld tax in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting or, for a body, in the aangifte Vpb ([aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen), [als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)).
- Conditionele bronbelasting: withhold 25,8% on interest, royalties and dividends to a related body in a low-tax jurisdiction, and credit any dividendbelasting withheld on the same payment ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/), [Wet bronbelasting 2021, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)).
- Jaarrekening: deposit within eight days after vaststelling and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends. If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, deposit the unadopted accounts marked as unadopted. There is no extension, late filing is a criminal offence, and from boekjaar 2025 all legal persons file through SBR ([Boek 2 BW 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).
- Records: lichamen are administratieplichtig and keep their records for seven years, and data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement ([AWR 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). Records on onroerende zaken are kept for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten, and OSS records for ten years ([Wet OB 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).
- Fiscale eenheid: the daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another ([fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)).
- Switch to a BV: request at the office that handles the inkomstenbelasting, register the voorovereenkomst or intentieverklaring with the Geleideformulier, and note that the BV is a bv in oprichting until the notarial deed. The new legal form may get a new btw-identificatienummer, which goes to EU suppliers at once, and payroll gets a new loonheffingennummer if there are employees ([rechtsvorm wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).
- Innovatiebox: a smaller taxpayer needs an S&O-verklaring, and the K/T calculation uses the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, so the qualifying expenditure must be recorded ([innovatiebox](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox), [Wet Vpb 1969 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Koepelvrijstelling and BUA: Status unknown and Check UNVERIFIED in section 2. The beleidsbesluit on belastingplicht en fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting (Stcrt. 2024, 38545) does not cover them, and the Wet OB 1968 article and the BUA were not opened ([Stcrt. 2024, 38545](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). Reading the Wet OB 1968 article on the koepelvrijstelling and the BUA would settle it.
- Conflict on the large class turnover bound: the KVK table prints more than € 51 mln ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)), while Stb. 2024, 52 art. 3 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 print € 50 miljoen ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). Settled at € 50 miljoen.
- Conflict on the micro thresholds: Stb. 2024, 52 art. 1 raises art. 395a to € 450.000 and € 900.000, and KVK applies these ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)), while the consolidated Boek 2 BW at 2025-01-01 and at 2026-07-01 still prints € 350.000 and € 700.000 in art. 395a and prints the raised amounts in art. 396 and 397 ([Boek 2 BW 2025-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [Boek 2 BW 2026-07-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01)). The Nota van toelichting says the AMvB basis for art. 395a was missing and was to be added to art. 2:398 lid 4, which the 2026-07-01 text now does. The value used in section 2 is € 450.000 and € 900.000. A KVK or Justitie statement on the legal effect for the book year would close it.
- The 2025 list of open points names no other unknown, proposed or UNVERIFIED value for the year.
