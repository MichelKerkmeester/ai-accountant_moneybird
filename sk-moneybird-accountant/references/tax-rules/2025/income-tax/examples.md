## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Box 1 below state pension (AOW) age

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) is € 60.000, before rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and tax credits (heffingskortingen).

1. First bracket (eerste schijf): € 38.441 × 35,82% = € 13.769,57.
2. Income in bracket 2: € 60.000 − € 38.441 = € 21.559.
3. Tax on bracket 2: € 21.559 × 37,48% = € 8.080,31.
4. Total at the published bracket rates: € 13.769,57 + € 8.080,31 = € 21.849,88.

This illustrates the published combined rates. It does not calculate rate adjustment or tax credits. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

### Box 1 for whole year at state pension age

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income is € 20.000 and the taxpayer is at state pension age for the whole year, born on or after 1 January 1946.

1. € 20.000 is within the first bracket, which ends at € 38.441.
2. € 20.000 × 17,92% = € 3.584.

For a person born before 1 January 1946, the first bracket instead ends at € 40.502. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10a.)

### First-bracket rate in the year state pension age is reached

**Calculated example.** Assume state pension age is reached in July and taxable income is € 20.000.

1. The July first-bracket rate is 26,87%.
2. € 20.000 × 26,87% = € 5.374.

This is a bracket-rate illustration before credits and any applicable rate adjustment. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening.)

### National insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling and rate

**Calculated example.** Assume contribution income (premie-inkomen) is € 20.000 and is below the ceiling.

1. € 20.000 × 27,65% = € 5.530.

The published maximum premium at the 2025 ceiling is € 10.628. This example does not determine personal insurance status. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Rate adjustment

**Calculated example.** Assume taxable income after relevant deductions is € 80.000 and those deductions total € 10.000.

1. Income before those deductions: € 80.000 + € 10.000 = € 90.000.
2. Amount above the top bracket boundary: € 90.000 − € 76.817 = € 13.183.
3. Rate adjustment on that excess: € 13.183 × 12,02% = € 1.584,60.
4. Maximum adjustment based on the deductions: € 10.000 × 12,02% = € 1.202.
5. The adjustment is capped at the lower amount, so it is € 1.202.

(Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2.)

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume the taxpayer is below state pension age and has aggregate income (verzamelinkomen) of € 50.000.

1. Income above the phase-out point (afbouwpunt): € 50.000 − € 28.406 = € 21.594.
2. Phase-out: € 21.594 × 6,337% = € 1.368,41.
3. AHK after phase-out: € 3.068 − € 1.368,41 = € 1.699,59.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)

**Calculated example, whole year at state pension age.** Assume aggregate income is € 40.000.

1. Income above the phase-out point: € 40.000 − € 28.406 = € 11.594.
2. Phase-out: € 11.594 × 3,170% = € 367,53.
3. AHK after phase-out: € 1.536 − € 367,53 = € 1.168,47.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume earned income (arbeidsinkomen) is € 60.000 and the taxpayer is below state pension age.

1. This is in the fourth band.
2. Income above the start of that band: € 60.000 − € 43.071 = € 16.929.
3. Phase-out: € 16.929 × 6,510% = € 1.102,08.
4. AK: € 5.599 − € 1.102,08 = € 4.496,92.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

**Calculated example, whole year at state pension age.** At the same earned income of € 60.000:

1. Income above € 43.071: € 60.000 − € 43.071 = € 16.929.
2. Phase-out: € 16.929 × 3,257% = € 551,38.
3. AK: € 2.802 − € 551,38 = € 2.250,62.

(Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting)

**Calculated example.** Assume earned income is € 20.000 and the taxpayer meets the income-related combination tax credit (IACK) conditions.

1. Income above the threshold: € 20.000 − € 6.145 = € 13.855.
2. IACK at 11,45%: € 13.855 × 11,45% = € 1.586,40.
3. This is below the maximum of € 2.986, so the calculated credit is € 1.586,40.

For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year, the same income above the threshold gives € 13.855 × 5,73% = € 793,89, below the maximum of € 1.495. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Payout of unused tax credit (heffingskorting)

**Official example.** The source gives an example of a taxpayer born on 20 December 1962 with no income, for whom the full AHK of € 3.068 is paid out. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Tax credit tax portion (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen)

**Official example.** For an AK of € 184 with one third of the year subject to premium liability, the source gives a tax portion of € 42 and a contribution part (premiedeel) of € 143. One third of € 143 is € 48. The total is € 42 + € 48 = € 90. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) and taxable income

**Calculated example.** Assume business profit is € 60.000, the taxpayer qualifies for the stated self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), and no other entrepreneur deductions apply.

1. Business profit: € 60.000.
2. Subtract self-employed person's deduction: € 60.000 − € 2.470 = € 57.530.
3. SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling): € 57.530 × 12,7% = € 7.306,31.
4. Taxable business profit: € 57.530 − € 7.306,31 = € 50.223,69.
5. If there are no other items of income from work and home or losses to set off, taxable income from work and home is € 50.223,69 before any further box 1 calculation.

(Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.)
