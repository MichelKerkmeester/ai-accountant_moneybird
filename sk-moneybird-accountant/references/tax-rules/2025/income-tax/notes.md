## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- The published box 1 table for taxpayers below state pension (AOW) age changed from 36,97% up to € 75.518 and 49,50% above, to three brackets: 35,82% up to € 38.441, 37,48% to € 76.817, and 49,50% above. The top rate remained 49,50%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)
- For taxpayers at state pension age for the whole year, the first rate changed from 19,07% to 17,92%. The second rate changed from 36,97% to 37,48%, and its upper boundary changed from € 75.518 to € 76.817. The first-bracket boundary changed from € 38.098 to € 38.441 for people born on or after 1 January 1946, and from € 40.021 to € 40.502 for those born before that date. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)
- The first-bracket rate in the state pension transition year changed month by month. January 19,07% to 17,92%. February 20,56% to 19,41%. March 22,05% to 20,90%. April 23,54% to 22,39%. May 25,03% to 23,88%. June 26,52% to 25,37%. July 28,02% to 26,87%. August 29,51% to 28,36%. September 31,00% to 29,85%. October 32,49% to 31,34%. November 33,99% to 32,83%. December 35,47% to 34,32%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening.)
- The standard statutory fixed amounts changed from € 3.550 and € 17.384 to € 3.140 and € 17.523. For people born before 1 January 1946, they changed from € 3.729 and € 16.852 to € 3.309 and € 16.919. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)
- The national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) ceiling changed from € 38.098 to € 38.441, and the maximum premium changed from € 10.534 to € 10.628. The rate remained 27,65%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) changed from 12,53% to 12,02%. The top boundary used for the adjustment changed from € 75.518 to € 76.817, and the stated maximum deduction value changed from 36,97% to 37,48%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)
- The AHK for taxpayers at state pension age for the whole year changed from € 1.735 to € 1.536. Its phase-out point changed from € 24.812 to € 28.406, its phase-out rate from 3,421% to 3,170%, and its nil point from € 75.519 to € 76.818. The 2024 rate and nil point have source differences described in section 7. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen.)
- From 2025, the AHK phase-out base is aggregate income (verzamelinkomen). In 2024, the base was taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning). (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025.)

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Keep the business records needed to establish profit and identify exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) and applicable entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek). Use the resulting taxable business profit in the box 1 calculation, where it forms part of income from work and home (inkomen uit werk en woning). The checked material describes the calculation but does not state a record-retention period or an income tax (inkomstenbelasting) filing deadline (aangiftetermijn). (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.)

The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) is applied automatically in the return and may also be applied in payroll. The employment tax credit (arbeidskorting, AK) can be affected by an amount already applied by an employer. The income-related combination tax credit (IACK, inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting) is claimed in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not in payroll. In the year state pension (AOW) age is reached, use the 2025 provisional assessment form for the AHK, AK and IACK. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2025.)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **IACK co-parent day test, unresolved for 2025.** The conditions page at https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting says at least 156 days per calendar year in each household, prorated where co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year. The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen says at least 78 days in a period of 6 months in a repeating pattern with each parent. Both are marked enacted and Verified in the checked material. The checked material does not settle how the tests apply to shorter or irregular periods. The current statutory text or a clarifying primary-source statement from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) would settle the wording.
- **State pension (AOW) transition-year bracket 2 conflict, resolved in favor of 37,48%.** The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening returned 36,97% for bracket 2 in the transition-year table. The pages https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 give 37,48%, which governs.
- **AHK nil point wording, resolved as one rule.** The page https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025 says nil from € 76.818, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen says nil from € 76.817. The checked material resolves this as the same rule: the phase-out ends at the top bracket boundary and the credit is nil for higher income.
- **Prior-year AHK state pension phase-out rate used for comparison.** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2024 gives 3,421% for 2024, while https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives 3,420%. The checked material does not resolve this conflict. The figures in section 5 use the year table value.
- **Prior-year IACK maximum point used for comparison.** https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2024 shows the maximum of € 2.950 from € 31.839. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/heffingskortingen gives € 31.837. The checked material does not settle the exact point.
- **Insurance components at state pension age.** The checked material states that the state pension premium stops at state pension age. Whether the Anw and Wlz premiums continue is an inference from the published rates at state pension age, not a stated rule. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1.)
- **Order of the final box 1 steps.** The checked material verifies the box 1 balance, rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing), and tax credits (heffingskortingen), but does not print the full sequence as one statement. The sequence described in section 3 is an inference from the article structure. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)
