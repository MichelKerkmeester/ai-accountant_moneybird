## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below state pension (AOW) age

The published rate includes the income-tax part and, up to the premium ceiling, the national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen). The income-tax part of the first bracket is 8,17%, and the premium rate is 27,65%, giving the published combined rate of 35,82%. The next brackets are 37,48% and 49,50%. The statutory fixed amounts are listed in the figures table. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

The rate applies to taxable income from work and home. The premium component is charged only on contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to the stated ceiling. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Box 1 for whole year at state pension age, born on or after 1 January 1946

For this group, the first bracket ends at € 38.441 and has a published rate of 17,92%. The other published rates and the bracket boundary are the same as for the table for taxpayers below state pension age. The lower first-bracket rate reflects that the state pension premium stops when state pension age is reached. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen.)

### Box 1 for whole year at state pension age, born before 1 January 1946

For this group, the first bracket ends at € 40.502. The remaining bracket boundary and rates follow the figures table. Wet IB 2001 article 2.10a provides the higher first boundary for this birth group. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10a.)

### First-bracket rate in the year state pension age is reached

The first-bracket rate depends on the month in which state pension age is reached. Use the month-by-month rate in the figures table for that year. The rates in brackets 2 and 3 remain the normal rates. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/belastingberekening.)

### National insurance contributions ceiling and rate

The premium is charged on contribution income up to the ceiling in the figures table. Its components are state pension, Anw and Wlz. The source states that the state pension premium stops at state pension age. The precise treatment of the Anw and Wlz components after state pension age is noted in section 7. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/premie_volksverzekeringen.)

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) applies where deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten) have been deducted and taxable income from work and home plus those deductions exceeds the top bracket boundary. The tax calculated under article 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 12,02% of the amount above that boundary, but the increase cannot exceed 12,02% of the deductions themselves. The resulting maximum value of the deductions is 37,48%. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (aanslag). (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025.)

The relevant deductions include personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), such as maintenance obligations including partner maintenance, weekend expenses for disabled people, deductible gifts, specific healthcare costs and carried-forward personal deductions. They also include deductible owner-occupied home costs, tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) and the exemption for making assets available. Entrepreneur facilities in this rule include self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), research and development deduction, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling). (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.)

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting)

The AHK is granted to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the income tax return and can also be applied by an employer or benefit provider in payroll. In 2025 its phase-out base is aggregate income (verzamelinkomen), rather than only taxable income from work and home. Aggregate income consists of taxable income from work and home, taxable income from a substantial interest and taxable income from savings and investments, less personal deductions that can be set off. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18.)

Below state pension age, the maximum AHK is reduced as aggregate income rises above the stated phase-out point. For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year, the separate amount and phase-out rate in the figures table apply. The ordinary table does not apply in the year state pension age is reached. For that transition year, calculate the amount using the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). A rounding difference of a few euros from the table can occur, and the assessment amount is correct. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting)

The AK is based on earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), earned income includes profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and SME profit exemption. It excludes the portion earned as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) or a profit-sharing creditor (winstdelende schuldeiser). (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The AK builds up in the first three bands and phases out in the fourth band. The amounts for a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year use the separate schedule in the figures table. In the year state pension age is reached, the ordinary AK table does not apply. Use the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in My Tax and Customs Administration. A rounding difference of a few euros from the table can occur, and the assessment amount is correct. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

From 2024, where an employer applied a higher AK and earned income is not above the third-band boundary, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) takes over the employer's amount up to the applicable maximum. The 2025 limit and maximum are in the figures table. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2025.)

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting)

The IACK is calculated over earned income above the threshold in the figures table, up to its maximum. The rate and maximum for taxpayers at state pension age for the whole year are lower. The IACK is claimed in the income tax return or through a provisional assessment. It is not applied in payroll. In the year state pension age is reached, use the form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2025 in My Tax and Customs Administration. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The child must be under age 12 on 1 January and born after 31 December 2012. The child must belong to the taxpayer's household for at least 6 months. If the taxpayer or child dies before that period is complete, the credit can still apply. Qualifying children include a taxpayer's own, step, adopted, or foster child, a child from a spouse's earlier relationship, or a fiscal partner's child. A foster child must be raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a foster care allowance. Residents of the EU, EEA, Switzerland, or BES can qualify. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

A taxpayer qualifies if there is no fiscal partner, the fiscal partnership lasts 6 months or less, or the taxpayer has lower earned income than the partner. If both incomes are equal, only the older partner qualifies. Co-parenting has two different day-count descriptions in the checked sources. Follow the open point in section 7 until the source wording is settled. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Payout of unused tax credit (heffingskorting)

The payout is limited to the AHK for people born before 1 January 1963. It applies only to the part of the credit that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and premiums. The fiscal partner must owe enough tax, and the couple must have been fiscal partners for more than 6 months. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

The own-income and partner-income limits are in the figures table. The partner limit is higher where the partner is at state pension age. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### Tax credit tax portion (belastingdeel)

Each tax credit has a tax portion (belastingdeel) and premium parts. The 2025 tax portion is 8,17%. For taxpayers below state pension age, the total first-bracket rate is 35,82%. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01.)

### Tax credit national insurance contribution components (premiedelen)

The premium parts are for state pension, Anw and Wlz. A person who is not insured for one of these schemes does not receive that part of the credit. Where someone is liable for premiums for only part of the year, the relevant premium part is time-proportional. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

### SME profit exemption

The SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) is calculated on profit remaining after entrepreneur's allowance. The 2025 rate is in the figures table. A person entitled to a share or lender (geldverstrekker) does not receive this exemption. (Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.)

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

No new additions to the old-age reserve are allowed in 2025. An existing reserve can remain on the balance sheet. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve.)

### Profits from business activities and taxable income

Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) require that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it, and that the taxpayer is liable for its debts. Commercial Register (Handelsregister) registration alone does not decide whether someone is an entrepreneur for income tax. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs factors including profit, independence, capital, time spent, customers, marketing and business risk. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.)

Start with business profit and remove exempt profit components first. These include the forestry exemption, agricultural exemption, the qualifying part of debt forgiveness profit, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, compensation for business termination, Waz benefits, certain forestry and nature subsidies and the stated final levy, mobility project and peak avoidance exemptions. A person entitled to a share or money lender receives neither entrepreneur's allowance nor SME profit exemption. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.)

Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is profit after entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption. The SME profit exemption is calculated after entrepreneur's allowance. Taxable income from work and home includes taxable business profit and is reduced by losses available to be set off. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.)

The resulting box 1 income is subject to the statutory box 1 calculation, the rate adjustment where it applies and the tax credits (heffingskortingen). The complete ordering of those final steps as one calculation is an inference from the statutory article structure and is noted in section 7. (Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 2.10, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/heffingskortingen.)

An income-related Zvw contribution also exists. Its amounts and calculation belong in the Zvw file. (Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.)
