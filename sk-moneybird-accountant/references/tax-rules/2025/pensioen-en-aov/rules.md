## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Jaarruimte percentage

The jaarruimte percentage is 30% for 2025. The law text valid on 1 January 2025 sets this in art. 3.127 lid 1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

A taxpayer with a pensioentekort (pension shortfall) in the previous calendar year may deduct lijfrentepremies up to this percentage of the premiegrondslag (pension contribution base), reduced by the lid 4 reduction for pension accrual. Source: same URL.

The 30% applies from the Wtp (Wet toekomst pensioenen, Future Pensions Act) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2023. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is 30% x (min(income of the previous year, aftoppingsgrens) minus franchise). Source: law 2025 art. 3.127.

The checked material corrects an earlier attribution. Stcrt. 2023, 34571 (Government Gazette) and Stb. 2025, 431 (Bulletin of Acts and Decrees) did not change the 30% itself. Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6 back to 1 January 2023. Stb. 2025, 431 only rewrites the partnerpensioen (partner pension) wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

Art. 10.2b lid 3 sets the jaarruimte percentage each year equal to the art. 18a lid 1 Wet LB 1964 (Wage Tax Act 1964) percentage in the 2024 to 2027 texts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b)

### Maximum jaarruimte

The maximum jaarruimte for 2025 is € 35.798. This is enacted and Verified by the Ministry of Finance table. Source: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/dcbb490f-d406-4842-ac33-b5c397e19df0/file

The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

Premiums above the ruimte are never deductible but are taken into account when the uitkering (benefit) is taxed. Source: same Belastingdienst page and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130)

The maximum follows from the 30% rate and the premiegrondslag rules in this year. The arithmetic is in section 4. Source: law 2025 art. 3.127.

### Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag

The aftoppingsgrens for 2025 is € 137.800. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

Art. 3.127 lid 3 counts the previous calendar year's winst uit onderneming (profit from business) vóór de ondernemersaftrek (before the self-employed deduction), belastbaar loon (taxable wages), belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (taxable result from other activities) and belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen (taxable periodic payments and benefits) up to the eerstvermelde bedrag (aftoppingsgrens) and then reduces by the laatstvermelde bedrag (franchise). Source: law 2025 art. 3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.127)

The aftoppingsgrens was not indexed at the start of 2025. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf

Art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor (contract wage development factor) in the 2024 to 2027 texts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b)

In the 2020 text art. 10.2b lid 2 indexes the aftoppingsgrens with the contractloonontwikkelingsfactor and the franchise follows art. 10.1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 10.2b)

### Franchise premiegrondslag

The franchise for 2025 is € 18.475. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

The franchise is subtracted from the income capped at the aftoppingsgrens. Source: law 2025 art. 3.127.

From 2024 art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the AOW benefit for married persons without toeslag (supplement) plus vakantiebijslag (holiday allowance) x 100/75. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10.2b)

### Look-back window

The look-back window for 2025 is 10 years and covers the jaarruimte of 2015 to 2024. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows unused jaarruimte of the preceding period to be used on request in the aangifte (tax return), oldest unused year first. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

The period is 10 years from the 2023-07-01 text. Before that it was 7 years in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

The Belastingdienst page advises using reserveringsruimte before jaarruimte so older unused ruimte does not lapse. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Reserveringsruimte maximum

The reserveringsruimte maximum for 2025 is € 42.108. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

From the 2023-07-01 text there is one flat euro maximum, no percentage and no age split. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

For 2020 to 2022 the cap was at most 17% of the premiegrondslag and at most a euro amount, raised to a higher euro amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and the FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

The taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age at the start of the year. Source: FISIN 2025.

### Age limit jaarruimte

For 2025 the taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age at the start of the year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

From the Wtp 2023-07-01 text to 2027 the taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age. FISIN 2023 to 2026 say "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd" (the age five years above the state pension age). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

For 2020 to 2022 and the 2023-01-01 text the taxpayer must not yet have reached the AOW age at the start of the calendar year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127

### Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap

The cap for 2025 is € 26.781 per year. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Art. 3.125 lid 1 c requires at least five years of termijnen (instalments), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to the statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. Source: law 2025 art. 3.125 and FISIN 2025.

Above the cap premiums are deductible only if the uitkeringen (benefits) run at least 20 years. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

FISIN 2025 prints "€ 26.7813" in the rekening (account) row. This is a typo. The law states € 26.781. Source: FISIN 2025 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

The three lijfrente forms are lijfrenteverzekering (annuity insurance), lijfrenterekening (annuity account) and lijfrentebeleggingsrecht (annuity investment right). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.124 and 3.125)

For an oudedagslijfrente (old-age annuity) the first termijn must start at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five and payments run until death. For a rekening or beleggingsrecht the termijn must be at least 20 years plus the number of years the uitkering starts before the AOW year. Source: law 2026 art. 3.125.

### Overbruggingslijfrente cap

The cap for 2025 is € 63.288 per year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

The overbruggingslijfrente (bridge annuity) is only for lijfrentetegoeden (annuity balances) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching AOW age or starting a pensioen. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026 and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier a

The tier a maximum for 2025 is € 566.197. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the stakingswinst, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions. The provisions include the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts, and from 2023 the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129)

Tier a applies if the age at staking is at most five years below the AOW age, or the taxpayer is at least 45% arbeidsongeschikt (disabled) with the lijfrente starting within six months, or the staking is by death. Source: law 2026 art. 3.129.

FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder" (61 years and 10 months or older), which matches only 2023. The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd" (at most five years below the state pension age). For 2026 the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" (62 years or older). Source: FISIN 2025, law 2026 art. 3.129 and https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier b

The tier b maximum for 2025 is € 283.110. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Tier b applies if the age at staking is at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or the lijfrente starts immediately. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129)

The same subtraction of already built provisions applies. Source: law 2026 art. 3.129.

### Stakingswinst maximum, tier c

The tier c maximum for 2025 is € 141.564. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Tier c applies in other cases. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.129)

The payment deadline for a stakingswinst conversion is before 1 July 2026. Source: FISIN 2025.

### Oudedagsreserve from 2023

From 2023 no toevoeging (addition) to the oudedagsreserve is possible. An existing FOR stays under the old rules under art. 10a.29 lid 1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

Access to a FOR toevoeging required the urencriterium (hours criterion) and not yet AOW age at the start of the year under art. 3.67. Art. 3.68 gave a toevoeging of 9,44% of the winst (profit), capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the winst, and not above the growth of ondernemingsvermogen (business assets). Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 (art. 3.67, 3.68)

Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows afname (decrease) by a chosen amount up to the lijfrente premiums taken into account, or by the excess over ondernemingsvermogen at staking, AOW age at the start of the year, or failing the urencriterium this year and last year. Source: law 2022 art. 3.70.

The FOR-lijfrente conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023. Source: law 2022 art. 3.128 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.29)

Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 add the transition for the jaarruimte. For a taxpayer with a FOR the premiegrondslag uses the winst vóór afneming of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, vóór toevoeging and vóór ondernemersaftrek. For 2023 and 2024 the jaarruimte is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's toevoeging over the year-end afneming. Source: law 2024 art. 10a.29. These provisions applied for the last time in 2024.

On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues and takes it over on request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve

### Afkoop kleine lijfrente

The small lijfrente afkoop (commutation) amount for 2025 is € 5.429. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Afkoop of a small lijfrente in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount under art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d. Source: art. 3.133 per year and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

If a lijfrente stops meeting the conditions (gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, afkoop, deblokkering (unblocking)) the value is taxed and in most cases revisierente (revisionary interest) is due. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

### Afkoop cap bij langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid

The cap for 2025 is € 50.664. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

Art. 3.133 lid 9: afkoop is not a prohibited act when the verzekeringnemer (policyholder) or gerechtigde (beneficiary) is langdurig arbeidsongeschikt under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the afkoop received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average premiegrondslag plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The afkoop then counts as a lijfrente termijn. Source: art. 3.133 per year and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01

### AOV premium with periodic uitkeringen

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen ter zake van invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval for the taxpayer uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen (expenses for income provisions), with no ruimte cap in the article. FISIN says "volledig aftrekken" (fully deductible). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 (art. 3.124)

Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for loonheffing (payroll tax), mandatory Ziektewet (Sickness Benefits Act) and WIA (Work and Income according to Labour Capacity Act) premiums, policies paying a lump sum such as kapitaalverzekeringen (capital insurance policies), and Zvw (Health Insurance Act) premiums. Source: FISIN 2025. The four exclusions and section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

As ondernemer the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers

Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" (income from previous employment) with loonbelasting withheld as voorheffing (withholding tax). Source: same Belastingdienst AOV page.

A mandatory beroepspensioen (occupational pension) or bedrijfstakpensioen (sector pension) premium is not a lijfrentepremie. It is deductible as a business cost of the onderneming with a restriction. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### AOV premium with lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

Rule of thumb on the page: premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. Source: Belastingdienst AOV page.

### BAZ premium, Raad van State version

The BAZ is the Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen. In the Raad van State (Council of State) version the premium is 5,4% of winst, at most € 171 gross per month. Status proposed, Verified. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state

The BAZ design in that version: every zelfstandige up to AOW age insured, benefit at most the minimumloon (minimum wage), private policy as alternative with a yearly switch moment at the end of the calendar year, no premium for someone whose WIA rights already reach minimumloon level. Source: same URL.

This is proposed only. No enacted BAZ law is in the checked material. Source: same URL.

### BAZ wachttijd, Raad van State version

In the Raad van State version the wachttijd (waiting period) is 2 years. Status proposed. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state

The value is proposed only. Later material for the 2026 bill states a wachttijd of 104 weken in bill art. 16. The docket summary still says "ongeveer één jaar" (about one year). See section 7. Source: [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html) and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912

### BAZ consultation responses

The Raad van State version page reports 2260 consultation responses. Status proposed, Verified. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/09/12/wetsvoorstel-voor-basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-voor-zelfstandigen-naar-de-raad-van-state

UWV (Employee Insurance Agency) and Belastingdienst were critical, and later considered the bill uitvoerbaar (executable) under conditions. Source: same URL.

### Payment deadline for FOR or stakingswinst conversion

For 2025 the payment deadline for a FOR or stakingswinst conversion is before 1 July 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

Premiums count in the year they are paid or verrekend (settled) under art. 3.130 lid 1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.130)

Premiums for the stakingswinst conversion (art. 3.129), the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end under art. 3.130 lid 2. From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14. Source: law 2026 art. 3.130 and art. 10a.29.

The maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies

### Conversiedeadline einddatum 2024

An einddatum (end date) 2024 means the lijfrente must be converted or started by 31 December 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

A lijfrente that reaches its ingangsdatum (FISIN 2023 onward: einddatum) must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

Extension on request in bijzondere omstandigheden is possible. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

### Conversiedeadline einddatum 2025

An einddatum 2025 means the lijfrente must be converted or started by 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

The same full calendar year rule applies. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

### Nabestaandenlijfrente deadline after death in 2024

A death in 2024 means the nabestaandenlijfrente (survivor annuity) must be converted or started by 31 December 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen

Nabestaandenlijfrente: 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2023 onward says "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren" (the year of death plus 2 calendar years), same outcome. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

Extension on request in bijzondere omstandigheden is possible. Source: FISIN chapters 2020 to 2026.

For blood or in-law relatives other than the partner the nabestaandenlijfrente ends at death or at the latest at age 30. For a rekening or beleggingsrecht it runs at least 20 years or for children under 30 at least 5 years. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.125)

A lijfrente for a meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind (adult disabled child or grandchild) is deductible without pensioentekort under art. 3.124 lid 1 b. Source: law 2026 art. 3.124.

### Proef zelfstandigen in sectoraal pensioenfonds

A self-employed person may join the pensioenfonds (pension fund) of the branch if the fund allows it. Premiums are then deductible. The proef (pilot) runs until 1 July 2028. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij

Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimenteerbepaling (experimental provision) zelfstandigen pension schemes inside the pension definitions. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 (art. 10a.26)
