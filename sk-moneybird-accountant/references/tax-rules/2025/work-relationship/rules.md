## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Wet DBA

The Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties is the Wet van 3 februari 2016. It entered into force on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (Verklaring arbeidsrelatie, the old advance declaration about the status of a work relationship). Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The act governs how a work relationship is assessed, and for 2025 the enforcement of that assessment changed because the moratorium ended, see the later subsections. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Loondienst test

Loondienst (employment) has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid, and pay for the work. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Same source. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer (entrepreneur) for income tax or VAT. Same source.

### Fictieve dienstbetrekkingen and the opt-out

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen (deemed employments), among them aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden (usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage), stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefit), WIA (disability benefit for work and income) and WW (unemployment benefit). Same source.

### Stb. 2016, 165

Correction to a common mistake: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. Source: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).

### Case law: HR 6 November 2020 and HR 24 March 2023

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of partijbedoeling (the intention of the parties). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), works with an omstandighedencatalogus (catalogue of circumstances) that names inbedding (embedding in the organisation) and ondernemerschap (entrepreneurship), and vrije vervanging (free replacement of the worker) is not an absolute element. Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### HR Uber ruling

In the Uber case (prejudiciële vragen, preliminary questions) the Hoge Raad held that there is no rangorde (ranking) between the gezichtspunten (points of view), so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, 27 March 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### End of the handhavingsmoratorium

Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing: reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte (payroll tax return), usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Same source.

### How far back corrections go

Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). This is the ingroeimodel that runs to 2030.

### DBA boetebeleid and zachte landing 2025

For 2025 there are no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties, and for correcties over 2025 this stays so. The basis is motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The zachte landing in 2025 consisted of the ingroeimodel, the supporting measures of the Kamerbrief of 6 September 2024 and the adopted moties of 1 October 2024. Under that letter there were no vergrijpboetes for 2025 for parties demonstrably working on correct qualification, then extended by motie Flach/Aartsen to no verzuim- and vergrijpboetes at all. Same source. After motie Aartsen (31 311, nr. 264) risk-based handhaving continues so problem cases can get naheffingen. Same source. Forward path: for 2026 there are no verzuimboetes and vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026, and on 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). This is enforcement policy, not statute.

### Enforcement practice 2025

In 2025 a control in principle starts with a bedrijfsbezoek (business visit), possibly with a waarschuwing (warning) that is not an official instrument like the aanwijzing. A boekenonderzoek (full audit) remains possible with large risks. Capacity in 2025 was 80 fte (full-time equivalents), with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. NLA signals are expected to be picked up in 2025 through the GKT-meldingenproces. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Vooroverleg

The Belastingdienst takes a standpoint on a complete verzoek vooroverleg (advance consultation request). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn (target term) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Regular handhaving per the 2025 plan

The tranche 2025 plan stated regular handhaving from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Doorwerking naar IH en OB

Richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB (guidelines on the knock-on effect to income tax and VAT) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the loonheffingen at the opdrachtgever. After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie (qualification at the source). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid (false certainty) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging

The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon, or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months, ended on 10 July 2021, and the online pilot environment stayed active, with no rechtskracht (legal force) during or after the pilot. Sources: [Ondernemersplein, webmodule beoordeling arbeidsrelatie](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring (client statement) as one outcome. Same sources.

### VBAR bill ingediend

The internet consultation of the original VBAR ran from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025. The bill went to the Raad van State on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and on 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer, with the bill documents dated 9 July 2025. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The bill was not in force in tax year 2025. The later path: the nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part, the Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Eerste Kamer adopted it on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan (the sitting-and-standing vote), and it became the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR presumption in art. 7:610aa BW

New art. 7:610aa BW reads as follows. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the article is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). The presumption does not apply in tax year 2025 because the law was not in force yet, see the subsection on entry into force.

### VBAR effect and evaluation

The effect is that the werkende (the working person) can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 provides that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR amount at peildatum 1 January 2025

The memorie van toelichting explains the amount as follows: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag for AOV (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, disability insurance) and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Source: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Same source. The arithmetic is written out in section 4.

### WML per hour at 1 January 2025

The WML was € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025).

### VBAR amount at peildatum 1 January 2026

The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount follows the WML percentage from the € 35,43. The Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state € 38 for peildatum 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [rijksoverheid.nl, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers) and [Ondernemersplein, laag uurtarief van zzper](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). The arithmetic is written out in section 4.

### WML per hour at 1 January 2026

The WML was € 14,71 per hour at 1 January 2026. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

### VBAR entry into force

The law entered into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. Source: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). For tax year 2025 this means the presumption was not yet part of the law, and the ordinary assessment of the work relationship applies.

### BAZ bill 36 912

The BAZ bill was ingediend on 24 March 2026. A technische briefing followed on 13 May 2026, the inbreng verslag was delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026. On 2026-10-11 the bill was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote. Source: [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Same source. Because the bill is proposed, nothing follows from it for 2025.
