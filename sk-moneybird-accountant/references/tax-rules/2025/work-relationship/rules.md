## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties)

The Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) is the Act of 3 February 2016. It entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 May 2016 and replaced the old advance declaration about the status of a work relationship (VAR, Verklaring arbeidsrelatie). Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The act governs how a work relationship is assessed, and for 2025 the enforcement of that assessment changed because the moratorium ended, see the later subsections. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Employment (loondienst) test

Employment (loondienst) has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform work in person (persoonlijke arbeid), and pay for the work. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Same source. Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) or VAT. Same source.

### Deemed employments (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen) and the opt-out

The employment page lists the deemed employments (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen), among them contractors for work (aannemers van werk) and their helpers, agents (agenten), artists and professional athletes (artiesten en beroepssporters), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden), usually 2 or more days a week for the same client and at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (minimumloon), interns (stagiairs), home workers (thuiswerkers) and contractors working through an intermediary (intermediair). Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). Home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The contractor then has no entitlement to sickness benefit (ZW), disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) for work and income (WIA) and unemployment benefit (WW). Same source.

### Stb. 2016, 165

Correction to a common mistake: the Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree (Besluit) of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment for home workers, persons treated as employees and artists by agreement. Source: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).

### Case law: HR 6 November 2020 and HR 24 March 2023

Supreme Court of the Netherlands (HR, hoge raad) 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of the intention of the parties (partijbedoeling). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), works with a catalogue of circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) that names embedding in the organisation (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), and free replacement of the worker (vrije vervanging) is not an absolute element. Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### HR Uber ruling

In the Uber case (preliminary questions, prejudiciële vragen) the Supreme Court of the Netherlands held that there is no ranking (rangorde) between the points of view (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on enforcement (handhaving). Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, 27 March 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 are UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### End of the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium)

Until 1 January 2025 the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) applied. In a case not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing): reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte, belastingaangifte, aangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid), knowing or able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship. Cases acting in bad faith could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary assessments for payroll taxes (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and fines (boetes). Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions are given. False self-employment can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes. Same source.

### How far back corrections go

Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). This is the phase-in model (ingroeimodel) that runs to 2030.

### DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid) and soft landing (zachte landing) 2025

For 2025 there are no default fines (verzuimboetes) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent (opzet) for the qualification of employment relationships, and for corrections over 2025 this stays so. The basis is the Flach/Aartsen motion (motie Flach/Aartsen), Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). The soft landing (zachte landing) in 2025 consisted of the phase-in model, the supporting measures of the parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 6 September 2024 and the adopted motions (moties) of 1 October 2024. Under that letter there were no penalties for gross negligence or intent for 2025 for parties demonstrably working on correct qualification, then extended by the Flach/Aartsen motion to no default fines and no penalties for gross negligence or intent at all. Same source. After the Aartsen motion (31 311, nr. 264) risk-based enforcement continues so problem cases can get supplementary assessments (naheffingen). Same source. Forward path: for 2026 there are no default fines and penalties for gross negligence or intent are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections over 2026, and on 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing lapse. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). This is enforcement policy, not statute.

### Enforcement practice 2025

In 2025 a control in principle starts with a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek), possibly with a warning (waarschuwing) that is not an official instrument like the instruction. A tax audit (boekenonderzoek) remains possible with large risks. Capacity in 2025 was 80 fte (full-time equivalents), with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. NLA signals are expected to be picked up in 2025 through the GKT notification process (meldingenproces). Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Advance consultation (vooroverleg)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes a position on a complete advance consultation request (verzoek vooroverleg). The position gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The target period (streeftermijn) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist for advance consultation exists. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving) per the 2025 plan

The tranche 2025 plan stated regular enforcement from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Knock-on effect to income tax and VAT (doorwerking naar IH en OB)

Guidelines on the knock-on effect to income tax (IH) and VAT (OB) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium). The focus is on the payroll taxes at the client (opdrachtgever). After payroll tax corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the payroll tax correction. A contractor in regular income tax or VAT supervision has the most recent open profit tax return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten)

Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after the Van Oostenbruggen motion (motie van oostenbruggen) (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Model agreements on free substitution (vrije vervanging)

The approval of the model agreements on free substitution (modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Source: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

### Web module for the assessment of an employment relationship (webmodule beoordeling arbeidsrelatie)

The web module is for clients with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside an employment relationship, an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a bv, for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon), or for a foreign contractor. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months, ended on 10 July 2021, and the online pilot environment stayed active, with no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. Sources: [Ondernemersplein, webmodule beoordeling arbeidsrelatie](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The pilot design used a points system with a client statement (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome. Same sources.

### VBAR bill submitted

The internet consultation (internetconsultatie) of the original bill for the Assessment of Employment Relationships Act (Wet beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, VBAR) ran from 6 October 2023, with an intended start on 1 July 2025. The bill went to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024 and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and on 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer), with the bill documents dated 9 July 2025. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). The bill was not in force (in werking) in tax year 2025. The later path: the memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part, the House of Representatives adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD), the Senate adopted it on 16 June 2026 in a vote by sitting and standing (zitten en opstaan), and it became the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published on 29 June 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR presumption in art. 7:610aa BW

New art. 7:610aa BW reads as follows. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). Lid 2: the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment (Sociale Zaken en Werkgelegenheid, SZW) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 of the Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the article is not applicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). The presumption does not apply in tax year 2025 because the law was not in force yet, see the subsection on entry into force (inwerkingtreding).

### VBAR effect and evaluation

The effect is that the working person (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 provides that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### VBAR amount at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains the amount as follows: 120% of the statutory minimum wage (WML, wettelijk minimumloon) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding (excl.) VAT at the reference date 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Source: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. Same source. The arithmetic is written out in section 4.

### Statutory minimum wage per hour at 1 January 2025

The statutory minimum wage was € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025).

### VBAR amount at the reference date 1 January 2026

The statutory minimum wage rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount follows the WML percentage from the € 35,43. The Senate, the Government of the Netherlands (rijksoverheid, rijksoverheid.nl) and the central government business portal (Ondernemersplein) state € 38 for the reference date 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at the reference date 1 January 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at the reference date 1 January 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [rijksoverheid.nl, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers) and [Ondernemersplein, laag uurtarief van zzper](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). The arithmetic is written out in section 4.

### Statutory minimum wage per hour at 1 January 2026

The statutory minimum wage was € 14,71 per hour at 1 January 2026. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

### VBAR entry into force

The law entered into force on 31 December 2026 by the Decree (Besluit) of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. Source: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html). For tax year 2025 this means the presumption was not yet part of the law, and the ordinary assessment of the work relationship applies.

### BAZ bill 36 912

The Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ) bill was submitted on 24 March 2026. A technical briefing followed on 13 May 2026, the written contribution for the report (inbreng verslag) was submitted on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May, and the report (verslag, 36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May 2026. On 2026-10-11 the bill was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote. Source: [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). The docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period (wachttijd) of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. Same source. Because the bill is proposed, nothing follows from it for 2025.
