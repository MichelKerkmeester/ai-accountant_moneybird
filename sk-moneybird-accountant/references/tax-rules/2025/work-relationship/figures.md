## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the work-relationship side of the rules for a self-employed person (zelfstandige) without employees (ZZP): the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) and its enforcement, the employment test (loondienst test), model agreements (modelovereenkomsten), the VBAR bill with the low hourly rate presumption, and the status of the BAZ bill. The most important fact for 2025 is that the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) ended on 1 January 2025: the normal rules apply, no instructions (aanwijzingen) are given, and false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen) and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). The phase-in model (ingroeimodel) keeps corrections to 1 January 2025 and later unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed, and for 2025 no default fines (verzuimboetes) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent are charged for the qualification of employment relationships. The Wet DBA itself is enacted law since 2016, the 2025 enforcement stance is policy rather than statute, model agreements valid on 6 September 2024 stay usable to and including 31 December 2029, and the VBAR and BAZ bills were not in force (in werking) in tax year 2025.
