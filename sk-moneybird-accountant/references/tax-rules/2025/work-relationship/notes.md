## 5. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Handhavingsmoratorium: 2024 was the last year of the moratorium. From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply, no aanwijzingen are given, and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Aanwijzing route: under the moratorium a non-kwaadwillend case first got an aanwijzing to reshape the relationship or process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months, and a correctieverplichting applied from the date of the aanwijzing. From 1 January 2025 no aanwijzingen are given. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Look-back: the old route gave a correctieverplichting from the date of the aanwijzing. The new rule keeps corrections to 1 January 2025 and later, unless kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, and then up to 5 years. The full 5 years is possible again only in 2030. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- DBA boetes: 2025 has no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and this stays so for correcties over 2025. The checked 2024 material states no DBA boetebeleid for 2024. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Enforcement approach: from 2025 a control in principle starts with a bedrijfsbezoek, possibly with a waarschuwing, while a boekenonderzoek remains possible with large risks. Capacity was 80 fte, with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Modelovereenkomsten: the stop started on 6 September 2024, with no new assessments and no renewals, and existing agreements stayed usable to and including 31 December 2029. In 2025 the tranche 2025 plan confirms that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), if they comply with law and regulations. Sources: [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2024 the bill was with the Raad van State (21 June 2024) and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. The original plan had an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2025 bill 36 783 was ingediend, on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer, and it was not in force in 2025. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- VBAR amount: new in the bill as it stands for 2025 is the amount of € 36 at peildatum 1 January 2025, built from the WML of € 14,06 and the opslagen in the memorie van toelichting, while the € 38 that appears on the Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein pages is the indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [rijksoverheid.nl, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers).
- Preparations in 2024 for 2025: the Kamerbrief of 6 September 2024 announced lifting the moratorium per 1 January 2025, the commissiedebat of 12 September 2024 and the moties of 1 October 2024 shaped the zachte landing, and the handhavingsplan tranche 2025 (versie 1.0 of 18 December 2024, sent to the Tweede Kamer that day, 28 pages) set out the approach. The 2024 enforcement plan was published on 28 February 2024 with a perspectiefnota and the memo richtlijnen doorwerking IH and OB, and the Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking was published on 1 November 2024. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the basis of the qualification together: the written contract, any approved modelovereenkomst and any vooroverleg standpoint. Be able to show that the work is carried out exactly as described, because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done and a modelovereenkomst can create schijnzekerheid. Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten), [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- For thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten the exclusion must be agreed in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay, otherwise the fictieve dienstbetrekking stands and the entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW is not excluded. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- A reclassification correction lands at the opdrachtgever as a correctieverplichting and a naheffingsaanslag loonheffingen. For correcties over 2025 this carries no verzuimboete and no vergrijpboete for the qualification, per the 2025 policy. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Doorwerking at the opdrachtnemer: after LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years aimed at bronkwalificatie. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Keep the administratie for seven years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 requires administratieplichtigen to keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise, and art. 52 lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions of the AWR. Sources: [AWR 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst, hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Timing: a doorwerking to the 2025 winst uit onderneming lands in the 2025 income tax return. That return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May, here 1 May 2026, and uitstel requested by then gives 1 September 2026. Sources: [Belastingdienst, kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte) and [Belastingdienst, wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- VBAR ingediend date: the Eerste Kamer page gives 4 July 2025, the Tweede Kamer page gives 7 July 2025, and the bill documents are dated 9 July 2025. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783). What would settle it: the date of the Koninklijke Boodschap in the Kamerstuk.
- VBAR first-application amount: unknown. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application after entry into force was not found on 2026-10-11. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). What would settle it: publication of that ministeriële regeling.
- HR Uber ruling date: UNVERIFIED. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. The substance, no rangorde between the gezichtspunten and ondernemerschap as a full criterion with no effect on handhaving, is verified on the government page. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, 27 March 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). What would settle it: opening the ruling itself.
- Regular handhaving start date: the tranche 2025 plan says from 1 January 2026, while the 2026 plan records that ambition, states that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). What would settle it: the next handhavingsplan.
- BAZ: proposed. On 2026-10-11 the bill was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with no debate and no vote, so no rights follow from it yet. Source: [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). What would settle it: the nota and the further parliamentary handling.
- Settled and recorded to avoid confusion, the € 36 against € 38 question for the VBAR amount. Both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025 and € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
