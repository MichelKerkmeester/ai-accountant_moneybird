## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium): 2024 was the last year of the moratorium. From 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply, no instructions (aanwijzingen) are given, and false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen) and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Instruction (aanwijzing) route: under the moratorium a case not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) first got an instruction to reshape the relationship or process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months, and a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. From 1 January 2025 no instructions are given. Source: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Look-back: the old route gave a correction obligation from the date of the instruction. The new rule keeps corrections to 1 January 2025 and later, unless bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed, and then up to 5 years. The full 5 years is possible again only in 2030. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- DBA fines (boetes): 2025 has no default fines (verzuimboetes) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent (opzet) for the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties, dienstbetrekkingen) (Flach/Aartsen motion, motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and this stays so for corrections over 2025. The checked 2024 material states no DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid) for 2024. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Enforcement approach: from 2025 a control in principle starts with a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek), possibly with a warning (waarschuwing), while a tax audit (boekenonderzoek) remains possible with large risks. Capacity was 80 fte, with no extra fte for lifting the moratorium. Source: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten): the stop started on 6 September 2024, with no new assessments and no renewals, and existing agreements stayed usable to and including 31 December 2029. In 2025 the tranche 2025 plan confirms that they are honoured to end 2029 after the Van Oostenbruggen motion (motie van oostenbruggen) (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), if they comply with law and regulations. Sources: [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR): in 2024 the bill was with the Council of State (Raad van State) (21 June 2024) and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. The original plan had an intended start on 1 July 2025. In 2025 bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend), on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer), and it was not in force (in werking) in 2025. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- VBAR amount: new in the bill as it stands for 2025 is the amount of € 36 at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, built from the statutory minimum wage (WML, wettelijk minimumloon) of € 14,06 and the surcharges (opslagen) in the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting), while the € 38 that appears on the Senate, the Government of the Netherlands (rijksoverheid, rijksoverheid.nl) and the central government business portal (Ondernemersplein) pages is the indexed amount at the reference date 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [rijksoverheid.nl, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers).
- Preparations in 2024 for 2025: the parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 6 September 2024 announced lifting the moratorium per 1 January 2025, the committee debate (commissiedebat) of 12 September 2024 and the motions (moties) of 1 October 2024 shaped the soft landing (zachte landing), and the enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) tranche 2025 (version 1.0 of 18 December 2024, sent to the House of Representatives that day, 28 pages) set out the approach. The 2024 enforcement plan was published on 28 February 2024 with an outlook memorandum (perspectiefnota) and the memo on the knock-on guidelines for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) and VAT, and the Decision and assessment framework for private-law employment relationships (Beslis- en afwegingskader privaatrechtelijke dienstbetrekking) was published on 1 November 2024. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep the basis of the qualification together: the written contract, any approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) and any advance consultation (vooroverleg) position. Be able to show that the work is carried out exactly as described, because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done and a model agreement can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid). Sources: [Belastingdienst, werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten), [Belastingdienst, geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- For home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) the exclusion must be agreed in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay, otherwise the deemed employment (fictieve dienstbetrekking) stands and the entitlement to sickness benefit (ZW), disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) for work and income (WIA) and unemployment benefit (WW) is not excluded. Source: [Belastingdienst, wanneer is sprake van loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- A reclassification correction lands at the client (opdrachtgever) as a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) and a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for payroll taxes (loonheffing, loonheffingen). For corrections over 2025 this carries no default fine (verzuimboete) and no penalty (vergrijpboete) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent for the qualification, per the 2025 policy. Sources: [Belastingdienst, handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).
- Knock-on effect (doorwerking) at the contractor (opdrachtnemer): after payroll tax (LH) corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). A contractor in regular income tax (IH) or VAT (OB) supervision has the most recent open profit return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Keep the records (administratie) for seven years. AWR art. 52 lid 4 requires persons with an administration duty (administratieplichtigen) to keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise, and art. 52 lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions of the AWR. Sources: [AWR 1 January 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR 1 January 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst, hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Timing: a knock-on effect to the 2025 profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) lands in the 2025 income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). That return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), often 1 May, here 1 May 2026, and deferral (uitstel) requested by then gives 1 September 2026. Sources: [Belastingdienst, kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte) and [Belastingdienst, wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- VBAR submission (ingediend) date: the Senate page gives 4 July 2025, the House of Representatives page gives 7 July 2025, and the bill documents are dated 9 July 2025. Sources: [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783). What would settle it: the date of the Royal Message (Koninklijke Boodschap) in the parliamentary paper (Kamerstuk) (Kamerstuk).
- VBAR first-application amount: unknown. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application after entry into force was not found on 2026-10-11. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). What would settle it: publication of that ministerial regulation.
- HR Uber ruling date: UNVERIFIED. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. The substance, no ranking (rangorde) between the points of view (gezichtspunten) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap) as a full criterion with no effect on enforcement (handhaving), is verified on the government page. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl, 27 March 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). What would settle it: opening the ruling itself.
- Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving) start date: the tranche 2025 plan says from 1 January 2026, while the 2026 plan records that ambition, states that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties, tranche 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). What would settle it: the next enforcement plan (handhavingsplan).
- Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ): proposed. On 2026-10-11 the bill was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with no debate and no vote, so no rights follow from it yet. Source: [Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). What would settle it: the note and the further parliamentary handling.
- Settled and recorded to avoid confusion, the € 36 against € 38 question for the VBAR amount. Both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025 and € 38 is the same amount indexed to the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon) of € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kamerstuk 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [rijksoverheid.nl, bedragen minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
