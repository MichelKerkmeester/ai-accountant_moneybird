## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)

The tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen) is subtracted from the return base (rendementsgrondslag) in step 3 of the calculation. For 2025 it is € 57.684 per person and € 115.368 with a fiscal partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year. It also doubles for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)).

Partners allocate the joint tax base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen). Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share allocated under art. 2.17, and the partners may divide the joint tax base (grondslag) in any ratio as long as the shares total 100% ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The statute text valid on 1 January 2027 adds that the effective percentage then uses the joint return base and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values ([statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Box 3 rate (tarief)

The benefit (voordeel) from step 5 of the calculation is taxed at 36% in step 6 ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the rate is 36%.

The benefit itself cannot be negative. A negative return (rendement) is set at nil under art. 5.2 of the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3), and art. 4.14 lid 1 makes the flat-rate benefit at least nil ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

Only the part of the total debts above the threshold (drempel) is deductible. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) computes this in the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

The statute writes the rule in art. 5.3 lid 3 ("daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ..."). The amount is indexed under art. 10.1 ([statute text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the threshold is € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

The threshold doubles for whole-year partners. A joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).

### Bank percentage

Bank and savings balances (banktegoeden) are multiplied by the bank percentage. The percentage is fixed after the year by ministerial regulation under art. 10.6ter lid 2, with retroactive effect to 1 January. It is the average of the Dutch central bank (DNB) average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice, so the sum divided by twelve ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

For 2025 the bank percentage is 1,37%. The figure previously printed for this calculation, 1,44%, was replaced by the regulation (regeling) of 12 February 2026 with effect back to 1 January 2025 ([regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration percentages page lists 1,37% for 2025 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).

### Other-assets percentage

Other assets (overige bezittingen, for example investments and a second home) are multiplied by the other-assets percentage ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)).

The percentage is not an indexed base value. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 replaces it at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the value is 5,88%, printed in the statute text valid on 1 January 2025 and on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration percentages page ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage applies only to the deductible part of the debts, which is the total debts minus the threshold ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)).

Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method as the bank percentage, on the Dutch central bank average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages ([statute text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2025 the debt percentage is 2,70%, replacing 2,61% back to 1 January 2025 by the regulation of 12 February 2026. The regulation cites no monthly Dutch central bank figures ([regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)).

### Box 3 income calculation (berekening box 3 inkomen)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the box 3 income for 2025 in six steps ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)):

1. Taxable return (belastbaar rendement) = bank balances x bank percentage plus other assets x other-assets percentage minus (debts (schulden) minus threshold (drempel)) x debt percentage.
2. Return base = assets minus (debts minus threshold).
3. Tax base for savings and investments = return base minus tax-free allowance. Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%.
4. Share (aandeel) = tax base / return base x 100. The 2025 page instructs 2 decimals.
5. Benefit = taxable return x share.
6. Tax = 36% x benefit.

The values of the assets and debts are taken at the reference date (peildatum), 1 January (art. 5.24 speaks of "die peildatum" and of "een aaneengesloten periode van drie maanden die aanvangt voor en eindigt na die peildatum") ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

Transactions around the reference date inside that three-month period are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes it plausible that "zakelijke overwegingen" lay behind them (art. 5.24, reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage)) ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)).

The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year. The 2023 and 2025 pages use 2 decimals, the 2024 and 2026 pages use 3 decimals ([2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)).

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

Art. 5.13 keeps green investments (groene beleggingen) out of the assets up to a fixed amount. The amount doubles for whole-year partners ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the amount is € 26.312 per person and € 52.624 with a whole-year partner, after the Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025) cut of the green facilities ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Art. 5.13 lid 2, in the text from 2023, applies the exemption first to green other assets (overige bezittingen) and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January ([statute text, art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)).

A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration example for 2025 is a minor child with a green account of € 80.000 on 1 January 2025. The child claims € 26.312 in its own return and the parents report € 53.688 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).

### Green investment tax credit (korting groene beleggingen)

Art. 8.19 gives a green investment tax credit (korting voor groene beleggingen) of 0,1% for 2025 of the exempt amount on the reference date. The amount is computed automatically and is split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

The rate was 0,7% for 2020 to 2024 and is 0,1% for 2025 to 2027 ([statute text, art. 8.19](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).

### Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) dates

The Rebuttal Scheme Act box 3 (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. Entry into force followed from the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, in force the day after issue, so 19 July 2025. The Senate (Eerste Kamer) adopted the law on 8 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html), [Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

Art. V gives retroactive effect. Arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV to 1 January 2017. Art. II, on own use of real estate in art. 5.27a, applies from 1 January 2026 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes it plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit, the income from savings and investments (sparen en beleggen) is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lid 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur) applies, in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents may follow up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lid 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus increase in value (vermogensaanwas) ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Art. III: the personal deductions after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024 follows the partners' chosen split, and another split follows from an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve-vermindering) request. Art. IV: art. 6a in the Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel) with the same form and six-week rule ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

The explanatory note (nota van toelichting) to Stb. 2025, 196 names Supreme Court (Hoge Raad) rulings of 6 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:704 and 705), 14 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857), 2 August 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084) and 20 December 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788) ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)).

Background: the Supreme Court ruled on 6 June 2024 that the Restoration of Rights Act and the Bridge Act still conflict with the European Convention on Human Rights (EVRM) when the flat-rate return is higher than the actual return. The 2024 calculation page says a letter follows if the ruling affects your box 3 income ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3), [2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)).

Content rules for the actual return: income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No tax-free allowance is used ("De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen"). Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

### Actual-return reporting 2025

From 2025 the actual return is reported in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). The return asks whether you want to report the actual return when your assets exceed the tax-free allowance. Reporting is not mandatory ("Het doorgeven is niet verplicht") and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the amount that is most favourable for you ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

No deadline is stated on these pages ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

Earlier routes: 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD, outside My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). 2020 to 2024 used an online form in My Tax and Customs Administration ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"), a paper form via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call) or several forms via the Tax Information Line (Belastingtelefoon) 0800 - 0543. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. For 2017 to 2020 all letters have been sent, and without a letter you may not report ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen)).
