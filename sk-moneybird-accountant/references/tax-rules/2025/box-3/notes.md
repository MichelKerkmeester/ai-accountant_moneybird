## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 57.000 to € 57.684 per person, and € 114.000 to € 115.368 with a fiscal partner ([2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [statute text, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.700 to € 3.800 per person, and € 7.400 to € 7.600 with a whole-year partner ([statute text, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Bank percentage: 1,44% to 1,37% ([regeling 7 March 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html), [regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)).
- Other-assets percentage: 6,04% to 5,88% ([statute text, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Debt percentage: 2,61% to 2,70% ([regeling 7 March 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html), [regeling 12 February 2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)).
- Aandeel rounding in step 4: 3 decimals in 2024 to 2 decimals in 2025 ([2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024), [2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)).
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 71.251 to € 26.312 per person, and € 142.502 to € 52.624 with a whole-year partner ([statute text, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,7% to 0,1% ("Voor 2024 was deze heffingskorting nog 0,7 %") ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- Actual-return reporting: in 2024 through the online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, which could always be filed without a letter, and from 2025 inside the aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2025 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).
- Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3: new in 2025, in force 19 July 2025, with statutory effect from 1 January 2023 for arts. I and III and from 1 January 2017 inside the Wet rechtsherstel for art. IV ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html), [Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).
- Box 3 tarief: 36% in both 2024 and 2025, unchanged ([statute text, 1 January 2024](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [statute text, 1 January 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Value the box 3 assets and debts at the peildatum 1 January 2025 and keep them split over the three categories, because each category uses its own percentage: 1,37% for banktegoeden, 5,88% for overige bezittingen and 2,70% for the deductible part of the schulden ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)).
- Only the part of the total debts above € 3.800, or above € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, enters the calculation. The Belastingdienst computes this in the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Partners choose the split of the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen, and the shares must total 100%. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns ([2025 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Keep the records that support the optional actual return: income and value changes over the calendar year, because gains and losses are netted and a negative total becomes € 0 with no carry-forward. Keep the documents for interest paid on a box 3 debt and for a second-home investment that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, because those are the only deductible costs ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement)).
- The actual return is reported in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2025 and the report is optional. The Belastingdienst uses the most favourable of the fictitious and the actual amount, and you never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).
- For the counterproof route the inspecteur provides a form, and further documents can be requested up to six weeks after the form is sent. This does not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).
- A minor child with green investments files its own return for the exemption of € 26.312 and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).
- No deadline for reporting the actual return is stated on the Belastingdienst pages ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Open point 2025: the rekenvoorbeelden page prints € 11.500 in step 4 of the debt example while its own sum is € 13.000. Only a correction of the page by the Belastingdienst settles it. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/rekenvoorbeelden-berekening-werkelijk-rendement
- Conflict in the bank and debt percentages: the yearly regulation replaces the figures in the statute text and works back to 1 January, so the two can differ. Operative for 2025 are 1,37% for banktegoeden and 2,70% for schulden under the regeling of 12 February 2026, which replaced 1,44% and 2,61% back to 1 January 2025. The statute text at https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 carries the earlier figures. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01
- Conflict on the entry date of the Wet rechtsherstel box 3: statute art. 7 gives the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". The statute text settles it. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- UNVERIFIED: the claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return. hogeraad.nl still returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. The memorie van toelichting describes the Kerstarrest of 24 December 2021 as a breach of art. 1 EP in combination with art. 14 EVRM, and stresses that the legislator chose a recalculation with the actual composition of the assets in three categories, not a levy on the actual return. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- UNVERIFIED: the "or objected late" part of the finding of 25 June 2026 on non-objectors. The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms only that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- Proposed: the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, bill 36 748, submitted 16 May 2025. Adopted by the Tweede Kamer on 12 February 2026. No Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 2026-10-11, so it is not law. Entry into force by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028. https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- Proposed: the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 with a novelle to bill 36 748. The Kamerstuk number is UNVERIFIED. The letter mentions a vermogenswinstbelasting for all financial instruments from 2028 (about 90% of assets with value development), a full switch for the other assets in 2030, the box 3 heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027, a heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028 and a rise of the forfait for overige bezittingen by 1,5 percentage point in 2027. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page. [Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255) and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
