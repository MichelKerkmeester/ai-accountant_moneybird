## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

For tax year 2025 this file covers the business costs that a sole trader can deduct and the treatment of the car. The year's key figures are the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) of € 5.700, the kilometre allowance of € 0,23 per business kilometre, and the car percentages of 22%, at least 25% for a car first admitted by 31 December 2016 and 35% of the value for an old car. Two 2025 points stand out: art. 3.16 lid 14 is new from 1 January 2025 and limits the costs of a workspace in an owned home, and zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during the year. All 2025 values in this file are enacted and Verified, except the art. 3.16 lid 14 item, which is Corrected and returns in section 7.
