## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1. Mixed costs (gemengde kosten), threshold against the 80% election (keuze)

**Calculated example.** A sole trader has € 8.000 of mixed costs in 2025.

- Route 1, the threshold: € 8.000 minus € 5.700 = € 2.300 deductible.
- Route 2, the 80% election: 80% x € 8.000 = € 6.400,00, rounded to € 6.400.

In this example the 80% election gives the higher deduction. The figures used are the 2025 threshold of € 5.700 and the 80% election under art. 3.15 lid 5.

### Example 2. Course travel above the cap

**Calculated example.** Travel and stay for a course (cursus) cost € 2.000 in 2025. Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f limits the deduction of travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession (beroep) to € 1.500 per year. Art. 3.16 lid 8 makes that cap apply only when attendance is not necessary because of the nature of the work. Assume attendance is not necessary. The amount above the cap is € 2.000 minus € 1.500 = € 500. That € 500 is not deductible. If the nature of the work makes attendance necessary, the cap does not apply and the € 2.000 is not capped by this rule. In both cases the amount within the cap, or the full amount when the cap does not apply, must still meet the other conditions for the cost concerned.

### Example 3. Kilometre allowance with a car in private assets (privévermogen)

**Calculated example.** The sole trader drives 12.000 business kilometres in 2025 in a car that he keeps in his private assets. The deduction is 12.000 km x € 0,23 = € 2.760,00, rounded to € 2.760. Fuel, insurance, toll and parking sit inside this amount and cannot be claimed separately. Under the rule for public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig) the actual costs of business travel are deductible at 100%, so a business train trip with a ticket of € 120 is deductible in full.

### Example 4. Relocation costs (verhuiskosten)

**Calculated example.** The move of the household contents (inboedel) costs € 850 in 2025. Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a allows the cost of moving the household contents plus the fixed amount of € 7.750. The deduction is € 850 plus € 7.750 = € 8.600. Housing outside the place of residence is a separate part of the same item (onderdeel) and is allowed for at most two years. No such double housing is claimed in this example.

### Example 5. Small purchase against asset

**Calculated example.** A laptop that is classified as a business asset and has an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) of € 400 is below the € 450 line, so € 400 is deducted in one go in 2025. A laptop that is classified as a business asset and has an acquisition cost of € 900 is € 450 or more and is used longer than a year, so it is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account instead of being expensed at once. This example does not compute a depreciation amount because no residual value is given.

### Example 6. Zero-emission car first admitted in 2025

**Calculated example.** The car has 0 g CO2 per km, was first admitted in 2025 and has a value of the car (waarde van de auto) of € 40.000.

- Route 1: 22% x € 40.000 = € 8.800,00, rounded to € 8.800. The reduction is 5% x € 40.000 = € 2.000,00, rounded to € 2.000, but the cap is € 1.500, so the reduction is € 1.500. The withdrawal (onttrekking) is € 8.800 minus € 1.500 = € 7.300.
- Route 2, the same rule printed as 17% up to € 30.000: 17% x € 30.000 = € 5.100,00, rounded to € 5.100, plus 22% x € 10.000 = € 2.200,00, rounded to € 2.200, together € 7.300.

For a value of exactly € 30.000 the outcome is 17% x € 30.000 = € 5.100,00, rounded to € 5.100.

### Example 7. Youngtimer

**Calculated example.** A car first taken into use more than 15 years before 2025 has a market value (WEV) of € 10.000. The withdrawal is 35% x € 10.000 = € 3.500,00, rounded to € 3.500. If the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation that the taxpayer bore are € 2.800, the withdrawal is capped at € 2.800.

### Example 8. Bicycle (fiets)

**Calculated example.** A business e-bike with a recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) of € 2.000 is also used privately. The withdrawal is 7% x € 2.000 = € 140,00, rounded to € 140 per year, capped at the costs borne. If the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the withdrawal is nil under lid 5.

### Example 9. VAT correction on a private car

**Calculated example.** The catalogue price (catalogusprijs) of a private car used for business, including VAT and bpm, is € 40.000 and there are no conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie). The year-end correction is 1,5% x € 40.000 = € 600,00, rounded to € 600 in rubriek 1d. It is never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance of the car.

### Example 10. 500 km rule

**Calculated example.** The private use of a business car is 400 km in 2025 and the taxpayer shows this with a trip registration (rittenregistratie). Because 400 km is at most 500 km, the withdrawal is nil and the 22% is not applied for the year.

### Example 11. Performance grant (prestatiebeurs), official example

**Official example.** In the FISIN 2025 example the performance grant amounts are € 333 and € 467. € 333 plus € 467 = € 800. The € 250 threshold (drempel) is subtracted: € 800 minus € 250 = € 550 ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)).

### Example 12. Performance grant, calculated example

**Calculated example.** After the diploma period (diplomatermijn) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, one mbo (senior secondary vocational education) study year has an actual performance grant of € 1.000. The cap of € 1.693 per study year is not binding, so the lower amount of € 1.000 applies. The € 250 threshold applies per person. € 1.000 minus € 250 = € 750 deductible.

### Example 13. Vpb variant for a private limited company (BV)

**Calculated example.** A private limited company has employees, a wage total (loonsom) of € 1.500.000 and € 10.000 of mixed costs in 2025. 0,4% x € 1.500.000 = € 6.000,00, rounded to € 6.000, which is higher than the fixed amount of € 5.700, so the threshold is € 6.000.

- Threshold route: € 10.000 minus € 6.000 = € 4.000 deductible.
- Election route: 73,5% x € 10.000 = € 7.350,00, rounded to € 7.350 deductible.
