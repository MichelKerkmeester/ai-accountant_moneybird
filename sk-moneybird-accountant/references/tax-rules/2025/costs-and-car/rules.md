## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2025

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel)

Costs connected with food (voedsel), drinks (drank) and stimulants (genotmiddelen such as tobacco), with representation expenses (representatie, including receptions, festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak)) and with congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and the like are not deductible up to a yearly threshold (drempel) of € 5.700 ([art. 3.15 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Travel and stay that belong to these posts count as part of them ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover ([lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Per category, food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including gratuities (fooien). Drinks cover coffee, tea, milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank). Stimulants cover cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren). Food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen) used privately are never deductible. Representation expenses include receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses, as a subcategory per the legislative history (wetsgeschiedenis) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964 ([Belastingdienst overview](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The threshold is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year. For a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either ([section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not salary (loon) in kind (in natura) ([section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

The threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on the threshold confirms the 2025 amount: "In 2025 was de drempel ook € 5.700" ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026)).

### 3.2 Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van drempel)

Instead of the threshold (drempel) the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs ([art. 3.15 lid 5, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters). The election replaces the threshold, so a taxpayer does not apply both at once. The election is made in the return itself, so no separate request is needed. Which route gives the higher deduction depends on the level of the costs and on the rest of the return.

### 3.3 Corporate income tax variant of the threshold for a private limited company (BV)

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount, which is the art. 3.15 lid 1 amount of € 5.700 in 2025. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. The rule applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). See section 7 for the coverage note on the 2025 text of the Wet Vpb.

### 3.4 Travel and accommodation (reis en verblijf) for congresses and courses

Travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession (beroep), and for congresses, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [Belastingdienst overview](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). So € 1.500 stays deductible and everything above it does not. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary ([same sources](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Travel and stay for congresses and study trips also count as part of the mixed costs (gemengde-kosten) posts of art. 3.15 lid 2 ([art. 3.15 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.5 Study costs (studiekosten) as business costs (bedrijfskosten)

Study costs (studiekosten) are deductible when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs. The boundary between the two is hard to draw in practice ([studiekosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers), [overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)). The personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) for study costs (scholingsuitgaven) was abolished, so this business route is the route that remains. See section 3.24 for the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) exception.

### 3.6 Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding)

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, such as a car, motorcycle or bicycle (fiets), are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. For 2025 the amount is € 0,23 per kilometre ([art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is all-in, so fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll (tol) and parking (parkeren) cannot be deducted separately (same source). Household members are treated as the taxpayer ([art. 3.15 lid 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1 ([art. 10.1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page confirms: "In 2025 was dit € 0,23" ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026)).

### 3.7 Public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi and aircraft (vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig) is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (ov-chipkaart) trip overview ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

### 3.8 Relocation costs (verhuiskosten) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

Under art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a the moving costs are the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7.750. Housing outside the place of residence is deductible for at most two years. The rule is unchanged from 2020 to 2027 ([art. 3.17, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.9 Asset classification (vermogensetikettering) of business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen)

The income-tax treatment of a laptop and computer follows asset classification (vermogensetikettering). Equipment (apparatuur) and instruments (instrumenten) that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business is classified as part of the compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). One used 90% or more for business is classified as part of the mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). In between, it is freely classifiable (keuzevermogen), within reasonable bounds ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

For a car the same page gives the boundaries. A car is classified as part of the mandatory business assets when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. When a car is used 90% or more privately, it is classified as part of the compulsory private assets. All other cases are freely classifiable assets ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).

On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)). A choice to classify an asset as freely classifiable can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use of an asset included in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes it plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [Kennisgroep publication of 6 March 2026](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/)).

### 3.10 Work space at home (werkruimte thuis)

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not classified as a business asset are not deductible unless the workspace is a self-contained (zelfstandig) part according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting) of the home and an income test is met ([art. 3.16 lid 1, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [werkruimte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)).

The income test has two forms. With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit, salary and income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, that income must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it (same sources). Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) forms part of the business assets (same sources).

The official factors for being self-contained (zelfstandig) are a private entrance or stairway (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the ability to rent the space to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained. The overview page lists work space at home (werkruimte thuis) at 0%, with exceptions (uitzonderingen) ([werkruimte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning), [overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

### 3.11 Work space in an owned home included in the business assets

From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home included in the business assets that serves as main residence (hoofdverblijf) are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private ([art. 3.16 lid 14, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The lid is absent in the 1 January 2024 text ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)).

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) calls this a codification of case law. For a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workspace in such a home the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) are not deductible. The MvT names fixtures and fittings such as furniture (meubilair), soft furnishings (stoffering), wallpaper (behang) and painting work (schilderwerk), and gas, water and electricity (licht) ([MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page still presents the owner route without this limit. See section 7.

### 3.12 Home telephone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis)

A home telephone subscription (telefoonabonnement) for a connection in the taxpayer's own home is not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In practice a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but the call costs for private calls made on it are not. The overview page prints home telephone subscription 0% and business (zakelijke) telephone calls (telefoongesprekken) at home (thuis) 100% ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).

### 3.13 Private computers and equipment

Costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other such equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur) are not deductible ([art. 3.16 lid 3, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For a laptop or computer that does belong to the business, the treatment follows the asset classification of section 3.9. In short, an asset used 10% or less for business is classified as part of the compulsory private assets and gives 0% deduction. An asset used 90% or more for business is classified as part of the mandatory business assets. In between, the asset is freely classifiable ([vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel), [art. 3.16 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.14 Small purchases (kleine aankopen) under € 450

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are small office supplies (kantoorartikelen), professional literature (vakliteratuur) and car maintenance (onderhoud van de auto). A purchase of € 450 or more that is used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [vermogensetikettering page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)). Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).

### 3.15 Other exclusions (uitsluitingen) art. 3.16

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027 are literature (literatuur) except professional literature ([lid 2 onderdeel b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)), clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) ([lid 2 onderdeel c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), work clothing defined in [lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) and personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) ([lid 2 onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) ([art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction are rent (huur) and maintenance of a building (pand) split into business and private parts, telephone costs (telefoonkosten), a business car, congresses, study trips, symposia, seminars and excursions, business lunches and dinners (diners), receptions and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business (for example a driving licence (rijbewijs)), health costs (pedicure, health insurance (zorgverzekering)), and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser (kapper). Work clothing is deductible ([zakelijk of prive page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive)).

### 3.16 Partner compensation (partnervergoeding)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5.000 ([art. 3.16 lid 4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.17 Fines (geldboetes)

Not deductible are costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines ([art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Also not deductible are costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted ([onderdeel d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)) or for which a penal order (strafbeschikking) became irrevocable ([onderdeel e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)), and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb ([onderdeel i](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) traffic fines (verkeersboeten) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines ([Besluit van 23 april 2020, section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). The overview page prints fines 0% ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).

Timing matters. Costs made before the fine (geldboete) is imposed are not costs that relate to the imposed fine, and they stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment (dwangsom) the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught ([Kennisgroep publication of 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).

The criminal offence rule is scoped to the offence. Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by settlement (transactie) or penal order, are excluded. The decree (besluit) gives the example of a taxpayer convicted only for the possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten) and not for trade, where only the costs of the possession are excluded ([section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### 3.18 Private-use addition (bijtelling) for a car, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). This income-tax addition for private use of a business car is often called private-use addition.

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that the private use is at most 500 km a year. With a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another way of showing at most 500 private kilometres, the withdrawal is nil ([art. 3.20](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [privegebruik auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto)). A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked (same sources). Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private for this rule ([art. 3.20 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). In practice it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation (same source).

The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes it as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT and bpm, and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The RDW site carries the catalogue price for most cars. For an old car the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer, WEV) replaces it ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto), [art. 3.20 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car that is part of the business assets is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price (same sources).

The term car (auto) covers a passenger car (personenauto) or a van (bestelauto), except a van that is almost exclusively suited for the transport of goods ([lid 5 onderdeel a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For that excepted van the private kilometres are settled at the actual cost per kilometre. A declaration that a van is used exclusively for business (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible ([lid 6 to 10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.19 Private-use addition for a car, pre-2017 car

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a is 25% instead of 22% ([art. 10a.4, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). Art. 10a.4 does not affect the 35% rule for old cars ([art. 10a.4, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months (same source).

### 3.20 Youngtimer

When a car was first taken into use more than the age threshold ago, the withdrawal is 35% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) ([art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). For 2025 the age threshold is 15 years, so the rule covers cars first taken into use more than 15 years before. Such a car is often called a youngtimer. The 35% is calculated on the WEV of the car, because for an old car the WEV replaces the catalogue price ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). As with the 22%, the withdrawal is capped at the total car costs including depreciation ([lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 25% minimum of art. 10a.4 does not affect this rule ([art. 10a.4, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). When the threshold is crossed mid-year, the year is split by month ([waarde van de auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)).

### 3.21 Zero-emission

The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission ([lid 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). After those 60 months the rules then in force apply (same source).

By year of first admission, up to 2025:
- 2020: 14% off, capped at € 6.300 (8% up to € 45.000). Only hydrogen (waterstof) was uncapped in 2020.
- 2021: 10% off, capped at € 4.000 (12% up to € 40.000).
- 2022: 6% off, capped at € 2.100 (16% up to € 35.000).
- 2023 and 2024: 6% off, capped at € 1.800 (16% up to € 30.000).
- 2025: 5% off, capped at € 1.500 (17% up to € 30.000).

Cars on hydrogen are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead. From the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by the consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7 ([2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). All this is the statute ([art. 3.20 lid 2, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

During 2025, cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation, because the 14% off regime ends 60 months after the month after first admission. From then the rules in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).

### 3.22 Private-use addition for a bicycle

When a bicycle of the business, including an e-bike or a speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power, is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year ([art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.20a, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The withdrawal is capped at the costs borne (same source). Availability for commuting counts as private availability (same source). A lid 5, added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020, sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address (same source, the note on retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) is also shown in the 2020 to 2025 texts).

### 3.23 VAT correction (btw-correctie) for a private car

VAT on the use and maintenance of a private car that is also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, although it is business for income tax. In a private limited company the director and substantial shareholder (DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer) for this purpose, he counts as an employee ([btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)). Without conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) all the VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d. For a part year the correction is pro rata, and it is never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance (same source).

### 3.24 Performance grant cap, mbo (senior secondary vocational education)

Up to and including 2021 training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) were deductible above a threshold (drempel) of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15.000 ([art. 6.27 to 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No deduction applied when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study (same source). The Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven) (Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349 ([Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)). Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses that relate to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant case ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

For 2025 the exception works as follows. When the diploma period (diplomatermijn, usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). For an mbo (senior secondary vocational education) study the cap is € 1.693 per study year, or € 141,09 per month for a part-year (same source).

### 3.25 Performance grant cap, hbo (university of applied sciences) or wo (university)

For an hbo (university of applied sciences) or wo (university) study the cap is € 2.443 per study year, or € 203,59 per month for a part-year ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). When the actual performance grant was lower than the cap, the lower amount applies (same source).

### 3.26 Threshold (drempel) in the performance grant case

The € 250 threshold applies per person ([FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)). When a return covers more than one study year, the amounts are added and the threshold is applied once. FISIN 2025 gives the example € 333 plus € 467 is € 800 minus € 250 gives € 550 (same source). The non-fiscal STAP-budget that replaced the deduction took effect at the same moment as the abolition, 1 January 2022, with a maximum subsidy of € 1.000 including VAT per training (scholing) (art. 7) and six application windows per calendar year ([regeling](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01), [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html)). The regulation (regeling) is marked "Regeling vervallen per 01-01-2024", so there is no STAP-budget in 2025 (same source).
