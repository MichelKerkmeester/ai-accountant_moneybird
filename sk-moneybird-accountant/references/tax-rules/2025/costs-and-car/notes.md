## 2. CHANGES FROM 2024

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel): € 5.600 in 2024 to € 5.700 in 2025 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page also says "In 2025 was de drempel ook € 5.700" ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026)).
- Art. 3.16 lid 14 is new from 1 January 2025. The lid is absent in the 1 January 2024 text and present in the 1 January 2025 text ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)). The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) names fixtures and fittings (inrichting), gas, water and electricity (licht) as the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) caught ([MvT section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)).
- Zero-emission, first admission 2025: 5% off capped at € 1.500, so 17% up to € 30.000. For a car first admitted in 2024 the rates were 6% off capped at € 1.800, so 16% up to € 30.000 ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during 2025. The 14% off regime ends 60 months after the month after first admission, and then the rules in force apply ([art. 3.20 lid 11, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Unchanged against 2024: the kilometre allowance of € 0,23 per km, the relocation costs (verhuiskosten) of household contents (inboedel) plus € 7.750, the 22% standard, the at least 25% for a pre-2017 car, the 35% for a youngtimer of more than 15 years, the bicycle (fiets) rate of 7%, the € 1.500 cap for congress and course travel, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) caps of € 1.693 and € 2.443 with the € 250 threshold (drempel), home telephone subscription (telefoonabonnement thuis) 0%, private computers and equipment (apparatuur) 0% and fines (geldboetes) 0% ([2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/aftrek_studiekosten), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Business costs go into the profit tax return (winstaangifte), the profit part of the income tax return. Book them net of VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, which is the small businesses scheme (KOR) case, deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax return ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [KOR page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- The threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) or the 80% election is applied in the profit tax return (winstaangifte). The election is made in the return ([art. 3.15 lid 5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- Keep the receipts and invoices for every cost. Keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or other proof for the 500 km rule on the car, and conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) for the VAT position on the car ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten), [privegebruik auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto), [btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Private use of a car or bicycle (fiets) that is part of the business assets is a withdrawal (onttrekking). It is added in the profit calculation (winstberekening) and not booked as a cost, and it is capped at the car costs (autokosten) including depreciation ([art. 3.20 lid 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [art. 3.20a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01)).
- For travel by public transport (openbaar vervoer), taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig), keep the ticket or the public transport smart card (ov-chipkaart) trip overview to show the actual costs ([overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).
- The VAT correction of 1,5% of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) including VAT and bpm goes in rubriek 1d of the VAT return (btw-aangifte) at year end, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance ([btw auto page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work ([welke-kosten page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Costs after a fine (geldboete) that relate to the fine cannot be booked as deductible costs. The date of the fine decides ([Kennisgroep publication of 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/)).
- Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid and no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted, also not VAT paid in another EU country. Earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening). The low van (bestelauto) motor vehicle tax (mrb) rate stays available. Exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter, and immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year. The choice is per entrepreneur and covers all sub-numbers (subnummers) ([KOR page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- For a private limited company (BV): the threshold (drempel) applies per body when there are one or more employees in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher, and in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) it applies once, at the parent ([Wet Vpb 2020](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [Wet Vpb 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Work space (werkruimte) in an owned home included in the business assets. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page on the work space at home (werkruimte in de woning) has no date, does not mention art. 3.16 lid 14 and still presents the route for an owner whose home is included in the business assets ([page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning)). The 2025 statute and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) bar costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, and the MvT names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01), [MvT section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html)). A dated Dutch Tax and Customs Administration publication or case law on art. 3.16 lid 14 would settle the practical line for 2025.
- Which owner costs of a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workspace stay deductible, for example depreciation (afschrijving) or maintenance (onderhoud), is not stated on a fetched primary page beyond the MvT's tenant's costs list. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page on the work space or an official publication would settle it.
- The private limited company (BV) variant of the threshold (drempel) rests here on the checked 2020 and 2026 texts of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 and on section 3.4 of the decree (besluit) ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [besluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). A 2025 text of the Wet Vpb was not among the checked sources. The 2025 statute text would settle the exact 2025 wording.
