## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers which business costs (bedrijfskosten) a sole trader with profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) may deduct in tax year 2027, and how the car, bicycle (fiets) and van (bestelauto) rules work, including the private limited company (BV) variant where the checked material states one. Three layers matter for 2027. The published 2027 text of the Income Tax Act (Wet IB) is the base layer and its values carry the status adopted-not-yet-in-force. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) is a bill that is still in the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) on 2026-10-11, so its values carry the status proposed, and the effective threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) for 2027 is unknown because it waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation.
