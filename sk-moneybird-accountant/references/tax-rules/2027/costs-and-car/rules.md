## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel), base in the 2027 text

Art. 3.15 lid 1 Wet IB says costs connected with food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), representation expenses (representatie, including receptions (recepties), festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak)) and conferences, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like are not deductible up to a yearly threshold (drempel). The related travel and stay count as part of these posts (lid 2). The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover (lid 3). No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs (lid 4). The 2027 text prints € 5.700 before indexation, and the threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Source: [art. 3.15 and art. 10.1, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

The content of the posts is worked out on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) overview page and in the 2020 policy decree (beleidsbesluit). Food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including tips (fooien). Drink covers coffee, tea, milk (melk) and soft drinks (frisdrank). Stimulants covers cigarettes (sigaretten) and cigars (sigaren). Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation includes receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation. Business gifts are a subcategory of representation expenses per the legislative history (wetsgeschiedenis) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Sources: [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten) and [Besluit van 23 april 2020, nr. 2020-54978, section 3.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html).

Two mechanical points. The fixed amount is not adjusted proportionally when a taxpayer starts an enterprise during the calendar year, and for a financial year longer than 12 months the fixed amount is not increased proportionally either ([section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)). Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible, and when an employer gives free meals to staff the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not a benefit in kind (loon in natura) ([section 3.2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

### 3.2 Threshold for mixed costs, effective 2027

The effective 2027 amount is not published. It waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation, and Tax Plan 2027 does not amend art. 3.15 lid 1 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The bill uses a table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of 1,01248 for other amounts in art. L, and that factor is not confirmed for art. 3.15. Until the regulation is published, work from the base amount of € 5.700. See section 7.

### 3.3 Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel)

Instead of the threshold the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs (art. 3.15 lid 5). The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters), and the 2027 text keeps this percentage. Source: [art. 3.15 lid 5, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The threshold and the 80% election are alternatives, so the election is a choice made in the return and not an addition to the threshold.

### 3.4 Corporate income tax (Vpb) variant of the threshold (BV)

Under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount, and only in a fiscal unity does it apply once, at the parent. Verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute. Sources: [Wet Vpb 1969, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) and [Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01) (art. 8 lid 5), [beleidsbesluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked. See section 7.

### 3.5 Travel and accommodation for conferences and courses (reis en verblijf)

Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8 Wet IB limit travel and stay for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study and profession, and for conferences (congressen), seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like. They are not deductible above € 1500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). The same conference and study trip posts also stand in the art. 3.15 lid 1 list, where their travel and stay count as part of those posts (art. 3.15 lid 2).

### 3.6 Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding), enacted 2027 text

Art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b Wet IB limit the costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately (car, motorcycle (motor), bicycle) to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The amount is all-in, so fuel (brandstof), insurance, toll and parking cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer (art. 3.15 lid 7). The 2027 text prints € 0,23 per km. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1, because art. 10.1 lists art. 3.15 lid 1 only. Sources: [art. 3.15 and art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). For 2026 an approving policy decree allowed € 0,25 retroactively to 1 January 2026 and that decree lapses on 1 January 2027, so € 0,25 in 2027 depends on the bill ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html)).

### 3.7 Mileage allowance, Tax Plan 2027

The bill proposes € 0,25 per km in art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b (art. I onderdelen C and D). Bill status on 2026-10-11: introduced 15 September 2026, written report 1 October 2026, memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) 2 October 2026, awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), no plenary debate or vote and no Senate (Eerste Kamer) activity. Sources: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html) and [wetsvoorstel 37022 in the Tweede Kamer](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022). The bill also codifies the 2026 € 0,25 approval retroactively to 1 January 2026 per art. LXIII lid 1 a. Until adoption, use € 0,23.

### 3.8 Relocation costs (verhuiskosten)

Art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a Wet IB gives moving costs as the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750. The amount is unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.9 Double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

The same article allows the costs of housing outside the place of residence for at most two years. Unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.17, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.10 Private-use addition (bijtelling) for a car, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year (art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a). The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private (lid 3). The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself (lid 4), so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. Car means passenger car (personenauto) or van (bestelauto), except a van that is (almost) exclusively suited for goods, for which private km times the actual cost per km is settled (lid 5 a). A declaration of exclusive business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible (lid 6 to 10). Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [privegebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm, which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT (btw) and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer, WEV) replaces it. A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car classified as a business asset (ondernemingsvermogen) is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Source: [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto). Private use of an asset classified as a business asset is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). Source: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel).

### 3.11 Private-use addition for a car, pre-2017 car

Art. 10a.4 Wet IB sets the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel a at 25% instead of 22% for a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also kept the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Sources: [art. 10a.4, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) and [art. 10a.4, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.12 500 km rule and part-year availability

With mileage records (rittenregistratie) or another means of showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive, though trips can still be checked. Source: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Part-year availability is pro-rated, so 5 months gives 5/12 times 22% times the catalogue value, and private km are converted to a yearly basis, so 100 km in 5 months is 240 km. Source: [privegebruik auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto).

### 3.13 Youngtimer age, enacted 2027 text

Art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b makes 35% of the value of the car the withdrawal for a car first taken into use more than the age threshold ago. The 2027 text sets that threshold at 25 years. The step to 25 years was set by Tax Plan 2026 (Belastingplan 2026) art. II onderdeel aA, per the knowledge group (Kennisgroep) publication. The 2026 youngtimer decree states that per 1 January 2027 the threshold rises to 25 years and the transition lapses, and Rijksoverheid still writes that from 2027 the threshold goes from 16 to 25 years. Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [KG:213:2026:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html) and [Rijksoverheid on the car of the zaak](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen).

The WEV switch has its own age. Art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 years (2026) and 25 years (2027 text). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026, and Tax Plan 2027 would repair lid 5 onderdeel b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a). When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. Sources: [art. 3.20 lid 5, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto) and [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### 3.14 Youngtimer age, Tax Plan 2027

The bill proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028, and art. XLVI onderdeel B removes the 25-year step (art. I onderdeel E and art. II onderdeel B). Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The bill is not adopted on 2026-10-11, so use 25 years until adoption. Only the vote and Staatsblad publication settle the conflict. See section 7.

### 3.15 Youngtimer transition 2027, Tax Plan 2027

The bill keeps the 2026 rule in 2027 for a car that was available on or before 31 December 2025 and that became more than 16 but not more than 17 years old during 2026 (art. XLVII lid 3). Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). For 2026 the transition was an optional approval: the 2025 rule of 35% of the WEV stayed for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025, under the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, nr. 2026-2493, retroactive to 1 January 2026 and lapsing on 1 January 2027 or on codification. Source: [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html).

### 3.16 Zero-emission car, first admission 2027

Art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11 reduce the 22% for a car with 0 g CO2 per km by a percentage of the value, capped in euros. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission, and after that the rules then in force apply (lid 11). For a car first admitted in 2027 the reduction is 2% capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000 and 22% above. Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead, and from the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7. Sources: [art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.20, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01) and [art. 3.20, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01).

Reduction by year of first admission, per the statute: 2020 14% off capped € 6300 (so 8% up to € 45.000), 2021 10% off capped € 4.000 (so 12% up to € 40.000), 2022 6% off capped € 2.100 (so 16% up to € 35.000), 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800 (so 16% up to € 30.000), 2025 5% off capped € 1.500 (so 17% up to € 30.000), 2026 4% off capped € 1.200 (so 18% up to € 30.000), 2027 text 2% off capped € 600 (so 20% up to € 30.000). Source: [art. 3.20 lid 2, versions 2020 to 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Only hydrogen was uncapped in 2020. Tax Plan 2027 does not amend art. 3.20 lid 2. Source: [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### 3.17 Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets)

When a bicycle (fiets, including an e-bike or speed pedelec that also runs on muscle power) of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for commuting counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is parked at the home address only incidentally. Source: [art. 3.20a, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.18 Home workspace (werkruimte thuis)

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not a business asset are not deductible unless the workspace is a self-contained part of the home according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig) and the income test is met (art. 3.16 lid 1, lid 13 and from 2025 lid 14). With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit, wage (loon) and result from other activities must be earned mainly (hoofdzakelijk) in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent (in belangrijke mate) in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is business assets. The official criteria for a self-contained workspace are its own entrance (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the possibility of renting the space to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained. The overview page prints Home workspace 0%, with exceptions. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01) and [werkruimte in de woning](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning).

From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home classified as a business asset and used as the main residence (hoofdverblijf) are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The explanatory memorandum (MvT, memorie van toelichting) calls this a codification of case law. For a non-self-contained workspace in such a home the tenant's costs (huurderslasten, fixtures such as furniture (meubilair), soft furnishings (stoffering), wallpaper (behang) and painting work (schilderwerk), and gas, water and electricity) are not deductible. Corrected: the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page still presents the second route (an owner-occupied home classified as a business asset) without this limit, and the statute decides. Sources: [MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html) and [art. 3.16, 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01). The 2027 text of art. 3.16 matches 2026.

### 3.19 Telephone subscription at home (telefoonabonnement thuis)

Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel a Wet IB makes telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home not deductible. In practice a private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are, and a business telephone subscription is deductible while call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Telephone subscription at home 0% and Business telephone calls at home 100%. The statutory basis is unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Sources: [art. 3.16 lid 2 a, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten) and [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten).

### 3.20 Private computers and equipment (apparatuur)

Art. 3.16 lid 3 Wet IB excludes the costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments (muziekinstrumenten), audio equipment (geluidsapparatuur), tools (gereedschappen), computers and other similar equipment and video equipment (beeldapparatuur). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). The income-tax treatment of a laptop or computer that is business property follows asset classification, so equipment and instruments that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible. Source: [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel). Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Source: [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.21 Other exclusions: literature, clothing and personal care

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: literature except professional literature (vakliteratuur) (art. 3.16 lid 2 b), clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) (lid 2 c, work clothing defined in lid 5) and personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) (lid 2 d). Also not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business such as a driving licence (rijbewijs), health costs such as pedicure and health insurance (zorgverzekering), and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser (kapper). Work clothing is deductible. Sources: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.22 Pay to the partner (betaling aan de partner)

Pay to the partner is not deductible when it is lower than € 5000 (art. 3.16 lid 4). Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer (lid 11). Unchanged from 2020 to 2027. Source: [art. 3.16, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### 3.23 Asset classification (vermogensetikettering) of a business asset and a car

A business asset used 10% or less for business must be classified as compulsory private assets (verplicht privévermogen), while one used 90% or more must be classified as mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen). Intermediate use means freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen) within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once. A car must be classified as mandatory business assets when it is used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. It must be classified as compulsory private assets when used 90% or more privately. All other cases are freely classifiable assets. Sources: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel) and [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

A choice to classify an asset as freely classifiable can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable, and afterwards only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The knowledge group applies this to the youngtimer change, so revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Sources: [vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel) and [KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/).

### 3.24 Small purchases (kleine aanschaffingen)

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred, for example small office supplies (kantoorartikelen), professional literature and maintenance (onderhoud) of the car. A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when they are made for the work, and receipts and invoices must be kept. Source: [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten). Equipment and instruments that do not belong to the business assets stay at 0% under art. 3.16 lid 3. Source: [art. 3.16 lid 3, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.25 Fines and penalty payments (geldboetes en dwangsommen)

Not deductible under art. 3.14 lid 1: costs connected with fines (geldboeten) from a criminal court (strafrechter) and settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penal orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines (onderdeel c), costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted (onderdeel d) or for which a penal order became irrevocable (onderdeel e), and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb (onderdeel i). The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Traffic fines (verkeersboeten) under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. The overview page prints Fines 0%. Sources: [art. 3.14, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [beleidsbesluit section 1.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

Two scope rules. Costs made before the fine is imposed are not costs that relate to the imposed fine and stay deductible, while costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring), and costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught. Source: [KG:213:2024:5, published 11-03-2024](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/). Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum or settled by settlement (transactie) or penal order, are excluded. The example in the decree is a taxpayer convicted only for possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten) and not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Source: [beleidsbesluit section 2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html).

### 3.26 Study costs as business costs (studiekosten)

Study costs are deductible as a business cost when they are incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs, and the boundary is hard to draw. The 2022 abolition removed only the personal route, not this business-cost route. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f. Sources: [studiekosten voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers) and [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten). Study costs are also named under the costs with a mixed character that are deductible only for the business part. Source: [zakelijk of prive](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive).

### 3.27 Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek, personal route)

Up to and including 2021 training expenses (scholingsuitgaven: tuition fees (lesgeld), course fees (cursusgeld), college tuition (collegegeld), examination fees (examengeld), doctorate costs (promotiekosten) and required study materials (leermiddelen) excluding computers) were deductible above a threshold of € 250 and, outside the standard study period (standaardstudieperiode), up to € 15 000 (art. 6.27 to 6.30). No deduction applied when there was a right to student finance (studiefinanciering) for the study. The Act on the abolition of the fiscal deduction for training expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven, Stb. 2019, 514) took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349, and in the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked lapsed (vervallen) per 01-01-2022. Sources: [art. 6.27 to 6.30, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [art. 6.27, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01) and [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).

Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015, the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case. When the diploma period (diplomatermijn, usually 10 years) has passed and DUO definitively did not convert the performance grant into a gift, the taxpayer deducts in that return year the amount he could not deduct earlier because of the performance grant. The caps are € 1.693 per study year (mbo) and € 2.443 per study year (hbo or wo), part-year € 141,09 per month (mbo) and € 203,59 per month (hbo or wo), and when the actual performance grant was lower the lower amount applies. The € 250 threshold applies per person. FISIN 2026 narrows the case to an extended diploma period of at most 15 years, so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. Sources: [art. 10a.16, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [FISIN 2026, aftrek studiekosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten). No FISIN 2027 is published, so the 2027 position is unknown, although art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text. See section 7.

The non-fiscal replacement was the STAP budget (STAP-budget), which took effect at the same moment as the abolition and had a maximum subsidy of € 1.000 including VAT per training with six application windows per calendar year (art. 7 of the regulation). The regulation is marked lapsed (Regeling vervallen) per 01-01-2024. Sources: [Regeling STAP-budget, 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045419/2023-01-01) and [Stb. 2021, 349](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html).

### 3.28 Public transport, taxi and aircraft (openbaar vervoer en vliegtuig)

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview. Source: [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten).

### 3.29 VAT (btw) on a private car used for business

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, though it is business for the income tax. In a BV the director and substantial shareholder (DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer), he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without conclusive mileage records all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Source: [zakelijk gebruik van de priveauto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto).

### 3.30 Small businesses scheme (KOR) and the income-tax cost base

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or VAT may not be deducted, as under the small businesses scheme (KOR), deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted (also not VAT paid in another EU country), earlier deductions may need adjustment (herziening), the reduced motor vehicle tax rate for vans (mrb) remains available, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year, and the choice is per entrepreneur covering all sub-numbers (subnummers). Sources: [welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten) and [wat betekent meedoen met de kleineondernemersregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/). The KOR threshold history per year belongs to the VAT topic.

### 3.31 Pseudo-final levy (pseudo-eindheffing) on new petrol or diesel lease cars (BV)

Separate from the income-tax car rules, and on the same Rijksoverheid page, from 1 January 2027 employers pay a pseudo-final levy of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars that are also used privately, with a transition until 16 September 2030 for cars provided before 2027. This is relevant for a director and substantial shareholder with a BV car and it was not checked further in the material. Source: [Rijksoverheid on the car of the zaak](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen). See section 7.
