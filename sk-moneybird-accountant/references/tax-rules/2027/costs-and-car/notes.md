## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel): 2026 was € 5.700, final and enacted ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026)). For 2027 the 2027 text gives € 5.700 before indexation, and the effective amount is not published yet, so the 2027 value is unknown until the December 2026 indexation regulation ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Election instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel): 80% in both years, also for recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters) ([2026 change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Corporate income tax (Vpb) variant: 73,5% and the fixed amount or 0,4% of wage (loon) if higher, verified for 2026 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked, see section 7.
- Travel and accommodation for conferences and courses (reis en verblijf congressen en cursussen): maximum € 1500 in both years, not indexed ([2026 overview page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding): 2026 applied € 0,25 per km retroactively to 1 January 2026 under the policy decree (beleidsbesluit) of 17 May 2026, nr. 2026-8260, and that decree lapses on 1 January 2027 ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026)). The enacted 2027 text goes to € 0,23, and Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) proposes € 0,25 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Same decree, other travel expenses in 2026: the wage tax (loonbelasting) travel allowance (reiskostenvergoeding) of art. 31a lid 2 a 3° Wet LB went to € 0,25 per km instead of € 0,23, and the other flat-rate travel expenses (forfaitaire reiskosten) in the Wet IB (art. 6.17 lid 6 a, 6.26 with art. 40 lid 1 b URIB and 6.36 lid 2) also went to € 0,25 ([Stcrt. 2026, 18302](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html)).
- Relocation costs (verhuiskosten) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting): the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 and a maximum of two years for housing outside the place of residence, unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Private-use addition (bijtelling) for a car, standard 22%, and the pre-2017 minimum of 25%: unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Youngtimer: 2026 used 16 years, with an optional transition for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html)). The enacted 2027 text goes to 25 years and the 2026 transition lapses per 1 January 2027, while the bill proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028 with its own transition for cars covered by the 2026 rule ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Stcrt. 2026, 6596](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Zero-emission reduction: 2026 was 4% off capped € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000, and the 2027 text goes to 2% off capped € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets) 7%, home workspace (werkruimte thuis) 0% with exceptions, telephone subscription at home 0%, private computers and equipment (apparatuur) 0%, fines (geldboetes) 0%, public transport 100% of actual costs (werkelijke kosten), small businesses scheme (kleineondernemersregeling) (KOR) turnover limit € 20.000 and the VAT car flat rate (forfait) of 1,5%: unchanged from 2026 to 2027 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten), [KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/), [btw en de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek): 2026 was limited to the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case with an extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) of at most 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten)). No FISIN 2027 is published, so the 2027 position is unknown, although art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- New from 1 January 2027: the pseudo-final levy (pseudo-eindheffing) of 12% of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately, with a transition until 16 September 2030 for cars provided before 2027 ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record mixed costs (gemengde kosten) separately from other costs, so the threshold (drempel) or the 80% election (keuze) can be computed. The election is made in the return per art. 3.15 lid 5, and the threshold is not time-proportioned when the enterprise starts during the year ([art. 3.15, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [besluit section 3.1](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).
- Keep receipts and invoices for deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten). Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte), and including VAT when no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted ([welke kosten mag ik aftrekken van mijn bijverdiensten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten)).
- Under the KOR, no VAT is charged or paid and no VAT on business costs (bedrijfskosten) and investments is deducted, the choice is per entrepreneur covering all sub-numbers (subnummers), and exit is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year ([KOR](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/)).
- For a private vehicle (privévervoermiddel) used for business, record the business kilometres, because the deduction is the fixed amount per kilometre and fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll and parking are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) separately ([art. 3.15 lid 6, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- For the car, keep trip registration (rittenregistratie) or another record to show private kilometres (privékilometers). A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive, and a declaration of exclusive business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible ([art. 3.20, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Private use (privégebruik) of an asset classified as a business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) is recorded as a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking) ([vermogensetikettering bedrijfsmiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel)).
- For a private car (privéauto) used for business on the VAT side, without conclusive mileage records (zonder sluitende kilometeradministratie) deduct all VAT on use and maintenance and correct at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) including VAT and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance ([btw en de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto)).
- Keep the proof for travel: the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview for public transport, taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig), where 100% of the actual costs (werkelijke kosten) is deductible ([overzicht mogelijk aftrekbare zakelijke kosten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten)).
- For a BV, the Vpb threshold variant applies per body when there is at least one employee in the Wet LB sense, and in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) it applies once at the parent ([Wet Vpb 1969, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), art. 8 lid 5, and [besluit section 3.4](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Effective threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) for 2027 is unknown. It waits for the December 2026 indexation regulation. Tax Plan 2027 does not amend art. 3.15 lid 1. The bill uses a table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of 1,01248 for other amounts in art. L, and that factor is not confirmed for art. 3.15. Sources: [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01) and [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The published regulation settles it.
- Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding) 2027, € 0,23 against € 0,25. The enacted 2027 text prints € 0,23 in art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 b ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Tax Plan 2027 proposes € 0,25 (art. I onderdelen C and D, [kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The bill is not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([wetsvoorstel 37022](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022)), so use € 0,23 until adoption.
- Youngtimer age 2027, 25 years against 17 years. The enacted 2027 text sets 25 years ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Tax Plan 2027 proposes 17 years in 2027 and 20 years from 1 January 2028, with transitional rules in art. XLVII ([kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). Only the vote and Staatsblad publication settle it, and the instruction here is to use 25 until adoption. The claim that the CPB note describes a further gradual raise to 25 years is not supported, because the bill moves away from 25.
- WEV switch age. Art. 3.20 lid 5 onderdeel b keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 years (2026) and 25 years (2027 text) ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026 ([waarde van de auto](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto)). Tax Plan 2027 would replace the word "vijftien" by 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026 (art. I onderdeel E onder 2 with art. LXIII lid 1 a, [kst-37022-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)), proposed. In practice the Belastingdienst position settles it, in law only adoption does.
- Vpb variant of the threshold for 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The mechanism of 0,4% of wage if higher than the fixed amount and the 73,5% election is verified for 2020 and 2026 in the statute ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text of art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 was not checked. Checking the 2027 Wet Vpb text settles it.
- Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) 2027 is unknown. No FISIN 2027 is published, and the FISIN 2026 page is the last checked one ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten)). Art. 10a.16 stays in the 2027 text ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)), but whether any extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) case remains in 2027 is not on a fetched page. A published FISIN 2027 page settles it.
- The content of the memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 2 October 2026 on Tax Plan 2027 was not fetched, because kst-37022 numbers above 2 did not resolve on zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl. It could touch these items. The published document (stuk) settles it.
- CPB certification date. The certification note (certificeringsnotitie) is dated 1 March 2026 on Rijksoverheid and is an annex to a parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 24 March 2026 ([certificeringsnotitie](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/2026/03/01/certificeringsnotitie-cpb-verhogen-leeftijdsgrens-youngtimerregeling)). A CPB certification date of 27 March 2026 from cpb.nl is not confirmed by a listed primary source, and the note page itself does not state the age path.
- Pseudo-final levy (pseudo-eindheffing) for new petrol or diesel lease cars from 1 January 2027 is UNVERIFIED beyond the Rijksoverheid page, which was not checked further ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen)). It concerns a BV with a lease car and not the income-tax car rules. The underlying act text settles it.
