## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 KIA base table in the 2027 text, before indexation

The small-scale investment deduction (kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek) is a per business (per-onderneming) deduction, claimed by choice in the tax return (aangifte), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year. The 2027 text of art. 3.41 lid 2 has five bands. Nil up to € 2.900. 28% of the investment up to € 71.683. A fixed € 20.072 up to € 132.746. Then € 20.072 minus 7,56% of the part above € 132.746, up to € 398.236. Nil above € 398.236. Those amounts are the 2026 amounts before the indexation of art. 10.1, so the final 2027 amounts are not yet known (section 7). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) year pages phrase the same window as a range the taxpayer must invest in, for 2023 as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.601 en € 353.973 investeren" and for 2026 as "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236". The deduction is taken once on the total investment in the calendar year and not per asset, while the per-asset minimum of 3.3 still applies to every asset.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026

### 3.2 KIA in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband)

In a partnership (samenwerkingsverband, for example a vof or a maatschap) the investments for the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by that sum. This is art. 3.41 lid 3. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KIA page adds that each partner (firmant or vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)profit (winst), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently. The same article is in the 2027 text, so the method holds for 2027.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia

### 3.3 KIA per-asset minimum and the exclusions of art. 3.45

Every asset must have an investment amount of at least € 450 to count for the KIA. That is art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b in the 2027 text. Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes assets intended mainly for certain uses from every investment deduction (investeringsaftrek), so from the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. The list covers use in a business (onderneming) whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting) (a), land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering) (b), dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen) (c), passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 of the Wet BPM 1992 (d), vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), securities (effecten), receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies) (f), and animals (dieren) (g). Lid 2 adds two KIA-only exclusions. Assets intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 450 (b). Lid 3 makes the MIA more generous, art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdelen c and d do not apply to the MIA, so dwellings and passenger cars can still earn the MIA. Lid 4 adds two EIA and MIA exclusions, assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises (a) and assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 (b). Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag".

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.4 Excluded obligations, including assets bought from an own BV

Art. 3.46 gives no investment deduction for obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam, for example a BV) and that entity (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1 under lid 2, which is the exemption (ontheffing) route. FISIN states it plainly. Buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek". This matters for a sole trader who also owns a BV.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.5 KIA 2027 indexation factor

Bill 37 022, Article LI lid 1, multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts at the start of 2027 by 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor). The bill was submitted on Budget Day (Prinsjesdag), its parliamentary paper (kamerstuk) was published on 29 September 2026 and it was not adopted on 2026-10-11, so the factor is proposed. The bill text itself contains no change to art. 3.41, so the bands and the percentages of the KIA stay as they are and only the amounts move.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2

### 3.6 Share of the inflation correction applied

Only secondary sources state that 48% of an inflation correction of 2,6% is applied. Those documents were not readable to the fetch tool. The bill text itself states the factor 1,01248 and not the 48% share. So this item is UNVERIFIED and it is listed again in section 7.

Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1

### 3.7 Indexed 2027 KIA amounts

The indexed 2027 KIA amounts are not published. They wait for the December 2026 indexation regulation and for the vote on bill 37 022. For 2027 the unindexed 2027 text table of 3.1 is the only published version, and it carries status adopted-not-yet-in-force.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html

### 3.8 EIA percentage, enacted 2027 text

The energy investment deduction (EIA, energie-investeringsaftrek) is 40% in art. 3.42 lid 3 of the 2027 text. The EIA applies only to new assets, so to assets that are "niet eerder gebruikt" (not used before). A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. The EIA asset must be on the Energy List (Energielijst). Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Energy List requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as production costs (voortbrengingskosten, costs of own production). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km, see 3.14. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset. The EIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15. The maximum investment amount for the EIA is € 153.000.000, see 3.10. Whether the rate stays at 40% or becomes 45,5% depends on the adoption of bill 37 022, see 3.9 and section 7.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.9 EIA percentage, bill 37 022

Article I part F of bill 37 022 replaces "«40 percent»" with "«45,5 percent»" from 1 January 2027. The status is proposed, not adopted on 2026-10-11. The Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) news item of 15 September 2026 says "van 40% naar 45%". The bill text is the legal proposal, and the difference is a conflict that only the adopted law settles. It is listed in section 7.

Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien

### 3.10 EIA and MIA maximum investment amount

The maximum investment amount for the EIA and the MIA is € 153.000.000 in art. 3.42 lid 4 of the 2027 text, before indexation. The bill multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts, so the final maximum waits for the December 2026 regulation. Separately, RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year for the MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List (Milieulijst) sets a lower maximum, see 3.16. From 2026 the EIA page adds the investments in a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) and in a partnership together for the EIA maximum, and the checked 2027 material states no change to that rule.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia

### 3.11 MIA percentages

The environmental investment deduction (MIA, milieu-investeringsaftrek) has three categories (categorieën). From 1 January 2022 the Environmental List maps codes starting with F or G to category I (categorie I) (45%), codes starting with A or D to category II (36%) and codes starting with B or E to category III (27%). The 2027 text of art. 3.42a lid 3 has 45%, 36% and 27%, and bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.42a. The MIA asset must be on the Environmental List. Only new assets qualify, with the same newness conditions as the EIA, see 3.8. A MIA investment has a minimum of € 2.500 per asset, see 3.12. No MIA is given on costs that already received the EIA, and the Vamil remains possible for those costs. The EIA and the MIA cannot be combined for one asset, while the MIA can be combined with the KIA. Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. The MIA must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15. The checked sources give no 2027 budget for the MIA.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.12 EIA and MIA per-asset minimum

An asset with an investment amount below € 2.500 does not qualify for the EIA or the MIA. That is art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b in the 2027 text. RVO phrases the MIA and Vamil floor as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag". The per-asset amount is the investment amount per business asset and not the total of the year.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden

### 3.13 Vamil percentage

Art. 3.31 lid 1 says that the costs of a designated environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven" (may be depreciated at will for 75 percent by the taxpayer), and that the rest follows the normal schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit for buildings, does not apply to these assets. Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that were not used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil. RVO states that the total tax benefit of the MIA and the Vamil together "kan netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (can add up to more than 14% of the investment amount net). The asset must be on the Environmental List and must be reported to RVO within 3 months, see 3.15.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.14 RVO newness test for a vehicle

Only new assets qualify for the EIA and the MIA, so an asset that was never used before. RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and for at most 6.000 km. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List requirements can qualify, and (second-hand) parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the taxpayer can qualify as production costs.

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers

### 3.15 RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

The investment must be reported to the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO, Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland) within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order for acquisition costs (aanschafkosten). For production costs the report is due within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Environmental List that is valid on the signing or order date applies. FISIN 2020 to 2026 all say "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)."

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.16 MIA and Vamil maximum per business asset and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per business asset and per applicant per year for the MIA and the Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum. The RVO page states no year, so it is unknown which years the cap covers for 2027. It is a condition of the designation (aanwijzing) and stands next to the statutory maximum of € 153.000.000 of 3.10. The Environmental List regulation of each year would settle the years and any lower maximum. This item is UNVERIFIED and is listed again in section 7.

Sources: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4

### 3.17 Payment cap for an asset not yet in use

Art. 3.44 lid 1 applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA. If the asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments that were made, and the excess follows in later years. Lid 2 states that at cessation (staking) of the business the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.44, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek

### 3.18 Ordinary depreciation caps

Art. 3.30 lid 1 says that the yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition or production costs (aanschaffings- of voortbrengingskosten) that is attributable to the year. Lid 2 caps the yearly depreciation at 10% of the cost for goodwill and at 20% for other business assets, so at least 10 years for goodwill and at least 5 years for other business assets. Lid 3 allows production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) to be written off at once in the year of production (voortbrenging). Lid 4 allows low-value items (voorwerpen van geringe waarde) that are normally treated as current costs to be written off at once in the year of acquisition or production.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30

### 3.19 Minimum value for buildings

Art. 3.30a lid 1 allows depreciation on a building only while its book value is above the minimum value, and the depreciation is at most the difference. Lid 2 treats a building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) as one business asset, with detachable machinery (werktuigen) as separate assets. Lid 3 sets one minimum value, "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" (the WOZ value of the building). That has been the rule from 2024, while 2020 to 2023 used the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another building. Lid 4 says the WOZ value is the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Wet WOZ for the calendar year of the depreciation. The 2026 text also has lid 5 to 10 on a co-owned building pro rata, ground owned by an unrelated party that reduces the WOZ value, and related persons and related entities (verbonden lichamen). Bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.30a.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a

### 3.20 Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold

Art. 3.47 lid 1 says that if in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than the yearly threshold, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage that was taken is added to profit. The 2027 text has € 2.900 as the base amount. The indexed amount is unknown until the December 2026 regulation, and the bill multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts, so the final threshold is not published yet. For 2026 the threshold was € 2.900.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

### 3.21 Disinvestment addition window and what counts as a disposal

Paragraph 2 limits the disinvestment addition (bijtelling) to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken. Paragraph 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the business (a), a change of its destination so that it meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a (mainly made available to third parties, KIA) or lid 4 onderdeel a (made available to non-residents, EIA and MIA) (b), and a change that brings it under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). Paragraph 4 counts the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) as the transfer price in those cases. Paragraph 5 counts the undoing of an investment, or the receipt of a reduction, refund or compensation (vermindering, teruggaaf or vergoeding), as a disposal for that amount. Paragraph 7 lets the Minister disapply paragraph 3, subparagraph c and paragraph 6. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA and not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds cessation of part or all of the business within the term. Bill 37 022 would drop ", onder 2°" from art. 3.47 lid 3 onderdeel c from 1 January 2029, if it is adopted.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article III part E

### 3.22 Disinvestment undo test

Lid 6 defines the undoing of an investment to include not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation, unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year. Lid 7 lets the Minister disapply this paragraph.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 6 and lid 7, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling

### 3.23 Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (willekeurige afschrijving): yearly cap

The arbitrary depreciation scheme for start-up entrepreneurs rests on art. 3.34 of the Wet IB 2001, which allows arbitrary depreciation on assets designated by ministerial regulation that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship. URWA art. 7 lid 1 designates assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), applies. Paragraph 2 also covers the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Paragraph 3 limits the scheme to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer). Art. 8 allows the arbitrary depreciation "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already randomly depreciated on another ground. For 2027 that maximum is the top of the 2027 KIA table, € 398.236 before indexation, and the final amount follows the indexed KIA table. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the scheme is open to a sole proprietorship, civil-law partnership (maatschap), cv or vof, that the starter's deduction conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for the investment deduction count, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value, with the difference as profit, is required within 5 years, or 10 years for sea-going vessels (zeeschepen), if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf). The cap rests on reading the art. 8 cross-reference. No primary page prints the cap as a number.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 and art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer

### 3.24 Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs: period

URWA art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 sets ten years for the sea-going vessels of art. 10. The period is the window in which the revaluation rule of 3.23 can be triggered.

Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 9 and art. 12

### 3.25 Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs: abolition

The Government of the Netherlands news item of 15 September 2026 states that the start-up depreciation scheme is abolished per 2028. The status is proposed and the start-up depreciation scheme is still available in 2027. The URWA is a ministerial regulation, and the fetched bill text does not name the scheme. Bill 37 022 sets the art. 3.76 lid 3 starter's deduction amount to € 10 under Article I part G, and the fetched summary gave its entry date inconsistently, so the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) check owns that date. URWA art. 7 only needs the increased self-employed deduction to apply, so a € 10 starter's deduction would keep the start-up depreciation scheme available in 2027.

Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part G, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7

### 3.26 Temporary arbitrary depreciation 2023

URWA art. 13 lid 1 covers assets not used before for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in calendar year 2023 and which the taxpayer took into use before 1 January 2026. Paragraph 2 excludes buildings (gebouwen), vessels, aircraft (vliegtuigen), mopeds (bromfietsen), motorcycles (motorrijwielen) and passenger cars, except for commercial road transport (beroepsvervoer over de weg) or 0 gram CO2 per km, and also intangible assets, animals, assets mainly made available to third parties (short rental to successive hirers excepted), public roads and paths, and assets already randomly depreciated on another ground. Paragraph 3 moves the 1 January 2026 date by the days that special circumstances justify. Art. 14 allows at most 50% of the costs, once, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 15 ends the period in which the asset must keep meeting art. 13 on 31 December 2025. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that only 2023 costs count and that an asset not yet in use is capped at the amount paid in the investment year. IB and Vpb both. In 2027 no new claim can arise, because the use deadline has passed.
