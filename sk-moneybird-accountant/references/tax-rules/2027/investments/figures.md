## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the investment deductions for 2027, the small-scale investment deduction (KIA, kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), the energy investment deduction (EIA, energie-investeringsaftrek), the environmental investment deduction (MIA, milieu-investeringsaftrek), the Vamil (arbitrary depreciation for designated environmental assets), the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling), ordinary depreciation including the minimum value (bodemwaarde) for buildings, and arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for start-up entrepreneurs. The year is a mix: the text of the Wet IB 2001 that is valid on 1 January 2027 has been published and still prints the 2026 amounts before indexation, bill 37 022 was submitted on Budget Day (Prinsjesdag), its parliamentary paper (kamerstuk) was published on 29 September 2026 and it was not adopted on 2026-10-11, and the December 2026 indexation regulation with the final 2027 amounts is not yet published. The bill proposes a higher EIA percentage, a multiplier of 1,01248 on the art. 10.1 amounts and the abolition of the start-up depreciation scheme per 2028. Everything proposed or not yet published is marked in section 2 and collected in section 7.
