## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA table. 2026 was enacted with nil up to € 2.900, 28% to € 71.683, € 20.072 to € 132.746, € 20.072 minus 7,56% of the excess over € 132.746 up to € 398.236 and nil above € 398.236. The 2027 text prints the same amounts before indexation, and bill 37 022 Article LI lid 1 multiplies the art. 10.1 amounts by 1,01248 at the start of 2027, so the final 2027 amounts will be higher. The final amounts are not published. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1
- EIA percentage. 2026 was enacted at 40%. The 2027 text also reads 40%, and bill 37 022 proposes 45,5% from 1 January 2027. The Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) news item says 45%. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount. € 153.000.000 in 2026 and € 153.000.000 in the 2027 text before indexation. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4
- MIA percentages. 45% (I), 36% (II) and 27% (III) in 2026 and the same three percentages in the 2027 text. Bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.42a. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3
- KIA per-asset minimum. € 450 in 2026 and € 450 in the 2027 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b
- EIA and MIA per-asset minimum. € 2.500 in 2026 and € 2.500 in the 2027 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b
- Vamil percentage. 75% arbitrary depreciation in 2026 and 75% in the 2027 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1
- Ordinary depreciation caps. Goodwill 10% and other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) 20% per year in 2026 and in the 2027 text. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2
- Minimum value (bodemwaarde). The WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) of the building in 2026 and in the 2027 text, and bill 37 022 does not change art. 3.30a. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3
- Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold. € 2.900 in 2026 and € 2.900 as the base in the 2027 text before indexation. The indexed 2027 amount is unknown until December 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (willekeurige afschrijving). The 2026 cap was € 398.236 of total costs, read from the top of the KIA table. The 2027 cap is the top of the 2027 KIA table, € 398.236 before indexation, and the final amount follows the indexed KIA table. Bill 37 022 proposes to abolish the scheme per 2028, so 2027 is the last year in which the scheme is available. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien
- MIA and Vamil budget. 2026 had MIA € 135.000.000 and Vamil € 20.000.000. The checked sources give no 2027 budget. Source: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers
- EIA aggregation rule. In 2026 the EIA page added the investments in a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) and in a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) together for the EIA maximum. The checked 2027 material states no change to that rule. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record each investment with the date of the obligation or the production costs (voortbrengingskosten), the supplier invoice and the amount paid, because the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) is taken on the total investment in the calendar year and the KIA is claimed by choice in the tax return (aangifte). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 1 and lid 2
- If an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, take the investment deduction only over the payments made and carry the excess to later years. At cessation (staking) of the business (onderneming) the full investment deduction is taken in the year of cessation. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.44
- Keep the newness evidence for assets intended for the EIA or the MIA, because a second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify, and a vehicle counts as new only if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden
- Register the Energy List (Energielijst) or Environmental List (Milieulijst) code of each asset. The code decides the deduction, with F or G as MIA category I (categorie I) (45%), A or D as category II (36%) and B or E as category III (27%), and codes starting with A, B, C or F for 75% Vamil. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html
- File the RVO notification within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) or placing the order, and within 3 months after the quarter in which production costs were made. The Environmental List valid on the signing or order date applies. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers
- Watch the € 450 per-asset minimum for the KIA and the € 2.500 per-asset minimum for the EIA and MIA. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b and lid 4 onderdeel b
- An asset bought from a BV in which the taxpayer holds at least one third of the shares in principle gives no investment deduction, with the ministerial exemption (ontheffing) route as the exception. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.46, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek
- Keep each asset in the records for five years after the investment year, because a disposal within that window triggers the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling), rental counts for the KIA, a withdrawal or a change of use counts as a disposal, and the 25% payment test and the three-year use test apply. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling
- Keep the yearly WOZ decision (WOZ-beschikking) for each building, because the minimum value (bodemwaarde) is the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) for the calendar year and depreciation is possible only while the book value is above it and at most up to the difference. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 1 and lid 4
- Keep a 10-year schedule for goodwill and a 5-year schedule for other business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) at the maximum yearly rates of 10% and 20%. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2
- For a starter, keep the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) evidence and record the free choice of assets. The minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply to the arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving), and a rented-out asset, an asset under a double-tax treaty or an asset used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf) must be revalued to the normal book value within 5 years, or 10 years for sea-going vessels (zeeschepen). https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9 and art. 12, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer
- The temporary arbitrary depreciation of 2023 applied to IB and Vpb both, but the use deadline of 1 January 2026 has passed, so no new claim can be booked in 2027. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- EIA 2027 conflict. The bill text says 45,5%, the Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) news item says "van 40% naar 45%". Bill text: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part F. News item: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/15/belastingplan-2027-voorstellen-voor-beter-werkend-belastingstelsel-en-gezonde-overheidsfinancien. The bill text is the legal proposal. The conflict is settled only by the adopted law.
- Indexed 2027 KIA amounts, disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold and EIA and MIA maximum. Not published. They wait for the December 2026 indexation regulation and the vote on bill 37 022. Until then the 2027 text amounts apply, with status adopted-not-yet-in-force. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1
- Share of the inflation correction applied. The 48% of 2,6% rests on secondary sources only, and those documents were not readable to the fetch tool. The bill text states the factor 1,01248. A readable framework (kader) document or the December 2026 indexation regulation would settle it. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article LI lid 1
- Two KIA claims could not be confirmed. First, that an earlier plan to slim the KIA was replaced by another measure. Second, a search snippet of the factsheet that suggested a lower KIA maximum. The fetched bill text contains no change to art. 3.41. The factsheet PDF exceeded the fetch limit and the CW 3.1 PDF was unreadable. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html
- MIA and Vamil maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant per year. The RVO page states no year, so it is unknown which years the cap covers for 2027. It is a condition of the designation (aanwijzing), separate from the statutory € 153.000.000 of art. 3.42 lid 4. The Environmental List (Milieulijst) regulation of each year would settle it. https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden
- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek) in bill 37 022. The bill sets the art. 3.76 lid 3 amount to € 10 under Article I part G, and the fetched summary gave its entry date inconsistently, so the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) check owns that date. URWA art. 7 only needs the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) to apply, so a € 10 starter's deduction keeps the start-up depreciation scheme available in 2027. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html Article I part G, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7
- The rule that a co-entitled person (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) cannot use the EIA or the MIA was not found on the fetched pages. UNVERIFIED. The pages checked are https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia
- Arbitrary depreciation for start-up entrepreneurs (willekeurige afschrijving): cap, UNVERIFIED: the cap for 2027 is the top of the 2027 KIA table at € 398.236 before indexation, read from the URWA art. 8 cross-reference. No primary page prints the cap as a number. The final amount follows the indexed KIA table. https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 8, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2
