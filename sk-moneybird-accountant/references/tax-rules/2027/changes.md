## 1. OVERVIEW

This note lists what changed for tax year 2027. Section 2 holds the changes unique to 2027 and section 3 the generated table of every change by topic.

---

## 2. UNIQUE TO 2027

- The self-employed person (zelfstandige)'s deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) fell from € 1.200 in 2026 to € 900 in 2027 and the amount for those at state pension age (AOW) from € 600 to € 450. [Income tax (inkomstenbelasting)](income-tax/notes.md#5-changes)
- The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) fell from 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 to 11,91% above € 79.569 in the 2027 text. [entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek)](business-deductions/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto) first admitted in 2027 gets 2% off capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000, against 4% off capped at € 1.200 in 2026. [Costs and the car (kosten en auto)](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- New from 1 January 2027 is a pseudo-final levy (pseudo-eindheffing) of 12% of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately. [Costs and the car](costs-and-car/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) by assessment (aanslag) goes from 4,85% in 2026 to 5,02% in 2027 (proposed, a budget estimate until the ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) sets the rate). [Zvw contribution](health-insurance-contribution/notes.md#5-changes)
- The income cap (aftoppingsgrens) on the contribution base (premiegrondslag) stays € 137.800 in 2026 and € 137.800 in the 2027 text, with a proposed freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032. [Pension and disability insurance (pensioen en AOV)](pension-and-disability-insurance/notes.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) lapse, so default penalties (verzuimboetes) are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. [Procedure](procedure/notes.md#5-changes)
- The VBAR entered into force (in werking trad) on 31 December 2026, so the legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) applies throughout 2027. [employment relationship (arbeidsrelatie)](work-relationship/notes.md#5-changes)
