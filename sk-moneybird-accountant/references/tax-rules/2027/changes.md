## 1. Unique to 2027

- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 1.200 in 2026 to € 900 in 2027 and the AOW-age amount from € 600 to € 450. [inkomstenbelasting](inkomstenbelasting.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing fell from 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 to 11,91% above € 79.569 in the 2027 text. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2027 gets 2% off capped at € 600, so 20% up to € 30.000, against 4% off capped at € 1.200 in 2026. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto.md#5-changes)
- New from 1 January 2027 is a pseudo-eindheffing of 12% of the catalogue price per year for new petrol or diesel lease cars used privately. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag goes from 4,85% in 2026 to 5,02% in 2027 (proposed, a budget estimate until the ministerial regulation sets the rate). [zvw](zvw.md#5-changes)
- The aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag stays € 137.800 in 2026 and € 137.800 in the 2027 text, with a proposed freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032. [pensioen-en-aov](pensioen-en-aov.md#5-changes)
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse, so verzuimboetes are possible again and the default bedrijfsbezoek start ends. [procedure](procedure.md#5-changes)
- The VBAR entered into force on 31 December 2026, so the rechtsvermoeden applies throughout 2027. [arbeidsrelatie](arbeidsrelatie.md#5-changes)
