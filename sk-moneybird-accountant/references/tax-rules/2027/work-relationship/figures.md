## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Tax year 2027 is the second calendar year in which the normal enforcement rules for employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) apply, after the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) ended on 1 January 2025. The Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, Wet DBA) (the law on assessing work relationships) and the case law on employment (loondienst) still decide whether work is an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). The Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) (the act on a legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) of an employment contract based on an hourly rate) is in force (in werking) from 31 December 2026, so its low hourly rate presumption applies throughout 2027 at about € 38. On 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the DBA soft landing (zachte landing) lapse, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) ambition is to move to regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving), but no separate 2027 enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) was published on 2026-10-11. The Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ) bill and the Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) are proposed and are not in force in 2027.
