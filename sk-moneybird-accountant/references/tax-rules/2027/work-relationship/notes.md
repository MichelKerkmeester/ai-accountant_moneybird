## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA boetebeleid: 2026 had no verzuimboetes and allowed vergrijpboetes under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, with no vergrijpboetes for correcties over 2025. In 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse on 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2027 verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Start of a control: in 2026 the control started in principle with a bedrijfsbezoek, and the starting point to examine only the most recent aangiftetijdvak in a boekenonderzoek lapsed. In 2027 there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Handhavingsplan: 2026 had a separate plan, versie 2.0 of 31 December 2025, made after Kamerstukken 31 311, nrs. 298 and 301 of 18 December 2025 and the Kamerbrief of 19 December 2025, with 80 fte capacity. For 2027 the ambition is full integration into regular handhaving with no separate plan, and no 2027 plan was published on 2026-10-11. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Regular handhaving: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Ingroeimodel: no change. Corrections not before 1 January 2025, full 5 years only from 2030. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- NLA meldingen: usable in the handhaving from 2026. The checked 2027 sources state no change. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- 2026 enforcement focus: the 2026 plan pays extra attention to government bodies as opdrachtgevers, uses the detectiemodule TKA, and keeps horizontal supervision levels for fiscal service providers. The checked 2027 sources state no change but also no new plan. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2026 the act was adopted-not-yet-in-force with entry into force on 31 December 2026. In 2027 it applies throughout the year. The hourly amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is € 38. The 2027 amount is about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026 and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027, UNVERIFIED. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html) and [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).
- VBAR first-application regulation: UNVERIFIED in 2026 and still UNVERIFIED in 2027. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 was not found on 2026-10-11. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).
- Modelovereenkomsten: usable to 31 December 2029 and no new ones since 6 September 2024. No change from 2026 to 2027. Source: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- BAZ: in 2026 the status was awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag. In 2027 there is no later status than awaiting the nota, and a procedure meeting was set for 13 October 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- BAZ waiting period: the 2026 open point, 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary, is not resolved in the 2027 sources. Both are proposed and the bill text was not reopened. Sources: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912) and [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html).
- Zelfstandigenwet: in 2026 the internet consultation was open until 29 October 2026, with an intended entry into force in January 2028. In 2027 the act is still not in force and the intended date stays January 2028. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the facts that decide the qualification for each engagement. The contract, any modelovereenkomst, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, whether the work must be done personally, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. These are the weighing factors of the loondienst test. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Not being in loondienst does not by itself make the income winst uit onderneming (business profit) for income tax or a business for VAT. Check the qualification separately for each box. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The checked sources for this topic do not state the exact return line. They state only that not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT.
- Record the hourly rate for each engagement. Under art. 7:610aa BW a rate of at most € 36 per hour, or the indexed amount, gives the werkende a rechtsvermoeden (legal presumption). The opdrachtgever then must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html) and [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The first-application regulation is not published, see section 7.
- If a modelovereenkomst is used, keep the approved agreement and evidence that the parties work exactly as described. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used to and including 31 December 2029. Sources: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- If the qualification is a dienstbetrekking, the pay goes into the loonaangifte of the opdrachtgever. Since 1 January 2025 there is no aanwijzing step and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. From 1 January 2027 verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Where a bv is the opdrachtgever, the same Wet DBA test and the VBAR presumption apply. The VBAR does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).
- Keep the follow-up of any standpunt or vooroverleg in the file. Vooroverleg gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- After LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie. Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. An opdrachtnemer in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open (winst)aangifte or tijdvakken examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Bewaarplicht (record-keeping duty): AWR art. 52 lid 2 covers natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden (income from other activities). Keep the gegevensdragers for seven years under AWR art. 52 lid 4. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table gives 7 years for basisgegevens, 10 years for onroerende zaken (immovable property) and One Stop Shop data, and 7 years or shorter for other data by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- The checked 2027 sources state no separate arbeidsrelatie filing deadline. For completeness, the income tax return for tax year 2026, filed in 2027, was not published on 2026-10-11 and is usually due 1 May 2027. Source: [Belastingdienst aangifte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Separate 2027 handhavingsplan: unknown, UNVERIFIED. It was not published on 2026-10-11. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). A new plan or an update of the page would settle it.
- Regular handhaving from 1 January 2027: proposed, Verified. It is stated as an ambition, not as a fixed legal change. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). A later plan or a published decision would settle it.
- VBAR hourly amount 2027: about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. adopted-not-yet-in-force, UNVERIFIED. Source: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027 would settle it.
- VBAR first-application regulation: not found on 2026-10-11. adopted-not-yet-in-force, UNVERIFIED. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). Publication in the Staatscourant would settle it.
- BAZ status: proposed, UNVERIFIED. No later status than awaiting the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, with a procedure meeting on 13 October 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). The nota and further Kamerstukken would settle it.
- BAZ waiting period and percentage: the docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage and a waiting period of about one year. proposed, Verified. The 2027 sources fix no 2027 figure and give no 2027 minimum wage. The 2026 open point about 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year stays open, see section 5. The bill text or the nota would settle it.
- Handhaving page silence on 2027: the 2027 rules in this file come from the Handhavingsplan 2026, because the handhaving page says nothing about 2027. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). A new plan or an updated page would settle it.
- HR Uber date: the date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. UNVERIFIED. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it.
- 2027 boete amounts: the 2027 amounts of the verzuimboetes are not in the checked sources, while the DBA boetebeleid allows verzuimboetes again from 1 January 2027. The general Belastingdienst boete page is the page for boete amounts. Source: [boete page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The 2027 boete table would settle it.
- 2027 capacity of the arbeidsrelaties enforcement: no 2027 plan was published, so no 2027 capacity figure is in the checked sources. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). A 2027 plan or a budget document would settle it.
- Belastingplan 2027 procedure: the bill text as introduced changes no AWR, Invorderingswet or belastingrente rule. Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 and Fiscale verzamelwet 2027 were not read in full, so a procedure change in those bills cannot be excluded. Source: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf). Reading those bills would settle it.
- Conflict, regular handhaving start date: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Both values are in the plans. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The 2026 plan text settles it.
- Settled conflict, VBAR € 36 against € 38: both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, from unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 of € 14,71. Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). No open point remains for 2027 beyond the indexation in section 3.14.
- Settled conflict, BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026: both are true. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May, the verslag was published on 29 May. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Correction, Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA: it is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for some fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force on 1 May 2016. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
