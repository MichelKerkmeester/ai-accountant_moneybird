## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid): 2026 had no default penalties (verzuimboetes) and allowed penalties for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboetes) under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, with no such penalties for corrections (correcties) over 2025. In 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) lapse on 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2027 default penalties are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Start of a control: in 2026 the control started in principle with a business visit, and the starting point to examine only the most recent return period (aangiftetijdvak) in a tax audit (boekenonderzoek) lapsed. In 2027 a business visit is no longer the default starting point for a control. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Enforcement plan (Handhavingsplan): 2026 had a separate plan, version (versie) 2.0 of 31 December 2025, made after Kamerstukken 31 311, nrs. 298 and 301 of 18 December 2025 and the Parliamentary letter (Kamerbrief) of 19 December 2025, with 80 fte capacity. For 2027 the ambition is full integration into regular enforcement (handhaving) with no separate plan, and no 2027 plan was published on 2026-10-11. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).
- Regular enforcement (handhaving): the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Phase-in model (ingroeimodel): no change. Corrections not before 1 January 2025, full 5 years only from 2030. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- NLA notifications (meldingen): usable in enforcement from 2026. The checked 2027 sources state no change. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- 2026 enforcement focus: the 2026 plan pays extra attention to government bodies as clients (opdrachtgevers), uses the detection module (detectiemodule) TKA, and keeps horizontal supervision levels for fiscal service providers. The checked 2027 sources state no change but also no new plan. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR: in 2026 the act was adopted-not-yet-in-force with entry into force on 31 December 2026. In 2027 it applies throughout the year. The hourly amount at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2026 is € 38. The 2027 amount is about € 38 by the explanatory memorandum (MvT) method on the statutory minimum wage (WML) of 1 July 2026 and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027, UNVERIFIED. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html) and [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).
- VBAR first-application regulation: UNVERIFIED in 2026 and still UNVERIFIED in 2027. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 was not found on 2026-10-11. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).
- Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten): usable to 31 December 2029 and no new ones since 6 September 2024. No change from 2026 to 2027. Source: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- BAZ: in 2026 the status was awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag). In 2027 there is no later status than awaiting the memorandum, and a procedure meeting was set for 13 October 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- BAZ waiting period: the 2026 open point, 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary, is not resolved in the 2027 sources. Both are proposed and the bill text was not reopened. Sources: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912) and [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): in 2026 the internet consultation was open until 29 October 2026, with an intended entry into force in January 2028. In 2027 the act is still not in force and the intended date stays January 2028. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the facts that decide the qualification for each engagement. The contract, any model agreement (modelovereenkomst), how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, whether the work must be done personally, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. These are the weighing factors of the employment (loondienst) test. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- Not being in employment does not by itself make the income profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) (business profit) for income tax or a business for VAT. Check the qualification separately for each box. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). The checked sources for this topic do not state the exact return line. They state only that not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax or VAT.
- Record the hourly rate for each engagement. Under art. 7:610aa BW a rate of at most € 36 per hour, or the indexed amount, gives the working person (werkende) a legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden). The client (opdrachtgever) then must prove there is no employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html) and [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The first-application regulation is not published, see section 7.
- If a model agreement is used, keep the approved agreement and evidence that the parties work exactly as described. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used to and including 31 December 2029. Sources: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten) and [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).
- If the qualification is an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking), the pay goes into the payroll tax return (loonaangifte) of the client. Since 1 January 2025 there is no instruction (aanwijzing) step and false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen) and supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). From 1 January 2027 default penalties (verzuimboetes) are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Where a BV is the client, the same Wet DBA test and the VBAR presumption apply. The VBAR does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).
- Keep the follow-up of any position (standpunt) or preliminary consultation (vooroverleg) in the file. Preliminary consultation gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The target period (streeftermijn) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A preliminary consultation checklist exists. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- After payroll tax (LH) corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. A contractor in regular income tax (IH) or VAT (OB) supervision has the most recent open profit tax return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht): AWR art. 52 lid 2 covers natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years under AWR art. 52 lid 4. Paragraph 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives 7 years for basic records (basisgegevens), 10 years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data, and 7 years or shorter for other data by agreement with the tax office (belastingkantoor). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- The checked 2027 sources state no separate employment relationship (arbeidsrelatie) filing deadline. For completeness, the income tax return for tax year 2026, filed in 2027, was not published on 2026-10-11 and is usually due 1 May 2027. Source: [Belastingdienst aangifte page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Separate 2027 enforcement plan (handhavingsplan): unknown, UNVERIFIED. It was not published on 2026-10-11. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). A new plan or an update of the page would settle it.
- Regular enforcement from 1 January 2027: proposed, Verified. It is stated as an ambition, not as a fixed legal change. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). A later plan or a published decision would settle it.
- VBAR hourly amount 2027: about € 38 by the explanatory memorandum (MvT) method on the statutory minimum wage (WML) of 1 July 2026, may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. adopted-not-yet-in-force, UNVERIFIED. Source: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027 would settle it.
- VBAR first-application regulation: not found on 2026-10-11. adopted-not-yet-in-force, UNVERIFIED. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). Publication in the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) would settle it.
- BAZ status: proposed, UNVERIFIED. No later status than awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), with a procedure meeting on 13 October 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). The memorandum and further Parliamentary papers (Kamerstukken) would settle it.
- BAZ waiting period and percentage: the docket summary gives about 70% of income up to the minimum wage and a waiting period of about one year. proposed, Verified. The 2027 sources fix no 2027 figure and give no 2027 minimum wage. The 2026 open point about 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year stays open, see section 5. The bill text or the nota would settle it.
- Enforcement page silence on 2027: the 2027 rules in this file come from the Enforcement plan 2026, because the enforcement page says nothing about 2027. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). A new plan or an updated page would settle it.
- HR Uber date: the date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. UNVERIFIED. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid). A fetched copy of the ruling would settle it.
- 2027 penalty (boete) amounts: the 2027 amounts of the default penalties (verzuimboetes) are not in the checked sources, while the DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid) allows default penalties again from 1 January 2027. The general Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) penalty page is the page for penalty amounts. Source: [boete page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The 2027 penalty table would settle it.
- 2027 capacity of the employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) enforcement: no 2027 plan was published, so no 2027 capacity figure is in the checked sources. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). A 2027 plan or a budget document would settle it.
- Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) procedure: the bill text as introduced changes no AWR, Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) or tax interest (belastingrente) rule. Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) and Tax Omnibus Act (Fiscale verzamelwet) 2027 were not read in full, so a procedure change in those bills cannot be excluded. Source: [wetsvoorstel Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf). Reading those bills would settle it.
- Conflict, regular enforcement start date: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Both values are in the plans. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). The 2026 plan text settles it.
- Settled conflict, VBAR € 36 against € 38: both are right. € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, from unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of 1 January 2026 of € 14,71. Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). No open point remains for 2027 beyond the indexation in section 3.14.
- Settled conflict, BAZ report (verslag) date 27 May against 29 May 2026: both are true. The committee input (inbreng) was delivered on 27 May, the report (verslag) was published on 29 May. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Correction, Staatsblad 2016, 165 as the Wet DBA: it is the Decree (Besluit) of 8 April 2016 on the opt-out for some deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force on 1 May 2016. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
