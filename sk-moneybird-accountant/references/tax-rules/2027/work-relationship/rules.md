## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Wet DBA

Applies to every client (opdrachtgever) and contractor (opdrachtnemer) in the Netherlands. The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016. It entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 May 2016 and replaced the VAR (the previous declaration). The law did not create a separate test. The qualification follows the elements of employment (loondienst) and the facts of the case. Source: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

One correction on a detail. Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree (Besluit) of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) or VAT. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst). No filing step follows from the law itself.

### 3.2 Employment test (loondiensttoets)

Employment has three elements. The possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform personal work (persoonlijke arbeid), and pay for the work. The courts weigh, among others, the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Source: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

Case law fills this in. HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, deals with the role of the parties' intention (partijbedoeling). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), gives a Catalogue of Circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) that names integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), with free substitution (vrije vervanging) not as an absolute element. HR Uber (preliminary questions, prejudiciële vragen) holds that there is no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on enforcement (handhaving). Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file) and [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. Treat that date as UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

Filing step: none. The test decides the qualification, and the qualification decides whether the pay belongs in the payroll-tax return (loonaangifte) of the client or in the contractor's own profit. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### 3.3 Opt-out of a deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking)

Applies to home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten). The employment page also lists work contractors (aannemers van werk) and helpers, agents (agenten), artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees, interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors (opdrachtnemers) working through an intermediary (intermediair) as deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). Persons treated as employees usually work 2 or more days a week for the same client and for at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (minimumloon). Only home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can opt out. Condition: the exclusion is in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. Effect: the contractor then has no entitlement to ZW (sickness benefits), WIA (disability benefits) and WW (unemployment benefits). Sources: [loondienst page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst) and [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html). Interaction: the opt-out removes only the deemed employment relationship. The ordinary employment test in 3.2 still applies. Deadline: before the first payment, not after.

### 3.4 Enforcement from 1 January 2025

Applies to clients (opdrachtgevers) and contractors where false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) is possible. Until 1 January 2025 the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) applied. In cases where the client is not acting in bad faith (non-kwaadwillend) the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing): reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll-tax return, usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally (opzettelijk) creating or continuing a situation of evident false self-employment while knowing or being able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking). Cases of acting in bad faith could get correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) and penalties (boetes). The second restriction of the moratorium exception and the fourth postponement to 1 January 2021 were announced in the third progress letter (derde voortgangsbrief) of 24 June 2019. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions (aanwijzingen) are given. False self-employment can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Sources: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

The web module (webmodule) Assessment of the Employment Relationship (Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie) is a tool for clients with a business in the Netherlands. It gives three outcomes: an indication that the work can be done outside an employment relationship, an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a BV, for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon), or for a foreign contractor. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active, with no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with a client declaration (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome. Sources: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

Preliminary consultation (vooroverleg) is available. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes a position on a complete request for preliminary consultation (verzoek vooroverleg). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty, valid if the work is carried out exactly as described. The target period (streeftermijn) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A preliminary consultation checklist exists. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

Filing step: the parties themselves have no filing step under this item. The client may have to correct the payroll-tax return.

### 3.5 Phase-in model (ingroeimodel) 2027

Applies to the lookback period on corrections. In 2027 the phase-in model (ingroeimodel) continues. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith or an instruction given before that date that was not followed. The full 5-year lookback returns only in 2030. Source: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Interaction: this limit sits in front of the normal supplementary-assessment periods (naheffingstermijnen) for payroll taxes (loonheffingen). It does not limit a correction for a pre-2025 instruction that was not followed.

The guidelines (richtlijnen) on the knock-on effect (doorwerking) to income tax (IH) and VAT (OB) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium (einde moratorium). After LH corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. A contractor in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open profit-tax return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Filing step: none. The contractor's own returns stay in the regular IH or OB supervision.

### 3.6 DBA penalty policy (boetebeleid) 2027

Applies to the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties). This is enforcement policy, not statute. 2025: no default penalties (verzuimboetes) (late-filing penalties) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent for the qualification of employment relationships, per Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266. For corrections (correcties) over 2025 this stays so. 2026: no default penalties, while penalties for gross negligence or intent are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. 2027: the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) lapse on 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2027 default penalties are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. Sources: [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf) and [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Interaction: the change is in the penalty (boete) policy and the start of a control, not in the correction lookback in 3.5. Deadline: none.

### 3.7 Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving)

Applies to the organisation of the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration enforcement of employment relationships. The ambition is that from 1 January 2027 the enforcement is fully part of regular enforcement (handhaving), with no separate plan. This is stated as an ambition, so the status is proposed. The tranche 2025 plan had stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf) and [plan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf). Interaction: if the ambition is met, the separate DBA reporting line ends. No confirmed date beyond the ambition is in the checked sources.

### 3.8 Separate 2027 enforcement plan (handhavingsplan)

Applies to anyone who follows the enforcement policy for 2027. A separate enforcement plan for 2027 was not published on 2026-10-11. The status is unknown and the check is UNVERIFIED. The enforcement page says nothing about 2027. Source: [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving). Interaction: the 2027 rules in this file come from the Enforcement plan 2026, which is the last plan in the checked material. What would settle it: a new plan or an update of the enforcement page. See section 7.

### 3.9 Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten), end of assessment

Applies to clients and contractors who use a model agreement (modelovereenkomst). Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. Reason given: model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Source: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer). The tranche 2025 plan adds that the agreements are honoured to end 2029 after the Parliamentary paper (kamerstuk) 31 311, nr. 275, on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. Source: [plan 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf). Interaction with 3.10: the agreement's own end date is not decisive. Deadline: 31 December 2029.

### 3.10 Model agreements, condition

Working under an approved model agreement means no employment, but only if the parties work as described in it. If the actual work differs, the model agreement does not protect. Source: [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten). The approval of the model agreements for free substitution (modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer) and [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file). Interaction: the model agreement is evidence for the qualification, not a guarantee. The ordinary employment test in 3.2 stays in place. Filing step: keep the approved agreement and evidence of the actual way of working in the administration, see section 6.

### 3.11 VBAR entry into force (inwerkingtreding)

Applies to work relationships where someone works for another for pay. The VBAR is bill 36 783. The original bill had an internet consultation (internetconsultatie) from 6 October 2023 with an intended start on 1 July 2025. It went to the Council of State (Raad van State) on 21 June 2024, and the critical advice was published on 11 November 2024. Bill 36 783 was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer). The memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026, 36 783, nr. 6, removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part. The House of Representatives adopted the bill on 21 April 2026, with all parties except FVD in favour. The Senate adopted it on 16 June 2026 by a vote by sitting and standing (zitten en opstaan). The Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, was published on 29 June 2026. The act is in force (in werking) on 31 December 2026 by Decree (Besluit) of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published on 15 July 2026. That date was chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. So the VBAR applies throughout 2027. The status at the check date is adopted-not-yet-in-force, because the status changes on 31 December 2026. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

### 3.12 VBAR statutory rule

Applies to someone who works for another for pay. New art. 7:610aa BW. Paragraph 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). Paragraph 2: the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment (SZW) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 of the Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Paragraph 3: the presumption does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Article II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Article III lid 2: for the first application (eerste toepassing) after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). Effect: the working person (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract. The rule is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html) and [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/). Interaction: the ordinary employment test in 3.2 still decides a case where the presumption is rebutted or does not apply. The amount is € 36 in the statutory text and is indexed later, see 3.13 and 3.14.

### 3.13 VBAR hourly amount, base and indexation

The € 36 in the statutory text is built as follows. 120% of the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML, Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (btw) at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025. That rounds up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. So € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 2026. Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/), [WML 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025) and [WML 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026). The calculation steps are written out in section 4.

### 3.14 VBAR hourly amount 2027

Applies to the first application of the VBAR in 2027. The amount is about € 38 by the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and it may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. The status is adopted-not-yet-in-force and the check is UNVERIFIED, because the amount is derived and the regulation is not published. Source: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html). The 2027 WML is not in the checked sources, so no exact 2027 amount can be given here. The ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2 and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027 would settle it, see 3.15 and section 7.

### 3.15 VBAR first-application regulation

Artikel III lid 2 of Stb. 2026, 158 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation. That regulation was not found on 2026-10-11. The status is adopted-not-yet-in-force and the check is UNVERIFIED. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html). Effect: the exact amount that applies from 31 December 2026 and in 2027 is not fixed in the checked sources. What would settle it: the ministerial regulation in the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027. See section 7.

### 3.16 BAZ status

Applies to self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) without employee insurance. Bill 36 912 is the BAZ. It was submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026. A technical briefing (technische briefing) was held on 13 May 2026. The written contribution for the report (inbreng verslag) was delivered on 27 May 2026 after a postponement from 20 May. The report (verslag) was published on 29 May 2026 as 36 912, nr. 5. On 2026-10-11 the bill is awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag). No debate and no vote have taken place. A procedure meeting was set for 13 October 2026. The status is proposed and the check is UNVERIFIED, because no later status than awaiting the memorandum is known. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). So the BAZ is not law in 2027 and creates no 2027 entitlement.

### 3.17 BAZ content per docket

The docket summary states: about 70% of income up to the minimum wage, a waiting period (wachttijd) of about one year, and an opt-out with a qualifying private policy. The status is proposed and the check is Verified. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912). These are bill plans, not 2027 law. The checked sources do not state a qualification effect of the BAZ on the employment relationship. Any 2027 planning can note the proposal but cannot book an entitlement. See section 4 for a calculation of the proposed percentage and section 7 for the open points.

### 3.18 Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet)

Applies to future qualification rules for self-employed persons. The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026. The intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) is January 2028. The proposal has a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) with self-invoicing, the number of clients, Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) registration and provisions against business risks (ondernemersrisico's). It also has a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) with freedom to organise the work, own account and risk. Meeting both and working that way gives a safe harbour (veilige haven). Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Status proposed and check Verified. Source: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers). Interaction: the Self-Employed Persons Act is not in force in 2027, so for 2027 the Wet DBA, the model agreements and the VBAR stay the rules. No filing step in 2027.
