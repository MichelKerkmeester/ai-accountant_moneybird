## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for this topic was found in the checked 2027 material, so every example below is marked `Calculated example`.

**Example 1, `Calculated example`: the VBAR amounts and the unsettled 2027 rate test.**
Rule: art. 7:610aa BW, in force from 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, so it applies throughout 2027. The werkende is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst when the pay is at most the applicable hourly amount. Lid 2 indexes the amount with the WML, rounded up from the unrounded amount. The first applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2. When the presumption applies, the werkende can invoke it and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst.
Amounts in the checked material: € 36 (the statutory base text, peildatum 1 January 2025) and € 38 (the indexed amount, peildatum 1 January 2026).
Input: invented rates. A ZZP works for a bv.
- € 34 per hour: € 34 is at most € 36 and at most € 38.
- € 37 per hour: € 37 is more than € 36 and at most € 38.
- € 40 per hour: € 40 is more than € 36 and more than € 38.
The lines are arithmetic against the two stated amounts. They are not a settled 2027 outcome, because the applicable 2027 amount is not settled. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 was not found, and the 2027 amount is UNVERIFIED, about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. In particular, the result at € 40 cannot be concluded, since the applicable 2027 amount is not settled. The same reservation applies at € 34 and € 37.
Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html), [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

**Example 2, `Calculated example`: the VBAR hourly amount by the MvT method.**
Inputs from the checked material: WML € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025 and € 14,71 per hour at 1 January 2026.
Step 1: 120% of the WML: € 14,06 × 1,20 = € 16,87 (rounded down).
Step 2: the 25% opslag for AOV and pension and the 15% opslag for general costs together are 40%, so × 1,40: € 16,87 × 1,40 = € 23,62 (rounded).
Step 3: × 1,50 for non-billable hours: € 23,62 × 1,50 = € 35,43. This is the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2025, excl. btw.
Step 4: € 35,43 becomes € 36,00 (rounded up to whole euros).
Step 5: for peildatum 1 January 2026: € 14,71 × 1,20 = € 17,65 (rounded down).
Step 6: € 17,65 × 1,40 = € 24,71.
Step 7: € 24,71 × 1,50 = € 37,07 (rounded). The checked material gives about € 37,07 for this peildatum.
Step 8: € 37,07 becomes € 38,00 (rounded up to whole euros).
Check: every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The € 36 and € 38 match the amounts stated in the checked material. The exact 2027 amount is UNVERIFIED, about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and may change with the WML of 1 January 2027.
Sources: [MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [minimumloon 2025](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2025), [minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

**Example 3, `Calculated example`: the ingroeimodel lookback in a 2027 correction.**
Inputs: the relationship is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Neither exception applies: no kwaadwillendheid, and no aanwijzing given before 1 January 2025 that was not followed. The Belastingdienst corrects in 2027.
Step 1: the correction period starts on 1 January 2025.
Step 2: the correction can cover the calendar years 2025, 2026 and 2027 up to the correction date. That is 3 calendar years.
Step 3: a full 5-year lookback from 2027 would reach 2022. Corrections are blocked before 1 January 2025, so 2022, 2023 and 2024 are outside the correction period.
Step 4: only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst go back the full 5 years. Check: 2030 minus 5 = 2025, so from 2030 the full lookback reaches 2025.
Sources: [Handhavingsplan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

**Example 4, `Calculated example`: the boetebeleid by year for the same relationship.**
Input: the same relationship, no kwaadwillendheid.
- Correcties over 2025: no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. This stays so.
- Correcties over 2026: no verzuimboetes. A vergrijpboete is possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026.
- Correcties over 2027: the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse on 1 January 2027. Verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start.
The period from 1 January 2025 to 1 January 2027 is 24 months, or 2 years. This is enforcement policy, not statute.
Sources: [Handhavingsplan 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [handhaving page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

**Example 5, `Calculated example`: the modelovereenkomst end date.**
Input: an approved modelovereenkomst that was valid on 6 September 2024 and states that it is valid until 31 December 2027.
Step 1: the stated end date is 31 December 2027.
Step 2: the checked rule allows use up to and including 31 December 2029, even though the agreement states another validity date.
Step 3: the extra period is 2028 and 2029, 2 years.
Condition: the parties must work as described. If not, the modelovereenkomst gives no protection.
Sources: [geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer), [werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

**Example 6, `Calculated example`: the Zelfstandigenwet timing.**
Input: the Zelfstandigenwet is proposed, with an intended entry into force in January 2028.
Step 1: in 2027 the zelfstandigentoets and werkrelatietoets are not in force.
Step 2: the period from 1 January 2027 to 1 January 2028 is 12 months.
Step 3: a relationship in 2027 cannot rely on the veilige haven.
Source: [rijksoverheid.nl](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

No example is given for BAZ (bill 36 912). It is a proposed bill, and the checked material does not resolve its wording (about 70% of income up to the minimum wage) or state the 2027 minimum wage, so no percentage calculation is given.
