## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

The checked material holds no official worked example with amounts for the Zvw in 2027. [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335) holds 2027 koopkracht rekenvoorbeelden and a startersaftrek effect table including the Zvw, but they are policy illustrations, not year-table examples, and the checked material prints no amounts from them ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). All examples below are therefore calculated from the section 2 figures, with invented inputs.

**Calculated example 1. Winst-ondernemer, profit of € 60.000, no startersaftrek.**

Inputs: profit of € 60.000,00, zelfstandigenaftrek € 900, MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,70%, Zvw rate by aanslag 5,02%.

1. Winst uit onderneming: € 60.000,00.
2. Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 900,00.
3. Winst after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 900,00 = € 59.100,00.
4. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,70% of € 59.100,00 = € 7.505,70.
5. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 59.100,00 minus € 7.505,70 = € 51.594,30.
6. The maximum is € 82.547 and the base is at least nil, so the bijdrage-inkomen is € 51.594,30.
7. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 51.594,30 = € 2.590,03.
8. For comparison, the same base at the werkgeversheffing rate of 6,27%: 0,0627 times € 51.594,30 = € 3.234,96. The difference is € 3.234,96 minus € 2.590,03 = € 644,93.

Caveats: the 5,02% and 6,27% rates are proposed (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77), the maximum of € 82.547 is UNVERIFIED, and the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 12,70% is adopted-not-yet-in-force.

**Calculated example 2. Winst-ondernemer, profit of € 100.000, the maximum is reached.**

Inputs: profit of € 100.000,00, zelfstandigenaftrek € 900, MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,70%, maximum bijdrage-inkomen € 82.547, Zvw rate by aanslag 5,02%.

1. Winst uit onderneming: € 100.000,00.
2. Winst after ondernemersaftrek: € 100.000,00 minus € 900,00 = € 99.100,00.
3. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,70% of € 99.100,00 = € 12.585,70.
4. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 99.100,00 minus € 12.585,70 = € 86.514,30.
5. That is above the maximum of € 82.547,00, so the bijdrage-inkomen is capped at € 82.547,00.
6. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 82.547,00 = € 4.143,86.
7. For comparison, the same capped base at 6,27%: 0,0627 times € 82.547,00 = € 5.175,70.

Caveat: the cap of € 82.547,00 is UNVERIFIED, so the outcome moves with the final maximum.

**Calculated example 3. Stacking with loon from employment.**

The maximum applies once per person across channels, so loon that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

Variant A. Loon uit loondienst of € 40.000,00 and a Zvw bijdrage-inkomen from winst of € 50.000,00.

1. Headroom: € 82.547,00 minus € 40.000,00 = € 42.547,00.
2. The winst part of € 50.000,00 exceeds the headroom, so the aanslag contribution is due over € 42.547,00.
3. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 42.547,00 = € 2.135,86.

Variant B. Loon uit loondienst of € 80.000,00 and a Zvw bijdrage-inkomen from winst of € 15.000,00.

1. Headroom: € 82.547,00 minus € 80.000,00 = € 2.547,00.
2. The winst part of € 15.000,00 exceeds the headroom, so the aanslag contribution is due over € 2.547,00.
3. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 2.547,00 = € 127,86.

The structure of this example follows the official 2026 example in https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, where loon of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 50.000 gives 4,85% over € 39.409 and where loon of € 80.000 plus freelance income of € 15.000 gives no bijdrage because the 2026 maximum of € 79.409 is already passed by the loon alone (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). With the 2027 maximum of € 82.547 the second case still leaves headroom of € 2.547,00. Caveat: the 2027 maximum is UNVERIFIED.

**Calculated example 4. Startersaftrek conflict and the Zvw base.**

Inputs: profit of € 60.000,00, zelfstandigenaftrek € 900, MKB-winstvrijstelling 12,70%, Zvw rate by aanslag 5,02%.

Variant A, enacted 2027 text, startersaftrek € 2.123.

1. Winst after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 900,00 minus € 2.123,00 = € 56.977,00.
2. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,70% of € 56.977,00 = € 7.236,08, rounded from € 7.236,079.
3. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 56.977,00 minus € 7.236,08 = € 49.740,92.
4. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 49.740,92 = € 2.496,99.

Variant B, bill, startersaftrek € 10.

1. Winst after ondernemersaftrek: € 60.000,00 minus € 900,00 minus € 10,00 = € 59.090,00.
2. MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12,70% of € 59.090,00 = € 7.504,43.
3. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 59.090,00 minus € 7.504,43 = € 51.585,57.
4. Zvw bijdrage at 5,02%: 0,0502 times € 51.585,57 = € 2.589,60.

Difference between the two variants: € 2.589,60 minus € 2.496,99 = € 92,61. The startersaftrek value for 2027 is an open point (section 7).
