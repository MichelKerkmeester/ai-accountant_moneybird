## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the income-dependent contribution under the Health Insurance (zorgverzekering) Act (Zorgverzekeringswet, Zvw) for an entrepreneur in tax year 2027. For 2027 the contribution (bijdrage) paid by a self-employed person (zelfstandige) through the assessment (aanslag) channel is 5,02%, and the employer health insurance contribution (zvw-werkgeversheffing) (werkgeversheffing) paid by an employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) is 6,27%. Both rates are proposed estimates from Table 77 of the budget document: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77. The maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) for 2027 is € 82.547 in a secondary source only, so it is UNVERIFIED, while https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw give no 2027 maximum. The year's law is a mix of layers: the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) text valid from 1 January 2027 is adopted but not yet in force (in werking), Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, bill 37 022 of 15 September 2026) is proposed, and the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 is now in the bill through the second memorandum of amendment (Tweede nota van wijziging) and is still proposed.
