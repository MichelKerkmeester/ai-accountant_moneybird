## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Zvw contribution by assessment for a self-employed person (verlaagde IAB)

The income-related contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage, IAB) under the Health Insurance Act (Zorgverzekeringswet, Zvw) has three collection channels. This is the rate for the assessment (aanslag) channel used by entrepreneurs. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 prints 5,02% for the reduced income-related contribution (verlaagde IAB) and its footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced contribution (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77). The status is proposed: the table is a budget estimate and the final rates follow by ministerial regulation under Zvw art. 45 (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The rate is applied to the contribution income for the year (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.2 Zvw employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing, normale IAB)

The employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing) is the normal rate, 6,27% for 2027 (proposed, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77). It is paid by the employer or the benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) over wage from employment (loon uit loondienst), over director and substantial shareholder (DGA) wage when the director and major shareholder is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen), over social assistance (bijstand), over early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) insured people of 2005, and over WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). It differs from the entrepreneur's rate in three ways. The rate is 6,27% instead of 5,02%. It is paid by the employer or the benefit agency. And it is not levied by assessment, while the entrepreneur's contribution is (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Both rates share one maximum contribution income per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

### 3.3 Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen)

The maximum contribution income for 2027 is € 82.547 in one secondary source, so the value is UNVERIFIED (https://mijn.bovag.nl/actueel/nieuws/voorlopige-sociale-en-sectorpremies-2027-bekendgemaakt). https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw give no 2027 maximum. Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the contribution income at no less than nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The definitive figure therefore comes from that regulation or from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Zvw page in late 2026. The maximum applies once per person across all channels (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

### 3.4 Contribution income from profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming)

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b includes the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 defines taxable profit as profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) minus the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). So for an entrepreneur with profit from business the Zvw base is the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The contribution income is at least nil and at most the yearly maximum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). This rule was read in the 2026 version of the statute only, not in the 2027 version (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.5 Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) and the base

The base follows the taxable profit, with one exclusion: the amount added under Wet IB 2001 art. 3.70 lid 2 for a reduction of the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) is not part of the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity (lijfrente) is also not in the assessment channel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed and an existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet, so a reduction of the reserve can add to the profit while that added amount stays out of the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### 3.6 One maximum per person across channels

The maximum contribution income applies once per person across channels, so income that already bore a contribution uses up the headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). For a sole trader with wage from employment, or with DGA wage from an own private limited company (BV), the assessment contribution over the profit is therefore limited to the remaining headroom, and it can be nil when the other income already reaches the maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

### 3.7 Assessment channel income

The contribution by (provisional) assessment covers profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland), except the conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity, plus income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage from a foreign employer (2020 to 2024 pages), pension from a foreign benefit agency and periodic benefits (periodieke uitkeringen) without payroll tax such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The foreign-employer item is listed on the 2020 to 2024 FISIN Zvw pages only, so its 2027 treatment is not confirmed by the checked material.

### 3.8 Employer health insurance contribution channel income

The employer health insurance contribution is paid by the employer or the benefit agency over wage from employment, DGA wage when the DGA is insured for the employee insurance schemes, social assistance, early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for health insurance fund insured people of 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). For a sole trader who also owns a BV, the DGA wage of that BV falls in this channel when the insurance condition is met, and that wage uses up the single maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

### 3.9 Withheld contribution channel income

The withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) is withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits with payroll tax (loonheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). For 2026 the withheld contribution was also 4,85%, the same as the assessment rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked material does not state a separate withheld contribution rate for 2027.

### 3.10 Exceptions

Four exceptions apply. Military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income are excluded. For share fishermen (deelvissers) there is an exclusion where another pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip. A correction applies for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution. A reduction applies for the part of the year in which the person is insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).

### 3.11 Rates and maximum set by ministerial regulation

Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the yearly maximum of the contribution income by ministerial regulation and Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The 2027 rate pair of 5,02% and 6,27% and the 2027 maximum of € 82.547 are therefore not final in the checked material: the rates come from a proposed budget estimate (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77) and the maximum comes from a secondary source only (https://mijn.bovag.nl/actueel/nieuws/voorlopige-sociale-en-sectorpremies-2027-bekendgemaakt). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration may process the 2027 provisional assessments on older parameters if the amendments come too late ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329) implementation test paragraph (uitvoeringstoets)).

### 3.12 Reduced income-related contribution for self-employed persons and pensioners (verlaagde IAB)

https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons and pensioners pay the reduced income-related contribution (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77). This is the condition that puts a sole trader on the 5,02% rate instead of the 6,27% employer health insurance contribution (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77).

### 3.13 Zvw at state pension age (AOW)

No AOW-age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, so the continuation of the contribution after the AOW age is an inference from absence and stays UNVERIFIED (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages do not mention the AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). On the same inference, a self-employed person at AOW age stays in the assessment channel at the reduced rate, and the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) is then halved to € 450 by the 50% rule (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Tabel 77, [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### 3.14 Assessment timing for the Zvw

The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). So the entrepreneur does not meet a separate payroll step for the profit part, and the Zvw amount for the year appears on the same assessment as the income tax. A (provisional) assessment can be issued during the year, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration may process the 2027 provisional assessments on older parameters if the amendments come too late (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329) implementation test paragraph (uitvoeringstoets)).

### 3.15 Zvw return and deadlines

This item is UNVERIFIED and was not re-opened. The checked material states that the same return (aangifte) is used, that a provisional Zvw assessment (voorlopige aanslag Zvw) exists and that the Zvw follows the IB filing timeline. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet confirms only that the Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax assessment. The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic.

### 3.16 Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek)

The self-employed person's deduction is € 900 for 2027 in the enacted 2027 text, or € 450 at AOW age per the 50% rule (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/zelfstandigenaftrek-verder-omlaag-0/ and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1). The status is adopted-not-yet-in-force. The page states that the law is adopted, and the € 450 is the 50% rule applied, not a printed amount (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/zelfstandigenaftrek-verder-omlaag-0/ and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1). The deduction reduces the profit before the SME profit exemption, and it therefore reduces the Zvw base (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, MKB, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### 3.17 SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)

The SME profit exemption is 12,70% for 2027 and is computed on the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and MKB). The status is adopted-not-yet-in-force. Art. 3.79a of the 2027 text was not reachable, so the verified figure rests on the baseline citation of [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) Tabel 1 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf). Because the exemption is subtracted before the taxable profit, it lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

### 3.18 Starter's deduction (startersaftrek), enacted text

The starter's deduction in the enacted 2027 text is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age, and the status is adopted-not-yet-in-force with Check UNVERIFIED because art. 3.76 of the 2027 text was not reachable (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The amount forms part of the entrepreneur's allowance and therefore lowers the Zvw base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.19 Starter's deduction, bill

Tax Plan 2027 reduces the starter's deduction by € 2.113, to € 10 (proposed, [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). That item belongs to the entrepreneur's allowance topic. If the bill is adopted, the entrepreneur's allowance falls, the taxable profit rises and the Zvw base rises by the same amount ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335), https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
