## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Vpb rate table (art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969)

The 2027 table reads up to and including € 200.000 19%, and above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([Wet Vpb 1969, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) above the column I amount. This value is carried because https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2027-01-01 returns HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11, so the Check tag is UNVERIFIED. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, bill 37 022) does not touch art. 22 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rates page states 19,0% and 25,8% for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([tarieven page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting)). If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 gives (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn). X is the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22 ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The earlier series read: 2020 up to and including € 200.000 at 16,5% and above that € 33.000 plus 25%, 2021 up to and including € 245.000 at 15% and above that € 36.750 plus 25%, 2022 up to and including € 395.000 at 15% and above that € 59.250 plus 25,8%, and 2023 to 2026 up to and including € 200.000 at 19% and above that € 38.000 plus 25,8% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01), [2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01), [2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.2 Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (art. 20 Wet Vpb 1969)

A loss is set off against the profit of another year. From 2022 the rule is one year back (één jaar terug) and unlimited forward, but per year only up to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000 ([2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. In 2020 and 2021 a loss was set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by decision (beschikking) ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01)). For 2027 the current rule is carried and UNVERIFIED ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.3 Generic interest deduction (renteaftrek) limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) (art. 15b lid 1 Wet Vpb 1969)

The balance of interest (saldo aan renten, net interest balance, interest costs on loans minus interest income) is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000 ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The percentage per dated text is 30% in 2020 and 2021, 20% in 2022, 2023 and 2024, and 24,5% in 2025 and 2026 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01) to [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), each year). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration change pages state 20% for 2024, 24,5% for 2025 and 24,5% for 2026, and say non-deductible interest carries forward ([change page 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2025), [change page 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026)). For 2027 the values are 24,5% and € 1.000.000, carried and UNVERIFIED ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.4 Innovation box (innovatiebox) fraction (art. 12b tot 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

Qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset (kwalificerend immaterieel activum) count for 9/H, H being the highest art. 22 rate, so an effective 9% ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%" ([innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The 2020 text reads 7/H, an effective 7% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01)). Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate ([innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). The value is carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.5 Innovation box flat rate (forfait) (art. 12bd Wet Vpb 1969)

For a taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or the two preceding years and chooses the flat rate, the amount is 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000, and only when that profit is positive ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [innovatiebox page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.6 Innovation box flat rate ceiling in the bill

Tax Plan 2027 Article XIX A raises the flat rate ceiling to € 100.000 instead of € 25.000, and Article LXIII lid 1 sub d makes it first apply to book years starting on or after 1 January 2027 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The item is proposed and was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.7 Innovation box smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) (art. 12ba lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D statement (S&O-verklaring). Smaller means that the advantages plus acquisition costs (aanschaffingskosten) from S&O assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and that the net turnover (netto-omzet) over those five years stays below € 250.000.000 ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Other taxpayers also need an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Trademarks, logos and comparable assets (merken, logo's) are excluded by art. 12ba lid 6. Art. 12bb gives the K/T part, K being the qualifying expenditure (kwalificerende uitgaven) multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.8 Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) (art. 13 lid 1 en 2 Wet Vpb 1969)

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. A participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (nominaal gestort kapitaal) (art. 13 lid 2) ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). In a holding structure the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test compares with the highest wage in connected bodies, where connected means a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party (derde) ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A tax-neutral contribution (geruisloze inbreng) into an existing holding is approved under onderdeel 3.4 of the Decree on tax-neutral conversion (Besluit geruisloze omzetting) when the conditions there are met ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.9 Art. 13 lid 7 Wet Vpb 1969 in the bill

The bill replaces "vooraf" with "op verzoek van de belastingplichtige" (on request of the taxpayer), uses "niet-ingeprijsd voordeel" (non-priced advantage) and adds two new paragraphs ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The item is proposed and was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.10 Transitional art. 34j in the bill

Art. 34j keeps the old art. 13 lid 7 for advantages attributable before the first 2027 book year when the decision (beschikking) or the full request came before it, and through 31 December 2027 for transactions entered into and requested before 15 September 2026 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The article is proposed ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.11 Tax Plan 2027 (bill 37 022)

Tax Plan 2027 enters into force on 1 January 2027 if adopted. Article XIX A and B first apply to book years starting on or after 1 January 2027. In Article XIX the bill changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j. In Article XX it deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.12 2027 status of the Wet Vpb 1969 text

The 2027-01-01 URL returns HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11. The 2026-01-01 version reads "Geldend van 01-01-2026 t/m heden" and announces "Toekomstige wijziging(en) voorzien met ingang van: 01-01-2029". Art. 22 carries the note that changes without an entry date exist ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Three layers stay apart: enacted law (the 2026 Vpb text and the 2027 future texts of the Wet LB 1964 and the Wet IB 2001), bill 37 022, not adopted on 2026-10-11, and the December 2026 indexation, not published ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### 3.13 Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) ownership requirement (bezitseis) (art. 15 Wet Vpb 1969)

A fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) needs the mother to hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the daughter, carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights (statutaire stemrechten) and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the mother must be a BV, NV, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or a foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie), or a comparable foreign form, that the daughter must be a BV or NV, that both need the same book years and profit rules and that both must be effectively established in the Netherlands ([Belastingdienst fiscale eenheid](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/)). The daughter keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the mother at once. Subjective exemption (subjectieve vrijstelling) of one daughter ends it for that daughter only (art. 15 lid 10) ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)).

### 3.14 Customary salary standard amount (normbedrag) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of three amounts. The third is the standard amount (normbedrag). The 2027 future text of art. 12a Wet LB 1964 prints € 58.000, with status adopted-not-yet-in-force ([Wet LB 1964, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01)). The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)). The standard amount was € 58.000 in 2026 ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01)), and earlier € 46.000 in 2020, € 47.000 in 2021, € 48.000 in 2022, € 51.000 in 2023 and € 56.000 in 2024 and 2025 ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) to [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), each year).

### 3.15 Customary salary indexed amount 2027

The indexed amount for 2027 is not published. Art. 12a lid 7 uses the table correction factor, and the bill's 1,01248 clause does not name art. 12a ([future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The December 2026 regulation under art. 12a lid 7 will settle the amount ([future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01)).

### 3.16 Customary salary test and floor (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

The wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), at 100% from 2023 and 75% in 2020 to 2022, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) the standard amount ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01) to [1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01), each year, [future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 (paragraph 6 in the 2020 text, present in every year) ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01)). The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage (loon) for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) who hold shares ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). From 2023, if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), the wage is the comparable wage ([1 January 2023 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). A settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). For starters, the statute of 2020 to 2022 (art. 12a lid 3 and 4) gave a lower wage for a body with an R&D statement (S&O-verklaring) to which the start-up percentage (starterspercentage) of art. 23 lid 7 WVA applied, at least 108% of twelve times the art. 8 lid 1 sub a WML amount, within the de-minimis ceiling of Regulation (EU) nr. 1407/2013. The de-minimis condition did not apply to a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA), and an R&D statement for part of a year was enough for the whole year. The exception lapsed for new cases from 2023. A first use for 2021 or 2022 could run at most 3 years, and the Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 says the transitional right lapsed for all starters from 2025 ([1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst loon en aanmerkelijk belang](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang), [Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). Separate policy (Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025): a starting company may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the WML for the hours worked (gewerkte uren), and the 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The test and the floor are carried for 2027 ([future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01)).

### 3.17 Zvw contribution (bijdrage zorgverzekeringswet) and employer levy (werkgeversheffing) 2027

For 2027 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration percentages page gives no figures yet. It names only 2025 and 2026. The 2027 figures from the baseline budget rows are 5,02% for the contribution (bijdrage), 6,27% for the employer levy and a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 82.547. These are secondary and proposed, UNVERIFIED, and wait for the Health Insurance Regulation (Regeling zorgverzekering) 2027 ([percentages Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw)). Per FISIN year page the series is: 2020 5,45%, 6,70% and € 57.232, 2021 5,75%, 7,00% and € 58.311, 2022 5,50%, 6,75% and € 59.706, 2023 5,43%, 6,68% and € 66.956, 2024 5,32%, 6,57% and € 71.628, 2025 5,26%, 6,51% and € 75.864, 2026 4,85%, 6,10% and € 79.409 ([FISIN 2026 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), each year 2020 to 2026). Each FISIN page says the DGA wage falls under the employer levy only if he is insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen) ([FISIN 2026 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).

### 3.18 DGA Zvw route (zvw-route)

A DGA is employed (in dienst) by his own company (eigen vennootschap) for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs the wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing) ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)). A DGA who, alone or with spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (employee insurances) ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). For him the company pays no employee insurance premiums (premies werknemersverzekeringen) and no employer levy Zvw, and instead withholds the income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) from his net wage up to the maximum contribution wage (bijdrageloon) and pays it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). The Payroll Taxes Handbook 2025 gives 5,26% up to € 75.864 as the 2025 figure ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1). The route is withholding on net wage, not an assessment (aanslag) of the DGA ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the DGA pays the contribution himself, collected by withholding from net wage (inhouding op nettoloon) or by assessment ([Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)). A DGA who is insured falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV ([Handboek Loonheffingen 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf), [Belastingdienst DGA table](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/)).

### 3.19 Box 2 table in the future text (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001)

The future 2027 text of art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 prints up to and including € 68.843 24,5% and above that € 16.866 plus 31%, the same table as 2026, before indexation ([Wet IB 2001, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the table correction factor ([Wet IB 2001, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The series per dated text is: 2020 26,25%, 2021 to 2023 26,9%, 2024 up to and including € 67.000 at 24,5% and above that € 16.415 plus 33%, 2025 up to and including € 67.804 at 24,5% and above that € 16.611 plus 31%, 2026 up to and including € 68.843 at 24,5% and above that € 16.866 plus 31% ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01) to [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), each year).

The bill multiplies the art. 10.1 lid 1 amounts by 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor, and Article LI lid 1 sets that factor ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The December 2026 indexation regulation will settle the exact amount. See section 7.

### 3.22 Dividend tax (dividendbelasting) rate (art. 5 Wet DB 1965)

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 makes the tax 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst), in every version read ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). Art. 1 makes the shareholder the taxpayer and the company the withholding agent. If the company bears the tax, art. 6 lid 1 multiplies the proceeds by 100/85. Once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due (art. 9 lid 1) ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). A participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder with a holding of 5% or more is usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption, and no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration invites one. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding ([als u dividend uitkeert](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert)). The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the tax return (aangifte) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) and bodies in the tax return Vpb ([dividendbelasting aangifte en betalen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen), [dividendbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/)). The 15% is carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([Wet DB 1965, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).

### 3.23 Dividend stripping (art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965)

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds it. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd" ([2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01), [2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)). Carried for 2027 and UNVERIFIED ([2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01)).

### 3.24 Buy-back of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)

Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital (gemiddeld gestort kapitaal) on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. The company bears the tax and art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies ([the source](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html)).

### 3.25 Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting) (art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021)

Art. 4.1 sets the tax at the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 ([Wet bronbelasting 2021, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)). Interest and royalties are in scope from the 2021 version (the first version with the levy articles), and dividends enter from the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on the overlap with dividend tax) ([2021 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2021-01-01), [2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01)). The tax hits payments to a related body, with a qualifying interest that is always above 50% of the voting rights, in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie) (profit tax below 9% or on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, niet-coöperatieve jurisdicties), and abuse cases ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026 ([bronbelasting op rente en royalty's](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/)). With art. 22 unchanged, 2027 is 25,8%, carried and UNVERIFIED ([1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01)).

### 3.26 Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001)

The discontinuation deduction (termination relief) is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001). The future 2027 text prints € 3.630, with status adopted-not-yet-in-force ([Wet IB 2001, future text 2027](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### 3.27 Discontinuation deduction proposal

A proposal reduces the discontinuation deduction to € 908 from 1 January 2027 and lets it disappear in 2030. The page names no bill. This matters for a taxable contribution (ruisende inbreng) in 2027 ([ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The item is proposed ([ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)).

### 3.28 Taxable contribution

Without the art. 3.65 facility, a change of legal form (wijziging van rechtsvorm) is a cessation. The owner draws up a final balance and pays IB on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) (termination profit), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)). The discontinuation deduction (art. 3.79, at most € 3.630, once in a lifetime) and an extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction apply ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm), [stakingswinst berekenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen), [ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/)). Hidden reserves (stille reserves) realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129) ([stakingswinst berekenen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen), [Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### 3.29 Tax-neutral conversion credit (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not ceased. The whole business must be contributed, and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for IB, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree, the Zvw contribution materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000 ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. Selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption. Into an existing company only when it already runs a business in the same line, into a fiscal unity only through the mother whose shares the contributor holds directly. Retroactivity: if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). The request is filed at the office that handles income tax, the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) is registered with the form Geleideformulier, and the BV is a BV in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)). The inspector decides by decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar) that contains the conditions. A request is timely until omitting it would have irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties. In the published case the request could be filed until the 2022 IB/PVV assessment was irrevocable, unless transfer gain (overdrachtswinst) was reported for 2021, in which case the 2021 assessment decides. The position (standpunt) was adopted on 27-06-2022 and published on 12-01-2023 ([KG:212:2022:1](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21220221-geruisloze-omzetting-termijn-indienen-verzoek-artikel-3-65-wet-ib-2001/)).

It replaces the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M ([Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)). ### 3.31 VAT for a new BV

The new legal form is a new business for VAT (btw) and may get a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which goes to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change ([uw onderneming wijzigt van rechtsvorm](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm)).

### 3.32 VAT fiscal unity (btw fiscale eenheid) (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968)

A VAT fiscal unity (btw fiscale eenheid) arises by operation of law (van rechtswege) once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically intertwined, and changes by law on entry or exit. A decision, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability (hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid). Financial intertwining means at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights in the same hands ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). The Policy Decree (beleidsbesluit) on tax liability (belastingplicht) and VAT fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Staatscourant 2024, 38545, published on 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025" ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html)). A pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for VAT (btw) and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request ([Beleidsbesluit](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html), [Besluit 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html)).

### 3.33 Tax return Vpb deadline

For a book year equal to the calendar year, file before 1 June of the next year. For a broken book year, file within 5 months after its end. For a short book year ending 31 December, file before 1 June, and for a short book year ending in another month, file before 1 April of the next calendar year ([aangifte vennootschapsbelasting doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen)). These are the current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.34 Deferral (uitstel) tax return Vpb

The online extension is only for a calendar book year, runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for more than 5 months and must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons ([uitstel aangifte vennootschapsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting)). These are the current pages, read for the 2025 and 2026 filing seasons.

### 3.35 Annual accounts (jaarrekening) size classes (grootteklassen, Boek 2 BW art. 395a, 396, 397)

A legal person falls in a class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html)). Micro is at most € 450.000 assets, € 900.000 net turnover and fewer than 10 employees. Small is € 7,5 million, € 15 million and fewer than 50. Medium is € 25 million, € 50 million and fewer than 250. The 2020 statute read micro at most € 350.000 and € 700.000 and fewer than 10, small € 6.000.000 and € 12.000.000 and fewer than 50, medium € 20.000.000 and € 40.000.000 and fewer than 250 ([Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2020-01-01)). Stb. 2024, 52 of 5 March 2024 raises the amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, with an option from 1 January 2023, and leaves the employee counts unchanged ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01)). The turnover bound is settled at € 50 million against a Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) page printing more than € 51 million ([Stb. 2024, 52](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html), [Boek 2 BW, 1 January 2025 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/deponeren/in-welke-bedrijfsklasse-valt-je-bedrijf/)).

### 3.36 Annual accounts deposit (art. 394 Boek 2 BW)

The annual accounts are deposited within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends ([jaarrekening indienen](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)). If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts and marks them as such. There is no extension, and late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf). From financial year (boekjaar) 2025 (filing from 1 January 2026) all legal persons file through SBR ([jaarrekening indienen](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/)).

### 3.37 Record-keeping obligation (administratieplicht) and retention period (bewaartermijn) (art. 52 AWR)

Art. 52 AWR makes legal entities (lichamen) required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires the records to be kept for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement (resultatenrekening) ([AWR, 1 January 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2020-01-01), [AWR, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)). No basis was found for a longer general term ([AWR, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01)).

### 3.38 VAT record-retention periods (art. 34a, 28rl, 28sj Wet OB 1968)

Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten. Art. 28rl and 28sj keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction ([Wet OB 1968, 1 January 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)).
