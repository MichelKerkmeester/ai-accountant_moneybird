## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

2027 is the last year covered by this knowledge base. This file covers the corporate income tax (Vpb, vennootschapsbelasting) of a BV, director and substantial shareholder (DGA, directeur-grootaandeelhouder) pay rules, health insurance (zorgverzekering) contributions (Zvw, Zorgverzekeringswet), box 2 of the income tax (inkomstenbelasting), dividend tax (dividendbelasting) and the filing and annual accounts (jaarrekening) duties of a BV. The year's law is a mix: the 2026 text of the Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969) still reads "Geldend van 01-01-2026 t/m heden" and no dated 2027 text exists, so every Vpb value is carried and marked UNVERIFIED ([Wet Vpb 1969](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01)). Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, bill 37 022) was not adopted on 2026-10-11, and the December 2026 indexation is not published ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
