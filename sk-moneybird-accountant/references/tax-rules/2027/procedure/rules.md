## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

One subsection per item in the key figures table, in the same order. Every statement follows the linked checked source.

### 3.1 IB return over 2026: filing period and deadline

Applies to anyone who receives an aangiftebrief for the income tax return over 2026. The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. The aangiftebrief itself sets the date, so a different date in the brief replaces the usual date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.2 IB filing period opens

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March. This gives the taxpayer the window in which the return can be filed before the deadline in the aangiftebrief. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.3 IB uitstel

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. Uitstel usually means belastingrente is charged. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.4 IB uitstel channel

Online in Mijn Belastingdienst is possible until 1 May and gives uitstel to 1 September, but only for someone who received an aangiftebrief. The uitstel page says online is not possible. Read in full, that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both texts are current Belastingdienst text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date and the paper form otherwise. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.5 Fiscal partner and the uitstel form

The fiscal partner must request uitstel separately. The form can be used only for the first uitstel request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.6 AWR art. 9 lid 1 and 2

For aanslagbelastingen the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the invitation. Art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions and no 2027 change was found. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.7 Definitive assessment term, AWR art. 11 lid 3

The power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. The same text holds on 1 January 2020 and on 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.8 Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens 2027

The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before the end of the year count toward the grens, a paid voorlopige aanslag does not, and a refund received after filing does not. For 2027 the two values were not published on 2026-10-11. Older captures used gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, which for whole-euro amounts describes the same threshold. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.9 Navorderingstermijn, AWR art. 16

Lid 1 requires a new fact. The fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw. Lid 2 adds the cases where a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar, always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3 gives 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel, and for a fout-based navordering only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5 extends by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Belastingdienst page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering was possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

### 3.10 Payment terms of assessments, Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9

An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date, with the first termijn one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. The text is the same on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, and no 2027 change was found. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01

### 3.11 Bezwaartermijn

Bezwaar is filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. For income tax it can be filed online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if the bezwaar asks for that. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. Against a voorlopige aanslag IB no bezwaar is possible, only a change. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar

### 3.12 Verzoek om vermindering

After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible. It can be filed online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The page gives aanslag 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the verzoek goes by letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.13 Voorlopige aanslag change deadline

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year. With an aangiftebrief the deadline is the filing date or the uitstel date, and changing is no longer possible after filing. The page example for 2025 gives usually until 1 May 2026, with uitstel until 1 September 2026, and a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen

### 3.14 Belastingrente 2027 rate

The 2027 rate was not published on 2026-10-11. It is set before 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB herfinancieringsrente published before 1 November, plus 3 points for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen, rounded to half percentages, with a minimum of 4,5%. For Vpb, bronbelasting, minimumbelasting and solidariteitsbijdrage the addition is 5,5 points with a minimum of 5,5%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.15 Belastingrente, last set rate before 2027

The last set rate is 5% from 1 January 2026. Earlier rates for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen: 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 (for the inkomstenbelasting the reduction started 1 July 2020), 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024 and 6,5% in 2025. The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, btw and the other taxes. The update date of the mass-objection page is 25 February 2026, and the page gives no date for the collective decisions. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage

### 3.16 Belastingrente on income tax in 2027 and the count basis

Belastingrente is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. For the return over 2026 the rente starts 1 July 2027 at the 2027 rate. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### 3.17 Belastingrente on btw

Belastingrente on btw is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment. For late payment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a naheffingsaanslag it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen, with an end at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting

### 3.18 Vpb belastingrente

For Vpb the addition to the ECB rate is 5,5 points, with a minimum of 5,5%. After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes: 2026 5 instead of 7,5, 2025 6,5 instead of 9, 2024 7,5 instead of 10, 1 July to 31 December 2023 6 instead of 8, and 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2023 4 instead of 8. For 2027 the Vpb rate is set by the same yearly rule and was not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.19 VAT filing and payment dates for 2027

AWR art. 10 lid 2 gives the return for a tijdvak a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak. AWR art. 19 lid 1 requires payment within one month after the end of the tijdvak. The Belastingdienst tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, for monthly and quarterly returns, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. The return and the payment must both be received by that date. The later 2027 tables were not published on 2026-10-11, so the statutory rule stays. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.20 VAT dates already published for 2027

Q4 2026 and December 2026 are due by 31 January 2027. The yearly return 2026 is due by 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums

### 3.21 VAT software submission

Software can submit a btw-aangifte from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums

### 3.22 Uitstel btw-aangifte

Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties, an ernstige calamiteit. The request is made in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date. A naheffingsaanslag and boete remain possible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte

### 3.23 Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2027

The 2027 amount was not published on 2026-10-11. The rule is 7 kalenderdagen coulance after the deadline, then a boete. The amount was € 68 for 2020 to 2024 and € 82 for 2025 and 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte

### 3.24 IB boete amounts 2027

The 2027 amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. The last published amounts are for 2025 and 2026: verzuimboete IB € 469, up to € 6.709, not requesting a return € 3.354, and the betaalverzuimboete up to € 6.709. The 2026 page adds 5% of the open amount of the (voorlopige) aanslag, minimum € 50, maximum € 6.709 per default, with a proportional reduction and a € 50 floor if the aanslag is reduced. For 2020 to 2024 the amounts were € 385, up to € 5.514, € 2.757 and up to € 5.514. For Vpb the amounts are € 3.354 up to € 6.709. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.25 IB missed deadline route

After the deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering and then an aanmaning with 10 werkdagen. Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete. The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag. No return at all gives an ambtshalve aanslag plus a verzuimboete. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a vergrijpboete, also with a navorderingsaanslag. No boete is given when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.26 Vergrijpboete percentages

The vergrijpboete is 25% for grove schuld and 50% for opzet. In box 3 the percentages are 75% and 150%, and aggravated forms are 100% and 300%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.27 Requesting a return

The Belastingdienst requests a return within 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks coulance. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.28 Filing without an invitation, tax year 2022

Without an aangiftebrief a return can still be filed. The window is up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2022 the last day is 31 December 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen

### 3.29 Bewaarplicht

AWR art. 52 lid 4 requires administratieplichtigen to keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager, except for the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table gives basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basisgegevens include the grootboek, the debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, the voorraadadministratie, the in- and verkoopadministratie, the loonadministratie and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### 3.30 KVK registration window

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 requires the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.31 KVK and Belastingdienst registration

KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration with the Belastingdienst is needed. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. The KVK page states the btw-id and omzetbelastingnummer within two weeks or within ten werkdagen, ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks, and the Belastingdienst page settles at most 10 werkdagen. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/ and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.32 KVK service terms

KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when the registration is within the window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.33 End of the remaining zachte landing

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply, no aanwijzingen are given, and schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, in which case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. The boetebeleid by year: 2025 no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and for correcties over 2025 this stays so. 2026 no verzuimboetes, vergrijpboetes possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. In 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse on 1 January 2027, so verzuimboetes are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. This is enforcement policy, not statute. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. For 2027 the stated ambition is to be fully part of regular handhaving with no separate plan. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf

### 3.35 Separate 2027 handhavingsplan

No separate 2027 handhavingsplan was published on 2026-10-11. The handhaving page says nothing about 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

### 3.36 Ingroeimodel

The ingroeimodel continues. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed, and only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf

### 3.37 VBAR in 2027

The Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, adds art. 7:610aa BW. The Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD) and the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan. The nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part. The act enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. It therefore applies throughout 2027. Lid 1 presumes an arbeidsovereenkomst for someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour. The werkende can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering and https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783 and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/

### 3.38 VBAR hourly amount 2027

The memorie van toelichting explains the € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% opslag for AOV and pension and a 15% opslag for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. The Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. For 2027 the amount was not published on 2026-10-11, by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026 it is about € 38 and it may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. ### 3.39 VBAR scope and articles

Lid 2 says the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3 makes the article inapplicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount follows from a ministeriële regeling. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html

### 3.40 Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to the end of 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf

### 3.41 Zelfstandigenwet

The Zelfstandigenwet is proposed. The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026 and the beoogde inwerkingtreding is January 2028, so the act does not apply in 2027. It would introduce a zelfstandigentoets (self-invoicing, KVK registration, number of opdrachtgevers, provisions against ondernemersrisico's) and a werkrelatietoets (freedom to organise the work, own account and risk). Meeting both and working that way gives a veilige haven. Those who do not qualify keep the current rules.
