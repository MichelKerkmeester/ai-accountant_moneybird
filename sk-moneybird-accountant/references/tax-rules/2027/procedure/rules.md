## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

One subsection per item in the key figures table, in the same order. Every statement follows the linked checked source.

### 3.1 IB return over 2026: filing period and deadline

Applies to anyone who receives a filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) for the income tax return over 2026. The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter, often 1 May. The filing period opens on 1 March. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. The filing invitation letter itself sets the date, so a different date in the brief replaces the usual date. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.2 IB filing period opens

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March. This gives the taxpayer the window in which the return can be filed before the deadline in the filing invitation letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

### 3.3 IB deferral (uitstel)

Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente) is charged. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.4 IB deferral channel

Online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) is possible until 1 May and gives deferral to 1 September, but only for someone who received a filing invitation letter. The deferral page says online is not possible. Read in full, that page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both texts are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) text, so online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date and the paper form otherwise. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte

### 3.5 Fiscal partner and the deferral form

The fiscal partner must request deferral separately. The form can be used only for the first deferral request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.6 AWR art. 9 lid 1 and 2

For assessment taxes (aanslagbelastingen) the return is filed within a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the invitation. Art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspector extend that term under conditions. The text is the same in the 2020 and 2026 versions and no 2027 change was found. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.7 Definitive assessment term, AWR art. 11 lid 3

The power to set the assessment (aanslag) lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file under art. 6 lid 2 or 3 is made, or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. The same text holds on 1 January 2020 and on 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.8 Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2027

The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (hoger dan) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) before the end of the year count toward the threshold (grens), a paid provisional assessment does not, and a refund received after filing does not. For 2027 the two values were not published on 2026-10-11. Older captures used equal to or more than (gelijk aan of meer dan) and printed each threshold one euro higher, which for whole-euro amounts describes the same threshold. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag and https://web.archive.org/web/20250328/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

### 3.9 Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), AWR art. 16

Paragraph 1 requires a new fact. The fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Paragraph 2 adds the cases where a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (voorlopige teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (voorlopige verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and an error (fout) that was reasonably knowable (redelijkerwijs kenbaar), always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Paragraph 3 gives 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral, and for an error-based (fout) additional assessment (navordering) only 2 years after the assessment or decision. Paragraph 4 gives 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Paragraph 5 extends by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, where additional assessment was possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag

### 3.10 Payment terms of assessments, Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9

An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. A provisional assessment IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date, with the first term (termijn) one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. The text is the same on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026, and no 2027 change was found. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2020-01-01

### 3.11 Objection period (bezwaartermijn)

Objection (bezwaar) is filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment. For income tax it can be filed online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if the objection asks for that. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. Against a provisional assessment IB no objection is possible, only a change. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar

### 3.12 Request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering)

After the objection term a request for ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) is possible. It can be filed online only if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The page gives assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025 and assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request goes by letter. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

### 3.13 Provisional assessment change deadline

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst)). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year. With a filing invitation letter the deadline is the filing date or the deferral date, and changing is no longer possible after filing. The page example for 2025 gives usually until 1 May 2026, with deferral until 1 September 2026, and a change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen

### 3.14 Tax interest 2027 rate

The 2027 rate was not published on 2026-10-11. It is set before 1 January 2027. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year from the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen), rounded to half percentages, with a minimum of 4,5%. For Vpb, withholding tax (bronbelasting), minimum tax (minimumbelasting) and solidarity contribution (solidariteitsbijdrage) the addition is 5,5 points with a minimum of 5,5%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.15 Tax interest, last set rate before 2027

The last set rate is 5% from 1 January 2026. Earlier rates for all taxes except Vpb and allowances: 4% from 1 April 2014 to 31 May 2020, 0,01% from 1 June 2020 to 30 September 2020 (for income tax the reduction started 1 July 2020), 4% from 1 October 2020 to 30 June 2023, 6% from 1 July 2023 to 31 December 2023, 7,5% in 2024 and 6,5% in 2025. The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT and the other taxes. The update date of the mass-objection page is 25 February 2026, and the page gives no date for the collective decisions. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage

### 3.16 Tax interest on income tax in 2027 and the count basis

Tax interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No tax interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The count is 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. For the return over 2026 the tax interest starts 1 July 2027 at the 2027 rate. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting

### 3.17 Tax interest on VAT

Tax interest on VAT is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment. For late payment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No tax interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen), with an end at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is not technically feasible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting

### 3.18 Vpb tax interest

For Vpb the addition to the ECB rate is 5,5 points, with a minimum of 5,5%. After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes: 2026 5 instead of 7,5, 2025 6,5 instead of 9, 2024 7,5 instead of 10, 1 July to 31 December 2023 6 instead of 8, and 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2023 4 instead of 8. For 2027 the Vpb rate is set by the same yearly rule and was not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

### 3.19 VAT filing and payment dates for 2027

AWR art. 10 lid 2 gives the return for a period (tijdvak) a term set by the inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1 requires payment within one month after the end of the period. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together, for monthly and quarterly returns, and 31 March of the next year for a yearly return. The return and the payment must both be received by that date. The later 2027 tables were not published on 2026-10-11, so the statutory rule stays. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

### 3.20 VAT dates already published for 2027

Q4 2026 and December 2026 are due by 31 January 2027. The yearly return 2026 is due by 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums

### 3.21 VAT software submission

Software can submit a VAT return (btw-aangifte) from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums

### 3.22 Deferral of the VAT return

Deferral for a VAT return (btw-aangifte) is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as in the event of a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit). The request is made in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and penalty remain possible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte

### 3.23 VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2027

The 2027 amount was not published on 2026-10-11. The rule is 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance) after the deadline, then a penalty. The amount was € 68 for 2020 to 2024 and € 82 for 2025 and 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte

### 3.24 IB penalty amounts 2027

The 2027 amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. The last published amounts are for 2025 and 2026: default fine (verzuimboete) IB € 469, up to € 6.709, not requesting a return € 3.354, and the payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) up to € 6.709. The 2026 page adds 5% of the open amount of the (provisional) assessment, minimum € 50, maximum € 6.709 per default, with a proportional reduction and a € 50 floor if the assessment is reduced. For 2020 to 2024 the amounts were € 385, up to € 5.514, € 2.757 and up to € 5.514. For Vpb the amounts are € 3.354 up to € 6.709. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.25 IB missed deadline route

After the deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering) and then a formal demand (aanmaning) with 10 working days (werkdagen). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. No return at all gives an ex officio assessment plus a default fine. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return gives a penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete), also with an additional assessment. No penalty is given when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.26 Penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete) percentages

The penalty is 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld) and 50% for intent (opzet). In box 3 the percentages are 75% and 150%, and aggravated forms are 100% and 300%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.27 Requesting a return

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration requests a return within 6 months after the tax debt arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks leniency. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete

### 3.28 Filing without an invitation, tax year 2022

Without a filing invitation letter a return can still be filed. The window is up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. For tax year 2022 the last day is 31 December 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen

### 3.29 Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4 requires persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) to keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Paragraph 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Paragraph 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager), except for the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current, so a 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), the accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), the inventory records (voorraadadministratie), the purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), the payroll records (loonadministratie) and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren

### 3.30 KVK registration window

Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet) art. 20 lid 1 requires the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required returns (opgaven) are made within one week after the fact. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.31 KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration

KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other forms get an RSIN, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes at most 10 working days. The KVK page states the VAT ID (btw-id) and VAT number within two weeks or within ten working days, ten working days equals two calendar weeks, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page settles at most 10 working days. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Start-up notification (Opgaaf startende onderneming) (not registered (niet ingeschreven) in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister)) is used. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/ and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.32 KVK service terms

KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) immediately when the registration is within the window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead and appointments up to eight weeks ahead. Source: https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/

### 3.33 End of the remaining soft landing (zachte landing)

Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply, no instructions (aanwijzingen) are given, and false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen) and supplementary payroll tax assessments (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen). Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction (aanwijzing) given before that date was not followed, in which case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. The penalty policy (boetebeleid) by year: 2025 no default penalties (verzuimboetes) and no penalties for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboetes) for the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) (the Flach/Aartsen motion (motie Flach/Aartsen), Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266), and for corrections (correcties) over 2025 this stays so. 2026 no default penalties, penalties for gross negligence or intent possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. In 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing lapse on 1 January 2027, so default penalties are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. This is enforcement policy, not statute. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

### 3.34 Regular enforcement (handhaving)

The tranche 2025 plan had stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. For 2027 the stated ambition is to be fully part of regular enforcement (handhaving) with no separate plan. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf

### 3.35 Separate 2027 enforcement plan (handhavingsplan)

No separate 2027 enforcement plan was published on 2026-10-11. The enforcement page says nothing about 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

### 3.36 Phase-in model (ingroeimodel)

The phase-in model continues. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith or an instruction given before that date was not followed, and only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf

### 3.37 VBAR in 2027

The Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026, adds art. 7:610aa BW. The House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD) and the Senate (Eerste Kamer) on 16 June 2026 by a vote by sitting and standing (zitten en opstaan). The memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the clarification (verduidelijking) part. The act enters into force on 31 December 2026 by Decree (Besluit) of 13 July 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026, chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone of being in force before 1 January 2027. It therefore applies throughout 2027. Paragraph 1 presumes an employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst) for someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour. The working person (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client (opdrachtgever) must prove there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. ### 3.38 VBAR hourly amount 2027

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains the € 36: 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025, plus a 25% surcharge (opslag) for AOV and pension and a 15% surcharge for general costs, times 1,5 for non-billable hours, gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (btw) at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, rounded up to € 36,00. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026, so the unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. The Senate, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 2026. For 2027 the amount was not published on 2026-10-11, by the MvT method on the statutory minimum wage (WML) of 1 July 2026 it is about € 38 and it may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 that sets the amount for the first application was not found on 2026-10-11. ### 3.39 VBAR scope and articles

Lid 2 says the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment (SZW) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 of the Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3 makes the article inapplicable when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Article II provides an evaluation within 5 years. Article III, paragraph 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount follows from a ministerial regulation. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html

### 3.40 Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten)

Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment (loondienst), but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. Approved agreements that were valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to the end of 2029 after motion (motie) Van Oostenbruggen (Parliamentary paper 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. The approval of the model agreements for free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer and https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf

### 3.41 Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet)

The Self-Employed Persons Act is proposed. The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026 and the intended (beoogde) entry into force is January 2028, so the act does not apply in 2027. It would introduce a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) (self-invoicing, KVK registration, number of clients (opdrachtgevers), provisions against business risks (ondernemersrisico's)) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) (freedom to organise the work, own account and risk). Meeting both and working that way gives a safe harbour (veilige haven). Those who do not qualify keep the current rules.
