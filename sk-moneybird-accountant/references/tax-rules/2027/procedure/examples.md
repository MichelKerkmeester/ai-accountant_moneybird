## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

All rules, rates, thresholds and official examples below come from the figures table or the checked material. Round example inputs are used only to show the method. Every step is written out. Calculated examples are built here. Official examples are copied from a primary source with the URL.

**Example 1. Uitstel for the income tax return over 2026 (Calculated example).**
Input: the figures table marks the deadline for the return over 2026 as not published and usually 1 May 2027. The taxpayer requests uitstel before that date. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra.
Step 1: start from 1 May 2027.
Step 2: add 4 months. The four months are May, June, July and August.
Step 3: the new filing date is 1 September 2027.
Note: uitstel usually means belastingrente is charged.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen

**Example 2. Bezwaar and the verzoek om vermindering (Calculated example).**
Input: a definitive aanslagbiljet dated 15 June 2027. The taxpayer disagrees.
Step 1: the bezwaartermijn is 6 weeks, which is 6 x 7 = 42 days.
Step 2: 15 June plus 15 days is 30 June, since June has 30 days.
Step 3: 42 minus 15 leaves 27 days, so the last day for bezwaar is 27 July 2027.
Step 4: the checked material states acknowledgement within 3 weeks. The example does not give the date the bezwaar is received, and the material does not give a clear start point for this term, so no acknowledgement date is calculated here.
Step 5: after the bezwaar term a verzoek om vermindering is possible. The online route needs an online filed return and fewer than 5 years after the tax year. The examples in the material are aanslag 2020 through 31 December 2025 and aanslag 2023 through 31 December 2028. For an aanslag over 2027 the same pattern gives 31 December 2032, since 2027 plus 5 is 2032.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting

**Example 3. Payment terms on assessments (Calculated example).**
Inputs: three assessments, each with the date 1 June 2027.
Step 1: aanslag. Invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date, which is 6 x 7 = 42 days. 1 June plus 29 days is 30 June. 42 minus 29 leaves 13 days, so 13 July 2027.
Step 2: navorderingsaanslag. Invorderbaar 1 month after the date, so 1 July 2027.
Step 3: naheffingsaanslag. Invorderbaar 14 days after the date, so 15 June 2027.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01

**Example 4. Voorlopige aanslag IB dated 15 March 2027 (Calculated example).**
Input: a paid voorlopige aanslag IB with the date 15 March 2027.
Step 1: the months remaining after March 2027 are April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December, which is 9 months.
Step 2: the aanslag is payable in 9 equal monthly termijnen.
Step 3: the first termijn is one month after the date, 15 April 2027. The termijnen then fall on 15 May, 15 June, 15 July, 15 August, 15 September, 15 October, 15 November and the last on 15 December 2027.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01

**Example 5. Navordering (Official example, then Calculated example).**
Official example: tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, where navordering was possible until 31 May 2022.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag
Calculated example: tax year 2023 with 4 months uitstel.
Step 1: the official example gives tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel and a navordering date of 31 May 2022. Counting 5 years from the end of the tax year gives 31 December 2021. Adding 5 months gives 31 May 2022.
Step 2: for 2023, 31 December 2023 plus 5 years is 31 December 2028.
Step 3: add 4 months of uitstel. 31 December 2028 plus 4 months is 30 April 2029, since 31 April does not exist.
Step 4: for a fout-based navordering the term is only 2 years after the assessment or decision. For income or assets held or arising abroad it is 12 years.
Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

**Example 6. Belastingrente on the 2026 return (Calculated example, method only).**
Inputs: the return over 2026 is filed on 20 April 2027, before 1 May. The aanslag deviates from the return, so rente is charged. Aanslag date 1 October 2027. Tax due € 10.000,00.
Step 1: the period starts 1 July 2027.
Step 2: it ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, 6 x 7 = 42 days. 1 October plus 30 days is 31 October. 42 minus 30 leaves 12 days, so 12 November 2027.
Step 3: count with 30 days per month and 360 days per year. 1 July to 1 November is 4 months, 4 x 30 = 120 days. 1 November to 12 November is 11 days. 120 plus 11 is 131 days.
Step 4: the 2027 rate was not published on 2026-10-11, so the 2027 amount cannot be given. With the last set rate of 5% from 1 January 2026 as a method illustration only:
€ 10.000,00 x 5% = € 500,00 per year.
€ 500,00 x 131 = € 65.500,00.
€ 65.500,00 / 360 = € 181,9444, rounded down to whole euros is € 181,00.
Note: the 5% is the 2026 rate, not the 2027 rate, so € 181,00 is not the 2027 amount.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente

**Example 7. Definitive assessment term for the return over 2027 (Calculated example).**
Input: assume the usual 1 May 2028 filing date and a granted uitstel to 1 September 2028.
Step 1: the Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2028.
Step 2: 3 years from 1 January 2028 is 1 January 2031.
Step 3: the term is extended by the duration of the uitstel. The uitstel runs from 1 May 2028 to 1 September 2028, which is 4 months.
Step 4: on a month count 1 January 2031 plus 4 months is 1 May 2031.
Note: the sources state the 3 years, the counting from 1 January and the extension by the duration of the uitstel. They do not print the exact end day.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag

**Example 8. Bewaarplicht for a 4-year lease starting in 2027 (Calculated example).**
Input: a lease with a term of 4 years, starting 1 January 2027.
Step 1: the retention term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. For the lease that is after the 4 years of the lease, so after 31 December 2030.
Step 2: the lease records are kept for those 4 years and then 7 more.
Step 3: 4 plus 7 is 11 years in total from the start of the lease. The 7-year retention term runs from 1 January 2031 and ends 31 December 2037.
Note: basisgegevens are kept 7 years. Onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data are kept 10 years. This example uses the 7-year term for the lease records.
Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01

**Example 9. KVK registration for a business that starts on 1 June 2027 (Calculated example).**
Step 1: the registration window starts one week before the start. 1 June minus 7 days is 25 May 2027.
Step 2: the window ends one week after the start. 1 June plus 7 days is 8 June 2027.
Step 3: registering within the window gives the KVK-nummer immediately.
Step 4: registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen, and ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks.
Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/ and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/

**Example 10. Betaalverzuimboete (Calculated example, method only, 2026 terms).**
Input: an open amount of € 4.000,00 on a (voorlopige) aanslag.
Step 1: the 2027 boete amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. The 2026 terms are 5% of the open amount, minimum € 50,00, maximum € 6.709,00 per default.
Step 2: 5% of € 4.000,00 is € 200,00.
Step 3: € 200,00 is above the minimum of € 50,00 and below the maximum of € 6.709,00, so under those terms the boete is € 200,00. No rounding is needed.
Note: this shows the method with 2026 terms, not the 2027 amount.
Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
