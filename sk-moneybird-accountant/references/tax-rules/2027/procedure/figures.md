## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedure for tax year 2027 for a ZZP sole trader and for a BV owner. It describes the income tax return deadline and deferral (uitstel) (extension), the VAT filing and payment dates, the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and the KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration duties. 2027 is a mixed year: the AWR terms, the Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) terms and the VBAR hold in their current texts, the Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) bill as introduced changes no AWR, Collection of State Taxes Act or tax interest rule, and several 2027 amounts were still unpublished on 2026-10-11, including the tax interest rate, the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens), the penalty (boete) amounts and the VBAR hourly amount after the 1 January 2027 indexation. From 1 January 2027 the remaining soft landing (zachte landing) in the enforcement of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) lapses, so default penalties (verzuimboetes) are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control.
