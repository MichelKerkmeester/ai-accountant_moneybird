## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA verzuimboetes: in 2026 no verzuimboetes and vergrijpboetes possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the zachte landing lapse, so verzuimboetes are possible again and the default bedrijfsbezoek start ends. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf
- DBA handhaving organisation: 2026 had a separate handhavingsplan, versie 2.0 of 31 December 2025, with 80 fte, NLA meldingen usable from 2026 and extra attention for government bodies as opdrachtgevers, the detectiemodule TKA and horizontal supervision levels. For 2027 the ambition is full integration into regular handhaving with no separate plan, and no separate 2027 plan was published on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- VBAR: in 2026 adopted but not yet in force. Entry into force on 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207, so the rechtsvermoeden applies throughout 2027. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html
- VBAR hourly amount: € 36 statutory base with unrounded € 35,43 at peildatum 1 January 2025, and € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. For 2027 about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, may change with the WML of 1 January 2027, UNVERIFIED, and the regulation was not published. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering
- Belastingrente: 2026 5%. For 2027 the rate was not published on 2026-10-11. It is set before 1 January 2027 by the rule, the last ECB herfinancieringsrente published before 1 November 2026 plus 3 points, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Belastingrente on the 2026 return: starts 1 July 2027 at the 2027 rate. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting
- Aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens: 2026 € 58 and € 18. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- IB boete amounts: 2026 verzuimboete € 469, up to € 6.709, not requesting a return € 3.354, and the betaalverzuimboete 5% of the open amount with a minimum of € 50 and a maximum of € 6.709. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- Btw aangifteverzuimboete: 2025 and 2026 € 82 after 7 kalenderdagen coulance, against € 68 for 2020 to 2024. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- IB filing deadline: the 2025 return had a usual date of 1 May 2026, with uitstel until 1 September 2026 in the page example. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen
- VAT dates: 2026 had full monthly and quarterly tables, from December 2025 by 31 January 2026 through December 2026 by 31 January 2027, and Q4 2025 by 31 January 2026 through Q4 2026 by 31 January 2027, with the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027. For 2027 only Q4 2026 and December 2026 by 31 January 2027 and the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027 were published on 2026-10-11, the later tables were not, so the statutory rule stays. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums
- Filing without an invitation: the five-year window moves one year forward. Tax year 2022 is open until 31 December 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen
- Vpb belastingrente: after HR 16 January 2026 the Vpb rate from 2022 equals the general rate, so for a BV in 2027 the general rate applies. Old to new: 2026 5 instead of 7,5, 2025 6,5 instead of 9, 2024 7,5 instead of 10, 1 July to 31 December 2023 6 instead of 8, 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2023 4 instead of 8. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Zelfstandigenwet: consultation open until 29 October 2026, intended entry into force January 2028, so no change in 2027. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers
- BAZ: no later status than awaiting the nota on 2026-10-11, with a procedure meeting set for 13 October 2026. The waiting period conflict, 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary, stays open. Sources: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)
- Belastingplan 2027: the bill text as introduced changes no AWR, Invorderingswet or belastingrente rule. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf
- Unchanged in 2027, the enacted law holds in the current text: AWR art. 9, 11, 16 and 52, the Invorderingswet art. 9 payment terms, the bezwaartermijn of 6 weeks, the verzoek om vermindering window, the bewaarplicht of 7 years and 10 years for onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data, the KVK registration window of one week before to one week after the start, and the modelovereenkomsten usable to 31 December 2029. No 2027 change was found. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- File the income tax return over 2026 in 2027 before the date in the aangiftebrief, usually 1 May 2027. Request uitstel before the filing date if needed. Uitstel gives 4 months, to 1 September, and usually means belastingrente. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting
- File and pay each btw-aangifte by the date in the table. The return and the payment must both be received by that date. The yearly return is due by 31 March of the next year. Q4 2026 and December 2026 are due by 31 January 2027 and the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums
- Settle the amounts of the voorlopige aanslag in the return. When the expected profit changes, change it in the Winst uit onderneming screen before the deadline, usually 1 May of the next year and not after filing. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen
- Keep the administration so that the bewaarplicht is met. Basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01
- Register the business through KVK within the window of one week before to one week after the start. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed, and the Belastingdienst registration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/
- A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete, and a btw correction or late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year gives no belastingrente. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting
- For a BV, the Vpb belastingrente follows the general rate from 2022 after HR 16 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining zachte landing lapses, so verzuimboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties are possible again and there is no default bedrijfsbezoek start. The ingroeimodel still limits corrections to periods from 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an earlier aanwijzing was not followed, and only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

### Open points for 2027

- The 2027 belastingrente rate is unknown and UNVERIFIED. The rate-setting rule is Verified, the rate itself is not set on 2026-10-11. It is settled by the Belastingdienst rate table after the ECB publicatie before 1 November 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- The 2027 VBAR hourly amount is adopted-not-yet-in-force and UNVERIFIED. The material gives about € 38 by the MvT method on the WML of 1 July 2026, and the amount may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. Using the MvT method with the WML of € 14,99 from 1 July 2026, the unrounded amount is about € 37,77, which also rounds up to € 38, but that is arithmetic, not a published figure. It is settled by the ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026
- The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 that sets the first applicable VBAR amount was not found on 2026-10-11. UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html
- The deadline for the income tax return over 2026, filed in 2027, is unknown and UNVERIFIED. The usual date is 1 May 2027. It is settled by the aangiftebrief. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen
- The 2027 aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens were not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- The 2027 boete amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- No separate 2027 handhavingsplan was published on 2026-10-11, status unknown and UNVERIFIED. The handhaving page says nothing about 2027. It is settled by a new plan or by the handhaving page. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- The 2027 DBA enforcement wording beyond the 2026 plan is open. The handhaving page is silent on 2027, but the Handhavingsplan 2026 states that the remaining zachte landing lapses on 1 January 2027 and that regular integration is the ambition from 2027. It is settled by a new plan or by the handhaving page. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- BAZ is proposed and UNVERIFIED. No later status than awaiting the nota on 2026-10-11, with a procedure meeting set for 13 October 2026. The 104 weeks waiting period in the bill text is UNVERIFIED against about one year in the docket summary. It is settled by reading the bill text or the nota. Sources: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)

### Conflicts and corrected details carried in this file

- VBAR hourly amount € 36 against € 38 is settled. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025, from the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the WML of € 14,71 at peildatum 1 January 2026. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026
- BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026 is settled, both are true. The inbreng was delivered on 27 May and the verslag (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Deciding source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912
- Uitstel channel online against paper is settled. Online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May for someone with an aangiftebrief, the paper form for other filing dates and after 1 May. Deciding sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte
- KVK btw-number term two weeks against ten werkdagen is settled. Both wordings remain on the KVK page, ten werkdagen equals two calendar weeks, and the Belastingdienst states at most 10 werkdagen. Deciding source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/
- Regular DBA handhaving start date is settled. The tranche 2025 plan stated 1 January 2026, the 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026 and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Deciding source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf
- Corrected detail: 25 February 2026 is the update date of the mass-objection page, and the page gives no date for the collective decisions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage
