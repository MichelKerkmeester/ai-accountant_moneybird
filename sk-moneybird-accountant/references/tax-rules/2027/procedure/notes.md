## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA default penalties (verzuimboetes): in 2026 no default penalties and penalties for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboetes) possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. From 1 January 2027 the remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) lapse, so default penalties are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf
- DBA handhaving organisation: 2026 had a separate enforcement plan (handhavingsplan), version (versie) 2.0 of 31 December 2025, with 80 fte, NLA notifications (meldingen) usable from 2026 and extra attention for government bodies as clients (opdrachtgevers), the detection module (detectiemodule) TKA and horizontal supervision levels. For 2027 the ambition is full integration into regular enforcement (handhaving) with no separate plan, and no separate 2027 plan was published on 2026-10-11. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- VBAR: in 2026 adopted but not yet in force. Entry into force on 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207, so the legal presumption (rechtsvermoeden) applies throughout 2027. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html
- VBAR hourly amount: € 36 statutory base with unrounded € 35,43 at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, and € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026. For 2027 about € 38 by the explanatory memorandum (MvT) method on the statutory minimum wage (WML) of 1 July 2026, may change with the WML of 1 January 2027, UNVERIFIED, and the regulation was not published. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering
- Tax interest (Belastingrente): 2026 5%. For 2027 the rate was not published on 2026-10-11. It is set before 1 January 2027 by the rule, the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November 2026 plus 3 points, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Tax interest on the 2026 return: starts 1 July 2027 at the 2027 rate. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens): 2026 € 58 and € 18. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- IB penalty (boete) amounts: 2026 default fine (verzuimboete) € 469, up to € 6.709, not requesting an invitation to file a return € 3.354, and the payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) 5% of the open amount with a minimum of € 50 and a maximum of € 6.709. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete): 2025 and 2026 € 82 after 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance), against € 68 for 2020 to 2024. For 2027 not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte
- IB filing deadline: the 2025 return had a usual date of 1 May 2026, with deferral (uitstel) until 1 September 2026 in the page example. For the return over 2026, filed in 2027, the deadline was not published on 2026-10-11 and the usual date is 1 May 2027. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen
- VAT dates: 2026 had full monthly and quarterly tables, from December 2025 by 31 January 2026 through December 2026 by 31 January 2027, and Q4 2025 by 31 January 2026 through Q4 2026 by 31 January 2027, with the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027. For 2027 only Q4 2026 and December 2026 by 31 January 2027 and the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027 were published on 2026-10-11, the later tables were not, so the statutory rule stays. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums
- Filing without an invitation: the five-year window moves one year forward. Tax year 2022 is open until 31 December 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen
- Vpb tax interest: after HR 16 January 2026 the Vpb rate from 2022 equals the general rate, so for a BV in 2027 the general rate applies. Old to new: 2026 5 instead of 7,5, 2025 6,5 instead of 9, 2024 7,5 instead of 10, 1 July to 31 December 2023 6 instead of 8, 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2023 4 instead of 8. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): consultation open until 29 October 2026, intended entry into force January 2028, so no change in 2027. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers
- BAZ: no later status than awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag) on 2026-10-11, with a procedure meeting set for 13 October 2026. The waiting period conflict, 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary, stays open. Sources: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)
- Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027): the bill text as introduced changes no AWR, Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) or tax interest rule. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf
- Unchanged in 2027, the enacted law holds in the current text: AWR art. 9, 11, 16 and 52, the Collection of State Taxes Act art. 9 payment terms, the objection period (bezwaartermijn) of 6 weeks, the request for reduction (verzoek om vermindering) window, the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) of 7 years and 10 years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data, the KVK registration window of one week before to one week after the start, and the model agreements (modelovereenkomsten) usable to 31 December 2029. No 2027 change was found. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- File the income tax return over 2026 in 2027 before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), usually 1 May 2027. Request deferral (uitstel) before the filing date if needed. Deferral gives 4 months, to 1 September, and usually means tax interest (belastingrente). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting
- File and pay each VAT return (btw-aangifte) by the date in the table. The return and the payment must both be received by that date. The yearly return is due by 31 March of the next year. Q4 2026 and December 2026 are due by 31 January 2027 and the yearly return 2026 by 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums
- Settle the amounts of the provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) in the return. When the expected profit changes, change it in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen before the deadline, usually 1 May of the next year and not after filing. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen
- Keep the administration so that the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) is met. Basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office (belastingkantoor). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01
- Register the business through KVK within the window of one week before to one week after the start. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), so no separate registration is needed, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/
- A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete), and a VAT correction or late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year gives no tax interest. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting
- For a BV, the Vpb tax interest follows the general rate from 2022 after HR 16 January 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- From 1 January 2027 the remaining soft landing (zachte landing) lapses, so default penalties (verzuimboetes) for the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) are possible again and a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek) is no longer the default starting point for a control. The phase-in model (ingroeimodel) still limits corrections to periods from 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an earlier instruction (aanwijzing) was not followed, and only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

### Open points for 2027

- The 2027 tax interest (belastingrente) rate is unknown and UNVERIFIED. The rate-setting rule is Verified, the rate itself is not set on 2026-10-11. It is settled by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rate table after the ECB publication before 1 November 2026. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente
- The 2027 VBAR hourly amount is adopted-not-yet-in-force and UNVERIFIED. The material gives about € 38 by the explanatory memorandum (MvT) method on the statutory minimum wage (WML) of 1 July 2026, and the amount may change with the WML of 1 January 2027. Using the MvT method with the WML of € 14,99 from 1 July 2026, the unrounded amount is about € 37,77, which also rounds up to € 38, but that is arithmetic, not a published figure. It is settled by the ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 and the WML regulation for 1 January 2027. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026
- The ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2 that sets the first applicable VBAR amount was not found on 2026-10-11. UNVERIFIED. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html
- The deadline for the income tax return over 2026, filed in 2027, is unknown and UNVERIFIED. The usual date is 1 May 2027. It is settled by the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen
- The 2027 assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) were not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag
- The 2027 penalty (boete) amounts were not published on 2026-10-11. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete
- No separate 2027 enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) was published on 2026-10-11, status unknown and UNVERIFIED. The enforcement (handhaving) page says nothing about 2027. It is settled by a new plan or by the enforcement page. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- The 2027 DBA enforcement wording beyond the 2026 plan is open. The enforcement page is silent on 2027, but the Enforcement plan 2026 states that the remaining soft landing (zachte landing) lapses on 1 January 2027 and that regular integration is the ambition from 2027. It is settled by a new plan or by the enforcement page. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving
- BAZ is proposed and UNVERIFIED. No later status than awaiting the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag) on 2026-10-11, with a procedure meeting set for 13 October 2026. The 104 weeks waiting period in the bill text is UNVERIFIED against about one year in the docket summary. It is settled by reading the bill text or the memorandum. Sources: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912 and [Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)

### Conflicts and corrected details carried in this file

- VBAR hourly amount € 36 against € 38 is settled. € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025, from the unrounded € 35,43. € 38 is the same amount indexed to the statutory minimum wage (WML) of € 14,71 at reference date 1 January 2026. Deciding sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026
- BAZ report (verslag) date 27 May against 29 May 2026 is settled, both are true. The committee input (inbreng) was delivered on 27 May and the report (verslag) (36 912, nr. 5) was published on 29 May. Deciding source: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912
- Deferral (Uitstel) channel online against paper is settled. Online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May for someone with a filing invitation letter, the paper form for other filing dates and after 1 May. Deciding sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte
- KVK VAT number processing time of two weeks compared with ten working days (werkdagen) is settled. Both wordings remain on the KVK page, ten working days equals two calendar weeks, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states at most 10 working days. Deciding source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/
- Regular DBA enforcement (handhaving) start date is settled. The tranche 2025 plan stated 1 January 2026, the 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026 and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Deciding source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf
- Corrected detail: 25 February 2026 is the update date of the mass-objection page, and the page gives no date for the collective decisions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage
