## 5. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 1 below AOW age. In 2026 the table was 35,75% up to € 38.883, with 8,10% as the income-tax part and 27,65% as the premium part, then 37,56% to € 78.426, then 49,50% above, with statute fixed amounts of € 3.149 and € 18.001 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). For 2027 the enacted text gives 8,01% up to € 38.883, € 3.114 plus 37,59% to € 79.569 and € 18.407 plus 49,50% above (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The amended bill gives 36,23% with an income-tax part of 8,58% up to € 39.247, 38,16% to € 80.578 and € 19.138 plus 49,50% above ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). The top rate stays 49,50%.
- AOW age, full year. In 2026 the first rate was 17,85% up to € 38.883, then 37,56% to € 78.426, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2027 the bill gives 18,33%, with the first boundary at € 39.247 for people born in 1946 or later ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- People born before 1 January 1946. In 2026 the first rate was 17,85% up to € 41.123, then 37,56% to € 78.426, then 49,50% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). For 2027 the enacted text keeps the € 41.123 boundary at 8,01%, and the amended bill moves the first boundary to € 41.637 and the top-band fixed amount to € 18.431 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)).
- Premie volksverzekeringen. In 2026 the ceiling was € 38.883 at 27,65%, giving a maximum premie of € 10.751 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1). The checked 2027 material states the end of the first band as € 38.883 in the enacted text and € 39.247 in the bill but gives no separate 2027 premium ceiling or maximum premie (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- Tariefsaanpassing. In 2026 the percentage was 11,94% above € 78.426 and a deduction was worth at most 37,56% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen). The enacted 2027 text gives 11,91% above € 79.569 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2). The bill states an aftrektarief of 38,16% and no new lid 2 percentage (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf).
- Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age. In 2026 the amount was € 3.115 up to € 29.736 with a phase-out of 6,398% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026). The amended bill for 2027 gives € 3.154 up to € 30.910 with a phase-out of 6,350% ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). The phase-out base is the verzamelinkomen from 2025 onward.
- Algemene heffingskorting at AOW age. In 2026 the amount was € 1.556 with a phase-out of 3,195% above € 29.736 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026). The 2027 bill gives € 1.596, an afbouwpunt of € 30.910 and 3,358% up to € 78.426, and the amended AOW-age table is not published ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- Arbeidskorting. In 2026 the maximum was € 5.685, the afbouwpunt € 45.593 and the phase-out 6,510% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026). The 2027 bill first gave a maximum of € 5.929 and the amended bill gives € 5.889, with step amounts of € 1.142 and € 5.500, an afbouwpunt of € 47.834 and the same 6,51% phase-out ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)). The 2027 band thresholds and build-up percentages are not published, so a full band by band comparison with 2026 is not possible.
- IACK. In 2026 the threshold was € 6.239, the rate 11,450% and the maximum € 3.032 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026). The 2027 bill gives a threshold of € 6.316, the same 11,45% rate and a maximum of € 2.918 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). The IACK phase-down starts in 2027 under the enacted Belastingplan 2024 measure ([Kamerstuk 36725 XV nr. 5, verslag](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36725-XV-5.html)).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek. In 2026 the amount was € 1.200, or € 600 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). For 2027 it is € 900, or € 450 at AOW age under the 50% rule (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/zelfstandigenaftrek-verder-omlaag-0/ and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- Startersaftrek. In 2026 the amount was € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The 2027 enacted text is listed at the same € 2.123 and € 1.062 but is UNVERIFIED because art. 3.76 of the 2027 text was not reachable, and the bill reduces the amount by € 2.113 to € 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- MKB-winstvrijstelling. The rate stays the same, printed as 12,7% for 2026 and as 12,70% for 2027 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- Meewerkaftrek. In 2026 the rates were 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). From 1 January 2027 the proposed rates are 0,313%, 0,5%, 0,75% and 1%, and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/).
- Stakingsaftrek. In 2026 the maximum was € 3.630 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). From 1 January 2027 the proposed amount is € 908, and the facility is abolished in 2030 (https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/).
- Oudedagsreserve. No new additions have been possible since 2023 and this does not change for 2027 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- Indexation. The bill stops the indexation of art. 2.10 and 2.10a at the start of 2027 and indexes other amounts by 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor of 1,026, while the Tweede nota van wijziging undoes the freeze of the top bracket boundary for 2027 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)).
- The AHK and AK afbouwpunten for 2027 are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon and are final only after that wage is set in November 2026 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the profit calculation in the steps the law uses: winst, then removal of the vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen, then the ondernemersaftrek (including the zelfstandigenaftrek of € 900 for 2027 and the startersaftrek when it applies), then the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 12,70% over the profit after the ondernemersaftrek (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- The result is the belastbare winst uit onderneming that enters box 1. That income is combined with the other income from work and home and reduced by te verrekenen verliezen at that level (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.1 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- Keep the deduction items visible in the records, because the tariefsaanpassing is calculated from them when the income before deductions exceeds the top bracket boundary of € 79.569 in the enacted text. The adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen).
- The algemene heffingskorting and the arbeidskorting are applied automatically in the return and can be applied in the payroll by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie. The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in the payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting).
- The AHK payout to a minstverdienende partner is limited to a person born before 1 January 1963 and needs fiscal partnership for more than 6 months and enough tax at the partner (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The 2027 AHK and AK afbouwpunten are still linked to the wettelijk minimumloon of November 2026, and the Belastingdienst may process the 2027 voorlopige aanslagen on older parameters if the amendments come too late ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)).
- The checked sources state no 2027 income-tax filing deadline and no record-retention period. The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic.
- An income-related Zvw contribution exists alongside the income tax, and its base and rates belong in the Zvw reference (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Belastingplan 2027 is still a bill. The Tweede Kamer overview shows no vote at the time checked and a wetgevingsoverleg was scheduled around 11 or 12 October 2026 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022). The adopted law decides box 1, the algemene heffingskorting, the arbeidskorting and the IACK for 2027.
- Every proposed value in this file is open until the bill is adopted. That includes the bill box 1 tables (36,23%, 38,16% and 49,50% with boundaries € 39.247, € 41.637 and € 80.578 and fixed amounts € 19.138 and € 18.431), the algemene heffingskorting (€ 3.154, € 30.910, 6,350% and the AOW-age € 1.596 and 3,358%), the arbeidskorting (€ 1.142, € 5.500, € 5.889, € 47.834 and 6,51%), the IACK (€ 6.316, 11,45% and € 2.918), the aftrektarief of 38,16%, the startersaftrek reduction by € 2.113 to € 10, the meewerkaftrek rates and the stakingsaftrek of € 908 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)).
- The enacted 2027 amounts of the algemene heffingskorting, the arbeidskorting and the IACK in chapter 8 of the 2027 text were not reachable, so the enacted chapter 8 values are unknown (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
- The AOW-age algemene heffingskorting after the Tweede nota van wijziging is not published. The 3,358% belongs to the bill version ending at € 78.426, while the amendment moves the top bracket boundary to € 80.578 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- The arbeidskorting band income thresholds, the build-up percentages and the AOW-age arbeidskorting table for 2027 are not published. Only the step amounts, the afbouwpunt and 6,51% are printed ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- The first bracket rate for the year in which the AOW age is reached in 2027 is not published ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- The IACK cap income and the AOW-age IACK for 2027 are not published ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- The tariefsaanpassing percentage under the bill is not stated. The bill excerpt shows no change to the art. 2.10 lid 2 text, and the fiscal key table for 2027 gives only the aftrektarief of 38,16% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf).
- The 2027 startersaftrek in the enacted text is UNVERIFIED because art. 3.76 of the 2027 text was not reachable. The listed € 2.123 and € 1.062 conflict with the proposed reduction by € 2.113 to € 10, and the adopted law settles that (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- Conflict on the top bracket boundary. The earlier bill text has € 78.426 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)) and the Tweede nota van wijziging has € 80.578 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)). The € 80.578 is now the bill text, and it is still proposed. The nota naar aanleiding van het verslag confirms the rates are unchanged ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)).
- Conflict on the arbeidskorting maximum. The earlier bill text has € 5.929 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf) and the amended bill has € 5.889 after an increase of € 133 instead of € 173 on each of the three step amounts ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329) and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). The € 5.889 is now the bill text, and it is still proposed.
- Conflict on the below-AOW algemene heffingskorting phase-out. The earlier bill text has 6,638% ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301) and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf) and the amended bill has 6,350%, because the phase-out now ends at € 80.578 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). The 6,350% is now the bill text, and it is still proposed.
- Inference, not a stated rule. The continuation of the Anw and Wlz premiedelen at AOW age follows from the AOW-age first-bracket rates. The checked cross-year material shows the split for 2026, where 17,85% equals 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65%. The page checked states only that the AOW premium stops at AOW age, so this continuation stays UNVERIFIED as a stated rule (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).
- Inference, not a printed sequence. The order of the box 1 saldo under art. 2.10 lid 1, then the tariefsaanpassing under lid 2, then the heffingskortingen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen is not printed as one statement on any page read. It rests on the article structure (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The AHK and AK afbouwpunten of € 30.910 and € 47.834 are linked to the wettelijk minimumloon and are final only after the minimum wage is set in November 2026 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).
- The Belastingdienst may process the 2027 voorlopige aanslagen on older parameters if the amendments come too late ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), tweede nota van wijziging](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50329)).
- The 2027 eligibility continuation of the IACK child and co-ouder conditions is not confirmed by a 2027-specific page. The rules stated here come from the page covering 2024 to 2026 and from the annual pages for those years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
- The 2027 income limits for the AHK payout to a minstverdienende partner are not in the checked material. Only the age condition, the 6-month partnership condition and the set-off rule are checked (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen).
