## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

The memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag) holds 2027 purchasing power calculation examples (koopkracht rekenvoorbeelden) for households with two children and with mortgage interest (hypotheekrente) deduction (renteaftrek) (hypotheekrenteaftrek), and a starter's deduction (startersaftrek) effect table including the Zvw ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), nota naar aanleiding van het verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D50335)). Those are policy illustrations, not year-table examples, and they are not reproduced here. All examples below are calculated examples that use only the section 2 figures of this file.

### Calculated example: bill box 1 table below AOW age

Assume a taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) of € 100.000 and use the amended bill table, before deductions, rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and tax credits (heffingskortingen).

1. First band: € 39.247 at 36,23% is € 39.247 × 0,3623 = € 14.219,1881.
2. Second band: € 80.578 less € 39.247 is € 41.331. That amount at 38,16% is € 41.331 × 0,3816 = € 15.771,9096.
3. Top band: € 100.000 less € 80.578 is € 19.422. That amount at 49,50% is € 19.422 × 0,495 = € 9.613,89.
4. Total: € 14.219,1881 plus € 15.771,9096 plus € 9.613,89 is € 39.604,9877, about € 39.604,99 before any statutory rounding.

### Calculated example: income-tax part (belastingdeel) and premium part of the first bracket (eerste schijf)

The bill prints 36,23% as the combined first rate and 8,58% as the income-tax part.

1. Premium part below AOW age: 36,23% less 8,58% is 27,65%, which matches the checked cross-year national insurance contribution (premie volksverzekeringen) rate.
2. The same split is visible in the checked cross-year material for 2026, where 17,85% equals 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65%. The checked material treats the continuation of the Anw and Wlz parts at AOW age as inference, not as a stated rule.

### Calculated example: full year at AOW age, bill

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 40.000.

For a person born in 1946 or later:

1. First band: € 39.247 at 18,33% is € 39.247 × 0,1833 = € 7.193,9751.
2. Remainder: € 40.000 less € 39.247 is € 753.
3. € 753 at 38,16% is € 753 × 0,3816 = € 287,3448.
4. Total: € 7.193,9751 plus € 287,3448 is € 7.481,3199, about € 7.481,32 before any statutory rounding.

For a person born before 1 January 1946:

5. The whole € 40.000 falls within the first boundary of € 41.637. At 18,33% that is € 40.000 × 0,1833 = € 7.332.

### Calculated example: enacted statutory box 1 table

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 100.000 and use the enacted text, which shows the income-tax part only.

1. The statute prints € 18.407 as the tax at the top bracket boundary of € 79.569.
2. Top band: € 100.000 less € 79.569 is € 20.431.
3. € 20.431 at 49,50% is € 20.431 × 0,495 = € 10.113,345.
4. Total income-tax part: € 18.407 plus € 10.113,345 is € 28.520,345, about € 28.520,35 before any statutory rounding.
5. This figure is the income-tax part only. The national insurance contribution is charged on top of it, up to the end of the first band.

### Calculated example: rate adjustment under the enacted text

Assume a taxable income from work and home of € 85.000 and qualifying deductions of € 10.000, and use the enacted 2027 figures only.

1. Income plus deductions: € 85.000 plus € 10.000 is € 95.000.
2. Excess over the enacted boundary: € 95.000 less € 79.569 is € 15.431.
3. Increase before the cap: € 15.431 × 0,1191 = € 1.837,8321.
4. Cap: € 10.000 × 0,1191 = € 1.191.
5. The increase is the lower amount, € 1.191.
6. Net effect of the deduction: the € 10.000 lowered the tax by € 10.000 × 0,495 = € 4.950, the adjustment adds € 1.191, so the net benefit is € 3.759, which is € 10.000 × 37,59%. On € 1.000 of deduction the net benefit is therefore at most € 375,90. This step is arithmetic built here, not an official example from a primary source.

### Calculated example: business profit to taxable business profit

Assume an entrepreneur who meets the conditions, a profit of € 60.000 and a claim to the self-employed person (zelfstandige)'s deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek). Ignore other entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) items and exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen).

1. Profit after the self-employed person's deduction: € 60.000 less € 900 is € 59.100.
2. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): € 59.100 × 0,1270 = € 7.505,70.
3. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 59.100 less € 7.505,70 is € 51.594,30.

This is the taxable business profit before other box 1 income is added and losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen) (te verrekenen verliezen) are taken into account.

### Calculated example: proposed general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting)

Assume an aggregate income (verzamelinkomen) of € 40.000 and use the amended below-AOW figures.

1. Income above the phase-out point (afbouwpunt): € 40.000 less € 30.910 is € 9.090.
2. Phase-out: € 9.090 × 0,06350 = € 577,215.
3. Credit: € 3.154 less € 577,215 is € 2.576,785, about € 2.576,79 before any statutory rounding.

### Calculated example: proposed employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) phase-out

Assume an earned income (arbeidsinkomen) of € 50.000, which is inside the phase-out range, and use the amended maximum and phase-out point.

1. Income above the phase-out point: € 50.000 less € 47.834 is € 2.166.
2. Phase-out: € 2.166 × 0,0651 = € 141,0066.
3. Credit: € 5.889 less € 141,0066 is € 5.747,9934, about € 5.747,99 before any statutory rounding.

### Calculated example: proposed income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK)

Assume the taxpayer meets the child and partner conditions and has an earned income of € 10.000.

1. Income above the threshold: € 10.000 less € 6.316 is € 3.684.
2. Amount before the cap: € 3.684 × 0,1145 = € 421,818.
3. This is below the maximum of € 2.918, so the amount is € 421,818, about € 421,82 before any statutory rounding.
