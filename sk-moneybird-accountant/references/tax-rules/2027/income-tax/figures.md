## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the box 1 brackets and rates for 2027, the general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting), the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) and the income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK), the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) and the route from profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) to taxable box 1 income. Three layers apply for 2027. The Wet IB 2001 text valid from 1 January 2027 is adopted but not yet in force, Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) is still a bill with a second memorandum of amendment (Tweede nota van wijziging), and several tables and amounts are not published at all. The proposed box 1 tables, the tax credits and the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) can therefore still change before the year starts.
