## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official examples for 2027 were found in the checked sources, so every example below is marked `Calculated example`. Every figure used comes from section 2. All inputs are invented round amounts.

### Calculated example: annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2027 with a profit of € 60.000

A self-employed person (zelfstandige) without pension accrual and without an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve, FOR) had a profit of € 60.000 in 2026. The 2027 annual allowance uses the 2026 income. The 2027 franchise is UNVERIFIED, so the calculation uses the € 19.172 still in the 2027 text.

1. Cap the income at the income cap (aftoppingsgrens): min(€ 60.000, € 137.800) = € 60.000.
2. Subtract the franchise: € 60.000 - € 19.172 = € 40.828.
3. Apply the percentage: € 40.828 x 0,30 = € 12.248,40.

Outcome: at most € 12.248,40 of annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) is deductible in 2027. If the 2027 franchise is set higher than € 19.172, the deductible amount falls.

### Calculated example: maximum annual allowance 2027 with the full income cap

A taxpayer had an income of € 200.000 in 2026.

1. Cap the income at the income cap: min(€ 200.000, € 137.800) = € 137.800.
2. Subtract the franchise: € 137.800 - € 19.172 = € 118.628.
3. Apply the percentage: € 118.628 x 0,30 = € 35.588,40.

Outcome: € 35.588,40 on the amounts still in the 2027 text. This matches the open point that with the frozen income cap and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589.

### Calculated example: catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) in 2027

A taxpayer has unused annual allowance of € 8.000 from 2017 and € 5.000 from 2018.

1. Window for 2027: the annual allowance of 2017 to 2026. Both years fall inside it. Unused 2016 annual allowance cannot be used because it lapses in 2027.
2. Unused annual allowance inside the window: € 8.000 + € 5.000 = € 13.000.
3. Cap: min(€ 13.000, € 42.753) = € 13.000. The € 42.753 is the amount still in the 2027 text and is UNVERIFIED.

Outcome: up to € 13.000 of extra annuity premiums is deductible on request in the tax return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. Using catch-up allowance before annual allowance prevents the older 2017 annual allowance from lapsing.

### Calculated example: temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) above the cap

A taxpayer wants a yearly benefit (uitkering) of € 30.000 with 5 yearly instalments (termijnen) and pays premiums in 2027. The 2027 cap is not published, so the calculation uses the € 27.192 still in the 2027 text.

1. Amount above the cap: € 30.000 - € 27.192 = € 2.808.
2. Instalments: 5 years, which meets the minimum of at least five years of instalments.
3. Deductibility: the premiums above the cap are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years. With 5 instalments the € 2.808 above the cap is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar).

Outcome: with 5 instalments the premiums up to the cap are deductible and the above-cap part is not. If the same benefit runs at least 20 years, the above-cap premiums stay deductible.

### Calculated example: profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) to annuity (lijfrente) in tier b

A taxpayer ceases the business (onderneming) in 2027 at an age 10 years below the AOW age and does not start the annuity immediately. That puts the case in tier b. The 2027 amounts are not published, so the calculation uses the € 287.445 still in the 2027 text. The profit from business discontinuation is € 200.000 and € 25.000 of annuity premiums was deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years.

1. Cap after subtracting the earlier deductions: € 287.445 - € 25.000 = € 262.445.
2. Deduction limit: min(€ 200.000, € 262.445) = € 200.000.

Outcome: up to € 200.000 of the profit from business discontinuation can be converted into an annuity. Because the case is tier b, not tier a, the lower cap applies. The cap amounts are UNVERIFIED for 2027.

### Calculated example: the proposed 1,01248 indexation

Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) art. LVII proposes to index the annuity maxima with 1,01248. The bill was not adopted on 2026-10-11, so these outcomes are arithmetic on the amounts still in the 2027 text, not published figures.

- Catch-up allowance maximum: € 42.753 x 1,01248 = € 42.753 + (€ 42.753 x 0,01248) = € 42.753 + € 533,56 = € 43.286,56.
- Temporary old-age annuity cap: € 27.192 x 1,01248 = € 27.192 + (€ 27.192 x 0,01248) = € 27.192 + € 339,36 = € 27.531,36.
- Profit from business discontinuation tier a: € 574.867 x 1,01248 = € 574.867 + (€ 574.867 x 0,01248) = € 574.867 + € 7.174,34 = € 582.041,34.
- Profit from business discontinuation tier b: € 287.445 x 1,01248 = € 287.445 + (€ 287.445 x 0,01248) = € 287.445 + € 3.587,31 = € 291.032,31.
- Profit from business discontinuation tier c: € 143.732 x 1,01248 = € 143.732 + (€ 143.732 x 0,01248) = € 143.732 + € 1.793,78 = € 145.525,78.
- Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity (kleine lijfrente): € 5.513 x 1,01248 = € 5.513 + (€ 5.513 x 0,01248) = € 5.513 + € 68,80 = € 5.581,80.
- Commutation in case of long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid): € 51.440 x 1,01248 = € 51.440 + (€ 51.440 x 0,01248) = € 51.440 + € 641,97 = € 52.081,97.

### Calculated example: disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) against a lump-sum policy

A self-employed person with a profit of € 60.000 pays an invented AOV premium of € 2.400 in 2027.

1. Policy with periodic benefits (uitkeringen): the whole € 2.400 is an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and is deductible in full. It is not a business cost, so the profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) stays at € 60.000.
2. Policy that pays a lump sum: the € 2.400 is not deductible, so the deductible amount is € 0. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3.

Outcome: the form of the benefit decides the treatment, not the size of the premium.

### Calculated example: proposed BAZ premium and benefit

The BAZ is not in force (in werking) in 2027, so this example only shows the proposed percentages from the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT).

1. Profit (winst) € 38.000: € 38.000 x 5,4% = € 2.052 per year. € 2.052 / 12 = € 171 per month.
2. Profit € 25.000: € 25.000 x 5,4% = € 1.350 per year. € 1.350 / 12 = € 112,50, about € 113 per month.
3. Profit € 45.000: above the maximum insured-income base (grondslag) of € 38.000 used in the MvT examples, so the premium stays about € 171 per month.
4. Benefit check: 0,70 x 1,4286 = 1,00002, so at most about 100% of the minimum wage (minimumloon).
5. Profit € 25.000 plus € 25.000 social insurance wage (SV-loon): the insured-income base becomes € 13.000 and the premium about € 59 per month.
