## 5. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Maximum jaarruimte: € 35.589 in 2026 to not published for 2027. The open point is that with the frozen aftoppingsgrens and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589 ([Ministry 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Jaarruimte percentage: 30% in 2026 to 30% in 2027, unchanged ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Aftoppingsgrens premiegrondslag: € 137.800 in 2026, not indexed in 2026 by the amendement Grinwis c.s., to € 137.800 still in the 2027 text and not yet indexed. Belastingplan 2027 art. LVII proposes a freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Franchise premiegrondslag: € 19.172 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with € 19.172 still in the 2027 text ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Reserveringsruimte maximum: € 42.753 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with € 42.753 still in the 2027 text. Belastingplan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Reserveringsruimte look-back window: jaarruimte 2016 to 2025 in 2026 to jaarruimte 2017 to 2026 in 2027. Unused 2016 ruimte lapses in 2027 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Age limit jaarruimte: not yet AOW age plus 5 years in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged. The 2026 birth date limit for taxpayers born before 1 September 1953 has no published 2027 equivalent ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap: € 27.192 in 2026 by art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel c and the Ministry table, against € 26.781 in the stale FISIN 2026 rekening row, to not published for 2027 with € 27.192 still in the 2027 text. Belastingplan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.125, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap: € 63.288 in 2026 to € 63.288 for 2027, marked UNVERIFIED because FISIN 2027 returns 404 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Stakingswinst to lijfrente maxima: € 574.867 / € 287.445 / € 143.732 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with the same amounts still in the 2027 text. Belastingplan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Afkoop amounts: € 5.513 for a small lijfrente and € 51.440 for langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid in 2026 to not published for 2027, with the same amounts still in the 2027 text ([art. 3.133, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- AOV premium: fully deductible with periodic uitkeringen under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged, with the same four exclusions and the same section 15.4 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- AOV premium with a lump-sum payout: not deductible, payout in box 3 in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Oudedagsreserve: closed to new toevoegingen in 2026 and in 2027, an existing FOR follows art. 10a.29. The FOR or stakingswinst conversion premium for the 2026 year had to be paid before 1 July 2027, and the same six-month option through art. 10a.29 lid 14 applies after 2027 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 10a.29, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- BAZ: ingediend 24 March 2026, "In afwachting nota nav verslag" in 2026, inbreng verslag on 27 May 2026, Verslag 36 912 nr. 5 published on 29 May 2026, no plenary date, to still not in force in 2027. Entry into force by koninklijk besluit, with MvT examples assuming 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Zelfstandigenwet: new on the 2027 timeline. Internetconsultatie until 29 October 2026 and a beoogde inwerkingtreding of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension ([Rijksoverheid, Zelfstandigenwet](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers)).
- Sectoraal pensioenfonds proef: unchanged, runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)).
- Belastingplan 2027 proposals are new for 2027: the aftoppingsgrens freeze to 2032 and the 1,01248 indexation of the lijfrente maxima, not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record lijfrentepremies in the year of payment or verrekening, because art. 3.130 lid 1 counts them in that year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Keep the premiums above the ruimte visible in the records. They are never deductible but they are taken into account when the uitkering is taxed, and the maximum applies to all lijfrenteproducten together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book the AOV premium as an uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen and keep it out of the winst uit onderneming, because it is not a business cost ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Book a mandatory beroeps- or bedrijfstakpensioen premium as a business cost of the onderneming, not as a lijfrentepremie ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep the FOR history, including the FOR at the start of the year and earlier conversions, because those amounts reduce the art. 3.129 cap ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- For the stakingswinst conversion and the staking option of art. 3.127 lid 5, pay within six months after the year end and record the choice for the aangifte, because art. 3.130 lid 2 then counts the premium in the earlier year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Diary the einddatum of every lijfrente. An einddatum in 2026 must be converted or started by 31 December 2027, and a death in 2026 opens a nabestaandenlijfrente window that ends on 31 December 2028 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Do not finalise the 2027 deduction figures before the December 2026 indexation regulation and the vote on Belastingplan 2027, because the franchise, the maximum jaarruimte, the reserveringsruimte maximum, the tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap, the stakingswinst maxima and the afkoop amounts are not published yet ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- If the sectoraal pensioenfonds of the branch allows joining, the premiums are deductible during the proef that runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Franchise premiegrondslag 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 19.172. Settling source: the Staatscourant indexation regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Maximum jaarruimte 2027 is not published and no Ministry table for 2027 exists. With the frozen aftoppingsgrens of € 137.800 and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589, but the exact figure cannot be given until the franchise is published ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Reserveringsruimte maximum 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 42.753. Belastingplan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 instead of the tabelcorrectiefactor 1,026, and the bill was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 27.192 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Stakingswinst to lijfrente maxima 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 574.867, € 287.445 and € 143.732 ([art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Afkoop amounts 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 5.513 and € 51.440, and the December 2026 regulation is pending ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- All indexed 2027 amounts wait for the December 2026 regulation and the vote on Belastingplan 2027. Settling sources: the Staatscourant regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation. The Belastingplan 2027 items, the aftoppingsgrens freeze for 2027 to 2032 and the 1,01248 indexation, are proposed and were not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Overbruggingslijfrente cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED at € 63.288. No 2027 page exists, because FISIN 2027 returns 404, so the amount is carried from FISIN 2026. Settling source: the FISIN 2027 chapter ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- No 2027 birth date limit for the jaarruimte is published, so the 2026 rule for taxpayers born before 1 September 1953 cannot be carried forward to 2027. Settling source: the Belastingdienst page for 2027 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- BAZ wachttijd conflict: the docket summary says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar" and bill art. 16 lid 1 says 104 weken. The conflict is settled in favour of 104 weken by the bill text and MvT 5.3.1 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)).
- BAZ is proposed, not in force. It awaits the nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, has no statutory entry date and the MvT examples assume 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- The 2027-01-01 law text exists at the base URL even though the article-level path returned HTTP 404 in the research. The base URL is the settling source ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- ODB 2026 factor A line: the ODB 2026 sheet lists factor A 6,27 under art. 3.127 lid 4 onderdeel a, but the 2026 law text no longer has a factor A in lid 4, which survives only through art. 10a.25 lid 1 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).
- No official examples for 2027 were found in the checked sources.
