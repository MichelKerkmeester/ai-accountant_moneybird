## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte): € 35.589 in 2026 to not published for 2027. The open point is that with the frozen income cap (aftoppingsgrens) and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589 ([Ministry 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Annual allowance percentage: 30% in 2026 to 30% in 2027, unchanged ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) contribution base (premiegrondslag): € 137.800 in 2026, not indexed in 2026 by the Grinwis et al. amendment (amendement Grinwis c.s.), to € 137.800 still in the 2027 text and not yet indexed. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) art. LVII proposes a freeze at € 137.800 for 2027 to 2032 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Franchise contribution base: € 19.172 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with € 19.172 still in the 2027 text ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum: € 42.753 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with € 42.753 still in the 2027 text. Tax Plan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Catch-up allowance look-back window: annual allowance 2016 to 2025 in 2026 to annual allowance 2017 to 2026 in 2027. Unused 2016 annual allowance lapses in 2027 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Age limit annual allowance: not yet state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) plus 5 years in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged. The 2026 birth date limit for taxpayers born before 1 September 1953 has no published 2027 equivalent ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap: € 27.192 in 2026 by art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel c and the Ministry table, against € 26.781 in the stale FISIN 2026 account (rekening) row, to not published for 2027 with € 27.192 still in the 2027 text. Tax Plan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.125, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap: € 63.288 in 2026 to € 63.288 for 2027, marked UNVERIFIED because FISIN 2027 returns 404 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) to annuity (lijfrente) maxima: € 574.867 / € 287.445 / € 143.732 in 2026 to not published for 2027, with the same amounts still in the 2027 text. Tax Plan 2027 proposes indexation with 1,01248 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Commutation (afkoop) amounts: € 5.513 for a small annuity (kleine lijfrente) and € 51.440 for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) in 2026 to not published for 2027, with the same amounts still in the 2027 text ([art. 3.133, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premium: fully deductible with periodic benefits (uitkeringen) under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged, with the same four exclusions and the same section 15.4 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- AOV premium with a lump-sum payout: not deductible (niet aftrekbaar), payout in box 3 in 2026 and in 2027, unchanged ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): closed to new additions (toevoegingen) in 2026 and in 2027, an existing old-age reserve (FOR) follows art. 10a.29. The FOR or profit from business discontinuation conversion premium for the 2026 year had to be paid before 1 July 2027, and the same six-month option through art. 10a.29 lid 14 applies after 2027 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [art. 10a.29, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- BAZ: submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026, "In afwachting nota nav verslag" in 2026, written contribution for the report (inbreng verslag) on 27 May 2026, the report (Verslag) 36 912 nr. 5 published on 29 May 2026, no plenary date, to still not in force (in werking) in 2027. Entry into force (inwerkingtreding) by royal decree (koninklijk besluit), with explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) examples assuming 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): new on the 2027 timeline. Internet consultation (internetconsultatie) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) until 29 October 2026 and an intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension ([Rijksoverheid, Zelfstandigenwet](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers)).
- Sectoral pension fund (sectoraal pensioenfonds) test (proef): unchanged, runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)).
- Tax Plan 2027 proposals are new for 2027: the income cap freeze to 2032 and the 1,01248 indexation of the annuity maxima, not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) in the year of payment or set-off (verrekening), because art. 3.130 lid 1 counts them in that year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Keep the premiums above the annual allowance (jaarruimte) visible in the records. They are never deductible but they are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed, and the maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Book the disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premium as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and keep it out of the profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming), because it is not a business cost ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)).
- Book a mandatory occupational or industry-wide pension (beroeps- of bedrijfstakpensioen) premium as a business cost of the business (onderneming), not as an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie) ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- Keep the FOR history, including the FOR at the start of the year and earlier conversions, because those amounts reduce the art. 3.129 cap ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- For the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion and the cessation (staking) option of art. 3.127 lid 5, pay within six months after the year end and record the choice for the tax return (aangifte), because art. 3.130 lid 2 then counts the premium in the earlier year ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- Diary the end date (einddatum) of every annuity (lijfrente). An end date in 2026 must be converted or started by 31 December 2027, and a death in 2026 opens a survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) window that ends on 31 December 2028 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- Do not finalise the 2027 deduction figures before the December 2026 indexation regulation and the vote on Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027), because the franchise, the maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte), the catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum, the temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap, the profit from business discontinuation maxima and the commutation (afkoop) amounts are not published yet ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- If the sectoral pension fund (sectoraal pensioenfonds) of the branch allows joining, the premiums are deductible during the test (proef) that runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Franchise contribution base (premiegrondslag) 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 19.172. Settling source: the Government Gazette (Staatscourant) indexation regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Maximum annual allowance (jaarruimte) 2027 is not published and no Ministry table for 2027 exists. With the frozen income cap (aftoppingsgrens) of € 137.800 and a higher franchise the 2027 maximum would fall below € 35.589, but the exact figure cannot be given until the franchise is published ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 42.753. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) proposes indexation with 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, and the bill was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 27.192 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) to annuity (lijfrente) maxima 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 574.867, € 287.445 and € 143.732 ([art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Commutation (afkoop) amounts 2027 are UNVERIFIED. The text still shows € 5.513 and € 51.440, and the December 2026 regulation is pending ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- All indexed 2027 amounts wait for the December 2026 regulation and the vote on Tax Plan 2027. Settling sources: the Government Gazette regulation of December 2026 and the 2027-01-01 law text after that regulation. The Tax Plan 2027 items, the income cap freeze for 2027 to 2032 and the 1,01248 indexation, are proposed and were not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).
- Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap 2027 is UNVERIFIED at € 63.288. No 2027 page exists, because FISIN 2027 returns 404, so the amount is carried from FISIN 2026. Settling source: the FISIN 2027 chapter ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).
- No 2027 birth date limit for the annual allowance is published, so the 2026 rule for taxpayers born before 1 September 1953 cannot be carried forward to 2027. Settling source: the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page for 2027 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
- BAZ waiting period (wachttijd) conflict: the docket summary says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar" and bill art. 16 lid 1 says 104 weeks. The conflict is settled in favour of 104 weeks by the bill text and MvT 5.3.1 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)).
- BAZ is proposed, not in force (in werking). It awaits the memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), has no statutory entry date and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) examples assume 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)).
- The 2027-01-01 law text exists at the base URL even though the article-level path returned HTTP 404 in the research. The base URL is the settling source ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- ODB 2026 factor A line: the ODB 2026 sheet lists factor A 6,27 under art. 3.127 lid 4 onderdeel a, but the 2026 law text no longer has a factor A in lid 4, which survives only through art. 10a.25 lid 1 ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).
- No official examples for 2027 were found in the checked sources.
