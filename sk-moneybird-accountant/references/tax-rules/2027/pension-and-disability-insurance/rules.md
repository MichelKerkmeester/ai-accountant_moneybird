## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

Art. 3.127 lid 1 lets a taxpayer with a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year deduct annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to a percentage of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the art. 3.127 lid 4 reduction for pension accrual ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 that percentage is 30% ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027-01-01 text exists at the base URL of the law. The percentage is not fixed in the law itself. Art. 10.2b lid 3 sets the annual allowance percentage each year equal to the art. 18a lid 1 Wet LB 1964 percentage, so the December regulation can reset it ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 30% runs from the Wtp and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" ([Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). The old percentage was 13,3% in the 2020-01-01, 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01 and 2023-01-01 texts, and up to 2022 the outcome was also reduced by additions to the old-age reserve (dotaties aan de oudedagsreserve) ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). From the 2023-07-01 text lid 1 names only the pension accrual reduction ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

The retroactivity of the surrounding changes is narrower than the 30% change itself. Stcrt. 2023, 34571 only deletes "onderdeel a," in lid 6, back to 1 January 2023, and Stb. 2025, 431, in force 1 January 2026, only rewrites the partner pension (partnerpensioen) wording of lid 4 back to 1 July 2023 ("partnerpensioen bij overlijden op of na pensioendatum ... voor zover deze ingelegde premies het gevolg zijn van de toeneming van de diensttijd in dat kalenderjaar") ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2026-01-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

A mandatory occupational (beroeps-) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). It is deductible as a business cost of the business (onderneming), with a restriction ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

### Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) contribution base

Art. 3.127 lid 3 builds the contribution base from the previous calendar year. The components are (a) profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) in the 2023-07-01 and later texts, and in the 2020 to 2023-01-01 texts profits from business activities before addition to and withdrawal from the old-age reserve (vóór toevoeging aan en afneming van de oudedagsreserve) and before the entrepreneur's allowance (en vóór de ondernemersaftrek), (b) taxable wages (belastbaar loon), (c) taxable income from other work (belastbaar resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) and (d) taxable periodic payments and benefits in kind (belastbare periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) ([art. 3.127, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The sum is counted up to the first-mentioned amount (eerstvermelde bedrag, the income cap) and then reduced by the last-mentioned amount (laatstvermelde bedrag, the franchise) ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

For 2027 the income cap in the text is still € 137.800 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes this amount with the contract wage development factor (contractloonontwikkelingsfactor) in the December regulation, so the number in the text changes with that regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). In the 2020 text art. 10.2b lid 1 derived the FOR maximum from the art. 3.127 lid 3 amounts times the FOR percentage and lid 2 indexed the income cap ([art. 10.2b, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) says the income cap was not indexed at the start of 2025 and 2026 (the Grinwis c.s. amendment (amendement Grinwis c.s.), Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90) ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)). Art. 3.127 lid 5 gives a cessation (staking) option. On (partial) cessation the taxpayer may ask to use the data of the year itself instead of the previous year ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

### Franchise contribution base

The franchise is the last-mentioned amount subtracted from the capped income ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 10.2b lid 4 sets the franchise at the AOW benefit for married persons without allowance (toeslag) plus holiday allowance (vakantieuitkering) times 100/75, fixed in the December regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01), [art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 franchise is not published. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 19.172, which is the 2026 amount ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Any 2027 calculation made with € 19.172 is therefore provisional and marked UNVERIFIED.

### Maximum annual allowance

For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: percentage x (min(income of the previous year, income cap) minus franchise) ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). No maximum annual allowance for 2027 is published and no Ministry table for 2027 exists ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The maximum applies to all annuity products (lijfrenteproducten) together. Premiums above the annual allowance (ruimte) are never deductible but are taken into account when the benefit (uitkering) is taxed ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).

When the taxpayer accrues pension, the outcome is reduced by the art. 3.127 lid 4 amount. The 2020 to 2023-07-01 texts use 6,27 times (keer) the growth (aangroei) of the yearly pension entitlement from additional service time (extra diensttijd), plus net pension (nettopensioen) premiums divided by the net factor (nettofactor). From the 2024-01-01 text the reduction is the premiums contributed (ingelegd) in the previous year for old-age pension (ouderdomspensioen) and partner pension, excluding a compensation premium (compensatiepremie) under art. 38s Wet LB 1964, plus net pension premiums divided by the net factor ([art. 3.127, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The transitional rules sit in art. 10a.25, not in art. 3.127. Lid 1 keeps the lid 4 text as it read on 31 December 2023 for a taxpayer accruing under art. 38q Wet LB 1964, and that also covers accrual between 1 January 2023 and 30 June 2023. Lid 2 gives, for a fixed premium per year of service (dienstjaar) under art. 38r, the formula as amended by Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 July 2023: A = ((1 - (X - Y) / X) x B) + Z, where Z is the net pension premium divided by the net factor. The older formula was A = (1 - (X - Y) / X) x B ([art. 10a.25, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

Deduction timing follows the payment. Premiums count in the year they are paid or offset (verrekend) (art. 3.130 lid 1) ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Premiums for the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) conversion (art. 3.129), the cessation option of art. 3.127 lid 5 and, up to 2022, the FOR conversion (art. 3.128) may on choice be counted in the year itself when paid within six months after the year end (art. 3.130 lid 2) ([art. 3.130, 2020 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). From 2023 the FOR conversion keeps this option through art. 10a.29 lid 14 ([art. 10a.29, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

### Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum

Art. 3.127 lid 2 caps the catch-up allowance ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 42.753, which is the 2026 amount ([art. 3.127, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). From the 2023-07-01 text there is one flat euro maximum, with no percentage cap and no age split ([art. 3.127, 2023-07-01 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). In 2020 to 2022 the cap was at most 17% of the contribution base and at most a euro amount, raised to a higher euro amount for a taxpayer whose age at the start of the year is at most ten years below the AOW age of that year ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). Tax Plan 2027 art. LVII proposes to index the catch-up allowance maximum with 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) 1,026, and that proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Catch-up allowance look-back window

Art. 3.127 lid 2 lets unused annual allowance of the preceding period be used on request in the tax return (aangifte), oldest unused year first ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text says "tien jaar", so for 2027 the window is the annual allowance of 2017 to 2026 ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states unused 2016 annual allowance lapses in 2027 and advises using catch-up allowance before annual allowance so older unused allowance does not lapse ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)). The period was "zeven jaar" in the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023-01-01 texts ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)).

### Age limit annual allowance

From the Wtp to 2027 the taxpayer must not yet have reached the age five years above the AOW age at the start of the calendar year ([art. 3.127, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe the limit as "de leeftijd die 5 jaar hoger is dan de AOW-leeftijd" ([FISIN 2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). In 2020 to 2022 and in the 2023-01-01 text the limit was the AOW age itself ([art. 3.127, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page for 2026 adds that a taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the annual allowance 2026 but may still use the catch-up allowance 2026 ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)). No 2027 birth date limit is published.

### Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

There are three annuity (lijfrente) forms: the annuity insurance (lijfrenteverzekering), the annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) and the annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht) ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). For an old-age annuity (oudedagslijfrente) under art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel a the first instalment (termijn) starts at the latest in the year the taxpayer reaches AOW age plus five, and the payments run until death. The FISIN example gives AOW age in 2030 and a start at the latest on 31 December 2035. For an account (rekening) or investment right (beleggingsrecht) the term runs at least 20 years plus the number of years the benefit starts before the AOW year, so a start at 63 with AOW age 67 gives 24 years ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). A survivor annuity (nabestaandenlijfrente) under art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel b for blood or in-law relatives other than the partner ends at death or at the latest at age 30, and for an account or investment right it runs at least 20 years, or at least 5 years for children under 30 ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). An annuity for an adult disabled child or grandchild (meerderjarig invalide (klein)kind) is deductible without a pension shortfall (art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel b) ([art. 3.124, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).

The temporary old-age annuity of art. 3.125 lid 1 onderdeel c needs at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of AOW age and at the latest in the year of AOW age plus five, with a yearly amount up to a statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The caps per year were € 22.089 (2020), € 22.443 (2021), € 22.735 (2022), € 24.168 (2023), € 26.463 (2024), € 26.781 (2025) and € 27.192 (2026) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The 2027 cap is not published. The 2027-01-01 text still shows € 27.192 ([art. 3.125, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN adds that premiums above the cap are deductible only if the benefits run at least 20 years ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). Tax Plan 2027 proposes to index this maximum with 1,01248 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Annuity end date (einddatum) 2026 deadline

An annuity that reaches its start date (ingangsdatum), called the end date in FISIN 2023 and later, must be converted or started within 1 full calendar year. FISIN 2026 gives end date 2025 meaning by 31 December 2026, and end date 2026 meaning by 31 December 2027. An extension can be requested in special circumstances (bijzondere omstandigheden) ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). If an annuity stops meeting the conditions, for example by gift, sale, pledge, loan, change of terms, commutation (afkoop) or unblocking (deblokkering), the value is taxed and in most cases revision interest (revisierente) is due ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Survivor annuity deadline for a death in 2026

For a survivor annuity the deadline is 2 full calendar years after the year of death. FISIN 2023 and later phrase it as "het jaar van overlijden plus de 2 kalenderjaren", with the same outcome. FISIN 2026 gives a death in 2026 meaning by 31 December 2028. An extension can be requested in special circumstances ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Profit from business discontinuation to annuity maxima

Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at a tier maximum minus the provisions already built. The amounts to subtract are the value of pension rights charged to profit, amounts deducted under art. 3.127 lid 1 and 2 in earlier years, earlier art. 3.129 amounts and, from 2023, the FOR at the start of the year and earlier FOR conversions under art. 10a.29 lid 13 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The tiers are: tier a for a taxpayer whose age at cessation is at most five years below the AOW age, or who is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity starting within six months, or who ceases by death, tier b for an age at most fifteen years below the AOW age, or an annuity that starts immediately, and tier c for the other cases ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 574.867, € 287.445 and € 143.732. The 2027 amounts are not published ([art. 3.129, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

FISIN 2023 to 2026 describe tier a as "61 jaar en 10 maanden of ouder", which matches only 2023, when the AOW age was 66 years and 10 months ([FISIN 2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2024/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). The law keeps "ten hoogste vijf jaar lager dan de pensioengerechtigde leeftijd" and the ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for 2026 ([art. 3.129, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). No 2027 age figure is published. Tax Plan 2027 proposes to index the profit from business discontinuation maximum with 1,01248 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity

Art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d allows commutation of a small annuity in one payment below a yearly amount ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 5.513, which is the 2026 amount. The December 2026 regulation is pending ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). A refund of a deducted premium is a negative expenditure (negatieve uitgave) without revision interest, and for an annuity only if the refund is undone within 30 days ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Commutation maximum in case of long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

Under art. 3.133 lid 9 commutation is not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached AOW age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if that is higher, the average contribution base plus franchise of the year and the previous year. The commutation then counts as an annuity term ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text still shows € 51.440, which is the 2026 amount of art. 3.133 lid 9 onderdeel c. The 2027 amount is not published ([art. 3.133, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

The bridge annuity is only available for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, in the year of reaching AOW age or in the year of starting a pension (pensioen). The yearly maximum is € 63.288 on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf)). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text ([art. 3.125, 2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). FISIN 2027 returns 404, so the 2027 value is carried at € 63.288 and marked UNVERIFIED.

### Disability insurance (AOV) premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen)

Art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c makes premiums for rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind (periodieke uitkeringen en verstrekkingen) in case of disability, illness or accident (invaliditeit, ziekte of ongeval) for the taxpayer expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen), with no annual allowance cap in the article ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). FISIN states the premiums can be "volledig aftrekken" ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)). The article is unchanged in the 2027 text ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Four exclusions apply. Not deductible are premiums the employer takes into account for payroll taxes (loonheffing), mandatory Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet) and WIA premiums, policies that pay a lump sum such as capital insurance policies (kapitaalverzekeringen), and Zvw premiums. These four exclusions and section 15.4 are word for word the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026 ([FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

For an entrepreneur (ondernemer) the AOV premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" and wage tax (loonbelasting) is withheld as prepayment (voorheffing) ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)). The page gives the rule of thumb that a premium that is not deductible means the payout is not taxed, and the reverse.

### AOV premium with a lump-sum payout

A lump-sum AOV premium is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3 ([Belastingdienst, AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers)). This follows from art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c, which requires rights to periodic payments and benefits in kind, so a policy that pays a lump sum such as a capital insurance policy (kapitaalverzekering) falls outside the deduction ([art. 3.124, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen)).

### Addition to the old-age reserve (toevoeging aan de oudedagsreserve)

Access to an addition to the old-age reserve (FOR addition) requires the hours criterion (urencriterium) and not yet having reached AOW age at the start of the year (art. 3.67). Art. 3.68 set the addition at 9,44% of the profit (winst), capped at a euro amount, reduced by pension premiums charged to the profit, and not above the growth of the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen). Art. 3.70 lid 1 allows a decrease (afname) by a chosen amount up to the annuity premiums taken into account, or by the excess over the business capital at cessation, at AOW age at the start of the year, or on failing the hours criterion this year and last year ([art. 3.67, 3.68, 3.70, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01)).

From 2023 no addition is possible and an existing FOR stays under the old rules (art. 10a.29 lid 1). Art. 10a.29 lid 10 and 11 add the transition for the annual allowance. For a taxpayer with a FOR the contribution base uses the profit before withdrawal (vóór afneming) of the FOR and, for 2023 and 2024, before addition and before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór toevoeging en vóór ondernemersaftrek), and for 2023 and 2024 the annual allowance is also reduced by the excess of the previous year's addition over the year-end withdrawal (afneming) ([art. 10a.29, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). The FOR-annuity conversion deduction is art. 3.128 in 2020 to 2022 and art. 10a.29 lid 12 from 2023 ([art. 3.128, 2022 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01), [art. 10a.29, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). On death the FOR is taxed unless the partner continues the business and takes the FOR over on request ([Belastingdienst, oudedagsreserve](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The 2027-01-01 text keeps the FOR closed to new additions (toevoegingen) ([art. 10a.29, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Income cap freeze 2027 to 2032

Article LVII of the Tax Plan 2027 bill proposes no indexation of the income cap of art. 3.127 lid 3 and of the pensionable salary (pensioengevend loon) at the start of 2027 to 2032, so the amount would stay € 137.800. The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)). Without the bill, art. 10.2b lid 1 indexes the income cap with the contract wage development factor in the December 2026 regulation ([art. 10.2b, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).

### Limited indexation of the annuity maxima

The bill applies a limited inflation correction of 48% of the table correction factor 1,026, giving 1,01248, to the annuity maxima listed in the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting). Those are the maximum for annuity provisions (lijfrentevoorzieningen) (art. 3.125), the maximum for the account and the investment right, the maximum premiums to be taken into account (in aanmerking te nemen premies) (art. 3.127), the profit from business discontinuation maximum (art. 3.129) and the maximum for negative expenditures (negatieve uitgaven). The proposal was not adopted on 2026-10-11 ([Belastingplan 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf)).

### BAZ in force in 2027

No. The Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen), bill 36 912, was submitted to the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) on 24 March 2026 and the docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag". The contribution to the debate (inbreng verslag) was delivered on 27 May 2026 and the report (Verslag) 36 912 nr. 5 was published on 29 May 2026. There is no plenary date. Entry into force is by royal decree (koninklijk besluit) and the explanatory memorandum (MvT) examples assume 1 January 2030 ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)). There is no mandatory AOV for self-employed persons in 2027.

### BAZ premium estimate

The bill provides a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks in art. 16 lid 1 ([bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)). The premium would be set each year by UWV and is estimated at about 5,4% of the profits from business activities up to the maximum insured-income base (grondslag). The MvT examples use a maximum insured-income base of € 38.000, giving about € 171 gross per month ([MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)). Self-employed persons who are also employees have their social insurance wage (SV-loon) subtracted from the maximum contribution base. The public premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not as a business cost, and UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes. Private policies keep the current tax treatment ([MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)). All of this is proposed and not in force in 2027.

### BAZ benefit maximum

The benefit is 70% of the insured-income base. That base is at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so the benefit is at most 100% of the minimum wage. The MvT examples give about € 171 per month for a profit of € 38.000 or € 45.000, about € 113 per month for a profit of € 25.000, € 0 for a negative profit, and an insured-income base of € 13.000 with about € 59 per month for a profit of € 25.000 plus € 25.000 SV-loon ([MvT, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html)). All of this is proposed and not in force in 2027.

### BAZ waiting period

Bill art. 16 lid 1 sets a waiting period of 104 weeks ([bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)). The docket summary still says "wachttijd van ongeveer één jaar", which conflicts with the bill text. The bill text and MvT 5.3.1 settle this in favour of 104 weeks ([Tweede Kamer, wetsvoorstel 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912), [bill text, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html)).

### Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) start

The internet consultation (internetconsultatie) for the Self-Employed Persons Act runs until 29 October 2026. The bill introduces a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets) that lead to a "veilige haven", with an intended (beoogde) entry into force of January 2028. The news item does not mention AOV, BAZ or pension ([Rijksoverheid, Zelfstandigenwet](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers)). It is not an AOV.

### Sectoral pension fund (pensioenfonds) test (proef)

A self-employed person may join the pension fund of the branch if the fund allows it. The premiums are then deductible and the test runs until 1 July 2028 ([Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij)). Art. 10a.26 keeps the experimental provision (experimenteerbepaling) for self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) pension schemes inside the pension definitions ([art. 10a.26, 2024 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01)). Separately from the test, a mandatory occupational (beroeps-) or industry-wide pension (bedrijfstakpensioen) premium is not an annuity premium (lijfrentepremie). It is deductible as a business cost of the business, with a restriction ([Belastingdienst, aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies)).
