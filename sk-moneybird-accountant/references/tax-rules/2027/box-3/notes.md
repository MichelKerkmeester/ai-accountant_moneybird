## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: 2026 was € 59.357 per person and € 118.714 with a fiscal partner, enacted ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [heffingsvrij vermogen page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)). The 2027 text prints € 59.357 per person before the December 2026 indexation, adopted-not-yet-in-force ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Belastingplan 2026 (Stb. 2025, 444) art. XLI lid 3 kept art. 5.5 out of the special 2026 indexation factor 1,027782 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html)).
- Heffingsvrij vermogen, new proposal: the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 wants the 2020 level of € 30.846 back from 2027. The proposal is not in bill 37 022 as published ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Tarief box 3: 36% in 2026 and 36% in the 2027 text, so no change ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Bank percentage: 2026 used 1,28% provisionally, with the final figure due by ministerial regulation in early 2027 ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). For 2027 the percentages are not fixed until after the year, and the 2027 text prints 1,37% as carry-over ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Forfait overige bezittingen: 2026 was 6,00% fixed, and the 2026 statute printed 6% ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026), [2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The 2027 text prints 6% and art. 10.6ter lid 3 has it replaced at the start of 2027 by ministerial regulation, so the 2027 figure is unknown on 2026-10-11 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Cabinet letter proposes a rise of 1,5 percentage point ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)).
- Debt percentage: 2026 used 2,70% provisionally, with the final figure due by regulation in early 2027 ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). For 2027 the percentages are not fixed until after the year, and the 2027 text prints 2,70% as carry-over ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Schuldendrempel: 2026 was € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, enacted ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [drempel page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). The 2027 text prints the same amounts before the December 2026 indexation ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: 2026 was € 26.715 per person and € 53.430 with a whole-year partner, enacted ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). For 2027 the amounts are € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner, and they are excluded from the 2027 indexation ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,1% in 2026 and 0,1% in the 2027 text, so no change ([2026 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [korting page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)).
- Repeal date of the green facilities: the planned lapse of afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 on 1 January 2027 was moved to 1 January 2028 by Stb. 2025, 444 art. XXXVIII onderdeel B, so 2027 still has both facilities ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2028 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2028-01-01)).
- Own use of real estate: art. 5.27a applies from 1 January 2026 and continues in 2027. The checked sources state 5,06% of the WOZ value with peildatum 1 January 2025 for 2026, and no 2027 percentage or WOZ peildatum ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).
- Reporting route for the actual return: 2020 to 2024 used a separate online form in Mijn Belastingdienst, and from 2025 the reporting is in the income tax return, so 2027 follows the aangifte route ([formulier page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).
- Wet werkelijk rendement box 3: in 2026 it was a bill and 2026 ran on the Overbruggingswet ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). In 2027 it is still a bill, now adopted by the Tweede Kamer on 12 February 2026 and still without an Eerste Kamer vote on 2026-10-11, so 2027 also runs on the category method ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- New in the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026: a novelle with the vermogenswinstbelasting for all financial instruments from 2028, about 90% of the assets with value development, a full switch for the other assets in 2030, and a heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)).
- Voorlopige aanslag and the actual return: for 2026 the Belastingdienst stated that no actual return can be taken into account in the voorlopige aanslag and that it is adjusted in the aangifte 2026 ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). The checked sources state no 2027 rule on this point, see section 7.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the value of banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden on the peildatum 1 January 2027, because the fictitious calculation works from the peildatum values (the peildatum is named in art. 5.24 and in art. 5.13, [Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html), [art. 5.13, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). Real estate such as a second home or a holiday home belongs in overige bezittingen in the official examples ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)).
- Keep green investment records: the value on 1 January, the split between green overige bezittingen and green banktegoeden because art. 5.13 lid 2 fills the exemption in that order, and the balance per partner because the korting is split in the same ratio as the green balance and is computed automatically ([art. 5.13, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie), [groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen), [korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)). A minor child must file its own return for its part and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)).
- The schuldendrempel is applied by the Belastingdienst in the return, so no manual step is needed. A joint drempel needs both partners' signatures on the return(s) ([drempel page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)).
- Partners: the heffingsvrij vermogen and the drempel double for whole-year partners, and the partners divide the joint grondslag in any ratio that totals 100%. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the joint rendementsgrondslag and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values ([heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- If the actual return may be used, keep the regular benefits plus the value changes over the calendar year. Gains and losses are netted in the year, a negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried forward, no heffingsvrij vermogen applies, and only interest on a box 3 debt and an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality are deductible costs ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). The Belastingdienst can send a form and ask for further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent, unless the data were already asked in the invitation to file ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).
- If a property is used privately or let, keep the days of own use, letting or leasing, construction and unusable days, because art. 5.27a excludes those days and the actual return counts changes during the year ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).
- What lands where: the box 3 voordeel is part of the 2027 income tax return. The actual return is optional and is reported in the same return from 2025 onward. No deadline for the opgaaf is stated on the checked pages ([werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte), [formulier page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement)).
- For 2026 the Belastingdienst stated that the voorlopige aanslag cannot take the actual return into account and that it is adjusted in the aangifte 2026. No such statement for 2027 is in the checked sources ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)).
- Box 3 is part of the personal income tax return of the sole trader and of the owner of a BV. This file covers the personal rules only. The checked sources do not describe how assets on the BV's own balance sheet are treated.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Indexed 2027 heffingsvrij vermogen and schuldendrempel: unknown until the December 2026 regulation and the vote on bill 37 022. The 2027 text prints € 59.357, € 3.800 and € 7.600 before indexation ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- 2027 other-assets percentage: unknown until the start-of-year ministerial regulation under art. 10.6ter lid 3. The 2027 text prints 6%. The Cabinet letter's plus 1,5 percentage point has no bill text and the resulting percentage is not stated ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)).
- Conflict, heffingsvrij vermogen: € 59.357 in the enacted 2027 text, before indexation, against € 30.846 in the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026. The € 30.846 is not in bill 37 022 as published, which touches no chapter 5 article. This is settled only by an adopted amendment or a nota van wijziging ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)).
- Conflict, green facilities in 2027: the statute gives € 200 per person, € 400 with a whole-year partner and 0,1% in 2027 and moves the repeal to 1 January 2028 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Rijksoverheid page still says the exemption and the korting "Per 1 januari 2027 vervallen deze volledig" ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds)). The statute prevails, but the Rijksoverheid page may reflect a later plan. Settled by the 2027 Belastingdienst green page.
- Proposed: heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028. Not law on 2026-10-11 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)).
- Proposed: Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, bill 36 748. Adopted by the Tweede Kamer, no Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 2026-10-11. 2027 runs on the category method ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)).
- UNVERIFIED: the Kamerstuk number "nr. 62" of the Cabinet letter and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page. The Eerste Kamer dossier lists the letter under 37.020 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255), [Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)).
- Unknown until after 2027: the final 2027 bank and debt percentages, which are set with retroactive effect to 1 January 2027 (art. 10.6ter lid 2 and 4). The 2027 text carries 1,37% and 2,70% ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Not in the checked sources: the aandeel rounding instruction for 2027. The 2026 page instructs 3 decimals in step 4 while its examples show 2 decimals, and the examples cut off rather than round. Settled by the 2027 aangifte software or a corrected page ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)).
- Not in the checked sources: a 2027 percentage and WOZ peildatum for the fixed method for own use of real estate. The stated figures are 5,06% of the WOZ value on 1 January 2025 for 2026 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).
- Not in the checked sources: a 2027 rule for the voorlopige aanslag and the actual return. For 2026 the Belastingdienst said it cannot be processed in the voorlopige aanslag and is adjusted in the aangifte 2026 ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)).
- UNVERIFIED: the claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return. hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-11. The available sources are the memorie van toelichting and the Rijksoverheid timeline ([Kamerstuk 36 203, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html), [Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).
- UNVERIFIED: the "or objected late" part of the 25 June 2026 non-objector rule is not on the Rijksoverheid timeline, which confirms only that non-objectors for 2017 to 2020 have no right to restoration for that period ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).
