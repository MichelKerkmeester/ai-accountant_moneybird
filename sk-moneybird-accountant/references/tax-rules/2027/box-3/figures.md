## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Box 3 (sparen en beleggen, savings and investments) taxes a fictitious return on banktegoeden (bank balances), overige bezittingen (other assets) and schulden (debts) with the category method, because the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (bill 36 748) is still a bill and the Belastingdienst says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028" ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box), [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). For 2027 the published text of the act, adopted but not yet in force on 2026-10-11, carries a rate of 36%, a heffingsvrij vermogen (tax-free allowance) of € 59.357 before the December 2026 indexation, a schuldendrempel (debt threshold) of € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, and green investment facilities of € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner plus a 0,1% korting (tax credit) ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The year is a mix of law, bill and announcement: bill 37 022 proposes an indexation factor of 1,01248 for the art. 10.1 amounts, and the Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes an heffingsvrij vermogen of € 30.846 and a rise of 1,5 percentage point in the forfait for overige bezittingen ([bill](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The December 2026 indexation and the final 2027 percentages for banktegoeden, schulden and overige bezittingen are not published on 2026-10-11, so the figures printed in the 2027 text are the working values for this year.
