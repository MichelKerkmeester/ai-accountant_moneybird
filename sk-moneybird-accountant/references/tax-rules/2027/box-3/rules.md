## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Category method (categorieënmethode, art. 5.2)

From 1 January 2023 the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3, law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534) applies the category method to everyone, and the 2027 text of the act still carries it in art. 5.2 ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Under art. 5.2 the benefit (voordeel, box 3 income) is the effective return percentage (rendementspercentage) times the taxable base for savings and investments (grondslag sparen en beleggen), a negative return (rendement) is set at nil, and art. 4.14 lid 1 keeps the flat-rate benefit (forfaitaire voordeel) at least nil ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) describes the calculation in six steps ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)):

1. taxable return (belastbaar rendement) = bank balances x bank percentage + other assets x other-assets percentage - (debts - threshold) x debt percentage.
2. return base (rendementsgrondslag) = assets - (debts minus threshold).
3. taxable base for savings and investments = return base - tax-free allowance. Partners may divide it in any ratio that totals 100%.
4. share (aandeel) = taxable base / return base x 100.
5. benefit = taxable return x share.
6. tax = rate x benefit.

The six-step description comes from the calculation pages for 2023 to 2026 ([2023](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023), [2024](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024), [2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025), [2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). The checked sources do not include a 2027 calculation page, and they list no official 2027 example. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year in the checked material, with 2 decimals on the 2023 and 2025 pages and 3 decimals on the 2024 and 2026 pages, so the 2027 instruction is not stated. It applies to everyone with assets and debts in box 3 on the reference date (peildatum).

### Reference date (peildatum)

The box 3 values are taken at the reference date 1 January. Art. 5.24 speaks of "die peildatum" and the green exemption is capped at the value of the green investments on 1 January, art. 5.13 ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html), [art. 5.13, 2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). Art. 5.24 adds a rule against reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage): transactions around 1 January within one continuous three-month period that starts before and ends after the reference date are disregarded unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons (zakelijke overwegingen) lay behind them ([Stb. 2022, 534](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html)). That rule does not apply to the actual return route, where income and value changes over the calendar year count and changes during the year count ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)).

### Tax-free allowance, 2027 text

The tax-free allowance applies to every taxpayer with box 3 assets. The 2027 text prints € 59.357 per person, before the December 2026 indexation. Art. 5.5 is in the art. 10.1 indexation list, so the published amount is indexed and the indexed figure is not published on 2026-10-11 ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount doubles with a fiscal partner for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership ([Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen)). Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint taxable base allocated under art. 2.17, and the 2027 text of lid 5 adds that the effective percentage uses the joint return base and the joint bank, other-asset and debt values ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount enters step 3 of the six-step calculation. If the return base is lower than the tax-free allowance, there is no taxable base to tax.

### Tax-free allowance, Cabinet letter

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes to bring the tax-free allowance back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The proposal is not in bill 37 022 as published, which touches no chapter 5 article ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). It therefore has no effect unless it is adopted in a bill text. The proposal conflicts with the € 59.357 in the 2027 text, see section 7.

### Indexation factor (indexatiefactor) art. 10.1, bill 37 022

Bill 37 022 art. LI proposes indexation factor 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor for the amounts in the art. 10.1 list ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). That list includes the tax-free allowance (art. 5.5) and the debt threshold (schuldendrempel, art. 5.3), so this bill is the second reason why the definitive 2027 amounts for those two items are not yet known ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amounts are not published. Status proposed.

### Box 3 rate (tarief box 3)

The rate is 36% for 2027 (art. 2.13 of the 2027 text). It applies to the benefit that comes out of step 6 of the six-step calculation, whatever the composition of the assets is ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026)). For the actual return route, art. 5.25 lid 1 replaces the benefit by the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek), so the rate is then applied to that amount ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

### Flat rate for other assets (forfait overige bezittingen)

The flat rate (forfait) for other assets applies to everything in box 3 that is not a bank balance, including investments (beleggingen), a second home and the non-exempt part of green investments ([2026 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The 2027 text prints 6%. Unlike the tax-free allowance and the threshold, this percentage is not indexed. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 has it replaced at the start of the year by ministerial regulation, set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds ([2027 text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 figure is therefore unknown until that regulation is published, and the printed 6% is what the text says until the replacement. The percentage enters step 1 as the multiplier on other assets. Its effect also works through the share, because the same base is in step 2 as well.

### Flat rate for other assets, Cabinet letter

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a rise of 1,5 percentage point in the flat rate for other assets in 2027, including rent and own-use benefits ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). The resulting percentage is not stated in the checked material, so it cannot be used in a calculation, and no bill text with the change was published on 2026-10-11. See section 7.

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

Only the part of the total debts (schulden) above the threshold is deductible. Art. 5.3 lid 3 says "daarvan meer bedraagt dan € ...", and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration computes the threshold in the return ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel), [2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 text prints € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner, before the December 2026 indexation. Art. 5.3 is in the art. 10.1 indexation list, so the definitive 2027 figure is not published on 2026-10-11. The threshold doubles for whole-year partners, and a joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return(s) ([Belastingdienst, drempel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel)). It enters steps 1 and 2 as the amount (debts minus threshold). Debts below the threshold are left out completely.

### Bank and debt percentage (bank- en schuldenpercentage)

Art. 10.6ter lid 2 fixes the bank percentage after the year, with retroactive effect to 1 January, at the sum of the DNB average monthly return on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, divided by twelve, with November counted twice. So it is an average, not a twelve-month sum. Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages ([2027 text, art. 10.6ter](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The final 2027 percentages are therefore set after 2027 and are unknown on 2026-10-11. The 2027 text prints bank balances 1,37% and debts 2,70%. These equal the 2025 figures in the published series, so they are carry-over ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, percentages](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend)). The checked material includes two regulation pages ([2025 regulation](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-8295.html), [2026 regulation](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html)). Both percentages enter step 1. The other-assets percentage follows the different route of art. 10.6ter lid 3 described above.

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

Art. 5.13 keeps green investments out of the assets up to € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner. The amount is not indexed for 2027 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2027 text art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie)). Conditions and exceptions: under art. 5.13 lid 2, from the 2023 text, the exemption applies first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption, and the parents report the rest as a normal asset ([2027 text art. 5.13](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk5/Afdeling5.3/Artikel5.13/informatie), [Belastingdienst, groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen)). The exempt part is not an asset, so it leaves both step 1 and step 2 of the calculation.

### Green investment credit (korting groene beleggingen)

Art. 8.19 gives a credit (korting) of 0,1% of the exempt amount on the reference date. The rate is 0,1% for 2025 to 2027 ([2027 text art. 8.19 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst, korting voor groene beleggingen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen)). It is computed automatically and split between whole-year partners in the same ratio as the green balance ([2027 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). In 2027 the credit follows the exemption amounts of € 200 per person and € 400 with a whole-year partner.

### End of the green schemes (einde groene regelingen)

Afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 lapse on 1 January 2028. Stb. 2025, 444 art. XXXVIII onderdeel B moved the date from 1 January 2027 to 1 January 2028, and the 2028 text has no art. 5.13 ([Stb. 2025, 444](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html), [2028 text](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2028-01-01)). Both facilities therefore still apply in 2027. The Rijksoverheid page says something different, see section 7.

### Tax-free result (heffingsvrij resultaat)

The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a tax-free result (heffingsvrij resultaat) of € 1.000 from 2028 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255)). It is not a 2027 rule and it is not law on 2026-10-11. It belongs to the package around the Actual Return Box 3 Act.

### Actual Return Box 3 Act (Wet werkelijk rendement box 3)

Bill 36 748 was submitted on 16 May 2025 and adopted by the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) on 12 February 2026. The Senate (Eerste Kamer) held an oral consultation (mondeling overleg) on 17 March 2026, a written report (verslag) on 7 April 2026, a memorandum in response to the report (nota naar aanleiding van het verslag) on 24 April 2026 and expert meetings on 19 May 2026, received a second written report on 29 May 2026 and a reply on 12 June 2026, received a letter on 19 June 2026 about improvements and the capital gains route, held a plenary session on 30 June 2026 with motions Van Rooijen (L), Van den Oetelaar c.s. (K), Kroon c.s. (J) and Schalk c.s. (M), where member Crone made a procedural motion (ordevoorstel) to postpone the vote until the plenary treatment of the announced amending bill(s) (novelle(s)), and voted on motion Schalk on 7 July 2026. Letters followed on 14 September 2026 (EK Q) and 30 September 2026 (EK 37.020 / 36.748, B), and a committee oral consultation on 5 October 2026. There is no Senate vote and no Staatsblad on 2026-10-11. Entry into force is by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028 ([Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). So 2027 runs on the category method, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, "probably 1 January 2028" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 announces an amending bill (novelle) that extends the capital gains tax on assets (vermogenswinstbelasting) to all financial instruments from 2028, about 90% of the assets with value development, with a full switch for the other assets in 2030. The amending bill must be adopted by the Senate before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Council of State (Raad van State) for urgent advice. The Senate dossier lists the letter under 37.020 ([Cabinet letter, Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255), [Eerste Kamer dossier](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). The Kamerstuk number "nr. 62" and a Council of State request on 2 October 2026 are not on a fetched page, see section 7.

### Actual-return report (opgaaf werkelijk rendement)

The Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3, law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195) entered into force on 19 July 2025 by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025 (Stb. 2025, 196), issued 18 July 2025 ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html), [Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). Art. V works arts. I and III back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV back to 1 January 2017. Art. II (own use of real estate, art. 5.27a) applies from 1 January 2026. The core rule is art. 5.25 lid 1: if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit, the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions. Lid 2: the actual return is at least nil. Lids 4 and 5: whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Lid 6: a form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur), in deviation from Awb arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Lid 7: further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Lid 8: lids 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Art. 5.26: the actual return is all benefits from assets and debts, regular benefits plus increase in value (vermogensaanwas). Art. III gives a rule for the personal deductions after a recalculation for 2023 or 2024, and art. IV adds art. 6a to the Restoration Act (Wet rechtsherstel) with the same form and six-week rule ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)).

Content rules for the actual return: income plus value changes over the calendar year, and changes during the year count. Gains and losses in one year are netted. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. There is no tax-free allowance in this calculation, "De Hoge Raad heeft bepaald dat u daarbij geen rekening mag houden met het heffingsvrij vermogen". Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. Reporting is optional and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount. You never pay more than the fictitious calculation ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement), [FISIN 2025](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/Box_3_Werkelijk_rendement), [Belastingdienst, heffingsvrij vermogen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen), [Belastingdienst, werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)).

Background: the route follows the Christmas ruling (Kerstarrest) of 24 December 2021 (Supreme Court (Hoge Raad), ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963) and the later rulings. On 6 June 2024 the Supreme Court held that the Restoration Act (Wet rechtsherstel) and the Bridge Act still conflict with the European Convention on Human Rights (EVRM) when the flat-rate return is higher than the actual return ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3), [2024 calculation page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024)). The explanatory note (nota van toelichting) to Stb. 2025, 196 also names 14 June 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:857), 2 August 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1086 and 1084) and 20 December 2024 (ECLI:NL:HR:2024:1788) ([Stb. 2025, 196](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html)). On 25 June 2026 the Rijksoverheid timeline confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode" ([Rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3)).

Own use of real estate: art. 5.27a sets the benefit at the economic rent value per year and excludes days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable ([Stb. 2025, 195](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html)). The fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value, with reference date 1 January of the previous year, times the usable days is a Dutch Tax and Customs Administration calculation method and it is not in the text of Stb. 2025, 195 ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)). The checked sources state that percentage for 2026, not for 2027.

Route: 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD, outside My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). 2020 to 2024 used an online form in My Tax and Customs Administration ("Mijn werkelijk rendement box 3 opgeven"), or a paper form via 0800 - 023 01 07 (one form per call) and several forms via the Tax Administration helpline (BelastingTelefoon) 0800 - 0543. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. 2017 to 2020 needed a letter, and all those letters had been sent. From 2025 the reporting is in the income tax return. No deadline is stated on these pages ([formulier page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement), [zonder brief page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen), [werkelijk rendement in de aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte)). So in 2027 the report goes in the 2027 income tax return.
