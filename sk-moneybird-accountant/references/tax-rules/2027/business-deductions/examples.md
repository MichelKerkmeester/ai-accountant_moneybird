## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

No official example for the tax year 2027 was found in the checked sources, so every example below is marked `Calculated example` and is built from the section 2 figures and the checked material. The inputs are invented round numbers and no real person's data is used. The enacted 2027 figures used here are adopted-not-yet-in-force. Every product is written with two decimals, and every step that rounds says "rounded" or "rounded down". Where an example touches a proposal, the proposal is named as proposed.

### Calculated example A. Self-employed person (zelfstandige)'s deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) and SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) on a profit of € 60.000

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur (ondernemer) € 60.000, the hours criterion (urencriterium) is met, no right to the starter's deduction (startersaftrek).

1. Profit as entrepreneur: € 60.000.
2. Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) under art. 3.76 lid 2: € 900. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not bite because € 900 is less than the profit of € 60.000.
3. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek): € 60.000 minus € 900 = € 59.100.
4. SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 12,7% of € 59.100 = € 7.505,70.
5. Taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming): € 59.100 minus € 7.505,70 = € 51.594,30.
6. The maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption together is 37,59% under the enacted text (49,50% minus 11,91%). Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) proposes 38,16% instead.

### Calculated example B. Starter with the enacted starter's deduction against the proposed bill amount

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 40.000, the hours criterion is met, the starter conditions of art. 3.76 lid 3 are met, below the state pension (AOW) age.

Enacted 2027 text:

1. Self-employed person's deduction: € 900.
2. Starter's deduction: € 2.123.
3. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 900 plus € 2.123 = € 3.023.
4. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 40.000 minus € 3.023 = € 36.977.
5. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 36.977 = € 4.696,08 (rounded).
6. Taxable profit from business: € 36.977 minus € 4.696,08 = € 32.280,92.

At the € 10 amount proposed in bill 37 022:

7. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 900 plus € 10 = € 910.
8. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 40.000 minus € 910 = € 39.090.
9. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 39.090 = € 4.964,43.
10. Taxable profit from business: € 39.090 minus € 4.964,43 = € 34.125,57.

The difference is € 34.125,57 minus € 32.280,92 = € 1.844,65 more taxable profit under the proposed € 10 amount. Bill 37 022 is still a proposal. It would abolish the starter's deduction from 1 January 2028. The House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) and the Senate (Eerste Kamer) still have to decide on it.

### Calculated example C. Starter at the AOW age

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 30.000, the hours criterion is met, the starter conditions are met, the AOW age has been reached at the start of 2027.

1. Self-employed person's deduction under art. 3.76 lid 4: 50% of € 900 = € 450,00.
2. Starter's deduction under art. 3.76 lid 4: 50% of € 2.123 = € 1.061,50, rounded to € 1.062.
3. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 450 plus € 1.062 = € 1.512.
4. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 30.000 minus € 1.512 = € 28.488.
5. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 28.488 = € 3.617,98 (rounded).
6. Taxable profit from business: € 28.488 minus € 3.617,98 = € 24.870,02.

This example uses the enacted text only, because the AOW-age starter's deduction under bill 37 022 is not stated in the checked material.

### Calculated example D. The profit cap and unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek)

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 800, the hours criterion is met, no right to the starter's deduction.

1. Nominal self-employed person's deduction: € 900.
2. Cap of art. 3.76 lid 5: the deduction is not more than the profit, so € 900 is reduced to € 800.
3. Unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek): € 900 minus € 800 = € 100. The tax inspector (inspecteur) sets this amount by a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar).
4. The € 100 is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. The offset is also set by a decision open to objection.
5. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 800 minus € 800 = € 0.
6. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 0 = € 0,00.
7. Taxable profit from business: € 0.
8. If the cap sets the deduction to nil in a year, that year still counts as a year in which the self-employed person's deduction was applied for the starter's deduction prior-use limit.

### Calculated example E. Starter whose combined deduction exceeds the profit

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 2.000, the hours criterion is met, the starter conditions of art. 3.76 lid 3 are met.

1. Self-employed person's deduction € 900 plus starter's deduction € 2.123 = € 3.023.
2. The cap of art. 3.76 lid 5 does not apply, because of the exception for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction.
3. The combined deduction exceeds the profit by € 3.023 minus € 2.000 = € 1.023. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) states that this excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years.
4. The SME profit exemption is a percentage of the amount after the entrepreneur's allowance. The Belastingdienst notes that in a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which it calls disadvantageous.

### Calculated example F. R&D work deduction and the R&D starter increase

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 60.000, the hours criterion is met, at least 500 hours of R&D work, an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO), and the starter conditions for the increase are met. The amounts are the enacted text before the December 2026 indexation, because the indexed 2027 amounts are not published.

1. R&D work deduction (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 15.979.
2. R&D starter increase: € 7.996.
3. R&D total: € 15.979 plus € 7.996 = € 23.975.
4. Self-employed person's deduction: € 900, because the hours criterion is met.
5. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 23.975 plus € 900 = € 24.875.
6. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 60.000 minus € 24.875 = € 35.125.
7. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 35.125 = € 4.460,88 (rounded).
8. Taxable profit from business: € 35.125 minus € 4.460,88 = € 30.664,12.

### Calculated example G. Assisting partner (meewerkende partner) deduction for a partner with 1.000 hours

Inputs: profit € 50.000 with no excluded components in the art. 3.78 base, the hours criterion is met, the partner works in the business without pay for 1.000 hours.

1. Band: 1.000 hours lies between 875 and under 1.225 hours, so the percentage is 2%.
2. Assisting partner deduction (meewerkaftrek): 2% of € 50.000 = € 1.000,00.
3. Self-employed person's deduction: € 900.
4. Total entrepreneur's allowance: € 900 plus € 1.000 = € 1.900.
5. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 50.000 minus € 1.900 = € 48.100.
6. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 48.100 = € 6.108,70.
7. Taxable profit from business: € 48.100 minus € 6.108,70 = € 41.991,30.

Boundary check: at exactly 1.225 hours the 3% band applies, giving 3% of € 50.000 = € 1.500,00.

Pay check: if the partner were paid € 4.000, that pay is below € 5.000, so it is not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) from profit. The partner then counts as working without pay, and the pay is no income for the partner.

The percentages are the enacted 2027 text. The start-ups bill proposes lower percentages and abolition from 2030, both still proposed.

### Calculated example H. Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) on a cessation profit of € 5.000

Inputs: profit made with the cessation (staking) of one whole business € 5.000, no discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years.

1. Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): it equals that profit, at most € 3.630, so the deduction is € 3.630.
2. Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 5.000 minus € 3.630 = € 1.370.
3. SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 1.370 = € 173,99.
4. Taxable profit from business: € 1.370 minus € 173,99 = € 1.196,01.

Variant: if € 1.000 of discontinuation deduction had been enjoyed in earlier years, the maximum would be reduced, not below nil, to € 3.630 minus € 1.000 = € 2.630, and the deduction would be € 2.630. The profit after the entrepreneur's allowance would then be € 5.000 minus € 2.630 = € 2.370, the SME profit exemption 12,7% of € 2.370 = € 300,99, and the taxable profit from business € 2.370 minus € 300,99 = € 2.069,01.

The maximum of € 3.630 is the enacted 2027 text. The start-ups bill proposes a maximum of € 908 and abolition from 2030, both still proposed.

### Calculated example I. Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) at a profit of € 20.000

Inputs: profit as entrepreneur € 20.000, a WIA benefit in the year, the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) of 800 hours is met but the hours criterion of 1.225 hours is not, the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of 2022 to 2026, below the AOW age. No self-employed person's deduction applies because the hours criterion is not met. The SME profit exemption applies with no hours condition.

1. Deduction not applied in the five preceding years: € 12.000.
   - Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000 minus € 12.000 = € 8.000.
   - SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 8.000 = € 1.016,00.
   - Taxable profit from business: € 8.000 minus € 1.016,00 = € 6.984,00.
2. Deduction applied for one year: € 8.000.
   - Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000 minus € 8.000 = € 12.000.
   - SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 12.000 = € 1.524,00.
   - Taxable profit from business: € 12.000 minus € 1.524,00 = € 10.476,00.
3. Deduction applied for two years: € 4.000.
   - Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000 minus € 4.000 = € 16.000.
   - SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 16.000 = € 2.032,00.
   - Taxable profit from business: € 16.000 minus € 2.032,00 = € 13.968,00.
4. Deduction applied for more than two years: the statute has no tier, so the deduction is € 0.
   - Profit after the entrepreneur's allowance: € 20.000.
   - SME profit exemption: 12,7% of € 20.000 = € 2.540,00.
   - Taxable profit from business: € 20.000 minus € 2.540,00 = € 17.460,00.
5. Profit cap: each amount is not more than the profit, so at a profit of € 3.000 an entitlement of € 4.000 would be reduced to € 3.000.

Bill 37 022 proposes to abolish this deduction from 1 January 2029, which leaves 2027 unchanged.

### Calculated example J. Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) on € 11.000 of deductions

Inputs: taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) after the deductions € 90.000, entrepreneur's allowance plus SME profit exemption together € 11.000.

1. Taxable box 1 income plus the deductions: € 90.000 plus € 11.000 = € 101.000.
2. Excess over the top bracket start of € 79.569: € 101.000 minus € 79.569 = € 21.431.
3. 11,91% of € 21.431 = € 2.552,43 (rounded down).
4. Cap: at most 11,91% of the deductions, being 11,91% of € 11.000 = € 1.310,10.
5. The rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) therefore adds € 1.310,10, the lower of the two outcomes.
6. Cross-check on the maximum value: 49,50% minus 11,91% = 37,59% under the enacted text. Tax Plan 2027 proposes 38,16% instead.
