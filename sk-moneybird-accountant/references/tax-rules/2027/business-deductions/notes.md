## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 1.200 in 2026 to € 900 in 2027 ([art. 3.76, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 amount is not indexed.
- Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age: € 600 in 2026 to € 450 in 2027, both being 50% of the amount for someone below the AOW age ([Belastingdienst, FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Hours criterion (urencriterium): unchanged at 1.225 hours ([art. 3.6, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Second test waived, lookback: the window moves from 2021 to 2025 in 2026 to 2022 to 2026 in 2027 ([Belastingdienst, FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.6, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium): unchanged at 800 hours, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) wording in 2026 was at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([art. 3.78a, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)).
- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek), enacted text: unchanged at € 2.123 ([art. 3.76, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Bill 37 022 would cut it to € 10 from 1 January 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- Starter's deduction at AOW age, enacted text: unchanged at € 1.062 ([Belastingdienst, FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). No page states the amount under the bill.
- Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid): unchanged at € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000 in 2026 and 2027, and bill 37 022 would end it on 1 January 2029 ([Belastingdienst 2026 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026), [art. 3.78a, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).
- R&D work deduction (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 15.979 in 2026 and the same € 15.979 in the 2027 statute text, which is the 2026 amount before the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.77, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amount is not published.
- R&D starter increase: € 7.996 in 2026 and the same € 7.996 in the 2027 statute text before the December 2026 indexation ([Belastingdienst, FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek), [art. 3.77, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): unchanged at 12,7%, and the explanatory memorandum table shows 12,70% for 2027 ([art. 3.79a, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.79a, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Assisting partner deduction (meewerkaftrek): the enacted bands and percentages are unchanged, being 1,25% / 2% / 3% / 4% over the same hourly bands ([art. 3.78, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.78, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The start-ups bill would cut them to 0,313% / 0,5% / 0,75% / 1%, with 0,32% in the article text for the first band, and abolish the deduction for assisting partner in 2030 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/), [start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)).
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): unchanged at € 3.630 maximum in 2026 and 2027 ([art. 3.79, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 3.79, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The start-ups bill would cut it to € 908 and abolish it in 2030 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): no additions in 2026 and no additions in 2027, the reserve stays and the reduction (afneming) rules run through art. 10a.29, which reads the same as in 2026 ([Belastingdienst, FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve), [art. 10a.29, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 to 11,91% above € 79.569 in the 2027 statute text ([art. 2.10, 2026 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [art. 2.10, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption: 37,56% in 2026, being 49,50% minus 11,94%, to 37,59% in the enacted 2027 text, being 49,50% minus 11,91% ([Belastingdienst, MKB page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling), [art. 2.10, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) proposes 38,16% ([sleuteltabel 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf)).
- Zvw maximum contribution income: not published for 2027, so no change can be stated here. The detail belongs to the Zvw topic ([rijksbegroting XVI](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business in a way that can be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. All hours on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count, so the Belastingdienst example counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage for an owner who is reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).
- Record the hours of a partner who works in the business without pay, because the deduction for assisting partner band depends on them and those hours must be made plausible ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).
- The entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption are applied on the profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) in the income tax return, in the order profit, then entrepreneur's allowance, then SME profit exemption ([art. 3.2 and 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)).
- A capped self-employed person's deduction creates an unused amount that the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets by a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar), and the offset in a later year is also set by a decision open to objection, so keep the decision with the year's working papers ([art. 3.76 lid 6 and lid 8](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- For an existing old-age reserve, annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) for converting the reserve that are paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return ([art. 10a.29 lid 14](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)).
- On death the old-age reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)).
- Someone who is not an IB entrepreneur (IB-ondernemer) reports the profit as income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), calculated the same way, without the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)).
- Because the starter's deduction, the deduction for assisting partner and the discontinuation deduction proposals are still with the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) and the Senate (Eerste Kamer), keep the enacted 2027 text and the proposed amounts apart in the working papers ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Conflict on the € 10 starter's deduction date. Artikel LXIII lid 1 onderdeel a of bill 37 022 names Artikel I onderdeel G, the starter's deduction of € 10, among the parts that work back to 1 January 2026, while the explanatory memorandum (MvT, memorie van toelichting) says the € 10 applies from 1 January 2027 and that 2026 is unaffected ([bill 37 022 and MvT](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [MvT, Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). The memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 2 October 2026 (2026D48289) does not change this. Settled only by a later memorandum of amendment or the adopted text.
- Conflict on the first deduction for assisting partner band under the start-ups bill: 0,32% in the bill article text against 0,313% in the bill explanatory memorandum table and on Ondernemersplein ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The article text is the operative proposal. Settled by the adopted text.
- Open timing of the start-ups bill: its text leaves the date to a royal decree (koninklijk besluit), and only the explanatory memorandum and Ondernemersplein name 1 January 2027 and 2030 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). Settled by the royal decree or the adopted text.
- Conflict on the 2027 starter's deduction: KVK states that the starter's deduction "vervalt" in 2027 and that 2026 is the last year, which matches neither the enacted 2027 text of € 2.123 nor bill 37 022 with € 10 in 2027 and abolition in 2028 ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium), [art. 3.76, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). Settled by the adopted text.
- Unknown: the starter's deduction at AOW age under bill 37 022 is not stated on any checked page, status unknown and UNVERIFIED ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The bill leaves art. 3.76 lid 4 unchanged, which would give 50% of € 10. Settled by a later memorandum of amendment, the adopted text or an explanatory page.
- Unknown: the indexed 2027 amounts of the R&D deduction and the R&D starter increase are not published, because they wait for the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77, 2027 version](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Settled by the published indexation.
- Unknown: the Zvw maximum contribution income for 2027 is not published, status unknown and UNVERIFIED ([rijksbegroting XVI](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)). Settled in the Zvw topic.
- Proposed and still pending, with the House of Representatives and the Senate yet to decide: starter's deduction € 10 from 1 January 2027 and abolition from 1 January 2028, starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs abolition from 1 January 2029 and the end of the arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for starters on 1 January 2028 through the End-of-Year Scheme 2026 (Eindejaarsregeling 2026) ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). Also proposed: deduction for assisting partner 0,313% / 0,5% / 0,75% / 1% with 0,32% in the article text, discontinuation deduction € 908 and abolition of both in 2030, the deletion of art. 10a.29 lid 7, and a maximum deduction value of 38,16% under Tax Plan 2027 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/), [sleuteltabel 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf)). Settled by the votes in both chambers and the published texts.
