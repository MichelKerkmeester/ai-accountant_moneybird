## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Hours criterion (urencriterium)

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur for income tax purposes (IB-ondernemer), and either (a) the total time spent on those businesses and on employment and other work is mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses, or (b) the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The hours criterion is a condition for the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) (art. 3.76 lid 1), for the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) through its link to the self-employed person's deduction, for the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) (art. 3.78) and for the R&D deduction. Who is an entrepreneur for income tax purposes is judged per activity and separately from the VAT, weighing whether profit is made and how much, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, entrepreneurial risk and liability for business debts, and activities in the hobby or family sphere do not count ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/)). Someone who is not an IB entrepreneur reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), calculated the same way, without the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/)). All hours spent on the business count, also non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). Standby time does not count, so the Belastingdienst example of a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage, and hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)). Hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur: a webhosting business started on 1 July means 6 months as an entrepreneur and still at least 1.225 hours in that period ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)), and a start on 1 May leaves eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium)).

### Second test waived, lookback

Art. 3.6 lid 1 onderdeel b waives the second test (the mainly test) for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, so for 2027 the lookback window runs over 2022 to 2026 ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The second test comes back only after five straight years as an entrepreneur. The FISIN handbooks print the window per year and older editions contain misprints: FISIN 2020 prints 2014 to 2018 and FISIN 2022 prints "20167 tot en met 2021", against the statute, which gives 2015 to 2019 and 2017 to 2021 ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). For 2027 the window is 2022 to 2026.

### Partnership (samenwerkingsverband), mainly supporting

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband, a working partnership) with connected persons do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-connected persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a connected person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). Connected persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood or affinal relatives in the straight line or members of their household, and a partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a connected person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) reads "mainly supporting" as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) as the example for (b) ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium)).

### Pregnancy interruption

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees ([art. 3.6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium)

Art. 3.78a lid 3 replaces the 1.225 hours of the art. 3.6 test with 800 hours ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), so it does not open the self-employed person's deduction, the deduction for assisting partner or the R&D deduction. The Belastingdienst words it as at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Self-employed person's deduction

Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the self-employed person's deduction to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion, and lid 2 sets the amount for an entrepreneur below the state pension (AOW) age at the start of the year at € 900 for 2027 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The amount is a fixed amount in the statute and is not indexed, and the law with this amount is adopted ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). Lid 5 caps the deduction at the profit unless the entrepreneur qualifies for the starter's deduction, and the amount cut off by that cap is the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek). If the cap sets the deduction to nil, the year still counts as a year with self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Co-entitled parties (medegerechtigden, persons entitled to a share of the profit other than as an entrepreneur) get no self-employed person's deduction on their co-entitled profit ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Self-employed person's deduction at AOW age

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at the AOW age at the start of the year the deduction is 50% of the amount under lid 2 and lid 3, so the starter's deduction is halved too ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 that is € 450, being 50% of € 900. The Belastingdienst page prints the 50% rule ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### Unused self-employed person's deduction, carry-forward

The amount cut off by the cap is the unused self-employed person's deduction, and the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets it in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar) under art. 3.76 lid 6 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Lid 7 offsets it in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Lid 8 states that the offset is also set by a decision open to objection ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst explains the same mechanism on its carry-forward page ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek)).

### Starter's deduction, enacted text

The starter's deduction is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction under art. 3.76 lid 3, not a separate part of the art. 3.74 list ([art. 3.74 and 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The enacted 2027 text prints € 2.123. The arrangement requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, in which case the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)). Bill 37 022 would replace the € 2.123 from 1 January 2027, see the bill row below.

### Starter's deduction at AOW age, enacted text

At the AOW age at the start of the year the starter's deduction is halved by art. 3.76 lid 4, so the enacted 2027 text gives € 1.062, being 50% of € 2.123 ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 50% rule is printed by the Belastingdienst ([Belastingdienst, FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). No page states what this amount becomes under bill 37 022.

### Starter's deduction prior use limit

Art. 3.76 lid 3 gives the increase if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period ([art. 3.76](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 (a tax-neutral return from a private limited company (BV) to a sole trader business) if that business started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years. The Belastingdienst adds that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur, that it requires a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and it words the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test as the statute ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek)).

### Starter's deduction, bill 37 022

Bill 37 022 (Tax Plan 2027, Belastingplan 2027) is a proposal dated 29 September 2026 in the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer), and Artikel I onderdeel G would set the starter's deduction at € 10 from 1 January 2027, with Artikel II onderdeel C abolishing it from 1 January 2028 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The explanatory memorandum (MvT, memorie van toelichting) says abolition of the starter's deduction on 1 January 2027 is not workable, so it first drops to € 10 on 1 January 2027 and is abolished on 1 January 2028, without transitional rules, also for entrepreneurs who started in 2025 ([MvT, Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). The same bill would end the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs on 1 January 2029 and the arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for starters on 1 January 2028 through the End-of-Year Scheme 2026 (Eindejaarsregeling 2026) ([MvT, Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)). Ondernemersplein states that the House of Representatives and the Senate (Eerste Kamer) still have to decide on this proposal ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The retroactivity conflict on the € 10 sits in section 7.

### Starter's deduction at AOW age, bill 37 022

No page states what the starter's deduction at AOW age becomes under bill 37 022, so the value is unknown and UNVERIFIED ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)). The bill leaves art. 3.76 lid 4 unchanged, which would give 50% of € 10 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs

Art. 3.78a applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion, and has not reached the AOW age at the start of the year ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It is not for an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation, and a periodic benefit from a disability (invaliditeit) or accident (ongeval) insurance. The amount is € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then, and FISIN prints € 0. The text and amounts are identical 2020 to 2027 and unchanged by the bill for 2027 ([art. 3.78a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01), [Belastingdienst 2026 page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026)).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs abolition

Bill 37 022, Artikel III onderdelen F and G, would delete the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs from art. 3.74 onderdeel d from 1 January 2029 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)). The proposal is pending and Ondernemersplein states that the House of Representatives and the Senate still have to decide ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/startersaftrek-gaat-omlaag-en-verdwijnt/)).

### R&D work deduction (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), enacted text

The deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, R&D deduction) is part of the entrepreneur's allowance under art. 3.74 and is worked out in art. 3.77 ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). It is a base amount plus a starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period, and FISIN adds that it requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO), at least 500 hours of R&D work and the hours criterion ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The amounts are indexed per year, and the enacted 2027 text prints € 15.979, which is the 2026 amount before the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amount is not published.

### R&D starter increase, enacted text

The starter increase on the R&D deduction follows the same conditions as the base amount, being a taxpayer who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and who had the deduction applied at most twice in that period ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01), [FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)). The enacted 2027 text prints € 7.996, the 2026 amount before the December 2026 indexation ([art. 3.77](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The indexed 2027 amount is not published.

### R&D hours condition

The R&D deduction requires at least 500 hours of R&D work in the year, next to the R&D declaration from RVO and the hours criterion ([FISIN](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek)).

### SME profit exemption

Art. 3.79a makes the SME profit exemption a percentage of the total profit as entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance, and for 2027 that percentage is 12,7% ([art. 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Art. 3.2 defines the taxable profit from business (belastbare winst uit onderneming) as the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, so the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption ([art. 3.2 and 3.79a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). No hours criterion is needed, the statute has no such condition and the Belastingdienst page names none ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)). There is no exemption on profit as co-entitled party, and in a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Belastingdienst calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing).

### Assisting partner deduction, enacted text

Art. 3.78 gives the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek, allowance for a working partner) to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay ([art. 3.78](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number: 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. Exactly 875 hours therefore falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band ([art. 3.78](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)). The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. The hours must be made plausible, and co-entitled parties get no deduction for assisting partner on their co-entitled profit ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)).

### Pay to the partner, art. 3.16 lid 4

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit ([art. 3.16](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The Belastingdienst therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek)). In that case the deduction for assisting partner can still apply if the hours band and the other conditions are met. The text is identical 2020 to 2027.

### Assisting partner deduction, start-ups bill (explanatory memorandum and Ondernemersplein)

The Act on fiscal stimulation of start-ups and scale-ups (Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups), a Kamerstuk of 15 September 2026, has Artikel I cutting the deduction for assisting partner percentages and the discontinuation deduction maximum, Artikel II deleting art. 3.78 and 3.79 and art. 10a.29 lid 7, and Artikel XI letting the entry into force run by royal decree (koninklijk besluit), with Artikel II at the start of the third calendar year after Artikel I onderdelen B and C took effect ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)). The explanatory memorandum says the cuts follow the start of the option scheme on 1 January of a year set by royal decree, and Ondernemersplein gives 1 January 2027 and 2030 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The explanatory memorandum table and Ondernemersplein print the cut percentages as 0,313% / 0,5% / 0,75% / 1%, and Ondernemersplein states that the House of Representatives and the Senate still have to decide.

### Assisting partner deduction, start-ups bill article text, first band

The article text of the start-ups bill, Artikel I onderdeel B, prints 0,32% for the first band, where the bill explanatory memorandum table and Ondernemersplein print 0,313% ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)). The article text is the operative proposal, and the conflict is in section 7.

### Assisting partner deduction abolition

Artikel II onderdeel C and Artikel XI lid 2 of the start-ups bill would delete art. 3.78, and Ondernemersplein says 2030, being the start of the third calendar year after the cuts ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/meewerkaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The proposal is pending.

### Discontinuation deduction, enacted text

Art. 3.79 gives the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek, cessation allowance) on profit made with or on the cessation of one or more whole businesses ([art. 3.79](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). It equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under a tax-neutral rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. The maximum is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and there is no deduction on profit as co-entitled party ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek)). Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction (afneming) of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover or conversion (art. 3.59 lid 2, 3.62, 3.63 or 3.65), that onderdeel was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023, and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022 ([art. 3.79 and 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). For 2027 the relevant rule is art. 10a.29 lid 7.

### Discontinuation deduction, start-ups bill

Artikel I onderdeel C of the start-ups bill would cut the discontinuation deduction maximum to € 908, and Ondernemersplein agrees ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The proposal is pending.

### Discontinuation deduction abolition

Under the start-ups bill the discontinuation deduction would be abolished in 2030, being the start of the third calendar year after the cut, and Artikel II also deletes art. 10a.29 lid 7 ([Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/stakingsaftrek-wordt-minder-en-verdwijnt-daarna/)). The bill text gives the timing through Artikel XI lid 2, and Ondernemersplein says 2030 ([start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)).

### Old-age reserve additions

Articles 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition to the old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) is possible from 2023, and 2027 follows that position ([art. 3.67 to 3.72](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01)). Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last financial year not coinciding with the calendar year (gebroken boekjaar) that started before 1 January 2023 ([art. 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The reserve is reduced by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and it decreases by the excess over the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end if the business or part of it was discontinued in the year, the AOW age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Lid 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business, lid 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and lid 7 keeps the discontinuation deduction for a reduction after a tax-neutral rollover. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve)). The text of art. 10a.29 is the same 2023 to 2027.

### Rate adjustment, enacted text

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions ([art. 2.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). For 2027 the enacted text sets that percentage at 11,91% above € 79.569. The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage, see the next row.

### Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and SME profit exemption, enacted text

The maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment percentage, so the enacted 2027 text gives 49,50% minus 11,91% and a maximum value of 37,59% ([art. 2.10](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Tax Plan 2027 proposes a different figure, see the next row.

### Maximum deduction value, Tax Plan 2027

Tax Plan 2027 proposes a maximum deduction value of 38,16%, printed in the key table (sleuteltabel) row "Aftrektarief tariefmaatregel 38,16%" and in the explanatory memorandum second bracket ([sleuteltabel 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf)). The figure is proposed and the enacted text keeps 37,59% until the bill is adopted.

### Zvw maximum contribution income

The taxable profit from business is part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, and the maximum for 2027 is not in the checked material ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), [rijksbegroting XVI](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf)). This interaction is verified for 2020 and 2021 only, and the detail belongs to the Zvw topic. FISIN 2020 states that the contribution is not levied on the conversion (omzetting) of an old-age reserve into an annuity ([FISIN 2020](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet)).
