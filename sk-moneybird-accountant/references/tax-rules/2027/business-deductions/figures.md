## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) for tax year 2027, the last year in this knowledge base, and every part of it requires status as an entrepreneur for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) purposes (IB-ondernemer), which the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) tests per activity and separately from the VAT (btw) ([Belastingdienst, wanneer bent u ondernemer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/), [art. 3.4 and 3.5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01)). The entrepreneur's allowance has five parts under art. 3.74, and the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is not a sixth part but an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person (zelfstandige)'s deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) under art. 3.76 lid 3 ([art. 3.74](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). The 2027 statute text, the version of the law valid on 1 January 2027, is adopted but not yet in force (in werking), so the rows taken from it carry the status adopted-not-yet-in-force, and it largely repeats 2026: the self-employed person's deduction is € 900, the SME profit exemption is 12,7% and the enacted rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) is 11,91% above € 79.569 ([art. 2.10, 3.6, 3.74, 3.76, 3.78, 3.78a, 3.79, 3.79a and 10a.29](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01)). Against that adopted text stand two pending proposals, Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, bill 37 022), which would cut the starter's deduction to € 10 from 1 January 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028, and the Act on fiscal stimulation of start-ups and scale-ups (Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups), which would cut the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkende partner) (meewerkaftrek) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) and abolish both in 2030 ([bill 37 022](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html), [start-ups bill](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf)).
