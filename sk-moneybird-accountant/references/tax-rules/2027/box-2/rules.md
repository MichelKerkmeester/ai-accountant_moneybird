## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 bracket 1 (art. 2.12)

Box 2 income is the regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus the deductible costs plus the disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (art. 4.12) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling) (sub e), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction (sub f) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The text per 1 January 2027 taxes the part of the box 2 income up to € 68.843 at 24,5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). That version is the pre-indexation text and it "kan voor inwerkingtreding nog wijzigen" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor), so the boundary normally changes each January (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The table applies per taxpayer (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). With fiscal partnership for the whole year the income from the substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) is a common income component (gemeenschappelijk inkomensbestanddeel) that the partners may divide as they choose in the return, "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is", and because art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer each partner applies his own first bracket to his share (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The taxpayer with the substantial interest declares the income, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from the substantial interest of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked material gives no separate filing deadline for box 2 income beyond the return itself.

### Tax at the top of bracket 1 (art. 2.12, column III)

The art. 2.12 table states the tax at the top of bracket 1 in column III, € 16.866 in the text per 1 January 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). This is the amount that the calculation uses for the part of the income in bracket 1 when part of the income falls in bracket 2 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The figure belongs to the pre-indexation text, so the December 2026 ministerial regulation can replace it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Box 2 bracket 2 (art. 2.12)

Income above the bracket 1 boundary is taxed at 31% in the text per 1 January 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The two brackets exist from 2024. One flat rate applied in 2020 to 2023, 26,25% in 2020 and 26,9% in 2021, 2022 and 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The second bracket was announced in Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) and took effect on 1 January 2024, with 24,5% over the first € 67.000 per person and 33% above it (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024). The 33% itself came from Tax Plan 2024, where the Van der Lee c.s. amendment (amendement) raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). Tax Plan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). The rate is per taxpayer, like bracket 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Second-bracket rate 2027 to 2030 (cabinet letter of 29 September 2026)

The cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 ("Kamerbrief over voorstellen op box 3, koopkracht werkenden en sociale zekerheid", Ministry of Finance (ministerie van Financiën)) proposes "voor de duur van 4 jaar het tarief van de tweede schijf in box 2 met 1,8%-punt verlaagd naar 29,2% per 2027" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). So the proposal is a second-bracket rate of 29,2% for 4 years from 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text with the measure was found on 2026-10-11, and the letter does not name the legislative vehicle (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The measure is proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). This corrects the earlier note that no box 2 change was proposed for 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). A change of the second-bracket rate would not change the tax credit (belastingkorting) percentage, which follows the first-row percentage of the art. 2.12 table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Indexation multiplier for art. 10.1 amounts in 2027 (Tax Plan 2027)

Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, Kamerstuk 37 022 nr. 2, 15 September 2026) sets in Artikel LI lid 1 the 2027 multiplier for the art. 10.1 amounts at 1,01248 instead of the table correction factor (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Art. 2.12 is an art. 10.1 amount, so the box 2 boundary follows this multiplier if the bill is adopted (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The bill does not change art. 2.12, chapter 4 Wet IB 2001 or art. 12a Wet LB 1964 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The factsheet states the table correction factor is applied for 48% (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). On 2026-10-11 the bill is not adopted, the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) dossier shows a memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 2 October 2026 and no vote yet (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The multiplier does not name art. 12a Wet LB 1964, so it does not state the indexed customary salary amount (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### Indexed 2027 bracket 1 boundary

The indexed boundary is not published on 2026-10-11 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The December 2026 ministerial regulation will settle it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Two secondary sources disagree. ABN AMRO gives € 69.607 (https://financialfocus.abnamro.nl/fiscaliteiten/prinsjesdag-2026-negen-fiscale-aandachtspunten-voor-de-dga/) and holdwise.nl gives € 69.703 (https://holdwise.nl/kennisbank/box-2-tarief-2027). Both figures are secondary and the material marks the value UNVERIFIED (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).

### Substantial interest, the 5% test (art. 4.6)

A taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly (a) holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, or (d) is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and 1 January 2022 texts, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969 (2022 numbering) with at least 5% of its profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of the profit, the liquidation proceeds or the voting rights (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). In sub d "tot" became "om" in the same period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). The future text per 1 January 2027 keeps the art. 4.6 to 4.16 texts unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The 5% test is met "al dan niet tezamen met zijn partner", so the holdings of the fiscal partner count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from the substantial interest of minor children, until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) of art. 4.9 brings the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in the same company into the substantial interest once such an interest exists (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) of art. 4.10 brings shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does into a substantial interest (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.7 to 4.10 have identical text in every year 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.11) exists for shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles if art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%, on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest, and art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The exempt investment institution (vbi) status is a deemed disposal case listed by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, not the art. 4.11 list (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.16 also lists a buy-back (inkoop) of shares, a commutation (afkoop) and buy-back of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death, granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of a segregated private asset (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a, and a liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb counts as a liquidation payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Flat-rate benefit for an exempt investment institution (vbi) or a foreign investment company (art. 4.14)

The flat-rate benefit of art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a applies to shares in an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). It is a yearly percentage of the value at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The percentage is a yearly value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). In the text per 1 January 2027 the percentage is 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The shares starting to yield this flat-rate benefit are also a deemed disposal under art. 4.16 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, the regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and they are written off on the acquisition price (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction, that fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 bill (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027, Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from this facility from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). That proposal is not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The loss is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment, shown separately on it, and open to objection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1). The art. 4.49 text is identical in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For losses that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the loss is "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar", so the offset period is 9 years instead of 6 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### Tax credit on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53)

Lid 1 applies when the taxpayer and his partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before. The unoffset loss is then converted on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 2 gives the percentage, 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", which is 24,5% in 2024 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 3 lets the tax inspector (inspecteur) set the credit by a decision open to objection (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 4 lets the credit reduce the box 1 tax of that year and of the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Lid 5 allows part of the credit to pass to the surviving partner on the death of a partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting). Knowledge group position (kennisgroepstandpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block the conversion, and ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/). The FISIN 2025 and FISIN 2026 statement "De korting is 26,9%" is overruled by art. 4.53 lid 2, see section 7 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Excessive borrowing maximum (art. 4.14a lid 2)

The excessive borrowing fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f ("het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)"), where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations, and the amount rule sits in art. 4.14a, with the related-person rule (verbonden-personenregel) in art. 4.14b and the immigration rule in art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). The statute is Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531 (issued 27 December 2022), in force 1 January 2023 under Artikel III, and it is cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). Lid 1 makes a positive amount equal to the total debts above the maximum, and a negative amount equal to the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier or under a comparable foreign rule and not yet reversed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 2 sets the maximum at € 700.000 in the 2023 text and € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The bill proposed € 500.000, the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging, Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced it by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c lid 1, and the Grinwis c.s. amendment (amendement, Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 in the same articles (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html). The 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts print € 500.000 in art. 4.14a, twice in art. 4.14b and in art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01). Lid 3 counts debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both once for the two together and applies the maximum to them jointly (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 4 measures the debts at the end of the calendar year at nominal value (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 5 applies lid 1 as if the taxpayer still had a substantial interest and the debts were nil when he no longer has a substantial interest at year end other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, which releases a negative amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 6 excludes an owner-occupied home debt (eigenwoningschuld) to the extent a right of mortgage (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company, and art. 10a.23 excludes an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Lid 7, added by Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023), removes the double counting of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debt among several participants (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html). Debts arising from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a related person for a debt of a partnership (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit are left out (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Those lids were inserted by Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023 as lid 6 and 7, numbered lid 4 and 5 for 2023, and Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) repaired the internal reference "zesde lid" to "vierde lid" retroactive to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added and that the threshold "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). The loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividend tax (dividendbelasting) on the part taxed as box 2 income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). A negative deemed regular benefit is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The text per 1 January 2027 keeps € 500.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Indexation of the excessive borrowing maximum

Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation with the table correction factor (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.14a is not on that list, so the € 500.000 maximum of art. 4.14a is not indexed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2027 text keeps the same € 500.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessive borrowing, related persons and immigration (art. 4.14b, art. 4.14c)

Debts of a related person (verbonden persoon), a blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line of the taxpayer or partner, to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000 from 2024, applied to that person and his partner together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, at least € 500.000 from 2024 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2027 text keeps € 500.000 in art. 4.14b and art. 4.14c (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessive borrowing phase-down (cabinet letter of 29 September 2026)

The cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes a phase-down of the excessive borrowing maximum to € 100.000 in five steps of € 80.000 from 2027, and it states "Leningen ten behoeve van de eigen woning worden hierin niet meegenomen" (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text with the measure was found on 2026-10-11 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The measure is proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).

### Excessive borrowing maximum at 31 December 2027 under the letter (€ 420.000)

One step of € 80.000 below € 500.000 gives € 420.000 for 31 December 2027 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). The letter does not print € 420.000. The figure is arithmetic from the letter and is stated by secondary sources, so the material marks it UNVERIFIED (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).

### Customary salary fixed amount (art. 12a lid 7 Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage (loon) is at least the highest of (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), 75% of it in the 2020 to 2022 texts and 100% from the 2023 text, (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or connected bodies and (c) a fixed amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). A lower wage applies if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and is known to the withholding agent and the tax inspector (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the table correction factor, in lid 9 in 2020 and lid 7 from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The text per 1 January 2027 prints € 58.000, which is the pre-indexation text, and art. 12a lid 7 replaces it at the start of 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01). The Tax Plan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 does not name art. 12a, and the indexed amount is not published (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). The material corrects the reading that € 58.000 is itself the 2027 norm (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01). The start-up rule of art. 12a lid 3 and 4 in the 2020 to 2022 texts, at least 108% of twelve times the monthly minimum wage of art. 8 lid 1 sub a Wet minimumloon as at 1 January or the comparable wage if lower, subject to the de minimis ceiling (de-minimisplafond), is absent from the 2023 text (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2022-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen", and the rule could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 to 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit (voordeel) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). The statement that the wage reduces the residual profit available for a dividend is reasoning, not a sourced statement (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Customary salary, comparable wage (art. 12a lid 1 sub a)

The wage from the most comparable employment counts at 100% from the 2023 text, against 75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and is known to the withholding agent and the tax inspector (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). A lower wage applies if the withholding agent makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, in 2020 to 2022 with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself is lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01).

### Customary salary minimum threshold (€ 5.000)

The customary salary rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year, in lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts and in lid 4 from 2023 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The text per 1 January 2027 keeps € 5.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01).

### Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate for 2027

The old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation, art. 12.3a Wage Tax Implementing Regulation 2011 (Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011), the average of the monthly U-yields (U-rendementen) of the preceding year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The 2026 rate is 2,593%, set by Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025, which replaced "2,602%" by "2,593%" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). The 2027 rate is not published on 2026-10-11. The 2026 table runs to October 2026 with 2,871%, and the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation will set the 2027 rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). A negative market rate must also be applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The ODV can be used tax-neutral (geruisloos) for an annuity (lijfrente), an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or an annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension (AOW) age and at the latest two months after it, and an earlier start lengthens the twenty years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). On death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). Under ministerial conditions the ODV can also be converted into an annuity after the instalments have started (art. 38p lid 5, added by Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). From 1 January 2020 art. 38n lid 2 to 5, art. 38o, art. 38p lid 5 and art. 38q lapsed, and the 2020 Wet LB 1964 text shows art. 38o and 38q as "[Vervallen per 01-01-2020]" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). Commutation (afkoop) or conversion was possible "vanaf 1 april 2017 tot en met 31 december 2019", and V&A 17-007 is marked lapsed (vervallen) (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/). A remaining pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer) keeps the old rules of art. 38n, the company must index the accrued rights every year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision every year actuarially, and "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2, in the company the ODV is valued with the market rate of art. 38p, and PEB payouts are income from former employment in box 1 (art. 10a.18 lid 2 and 3) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).

### Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Kamerstuk 37 023) box 2 measures

The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) of Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027, Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) carries two box 2 measures from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). The first excludes the deemed regular benefit from the art. 4.12a inherited-interest facility (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). The second sets the acquisition price at the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) when a company moves its seat to the Netherlands (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf). Both measures are proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).
