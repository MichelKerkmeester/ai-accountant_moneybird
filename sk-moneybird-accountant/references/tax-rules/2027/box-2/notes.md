## 2. CHANGES FROM 2026

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 2 bracket 1. 2026, enacted: 24,5% up to € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text, adopted-not-yet-in-force: still 24,5% up to € 68.843, pre-indexation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). The Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) bill proposes the 1,01248 multiplier for the art. 10.1 amounts, which include art. 2.12, so the final 2027 boundary is still open (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).
- Tax at the top of bracket 1. 2026: € 16.866 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: € 16.866 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
- Box 2 bracket 2. 2026, enacted: 31% above € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: 31% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Proposed new: 29,2% for 4 years from 2027 in the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).
- Substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) definition (art. 4.6). Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445, in force 1 January 2026) added sub f retroactively to 1 January 2025 and repaired the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) cross-reference (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). The 2027 future text keeps the art. 4.6 to 4.16 texts unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
- Tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss. 2026: 24,5%, and the live page states "In 2026 is de korting 24,50%" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: 24,5%, the first-row percentage of the art. 2.12 table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).
- Excessive borrowing maximum. 2026: € 500.000 plus amounts taxed earlier (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). Proposed new: phase-down to € 100.000 in five steps of € 80.000 from 2027, with the loans for the owner-occupied home left out (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file).
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount. 2026: € 58.000, with the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) stating "ten minste € 58.000 in 2026" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: € 58.000 pre-indexation, replaced at the start of 2027 by ministerial regulation, with the indexed amount not published (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01).
- Customary salary minimum threshold. 2026: € 5.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: € 5.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2027-01-01).
- Flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) (art. 4.14). 2026: 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). 2027 future text: 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01). No change.
- Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate. 2026: 2,593%, set by Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025, which replaced "2,602%" by "2,593%" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). 2027: not published on 2026-10-11, to be set by the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/).
- New 2027 proposal. Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027, Kamerstuk 37 023 nr. 3) proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from the art. 4.12a inherited substantial interest facility and to set the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) at the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) when a company moves its seat to the Netherlands, both from 1 January 2027 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record every dividend and other profit distribution from the private limited company (BV) with the date, the amount and the dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withheld. Dividend tax is not deducted from the box 2 income but credited as a prepayment (voorheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Keep the acquisition price per share. Notary costs are added to the acquisition price, selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs), and at a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs) the market value applies to both sides (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Keep the balance of all debts to the company at 31 December, at nominal value. Debts at several companies are added, receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, and the threshold "never drops below € 500.000 (€ 700.000 in 2023)". Keep the amounts taxed earlier in a separate record (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- The loan stays a loan in full. The company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividend tax on the part taxed as box 2 income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- The customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit (voordeel) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 benefit but income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is set by a decision (beschikking) together with the assessment and shown separately on it, open to objection. The offset runs against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose, and losses that arose in 2018 or earlier get the longer 9-year term (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).
- The tax credit (belastingkorting) request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest. The tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the credit by a decision open to objection. The credit reduces the box 1 tax of that year and of the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting).
- A remaining pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB) keeps the old rules. The company must index the accrued rights every year if the scheme provides for indexation and must value the provision every year actuarially, and not indexing can be seen as a form of waiving (prijsgeven) with possible fiscal consequences. PEB payouts are income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking) in box 1 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). The ODV is increased each year by the market rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). The acquisition price of the shares is not increased by the 2017, 2018 or 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01).
- The taxpayer declares the box 2 income in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting) and must also declare the income from the substantial interest of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked material gives no other filing deadline for box 2 income.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Indexed 2027 bracket 1 boundary: unknown and UNVERIFIED. Two secondary sources conflict, € 69.607 (ABN AMRO, https://financialfocus.abnamro.nl/fiscaliteiten/prinsjesdag-2026-negen-fiscale-aandachtspunten-voor-de-dga/) against € 69.703 (holdwise.nl, https://holdwise.nl/kennisbank/box-2-tarief-2027). The Tax Plan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 for the art. 10.1 amounts is proposed and not adopted (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html). Section 4 works the multiplication out and the outcome about € 69.702 fits the higher figure. The December 2026 ministerial regulation settles it (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).
- The 29,2% second-bracket rate for 4 years from 2027 is proposed in the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). No bill text was found on 2026-10-11, the letter does not name the legislative vehicle, and it is open whether the rate enters a memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) or a separate bill and whether it is adopted before 1 January 2027 (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). The House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) dossier and the Staatsblad settle it.
- The excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) phase-down to € 100.000 in five steps of € 80.000 from 2027 is proposed in the same letter (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). The per-reference-date (peildatum) amounts and the legislative vehicle are open, and the letter does not print them (https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?qry=wetsvoorstel%3A37022&cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails). A bill text and the Staatsblad settle it.
- Excessive borrowing maximum at 31 December 2027 under the letter, € 420.000: proposed and UNVERIFIED. The figure is arithmetic from the letter (€ 500.000 minus one step of € 80.000) and is stated by secondary sources, the letter itself does not print € 420.000 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file). A bill text and the Staatsblad settle it.
- Indexed 2027 customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) amount: not published. It depends on the table correction factor regulation, and the Tax Plan 2027 multiplier of 1,01248 does not name art. 12a Wet LB 1964 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html).
- Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) market rate for 2027: not published. The 2026 table runs to October 2026 with 2,871%, and the December 2026 figure and the year-end regulation set the 2027 rate (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/).
- Summed two-partner first bracket: UNVERIFIED. No primary page states a summed two-partner amount. Each partner applies his own first bracket of € 68.843, and section 4 shows the arithmetic of a 50/50 division (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Rollover (doorschuiving) list for gift, inheritance and emigration: UNVERIFIED. The list was not re-fetched on a primary page in this check. The APV loss deferral term of 10 years is Verified, with offset after a third-party sale, death or 10 years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Non-resident statement about a 2025 decision on partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht): UNVERIFIED. It was not found on the fetched Dutch Tax and Customs Administration pages. Art. 7.5 is Verified for the base of a non-resident, a substantial interest in a Netherlands-established company that is not part of an enterprise (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Resolved conflict, tax credit percentage: FISIN 2026 states "De korting is 26,9%" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang) against art. 4.53 lid 2, which refers to "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", 24,5%, with the live page giving € 4.900 for a loss of € 20.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Settled at 24,5%, the handbook figure is stale.
- Resolved conflict, placement of the excessive borrowing fiction: art. 4.12a against art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f. Settled at art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c, while art. 4.12a carries the inherited substantial interest facility (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html).
- Resolved conflict, art. 4.6 "unchanged 2020 to 2027" against the year texts. Settled at sub e from the 2022 text and sub f from 2025 with retroactive effect (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html).
- Resolved conflict, "no box 2 change proposed for 2027" against the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 and the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill. Settled, box 2 changes are proposed (29,2% second bracket, excessive borrowing phase-down, two technical measures), and none is adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).
- Resolved conflict, Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) against Tax Plan 2024 (Belastingplan 2024) as the source of the 2024 brackets. Both are right for different parts. Tax Plan 2023 introduced the second bracket, Tax Plan 2024 (amendment (amendement) Van der Lee c.s.) set its rate at 33% (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html).
- Resolved conflict, PEB start date of 1 April 2017 against 1 July 2017. The measures apply from 1 April 2017 (consolidated BWBR0039380 "Geldend van 01-04-2017", Central Point of Contact for Pensions (Centraal Aanspreekpunt Pensioenen)), the leniency period (coulanceperiode) ran to 30 June 2017 and lapsed on 1 July 2017, which is the date the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page uses for the end of accrual (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0039380/2017-04-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-18118.html). The published Stb. 2017, 115 Artikel XIX prints 1 January 2017 under the Advisory Referendum Act (Wet raadgevend referendum) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).
