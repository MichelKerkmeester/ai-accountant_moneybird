## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers box 2 income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) in tax year 2027, so the 5% test, the two-bracket rate, dividends and disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), the tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss, the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction for loans from the own private limited company (BV) and the link with the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) of a director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder). The law for 2027 is a mix of enacted law, bills and an announcement. The future Wet IB 2001 text per 1 January 2027 keeps the 2026 box 2 table, the € 500.000 excessive borrowing maximum and the art. 4.6 to 4.16 texts, and the future Wet LB 1964 text keeps € 58.000 and € 5.000. The December 2026 indexation regulation, the Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) bill (Kamerstuk 37 022 nr. 2), the Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill (Kamerstuk 37 023) and the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 still have to settle the final numbers. The most important open items are the indexed 2027 bracket boundary, the proposed 29,2% second-bracket rate from 2027 and the proposed phase-down of the excessive borrowing maximum. All sources in this file were checked on 2026-10-11.
