## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the Dutch VAT rates, exemptions, small-business scheme, returns, invoices, private use, and cross-border transactions that matter to a sole trader or a BV. The enacted rate structure remains 21% standard, 9% reduced and 0% for qualifying zero-rated supplies. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01) The year's rules also include an adopted law that is scheduled to take effect in 2027 and proposed changes whose VAT effects are scheduled for a later year. [Bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn), [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2027

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard VAT rate

Charge 21% on taxable goods and services unless a reduced rate, zero rate, exemption or reverse-charge rule applies. The rate is in Article 9 of the Wet op de omzetbelasting. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### Reduced VAT rate

Charge 9% only for goods and services listed in Tabel I. The listed goods include food, water, ornamental plants, medicines and aids, art, collectors' items and antiques, books and periodicals. Listed services include bicycle, shoe, leather goods, clothing and household linen repairs, hairdressing, certain home work, camping, cultural and recreational access, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, qualifying electronic publications, and qualifying news websites and apps. [Goods at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Services at 9%](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/), [Tabel I explanatory decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-9114.html)

### Zero rate

The zero rate is not an exemption. A zero-rated supply retains the right to deduct input VAT, while an exempt supply does not. For an intra-EU supply of goods, the records must show that the goods went to another EU country, the buyer must have a valid VAT ID from another member state, and the opgaaf ICP, or intra-Community listing, must be filed on time, correctly and completely. A late or incorrect ICP can put the zero rate at risk and lead to a fine. [VAT return explanation for ICP](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Lodging

Charge 21% on lodging from 1 January 2026. This also applies to certain facilities connected to lodging, including gas, electricity, water, sanitary facilities, laundry and parking. Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain at 9%. If an all-in price covers lodging and separately offered items taxed at 9%, split the price by market value. [Lodging VAT rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies), [Lodging rate news](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Solar panels

The 0% rate covers qualifying supply and installation of solar panels on or near homes. The listed covered items include panels, cables, PV distributors, mounting material, optimizers, inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The 21% rate applies to specified items and work outside that scope, including multi-function panels, solar heat collectors, panels on non-residential buildings, roof reinforcement, milling cable routes, replacing a whole group cabinet, a smart meter and separately purchased batteries. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home follow a different rule from panels supplied and installed on their own. [Solar panel decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html), [Belastingdienst solar panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen)

### Exemptions

Article 11 exemptions cover specified supplies and services, including some immovable property transactions and letting, healthcare, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, postal services, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, specified work by composers, writers and journalists, certain movable goods, organs and blood, member services, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list also includes investment gold and canteens. Exempt supplies do not carry a right to deduct input VAT. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### Dutch KOR ceiling and qualifying turnover

The KOR is the small businesses scheme. A business established in the Netherlands may choose the exemption when Dutch annual turnover meets the statutory ceiling. The Belastingdienst describes the test as applying to both the calendar year of application and the preceding calendar year. The turnover calculation includes Dutch-taxed supplies, including 0% supplies and domestic reverse-charged supplies, plus specified exempt property, payments, securities, financial and insurance turnover. Other exempt turnover is excluded. Do not count VAT on private use, sales of business investment goods, supplies reverse-charged to the business as buyer, supplies taxed abroad or intra-Community acquisitions. Before entry, turnover is measured excluding VAT. During participation, use the total amount charged. Add turnover from all sub-numbers because the KOR applies to all of them. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)

Under the KOR, do not deduct input VAT and do not state VAT on invoices. The exemption applies to all qualifying activities covered by the scheme. If turnover passes the ceiling, the entire supply that crosses it is subject to VAT. The exemption then does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

An entrepreneur who has not registered with the inspector or the KVK, is not required to register, and does not use the exemption in another member state may qualify for the registration threshold without prior notice. The statute also excludes certain business-used immovable property and new means of transport sent to another member state from the KOR. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Decision on the registration threshold](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html)

### EU KOR Union ceiling

A business established in the EU but not in the Netherlands may qualify for the EU KOR in the Netherlands if the Union turnover conditions are met. The Union ceiling is assessed for the current and preceding calendar years, with some member states applying an additional year under the EU rules. A prior notification and an individual number ending in EX are required. The exemption starts when the inspector issues or confirms that number. The business cannot use it if Union turnover passes the ceiling, if the relevant state reports that its national limit is exceeded or the conditions are not met, or if more time is needed to address fraud concerns. A participant files quarterly turnover totals per state, including a zero where there is no turnover to report. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [EU KOR participation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen), [Quarterly turnover report](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet)

The EU KOR can be combined with the Union scheme per country, but not with the import scheme. Intra-Community acquisitions generally remain taxable in the arrival state. The material lists an exception when the supplier also uses the KOR, and excludes new means of transport from that exception. A business exempt in the arrival state generally does not file a return or ICP for exempt intra-EU supplies, except for new means of transport. A business without a Dutch exemption may still have an ICP obligation when it participates in the arrival state's scheme. Reverse-charged services may still require an incidental return there. [Belastingdienst EU KOR guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen)

### KOR without prior notice

The special registration threshold is for an entrepreneur who has not registered with the inspector or the KVK, is not obliged to register, and does not use the exemption in another member state. From the stated effective rule, the threshold is the annual turnover amount in the figures table. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Decision on the KOR registration threshold](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html)

### KOR application and withdrawal timing

Apply only after receiving the omzetbelastingnummer and VAT ID. The application should arrive at least four weeks before the intended start. For quarterly filers, the published cut-offs correspond to starts on the first day of the next quarter. A late application starts in the next quarter. Returns continue until the confirmed start date, and the Belastingdienst sends confirmation within the stated response period. [KOR application](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor)

Voluntary withdrawal is made through Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk and takes effect only from the first day of an aangiftetijdvak, or VAT filing period. Give notice at least four weeks ahead. After withdrawal, re-entry is barred for the rest of the calendar year in which the exemption ends and the following calendar year. Confirmation is sent within the stated response period. Paper forms are no longer handled. [KOR withdrawal](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### Annual VAT return limit

A yearly return may be available when annual VAT due is below the stated limit and the other conditions are met. The checked 2027 material marks the limit as unverified for this year. The current Belastingdienst page does not identify a year for the amount, and the checked material does not include a 2027 handbook. Treat eligibility under this amount as unconfirmed for 2027. [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

### Annual return cross-border limits

The checked Handboek Ondernemen states that each of the listed intra-EU activity categories must remain below the amount shown in the figures table. The categories are intra-Community supplies of goods, intra-Community services, intra-Community acquisitions and services received from other EU countries. The amount is tested separately for each activity. The checked handbooks confirm this for earlier years, but the 2027 handbook is not published in the checked material. Treat the 2027 limit as unverified. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### VAT return periods

Quarterly filing is usual. A monthly or yearly period can be requested by letter to the tax office. The tax office responds within the period shown in the figures table, and an approved change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

### Monthly period trigger

The 2020 handbook said that a business that usually paid more than the quarterly amount shown in the figures table had a monthly period. That trigger is absent from the checked 2026 handbook and current period page. The 2027 handbook is not available in the checked material, so do not apply the earlier trigger without confirmation. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

### Period return and payment deadline

A period return is filed within the inspector's deadline, which must be at least a month after the period ends. Payment is due within a month after the period ends. The Belastingdienst states that monthly and quarterly returns are due by the last day of the month after the period. A yearly return is due before the stated April date in the next year. Filing and payment share a due date. The filing date is the date the return is received. An extension is available only in a calamity. [AWR](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/), [Due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Software filing window

When filing through software, submit from the stated 24th-day window before the end of the relevant period. Submitting earlier can produce an error message. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Suppletie timing and correction horizon

A suppletie is a VAT correction filing. Correct the returns for the past five calendar years when a return is wrong or incomplete. Under the rule in force from 2025, notify the inspector no later than the stated number of weeks after discovering the error. The correction must be made before the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector knows. Failure to comply is an offence, and the power to impose an offence penalty expires under the stated five-year rule. The decision notes that a penalty can reach 100% of the VAT for intent or gross negligence. [UBOB](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html)

The Belastingdienst says there is no tax interest if the correction is filed within the stated period after the year, or within the year itself. The Belastingdienst aims to respond within the period in the figures table. A correction to a return already filed for the final period of the year is made by suppletie. [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar)

### Electronics reverse charge threshold

The reverse-charge rule, or verleggingsregeling, shifts VAT accounting to the customer in specified cases. For the listed electronics, it applies only when the total amount per delivery per type of good reaches the threshold in the figures table. The supplier states “btw verlegd”, the customer’s VAT ID and the consideration per rate on the invoice. The supplier remains responsible for correct treatment. The customer reports the VAT in the appropriate return section and may deduct it when the purchase is used for taxable activity. For subcontracting, the material lists exceptions for work mainly done in the supplier's own workshop, staff lent for that work, subordinate work on goods sold, and disputes referred to the main contractor's tax office. ### ICP reports and threshold

The opgaaf ICP lists intra-EU supplies of goods and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. The reported total for goods and services must match return section 3b for the same period. Report goods in the period of the invoice date and services in the period the service was supplied. Do not file an ICP for a period with nothing to report. Correct an ICP in the next ICP. Goods may be reported monthly or quarterly subject to the threshold and past-quarter test in the figures table. If goods cross that threshold, the applicable monthly reporting periods depend on the month of the crossing. After four consecutive qualifying quarters, quarterly reporting may resume from the fifth quarter if that quarter also qualifies. The threshold test does not apply to services. Services may be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with a permit. A combined goods and services report follows the goods rules. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [ICP reporting periods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)

For call-off stock, the checked guide requires an agreement with a known buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, transport arranged by the supplier, no establishment in the arrival country, supply within the stated period after arrival, a register, and listing each buyer in the ICP for the period when transport began. The 2027 ICP deadline was not found in the checked material. [ICP guidance](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### EU distance sales

The € 10.000 distance-sales relief applies only to suppliers established in one member state and only when the qualifying total does not exceed the threshold in either the current or preceding calendar year. It covers qualifying goods sent from the Netherlands to non-VAT-filing consumers in another EU country when the supplier arranges or pays for transport, and digital services to consumers. The material excludes margin goods, nearly new or new means of transport, and assembly supplies. The threshold is measured excluding VAT. Once exceeded, apply the destination country's VAT from the supply that crosses the limit. An optional choice to apply foreign VAT below the threshold lasts at least two years. After a full calendar year at or below the threshold, Dutch VAT resumes from the start of the following year. The Union scheme is optional, uses one return per quarter for the covered EU sales, and does not reclaim foreign input VAT through that return. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [EU VAT notification](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/), [Goods to EU consumers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

### EU KOR reports

The inspector issues or confirms the EX number within the period in the figures table. The quarterly turnover report is due within the stated period after the quarter. A participant must notify the inspector within the stated period after passing the Union threshold. The end of participation generally takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter. If the relevant information arrives in the last month of a quarter, the end takes effect on the first day of the second month of the next quarter. The notice includes business and activity details, participating states, prior-year turnover totals per state and current-year totals through the notice date. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [EU KOR participation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen), [Quarterly turnover report](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet)

### OSS and import scheme

OSS is the one-stop system for covered cross-border VAT reporting. Under the Union scheme, file one quarterly return in the following month, including a nil return. Use it from the first day of the quarter after registration. For a first supply, registration must arrive by the stated day of the month after the month of supply. Three consecutive missed returns can lead to exclusion for two years. A return cannot be resubmitted. Put corrections in a later return within the stated correction period. Keep OSS records for the stated retention period. Use euros and the ECB rate from the last day of the quarter, and make one payment for all states. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

The import scheme is monthly and applies to shipments up to the listed value. Three consecutive missed monthly returns can lead to exclusion for two years. The claim that participation ends after two years without supplies while returns continue is unverified. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### Private use and personnel gifts

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur's family. It can involve mixed-use items, such as a car, business computer or partly private building, or goods withdrawn for exclusively private use. Staff private use can restrict deduction under the BUA, or VAT deduction exclusion decree. The final return of the year settles private use of goods and services, the business car, the pro rata for taxable and exempt turnover, and personnel provisions and gifts where the BUA applies. The checked handbooks also list utilities, deemed services and adjustments. If the final return has already been filed, use a suppletie to correct it. [Belastingdienst private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Business car private use standard forfait

If usage records are not kept, the standard private-use correction is the stated percentage of catalogue price including VAT and BPM. ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### VAT rates: Calculated example

A business sells taxable goods for € 1.000,00 excluding VAT.

- At 21%: € 1.000,00 × 21% = € 210,00 VAT. Total invoice amount: € 1.000,00 + € 210,00 = € 1.210,00.
- At 9%: € 1.000,00 × 9% = € 90,00 VAT. Total invoice amount: € 1.000,00 + € 90,00 = € 1.090,00.
- At 0%: € 1.000,00 × 0% = € 0,00 VAT. The supply is still zero-rated rather than exempt if it meets the conditions for the zero rate.

The rates and the difference between zero rating and exemption follow Article 9 of the Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968 and the Belastingdienst guidance. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [ICP return explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### KOR eligibility and crossing supply: Calculated example

Assume a business has Dutch turnover that counts for the KOR of € 19.000,00 in the application year and € 19.000,00 in the preceding calendar year. Both amounts are below the € 20.000,00 ceiling. A later supply of € 2.000,00 brings the current-year turnover to € 19.000,00 + € 2.000,00 = € 21.000,00. The entire € 2.000,00 crossing supply is taxable. The exemption does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year. [KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### Annual return conditions: Calculated example

Assume a sole trader meets the legal-form condition, has no vergunning artikel 23, and has € 1.000,00 VAT due for the year. The amount is below € 1.883,00. Assume also that each listed intra-EU activity is € 9.000,00. Each amount is below € 10.000,00. This arithmetic meets the stated thresholds, but the 2027 status of those annual-return limits is unverified, so it does not confirm eligibility for 2027. [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Reverse charge for electronics: Calculated example

Assume one delivery contains one type of listed electronic good for € 12.000,00. The amount per delivery for that type is € 12.000,00, which is at least € 10.000,00. The supplier therefore applies the reverse-charge rule if the other conditions are met. The customer reports the VAT and deducts it only to the extent that the purchase is used for taxable activity. [UBOB](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [How reverse charging works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### EU distance-sales threshold: Calculated example

Assume qualifying distance sales are € 8.000,00 in the preceding calendar year and € 3.000,00 in the current calendar year before a new sale. The preceding-year amount is tested separately and is below € 10.000,00. If the new sale is € 8.000,00, the current-year total is € 3.000,00 + € 8.000,00 = € 11.000,00. The € 10.000,00 threshold is exceeded in the current calendar year, so the destination VAT rules apply from the moment of overrun, subject to the stated scope conditions. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [EU VAT notification](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/wijziging_in_digitale_diensten_vanaf_2015/eu_btw_melding_doen/)

### Suppletie timing: Calculated example

Assume a business discovers an error on a filed return. The correction must be submitted no later than 8 weeks after discovery. The correction must concern one of the past five calendar years. The amount boundary for putting a correction in the next return is € 1.000,00 or less under the stated policy, but 2027 confirmation is not published in the checked material. [UBOB](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

### Business car private use: Calculated example

Assume the car's catalogue price including VAT and BPM is € 30.000,00, with no usage records and no cap that produces a lower result. The calculation is € 30.000,00 × 2,7% = € 810,00. That amount is included in the last return of the year, subject to any proportional reduction for exempt use and any part-year adjustment. [Car VAT decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

### BUA annual threshold: Calculated example

Assume one employee receives qualifying provisions costing € 200,00 per boekjaar. That total is € 227,00 minus € 200,00 = € 27,00 below the € 227,00 threshold, so it stays outside the BUA exclusion. If the total for that person is € 250,00, it is € 250,00 minus € 227,00 = € 23,00 above the threshold and the BUA exclusion may apply if the other conditions are met. [BUA](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Invoice deadline: Official example

The Handboek Ondernemen example says a supply made on 10 April is invoiced by 15 May. This illustrates the deadline to invoice by the 15th day of the month following the month of supply. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Foreign-established business late-payment penalty: Calculated example

This example applies only to the penalty page for businesses established outside the Netherlands. Assume unpaid VAT of € 1.000,00. Three percent of € 1.000,00 is € 1.000,00 × 3% = € 30,00. The stated minimum is € 50,00, so the penalty would be € 50,00 under that page's rule. [Belastingdienst page for foreign-established businesses](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte)

- The enacted VAT rates remain 21%, 9% and 0%. No 2027 rate change was found. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)
- Lodging remains at 21% in 2027. The change from 9% to 21% took effect in 2026, so this is not a new 2027 change. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)
- The Dutch KOR ceiling remains € 20.000 and the EU KOR Union ceiling remains € 100.000. The KOR registration threshold without prior notice remains € 2.200. No 2027 change was found. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [KOR registration threshold decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html)
- Bill 36.920 moved from adopted by both chambers in 2026 to scheduled commencement of its first provisions in 2027. The scheduled start is 1 January 2027. [Eerste Kamer bill page](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn), [Bill memorandum](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36920-3.html)
- The Belastingplan 2027 proposes VAT changes to Tabel I from 1 January 2028. They do not change the 2027 rates or KOR. [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)
- The checked material identifies no indexation of the VAT amounts listed in this file. It says the amounts are fixed in their statute, regulation or policy and that no indexation clause was found. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)
- The remainder of the 2027 return-date table and 2027 handbook values were not yet published in the checked material. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

Keep sales invoices and supporting records that show the supply, rate, VAT amount, customer and supply date. Issue invoices by the 15th day of the month after supply when invoicing is required. Keep digital invoices in a form that preserves their origin, contents and readability. [Invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen)

Reconcile output VAT, deductible input VAT, reverse-charged purchases and exempt activity to the VAT return. For reverse-charged purchases, keep the supplier invoice, VAT ID and details needed to report the amount in the correct return section. For intra-EU supplies, keep evidence of transport to the other member state and the customer's valid VAT ID. File ICP information consistently with return section 3b. [Reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen), [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp)

Record KOR turnover across all sub-numbers. If using the KOR, do not deduct input VAT and do not show VAT on invoices. If the threshold is exceeded, identify the crossing supply because that entire supply is taxable. [KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

Keep records for OSS transactions for the stated 10-year period. File the quarterly OSS return and any required nil return. Correct errors in a later OSS return within the stated period. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

Include the year-end correction for private use, the car, the taxable and exempt turnover split, and applicable personnel provisions in the final VAT return. Use a suppletie if that return has already been filed. Correct other errors within the stated suppletie period. [Last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

File and pay by the applicable due date. The checked material states the general period rules and the known dates falling in 2027, but it does not provide the remaining 2027 deadline table. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Annual VAT return limit:** Less than € 1.883 is marked UNVERIFIED for 2027. The current page has no 2027 publication, and the checked material does not include a 2027 handbook. A 2027 handbook or an updated official period page would settle it. [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)
- **Annual return cross-border limits:** The less-than-€ 10.000 limit per activity is confirmed in the 2020 and 2026 handbooks but not confirmed for 2027. A 2027 handbook would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Monthly period trigger:** More than € 15.000 VAT per quarter appeared in the 2020 handbook. It is absent from the 2026 handbook and the current period page. A 2027 handbook or an updated official page would settle whether it applies. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)
- **2027 filing dates:** The rest of the date table is not published. An official 2027 date table would settle the remaining return dates. [Belastingdienst due dates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)
- **ICP deadline:** No 2027 deadline was found. The 2024 guide gives a deadline within one month after the selected period, but a 2027 ICP page or guide would settle the 2027 deadline. [ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [2024 ICP guide](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)
- **Suppletie boundary:** The € 1.000 boundary is UNVERIFIED for 2027 because the material identifies the current guidance as policy without a 2027 publication. An updated 2027 policy page or handbook would settle it. [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)
- **Corrections for earlier years:** The checked material leaves the suppletie amount boundary for 2021 through 2025 open. The Handboek Ondernemen 2020 says less than € 1.000, while the Handboek Ondernemen 2026 says € 1.000 or less. A year-specific handbook or official clarification for each period would settle the treatment. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
- **Lease car forfait conflict:** The current Belastingdienst car page says the 1,5% rate also applies after the relevant period where the car is leased. The Handboek Ondernemen 2026 says lease cars remain at 2,7%. The car decision does not mention lease cars. A Belastingdienst ruling or corrected official guidance would settle whether the lower rate applies to lease cars. [Belastingdienst car page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Car VAT decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)
- **EU KOR participation without supplies:** The claim that participation ends after two years without supplies while returns are still filed is UNVERIFIED. Updated Belastingdienst guidance would settle it. [Belastingdienst OSS guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)
- **Bill 36.920 publication and scope:** The Staatsblad publication was pending in the checked material. If publication occurs after the scheduled start, the bill provides for entry into force the day after publication with retroactive effect to 1 January 2027. The exact scope of the distance-sales change was taken from the memorandum summary. The published amended bill text and Staatsblad would settle both points. [Eerste Kamer bill page](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn), [Bill memorandum](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36920-3.html)
- **Belastingplan 2027:** The changes to Tabel I posts a.48 and b.9, and the statement that the bill makes no KOR change, are proposed. The bill is not adopted in the checked material. An adopted law would settle whether they take effect. [Belastingplan 2027](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html)
- **National e-invoicing and reporting:** The dates for national B2B e-invoicing and reporting, the KOR exemption and the expected bill are proposed. A bill and subsequent enacted law would settle the duty and its scope. [Rijksoverheid announcement](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven)
- **Remaining 2027 handbook values:** These were not published in the checked material. A 2027 handbook would settle any additional annual return, filing period or administrative details. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
