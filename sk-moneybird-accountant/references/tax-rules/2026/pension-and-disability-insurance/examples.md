## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Example 1: Jaarruimte calculation (Calculated example)

A sole trader has a winst uit onderneming (profit) of € 60.000 in 2025 and no pension accrual. The premiegrondslag for 2026 is: min(€ 60.000, € 137.800) = € 60.000 minus franchise € 19.172 = € 40.828. The jaarruimte is 30% x € 40.828 = € 12.248,40. This is the maximum deductible lijfrentepremie for 2026, assuming no reduction for pension accrual. Source for figures: [Art. 3.127](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### Example 2: Jaarruimte with maximum income (Calculated example)

A sole trader has a winst uit onderneming of € 150.000 in 2025 and no pension accrual. The premiegrondslag is: min(€ 150.000, € 137.800) = € 137.800 minus franchise € 19.172 = € 118.628. The jaarruimte is 30% x € 118.628, which the Ministry 2026 table gives as € 35.589. Source: [Art. 3.127](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Ministry of Finance 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file).

### Example 3: Official example of jaarruimte and premiums paid (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives an example where the jaarruimte is € 1.555 and € 1.500 in premiums are paid. The € 1.500 is deductible, and the € 55 carries into the reserveringsruimte of a later year. Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 4: Official example of exceeding the jaarruimte (Official example)

The Belastingdienst gives an example where the jaarruimte 2026 is € 1.600, there is no reserveringsruimte, and € 1.500 premium plus € 500 storting (deposit) is paid. At most € 1.600 is deductible. The € 400 is never deductible but is counted at the uitkering (payout). Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### Example 5: BAZ premium (Calculated example based on proposed law)

The MvT gives examples for the proposed BAZ. At a winst of € 38.000 or € 45.000 the premium is about € 171 per month. At a winst of € 25.000 the premium is about € 113 per month. Negative winst gives € 0. A winst of € 25.000 plus € 25.000 SV-loon gives a grondslag of € 13.000 and about € 59 per month. These are proposed figures and not enacted law. Source: [MvT 36912, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).
