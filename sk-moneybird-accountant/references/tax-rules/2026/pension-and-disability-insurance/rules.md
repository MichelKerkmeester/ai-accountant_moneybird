## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Annual allowance (jaarruimte) percentage

This applies to any taxpayer who had a pension shortfall (pensioentekort) in the previous calendar year (2025). Art. 3.127 lid 1 allows a deduction for annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) up to 30% of the contribution base (premiegrondslag), reduced by the reduction for pension accrual under lid 4. The 30% rate applies to 2026 because it was enacted in the 2023-07-01 law text and has been confirmed in the 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01, and 2027-01-01 texts. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) Wtp page states the 30% applies "met terugwerkende kracht vanaf 1 januari 2023" (retroactively from 1 January 2023). For a self-employed person without pension accrual the formula is: 30% x (min(income of previous year, € 137.800) minus € 19.172). Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst Wtp page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).

### 3.2 Income cap (aftoppingsgrens) for the contribution base (premiegrondslag)

The income cap (aftoppingsgrens) for the contribution base is € 137.800 for 2026. This amount is the maximum income base taken into account when calculating the annual allowance. The amount was not indexed at the start of 2026 (amendment (amendement) Grinwis c.s., Parliamentary papers (kamerstukken) II 2024/25, 36602, nr. 90). Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingplan 2027 memorie](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027/wetsvoorstel-belastingplan-2027.pdf).

### 3.3 Threshold (franchise) for the contribution base (premiegrondslag)

The threshold (franchise) for the contribution base is € 19.172 for 2026. This amount is subtracted from the capped income to arrive at the contribution base. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### 3.4 Maximum annual allowance

The maximum annual allowance for 2026 is € 35.589. This is calculated as 30% x (€ 137.800 minus € 19.172), a base of € 118.628, which the Ministry 2026 table and the ODB 2026 sheet give as € 35.589. Source: [Ministry of Finance 2026 table](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/c3d48c04-1009-4ffe-a54b-cebf13427ab5/file), [Art. 3.127 lid 3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127).

### 3.5 Catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum

The catch-up allowance (reserveringsruimte) maximum for 2026 is € 42.753. This is the maximum amount of unused annual allowance from previous years that can be used in 2026. Art. 3.127 lid 2 allows a taxpayer to use unused annual allowance of the preceding period on request in the return (aangifte), oldest unused year first. The look-back period is 10 years, covering annual allowance from 2016 to 2025. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration advises using catch-up allowance before annual allowance so older unused allowance (ruimte) does not lapse. Unused allowance from 2016 lapses in 2027. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.6 Look-back window

The look-back window for 2026 is 10 years, covering annual allowance from 2016 to 2025. This means unused annual allowance from those years can be used in 2026 as catch-up allowance. The period was 7 years in the 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023-01-01 texts, and was extended to 10 years from the 2023-07-01 text. FISIN 2026 agrees. Source: [Art. 3.127 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01/0/Hoofdstuk3/Afdeling3.7/Artikel3.127), [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.7 Annual allowance birth date limit

A taxpayer born before 1 September 1953 cannot use the annual allowance 2026 but may still use the catch-up allowance 2026. This is stated on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page for 2026. Source: [Belastingdienst: Aftrekken lijfrentepremies](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies).

### 3.8 Temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) cap

The temporary old-age annuity (tijdelijke oudedagslijfrente) has a yearly cap of € 27.192 for 2026. Art. 3.125 lid 1 c requires at least five years of instalments (termijnen), starting not before the year of state pension (AOW) age and at the latest in the year of state pension age plus five, with a yearly amount up to the statutory cap judged at the time of premium payment. FISIN 2026 shows € 27.192 for the insurance (verzekering) row, but its account (rekening) row still prints € 26.781 (stale). The Ministry and ODB give € 27.192. Source: [Art. 3.125 lid 1 c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### 3.9 Bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap

The bridge annuity (overbruggingslijfrente) cap is € 63.288 per year for 2026. This product is only for annuity entitlements (lijfrentetegoeden) built up before 2006. It ends at choice in the year of turning 65, reaching state pension age, or starting a pension (pensioen). The amount is not in the current art. 3.125 text but appears on every FISIN chapter 2020 to 2026 and on the ODB 2026 sheet. Source: [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.10 Profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum, tier a

The profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst) maximum for tier a is € 574.867 for 2026. Art. 3.129 allows a deduction up to the profit from business discontinuation, capped at a tier maximum minus already built provisions. Tier a applies if the age at cessation (staking) is at most five years below the state pension age, or if the taxpayer is at least 45% incapacitated for work (arbeidsongeschikt) with the annuity (lijfrente) starting within six months, or on cessation by death. The ODB 2026 sheet gives "62 jaar of ouder" for this tier. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.11 Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier b

The profit from business discontinuation maximum for tier b is € 287.445 for 2026. Tier b applies if the age at cessation is at most fifteen years below the state pension age, or if the annuity starts immediately. The ODB 2026 sheet gives "52 to under 62" for this tier. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [ODB 2026 sheet](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/20251218-Overzicht-cijfers-leven-2026-FsL.pdf).

### 3.12 Profit from business discontinuation maximum, tier c

The profit from business discontinuation maximum for tier c is € 143.732 for 2026. Tier c applies to all other cases. Source: [Art. 3.129](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.13 Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity

Commutation (afkoop) of a small annuity in one payment is allowed below a yearly amount of € 5.513 for 2026. This is in art. 3.133 lid 2 onderdeel d. Source: [Art. 3.133 lid 2 d](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.14 Commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid)

The commutation cap for long-term disability (langdurige arbeidsongeschiktheid) is € 51.440 for 2026. Art. 3.133 lid 9 states that commutation is not a prohibited act when the policyholder (verzekeringnemer) or beneficiary (gerechtigde) is long-term disabled (langdurig arbeidsongeschikt) under ministerial conditions, has not yet reached state pension age, and the commutation received in the year stays within the cap or, if higher, the average contribution base plus the threshold (franchise) of the year and the previous year. The commutation then counts as an annuity term (termijn). Source: [Art. 3.133 lid 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### 3.15 Disability insurance (AOV) premium with periodic benefits (uitkeringen)

Disability insurance (AOV, arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) premiums are fully deductible under art. 3.124 lid 1 onderdeel c if the policy pays periodic benefits (uitkeringen) for incapacity for work, illness or accident. FISIN 2026 states "volledig aftrekken" (fully deductible). There is no allowance cap in the article. As an entrepreneur (ondernemer) the disability insurance premium is not a business cost but an "uitgave voor andere inkomensvoorzieningen". Periodic benefits are "inkomsten uit vroegere dienstbetrekking" with wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld as prepayment (voorheffing). Source: [Art. 3.124 lid 1 c](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01), [FISIN 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/uitgaven_voor_inkomensvoorzieningen).

### 3.16 Disability insurance premium with lump-sum payout

A disability insurance premium for a policy that pays a lump sum (kapitaalverzekering) is not deductible. The payout is not box 1 income and counts in box 3. The rule of thumb is: premium not deductible means payout not taxed, and the reverse. The four exclusions and section 15.4 are the same in FISIN 2020 to 2026. Source: [Belastingdienst: AOV voor ondernemers](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

### 3.17 BAZ bill status

Bill 36 912, for the Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ), was submitted (ingediend) on 24 March 2026. The docket status on 2026-10-11 is "In afwachting nota nav verslag" (awaiting note after report). There is no plenary date. The committee input (inbreng) took place on 27 May 2026, and the report (Verslag) document 36 912 nr. 5 was published on 29 May 2026. Source: [Tweede Kamer docket 36912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).

### 3.18 BAZ waiting period (wachttijd) in the bill

The BAZ bill art. 16 lid 1 sets a waiting period (wachttijd) of 104 weeks (weken). The docket summary still says "ongeveer één jaar" (about one year), which conflicts with the bill text. The bill text is authoritative. Source: [Bill 36912, art. 16, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html).

### 3.19 BAZ premium estimate

The BAZ premium is set yearly by UWV and estimated at about 5,4% of profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) up to the maximum tax base (grondslag). At a maximum tax base of € 38.000 the MvT examples give about € 171 gross per month. Self-employed persons who are also employees have their social insurance wage (SV-loon) subtracted from the maximum contribution base. Source: [MvT 36912, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).

### 3.20 BAZ benefit maximum

The BAZ benefit is 70% of the tax base. The tax base is at most 142,86% of the minimum wage (minimumloon), so the benefit is at most 100% of the minimum wage. Source: [MvT 36912, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).

### 3.21 BAZ premium tax treatment

The public BAZ premium is proposed to be deductible as an expenditure for income provisions (uitgave voor inkomensvoorzieningen) and not from the profit (winst). UWV benefits would carry payroll taxes (loonheffing). Private policies keep the current tax treatment. Source: [MvT 36912, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).

### 3.22 BAZ entry into force

The BAZ entry into force is by royal decree (koninklijk besluit). There is no statutory date. The MvT examples assume 1 January 2030. Source: [MvT 36912, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 3, memorie van toelichting](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-3.html).
