## 5. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- KIA, second band endpoint, € 70.602 in 2025 to € 71.683 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- KIA, fixed amount and third band endpoint, € 19.769 up to € 130.744 in 2025 to € 20.072 up to € 132.746 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- KIA, top amount, € 392.230 in 2025 to € 398.236 in 2026, and nil above that amount in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026).
- KIA, lower threshold, unchanged at € 2.900, so the Belastingdienst window for 2026 is "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026).
- EIA and MIA maximum investment amount, € 151.000.000 in 2025 to € 153.000.000 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving yearly cap, € 392.230 in 2025 to € 398.236 in 2026, following the top of the KIA table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2).
- New rule for 2026, the EIA investments in an eenmanszaak and in a samenwerkingsverband such as a vof are added together for the EIA maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).
- Published for 2026, the RVO budget of MIA € 135.000.000 and Vamil € 20.000.000. The checked 2025 material gives no budget figure, so no old value is available (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).
- The monitoring period of the temporary willekeurige afschrijving of URWA art. 13 to 15, the period in which the asset had to keep meeting the art. 13 conditions, ended on 31 December 2025. The scheme itself only covered obligations entered or voortbrengingskosten made in 2023 with the asset taken into use before 1 January 2026, at most 50% of the costs once, so no 2026 investment can enter it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 13 to 15, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).
- Unchanged from 2025 to 2026, EIA 40%, MIA 45% / 36% / 27%, KIA per-asset minimum € 450, EIA and MIA per-asset minimum € 2.500, Vamil 75% willekeurig, depreciation caps of 10% for goodwill and 20% for other bedrijfsmiddelen, bodemwaarde equal to the WOZ-waarde of the building with the transition exception for a qualifying own-use building taken into use before 2024, and the desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold of € 2.900 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 2, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.31 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.45, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep a list of every bedrijfsmiddel with its aanschaffings- or voortbrengingskosten and the date of the obligation or the costs. The KIA is taken per onderneming on the total investment in bedrijfsmiddelen in the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41).
- Assets below € 450 do not count for the KIA, and assets below € 2.500 do not count for the EIA or the MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b).
- Record the koopovereenkomst or order date and the RVO notification, because the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months and the Milieulijst valid on the signing or order date is the one that applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).
- Where an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, take the deduction only over the payments made in that year and carry the excess to later years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 1).
- Set up the depreciation schedule within the caps, at most 10% per year for goodwill and at most 20% per year for other bedrijfsmiddelen. Voorwerpen van geringe waarde are written off at once in the year of acquisition or voortbrenging (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30).
- For a building, depreciate only while the book value is above the bodemwaarde, which is the WOZ-waarde of the building for the calendar year of the depreciation, except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ-waarde until 3 years after first use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 4).
- Record disposals, rentals and changes of use of any asset that received an investeringsaftrek. A disposal within five years above € 2.900 of transfer prices leads to a desinvesteringsbijtelling, and making the asset available to third parties counts as a disposal. Rental counts for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).
- Buying an asset from a BV in which the taxpayer holds at least one third of the shares gives in principle no investeringsaftrek, which is a point to check before recording the purchase (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- The KIA is claimed by choice in the aangifte (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41). For a BV, the same MIA percentages and the same EIA maximum are stated on the Belastingdienst Vpb information page (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).
- In a samenwerkingsverband each firmant or vennoot takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the (over)winst, and a different reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- MIA and Vamil maximum of € 25 miljoen per bedrijfsmiddel and per applicant per year. Status unknown, Check UNVERIFIED. The value stands on the current RVO page without a stated year, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden, and it is separate from the statutory € 153.000.000 in https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4 and on the Belastingdienst EIA page, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia. The Milieulijst regulation of the relevant year would settle which years this cap covers.
- The rule that a medegerechtigde or a geldverstrekker cannot use the EIA or the MIA is UNVERIFIED. It was not found on the fetched pages, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers, https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia. A statement in the statutory text of the Wet IB 2001 or on a Belastingdienst page for 2026 would settle it.
- Starters willekeurige afschrijving cap, UNVERIFIED: the € 398.236 rests on reading the cross-reference in art. 8 of the URWA to the maximum amount of the art. 3.41 lid 2 table, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9, read with the Belastingdienst KIA window of "tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236", https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026. No primary page prints this cap as a number. An earlier reading used € 71.683, the end of the 28% band, and that value is not used here. A Belastingdienst or RVO publication that prints the cap as a number would settle it.
- No other source conflicts are open for 2026.
