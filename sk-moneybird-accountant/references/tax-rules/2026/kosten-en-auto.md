## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

The 2026 rules for business costs and cars are largely enacted in the Wet inkomstenbelasting (Income Tax Act) and the Wet Vpb 1969 (Corporate Income Tax Act). For 2026 the gemengde kosten drempel (mixed costs threshold) is € 5.700, with an 80% election, the applied kilometre allowance for a private vehicle is € 0,25 although the statute still prints € 0,23, and the youngtimer age for the 35% rule is 16 years. Two decrees give temporary approvals for 2026, the kilometre allowance and the youngtimer transition, and Belastingplan 2027 contains proposed retroactive codification. The remaining fixed exclusions and the zero-emission car regime are enacted, with open points listed in section 7.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 1. Gemengde kosten drempel

The restriction applies to costs for voedsel (food), drank (drinks) and genotmiddelen (stimulants), to representatie (representation) including recepties (receptions), feestelijke bijeenkomsten (festive gatherings) en vermaak (entertainment), and to congressen (congresses), seminars, symposia, excursies (excursions), studiereizen (study trips) en dergelijke. Travel and stay connected with these posts count as part of them. The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover. No onttrekking (withdrawal) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs. The drempel is € 5.700 for 2026. It is not time-proportioned when an enterprise starts during the calendar year, and a boekjaar longer than 12 months does not increase the fixed amount. Food costs that qualify as loonkosten (wage costs) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not loon in natura (wages in kind). The drempel is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 and art. 10.1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3).

The content of the posts is practical. Voedsel covers zakenlunches and zakendiners including fooien (tips). Drank covers koffie, thee, melk en frisdrank. Genotmiddelen covers sigaretten en sigaren. Voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen used privately are never deductible. Representatie includes recepties, and relatiegeschenken generally fall under representatie. Relatiegeschenken are a subcategory of representatiekosten per the wetsgeschiedenis of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.3).

### 2. Gemengde kosten keuze

Instead of the drempel, the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs. The same 80% applies to resultaatgenieters (persons with income from other activities). The election is made in the return for the year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.

### 3. Vpb variant gemengde kosten

For a BV, under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount of € 5.700. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount. Only in a fiscale eenheid (fiscal unity) does it apply once, at the parent. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 8 lid 5) and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.4).

### 4. Congress, course and study-trip travel cap

Travel and stay for cursussen en opleidingen voor studie en beroep, and for congressen, seminars, symposia, excursies, studiereizen en dergelijke, are not deductible above € 1.500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 5. Kilometervergoeding, statute

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets or is rented privately, such as an auto, motor or fiets, are limited to a fixed amount per business kilometre. The statute prints € 0,23 for 2026 under art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b. The amount is all-in, so brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer under art. 3.15 lid 7. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 lid 6, lid 7 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b).

### 6. Kilometervergoeding, applied

For 2026 the Belastingdienst applies € 0,25 per business kilometre, retroactive to 1 January 2026. The legal basis is the Beleidsbesluit fiscale maatregelen naar aanleiding van de energieschok of 17 May 2026, nr. 2026-8260, Stcrt. 2026, 18302. The decree itself lapses on 1 January 2027. Its approvals are to be codified in Belastingplan 2027. Use € 0,25 for 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026.

### 7. MRB bestelauto and vrachtauto, 2026 decree

The same decree of 17 May 2026 temporarily lowers mrb (motorrijtuigenbelasting, motor vehicle tax) for bestelauto's (vans) of btw-ondernemers and sets nil for vrachtauto's (trucks) from 1 July to 31 December 2026, for two tijdvakken (periods). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.

### 8. Reiskostenvergoeding loonbelasting

The same decree allows € 0,25 per kilometre instead of € 0,23 under art. 31a lid 2 onderdeel a 3° Wet LB. Correctieberichten are allowed. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html (section 2.1).

### 9. Other forfaitaire reiskosten in Wet IB

The same decree sets € 0,25 instead of € 0,23 for the other forfaitaire reiskosten in the Wet IB under art. 6.17 lid 6 onderdeel a, art. 6.26 with art. 40 lid 1 onderdeel b URIB and art. 6.36 lid 2. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html (section 2.2).

### 10. Verhuiskosten and dubbele huisvesting

Moving costs are the cost of moving the inboedel (household contents) plus € 7.750. Housing outside the place of residence is deductible for at most two years. Unchanged 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a).

### 11. Bijtelling auto, standard

When a car is also available for private use, at least 22% of the waarde van de auto is an onttrekking per year. The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer (commuting) is not private under lid 3. With a rittenregistratie (trip registration) or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend (conclusive), though trips can still be checked. The onttrekking is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself under lid 4, so it is capped at the total autokosten including depreciation. Auto means personenauto or bestelauto, except a bestelauto that is almost exclusively suited for goods under lid 5 a. For that exception private km x the actual cost per km is settled. A verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto is possible under lid 6 to 10. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.

### 12. Bijtelling auto, pre-2017 car

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22% under art. 10a.4. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.4).

### 13. Youngtimer

When a car was first used more than 16 years ago, 35% of the waarde in het economische verkeer (WEV, fair market value) is the onttrekking per year. For 2026 the age threshold is 16 years. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b in the 2026 text still says meer dan vijftien jaar for the WEV switch, while lid 1 moved to 16. The Belastingdienst applies 16 years. Belastingplan 2027 art. I onderdeel E onder 2 would replace vijftien by 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026. See section 7. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 14. Youngtimer transition 2026

For 2026 there is an optional transition. The 2025 rule of 35% of WEV stays for 2026 for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025. The legal basis is the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, nr. 2026-2493, Stcrt. 2026, 6596, retroactive to 1 January 2026. It lapses on 1 January 2027 or on codification. The 2026 youngtimer decree explains that a car that became more than 15 but not more than 16 years old during 2025 is otherwise taxed at 22%, or 25% for pre-2017 cars, of the catalogue price until it passes 16 in 2026. The approval is a keuzeregeling. Cars that pass 15 only during 2026 get no transition. Rijksoverheid summarises the transition as youngtimers that turn 16 in 2026 staying under the youngtimer rule until 1 January 2027. The Belastingdienst says that a car used in 2025 that was older than 15 years on 31 December 2025 also falls under 35% of WEV in 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 15. Bijtelling auto 500 km rule

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Woon-werkverkeer is not private for the inkomstenbelasting. With a rittenregistratie or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the onttrekking is nil. A keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem is assumed to be sluitend, though trips can still be checked. Part-year availability is pro-rated. The yearly amount is pro-rated, so 5 months gives 5/12 x 22% x catalogue value. Private km are converted to a yearly basis, so 100 km in 5 months is 240 km. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.

### 16. Auto vermogensetikettering

A car is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen when used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres and at most 500 private kilometres a year. Used 90% or more privately it is verplicht privévermogen. All other cases are keuzevermogen. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel.

### 17. Waarde van de auto

The waarde is the catalogusprijs (list price) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus bpm. The Belastingdienst describes it as the original cataloguswaarde including btw and bpm and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the kenteken (registration) was issued. The RDW site carries it for most cars. For an old car the waarde in het economische verkeer replaces it. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b keeps the words meer dan vijftien jaar in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 in 2026 and 25 in the enacted 2027 text. The Belastingdienst applies 16 years for the WEV switch from 2026. Belastingplan 2027 would repair lid 5 b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026. See section 7. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. A laadpaal (charging point) for a zero-emission car in the ondernemingsvermogen is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 5), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html.

### 18. Zero-emission, first admission 2026

The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros. For a car first admitted in 2026 the reduction is 4% off capped € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000 and 22% above. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force apply. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 19. Uncapped zero-emission categories 2026

Cars on waterstof are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowattpiek and a battery without lead. From the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in wattpiek divided by consumption in wattuur per km is at least 7. The 2026 Belastingdienst page says at least 1 kilowattpiek. That was the 2021 to 2023 text. The statute decides. See section 7. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01.

### 20. Zero-emission 60-month fixation

The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. For example, first admission on 7 March means start on 1 April. After the 60 months the rules then in force apply. Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during 2025. Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2021 use 12% until the 60 months end, then the 2026 rate for the remaining months. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 11) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 21. Zero-emission rates by first admission year

By year of first admission: 2020 14% off capped € 6.300, which is 8% up to € 45.000. 2021 10% off capped € 4.000, which is 12% up to € 40.000. 2022 6% off capped € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000. 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800, which is 16% up to € 30.000. 2025 5% off capped € 1.500, which is 17% up to € 30.000. 2026 4% off capped € 1.200, which is 18% up to € 30.000. 2027 text 2% off capped € 600, which is 20% up to € 30.000. Only waterstof was uncapped in 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 22. Bijtelling fiets

When a fiets, including an e-bike or speed-pedelec that also runs on muscle power, of the business is also available privately, 7% of the consumentenadviesprijs (consumer advisory price) is an onttrekking per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for woon-werkverkeer counts as private availability. A lid 5 added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2020 sets the onttrekking at nil when the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a).

### 23. Studiekostenaftrek 2026

The persoonsgebonden aftrek for scholingsuitgaven (training expenses) ended with the Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven, which took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349. In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked vervallen per 01-01-2022. Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for scholingsuitgaven relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 in the prestatiebeurs case. For 2026 FISIN narrows the case to an extended diplomatermijn (diploma period) of maximum 15 years, so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. The checked material does not restate the per-study-year caps for 2026. See section 7. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.16).

### 24. Werkruimte thuis

Costs of a workspace including its inrichting (furnishings) in a home that is not ondernemingsvermogen are not deductible unless the workspace is a naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig part of the home and an income test is met. With another workspace outside the home, the combined winst, loon and resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden must be earned mainly in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent in it. Lid 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose huurrecht is ondernemingsvermogen. The official factors for zelfstandig are an own entrance or opgang, own sanitary facilities, and that the space could be rented to a third party. A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not zelfstandig. From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home in the ondernemingsvermogen that serves as hoofdverblijf are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The MvT calls this a codification of case law. For a non-zelfstandige workspace in such a home the huurderslasten, inrichting such as meubilair, stoffering, behang- en schilderwerk, and gas, water en licht, are not deductible. Corrected for 2025 onward: the Belastingdienst page still presents the second route, owner and home in ondernemingsvermogen, without this limit. See section 7. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.16), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html (MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning.

### 25. Telefoonabonnement thuis

A private telephone subscription is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints Telefoonabonnement thuis 0% and Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis 100%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 26. Apparatuur not in ondernemingsvermogen

Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 3. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### 27. Laptop and computer etikettering

The income-tax treatment follows vermogensetikettering (asset classification). Apparatuur en instrumenten that do not belong to the ondernemingsvermogen are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 3. A bedrijfsmiddel (business asset) used 10% or less for business is verplicht privévermogen, 90% or more is verplicht ondernemingsvermogen, in between is keuzevermogen within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an aanschafwaarde (purchase value) under € 450 is expensed at once. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### 28. € 450 rule and depreciation

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are kleine kantoorartikelen, vakliteratuur and onderhoud van de auto. A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the restwaarde (residual value) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.

### 29. Other fixed exclusions in art. 3.16

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: telefoonabonnementen for connections in the taxpayer's own home under lid 2 a, literatuur except vakliteratuur (professional literature) under lid 2 b, kleding except werkkleding (work clothing) under lid 2 c, werkkleding defined in lid 5, persoonlijke verzorging (personal care) under lid 2 d, costs of privately owned or privately rented muziekinstrumenten, geluidsapparatuur, gereedschappen, computers en andere dergelijke apparatuur and beeldapparatuur under lid 3, and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5.000 under lid 4. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer under lid 11. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16).

### 30. Geldboetes and dwangsommen

Not deductible: costs connected with geldboetes (fines) from a strafrechter, settlement payments to avoid prosecution, strafbeschikkingen (criminal penalty orders), bestuurlijke boeten (administrative fines) and comparable foreign fines, tuchtrechtelijke boeten (disciplinary fines) and EU fines under art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c. Also not deductible: costs connected with a misdrijf (criminal offence) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted under onderdeel d, or for which a strafbeschikking became irrevocable under onderdeel e, and dwangsommen (penalty payments) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb under onderdeel i. The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Wahv verkeersboeten are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. Costs made before the geldboete is imposed are not costs that verband houden met the imposed fine and stay deductible. Costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a dwangsom the line is the verbeurdverklaring. Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited dwangsommen are also caught. Only costs connected with the misdrijf for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by transactie or strafbeschikking, are excluded. Example: convicted only for possession of hennepplanten, not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.14), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (sections 1.1 and 2) and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/.

### 31. Openbaar vervoer, taxi and vliegtuig

Business travel by openbaar vervoer (public transport), taxi or vliegtuig is deductible at 100% of the actual costs. The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or an OV-chipkaart trip overview. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 32. KOR omzetgrens and cost base

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, the KOR case, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs and investments is deducted, also not VAT paid in another EU country. Earlier deductions may need herziening (revision). The low bestelauto mrb rate stays available. Exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year. The choice is per ondernemer covering all subnummers (VAT subnumbers). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.

### 33. BTW forfait private car without km administration

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for belaste omzet (taxed turnover). Woon-werkverkeer counts as private use for the btw, though it is business for the inkomstenbelasting. In a BV the DGA (directeur-grootaandeelhouder) is not the btw-ondernemer, he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without a sluitende kilometeradministratie all VAT may be deducted and corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto.

### 34. Keuze herzien and heretikettering

A keuzevermogen choice can be revised until the aanslag for the year of the choice is irrevocable. Afterwards this is possible only on a bijzondere omstandigheid (special circumstance). Private use of an asset in the ondernemingsvermogen is a privéonttrekking (private withdrawal). A change in the law can be such a bijzondere omstandigheid when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The Kennisgroep applies this to the youngtimer change. Revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Kennisgroep KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/.

### 35. Mixed character costs

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character dominates. Examples with only a business part or a restriction are huur and onderhoud of a pand split into business and private parts, telefoonkosten, a car of the business, congressen, studiereizen, symposia, seminars en excursies, zakenlunches en diners, recepties and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business, for example rijbewijs, health costs such as pedicure and zorgverzekering, and personal care such as clothing and the kapper. Werkkleding is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive.

### 36. Studiekosten as a business cost

Deductible when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing vakkennis (professional knowledge). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs. The boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f. The 2022 abolition removed only the persoonsgebonden route. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### Example 1: Gemengde kosten drempel and 80% election

Calculated example. Gemengde kosten are € 8.000,00. The drempel is € 5.700,00. Without the election, the deductible amount is € 8.000,00 minus € 5.700,00 = € 2.300,00. With the 80% election, the deductible amount is 0,80 x € 8.000,00 = € 6.400,00. The election is made in the return. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 lid 1 and lid 5) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.

### Example 2: Vpb variant for a BV

Calculated example. The employees' wage total is € 2.000.000,00. 0,4% of that is 0,004 x € 2.000.000,00 = € 8.000,00. The fixed amount is € 5.700,00. The higher amount is € 8.000,00. Gemengde kosten are € 10.000,00. Without the election, the deductible amount is € 10.000,00 minus € 8.000,00 = € 2.000,00. With the 73,5% election, the deductible amount is 0,735 x € 10.000,00 = € 7.350,00. The fixed amount applies per body, and once at the parent in a fiscale eenheid. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 8 lid 5).

### Example 3: Congress travel and stay cap

Calculated example. Travel and stay for a congress are € 2.000,00. Attendance is not necessary because of the nature of the work. The cap is € 1.500,00. The deductible amount is € 1.500,00. The excess is € 2.000,00 minus € 1.500,00 = € 500,00. If the nature of the work makes attendance necessary, the cap does not apply and the full € 2.000,00 is deductible. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### Example 4: Kilometre allowance private car

Calculated example. A private car is used for 10.000 business kilometres in 2026. The statute amount gives 10.000 x € 0,23 = € 2.300,00. The applied amount gives 10.000 x € 0,25 = € 2.500,00. The difference is € 2.500,00 minus € 2.300,00 = € 200,00. Use € 0,25 for 2026. The amount is all-in, so brandstof, verzekering, tol and parkeren cannot be deducted separately. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 lid 6) and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.

### Example 5: Bijtelling auto

Official example. Example 1: catalogusprijs € 30.000,00. 22% is 0,22 x € 30.000,00 = € 6.600,00. Costs are mrb € 1.000,00 plus verzekering € 1.000,00 plus afschrijving € 4.000,00 plus brandstof € 3.000,00 plus onderhoud € 1.000,00 = € 10.000,00. The onttrekking is € 6.600,00. The net deductible costs are € 10.000,00 minus € 6.600,00 = € 3.400,00. Example 2: costs are € 9.000,00. Catalogusprijs is € 50.000,00. 22% is 0,22 x € 50.000,00 = € 11.000,00. The onttrekking is capped at the costs of € 9.000,00. Net effect € 9.000,00 minus € 9.000,00 = € 0,00. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/voorbeelden_berekening_privegebruik_auto.

### Example 6: Zero-emission car first admission 2026

Calculated example. A zero-emission car is first admitted in 2026 with a catalogusprijs of € 40.000,00. The standard 22% is 0,22 x € 40.000,00 = € 8.800,00. The reduction is 4% of the catalogusprijs, capped at € 1.200,00. The cap is reached at a catalogusprijs of € 30.000,00, since 0,04 x € 30.000,00 = € 1.200,00. The onttrekking is € 8.800,00 minus € 1.200,00 = € 7.600,00. Cross-check: 0,18 x € 30.000,00 = € 5.400,00 plus 0,22 x € 10.000,00 = € 2.200,00, total € 7.600,00. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### Example 7: Zero-emission car first admitted in 2021 and 60-month end in 2026

Calculated example. A zero-emission car is first admitted on 7 March 2021 with a catalogusprijs of € 40.000,00. The 60-month period starts on 1 April 2021 and ends on 31 March 2026. January to March 2026 use the 2021 rate. The 2021 rate is 10% off capped € 4.000,00, which is 12% up to € 40.000,00. For this car, 0,12 x € 40.000,00 = € 4.800,00 per year. 3/12 x € 4.800,00 = € 1.200,00. April to December 2026 use the 2026 rate. The 2026 rate is 4% off capped € 1.200,00, which is 18% up to € 30.000,00 and 22% above. 0,18 x € 30.000,00 = € 5.400,00 plus 0,22 x € 10.000,00 = € 2.200,00, total € 7.600,00 per year. 9/12 x € 7.600,00 = € 5.700,00. Total 2026 onttrekking is € 1.200,00 plus € 5.700,00 = € 6.900,00. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### Example 8: Bijtelling fiets

Calculated example. A business fiets has a consumentenadviesprijs of € 3.000,00. 7% is 0,07 x € 3.000,00 = € 210,00 per year. If the costs borne are € 150,00, the onttrekking is capped at € 150,00. If the fiets is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address, the onttrekking is nil. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a).

### Example 9: € 450 rule and depreciation

Official example. A camera costs € 6.000,00, is used for 5 years and has a restwaarde of € 1.500,00. Depreciation is (€ 6.000,00 minus € 1.500,00) / 5 = € 4.500,00 / 5 = € 900,00 per year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.

Calculated example. A printer costs € 400,00. It is under € 450,00 and is deducted in one go. A laptop costs € 1.200,00, is used for 3 years and has a restwaarde of € 300,00. Depreciation is (€ 1.200,00 minus € 300,00) / 3 = € 900,00 / 3 = € 300,00 per year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.

### Example 10: BTW private car

Official example. Example 1: btw on brandstof is € 840,00 on € 4.000,00, btw on onderhoud is € 105,00 on € 500,00, verzekering is € 400,00 and mrb is € 350,00. Total btw is € 840,00 plus € 105,00 = € 945,00. Business use is 45.000 of 60.000 km. 45.000 / 60.000 = 0,75. 0,75 x € 945,00 = € 708,75, rounded € 709 in rubriek 5b. At 50% vrijgestelde omzet, 0,50 x € 709,00 = € 354,50, rounded € 355. Example 2: catalogusprijs is € 45.000,00. 1,5% is 0,015 x € 45.000,00 = € 675,00. If the car is bought on 1 September, the part-year correction is 4/12 x € 675,00 = € 225,00 in rubriek 1d. Separately, at 40% vrijgestelde omzet, the deductible part is 0,60 x € 675,00 = € 405,00. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto.

### Example 11: BTW computer partly business

Official example. A computer costs € 7.000,00 plus € 1.470,00 btw. Business use is 80%. The deductible btw is 0,80 x € 1.470,00 = € 1.176,00. Use costs are € 1.100,00 plus € 231,00 btw. Year 2 private use is 30%. The 1/5 part of the investment btw is € 1.470,00 / 5 = € 294,00. The deducted 1/5 is 0,80 x € 294,00 = € 235,20, rounded down € 235. The year 2 entitlement is 0,70 x € 294,00 = € 205,80, rounded € 206. Pay back € 235,00 minus € 206,00 = € 29,00. The use-cost correction is 0,20 x € 231,00 = € 46,20, rounded down € 46 in year 1, and 0,30 x € 231,00 = € 69,30, rounded down € 69 in year 2, in rubriek 1d. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/gemengd_gebruik/investeringsgoederen/roerende_investeringsgoederen/voorbeeld_computer_gedeeltelijk_bedrijfsvermogen.

### Example 12: KOR threshold

Calculated example. Turnover is € 16.000,00 in 2026. That stays under the € 20.000,00 threshold. If turnover passes € 20.000,00, the KOR ends immediately. If the choice is to exit, exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter. The choice is per ondernemer and covers all subnummers. If the KOR applies, costs are deducted including VAT in the IB return. ### Example 13: Studiekosten and the travel cap

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record business costs in the winst from onderneming. Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the btw-aangifte. When no btw-aangifte is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, for example under the KOR, deduct the costs including VAT in the IB return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.
- Record the 80% election in the return when it is chosen instead of the € 5.700 drempel. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.
- Keep receipts and invoices for all costs. The Belastingdienst requires this for the € 450 rule, for depreciation and for costs made before the start of the business. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.
- For gemengde kosten, record voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen, representatie, congressen and comparable posts separately so the yearly total against the € 5.700 drempel is clear. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15).
- For a car in the ondernemingsvermogen, record the catalogusprijs, the first admission date, the private kilometres and the total autokosten including depreciation. Keep a rittenregistratie or other evidence for the 500 km rule. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.
- For a private car used for business, record the business kilometres and apply € 0,25 per km for 2026. The amount is all-in, so do not add brandstof, verzekering, tol or parkeren separately. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026.
- For a zero-emission car, record the first admission date, the CO2 value, the solar panel specifications, the battery type and the end of the 60-month period. Keep the laadpaal invoice separate. The laadpaal does not raise the catalogue price. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto.
- For a fiets of the business, record the consumentenadviesprijs, the private availability and whether the fiets is stalled at the home address no more than incidentally. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a).
- For a congress, course or study trip, record travel and stay separately and apply the € 1.500 cap unless the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8).
- For assets, apply the € 450 rule. Record the aanschafwaarde, the restwaarde and the depreciation schedule. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.
- For werkruimte thuis, record the facts for zelfstandigheid and the income test. A non-zelfstandige workspace is 0% in principle. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 1 and lid 14).
- For fines and dwangsommen, do not deduct. Keep documentation of when the fine was imposed to separate pre-imposition costs. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.14) and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/.
- For study costs, record the course content to show that it maintains existing vakkennis. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers.
- For the KOR, monitor turnover against € 20.000. Under the KOR no VAT is charged or paid and no VAT is deducted, and costs are deducted including VAT in the IB return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.
- For btw on a private car used for business, keep a sluitende kilometeradministratie to deduct VAT by business use. Without it, correct 1,5% of the catalogue price including btw and bpm in rubriek 1d at year end. Woon-werkverkeer counts as private for the btw. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto.
- For a change in etikettering, record the year of the keuze and the moment the aanslag for that year becomes irrevocable. A later change needs a bijzondere omstandigheid. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel.

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- WEV switch age in 2026. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b in the 2026 text says meer dan vijftien jaar, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Belastingdienst applies 16 years, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto. Belastingplan 2027 art. I onderdeel E onder 2 replaces vijftien by 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html, and the bill is proposed on 2026-10-11. This is settled in practice by the Belastingdienst position. It is settled in law only when the bill is adopted. What would settle it is adoption of Belastingplan 2027.
- Legal codification of the € 0,25 kilometre allowance and of the youngtimer transition for 2026. The codification sits in Belastingplan 2027 art. I onderdelen C, D, H, I and art. XLVII lid 1, retroactive to 1 January 2026 per art. LXIII lid 1 a, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html. The bill is proposed on 2026-10-11. What would settle it is adoption of Belastingplan 2027. Until then the 2026 decree of 17 May 2026, Stcrt. 2026, 18302, applies, and it lapses on 1 January 2027, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.
- Studiekostenaftrek 2026 caps. FISIN 2026 narrows the prestatiebeurs case to an extended diplomatermijn of maximum 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten. The checked material does not restate the per-study-year caps for 2026. The caps verified for 2022 to 2025 are € 1.693 per study year for mbo, € 2.443 per study year for hbo or wo, € 141,09 per month part-year for mbo, € 203,59 per month part-year for hbo or wo, and a € 250 drempel per person, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten. What would settle it is a 2026 FISIN page or law text that states the caps for 2026.
- Solar panel exemption from the zero-emission cap. The 2026 Belastingdienst page says at least 1 kilowattpiek, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026. The statute says 1 kilowattpiek only in the 2021 to 2023 texts, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and from the 2024 text requires wattpiek divided by wattuur per km of at least 7, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01. The statute decides. What would settle it is a corrected Belastingdienst page or a court decision.
- Werkruimte owner route. The Belastingdienst page offers deduction for a non-zelfstandige workspace when the home is owned and in the ondernemingsvermogen, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning. From 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 bars costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. The MvT names the huurderslasten, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html. Which owner costs of a non-zelfstandige workspace stay deductible beyond the huurderslasten is not stated on a fetched primary page. What would settle it is a Belastingdienst policy decision or case law.
- Youngtimer transition 2026 wording. Rijksoverheid says cars turning 16 in 2026 stay under the youngtimer rule until 1 January 2027, source https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen. The legal basis is the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, Stcrt. 2026, 6596, an optional approval for a car that passed 15 during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html. The decree wording is narrower than the summary. The decree itself is the legal basis.
- Old-car minimum, 35% against 25%. Both apply to different cars. 35% of WEV applies to old cars under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b. At least 25% instead of 22% applies to a car first admitted by 31 December 2016 under art. 10a.4. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Zero-emission 2020, 8% up to € 45.000 against 14% off capped € 6.300. This is the same regime. The 2020 statute prints 14% with a maximum of € 6.300, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. Only waterstof was uncapped in 2020. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01.
