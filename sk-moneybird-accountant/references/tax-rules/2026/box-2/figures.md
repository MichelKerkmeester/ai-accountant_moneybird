## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

Box 2 and substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) covers income from a stake of at least 5% in a company, most visibly dividends from and gains on shares in the taxpayer's own private limited company (BV). For 2026 the enacted art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001 table charges 24,5% up to € 68.843 and 31% above it, per taxpayer, and the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction above € 500.000 of debts to the own company is also box 2 income. The 2026 law is enacted: Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) took effect on 1 January 2026, repaired a cross-reference in the excessive borrowing rules retroactively and added art. 4.6 sub f, while the box 2 table and the € 500.000 maximum are unchanged (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). One figure, the sum of two partners' first brackets of € 137.686, is stated by no primary page and is marked unknown below.
