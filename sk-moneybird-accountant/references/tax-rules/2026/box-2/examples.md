## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example 1, box 2 table on a dividend of € 70.000 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2).** A dividend of € 70.000 is received without dividendbelasting. Step 1, the part up to the boundary: € 68.843 × 24,5% = € 16.866,54, rounded down to € 16.866. Step 2, the part above the boundary: € 70.000 minus € 68.843 = € 1.157. € 1.157 × 31% = € 358,67, rounded down to € 358. Step 3, the total is € 16.866 + € 358 = € 17.224.

**Official example 2, excessief lenen with an earlier taxed amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).** The 2026 drempel is € 600.000, being € 500.000 plus € 100.000 taxed in 2025. The loan to the BV is € 650.000 on 31 December 2026. The part above the drempel is € 650.000 minus € 600.000 = € 50.000, and that € 50.000 is taxed as fictief regulier voordeel.

**Official example 3, dividend with costs and dividendbelasting (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).** Gross dividend € 5.000, bank costs € 250 and dividendbelasting € 750. Box 2 income is € 5.000 minus € 250 = € 4.750. The € 750 dividendbelasting is credited as voorheffing.

**Official example 4, onzakelijke prijs (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).** Shares are sold to a son-in-law for € 20.000 where a third party would pay € 35.000. The value in het economisch verkeer of € 35.000 applies to both sides.

**Calculated example 1, income inside the first bracket.** Box 2 income is € 60.000 on invented facts. This is below € 68.843, so the whole amount is in bracket 1: € 60.000 × 24,5% = € 14.700,00.

**Calculated example 2, income in both brackets.** Box 2 income is € 200.000 on invented facts. Bracket 1: € 68.843 × 24,5% = € 16.866,54, rounded down to € 16.866, which matches the tax at the top of bracket 1 in section 2. The part above the boundary: € 200.000 minus € 68.843 = € 131.157. Bracket 2: € 131.157 × 31% = € 40.658,67. Total: € 16.866 + € 40.658,67 = € 57.524,67.

**Calculated example 3, regulier voordeel with costs.** Gross dividend € 20.000 and bank costs € 1.000 on invented facts. The voordeel is € 20.000 minus € 1.000 = € 19.000. The income is below € 68.843, so the tax is € 19.000 × 24,5% = € 4.655,00.

**Calculated example 4, dividendbelasting as voorheffing.** Gross dividend € 5.000, bank costs € 250 and dividendbelasting € 750, the FISIN 2026 pattern. Box 2 income is € 5.000 minus € 250 = € 4.750. The tax before the credit is € 4.750 × 24,5% = € 1.163,75. The dividendbelasting of € 750 is credited as voorheffing, leaving € 1.163,75 minus € 750 = € 413,75.

**Calculated example 5, onzakelijke prijs with a known verkrijgingsprijs.** The verkrijgingsprijs is € 10.000 on invented facts. The shares are sold to a son for € 20.000 where a third party would pay € 35.000, so € 35.000 applies to both sides. The vervreemdingsvoordeel is € 35.000 minus € 10.000 = € 25.000, and the tax is € 25.000 × 24,5% = € 6.125,00.

**Calculated example 6, excessief lenen.** Debts to the BV on 31 December 2026 are € 650.000 on invented facts, and no amounts were taxed earlier. The maximum is € 500.000. The positive amount is € 650.000 minus € 500.000 = € 150.000. If this is the only box 2 income, the tax is bracket 1 € 68.843 × 24,5% = € 16.866,54, rounded down to € 16.866, plus bracket 2 (€ 150.000 minus € 68.843 = € 81.157) × 31% = € 25.158,67, in total € 16.866 + € 25.158,67 = € 42.024,67.

**Calculated example 7, negative fictief regulier voordeel.** Debts to the BV on 31 December 2026 are € 480.000 on invented facts, and no amounts were taxed earlier. The shortfall below the € 500.000 maximum is € 500.000 minus € 480.000 = € 20.000. Because the negative amount is capped at amounts taxed earlier and not yet reversed, and there are none, the negative amount is nil.

**Calculated example 8, loss conversion.** An unoffset loss uit aanmerkelijk belang is € 20.000 on invented facts. The conversion conditions of art. 4.53 are met. The belastingkorting is € 20.000 × 24,5% = € 4.900,00. The FISIN 2026 official example states € 5.380 at 26,9%, which is stale for 2026. The korting reduces box 1 tax of the year of the beschikking and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year.

**Calculated example 9, loss offset window.** A loss uit aanmerkelijk belang of € 20.000 arises in 2026 on invented facts. It can be set against box 2 income of 2025 and then against the six following years, 2027, 2028, 2029, 2030, 2031 and 2032, in the order in which losses arose.

**Calculated example 10, forfaitair voordeel.** The value of the shares in a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling at 1 January 2026 is € 100.000 on invented facts, and other reguliere voordelen from those shares are € 1.000. The forfaitair voordeel is 6% × € 100.000 = € 6.000,00, reduced by the € 1.000, so € 5.000. If the other voordelen were € 7.000, the outcome would be nil, because the amount never drops below nil. If the shares were held for half the year, the yearly amount would be time-apportioned.

**Calculated example 11, partner division.** Two fiscale partners have total box 2 income of € 120.000 on invented facts and choose a 50/50 division. Each share is € 60.000. Each is taxed in his own first bracket: € 60.000 × 24,5% = € 14.700,00 for each, in total € 29.400,00. FISIN 2026 allows any division as long as the total is 100%.

**Calculated example 12, gebruikelijk loon.** The comparable loon is € 50.000, the highest loon of the employees of the body or connected bodies is € 62.000, and the fixed amount is € 58.000, all invented facts. The highest of the three is € 62.000, so that is the minimum loon.

**Calculated example 13, gebruikelijk loon threshold.** The loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is € 4.500 on invented facts. That is not more than € 5.000, so the gebruikelijk-loon rule does not apply. If two companies pay € 3.000 and € 2.500, the total is € 5.500, which is more than € 5.000, so the rule does apply, because the threshold is tested on the total and "niet per onderneming".

**Calculated example 14, ODV increase.** The ODV balance at the start of 2026 is € 200.000 on invented facts. The increase is 2,593% × € 200.000 = € 5.186,00.

**Calculated example 15, inherited belang facility.** Shares are inherited, and box 2 income of € 25.000 was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer. Reguliere voordelen of € 30.000 are received within 24 months after the death, on invented facts. On request the exclusion is capped at € 25.000, so € 25.000 is not counted as box 2 income and € 25.000 is written off on the verkrijgingsprijs. The remaining € 5.000 is box 2 income. If this is the only box 2 income, the tax on it is € 5.000 × 24,5% = € 1.225,00.
