## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Box 2 bracket 1 boundary moves from € 67.804 in 2025 to € 68.843 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The tax at the top of bracket 1 moves from € 16.611 in 2025 to € 16.866 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Box 2 bracket 2 stays at 31% above the boundary in both years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) of art. 4.14 moves from 5,88% in 2025 to 6% in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The old-age obligation (ODV) market rate moves from 2,602% for 2025 to 2,593% for 2026. Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025 replaces "'2,602%' door '2,593%'" (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html).
- The customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount moves from € 56.000 in 2025 to € 58.000 in 2026, with the € 5.000 threshold unchanged (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01).
- The excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) maximum stays at € 500.000 plus amounts taxed earlier in both years, and the amount remains unindexed (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The tax credit (belastingkorting) on an unoffset loss stays at 24,5% in both years, taken from the first row of the fourth column of the art. 2.12 table (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Art. 4.6 carries sub a to f in both years. Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025, so the ground also applies to 2025. In the 2026 text, sub e refers to art. 2 lid 11 and art. 9 lid 1 sub e Wet Vpb 1969 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) enters into force on 1 January 2026 and repairs the excessive borrowing cross-reference "«zesde lid»" to "«vierde lid»" retroactively to 1 January 2023, not for 2024 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html).
- The partnership (samenwerkingsverband) exception in art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5, inserted by Tax Plan 2025 (Belastingplan 2025, Stb. 2024, 434, retroactive to 1 January 2023), continues to apply in 2026 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The loss offset period, the 9-year rule for losses from 2018 or earlier, the 10-year segregated private assets (afgezonderd particulier vermogen, APV) term and the 24-month inherited substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) facility are unchanged from 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record a dividend from the own company at the gross amount as box 2 income, deduct the costs of the shareholding from the benefit (voordeel), and treat dividend tax (dividendbelasting) as a prepayment (voorheffing) that is credited, not as a cost (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Keep a per-share record of the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs), with notary costs included, and of the value at acquisition for the special cases described by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) (inheritance, gift with or without a rollover (doorschuiving), reaching 5% during the year, and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs)) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Interest on a loan taken to buy the shares is deductible in box 2. Interest on a receivable on the own company belongs to income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 wage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- For excessive borrowing, list all debts to the own company and to companies where a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) exists, per 31 December and at nominal value. Receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, and debts at several companies are added (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- Keep the loan in the company accounts as a loan in full. The company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays corporate income tax (Vpb) on it, and it does not withhold dividend tax on the part taxed as box 2 income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt).
- Track debts of a blood relative or relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner, and of their partners, to such a company, for the part above € 500.000, applied to that person and his partner together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Run the payroll at a customary salary of at least € 58.000, unless the € 5.000 total threshold applies or the comparable-wage route is made plausible. This is box 1 wage (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).
- Increase an old-age obligation by the 2026 market rate of 2,593% and keep the rate documentation. For a remaining pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB), index the accrued rights every year and value the provision actuarially (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/).
- Keep the decision (beschikking) that sets a loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang), because it is issued with the assessment and shown separately on it. The loss conversion request comes only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting).
- Box 2 income and the deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) from excessive borrowing land on the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) return, where fiscal partners divide the income from a substantial interest in a proportion that totals 100% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).
- For an inherited substantial interest, claim the 24-month facility in the return and write the excluded amounts off on the acquisition price (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Sum of two partners' first brackets, € 137.686: unknown and UNVERIFIED. It is the arithmetic sum of two times € 68.843, and no primary page states it. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) box 2 page has no partner column (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2). The sourced rules are only that art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer and that partners may divide the income (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). A Tax and Customs Administration page or form that states a combined partner amount would settle it.
- Loss-conversion credit, 26,9% against 24,5%: FISIN 2026 states "De korting is 26,9%" and its loss example gives a loss of € 20.000 a credit of € 5.380 at 26,9% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The statute and the live page give 24,5% for 2026, which on the same loss is € 4.900 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting). Settled at 24,5% by art. 4.53 lid 2 with art. 2.12. A corrected FISIN page would close it.
- Rollover (doorschuiving) list, gift, inheritance and emigration rollovers: UNVERIFIED. The rollover on request for gift, inheritance and emigration is not confirmed on a primary page in the checked sources. FISIN 2020 and FISIN 2026 are the available pages (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The art. 4.17 to 4.17c texts or a Tax and Customs Administration page would settle it.
- Non-residents and the partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) decision: art. 7.5 is Verified, a non-resident declares only a substantial interest in a Netherlands company that is not part of an enterprise (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The statement that a 2025 decision on partial non-resident taxpayer status has the same effect is UNVERIFIED, it was not found on the fetched Tax and Customs Administration pages. The decision text or a Tax and Customs Administration page would settle it.
- Proposed for 2027, not adopted on 2026-10-11: a second-bracket rate of 29,2%, a phase-down (afbouw) of the excessive borrowing rules, two technical measures, and the exclusion of the deemed regular benefit from the art. 4.12a facility from 1 January 2027. These do not change 2026 (https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/5d1a02b8-ff11-4b87-b6a4-bb0f9828eca0/file, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).
