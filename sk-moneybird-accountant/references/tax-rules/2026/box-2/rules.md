## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 bracket 1

The first bracket charges 24,5% on box 2 income up to € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). It applies to every taxpayer with inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang, and art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each fiscal partner has his own bracket for his own share of the income (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The boundary is not fixed by statute. Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the tabelcorrectiefactor (table correction factor), which is why the amount moves each year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Tax at the top of bracket 1

The fourth column of the art. 2.12 table states the tax at the top of bracket 1, € 16.866 for 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst uses this as the first step of the box 2 calculation and then adds the second-bracket tax on the part above the boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2).

### Box 2 bracket 2

The second bracket charges 31% on box 2 income above € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The second bracket itself was announced in Belastingplan 2023 and took effect on 1 January 2024, at 24,5% over the first € 67.000 per person and 33% above (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024). The 33% came from Belastingplan 2024: the amendement Van der Lee c.s. raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024. Belastingplan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). The bracket applies per taxpayer.

### Inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.12)

The income is reguliere voordelen (regular benefits) minus deductible costs plus vervreemdingsvoordelen (gains on disposal), minus persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Reguliere voordelen are dividends and other profit distributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as reguliere voordelen the forfaitair voordeel from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the verkrijgingsprijs or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from a beleggingsinstelling (sub e), and from 2023 the excessief-lenen fiction (sub f) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Interest on a receivable on the own company is not a box 2 voordeel but income from making assets available, and salary from the company is box 1 loon (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Deductible is the interest on a loan taken to buy the shares, notary costs are added to the verkrijgingsprijs and selling costs reduce the overdrachtsprijs, and dividendbelasting is not deducted but credited as voorheffing (withholding credit) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Aanmerkelijk belang test (art. 4.6)

A taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly meets one of six tests, each at 5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). (a) he holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) he has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) he holds winstbewijzen (profit-sharing certificates) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, (d) he is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a coöperatie as meant in art. 4.5a, (e) he has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 11 and art. 9 lid 1 sub e Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit, and (f) he has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and the 1 January 2022 texts and referred in the 2022 numbering to art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969, and in sub d the word "tot" became "om" in the same period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Sub f was added by Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect to 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). The partner counts, and the Belastingdienst states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from aanmerkelijk belang of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). A non-resident declares only an aanmerkelijk belang in a Netherlands company that is not part of an enterprise (art. 7.5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The statement that a 2025 beschikking partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht has the same effect is not in the checked sources, see section 7. Fiscal partners may divide the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang in the proportion they choose each year in the return, and FISIN 2026 states that with fiscal partnership for the whole year "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Meesleep, meetrek and fictief aanmerkelijk belang (art. 4.9 to 4.11)

The meesleepregeling (pull-along rule) of art. 4.9 says that once there is an aanmerkelijk belang in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and winstbewijzen in that same company also belong to it. The meetrekregeling (bring-along rule) of art. 4.10 says that shares in a company where the taxpayer has no aanmerkelijk belang but his partner or a blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn (blood relative or relative by marriage in the direct line) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to an aanmerkelijk belang. The Belastingdienst names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.11 gives a fictief aanmerkelijk belang (deemed substantial shareholding) for shares or winstbewijzen that do not belong to an aanmerkelijk belang under the other articles when art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a fictief aanmerkelijk belang. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Fictieve vervreemding (art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists the events that count as a disposal: inkoop of shares, afkoop and inkoop of winstbewijzen, liquidatie-uitkeringen, becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or a werkzaamheid, no longer having an aanmerkelijk belang, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a koopoptie, shares of an afgezonderd particulier vermogen no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a forfaitair voordeel under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 counts as a liquidation payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027.

### Verkrijgingsprijs and onzakelijke prijs

The vervreemdingsvoordeel is the overdrachtsprijs (transfer price) minus the verkrijgingsprijs (acquisition price). The verkrijgingsprijs is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Special cases are inheritance, a gift with or without doorschuiving (rollover), reaching 5% during the year, and an onzakelijke prijs (non-arm's-length price). At an onzakelijke prijs the value in het economisch verkeer (market value) applies to both sides of the transaction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The rollover rules for gift, inheritance and emigration are UNVERIFIED in the checked sources, see section 7.

### Forfaitair voordeel (art. 4.14)

Art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a treats a forfaitair voordeel (notional benefit) from a vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling or a foreign investment company as a regulier voordeel. Art. 4.14 calculates it as a yearly percentage of the value of those shares at the start of the year, reduced by other reguliere voordelen from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The percentage for 2026 is 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The moment the shares start to yield this forfaitair voordeel is itself a fictieve vervreemding under art. 4.16 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Excessief lenen maximum (art. 4.14a)

Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap", entered into force on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, "het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)", where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations, and the amount rule sits in art. 4.14a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Lid 1: a positive amount equals the total debts above the maximum. A negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. Lid 2: the maximum is € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier (the 2023 text printed € 700.000). Lid 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Lid 4: the debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Lid 5: if the taxpayer no longer has an aanmerkelijk belang at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount. Lid 6: an eigenwoningschuld is excluded to the extent a recht van hypotheek on the home was given to the company, and art. 10a.23 excludes an eigenwoningschuld existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage. Lid 7 was added by Belastingplan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023 and removes double counting of a samenwerkingsverband debt among several participants (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html). Art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5 leave out debts from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a verbonden person for a debt of a samenwerkingsverband (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the drempel "never drops below € 500.000" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). FISIN 2026 states the test as "op 31 december 2026 meer dan € 500.000" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The amount is not indexed, because art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 list (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Consequences: the loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays Vpb on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividendbelasting on the part taxed as box 2 income. Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold. A negative fictief regulier voordeel is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The threshold history: the bill proposed € 500.000, the derde nota van wijziging (Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced € 500.000 by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, 4.14b and 4.14c lid 1, and amendement Grinwis c.s. (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html).

### Excessief lenen, verbonden personen (art. 4.14b)

Debts of a verbonden person (blood or aanverwant in de rechte lijn of the taxpayer or partner) to a company where the taxpayer has an aanmerkelijk belang and that person has none count with the taxpayer for the part above € 500.000, applied to that person and his partner together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2023 text used € 700.000. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessief lenen, immigration (art. 4.14c)

A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, and at least € 500.000 from the 2024 text (at least € 700.000 in the 2023 text) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Gebruikelijk loon fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has an aanmerkelijk belang, the loon is at least the highest of three amounts: (a) the loon from the most comparable employment, which is 100% of that loon from the 2023 text (75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts), (b) the highest loon of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount, € 58.000 for 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst states the same figure: "ten minste € 58.000 in 2026" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). A lower loon is possible: if the inhoudingsplichtige makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable loon, the comparable loon applies, in the 2020 to 2022 texts with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable loon itself was lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which an aanmerkelijk belang plays no role and that is known to the inhoudingsplichtige and the inspecteur. The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation with the tabelcorrectiefactor (lid 7 from the 2023 text) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The start-up rule of the 2020 to 2022 texts is absent from the 2023 text. The Belastingdienst states that "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" and that it could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The gebruikelijk loon is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 voordeel, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the aanmerkelijkbelanghouder and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked sources do not state that the loon reduces the profit available for a dividend.

### Gebruikelijk loon minimum threshold

The rule does not apply if the loon for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 4 from the 2023 text, lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### ODV market rate (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The oudedagsverplichting (ODV, old-age obligation) arises when a pensioen in eigen beheer (PEB, pension in own management) was afgekocht or converted during the window from 1 April 2017 to 31 December 2019 (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/). The ODV is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011, the average of the monthly U-rendementen of the preceding year), and a negative market rate must also be applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01, https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). For 2026 the rate is 2,593%. Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025 replaces "'2,602%' door '2,593%'", so 2,602% was the 2025 rate (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). The ODV can be used geruisloos for a lijfrente (annuity), lijfrenterekening or lijfrentebeleggingsrecht, or is paid in instalments over twenty years as loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking, each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before AOW age and at the latest two months after it, an earlier start lengthens the twenty years, and on death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, allows conversion into a lijfrente after the instalments have started under ministerial conditions (art. 38p lid 5). Art. 10a.18 lid 2 values the ODV in the company with the market rate of art. 38p (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). A remaining PEB keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation, the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). Art. 10a.18 lid 3 keeps the 2017, 2018 and 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 out of the verkrijgingsprijs of the shares, and PEB payouts are loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking in box 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).

### Belastingkorting on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53)

Lid 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no aanmerkelijk belang in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted into a belastingkorting on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no aanmerkelijk belang at death. Lid 2: the rate is 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", so 24,5% in 2024 to 2027 and 24,5% in 2026. Lid 3: the inspecteur sets the korting by beschikking (decision) open to objection. Lid 4: the korting reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Lid 5: on the death of a partner part of the korting can pass to the surviving partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Belastingdienst adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without aanmerkelijk belang (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting). Kennisgroep standpunt KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new aanmerkelijk belang at the request does not block the conversion, and ambtshalve vermindering of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/). FISIN 2026 states "De korting is 26,9%". The statute decides at 24,5%, see section 7.

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The loss is set by beschikking together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment, and is open to objection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Belastingdienst states the loss can be carried "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### APV loss deferral term

For an afgezonderd particulier vermogen (APV, separated private asset) the loss is not offset immediately but after a sale to a third party, on death, or after 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang). The wider doorschuiving list is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### Inherited aanmerkelijk belang facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, reguliere voordelen received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the verkrijgingsprijs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessief-lenen fiction, which sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. The Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027 bill proposes to exclude the fictief regulier voordeel from this facility from 1 January 2027, proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

### Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 in force

Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) is in force on 1 January 2026. It repaired the excessief-lenen cross-reference "«zesde lid»" to "«vierde lid»" retroactively to 1 January 2023, not for 2024, and added art. 4.6 sub f retroactively to 1 January 2025. The € 500.000 maximum and the box 2 table are unchanged (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html).

### Sum of two partners' first brackets

The amount € 137.686 is the arithmetic sum of two times € 68.843. No primary page states it and the Belastingdienst box 2 page has no partner column (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2). What the sources do state is that the art. 2.12 table applies per taxpayer and that partners may divide the inkomsten uit aanmerkelijk belang in any proportion that totals 100% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). Treat € 137.686 as an illustration, not as a statutory amount, and see section 7.
