## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 2 bracket 1

The first bracket (eerste schijf) charges 24,5% on box 2 income up to € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). It applies to every taxpayer with income from a substantial interest (inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang), and art. 2.12 applies per taxpayer, so each fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) has his own bracket for his own share of the income (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The boundary is not fixed by statute. Art. 10.1 lid 1 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts that are replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor), which is why the amount moves each year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Tax at the top of bracket 1

The fourth column of the art. 2.12 table states the tax at the top of bracket 1, € 16.866 for 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) uses this as the first step of the box 2 calculation and then adds the second-bracket tax on the part above the boundary (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2).

### Box 2 bracket 2

The second bracket charges 31% on box 2 income above € 68.843 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The second bracket itself was announced in Tax Plan 2023 (Belastingplan 2023) and took effect on 1 January 2024, at 24,5% over the first € 67.000 per person and 33% above (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/12/20/belangrijkste-belastingwijzigingen-per-1-januari-2024). The 33% came from Tax Plan 2024: the Van der Lee c.s. amendment (amendement) raised the second-bracket rate by 2 percentage points to 33% from 1 January 2024. Tax Plan 2025 ("Terugdraaien verhoging box 2-tarief", Kamerstuk 36 602 nr. 3, section 5.2) lowered it by 2 points to 31% from 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html). The bracket applies per taxpayer.

### Income from a substantial interest (art. 4.12)

The income is regular benefits (reguliere voordelen) minus deductible costs (aftrekbare kosten) plus disposal gains (vervreemdingsvoordelen), minus personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Regular benefits are dividends and other profit distributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.13 lid 1 adds as regular benefits the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) from an exempt investment institution (vrijgestelde beleggingsinstelling) or a foreign investment company (sub a), certain repayments of paid-in capital above the acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) or without the required decision and statutory reduction (sub b to d), bonus shares from an investment institution (beleggingsinstelling) (sub e), and from 2023 the excessive borrowing (excessief lenen) fiction (sub f) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Interest on a receivable on the own company (eigen vennootschap) is not a box 2 benefit (voordeel) but income from making assets available (terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen), and salary from the company is box 1 wage (loon) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Deductible is the interest on a loan taken to buy the shares, notary costs are added to the acquisition price and selling costs reduce the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs), and dividend tax (dividendbelasting) is not deducted but credited as a prepayment (voorheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Substantial interest test (art. 4.6)

A taxpayer has a substantial interest if he, alone or together with his partner, directly or indirectly meets one of six tests, each at 5% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). (a) he holds at least 5% of the issued capital, (b) he has rights to acquire shares up to at least 5% of the issued capital, (c) he holds profit-sharing certificates (winstbewijzen) for at least 5% of the annual profit or of the liquidation proceeds, (d) he is entitled to at least 5% of the votes in a cooperative (coöperatie) as meant in art. 4.5a, (e) he has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 11 and art. 9 lid 1 sub e Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of its profit, and (f) he has a share in a body as meant in art. 2 lid 1 onderdeel h or art. 3 lid 2 Wet Vpb 1969 with at least 5% of profit, liquidation proceeds or voting rights (stemrechten) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Sub e was added between the 1 January 2021 and the 1 January 2022 texts and referred in the 2022 numbering to art. 2 lid 12 Wet Vpb 1969, and in sub d the word "tot" became "om" in the same period (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01). Sub f was added by Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2026, Stb. 2025, 445) with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2025 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html). The partner counts, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that the taxpayer must also declare the income from a substantial interest of minor children until the child turns 18 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). A non-resident declares only a substantial interest in a Netherlands company that is not part of an enterprise (art. 7.5, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The statement that a 2025 decision on partial non-resident taxpayer status (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) has the same effect is not in the checked sources, see section 7. Fiscal partners may divide the income from a substantial interest in the proportion they choose each year in the return, and FISIN 2026 states that with fiscal partnership for the whole year "Elke verdeling mag, als het totaal maar 100% is" (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Drag-along rule, carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) and deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) (art. 4.9 to 4.11)

The drag-along rule (meesleepregeling) of art. 4.9 says that once there is a substantial interest in a company, the taxpayer's other shares and profit-sharing certificates in that same company also belong to it. The carry-along rule (meetrekregeling) of art. 4.10 says that shares in a company where the taxpayer has no substantial interest but his partner or a blood relative or relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner does, also belong to a substantial interest. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration names parents, children and grandchildren of the taxpayer and the fiscal partner, plus their fiscal partners, and says a 5% holding in one class of shares also counts (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang/wanneer_hebt_u_een_aanmerkelijk_belang). Art. 4.11 gives a deemed substantial interest (fictief aanmerkelijk belang) for shares or profit-sharing certificates that do not belong to a substantial interest under the other articles when art. 3.65, 4.17, 4.17a, 4.17b, 4.17c, 4.40 or 4.41 has been applied. Art. 4.40 is the route when a holding drops below 5%: on request the deemed sale of art. 4.16 lid 1 sub g is not applied to the shares the taxpayer keeps, and those shares then carry a deemed substantial interest. Art. 4.16 lid 3 lets the taxpayer request a deemed sale of such shares (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Deemed disposal (fictieve vervreemding, art. 4.16)

Art. 4.16 lists the events that count as a disposal: a purchase (inkoop) of shares, a commutation (afkoop) and purchase of profit-sharing certificates, liquidation distributions (liquidatie-uitkeringen), becoming a shareholder in another company by a transfer under general title, transfer under general title and by inheritance under special title, bringing the shares into an enterprise or an activity (werkzaamheid), no longer having a substantial interest, ceasing to be a resident other than by death (emigration), granting a purchase option (koopoptie), shares of a segregated private asset (afgezonderd particulier vermogen) no longer being attributed, and the shares starting to yield a flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). A liquidation with continuation under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 counts as a liquidation payment (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027.

### Acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) and non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs)

The disposal gain (vervreemdingsvoordeel) is the transfer price minus the acquisition price. The acquisition price is the price paid or the value at acquisition, with notary costs included (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). Special cases are inheritance, a gift with or without a rollover (doorschuiving), reaching 5% during the year, and a non-arm's-length price (onzakelijke prijs). At a non-arm's-length price the market value (waarde in het economisch verkeer) applies to both sides of the transaction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The rollover rules for gift, inheritance and emigration are UNVERIFIED in the checked sources, see section 7.

### Flat-rate benefit (art. 4.14)

Art. 4.13 lid 1 sub a treats a flat-rate benefit from an exempt investment institution or a foreign investment company as a regular benefit. Art. 4.14 calculates it as a yearly percentage of the value of those shares at the start of the year, reduced by other regular benefits from those shares, never below nil, and time-apportioned (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The percentage for 2026 is 6% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The moment the shares start to yield this flat-rate benefit is itself a deemed disposal under art. 4.16 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Excessive borrowing maximum (art. 4.14a)

Wet van 21 december 2022, Staatsblad 2022, 531, cited as "Wet excessief lenen bij eigen vennootschap", entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 January 2023 (Artikel III) (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The fiction sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f, "het bovenmatige deel van schulden ... (het fictief reguliere voordeel)", where debts means all civil-law debt relations and obligations, and the amount rule sits in art. 4.14a (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01). Subsection 1: a positive amount equals the total debts above the maximum. A negative amount equals the shortfall below the maximum, capped at amounts taxed earlier (or under a comparable foreign rule) and not yet reversed. Subsection 2: the maximum is € 500.000 from the 2024 text, plus amounts taxed earlier (the 2023 text printed € 700.000). Subsection 3: debts of the taxpayer, the partner or both count once for the two together and the maximum applies to them jointly. Subsection 4: the debts are measured at the end of the calendar year at nominal value. Subsection 5: if the taxpayer no longer has a substantial interest at year end, other than by emigration under art. 4.16 lid 1 sub h, lid 1 is applied as if he still had one and the debts were nil, which releases a negative amount. Subsection 6: an owner-occupied home (eigen woning) debt (eigenwoningschuld) is excluded to the extent a right of mortgage (recht van hypotheek) on the home was given to the company, and art. 10a.23 excludes an owner-occupied home debt existing on 31 December 2022 without such a mortgage. Subsection 7 was added by Tax Plan 2025 (Stb. 2024, 434) retroactive to 1 January 2023 and removes double counting of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) debt among several participants (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-434.html). Art. 4.13 lid 4 and 5 leave out debts from a civil-law joint liability of the taxpayer or a related person (verbonden persoon) for a debt of a partnership (vof, cv, maatschap or a comparable foreign form) from which they derive business profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that receivables on the company do not reduce the debts, that debts at several companies are added, and that the threshold (drempel) "never drops below € 500.000" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt). FISIN 2026 states the test as "op 31 december 2026 meer dan € 500.000" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The amount is not indexed, because art. 4.14a is not on the art. 10.1 lid 1 list (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Consequences: the loan stays a loan in full, the company keeps charging interest on the whole loan and pays corporate income tax (Vpb) on it, the other terms stay, and the company does not withhold dividend tax on the part taxed as box 2 income. Existing arrangements on company debts can lapse when the total exceeds the threshold. A negative deemed regular benefit (fictief regulier voordeel) is offset against box 2 income of that year, the previous year or the following six years in the income tax (inkomstenbelasting) return (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/bijzondere_situaties/geld_lenen_van_uw_bv/excessief-lenen-van-bv-beperkt, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). The threshold history: the bill proposed € 500.000, the third memorandum of amendment (derde nota van wijziging, Kamerstuk 35 496 nr. 15) replaced € 500.000 by € 700.000 in art. 4.14a lid 2, 4.14b and 4.14c lid 1, and the Grinwis c.s. amendment (Kamerstuk 36 418 nr. 60), included in the adopted text (nr. A), replaced € 700.000 by € 500.000 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35496-15.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-60.html, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36418-A.html).

### Excessive borrowing, related persons (verbonden personen) (art. 4.14b)

Debts of a related person (verbonden persoon), meaning a blood relative or a relative by marriage (aanverwant) in the direct line (rechte lijn) of the taxpayer or partner, to a company where the taxpayer has a substantial interest and that person has none are counted together with the taxpayer's debts for the part above € 500.000, with the threshold applied to that related person and the person's partner together (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The 2023 text used € 700.000. Debts already counted under art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f for the taxpayer and partner, and debts on which a regular box 2 benefit was taken, are excluded. If several taxpayers count the same debts, they share them equally (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01).

### Excessive borrowing, immigration (art. 4.14c)

A taxpayer who comes to live in the Netherlands gets a maximum equal to his debts at that moment, and at least € 500.000 from the 2024 text (at least € 700.000 in the 2023 text) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01).

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) fixed amount (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for a body in which he or his partner has a substantial interest, the wage (loon) is at least the highest of three amounts: (a) the wage from the most comparable employment relationship (loon uit de meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), which is 100% of that wage from the 2023 text (75% in the 2020 to 2022 texts), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the body or of connected bodies, and (c) a fixed amount, € 58.000 for 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the same figure: "ten minste € 58.000 in 2026" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). A lower wage is possible: if the withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) makes plausible that the highest amount exceeds the (75% of the) comparable wage, the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) applies, in the 2020 to 2022 texts with the fixed amount as a floor unless the comparable wage itself was lower (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2020-01-01). The comparable employment is one in which a substantial interest plays no role and that is known to the withholding agent and the tax inspector (inspecteur). The fixed amount is indexed at the start of each year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) with the table correction factor (lid 7 from the 2023 text) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The start-up rule of the 2020 to 2022 texts is absent from the 2023 text. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that "Vanaf 2023 is deze uitzondering voor nieuwe gevallen vervallen" and that it could be used at most 3 years, so a first use in 2021 ran to 2023 and a first use in 2022 ran to 2024 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang). The customary salary is declared as income from employment in box 1, not as a box 2 benefit, and the rule also applies when the fiscal partner or a minor child is the substantial interest holder (aanmerkelijkbelanghouder) and the taxpayer makes assets available to that company (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/inkomsten_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang). The checked sources do not state that the wage reduces the profit available for a dividend.

### Customary salary minimum threshold

The rule does not apply if the wage for the work for the body and connected bodies is not more than € 5.000 in the year (lid 4 from the 2023 text, lid 6 in the 2020 to 2022 texts) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the € 5.000 applies to the total of the work for all companies, "niet per onderneming" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang).

### Old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting) market rate (marktrente) (art. 38p Wet LB 1964)

The old-age obligation (oudedagsverplichting, ODV) arises when a pension in own management (pensioen in eigen beheer, PEB) was commuted or converted during the window from 1 April 2017 to 31 December 2019 (https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/va_17-007_v170401/). The ODV is increased each year by a market rate set by ministerial regulation (art. 12.3a of the Wage Tax Implementing Regulation 2011 (Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011), the average of the monthly U-rendement rates of the preceding year), and a negative market rate must also be applied (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01, https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-u-rendementen-en-marktrenten/). For 2026 the rate is 2,593%. Stcrt. 2025, 42873 of 24 December 2025 replaces "'2,602%' door '2,593%'", so 2,602% was the 2025 rate (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42873.html). The ODV can be used on a tax-neutral basis (geruisloos) for an annuity (lijfrente), an annuity savings account (lijfrenterekening) or an annuity investment right (lijfrentebeleggingsrecht), or is paid in instalments over twenty years as income from former employment (loon uit vroegere dienstbetrekking), each instalment being the balance at the start of the year divided by the remaining years. The first instalment comes no earlier than five years before state pension (AOW) age and at the latest two months after it, an earlier start lengthens the twenty years, and on death before the start the instalments begin within twelve months for the natural-person heirs (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01). Stb. 2022, 532, retroactive to 1 January 2020, allows conversion into an annuity after the instalments have started under ministerial conditions (art. 38p lid 5). Art. 10a.18 lid 2 values the ODV in the company with the market rate of art. 38p (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). A remaining PEB keeps the old rules of art. 38n. If the scheme provided for indexation (indexatie), the company must index the accrued rights every year and must value the provision every year actuarially. "Als de bv niet jaarlijks indexeert, dan kunnen wij dit zien als een vorm van prijsgeven van de rechten", with possible fiscal consequences (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitfaseren-pensioen-in-eigen-beheer/). Art. 10a.18 lid 3 keeps the 2017, 2018 and 2019 application of art. 38n lid 2 out of the acquisition price of the shares, and PEB payouts are income from former employment in box 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2017-115.html).

### Tax credit (belastingkorting, heffingskorting) on an unoffset loss (art. 4.53)

Subsection 1: if the taxpayer and partner had no substantial interest in the calendar year and the year before, the unoffset loss is converted into a tax credit (belastingkorting) on request. A taxpayer without a partner who died in the year only needs to have had no substantial interest at death. Subsection 2: the rate is 26,25% in the 2020 text, 26,9% in the 2021 to 2023 texts, and from the 2024 text "het in de eerste regel van de vierde kolom van de tabel in artikel 2.12 opgenomen percentage", so 24,5% in 2024 to 2027 and 24,5% in 2026. Subsection 3: the inspector sets the credit by decision (beschikking) open to objection. Subsection 4: the credit reduces box 1 tax of that year and the seven following years, at the latest the ninth year after the loss year. Subsection 5: on the death of a partner part of the credit can pass to the surviving partner (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the request is possible only after the loss is definitively established and from the second year after the year without a substantial interest (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_omzetten_in_belastingkorting). Knowledge group (kennisgroep) position (kennisgroepstandpunt) KG:003:2023:8 (published 26-09-2023, updated 22-07-2026) tests the condition for the tax year, not at the request date, so a new substantial interest at the request does not block the conversion, and an ex officio reduction (ambtshalve vermindering) of earlier assessments is possible unless five years have passed after the year of the assessment (https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg00320238-omzetting-verlies-uit-aanmerkelijk-belang-in-een-belastingkorting/). FISIN 2026 states "De korting is 26,9%". The statute decides at 24,5%, see section 7.

### Loss offset period (art. 4.49)

A loss from a substantial interest (verlies uit aanmerkelijk belang) is offset against box 2 income of the previous year and the six following years, in the order in which the losses arose (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The loss is set by decision together with the assessment, is shown separately on the assessment, and is open to objection (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### Losses that arose in 2018 or earlier

For a loss that arose in 2018 or earlier the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states the loss can be carried "langer te verrekenen ... in de komende 9 jaar" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang/verlies_uit_aanmerkelijk_belang_1).

### Loss deferral term for segregated private assets (privévermogen, APV)

For a segregated private asset (afgezonderd particulier vermogen, APV) the loss is not offset immediately but after a sale to a third party, on death, or after 10 years. FISIN 2020 confirms the 10-year term (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/aanmerkelijk_belang). The wider rollover list is UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### Inherited substantial interest facility (art. 4.12a)

If shares were inherited and box 2 income was taken into account with the deceased on that transfer, regular benefits received within 24 months after the death are on request not counted as box 2 income, up to that amount, and are written off on the acquisition price (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Art. 4.12a does not carry the excessive borrowing fiction, which sits in art. 4.13 lid 1 sub f with art. 4.14a to 4.14c (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-531.html). The text is identical in 2020 to 2027. The Other Fiscal Measures 2027 (Overige fiscale maatregelen 2027) bill proposes to exclude the deemed regular benefit from this facility from 1 January 2027, proposed and not adopted on 2026-10-11 (https://www.eerstekamer.nl/behandeling/20260915/memorie_van_toelichting_56/document3/f=/vn15lhsbrhzz.pdf).

### Other Fiscal Measures 2026 in force (in werking)

Other Fiscal Measures 2026 (Stb. 2025, 445) is in force on 1 January 2026. It repaired the excessive borrowing cross-reference "«zesde lid»" to "«vierde lid»" retroactively to 1 January 2023, not for 2024, and added art. 4.6 sub f retroactively to 1 January 2025. The € 500.000 maximum and the box 2 table are unchanged (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-445.html).

### Sum of two partners' first brackets

The amount € 137.686 is the arithmetic sum of two times € 68.843. No primary page states it and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration box 2 page has no partner column (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2). What the sources do state is that the art. 2.12 table applies per taxpayer and that partners may divide the income from a substantial interest in any proportion that totals 100% (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). Treat € 137.686 as an illustration, not as a statutory amount, and see section 7.
