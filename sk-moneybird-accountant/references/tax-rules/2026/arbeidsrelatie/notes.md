## 5. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA boetes: in 2025 there were no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties (motie Flach/Aartsen, Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266). In 2026 there are no verzuimboetes, but vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. For correcties over 2025 the 2025 regime stays. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Start of control: in 2025 the control started in principle with a bedrijfsbezoek, possibly a waarschuwing that was not an official instrument like the aanwijzing, and a boekenonderzoek remained possible with large risks. In 2026 the control starts in principle with a bedrijfsbezoek and the starting point to examine only the most recent aangiftetijdvak in a boekenonderzoek lapses. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Regular handhaving: the tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Belastingdienst aims to integrate from 2027. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- NLA signals: in 2025 NLA signals were expected to be picked up in 2025 through the GKT-meldingenproces. In 2026 NLA meldingen are usable in the handhaving. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR procedure: in 2025 the bill was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. In 2026 the Tweede Kamer adopted it on 21 April 2026, the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026, the act is the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, and entry into force is 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207. Sources: [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer 36 783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783), [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html).
- VBAR verduidelijking: the bill as introduced in 2025 included a verduidelijking part. The nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed it. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783).
- VBAR hourly amount: the statutory base is € 36 at peildatum 1 January 2025. The indexed amount is € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- WML: € 14,06 at 1 January 2025. € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. € 14,99 from 1 July 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- Zelfstandigenwet: new in 2026. Internet consultation open until 29 October 2026, beoogde inwerkingtreding January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- BAZ: new bill in 2026. Ingediend 24 March 2026, inbreng 27 May 2026, verslag published 29 May 2026, awaiting the nota on 2026-10-11. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Belastingrente: 6,5% in 2025 to 5% from 1 January 2026 for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen. Source: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Handhavingsplan: the 2025 plan was replaced by the Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026, versie 2.0 of 31 December 2025, after moties of 18 December 2025 and the Kamerbrief of 19 December 2025. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record how the work is actually carried out, not only what the contract says. An approved modelovereenkomst gives no loondienst only if the parties work as described. Source: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- Keep the written opt-out for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten before the first payment. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. Source: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- For a BV as opdrachtgever, if the relationship is loondienst, process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte. A correction can give a naheffingsaanslag loonheffingen, payable 14 days after the date. Sources: [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).
- DBA boetes 2026: no verzuimboetes, but vergrijpboetes are possible from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. For correcties over 2025 there are no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Ingroeimodel: corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing not followed, then up to 5 years. Full 5 years only from 2030. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Doorwerking: after LH corrections at the opdrachtgever the Belastingdienst in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the opdrachtnemer aimed at bronkwalificatie. Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- For the sole trader, not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. Keep records of rate, hours, agreements and invoices. The VBAR presumption puts the proof on the opdrachtgever if the rate is at or below the amount. Sources: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).
- Retention: keep the administratie for 7 years under AWR art. 52 lid 4. The Belastingdienst table says 10 years for onroerende zaken and One Stop Shop data, and 7 years or shorter by agreement for other data. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Source: [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Registration: register the onderneming with KVK within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Sources: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 art. 20 lid 1), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [Belastingdienst inschrijven](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- If an opdrachtnemer invokes the VBAR presumption, the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. Failing that, employee rights such as loondoorbetaling bij ziekte and ontslagbescherming apply. Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).
- Belastingrente: the rate for IB and btw is 5% in 2026. If a correction leads to an aanslag that deviates from the return, belastingrente can run from 1 July after the tax year. Source: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- VBAR first-application regulation: UNVERIFIED. The ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 was not found on 2026-10-11. It would settle the exact figure for 31 December 2026. See the calculated example in section 4 for an illustration based on the WML of € 14,99. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- BAZ waiting period: 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary. UNVERIFIED. Stays open until the bill text or the nota is read. Sources: [Tweede Kamer document, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html), [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Case law date: 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. UNVERIFIED. Sources: [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
- VBAR hourly amount € 36 against € 38: settled. € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 1 January 2025 (unrounded € 35,43). € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- Regular DBA handhaving start date: settled. 1 January 2026 in the tranche 2025 plan, moved to an ambition for 1 January 2027 in the 2026 plan. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Staatsblad 2016, 165: corrected. It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 on the opt-out for some fictieve dienstbetrekkingen. The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016, in force 1 May 2016. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
- BAZ verslag date 27 May against 29 May 2026: settled. Inbreng delivered 27 May, verslag (36 912, nr. 5) published 29 May. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Zelfstandigenwet: proposed. Consultation to 29 October 2026, intended January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- BAZ: proposed. Awaiting the nota on 2026-10-11, no debate and no vote. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- VBAR: adopted-not-yet-in-force. Entry into force 31 December 2026. Source: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html).
