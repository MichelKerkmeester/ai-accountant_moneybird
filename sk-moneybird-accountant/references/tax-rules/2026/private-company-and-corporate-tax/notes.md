## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Vpb rates: unchanged. Both 2025 and 2026 tax the first € 200.000 at 19% and the part above it at € 38.000 plus 25,8%, and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) prints 19,0% and 25,8% for both years. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01 (art. 22), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 22), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking): unchanged at 24,5% with a € 1.000.000 threshold, and non-deductible interest still carries forward. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01 (art. 15b), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15b).
- Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening): unchanged from 2025, € 1.000.000 plus 50% per year, one year back (één jaar terug) and unlimited forward. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01 (art. 20), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 20).
- Innovation box (innovatiebox): unchanged at 9/H with an effective 9% and a flat rate (forfait) of 25% with a € 25.000 maximum. The Innovation Box Decree 2025 (Innovatieboxbesluit 2025), BWBR0051317, has been in force (in werking) since 25 July 2025 and applies in 2026, with a cost-related markup that is usually 8% to 15% of costs and the box open after an art. 3.65 conversion. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01 (art. 12b, art. 12bd), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12b, art. 12bd), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051317/.
- Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) at 5% and the fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) ownership requirement (bezitseis) of at least 95%: unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2025-01-01 (art. 13, art. 15), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 13, art. 15).
- Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) standard amount (normbedrag): € 56.000 in 2025 to € 58.000 in 2026. The comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) stays at 100% and the floor stays € 5.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2025-01-01 (art. 12a), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a), https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Start-up relief: the transitional right had already lapsed for all starters from 2025, so 2026 has no start-up use of the old art. 12a lid 3 and 4 regime. Source: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw): 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026. Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 75.864 to € 79.409. Employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw: 6,51% to 6,10%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- Zvw route (zvw-route) for the non-insured director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA): the 2025 Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) has the BV withholding the contribution (bijdrage) on net wage (nettoloon) and paying it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen), instead of an assessment (aanslag) for the director and substantial shareholder. This correction carries into 2026, see section 7. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- Box 2: first bracket (eerste schijf) € 67.804 in 2025 to € 68.843 in 2026, and the column III amount € 16.611 to € 16.866. The percentages 24,5% and 31% are unchanged. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 2.12), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 2.12).
- Dividend tax (dividendbelasting): 15% in both years. The Dividend Tax Consolidated Decree 2025 (Verzamelbesluit dividendbelasting 2025) of 7 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 34752, replaces the 2022 decision and applies in 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 5), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.
- Dividend stripping article: corrected to art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, readable in the dated texts, with the 2024 text adding "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7).
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): 25,8% in both years. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.
- Decree (besluit) tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting): the citation is Stcrt. 2025, 37092, published 4 November 2025, not Stcrt. 2025, 3709. The decree of 24 October 2025 replaced the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M, and is in force the day after publication, so 2026 requests follow it. It adds a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000, and the Zvw contribution (bijdrage) credit. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-37092.html.
- VAT fiscal unity (btw-eenheid) policy decree (beleidsbesluit): the Policy decree tax liability (belastingplicht) and fiscal unity turnover tax (omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, has been in force since 1 July 2025 and applies in 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening) thresholds: unchanged from 2024 and 2025 at € 450.000 and € 900.000, € 7,5 million and € 15 million, € 25 million and € 50 million. The Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) figure of more than € 51 mln for the large class (grote klasse) conflicts with Stb. 2024, 52 and Boek 2 BW art. 397 and is settled at € 50 million. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 396 and 397), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01 (art. 395a, 396 and 397), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.
- SBR deposit: from financial year (boekjaar) 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR, so the 2026 deposit season is the first full SBR season. Source: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/.
- Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek): € 3.630 in both years. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.79), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.79).
- Filing season (aangifteseizoen): the Vpb return for book year 2025 is due before 1 June 2026, with an online extension to 1 November, requested from 1 March to 1 June. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Return (aangifte) Vpb: record the return for book year 2025 before 1 June 2026, or request the online extension between 1 March and 1 June, which runs to 1 November. A broken book year is filed within 5 months after its end, with a paper extension request if needed. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.
- Payroll records (loonadministratie): run the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) wage through the payroll records and withhold payroll taxes (loonheffing). Record the customary salary (gebruikelijk loon) test against € 58.000 and the comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon), and use the € 5.000 floor only when all work in the entity and related entities (verbonden lichamen) stays at or below it. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a), https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.
- Zvw: for a non-insured director and substantial shareholder record the 4,85% contribution (bijdrage) on net wage (nettoloon) up to € 79.409 and pay it through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). For an insured director and substantial shareholder (verzekerde dga) record the 6,10% employer levy (werkgeversheffing) paid by the BV up to € 79.409. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- Dividend: record the dividend statement (dividendnota) when the proceeds (opbrengst) is paid, credited, settled or handed over, withhold 15% dividend tax (dividendbelasting), file and pay within 1 month after the dividend is made available, and credit the withheld tax in the return Vpb. A fully exempt participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder usually needs no return unless the company is invited to file. A qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) triggers a return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert.
- Annual accounts (jaarrekening): draw up the accounts within the drawing-up term, deposit them within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends, and file through SBR from financial year (boekjaar) 2025 onward. Late filing is a criminal offence (misdrijf) and there is no extension. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 394 Boek 2 BW).
- Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht): keep the records for seven years (art. 52 AWR), for nine years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) (art. 34a Wet OB 1968) and for ten years for OSS records (art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968). Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and profit and loss statement (resultatenrekening). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 (art. 52 AWR), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01 (art. 34a, 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968).
- Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid): the subsidiary (dochter) keeps existing but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below the 95% ownership requirement (bezitseis) ends the fiscal unity at that moment. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/.
- Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting): record 25,8% on in-scope interest and royalties to a related entity (verbonden lichaam) in a low-tax jurisdiction (laagbelastende jurisdictie), and on in-scope dividends since the 2024 version of the Withholding Tax Act 2021 (Wet bronbelasting 2021). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 (art. 4.1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2026 was not read, so the 2026 Zvw withholding route for a non-insured director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) rests on the Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 (paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1) and on the 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) table. A Payroll Taxes Handbook 2026 would settle it. Sources: https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/.
- The 2026-01-01 URL of the Dividend Tax (dividendbelasting) Act 1965 (Wet DB 1965) returns 404. The 15% rate is read from the version applicable from 01-01-2025 to the present. A consolidated 2026-01-01 text would settle it. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01.
- The Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969) text valid on 2026-01-01 reads "Geldend van 01-01-2026 t/m heden" and announces "Toekomstige wijziging(en) voorzien met ingang van: 01-01-2029". Art. 22 carries a note that changes without an entry date exist. The 2027-01-01 URL of the Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969) returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-11. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027, bill 37 022) is proposed and not enacted, it changes art. 12bd, art. 13 lid 7 and adds art. 34j in Article XIX, and it deletes ", 3.11" from art. 8 lid 1 from 1 January 2029 in Article XX. It does not touch art. 22, art. 15 or art. 15b. Publication of the entry dates in a later consolidated text would settle the picture. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html.
- The VAT cost-sharing exemption (koepelvrijstelling) and the BUA are unknown in the checked sources, which mark them UNVERIFIED and not opened, with the policy decree (beleidsbesluit) as the only URL. Reading the decree (besluit) would settle it. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38545.html.
- The checked sources found no basis for retention periods (bewaartermijnen) longer than seven years besides the VAT (btw) terms, nine years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) under art. 34a Wet OB 1968 and ten years for OSS under art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01.
