## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Corporate income tax rate (vpb-tarief), first bracket (eerste schijf)

A BV is subject to Vpb on its taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag). Under art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969 the tax is the column III amount plus the column IV percentage of the part of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) above the column I amount. For 2026 the first bracket runs up to and including € 200.000 at 19%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rates (tarieven) page prints 19,0% and 25,8% and states the same rates for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 22), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/tarieven_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Corporate income tax rate, above the first bracket

For the part of the taxable amount (belastbaar bedrag) above € 200.000 the tax is € 38.000 plus 25,8% of that part. The € 38.000 is the fixed column III amount and equals 19% of the first € 200.000. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 22). If a book year starts before and ends on or after the date a change of art. 22 takes effect, art. 31 Wet Vpb 1969 spreads the tax as (X/B x To) + (Y/B x Tn), with X the days before the change, Y the days from the change, B all days of the year, To the tax under the old art. 22 and Tn the tax under the new art. 22. No change of art. 22 is enacted for 2026, so this spread does not arise for a 2026 book year. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 31), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 31).

### Generic interest deduction limitation (generieke renteaftrekbeperking) (art. 15b)

Under art. 15b lid 1 the balance of interest (saldo aan renten), meaning interest costs on loans minus interest income, is not deductible in so far as it exceeds the higher of a percentage of the adjusted profit (gecorrigeerde winst) and € 1.000.000. The percentage in the 2026 text is 24,5%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page for the 2026 changes states 24,5% and notes that non-deductible interest carries forward. The taxpayer tests both legs and uses the higher one, so a balance at or below € 1.000.000 is not limited at all, and a balance above 24,5% of the adjusted profit is limited only where that percentage leg is itself above € 1.000.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15b lid 1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) (art. 20)

From 2022 a loss is set off against the profit of the preceding year and after that carried forward without a time limit. Per year the set-off is limited to € 1.000.000 plus 50% of the profit above € 1.000.000. Set-off follows the order in which the losses arose. The 2020 and 2021 texts were different, a loss was then set off against the profit of the preceding year and the six following years, after the inspector fixed it by decision (beschikking). For a 2026 book year only the one-year-back and unlimited-forward system with the € 1.000.000 plus 50% cap applies. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 20), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2021-01-01 (art. 20), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2022-01-01 (art. 20).

### Innovation box (innovatiebox), fraction (breuk) (art. 12b)

Under art. 12b to 12bd Wet Vpb 1969 qualifying advantages from a self-made qualifying intangible asset count for 9/H, where H is the highest art. 22 rate. With H at 25,8% this gives an effective rate of 9%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states "Het effectieve tarief van de vennootschapsbelasting voor deze winsten is 9%". A smaller taxpayer needs only an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) for the R&D. A taxpayer who is not smaller also needs an access ticket of art. 12ba lid 1 sub b. Trademarks, logos and comparable assets are excluded (art. 12ba lid 6). Innovation losses are deductible at the normal rate. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12b), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/innovatiebox/innovatiebox.

### Innovation box, flat rate (forfait) (art. 12bd)

A taxpayer who made a qualifying asset in the year or in the two preceding years and chooses the flat rate applies 25% of the profit before art. 12b, with a maximum of € 25.000. The flat rate is only available when that profit is positive. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12bd).

### Innovation box, smaller taxpayer (kleinere belastingplichtige) (art. 12ba lid 2)

A smaller taxpayer is one whose advantages plus acquisition costs from research and development (S&O) assets in the year and the four preceding years stay below € 37.500.000 and whose net turnover over those five years stays below € 250.000.000. A smaller taxpayer needs only the R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) and no access ticket. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12ba lid 2). Where the taxpayer does not use the flat rate, art. 12bb limits the box to K/T of the advantage, with K the qualifying expenditure multiplied by 1,3, capped at the whole advantage after the threshold (drempel) of art. 12bc. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 12bb, art. 12bc).

### Participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling) (art. 13)

Art. 13 lid 1 leaves advantages from a participation (deelneming) out of the profit. A participation exists from 5% of the nominal paid-up capital (art. 13 lid 2). The threshold is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. In a holding structure this matters twice, the profit on the shares stays untaxed at the level of the holder, and a participation dividend (deelnemingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder is usually exempt from dividend tax (dividendbelasting) withholding, see the dividend tax subsections below. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 13), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 13).

### Fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) (art. 15)

The parent company (moeder) must hold the full legal and economic ownership of at least 95% of the shares in the nominal paid-up capital of the subsidiary (dochter), carrying at least 95% of the statutory voting rights and a right to at least 95% of the profit and of the assets. On the request of both, tax is levied as if there is one taxpayer. The wording is identical in every text from 2020 to 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the mother is a bv, nv, mutual guarantee company (onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij), cooperative (coöperatie), or foundation (stichting) or association (vereniging) acting as housing corporation (woningcorporatie), or a comparable foreign form, that the subsidiary is a bv or nv, that both have the same book years and profit rules and that both are effectively established in the Netherlands. The subsidiary remains in existence but files no separate return, and losses of one company offset profits of another. A drop below 95% ends the fiscal unity at that moment, also when an option holder must offer the shares to the parent company at once. Subjective exemption of one subsidiary ends it for that subsidiary only (art. 15 lid 10). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 15), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2020-01-01 (art. 15), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-11814.html.

### Customary salary (gebruikelijk loon), standard amount (normbedrag) (art. 12a Wet LB 1964)

For an employee who works for an entity in which he or his partner holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang), the wage is at least the highest of (a) the wage (loon) from the most comparable employment relationship (meest vergelijkbare dienstbetrekking), (b) the highest wage of the employees of the entity or of related entities, (c) the standard amount. The dated 2026-01-01 text of the Wage Tax Act 1964 (Wet LB 1964) prints a standard amount of € 58.000, matching the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page. The standard amount is replaced at the start of each calendar year by ministerial regulation, multiplied by the table correction factor (tabelcorrectiefactor) of art. 10.2 Wet IB 2001 (lid 9 in 2020 to 2022, lid 7 from 2023). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang.

### Customary salary, comparable wage (vergelijkbaar loon) and escape

Leg (a) is 100% of the comparable wage from 2023, and was 75% in 2020 to 2022. The escape in art. 12a lid 2 reads, from 2023, that if the highest amount exceeds the comparable wage, the wage is the comparable wage. The withholding agent (inhoudingsplichtige) must make that plausible. The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 adds that the test wage is the wage for wage tax (loonbelasting) and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) including the car private-use addition (bijtelling), that a connected company is one with a 1/3 interest either way or through a common third party, and that the rule applies the same way to the partner and to working relatives in the direct line who hold shares. A settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst) made before 2023 on the old 75% basis stays valid for its term, with the 100% wage. A starting company that falls outside the start-up relief may set a lower wage for at most 3 years when it cannot pay the usual wage, starting from the statutory minimum wage (WML) for the hours worked, and those 3 years are cut by the period the business ran as a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak). A loss-making company may set a lower wage only when continuity is at risk. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a lid 1 and lid 2), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2023-01-01 (art. 12a lid 2), https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Customary salary, lower limit (ondergrens)

The rule does not apply when the wage for all work in the entity and related entities is not more than € 5.000. That floor sits in lid 6 in the 2020 text and is present in every year. A director and substantial shareholder (DGA) who takes only a small wage from all the entities together therefore stays outside the minimum. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01 (art. 12a lid 6).

### Zvw contribution (bijdrage Zvw), non-insured DGA (niet-verzekerde DGA)

A director and substantial shareholder is employed by (in dienst van) his own company for wage tax, national insurance contributions and Zvw, so the BV runs his wage through its payroll records (loonadministratie) and withholds payroll taxes (loonheffing). A director and substantial shareholder who alone or with a spouse and relatives up to the third degree holding at least 2/3 of the votes, or through an entity, can decide on his own dismissal, or who holds an equal share with all fellow directors, is not insured for the employee insurance schemes (werknemersverzekeringen). For him the company pays no premiums (premies) for employee insurance schemes and no employer levy (werkgeversheffing) Zvw. Instead the income-related Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zvw) is levied up to the maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) and paid through the payroll tax return (aangifte loonheffingen). The 2026 contribution (bijdrage) is 4,85%. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says the uninsured director and substantial shareholder pays the contribution (bijdrage) himself, collected by withholding (inhouding) on net wage (nettoloon) or by assessment (aanslag). The Payroll Taxes Handbook (Handboek Loonheffingen) 2025 describes the withholding on net wage by the BV (paragraphs 8.2.2 and 18.1.1). The Handbook (Handboek) for 2026 was not read, see section 7. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet/hoe_wordt_de_inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zvw_betaald/tabel_werkgeversheffing_zvw_of_bijdrage_zvw/ik_ben_directeur_grootaandeelhouder/, https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t51d.pdf.

### Maximum contribution income Zvw

The maximum contribution income for 2026 is € 79.409. The contribution (bijdrage) and the employer levy are levied up to this maximum, so a wage above it does not increase the contribution. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet. Each FISIN page says director and substantial shareholder wage falls under the employer levy only if he is insured for the employee insurance schemes.

### Employer levy Zvw, insured director and substantial shareholder (verzekerde DGA)

A director and substantial shareholder who is insured for the employee insurance schemes falls under the employer levy Zvw paid by the BV. For 2026 the percentage is 6,10%, up to the maximum contribution income of € 79.409. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw.

### Box 2, rate (tarief)

For the director and substantial shareholder as a shareholder, dividends and gains on the shares are taxed in box 2 of income tax (inkomstenbelasting), the income from a substantial interest. For 2026 the first bracket runs to € 68.843 at 24,5%, and above it the tax is € 16.866 plus 31% of the part above € 68.843 (art. 2.12 Wet IB 2001). Art. 10.1 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 lists art. 2.12 among the amounts indexed each year with the table correction factor. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 2.12 and art. 10.1 lid 1).

### Dividend tax

Art. 5 Wet DB 1965 sets the tax at 15% of the proceeds (opbrengst) in every version read, including the version applicable from 1 January 2025 that is still current, because the 2026-01-01 URL returns 404. The shareholder is the taxpayer and the company withholds (art. 1). If the company bears the tax, the proceeds are multiplied by 100/85 (art. 6 lid 1). Once the proceeds are paid, credited, settled or handed over, a dividend statement (dividendnota) is due (art. 9 lid 1). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states that dividends paid on a participation of 5% or more to a Dutch shareholder are usually exempt from withholding under the participation exemption, and that no return is needed for a fully exempt payment unless the company is invited to file. An investment dividend (beleggingsdividend) to a Dutch shareholder carries 15% withholding. The return and payment are due within 1 month after the day the dividend is made available. A return is also needed when a qualification decision (kwalificatiebeschikking) is applied. Individuals credit the withheld tax in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting), entities in the corporate income tax return (Vpb). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 1, art. 5, art. 6 lid 1, art. 9 lid 1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/als_u_dividend_uitkeert, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/dividendbelasting/als_u_dividend_uitkeert/dividendbelasting-aangifte-betalen.

### Dividend tax on the buyback of own shares (inkoop van eigen aandelen)

Dividend tax is due on the difference between the buyback price and the average paid-up capital on those shares, also on an exchange buyback. Where the company bears the tax, art. 6 grosses it up by 100/85, unless the art. 4c facility for listed companies applies. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-34752.html.

### Dividend stripping, beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde)

The rule sits in art. 4 lid 7 Wet DB 1965, not in art. 4 lid 3. A person who made a counter-performance as part of a series of transactions is not the beneficial owner (uiteindelijk gerechtigde) when the proceeds benefit someone with a lesser right to exemption or refund who keeps a position in the shares. The 2020 text holds the rule. From the 2024 text it reads "wordt in ieder geval niet als uiteindelijk gerechtigde beschouwd". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2025-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2020-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002515/2024-01-01 (art. 4 lid 7).

### Conditional withholding tax (conditionele bronbelasting)

Under art. 4.1 Wet bronbelasting 2021 the tax is the highest percentage of art. 22 Wet Vpb 1969, which is 25,8% in 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration states 25,8% for 2024, 2025 and 2026. Interest and royalties are in scope in the 2021 version, the first version with the levy articles. Dividends enter in the 2024 version (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2 on overlap with dividend tax). The levy applies to payments to a related entity in a low-tax jurisdiction where the profit tax is below 9% or that is on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, when the payer has a qualifying interest in the entity. A qualifying interest is always more than 50% of the voting rights. The levy also applies in abuse cases. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2026-01-01 (art. 4.1), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0042952/2024-01-01 (art. 3.4a and art. 5.2), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/bronbelasting-rente-en-royalty/.

### Corporate income tax return, deadlines (termijnen)

A book year equal to the calendar year must be filed before 1 June of the next year, so the return for book year 2025 is due before 1 June 2026. A broken book year must be filed within 5 months after its end. A short book year ending 31 December is filed before 1 June, and one ending in another month before 1 April of the next calendar year. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen/aangifte-vennootschapsbelasting-doen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting.

### Deferral (uitstel) of the corporate income tax return

An online extension is only available for a calendar book year. It runs to 1 November and is requested from 1 March to 1 June. A paper request is needed for a broken book year or for a request of more than 5 months, and it must be filed within 5 months after the book year ends, with reasons. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting/uitstel_aangifte_vennootschapsbelasting. A provisional (voorlopige) assessment (aanslag) Vpb 2026 for a foreign taxpayer can only be requested digitally. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2026.

### Annual accounts (jaarrekening), size classes (grootteklassen)

A legal person falls in a size class when on two consecutive balance dates it meets two of the three criteria, balance sheet total, net turnover and number of employees. The 2026 thresholds are micro at most € 450.000 assets and € 900.000 net turnover with fewer than 10 employees, small € 7,5 million and € 15 million with fewer than 50 employees, and medium € 25 million and € 50 million with fewer than 250 employees. Stb. 2024, 52 raised the amounts for book years from 1 January 2024, optional from 1 January 2023, and the employee counts are unchanged from the older statute. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2026-07-01 (art. 395a, 396 and 397 Boek 2 BW), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-52.html.

### Annual accounts, filing (deponering) and SBR

The annual accounts are deposited within eight days after adoption (vaststelling) and at the latest twelve months after the book year ends (art. 394 Boek 2 BW). If the accounts are not adopted within two months after the drawing-up term, the board deposits the unadopted accounts marking them so. Ondernemersplein states there is no extension, that late filing is a criminal offence, and that from financial year (boekjaar) 2025, filing from 1 January 2026, all legal persons file through SBR. Sources: https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/jaarrekening-indienen/, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0003045/2025-01-01 (art. 394 Boek 2 BW).

### Record-keeping and retention periods (administratie- en bewaartermijnen)

Art. 52 AWR makes entities (lichamen) required to keep records (administratieplichtig) and requires them to keep their records for seven years unless a tax law says otherwise. Data may move to another carrier except the paper balance sheet and the profit and loss statement. Art. 34a Wet OB 1968 keeps records on immovable property (onroerende zaken) for nine years following the year in which use began, in effect ten. Art. 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968 keep OSS records for ten years after the year of the transaction. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01 (art. 52 AWR), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01 (art. 34a, 28rl and 28sj Wet OB 1968).

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) on a taxable contribution (ruisende inbreng)

Without the tax-neutral conversion (geruisloze omzetting) facility a change of legal form is a cessation (taxable contribution, a taxed contribution). The owner draws up a final balance and pays income tax on the profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst), the difference between the book value and the real value of the business, and the fiscal reserves are released. The discontinuation deduction (art. 3.79 Wet IB 2001) is at most € 3.630 and can be used once in a lifetime. An extra annuity (lijfrente) premium deduction applies. Hidden reserves realised by a direct withdrawal (onttrekking) before a tax-neutral conversion do not get the discontinuation deduction but may go into an annuity (art. 3.129 Wet IB 2001). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.79 and art. 3.129), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/u_staakt_uw_onderneming/stakingswinst_berekenen, https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/geldzaken-en-belastingen/alles-over-aangifte-doen/bedrijfsbeeindiging-stakingswinst-en-stakingsaftrek/.

### Tax-neutral conversion (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001)

On request the business is deemed not ceased (art. 3.65 Wet IB 2001). The whole business must be contributed and the shares for the contributed assets are placed with the contributor and fully paid up. The company steps into the contributor's place for profit determination. The contributor may be credited for income tax, national insurance contributions and, from the 2025 Decree (besluit), the Zvw contribution (bijdrage) materially due at the transition, plus a rounding credit of at most 5% of the paid-up amount and not more than € 30.000. The acquisition price (verkrijgingsprijs) of the shares is the sum of the fiscal book values. The Tax-neutral conversion decree 2025 of 24 October 2025, Stcrt. 2025, 37092, published on 4 November 2025 and in force the day after publication, replaces the decree of 30 June 2010, nr. DGB 2010/3599M. Its standard conditions include that selling shares within three years after the contribution is presumed part of a plan to transfer the business unless the contributor proves otherwise, a rebuttable presumption and not an absolute sale ban. Into an existing company the contribution is allowed only when it already runs a business in the same line, and into a fiscal unity only through the parent company whose shares the contributor holds directly. The decree also changes or clarifies onderdeel 2.2.2.a (a rented-out business or profit right qualifies when it amounts to co-entitlement (medegerechtigdheid)), 3.4 (approval for a holding, widened to an indirect holding and to a new or activity-free subsidiary), 4.2.2 (lettered shares (letteraandelen) besides cumulative preference shares (cumulatief preferente aandelen), plus art. 14a transfers), 6.1 (which reserves to settle), 8 (the Zvw contribution credit), 12 (a new eighth standard condition for a shareholder resident abroad), 13.1.2 (an incidental tax advantage defined more sharply) and 13.2 (R&D approval (S&O) in the pre-preceding period (voor-voorperiode)). Retroactivity, if the preliminary agreement (voorovereenkomst) is received by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration within nine months after the last full book year, the conversion may take effect from the first day of the next book year, strictly applied and not for an incidental tax advantage. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says to request at the office that handles income tax, to register the preliminary agreement or letter of intent (intentieverklaring) with the accompanying form (Geleideformulier), and that the BV is a BV in formation (in oprichting) until the notarial deed. A request is timely until omitting it has irrevocable tax consequences for one of the parties, and the inspector decides by decision open to objection (bezwaar) that contains the conditions (onderdeel 14.2 of the old Decree, position (standpunt) of 27-06-2022, published 12-01-2023). ### VAT (btw) for a BV and the VAT fiscal unity (btw-fiscale eenheid)

The new legal form is a new business for the VAT and may receive a new VAT identification number (btw-identificatienummer), which must go to EU suppliers at once. Payroll gets a new payroll tax number (loonheffingennummer) if there are employees. Transfer tax on business real estate is usually not due on the change of legal form. A VAT fiscal unity (art. 7 lid 4 Wet OB 1968) arises by operation of law once the persons are financially, organisationally and economically interconnected, and it changes by law on entry or exit. A decision, on request or by the inspector, states the start date and is the condition for joint and several liability. Financial interconnection means that at least the majority of the ordinary shares with voting rights are in the same hands. The Policy decree (beleidsbesluit) tax liability (belastingplicht) and fiscal unity for turnover tax (omzetbelasting) of 4 December 2024, nr. 2024-13987, Stcrt. 2024, 38545, published on 10 December 2024, states "Dit besluit treedt in werking met ingang van 1 juli 2025", so it is in force in 2026. A pure holding that only holds shares is not an entrepreneur (ondernemer) and cannot join a VAT fiscal unity, but a holding that steers the group as policy-making body can join on written request.
