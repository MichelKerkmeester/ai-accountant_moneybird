## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the work relationship rules for a ZZP sole trader and for a BV that hires one. It covers the Wet DBA (Wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties, the law on assessing work relationships), its enforcement in 2026, modelovereenkomsten (model agreements), the VBAR bill with its low hourly rate presumption, and the BAZ bill. The most important 2026 changes are that vergrijpboetes (penalties for intent or gross negligence) become possible from 1 January 2026, that the VBAR is adopted and enters into force on 31 December 2026, and that the Zelfstandigenwet and the BAZ are still proposed. The year's law is mixed: the DBA enforcement policy and the modelovereenkomsten rules are enacted, the VBAR is adopted-not-yet-in-force, and the Zelfstandigenwet and BAZ are proposed.

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Wet DBA and the loondienst test

The Wet DBA is the Wet van 3 februari 2016 and entered into force on 1 May 2016. It replaced the VAR. The test for loondienst (employment) has three elements: the possibility of werkgeversgezag (employer authority), the duty to perform (persoonlijke) arbeid (personal work), and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the opdrachtnemer acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Not being in loondienst does not make someone an ondernemer for income tax or VAT. Sources: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

A correction to the record: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Besluit van 8 april 2016 that lets parties exclude the fictieve dienstbetrekking for thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten by agreement. Source: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).

### Fictieve dienstbetrekkingen and the opt-out

The loondienst page lists the fictieve dienstbetrekkingen (deemed employments). Among them are aannemers van werk and helpers, agenten, artiesten and beroepssporters, gelijkgestelden, stagiairs, thuiswerkers and opdrachtnemers working through an intermediair. Gelijkgestelden usually work 2 or more days a week for the same client and earn at least 2/5 of the minimum wage. Thuiswerkers, gelijkgestelden and artiesten can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The opdrachtnemer then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. Source: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### End of the moratorium

Until 1 January 2025 the handhavingsmoratorium (enforcement moratorium) applied. In a non-kwaadwillend case the Belastingdienst first gave an aanwijzing (instruction) to reshape the relationship so the opdrachtnemer really works as a zelfstandige, or to process it as a dienstbetrekking in the loonaangifte, usually within 3 months. If the aanwijzing was not followed, a correctieverplichting (correction obligation) applied from the date of the aanwijzing. Kwaadwillend means opzettelijk een situatie van evidente schijnzelfstandigheid laten ontstaan of voortbestaan, knowing or able to know that there is in fact a dienstbetrekking. Kwaadwillend cases could get correctieverplichtingen, naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen and boetes. Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No aanwijzingen are given. Schijnzelfstandigheid can lead directly to correctieverplichtingen and naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen. Sources: [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### Ingroeimodel

The ingroeimodel (phase-in model) limits how far back corrections can go. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is kwaadwillendheid or an aanwijzing given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Belastingdienst again go back the full 5 years. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### DBA enforcement 2026

In 2026 there are no verzuimboetes (late filing penalties) for the qualification of arbeidsrelaties. Vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for correcties over 2026. For correcties over 2025 there are no verzuimboetes and no vergrijpboetes. This is enforcement policy, not statute. In principle the control starts with a bedrijfsbezoek (business visit). The starting point to examine only the most recent aangiftetijdvak in a boekenonderzoek (tax audit) lapses. The remaining parts of the zachte landing (no verzuimboetes, in principle starting with a bedrijfsbezoek) lapse on 1 January 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Vooroverleg

The Belastingdienst takes a standpoint on a complete verzoek vooroverleg (preliminary consultation request). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty. It is valid only if the work is carried out exactly as described. The streeftermijn is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist vooroverleg exists. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Modelovereenkomsten

Working under an approved modelovereenkomst means no loondienst, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new modelovereenkomsten are assessed and none are renewed. The reason given is that modelovereenkomsten can create schijnzekerheid because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motie Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. The approval of the modelovereenkomsten vrije vervanging was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten), [Geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).

### Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie

The webmodule is for opdrachtgevers with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that work can be done outside dienstbetrekking, an indication of a dienstbetrekking, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a rechtspersoon such as a bv, for a bemiddelaar or tussenpersoon, or for a foreign opdrachtnemer. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active. There was no rechtskracht during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with an opdrachtgeversverklaring as one outcome. Sources: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### Case law on the arbeidsovereenkomst

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, addressed the role of partijbedoeling. HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), gave an omstandighedencatalogus that names inbedding and ondernemerschap, and vrije vervanging not as an absolute element. In the HR Uber case (prejudiciële vragen) there is no rangorde between the gezichtspunten, so ondernemerschap is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on handhaving. The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. They are UNVERIFIED. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

### VBAR act and scope

The VBAR is the Wet van 18 juni 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026. It inserts art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an arbeidsovereenkomst. Lid 2: the Minister of SZW changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag, rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the article does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Artikel II provides an evaluation within 5 years. The effect is that the werkende can invoke the presumption and the opdrachtgever must prove there is no arbeidsovereenkomst. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

Bill 36 783 was ingediend on 4 July 2025 per the Eerste Kamer and 7 July 2025 per the Tweede Kamer. The nota van wijziging of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed the verduidelijking part. The Tweede Kamer adopted the bill on 21 April 2026 (all parties except FVD). The Eerste Kamer adopted it on 16 June 2026 by zitten en opstaan. Entry into force is 31 December 2026 by Besluit van 13 juli 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026. ### VBAR statutory hourly amount

The statutory base is € 36 per hour at peildatum 1 January 2025. The memorie van toelichting explains it as follows. Take 120% of the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Add a 25% opslag for AOV and pension. Add a 15% opslag for general costs. Multiply by 1,5 for non-billable hours. This gives an unrounded € 35,43 excl. btw at peildatum 1 January 2025. That amount is rounded up to € 36,00. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

### VBAR indexed hourly amount

The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The unrounded amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. The Eerste Kamer, rijksoverheid.nl and Ondernemersplein state € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at peildatum 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at peildatum 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Rijksoverheid 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

### VBAR first-application regulation

Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministeriële regeling. The ministeriële regeling was not found on 2026-10-11. It would settle the exact figure for 31 December 2026. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### WML at 1 January 2025 used in the VBAR base

The memorie van toelichting uses the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

### WML from 1 January 2026 and 1 July 2026

The WML is € 14,71 at 1 January 2026 and € 14,99 from 1 July 2026. These figures are used in the open point on the first VBAR amount. Source: [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

### Zelfstandigenwet

The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026. The beoogde inwerkingtreding is January 2028. The bill has a zelfstandigentoets and a werkrelatietoets. The zelfstandigentoets looks at self-invoicing, KVK registration, the number of opdrachtgevers, and provisions against ondernemersrisico's. The werkrelatietoets looks at freedom to organise the work, own account and risk. Meeting both and working that way gives a veilige haven. Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

### Belastingrente IB and btw 2026

The rate for all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen is 5% from 1 January 2026. For income tax, belastingrente is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date, and when the aanslag follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. There is no rente when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month, 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. For btw, rente is charged on a naheffingsaanslag from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. There is no rente for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The mass objection on the rate was rejected for income tax, btw and the other general taxes, and granted for Vpb and similar. Sources: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente), [Belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [Belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting), [Massaal bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage).

### Payment terms 2026

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2026. An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).

### Bewaarplicht

AWR art. 52 lid 4 says administratieplichtigen keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table says basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. Basisgegevens include the grootboek, debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, voorraadadministratie, in- and verkoopadministratie, loonadministratie, and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### KVK and Belastingdienst registration

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 says the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven are made within one week after the fact. KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when registering within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead, appointments up to eight weeks ahead. KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Sources: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 art. 20 lid 1), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [Belastingdienst inschrijven](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).

## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example: VBAR indexation from the MvT.**
The MvT gives a fictitious example. Start from the unrounded base of € 35,43. A 3,75% WML rise gives € 35,43 x 1,0375 = € 36,758625. Rounded to two decimals this is € 36,76. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 37,00. A further 1,2% rise gives € 36,76 x 1,012 = € 37,20112. Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,20. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38,00. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

**Calculated example: VBAR indexation to 1 January 2026.**
Use the base € 35,43 at WML € 14,06. The WML at 1 January 2026 is € 14,71. The ratio is € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,046230... Multiply € 35,43 x 1,046230... = € 37,067944... Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,07. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38. This matches the published € 38 at peildatum 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).

**Calculated example: VBAR indexation with the WML from 1 July 2026.**
Use the base € 35,43 at WML € 14,06. The WML from 1 July 2026 is € 14,99. The ratio is € 14,99 / € 14,06 = 1,066145... Multiply € 35,43 x 1,066145... = € 37,773520... Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,77. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38. This is an illustration only. The first-application amount is set by a ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2 and that regeling was not found. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

**Calculated example: VBAR first-application threshold.**
The act enters into force on 31 December 2026. The statutory base in art. 7:610aa lid 1 BW is € 36 per hour at peildatum 1 January 2025. The indexed amount at peildatum 1 January 2026 is € 38. The amount for the first application after entry into force is set by a ministeriële regeling under art. III lid 2. That regeling was not found, so the first-applicable amount is not established. A rate above the indexed € 38 is above both known amounts, but it may still be at or below the first-applicable amount. This example therefore does not conclude that the presumption is excluded for a rate above € 38. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).

**Calculated example: how far back a DBA correction can go in 2026.**
Suppose the Belastingdienst finds schijnzelfstandigheid in 2026. There is no kwaadwillendheid and no earlier aanwijzing. The correction cannot go back before 1 January 2025. In 2026 the correction can cover 2025 and 2026. That is the current year plus one year back. The full 5-year lookback returns in 2030, when 2030 minus 5 = 2025. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example: vooroverleg streeftermijn.**
A complete verzoek vooroverleg is received on 1 March 2026. The streeftermijn is 8 weeks. 8 weeks is 56 days. March has 31 days. From 1 March to 1 April is 31 days. 56 minus 31 = 25 days. 1 April plus 25 days is 26 April 2026. The term may be extended. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example: payment terms.**
A naheffingsaanslag loonheffingen is dated 1 June 2026. It is payable 14 days after the date. 1 June 2026 plus 14 days is 15 June 2026. An aanslag dated 1 June 2026 is payable 6 weeks after the date. 6 weeks is 42 days. From 1 June to 1 July is 30 days. 42 minus 30 = 12 days. 1 July plus 12 days is 13 July 2026. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).

**Calculated example: belastingrente.**
An open income tax amount is € 10.000,00. The rate is 5% per year. The period is 180 days. The calculation uses 360 days per year and 30 days per month. Interest for a full year is € 10.000,00 x 0,05 = € 500,00. For 180 days the factor is 180 / 360 = 0,5. The interest is € 500,00 x 0,5 = € 250,00. Rounded down to whole euros gives € 250. Source: [Belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

**Calculated example: Zelfstandigenwet consultation window.**
The consultation is open until 29 October 2026. On 11 October 2026, 18 days remain. 29 minus 11 = 18 days. The intended entry into force is January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

**Calculated example: modelovereenkomst end date.**
An approved modelovereenkomst valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029 even if the agreement states another validity date. The end date is fixed at 31 December 2029. Source: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).

Failing that, employee rights such as loondoorbetaling bij ziekte and ontslagbescherming apply. Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).
