## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Official example: VBAR indexation from the MvT.**
The MvT gives a fictitious example. Start from the unrounded base of € 35,43. A 3,75% rise in the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML) gives € 35,43 x 1,0375 = € 36,758625. Rounded to two decimals this is € 36,76. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 37,00. A further 1,2% rise gives € 36,76 x 1,012 = € 37,20112. Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,20. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38,00. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

**Calculated example: VBAR indexation to 1 January 2026.**
Use the base € 35,43 at WML € 14,06. The WML at 1 January 2026 is € 14,71. The ratio is € 14,71 / € 14,06 = 1,046230... Multiply € 35,43 x 1,046230... = € 37,067944... Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,07. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38. This matches the published € 38 at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).

**Calculated example: VBAR indexation with the WML from 1 July 2026.**
Use the base € 35,43 at WML € 14,06. The WML from 1 July 2026 is € 14,99. The ratio is € 14,99 / € 14,06 = 1,066145... Multiply € 35,43 x 1,066145... = € 37,773520... Rounded to two decimals this is € 37,77. Rounded up to a whole euro this is € 38. This is an illustration only. The first-application amount is set by a ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 and that regulation (regeling) was not found. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

**Calculated example: VBAR first-application threshold.**
The act enters into force on 31 December 2026. The statutory base in art. 7:610aa lid 1 BW is € 36 per hour at reference date 1 January 2025. The indexed amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is € 38. The amount for the first application (eerste toepassing) after entry into force (inwerkingtreding) is set by a ministerial regulation under art. III lid 2. That regulation (regeling) was not found, so the first-applicable amount is not established. A rate above the indexed € 38 is above both known amounts, but it may still be at or below the first-applicable amount. This example therefore does not conclude that the presumption is excluded for a rate above € 38. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).

**Calculated example: how far back a DBA correction can go in 2026.**
Suppose the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) finds false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) in 2026. There is no bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) and no earlier instruction (aanwijzing). The correction cannot go back before 1 January 2025. In 2026 the correction can cover 2025 and 2026. That is the current year plus one year back (één jaar terug). The full 5-year lookback returns in 2030, when 2030 minus 5 = 2025. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example: preliminary consultation (vooroverleg) target period (streeftermijn).**
A complete preliminary consultation request (verzoek vooroverleg) is received on 1 March 2026. The target period is 8 weeks. 8 weeks is 56 days. March has 31 days. From 1 March to 1 April is 31 days. 56 minus 31 = 25 days. 1 April plus 25 days is 26 April 2026. The term may be extended. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

**Calculated example: payment terms.**
A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for payroll taxes (loonheffingen) is dated 1 June 2026. It is payable 14 days after the date. 1 June 2026 plus 14 days is 15 June 2026. An assessment (aanslag) dated 1 June 2026 is payable 6 weeks after the date. 6 weeks is 42 days. From 1 June to 1 July is 30 days. 42 minus 30 = 12 days. 1 July plus 12 days is 13 July 2026. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).

**Calculated example: tax interest (belastingrente).**
An open income tax (inkomstenbelasting) amount is € 10.000,00. The rate is 5% per year. The period is 180 days. The calculation uses 360 days per year and 30 days per month. Interest for a full year is € 10.000,00 x 0,05 = € 500,00. For 180 days the factor is 180 / 360 = 0,5. The interest is € 500,00 x 0,5 = € 250,00. Rounded down to whole euros gives € 250. Source: [Belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting).

**Calculated example: Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) consultation window.**
The consultation is open until 29 October 2026. On 11 October 2026, 18 days remain. 29 minus 11 = 18 days. The intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) is January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

**Calculated example: model agreement (modelovereenkomst) end date.**
An approved model agreement valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029 even if the agreement states another validity date. The end date is fixed at 31 December 2029. Source: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
