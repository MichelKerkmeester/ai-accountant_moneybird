## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties) and the employment (loondienst) test

The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016 and entered into force (in werking trad) on 1 May 2016. It replaced the VAR. The test for employment (loondienst) has three elements: the possibility of employer authority (werkgeversgezag), the duty to perform personal work (persoonlijke arbeid), and pay for the work. The courts weigh among others the nature and duration of the work, how work and working hours are set, embedding in the organisation, the duty to work personally, how the agreements were made, how pay is set and paid and its level, commercial risk, and how far the contractor (opdrachtnemer) acts or can act as an entrepreneur. Not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) or VAT. Sources: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

A correction to the record: Staatsblad 2016, 165 is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree (besluit) of 8 April 2016 that lets parties exclude the deemed employment relationship (fictieve dienstbetrekking) for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) by agreement. Source: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html).

### Deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen) and the opt-out

The employment page lists the deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). Among them are building contractors (aannemers van werk) and helpers, agents (agenten), artists and professional athletes (beroepssporters), persons treated as employees, interns (stagiairs), home workers and contractors (opdrachtnemers) working through an intermediary (intermediair). Persons treated as employees usually work 2 or more days a week for the same client and earn at least 2/5 of the minimum wage (minimumloon). Home workers, persons treated as employees and artists can be excluded in writing in the contract before the first payment of pay. The contractor then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. Source: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).

### End of the moratorium (einde moratorium)

Until 1 January 2025 the enforcement moratorium (handhavingsmoratorium) applied. In cases not involving bad faith (non-kwaadwillend), the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) first gave an instruction (aanwijzing) to reshape the relationship so the contractor really works as a self-employed person (zelfstandige), or to process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte), usually within 3 months. If the instruction was not followed, a correction obligation (correctieverplichting) applied from the date of the instruction. Acting in bad faith (kwaadwillend) means intentionally allowing a situation of evident false self-employment (evidente schijnzelfstandigheid) to arise or continue, while knowing or being reasonably able to know that there is in fact an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking). Cases involving bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) could lead to correction obligations (correctieverplichtingen), supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen) for payroll taxes (loonheffingen) and penalties (boetes). Since 1 January 2025 the normal rules apply. No instructions (aanwijzingen) are given. False self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) can lead directly to correction obligations and supplementary assessments for payroll taxes. Sources: [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### Phase-in model (ingroeimodel)

The phase-in model (ingroeimodel) limits how far back corrections can go. Corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless there is bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction given before that date was not followed. In that case up to 5 years back. Only in 2030 can the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration again go back the full 5 years. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### DBA enforcement 2026

In 2026 there are no default penalties (verzuimboetes) for the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties). Penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent (opzet) are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. For corrections over 2025 there are no default penalties and no penalties for gross negligence or intent. This is enforcement policy, not statute. In principle the control starts with a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek). The starting point to examine only the most recent return period (aangiftetijdvak) in a tax audit (boekenonderzoek) lapses. The remaining parts of the soft landing (zachte landing) expire on 1 January 2027. They are the absence of default penalties and the principle of starting with a business visit. Sources: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving).

### Enforcement plan (handhavingsplan) Employment relationships 2026

The plan is version (versie) 2.0 of 31 December 2025. It was made after motions (moties) Ergin c.s. (31 311, nr. 298) and Martens-America/Vermeer (31 311, nr. 301) of 18 December 2025 and the Parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 19 December 2025. Capacity is 80 fte. NLA notifications (meldingen) are usable for enforcement (handhaving) from 2026. The plan pays extra attention to government bodies as clients (opdrachtgevers), uses the detection module (detectiemodule) TKA, and keeps horizontal supervision levels for fiscal service providers. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving) ambition

The tranche 2025 plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration aims to integrate from 2027. Sources: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf), [Handhavingsplan tranche 2025](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z52fd.pdf).

### Interaction (doorwerking) with income tax (IH) and VAT (OB) for the contractor

Guidelines (richtlijnen) for the interaction (doorwerking) with income tax (IH) and VAT (OB) exist since 2023 and were updated for the end of the moratorium. The focus is on the payroll taxes (loonheffingen) at the client (opdrachtgever). After LH corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. A contractor in regular IH or OB supervision has the most recent open profit return (winstaangifte) or periods (tijdvakken) examined, with attention to future returns. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Preliminary consultation (vooroverleg)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration takes a standpoint on a complete request (verzoek) preliminary consultation (vooroverleg). The standpoint gives only conditional certainty. It is valid only if the work is carried out exactly as described. The target period (streeftermijn) is 8 weeks and may be extended under a large request stream. A checklist preliminary consultation exists. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

### Model agreements (modelovereenkomsten)

Working under an approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) means no employment, but only if the parties work as described. Since 6 September 2024 no new model agreements are assessed and none are renewed. The reason given is that model agreements can create false certainty (schijnzekerheid) because the qualification depends on how the work is actually done. Approved agreements valid on 6 September 2024 can be used up to and including 31 December 2029, even if the agreement states another validity date. The tranche 2025 plan adds that they are honoured to end 2029 after motion (motie) Van Oostenbruggen (Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 275), on the condition that they comply with law and regulations. The approval of the model agreements free substitution (vrije vervanging) was withdrawn on 14 August 2023 after the Deliveroo ruling. Sources: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten), [Geen nieuwe modelovereenkomsten meer](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer).

### Web module assessment of the employment relationship (webmodule beoordeling arbeidsrelatie)

The web module is for clients with a business in the Netherlands. It has three outcomes: an indication that work can be done outside an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking), an indication of an employment relationship, or no judgment possible. It is not a legal decision and gives no rights. It is not useful for a contract with a legal entity (rechtspersoon) such as a BV, for a broker (bemiddelaar) or intermediary (tussenpersoon), or for a foreign contractor. The pilot ran from 11 January 2021 for 6 months and ended 10 July 2021. The online pilot environment stayed active. There was no legal force (rechtskracht) during or after the pilot. The pilot design used a points system with a client declaration (opdrachtgeversverklaring) as one outcome. Sources: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/personeel/inhuren/webmodule-beoordeling-arbeidsrelatie/), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).

### Case law on the employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst)

HR 6 November 2020, ECLI:NL:HR:2020:1746, addressed the role of intention of the parties (partijbedoeling). HR 24 March 2023, ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443 (Deliveroo), gave a Catalogue of Circumstances (omstandighedencatalogus) that names integration (inbedding) and entrepreneurship (ondernemerschap), and free substitution not as an absolute element. In the HR Uber case involving preliminary questions (prejudiciële vragen) there is no order of priority (rangorde) between the factors (gezichtspunten), so entrepreneurship is a full criterion. The government states this has no effect on enforcement (handhaving). The date 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. They are UNVERIFIED. Sources: [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid).

### VBAR act and scope

The Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) is the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, published 29 June 2026. It inserts art. 7:610aa BW. Lid 1: someone who works for another for pay of at most € 36 per hour is presumed to work under an employment contract. Lid 2: the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment (Sociale Zaken en Werkgelegenheid, SZW) changes the amount in the manner of art. 14 lid 1 and 2 Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act (Wet minimumloon en minimumvakantiebijslag), rounded up to whole euros, always starting from the unrounded amount. Lid 3: the article does not apply when the other party is a natural person not acting in a profession or business. Article II provides for an evaluation within 5 years. The effect is that the worker (werkende) can invoke the presumption and the client must prove there is no employment contract. It is civil law only, with no fiscal qualification rule. Sources: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

Entry into force (inwerkingtreding) is 31 December 2026 by Decree of 13 July (juli) 2026, Stb. 2026, 207, published 15 July 2026. The date was chosen to meet the Recovery and Resilience Plan (Herstel- en Veerkrachtplan) milestone, in force (in werking) before 1 January 2027. Sources: [Tweede Kamer 36 783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html).

### VBAR statutory hourly amount

The statutory base is € 36 per hour at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) explains it as follows. Take 120% of the statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML) of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Add a 25% surcharge (opslag) for disability insurance (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering, AOV) and pension. Add a 15% surcharge for general costs. Multiply by 1,5 for non-billable hours. This gives an unrounded € 35,43 excluding VAT (btw) at reference date 1 January 2025. That amount is rounded up to € 36,00. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

### VBAR indexed hourly amount

The WML rose to € 14,71 on 1 January 2026. Every later change starts from the unrounded € 35,43 and follows the WML percentage. The unrounded amount at reference date 1 January 2026 is about € 37,07 and the rounded amount is € 38. The Senate, Government of the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid) and Ondernemersplein state € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026. So € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date 2025 and € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 2026. Sources: [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Rijksoverheid 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).

### VBAR first-application regulation

Artikel III lid 2 says that for the first application after entry into force the applicable amount is given by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) was not found on 2026-10-11. It would settle the exact figure for 31 December 2026. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html).

### WML at 1 January 2025 used in the VBAR base

The explanatory memorandum uses the WML of € 14,06 per hour at 1 January 2025. Source: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html).

### WML from 1 January 2026 and 1 July 2026

The WML is € 14,71 at 1 January 2026 and € 14,99 from 1 July 2026. These figures are used in the open point on the first VBAR amount. Source: [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).

### Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet)

The internet consultation is open until 29 October 2026. The intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) is January 2028. The bill has a self-employment test (zelfstandigentoets) and a work relationship test (werkrelatietoets). The self-employment test looks at self-invoicing, KVK registration, the number of clients, and provisions against business risks (ondernemersrisico's). The work relationship test looks at freedom to organise the work, own account and risk. Meeting both and working that way gives a safe harbour (veilige haven). Those who do not qualify keep the current rules. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).

### Tax interest (belastingrente) IB and VAT 2026

The rate for all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen) is 5% from 1 January 2026. For income tax, tax interest is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment (aanslag) deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral (uitstel). It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date, and when the assessment follows the return unchanged no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. There is no tax interest when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month, 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. For VAT, tax interest is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. There is no tax interest for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The mass objection (massaal bezwaar, massaal-bezwaar) on the rate was rejected for income tax, VAT and the other general taxes, and granted for Vpb and similar. Sources: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente), [Belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting), [Belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting), [Massaal bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage).

### Payment terms 2026

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9, same text on 1 January 2026. An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment 1 month after. A supplementary assessment 14 days after. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).

### Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4 says persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, and other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office (belastingkantoor). The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Basic records include the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration

Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1 says the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required returns (opgaven) are made within one week after the fact. KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) number (KVK-nummer) immediately when registering within that window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead, appointments up to eight weeks ahead. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other forms get a legal entity identification number (RSIN), and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) from it. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen). Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Sources: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 art. 20 lid 1), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [Belastingdienst inschrijven](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
