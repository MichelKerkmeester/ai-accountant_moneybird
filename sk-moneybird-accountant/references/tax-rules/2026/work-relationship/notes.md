## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- DBA penalties (boetes): in 2025 there were no default penalties (verzuimboetes) and no penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent for the qualification of employment relationships (arbeidsrelaties) (Flach/Aartsen motion (motie Flach/Aartsen), Kamerstuk 31 311, nr. 266). In 2026 there are no default penalties, but penalties for gross negligence or intent are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. For corrections over 2025 the 2025 regime stays. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Start of control: in 2025 the control started in principle with a business visit (bedrijfsbezoek), possibly a warning (waarschuwing) that was not an official instrument like the instruction (aanwijzing), and a tax audit (boekenonderzoek) remained possible with large risks. In 2026 the control starts in principle with a business visit and the starting point to examine only the most recent return period (aangiftetijdvak) in a tax audit lapses. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Regular enforcement (reguliere handhaving): the 2025 tranche plan stated integration from 1 January 2026. The 2026 plan records that the ambition was 1 January 2026, that a separate plan was made again for 2026, and that the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) aims to integrate from 2027. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- NLA signals: in 2025 NLA signals were expected to be picked up in 2025 through the GKT-meldingenproces. In 2026 NLA notifications (meldingen) are usable for enforcement (handhaving). Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- VBAR procedure: in 2025 the bill was submitted (ingediend) on 4 July 2025 per the Senate (Eerste Kamer) and 7 July 2025 per the House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer). In 2026 the House of Representatives adopted it on 21 April 2026, the Senate on 16 June 2026, the act is the Act of 18 June 2026, Stb. 2026, 158, and entry into force (inwerkingtreding) is 31 December 2026 by Stb. 2026, 207. Sources: [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering), [Tweede Kamer 36 783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783), [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html).
- VBAR clarification (verduidelijking): the bill as introduced in 2025 included a clarification (verduidelijking) part. The memorandum of amendment (nota van wijziging) of 10 March 2026 (36 783, nr. 6) removed it. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 783](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36783).
- VBAR hourly amount: the statutory base is € 36 at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025. The indexed amount is € 38 at reference date 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- Statutory minimum wage (wettelijk minimumloon, WML): € 14,06 at 1 January 2025. € 14,71 at 1 January 2026. € 14,99 from 1 July 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): new in 2026. Internet consultation (internetconsultatie) open until 29 October 2026, intended entry into force (beoogde inwerkingtreding) January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- BAZ: new bill in 2026. Submitted 24 March 2026, committee input (inbreng) 27 May 2026, report (verslag) published 29 May 2026, awaiting the memorandum (nota) on 2026-10-11. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Tax interest (belastingrente): 6,5% in 2025 to 5% from 1 January 2026 for all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen). Source: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Enforcement plan (handhavingsplan): the 2025 plan was replaced by the Enforcement plan Employment relationships 2026, version (versie) 2.0 of 31 December 2025, after motions (moties) of 18 December 2025 and the Parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) of 19 December 2025. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record how the work is actually carried out, not only what the contract says. An approved model agreement (modelovereenkomst) gives no employment (loondienst) only if the parties work as described. Source: [Werken met modelovereenkomsten](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/werken-met-modelovereenkomsten).
- Keep the written opt-out for home workers (thuiswerkers), persons treated as employees (gelijkgestelden) and artists (artiesten) before the first payment. The contractor (opdrachtnemer) then has no entitlement to ZW, WIA and WW. Source: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst).
- For a BV as client (opdrachtgever), if the relationship is employment, process it as an employment relationship (dienstbetrekking) in the payroll tax return (loonaangifte). A correction can lead to a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for payroll taxes (loonheffingen), payable 14 days after the date. Sources: [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving), [Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01 art. 9).
- DBA penalties (boetes) 2026: no default penalties (verzuimboetes), but penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent are possible from 1 January 2026, also for corrections (correcties) over 2026. For corrections over 2025 there are no default penalties and no penalties for gross negligence or intent. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Phase-in model (ingroeimodel): corrections go no further back than 1 January 2025 unless bad faith (kwaadwillendheid) or an instruction (aanwijzing) not followed, then up to 5 years. Full 5 years only from 2030. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Interaction (doorwerking): after LH corrections at the client the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) in principle takes no targeted action for earlier years at the contractor aimed at source qualification (bronkwalificatie). Earlier years means tax years before the year of the LH correction. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- For the sole trader, not being in employment does not make someone an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) or VAT. Keep records of rate, hours, agreements and invoices. The VBAR presumption puts the proof on the client if the rate is at or below the amount. Sources: [Belastingdienst loondienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/wanneer-is-sprake-van-loondienst), [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).
- Retention: keep the records (administratie) for 7 years under AWR art. 52 lid 4. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table says 10 years for immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data, and 7 years or shorter by agreement for other data. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Source: [Belastingdienst bewaarplicht](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Registration: register the business (onderneming) with the Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel, KVK) within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days (werkdagen). Sources: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16 art. 20 lid 1), [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/), [Belastingdienst inschrijven](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- If a contractor invokes the VBAR presumption, the client must prove there is no employment contract (arbeidsovereenkomst). Failing that, employee rights such as continued payment of wages during illness (loondoorbetaling bij ziekte) and dismissal protection (ontslagbescherming) apply. Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/laag-uurtarief-van-zzper-kan-leiden-tot-arbeidsovereenkomst/).
- Tax interest (belastingrente): the rate for IB and VAT (btw) is 5% in 2026. If a correction leads to an assessment (aanslag) that deviates from the return, tax interest can run from 1 July after the tax year. Source: [Overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- VBAR first-application regulation: UNVERIFIED. The ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) under art. III lid 2 was not found on 2026-10-11. It would settle the exact figure for 31 December 2026. See the calculated example in section 4 for an illustration based on the WML of € 14,99. Source: [Stb. 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.html), [Rijksoverheid minimumloon 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon-2026).
- BAZ waiting period (wachttijd): 104 weeks in the bill text against about one year in the docket summary. UNVERIFIED. Stays open until the bill text or the memorandum (nota) is read. Sources: [Tweede Kamer document, Kamerstuk 36912 nr. 2, voorstel van wet](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36912-2.html), [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Case law date: 21 February 2025 and ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319 come from a later court citation found by search, not from a fetched copy of the ruling. UNVERIFIED. Sources: [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/03/27/ondernemerschap-blijft-volwaardig-criterium-bij-beoordelen-schijnzelfstandigheid), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
- VBAR hourly amount € 36 against € 38: settled. € 36 is the statutory base text at reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2025 (unrounded € 35,43). € 38 is the indexed amount at reference date 1 January 2026. Sources: [Kst. 36 783, nr. 3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36783-3.html), [Eerste Kamer 36 783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering).
- Regular DBA enforcement (handhaving) start date: settled. 1 January 2026 in the tranche 2025 plan, moved to an ambition for 1 January 2027 in the 2026 plan. Source: [Handhavingsplan Arbeidsrelaties 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-lh0021z62fd.pdf).
- Staatsblad 2016, 165: corrected. It is not the Wet DBA itself. It is the Decree (besluit) of 8 April 2016 on the opt-out for some deemed employment relationships (fictieve dienstbetrekkingen). The Wet DBA is the Act of 3 February 2016, in force (in werking) 1 May 2016. Sources: [Stb. 2016, 165](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2016-165.html), [open.overheid.nl](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/d2c6b4e8-537c-48a8-9e17-85f1acf4825d/file).
- BAZ report (verslag) date 27 May against 29 May 2026: settled. Contribution to the debate (inbreng) delivered 27 May, report (verslag) (36 912, nr. 5) published 29 May. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet): proposed. Consultation to 29 October 2026, intended January 2028. Source: [Rijksoverheid 1 October 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/01/zelfstandigenwet-biedt-meer-duidelijkheid-en-erkenning-voor-zzpers).
- BAZ: proposed. Awaiting the memorandum (nota) on 2026-10-11, no debate and no vote. Source: [Tweede Kamer 36 912](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel%3A36912).
- VBAR: adopted-not-yet-in-force. Entry into force (inwerkingtreding) 31 December 2026. Source: [Stb. 2026, 207](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-207.html).
