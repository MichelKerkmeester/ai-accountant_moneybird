## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the work relationship rules for a ZZP sole trader and for a BV that hires one. It covers the Deregulation of Employment Relationships Assessment Act (wet deregulering beoordeling arbeidsrelaties), the law on assessing work relationships, its enforcement in 2026, model agreements (modelovereenkomsten), the Act on Clarification of the Assessment of Employment Relationships and Legal Presumption (Wet verduidelijking beoordeling arbeidsrelaties en rechtsvermoeden, VBAR) bill with its low hourly rate presumption, and the Basic Disability Insurance for Self-Employed Persons Act (Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen, BAZ) bill. The most important 2026 changes are that penalties (vergrijpboetes) for gross negligence (grove schuld) or intent (opzet) become possible from 1 January 2026, that the VBAR is adopted and enters into force on 31 December 2026, and that the Self-Employed Persons Act (Zelfstandigenwet) and the BAZ are still proposed. The year's law is mixed: the DBA enforcement policy and the model agreements rules are enacted, the VBAR is adopted-not-yet-in-force, and the Self-Employed Persons Act and BAZ are proposed.
