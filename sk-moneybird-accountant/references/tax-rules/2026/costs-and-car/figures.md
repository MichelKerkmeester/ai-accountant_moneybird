## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

The 2026 rules for business costs and cars are largely enacted in the Income Tax Act (Wet inkomstenbelasting) and the Corporate Income Tax Act 1969 (Wet Vpb 1969). For 2026 the threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel) is € 5.700, with an 80% election, the applied mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding) for a private vehicle (privévervoermiddel) is € 0,25 although the statute still prints € 0,23, and the youngtimer age for the 35% rule is 16 years. Two decrees give temporary approvals for 2026, the mileage allowance and the youngtimer transition, and Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) contains proposed retroactive codification. The remaining fixed exclusions and the zero-emission car regime are enacted, with open points listed in section 7.
