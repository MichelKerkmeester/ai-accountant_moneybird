## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel): € 5.700 in 2025 to € 5.700 in 2026, unchanged. The 2026 Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page also says "In 2025 was de drempel ook € 5.700". Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.
- Choice instead of the threshold (keuze in plaats van de drempel): 80% deductible in 2025 to 80% deductible in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Travel and accommodation for conferences and courses (reis en verblijf): not deductible above € 1500 in 2025 to not deductible above € 1.500 in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding): € 0,23 per km in 2025. The 2026 statute still prints € 0,23, but the applied amount for 2026 is € 0,25 per km, retroactive to 1 January 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.
- Motor vehicle tax (MRB) for vans and trucks: new temporary lower motor vehicle tax for vans (bestelauto's) of VAT entrepreneurs (btw-ondernemers) and nil for trucks (vrachtauto's) from 1 July to 31 December 2026, for two periods (tijdvakken). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.
- Travel allowance for wage tax (reiskostenvergoeding loonbelasting) and other flat-rate travel expenses (forfaitaire reiskosten) in the Income Tax Act: new € 0,25 per km instead of € 0,23. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html (sections 2.1 and 2.2).
- Relocation costs (verhuiskosten): household contents (inboedel) plus € 7750 in 2025 to household contents plus € 7.750 in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Private-use addition for a car (bijtelling), standard: 22% in 2025 to 22% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Private-use addition for a car, pre-2017 car: at least 25% in 2025 to at least 25% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Youngtimer: 35% of the market value (WEV) for a car first used more than 15 years ago in 2025 to 35% of the WEV for a car first used more than 16 years ago in 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.
- Youngtimer transition 2026: new optional rule. The 2025 rule of 35% of the WEV stays for 2026 for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html.
- Zero-emission first admission: 2025 5% off capped € 1.500, which is 17% up to € 30.000. 2026 4% off capped € 1.200, which is 18% up to € 30.000. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during 2025. From then the rules then in force apply. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 11).
- Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets): 7% in 2025 to 7% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek): the 2025 FISIN page verifies the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) caps. The 2026 FISIN page narrows the case to an extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) of maximum 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten.
- Home workspace (werkruimte) in an owned home that is part of the business assets under art. 3.16 lid 14: new from 1 January 2025, so 2025 and 2026 are the same. Costs that lid 1 would exclude for a private home are not deductible, and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten) of a non-self-contained workspace. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html.
- Telephone subscription at home (telefoonabonnement thuis): 0% in 2025 to 0% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Private computers and equipment (apparatuur): 0% in 2025 to 0% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Fines (geldboetes): 0% in 2025 to 0% in 2026, unchanged. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record business costs in the profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming). Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, for example under the small businesses scheme (KOR), deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax return. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.
- Record the 80% election in the return when it is chosen instead of the € 5.700 threshold. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.
- Keep receipts and invoices for all costs. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) requires this for the € 450 rule, for depreciation and for costs made before the start of the business. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.
- For mixed costs (gemengde kosten), record food, drink and stimulants, representation expenses, conferences and comparable items separately so the yearly total against the € 5.700 threshold is clear. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15).
- For a car included in the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), record the catalogue price (catalogusprijs), the first admission date, the private kilometres and the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. Keep a trip registration (rittenregistratie) or other evidence for the 500 km rule. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.
- For a private car used for business, record the business kilometres and apply € 0,25 per km for 2026. The amount is all-in, so do not add fuel, insurance, toll or parking separately. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026.
- For a zero-emission car, record the first admission date, the CO2 value, the solar panel specifications, the battery type and the end of the 60-month period. Keep the charging point (laadpaal) invoice separate. The charging point does not raise the catalogue price. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2 and lid 11) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto.
- For a bicycle (fiets) of the business, record the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs), the private availability and whether the bicycle is stalled at the home address no more than incidentally. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a).
- For a congress, course or study trip, record travel and stay separately and apply the € 1.500 cap unless the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8).
- For assets, apply the € 450 rule. Record the acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde), the residual value (restwaarde) and the depreciation schedule. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.
- For the home workspace (werkruimte thuis), record the facts for self-containment (zelfstandigheid) and the income test. A non-self-contained workspace is 0% in principle. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 1 and lid 14).
- For fines and penalty payments (dwangsommen), do not deduct. Keep documentation of when the fine was imposed to separate pre-imposition costs. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.14) and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/.
- For study costs, record the course content to show that it maintains existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers.
- For the small businesses scheme, monitor turnover against € 20.000. Under the small businesses scheme no VAT is charged or paid and no VAT is deducted, and costs are deducted including VAT in the income tax return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.
- For VAT on a private car used for business, keep conclusive mileage records (sluitende kilometeradministratie) to deduct VAT by business use. Without it, correct 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and BPM in rubriek 1d at year end. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) counts as private for the VAT. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto.
- For a change in labelling (etikettering), record the year of the choice (keuze) and the moment the assessment (aanslag) for that year becomes irrevocable. A later change needs a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Age for the switch to the market value (WEV) in 2026. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b in the 2026 text says "meer dan vijftien jaar", source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) applies 16 years, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto. Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) art. I onderdeel E onder 2 replaces "vijftien" by "16" retroactively to 1 January 2026, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html, and the bill is proposed on 2026-10-11. This is settled in practice by the Tax and Customs Administration position. It is settled in law only when the bill is adopted. What would settle it is adoption of Tax Plan 2027.
- Legal codification of the € 0,25 mileage allowance and of the youngtimer transition for 2026. The codification sits in Tax Plan 2027 art. I onderdelen C, D, H, I and art. XLVII lid 1, retroactive to 1 January 2026 per art. LXIII lid 1 a, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html. The bill is proposed on 2026-10-11. What would settle it is adoption of Tax Plan 2027. Until then the 2026 decree of 17 May 2026, Stcrt. 2026, 18302, applies, and it lapses on 1 January 2027, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.
- Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) 2026 caps. FISIN 2026 narrows the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case to an extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) of maximum 15 years, study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten. The checked material does not restate the per-study-year caps for 2026. The caps verified for 2022 to 2025 are € 1.693 per study year for mbo, € 2.443 per study year for hbo or wo, € 141,09 per month part-year for mbo, € 203,59 per month part-year for hbo or wo, and a € 250 threshold (drempel) per person, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/aftrek_studiekosten. What would settle it is a 2026 FISIN page or law text that states the caps for 2026.
- Solar panel exemption from the zero-emission cap. The 2026 Tax and Customs Administration page says at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek), source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026. The statute says 1 kilowatt peak only in the 2021 to 2023 texts, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, and from the 2024 text requires watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by watt-hour (wattuur) per km of at least 7, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01. The statute decides. What would settle it is a corrected Tax and Customs Administration page or a court decision.
- Home workspace (werkruimte) owner route. The Tax and Customs Administration page offers deduction for a non-self-contained workspace when the home is owned and included in the business assets, source https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning. From 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 bars costs that lid 1 would bar for a private home, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) names the tenant's costs (huurderslasten), source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html. Which owner costs of a non-self-contained workspace stay deductible beyond the tenant's costs is not stated on a fetched primary page. What would settle it is a Tax and Customs Administration policy decision or case law.
- Youngtimer transition 2026 wording. The national government (Rijksoverheid) says cars turning 16 in 2026 stay under the youngtimer rule until 1 January 2027, source https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen. The legal basis is the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, Stcrt. 2026, 6596, an optional approval for a car that passed 15 during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025, source https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html. The decree wording is narrower than the summary. The decree itself is the legal basis.
- Old-car minimum, 35% against 25%. Both apply to different cars. 35% of the WEV applies to old cars under art. 3.20 lid 1 onderdeel b. At least 25% instead of 22% applies to a car first admitted by 31 December 2016 under art. 10a.4. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.
- Zero-emission 2020, 8% up to € 45.000 against 14% off capped € 6.300. This is the same regime. The 2020 statute prints 14% with a maximum of € 6.300, source https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01. Only hydrogen (waterstof) was uncapped in 2020. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01.
