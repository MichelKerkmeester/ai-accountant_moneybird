## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 1. Threshold for mixed costs (gemengde kosten drempel)

For the income tax (inkomstenbelasting), the restriction applies to costs for food, drink and stimulants (voedsel, drank en genotmiddelen), to representation expenses (representatie) including receptions, festive gatherings (feestelijke bijeenkomsten) and entertainment (vermaak), and to conferences, seminars, symposia, excursions (excursies), study trips (studiereizen) and the like. Travel and stay connected with these items count as part of them. The restriction does not apply where the enterprise directly makes or trades these goods or services and they are part of turnover. No withdrawal (onttrekking) is taken into account for the taxpayer himself on these costs. The threshold is € 5.700 for 2026. It is not time-proportioned when an enterprise starts during the calendar year, and a financial year (boekjaar) longer than 12 months does not increase the fixed amount. Food costs that qualify as labour costs (loonkosten) are fully deductible. When an employer gives free meals to staff, the art. 3.15 restriction applies only to the part of the meal cost that is not wages in kind (loon in natura). The threshold is indexed under art. 10.1, which lists art. 3.15 eerste lid only, so the kilometre amount in lid 6 is not indexed. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 and art. 10.1), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026 and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3).

The content of the items is practical. Food covers business lunches (zakenlunches) and business dinners (zakendiners) including gratuities (fooien). Drink covers coffee, tea, milk and soft drinks. Stimulants covers cigarettes and cigars. Food, drink and stimulants used privately are never deductible. Representation expenses include receptions, and business gifts (relatiegeschenken) generally fall under representation expenses. Business gifts are a subcategory of representation expenses per the legislative history (wetsgeschiedenis) of art. 8a Wet IB 1964. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.3).

### 2. Mixed costs choice (gemengde kosten keuze)

Instead of the threshold, the taxpayer may elect in the return to deduct 80% of these costs. The same 80% applies to recipients of income from other activities (resultaatgenieters). The election is made in the return for the year. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026.

### 3. Corporate income tax (Vpb) variant for mixed costs

For a BV, under art. 8 lid 5 Wet Vpb 1969 the art. 3.15 restriction applies to a body when there are one or more employees in the Wet LB sense in the year. The fixed amount is replaced by 0,4% of the employees' wage total when that is higher than the fixed amount of € 5.700. The election percentage is 73,5% instead of 80%. It applies per body, so each company in a holding structure with an employee has its own fixed amount. Only in a fiscal unity (fiscale eenheid) does it apply once, at the parent. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01 (art. 8 lid 5) and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (section 3.4).

### 4. Congress, course and study-trip travel cap

Travel and accommodation for courses and training (cursussen en opleidingen) for study or professional purposes, and for conferences, seminars, symposia, excursions, study trips and the like, are not deductible (niet aftrekbaar) above € 1.500 per year. The cap does not apply when the nature of the work makes attendance necessary. The amount is not indexed and is the same in every year 2020 to 2027. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel f and lid 8) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 5. Mileage allowance (kilometervergoeding), statute

Costs of a vehicle that belongs to the private assets (privévermogen) or is rented privately, such as a car, motorcycle or bicycle (fiets), are limited to a fixed amount for each business kilometre. The statute prints € 0,23 for 2026 under art. 3.15 lid 6 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b. The amount is all-in, so fuel (brandstof), insurance (verzekering), toll and parking cannot be deducted separately. Household members are treated as the taxpayer under art. 3.15 lid 7. The amount is not indexed under art. 10.1. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.15 lid 6, lid 7 and art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel b).

### 6. Mileage allowance, applied

For 2026 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) applies € 0,25 for each business kilometre, retroactive to 1 January 2026. The legal basis is the policy decree (beleidsbesluit) on fiscal measures in response to the energy shock (energieschok) of 17 May 2026, nr. 2026-8260, Stcrt. 2026, 18302. The decree itself lapses on 1 January 2027. Its approvals are to be codified in Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027). Use € 0,25 for 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026.

### 7. Motor vehicle tax for vans and trucks, 2026 decree

The same decree of 17 May 2026 temporarily lowers motor vehicle tax (motorrijtuigenbelasting, MRB) for vans (bestelauto's) of VAT entrepreneurs (btw-ondernemers) and sets nil for trucks (vrachtauto's) from 1 July to 31 December 2026, for two periods (tijdvakken). Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html.

### 8. Travel allowance (reiskostenvergoeding) for wage tax (loonbelasting)

The same decree allows € 0,25 per kilometre instead of € 0,23 under art. 31a lid 2 onderdeel a 3° Wet LB. Correction notices (correctieberichten) are allowed. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html (section 2.1).

### 9. Other flat-rate travel expenses (forfaitaire reiskosten) in the Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001)

The same decree sets € 0,25 instead of € 0,23 for the other flat-rate travel expenses (forfaitaire reiskosten) in the Income Tax Act under art. 6.17 lid 6 onderdeel a, art. 6.26 with art. 40 lid 1 onderdeel b URIB and art. 6.36 lid 2. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18302.html (section 2.2).

### 10. Relocation costs (verhuiskosten) and double accommodation (dubbele huisvesting)

Moving costs are the cost of moving the household contents (inboedel) plus € 7.750. Housing outside the place of residence (dubbele huisvesting) is deductible for at most two years. Unchanged 2020 to 2027. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.17 lid 1 onderdeel a).

### 11. Private-use addition (bijtelling) for a car, standard

When a car is also available for private use (privégebruik), at least 22% of the value of the car (waarde van de auto) is a withdrawal per year. The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting (woon-werkverkeer) is not private under lid 3. With trip registration (rittenregistratie) or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system (keurmerk rittenregistratiesysteem) is assumed to be conclusive (sluitend), though trips can still be checked. The withdrawal is taken into account only insofar as it exceeds what the taxpayer has borne himself under lid 4, so it is capped at the total car costs (autokosten) including depreciation. Car means passenger car (personenauto) or van (bestelauto), except a van that is almost exclusively suited for goods under lid 5 a. For that exception private km x the actual cost per km is settled. A declaration of exclusive business use of a van (verklaring uitsluitend zakelijk gebruik bestelauto) is possible under lid 6 to 10. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.

### 12. Private-use addition for a car (bijtelling auto), pre-2017 car

For a car first admitted on or before 31 December 2016 the minimum under art. 3.20 lid 1 a is 25% instead of 22% under art. 10a.4. In the 2020 to 2025 texts art. 10a.4 also keeps the reduction rules that applied at first registration for 60 months. It does not affect the 35% rule for old cars. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.4).

### 13. Youngtimer

When a car was first used more than 16 years ago, 35% of the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer, WEV) is the withdrawal per year. For 2026 the age threshold is 16 years. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b in the 2026 text still says "meer dan vijftien jaar" for the switch to the WEV, while lid 1 moved to 16. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies 16 years. Tax Plan 2027 art. I onderdeel E onder 2 would replace "vijftien" by "16" retroactively to 1 January 2026. See section 7. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 14. Youngtimer transition 2026

For 2026 there is an optional transition. The 2025 rule of 35% of the WEV stays for 2026 for a car that became more than 15 but less than 16 years old during 2025 and was available on or before 31 December 2025. The legal basis is the Besluit van 12 februari 2026, nr. 2026-2493, Stcrt. 2026, 6596, retroactive to 1 January 2026. It lapses on 1 January 2027 or on codification. The 2026 youngtimer decree explains that a car that became more than 15 but not more than 16 years old during 2025 is otherwise taxed at 22%, or 25% for pre-2017 cars, of the catalogue price (catalogusprijs) until it passes 16 in 2026. The approval is an optional scheme (keuzeregeling). Cars that pass 15 only during 2026 get no transition. The national government (Rijksoverheid) summarises the transition as youngtimers that turn 16 in 2026 staying under the youngtimer rule until 1 January 2027. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration says that a car used in 2025 that was older than 15 years on 31 December 2025 also falls under 35% of the WEV in 2026. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-6596.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/belastingen-op-auto-en-motor/ik-krijg-een-auto-van-de-zaak.-welke-belastingen-moet-ik-betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 15. Private-use addition for a car, 500 km rule

The car is deemed available for private use unless it is shown that private use is at most 500 km a year. Commuting is not private for income tax (inkomstenbelasting). With a trip registration or otherwise showing at most 500 private km, the withdrawal is nil. A certified trip registration system is assumed to be conclusive, though trips can still be checked. Part-year availability is pro-rated. The yearly amount is pro-rated, so 5 months gives 5/12 x 22% x catalogue value. Private km are converted to a yearly basis, so 100 km in 5 months is 240 km. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/privegebruik_auto.

### 16. Asset classification (vermogensetikettering) of a car

A car is classified as part of the mandatory business assets (verplicht ondernemingsvermogen) when used 90% or more for business, or when the entrepreneur drives more business than private kilometres (privékilometers) and at most 500 private kilometres a year. When used 90% or more privately, it is classified as part of the mandatory private assets (verplicht privévermogen). All other cases are freely classifiable assets (keuzevermogen). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel.

### 17. Value of the car

The value is the catalogue price (catalogusprijs, list price) within the meaning of art. 9 Wet BPM 1992 plus BPM. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration describes it as the original list price (cataloguswaarde) including VAT and BPM and including accessories fitted by or for the manufacturer or importer before the registration number (kenteken) was issued. The Netherlands Vehicle Authority (RDW) site carries it for most cars. For an old car the market value replaces it. The statute art. 3.20 lid 5 b keeps the words "meer dan vijftien jaar" in the 2026 and 2027 texts, while lid 1 moved to 16 in 2026 and 25 in the enacted 2027 text. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration applies 16 years for the switch to the WEV from 2026. Tax Plan 2027 would repair lid 5 b to 16 retroactively to 1 January 2026. See section 7. When the threshold is crossed mid-year the year is split by month. A charging point (laadpaal) for a zero-emission car (zero-emissie auto) included among the business assets is deductible and does not raise the catalogue price. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 5), https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privegebruik_auto/waarde_van_de_auto and https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html.

### 18. Zero-emission, first admission 2026

The 22% is reduced by a percentage of the value for a car with 0 g CO2 per km, capped in euros. For a car first admitted in 2026 the reduction is 4% off capped € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000 and 22% above. The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. After that the rules then in force (in werking) apply. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 19. Uncapped zero-emission categories 2026

Cars on hydrogen (waterstof) are uncapped in every year. Cars with integrated solar panels (zonnepanelen) are uncapped from the 2021 text, with panels of at least 1 kilowatt peak (kilowattpiek) and a battery without lead. From the 2024 text the solar test is that the panel power in watt-peak (wattpiek) divided by consumption in watt-hour (wattuur) per km is at least 7. The 2026 Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page says at least 1 kilowatt peak. That was the 2021 to 2023 text. The statute decides. See section 7. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2), https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01.

### 20. Zero-emission 60-month fixation

The reduction that applied at first admission stays fixed for 60 months from the first day of the month after first admission. For example, first admission on 7 March means start on 1 April. After the 60 months the rules then in force apply. Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2020 lose their 60-month fixation during 2025. Zero-emission cars first admitted in 2021 use 12% until the 60 months end, then the 2026 rate for the remaining months. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 11) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 21. Zero-emission rates by first admission year

By year of first admission: 2020 14% off capped € 6.300, which is 8% up to € 45.000. 2021 10% off capped € 4.000, which is 12% up to € 40.000. 2022 6% off capped € 2.100, which is 16% up to € 35.000. 2023 and 2024 6% off capped € 1.800, which is 16% up to € 30.000. 2025 5% off capped € 1.500, which is 17% up to € 30.000. 2026 4% off capped € 1.200, which is 18% up to € 30.000. 2027 text 2% off capped € 600, which is 20% up to € 30.000. Only hydrogen was uncapped in 2020. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.20 lid 2) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/bijtelling-privegebruik-auto-2026.

### 22. Private-use addition for a bicycle (bijtelling fiets)

When a bicycle, including an e-bike or a speed pedelec (speed-pedelec) that also runs on muscle power, of the business is also available privately, 7% of the recommended retail price (consumentenadviesprijs) is a withdrawal per year, capped at the costs borne. Availability for commuting counts as private availability. Subsection 5, added by Stb. 2025, 444 with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January 2020, sets the withdrawal at nil when the bicycle is stalled no more than incidentally at the home address. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.20a).

### 23. Study cost deduction (studiekostenaftrek) 2026

The personal deduction (persoonsgebonden aftrek) for training expenses (scholingsuitgaven) ended with the Act Abolishing the Tax Deduction for Training Expenses (Wet afschaffing fiscale aftrek scholingsuitgaven), which took effect on 1 January 2022 by the Besluit van 12 juli 2021, Stb. 2021, 349. In the 2022 statute art. 6.27, 6.28 and 6.30 are marked as lapsed as of 01-01-2022. Art. 10a.16 keeps the old rules for training expenses relating to study years up to and including 2014/2015 in the performance grant (prestatiebeurs) case. For 2026 FISIN narrows the case to an extended diploma period (diplomatermijn) of maximum 15 years, so only study years 2010/2011 to 2014/2015. The checked material does not restate the per-study-year caps for 2026. See section 7. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aftrek_studiekosten, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2021-349.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 10a.16).

### 24. Home workspace

Costs of a workspace including its fixtures and fittings (inrichting) in a home that is not part of the business assets are not deductible unless the workspace is a self-contained part of the home according to prevailing views (naar verkeersopvatting zelfstandig) and an income test is met. With another workspace outside the home, the combined profit, wage and income from other activities (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden) must be earned mainly in the home workspace. Without another workspace, it must be earned mainly in or from the home workspace and to a significant extent in it. Subsection 13 applies the same test to a rented home whose tenancy right (huurrecht) is included in the business assets. The official factors for being self-contained are a separate entrance or stairway (opgang), its own sanitary facilities and the possibility of renting the space to a third party (derde). A loose desk in the living room or a bedroom furnished as an office is not self-contained. From 1 January 2025 art. 3.16 lid 14 adds that costs connected with an owned home that is part of the business assets and serves as the main residence (hoofdverblijf) are not deductible when they would not be deductible under lid 1 if the home were private. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) calls this a codification of case law. For a non-self-contained (non-zelfstandige) workspace in such a home the tenant's costs (huurderslasten), fixtures and fittings such as furniture (meubilair), soft furnishings (stoffering), wallpaper and painting work (behang- en schilderwerk), and gas, water and electricity, are not deductible. Corrected for 2025 onward: the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page still presents the second route for a home that is owned and part of the business assets, without this limit. See section 7. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 (art. 3.16), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36602-3.html (MvT Belastingplan 2025, section 5.3) and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning.

### 25. Telephone subscription at home

A private telephone subscription (telefoonabonnement) is not deductible, but business calls made through it are. A business telephone subscription is deductible, but call costs for private calls on it are not. The overview page prints "Telefoonabonnement thuis" 0% and "Zakelijke telefoongesprekken thuis" 100%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 26. Equipment not in business assets

Equipment and instruments (apparatuur en instrumenten) that do not belong to the business assets are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 3. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### 27. Laptop and computer labelling

The income-tax treatment follows asset classification (vermogensetikettering). Equipment and instruments that are not part of the business assets are 0% deductible under art. 3.16 lid 3. A business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) used 10% or less for business is classified as part of the mandatory private assets, while one used 90% or more is classified as part of the mandatory business assets. Between those levels it falls within the freely classifiable assets category within reasonable bounds. On the balance sheet the asset is depreciated, and an asset with an acquisition cost (aanschafwaarde) under € 450 is expensed at once. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16 lid 3).

### 28. € 450 rule and depreciation

A purchase under € 450, or a cost that covers one year, is deducted in one go in the year it is incurred. Examples are small office supplies (kantoorartikelen), professional literature and car maintenance. A purchase of € 450 or more used longer than a year is depreciated taking the residual value (restwaarde) into account. Costs made before the start of the business are deductible when made for the work. Receipts and invoices must be kept. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten.

### 29. Other fixed exclusions (overige vaste uitsluitingen) in art. 3.16

Not deductible in every year 2020 to 2027: telephone subscriptions (telefoonabonnementen) for connections in the taxpayer's own home under lid 2 a, literature (literatuur) except professional literature under lid 2 b, clothing (kleding) except work clothing (werkkleding) under lid 2 c, work clothing defined in lid 5, personal care (persoonlijke verzorging) under lid 2 d, costs of privately owned or privately rented musical instruments, audio equipment, tools, computers and other such equipment and video equipment under lid 3, and pay to the partner when it is lower than € 5.000 under lid 4. Persons in the household are treated as the taxpayer under lid 11. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 (art. 3.16).

### 30. Fines (geldboetes) and penalty payments (dwangsommen)

Not deductible are costs connected with fines (geldboetes) from a criminal court (strafrechter), settlement payments to avoid prosecution, penalty orders (strafbeschikkingen), administrative fines (bestuurlijke boeten) and comparable foreign fines, disciplinary fines (tuchtrechtelijke boeten) and EU fines under art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel c. Also not deductible are costs connected with a criminal offence (misdrijf) for which the taxpayer was irrevocably convicted under onderdeel d, or for which a penalty order became irrevocable under onderdeel e, and penalty payments (dwangsommen) under afdeling 5.3.2 Awb under onderdeel i. The text is the same in every version 2020 to 2027. Traffic fines (verkeersboeten) under the Traffic Regulations Enforcement Act (Wahv) are caught, including payments by car-rental companies following such fines. Costs made before the fine is imposed are not costs that relate to the imposed fine and stay deductible, while costs made after imposition that relate to the fine are not deductible. For a penalty payment the line is the forfeiture (verbeurdverklaring). Costs of an internal legal department that in any way relate to imposed fines or forfeited penalty payments are also caught. Only costs connected with the criminal offence for which the taxpayer was actually convicted, or which was taken ad informandum, or settled by settlement or penalty order, are excluded. Example: convicted only for possession of cannabis plants (hennepplanten), not for trade, so only the costs of possession are excluded. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 to https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 (art. 3.14), https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2020-25430.html (sections 1.1 and 2) and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320245-aftrek-kosten-en-lasten-in-verband-met-geldboeten-en-dwangsommen/.

### 31. Public transport, taxi and aircraft

Business travel by public transport, taxi or aircraft (vliegtuig) is deductible at 100% of the actual costs (werkelijke kosten). The costs must be shown, for example with the train ticket or a public transport smart card (OV-chipkaart) trip overview. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.

### 32. Small businesses scheme (kleineondernemersregeling) turnover threshold (omzetgrens) and cost base (kostengrondslag)

Deduct costs excluding VAT when the VAT is deductible in the VAT return (btw-aangifte). When no VAT return is filed or the VAT may not be deducted, as in the small businesses scheme (kleineondernemersregeling, KOR) case, deduct the costs including VAT in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). Under the small businesses scheme no VAT is charged or paid, no VAT on business costs (bedrijfskosten, zakelijke kosten) and investments is deducted, nor is VAT paid in another EU country. Earlier deductions may need an adjustment (herziening). The low van motor vehicle tax rate stays available. Exit is possible from the first day of the next quarter and is immediate once turnover exceeds € 20.000 in a calendar year. The choice is per entrepreneur covering all VAT sub-numbers (subnummers). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/welke-kosten-mag-ik-aftrekken-van-mijn-bijverdiensten and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/hoe_werkt_de_btw/kleineondernemersregeling/wat-betekent-meedoen-met-de-kleineondernemersregeling/.

### 33. VAT flat rate (forfait) for a private car (privéauto) without mileage records (kilometeradministratie)

VAT on use and maintenance of a private car also used for business is deductible only for the part used for taxed turnover (belaste omzet). Commuting counts as private use for the VAT, though it is business for income tax. In a BV the director and substantial shareholder (directeur-grootaandeelhouder, DGA) is not the VAT entrepreneur (btw-ondernemer), he counts as an employee for this purpose. Without conclusive mileage records (zonder sluitende kilometeradministratie) all VAT may be deducted, and is corrected at year end by 1,5% of the catalogue price including VAT and BPM in rubriek 1d, pro rata for a part year and never above the VAT deducted on use and maintenance. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto/zakelijk_gebruik_van_de_priveauto.

### 34. Revising the choice and reclassification

A freely classifiable assets choice can be revised until the assessment (aanslag) for the year of the choice is irrevocable. Afterwards this is possible only on a special circumstance (bijzondere omstandigheid). Private use of an asset included in the business assets is a private withdrawal (privéonttrekking). A change in the law can be such a special circumstance when the taxpayer makes plausible that he would have chosen differently under the new rule. The knowledge group (kennisgroep) applies this to the youngtimer change. Revision is possible after the new rule takes effect on 1 January 2027. Knowledge group KG:213:2026:1, 6 March 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/vermogensetikettering-bedrijfsmiddel and https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg21320261-heretikettering-bij-wetswijziging-privegebruik-auto/.

### 35. Costs of a mixed nature (kosten met een gemengd karakter)

Costs with both a business and a personal character are deductible only for the business part, and not at all when the personal character predominates (overheerst). Examples with only a business part or a restriction are rent (huur) and maintenance of premises split into business and private parts, telephone costs (telefoonkosten), a car of the business, conferences, study trips, symposia, seminars and excursions, business lunches and dinners, receptions and other festive gatherings. Not deductible at all are a diploma with substantial benefit outside the business, for example a driving licence (rijbewijs), health costs such as a pedicure and health insurance (zorgverzekering), and personal care such as clothing and the hairdresser. Work clothing is deductible. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/zakelijk_of_prive.

### 36. Study costs (studiekosten) as a business cost

Deductible when incurred in the interest of the enterprise and only to maintain existing professional knowledge (vakkennis). Costs to acquire new knowledge are not deductible as business costs. The boundary is hard to draw. Travel and stay for courses fall under the € 1.500 cap of art. 3.16 lid 2 f. The 2022 abolition removed only the personal route. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/studiekosten-voor-ondernemers and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijke_kosten/overzicht-mogelijk-aftrekbare-zakelijke-kosten.
