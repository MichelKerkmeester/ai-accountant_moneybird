## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the income-dependent Zvw contribution (inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage Zorgverzekeringswet), a contribution toward basic health insurance that a sole trader (ZZP'er) pays on profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) through an assessment (aanslag). For 2026 the assessment rate is 4,85% over a maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) of € 79.409, while wage from employment (loon uit loondienst) carries an employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing) of 6,10%. The 2026 rates, the maximum and the base rule are enacted and verified, and the base is the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). The one gap is the state pension (AOW) age rate for the reduced income-dependent contribution (verlaagde inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage), which the checked material does not give, see section 7.
