## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Contribution by assessment (bijdrage by aanslag): 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Maximum contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen): € 75.864 in 2025 to € 79.409 in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing): 6,51% in 2025 to 6,10% in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).
- Withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage): 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The official mixed example moves with the maximum: wage of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 50.000 carried 5,26% up to the 2025 maximum of € 75.864 and carries 4,85% up to the 2026 maximum of € 79.409 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The base rule itself does not change. The 2025 material marks the base UNVERIFIED because the 2025 text was not read, and for 2026 it is settled: the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) after the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling), minus the art. 3.70 lid 2 old-age reserve amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2, both read in the 2026 version only).
- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026, and at state pension (AOW) age 50% (€ 1.235) to € 600 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/verandering_inkomstenbelasting_vorige_jaren/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2025/ondernemersaftrek-2025/zelfstandigenaftrek-2025, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Because the Zvw base sits after the entrepreneur's allowance, the size of this deduction works through to the base (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2).
- SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling): 12,7% in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Starter's deduction (startersaftrek) € 2.123 and € 1.062, discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) maximum € 3.630, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands and the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) ladder are unchanged (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve): no new additions in both years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- The Zvw base follows the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming), so the same calculation as the box 1 profit serves: profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) minus the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- Keep the records that support the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, because those deductions also lower the Zvw base, including the profit figures and the hours on which the hour-band deductions rest (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and the SME profit exemption page).
- The base is capped at € 79.409 and floored at nil, even when the profit is higher or a loss arises (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).
- Income that already bore a Zvw contribution in another channel uses up the same € 79.409. Where the entrepreneur also has wage, a pension or a periodic payment (periodieke uitkering), only the remaining part of the profit bears the 4,85% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) assessment for income tax and national insurance contributions (aanslag inkomstenbelasting en premie volksverzekeringen), so the contribution is settled there rather than through an employment payroll (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- A director and substantial shareholder (DGA) who is insured for the employee insurances (werknemersverzekeringen) has the DGA pay in the employer health insurance contribution channel, paid by the BV, and that pay uses the same € 79.409 headroom for the same person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw).
- The filing deadline is not in the checked sources for 2026 and belongs to the procedure topic (see section 7).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Declaration and deadlines: UNVERIFIED. Confirmed is only that the Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) assessment for income tax and national insurance contributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The further statements that the same tax return (aangifte) is used, that a provisional Zvw assessment exists and that the Zvw follows the income tax filing timeline come from earlier notes and were not re-opened. The filing deadline belongs to the procedure topic. A read of the 2026 Tax and Customs Administration pages on the tax return and the Zvw assessment, and of the Zvw procedural articles, would settle it.
- Zvw rate at state pension (AOW) age: unknown. https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-dependent contribution, and no state pension age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence and not a stated rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages read do not mention the state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). No 2026 percentage for the reduced contribution (verlaagde bijdrage) is in the checked material. The 2026 ministerial regulation that sets the Zvw percentages, including the state pension age rate, would settle it.
- Scope of the base rule: Zvw art. 43 and Wet IB 2001 art. 3.2 were read in the 2026 version only (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The base rule for a profit entrepreneur (winst-ondernemer) is verified for 2026 and UNVERIFIED for the other years in this series.
- Item scope: "wage from a foreign employer" is listed as an assessment item on the 2020 to 2024 pages, and the 2026 page read for this check does not repeat it (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The 2026 page text would settle whether the item still stands.
- No conflict between the 2026 Zvw sources was found in this check.
