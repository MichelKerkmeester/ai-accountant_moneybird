## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Income-dependent Zvw contribution by (provisional) assessment, 4,85%

The Zvw contribution has three channels. This file's subject is the third channel, the contribution by (provisional) assessment (voorlopige aanslag), meaning the contribution that is settled by the final or (provisional) assessment (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). An income tax entrepreneur (IB-ondernemer) with profit from the Netherlands (winst uit Nederland) pays through this channel rather than through a payroll. The same channel covers income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), wage from a foreign employer, pension from a foreign benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) and periodic payments (periodieke uitkeringen) without wage tax such as spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The item "wage from a foreign employer" is stated on the 2020 to 2024 pages, and the 2026 page read for this check does not repeat it. The conversion of an old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) into an annuity (lijfrente) is expressly not part of this channel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

The rate for 2026 is 4,85% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Zvw art. 45 lets the minister set the percentages, and Zvw art. 43 lid 3 sets the yearly maximum of the contribution income by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) assessment for income tax and national insurance contributions (premie volksverzekeringen) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet), so the entrepreneur meets the contribution on the same assessment as the income tax, while the contribution itself follows its own Zvw calculation.

The filing route and the filing deadline are not in the checked sources for 2026 and stay UNVERIFIED, see section 7.

### 3.2 Maximum contribution income of € 79.409

The contribution income is at least nil and no more than a yearly maximum set by ministerial regulation (Zvw art. 43 lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). For 2026 that maximum is € 79.409 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). A loss or a nil base therefore gives no contribution, and the floor keeps a negative base out of the calculation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

The maximum applies once per person and counts across the three channels. Income that already bore a Zvw contribution elsewhere uses up the headroom, so only the remaining part of the profit bears the 4,85% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The official example on the 2026 page takes wage of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 50.000 and charges 4,85% over € 39.409 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Section 4 works this out.

### 3.3 Withheld contribution, 4,85%

The withheld contribution (ingehouden bijdrage) is withheld from pension, annuity and other benefits that carry payroll taxes (loonheffing) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2026 the rate is 4,85%, the same as the assessment rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). This channel uses the same € 79.409 headroom as the others, so a pension or an annuity payment can use up room before the profit has borne the full 4,85% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.4 Zvw employer health insurance contribution, 6,10%

The employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing) is paid by the employer or the benefit agency over wage from employment, director and substantial shareholder (DGA) pay when the director is insured for the employee insurances (werknemersverzekeringen), social assistance (bijstand), early-retirement benefits from before 2006 for people who were insured with a health insurance fund (ziekenfonds) in 2005, and WAO, WIA and WW benefits (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). For 2026 the rate is 6,10% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

The difference for an entrepreneur: profit from business activities never carries the 6,10% employer health insurance contribution, it carries the 4,85% assessment rate (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw). The channels differ in who pays and at which percentage, but the maximum of € 79.409 is shared by the same person. Wage that already carried the employer health insurance contribution uses the headroom, and DGA pay of a BV where the director is insured for the employee insurances does the same (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.5 Zvw base for a profit entrepreneur

Zvw art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b brings the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) determined under afdeling 3.2 Wet IB 2001 into the contribution, except the amount added under art. 3.70 lid 2 for a decrease of the old-age reserve (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines the taxable profit as the profit minus the entrepreneur's allowance minus the SME profit exemption (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.2). The Zvw base of a profit entrepreneur is therefore the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance and after the SME profit exemption, at least nil and no more than € 79.409 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01).

The components that shape that base in 2026 are the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 1.200, or € 600 at state pension (AOW) age, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) of € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) of € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000 and then € 0, capped at the profit, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) of 1,25%, 2%, 3% and 4% over the hour bands, the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) with a maximum of € 3.630, and the SME profit exemption of 12,7% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and the SME profit exemption page). The order is stated as: "Om de mkb-winstvrijstelling te bepalen, moet u deze winst eerst verminderen met de ondernemersaftrek" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Two further conditions: the self-employed person's deduction cannot exceed the profit before the entrepreneur's allowance unless the starter's deduction applies, and the SME profit exemption reduces a loss, which works against the taxpayer (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and the SME profit exemption page).

Who counts as an entrepreneur matters too, because the base is the taxable profit from business activities. Profit from business activities requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts, and registration in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) alone does not decide (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) leave the profit first, such as the forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), the agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the debt waiver profit (kwijtscheldingswinst) exemption for the part above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business termination payments (vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (Waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, benefits under a final levy (eindheffing), and the mobility project and peak avoidance project exemptions (mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming). A person entitled to a share in the profit (medegerechtigde) or a lender (geldverstrekker) receives neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption, so those reductions are absent from that income (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming).

### 3.6 Exceptions

Four exceptions go with the income-dependent contribution. Military personnel (militairen) in active service or on special leave (buitengewoon verlof) with retention of military income. Share fishermen (deelvissers) where another party pays 30% or more of the care costs of a sea trip. A correction for income that already bore a foreign statutory health contribution. And a reduction for the part of the year the person was insured in the Netherlands (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The sources state these as exceptions to the income-dependent contribution as a whole rather than to one channel only (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.7 Old-age reserve in the Zvw base

Since 2023 no new old-age reserve can be formed, and an existing reserve may stay on the balance sheet (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). Reduction or release of an existing reserve follows on buying an annuity with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen), on cessation, on reaching state pension age on 1 January, on failing the hours criterion (urencriterium) in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve). For the Zvw base the art. 3.70 lid 2 amount is excluded (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). Because 2026 allows no new additions, no addition arises in 2026, and an existing reserve only matters when it moves under one of the listed grounds. The conversion of an old-age reserve into an annuity is expressly not part of the assessment channel (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

### 3.8 Zvw rate at state pension age

https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2 states that self-employed persons (zelfstandigen) and pensioners (gepensioneerden) pay the reduced income-dependent contribution (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/xvi-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport-rijksbegroting-2027/16-volksgezondheid-welzijn-en-sport.pdf Table 77 footnote 2). No state pension age exemption appears in Zvw art. 41 to 45 as read, which is an inference from absence and not a stated rule (https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0018450&hoofdstuk=4&paragraaf=4.2&artikel=43&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01). The FISIN Zvw pages read for this check do not mention the state pension age (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet to https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The checked sources give no 2026 percentage for that reduced contribution, so this file cannot state the rate an entrepreneur pays from the state pension age. Section 7 lists what would settle it.

### 3.9 Declaration of the Zvw contribution

The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) assessment for income tax and national insurance contributions (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). Older notes state that the same tax return (aangifte) is used, that a provisional Zvw assessment exists and that the Zvw follows the income tax filing timeline, but that was not re-opened in this check and stays UNVERIFIED (see section 7). The deadline itself belongs to the procedure topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
