## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Example 1: wage plus profit (Official example, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).**
The 2026 page works with wage (loon) of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 50.000. Step 1: the two amounts total € 90.000 (€ 40.000 plus € 50.000). Step 2: the contribution income (bijdrage-inkomen) is capped at € 79.409. Step 3: the wage of € 40.000 already fills part of that room, so the remaining headroom is € 79.409 minus € 40.000, which equals € 39.409. Step 4: the 4,85% applies to € 39.409 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The same page states that wage of € 80.000 plus freelance income of € 15.000 gives no contribution at all, because the wage of € 80.000 lies above the maximum of € 79.409 and leaves no headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

**Example 2: only profit, no other income (Calculated example).**
Inputs: a profit (winst) of € 60.000, no wage, no pension, no other contribution income, and the 2026 self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) of € 1.200 applies. Step 1: profit before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) is € 60.000. Step 2: € 60.000 minus € 1.200 is € 58.800. Step 3: the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) is 12,7% of € 58.800, and € 58.800 times 12,7 / 100 is € 7.467,60. Step 4: the taxable profit (belastbare winst) is € 58.800 minus € 7.467,60, which equals € 51.332,40. Step 5: no old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) addition exists in 2026, so there is no art. 3.70 lid 2 amount to leave out. Step 6: the base lies between nil and € 79.409, so the full € 51.332,40 is the contribution income. Step 7: the Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) charged through an assessment (aanslag) is 4,85% of € 51.332,40, calculated as € 51.332,40 times 4,85 / 100 = € 2.489,62. The same € 51.332,40 is the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) in box 1.

**Example 3: wage plus profit (Calculated example).**
Inputs: wage of € 50.000 from an employer who pays the 6,10% employer health insurance contribution (werkgeversheffing Zvw), plus a taxable profit of € 40.000. Step 1: the wage uses headroom against the € 79.409 maximum, leaving € 79.409 minus € 50.000 = € 29.409. Step 2: the Zvw contribution charged through an assessment on the profit is 4,85% of € 29.409, calculated as € 29.409 times 4,85 / 100 = € 1.426,34. Step 3: the part of the profit above the headroom is € 40.000 minus € 29.409 = € 10.591. That amount bears no contribution because the maximum applies once per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

**Example 4: profit above the maximum (Calculated example).**
Inputs: a taxable profit of € 100.000 and no other income. Step 1: the maximum is € 79.409, so the contribution income is € 79.409 and not € 100.000. Step 2: 4,85% of € 79.409 is € 3.851,34, calculated as € 79.409 times 4,85 / 100. This is the maximum contribution for this income at this rate. Step 3: the remaining amount is € 100.000 minus € 79.409 = € 20.591, which bears no Zvw contribution.

No example can be built for the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) rate, because the checked sources give no 2026 percentage for the reduced income-dependent contribution (verlaagde inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage) (see section 7).
