## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

From 1 January 2023 the Bridge Act box 3 (Overbruggingswet box 3) applies the category method (categorieënmethode) to everyone. The 2026 and later texts of the act still carry the category method in art. 5.2. The Actual Return Box 3 Act (Wet werkelijk rendement box 3) is not law on 11 October 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) says the fictitious system stays until new legislation takes effect, probably 1 January 2028. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box

The 2026 calculation runs in six steps. Step 1 taxable return (belastbaar rendement) = bank balances (banktegoeden) x bank percentage plus other assets (overige bezittingen) x other-assets percentage minus (debts (schulden) minus threshold (drempel)) x debt percentage. A negative return is set at nil. Art. 4.14 lid 1 keeps the flat-rate benefit (forfaitair voordeel) at least nil. Step 2 return base (rendementsgrondslag) = assets (bezittingen) minus (debts minus threshold). Step 3 savings and investments tax base (grondslag sparen en beleggen) = return base minus tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen). Partners may divide it in any way that totals 100%. Step 4 share (aandeel) = tax base / return base x 100. Step 5 benefit (voordeel) = taxable return x share. Step 6 tax = rate x benefit. The 2026 rate is 36%. The rounding instruction for step 4 differs per year. The 2023 and 2025 pages say 2 decimals. The 2024 and 2026 pages say 3 decimals. The 2026 examples show 2 decimals. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2023 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025

Background for 2026 is the Supreme Court of the Netherlands (Hoge Raad) ruling of 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting) to the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act (Wet rechtsherstel box 3) describes the ruling as a breach of art. 1 EP in connection with art. 14 EVRM. The flat-rate system from 2017 cannot pass the proportionality test. Flat rates (forfaits) are allowed but must approach reality as closely as possible. The Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 533. The Bridge Act box 3 is the law of 21 December 2022, Stb. 2022, 534, in force (in werking) 1 January 2023. The Supreme Court held on 6 June 2024 that the Box 3 Restoration of Rights Act and the Bridge Act box 3 still conflict with the European Convention on Human Rights (EVRM) when the flat-rate return (forfaitair rendement) is higher than the actual return. The Supreme Court held on 25 June 2026 that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 have no right to recovery. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2024

### Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen)

The tax-free allowance for 2026 is € 59.357 per person. It doubles to € 118.714 with a fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) for the whole year, or for part of the year when the partners choose whole-year partnership. Art. 5.2 lid 5 uses the share of the joint tax base allocated under art. 2.17. Partners may divide the tax base in any way that totals 100%. The amount reduces the return base in step 3 of the calculation. In the actual return option the Supreme Court has determined that no account may be taken of the tax-free allowance. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Debt threshold (schuldendrempel)

Only the part of total debts above the threshold is deductible. The amount for 2026 is € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner. The Tax and Customs Administration computes the threshold in the return. Statute art. 5.3 lid 3 says the deductible part is the amount by which total debts exceed the threshold. The amount is indexed under art. 10.1. A joint threshold needs both partners' signatures on the return or returns. In steps 1 and 2 the calculation uses debts minus threshold. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Box 3 rate (box 3 tarief)

The box 3 rate for 2026 is 36%. It is applied in step 6 to the benefit. The same rate applies to the fictitious benefit and to the actual return when the actual return is lower and is reported. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

### Bank percentage

The bank percentage for 2026 is 1,28% provisional. It applies to bank balances on the reference date (peildatum) 1 January 2026. The 2026 calculation page says the percentages for bank balances and debts are still provisional and will be fixed at the start of 2027. The statute text of 1 January 2026 still printed 1,44% as a carry-over. Art. 10.6ter lid 2 fixes the final bank percentage after the year, with retroactive effect (terugwerkende kracht) to 1 January. It is the average of the monthly return of De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) on household deposits with a notice period of up to three months over the eleven months from 1 January to 30 November, with November counted twice. The sum is divided by twelve. The final figure can therefore differ from 1,28%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Other-assets percentage

The other-assets percentage for 2026 is 6,00% fixed. It applies to other assets on the reference date. Art. 10.6ter lid 3 replaces the figure at the start of the year by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling). The figure is set at 53% of the long-term return on real estate plus 33% on shares plus 14% on bonds. The 2026 statute prints 6%. Taxable green investments (groene beleggingen) above the exemption count as other assets at 6,00%. A second home counts as an other asset. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds

### Debt percentage

The debt percentage for 2026 is 2,70% provisional. It applies only to the part of debts above the debt threshold. Art. 10.6ter lid 4 uses the same method as the bank percentage on the DNB average monthly interest rate on the total outstanding household home mortgages. The final figure is due by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) early 2027. The 2026 statute text printed 2,61% as a carry-over. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01

### Green investment exemption (vrijstelling groene beleggingen)

The exemption for green investments (groene beleggingen) is € 26.715 per person and € 53.430 with a whole-year partner. Art. 5.13 says green investments are not counted as assets up to that amount. Art. 5.13 lid 2, from the 2023 text, applies the exemption first to green other assets and only the remaining room to green bank balances. The exemption never exceeds the value of the green investments on 1 January. A minor child must file its own return to claim it and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. The excess counts as other assets at 6,00%. Tax Plan 2026 (Belastingplan 2026), Stb. 2025, 444, art. XLI lid 3 kept art. 5.5 out of the special 2026 indexation factor 1,027782. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

### Green investment credit (korting groene beleggingen)

The green investment credit is 0,1% for 2026. Art. 8.19 sets it at 0,1% of the exempt amount on the reference date. The Tax and Customs Administration computes it automatically. With whole-year partners it is split in the same ratio as the green balance. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/korting_voor_groene_beleggingen

### Own use of real estate, fixed method

Art. 5.27a applies from 1 January 2026. It sets the benefit from own use of real estate at the economic rent value per year. Days the property is let or leased, under construction or unusable are excluded. The Tax and Customs Administration offers a fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) times usable days divided by 365. This 5,06% method is a Tax and Customs Administration calculation method on its actual-return page. It is not in the text of Stb. 2025, 195. Days of own use must be tracked. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### WOZ reference date (peildatum) for the fixed method

The WOZ value used in the 2026 fixed method has reference date 1 January 2025. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement

### Own use of real estate start date

Art. II of Stb. 2025, 195 makes art. 5.27a apply from 1 January 2026. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag) 2026

The Tax and Customs Administration cannot take the actual return into account in the 2026 provisional assessment. The actual return is adjusted in the 2026 tax return (aangifte). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

### Share rounding (aandeel)

Step 4 of the 2026 calculation page says "Rond af op 3 decimalen achter de komma". The official examples on the same page show 2 decimals and cut off rather than round. Examples are 90.643 / 150.000 = 60,4287% shown as 60,42%, 15.643 / 150.000 shown as 10,42%, and 269.443 / 328.800 = 81,947% shown as 81,94%. The conflict is on the same page. Rounding changes the benefit and the tax. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026

The final 2026 bank and debt percentages are unknown on 11 October 2026. They are due by ministerial regulation early 2027 under art. 10.6ter lid 2 and lid 4. ### Actual-return reporting 2026

Reporting the actual return is optional. The Tax and Customs Administration uses the most favourable amount and the taxpayer never pays more than the fictitious calculation. From 2025 the report is made in the income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting). For 2026 that is the 2026 tax return. No deadline is stated on the reporting pages. For earlier years the route differed. 2017 to 2019 used a form directly with DigiD outside My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). 2020 to 2024 used an online form in My Tax and Customs Administration, with a paper form available. 2021 to 2024 could always be filed without a letter. 2017 to 2020 all letters have been sent, and without a letter the taxpayer may not report. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/zonder-brief-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-invullen

### Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3) dates

The Box 3 Rebuttal Scheme Act is the law of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 195. It entered into force (in werking trad) by the decree (besluit) of 14 July 2025, Stb. 2025, 196, issued 18 July 2025, in force the day after issue, so 19 July 2025. The Senate adopted it on 8 July 2025. Art. V says arts. I and III work back to 1 January 2023 and art. IV works back to 1 January 2017. Art. II, on own use of real estate in art. 5.27a, applies from 1 January 2026. Art. 5.25 lid 1 says that if the taxpayer makes plausible that the actual return is lower than the benefit, the income is the actual return minus the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek). Subsection 2 sets the actual return at least nil. Subsections 4 and 5 say whole-year partners use the joint actual return. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3

### Actual-return documents deadline

Art. 5.25 lid 6 uses a form provided by the tax inspector (inspecteur), in deviation from the General Administrative Law Act (Algemene wet bestuursrecht, Awb) arts. 4:1, 4:2 lid 1, 6:4 lid 1 and 6:5. Subsection 7 allows further documents up to six weeks after the form is sent. Subsection 8 says subsections 6 and 7 do not apply when the data were asked in the invitation to file. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html

### Reference date arbitrage (peildatumarbitrage) window

Art. 5.24 disregards transactions around 1 January within a three-month continuous period that begins before and ends after the reference date, unless the taxpayer makes plausible that business reasons (zakelijke overwegingen) lay behind them. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
