## 5. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Heffingsvrij vermogen: € 57.684 to € 59.357 per person. With a fiscal partner € 115.368 to € 118.714. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/heffingsvrij-vermogen
- Schuldendrempel: € 3.800 per person and € 7.600 with a whole-year partner in both years. No change. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- Box 3 tarief: 36% in 2025 and 36% in 2026. No change. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- Bank percentage: the 2025 statute first printed 1,44%. A regeling of 12 February 2026 replaced it with 1,37%, back to 1 January 2025. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/met-welke-percentages-is-het-fictief-rendement-berekend. For 2026 the calculation page states 1,28% provisionally. The 2026 statute text still printed 1,44% as a carry-over. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- Other-assets percentage: 5,88% in 2025 to 6,00% in 2026. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- Debt percentage: the 2025 statute first printed 2,61%. A regeling of 12 February 2026 replaced it with 2,70%, back to 1 January 2025. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-3708.html. For 2026 the calculation page states 2,70% provisionally. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- Vrijstelling groene beleggingen: € 26.312 to € 26.715 per person. With a whole-year partner € 52.624 to € 53.430. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- Korting groene beleggingen: 0,1% in 2025 and 0,1% in 2026. No change. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- Aandeel rounding: 2 decimals on the 2025 page to an instruction of 3 decimals with 2-decimal examples on the 2026 page. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025 https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- Own use real estate: art. 5.27a applies from 1 January 2026. The statute sets the economic rent value per year. Belastingdienst adds a fixed method of 5,06% of the WOZ value with peildatum 1 January 2025 times usable days divided by 365. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- Actual-return route: in 2025 the report is in the aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2025 and it is optional. In 2026 the voorlopige aanslag cannot use the actual return and it is adjusted in the aangifte 2026. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3: law of 14 July 2025, issued 18 July 2025, in force 19 July 2025. Art. II applies from 1 January 2026. Eerste Kamer vote on 8 July 2025. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-196.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- Wet werkelijk rendement box 3: still a bill, not law on 11 October 2026. Tax year 2026 runs on the Overbruggingswet box 3. Source: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the peildatum values on 1 January 2026 for banktegoeden, overige bezittingen and schulden. These values feed steps 1 and 2 of the 2026 calculation. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- Keep the split between banktegoeden, overige bezittingen, green investments and schulden. Each category has its own percentage and the green exemption has its own order. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- For own-use real estate, keep the WOZ value with peildatum 1 January 2025 and a day count of own use, days let or leased, days under construction and days unusable. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-195.html
- If the actual return is reported, keep a calendar-year record of income and value changes, including regular benefits and vermogensaanwas. Costs are not deductible, except an investment in a second home that raised the WOZ value and was reported to the municipality, and interest paid on a box 3 debt. A negative total is € 0 and cannot be carried to another year. No heffingsvrij vermogen is taken into account. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement
- Report in the aangifte 2026. The voorlopige aanslag 2026 cannot include the actual return. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026
- For a joint schuldendrempel both partners sign the return or returns. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_zijn_uw_bezittingen_en_schulden/uw_schulden/drempel
- Record transactions around 1 January 2026. The three-month peildatumarbitrage rule can disregard them unless business reasons are plausible. Source: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-534.html
- For green investments, a minor child must file its own return to claim the exemption and the parents report the rest as a normal asset. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/beleggen_met_belastingvoordeel/groene_beleggingen

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Aandeel rounding conflict. The 2026 calculation page instruction says round to 3 decimals. The official examples on the same page show 2 decimals and cut off rather than round. The page gives 90.643 / 150.000 = 60,4287% shown as 60,42%, 15.643 / 150.000 shown as 10,42%, and 269.443 / 328.800 = 81,947% shown as 81,94%. Both are on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026. The 2026 aangifte software or a corrected page would settle it.
- Final 2026 bank and debt percentages are unknown. The 2026 calculation page carries 1,28% and 2,70% provisionally. The 2026 statute text still printed 1,44% and 2,61% as carry-over under art. 10.6ter. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026 https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01. A ministerial regulation early 2027 under art. 10.6ter lid 2 and lid 4 would settle it.
- The 5,06% own-use method is a Belastingdienst calculation method, not a statutory figure. The statute text of Stb. 2025, 195 sets the economic rent value per year. The 5,06% method is on https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement. A regulation or a corrected source attribution would settle it.
- Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, bill 36 748, is proposed and not law on 11 October 2026. There was no Eerste Kamer vote and no Staatsblad on 11 October 2026. Entry into force is by royal decree, with a rule referring to 1 January 2028. Source: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- The Cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 contains a novelle to bill 36 748. It proposes a vermogenswinstbelasting for all financial instruments from 2028, about 90% of assets with value development, with a full switch for the other assets in 2030. It proposes a box 3 heffingsvrij vermogen back to the 2020 level of € 30.846 from 2027, a heffingsvrij resultaat of € 1.000 from 2028 and an increase of the forfait for overige bezittingen, including rent and own-use benefits, by 1,5 percentage point in 2027. The novelle must be adopted by the Eerste Kamer before 31 December, and the Cabinet intends to ask the Raad van State for urgent advice. The Kamerstuk number is UNVERIFIED. The number "nr. 62" and a Raad van State request on 2 October 2026 were not found on a fetched page. Sources: [Kamerstuk 37020 nr. 62 (expected), brief regering](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D47255) https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box
- Green facilities 2027 conflict. The Rijksoverheid page says both facilities lapse completely on 1 January 2027. The enacted 2027 statute text and Stb. 2025, 444 say the 2027 amount is € 200 per person and € 400 for partners, with a 0,1% korting, and move the repeal of afdeling 5.3 and art. 8.19 to 1 January 2028. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/duurzame-economie/groen-sparen-in-een-groenfonds https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-444.html. The enacted text or a later law would settle it.
- Wet rechtsherstel box 3 entry date conflict. The Rijksoverheid timeline says the law "trad op 1 januari 2023 in werking". The statute art. 7 says it entered into force the day after the issue date of 27 December 2022, so 28 December 2022, with retroactive effect to 1 January 2017. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3 https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-533.html. The statute text settles it.
- Kerstarrest 24 December 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1963, the claim that the Hoge Raad itself ordered rechtsherstel on the basis of the actual return stays UNVERIFIED because hogeraad.nl returned HTTP 403 on 11 October 2026. The memorie van toelichting and the Rijksoverheid timeline confirm the ruling and the EVRM basis. Sources: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36203-3.html https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3. A reachable Hoge Raad text would settle it.
- Hoge Raad 25 June 2026. The Rijksoverheid timeline confirms that taxpayers who did not object for 2017 to 2020 "hebben geen recht op herstel van hun betaalde box 3-belasting over deze periode". The "or objected late" part is not on that page and stays UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/box-3/tijdlijn-rechtsherstel-box-3
- The first Belastingplan 2026 proposal of € 51.396 and 7,78% is proposed and not law. The 2026 statute prints € 59.357 and 6%. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01
- No deadline is stated on the actual-return reporting pages. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/werkelijk-rendement-belastingaangifte https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement. A Belastingdienst page or the aangifte instructions with a deadline would settle it.
