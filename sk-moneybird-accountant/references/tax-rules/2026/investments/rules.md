## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### 3.1 Small-scale investment deduction (KIA, kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), table

The KIA is a per business (per-onderneming) deduction. It is claimed by choice in the return (aangifte), on the total investment in business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen) in the calendar year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41). For 2026 the table has five bands. Up to € 2.900 the deduction is nil. From € 2.901 up to € 71.683 the deduction is 28% of the investment amount (investeringsbedrag). From € 71.683 up to € 132.746 the deduction is a fixed € 20.072. From € 132.746 up to € 398.236 the deduction is € 20.072 minus 7,56% of the part above € 132.746. Above € 398.236 the deduction is nil (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) phrases the 2026 window as "moet u een bedrag tussen € 2.901 en € 398.236 investeren" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026). The table is a step function, so the percentage in the second band applies to the whole investment and the amounts in the third and fourth bands are absolute amounts.

In a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) the investments of the partnership and the taxpayer's own investments are added, the table is applied to that sum, and the result is multiplied by the taxpayer's own investment amount divided by the sum (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 3). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration KIA page adds that each partner (firmant or vennoot) takes a share of the investment, normally in proportion to the profit (winst) or excess profit (overwinst), and that another reasonable key is allowed if it is applied consistently (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek_kia).

If an asset is not in use at the end of the calendar year, the deduction is taken over an amount equal to the payments made, and the excess follows in later years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 1). At cessation (staking) of the business (onderneming) the full investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) is taken in the year of cessation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.44 lid 2). This payment rule applies to the KIA, the EIA and the MIA.

The KIA can be combined with the EIA or with the MIA on the same asset, but the EIA and the MIA cannot be combined with each other for one asset (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

### 3.2 KIA per-asset minimum of € 450

An asset with an investment amount below € 450 does not count for the KIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel b). The same lid excludes for the KIA assets that are intended directly or indirectly mainly to be made available to third parties (onderdeel a).

Art. 3.45 lid 1 excludes for every investment deduction assets intended mainly for use in a business whose profit falls under a double taxation relief scheme (regeling ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting) (onderdeel a), land (gronden) including the ground under buildings except land improvement (grondverbetering) (b), dwellings (woonhuizen) and houseboats (woonschepen) (c), passenger cars (personenauto's) as defined in art. 3 Wet BPM 1992 (d), vessels (vaartuigen) of art. 3.14 lid 1 onderdeel b (e), securities, receivables (vorderingen), goodwill, permits (vergunningen), exemptions (ontheffingen) and concessions (concessies) (f), and animals (dieren) (g). Lid 3 makes an exception for the MIA, where lid 1 parts (onderdelen) c and d do not apply, so dwellings and passenger cars can still earn MIA. Lid 4 adds for the EIA and MIA the exclusions in section 3.5. Lid 5 lets the Minister disapply lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° and lid 4 onderdeel a. These amounts are the same in every version of the article from 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45).

Art. 3.46 excludes obligations between the taxpayer and members of the household (a), blood or in-law relatives in the direct line (rechte lijn) or their household members (b), heirs to an estate the asset belongs to (c), and a person holding at least one third of an entity (lichaam) and that entity (d). The Minister can disapply lid 1, which is the exemption (ontheffing) route (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.46). The FISIN 2026 chapter states that buying from "de bv waarvan u voor ten minste een derde deel aandeelhouder bent" gives "in principe geen recht op investeringsaftrek" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### 3.3 EIA percentage of 40%

The EIA is 40% of the investment amount (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must appear on the Energy List (Energielijst) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). Only new assets qualify, meaning assets that have not been used before. A second-hand, revised, refurbished or rebuilt asset does not qualify. Costs to adapt a second-hand asset to the Environmental List (Milieulijst) requirements can qualify, and so can second-hand parts bought from several suppliers and installed by the entrepreneur himself, as production costs (voortbrengingskosten) (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). RVO treats a vehicle as new if it was used for less than 6 months and at most 6.000 km (same URL).

Other subsidies for the same asset reduce the investment amount. Costs that already received EIA cannot also receive MIA, while Vamil remains possible (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). From 2026 the investments in a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) and in a partnership such as a general partnership (vof) are added together for the EIA maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). The investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months, see section 3.11.

### 3.4 EIA and MIA maximum investment amount of € 153.000.000

For 2026 the investment amount on which the EIA and the MIA can be granted is capped at € 153.000.000 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4). The FISIN 2026 chapter prints the amount as "€ 153 miljoen" and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration EIA page states the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). From 2026 the EIA investments in a sole proprietorship and in a partnership are added together for this maximum (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia).

For MIA and Vamil RVO publishes a separate and much lower yearly maximum of € 25 million per business asset (bedrijfsmiddel) and per applicant, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). That page does not state a year, so the value is UNVERIFIED here and is repeated in section 7.

### 3.5 EIA and MIA per-asset minimum of € 2.500

Art. 3.45 lid 4 adds for the EIA and the MIA that assets made available mainly to non-residents or to foreign enterprises do not qualify (onderdeel a) and that assets with an investment amount below € 2.500 do not qualify (onderdeel b). The € 2.500 floor also appears in the FISIN 2026 chapter and on the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration EIA page (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.45 lid 4 onderdeel b, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/energie_investeringsaftrek_eia). RVO phrases the same floor for MIA and Vamil as "minimaal € 2.500 per aanvraag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### 3.6 MIA percentages

The MIA is 45% for category I (categorie I), 36% for category II and 27% for category III (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The asset must appear on the Environmental List. The percentages changed from 36% / 27% / 13,5% in the 2021 text of art. 3.42a lid 3 to 45% / 36% / 27% with effect from 1 January 2022, and that has been the position since. The losing figures of 36% / 27% / 13,5% in the FISIN 2022 and 2023 chapters are settled to the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.42a lid 3, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/09/21/belastingplan-2022-beter-belastingstelsel, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023).

### 3.7 MIA and Vamil code mapping from the Environmental List

From 1 January 2022 the Environmental List maps the code of an environmental asset (milieu-bedrijfsmiddel) to a MIA category. Codes starting with F or G belong to category I at 45%. Codes starting with A or D belong to category II at 36%. Codes starting with B or E belong to category III at 27%. Codes starting with A, B, C or F qualify for 75% Vamil (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-47737.html, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023). The Environmental List version that applies is the one valid on the date the purchase agreement (koopovereenkomst) is signed or the order is placed (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden).

### 3.8 MIA and Vamil budget 2026

For 2026 the RVO budget is € 135.000.000 for the MIA and € 20.000.000 for the Vamil (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). Both are enacted and published. The checked 2025 material states no budget figure, so no old value is given for these amounts in section 5.

### 3.9 MIA and Vamil maximum per business asset and per applicant per year

RVO states a maximum of € 25 million per business asset and per applicant per year for MIA and Vamil, unless the Environmental List sets a lower maximum (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The checked page states no year for this cap. It is a condition of the designation (aanwijzing) and is separate from the statutory € 153.000.000 maximum of art. 3.42 lid 4 for the EIA and MIA (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.42 lid 4). Because the year is not stated, the value is unknown and UNVERIFIED in this file and is listed again in section 7.

### 3.10 Vamil percentage of 75%

Art. 3.31 lid 1 states that the costs of a designated environmental asset "kunnen door de belastingplichtige voor 75 percent willekeurig worden afgeschreven". The remaining part of the cost follows the normal depreciation schedule. Art. 3.30a lid 1, the minimum value (bodemwaarde) limit, does not apply to these assets (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.31). Only assets that are not yet common in the Netherlands, that have not been used before and that materially reduce environmental harm can be designated (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The same 75% appears in the 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027 texts of the article (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.31, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.31).

Vamil can be used together with the MIA on the same asset, and it remains possible on costs that already received EIA. RVO states that the total tax benefit (belastingvoordeel) of MIA and Vamil combined can be "netto oplopen tot ruim 14% van het investeringsbedrag" (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers). The investment must be notified to RVO within 3 months, see section 3.11.

### 3.11 RVO notification deadline for EIA, MIA and Vamil

For acquisition costs (aanschafkosten) the investment must be reported to RVO within 3 months after signing the purchase agreement or placing the order. For production costs the report must be made within 3 months after the quarter in which those costs were made. Environmental advisory costs (milieu-advieskosten) are reported together with those costs under the same deadlines. The Environmental List that is valid on the signing or order date applies (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers/voorwaarden). The FISIN chapters for 2020 to 2026 all state "U meldt uw investering binnen 3 maanden bij de Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO)" (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The same report covers the EIA, the MIA and the Vamil (https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mia-vamil/ondernemers).

### 3.12 Ordinary depreciation caps

The yearly depreciation is the part of the not yet depreciated acquisition or production costs (aanschaffings- of voortbrengingskosten) that is attributable to the year (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30 lid 1). Per year at most 10% of the cost may be written off for goodwill and at most 20% for other business assets, so the minimum periods are 10 years for goodwill and 5 years for other business assets (overige bedrijfsmiddelen) (lid 2). Production costs of intangible assets (immateriële activa) may be written off at once in the year of production (voortbrenging) (lid 3). Objects of small value (voorwerpen van geringe waarde) that are normally treated as current costs are written off at once in the year of acquisition or production (lid 4). The same text applies in 2020 to 2027 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2024-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 art. 3.30, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.30).

### 3.13 Minimum value of a building

Depreciation on a building is allowed only while its book value is above the minimum value, and at most up to the difference (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 1). A building, its parts, the ground under it and appurtenances (aanhorigheden) form one business asset, while detachable machinery (werktuigen) are separate assets (lid 2). From 2024 the minimum value is "de WOZ-waarde van het gebouw" (lid 3), except for an own-use building taken into use before 1 January 2024 and not yet depreciated for 3 years, which keeps 50% of the WOZ value (WOZ-waarde) until 3 years after first use (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/afschrijving_bedrijfspand). In 2020 to 2023 the minimum value was the WOZ value for an investment building (gebouw ter belegging) and 50% of the WOZ value for another, own use, building (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2021-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2022-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 3). The WOZ value is the value set by decision (beschikking) under the Valuation of Immovable Property Act (Wet WOZ) for the calendar year of the depreciation (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a lid 4). The 2026 text also has lid 5 for a co-owned building pro rata, lid 6 where the ground is owned by an unrelated party which reduces the WOZ value, and lid 7 to 10 on related persons (verbonden personen) and related entity (verbonden lichaam) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.30a).

### 3.14 Starter's arbitrary depreciation (starters willekeurige afschrijving), yearly cap of € 398.236

The statutory basis is art. 3.34, which allows arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) on assets designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling) that serve economic development, including entrepreneurship (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.34). Under art. 7 lid 1 of the URWA the designated assets are assets for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in a calendar year in which the increased self-employed deduction (verhoogde zelfstandigenaftrek) of art. 3.76 lid 3, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek), applies (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9). Lid 2 adds the year immediately before such a year if the ordinary self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) does not apply in that earlier year. Lid 3 restricts the scheme to costs made in a business from which the taxpayer earns profit as entrepreneur (ondernemer). Art. 8 allows arbitrary depreciation "voor zover het gezamenlijke bedrag van die kosten in het kalenderjaar niet uitgaat boven het in de tabel van artikel 3.41, tweede lid, van de wet opgenomen maximum bedrag waarover kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek kan worden verkregen", and not for assets that were already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. For 2026 that maximum is the top of the KIA table, € 398.236 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.41 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/investeringsaftrek-2026/kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek-2026). This cap is a reading of the cross-reference in art. 8 of the URWA, no primary page prints the cap as a number, see section 7.

Art. 9 sets the art. 3.38 period at five years from the start of the year of the obligation or the costs. Art. 12 allows ten years for the sea-going vessels (zeeschepen) of art. 10 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 7 to 9 and 12). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration adds that the scheme is open to a sole proprietorship, civil-law partnership (maatschap), limited partnership (cv) or general partnership (vof), that the starter's deduction conditions must be met, that only assets that qualify for investment deduction can be used, that the choice of assets is free, and that the minimum total investment of the KIA table does not apply. Revaluation (herwaardering) to the normal book value takes place within 5 years, or 10 years for sea-going vessels (zeeschepen), if the asset is rented out, falls under a double-tax treaty or is used in the forestry business (bosbedrijf), and the difference is profit (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/willekeurige_afschrijving/startende_ondernemer).

The temporary arbitrary depreciation (tijdelijke willekeurige afschrijving) of URWA art. 13 to 15 does not apply to 2026 investments. It covered assets not used before for which obligations were entered or production costs were made in calendar year 2023 and that were taken into use before 1 January 2026, at most 50% of the costs once, in the year of the obligation or the costs. Its exclusions include buildings (gebouwen), vessels, aircraft (vliegtuigen), mopeds (bromfietsen), motorcycles (motorrijwielen), passenger cars except for commercial transport (beroepsvervoer) by road or 0 gram CO2 per km, intangible assets, animals, assets mainly made available to third parties, public roads and paths, and assets already arbitrarily depreciated on another ground. The period in which the asset had to keep meeting the art. 13 conditions ended on 31 December 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012035/2025-01-01/0 art. 13 to 15, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-vorige-jaren/veranderingen-vennootschapsbelasting-2023, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-33377.html).

### 3.15 Disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling) threshold of € 2.900

If in a calendar year goods are disposed of against transfer prices for more than € 2.900 in total, a percentage of the transfer prices equal to the investment deduction percentage taken is added to the profit (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 1). For 2026 the threshold is € 2.900. The FISIN 2026 chapter and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration disinvestment addition page state the same amount (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling).

### 3.16 Disinvestment addition term and undoing tests

The disinvestment addition applies only to disposals within five years after the start of the calendar year of the investment, and never over more than the investment amount over which deduction (aftrek) was taken (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 3.47 lid 2). Lid 3 treats as a disposal the withdrawal of the good from the business (onderneming) (a), a change of destination so that the good meets art. 3.45 lid 2 onderdeel a, mainly made available to third parties for the KIA, or lid 4 onderdeel a, made available to non-residents for the EIA and MIA (b), and a change that brings the good under art. 3.45 lid 1 onderdeel a onder 2° (c). In those cases the market value (waarde in het economische verkeer) counts as the transfer price (overdrachtsprijs) (lid 4). Undoing an investment, or receiving a reduction (vermindering), refund (teruggaaf) or compensation (vergoeding), counts as a disposal for that amount (lid 5). Undoing includes not having paid at least 25% of the investment amount within twelve months after the obligation unless the asset is in use, and not having taken the asset into use within three years after the start of the investment year (lid 6). The Minister may disapply lid 3 onderdeel c and lid 6 (lid 7).

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page phrases rental as counting only for the KIA, not for the EIA or the MIA, and adds cessation of part or all of the business within the term as a case that triggers the disinvestment addition (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeringsaftrek_en_desinvesteringsbijtelling/desinvesteringsbijtelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 art. 3.47).
