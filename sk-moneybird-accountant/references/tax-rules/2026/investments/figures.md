## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the Dutch investment deductions for 2026, the small-scale investment deduction (investeringsaftrek, KIA, kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek), the Energy Investment Allowance (EIA, energie-investeringsaftrek), the Environmental Investment Allowance (MIA, milieu-investeringsaftrek) and the Vamil (arbitrary depreciation for environmental investments (willekeurige afschrijving milieu-investeringen)). It also covers ordinary depreciation of business assets (bedrijfsmiddelen), the minimum value (bodemwaarde) of buildings, arbitrary depreciation (willekeurige afschrijving) for starters and the disinvestment addition (desinvesteringsbijtelling). The 2026 figures are higher than the 2025 figures on several points, the EIA and MIA maximum investment amount (investeringsbedrag) is € 153.000.000, and from 2026 the EIA investments of a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) and of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) are added together for that maximum. All 2026 values in this file are enacted law or published conditions, except the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, RVO) cap of € 25 million for MIA and Vamil, which carries no year on the checked page and is listed in section 7.
