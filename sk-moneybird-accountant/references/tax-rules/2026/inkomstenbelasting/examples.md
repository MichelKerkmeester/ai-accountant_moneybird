## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

### Official example: box 1 calculation on a salary of € 80.000

Copied from the 2026 box 1 page, without heffingskortingen.

1. € 38.883 × 0,3575 = € 13.900,67, rounded down to € 13.900.
2. € 39.543 × 0,3756 = € 14.852,35, rounded down to € 14.852.
3. € 1.574 × 0,4950 = € 779,13, rounded down to € 779.
4. Total: € 13.900 plus € 14.852 plus € 779 = € 29.531.

The € 39.543 is the second band, € 78.426 minus € 38.883. The € 1.574 is the amount above € 78.426. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1.

### Calculated example: box 1 below AOW age

Assume taxable box 1 income of € 60.000, with no deductions, losses, tariefsaanpassing or heffingskortingen.

1. The first € 38.883 is taxed at 35,75%: € 38.883 × 0,3575 = € 13.900,6725, rounded to € 13.900,67.
2. The remaining income is € 60.000 minus € 38.883 = € 21.117.
3. The second bracket: € 21.117 × 0,3756 = € 7.931,5452, rounded to € 7.931,55.
4. Add the two amounts: € 13.900,67 plus € 7.931,55 = € 21.832,22.

This is the bracket calculation before credits and any tariefsaanpassing. Sources for rates: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening.

### Calculated example: box 1 at AOW age

Assume taxable box 1 income of € 78.426, with no deductions, losses, tariefsaanpassing or heffingskortingen. The taxpayer was born on or after 1 January 1946.

1. € 38.883 × 0,1785 = € 6.940,6155, rounded to € 6.940,62.
2. € 78.426 minus € 38.883 = € 39.543.
3. € 39.543 × 0,3756 = € 14.852,3508, rounded to € 14.852,35.
4. € 6.940,62 plus € 14.852,35 = € 21.792,97.

This is a bracket calculation before credits and tariefsaanpassing. Sources for rates: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening.

### Calculated example: tariefsaanpassing

Assume taxable box 1 income after deductions of € 75.000 and grondslagverminderende posten of € 5.000. Income before deductions is € 75.000 plus € 5.000 = € 80.000, which exceeds the € 78.426 boundary.

1. The amount over the boundary is € 80.000 minus € 78.426 = € 1.574.
2. The initial tariefsaanpassing is € 1.574 × 0,1194 = € 187,9356, rounded to € 187,94.
3. The cap is € 5.000 × 0,1194 = € 597,00.
4. The smaller amount applies, so the increase is € 187,94.

Sources for rates and rule: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.

### Calculated example: algemene heffingskorting

Assume the taxpayer is below AOW age and the verzamelinkomen is € 40.000.

1. Amount above the € 29.736 phase-out point: € 40.000 minus € 29.736 = € 10.264.
2. Phase-out: € 10.264 × 0,06398 = € 656,69072, rounded to € 656,69.
3. Remaining credit: € 3.115 minus € 656,69 = € 2.458,31.

Sources for rates and amounts: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Official example: payout of the algemene heffingskorting

The 2026 FISIN page states that someone born on 20 December 1962 with no income receives the full € 3.115 paid out. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Calculated example: arbeidskorting

Assume an arbeidsinkomen of € 40.000 and a taxpayer below AOW age.

1. € 40.000 falls in the band from € 25.846 to € 45.592.
2. Amount above the band start used in the formula: € 40.000 minus € 25.846 = € 14.154.
3. Increase: € 14.154 × 0,0195 = € 276,003, rounded to € 276,00.
4. Add the band amount: € 5.300 plus € 276,00 = € 5.576,00.

Sources for the formula: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Calculated example: inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting

Assume the taxpayer meets the child and household conditions, qualifies on the partner test, is below AOW age and has an arbeidsinkomen of € 20.000.

1. Income above the € 6.239 threshold: € 20.000 minus € 6.239 = € 13.761.
2. Credit before applying any maximum: € 13.761 × 0,11450 = € 1.575,6345, rounded to € 1.575,63.
3. This is below the stated maximum of € 3.032, so the calculated credit is € 1.575,63.

Sources for the formula: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Official example: IACK co-ouder days

The conditions page states that 164 days with the taxpayer and 201 days with the ex-partner meets the rule. From 1 April the stated test is 117 days per household. ### Calculated example: business profit to taxable business profit

Assume an eligible entrepreneur has a profit of € 60.000, qualifies for the € 1.200 zelfstandigenaftrek, has no other ondernemersaftrek or exempt profit components and has no losses to carry forward.

1. Profit after zelfstandigenaftrek: € 60.000 minus € 1.200 = € 58.800.
2. MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 58.800 × 0,127 = € 7.467,60.
3. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 58.800 minus € 7.467,60 = € 51.332,40.

This belastbare winst is included in the inkomen uit werk en woning. The box 1 income calculation then accounts for other applicable income, deductions and losses. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.

### Calculated example: from business profit to tax after credits

Assume one entrepreneur below AOW age with a profit of € 60.000, the € 1.200 zelfstandigenaftrek, no other ondernemersaftrek, no exempt profit components, no other income, no persoonsgebonden aftrek and no losses. The verzamelinkomen then equals the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning.

1. Profit after zelfstandigenaftrek: € 60.000 minus € 1.200 = € 58.800.
2. MKB-winstvrijstelling: € 58.800 × 0,127 = € 7.467,60.
3. Belastbare winst uit onderneming: € 58.800 minus € 7.467,60 = € 51.332,40. This is the box 1 income and the verzamelinkomen in this example.
4. Income before deductions for the tariefsaanpassing test: € 51.332,40 plus € 1.200 plus € 7.467,60 = € 60.000. That does not exceed € 78.426, so no tariefsaanpassing applies.
5. Box 1 tax on € 51.332,40: the first € 38.883 × 0,3575 = € 13.900,6725, rounded to € 13.900,67. The remainder € 51.332,40 minus € 38.883 = € 12.449,40, and € 12.449,40 × 0,3756 = € 4.675,99464, rounded to € 4.675,99. Total € 13.900,67 plus € 4.675,99 = € 18.576,66.
6. Arbeidskorting on the arbeidsinkomen of € 60.000: € 60.000 minus € 45.593 = € 14.407. € 14.407 × 0,06510 = € 937,8957, rounded to € 937,90. € 5.685 minus € 937,90 = € 4.747,10.
7. Algemene heffingskorting on the verzamelinkomen of € 51.332,40: € 51.332,40 minus € 29.736 = € 21.596,40. Phase-out: € 21.596,40 × 0,06398 = € 1.381,737672, rounded to € 1.381,74. Credit: € 3.115 minus € 1.381,74 = € 1.733,26.
8. Total credits: € 4.747,10 plus € 1.733,26 = € 6.480,36. Both credits fit within the combined tax and premium of this example.
9. Tax after credits: € 18.576,66 minus € 6.480,36 = € 12.096,30.

This example applies the credits in full and does not model the split into belastingdeel and premiedelen. The exact calculation before rounding each product gives the same final amount after credits.
