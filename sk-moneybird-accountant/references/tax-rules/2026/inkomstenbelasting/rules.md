## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below AOW age

For a person below AOW age the combined first-bracket rate is 35,75% up to € 38.883. It consists of the 8,10% income-tax part of the first band plus 27,65% premie volksverzekeringen. The second bracket is 37,56% and runs to € 78.426. Income above € 78.426 is taxed at 49,50%. The statute prints fixed amounts of € 3.149 and € 18.001 in the first table. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Box 1 at AOW age, born on or after 1 January 1946

At AOW age and born on or after 1 January 1946 the first bracket is 17,85% up to € 38.883, followed by 37,56% to € 78.426 and 49,50% above. The lower first-bracket rate follows from the AOW premium that stops once AOW age is reached, while the income-tax part stays 8,10%. One FISIN page prints the below-AOW table under this heading. The dedicated box 1 page gives 17,85%, consistent with the pre-1946 table and with the 8,10% income-tax part, and that value governs. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Box 1 at AOW age, born before 1 January 1946

For someone at AOW age and born before 1 January 1946 the first bracket runs to € 41.123 at 17,85%. The next bracket is 37,56% to € 78.426 and the top rate is 49,50%. Wet IB 2001 art. 2.10a holds this separate table, with fixed amounts of € 3.330 and € 17.341. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10a.

### First-bracket rate in the year AOW age is reached

In the calendar year in which AOW age is reached the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it. It rises by about 1,49 points per month from the AOW-age rate. The 2026 monthly rates are in the figures table, from 17,85% for January to 34,26% for December. Schijven 2 and 3 keep the normal rates of 37,56% and 49,50%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1. A second official page shows rounding differences of 0,01 point in six months, set out in section 7. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening.

### Premie volksverzekeringen ceiling

The premie volksverzekeringen is charged at 27,65% over the premie-inkomen up to € 38.883. The maximum premie for 2026 is € 10.751. The premium components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. The first-bracket ceiling equals the end of the first band. Once AOW age is reached no AOW premium is due. That the Anw and Wlz premiums continue is an inference from the AOW-age first-bracket rates, for example 17,85% as 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65% in 2026, and it is not verified as a stated rule. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Statutory fixed amounts in the box 1 rate table

Art. 2.10 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 carries fixed amounts of € 3.149 and € 18.001 in the statutory table. Art. 2.10a, the table for people born before 1 January 1946, uses € 3.330 and € 17.341. These amounts sit alongside the published combined rates that include the premie volksverzekeringen. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Tariefsaanpassing

Tariefsaanpassing applies when grondslagverminderende posten (deductions that lower the taxable base) have been deducted and the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning plus those posten exceeds € 78.426. The tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 11,94% of the excess, capped at 11,94% of the deducted posten. The effective value of the affected deductions is therefore at most 37,56%, which is 49,50% minus 11,94%. The posten are the persoonsgebonden aftrek (onderhoudsverplichtingen including partneralimentatie, weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten, aftrekbare giften, specifieke zorgkosten, restant persoonsgebonden aftrek voorgaande jaren), the aftrek kosten eigen woning (hypotheekrente, periodieke erfpacht, opstal of beklemming, kosten afsluiten hypotheek, restschuldkosten), the ondernemersfaciliteiten (zelfstandigenaftrek, aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk, meewerkaftrek, startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid, stakingsaftrek, mkb-winstvrijstelling) and the vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen. Tariefsaanpassing is applied automatically and shown on the aanslag. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.

### Algemene heffingskorting below AOW age

The algemene heffingskorting (general tax credit) is granted to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or uitkeringsinstantie in the payroll. Below AOW age the maximum is € 3.115. The phase-out starts at a verzamelinkomen of € 29.736 and runs at 6,398% to nil from € 78.427. The phase-out base is the verzamelinkomen from 2025. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Algemene heffingskorting for a full year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the AHK is € 1.556. It falls by 3,195% above € 29.736 and reaches nil from € 78.427. The ordinary AHK table does not apply in the year AOW age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in Mijn Belastingdienst. A rounding difference of a few euros between the table and the aanslag can occur and the aanslag is correct. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Arbeidskorting below AOW age

The arbeidskorting (employment tax credit) is calculated over the arbeidsinkomen. For an IB-ondernemer the arbeidsinkomen includes the winst uit onderneming before the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. It excludes the part earned as medegerechtigde or winstdelende schuldeiser. Apply the four bands in the figures table. The credit builds up in the first three bands and then falls by 6,510% to nil from € 132.921. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.

### Arbeidskorting for a full year at AOW age

For someone at AOW age for the whole year the reduced arbeidskorting table applies, with the rates and amounts in the figures table. The credit reaches nil from € 132.921. The ordinary arbeidskorting table does not apply in the year AOW age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in Mijn Belastingdienst. A rounding difference of a few euros between the table and the aanslag can occur and the aanslag is correct. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Arbeidskorting amount taken over from the employer

From 2024 the year tables state that if the employer applied a higher arbeidskorting and the arbeidsinkomen is not above the third-band boundary, the Belastingdienst takes over the employer's amount up to the maximum. For 2026 the boundary is € 45.593 and the maximum is € 5.685, or € 2.840 for a full year at AOW age. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting below AOW age

The inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting (income-dependent combination credit, IACK) is 11,450% of the arbeidsinkomen above € 6.239, up to a maximum of € 3.032. The 2026 table states the maximum from € 32.711, but the exact maximum point conflicts with other sources and is set out in section 7. The IACK is applied only in the return or through a voorlopige aanslag, not in the payroll. Conditions: a child under 12 on 1 January who belongs to the household for at least 6 months, no fiscal partner or one for 6 months or less or a lower arbeidsinkomen than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner. Qualifying children are own, step, adopted, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child. A pleegkind (foster child) qualifies only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a pleegvergoeding. A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months still gives the credit. Residents of the EU, EER, Switzerland or BES can qualify. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting for a full year at AOW age

For a taxpayer at AOW age for the whole year the year table states a rate of 5,72% and a maximum of € 1.513. The FISIN table gives the same rate and a maximum of € 1.514. The difference is an open point in section 7. The ordinary IACK table does not apply in the year AOW age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in Mijn Belastingdienst. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### IACK maximum point, conflicting source values

The 2026 IACK table states the maximum of € 3.032 from an arbeidsinkomen of € 32.711, which makes the band end look like a print slip. The FISIN table states € 32.719 and the Rijksoverheid answer states € 32.720. Arithmetic with € 3.032 / 11,45% plus € 6.239 gives about € 32.719. Treat the exact point as unresolved until an authoritative correction or clarification settles it. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/inkomstenbelasting/wat-is-een-heffingskorting-en-welke-heffingskortingen-zijn-er.

### IACK child and co-parent test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2013 and be part of the taxpayer's household for at least 6 months. For a co-ouder the conditions page, covering 2024 to 2026, requires roughly equal care with on average 3 or more days a week and at least 156 days per calendar year in each household. If co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year, the days are prorated. From 1 April the stated test is 117 days per household. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting.

### Payout of unused heffingskortingen

The payout of unused heffingskortingen to a minstverdienende partner (lower-earning partner) is limited to the AHK for people born before 1 January 1963. Conditions: the partners were fiscal partners for more than 6 months, the partner owes enough tax, and only the part that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and premium is paid. Own box 1 income must be below € 11.365. Partner income must be € 32.167, or € 36.537 at AOW age. The payout of the arbeidskorting and the IACK lapsed from 2023, and the AHK payout lapsed from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Belastingdeel and premiedelen of heffingskortingen

Each heffingskorting splits into a belastingdeel and premiedelen. The belastingdeel is the income-tax part of the first-bracket rate, 8,10% in 2026 against a total first-bracket rate of 35,75%. The premiedelen are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. Someone not insured for one scheme loses that scheme's part, and part-year premieplicht gives a time-proportional premiedeel. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Verzamelinkomen

Verzamelinkomen is the sum of the belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning, the belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang and the belastbaar inkomen uit sparen en beleggen, minus te verrekenen persoonsgebonden aftrek. It is the AHK phase-out base from 2025. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026.

### Box 1 income calculation

Belastbare winst uit onderneming is the winst as ondernemer reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning is the inkomen uit werk en woning reduced by te verrekenen verliezen, and that income includes the belastbare winst uit onderneming. The 2026 calculation page adds the reductions for reisaftrek openbaar vervoer, aftrekbare kosten eigen woning, uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen, persoonsgebonden aftrek and te verrekenen verliezen. The resulting box 1 balance is taxed under the box 1 table, then art. 2.10 lid 2 adds any tariefsaanpassing, and the heffingskortingen reduce the income tax and the premie volksverzekeringen through a belastingdeel and premiedelen. The order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.

### Test for entrepreneur for income tax

Winst uit onderneming requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. Handelsregister registration alone does not decide. The Belastingdienst weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Zelfstandigenaftrek

The 2026 zelfstandigenaftrek is € 1.200, or € 600 at AOW age. The deduction cannot exceed the profit before ondernemersaftrek unless the startersaftrek applies. The conditions for the deduction belong to the ondernemersaftrek rules. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Startersaftrek

The 2026 startersaftrek is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at AOW age. It is part of the ondernemersaftrek, so it is applied before the MKB-winstvrijstelling is calculated. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid

The 2026 startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid (start-up deduction for occupational disability) is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000, then € 0, capped at the profit. It is part of the ondernemersaftrek and is applied before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Meewerkaftrek

The meewerkaftrek is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the same hour bands. It is part of the ondernemersaftrek and reduces the profit used for the MKB-winstvrijstelling. The checked material does not state the hour-band boundaries. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Stakingsaftrek

The stakingsaftrek has a maximum of € 3.630. It is part of the ondernemersaftrek and is applied before the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### MKB-winstvrijstelling

The MKB-winstvrijstelling is 12,7% of the winst after the ondernemersaftrek. It reduces the belastbare winst uit onderneming. It can also reduce a loss, which works against the taxpayer. A medegerechtigde or geldverstrekker gets neither the ondernemersaftrek nor the MKB-winstvrijstelling. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen

Vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen are taken out of the winst first. The categories are the bosbouwvrijstelling, the landbouwvrijstelling, the vrijstelling kwijtscheldingswinst for the part above verrekenbare verliezen, pension claims under a mandatory scheme, vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging, Waz-uitkeringen, certain forest and nature subsidies, and benefits under the eindheffing, mobiliteitsproject and spitsmijdenproject vrijstellingen. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Oudedagsreserve

No new oudedagsreserve (old-age reserve) can be formed in 2026. An existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet. The option to add ended in 2023. Reduction or release follows on buying a lijfrente with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the ondernemingsvermogen, on cessation, on reaching the AOW age on 1 January, on failing the urencriterium in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve.
