# Changes tax year 2026

**Rule:** This note restates the changes that section 5 of the 2026 topic files records. It is a different record from the [correction log](changelog.md), which tracks status corrections, and it points back to the [year overview](README.md). This is not tax advice, and the Operator or a bookkeeper decides any personal case.

---

## 1. Unique to 2026

- Logies moved from 9% to 21% from 1 January 2026. [btw](btw/notes.md#5-changes)
- In 2026 there are no verzuimboetes, but vergrijpboetes are possible under the normal rules from 1 January 2026. [arbeidsrelatie](arbeidsrelatie/notes.md#5-changes)
- The zelfstandigenaftrek fell from € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026 and the AOW-age amount from € 1.235 to € 600. [inkomstenbelasting](inkomstenbelasting/notes.md#5-changes)
- The tariefsaanpassing fell from 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025 to 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026. [ondernemersaftrek](ondernemersaftrek/notes.md#5-changes)
- The heffingsvrij vermogen rose from € 57.684 per person in 2025 to € 59.357 in 2026. [box-3](box-3/notes.md#5-changes)
- The 2026 statute still prints € 0,23 per km, but the applied amount for 2026 is € 0,25 per km, retroactive to 1 January 2026. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- A zero-emission car first admitted in 2026 gets 4% off capped at € 1.200, so 18% up to € 30.000. [kosten-en-auto](kosten-en-auto/notes.md#5-changes)
- The Zvw bijdrage by aanslag fell from 5,26% in 2025 to 4,85% in 2026. [zvw](zvw/notes.md#5-changes)
