## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Box 1 below state pension (AOW) age

For a person below state pension age the combined first-bracket rate is 35,75% up to € 38.883. It consists of the 8,10% income-tax part (belastingdeel) of the first band plus 27,65% national insurance contribution (premie volksverzekeringen). The second bracket is 37,56% and runs to € 78.426. Income above € 78.426 is taxed at 49,50%. The statute prints fixed amounts of € 3.149 and € 18.001 in the first table. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Box 1 at state pension age, born on or after 1 January 1946

At state pension age and born on or after 1 January 1946 the first bracket (eerste schijf) is 17,85% up to € 38.883, followed by 37,56% to € 78.426 and 49,50% above. The lower first-bracket rate follows from the state pension contribution that stops once state pension age is reached, while the income-tax part stays 8,10%. One FISIN page prints the below-state-pension-age table under this heading. The dedicated box 1 page gives 17,85%, consistent with the pre-1946 table and with the 8,10% income-tax part, and that value governs. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Box 1 at state pension age, born before 1 January 1946

For someone at state pension age and born before 1 January 1946 the first bracket runs to € 41.123 at 17,85%. The next bracket is 37,56% to € 78.426 and the top rate is 49,50%. Income Tax Act 2001 (Wet IB 2001) art. 2.10a holds this separate table, with fixed amounts of € 3.330 and € 17.341. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10a.

### First-bracket rate in the year state pension age is reached

In the calendar year in which state pension age is reached the first-bracket rate depends on the month of reaching it. It rises by about 1,49 points per month from the state-pension-age rate. The 2026 monthly rates are in the figures table, from 17,85% for January to 34,26% for December. Bands 2 and 3 (schijven 2 en 3) keep the normal rates of 37,56% and 49,50%. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1. A second official page shows rounding differences of 0,01 point in six months, set out in section 7. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening.

### National insurance contribution ceiling

The national insurance contribution is charged at 27,65% over the contribution income (premie-inkomen) up to € 38.883. The maximum contribution for 2026 is € 10.751. The contribution components are AOW (General Old Age Pensions Act, Algemene Ouderdomswet) 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. The first-bracket ceiling equals the end of the first band. Once state pension age is reached no AOW contribution is due. That the Anw and Wlz contributions continue is an inference from the state-pension-age first-bracket rates, for example 17,85% as 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65% in 2026, and it is not verified as a stated rule. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01.

### Statutory fixed amounts in the box 1 rate table

Art. 2.10 lid 1 Wet IB 2001 carries fixed amounts of € 3.149 and € 18.001 in the statutory table. Art. 2.10a, the table for people born before 1 January 1946, uses € 3.330 and € 17.341. These amounts sit alongside the published combined rates that include the national insurance contribution. Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing)

Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) applies when deductions that lower the tax base (grondslag) (grondslagverminderende posten) have been deducted and the taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning) plus those deductions exceeds € 78.426. The tax under art. 2.10 lid 1 is increased by 11,94% of the excess, capped at 11,94% of the deducted amounts. The effective value of the affected deductions is therefore at most 37,56%, which is 49,50% minus 11,94%. The deductions are the personal deductions (persoonsgebonden aftrek) (maintenance obligations (onderhoudsverplichtingen) including spousal maintenance (partneralimentatie), weekend expenses for disabled people (weekenduitgaven voor gehandicapten), deductible gifts (aftrekbare giften), specific healthcare costs (specifieke zorgkosten), a remainder of personal deductions from earlier years (restant persoonsgebonden aftrek voorgaande jaren)), the deduction for owner-occupied home costs (aftrek kosten eigen woning) (mortgage interest (hypotheekrente), periodic ground rent (periodieke erfpacht), right of superficies (opstal) or long leasehold (beklemming), costs of taking out a mortgage (kosten afsluiten hypotheek), residual debt costs (restschuldkosten)), the tax reliefs for entrepreneurs (ondernemersfaciliteiten) (self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), deduction for research and development work (aftrek speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid), discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek), SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling)) and the exemption for making assets available (vrijstelling voor terbeschikkingstelling van bezittingen). Rate adjustment is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (aanslag). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.

### General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) below state pension age

The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting, AHK) is granted to every taxpayer. It is applied automatically in the return and by the employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) in the payroll. Below state pension age the maximum is € 3.115. The phase-out starts at an aggregate income (verzamelinkomen) of € 29.736 and runs at 6,398% to nil from € 78.427. The phase-out base is the aggregate income from 2025. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### General tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year the general tax credit is € 1.556. It falls by 3,195% above € 29.736 and reaches nil from € 78.427. The ordinary general tax credit table does not apply in the year state pension age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst). A rounding difference of a few euros between the table and the assessment can occur and the assessment is correct. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) below state pension age

The employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) is calculated over the earned income (arbeidsinkomen). For an income tax entrepreneur (IB-ondernemer) the earned income includes the profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming) before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption. It excludes the part earned as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) or a profit-sharing creditor (winstdelende schuldeiser). Apply the four bands in the figures table. The credit builds up in the first three bands and then falls by 6,510% to nil from € 132.921. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.

### Employment tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For someone at state pension age for the whole year the reduced employment tax credit table applies, with the rates and amounts in the figures table. The credit reaches nil from € 132.921. The ordinary employment tax credit table does not apply in the year state pension age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in My Tax and Customs Administration. A rounding difference of a few euros between the table and the assessment can occur and the assessment is correct. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Employment tax credit amount taken over from the employer

From 2024 the year tables state that if the employer applied a higher employment tax credit and the earned income is not above the third-band boundary, the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) takes over the employer's amount up to the maximum. For 2026 the boundary is € 45.593 and the maximum is € 5.685, or € 2.840 for a full year at state pension age. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.

### Income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting) below state pension age

The income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting, IACK) is 11,450% of the earned income above € 6.239, up to a maximum of € 3.032. The 2026 table states the maximum from € 32.711, but the exact maximum point conflicts with other sources and is set out in section 7. The IACK is applied only in the return or through a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not in the payroll. Conditions: a child under 12 on 1 January who belongs to the household for at least 6 months, no fiscal partner (fiscaal partner) or one for 6 months or less or a lower earned income than the partner, and with equal incomes only the older partner. Qualifying children are own, step, adopted, a child from the spouse's earlier relationship or a fiscal partner's child. A foster child (pleegkind) qualifies only when raised and maintained as the taxpayer's own child and not for a foster care allowance (pleegvergoeding). A death of the taxpayer or child before the 6 months still gives the credit. Residents of the EU, EEA (EER), Switzerland or BES can qualify. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Income-related combination tax credit for a full year at state pension age

For a taxpayer at state pension age for the whole year the year table states a rate of 5,72% and a maximum of € 1.513. The FISIN table gives the same rate and a maximum of € 1.514. The difference is an open point in section 7. The ordinary IACK table does not apply in the year state pension age is reached. In that year the amount is calculated with the online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag 2026 in My Tax and Customs Administration. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### IACK maximum point, conflicting source values

The 2026 IACK table states the maximum of € 3.032 from an earned income of € 32.711, which makes the band end look like a print slip. The FISIN table states € 32.719 and the national government (Rijksoverheid) answer states € 32.720. Arithmetic with € 3.032 / 11,45% plus € 6.239 gives about € 32.719. Treat the exact point as unresolved until an authoritative correction or clarification settles it. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/inkomstenbelasting/wat-is-een-heffingskorting-en-welke-heffingskortingen-zijn-er.

### IACK child and co-parent test

The child must have been born after 31 December 2013 and be part of the taxpayer's household for at least 6 months. For a co-parent (co-ouder) the conditions page, covering 2024 to 2026, requires roughly equal care with on average 3 or more days a week and at least 156 days per calendar year in each household. If co-parenting lasts at least 6 months but not the full year, the days are prorated. From 1 April the stated test is 117 days per household. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting.

### Payout of unused tax credits (heffingskortingen)

The payout of unused tax credits to a lower-earning partner (minstverdienende partner) is limited to the general tax credit for people born before 1 January 1963. Conditions: the partners were fiscal partners for more than 6 months, the partner owes enough tax, and only the part that cannot be set off against the recipient's own tax and contribution is paid. Own box 1 income must be below € 11.365. Partner income must be € 32.167, or € 36.537 at state pension age. The payout of the employment tax credit and the IACK lapsed from 2023, and the general tax credit payout lapsed from 2023 for people born in 1963 or later. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.

### Tax part and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen) of tax credits

Each tax credit (heffingskorting) splits into a tax part (belastingdeel) and national insurance contribution components (premiedelen). The tax part is the income-tax part of the first-bracket rate, 8,10% in 2026 against a total first-bracket rate of 35,75%. The contribution components are AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10% and Wlz 9,65%. Someone not insured for one scheme loses that scheme's part, and part-year contribution liability (premieplicht) gives a time-proportional contribution component (premiedeel). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.

### Aggregate income

Aggregate income is the sum of the taxable income from work and home, the taxable income from a substantial interest (belastbaar inkomen uit aanmerkelijk belang) and the taxable income from savings and investments (belastbaar inkomen uit sparen en beleggen), minus personal deductions to be set off (te verrekenen persoonsgebonden aftrek). It is the general tax credit phase-out base from 2025. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 art. 2.18, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026.

### Box 1 income calculation

Taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit as an entrepreneur (winst als ondernemer) reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling). Taxable income from work and home is the income from work and home reduced by losses to be set off (te verrekenen verliezen), and that income includes the taxable profit from business activities. The 2026 calculation page adds the reductions for the travel deduction for public transport (reisaftrek openbaar vervoer), deductible costs of the owner-occupied home (aftrekbare kosten eigen woning), expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen), personal deductions and losses to be set off. The resulting box 1 balance is taxed under the box 1 table, then art. 2.10 lid 2 adds any rate adjustment, and the tax credits reduce the income tax and the national insurance contribution through a tax part and contribution components. The order of the last two steps rests on the article structure and stays an inference. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.

### Test for entrepreneur for income tax

Profit from business activities requires that the business is run for the taxpayer's account, that the taxpayer can make binding agreements for it and is liable for its debts. Registration in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) alone does not decide. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration weighs profit, independence, capital, time, customers, marketing and business risk (ondernemersrisico). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Self-employed person's deduction

The 2026 self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) is € 1.200, or € 600 at state pension age. The deduction cannot exceed the profit before the entrepreneur's allowance unless the starter's deduction applies. The conditions for the deduction belong to the entrepreneur's allowance rules. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Starter's deduction

The 2026 starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is € 2.123, or € 1.062 at state pension age. It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance, so it is applied before the SME profit exemption is calculated. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs

The 2026 starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) is € 12.000, € 8.000, € 4.000, then € 0, capped at the profit. It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and is applied before the SME profit exemption. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Deduction for assisting partner

The deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) is 1,25%, 2%, 3% or 4% over the same hour bands. It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and reduces the profit used for the SME profit exemption. The checked material does not state the hour-band boundaries. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### Discontinuation deduction

The discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) has a maximum of € 3.630. It is part of the entrepreneur's allowance and is applied before the SME profit exemption. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek.

### SME profit exemption

The SME profit exemption is 12,7% of the profit after the entrepreneur's allowance. It reduces the taxable profit from business activities. It can also reduce a loss, which works against the taxpayer. A person entitled to a share or a lender (geldverstrekker) gets neither the entrepreneur's allowance nor the SME profit exemption. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen)

Exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen) are taken out of the profit first. The categories are the forestry exemption (bosbouwvrijstelling), the agricultural exemption (landbouwvrijstelling), the exemption for debt waiver profit (kwijtscheldingswinst) for the part above losses available for offset (verrekenbare verliezen), pension claims under a mandatory scheme, business termination payments (vergoedingen voor bedrijfsbeëindiging), WAZ benefits (Waz-uitkeringen), certain forest and nature subsidies, and benefits under the final levy (eindheffing), mobility project (mobiliteitsproject) and peak avoidance project (spitsmijdenproject) exemptions. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/winst_uit_onderneming.

### Old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve)

No new old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) can be formed in 2026. An existing reserve can stay on the balance sheet. The option to add ended in 2023. Reduction or release follows on buying an annuity (lijfrente) with a matching reserve decrease, when the reserve exceeds the business capital (ondernemingsvermogen), on cessation, on reaching state pension age on 1 January, on failing the hours criterion (urencriterium) in both the current and the prior year, and on death unless a continuing partner takes it over on request. Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve.
