## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Track the profit used as profit from business activities (winst uit onderneming), the exempt profit components (vrijgestelde winstbestanddelen), the applicable entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek), the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) and losses to be set off (te verrekenen verliezen). These amounts determine the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) and the taxable income from work and home (belastbaar inkomen uit werk en woning). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 arts. 3.1 and 3.2.
- Apply the entrepreneur's allowance first and compute the SME profit exemption on the profit after that allowance. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling.
- Keep the profit before the entrepreneur's allowance (vóór de ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption visible, because the employment tax credit (arbeidskorting) is calculated on that earned income (arbeidsinkomen). Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026.
- The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) and the employment tax credit are applied automatically in the return and can also be applied by an employer or benefit agency (uitkeringsinstantie) in the payroll. The income-related combination tax credit (inkomensafhankelijke combinatiekorting) is claimed through the return or a provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), not in the payroll. In the year state pension age is reached, use the 2026 online form Verzoek of wijziging voorlopige aanslag in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) for the general tax credit, the employment tax credit and the income-related combination tax credit. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026.
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) is applied automatically and shown on the assessment (aanslag). Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 art. 2.10 lid 2, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/afbouw-tarief-aftrekposten-bij-hoog-inkomen.
- The income-dependent Zvw contribution (Zvw-bijdrage) exists for 2026 and is handled in a separate reference. The Zvw assessment comes with the (provisional) income tax and national insurance contribution assessment (aanslag inkomstenbelasting en premie volksverzekeringen). Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet.
- The checked material does not state a filing deadline (aangiftetermijn) or a record-retention period for this topic. The filing procedure belongs to a separate reference.

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Monthly state pension transition rates:** the dedicated box 1 page gives the baseline monthly rates in section 2. The FISIN 2026 calculation page gives April 22,32%, May 23,81%, June 25,30%, October 31,27%, November 32,76% and December 34,25%, which are 0,01 point lower than the dedicated page in those six months. The dedicated box 1 page is the current page for box 1 and is used for the baseline, but no statute text holds the monthly rates. A direct official correction or clarification would settle the difference. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening.
- **IACK maximum point:** the 2026 IACK table says € 32.711, the 2026 FISIN tax credits (heffingskortingen) table says € 32.719 and the national government (Rijksoverheid) answer says € 32.720. Arithmetic with € 3.032 / 11,45% plus € 6.239 gives about € 32.719, so the table's € 32.710 band end looks like a print slip. The exact point remains UNVERIFIED until an authoritative correction or clarification. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/inkomstenbelasting/wat-is-een-heffingskorting-en-welke-heffingskortingen-zijn-er.
- **IACK maximum for a full year at state pension age:** the 2026 year table gives € 1.513 and the 2026 FISIN tax credits table gives € 1.514. The year table is used in section 2 with Check tag Corrected, but the difference stays open. An authoritative correction or clarification would settle it. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/inkomensafhankelijke_combikorting/inkomensafhankelijke-combinatiekorting-2026 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.
- **General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) nil point:** the 2026 general tax credit table says nil from € 78.427 and the 2026 FISIN tax credits table says nil above € 78.426. These are one rule. The phase-out ends at the top bracket boundary and the credit is nil for any higher income. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.
- **State pension age table heading:** the 2026 FISIN calculation page prints 35,75% below the heading for people at state pension age born on or after 1 January 1946. The dedicated box 1 page gives 17,85%, which agrees with the pre-1946 table and with the 8,10% income-tax part (belastingdeel). The resolved value is 17,85%. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/belastingberekening and https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01.
- **End-to-end calculation order:** the checked material treats the statement that the box 1 balance is taxed under art. 2.10 lid 1, then rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) is added under lid 2, then tax credits reduce tax and contributions, as an inference from the article structure. No page read prints that entire sequence as one rule. A direct official description of the complete sequence would settle it. Sources: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0011353&hoofdstuk=2&afdeling=2.2&artikel=2.10&z=2026-01-01&g=2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/heffingskortingen.
- **Continuation of Anw and Wlz at state pension age:** the contributions page states only that the AOW contribution stops at state pension age. That Anw and Wlz continue is an inference from the state-pension-age first-bracket rates, for example 17,85% as 8,10% plus 0,10% plus 9,65% in 2026, and it is UNVERIFIED as a stated rule. A statute text or an official note on the state-pension-age contribution split would settle it. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1.
