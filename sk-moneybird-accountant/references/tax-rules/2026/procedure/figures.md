## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the procedural side of tax year 2026 for a sole trader, and where relevant for a BV. It gives the filing and payment deadlines for income tax and VAT (btw), the deferral (uitstel) rules, the provisional (voorlopige) assessment (aanslag), tax interest (belastingrente), the additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn), objection (bezwaar), the record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht) and the KVK and Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) registration duties.

Most 2026 figures are enacted and verified in the checked sources. The tax interest rate for income tax and VAT is 5% from 1 January 2026. The exact income tax filing deadline for the 2026 return was not published on 2026-10-11 and the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states it is usually 1 May 2027.
