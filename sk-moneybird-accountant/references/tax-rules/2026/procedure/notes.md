## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Tax interest (belastingrente) for the general taxes, including income tax and VAT (btw), changes from 6,5% in 2025 to 5% from 1 January 2026. Source: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- The tax interest on tax year 2025 income tax runs from 1 July 2026 at 5%. Source: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) change from € 57 and € 18 in 2025 to € 58 and € 18 in 2026. Source: [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag).
- The income tax return for 2025 was due 1 May 2026, with deferral (uitstel) to 1 September 2026. The income tax return for 2026 was not published on 2026-10-11 and is usually 1 May 2027. Sources: [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte) and [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen).
- A change to the provisional (voorlopige) assessment (aanslag) 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Source: [tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen).
- The VAT dates roll forward. The December 2025 monthly return and payment were due 31 January 2026 and the December 2026 monthly return and payment are due 31 January 2027. The Q4 2025 quarterly return was due 31 January 2026 and the Q4 2026 quarterly return is due 31 January 2027. The yearly 2025 return was due 31 March 2026 and the yearly 2026 return is due 31 March 2027. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- The VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) is € 82 in 2025 and € 82 in 2026, unchanged. Source: [u doet geen of te laat aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte).
- The IB penalties (boetes) are € 469, up to € 6.709, and € 3.354 for not requesting an invitation to file a return in 2025 and in 2026, unchanged. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).
- The payment terms of an assessment, an additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) and a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) are 6 weeks, 1 month and 14 days in 2025 and in 2026, unchanged. Sources: [Invorderingswet 1990 2025-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2025-01-01) and [Invorderingswet 1990 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01).
- After HR 16 January 2026 the Vpb tax interest table from 2022 was aligned with the general rate. For 2026 the Vpb rate is 5 instead of 7,5, for 2025 6,5 instead of 9, for 2024 7,5 instead of 10, for 1 July to 31 December 2023 6 instead of 8, and for 1 January 2022 to 30 June 2023 4 instead of 8. Source: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- The mass objection on the rate was rejected for income tax, VAT and the other general taxes and granted for Vpb and similar. Source: [massaal bezwaar voor de hoogte van het belastingrentepercentage](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the VAT (btw) per period (tijdvak) and make sure the return and the payment are both received by the date in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) table. For 2026 the monthly, quarterly and yearly dates are in section 2. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).
- Keep the records (administratie) for seven years. Keep immovable property (onroerende zaken) and One Stop Shop data for ten years. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. Source: [hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Keep the basic records (basisgegevens): general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Source: [hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).
- Register the business (onderneming) with KVK within the window of one week before to one week after the start. KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. Source: [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/).
- Record the VAT ID (btw-id) and VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) when received. The KVK page states within two weeks or ten working days (werkdagen) after the start date. Source: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/).
- File the income tax return for 2026, usually by 1 May 2027, unless deferral (uitstel) is granted. Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). Sources: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen) and [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte).
- Settle the provisional (voorlopige) assessment (aanslag) in the return. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. Source: [in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen).
- If a BV is also in play, the Vpb default fine (verzuimboete) is € 3.354 up to € 6.709, and the Vpb tax interest from 2022 is aligned with the general rate after HR 16 January 2026. Sources: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete) and [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente).
- File objection (bezwaar) within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment if the assessment is wrong. Source: [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- The exact income tax return deadline for tax year 2026 is unknown and UNVERIFIED. On 2026-10-11 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page had not published it and stated it is usually 1 May 2027. The filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief) sets the date. Source: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). The filing invitation letter would settle it.
- Deferral (uitstel) channel conflict, settled. The form page says online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May is possible for someone who received a filing invitation letter. The deferral page says online is not possible. Both are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text. The settled reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise. Sources: [aanvraag uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting) and [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). Reading both pages in full settles it.
- KVK btw-number term conflict, settled. The KVK page uses both two weeks and ten working days (werkdagen) after the start date for the VAT ID (btw-id) and VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer). Ten working days equals two calendar weeks. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states the registration takes at most 10 working days. Sources: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/) and [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration wording settles it.
- Assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) wording conflict, settled. The current page says tax is only due if the amount is higher than (higher than) the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold. Pages up to the 2023 captures used equal to or more than and printed each threshold (grens) one euro higher. For 2023 the older wording printed € 52 and € 17 and the newer table prints € 51 and € 16. For 2026 the table prints € 58 and € 18. For whole-euro amounts the two forms describe the same threshold. Sources: [definitieve aanslag current](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag) and [archived 2021 page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210413/https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). The current page text is the source of the 2026 values and settles it.
