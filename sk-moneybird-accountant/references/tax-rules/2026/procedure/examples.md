### Calculated example: belastingrente on income tax

Invented inputs: tax year 2025, return filed 1 September 2026, final aanslag dated 15 December 2026, extra tax € 10.000. The rate for 2026 is 5%. The period runs from 1 July 2026 to 6 weeks after the aanslag date. 6 weeks after 15 December 2026 is 26 January 2027. Under 30 days per month and 360 days per year: July to December is 6 months = 180 days. 1 January 2027 to 26 January 2027 is 25 days. Total 205 days. Year rente = € 10.000 × 5% = € 500. Day rente = € 500 / 360 = € 1,388888. Rente = 205 × € 1,388888 = € 284,7222. Rounded down to whole euros = € 284. Check: 205 × 500 / 360 = 284,7222. Yes.

### Calculated example: belastingrente on btw

Invented inputs: btw naheffingsaanslag for tax year 2025 dated 15 May 2026 for € 5.000. The rate for 2026 is 5%. The period runs from 1 January 2026 to 14 days after the aanslag date. 14 days after 15 May 2026 is 29 May 2026. Under 30 days per month and 360 days per year: January to April is 4 months = 120 days. 1 May 2026 to 29 May 2026 is 28 days. Total 148 days. Year rente = € 5.000 × 5% = € 250. Day rente = € 250 / 360 = € 0,694444. Rente = 148 × € 0,694444 = € 102,7777. Rounded down to whole euros = € 102. Check: 148 × 250 / 360 = 102,7777. Yes.

### Calculated example: definitive assessment term for tax year 2026

The belastingschuld arose in 2026. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2027. 3 years later is 31 December 2029. If 4 months uitstel was granted, the term is extended by 4 months. 31 December 2029 plus 4 months is 30 April 2030. So with 4 months uitstel the definitive aanslag can be set at the latest on 30 April 2030. Without uitstel the term ends 31 December 2029. Check: 31 December 2029 plus January, February, March and April 2030 gives 30 April 2030. Yes.

### Calculated example: payment terms

Invented aanslagbiljet date 15 March 2026. An aanslag is payable 6 weeks after the date. 6 weeks after 15 March 2026 is 26 April 2026. A navorderingsaanslag is payable 1 month after the date. 1 month after 15 March 2026 is 15 April 2026. A naheffingsaanslag is payable 14 days after the date. 14 days after 15 March 2026 is 29 March 2026. Check: 15 March plus 42 days is 26 April 2026, 15 March plus 14 days is 29 March 2026, and 15 March plus one month is 15 April 2026. Yes.

### Calculated example: aanslaggrens and teruggaafgrens

The aanslaggrens is € 58. The rule is tax is only due if the amount is higher than € 58. An amount of exactly € 58 is not higher, so no tax is due. The first whole euro amount above € 58 is € 59. The teruggaafgrens is € 18. A refund of exactly € 18 is not higher, so no refund is paid. The first whole euro above € 18 is € 19. Check: 58 + 1 = 59 and 18 + 1 = 19. Yes.

### Calculated example: betaalverzuimboete

Open amount € 2.000. 5% of € 2.000 is € 100. The minimum is € 50 and the maximum is € 6.709. € 100 lies between them, so the boete is € 100. Open amount € 500. 5% is € 25. This is below the minimum of € 50, so the boete is € 50. Open amount € 200.000. 5% is € 10.000. This is above the maximum of € 6.709, so the boete is € 6.709. Check: 2.000 × 0,05 = 100, 500 × 0,05 = 25 and 200.000 × 0,05 = 10.000. Yes.

### Calculated example: bewaarplicht

A 4-year lease starts on 1 January 2026. The gegeven is no longer actual after 31 December 2029. The 7-year term starts then. 31 December 2029 plus 7 years is 31 December 2036. So the lease must be kept until 31 December 2036. Check: 2029 + 7 = 2036. Yes.

### Calculated example: KVK registration window

A business starts on 1 June 2026. The window runs from one week before to one week after the start. One week before 1 June 2026 is 25 May 2026. One week after is 8 June 2026. So the first inschrijving can be made from 25 May 2026 to 8 June 2026. Check: 1 June minus 7 days is 25 May and 1 June plus 7 days is 8 June. Yes.

### Calculated example: btw due dates

A monthly trader for January 2026 must have the return and payment received by 28 February 2026. A quarterly trader for Q1 2026 must have both received by 30 April 2026. A yearly trader for 2026 must have both received by 31 March 2027. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums). These dates come directly from the Belastingdienst table.
