### Official example: additional assessment (navordering) with deferral (uitstel)

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page gives tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, additional assessment possible until 31 May 2022. Source: [navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). ### Calculated example: tax interest (belastingrente) on income tax

Invented inputs: tax year 2025, return filed 1 September 2026, final assessment (aanslag) dated 15 December 2026, extra tax € 10.000. The rate for 2026 is 5%. The period runs from 1 July 2026 to 6 weeks after the assessment date. 6 weeks after 15 December 2026 is 26 January 2027. Under 30 days per month and 360 days per year: July to December is 6 months = 180 days. 1 January 2027 to 26 January 2027 is 25 days. Total 205 days. Annual interest = € 10.000 × 5% = € 500. Daily interest = € 500 / 360 = € 1,388888. Interest = 205 × € 1,388888 = € 284,7222. Rounded down to whole euros = € 284. Check: 205 × 500 / 360 = 284,7222. Yes.

### Calculated example: tax interest on VAT (btw)

Invented inputs: VAT supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) for tax year 2025 dated 15 May 2026 for € 5.000. The rate for 2026 is 5%. The period runs from 1 January 2026 to 14 days after the assessment date. 14 days after 15 May 2026 is 29 May 2026. Under 30 days per month and 360 days per year: January to April is 4 months = 120 days. 1 May 2026 to 29 May 2026 is 28 days. Total 148 days. Annual interest = € 5.000 × 5% = € 250. Daily interest = € 250 / 360 = € 0,694444. Interest = 148 × € 0,694444 = € 102,7777. Rounded down to whole euros = € 102. Check: 148 × 250 / 360 = 102,7777. Yes.

### Calculated example: definitive assessment term for tax year 2026

The tax debt (belastingschuld) arose in 2026. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year, so from 1 January 2027. 3 years later is 31 December 2029. If 4 months deferral was granted, the term is extended by 4 months. 31 December 2029 plus 4 months is 30 April 2030. So with 4 months deferral the definitive assessment can be set at the latest on 30 April 2030. Without deferral the term ends 31 December 2029. Check: 31 December 2029 plus January, February, March and April 2030 gives 30 April 2030. Yes.

### Calculated example: payment terms

Invented assessment notice (aanslagbiljet) date 15 March 2026. An assessment is payable 6 weeks after the date. 6 weeks after 15 March 2026 is 26 April 2026. An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) is payable 1 month after the date. 1 month after 15 March 2026 is 15 April 2026. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) is payable 14 days after the date. 14 days after 15 March 2026 is 29 March 2026. Check: 15 March plus 42 days is 26 April 2026, 15 March plus 14 days is 29 March 2026, and 15 March plus one month is 15 April 2026. Yes.

### Calculated example: assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens)

The assessment threshold is € 58. The rule is tax is only due if the amount is higher than € 58. An amount of exactly € 58 is not higher, so no tax is due. The first whole euro amount above € 58 is € 59. The refund threshold is € 18. A refund of exactly € 18 is not higher, so no refund is paid. The first whole euro above € 18 is € 19. Check: 58 + 1 = 59 and 18 + 1 = 19. Yes.

### Calculated example: payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete)

Open amount € 2.000. 5% of € 2.000 is € 100. The minimum is € 50 and the maximum is € 6.709. € 100 lies between them, so the penalty (boete) is € 100. Open amount € 500. 5% is € 25. This is below the minimum of € 50, so the penalty is € 50. Open amount € 200.000. 5% is € 10.000. This is above the maximum of € 6.709, so the penalty is € 6.709. Check: 2.000 × 0,05 = 100, 500 × 0,05 = 25 and 200.000 × 0,05 = 10.000. Yes.

### Calculated example: record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

A 4-year lease starts on 1 January 2026. The item (gegeven) is no longer actual after 31 December 2029. The 7-year term starts then. 31 December 2029 plus 7 years is 31 December 2036. So the lease must be kept until 31 December 2036. Check: 2029 + 7 = 2036. Yes.

### Calculated example: KVK registration window

A business starts on 1 June 2026. The window runs from one week before to one week after the start. One week before 1 June 2026 is 25 May 2026. One week after is 8 June 2026. So the first registration (inschrijving) can be made from 25 May 2026 to 8 June 2026. Check: 1 June minus 7 days is 25 May and 1 June plus 7 days is 8 June. Yes.

### Calculated example: VAT due dates

A monthly trader for January 2026 must have the return and payment received by 28 February 2026. A quarterly trader for Q1 2026 must have both received by 30 April 2026. A yearly trader for 2026 must have both received by 31 March 2027. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums). These dates come directly from the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table.
