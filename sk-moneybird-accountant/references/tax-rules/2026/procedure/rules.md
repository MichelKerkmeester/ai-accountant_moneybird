## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2026, uiterste datum

This applies to a sole trader who must file an income tax return for tax year 2026. The return must arrive before the date in the aangiftebrief, often 1 May. On 2026-10-11 the Belastingdienst page did not publish an exact date for the 2026 return and stated it is usually 1 May 2027. The exact date is set in the aangiftebrief. Source: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). This is the only value in this file marked unknown and UNVERIFIED.

### Start van het aangiftetijdvak inkomstenbelasting

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March. Source: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). The opening date is the same for every year in the checked material.

### Uitstel inkomstenbelasting

Uitstel must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted uitstel gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. The form can be used only for the first uitstel request. A fiscal partner must request separately. Source: [aanvraag uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). Uitstel usually means belastingrente. Source: [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte).

### Uitstel en belastingrente

Uitstel usually means belastingrente. Source: [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). The belastingrente on income tax runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). A later filing therefore does not stop the rente.

### Uitstelkanaal inkomstenbelasting

The form page says uitstel can also be requested online in Mijn Belastingdienst until 1 May, giving uitstel to 1 September, only for someone who received an aangiftebrief. The uitstel page says online is not possible. Read in full, the uitstel page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both are current Belastingdienst text. The settled reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise. Sources: [aanvraag uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting) and [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). See section 7.

### Wettelijke aangiftetermijn aanslagbelastingen

For aanslagbelastingen, AWR art. 9 lid 1 states the return is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the invitation. AWR art. 9 lid 2 lets the inspecteur extend that term under conditions. The same text holds on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Source: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). This is the legal basis for the aangiftebrief and for uitstel.

### Voorlopige aanslag 2025 wijzigen

A voorlopige aanslag is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (stakingswinst). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Winst uit onderneming screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A voorlopige aanslag can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with an aangiftebrief until the filing date or the uitstel date, and no longer after filing. A change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Sources: [in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen) and [tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). There is no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag IB, only a change. Source: [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).

### Betaaltermijnen aanslagen

Invorderingswet 1990 art. 9 gives the payment terms. The same text holds on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An aanslag is invorderbaar 6 weeks after the date of the aanslagbiljet. A navorderingsaanslag 1 month after. A naheffingsaanslag 14 days after. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). These terms are the starting point for the betaalverzuimboete if the amount is not paid.

### Betaaltermijnen voorlopige aanslag IB

A voorlopige aanslag IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly termijnen as months remain after the month of the date. The first termijn is one month after the date. A voorlopige aanslag dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly termijnen from the end of January. A voorlopige aanslag with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). These termijnen interact with the aanslaggrens because monthly refunds received before year end count toward the grens.

### Definitieve aanslagtermijn

AWR art. 11 lid 3, same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026, states the power to set the aanslag lapses three years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by the duration of filing uitstel. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The Belastingdienst counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For tax year 2026 this is the term within which the definitive aanslag can be set, see section 4.

### Aanslaggrens en teruggaafgrens 2026

The aanslaggrens for 2026 is € 58 and the teruggaafgrens is € 18. The current page states tax is only due if the amount is higher than the aanslaggrens and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the teruggaafgrens. Monthly refunds received through a voorlopige aanslag before year end count toward the grens. A paid voorlopige aanslag does not count. A refund received after filing does not count. Source: [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Older captures used the wording gelijk aan of meer dan and printed each grens one euro higher, see section 7.

### Navorderingstermijn

AWR art. 16, same in the 2020 and 2026 texts, governs navordering. Lid 1: a new fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably knowable to the inspecteur, unless the taxpayer acted te kwader trouw. Lid 2: navordering is also possible when a voorlopige aanslag, voorheffing, voorlopige teruggaaf or voorlopige verliesverrekening was wrongly settled, and for a fout that was redelijkerwijs kenbaar. That is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3: the general term is 5 years after the belastingschuld arose, extended by uitstel. For a fout-based navordering the term is 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4: 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5: extension by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Belastingdienst page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months uitstel, navordering possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no boete. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). This term is longer than the definitive assessment term and applies after the aanslag is set.

### Bezwaartermijn

A bezwaar must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the aanslag. For income tax it can be filed online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if asked for in the bezwaar. The Belastingdienst acknowledges within 3 weeks. There is no bezwaar against a voorlopige aanslag IB, only a change. Source: [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar). After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible. Source: [verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Verzoek om vermindering

After the bezwaar term a verzoek om (ambtshalve) vermindering is possible. Online is only possible if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The source gives the examples: aanslag 2020 online until 31 December 2025, aanslag 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the verzoek must be sent by letter. Source: [verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). This is the route after the 6-week bezwaar term has passed.

### Belastingrente 2026 en vaststelling van het percentage

For all taxes except Vpb and toeslagen the belastingrente is 5% from 1 January 2026. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year. The starting point is the last ECB herfinancieringsrente published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. For Vpb, bronbelasting, minimumbelasting and solidariteitsbijdrage the rate is plus 5,5 points, minimum 5,5%. Source: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). The same source shows 6,5% in 2025 and 7,5% in 2024 for the general taxes. The rate applies to both income tax and btw, see the next items.

### Massaal bezwaar tegen het belastingrentepercentage

The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, btw and the other general taxes. After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. Sources: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente) and [massaal bezwaar voor de hoogte van het belastingrentepercentage](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). The Belastingdienst mass-objection page was updated on 25 February 2026 but gives no date for the collective decisions. Source: [massaal bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). For a sole trader the rejection means the 5% rate for income tax and btw stands.

### Belastingrente op de inkomstenbelasting

Belastingrente on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the aanslag deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with uitstel. It ends 6 weeks after the aanslag date. When the aanslag follows the return unchanged it ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For a navorderingsaanslag the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No rente is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). See section 4 for a calculated example.

### Belastingrente op de btw

Belastingrente on btw is charged on a naheffingsaanslag and on late payment. For late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a naheffingsaanslag it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No rente is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The rentestop extension to requested naheffingsaanslagen, ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for btw because it is technically not executable. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting). See section 4 for a calculated example.

### Btw aangifte- en betaaldata 2026

The Belastingdienst table gives the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together. For 2026 the monthly dates are: December 2025 31 January 2026, January 28 February 2026, February 31 March 2026, March 30 April 2026, April 31 May 2026, May 30 June 2026, June 31 July 2026, July 31 August 2026, August 30 September 2026, September 31 October 2026, October 30 November 2026, November 31 December 2026, December 2026 31 January 2027. The quarterly dates are: Q4 2025 31 January 2026, Q1 30 April 2026, Q2 31 July 2026, Q3 31 October 2026, Q4 31 January 2027. The yearly return for 2026 is due by 31 March 2027. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Btw aangifte- en betaaltermijn, wettelijk en software

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states a return for a tijdvak is filed within a term set by the inspecteur of at least one month after the end of the tijdvak. AWR art. 19 lid 1 states the tax is paid within one month after the end of the tijdvak. The Belastingdienst tables give the exact dates for monthly, quarterly and yearly returns. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Uitstel btw-aangifte

Uitstel for a btw-aangifte is possible only in uitzonderlijke situaties, an ernstige calamiteit. The request must be made in writing to the belastingkantoor before the filing date. A naheffingsaanslag and a boete remain possible. Source: [uitstel aanvragen btw-aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte). This is stricter than the income tax uitstel, which is available in the annual period before 1 May.

### Btw aangifteverzuimboete 2026

For a late or missing btw-aangifte there are 7 kalenderdagen coulance. After that the boete is € 82 in 2026. Source: [u doet geen of te laat aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte). The same boete applied in 2025. The boete is separate from the belastingrente on a naheffingsaanslag.

### IB boetes 2026

For income tax the verzuimboete is € 469 and can go up to € 6.709. If the Belastingdienst must request a return, the boete is € 3.354. For Vpb the boete is € 3.354 up to € 6.709. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The amounts for 2025 were the same. The boete can be imposed together with an ambtshalve aanslag when no return is filed.

### Betaalverzuimboete aanslagbelasting

The betaalverzuimboete for an aanslagbelasting is 5% of the open amount of the (voorlopige) aanslag, minimum € 50, maximum € 6.709 per default. If the aanslag is reduced, the boete is reduced proportionally with a € 50 floor. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). See section 4 for calculated examples.

### Vergrijpboete percentages

The vergrijpboete is 25% for grove schuld and 50% for opzet. For box 3 the percentages are 75% and 150%. Aggravated percentages are 100% and 300%. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The 2020 to 2024 captures show the same 50% and 25%. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return can lead to a vergrijpboete, also with a navorderingsaanslag. Source: [wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig).

### IB na het verstrijken van de aangiftetermijn en aangifte vragen

After the deadline the Belastingdienst sends a herinnering, then an aanmaning with a term of 10 werkdagen. Filing after the aanmaning term gives the verzuimboete. The Belastingdienst can stop toeslagen and the voorlopige aanslag. If no return is filed at all, an ambtshalve aanslag plus verzuimboete follows. No boete is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the aangiftebrief. For intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return a vergrijpboete is possible, also with a navorderingsaanslag. Requesting a return by the Belastingdienst: within 6 months after the belastingschuld arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks coulance. Sources: [wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig) and [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Aangifte zonder uitnodiging

Without an aangiftebrief a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. Source: [hoeveel jaar terug belastingaangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). The same rule applies to the return for tax year 2026. The checked sources do not print a separate 2026 end date. This route is for someone who did not receive an aangiftebrief but still wants to file.

### Bewaarplicht

AWR art. 52 lid 4, same in 2020 and 2026, states administratieplichtigen keep the gegevensdragers for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with winst uit onderneming or resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden. Lid 5 allows conversion to another gegevensdrager except the paper balans and staat van baten en lasten. The Belastingdienst table: basisgegevens 7 years, onroerende zaken 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the belastingkantoor. The term starts when the gegeven is no longer actual. A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basisgegevens are the grootboek, debiteuren- and crediteurenadministratie, voorraadadministratie, in- and verkoopadministratie, loonadministratie, and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### KVK inschrijvingsvenster

Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 20 lid 1 states the opgave for the first inschrijving of an onderneming is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required opgaven within one week after the fact. Source: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). The KVK page also states the window. Source: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). See section 4 for a calculated window.

### KVK-nummer en afspraken

KVK issues the KVK-nummer immediately when registering within the window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead. Appointments can be made up to eight weeks ahead. Source: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). This applies to the first inschrijving.

### Btw-id en omzetbelastingnummer termijn

The KVK page states the btw-id and omzetbelastingnummer are issued within two weeks or within ten werkdagen after the start date. Ten werkdagen is two calendar weeks. The Belastingdienst page states the registration takes at most 10 werkdagen. Sources: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/) and [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). See section 7 for the wording difference.

### Belastingdienst inschrijving

KVK passes the data to the Belastingdienst, so no separate registration is needed. Registration in the Belastingdienst administration takes at most 10 werkdagen. An eenmanszaak is registered with the bsn, other forms get an RSIN. The Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer from it. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Source: [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). This is the registration duty for a new sole trader.
