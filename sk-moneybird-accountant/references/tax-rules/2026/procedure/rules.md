## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Income tax return (aangifte) 2026, deadline (uiterste datum)

This applies to a sole trader who must file an income tax return for tax year 2026. The return must arrive before the date in the filing invitation letter (aangiftebrief), often 1 May. On 2026-10-11 the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) page did not publish an exact date for the 2026 return and stated it is usually 1 May 2027. The exact date is set in the filing invitation letter. Source: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). This is the only value in this file marked unknown and UNVERIFIED.

### Start of the income tax return period (aangiftetijdvak)

The filing period for the income tax return opens on 1 March. Source: [wanneer moet ik aangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wanneer-moet-ik-aangifte-doen). The opening date is the same for every year in the checked material.

### Income tax deferral (uitstel)

Deferral must be requested before the filing date, in the annual period before 1 May. A granted deferral gives 4 months extra, to 1 September. The form can be used only for the first deferral request. A fiscal partner must request separately. Source: [aanvraag uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting). Deferral usually means tax interest (belastingrente). Source: [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte).

### Deferral and tax interest

Deferral usually means tax interest. Source: [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). The tax interest on income tax runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). A later filing therefore does not stop the tax interest.

### Income tax deferral channel (uitstelkanaal inkomstenbelasting)

The form page says deferral can also be requested online in My Tax and Customs Administration (Mijn Belastingdienst) until 1 May, giving deferral to 1 September, only for someone who received a filing invitation letter. The deferral page says online is not possible. Read in full, the deferral page addresses the period after 1 May and a filing date other than 1 May, where the paper form sent by post is the route. Both are current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration text. The settled reading is online until 1 May for the regular 1 May date, paper form otherwise. Sources: [aanvraag uitstel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/programmas_en_formulieren/aanvraag-uitstel-aangifte-inkomstenbelasting) and [kan ik nog uitstel aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/kan-ik-nog-uitstel-aanvragen-belastingaangifte). See section 7.

### Statutory filing deadline (wettelijke aangiftetermijn) for taxes levied by assessment (aanslagbelastingen)

For taxes levied by assessment, AWR art. 9 lid 1 states the return is filed within a term set by the tax inspector (inspecteur) of at least one month after the invitation. AWR art. 9 lid 2 lets the tax inspector extend that term under conditions. The same text holds on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026. Source: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01). This is the legal basis for the filing invitation letter and for deferral.

### Amending the 2025 provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag)

A provisional assessment is an estimate based on known data such as the previous return. Changing it is not mandatory. Reasons to change include buying a home, changing mortgage interest, higher or lower income, a change in business profit and stopping the business (profit from business discontinuation (stakingswinst)). Entrepreneurs change the expected profit in the Profits from business activities (winst uit onderneming) screen. Paid or received amounts are settled in the return. A provisional assessment can be requested or changed usually until 1 May of the next year, with a filing invitation letter until the filing date or the deferral date, and no longer after filing. A change for 2025 after 15 October is not carried over automatically to 2026. Sources: [in welke situaties moet ik mijn voorlopige aanslag wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/in-welke-situaties-moet-ik-mijn-voorlopige-aanslag-wijzigen) and [tot wanneer voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/tot-wanneer-voorlopige-aanslag-aanvragen-wijzigen). There is no objection (bezwaar) against a provisional assessment IB, only a change. Source: [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar).

### Payment deadlines (betaaltermijnen) for assessments (aanslagen)

Collection of State Taxes Act (Invorderingswet) 1990 art. 9 gives the payment terms. The same text holds on 1 January 2020, 2024, 2025 and 2026. An assessment is payable (invorderbaar) 6 weeks after the date of the assessment notice (aanslagbiljet). An additional assessment (navorderingsaanslag) 1 month after. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) 14 days after. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). These terms are the starting point for the payment default penalty (betaalverzuimboete) if the amount is not paid.

### Payment deadlines for the provisional income tax assessment

A provisional assessment IB dated in the tax year is payable in as many equal monthly instalments (termijnen) as months remain after the month of the date. The first term (termijn) is one month after the date. A provisional assessment dated before the tax year is payable in 12 monthly instalments from the end of January. A provisional assessment with a refund is paid out monthly from the month of the date. Source: [Invorderingswet 1990 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004770/2026-01-01). These instalments interact with the assessment threshold (aanslaggrens) because monthly refunds received before year end count toward the threshold (grens).

### Final assessment period (definitieve aanslagtermijn)

AWR art. 11 lid 3, same text on 1 January 2020 and 1 January 2026, states the power to set the assessment lapses three years after the tax debt (belastingschuld) arose, extended by the duration of filing deferral. If within the last six months of that term a request to be invited to file (art. 6 lid 2 or 3) is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied, the term is extended by six months. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration counts the 3 years from 1 January after the tax year. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). For tax year 2026 this is the term within which the definitive assessment can be set, see section 4.

### Assessment threshold and refund threshold (teruggaafgrens) 2026

The assessment threshold for 2026 is € 58 and the refund threshold is € 18. The current page states tax is only due if the amount is higher than the assessment threshold and a refund is only paid if it is higher than the refund threshold. Monthly refunds received through a provisional assessment before year end count toward the threshold. A paid provisional assessment does not count. A refund received after filing does not count. Source: [definitieve aanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aanslag/definitieve_aanslag). Older captures used the wording equal to or more than and printed each threshold one euro higher, see section 7.

### Additional assessment period (navorderingstermijn)

AWR art. 16, same in the 2020 and 2026 texts, governs additional assessment (navordering). Lid 1: a new fact is not usable if it was known or reasonably apparent to the tax inspector, unless the taxpayer acted in bad faith (te kwader trouw). Lid 2: additional assessment is also possible when a provisional assessment, prepayment (voorheffing), provisional refund (teruggaaf) or provisional loss offsetting (verliesverrekening) was wrongly settled, and for an error (fout) that was reasonably apparent (redelijkerwijs kenbaar). That is always so when the shortfall is at least 30% of the correct tax. Lid 3: the general term is 5 years after the tax debt arose, extended by deferral. For an error-based additional assessment the term is 2 years after the assessment or decision. Lid 4: 12 years for income or assets held or arising abroad. Lid 5: extension by 6 months if in the last 6 months a request for an invitation is made or data under art. 9 lid 4 are supplied. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page gives the example of tax year 2016 with 5 months deferral, additional assessment possible until 31 May 2022. A voluntary correction within 2 years generally gives no penalty (boete). Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [navorderingsaanslag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/navorderingsaanslag). This term is longer than the definitive assessment term and applies after the assessment is set.

### Objection period (bezwaartermijn)

An objection must be filed within 6 weeks after the date of the assessment. For income tax it can be filed online, by pdf form or by letter. The motivation can follow later if asked for in the objection. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration acknowledges within 3 weeks. There is no objection against a provisional assessment IB, only a change. Source: [eisen bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/eisen-bezwaar). After the objection period a request (verzoek) to (ex officio (ambtshalve)) reduction (vermindering) is possible. Source: [verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting).

### Request for a reduction (verzoek om vermindering)

After the objection period a request for (ex officio) reduction is possible. Online is only possible if the return was filed online and fewer than 5 years have passed after the tax year. The source gives the examples: assessment 2020 online until 31 December 2025, assessment 2023 until 31 December 2028. Otherwise the request must be sent by letter. Source: [verzoek om vermindering van aanslag inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/bezwaar-en-beroep/content/verzoek-om-vermindering-van-aanslag-inkomstenbelasting). This is the route after the 6-week objection term has passed.

### Tax interest 2026 and determination (vaststelling) of the rate (percentage)

For all taxes except Vpb and allowances (toeslagen) the tax interest is 5% from 1 January 2026. From 1 January 2024 the rate is set once a year for 1 January of the next year. The starting point is the last ECB refinancing rate (herfinancieringsrente) published before 1 November, plus 3 points for these taxes, rounded to half percentages, minimum 4,5%. For Vpb, withholding tax (bronbelasting), minimum tax (minimumbelasting) and solidarity contribution (solidariteitsbijdrage) the rate is plus 5,5 points, minimum 5,5%. Source: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente). The same source shows 6,5% in 2025 and 7,5% in 2024 for the general taxes. The rate applies to both income tax and VAT (btw), see the next items.

### Mass objection (massaal bezwaar) against the tax interest rate (belastingrentepercentage)

The mass objection on the rate was granted for Vpb and similar taxes and rejected for income tax, VAT and the other general taxes. After HR 16 January 2026 (ECLI:NL:HR:2026:59) the Vpb table from 2022 was aligned with the other taxes. Sources: [overzicht percentages belastingrente](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/overzicht_percentages_belastingrente) and [massaal bezwaar voor de hoogte van het belastingrentepercentage](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration mass-objection page was updated on 25 February 2026 but gives no date for the collective decisions. Source: [massaal bezwaar](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/massaal-bezwaar-voor-de-hoogte-van-het-belastingrentepercentage). For a sole trader the rejection means the 5% rate for income tax and VAT stands.

### Tax interest on income tax (inkomstenbelasting)

Tax interest on income tax is charged when the return arrives on or after 1 May after the tax year, or when the assessment deviates from the return, also if the return was filed before 1 May. The period runs from 1 July after the tax year, also with deferral. It ends 6 weeks after the assessment date. When the assessment follows the return unchanged it ends no later than 19 weeks after receipt of the return. For an additional assessment the period runs from 1 July after the tax year to 1 month after its date. No tax interest is charged when the return is filed before 1 May and taken over unchanged. The calculation uses 30 days per month and 360 days per year, rounded down to whole euros. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij inkomstenbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_inkomstenbelasting). See section 4 for a calculated example.

### Tax interest on VAT (btw)

Tax interest on VAT is charged on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and on late payment. For late payment the period runs from 1 January after the tax year to the payment date. For a supplementary assessment it runs from 1 January after the tax year to 14 days after its date. No tax interest is charged for a voluntary correction within 3 months after the tax year or a late payment within 3 months after the end of the tax year. The interest stop (rentestop) extension to requested supplementary assessments (naheffingsaanslagen), ending at most 10 weeks after receipt, applies from 1 January 2023 but is not yet in force for VAT because it is technically not feasible. Source: [belastingrente betalen bij loonbelasting, btw en overdrachtsbelasting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/belastingrente/belastingrente_betalen_bij_loonbelasting_btw_en_overdrachtsbelasting). See section 4 for a calculated example.

### VAT filing and payment dates (aangifte- en betaaldata) 2026

The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table gives the last day of the month after the period for return and payment together. For 2026 the monthly dates are: December 2025 31 January 2026, January 28 February 2026, February 31 March 2026, March 30 April 2026, April 31 May 2026, May 30 June 2026, June 31 July 2026, July 31 August 2026, August 30 September 2026, September 31 October 2026, October 30 November 2026, November 31 December 2026, December 2026 31 January 2027. The quarterly dates are: Q4 2025 31 January 2026, Q1 30 April 2026, Q2 31 July 2026, Q3 31 October 2026, Q4 31 January 2027. The yearly return for 2026 is due by 31 March 2027. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Source: [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### VAT filing and payment deadlines (aangifte- en betaaltermijn), statutory (wettelijk) and software

AWR art. 10 lid 2 states a return for a period (tijdvak) is filed within a term set by the tax inspector of at least one month after the end of the period. AWR art. 19 lid 1 states the tax is paid within one month after the end of the period. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration tables give the exact dates for monthly, quarterly and yearly returns. Return and payment must both be received by that date. Software can submit from the 24th of the month before the period ends. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [uiterste aangifte- en betaaldatums](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums).

### Deferral of the VAT return (btw-aangifte)

Deferral for a VAT return (btw-aangifte) is possible only in exceptional situations (uitzonderlijke situaties), such as a serious calamity (ernstige calamiteit). The request must be made in writing to the tax office (belastingkantoor) before the filing date. A supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag) and a penalty remain possible. Source: [uitstel aanvragen btw-aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uitstel-aanvragen-btw-aangifte). This is stricter than the income tax deferral, which is available in the annual period before 1 May.

### VAT filing default penalty (aangifteverzuimboete) 2026

For a late or missing VAT return (btw-aangifte) there are 7 calendar days (kalenderdagen) leniency (coulance). After that the penalty is € 82 in 2026. Source: [u doet geen of te laat aangifte](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/boetes/u_doet_geen_of_te_laat_aangifte). The same penalty applied in 2025. The penalty is separate from the tax interest on a supplementary assessment (naheffingsaanslag).

### Income tax penalties (IB boetes) 2026

For income tax the default fine (verzuimboete) is € 469 and can go up to € 6.709. If the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration must request a return, the penalty is € 3.354. For Vpb the penalty is € 3.354 up to € 6.709. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The amounts for 2025 were the same. The penalty can be imposed together with an ex officio assessment when no return is filed.

### Payment default penalty for a tax levied by assessment (aanslagbelasting)

The payment default penalty for a tax levied by assessment is 5% of the open amount of the (provisional) assessment, minimum € 50, maximum € 6.709 per default. If the assessment is reduced, the penalty is reduced proportionally with a € 50 floor. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). See section 4 for calculated examples.

### Penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete) percentages

The penalty is 25% for gross negligence (grove schuld) and 50% for intent (opzet). For box 3 the percentages are 75% and 150%. Aggravated percentages are 100% and 300%. Source: [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete). The 2020 to 2024 captures show the same 50% and 25%. Intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return can lead to a penalty for gross negligence or intent, also with an additional assessment. Source: [wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig).

### Income tax after the expiry of the filing deadline (verstrijken van de aangiftetermijn) and requesting an invitation to file a return

After the deadline the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration sends a reminder (herinnering), then a formal demand (aanmaning) with a term of 10 working days (werkdagen). Filing after the formal demand term gives the default fine. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration can stop allowances and the provisional assessment. If no return is filed at all, an ex officio assessment plus default fine follows. No penalty is imposed when the person died on or after the date of the filing invitation letter. For intentional non-filing or an intentionally wrong return a penalty for gross negligence or intent is possible, also with an additional assessment. Requesting a return by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration: within 6 months after the tax debt arose for IB and Vpb, with 2 weeks leniency. Sources: [wat gebeurt er als ik geen aangifte doe of te laat of onvolledig](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/belastingaangifte/content/wat-gebeurt-er-als-ik-geen-aangifte-doe-of-te-laat-of-onvolledig) and [boete](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/standaard_functies/prive/contact/rechten_en_plichten_bij_de_belastingdienst/boete).

### Return without an invitation (aangifte zonder uitnodiging)

Without a filing invitation letter a return can be filed up to 5 years back, by 31 December of the fifth year after the tax year. Source: [hoeveel jaar terug belastingaangifte doen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/hoe_werkt_inkomstenbelasting/aangifte_doen/hoeveel-jaar-terug-belastingaangifte-doen). The same rule applies to the return for tax year 2026. The checked sources do not print a separate 2026 end date. This route is for someone who did not receive a filing invitation letter but still wants to file.

### Record-retention obligation (bewaarplicht)

AWR art. 52 lid 4, same in 2020 and 2026, states persons required to keep records (administratieplichtigen) keep the data carriers (gegevensdragers) for seven years unless the tax law says otherwise. Lid 2 covers among others natural persons with profits from business activities or income from other work (resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden). Lid 5 allows conversion to another data carrier (gegevensdrager) except the paper balance sheet (balans) and statement of income and expenditure (staat van baten en lasten). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration table: basic records (basisgegevens) 7 years, immovable property (onroerende zaken) 10 years, One Stop Shop data 10 years, other data 7 years or shorter by agreement with the tax office. The term starts when the item (gegeven) is no longer current. A 4-year lease is kept for those 4 years and then 7 more. Basic records are the general ledger (grootboek), accounts receivable (debiteuren) and accounts payable records (crediteurenadministratie), inventory records (voorraadadministratie), purchase and sales records (in- en verkoopadministratie), payroll records (loonadministratie), and data relevant for the taxation of third parties. Sources: [AWR 2026-01-01](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01) and [hoelang moet u gegevens bewaren](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/hoelang-moet-u-gegevens-bewaren).

### KVK registration window (inschrijvingsvenster)

Commercial Register Act 2007 (Handelsregisterwet 2007) art. 20 lid 1 states the statement (opgave) for the first registration (inschrijving) of a business (onderneming) is made within a period of two weeks that starts one week before and ends one week after the start of the business. Other required returns (opgaven) within one week after the fact. Source: [Handelsregisterwet 2007](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777/2025-07-16). The KVK page also states the window. Source: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). See section 4 for a calculated window.

### Chamber of Commerce number (KVK-nummer) and appointments (afspraken)

KVK issues the Chamber of Commerce number immediately when registering within the window. An identity check is possible up to three months ahead. Appointments can be made up to eight weeks ahead. Source: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/). This applies to the first registration.

### VAT ID and VAT number (btw-id en omzetbelastingnummer) period

The KVK page states the VAT ID (btw-id) and VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) are issued within two weeks or within ten working days after the start date. Ten working days is two calendar weeks. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration page states the registration takes at most 10 working days. Sources: [KVK hulp bij inschrijven](https://www.kvk.nl/hulp-en-contact/hulp-bij-inschrijven/) and [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). See section 7 for the wording difference.

### Dutch Tax and Customs Administration registration

KVK passes the data to the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration, so no separate registration is needed. Registration in the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration administration takes at most 10 working days. A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) is registered with the citizen service number (bsn), other forms get a legal entity identification number (RSIN). The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration derives the VAT number from it. Where KVK registration is not possible, the form Opgaaf startende onderneming (niet ingeschreven in het Handelsregister) is used. Source: [schrijf uw onderneming in](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/schrijf-uw-onderneming-in/). This is the registration duty for a new sole trader.
