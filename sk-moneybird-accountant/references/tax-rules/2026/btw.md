## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers VAT for a sole trader, a BV and other entrepreneurs, including rates, exemptions, special schemes, returns and records. The main domestic rates are 21% and 9%, with a zero rate for qualifying supplies. The 2026 VAT rules described here are enacted, while a ViDA bill has been adopted but is not yet in force and the announced national B2B e-invoicing timetable remains proposed. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, version 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Eerste Kamer bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn), [Government announcement on e-invoicing](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven)

## 3. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard rate

The standard VAT rate is 21%. Apply it to taxable supplies unless the supply qualifies for a reduced rate, the zero rate or an exemption. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 9 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services in Tabel I. Goods include food, water, flower products, medicines and aids, art, collectibles and antiques, books and periodicals. Services include bicycle, shoe, leather-goods, clothing and household-linen repairs, hairdressing, certain work on homes, camping, access to cultural and recreational events and facilities, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, qualifying e-books, and access to qualifying news websites and apps. News sites and apps mainly consisting of advertising, video or audible music do not qualify. Named related services include delivering goods, selling food in horeca, renting and repairing medical aids, lending books and lending art by the artist. [Tabel I goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Tabel I services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to qualifying supplies in Tabel II. It is not an exemption. A zero-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, while an exempt supply does not. An intra-EU supply of goods needs proof in the records that the goods went to another EU country, a buyer with a valid VAT ID from another member state, and an opgaaf ICP filed on time, correctly and completely. Late or incorrect ICP reporting can cost the zero rate and may lead to a fine. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 9 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst digital ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Logies

From 1 January 2026, logies is taxed at 21%. This also applies to payments received in 2025 for stays in 2026 or later, single-use vouchers for such stays, and facilities tied to the logies, including gas, electricity, water, sanitary facilities, laundry and parking. Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain at 9%. If one all-in price covers logies and separately offered reduced-rate items, split the price using their market values. [Belastingdienst logies guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies), [Belastingdienst logies news](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies)

### Separate reduced-rate items and culture, media and sport

Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain at 9%. The 2025 legislation withdrew the planned lapse of the culture, media and sport posts, so these posts remain at 9% in 2026. [Belastingdienst logies news](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Solar panels

The 0% rate covers the supply and installation of qualifying solar panels on or near homes. Covered items include non-integrated and integrated panels, cables, PV distributors, mounting materials, optimizers, inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The rate does not cover multi-function panels, solar heat collectors, panels on non-residential business or public buildings, roof reinforcement, cutting cable channels into walls, replacing the whole group cabinet, smart meters, bird or snow guards, replacement equipment bought separately, heat pumps or batteries. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home are taxed at 21% when the home is also supplied. The panels alone are at 0% if they are supplied and installed separately. [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen), [published decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### Exemptions

Art. 11 lid 1, onderdelen a to w, covers specified supplies and services including immovable property supplies and letting with exceptions, care in institutions, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, the universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Belastingdienst list also names investment gold and canteens and states that exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies carry no deduction of input VAT. Check the specific exemption and its conditions before applying it. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### VAT return periods

Quarterly is the usual period. An entrepreneur may request monthly or yearly filing by letter to the tax office. The tax office replies within 6 weeks and a change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. The 2026 sources do not state the € 15.000 quarterly VAT trigger that appeared in the Handboek 2020. Treat that trigger as unverified for 2026. [Period-change guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Annual return eligibility

The annual return is available only when all conditions are met. VAT for the year must be less than € 1.883, the business must not have a vergunning under article 23, and the business must be a natural person or a partnership of natural persons, such as a maatschap or vof. Each of the following must separately be less than € 10.000 a year: intra-community supplies, intra-community services, intra-community acquisitions and received intra-community services. A BV does not meet the natural-person or partnership condition. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Return and payment deadlines

Monthly and quarterly returns and payments are due by the last day of the month after the period. The annual return is due before 1 April of the following year. For the 2026 tax year, the annual return deadline is 31 March 2027. The aangiftedatum is the date the return is received. Filing and payment use the same deadline. An extension is available only for a calamity. Software returns may be submitted from the 24th of the month before the period ends. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/)

### Domestic 2026 filing dates

Quarterly return deadlines are 30 April 2026 for Q1, 31 July 2026 for Q2, 31 October 2026 for Q3 and 31 January 2027 for Q4. Monthly returns are due on the last day of the following month. The 2026 schedule specifically gives 28 February 2026 for January and 31 January 2027 for December. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Foreign-established business filing dates and penalties

The published schedule for foreign-established businesses gives quarterly deadlines of 31 May 2026 for Q1, 31 August 2026 for Q2, 30 November 2026 for Q3 and 28 February 2027 for Q4. The page for foreign-established businesses states a late-filing fine of € 82 and a late-payment fine of 3% of unpaid VAT, with a minimum of € 50 and maximum of € 6.709. The checked source is specific to foreign-established businesses. Do not generalise these fine amounts to a domestic ZZP case based on this source. [Foreign-business deadlines and penalties](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte)

### Dutch KOR conditions and turnover

A business established in the Netherlands may choose the KOR if its Dutch annual turnover is no more than € 20.000 in the application year and the preceding calendar year. The statute defines annual turnover by consideration during a calendar year and excludes business-used immovable property and depreciable movable assets. The KOR applies to all of the entrepreneur's activities and subnummers. Count Dutch-taxed supplies, including 0% supplies, domestic supplies reverse-charged to a customer, and specified Dutch exempt supplies relating to immovable property, means of payment and securities, and financial and insurance services. Do not count other exempt turnover, VAT on private use, sale of business-used investment goods, domestic supplies reverse-charged to the business as buyer, supplies taxed in another country or intra-community acquisitions. Before joining, measure turnover excluding VAT. During participation, use the total amount charged. The KOR gives no input VAT deduction and the invoice must not mention VAT. A BV may qualify as an entrepreneur if it meets the conditions. The statute requires establishment in the Netherlands. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, arts. 25 and 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020 on legal persons](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR registration without a notice

A business not yet registered with the inspector or KVK, and not required to register, may use the KOR without a notice if annual turnover is at most € 2.200 and the other statutory conditions are met. It must not use the exemption in other member states. [Decision of 21 November 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### KOR start, exit and cut-offs

Apply only after receiving the omzetbelastingnummer and btw-id. The notice must arrive at least 4 weeks before the requested start. A quarterly filer has cut-offs of 3 March for a 1 April start, 2 June for a 1 July start, 2 September for a 1 October start and 3 December for a 1 January start. A late notice starts in the next quarter. Returns continue until the confirmed start date. Confirmation is sent within 8 weeks. There is no statutory minimum participation term. Voluntary exit must be at least 4 weeks ahead. Under the statute, exit takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter. Re-entry is barred for the exit year and the following calendar year. [KOR application guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor), [KOR exit guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### KOR overrun

If turnover exceeds the Dutch ceiling, the supply that crosses it is taxed in full. The exemption then does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year. Re-entry after a voluntary exit is also barred for the exit year and the following year. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### EU KOR

A business established in the EU but not in the Netherlands may qualify for the Dutch exemption when its Union turnover is at most € 100.000. EU KOR participation in another member state requires prior notification and an EX number. Participation starts when the inspector issues or confirms that number, normally within 35 working days. The inspector may refuse participation when Union turnover exceeds the limit, another state reports that its limit was exceeded, the conditions are not met or additional time is needed to check for fraud. Quarterly totals are reported for each state, including a zero where there is no turnover. A separate notice is due within 15 working days after Union turnover exceeds the limit. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, arts. 25c to 25e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst EU KOR guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen), [EU KOR quarterly turnover](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet)

### Suppletie

Correct VAT returns for the past 5 calendar years. Submit a correction within 8 weeks after discovering an error, and before the point at which the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector knows. The earlier limit governs. A correction of € 1.000 or less may be reported in the next VAT return. A correction of more than € 1.000 requires the suppletie form within the applicable reporting deadline. The last return already filed must be corrected through suppletie. There is no belastingrente when the correction is filed within 3 months after the year or corrected within that year. A 2024 period can be corrected through the end of 2029. Failure to report is an offence. For intent or gross negligence, the vergrijpboete can be up to 100% of the VAT. [UBOB art. 15, version 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Reverse-charge rules

The usual rule is that the supplier pays VAT. The charge shifts to the customer for specified supplies by non-established suppliers and for designated domestic transactions. A Dutch fixed establishment that is not involved in the supply can be treated as non-established. The listed cases include subcontracting and staff lending in construction, shipbuilding, cleaning and gardening, waste and scrap, specified electronics, certain immovable property transactions, execution sales, gas and electricity certificates, gold, emission rights and telecom services between providers in the Netherlands. The electronics rule covers mobile phones, chips before end-product, game consoles, tablets and laptops when the total is at least € 10.000 per delivery per type of good. The invoice states “btw verlegd”, the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate. The supplier remains responsible. The customer reports domestic reverse charge in rubric 2 or foreign reverse charge in rubric 4 and deducts it when used for taxable activity. Subcontracting exceptions include work performed more than half in the supplier's own workshop, staff lent for such work, subordinate work on sold goods and disputes sent to the main contractor's tax office. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [UBOB, Afdeling E](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst cases](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [How reverse charge works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### Intra-EU B2B services

For a qualifying service to a business customer in another EU country, VAT is generally due in the customer's country. Check the customer's VAT ID, issue the invoice without Dutch VAT with “btw verlegd”, report the supply in the VAT return and report it in the opgaaf ICP. Some services are exceptions. Services exempt or zero-rated in the customer's country, OSS services, property-related services, passenger transport and admission to cultural, sport and similar events are not listed in the ICP under the cited guidance. [Belastingdienst intra-EU services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen), [ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### Opgaaf ICP

Report intra-EU supplies and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. The total should equal rubric 3b for the same period. Report goods in the period of the invoice date and services in the period the service was supplied. Do not file an ICP for a period with nothing to report. Put corrections in the next ICP. Goods are reported monthly unless the threshold test for quarterly filing is met. If the threshold is crossed in the first month, file 3 monthly reports. If crossed in the second month, file for the first two months and then the last month. If crossed in the third month, file one quarterly report and monthly reports afterwards. Quarterly filing can resume after 4 consecutive qualifying quarters, starting from the fifth quarter if it also qualifies. Services may be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with a permit. The goods threshold does not apply to services. Combined goods and services follow the goods rules. The cited 2024 guide describes annual ICP eligibility and due dates, but the checked 2026 ICP pages do not state the current due date. Call-off stock requires an identified buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, own transport, no establishment in the destination state, supply within 12 months of arrival, a register and a per-buyer listing in the ICP for the period transport began. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp), [2024 ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### EU distance sales and OSS

The € 10.000 threshold applies to qualifying suppliers established in one member state. It covers qualifying goods sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country when the supplier arranges or pays for transport, excluding margin goods, nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital consumer services also count. Measure the threshold excluding VAT. Below the threshold, Dutch VAT applies under the normal rules unless the supplier opts for foreign VAT. When the threshold is exceeded, foreign VAT applies from the crossing supply. Dutch VAT can resume from 1 January after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The threshold does not apply when the supplier has a fixed establishment in another EU country. A warehouse alone is not a fixed establishment.

The Unieregeling OSS is optional. It allows one quarterly return for EU distance sales, but foreign input VAT cannot be reclaimed through that return. The return is due by the last day of the following month, including a nil return. The scheme normally begins on the first day of the quarter after registration. To include a first supply, registration must arrive by the 10th day of the month after that supply. Corrections go in a later return within 3 years of the original due date. Keep OSS records for 10 years. Use euros and the ECB rate for the last day of the quarter. Three consecutive missed returns can lead to exclusion for 2 years. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 6k](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst distance sales](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### Invoerregeling

The Invoerregeling is monthly and applies to consignments up to € 150. Three consecutive missed monthly returns can lead to exclusion for 2 years. It cannot be combined with the KOR under the adopted ViDA bill's stated rule, which is not yet in force. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem), [Eerste Kamer bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn)

### Private use and the final return

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur's family. It can arise from mixed business and private use, such as a car, business computer or partly private building, or from withdrawing goods for exclusively private use. Staff private use can limit deduction under the BUA. Settle private use of goods and services, the car, the pro rata for taxable and exempt turnover, personnel provisions, gifts and business gifts above € 227 in the last return of the year, usually Q4 or December. The Handboek also names gas, water and electricity, fictieve services and herzieningen. If the last return has already been filed, correct it with suppletie. [Belastingdienst private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Business car private use

Without records of actual private use, the standard amount is 2,7% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm. It applies to cars in business assets and cars obtained another way. The amount is capped at VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT during the applicable period. Use 1,5% when no purchase VAT could be deducted or after the fourth year following the year the entrepreneur began using the car. Commuting counts as private use. Exempt use reduces the amount proportionally. A part-year is pro-rated. Put the adjustment in the last return of the year. The lease-car scope of the 1,5% rule is disputed in the checked sources. [Belastingdienst business car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [decision on private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### BUA gifts and benefits

The BUA excludes deduction for certain staff and gift expenses, including representation, business gifts and other gifts, staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport, except making a car available. The exclusion does not apply up to the VAT owed on an amount charged to the recipient. Provisions to the same person with total cost of no more than € 227 per accounting year stay outside the exclusion. The recipient's own contribution does not reduce the tested amount. Gifts are affected when the recipient would have had less than 30% deduction. [BUA, version in force from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Belastingdienst threshold guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken), [decision on own contribution](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### BUA exceptions and staff meals

Carve-outs include food and drink for staff, licensed besloten busvervoer for commuting, qualifying commuting bicycles and outplacement. The bicycle carve-out is limited to € 749 including VAT and has conditions on prior bicycles and other travel arrangements. For meals supplied below cost plus 25%, deduction is excluded up to 9% of the difference. [BUA, version in force from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Invoicing

Issue an invoice for supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. No invoice is required for private customers except in specified cases. Issue the invoice by the 15th day of the month after delivery. VAT is due at the latest in the period in which the customer pays. An invoice must identify both parties, the invoice date, a unique consecutive number, the goods or services, the supply or prepayment date, amounts excluding VAT per rate, the rate and VAT amount. It must also include the VAT ID with NL and the KVK number when registered. A trade name may be used when it is registered with the address at KVK. A post office box alone is not enough. A simplified invoice is allowed up to € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice, subject to required details. It is not allowed for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or certain reverse-charged supplies. Digital invoicing requires customer agreement and assurance of origin, content and readability. [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [simplified invoice](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/vereenvoudigde_factuur)

Kasstelsel is compulsory for listed trades, including shops and webshops, market traders, hairdressers and horeca. It can be requested when more than 80% of sales go to consumers. ## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

### Core rates

**Calculated example.** A business makes a taxable sale of € 1.000,00 excluding VAT at the standard rate.

1. VAT: € 1.000,00 × 21% = € 210,00.
2. Invoice total: € 1.000,00 + € 210,00 = € 1.210,00.

At the reduced rate:

1. VAT: € 1.000,00 × 9% = € 90,00.
2. Invoice total: € 1.000,00 + € 90,00 = € 1.090,00.

For a zero-rated sale of € 1.000,00, output VAT is € 1.000,00 × 0% = € 0,00. Input VAT deduction remains possible if the other conditions are met. [Rates in Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### Logies and a separately offered breakfast

**Official example, with the VAT arithmetic shown here.** The Belastingdienst gives market values of € 280,00 for logies and € 70,00 for breakfast. The total market value is € 280,00 + € 70,00 = € 350,00. The logies share is € 280,00 ÷ € 350,00 = 0,80, so 80%. The breakfast share is € 70,00 ÷ € 350,00 = 0,20, so 20%.

For an all-in price of € 150,00 excluding VAT:

1. Logies: € 150,00 × 80% = € 120,00.
2. Breakfast: € 150,00 × 20% = € 30,00.
3. VAT on logies at 21%: € 120,00 × 21% = € 25,20.
4. VAT on breakfast at 9%: € 30,00 × 9% = € 2,70.
5. Total VAT: € 25,20 + € 2,70 = € 27,90.

[Belastingdienst logies example](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies)

### Dutch KOR overrun

**Calculated example.** A KOR business has Dutch turnover of € 19.800,00 in the calendar year before a new supply of € 500,00.

1. Turnover after the supply: € 19.800,00 + € 500,00 = € 20.300,00.
2. That supply takes the turnover above the € 20.000 ceiling.
3. The whole € 500,00 supply is taxed and must be invoiced with VAT, not only the part above the ceiling.
4. The exemption does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year.

[Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Annual VAT return conditions

**Calculated example.** A natural-person business has VAT of € 1.500,00 a year and no artikel 23 permit. Each of the four intra-EU categories is € 9.000,00.

1. VAT a year: € 1.500,00 is less than € 1.883.
2. Each intra-EU category, tested separately: € 9.000,00 is less than € 10.000.
3. The business is a natural person, so the legal-form condition is met.
4. All conditions are met, so the yearly period can be requested.
5. If even one intra-EU category reaches € 10.000, the condition for yearly filing is not met.

[Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Reverse charge for electronics

**Calculated example.** A supplier makes a delivery of € 10.000,00 of one type of qualifying electronic good, such as laptops, to a business customer.

1. The threshold is € 10.000 or more per delivery per type of good.
2. The delivery total of € 10.000,00 meets that threshold.
3. The supplier invoices with "btw verlegd" and the customer's VAT ID.
4. The customer reports the VAT at the applicable rate and may deduct it if the purchase is used for taxable activity.

[UBOB art. 24ba](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2020-01-01), [Belastingdienst reverse-charge guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### Suppletie boundary

**Calculated example.** A correction of € 700,00 is € 1.000 or less, so it may be included in the next VAT return. A correction of € 1.200,00 is more than € 1.000, so the suppletie form is required. The form is due within 8 weeks after the error is discovered.

[Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

### EU distance-sales threshold

**Calculated example.** Assume the supplier is established in one member state. Qualifying distance sales and digital consumer services to consumers in other member states were € 10.000 or less in the preceding calendar year, and the current calendar year's total is € 9.000,00 before one further qualifying supply of € 2.000,00.

1. Art. 6k tests the € 10.000 threshold in both the current calendar year and the preceding calendar year. With the figures assumed here, both parts of the test are met at the start of the current year.
2. The further supply brings the current-year total to € 9.000,00 + € 2.000,00 = € 11.000,00.
3. That supply takes the total above € 10.000, so the normal rules apply from the moment of overrun. The crossing supply and later supplies carry the VAT of the customer's member state.
4. The start date depends on the preceding year as well. Had the preceding year been above € 10.000, the threshold would not apply in the current year.

[Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 6k](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst distance-sales guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren)

### ICP quarterly threshold

**Calculated example.** Assume goods supplies are € 40.000,00 in the current quarter and € 40.000,00 in each of the previous 4 quarters. Every amount is at or below the € 50.000 threshold, so the goods threshold test for quarterly reporting is met. The € 50.000 test applies to goods supplies and not to services.

[Belastingdienst ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp)

### Business car private use

**Calculated example.** Assume the catalogue price including VAT and bpm is € 50.000,00, the car is used for a full year, and the standard calculation applies.

1. Standard private-use amount: € 50.000,00 × 2,7% = € 1.350,00.
2. Exempt use reduces the amount proportionally. 40% of the use is exempt use, so the taxable-use share is 60%.
3. Amount after the reduction: € 1.350,00 × 60% = € 810,00.

The cap is the VAT deducted in the year plus one-fifth of the purchase VAT. Assume the VAT deducted in the year is € 200,00 and the purchase VAT is € 5.000,00:

1. One-fifth of the purchase VAT: € 5.000,00 ÷ 5 = € 1.000,00.
2. Cap: € 200,00 + € 1.000,00 = € 1.200,00.
3. The cap is applied to the reduced amount of € 810,00. The lower of € 810,00 and € 1.200,00 is € 810,00, so the amount due is € 810,00.

[Belastingdienst business car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [car private-use decision](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

### BUA threshold

**Official example.** The Belastingdienst example gives a € 75,00 Christmas package for all staff, a € 400,00 staff outing for half the staff and a € 150,00 watch for one employee. Without the outing, the employee who received the watch stays at € 75,00 + € 150,00 = € 225,00, which is within the € 227 threshold, so the VAT on those provisions is deductible. The VAT on the outing is not deductible in the example.

[Belastingdienst BUA example](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken)

### Invoice timing

**Official example.** The Handboek example says that a supply delivered on 10 April must be invoiced by 15 May.

[Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/)

## 5. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Logies moved from 9% to 21% from 1 January 2026. The change also applies to the linked facilities described above and to relevant advance payments and single-use vouchers. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html), [Belastingdienst logies guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies)

- Culture, media and sport posts remain at 9%. The planned lapse of these reduced-rate posts was withdrawn by Stb. 2025, 339. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

- The Dutch KOR structure continues with the € 20.000 domestic limit, the current-year and preceding-year turnover test, and the € 2.200 registration threshold without a notice. The checked material identifies no change to these amounts from 2025 to 2026. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, version 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, version 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [2024 decision on the registration threshold](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html)

- The annual-return VAT limit remains less than € 1.883. It was UNVERIFIED in the checked 2025 material and is Verified for 2026. This is a change in verification status, not a confirmed change in the legal amount. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

- Bill 36.920 moved from pending to adopted by both chambers in 2026. It is adopted but not yet in force. [Eerste Kamer bill page](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn)

- The national B2B e-invoicing and reporting timetable was announced as a proposal in 2026. It is not a current 2026 filing duty. [Government announcement](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven)

- The checked material identifies no other substantive 2026 rate or threshold change from 2025.

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT rates applied, exempt supplies, zero-rated supplies, reverse-charge treatment and input VAT deductions. For intra-EU zero-rated goods, retain proof that the goods left for another EU country, verify the customer's VAT ID and file the ICP accurately and on time. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

- Keep invoices with the required details. Issue business invoices by the 15th day of the following month. Make sure digital invoices preserve authenticity of origin, content and readability. [Belastingdienst invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/)

- For a domestic KOR participant, track all Dutch turnover that counts toward the limit, across all activities and subnummers. Do not charge or mention VAT on KOR invoices and do not deduct input VAT. If the threshold is crossed, treat the full crossing supply as taxable and continue with the statutory post-overrun restriction. [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- Report domestic reverse-charged VAT in rubric 2 and foreign reverse-charged VAT in rubric 4. Deduct the amount only when the purchase is used for taxable activity. For intra-EU B2B services, check the customer's VAT ID and report the service in the return and ICP when the service belongs there. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge explanation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen), [Belastingdienst intra-EU services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen)

- Reconcile each return period and pay by its due date. A software return can be submitted from the 24th of the month before the period ends. For quarterly filers, check the 2026 dates in section 2. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

- In the final return of the year, account for private use, the business car, the pro rata between taxable and exempt turnover, personnel provisions, gifts and other listed year-end adjustments. If that return has already been submitted, use suppletie for the correction. [Belastingdienst final return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst suppletie guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

- OSS records must be kept for 10 years. Submit the OSS return even when there are no supplies for the quarter. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

- If a sole trader also owns a BV, check the VAT position and KOR conditions for each entrepreneur involved. The 2026 statute uses the condition that the entrepreneur is established in the Netherlands. The Handboek 2020 expressly identifies legal persons such as a BV as possible KOR participants, but the 2026 statute controls the current conditions. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Monthly VAT trigger:** The Handboek 2020 said that paying more than € 15.000 per quarter generally meant monthly returns. The Handboek 2026 and current period page omit that trigger. Its status for 2026 is unknown and UNVERIFIED. A current Belastingdienst rule or updated Handbook stating the trigger would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

- **ICP deadline:** The 2026 ICP pages do not state a deadline. The 2024 guide says domestic ICP reports are due within 1 month after the chosen period, but that timing is not confirmed by the checked 2026 pages. A current 2026 ICP deadline page or current primary guidance would settle it. [2026 ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [2024 ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

- **OSS inactivity:** The claim that 2 years without supplies ends OSS participation while returns continue is UNVERIFIED because the checked page does not say this. A current Belastingdienst rule on inactivity would settle it. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

- **Lease-car 1,5% calculation:** The current car page says the 1,5% calculation also applies after the fourth year where a car is leased. The Handboek 2026 says the 1,5% calculation does not apply to lease cars and that the calculation remains 2,7%. The decision on private car use does not mention lease cars. A Belastingdienst ruling or corrected guidance would settle the conflict. [Belastingdienst car page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [decision on private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

- **KOR establishment condition:** The Handboek 2026 describes the condition as “established in the Netherlands or have a fixed establishment in the Netherlands.” Art. 25a lid 1 of the statute requires the entrepreneur to be established in the Netherlands. The statute prevails. An updated Handbook or a formal interpretation would settle the discrepancy in the guidance. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- **KOR voluntary exit timing:** The Belastingdienst exit page says voluntary exit is on the first day of the aangiftetijdvak. The statute says the first day of the next calendar quarter. These coincide for quarterly filers but differ for monthly filers. The statute prevails. Updated guidance that matches the statute would settle the webpage discrepancy. [Belastingdienst KOR exit page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- **EU ViDA dates:** The EU adoption and publication dates of 11 March 2025 and 25 March 2025, and the stated date of 14 April 2025 for entry into force, remain UNVERIFIED against a Dutch primary source. A Dutch primary publication or statute setting those dates would settle them. [EU ViDA page used in the checked material](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)

- **ViDA OSS legislation:** The 2025 Kamerbrief said OSS legislation was expected in Q3 2026. The checked material does not establish whether that legislation was adopted or published. A current bill or official publication would settle its status. [Kamerbrief on ViDA](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-22112-4090.html)

- **Invoice example:** A separate business.gov.nl example for delivery on 10 January and invoicing by 15 February was not re-read. It is not relied upon here. The verified Handbook example is delivery on 10 April and invoicing by 15 May. Re-reading the original primary invoice guidance would settle that example. [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
