## 4. WORKED EXAMPLES

<a id="4-worked-examples"></a>

**Example 1: loon plus winst (Official example, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).**
The 2026 page works with loon of € 40.000 plus freelance income of € 50.000. Step 1: the two amounts together are € 40.000 plus € 50.000 is € 90.000. Step 2: the bijdrage-inkomen is capped at € 79.409. Step 3: the loon of € 40.000 already fills part of that room, so the remaining headroom is € 79.409 minus € 40.000 is € 39.409. Step 4: the 4,85% applies to € 39.409 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet). The same page states that loon of € 80.000 plus freelance income of € 15.000 gives no bijdrage at all, because the loon of € 80.000 lies above the maximum of € 79.409 and leaves no headroom (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

**Example 2: only winst, no other income (Calculated example).**
Inputs: a profit (winst) of € 60.000, no loon, no pension, no other contribution income, and the 2026 zelfstandigenaftrek of € 1.200 applies. Step 1: winst before ondernemersaftrek is € 60.000. Step 2: € 60.000 minus € 1.200 is € 58.800. Step 3: the MKB-winstvrijstelling is 12,7% of € 58.800, and € 58.800 times 12,7 / 100 is € 7.467,60. Step 4: the belastbare winst is € 58.800 minus € 7.467,60 is € 51.332,40. Step 5: no oudedagsreserve addition exists in 2026, so there is no art. 3.70 lid 2 amount to leave out. Step 6: the base lies between nil and € 79.409, so the full € 51.332,40 is the bijdrage-inkomen. Step 7: 4,85% of € 51.332,40 = € 51.332,40 times 4,85 / 100 is € 2.489,62, the Zvw contribution by aanslag. The same € 51.332,40 is the belastbare winst uit onderneming in box 1.

**Example 3: loon plus winst (Calculated example).**
Inputs: loon of € 50.000 from an employer who pays the 6,10% werkgeversheffing, plus a belastbare winst of € 40.000. Step 1: the loon uses headroom against the € 79.409 maximum, so the headroom is € 79.409 minus € 50.000 is € 29.409. Step 2: 4,85% of € 29.409 = € 29.409 times 4,85 / 100 is € 1.426,34, the contribution by aanslag on the winst. Step 3: the part of the winst above the headroom, € 40.000 minus € 29.409 is € 10.591, bears no contribution, because the maximum applies once per person (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

**Example 4: profit above the maximum (Calculated example).**
Inputs: a belastbare winst of € 100.000 and no other income. Step 1: the maximum is € 79.409, so the bijdrage-inkomen is € 79.409 and not € 100.000. Step 2: 4,85% of € 79.409 = € 79.409 times 4,85 / 100 is € 3.851,34, the maximum contribution for this income at this rate. Step 3: the remaining € 100.000 minus € 79.409 is € 20.591 bears no Zvw contribution.

No example can be built for the AOW-age rate, because the checked sources give no 2026 percentage for the verlaagde inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage (see section 7).
