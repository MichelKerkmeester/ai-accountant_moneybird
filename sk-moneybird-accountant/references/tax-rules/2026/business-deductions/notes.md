## 5. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Zelfstandigenaftrek: € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Zelfstandigenaftrek at AOW age: € 1.235 in 2025 to € 600 in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk: € 15.738 in 2025 to € 15.979 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- S&O starter increase: € 7.875 in 2025 to € 7.996 in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Tariefsaanpassing: 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025 to 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Maximum value of the ondernemersaftrek and MKB-winstvrijstelling: 37,48% in 2025 to 37,56% in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Second-test lookback window: 2020 to 2024 for 2025 to 2021 to 2025 for 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Unchanged from 2025 to 2026: the urencriterium of 1.225 hours, the verlaagd urencriterium of 800 hours and not more than 1.225, the startersaftrek of € 2.123 and € 1.062 at AOW age, the startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid of € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, the MKB-winstvrijstelling of 12,7%, the meewerkaftrek bands of 1,25% to 4%, the stakingsaftrek maximum of € 3.630, and the end of oudedagsreserve additions with the art. 10a.29 afneming rules (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- No rule change in the statute for this topic in 2026. The material states that the rules are the same as 2025, and the current Belastingdienst pages on the urencriterium, startersaftrek, meewerkaftrek, stakingsaftrek, MKB-winstvrijstelling and oudedagsreserve match the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The MvT of Belastingplan 2027 states that ondernemers can still use the startersaftrek in 2026 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).

## 6. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business, because the 1.225 hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. All hours on the business count, including non-billable work, but standby time does not count (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Record the partner's hours in the business, because the meewerkaftrek bands of 1,25% to 4% depend on those hours and the hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Pay to a partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit and is no income for the partner. The Belastingdienst treats such a partner as working without pay, so the meewerkaftrek can apply (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Keep the S&O-verklaring from RVO for the S&O aftrek, together with the proof of at least 500 hours of S&O work and the urencriterium (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Keep the beschikkingen for the niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek and for the offset of that amount, because both are given by beschikking open to bezwaar and the offset runs over the next nine calendar years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- No addition to the oudedagsreserve can be booked in 2026. An existing reserve stays and follows the art. 10a.29 afneming rules. Decreases are added to profit, and lijfrente premiums for converting the reserve are deductible up to the decrease of that year. Premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 10a.29, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- The belastbare winst uit onderneming is the profit reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and the MKB-winstvrijstelling, and it is taxed in box 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.2 and 3.79a).
- Keep the documentation of a staking of a whole business, because the stakingsaftrek of at most € 3.630 is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later staking, and there is no aftrek on profit as medegerechtigde (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

## 7. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Not in the checked sources for 2026: the dated starter examples for the second test of the urencriterium (starter in 2026, since 2024, since 2022, a 2018 starter who stopped in 2023 and restarted in 2026, since 2018). Fetched on 2026-10-11 the Belastingdienst urencriterium page has only the two webhosting examples. They are consistent with the statute and with the FISIN 2026 window of 2021 to 2025, but keep them out unless a Belastingdienst page with them is found (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Conflict in the FISIN 2026 carry-forward example. FISIN 2026 section 8.4.3 prints € 2.740 unused in the loss year, while the material marks € 2.470 as correct. The 2025 statute prints € 2.470. The example also has typos in the year labels and amounts. Both values and both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. The statute and the FISIN 2025 table settle it at € 2.470.
- Not in the checked sources: the base of the meewerkaftrek percentage. Art. 3.78 gives the aftrek as a percentage of the profit, and the checked sources do not state whether that base is first reduced by the other parts of the ondernemersaftrek. The statute text of art. 3.78 as valid on 1 January 2026 and a Belastingdienst calculation example would settle it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Not verified for 2026: the interaction with the Zvw contribution. The checked material covers the belastbare winst uit onderneming as part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The detail belongs to the Zvw topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. Bill 37 022 would cut the startersaftrek to € 10 in 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028, and would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) from 1 January 2029. The adopted 2027 statute text still holds € 2.123 for 2027, adopted but not yet in force. Both layers and both URLs: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. The Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups would cut the meewerkaftrek and the stakingsaftrek, with Artikel I onderdelen B and C carrying the cuts and Artikel II the abolition, and it would delete the meewerkaftrek and the stakingsaftrek (onderdelen c and d after renumbering) three calendar years after its cuts take effect. The enacted 2027 text still holds the 1,25% to 4% bands and the € 3.630 maximum, adopted but not yet in force. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Conflict for 2027, not 2026: the enacted statute text holds a tariefsaanpassing of 11,91% with a maximum value of 37,59%, while the Belastingplan 2027 figure is 38,16% (proposed). Both URLs: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf.
