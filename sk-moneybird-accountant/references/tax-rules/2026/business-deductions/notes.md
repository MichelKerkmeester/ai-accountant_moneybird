## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek): € 2.470 in 2025 to € 1.200 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Self-employed person's deduction at state pension (AOW) age: € 1.235 in 2025 to € 600 in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk): € 15.738 in 2025 to € 15.979 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- R&D (S&O) starter increase: € 7.875 in 2025 to € 7.996 in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing): 12,02% above € 76.817 in 2025 to 11,94% above € 78.426 in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- Maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption: 37,48% in 2025 to 37,56% in 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
- Second-test lookback window: 2020 to 2024 for 2025 to 2021 to 2025 for 2026 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Unchanged from 2025 to 2026: the hours criterion (urencriterium) of 1.225 hours, the reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium) of 800 hours and not more than 1.225, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) of € 2.123 and € 1.062 at state pension age, the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) of € 12.000, € 8.000 and € 4.000, the SME profit exemption of 12,7%, the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek) bands of 1,25% to 4%, the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek) maximum of € 3.630, and the end of old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) additions with the art. 10a.29 reduction (afneming) rules (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01 and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- No rule change in the statute for this topic in 2026. The material states that the rules are the same as 2025, and the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages on the hours criterion, starter's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction, SME profit exemption and old-age reserve match the statute (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).
- The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) of Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) states that entrepreneurs can still use the starter's deduction in 2026 ([Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Record the hours spent on the business, because the 1.225 hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices. All hours on the business count, including non-billable work, but standby time does not count (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Record the partner's hours in the business, because the deduction for assisting partner bands of 1,25% to 4% depend on those hours and the hours must be made plausible (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Pay to a partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit and is no income for the partner. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) treats such a partner as working without pay, so the deduction for assisting partner can apply (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Keep the R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO) for the R&D deduction, together with the proof of at least 500 hours of research and development work and the hours criterion (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).
- Keep the decisions (beschikkingen) for the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) and for the offset of that amount, because both are given by a decision open to objection (bezwaar) and the offset runs over the next nine calendar years (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek).
- No addition to the old-age reserve can be booked in 2026. An existing reserve stays and follows the art. 10a.29 reduction rules. Decreases are added to profit, and annuity (lijfrente) premiums for converting the reserve are deductible up to the decrease of that year. Premiums paid within six months after the year end can count for that year by choice in the return (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 10a.29, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).
- The taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) is the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, and it is taxed in box 1 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 3.2 and 3.79a).
- Keep the documentation of the cessation (staking) of a whole business, because the discontinuation deduction of at most € 3.630 is available only once in a lifetime, an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and there is no deduction on profit as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- Not in the checked sources for 2026: the dated starter examples for the second test of the hours criterion (starter in 2026, since 2024, since 2022, a 2018 starter who stopped in 2023 and restarted in 2026, since 2018). Fetched on 2026-10-11 the Tax and Customs Administration hours criterion page has only the two webhosting examples. They are consistent with the statute and with the FISIN 2026 window of 2021 to 2025, but keep them out unless a Tax and Customs Administration page with them is found (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).
- Conflict in the FISIN 2026 carry-forward example. FISIN 2026 section 8.4.3 prints € 2.740 unused in the loss year, while the material marks € 2.470 as correct. The 2025 statute prints € 2.470. The example also has typos in the year labels and amounts. Both values and both URLs: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2025-01-01. The statute and the FISIN 2025 table settle it at € 2.470.
- Not in the checked sources: the base of the deduction for assisting partner percentage. Art. 3.78 gives the deduction as a percentage of the profit, and the checked sources do not state whether that base is first reduced by the other parts of the entrepreneur's allowance. The statute text of art. 3.78 as valid on 1 January 2026 and a Tax and Customs Administration calculation example would settle it (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).
- Not verified for 2026: the interaction with the Zvw contribution. The checked material covers the taxable profit from business activities as part of the income on which the self-assessed Zvw contribution is levied, up to a maximum contribution income, verified for 2020 and 2021 only. The detail belongs to the Zvw topic (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2021/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. Bill 37 022 would cut the starter's deduction to € 10 in 2027 and abolish it from 1 January 2028, and would delete art. 3.74 onderdeel d (starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs) from 1 January 2029. The adopted 2027 statute text still holds € 2.123 for 2027, adopted but not yet in force. Both layers and both URLs: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-37022-2.html and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Proposed for later years, not 2026. The Act on Fiscal Stimulation of Start-ups and Scale-ups (Wet fiscale stimulering start-ups en scale-ups) would cut the deduction for assisting partner and the discontinuation deduction, with Artikel I onderdelen B and C carrying the cuts and Artikel II the abolition, and it would delete the deduction for assisting partner and the discontinuation deduction (onderdelen c and d after renumbering) three calendar years after its cuts take effect. The enacted 2027 text still holds the 1,25% to 4% bands and the € 3.630 maximum, adopted but not yet in force. Sources: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/wetsvoorstel-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups/wetsvoorstel-wet-fiscale-stimulering-start-ups-en-scale-ups-wetsvoorstel.pdf and https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01.
- Conflict for 2027, not 2026: the enacted statute text holds a rate adjustment of 11,91% with a maximum value of 37,59%, while the Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) figure is 38,16% (proposed). Both URLs: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2027-01-01 and https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/09/15/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027/fiscale-sleuteltabel-2027.pdf.
