## 1. OVERVIEW

<a id="1-overview"></a>

This file covers the entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) and the SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) for a sole trader in tax year 2026. The rules are the same as in 2025, with new amounts: the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) is € 1.200, the starter's deduction (startersaftrek) is € 2.123, the SME profit exemption is 12,7% and the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) on these deductions is 11,94% above € 78.426. Every 2026 value in this file is enacted and verified against the statute valid on 1 January 2026, and the current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) pages on the hours criterion (urencriterium), starter's deduction, deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek), SME profit exemption and old-age reserve (oudedagsreserve) match the statute. The explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) of Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) states that entrepreneurs can still use the starter's deduction in 2026, and later-year proposals are kept apart in section 7.
