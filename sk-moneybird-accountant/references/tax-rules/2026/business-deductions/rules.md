## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

The entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) of art. 3.74 has five parts: the self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), the deduction for research and development work (aftrek voor speur- en ontwikkelingswerk), the deduction for assisting partner (meewerkaftrek), the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs (startersaftrek bij arbeidsongeschiktheid) and the discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek). The starter's deduction is not a separate list item, it is an increase (verhoging) of the self-employed person's deduction in art. 3.76 lid 3 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). Only an income tax entrepreneur (IB-ondernemer) can use these schemes. Whether someone is an entrepreneur (ondernemer) for income tax (inkomstenbelasting) is judged per activity by the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst), separately from VAT (btw), on profit, independence, capital, time spent, number of clients, publicity, business risk (ondernemersrisico) and liability for business debts. Someone who is not an income tax entrepreneur reports income from other work (inkomsten uit overig werk), calculates the profit the same way but has no right to the self-employed person's deduction or the investment deduction (investeringsaftrek) (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/ and https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/bedrijf-starten/inschrijven-bij-kvk/wanneer-bent-u-ondernemer/). Persons entitled to a share (medegerechtigden) get no self-employed person's deduction, deduction for assisting partner, discontinuation deduction or SME profit exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) on their profit from co-entitlement. The order of application is the profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption (art. 3.2 and 3.79a, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Hours criterion (urencriterium), 1.225 hours

Art. 3.6 lid 1 requires at least 1.225 hours in the calendar year on work for one or more businesses from which the taxpayer earns profit as an entrepreneur (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The hours cannot be recalculated to the part of the year someone was an entrepreneur. The Tax and Customs Administration example starts a webhosting business on 1 July, which leaves six months as an entrepreneur, and still requires at least 1.225 hours in that period. The Chamber of Commerce (KVK) gives the same reading for a start on 1 May: eight months, about 117 working days, and at 8 hours a day 936 hours, so extra hours are needed (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium and https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-magische-1225-uur-alles-over-het-urencriterium).

All hours spent on the business count, including non-billable work such as quotes, administration and building the business website. Standby time does not count: a webhosting owner reachable 24 hours a day in the weekend counts only the 4 hours spent fixing the one outage. Hours must be made plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, timesheets and invoices (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Second test and the lookback window 2021 to 2025

Art. 3.6 lid 1 has a second element. Besides the 1.225 hours, either the total time spent on the businesses and on employment and other work must be mainly (grotendeels) spent on the businesses, or the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years. FISIN 2026 prints that lookback window as 2021 to 2025 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). The effect is that the second test falls away for anyone with at least one non-entrepreneur year in the window, and it comes back only after five straight entrepreneur years. The current Tax and Customs Administration hours criterion page does not carry the dated starter examples for this test, see section 7.

### Reduced hours criterion (verlaagd urencriterium), 800 hours

Art. 3.78a lid 3 applies the art. 3.6 test with 1.225 replaced by 800. The Tax and Customs Administration words it as at least 800 hours and not more than 1.225 hours. It is a condition only of the starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs, so someone between 800 and 1.225 hours does not get the self-employed person's deduction but can get that variant if the other conditions are met (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with related persons

Art. 3.6 lid 2 to 4: hours for a business that is part of a partnership (samenwerkingsverband) with related persons (verbonden personen) do not count if (a) the taxpayer's work is mainly supporting and such a partnership between non-related persons would be unusual, or (b) the partnership relates to a business from which a related person earns profit as an entrepreneur but the taxpayer does not. Related persons are members of the taxpayer's household and blood relatives or relatives by marriage in the direct line or members of their household. A partnership with a body in which the taxpayer or a related person holds a substantial interest (aanmerkelijk belang) counts under (b). The Tax and Customs Administration reads mainly supporting as 70% or more, with a dentist and dental assistant partnership as the example for (a) and a sub-partnership (ondermaatschap) for (b) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/voorwaarden_urencriterium).

### Pregnancy and the 16 weeks

Art. 3.6 lid 5: if the work is interrupted by the taxpayer's pregnancy, it counts as not interrupted during a period equal to the pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof) for employees. The Tax and Customs Administration states this as non-worked hours counting as worked hours over a total of 16 weeks (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.6 lid 5, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2023/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Self-employed person's deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek), € 1.200

Art. 3.76 lid 1 gives the self-employed person's deduction to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion. For 2026 the amount for an entrepreneur below state pension (AOW) age at the start of the year is € 1.200 (lid 2), and FISIN 2026 and the Ondernemersplein (government entrepreneur portal) agree (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). Art. 3.76 lid 5: the deduction is not more than the profit, except for an entrepreneur who qualifies for the starter's deduction. The amount cut off by this cap is called the unused self-employed person's deduction (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek), and if the cap sets the deduction to nil the year still counts as a year with a self-employed person's deduction for the starter's deduction count (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01).

### Self-employed person's deduction at state pension age, € 600

Art. 3.76 lid 4: at state pension age at the start of the year, the deduction is 50% of the amount under lid 2, so € 600, and the starter's deduction is halved too. FISIN 2026 prints € 600 for the self-employed person's deduction (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Unused self-employed person's deduction, offset over nine calendar years

Art. 3.76 lid 6: the tax inspector (inspecteur) sets the unused amount in a decision (beschikking) open to objection (bezwaar). Subsection 7: it is offset in the next nine calendar years by raising the self-employed person's deduction of those years, at most by the amount by which that year's profit exceeds that year's self-employed person's deduction, oldest first. Subsection 8: the offset is also set by a decision open to objection (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/zelfstandigenaftrek1/verrekenen_niet_gerealiseerde_zelfstandigenaftrek). The Tax and Customs Administration prints the mechanism on its carry-forward page, with the 2025 to 2026 example used in section 4.

### Starter's deduction (startersaftrek), € 2.123

Art. 3.76 lid 3 raises the self-employed person's deduction by the starter's deduction if the conditions in the next subsection are met. The Tax and Customs Administration adds that the starter needs a right to the self-employed person's deduction, and that the combined self-employed person's deduction and starter's deduction can exceed the profit, so the excess is a loss that can be offset against other box 1 income or carried to other years. The starter's deduction does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 by means of a tax-neutral return from a private limited company (geruisloze terugkeer uit een bv) if the business was started in the calendar year or one of the five preceding years (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek). For 2026 the amount is € 2.123, and the explanatory memorandum (memorie van toelichting, MvT) of Tax Plan 2027 (Belastingplan 2027) states that entrepreneurs can still use the starter's deduction in 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and [Belastingplan 2027 (Kamerstuk 37022), memorie van toelichting](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D43301)).

### Starter's deduction at state pension age, € 1.062

Because art. 3.76 lid 4 halves the amount under lid 3 as well, the starter's deduction is € 1.062 at state pension age at the start of the year, which is 50% of € 2.123. FISIN 2026 prints € 1.062 (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction prior-years condition

Art. 3.76 lid 3: the starter's deduction applies if the entrepreneur was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years and the self-employed person's deduction was applied not more than twice in that period. For 2026 the five preceding years are 2021 to 2025. The Tax and Customs Administration phrases the prior-years test as "U was in de afgelopen 5 jaar niet elk jaar ondernemer voor de inkomstenbelasting", which is the same test, and adds that the starter's deduction is given at most 3 times in the first 5 years as an entrepreneur. FISIN 2026 prints the condition in the same way (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/startersaftrek and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Starter's deduction for disabled entrepreneurs

Art. 3.78a applies to the entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding calendar years, has a right in the calendar year to a disability benefit (arbeidsongeschiktheidsuitkering) or to employment support (arbeidsondersteuning) under the Wajong, does not meet the hours criterion but does meet the reduced hours criterion of at least 800 and not more than 1.225 hours, and has not reached state pension age at the start of the year. It does not apply to an entrepreneur who continues a business under art. 14c Wet Vpb 1969 started in the year or the five years before. The benefits that count are WIA, WAO, WAZ, Wajong, a comparable foreign statutory scheme, a scheme designated by ministerial regulation (ministeriële regeling), and a periodic benefit from a disability or accident insurance (invaliditeit of ongeval). The amounts are € 12.000 if the deduction was not applied in the five preceding years, € 8.000 if it was applied for one year and € 4.000 if it was applied for two years, each not more than the profit. The statute has no tier for more than two uses, so there is no deduction then, and FISIN prints € 0 for that case (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/ondernemersaftrek-2026/startersaftrek-bij-arbeidsongeschiktheid-2026).

### Deduction for research and development work, € 15.979

The deduction for research and development work is part of the entrepreneur's allowance (art. 3.74 and art. 3.77). For 2026 the amount is € 15.979, and FISIN 2026 agrees (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek). FISIN adds that the deduction requires an R&D declaration (S&O-verklaring) from the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO), at least 500 hours of research and development work and the hours criterion (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2020/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### R&D starter increase, € 7.996

Art. 3.77 gives a base amount plus a starter increase for an entrepreneur who was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the five preceding years and had the deduction applied at most twice in that period. For 2026 the starter increase is € 7.996, per FISIN 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/ondernemersaftrek_en_investeringsaftrek).

### Deduction for assisting partner bands

Art. 3.78 applies to the entrepreneur who meets the hours criterion and whose partner works in the business without any pay. The bands are 525 to under 875 hours 1,25%, 875 to under 1.225 hours 2%, 1.225 to under 1.750 hours 3%, and 1.750 hours or more 4% of the profit. The statute bands run from "gelijk aan of meer dan" the lower number to "minder dan" the upper number, so exactly 875 hours falls in the 2% band, exactly 1.225 in the 3% band and exactly 1.750 in the 4% band. The profit base excludes profit replacing benefits lost by expropriation (onteigening), profit from (partial) cessation (staking) including cessation by death, and profit from moving assets abroad or the final settlement of art. 3.60 and 3.61. Hours must be made plausible (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek). The checked sources give the deduction for assisting partner as a percentage of the profit and do not state the base order against the other parts of the entrepreneur's allowance, see section 7.

### Pay to the partner below € 5.000

Art. 3.16 lid 4: pay to the partner below € 5.000 is not deductible from profit. The Tax and Customs Administration therefore treats a partner paid less than € 5.000 as working without pay, and such pay is no income for the partner. This matters for the deduction for assisting partner, because the band can still apply while the wage cannot be deducted (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2020-01-01, art. 3.16, text identical 2020 to 2027, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/meewerkaftrek).

### Discontinuation deduction (stakingsaftrek), € 3.630

Art. 3.79 applies to profit arising from the cessation (staking) of one or more whole businesses. The deduction equals that profit, at most € 3.630, and this maximum is reduced, not below nil, by a discontinuation deduction enjoyed in earlier years. For a business continued under a rollover (doorschuiving) on dissolution of a community of property (huwelijksgemeenschap) (art. 3.59 lid 2), on death (art. 3.62) or to entrepreneurs (art. 3.63), it applies to the benefits of that business only if it was run for the taxpayer's own account for at least three years. Until 2022 art. 3.79 lid 1 onderdeel b also covered profit from a reduction of the old-age reserve after a tax-neutral rollover (geruisloze doorschuiving) or conversion. That provision was removed from art. 3.79 on 1 January 2023 and the same rule now sits in the transitional art. 10a.29 lid 7 for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022. The Tax and Customs Administration adds that the maximum is available only once in a lifetime, that an unused remainder can be used at a later cessation, and that there is no deduction on profit as a person entitled to a share (medegerechtigde) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01, art. 3.79 and 10a.29, and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/ondernemersaftrek/stakingsaftrek).

### Old-age reserve in 2026

Art. 3.67 to 3.72 lapsed on 1 January 2023, so no addition to the old-age reserve is possible in 2026. Art. 10a.29 lid 1 keeps art. 3.70, 3.71, 3.72 lid 2 and 3 and 3.73 as they read on 31 December 2022 in force for an old-age reserve that existed on 31 December 2022, or at the end of the last financial year not coinciding with the calendar year that started before 1 January 2023. The reduction (afneming) rules are that the reserve decreases by an amount the taxpayer chooses, at most the annuity premiums (lijfrentepremies) deducted as expenditure for income provisions (uitgaven voor inkomensvoorzieningen) in the year, and that it decreases by the excess over the business assets (ondernemingsvermogen) at year end if the business or part of it ceased in the year, state pension age was reached at the start of the year, or the hours criterion was not met in the year and the previous year. Decreases are added to profit. Art. 10a.29 lid 12 lets the taxpayer deduct annuity premiums for converting the reserve up to the decrease of that year, and lid 14 lets premiums paid within six months after the year end count for that year by choice in the return. Subsections 3 to 5 carry the reserve over to a spouse or partner who continues the business, subsection 6 switches off art. 3.65 lid 1 for this reserve, and subsection 7 keeps the discontinuation deduction for a reduction after a tax-neutral rollover. On death the reserve is settled unless the partner continues the business and takes over the reserve on a request with the return (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01, art. 10a.29, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2023-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/fiscale_reserves/oudedagsreserve).

### SME profit exemption, 12,7%

Art. 3.79a makes the SME profit exemption a percentage of the total profit as an entrepreneur from one or more businesses after that amount is reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance. For 2026 the percentage is 12,7%. Art. 3.2 defines the taxable profit from business activities (belastbare winst uit onderneming) as the profit reduced by the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption, so the order is profit, then the entrepreneur's allowance, then the SME profit exemption. No hours criterion is needed, because the statute has no such condition and the Tax and Customs Administration page names none. There is no exemption (vrijstelling) on profit as a person entitled to a share. In a loss year the exemption enlarges the fiscal loss, which the Tax and Customs Administration calls disadvantageous. The benefit of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption is capped through the rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing) (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01 and https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Rate adjustment, 11,94% above € 78.426

Art. 2.10 lid 2 and 3: if deductions that reduce the tax base (grondslagverminderende posten), expressly the entrepreneur's allowance of art. 3.74 and the SME profit exemption, are deducted, the tax rises by a percentage of the amount by which taxable box 1 income plus those deductions exceeds the start of the top bracket, but at most that percentage of the deductions. For 2026 the percentage is 11,94% and the start of the top bracket is € 78.426 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01). The maximum value of these deductions is the top rate of 49,50% minus that percentage (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).

### Maximum value of the deductions, 37,56%

The maximum value of the entrepreneur's allowance and the SME profit exemption in 2026 is 37,56%, which is the top rate of 49,50% minus the rate adjustment of 11,94% (https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling).
