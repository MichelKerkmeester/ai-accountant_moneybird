## 2. CHANGES FROM 2025

<a id="5-changes"></a>

- Accommodation (logies) moved from 9% to 21% from 1 January 2026. The change also applies to the linked facilities described above and to relevant advance payments and single-use vouchers. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html), [Belastingdienst logies guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies)

- Culture, media and sport posts remain at 9%. The planned lapse of these reduced-rate posts was withdrawn by Stb. 2025, 339. [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

- The Dutch KOR structure continues with the € 20.000 domestic limit, the current-year and preceding-year turnover test, and the € 2.200 registration threshold without a notice. The checked material identifies no change to these amounts from 2025 to 2026. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, version 2025](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, version 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [2024 decision on the registration threshold](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html)

- The annual-return VAT limit remains less than € 1.883. It was UNVERIFIED in the checked 2025 material and is Verified for 2026. This is a change in verification status, not a confirmed change in the legal amount. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

- Bill 36.920 moved from pending to adopted by both chambers in 2026. It is adopted but not yet in force. [Eerste Kamer bill page](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn)

- The national B2B e-invoicing and reporting timetable was announced as a proposal in 2026. It is not a current 2026 filing duty. [Government announcement](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/11/kabinet-kiest-voor-invoering-e-facturatie-en-rapportage-voor-bedrijven)

- The checked material identifies no other substantive 2026 rate or threshold change from 2025.

## 3. IN THE BOOKS

<a id="6-in-the-books"></a>

- Keep sales and purchase records that support the VAT rates applied, exempt supplies, zero-rated supplies, reverse-charge treatment and input VAT deductions. For intra-EU zero-rated goods, retain proof that the goods left for another EU country, verify the customer's VAT ID and file the ICP accurately and on time. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

- Keep invoices with the required details. Issue business invoices by the 15th day of the following month. Make sure digital invoices preserve authenticity of origin, content and readability. [Belastingdienst invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen), [invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/)

- For a domestic KOR participant, track all Dutch turnover that counts toward the limit, across all activities and subnummers. Do not charge or mention VAT on KOR invoices and do not deduct input VAT. If the threshold is crossed, treat the full crossing supply as taxable and continue with the statutory post-overrun restriction. [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- Report domestic reverse-charged VAT in section (rubriek) 2 and foreign reverse-charged VAT in section 4. Deduct the amount only when the purchase is used for taxable activity. For intra-EU B2B services, check the customer's VAT ID and report the service in the return and ICP when the service belongs there. [Belastingdienst reverse-charge explanation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen), [Belastingdienst intra-EU services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen)

- Reconcile each return period and pay by its due date. A software return can be submitted from the 24th of the month before the period ends. For quarterly filers, check the 2026 dates in section 2. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

- In the final return of the year, account for private use, the business car, the pro rata between taxable and exempt turnover, personnel provisions, gifts and other listed year-end adjustments. If that return has already been submitted, use a supplementary return (suppletie) for the correction. [Belastingdienst final return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Belastingdienst suppletie guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren)

- OSS records must be kept for 10 years. Submit the OSS return even when there are no supplies for the quarter. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

- If a sole trader also owns a BV, check the VAT position and KOR conditions for each entrepreneur involved. The 2026 statute uses the condition that the entrepreneur is established in the Netherlands. The Handbook (Handboek) 2020 expressly identifies legal persons such as a BV as possible KOR participants, but the 2026 statute controls the current conditions. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

## 4. OPEN POINTS AND CONFLICTS

<a id="7-open-points"></a>

- **Monthly VAT trigger:** The Handbook (Handboek) 2020 said that paying more than € 15.000 per quarter generally meant monthly returns. The Handbook (Handboek) 2026 and current period page omit that trigger. Its status for 2026 is unknown and UNVERIFIED. A current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) rule or updated Handbook stating the trigger would settle it. [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [period-change page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw)

- **ICP deadline:** The 2026 ICP pages do not state a deadline. The 2024 guide says domestic ICP reports are due within 1 month after the chosen period, but that timing is not confirmed by the checked 2026 pages. A current 2026 ICP deadline page or current primary guidance would settle it. [2026 ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [2024 ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

- **OSS inactivity:** The claim that 2 years without supplies ends OSS participation while returns continue is UNVERIFIED because the checked page does not say this. A current Dutch Tax and Customs Administration rule on inactivity would settle it. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

- **Lease-car 1,5% calculation:** The current car page says the 1,5% calculation also applies after the fourth year where a car is leased. The Handbook (Handboek) 2026 says the 1,5% calculation does not apply to lease cars and that the calculation remains 2,7%. The decision on private car use does not mention lease cars. A Dutch Tax and Customs Administration ruling or corrected guidance would settle the conflict. [Belastingdienst car page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [decision on private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04)

- **KOR establishment condition:** The Handbook (Handboek) 2026 describes the condition as “established in the Netherlands or have a fixed establishment in the Netherlands.” Art. 25a lid 1 of the statute requires the entrepreneur to be established in the Netherlands. The statute prevails. An updated Handbook or a formal interpretation would settle the discrepancy in the guidance. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- **KOR voluntary exit timing:** The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration exit page says voluntary exit is on the first day of the return period (aangiftetijdvak). The statute says the first day of the next calendar quarter. These coincide for quarterly filers but differ for monthly filers. The statute prevails. Updated guidance that matches the statute would settle the webpage discrepancy. [Belastingdienst KOR exit page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

- **EU ViDA dates:** The EU adoption and publication dates of 11 March 2025 and 25 March 2025, and the stated date of 14 April 2025 for entry into force, remain UNVERIFIED against a Dutch primary source. A Dutch primary publication or statute setting those dates would settle them. [EU ViDA page used in the checked material](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)

- **ViDA OSS legislation:** The 2025 Parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) said OSS legislation was expected in Q3 2026. The checked material does not establish whether that legislation was adopted or published. A current bill or official publication would settle its status. [Kamerbrief on ViDA](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-22112-4090.html)

- **Invoice example:** A separate business.gov.nl example for delivery on 10 January and invoicing by 15 February was not re-read. It is not relied upon here. The verified Handbook example is delivery on 10 April and invoicing by 15 May. Re-reading the original primary invoice guidance would settle that example. [Belastingdienst invoice guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)
