## 2. HOW IT WORKS IN 2026

<a id="3-how-it-works"></a>

### Standard rate

The standard VAT rate is 21%. Apply it to taxable supplies unless the supply qualifies for a reduced rate, the zero rate or an exemption. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 9 lid 1](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### Reduced rate

The 9% rate applies to goods and services in Table I (tabel I). Goods include food, water, flower products, medicines and aids, art, collectibles and antiques, books and periodicals. Services include bicycle, shoe, leather-goods, clothing and household-linen repairs, hairdressing, certain work on homes, camping, access to cultural and recreational events and facilities, performing artists, sport and bathing, passenger transport, qualifying e-books, and access to qualifying news websites and apps. News sites and apps mainly consisting of advertising, video or audible music do not qualify. Named related services include delivering goods, selling food in hospitality (horeca), renting and repairing medical aids, lending books and lending art by the artist. [Tabel I goods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/goederen_met_9_btw), [Tabel I services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/diensten_9_btw/)

### Zero rate

The zero rate applies to qualifying supplies in Table II (tabel II). It is not an exemption. A zero-rated supply keeps the right to deduct input VAT, while an exempt supply does not. An intra-EU supply of goods needs proof in the records that the goods went to another EU country, a buyer with a valid VAT ID from another member state, and an ICP statement (opgaaf ICP) filed on time, correctly and completely. Late or incorrect ICP reporting can cost the zero rate and may lead to a fine. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 9 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst digital ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Accommodation (logies)

From 1 January 2026, accommodation is taxed at 21%. This also applies to payments received in 2025 for stays in 2026 or later, single-use vouchers for such stays, and facilities tied to the accommodation, including gas, electricity, water, sanitary facilities, laundry and parking. Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain at 9%. If one all-in price covers accommodation and separately offered reduced-rate items, split the price using their market values. [Belastingdienst logies guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-logies), [Belastingdienst logies news](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies)

### Separate reduced-rate items and culture, media and sport

Separately offered breakfast, pool access and attraction access remain at 9%. The 2025 legislation withdrew the planned lapse of the culture, media and sport posts, so these posts remain at 9% in 2026. [Belastingdienst logies news](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/berichten/nieuws/btw-logies), [Stb. 2025, 339](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-339.html)

### Solar panels

The 0% rate covers the supply and installation of qualifying solar panels on or near homes. Covered items include non-integrated and integrated panels, cables, PV distributors, mounting materials, optimizers, inverters, removal and reinstallation, and plug-and-play panels. The rate does not cover multi-function panels, solar heat collectors, panels on non-residential business or public buildings, roof reinforcement, cutting cable channels into walls, replacing the whole group cabinet, smart meters, bird or snow guards, replacement equipment bought separately, heat pumps or batteries. Integrated panels supplied with a new-build home are taxed at 21% when the home is also supplied. The panels alone are at 0% if they are supplied and installed separately. [Belastingdienst solar-panel guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_0_btw/btw-tarief-zonnepanelen), [published decision](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-17463.html)

### Exemptions

Art. 11 lid 1, parts (onderdelen) a to w, covers specified supplies and services including immovable property supplies and letting with exceptions, care in institutions, youth work, sport, social and cultural bodies, health care, funeral services, money, securities and fund management, credit and payments, insurance, games of chance, the universal postal service, public broadcasting, education, lectures at cost, composers, writers and journalists, movable goods used only for exempt supplies, organs and blood, member services of interest groups, cost-sharing groups, fundraising and childcare. The Dutch Tax and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst) list also names investment gold and canteens and states that exemptions also apply to imports. Exempt supplies carry no deduction of input VAT. Check the specific exemption and its conditions before applying it. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst exemptions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/vrijstellingen/)

### VAT return periods

Quarterly is the usual period. An entrepreneur may request monthly or yearly filing by letter to the tax office. The tax office replies within 6 weeks and a change starts at the beginning of a new period. Late filing or payment can lead to a forced monthly period. The 2026 sources do not state the € 15.000 quarterly VAT trigger that appeared in the Handbook (Handboek) 2020. Treat that trigger as unverified for 2026. [Period-change guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/wijziging-aangiftetijdvak-btw), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Annual return eligibility

The annual return is available only when all conditions are met. VAT for the year must be less than € 1.883, the business must not have a permit (vergunning) under Article 23, and the business must be a natural person or a partnership of natural persons, such as a civil-law partnership (maatschap) or general partnership (vof). Each of the following must separately be less than € 10.000 a year: intra-community supplies, intra-community services, intra-community acquisitions and received intra-community services. A BV does not meet the natural-person or partnership condition. [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Return and payment deadlines

Monthly and quarterly returns and payments are due by the last day of the month after the period. The annual return is due before 1 April of the following year. For the 2026 tax year, the annual return deadline is 31 March 2027. The filing date (aangiftedatum) is the date the return is received. Filing and payment use the same deadline. An extension is available only for a calamity. Software returns may be submitted from the 24th of the month before the period ends. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums), [return guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/)

### Domestic 2026 filing dates

Quarterly return deadlines are 30 April 2026 for Q1, 31 July 2026 for Q2, 31 October 2026 for Q3 and 31 January 2027 for Q4. Monthly returns are due on the last day of the following month. The 2026 schedule specifically gives 28 February 2026 for January and 31 January 2027 for December. [Belastingdienst deadlines](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/uiterste-aangifte-en-betaaldatums)

### Foreign-established business filing dates and penalties

The published schedule for foreign-established businesses gives quarterly deadlines of 31 May 2026 for Q1, 31 August 2026 for Q2, 30 November 2026 for Q3 and 28 February 2027 for Q4. The page for foreign-established businesses states a late-filing fine of € 82 and a late-payment fine of 3% of unpaid VAT, with a minimum of € 50 and maximum of € 6.709. The checked source is specific to foreign-established businesses. Do not generalise these fine amounts to a domestic ZZP case based on this source. [Foreign-business deadlines and penalties](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/uiterste_ontvangstdatum_aangifte_en_betaling_btw_aangifte)

### Dutch KOR conditions and turnover

A business established in the Netherlands may choose the KOR if its Dutch annual turnover is no more than € 20.000 in the application year and the preceding calendar year. The statute defines annual turnover by consideration during a calendar year and excludes business-used immovable property and depreciable movable assets. The KOR applies to all of the entrepreneur's activities and sub-numbers (subnummers). Count Dutch-taxed supplies, including 0% supplies, domestic supplies reverse-charged to a customer, and specified Dutch exempt supplies relating to immovable property, means of payment and securities, and financial and insurance services. Do not count other exempt turnover, VAT on private use, sale of business-used investment goods, domestic supplies reverse-charged to the business as buyer, supplies taxed in another country or intra-community acquisitions. Before joining, measure turnover excluding VAT. During participation, use the total amount charged. The KOR gives no input VAT deduction and the invoice must not mention VAT. A BV may qualify as an entrepreneur if it meets the conditions. The statute requires establishment in the Netherlands. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, arts. 25 and 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden), [Handboek Ondernemen 2020 on legal persons](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2020_aa9701z01fd.pdf)

### KOR registration without a notice

A business not yet registered with the inspector or KVK, and not required to register, may use the KOR without a notice if annual turnover is at most € 2.200 and the other statutory conditions are met. It must not use the exemption in other member states. [Decision of 21 November 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-38565.html), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25b](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2025-01-01)

### KOR start, exit and cut-offs

Apply only after receiving the VAT number (omzetbelastingnummer) and the VAT ID (btw-id). The notice must arrive at least 4 weeks before the requested start. A quarterly filer has cut-offs of 3 March for a 1 April start, 2 June for a 1 July start, 2 September for a 1 October start and 3 December for a 1 January start. A late notice starts in the next quarter. Returns continue until the confirmed start date. Confirmation is sent within 8 weeks. There is no statutory minimum participation term. Voluntary exit must be at least 4 weeks ahead. Under the statute, exit takes effect on the first day of the next calendar quarter. Re-entry is barred for the exit year and the following calendar year. [KOR application guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/aanmelden-kor), [KOR exit guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/afmelden-kor), [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01)

### KOR overrun

If turnover exceeds the Dutch ceiling, the supply that crosses it is taxed in full. The exemption then does not apply for the rest of that calendar year or the following calendar year. Re-entry after a voluntary exit is also barred for the exit year and the following year. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 25a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### EU KOR

A business established in the EU but not in the Netherlands may qualify for the Dutch exemption when its Union turnover is at most € 100.000. EU KOR participation in another member state requires prior notification and an EX number. Participation starts when the inspector issues or confirms that number, normally within 35 working days. The inspector may refuse participation when Union turnover exceeds the limit, another state reports that its limit was exceeded, the conditions are not met or additional time is needed to check for fraud. Quarterly totals are reported for each state, including a zero where there is no turnover. A separate notice is due within 15 working days after Union turnover exceeds the limit. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, arts. 25c to 25e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst EU KOR guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/deelnemen-aan-de-eu-kor-en-andere-btw-regelingen), [EU KOR quarterly turnover](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/opgaaf-kwartaalomzet)

### Supplementary return (suppletie)

Correct VAT returns for the past 5 calendar years. Submit a correction within 8 weeks after discovering an error, and before the point at which the taxpayer knows or should suspect that the inspector knows. The earlier limit governs. A correction of € 1.000 or less may be reported in the next VAT return. A correction of more than € 1.000 requires the supplementary return (suppletie) within the applicable reporting deadline. The last return already filed must be corrected through a supplementary return (suppletie). There is no tax interest (belastingrente) when the correction is filed within 3 months after the year or corrected within that year. A 2024 period can be corrected through the end of 2029. Failure to report is an offence. For intent or gross negligence, the penalty for gross negligence or intent (vergrijpboete) can be up to 100% of the VAT. [UBOB art. 15, version 2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Eindejaarsbesluit 2024](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-441.html), [Belastingdienst correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren/aangifte_corrigeren), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Reverse-charge rules

The usual rule is that the supplier pays VAT. The charge shifts to the customer for specified supplies by non-established suppliers and for designated domestic transactions. A Dutch fixed establishment that is not involved in the supply can be treated as non-established. The listed cases include subcontracting and staff lending in construction, shipbuilding, cleaning and gardening, waste and scrap, specified electronics, certain immovable property transactions, execution sales, gas and electricity certificates, gold, emission rights and telecom services between providers in the Netherlands. The electronics rule covers mobile phones, chips before end-product, game consoles, tablets and laptops when the total is at least € 10.000 per delivery per type of good. The invoice states “btw verlegd”, the customer's VAT ID and the consideration per rate. The supplier remains responsible. The customer reports domestic reverse charge in section (rubriek) 2 or foreign reverse charge in section 4 and deducts it when used for taxable activity. Subcontracting exceptions include work performed more than half in the supplier's own workshop, staff lent for such work, subordinate work on sold goods and disputes sent to the main contractor's tax office. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 12](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [UBOB, Afdeling E](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002633/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst cases](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/wanneer_btw_verleggen/), [How reverse charge works](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_berekenen_aan_uw_klanten/waarover_btw_berekenen/verleggingsregeling/hoe_werkt_btw_verleggen)

### Intra-EU B2B services

For a qualifying service to a business customer in another EU country, VAT is generally due in the customer's country. Check the customer's VAT ID, issue the invoice without Dutch VAT with “btw verlegd”, report the supply in the VAT return and report it in the ICP statement (opgaaf ICP). Some services are exceptions. Services exempt or zero-rated in the customer's country, OSS services, property-related services, passenger transport and admission to cultural, sport and similar events are not listed in the ICP under the cited guidance. [Belastingdienst intra-EU services](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten/btw_berekenen_bij_diensten_aan_afnemers_in_andere_eu_landen), [ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### ICP statement (opgaaf ICP)

Report intra-EU supplies and services, own goods moved to another EU country and call-off stock transfers. The total should equal rubriek 3b for the same period. Report goods in the period of the invoice date and services in the period the service was supplied. Do not file an ICP for a period with nothing to report. Put corrections in the next ICP. Goods are reported monthly unless the threshold test for quarterly filing is met. If the threshold is crossed in the first month, file 3 monthly reports. If crossed in the second month, file for the first two months and then the last month. If crossed in the third month, file one quarterly report and monthly reports afterwards. Quarterly filing can resume after 4 consecutive qualifying quarters, starting from the fifth quarter if it also qualifies. Services may be reported monthly, quarterly or yearly with a permit. The goods threshold does not apply to services. Combined goods and services follow the goods rules. The cited 2024 guide describes annual ICP eligibility and due dates, but the checked 2026 ICP pages do not state the current due date. Call-off stock requires an identified buyer, the buyer's VAT ID, own transport, no establishment in the destination state, supply within 12 months of arrival, a register and a per-buyer listing in the ICP for the period transport began. [Belastingdienst ICP guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/opgaaf_icp), [ICP period guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/goederen_en_diensten_naar_andere_eu_landen/opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp/tijdvak_opgaaf_icp), [2024 ICP explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting-digitale-opgaaf-intracomm-pres-ob1291t41fd.pdf)

### EU distance sales and OSS

The € 10.000 threshold applies to qualifying suppliers established in one member state. It covers qualifying goods sent from the Netherlands directly to non-VAT-filing customers in another EU country when the supplier arranges or pays for transport, excluding margin goods, nearly new means of transport and assembly supplies. Digital consumer services also count. Measure the threshold excluding VAT. Below the threshold, Dutch VAT applies under the normal rules unless the supplier opts for foreign VAT. When the threshold is exceeded, foreign VAT applies from the crossing supply. Dutch VAT can resume from 1 January after a full calendar year at or below the threshold. The threshold does not apply when the supplier has a fixed establishment in another EU country. A warehouse alone is not a fixed establishment.

The Union scheme (Unieregeling) OSS is optional. It allows one quarterly return for EU distance sales, but foreign input VAT cannot be reclaimed through that return. The return is due by the last day of the following month, including a nil return. The scheme normally begins on the first day of the quarter after registration. To include a first supply, registration must arrive by the 10th day of the month after that supply. Corrections go in a later return within 3 years of the original due date. Keep OSS records for 10 years. Use euros and the ECB rate for the last day of the quarter. Three consecutive missed returns can lead to exclusion for 2 years. [Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, art. 6k](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002629/2026-01-01), [Belastingdienst distance sales](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-eu-particulieren), [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem)

### Import scheme (invoerregeling)

The Import scheme is monthly and applies to consignments up to € 150. Three consecutive missed monthly returns can lead to exclusion for 2 years. It cannot be combined with the KOR under the adopted ViDA bill's stated rule, which is not yet in force. [Belastingdienst OSS reporting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-melden-eenloketsysteem), [Eerste Kamer bill 36.920](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36920_wet_implementatie_richtlijn)

### Private use and the final return

Private use includes use by the entrepreneur's family. It can arise from mixed business and private use, such as a car, business computer or partly private building, or from withdrawing goods for exclusively private use. Staff private use can limit deduction under the BUA. Settle private use of goods and services, the car, the pro rata for taxable and exempt turnover, personnel provisions, gifts and business gifts above € 227 in the last return of the year, usually Q4 or December. The Handbook (Handboek) also names gas, water and electricity, deemed services (fictieve diensten) and adjustments (herzieningen). If the last return has already been filed, correct it with a supplementary return (suppletie). [Belastingdienst private use](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/privegebruik), [last VAT return of the year](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/laatste-btw-aangifte-van-het-jaar), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### Business car private use

Without records of actual private use, the standard amount is 2,7% of the catalogue price including VAT and bpm. It applies to cars in business assets and cars obtained another way. The amount is capped at VAT deducted in the year plus 1/5 of purchase VAT during the applicable period. Use 1,5% when no purchase VAT could be deducted or after the fourth year following the year the entrepreneur began using the car. Commuting counts as private use. Exempt use reduces the amount proportionally. A part-year is pro-rated. Put the adjustment in the last return of the year. The lease-car scope of the 1,5% rule is disputed in the checked sources. [Belastingdienst business car guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak), [decision on private car use](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0043783/2020-07-04), [Handboek Ondernemen 2026](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek_ondernemen_2026_aa9701z62fd.pdf)

### BUA gifts and benefits

The BUA excludes deduction for certain staff and gift expenses, including representation, business gifts and other gifts, staff housing, wages in kind, sport, leisure and private transport, except making a car available. The exclusion does not apply up to the VAT owed on an amount charged to the recipient. Provisions to the same person with total cost of no more than € 227 per accounting year stay outside the exclusion. The recipient's own contribution does not reduce the tested amount. Gifts are affected when the recipient would have had less than 30% deduction. [BUA, version in force from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23), [Belastingdienst threshold guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/personeelsvoorzieningen_en_relatiegeschenken/drempelbedrag_personeelsvoorzieningen_giften_en_relatiegeschenken), [decision on own contribution](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2023-28124.html)

### BUA exceptions and staff meals

Carve-outs include food and drink for staff, licensed private bus transport (besloten busvervoer) for commuting, qualifying commuting bicycles and outplacement. The bicycle carve-out is limited to € 749 including VAT and has conditions on prior bicycles and other travel arrangements. For meals supplied below cost plus 25%, deduction is excluded up to 9% of the difference. [BUA, version in force from 23 February 2019](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002636/2019-02-23)

### Invoicing

Issue an invoice for supplies to another entrepreneur and to a legal person that is not an entrepreneur. No invoice is required for private customers except in specified cases. Issue the invoice by the 15th day of the month after delivery. VAT is due at the latest in the period in which the customer pays. An invoice must identify both parties, the invoice date, a unique consecutive number, the goods or services, the supply or prepayment date, amounts excluding VAT per rate, the rate and VAT amount. It must also include the VAT ID with NL and the KVK number when registered. A trade name may be used when it is registered with the address at KVK. A post office box alone is not enough. A simplified invoice is allowed up to € 100 including VAT or for a correction invoice, subject to required details. It is not allowed for intra-EU supplies, distance sales or certain reverse-charged supplies. Digital invoicing requires customer agreement and assurance of origin, content and readability. ### Cash accounting scheme (kasstelsel)

Cash accounting scheme is compulsory for listed trades, including shops and web shops (webshops), market traders, hairdressers and hospitality. It can be requested when more than 80% of sales go to consumers. When a customer pays before the invoice, VAT is due in the period of payment. ### E-invoicing

There is no general B2B e-invoicing duty in the checked 2026 guidance. Paper or digital invoicing is allowed under the invoice conditions. Suppliers to central government must send e-invoices. Municipalities, provinces and water boards may require them. The page does not give a start date. ### ViDA and the proposed national B2B timetable

The 2025 Parliamentary letter (kamerbrief) stated that ViDA e-invoicing and digital reporting should take effect on 1 July 2030, with platform rules by 30 June 2028 and a possible deferral to 1 January 2030. The Dutch government announced a national B2B plan for e-invoicing from 1 July 2030 and reporting from 1 July 2031. The national plan is proposed. KOR businesses are proposed to be exempt. ### Bill 36.920

The House of Representatives (Tweede Kamer) adopted bill 36.920 on 17 September 2026 and the Senate (Eerste Kamer) adopted it on 6 October 2026. It has no Bulletin of Acts and Decrees (Staatsblad) yet. Its stated entry into force is 1 January 2027, or the day after publication with retroactive effect to 1 January 2027 if it is published later. Articles I and II concern rules from 1 January 2027, including the rule that the Import scheme cannot be combined with the KOR. Article III is stated to take effect on 1 July 2028 and Article IV on 1 July 2029. No new call-off shipments are allowed from 1 July 2028. These provisions are adopted but not yet in force for 2026. ### Corona tax-debt repayment arrangement

The special arrangement covers debts built up from 12 March 2020 through 31 March 2022. Repayment runs from 1 October 2022 to 1 October 2027, with the first instalment due 31 October 2022 and 60 monthly instalments. Quarterly payment is possible. One pause of up to 6 months or 2 quarters may be requested. Extension to 84 months is available only for a scheme of at least € 10.000. For recipients of the “Afronding UHT en einde invorderingspauze” letter, the 60 months run from 1 July 2025.
