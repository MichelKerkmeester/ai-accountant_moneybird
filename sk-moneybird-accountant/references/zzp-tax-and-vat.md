# Dutch ZZP tax and VAT reference

**Rule:** These figures change every January. Check the current year before relying on one. This is not tax advice.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This reference summarizes general rules confirmed or announced in the checked research through 2026. It does not decide personal eligibility or replace the tax authority's current instructions. Each year-specific value includes a year and a direct source URL.

### Depreciation and buildings

Depreciation is based on acquisition cost, residual value, and expected useful life. The general annual ceiling for most depreciable assets is 20% of acquisition cost. Goodwill has a 10% annual ceiling. Land is not depreciated. An asset below €450 can generally be expensed at once. See the Belastingdienst pages on [depreciation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/), [annual caps](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/hoe_berekent_u_het_bedrag_van_de_afschrijving), and [land and buildings](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2025/winst_en_verliesrekening_en_balans).

### VAT returns and corrections

The tax authority assigns monthly, quarterly, or annual periods. Quarterly filing and payment are due by the last day of the month after the quarter. An annual return is due before 1 April of the following year. Confirm the assigned period and current deadline in the official notice. See [VAT filing and payment](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/btw-aangifte-waar-moet-u-aan-denken/).

A correction of €1,000 or less may be included in a later VAT return. A larger correction requires a supplementary return. The official correction guidance covers the current year and previous five years, and a payable correction should be made within eight weeks after discovery. The historical start date of the €1,000 rule is unverified. Do not rely on a guessed start date. See [correction guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aangifte_doen_en_betalen/aangifte_corrigeren) and the [supplementary return explanation](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/toelichting_suppletie_btw_ob1431t21pl.pdf).

### KOR and cross-border activity

The Dutch KOR threshold is €20,000 per calendar year for 2020 to 2026. Under the KOR, a participant does not charge Dutch VAT and cannot deduct input VAT. Periodic returns normally stop for ordinary domestic activity, with exceptions for some cross-border transactions.

### Deductible VAT and private use

VAT on private expenditure and exempt or non-taxable activity is not deductible. VAT on meals and drinks consumed in hospitality venues is not deductible. The 0% rate is a taxable rate and may allow input VAT deduction under the usual rules. See [non-deductible VAT](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/welke_btw_is_aftrekbaar/welke_btw_mag_u_niet_aftrekken) and [hospitality expenses](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/welke_btw_is_aftrekbaar/bijzondere_situaties/uitgaven_in_de_horeca).

A business car can require an annual private-use VAT correction. The researched flat rates are 2.7% of catalogue value including VAT and BPM, or 1.5% in specified cases such as after more than four years in use. Mixed-use assets may require no deduction, partial deduction, or a later private-use correction depending on the facts. Refer the classification and calculation to the operator or bookkeeper. See [private-use car VAT](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/btw_en_de_auto/privegebruik_auto_van_de_zaak/) and [mixed-use assets](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/btw_aftrekken/privegebruik/gemengd_gebruik/).

### Home workspace and equity movements

A home workspace is deductible only under the rules for a qualifying independent workspace or where the home or workspace is properly treated as business property. An independent workspace generally has its own entrance and sanitary facilities and can be rented separately. A desk in a living room or bedroom does not qualify by itself. See [home workspace rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/werkruimte_in_de_woning).

Private withdrawals and proprietor contributions are equity movements. They are not sales revenue or operating expenses. Private use of a business product or service may require a separate tax adjustment. Record the supporting facts and escalate the treatment. See [private withdrawals and deposits](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privestortingen_en_priveonttrekkingen).

## 5. ADMINISTRATION DUTIES

Core business records are generally retained for at least seven years. Immovable-property records generally have a ten-year retention period. Keep sales and purchase invoices, bank and cash records, contracts, stock records where relevant, VAT records, and data supporting income, deductions, assets, and declarations. Digital source invoices should remain in their received digital form. Digital records must remain accessible, readable, complete, and authentic. See the [administration duty](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/administratie-bijhouden/administratie-bijhouden) and [invoice retention rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/uw_facturen_bewaren).

An invoice identifies the supplier and customer by legal name and address, includes the supplier VAT ID and applicable Chamber of Commerce number, shows a date and unique consecutive invoice number, describes the supply and quantity or extent, gives a different supply or advance-payment date when applicable, lists the unit price and net amount by VAT rate, and states the VAT rate and amount. See [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/).
